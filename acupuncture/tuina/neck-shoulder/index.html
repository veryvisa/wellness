After an afternoon at the desk: self-massage for the neck and shoulders伏案一下午之后：颈肩的自我按摩

Four in the afternoon, your neck feels set in concrete and your shoulders have crept up without you noticing. This routine is done sitting in your chair, in eight minutes. Its biggest use is the reminder it gives you: time to stand up and change position.下午四点，脖子像灌了水泥，肩膀一直耸着自己都没发觉。这套按摩可以坐在椅子上做，八分钟。它最大的用处其实是提醒你：该站起来、换个姿势了。

⏱ About 8 minutes约 8 分钟 · After an hour or two of sitting; on a morning with a stiff neck, the last two steps also work.连续坐了一两个小时之后；落枕的早上也可以做后两步。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Neck pain with lasting numbness or weakness in one or both arms, dropping things, or unsteady walking颈痛伴一侧或双侧手臂持续麻木、无力，拿东西会掉，或走路不稳
  • Neck pain after a fall, car crash or other injury: see a doctor first; do not massage it yourself摔倒、车祸等外伤之后的颈痛：先看医生，不要自己按
  • Neck pain with fever or weight loss, or pain that wakes you at night and does not ease with any position颈痛伴发热、体重下降，或夜里痛醒、换什么姿势都不减轻
  • Sudden severe neck pain with headache, vomiting, slurred speech or double vision: call 911突然出现的剧烈颈痛伴头痛、呕吐、说话不清或视物重影：拨 911
  • Shoulder or arm pain with chest tightness, sweating or shortness of breath may be the heart: call 911肩臂痛伴胸闷、出汗、气短：可能是心脏问题，拨 911

✋ When not to press这些情况先别按

  • Do not crack your own neck, and do not let anyone without a licence do a 'click' neck manipulation on you不要自己扳脖子、也不要让没有执照的人给你做「咔」一声的扳颈手法
  • In pregnancy, press GB21 Jianjing only lightly: a traditional pregnancy caution孕期按肩井（GB21）要轻，这是传统的孕期禁忌
  • With osteoporosis or after neck surgery, do not press the neck firmly骨质疏松、颈椎做过手术的人不用力按颈部
  • If you take blood thinners, press lightly在吃抗凝药的人力度放轻

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1
    GB21 Jiānjǐng肩井Grasping拿法

    Reach your right hand across to the top of your left shoulder, thumb in front and fingers behind, and lift and release the muscle on top of the shoulder (around GB21 Jianjing); then switch sides.右手越过胸前放到左肩上，拇指在前、四指在后，把肩膀顶上那块肌肉（肩井一带）提起、放下；做完换边。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side, lift and release每侧约 1 分钟，一提一松

  2. 2
    GB20 Fēngchí风池Kneading揉法

    Cup the back of your head and place both thumbs in the hollows on either side under the base of the skull (GB20 Fengchi); knead upward, angled slightly toward the opposite eye.双手抱头，两拇指放在后脑勺下缘两侧的凹陷（风池），向上、略朝对侧眼睛的方向按揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

    ⚠️ Light to moderate pressure is enough; do not drive deep into the base of the skull. Stop at once if you feel dizzy or your vision dims.轻到中等力度即可，不要往颅底深处用力顶；出现头晕、眼前发黑马上停。

  3. 3
    Kneading揉法

    Put four fingers together on the muscles on each side of the back of the neck and knead section by section from the top down to the shoulders.四指并拢放在颈后两侧的肌肉上，从上往下一段一段揉到肩膀。

    How much力度与时间 3–5 passes from top to bottom从上到下 3–5 遍

  4. 4
    SI3 Hòuxī后溪Pressing按法

    Make a loose fist and find SI3 Houxi in the hollow at the end of the palm crease, just behind the knuckle of the little finger; hold it with the other thumb while you slowly turn your head left and right and nod down and up, only within a pain-free range.握一个松拳，在小指根部关节后面、掌横纹尽头的凹陷找到后溪；用另一手拇指按住，同时慢慢左右转头、低头抬头，只在不痛的范围里动。

    How much力度与时间 5 times in each direction; once with each hand每个方向 5 次；两手各做一遍

  5. 5
    EX-UE8 Wàiláogōng外劳宫（落枕）Pressing按法

    For a stiff neck, find EX-UE8 Wailaogong (the 'stiff-neck point') on the back of the hand between the bones of the index and middle fingers, about half a cun behind the knuckles; hold it and move your neck slowly as before.落枕时，在手背食指与中指的掌骨之间、指根关节后约半寸找到外劳宫（又叫落枕穴），按住后同样慢慢活动脖子。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

  6. 6

    Sit tall and slide your chin straight back (as if making a double chin), hold 3 seconds, relax.坐直，下巴水平向后收（像做双下巴），停 3 秒再放松。

    How much力度与时间 10 times10 次

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 A Cochrane review of 15 trials of massage for neck pain rated all the evidence low or very low: very low-level evidence suggested some techniques (including Chinese massage) did better than control on function and tenderness, but the authors could make no firm statement to guide practice.Cochrane 系统综述汇总了 15 项颈痛按摩试验，证据等级全部是低或极低：有极低等级证据显示某些按摩（包括中式推拿）在功能和压痛上比对照好，但作者无法给出指导临床的明确结论。 [1]
  • E5 Soreness after massage is the most common problem. Forceful neck manipulation is a different matter: it is reserved for licensed therapists and carries a rare but serious risk to blood vessels.按摩后的酸痛是最常见的不适；颈部用力扳动另当别论，那只该由持照的专业人员操作，有罕见但严重的血管风险。

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 GB21 and GB20 are the points tradition reaches for first on the neck and shoulders; SI3 is one of the eight confluent points and is traditionally linked to the Governor Vessel, which is why the neck is moved while SI3 is held. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.肩井、风池是传统上颈肩部最常用的穴；后溪是八脉交会穴，传统认为通督脉，所以常在按后溪时配合活动脖子。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
