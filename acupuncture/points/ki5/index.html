    Finger cun手指同身寸

    First locate KI3 between the medial malleolus and calcaneal tendon, then measure 1 cun directly toward the heel using the width of the patient's thumb interphalangeal joint; finger width is only a rough guide, and the bony hollow plus vertical alignment with KI3 decides.先定内踝尖与跟腱之间的太溪（KI3），再以患者拇指指间关节横纹宽（1 寸）向足底跟骨方向直量 1 寸；指宽仅为辅助参考，最终以骨性凹陷与太溪垂直关系为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting superiorly or anteriorly toward KI3 or below the medial malleolus: lands near the lower border of KI3 or peri-malleolar soft tissue rather than the medial calcaneal hollow, closer to posterior tibial neurovascular branches.取偏靠前上方（靠近太溪或内踝下缘）：容易误入太溪下方边缘或内踝下方软组织，偏离跟骨内侧面郄穴定位，且更易贴近胫后动脉后支走行区。
  • Drifting inferiorly or posteriorly toward the calcaneal tendon insertion or weight-bearing sole: enters the fat pad or thick weight-bearing skin of the heel, causing sharper pain and higher infection risk if fissures or calluses are present.取偏靠后下方（贴近跟腱止点或足底负重缘）：进入跟腱前缘脂肪垫或足底负重皮肤区，足底皮肤角质厚、压痛敏感，进针疼痛明显且易在足跟皮肤有裂口或胼胝时引发感染。

Where is KI5 Shuǐquán?水泉（KI5）在哪里？

In the heel region, 1 cun directly below KI3, in the depression on the medial side of the calcaneal tuberosity.跟区，太溪直下 1 寸，跟骨结节内侧凹陷中。

How is KI5 needled?水泉（KI5）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of KI5?水泉（KI5）是哪类特定穴？

Xi-cleft point郄穴

Main safety point at KI5?水泉（KI5）最要注意的安全问题？
