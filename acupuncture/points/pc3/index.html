📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine, palm up and elbow flexed about 30–60 degrees so the crease and biceps tendon are clear.坐位或仰卧，掌心向上，肘屈约 30–60°，使肘横纹与肱二头肌腱清楚显露。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the middle of the elbow crease and the palpable biceps tendon; PC3 lies in the hollow at its medial (ulnar, little-finger) edge.先找肘横纹中段及可触及的肱二头肌腱；曲泽在肘横纹内侧（尺侧、小指侧）腱缘的凹陷中。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Flex the elbow so the biceps tendon stands out; PC3 hugs the tendon's ulnar edge. The brachial artery runs just deep and ulnar to the tendon, with the median nerve further medial, so the artery lies almost directly beneath the point. Feel for the pulse first and place the needle on its radial side against the tendon; bloodletting here targets the superficial cubital veins, never this artery.屈肘让肱二头肌腱绷起，曲泽紧贴腱的尺侧缘。肱动脉就在腱尺侧的深面下行，正中神经再偏内侧——穴位正下方附近就是动脉。先用指腹摸到搏动，进针点放在搏动的桡侧、贴着腱缘；刺络放血刺的是肘窝浅静脉，绝不是这条动脉。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a fingertip from the biceps tendon toward the little-finger side; the shallow hollow just off the tendon edge is PC3. A pulse is usually felt right there or just medial to it: that is not a mislocation but a warning to keep the needle against the tendon and away from the pulse.指腹从肱二头肌腱向小指侧轻滑，刚离开腱缘的浅凹就是曲泽；搏动通常就在指下或紧挨内侧——这不是取错，而是提醒针要贴腱缘、避开搏动。

  4. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    PC3 is located on the elbow crease and needs no proportional measurement. The patient's finger width may help orient the tendon, but the crease and ulnar tendon edge determine the point.曲泽位于肘横纹，不需折量；以患者自身指宽粗核肱二头肌腱位置即可，最终以肘横纹和腱的尺侧缘为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • The radial side of the biceps tendon is LU5.   • With the elbow extended the tendon is less distinct, increasing the chance of landing on a superficial vein or off the crease; flex the elbow to expose the tendon first.伸肘时肌腱不清，容易误取到肘窝浅静脉或偏离横纹；应屈肘显腱后再定位。

Where is PC3 Qūzé?曲泽（PC3）在哪里？

At the elbow crease, on the ulnar side of the biceps tendon.肘横纹上，肱二头肌腱尺侧缘。

How is PC3 needled?曲泽（PC3）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun, or prick to bleed.直刺；1–1.5 寸，或点刺出血。

Special-point roles of PC3?曲泽（PC3）是哪类特定穴？

He-sea point, water合穴，属水

Main safety point at PC3?曲泽（PC3）最要注意的安全问题？

The brachial artery runs right along the ulnar side of the biceps tendon, almost directly beneath the point, with the median nerve medial to it: feel for the pulse first, insert hugging the tendon edge away from the pulse, and when bleeding prick only the superficial vein.肱动脉紧贴肱二头肌腱尺侧下行，几乎就在穴位正下方，正中神经在其内侧：进针前先摸搏动，针贴腱缘、避开搏动；刺络只刺浅静脉。
