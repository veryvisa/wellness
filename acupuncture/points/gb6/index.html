📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated upright or in a lateral-recumbent/leaning seated position with the head steady, exposing the temporal hairline region and keeping the masseter relaxed.正坐位或侧伏坐位，头部放正，显露侧头部鬓发区，咬肌放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First establish the two endpoints: anterosuperiorly at ST8 (4.5 cun lateral to the anterior hairline midpoint) and posteroinferiorly at GB7 (where the vertical line along the anterior auricular border meets the horizontal line at the ear apex). Along the curved line following the temporal hairline, locate GB6 at the junction of the lower one-quarter and upper three-quarters.先定两端点：前上方为额角发际点头维（ST8，前发际正中旁开 4.5 寸），后下方为耳前鬓发后缘的曲鬓（GB7，耳廓前缘垂直线与耳尖水平线交点）。沿鬓发弧形连线，在曲鬓上方 1/4、头维下方 3/4 的交点处取穴。

  2. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The transverse proportional distance between the two temporal hairlines (ST8 to ST8) is 9 cun; use this to verify ST8 (4.5 cun from the midline), then divide the curved ST8–GB7 line into four equal parts. GB6 lies at the lower one-quarter mark, halfway between GB5 (midpoint) and GB7 (lower end).两额角发际（头维之间）横向骨度折作 9 寸，先据此校准头维（旁开正中线 4.5 寸），再与曲鬓作弧形四等分。悬厘在头维与曲鬓弧形连线的下 1/4 处，居悬颅（中点）与曲鬓（下端）之间。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate the superficial temporal fossa superior to the zygomatic arch and anterosuperior to the auricle; asking the patient to clench lightly reveals the contracting belly of the temporalis muscle. GB6 sits in the epicranial aponeurosis/superficial temporal fascia overlying the temporalis; immediately deep and posteroinferior to it run the parietal/frontal bifurcation of the superficial temporal artery and branches of the auriculotemporal nerve—palpate and avoid the arterial pulse before inserting.在颧弓上方、耳廓前上方的颞窝浅层触摸，可触及颞肌肌腹，让患者做轻度咬牙动作即可感知颞肌收缩隆起。悬厘位于颞肌浅面的帽状腱膜/颞浅筋膜层内；其深面及后下方紧邻颞浅动脉顶支（或额支分叉附近）搏动与耳颞神经分支，针刺前务必指切避开动脉搏动线。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Find GB7 where the level of the ear apex meets the anterior hairline of the ear, then move upward along the curved temporal hairline by roughly one finger-breadth (about 0.7–1 cun, adjusted for head size) to reach GB6, sitting directly midway between GB5 (the midpoint of the arc) and GB7.先找耳尖水平线与耳廓前缘交点的曲鬓（GB7），再向上沿着鬓角发际弧线量约一横指（约 0.7–1 寸，视头颅大小微调）即达悬厘；它正好卡在悬颅（弧线中点）与曲鬓的正中间。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Glide a fingertip up and down just inside the temporal hairline arc to find a taut band or distinctly tender spot (especially pronounced during migraine attacks), while checking for arterial pulsation beneath the pad so you can needle beside any vessel.指腹沿鬓角发际内侧弧线上下轻推，常可触及一处条索状紧张或明显酸胀的敏感点（偏头痛发作期尤为显著），同时注意指下有无血管搏动感，避开搏动点旁开取穴。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting anterosuperiorly to the midpoint of the curve: this lands on GB5 (Xuanlu), which is on the same channel but higher along the arc.向前上方偏取至弧线中点：误取为悬颅（GB5），虽同属足少阳经，但解剖位置偏高。
  • Drifting posteroinferiorly down to the level of the ear apex: this hits GB7 (Qubin), sitting closer to the anterior auricle and the main trunk of the superficial temporal artery.向后下方偏取至耳尖水平线处：误取为曲鬓（GB7），更靠近耳廓前缘及颞浅动脉主干。
  • Shifting posteriorly outside the temporal hairline toward the preauricular groove: this leaves the Gallbladder arc and risks puncturing the main superficial temporal artery or auriculotemporal nerve trunk, causing a scalp hematoma or sharp radiating pain.向后偏出鬓发外侧（耳前沟）：偏离足少阳经弧线，易直接刺中颞浅动脉主干或耳颞神经主干，引发头皮血肿或剧烈放射性刺痛。
  • Needling perpendicularly or too deeply: subcutaneous tissue over the temple is thin; perpendicular insertion hits the cranial periosteum with sharp pain or pierces superficial temporal vessels. Always insert transversely posteriorly along the scalp to 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺过深：侧头部皮下组织薄，直刺易穿透颞肌触及颅骨骨膜引发剧痛，甚至刺破颞浅血管，必须沿头皮向后平刺 0.3–0.5 寸。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Migraine (with or without aura, throbbing unilateral temporal headache)偏头痛（无先兆或有先兆偏头痛，侧头部搏动性痛）

Typically presents as unilateral throbbing moderate-to-severe temporal headache with nausea, photophobia, and phonophobia aggravated by routine activity. Differentiate from giant cell (temporal) arteritis; new-onset headache in patients over 50 with a tender, thickened temporal artery or jaw claudication requires immediate medical referral.典型表现为单侧颞部搏动性中重度头痛，常伴恶心、呕吐、畏光畏声，活动后加重。需与巨细胞动脉炎（颞动脉炎）鉴别；若为 50 岁以上新发头痛伴颞动脉触痛增粗或咀嚼痛，必须立即转诊就医。

Auriculotemporal neuralgia / entrapment耳颞神经痛 / 耳颞神经卡压

Presents as paroxysmal stabbing or burning pain from the preauricular region into the temple (around GB6–GB7), often triggered by touching the sideburn or chewing; differentiate from temporomandibular disorder (TMD) and trigeminal neuralgia.表现为耳前至颞部（悬厘、曲鬓一带）阵发性针刺样、烧灼样痛，触摸鬓角或咀嚼时可诱发，需与颞下颌关节紊乱（TMD）及三叉神经痛鉴别。

Temporalis myofascial pain syndrome (associated with TMD)颞肌肌筋膜疼痛综合征（与颞下颌关节紊乱 TMD 相关）

Clenching, bruxism, or stress strains the anterior/middle temporalis fibers; palpating near GB6 reveals taut bands referring pain to the orbit and upper teeth. Limited jaw opening or joint clicking suggests concurrent TMD.紧咬牙、夜磨牙或压力过大导致颞肌前中束劳损，按压悬厘附近可触及紧绷肌束并向眼眶、上颌牙齿牵涉痛，张口受限或关节弹响提示合并 TMD。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is GB6 Xuánlí?悬厘（GB6）在哪里？

On the head, at the junction of the upper three quarters and lower quarter of the curved line from ST8 to GB7 (following the curve of the temple hairline).头部，从头维至曲鬓的弧形连线（其弧度与鬓发弧度相应）的上 3/4 与下 1/4 的交点处。

How is GB6 needled?悬厘（GB6）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of GB6?悬厘（GB6）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Hand/Foot-Shaoyang and Foot-Yangming channels手足少阳、足阳明交会穴

Main safety point at GB6?悬厘（GB6）最要注意的安全问题？

Thread transversely under the scalp aponeurosis; feel for the superficial temporal pulse first and press after withdrawal.沿头皮帽状腱膜下平刺；先触颞浅动脉搏动再进针，出针按压防血肿。
