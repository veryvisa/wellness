Six-Ingredient Rehmannia Pill六味地黄丸

Liù Wèi Dìhuáng Wán · Qian Yi, Xiao'er Yaozheng Zhijue (Song; originally 'Di Huang Wan', made by removing Gui Zhi and Fu Zi from Zhang Zhongjing's Shen Qi Wan); anchor text from Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie宋·钱乙《小儿药证直诀》（原名地黄丸，由仲景肾气丸减桂附而成）；原文锚点取清·汪昂《医方集解》

Original text原文

「錢氏仲陽因仲景八味丸減去桂、附，以治小儿，以小儿純陽，故減附桂」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Wang Ang records the origin: because children are 'pure yang', Qian Yi removed Gui Zhi and Fu Zi from Zhang Zhongjing's eight-ingredient kidney qi pill to treat children.汪昂记其来历：钱乙因小儿是「纯阳」之体，把仲景八味肾气丸里的桂枝、附子去掉，用来治小儿。

「治肝腎不足，真陰虧損，精血枯竭，憔悴羸弱，腰痛足酸，自汗盜汗，水泛爲痰」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

For liver-kidney insufficiency, depleted true yin and dried-up essence and blood: a haggard, wasted look, aching back and weak legs, spontaneous and night sweats.治肝肾不足、真阴亏损、精血枯竭：憔悴消瘦，腰痛足酸，自汗盗汗。

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Enriches and supplements liver and kidney滋补肝肾

Liver-kidney yin deficiency: sore weak lower back and knees, dizziness, tinnitus or deafness, night sweats and seminal emission, wasting-thirst, steaming-bone tidal fever, hot palms and soles, loose teeth, slow closure of the fontanelle in infants, red tongue with scant coating, a deep thin rapid pulse.肝肾阴虚证：腰膝酸软，头晕目眩，耳鸣耳聋，盗汗遗精，消渴，骨蒸潮热，手足心热，牙齿动摇，小儿囟门不合，舌红少苔，脉沉细数。

How the formula is built方解

'Three supplements and three drains': Shu Di, Shan Zhu Yu and Shan Yao supplement kidney, liver and spleen yin; Ze Xie, Dan Pi and Fu Ling drain damp-turbidity and deficiency fire. The supplements outweigh the drains, so it can be taken long without cloying. Wang Ang praised it as 'supplementing with draining built in' and criticised later doctors who piled extra tonics into it. Today it is one of the commonest patent medicines; add Zhi Mu and Huang Bai for Zhi Bai Di Huang Wan, Gou Qi and Ju Hua for Qi Ju Di Huang Wan, or put back Gui Zhi and Fu Zi for Shen Qi Wan.「三补三泻」：熟地、山茱萸、山药补肾肝脾之阴，泽泻、丹皮、茯苓泻湿浊与虚火；补药用量大于泻药，以补为主、补中有泻，所以能久服而不滋腻。汪昂说它「补而兼泻，有补而必有泻」；也批评后世随意往里加补药、喧宾夺主。今天它是最常见的中成药之一，加知母、黄柏为知柏地黄丸，加枸杞、菊花为杞菊地黄丸，加回桂附即肾气丸。

Cautions使用注意

Caution with spleen-deficiency diarrhoea or poor appetite (it is cloying); not for yang deficiency with cold (that calls for Shen Qi Wan). Long-standing tinnitus, dizziness, back pain, frequent night urination or thirst need a doctor first to rule out high blood pressure, diabetes or kidney disease.脾虚泄泻、食少者慎用（滋腻）；阳虚怕冷者不宜（应用肾气丸）。长期耳鸣、头晕、腰痛、夜尿多、口渴多饮，先就医排查高血压、糖尿病、肾病等。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Contains Mu Dan Pi (pharmacopoeia: use with caution in pregnancy); in pregnancy leave it to a licensed practitioner and do not take it on your own.含牡丹皮（药典：孕妇慎用）；孕期由持照中医师判断，不要自行服用。

Exam notes考点

Essentials: (1) three supplements, three drains; (2) its descent from Shen Qi Wan; (3) the Zhi Bai, Qi Ju, Mai Wei and Du Qi variants; (4) versus Zuo Gui Wan (supplementing without draining).必考：①三补三泻；②由肾气丸化裁的来历；③知柏、杞菊、麦味、都气等加减方；④与左归丸（纯补无泻）的区别。
