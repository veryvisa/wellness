Tonify the Middle and Augment the Qi Decoction补中益气汤

Bǔ Zhōng Yì Qì Tāng · Li Dongyuan's Neiwaishang Bianhuo Lun (Jin); anchor text from Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie (Qing)金·李东垣《内外伤辨惑论》；原文锚点取清·汪昂《医方集解》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Tonify the Middle and Augment the Qi Decoction补中益气汤

Original text原文

「治煩勞内傷，身熱心煩，頭痛恶寒，懒言恶食，脈洪大而虛，或喘或渴，或陽虛自汗」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Indicated for internal damage from overexertion presenting with body heat and irritability, headache with aversion to cold, reluctance to speak, poor appetite, a surging large yet deficient pulse, or panting, thirst, and spontaneous sweating from yang deficiency.主治因过度劳累所致的脾胃气虚内伤证，常见身热心烦、头痛怕冷、少气懒言、不思饮食，脉象洪大而无力，或伴气喘、口渴，或阳气虚弱导致白天自汗。

「一切清陽下陷，中氣不足之證」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Encompasses all conditions characterized by insufficiency of middle-jiao qi and downward sinking of clear yang.统括一切中焦脾胃之气不足、清阳之气向下陷落引起的病证。

Cautions使用注意

Contraindicated in fever from yin deficiency, excess internal heat, uncontrolled hypertension, and exterior excess patterns. Seek prompt medical evaluation and care rather than relying solely on this formula if persistent high fever, severe organ prolapse with bleeding or incarceration, severe headache, marked shortness of breath, or persistent diarrhea with dehydration occurs.阴虚发热、实热内炽、高血压未受控及外感表实证不宜使用。若出现持续高热、严重器官脱垂伴出血或嵌顿、剧烈头痛、明显呼吸困难或持续腹泻脱水等情况，必须及时就医进行规范检查与诊治，切勿仅依赖本方。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Astragalus, ginseng, licorice, Chinese angelica, and cimicifuga carry a pregnancy caution on this site based on textbook standards and our strict safety policy; the formula follows the strictest level of its herbs and requires evaluation by a licensed practitioner during pregnancy.方中黄芪、人参、甘草、当归、升麻在本站均列为孕期慎用（依据教材口径及本站从严原则），全方按最严标准定为孕期慎用，妊娠期须经持牌医师评估后方可使用。
