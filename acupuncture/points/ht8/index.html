🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5 寸

Insert with the palm relaxed, avoiding the flexor tendons and the common palmar digital nerve and vessels beside them; withdraw for electric tingling into the ring or little finger, and press after withdrawal.手掌放松后进针，避开屈肌腱和腱旁的指掌侧总神经、血管；有向环指、小指放射的电麻感即退针，出针后按压。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or seated with the forearm supinated, palm facing up and relaxed; make a loose fist first to locate, then open the hand with fingers slightly flexed to relax the palmar aponeurosis during needling.仰卧或坐位，前臂旋后、手掌向上自然平放，手腕与手指放松；定位时可先做自然握拳动作，取穴后手掌舒展、微屈以放松掌腱膜。

  1. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Fist-clenching method (standard quick method): have the patient curl the four fingers naturally into a fist (keeping the thumb outside); the hollow in the palm touched by the tip of the little finger is HT8.握拳定位法（国标首选简便法）：让患者四指自然屈曲握拳（拇指不包入掌心，或置于食指外侧），小指尖在掌心所指的凹陷处即是少府。

    Surface landmark体表标志

    Feel the 5th metacarpophalangeal joint on the ulnar palm and the head of the 4th metacarpal; HT8 is in the groove between the 4th and 5th metacarpals, level with the proximal border of the 5th MCP joint, roughly level with PC8 (between the 2nd and 3rd metacarpals).沿手掌尺侧摸到第 5 掌指关节，以及第 4 掌骨头；少府在第 4、5 掌骨之间、横平第 5 掌指关节近端的骨缝凹陷中，与劳宫（第 2、3 掌骨之间）大致同高。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palm up, fingers slightly flexed. The needle passes skin and palmar aponeurosis to lie between the superficial and deep flexor tendons of the ring and little fingers and the 4th lumbrical, with the interossei and the 4th and 5th metacarpals deeper.   • Inserting too distally into the 4th/5th metacarpophalangeal joint capsule or web margin: risks irritating the joint synovium or proper palmar digital nerves, causing sharp pain.偏远端进入第 4、5 掌指关节囊内或指蹼缘：易刺伤关节滑膜或指掌侧固有神经，引起剧烈刺痛。
  • Needling while the fist is tightly clenched or the palm is hyperextended: tension in the palmar aponeurosis and flexor tendons makes insertion intensely painful and risks piercing a tendon or causing a stuck needle; have the patient relax the hand in slight flexion first.患者紧握拳或手掌过度背伸时进针：掌腱膜与屈肌腱高度紧张，进针疼痛剧烈且极易刺中肌腱或滞针；必须让手掌放松微屈后再进针。

Acupuncture offers only adjunct symptomatic relief in early stages; refer to hand surgery if fixed flexion contracture develops.手掌尺侧（第 4、5 掌骨掌面）出现无痛性皮下硬结或条索，逐渐牵拉无名指、小指屈曲不能伸直；常见于中老年男性、糖尿病或长期饮酒者。针刺仅作早期局部软化辅助，出现明显手指屈曲畸形需转诊手外科。

Ulnar palmar pain/paresthesia from distal ulnar nerve entrapment (Guyon canal syndrome)尺神经远端卡压（Guyon 管综合征）引起的掌尺侧麻痛

Numbness and tingling in the little finger and ulnar half of the ring finger with interosseous weakness (positive Froment sign) after prolonged handlebar pressure or tool use; differentiate from C8–T1 radiculopathy and cubital tunnel syndrome.长期骑行压迫车把或使用工具后，出现小指及无名指尺侧半麻木、刺痛，伴骨间肌无力（夹纸试验阳性）；需与颈椎病（C8–T1 神经根受压）及肘管综合征鉴别。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is HT8 Shàofǔ?少府（HT8）在哪里？

On the palm, level with the proximal border of the 5th metacarpophalangeal joint, between the 4th and 5th metacarpals.手掌，横平第 5 掌指关节近端，第 4、5 掌骨之间。

How is HT8 needled?少府（HT8）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of HT8?少府（HT8）是哪类特定穴？

Ying-spring (fire)荥穴（火）

Main safety point at HT8?少府（HT8）最要注意的安全问题？

Insert with the palm relaxed, avoiding the flexor tendons and the common palmar digital nerve and vessels beside them; withdraw for electric tingling into the ring or little finger, and press after withdrawal.手掌放松后进针，避开屈肌腱和腱旁的指掌侧总神经、血管；有向环指、小指放射的电麻感即退针，出针后按压。
