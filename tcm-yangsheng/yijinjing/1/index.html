✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Frozen shoulder or shoulder pain: raise the arms only as high as is pain-free肩周炎或肩痛：两臂前抬只到不痛的高度
  • If standing still makes you dizzy: practise next to a chair or wall and sit down if dizzy站立久了头晕的人：靠近椅子或墙练，晕就坐下

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Step the left foot out half a step to about shoulder width, knees slightly soft, hands at the sides左脚向左开半步，约与肩同宽，两膝微屈，两手垂在体侧
  2. Raise the arms forward to shoulder height, palms facing each other两臂从体侧向前抬到与肩同高，掌心相对
  3. Bend the elbows and draw the hands back to press the palms together in front of the chest, the heels of the hands level with the middle of the breastbone, a little space under the armpits屈肘，两掌慢慢收回，在胸前合掌，掌根约与两乳连线中点同高，腋下留空
  4. Look forward and down, and stand still目视前下方，静站

🌬 Breathe naturally; while standing with palms together, let the breath slow and even out.自然呼吸，合掌静站时呼吸放慢放匀。

✅ Key points要领

  • Shoulders relaxed, elbows hanging naturally肩放松、肘自然下垂
  • Palms touch lightly rather than pressing hard合掌不用力挤压，两掌轻贴
  • Body upright, not leaning forward or back身体正直，不前倾后仰

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Hunching the shoulders or clamping the arms to the sides耸肩、夹紧腋下
  • Palms too high near the chin or too low at the belly合掌位置太高到下巴，或太低到腹部
  • Holding the breath憋气

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Seated on the front half of a chair, feet flat on the floor, press the palms together in the same way.坐在椅子前半部做：脚平放地面，上身同样合掌静坐。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Wei Tuo is the guardian figure in Buddhist temples who holds a vajra pestle; 'presenting the pestle' borrows his pose of holding it upright before the chest. The old verse asks the practitioner to stand upright, hands joined before the chest, breath settled and mind collected.韦驮是佛寺里手持金刚杵的护法神，「献杵」借他双手抱杵当胸的姿态。旧本歌诀：「立身期正直，环拱手当胸，气定神皆敛，心澄貌亦恭。」讲的是站正、合掌、收心。
