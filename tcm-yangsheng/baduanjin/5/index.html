✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • High blood pressure, glaucoma or retinal disease: do not keep the head below the heart for long高血压、青光眼、视网膜病变：不要俯身到头低于心脏太久
  • Vertigo or neck problems: sway the head only a little眩晕、颈椎病：头的摇摆幅度很小
  • During an acute disc prolapse: skip this form for now腰椎间盘突出急性期：先不做这一式
  • Osteoporosis: do not fold all the way down骨质疏松：不俯身到底

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Shift your weight left and take a wide step to the right; lift the hands above the head, then sink into a horse stance as the arms come down the sides and the hands rest just above the knees重心左移，右脚向右开一大步；两掌上托到头上方，随后两腿屈膝成马步，两臂从两侧落下，两掌扶在膝盖上方
  2. Rise slightly and shift your weight to the right; lean the upper body right, then fold forward, looking at the right foot重心稍升起后移向右侧，上身先向右倾，再俯身，目视右脚
  3. Shift your weight left while circling the upper body from right, through the front, to the left重心左移，上身由右经前方向左侧旋转
  4. Return to centre: swing the head back and bring the trunk upright while the hips (the 'tail') swing the opposite way, chin slightly tucked; then circle the other way重心回到中间，头向后摇、上身立起，臀部（尾闾）随之向相反方向摆动，下巴微收；然后换方向做

🌬 Breathe out as you fold, in as you come up; or breathe naturally.俯身时呼气，立起时吸气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • Head and tailbone are a pair: as the head goes one way the tail goes the other, like a fish flicking its tail头和尾闾是一对：头往一边，尾闾往另一边，像一条鱼摆尾
  • Slow and continuous; never fling动作慢而连贯，不甩
  • Let the hands on the knees take some weight so the lower back is not straining两手扶膝支撑，腰不要硬撑

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Swaying the head without moving the hips只摇头不摆臀
  • Too big and too fast, making yourself dizzy幅度太大、速度太快，转得头晕
  • Knees caving in as you fold俯身时膝盖内扣

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Stand higher and skip the forward fold, doing only small side leans of the trunk and a sway of the hips; or seated, make small circles with the upper body.站高一点，不俯身，只做上身小幅的左右倾和腰胯的摆动；或坐着做上身小圈。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 TCM calls a cluster of signs such as restlessness, poor sleep and mouth ulcers 'heart fire'. The rhyme says this form 'clears heart fire'; the traditional reading is that the swaying from top to bottom draws the 'fire' downward. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.中医把烦躁、失眠、口舌生疮等一类表现叫「心火」。口诀说这一式「去心火」，传统解释是上下一摇一摆，让上面的「火」往下走。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
