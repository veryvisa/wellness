Traditional actions and indications功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims; the modern evidence is graded further down.以下为传统说法，不是疗效主张；现代证据见下文分级。

  • Induces sweating and releases the muscle layer发汗解肌
  • Warms and frees the channels温通经脉
  • Assists yang and transforms qi助阳化气
  • Calms upsurge and lowers counterflow平冲降气

Wind-cold colds (with or without sweating, depending on its partners); cold pain in the upper and lower abdomen; cold-congealed blood with painful or absent periods and painful joints; phlegm-fluid, oedema and scanty urine when yang fails to transform qi; palpitations and 'running piglet' upsurge from weak heart yang.风寒感冒（有汗无汗均可配伍）；脘腹冷痛；寒凝血滞的痛经、经闭、关节痹痛；痰饮、水肿、小便不利（阳不化气）；心悸、奔豚（心阳不振、气上冲）。

How to recognise it怎么认

Growing植株 An evergreen tree with fragrant grey-brown bark; leathery oblong leaves with three strong veins running from near the base, smelling of cinnamon when crushed. Gui Zhi is the young leafy twig with the leaves stripped.常绿乔木，树皮灰褐色、芳香；叶革质、长椭圆形，有三条明显的纵向叶脉（离基三出脉），揉碎有桂皮香。桂枝是带叶的嫩枝去叶而成。

Decoction pieces饮片 Round to oval thick slices (or short sections) 0.3–1 cm across; red-brown to brown skin with ridges and dot-like lenticels; on the cut face the bark ring is red-brown, the wood yellowish-white and the pith squarish. A distinctive aroma; sweet and slightly pungent.类圆形或椭圆形厚片（或短段），直径 0.3–1 厘米；外皮红棕色至棕色，有纵棱与点状皮孔；切面皮部红棕色、木部黄白色，中心髓部略呈方形。有特异香气，味甜、微辛。

Processing炮制

  • Gui Zhi (raw)桂枝（生用）：: Sliced and used raw. Division of labour with Rou Gui: the twig goes to the surface and moves yang; the bark goes inward and supplements fire切薄片或段生用；与肉桂的分工：桂枝走表、通阳，肉桂入里、补火助阳

Cautions and incompatibilities使用注意与配伍禁忌

Avoid in warm-heat disease, yin deficiency with yang excess, and bleeding from blood heat; use with caution in pregnancy and with heavy periods.温热病、阴虚阳盛、血热妄行（出血）者忌用；孕妇及月经过多者慎用。

Interactions with medicines与西药的相互作用

No reliable study of the twig and drug interactions was found. Note that cassia bark (from the same tree, and the 'cassia cinnamon' powder sold in supermarkets) is high in coumarin, so large long-term amounts with liver-toxic drugs warrant liver checks; that is a problem of the powder, not a known problem of standard Gui Zhi decoctions.未查到桂枝饮片与西药相互作用的可靠研究。注意：肉桂（同树的树皮，以及超市卖的「Cassia 肉桂粉」）含香豆素较多，长期大量与肝毒性药物合用要留意肝功能——那是肉桂粉的问题，不是常规剂量桂枝汤剂的已知问题。

Modern research, graded现代研究（分级）

  • E4 The volatile oil (mainly cinnamaldehyde) dilates peripheral vessels, lowers fever and inhibits microbes in animal and test-tube work, one possible mechanism for 'releasing the muscle layer'; human evidence is scarce.桂枝挥发油（以桂皮醛为主）在动物与体外实验中有扩张外周血管、解热、抑菌等作用，可作为「发汗解肌」的一种机制猜想，人体证据很少。

「牡桂味辛溫。主治上氣咳逆、結氣，喉痹吐吸，利關節，補中益氣」——神农本草经

Shennong Bencao Jing (Divine Farmer's Materia Medica)

The Shennong Bencao Jing has no 'Gui Zhi', only Mu Gui and Jun Gui. Whether Zhang Zhongjing's 'Gui Zhi' is the same plant part as today's has been argued for centuries (some say he used peeled twigs or bark). Today's pharmacopoeia Gui Zhi is the young twig.《神农本草经》没有「桂枝」这个名字，只有牡桂、菌桂；张仲景方里的「桂枝」与后世桂枝是否同一药用部位，历代有争论（有说仲景时用的是去皮的嫩枝或桂皮）。今天药典的桂枝就是嫩枝。

Exam notes (HER)考点（HER）

HER essentials: (1) both Ma Huang and Gui Zhi are acrid-warm exterior herbs, Ma Huang strongly diaphoretic, Gui Zhi milder but warming and freeing; (2) harmonising nutritive and defensive qi with Bai Shao in Gui Zhi Tang; (3) warming yang to transform fluids (Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang), warming the vessels (Dang Gui Si Ni Tang), lowering upsurge; (4) twig versus bark.HER 必考：①与麻黄同为辛温解表，麻黄发汗力强、桂枝发汗力缓且能温通；②桂枝汤调和营卫（配白芍）；③温阳化饮（苓桂术甘汤）、温通血脉（当归四逆汤）、平冲（桂枝加桂汤）；④与肉桂的部位与功效之别。
