Dizziness and vertigo眩晕

'Dizzy' covers at least four different feelings: the room spinning (vertigo), nearly blacking out, being unsteady on the feet, and a foggy head. First ask which one it is, how long it lasts and what sets it off, then rule out stroke and heart causes. TCM divides dizziness into excess and deficiency: excess patterns are liver yang rising, phlegm-damp in the middle and blood stasis blocking the head; deficiency patterns are qi and blood deficiency and kidney essence deficiency. The textbook has one main set for excess and one for deficiency, both containing GV20 and GB20; this page lists both sets and adds points for each pattern.「头晕」至少包括四种不同的感觉：天旋地转（眩晕）、眼前发黑要晕倒、走路不稳、头昏沉。先问清是哪一种、持续多久、什么诱发，再排除卒中和心脏原因。中医把眩晕分虚实：实证是肝阳上亢、痰湿中阻、瘀血阻窍，虚证是气血两虚、肾精不足。教材对实证、虚证各有一组主穴，两组都用百会、风池；本页按这两组列出，再逐型加配穴。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Dizziness with any FAST sign (facial droop, one-sided arm weakness, slurred speech), or with double vision, trouble swallowing, slurred words, a clumsy arm or leg on one side, or being completely unable to stand or walk: call 911 now; this may be a cerebellar or brainstem stroke, which often lacks the classic droop and weakness.眩晕伴 FAST 任一表现（脸歪、一侧手臂无力、说话含糊），或伴复视、吞咽困难、口齿不清、一侧手脚笨拙、完全站不稳走不了：立即拨 911，警惕小脑或脑干卒中——后循环卒中常没有典型的脸歪手软。
  • Sudden continuous vertigo with a new headache or pain at the back of the neck, especially after neck manipulation or a strain: emergency department now to rule out vertebral artery dissection.突然出现的持续眩晕伴新发头痛或颈后痛（尤其颈部按摩、扭伤之后）：立即急诊，排除椎动脉夹层。
  • Dizziness with chest pain, palpitations or shortness of breath, or actually fainting and losing consciousness: call 911 or go to emergency now; it may be an arrhythmia or heart disease.头晕伴胸痛、心悸、气短，或真正晕倒失去意识：拨 911 或立即急诊，可能是心律失常或心脏病。
  • Sudden hearing loss in one ear, with or without tinnitus and vertigo: see a doctor within 72 hours; sudden deafness has the best chance of recovery when treated early.突然一侧听力下降（伴或不伴耳鸣眩晕）：72 小时内看医生，突发性耳聋越早治疗越有机会恢复。
  • Blacking out on standing with black stools, heavy periods or pallor, or dizziness and falls after starting a new blood-pressure drug: see a doctor soon to check blood pressure, haemoglobin and medicines. Repeated vomiting with no fluids kept down: emergency department today for fluids.站起就眼前发黑、伴黑便或月经量大、面色苍白；或开始新降压药后头晕跌倒：尽快就医查血压、血红蛋白和用药。反复呕吐不能进水：当天急诊补液。

「髓海不足，則腦轉耳鳴，脛痠眩冒，目無所見，懈怠安臥」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.33: the brain is the sea of marrow; when it is insufficient the head spins, the ears ring, the shins ache, vision goes dark and the person wants only to lie down. This is the basis of the kidney-essence pattern — the kidney makes marrow, so the deficiency set uses BL23, adding KI3 and GB39 (influential point of marrow) when essence is depleted.《灵枢·海论》：脑为髓海，髓海不足就会脑转耳鸣、小腿酸软、眩晕、眼前发黑、懒动嗜卧。这是「肾精不足」型眩晕的理论来源——肾生髓，所以虚证主穴用肾俞，肾精亏再加太溪、悬钟（髓会）。

「上氣不足，腦為之不滿，耳為之苦鳴，頭為之苦傾，目為之眩」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.28: when the qi of the upper body is insufficient the brain is not full, the ears ring, the head droops and vision swims. It describes qi and blood failing to reach the head — the qi-and-blood pattern needs tonifying, with GV20 to draw them upward.《灵枢·口问》：上部之气不足，脑就不充盈，耳鸣、头抬不起来、眼花发晕。讲的是气血不能上荣于头——气血两虚型眩晕要补气血，并用百会把气血引上头。

「頭痛眩暈百會好」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Shengyu song in Zhenjiu Dacheng: GV20 works well for headache and dizziness. Sitting at the vertex, it appears in both the excess and the deficiency main sets — drained or even method in excess, tonified or moxa'd in deficiency.《针灸大成·胜玉歌》：头痛、眩晕取百会效果好。百会位于巅顶，所以教材的实证、虚证两组主穴都有它；实证用泻或平补平泻，虚证宜补或灸。

Excess实证

Liver yang rising肝阳上亢

How it presents辨证要点 Dizziness and tinnitus with a distending headache, worse with stress or anger; red face and eyes, irritability, bitter taste, poor sleep with many dreams; red tongue, yellow coating, wiry rapid pulse.眩晕耳鸣，头胀痛，遇烦劳郁怒加重；面红目赤，急躁易怒，口苦，少寐多梦；舌红苔黄，脉弦数。

Principle治法 Calm the liver, anchor yang, clear the head and eyes (drain; tonify KI3)平肝潜阳，清利头目（泻法；太溪用补法）

Main主穴GV20 Bǎihuì百会GB20 Fēngchí风池LR3 Tàichōng太冲PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴LR2 Xíngjiān行间GB43 Xiáxī侠溪KI3 Tàixī太溪

💡 Excess main set: GV20 and GB20 clear the head, LR3 calms the liver, PC6 settles the stomach and the spinning; with obvious liver fire add LR2 (Liver ying-spring) and GB43 (Gallbladder ying-spring) to drain liver and gallbladder fire, and KI3 to tonify kidney water so it can hold wood. Very high or swinging blood pressure needs a doctor first; acupuncture does not replace blood-pressure medicine.实证主穴：百会、风池清利头目，太冲平肝，内关宽胸和胃止晕；肝火明显加行间（肝经荥穴）、侠溪（胆经荥穴）清泻肝胆之火，太溪补肾水以涵木。血压很高或波动大时先就医，针灸不代替降压药。

Excess实证

Phlegm-damp obstructing the middle痰湿中阻

How it presents辨证要点 Dizziness with a heavy wrapped head and the room turning, chest fullness, nausea, vomiting of phlegm, poor appetite, sleepiness; greasy white coating, soggy slippery pulse.眩晕而头重如裹，视物旋转，胸闷恶心，呕吐痰涎，纳少多寐；苔白腻，脉濡滑。

Principle治法 Dry damp, transform phlegm, strengthen the spleen and settle the stomach (drain or even method)燥湿化痰，健脾和胃（泻法或平补平泻）

Main主穴GV20 Bǎihuì百会GB20 Fēngchí风池LR3 Tàichōng太冲PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉

💡 'No phlegm, no vertigo': CV12, the stomach's front-mu and influential point of the fu organs, restores the middle burner; ST40, the Stomach luo point, transforms phlegm; SP9 drains damp. PC6, already in the main set, stops vomiting and works with CV12 to send stomach qi down. CV12 is on the upper abdomen and is not needled in pregnancy.「无痰不作眩」：中脘是胃募、腑会，健运中焦；丰隆是胃经络穴、化痰要穴；阴陵泉利湿。内关在主穴里止呕，与中脘配合和胃降逆。中脘在上腹，孕妇禁针。

Excess实证

Blood stasis blocking the head瘀血阻窍

How it presents辨证要点 Recurrent dizziness with a fixed stabbing headache, sometimes after an old head injury; forgetfulness, poor sleep, palpitations, dusky face and lips; dark tongue with stasis spots, choppy or thin choppy pulse.眩晕反复，头痛如刺、痛处固定，或有头部外伤史；健忘失眠，心悸，面唇紫暗；舌暗有瘀斑，脉涩或细涩。

Principle治法 Move blood, dispel stasis, open the head, stop dizziness (drain)活血化瘀，通窍止晕（泻法）

Main主穴GV20 Bǎihuì百会GB20 Fēngchí风池LR3 Tàichōng太冲PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴BL17 Géshū膈俞SP10 Xuèhǎi血海

💡 BL17, the influential point of blood, with SP10, the Spleen point for moving blood, disperses stasis; LR3 in the main set moves liver qi, and blood moves when qi moves. Dizziness after a recent head injury must first be checked for bleeding inside the skull.膈俞是血会，血海是脾经活血要穴，两穴相配化瘀；太冲在主穴里疏肝理气，气行则血行。新近外伤后的眩晕先排除颅内出血。

Deficiency虚证

Qi and blood deficiency气血两虚

How it presents辨证要点 Dizziness worse on moving and brought on by tiredness, blacking out on standing; pale face, pale lips and nails, palpitations, poor sleep, fatigue, reluctance to talk, poor appetite; pale tongue, thin white coating, thin weak pulse.眩晕动则加剧，劳累即发，站起时眼前发黑；面色㿠白，唇甲不华，心悸少寐，神疲懒言，纳少；舌淡苔薄白，脉细弱。

Principle治法 Tonify qi and blood, nourish heart and spleen (tonify; moxa allowed)补益气血，调养心脾（补法，可灸）

💡 Deficiency main set: GV20 lifts, GB20 clears the head, BL18 nourishes liver blood, BL23 tonifies kidney essence and ST36 builds postnatal qi; for qi and blood deficiency add CV6 for qi and BL20 and BL21 to strengthen the spleen and stomach that make qi and blood. Thoracic back-shu points are needled obliquely to avoid pneumothorax; CV6 is on the lower abdomen and is not needled in pregnancy.虚证主穴：百会升提、风池清头，肝俞养肝血、肾俞补肾精、足三里补后天；气血两虚加气海补气，脾俞、胃俞健运脾胃以生气血。背俞穴在胸背部斜刺防气胸，气海在小腹，孕妇禁针。

Deficiency虚证

Kidney essence deficiency肾精不足

How it presents辨证要点 Long-standing dizziness with low spirits, tinnitus, poor memory, sore weak lower back and knees, seminal emission; with yin deficiency hot palms and soles, red tongue with little coating, thin rapid pulse; with yang deficiency cold limbs, pale tongue, deep thin pulse.眩晕日久，精神萎靡，耳鸣，健忘，腰膝酸软，遗精；偏阴虚者五心烦热、舌红少苔、脉细数，偏阳虚者四肢不温、舌淡、脉沉细。

Principle治法 Tonify the kidney, replenish essence, fill the brain (tonify; moxa for yang deficiency)补肾填精，充养脑髓（补法；阳虚可灸）

Main主穴GV20 Bǎihuì百会GB20 Fēngchí风池BL18 Gānshū肝俞BL23 Shènshū肾俞ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

💡 KI3, the Kidney yuan point, pairs with BL23 in the main set as back-shu and source points; GB39, the influential point of marrow, fills the brain; SP6, where the Liver, Spleen and Kidney channels meet, nourishes yin and blood. SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy; BL23 lies at the level of L2, where deep needling risks the kidney.太溪是肾经原穴，与主穴里的肾俞俞原相配；悬钟是髓会，填髓荣脑；三阴交是肝脾肾三经交会，滋补阴血。三阴交孕妇禁针；肾俞在 L2 水平，深刺有伤肾风险。

GV20 Bǎihuì百会

Best for最适合 The shared main point for every dizziness pattern, especially an empty head or blacking out on standing各型眩晕的共用主穴；虚证头空、站起发黑尤宜

Why依据 Governor vessel point at the vertex, 'meeting of all yang', lifts sunken yang督脉巅顶穴，「诸阳之会」，升阳举陷

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More lifting and for deficiency than GB20, which clears the head and expels wind; tonify or moxa in deficiency, but in liver yang rising use only drainage or even method and no moxa.比风池更偏升提和虚证，风池偏清头祛风；虚证宜补或灸，肝阳上亢时只用泻法或平补平泻，不灸。

GB20 Fēngchí风池

Best for最适合 Dizziness with occipital pain, a tight neck or tinnitus眩晕伴后枕痛、项紧、耳鸣

Why依据 Meeting point of the Gallbladder channel and Yang Wei vessel; settles wind and clears the head足少阳与阳维脉交会穴，熄风清头

Versus others与其他穴的区别 In both sets, but more for excess and wind than GV20; needle 0.8–1.2 cun toward the tip of the nose, never deep upward and inward (medulla, vertebral artery); vertigo that follows neck manipulation needs vertebral dissection ruled out before needling.虚实两组都在，比百会偏实证与风；针尖朝鼻尖刺 0.8–1.2 寸，不可向内上方深刺（延髓、椎动脉）；颈部手法后出现的眩晕先排除椎动脉夹层再针。

LR3 Tàichōng太冲

Best for最适合 Liver yang rising: distending head, irritability, red face and eyes, raised blood pressure肝阳上亢：头胀、急躁、面红目赤、血压偏高

Why依据 Liver yuan and shu-stream point; calms the liver and settles wind肝经原穴、输穴，平肝熄风

Versus others与其他穴的区别 An excess main point that calms the liver as a yuan point, whereas LR2, the ying-spring point, clears liver fire; it is generally not drained in deficiency dizziness.实证主穴，比行间更偏平肝（原穴），行间是荥穴、偏清肝火；虚证眩晕一般不泻太冲。

PC6 Nèiguān内关

Best for最适合 Dizziness with nausea, vomiting, chest tightness or palpitations眩晕伴恶心呕吐、胸闷心悸

Why依据 Pericardium luo point, confluent point of the Yin Wei vessel; opens the chest, settles the stomach and sends qi down心包经络穴，八脉交会通阴维；宽胸和胃降逆

BL18 Gānshū肝俞

Why依据 Back-shu point of the liver; the liver stores blood and opens into the eyes肝之背俞穴；肝藏血、开窍于目

Versus others与其他穴的区别 A deficiency main point that regulates the liver like LR3 but nourishes blood where LR3 drains excess; it lies beside T9, so needle obliquely 0.5–0.8 cun, as deep needling risks pneumothorax.虚证主穴，与太冲同调肝但偏补血，太冲偏平肝泻实；位于 T9 旁，斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸，深刺有气胸风险。

BL23 Shènshū肾俞

Best for最适合 Deficiency dizziness with tinnitus, sore lower back and frequent urination at night虚证眩晕伴耳鸣腰酸、夜尿多

Why依据 Back-shu point of the kidney; the kidney makes marrow and the brain is the sea of marrow肾之背俞穴；肾生髓、脑为髓海

ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

Best for最适合 Qi and blood deficiency: dizziness with exertion, poor appetite, fatigue气血两虚：劳累即晕、纳少乏力

LR2 Xíngjiān行间

Best for最适合 Marked liver fire: distending headache, bitter taste, red eyes, insomnia肝火明显：头胀痛、口苦、目赤、失眠

Why依据 Liver ying-spring (fire) point; 'ying points govern heat'肝经荥穴（火），「荥主身热」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Clears fire rather than calming the liver as LR3 does, so add it only with fire signs; pairs with GB43, one clearing the liver, one the gallbladder.比太冲更偏清火、不偏平肝，只在有火象时加；与侠溪一清肝一清胆。

GB43 Xiáxī侠溪

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More about the ear and the lateral head along Shaoyang than LR2, which works on the Liver channel itself; not used in deficiency.比行间更偏耳和头侧少阳循行，行间偏肝本经；虚证不用。

ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆

Best for最适合 Phlegm-damp dizziness: heavy wrapped head, nausea, vomiting phlegm痰湿眩晕：头重如裹、恶心吐痰涎

CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘

Best for最适合 Phlegm-damp in the middle: epigastric fullness, poor appetite, nausea痰湿中阻：胃脘胀闷、纳呆、呕恶

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Treats the middle burner where phlegm is made, while ST40 clears phlegm already formed; on the upper abdomen, forbidden in pregnancy and not needled deeply after a full meal.比丰隆更偏治中焦生痰之源，丰隆偏化已成之痰；位于上腹，孕妇禁针，饱食后不宜深刺。

BL17 Géshū膈俞

Best for最适合 Blood-stasis dizziness: stabbing headache, dusky face and lips瘀血眩晕：头痛如刺、面唇紫暗

Why依据 Influential point of blood八会穴之血会

Versus others与其他穴的区别 With SP10 it moves blood, with BL20 and ST36 it nourishes blood; beside T7, needle obliquely 0.5–0.8 cun to avoid pneumothorax.配血海偏活血化瘀，配脾俞、足三里则偏养血；T7 旁，斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸防气胸。

KI3 Tàixī太溪

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

The commonest causes of vertigo are in the inner ear (peripheral): benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV — spinning on rolling over or looking up, lasting under a minute, treated with repositioning manoeuvres), vestibular neuritis (sudden vertigo lasting days without hearing loss), Ménière's disease (recurrent attacks with tinnitus, ear fullness and fluctuating hearing loss) and vestibular migraine. Among central causes the most important is posterior-circulation stroke, which can show only as vertigo and unsteadiness and needs a doctor's bedside examination and imaging. Near-fainting dizziness is mostly linked to low blood pressure on standing, anaemia, arrhythmia or medicines; unsteadiness raises peripheral neuropathy, Parkinson's disease and others. TCM patterns organise the presentation and the choice of treatment but cannot replace neurological, ear and cardiovascular assessment; for BPPV the first-line treatment is a repositioning manoeuvre, not acupuncture.眩晕最常见的原因是耳源性（周围性）：良性阵发性位置性眩晕（BPPV，翻身、抬头时天旋地转，持续不到一分钟，可用复位手法治疗）、前庭神经炎（突发持续眩晕数天、无听力下降）、梅尼埃病（反复发作，伴耳鸣、耳胀和波动性听力下降）和前庭性偏头痛。中枢性原因中最要紧的是后循环卒中，它可以只表现为眩晕和走路不稳，需要由医生做床旁检查和影像判断。眼前发黑型头晕多与体位性低血压、贫血、心律失常、药物有关；走路不稳要想到周围神经病和帕金森病等。中医证型用于整理表现、选择治法，不能代替神经科、耳科和心血管检查；BPPV 的首选是复位手法，而不是针灸。
