Trouble sleeping睡不着、睡不好

Awake at 2 a.m., counting the hours left until the alarm: almost every adult has had that night. A few bad nights are nothing to worry about. Several nights a week for months, with tired days, deserve real attention. Below, food, TCM and acupressure, exercise, psychology, modern medicine and sleep habits sit side by side, each line marked with how strong the evidence is, followed by the situations that need a doctor.凌晨两点还醒着，盯着天花板算还能睡几个小时——几乎每个成年人都有过这样的夜晚。偶尔几晚睡不好不必紧张；如果一周有好几晚、持续好几个月，并且白天没精神，就值得认真对待。下面把饮食、中医与穴位按摩、运动、心理、现代医学、睡眠习惯放在一起看，每一条都标了证据有多硬，最后是一定要去看医生的情况。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

See a doctor first, not just self-care, if: you snore loudly and someone has seen you stop breathing at night; you nod off while driving; your mood stays low or you have thoughts of harming yourself (in danger call 911, or call or text 9-8-8); sleeplessness comes with fever, marked weight loss, chest pain or palpitations.出现这些情况先就医，不要只靠自我调理：打鼾很响并被人看到夜里憋气；开车时打瞌睡；情绪持续低落或有伤害自己的念头（危险时拨 911，或拨打、短信 9-8-8）；失眠伴发热、体重明显下降、胸痛或心悸。

What E1–E5 meanE1–E5 是什么意思
  • E1 Systematic reviews / guidelines系统综述或指南
  • E2 Small or few randomised trials少量或小样本随机试验
  • E3 Observational studies (association)观察性研究（相关）
  • E4 Lab, animal or mechanism studies机制、动物或实验室研究
  • E5 Tradition, experience or case reports传统、经验或个案

E4 and E5 lines describe tradition, experience or lab findings only; they never claim that something works.E4、E5 只描述传统、经验或实验室发现，不作疗效主张。

🧠 Psychology: CBT-I comes first心理：CBT-I 是首选起点

  • E1 Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is the first-line starting point for chronic insomnia in adults: both the American College of Physicians and the American Academy of Sleep Medicine put it ahead of medication, as a strong recommendation.失眠的认知行为疗法（CBT-I）是成人慢性失眠的首选起点：美国内科医师学会和美国睡眠医学会的指南都把它排在吃药之前，并给出强推荐。 [9] [10]
  • E1 CBT-I is a bundle: a fixed wake-up time; going to bed only when sleepy; getting up to do something calm if you are still awake after about 20 minutes, and returning when sleepy; reworking thoughts such as "if I don't sleep tonight tomorrow is ruined"; plus relaxation practice. Guidelines give the full bundle a strong recommendation and the single parts on their own only a weaker (conditional) one.CBT-I 是几件事的组合：固定起床时间；只在真的困了才上床；躺下 20 分钟左右还睡不着就起来做件平静的事，困了再回床；纠正「今晚再睡不着明天就完了」这类想法；配合放松练习。指南对整套 CBT-I 给出强推荐，对单独做其中一项给出的是较弱的（条件）推荐。 [10]
  • E1 "Spend less time in bed" sounds backwards: temporarily shrinking time in bed under guidance (sleep restriction) builds up sleep pressure; it is a core part of CBT-I and guidelines also recommend it on its own (conditionally). People with bipolar disorder, epilepsy or safety-critical jobs should adjust it with a professional.「少躺一会儿」听起来反直觉：在专业人员指导下暂时压缩卧床时间（睡眠限制），让睡意积攒起来，是 CBT-I 的核心一环，指南也对它单独给出了条件推荐。有双相障碍、癫痫或需要高度警觉的工作时，要在专业人员指导下调整做法。 [10]

In BC, ask your family doctor about CBT-I, or call 8-1-1 for local options.在 BC，可以先跟家庭医生提「CBT-I」，或拨 8-1-1 问本地资源。

🛏 Sleep habits (sleep hygiene)睡眠习惯（睡眠卫生）

  • E1 Sleep hygiene alone is not enough for chronic insomnia: guidelines advise against using it as the only treatment; it works best as the base layer under CBT-I.只靠「睡眠卫生」不够解决慢性失眠：指南不建议把它当作唯一的办法，它更适合作为 CBT-I 的底子。 [10]
  • E3 Single tips (a regular schedule, a dark and quiet bedroom, less screen time before bed) go along with better nights, but how much any one of them changes on its own has mostly not been tested directly.单条建议——规律作息、卧室暗而安静、睡前少看屏幕——与夜里睡得好相关，但每一条单独做能带来多大变化，大多没有被直接检验过。 [16]

🍵 Food and drink饮食

  • E2 Caffeine lasts longer than most people think: in a small controlled trial, caffeine taken 6 hours before bed still clearly disturbed that night's sleep, and the authors advise none for at least 6 hours before bed. Coffee, strong tea, energy drinks, cola and some pain relievers contain it.咖啡因的作用比想象中长：一项小型对照试验里，睡前 6 小时摄入咖啡因仍明显扰乱当晚睡眠，作者建议睡前至少 6 小时不再摄入。咖啡、浓茶、能量饮料、可乐和部分止痛药都含咖啡因。 [13]
  • E2 Alcohol helps you fall asleep faster but makes the second half of the night lighter and more broken, and moderate to high doses cut REM (dream) sleep. Using a drink as a sleep aid trades away the second half of the night.酒精让人入睡更快，但后半夜更容易醒、睡得更碎，中高剂量还会减少做梦期（快速眼动）睡眠。把酒当助眠，换来的是更差的后半夜。 [14]
  • E5 From everyday experience, a late heavy dinner or a lot of fluid before bed makes it more likely that a full stomach or a bathroom trip will wake you.经验上，晚饭太晚太饱、睡前喝很多水，夜里更容易被胃胀或起夜打断。
  • E5 🏛 Traditional Chinese food culture classes lily bulb, millet and lotus seed as "calming the spirit" and often cooks them into an evening congee or soup; this is a traditional view, with no reliable research behind any effect on insomnia.🏛 中医饮食传统上把百合、小米、莲子等归为「安神」类食材，常做成晚间的粥或汤；这是传统说法，没有可靠研究支持它们对失眠的作用。

🪡 TCM and acupressure中医与穴位按摩

  • E2 There are many trials of acupuncture for insomnia, but a Cochrane systematic review (33 trials) found them of poor methodological quality and inconsistent: the evidence can neither support nor rule out an effect of acupuncture on insomnia.针刺用于失眠的试验不少，但 Cochrane 系统综述（33 项试验）认为它们方法学质量差、结果不一致，证据既不足以支持、也不足以否定针刺对失眠的作用。 [12]
  • E5 🏛 Points traditionally used to "calm the spirit": HT7 Shenmen (little-finger side of the wrist crease), PC6 Neiguan (2 cun above the wrist crease between the two tendons), SP6 Sanyinjiao (3 cun above the inner ankle bone), GV20 Baihui (top of the head) and the extra point Anmian (behind the ear).🏛 传统上用于「安神」的穴位有：神门（HT7，腕横纹小指侧）、内关（PC6，腕横纹上 2 寸两筋之间）、三阴交（SP6，内踝尖上 3 寸）、百会（GV20，头顶正中）和经外奇穴安眠（耳后）。 [6]
  • E5 Pressing these points yourself carries very little risk: use a fingertip until you feel a mild ache, a minute or two per point. In pregnancy, avoid SP6 and LI4 (a traditional pregnancy caution); never press over broken skin.自己按揉这些穴位风险很低：指腹按到微酸胀即可，每处一两分钟。孕期不要按三阴交和合谷（传统孕期禁忌）；皮肤破损处不按。 [6]

Herbal prescriptions are individual and belong to licensed TCM practitioners. No specific herb or patent remedy is recommended here; anyone already taking sleeping pills, antidepressants or blood thinners should ask a doctor or pharmacist before any herbal product or supplement.中药处方因人而异，属于持照中医师的执业范围。这里不推荐任何具体中药或中成药；已经在吃安眠药、抗抑郁药或抗凝药的人，任何中药或保健品都先问医生或药剂师。

🏃 Exercise运动

  • E1 Regular exercise does real good for sleep: a meta-analysis of 66 studies found a moderate improvement in sleep quality and small-to-moderate improvements in time to fall asleep and total sleep time.规律运动对睡眠有实在的好处：一项汇总 66 项研究的荟萃分析发现，它对睡眠质量有中等程度的改善，对入睡时间和总睡眠时间有小到中等的改善。 [15]
  • E5 🏛 Slow traditional practices such as tai chi and baduanjin are traditionally done as a daily way to settle body and mind; the movements are gentle, a reasonable start for people not used to vigorous exercise.🏛 太极、八段锦这类慢节奏功法，传统上作为每天放松身心的练习；动作温和，适合不习惯剧烈运动的人起步。

🩺 Modern medicine现代医学

  • E1 Sleeping pills are a decision for a doctor and pharmacist: guidelines place medication after CBT-I has not been enough, after weighing benefits and harms, and lean toward short-term use. Any change to a medicine you already take should be agreed with the prescriber first.安眠药是医生和药剂师的决定：指南把药物放在 CBT-I 不够用时、权衡利弊之后，而且倾向短期使用。已经在用的药，任何调整都先和开药的医生商量。 [9]
  • E1 The AASM medication guideline advises against melatonin or diphenhydramine (an ingredient in many over-the-counter sleep aids) for chronic insomnia (a weak recommendation).美国睡眠医学会的药物指南不建议用褪黑素或苯海拉明（很多非处方助眠产品的成分）治疗慢性失眠（弱推荐）。 [11]
  • E5 Ongoing insomnia is often tangled with something else: depression or anxiety, sleep apnoea, chronic pain, night-time urination, menopause, thyroid problems or a current medication. Have your family doctor look for these before working on sleep itself.持续失眠常常和别的问题缠在一起：抑郁或焦虑、睡眠呼吸暂停、慢性疼痛、夜尿、更年期、甲状腺问题或正在吃的药。先请家庭医生排查这些，再谈怎么睡。

BC: HealthLink BC 8-1-1 is free around the clock, with nurses and pharmacists.BC：HealthLink BC 8-1-1 全天免费，可以问护士和药剂师。

🚩 When you must see a doctor这些情况必须看医生

  • E5 Loud snoring, someone has seen you stop breathing or gasp at night, heavy daytime sleepiness: possible sleep apnoea; ask a doctor for a sleep assessment.打鼾很响、有人看到你夜里憋气或喘不过来、白天困得厉害：可能是睡眠呼吸暂停，请医生安排睡眠评估。
  • E5 Nodding off while driving or operating machinery: stop driving and see a doctor soon.开车或操作机器时打瞌睡：先停止开车，尽快就医。
  • E5 Low mood that won't lift, losing interest in everything, or thoughts of harming yourself: contact a doctor the same day; if you are in danger call 911, or call or text 9-8-8 (Canada's suicide crisis line, 24/7).情绪持续低落、对什么都提不起兴趣，或出现伤害自己的念头：当天联系医生；有危险时拨 911，或拨打、短信 9-8-8（加拿大自杀危机热线，全天）。
  • E5 An uncomfortable urge to move your legs when lying down: possible restless legs syndrome; see a doctor.躺下后腿里有说不出的难受、非动一动不可：可能是不宁腿综合征，找医生看。
  • E5 Poor sleep at least 3 nights a week for 3 months or more, affecting your days: this is chronic insomnia and worth a proper visit to your family doctor.每周至少 3 晚睡不好、持续 3 个月以上，并且影响白天：这已经是慢性失眠，值得找家庭医生正式处理。
  • E5 New sleeplessness with fever, marked weight loss, night sweats, chest pain or palpitations: see a doctor soon.新出现的失眠伴发热、体重明显下降、夜间大汗、胸痛或心悸：尽快就医。
  • E5 Pregnant, over 65, or on several medicines: ask a doctor or pharmacist before any sleep product, including supplements and herbal remedies.孕期、65 岁以上、同时在吃多种药的人：任何助眠产品（包括保健品和中成药）先问医生或药剂师。

This list is guidance on when to seek care, following usual clinical practice; it makes no treatment claims.这一栏是就医指引，按临床常规整理，不是疗效主张。
