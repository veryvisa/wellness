Heatstroke中暑

Many acupuncture points are traditionally associated with summer-heat illness, but clinical selection starts by distinguishing summer-heat with dampness, summer-heat obstructing consciousness, and collapse of qi and yin. Mild cases present with fever, reduced sweating, dizziness, chest oppression and nausea; severe cases develop high fever, altered consciousness or convulsions; qi-yin collapse presents with profuse sweating, pallor, cold limbs, breathlessness and a faint pulse. These patterns require different treatment strategies. Crucially, modern heatstroke is a life-threatening emergency: call emergency services and initiate active cooling immediately. Never wait for acupuncture to take effect.中暑相关穴位很多，但临床首先要判断患者是暑热夹湿、暑热蒙蔽心窍，还是气阴两脱。轻证以身热、少汗、头晕、胸闷、恶心为主；重证出现高热、神志改变、抽搐；气阴两脱则见大汗、面白、肢冷、气短、脉微。三者的治法和选穴方向完全不同。尤其要注意：现代医学中的热射病是危及生命的急症，必须立即呼叫急救、主动降温，不能等待针刺起效。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • After heat exposure, confusion, abnormal behaviour, incoherent speech, unsteady gait, seizures or fainting require an immediate 911 call. Sweating does not exclude heatstroke.高温暴露后出现意识混乱、行为异常、答非所问、步态不稳、抽搐或昏厥：立即拨打 911。即使皮肤仍在出汗，也不能排除热射病。
  • Markedly elevated temperature, hot skin and altered mental status after heat exposure, especially a core temperature around 40°C or higher, strongly suggest heatstroke. Call emergency services and begin rapid cooling without waiting for measurements or acupuncture.高温环境中出现体温明显升高、皮肤灼热、意识改变，尤其测得核心体温约 40°C 或以上：高度怀疑热射病，立即呼叫急救并持续快速降温，不等待体温测量或针灸处理。
  • Persistent vomiting, inability to drink, marked dizziness or fainting, or rapid heart rate and breathing that do not improve after cooling and rest require medical assessment. Seek assessment if symptoms persist beyond one hour; call 911 immediately for altered consciousness.持续呕吐、无法饮水、明显头晕或晕厥、心跳及呼吸明显加快，经过离开热环境、降温和休息仍未改善，或症状持续超过 1 小时：及时接受医疗评估；出现意识改变立即拨 911。
  • Chest pain, breathing difficulty, palpitations with fainting, markedly reduced urine output or dark urine after heat exposure require urgent medical assessment for cardiovascular complications, acute kidney injury or rhabdomyolysis.高温暴露后出现胸痛、呼吸困难、心悸伴晕厥、尿量显著减少或深色尿：立即就医，排查心血管并发症、急性肾损伤和横纹肌溶解。
  • Sudden collapse, facial droop, one-sided weakness, slurred speech or an abrupt severe headache require an immediate 911 call to exclude stroke. Do not assume this is summer-heat syncope.突然昏倒、口角歪斜、单侧肢体无力、言语不清或突发剧烈头痛：立即拨 911，排除脑卒中，不能简单归为暑厥。

「因於暑，汗，煩則喘喝，靜則多言，體若燔炭，汗出而散」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.3: injured by summerheat, a person sweats; when agitated they pant noisily, when quiet they talk too much, the body is hot as burning charcoal, and only sweating lets the heat out. A body 'hot as burning charcoal' together with rambling speech is high fever with altered consciousness — what today is treated at once as heatstroke, with emergency cooling.《素问·生气通天论》：被暑邪所伤，会大量出汗；烦躁时喘息有声，安静时也胡言多语，身体热得像烧着的炭，要出汗才能散热。「体若燔炭」与「多言」并见，正是高热伴神志改变——今天要按热射病立即急救降温的那一类。

「氣虛身熱，得之傷暑」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.53: deficient qi with a hot body comes from summerheat injury. Summerheat both drains fluids and exhausts qi, so later stages bring heavy sweating, breathlessness and a weak pulse — injury to qi and yin, and at worst their collapse; it is also why mild cases are cleared of heat rather than made to sweat more.《素问·刺志论》：气虚而身热，是被暑邪所伤。暑邪既伤津又耗气，所以中暑后期会出现大汗、气短、脉弱的气阴两伤，重者气阴两脱；这也是轻证只宜清暑、不宜过度发汗的道理。

「冒暑大熱，霍亂吐瀉」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

A treatment entry in Zhenjiu Dacheng, volume 7: 'struck by summerheat with high fever, sudden vomiting and diarrhoea', followed by BL40, Bailao, CV12, LI11, the Shixuan points, ST36 and LI4. Bleeding BL40, LI11 and Shixuan to clear heat in the severe pattern, and LI4 and ST36 in the mild one, both have their classical basis here; today high fever with vomiting and diarrhoea first needs fluids and an electrolyte check.《针灸大成》卷七治症条目：「冒暑大热，霍乱吐泻」，下列委中、百劳、中脘、曲池、十宣、足三里、合谷。重证主穴里的委中、曲池、十宣放血清热，轻证里的合谷、足三里，都能在这一条里找到古代依据；高热伴上吐下泻今天首先要补液、查电解质。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Mild轻证

Summer-heat with dampness obstructing the exterior (mild pattern)暑热夹湿，郁于肌表（轻证）

How it presents辨证要点 Develops during summer or heat exposure, with dizziness, headache, feverishness, reduced sweating, chest oppression, nausea, vomiting, thirst, fatigue and drowsiness. There is no marked alteration of consciousness or convulsion. The tongue has a white greasy coating and the pulse is soggy and rapid. The defining feature is the coexistence of heat and dampness: feverishness with impaired sweating, chest oppression, nausea and heaviness.夏季或高温环境中发病，头晕、头痛、身热、少汗，胸闷、恶心、呕吐、烦渴、疲倦、嗜睡；热象存在但尚无明显神志障碍和抽搐。舌苔白腻，脉濡数。重点是暑热与湿浊并见：身热而汗出不畅，兼有胸闷、恶心、困重。

Principle治法 Release the exterior, clear summer-heat, harmonize the middle burner and transform dampness (traditional draining method; modern care must first remove the patient from heat, cool them and assess dehydration).解表清暑，和中化湿（传统针刺泻法；现代临床必须先离开热环境、降温及评估脱水程度）

ST43 is the Stomach shu-stream point and regulates Yangming qi, complementing LI4 through an upper- and lower-limb pairing. PC6 is the Pericardium luo-connecting point and confluent point of Yin Wei, connecting the chest, diaphragm and stomach to address nausea and chest oppression. ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, supports middle-burner function and dampness transformation. Do not automatically apply the strong stimulation or bloodletting used in severe patterns merely because the patient feels hot.大椎为督脉经穴、诸阳之会，配合谷疏泄阳明、解表清暑；陷谷是胃经输穴，兼有调节阳明气机的作用，与合谷组成手足阳明远近配伍。内关为心包络穴、八脉交会穴，通阴维脉，联系胸膈与胃，针对恶心、胸闷；足三里为胃经合穴，健运中焦、和胃化湿，兼顾暑后气弱。此型不应仅因身热而直接套用重证的强刺激醒神、刺络放血。

Severe重证

Summer-heat obstructing consciousness (severe pattern)暑热蒙心（重证）

How it presents辨证要点 Abrupt severe summer-heat illness with high fever, absent sweating, burning-hot skin, dry lips and mouth, flushed face, red eyes, intense thirst, agitation and potentially coma or convulsions. The tongue is red or crimson with reduced moisture and a thick yellow greasy coating; the pulse is flooding and rapid. Unlike the mild pattern, consciousness is significantly impaired or heat is extreme, sometimes with wind stirring. Unlike qi-yin collapse, the initial presentation is dominated by excess heat obstructing consciousness rather than cold sweat, pallor, cold limbs and a faint pulse.暑热暴发，壮热无汗，皮肤灼热，口干唇燥，面红目赤，烦渴多饮，烦躁不安，甚至神志昏迷、抽搐、四肢拘急。舌红或绛、少津，苔黄厚腻，脉洪数。与轻证区别在于出现明显神志障碍、热势炽盛或热极动风表现；与气阴两脱区别在于初期以邪热闭阻为主，而非冷汗、面白、肢冷、脉微。

Principle治法 Clear summer-heat, open the orifices, restore consciousness and extinguish wind to relieve convulsions (traditional draining method; emergency care and cooling must never be delayed by acupuncture or bloodletting).清泄暑热，开窍醒脑，息风止痉（传统治法为泻法；现代热射病须立即急救，针刺及放血不得延误降温）

Modern grading (mild to moderate)现代分级（轻中度）

Heat exhaustion or heat cramps (mild to moderate heat illness)热衰竭或热痉挛（轻中度热相关疾病）

How it presents辨证要点 After heat exposure or exertion, weakness, headache, nausea, dizziness, rapid heartbeat, heavy sweating and thirst; heat cramps primarily cause painful muscle spasms. Consciousness is usually preserved without the marked confusion or seizures of heatstroke. Persisting worsening, vomiting that prevents oral hydration or symptoms beyond one hour require medical assessment.高温或高强度活动后出现乏力、头痛、恶心、头晕、心悸、大量出汗、口渴；热痉挛主要表现为肌肉痛性痉挛。患者通常尚清醒，无热射病所见的明显意识障碍或持续抽搐；离开热环境、休息和补液后仍持续加重、呕吐无法饮水或症状超过1小时者须就医。

Principle治法 Move to a cool place, rest, cool the body and replace fluids (acupuncture is adjunctive and cannot replace assessment of dehydration, electrolytes or organ function).离开热环境，休息降温和补充体液（针灸只作辅助，不替代脱水、电解质及器官功能评估）

Collapse (critical)脱证（危重）

Collapse of qi and yin (critical pattern)气阴两脱（危重证）

How it presents辨证要点 After severe heat illness, the patient develops profuse sweating or beads of sweat, pallor, shortness of breath, cold extremities, profound fatigue or deep coma, with a faint, thready or nearly imperceptible pulse. Dry mouth and a crimson tongue with little moisture may be present; circulatory collapse may instead produce a pale tongue and faint pulse. This contrasts with the flushed face, extreme fever and flooding rapid pulse of heat obstructing consciousness. Modern assessment must urgently consider heatstroke, hypovolemic shock and multiple-organ injury rather than treating this solely as a traditional deficiency pattern.重度暑热后出现大汗淋漓或汗出如珠，面色苍白，呼吸短促，四肢厥冷，精神萎靡甚至深度昏迷，脉微细或欲绝。可见口干、舌质红绛而少津；也可能因循环衰竭表现为舌淡、脉微。与暑热蒙心的壮热、面红、脉洪数形成区别。此型在现代医学中必须首先考虑热射病、低血容量性休克及多器官损伤，不能仅按中医虚脱处理。

Principle治法 Restore qi, secure collapse, nourish yin and reverse critical deterioration (traditionally using moxa and tonification; modern resuscitation, cooling and organ monitoring are essential).益气固脱，滋阴救逆（传统以艾灸、补法为主；现代医学必须同步复苏、降温和监测器官功能）

GV20 is a Du vessel meeting point of the yang channels, traditionally used to raise yang and secure collapse. LU9 is the Lung yuan-source and shu-stream earth point, supporting lung qi and pulse regulation. KI7 is the Kidney jing-river metal point, traditionally used in fluid and sweating disorders and to support kidney function. ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, supports qi and spleen function; CV4 is traditionally used to replenish original qi. 🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern medicine views heat-related illness as a spectrum that includes heat cramps, heat exhaustion and heatstroke. Heat cramps typically involve painful muscle spasms after activity in hot conditions and are associated with fluid and electrolyte loss. Heat exhaustion presents with heavy sweating, weakness, headache, nausea, dizziness, rapid heartbeat and thirst, usually without altered mental status. Heatstroke is a life-threatening failure of thermoregulation accompanied by central nervous system dysfunction; confusion, seizures, coma and multiple-organ injury may occur. Skin does not have to be dry, and the presence or absence of sweating cannot rule heatstroke in or out. Modern medicine also distinguishes classic heatstroke, often associated with prolonged environmental heat exposure, from exertional heatstroke, which commonly occurs during strenuous exercise or physical labour. Diagnosis focuses on heat exposure, core temperature, neurological manifestations and laboratory assessment. Hospital investigations may include electrolytes, renal and liver function, coagulation studies, creatine kinase, glucose, blood gases and urinalysis to assess dehydration, rhabdomyolysis, organ injury and coagulopathy. Suspected heatstroke requires an immediate 911 call and rapid active cooling. Cold-water immersion is an important rapid cooling method for exertional heatstroke when feasible, alongside monitoring of consciousness, breathing and circulation. Never force oral fluids or medication on an unconscious person. Heat exhaustion that is significant, persists beyond one hour, worsens or prevents oral hydration because of vomiting also requires medical assessment. TCM patterns do not correspond one-to-one with modern diagnoses. Summer-heat with dampness may overlap with some mild heat-related illnesses; summer-heat obstructing consciousness may overlap with severe heatstroke involving neurological dysfunction; qi-yin collapse may describe traditional features resembling severe dehydration or circulatory failure but is not equivalent to a modern diagnosis of shock. Clinical teaching must distinguish heat illness from stroke, acute coronary syndrome, hypoglycaemia, septic shock and drug-related hyperthermia. Acupuncture is an adjunctive traditional intervention, not first-line treatment for heatstroke. There is no evidence that needling or bloodletting can replace rapid cooling or organ support. For suspected heatstroke, emergency services, transport and cooling take priority over pattern differentiation, point selection and needle retention.现代医学将热相关疾病视为连续谱，主要包括热痉挛、热衰竭和热射病。热痉挛常表现为高温活动后肌肉疼痛和痉挛，与出汗、电解质及体液丢失有关；热衰竭表现为大量出汗、乏力、头痛、恶心、头晕、心跳加快和口渴，通常仍保持清醒；热射病则是体温调节失效并伴中枢神经系统功能障碍的危及生命急症，可出现意识混乱、抽搐、昏迷及多器官损伤。热射病不一定表现为皮肤干燥，也不能仅凭是否出汗判断。现代医学按病因还可区分经典型热射病（classic heatstroke，常与持续高温环境暴露有关）和劳力型热射病（exertional heatstroke，常发生于高强度运动或体力劳动）。诊断重点是热暴露史、核心体温、中枢神经系统表现及实验室检查。医院可能检查电解质、肾功能、肝功能、凝血功能、肌酸激酶、血糖、血气及尿液，以评估脱水、横纹肌溶解、肝肾损伤和凝血异常。疑似热射病应立即拨打 911，快速主动降温；条件允许时冷水浸泡是劳力型热射病的重要快速降温方式，同时持续监测意识、呼吸和循环。意识不清者不得强行喂水或口服药物。热衰竭症状不轻、持续超过 1 小时、加重或伴呕吐无法饮水，也应接受医疗评估。中医证型与现代病因并非一一对应：暑热夹湿可能与部分轻型热相关疾病的表现重叠；暑热蒙心可能与严重热射病伴中枢神经系统障碍的表现重叠；气阴两脱可能描述严重脱水、循环衰竭等传统证候特征，但不能直接等同于现代医学的休克诊断。临床教学中应特别区分中暑与脑卒中、急性冠脉综合征、低血糖、感染性休克及药物相关高热。针灸属于辅助性传统治疗，不是热射病的一线治疗，也没有证据证明针刺或放血能够替代快速降温和器官支持。疑似热射病时，优先呼叫急救、转运和降温，不应等待辨证、取穴或留针。
