Form 5: Pulling nine oxen backwards by the tail第5式 · 倒拽九牛尾势

Pull back 3 times on each side; one set left, then one right每侧向后拽 3 次；先左后右各做一组

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Acute disc herniation or a back strain that has not healed: skip it for now腰椎间盘突出急性期、腰扭伤未好：先不做
  • Osteoporosis: keep the turn small and comfortable, never combined with bending forward骨质疏松：转身幅度小，只转到舒服的角度，不同时弯腰
  • Knee pain: keep the bow stance high膝盖痛：弓步站高一些

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Step the left foot back into a right bow stance; make fists, right fist in front with the elbow bent at about shoulder height, left fist behind左脚后撤一步成右弓步，两手握拳，右拳在前、屈肘约与肩同高，左拳在身后
  2. Let the waist drive the arms: turn slightly to the right and draw both arms back, as if pulling an ox backwards by the tail腰带动两臂：身体稍向右转，两臂向后拽，像倒拉牛尾
  3. Turn back and let the arms ease forward and open身体转回，两臂顺势向前展开放松
  4. After 3 pulls, bring the foot back; step the right foot back with the left hand in front and do 3 more拽、展 3 次后，收脚还原；换右脚后撤、左手在前，再做 3 次

🌬 Breathe in as you pull back, out as you ease forward; or breathe naturally.向后拽时吸气，向前展时呼气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • The waist leads; the arms follow动作由腰带动，两臂只是跟着走
  • Front knee no further forward than the toes, back leg straight前腿膝盖不超过脚尖，后腿伸直
  • As you pull, the front fist is roughly at eye level and the eyes look at it拽的时候前臂拳头约在眼睛高度，目视前拳

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Pulling with the arms only, waist still只用手臂拉，腰不动
  • Bow stance too deep, knee caving inward弓步太低、膝盖内扣
  • Twisting too hard转身过猛

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Stand with feet parallel instead of a bow stance and do only the waist-led pull and release; or do the upper-body part seated.两脚平行站立不做弓步，只做腰带动两臂的拽和展；也可以坐着做上身部分。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Nine oxen stands for great strength and pulling backwards for the posture, a cue for the waist and arms working together. The old verse says the effort is in the shoulders and arms, and the eyes watch the fist.「九牛」形容力量大，「倒拽」是向后拉的姿态，借来提示腰臂协同用力的方向。旧本歌诀说「用力在于两膀，观拳须注双瞳」，即用肩臂发力、眼看拳头。
