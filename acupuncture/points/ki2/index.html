📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or seated with the foot relaxed or slightly turned medially so the medial border of the foot is clearly exposed.仰卧位或坐位，足部自然放松或微向内侧翻转，便于显露足内侧缘。

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Medial malleolus tip to sole = 3 cun. KI2 is not measured along the vertical line from the medial malleolus tip; it sits in the hollow inferior to the navicular tuberosity on the medial border of the foot at the red-white skin junction.内踝尖到足底折作 3 寸。然谷不在内踝垂线上下量寸，而在足内侧缘舟骨粗隆下方凹陷中，位于赤白肉际。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate posteriorly along the base of the first metatarsal to find the prominent bony bump on the medial border of the foot — the navicular tuberosity; the point sits in the depression just below its lower margin.顺着第一跖骨底向后滑摸，在足内侧缘摸到明显隆起的骨性突起——舟骨粗隆，穴位就在该粗隆下缘的凹陷处。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located in the hollow inferior to the navicular tuberosity at the red-white skin border. Superficially lie the medial plantar nerve and plantar venous branches; deeper lie the origin of abductor hallucis, the tibialis posterior tendon, and the navicular. Avoid larger superficial venous branches, insert perpendicularly 0.5–1 cun, and avoid repeated deep probing.穴位在足舟骨粗隆下方凹陷、赤白肉际处。浅层有足底内侧神经及足底静脉分支分布，深面为拇展肌起始部、胫后肌腱及舟骨；进针应避开较粗大的浅静脉支，直刺深度控制在 0.5–1 寸，勿向深处反复大幅提插探寻。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gentle pressure or transverse stroking in the hollow below the navicular tuberosity usually elicits local soreness and distension; a shock-like sensation radiating toward the sole or toes indicates contact with a medial plantar nerve branch and calls for slight withdrawal and redirection.指腹在舟骨粗隆下方凹陷处轻轻按压或横向拨动，常可触及局部酸胀感；若出现向足底或足趾放散的触电感，提示触及足底内侧神经分支，应略退针调整角度。

  • Drifting distally toward the first metatarsophalangeal joint or dorsally onto the foot dorsum: moving away from the hollow inferior to the navicular tuberosity at the red-white border shifts off the target landmark and alters local tendon and joint-capsule relationships.误取到第一跖趾关节内侧或偏足背侧：偏近端或偏背侧则离开舟骨粗隆下方赤白肉际，局部肌腱与关节囊结构不同。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Irregular periods月经不调

Suitable as an adjunct when menstrual irregularity involves kidney yin deficiency or internal deficient heat with early cycles, scant bright blood, dry throat, or five-center heat; not primary for pronounced cold stagnation or organic gynecologic disease, which require gynecologic evaluation first, and pregnancy contraindications/caution must be observed.偏于肾阴虚或虚热内扰致月经提前、量少色红或伴咽干五心烦热时可酌配然谷调经清虚热；若属实寒凝滞或器质性妇科病变则不以此穴为主，需先妇科检查，且孕期须注意禁针/慎针规范。

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

Helpful as an adjunct for dry throat with mild discomfort and afternoon warmth due to yin deficiency with blazing fire; for acute wind-heat or purulent tonsillitis with marked redness, swelling, and severe pain, points such as LU11, LI1, or LI4 are typically favored over KI2.偏于阴虚火旺、慢性或反复咽干微痛伴午后潮热时可配然谷滋阴降虚火；急性风热外感或化脓性扁桃体炎红肿热痛明显时，常首选少商、商阳、合谷等清泄实热，然谷非急性实热首选。

Knee pain膝痛 ⚖️

Used only as a secondary adjunct when mild medial or lower-limb aching relates to Kidney-channel disharmony; knee osteoarthritis, meniscus, or ligament injuries rely primarily on local knee points such as EX-LE4, EX-LE5, SP10, or GB34 rather than KI2.仅在足少阴肾经循行或下肢气血失调相关的膝内侧/下肢隐痛辅助调理中酌参；膝关节骨关节炎、半月板或韧带损伤以局部膝眼、内外膝眼、血海、阳陵泉等为主，然谷不作膝痛常规主穴。

Where is KI2 Rángǔ?然谷（KI2）在哪里？

On the medial foot, below the tuberosity of the navicular bone, at the border of the red and white skin.足内侧，足舟骨粗隆下方，赤白肉际处。

How is KI2 needled?然谷（KI2）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of KI2?然谷（KI2）是哪类特定穴？

Ying-spring (fire)荥穴（火）

Main safety point at KI2?然谷（KI2）最要注意的安全问题？

Bone is not far down; avoid superficial veins. The Jiayi Jing warns against drawing much blood, so press after withdrawal; withdraw for electric tingling into the sole.骨面不深，避开浅静脉；《甲乙经》说「刺无多见血」，出针按压；有向足底放射的电麻感即退针。
