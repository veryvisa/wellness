📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Palm up with thumb naturally abducted and thenar muscles relaxed; do not clench the thumb.手掌向上、拇指自然外展，鱼际肌放松；不要用力捏紧拇指。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the midpoint of the radial side of the first metacarpal and take LU10 on the border between palmar light skin and dorsal darker skin.沿第 1 掌骨桡侧找中点，在掌侧浅色皮肤与手背较深皮肤的赤白肉际取鱼际。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Abductor pollicis brevis and opponens lie beneath, with recurrent median and superficial radial nerve branches nearby; avoid broken/infected skin, scars and strong radiating tingling, and do not deeply drive toward the palm.穴下为拇短展肌和拇对掌肌，附近有正中神经返支及桡神经浅支末梢；避开皮肤破损、感染、瘢痕和强烈放射麻感，勿深刺向掌心。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently abduct the thumb to outline the thenar mound; follow the first metacarpal to its middle. The skin-colour border is more reliable than the thickest muscle belly.轻外展拇指使鱼际隆起，沿第 1 掌骨由拇指根向腕侧摸到中段；皮色交界线比肌腹最厚处更可靠。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    LU10 is the midpoint of the outer edge of the thumb mound, not the web space LI4 or the thumbnail point LU11.拇指根下“鱼肚”外缘的中点是鱼际；它不在虎口（合谷）也不在拇指甲旁（少商）。

Perpendicular; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。
