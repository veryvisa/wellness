📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or seated with slight neck extension, the head midline and shoulders relaxed; identify the suprasternal notch and do not self-needle the anterior neck.仰卧或坐位轻度后仰，颈部正中、肩放松；先确认胸骨上窝，切勿自行针刺颈前。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    CV22 lies in the central suprasternal hollow; identify the notch between the medial clavicles and upper manubrium, then confirm the midline.天突位于胸骨上窝中央凹陷；先辨认两侧锁骨内端与胸骨柄上缘形成的窝，再确认正中。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The trachea, esophagus and mediastinal inlet lie behind the notch; common carotid arteries, internal jugular veins and lower thyroid are lateral, with the pleural domes nearby. Deep posterior or lateral procedures risk airway, vascular or pleural injury. Qualified clinical assessment is essential; never insert blindly or deeply.窝后方邻气管、食管及纵隔入口，侧方为颈总动脉、颈内静脉和甲状腺下部；任何深向后或偏侧操作都可能伤及气道、大血管或胸膜顶。高风险穴仅供合格临床人员评估，绝不盲目深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Touch only the margins of the suprasternal hollow lightly; do not press its base. Stop and refer if a pulse, mass or thyroid abnormality is felt or voice changes are present.只轻触胸骨上窝边缘以辨认凹陷，不压迫窝底；如触及搏动、肿块、甲状腺异常或伴声音改变，应停止并转诊。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Cross-check the sequence with CV21 below the notch and CV23 above the hyoid; CV22 remains defined by the centre of the suprasternal notch.与璇玑（胸骨上窝下方）及廉泉（舌骨上方）作上下次序互校；天突始终以胸骨上窝中央为准。

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

Consider for sore throat or voice difficulty with suprasternal tightness; CV23 is more tongue-base and swallowing focused, while airway compromise is an emergency.咽喉肿痛、声音不利并伴胸骨上窝紧张时参考；廉泉更偏舌根和吞咽，气道受阻时先急救。

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

A supporting point for cough with globus or suprasternal discomfort; severe wheeze, hemoptysis or inspiratory difficulty needs care first.咳嗽兼咽部异物感或胸骨上窝不适时配穴参考；气喘急重、咳血或吸气困难优先就医。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

A traditional reference for throat constriction or voice change, but stridor signals airway narrowing and needs immediate assessment rather than needling.喉部紧缩或声音改变可作传统参考，但喘鸣是气道受限信号，不应先针刺，立即评估。

Where is CV22 Tiāntū?天突（CV22）在哪里？

In the anterior neck, at the centre of the suprasternal fossa, on the anterior midline.颈前区，胸骨上窝中央，前正中线上。

How is CV22 needled?天突（CV22）怎么刺？

First perpendicular 0.2–0.3 cun, then turn the tip downward and advance slowly close behind the manubrium; 0.5–1 cun.先直刺 0.2–0.3 寸，再将针尖转向下方，紧靠胸骨柄后缘缓慢刺入；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of CV22?天突（CV22）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Conception and Yin Linking vessels任脉、阴维脉交会穴

Main safety point at CV22?天突（CV22）最要注意的安全问题？

The trachea lies behind, the aortic arch, brachiocephalic trunk and left brachiocephalic vein lie behind and below the manubrium, and the common carotids and pleural domes lie laterally (pneumothorax): go in 0.2 cun perpendicular, then down along the back of the manubrium; withdraw on elastic resistance, coughing, or a needle that pulses.后方是气管，胸骨柄后下方是主动脉弓、头臂干与左头臂静脉，两侧有颈总动脉与胸膜顶（气胸）：先直刺 0.2 寸再贴胸骨柄后缘向下，针下有弹性阻力或患者呛咳、针体随搏动即退针。
