🪡 Needling刺法★

Seated, head slightly forward and neck relaxed; insert slowly toward the mandible正坐头微前倾、项部放松，向下颌方向缓慢刺入 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Where is GV16 Fēngfǔ?风府（GV16）在哪里？

In the posterior neck, directly below the external occipital protuberance, in the depression between the two trapezius muscles (about 1 cun above the posterior hairline).颈后区，枕外隆凸直下，两侧斜方肌之间凹陷中（约后发际正中直上 1 寸）。

How is GV16 needled?风府（GV16）怎么刺？

Seated, head slightly forward and neck relaxed; insert slowly toward the mandible; 0.5–1 cun.正坐头微前倾、项部放松，向下颌方向缓慢刺入；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of GV16?风府（GV16）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Governor and Yang Linking vessels督脉、阳维脉交会穴

Main safety point at GV16?风府（GV16）最要注意的安全问题？

Beyond the posterior atlanto-occipital membrane lie the cisterna magna and medulla: advance slowly toward the mandible only, never upward or deep toward the foramen magnum; withdraw on any electric sensation, dizziness or nausea. Classically no moxibustion.深面隔寰枕后膜即小脑延髓池与延髓：针尖只朝下颌方向缓刺，绝不向上或向枕骨大孔深刺；有触电感、头晕、恶心即退针。古籍禁灸。
