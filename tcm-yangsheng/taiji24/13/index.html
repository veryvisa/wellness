Form 13: Right heel kick第13式 · 右蹬脚

once1 次

Yòu dēngjiǎo

Qigong养生功法 › Tai chi (simplified 24-form)太极拳（简化二十四式） › Form 13: Right heel kick第13式 · 右蹬脚

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚

  • If you fall easily or your balance is poor: do the knee-lift-only version first, holding the back of a chair or a counter with one hand; lift the knee only as high as feels steady and do not kick; if one leg is too unsteady, keep the toes touching the floor容易跌倒、平衡不好：先做「抬膝不蹬」版，一手扶着椅背或台面，膝盖抬到自己稳的高度就停，脚不蹬出；单腿站不稳就脚尖点地不离地
  • Kick only as high as suits you: a heel below waist height is still tai chi, no need to reach chest height; keep the standing knee slightly bent, not locked蹬脚的高度按自己来：脚跟到腰以下也是太极，不必踢到胸口高；支撑腿膝盖微弯，不要锁直
  • After a hip replacement, with low back pain or tight hamstrings: do not kick, only lift the knee; if your surgeon gave you movement limits, follow them换过髋关节、腰背痛或腘绳肌（大腿后侧）紧：不蹬脚，只抬膝；有手术医生的动作限制，照医嘱

🧘 The movement动作

  1. From the high pat: the left hand, palm up, slides forward over the right wrist so the hands cross; lift the left foot and step it forward to the left, heel first接高探马：左手掌心向上，从右手腕上方向前穿出，两手交叉；左脚提起，向左前方迈出，脚跟先着地
  2. Shift forward into a left bow stance; the hands part, arcing out and down to the sides重心前移成左弓步；两手向两侧、向下划弧分开
  3. The hands keep arcing down and in, crossing in front of the chest, right hand outside, both palms in; draw the right foot in beside the left, toes touching两手继续向下、向里划弧，在胸前交叉合抱，右手在外，掌心都向里；右脚收到左脚内侧，脚尖点地
  4. Part the hands to the sides at a little below shoulder height, palms out, elbows slightly bent; at the same time lift the right knee and then slowly push the right heel out to the right front (about 30 degrees), toes pulled back; the right arm is above the right leg两手向左右分开，平举到略低于肩，掌心向外、肘微弯；同时右腿屈膝提起，再慢慢向右前方（约 30 度）蹬出，脚尖回勾、力在脚跟；右臂和右腿上下相对

🌬 Breathe in as the hands cross and the knee lifts, out as the hands part and the heel pushes; or breathe naturally.两手合抱、提膝时吸气，分手蹬脚时呼气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • Lift the knee first, then push the heel; two separate stages, never a swing先提膝、再蹬脚，两步分开做，不要甩腿
  • Standing knee slightly bent, trunk upright, no leaning back支撑腿微屈，上身竖直，不向后仰
  • Look towards the right hand; fixing your eyes on one point steadies you眼睛看右手方向，看定一点帮自己站稳

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Swinging the leg out fast and high用腿甩出去，踢得又快又高
  • Locking the standing knee支撑腿膝盖锁直
  • Leaning back or to one side as you kick蹬脚时上身后仰或歪向一侧

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Knee lift only: hold a chair back, lift the right knee to a comfortable height, hold for two or three seconds and lower it slowly; for more stability keep the toes on the floor and do only the crossing and parting of the hands.抬膝不蹬：扶着椅背，右膝抬到舒服的高度，停两三秒再慢慢放下；更稳一点就脚尖点地不离地，只做两手合抱、分开。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 A 'heel kick' is not a snap kick: the toes are pulled back and the heel is pushed out slowly. It is traditional teaching language, not a research finding.「蹬脚」和「踢脚」不同：蹬是脚尖回勾、用脚跟慢慢推出去。这是传统的教法用语，不是研究结论。
