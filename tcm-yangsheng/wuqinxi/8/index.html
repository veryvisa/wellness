Form 8: Monkey picking第8式 · 猿摘

Left then right is one round; 2 rounds in all先左后右各做一次为一遍，共 2 遍

Yuán zhāi

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚 ⚠ Deep squat深蹲

  • Poor balance or a history of falls: hold the back of a chair with one hand, no stepping and heels down容易跌倒、平衡不好：一只手扶椅背做，不迈步、不提脚跟
  • Knee pain or a hip or knee replacement: skip the T-step squat or bend only slightly, knees pointing the same way as the toes膝盖痛、换过髋或膝关节：丁步不下蹲或只微蹲，膝盖与脚尖同向
  • Neck problems or vertigo: turn the head only a little to follow the hand颈椎病、眩晕：转头看手的幅度小
  • Shoulder pain: reach up only as far as is pain-free肩痛：上伸到不痛的高度

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Step the left foot back and to the left, bending the right knee; the left hand hooks at the left waist while the right palm swings up forward and right左脚向左后方退一步，右腿屈膝；左手猿钩收到左腰侧，右掌向右前方摆起
  2. Shift your weight back into a squat on the left leg and bring the right foot in beside the left, toes touching the floor (a T-step); the right palm arcs down and left to beside the left temple, eyes following, as if searching a tree for fruit重心后移，左腿屈膝下蹲，右脚收到左脚内侧，脚尖点地成丁步；右掌向下、向左划弧到左太阳穴旁，目随手动，像在找树上的果子
  3. Take a big step forward and right with the right foot, straightening the left leg and lifting the left heel; the right hand drops into a hook as the left hand reaches up and right as if picking fruit右脚向右前方迈出一大步，左腿蹬直，左脚跟提起；右手下落变猿钩，左手向右上方伸出，像去摘果子
  4. Sit back, the left hand closing and drawing in, the right hand opening under the left elbow as if holding the peach you picked; bring the right foot back and repeat to the other side重心后移，左手变握拳收回，右手变掌托在左肘下，像捧着摘下的桃；收回右脚，换另一侧做

🌬 Breathe in as you crouch, out as you reach to pick; or breathe naturally.下蹲蓄势时吸气，伸手摘果时呼气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • Keep the eyes alert: look first, then pick眼神要灵，先看再摘
  • Squat in the T-step only as low as you can hold steady丁步下蹲只到稳得住的高度
  • Stretch the body up as you reach to pick伸手摘时身体向上舒展

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Squatting so low you struggle to rise蹲得太低、起不来
  • Stepping so far that the back foot is unsteady迈步太大、后脚站不稳
  • Eyes not following the hands, so the movement looks blank眼睛不跟手，动作发呆

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Chair version: hold a chair back with one hand; no stepping back or forward and no squat, both feet flat, and use the other hand only to search, pick and hold the peach.扶椅版：一只手扶椅背，不退步、不迈步、不下蹲，两脚平站，只用另一只手做找果、摘果、捧桃。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Traditional teaching says Monkey Picking imitates a monkey searching for, picking and holding fruit in the trees, and asks for the monkey's quick wits; the 'peach' is only an imagined prop.传统教法说猿摘学猴子在树间找果、摘果、捧果，要的是猴子的机灵；动作里的「桃」只是想象的道具。
