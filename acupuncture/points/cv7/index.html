Where is CV7 Yīnjiāo?阴交（CV7）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, 1 cun below the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.下腹部，脐中下 1 寸，前正中线上。

How is CV7 needled?阴交（CV7）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of CV7?阴交（CV7）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Conception and Penetrating vessels and Foot-Shaoyin channel任脉、冲脉、足少阴交会穴

Main safety point at CV7?阴交（CV7）最要注意的安全问题？
