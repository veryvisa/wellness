📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone or seated slightly leaning forward with shoulders and lower back relaxed, exposing the lumbar region and iliac crest landmarks.俯卧位或正坐微前倾位，双肩与腰背部放松，露出腰部与髂嵴标志。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: below the L2 spine.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），肾俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 2 腰椎棘突下定。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate the line joining the highest points of the iliac crests, which lies roughly level with the L4 spinous process, then count superiorly to the L2 spinous process; BL23 is located 1.5 cun lateral to the depression below the L2 spinous process.先摸两侧髂嵴最高点连线，该线约平第 4 腰椎棘突；再向上触摸第 3 腰椎棘突与第 2 腰椎棘突，肾俞位于第 2 腰椎棘突下凹陷处旁开 1.5 寸。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Use the patient's own index and middle fingers together (about 1.5 cun) or middle-finger cun as a lateral reference, while verifying against the posterior midline and erector spinae contour rather than shifting outward by finger width alone.以患者本人食指、中指并拢（约 1.5 寸）或中指同身寸作旁开参考，但须结合后正中线与竖脊肌隆起核对，不可单凭指宽盲目外移。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located 1.5 cun lateral to the lower border of the L2 spinous process; deep to the skin and thoracolumbar fascia lie the erector spinae and multifidus, with lumbar transverse processes and quadratus lumborum deeper, and the kidney anterolaterally at depth (with the right kidney slightly lower than the left). Needle obliquely toward the spine and never insert deeply perpendicularly or anterolaterally, protecting the kidney and dorsal branches of the lumbar vessels.位于第 2 腰椎棘突下旁开 1.5 寸处，皮肤与胸腰筋膜深面为竖脊肌外侧缘与多裂肌之间或竖脊肌肌腹，再深层为腰椎横突与腰方肌；其深部前外侧邻近肾脏（肾门约平 L1–L2，右肾比左肾略低）。进针须向脊柱方向浅斜刺，严禁向腹外侧深直刺，以防伤及肾脏及腰背动静脉分支。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gentle pressing and sliding palpation with the thumb pad 1.5 cun lateral to the L2 spinous process often reveals increased erector spinae tension, taut bands, or local tenderness, sometimes with aching distension toward the lower back or lower abdomen; excessive or lateral pressure can cause sharp discomfort or misread deeper structures.拇指指腹在 L2 棘突下旁开约 1.5 寸处轻柔按压与滑动触诊，可触及竖脊肌张力增高、条索或局部压痛，部分患者可出现向腰部或少腹方向的酸胀感；若用力过猛或向侧方顶压，易引起明显疼痛或误判深部结构。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Mistaking the L1 or L3 spinous process level (superiorly toward BL21 or inferiorly toward BL25): misreading the intercristal line as L2 shifts vertebral counting, and the L1 level sits closer to the renal hilum region where deep lateral needling carries greater risk.误取第 1 腰椎或第 3 腰椎棘突下旁开 1.5 寸（偏上为气海俞、偏下为大肠俞）：将髂嵴连线误认作 L2 水平会导致整组腰椎棘突数错，且 L1 水平更靠近肾门与肾蒂上方区域，向侧方深刺风险更高。
  • Moving too far laterally (beyond 1.5 cun toward the 3-cun line of BL52): shifting outward along the lateral border of erector spinae and quadratus lumborum moves closer to the posterolateral projection of the kidney and increases deep injury risk.旁开过宽（超过 1.5 寸达 3 寸一线，即志室 BL52 方向）：偏外侧进入竖脊肌外侧缘与腰方肌浅外侧，更靠近肾脏后外侧投影区，易导致深部误伤。
  • Deep perpendicular or anterolateral insertion: piercing too deeply through the thoracolumbar fascia and quadratus lumborum can injure the kidney, causing severe flank pain, hematuria, or retroperitoneal bleeding; follow textbook oblique insertion toward the spine at 0.5–1 cun, and do not needle during pregnancy.向腹外侧深直刺：垂直或偏向外侧深刺可穿透胸腰筋膜、腰方肌甚至伤及肾脏，引起剧烈腰痛、血尿或腹膜后出血；临床必须严格按教材向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–1 寸，孕期禁针。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Often selected for chronic deficiency-type low back pain or lingering lumbar soreness with fatigue (paired with KI3 and BL40), whereas acute sprain or strain with excess signs relies more on local ashi points, BL40, or GV26 rather than BL23 alone; avoid needling BL23 during pregnancy.慢性虚损性腰痛或久病腰酸乏力偏虚者常选肾俞，配合太溪、委中；急性扭挫伤实证偏重局部阿是穴或委中、水沟等，不单靠肾俞，且孕期腰痛禁针肾俞。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Suited to frequency, enuresis, or weak voiding related to deficient kidney qi and unstable lower burner (often paired with CV4 and SP6), whereas acute urinary retention, hematuria, or urinary infection requires prompt medical diagnosis rather than single-point care.偏于肾气不足、下元不固所致的小便频数、遗尿或老年排尿无力时可选肾俞配关元、三阴交；急性尿潴留、血尿或泌尿系感染者须先就医明确病因，不以单穴替代医疗。

Oedema水肿 ⚖️

Considered for chronic edema prominent below the waist with cold intolerance or spleen-kidney yang deficiency (paired with CV9, CV6, or SP9 as supportive care), whereas sudden generalized edema, oliguria, or dyspnea requires medical evaluation first.水肿日久、腰以下为甚、伴畏寒肢冷或脾肾阳虚者可选肾俞配水分、气海、阴陵泉等辅助调理；突发全身水肿、少尿、呼吸困难或心肾疾病者须先就医评估。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is BL23 Shènshū?肾俞（BL23）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of L2, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 2 腰椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL23 needled?肾俞（BL23）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–1 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of BL23?肾俞（BL23）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Kidney肾之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL23?肾俞（BL23）最要注意的安全问题？

The kidney is nearby at depth; avoid deep perpendicular insertion and use shallow oblique insertion toward the spine. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.深部邻近肾脏；避免深直刺，以向脊柱方向的浅斜刺为原则。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
