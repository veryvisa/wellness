Liuzijue (Six Healing Sounds)六字诀

Liuzijue is a breathing practice done through the mouth: you breathe in through the nose and, as you breathe out, shape the mouth for six different sounds, xu, he, hu, si, chui and xi, each paired with a slow movement. It is the gentlest and easiest to learn of the traditional sets, and people who cannot stand for long can do it seated. Because it trains the breath, most of the research on it comes from pulmonary rehabilitation for COPD. This page starts with when to stop and see a doctor, then shows how to say each sound and shape the mouth. Its usual English name, Six Healing Sounds, is a name, not a promise.六字诀是一套「用嘴吐气」的功法：鼻子吸气，嘴里吐气时摆出六种口型，念出嘘、呵、呼、呬、吹、嘻六个字，每个字配一组慢动作。它是几套传统功法里动作最轻、最好学的一套，站不久的人可以坐着做。也正因为练的是呼吸，它在研究里主要出现在慢阻肺的肺康复中。这一页先讲什么情况要停、要看医生，再讲六个字怎么念、口型怎么摆。英文里常叫它 Six Healing Sounds，这是习惯叫法，不是疗效承诺。

🚩 Before you start开始之前

  • Stop at once and call 911 with sudden chest pain or pressure, breathlessness that keeps getting worse, blue lips or nails, coughing up blood that will not stop, or being unable to finish a sentence马上停下、打 911：突然胸痛或胸口压榨感，喘不上气而且越来越重，嘴唇或指甲发紫，咯血不止，或者说话说不成句
  • See a doctor before practising if you are more short of breath than usual, are coughing up more phlegm, yellow-green phlegm or streaks of blood, or have a fever: this may be a flare-up, so let your doctor deal with it and wait until you are stable先看医生、先别练：气短比平时明显加重，痰变多、变黄绿或带血丝（咯血），发烧——这可能是急性加重，等医生处理、病情稳定后再练
  • With COPD, asthma or heart failure: in the research, Liuzijue was done by people in a stable phase, as part of pulmonary rehabilitation; keep your inhalers, oxygen and the rehab your doctor arranged, and do not cut them down because you are practising有慢阻肺、哮喘或心衰的人：六字诀在研究里是给稳定期病人做、放在肺康复里做的；吸入药、氧气和医生安排的肺康复照常，不要因为练功自己减量
  • If you feel dizzy or your hands, feet or lips tingle while breathing out, you are probably blowing too hard, too long or too fast: stop, breathe naturally, and next time make the sound softer and the breath shorter吐气时头晕、手脚或嘴唇发麻：多半是吐得太用力、太长或太快，马上停下，自然呼吸，下次声音放轻、吐气放短
  • Never hold the breath or force a long exhalation; take particular care, and check with your doctor first, with high blood pressure, glaucoma, a past collapsed lung, recent eye, chest or abdominal surgery, or a hernia不憋气、不硬拉长吐气：高血压、青光眼、有过气胸、近期做过眼部或胸腹部手术、有疝气的人尤其要注意，开始前先问医生
  • With pregnancy, heart disease, or recent surgery or a fracture, check with your doctor before starting; with knee pain, bend the knees only slightly and stand taller怀孕、心脏病、近期手术或骨折，开始前先问医生；膝盖痛的人下蹲只微微屈膝，站高一点

📜 Where it comes from从哪里来

Breathing out through different mouth shapes to look after the body is first described systematically in the Yangxing Yanming Lu, traditionally credited to the sixth-century Daoist Tao Hongjing. It says to breathe in through the nose and out through the mouth, gently and slowly, and that there is one way to breathe in but six ways to breathe out; its list is chui, hu, xi, he, xu and si (one early printed edition writes the last with a different character). Its pairings are not today's: it gives the he sound for the liver, where the modern set uses xu. By the Ming dynasty, Gao Lian's Eight Treatises on Following Life (1591) includes a rhyme pairing xu with the liver in spring, he with the heart in summer, si with the lungs in autumn, chui with the kidneys in winter, hu with the spleen in every season and xi with the 'triple burner'; that is where today's pairings come from. Another rhyme in the same book ends with a warning never to let the sound be heard, so the old method was silent. The Ming physician Li Chan, in his Introduction to Medicine, already cautioned that the six sounds do not suit everyone, naming people who are depleted and sweat easily. The old method was breath only, with no movements, and teachers did not agree on how to say the sounds. The Health Qigong Liuzijue most often practised today was compiled by China's General Administration of Sport around 2003: it fixed the pronunciation in pinyin, added a movement to each sound, and set the order xu, he, hu, si, chui, xi, with 6 exhalations per sound; beginners say the sounds aloud and later move to silent practice. The pronunciation, mouth shapes and movements below follow that version.用不同口型吐气来调养身体，现存最早的系统记载在旧题南朝梁陶弘景的《养性延命录·服气疗病篇》。书里说：「凡行气，以鼻纳气，以口吐气，微而引之」，又说「纳气有一，吐气有六」——吸气只有一种，吐气有六种，书里列的是吹、呼、唏、呵、嘘、呬（道藏本把呬写作「咽」，唏就是今天的嘻）。那时的配法和今天不一样：书里说「肝藏病者，眼疼，愁忧不乐，呵气出之」，用呵配肝，而今天是用嘘配肝。到明代，高濂《遵生八笺》（1591 年）收的《孙真人卫生歌》写成「春嘘明目夏呵心，秋呬冬吹肺肾宁，四季常呼脾化食，三焦嘻出热难停」，嘘肝、呵心、呼脾、呬肺、吹肾、嘻三焦的配法就是从这一路来的。同书《四季却病歌》最后一句是「切忌出声闻口耳」，古人要求吐气时不出声。明代医家李梴在《医学入门》里也提醒过：「至于六字诀，虽能发散外邪而中虚有汗者忌」，古代医家自己就说它不是人人都适合。古法原本只吐气、不配动作，各家读音也不统一。今天最常见的「健身气功·六字诀」由国家体育总局健身气功管理中心在 2003 年前后整理推广：用汉语拼音统一读音，每个字配一组动作，顺序是嘘、呵、呼、呬、吹、嘻，每个字吐 6 次；初学出声，熟练后逐渐转为无声。下面的读音、口型和动作按这一版。

🗓 How to practise怎么练

Start by learning the six sounds and mouth shapes in front of a mirror, saying each one aloud a few times to check the shape; add the movements once the shapes come easily. One round takes about 12 minutes; once a day is enough. Breathe out softly, slowly and evenly; never hold the breath and never force the exhalation longer; when the breath runs out, breathe in naturally, and do not try to make it as long as possible. If you cannot stand for long, sit on a chair and do the arm movements only. In the reviews, practice ran for 3 to 12 months.起步：先对着镜子学六个字的读音和口型，出声念，每个字念几遍，看口型对不对；口型熟了再配动作。整套一遍约 12 分钟，每天一遍即可。吐气轻、长、匀，不要憋气，也不要硬拉长；一口气吐完自然吸气，不追求吐得越长越好。站不久可以坐在椅子上做，只做手臂动作。综述里的练习周期从 3 个月到 12 个月不等。

Opening stance预备势

  1. Stand with feet together, then step the left foot out to shoulder width, knees slightly soft, arms hanging at the sides, eyes looking ahead and slightly down, a slight smile两脚并拢站立，左脚向左开步，与肩同宽，两膝微屈，两臂自然垂在体侧，目视前下方，面带微笑
  2. Opening: cup the palms in front of the belly and slowly raise them to the chest, then turn them over and press down to the navel起势：两掌在腹前捧起，慢慢上托到胸前，再翻掌下按到肚脐前
  3. Bend the knees a little and push the palms forward until the arms make a circle; turn the palms in, straighten up and bring the hands back to the navel微微屈膝，两掌向前拨出，两臂成圆；再转掌心向内，起身，两掌收回到肚脐前
  4. Cross the thumbs and rest the hands lightly over the navel; stand still for a few natural breaths两手虎口交叉，轻轻覆在肚脐上，静站几次自然呼吸

Closing收势

  1. Turn the palms in and bring them slowly back to the belly, thumbs crossed, resting over the navel; stand quietly for a moment两掌转掌心向内，慢慢抱回腹前，虎口交叉，轻覆肚脐，静站片刻
  2. Circle the hands around the navel, 6 times clockwise and then 6 times counter-clockwise两掌以肚脐为中心揉腹，顺时针 6 圈，再逆时针 6 圈
  3. Let the hands fall to the sides and bring the left foot back beside the right两手松开，垂回体侧，左脚收回，并步站立

🗣 The sounds at a glance几个字怎么念

#Form字诀Pinyin拼音IPASounds like近似读法
1Xu (the liver sound)嘘字诀xū[ɕy]Roughly 'shü': a soft 'sh' made with the tongue forward, gliding into the vowel of French 'tu' or German 'über'. Held long and quiet, like a long hushing sound.念「嘘」，声音轻而长，像让人小声一点时那一声「嘘——」；舌尖靠前，声音从槽牙两边的缝里出去。
2He (the heart sound)呵字诀hē[xɤ]Roughly 'huh': the h made at the back of the mouth, like the 'ch' in Scottish 'loch', the vowel like the 'u' in 'duh'. Said softly, like breathing warm air onto your hands.念「呵」，像哈一口热气那样轻轻吐出「hē——」，喉咙放松，声音从舌根和上腭之间出来。
3Hu (the spleen sound)呼字诀hū[xu]Roughly 'hoo', with the same throaty h as the he sound, lips rounded as if about to cool a spoonful of hot soup.念「呼」，嘴唇撮圆，像吹凉一口热汤之前那一声「hū——」。
4Si (the lung sound)呬字诀sī[sɹ̩]Say 'sss', a long hiss with almost no vowel, as if saying 'sz' through closed front teeth. Dictionaries give this character another reading, but Health Qigong standardised it as si, and this page follows that.念「斯」（sī），不是 xì。呬在一般字典里读 xì，健身气功版为统一读法定作 sī，本页照这一版。声音像一声长长的「丝——」。
5Chui (the kidney sound)吹字诀chuī[ʈʂʰuei]Roughly 'chway': 'ch' as in 'church' with the tongue curled back a little, then 'oo', then 'ee', run together. It is the only one of the six whose mouth shape moves.念「吹」，这是六个字里唯一一个口型会动的字：分三步，先「ch（吃）」，再「u（乌）」，最后「i（衣）」，连起来就是「吹」。
6Xi (the triple-burner sound)嘻字诀xī[ɕi]Roughly 'she', said with a slight smile.念「嘻」，像微笑着轻轻笑出的那一声「xī——」，嘴角微微上扬。

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 COPD: a meta-analysis of 40 randomised trials with 3,137 people found that the Liuzijue groups gained on average 0.17 litres in forced expiratory volume in one second, walked about 33 metres further in six minutes, scored about 2 points lower on the COPD Assessment Test, and had lower anxiety and depression scores. The authors concluded it may be an effective add-on for lung function, exercise capacity and quality of life.慢阻肺：一篇汇总 40 项随机试验、3137 人的荟萃分析，六字诀组的第一秒用力呼气量平均多 0.17 升，6 分钟步行多走约 33 米，慢阻肺评估测试（CAT）少约 2 分，焦虑抑郁量表分数也下降。作者的结论是：它「可能是一种有效的辅助方法」，用来改善肺功能、活动能力和生活质量。 [1]
  • E2 Stable COPD: a second meta-analysis of 14 randomised trials with 920 people, compared with usual care over 3 to 12 months, found improvements in breathlessness (mMRC), six-minute walk distance, lung function and quality-of-life scores. The authors stressed that because of methodological bias and possible placebo effects, more research is needed to confirm this.稳定期慢阻肺：另一篇汇总 14 项随机试验、920 人的荟萃分析，对照组是常规护理，练习 3–12 个月；气短评分（mMRC）、6 分钟步行距离、肺功能和生活质量问卷都有改善。作者特别说明，因为研究方法上的偏倚和安慰剂效应，还需要更多研究来确认。 [2]
  • E2 A 2026 meta-analysis of 22 randomised trials with 1,871 people looked at tai chi, Baduanjin, Liuzijue and Yijinjing together: compared with usual care or standard pulmonary rehabilitation, these practices improved lung function and six-minute walk distance, and in the subgroup analysis Liuzijue and Yijinjing showed the largest lung-function gains. No serious adverse events were reported in the included studies.2026 年一篇汇总 22 项随机试验、1871 人的荟萃分析，把太极、八段锦、六字诀、易筋经放在一起看：和常规护理或常规肺康复比，这类功法改善了肺功能和 6 分钟步行距离；分组分析里，六字诀和易筋经的肺功能进步最大。纳入的研究没有报告严重不良事件。 [3]
  • E3 Which set is best is unclear: a 2025 network meta-analysis of 83 trials with 6,707 COPD patients ranked six practices; Liuzijue came third for lung function, fourth for walking distance and sixth for the COPD Assessment Test, with Wuqinxi or Yijinjing ranked first on each. Such rankings are very uncertain; choosing a set you will keep doing matters more than its rank.哪一套最好，说不准：2025 年一篇汇总 83 项试验、6707 名慢阻肺病人的网状荟萃分析，把六套功法排名，六字诀在肺功能一项排第三、步行距离排第四、CAT 评分排第六，各项排名第一的是五禽戏或易筋经。这种排名本身不确定性很大；选一套能长期坚持的，比追排名更实在。 [4]
  • E5 Who this applies to: nearly all of these studies were done in people with stable COPD, usually on top of their usual care. There is no evidence of the same weight for people without lung disease, for people in a flare-up, or for asthma or other lung conditions. A PubMed search for Liuzijue in November 2026 found 91 records and no retracted papers.适用范围：这些研究几乎都是在稳定期慢阻肺病人中做的，常常是在原有的用药和护理之外加练。对没有肺病的人、急性加重期的病人、哮喘或其他肺病，没有同样分量的证据。2026 年 11 月在 PubMed 检索六字诀，91 篇记录里没有查到被撤稿的论文。 [5]
  • E5 The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) regards tai chi and qigong as generally safe mind-body practices; people who are pregnant should talk with their health care providers before starting.美国国家补充与整合健康中心（NCCIH）把太极、气功视为一般比较安全的身心练习；怀孕的人开始前先和医生商量。 [6]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Tradition pairs the six sounds with the five organ systems and the 'triple burner': xu with the liver, he with the heart, hu with the spleen, si with the lungs, chui with the kidneys and xi with the triple burner, and with the seasons too, more xu in spring, he in summer, si in autumn and chui in winter. The old books do not agree on these pairings; the earliest one differs from today's. These are traditional ideas and teaching cues, not research findings.传统把六个字配给五脏和三焦：嘘配肝、呵配心、呼配脾、呬配肺、吹配肾、嘻配三焦，还配上四季，说春天多嘘、夏天多呵、秋天多呬、冬天多吹。不同古书配法并不一致，最早的《养性延命录》就和今天不同。这些是传统的说法和教学提示，不是研究结论。

What E1–E5 meanE1–E5 是什么意思
  • E1 Systematic reviews / guidelines系统综述或指南
  • E2 Small or few randomised trials少量或小样本随机试验
  • E3 Observational studies (association)观察性研究（相关）
  • E4 Lab, animal or mechanism studies机制、动物或实验室研究
  • E5 Tradition, experience or case reports传统、经验或个案

E4, E5 and 🏛 lines describe tradition, experience or lab findings only; they never claim that something works.E4、E5 和 🏛 只描述传统、经验或实验室发现，不作疗效主张。
