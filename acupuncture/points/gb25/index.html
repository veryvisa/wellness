🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Quadratus lumborum myofascial pain syndrome腰方肌肌筋膜疼痛综合征

The quadratus lumborum lies in the posterior abdominal wall and connects the 12th rib with the iliac crest and lumbar transverse processes. Patients may experience deep flank aching aggravated by prolonged standing, lateral trunk flexion, or rising from a chair, with palpation reproducing familiar pain. Differentiate it from renal colic, lumbar radiculopathy, and hip disorders. Fever, hematuria, or nausea and vomiting should not be managed as ordinary myofascial pain.腰方肌位于后腹壁深层，连接第12肋与髂嵴及腰椎横突。患者可有侧腰深部酸痛，久站、躯干侧屈或由坐位起立时加重，局部按压可诱发熟悉的疼痛。应与肾绞痛、腰椎神经根病及髋部疾病鉴别。若伴发热、血尿或恶心呕吐，不能按普通肌筋膜痛处理。

Subcostal neuralgia or nerve irritation肋下神经痛或神经受刺激

The anterior ramus of the T12 spinal nerve forms the subcostal nerve, which runs below the 12th rib and may cause burning, stabbing, or altered sensation radiating from the flank toward the abdominal wall. Band-like pain with cutaneous hypersensitivity or numbness suggests a neural source rather than simple GB25 tenderness. Persistent or progressive neurological symptoms require medical assessment.第12胸神经前支即肋下神经，走行于第12肋下方，可引起沿侧腰向腹壁放射的烧灼痛、针刺痛或感觉异常。疼痛呈带状、伴皮肤触觉敏感或麻木时，应考虑神经来源；不能简单归因于京门穴压痛。持续或进行性神经症状应接受医学评估。

Renal and ureteric calculi肾结石与输尿管结石

Renal or ureteric calculi may cause severe unilateral colicky flank or abdominal pain radiating toward the lower abdomen or groin, sometimes accompanied by nausea, vomiting, or hematuria. Patients may be unable to find a comfortable position, unlike myofascial pain typically provoked by palpation or trunk movement. Urinalysis and imaging may be required; suspected stones should not be treated by deep needling at GB25.可出现单侧腰腹部剧烈绞痛，疼痛可能向下腹部、腹股沟放射，伴恶心、呕吐或血尿。患者常难以找到舒适体位，与按压或躯干活动诱发的肌筋膜痛不同。需要尿液检查及影像学评估；疑似结石时不应通过深刺京门处理。

Acute pyelonephritis急性肾盂肾炎

Acute pyelonephritis commonly presents with flank or costovertebral-angle pain, fever, chills, and malaise, sometimes with urinary frequency, urgency, or dysuria. Costovertebral-angle tenderness is not equivalent to tenderness at GB25. Suspected infection requires prompt medical assessment and appropriate antimicrobial treatment; acupuncture is not a substitute.常表现为侧腰或肋脊角疼痛、发热、寒战、乏力，可能伴尿频、尿急、尿痛。肾区叩击痛不能等同于京门穴压痛。疑似感染时应及时进行医学评估及抗感染治疗，针刺不能替代治疗。

Lumbar spinal pain and radiculopathy腰椎相关疼痛及神经根病

Lumbar facet disorders, disc disease, and radiculopathy may all produce lateral lumbar pain. Radiation into the buttock or leg, numbness, weakness, or worsening with coughing or sneezing warrants spinal and neurological assessment. The cause cannot be determined solely from tenderness near GB25.腰椎小关节、椎间盘及神经根病变均可能造成侧腰疼痛。若疼痛伴臀部或下肢放射、麻木、肌力下降，或咳嗽、打喷嚏时加重，应进行神经系统及脊柱评估，不能仅凭京门附近的局部压痛判断病因。

Herpes zoster and postherpetic neuralgia带状疱疹及带状疱疹后神经痛

Unilateral burning flank or abdominal pain, tingling, or skin tenderness may precede grouped vesicles distributed along a dermatome. Early pain may be mistaken for muscular or channel-related pain. If a rash appears, seek prompt medical assessment to evaluate the timing of antiviral treatment.可先出现单侧腰腹部灼痛、针刺感或皮肤触痛，随后出现沿皮节分布的簇集性水疱。早期疼痛可能被误认为肌肉或经络疼痛；一旦出现皮疹，应尽快就医评估抗病毒治疗时机。
