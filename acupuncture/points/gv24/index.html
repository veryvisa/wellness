    Locate 0.5 cun posterior to the anterior midline hairline on the sagittal line above the nose. The front-to-back hairline span is 12 cun; use the glabella and scalp midline if hairline is unclear.前发际正中后 0.5 寸，在鼻梁正上方的矢状中线；前后发际间按 12 寸折量，发际不清时以眉间和头皮中线复核。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Trace upward from Yintang between the brows along the nasal midline to the hairline; the point lies half a cun inside its edge. The medial-eye line is only a midline check, not a substitute for the hairline.从两眉之间印堂向上沿鼻梁中线量至前发际，穴在发际后缘内半寸；两眼内眦连线中点仅作中线参考，不以眉毛高低代替发际定位。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Under the scalp lie the frontal belly, galea and vessels over the frontal bone; the Jiayi Jing forbade needling here, and current textbooks allow only shallow transverse needling.额部头皮正中覆盖额骨，浅层为额肌、帽状腱膜及血管；《甲乙经》有禁刺记载，且明确警告误刺后果，现行教材只许平刺、不深刺。
