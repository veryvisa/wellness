The route循行

「任脈者，起於中極之下，以上毛際，循腹裏，上關元，至咽喉，上頤循面入目」

——《素问·骨空论》

  1. It arises in the lower abdomen (the uterus), emerges at the perineum and rises through the pubic region (CV2).起于小腹内（胞中），下出会阴部，向前上行于阴毛部（曲骨）。
  2. It ascends along the anterior midline inside the abdomen through CV4, CV8 and CV12 to the sternum, then over CV17 and CV22 to the throat.沿腹内前正中线上行，经关元、神阙、中脘至胸骨，过膻中、天突到达咽喉部。
  3. It continues to CV24 on the chin, encircles the lips and passes over the face to below the eyes (ST1), linking with Foot-Yangming; it meets the Governor Vessel at GV28.再上行至颏部承浆，环绕口唇，经面部进入目眶下（承泣），与足阳明相接；在龈交与督脉交会。

🏛 The Conception vessel is an extraordinary vessel and is not assigned a time in the twelve-channel horary cycle.任脉属奇经八脉，不列入十二时辰流注。 · The Conception and Governing vessels communicate with one another and are both among the eight extraordinary vessels.与督脉相互交通，均属奇经八脉
