📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or semi-reclined with the arms relaxed; this is surface localization only, not a site for self-needling.仰卧或半坐位，上肢自然放松；只作表面定位，胸前高风险穴不可自行针刺。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    CV16 is at the midpoint of the xiphisternal junction (the groove between sternal body and xiphoid), about level with the 5th intercostal space; confirm by counting down from the sternal angle (2nd rib).中庭在剑胸结合中点（胸骨体与剑突交界的横沟中点），约平第 5 肋间隙；从胸骨角（第 2 肋）向下逐肋核对。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Pleura, lung and mediastinal structures lie deep to the lower sternum and fifth interspace. Chest-wall thickness and sternal/intercostal anatomy vary; any procedure by a qualified clinician must remain superficial, avoiding deep perpendicular or chest-directed insertion.胸骨下段及第 5 肋间深面邻胸膜、肺与纵隔；胸壁厚度、胸骨角度和肋间隙有个体差异。针刺若由合格人员实施须严格限于浅层，避免深直刺及向胸腔方向进针。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate the second rib at the sternal angle, count gently to the fifth interspace and confirm the midline; do not press deeply on the lower sternum or xiphoid.沿胸骨角触认第 2 肋后向下轻数至第 5 肋间隙，再确认正中线；不要深压胸骨下段或剑突。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Cross-check its sequence with adjacent chest Ren points: CV16 lies below CV17; rib counting takes precedence over nipple position.可与任脉胸部穴的上下次序互校：中庭在膻中下方；肋间计数优先于乳头位置。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Miscounting ribs shifts the point by one or more interspaces; lateral or deep perpendicular insertion can injure pleura, lung or mediastinal structures. Keep any procedure superficial and away from the thoracic cavity.误认肋间隙会使穴位向上或向下错一至数个肋间；偏侧或深直刺可伤胸膜、肺及纵隔结构，必须浅表、避开胸腔方向。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

A neighbouring chest point for cough with lower sternal discomfort; severe wheeze or suspected pneumonia needs medical care, not point treatment.咳嗽伴胸骨下段局部胀痛时作邻近胸部穴参考；气喘急重或疑肺炎时不以此替代诊治。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Only a local adjunct for stable, assessed chest discomfort; acute breathlessness, cyanosis or asthma exacerbation needs emergency treatment.仅在稳定、已评估的胸部不适中作局部配穴参考；急性气促、发绀或哮喘发作优先急救。

Where is CV16 Zhōngtíng?中庭（CV16）在哪里？

On the chest, at the midpoint of the xiphisternal junction, on the anterior midline.胸部，剑胸结合中点处，前正中线上。

How is CV16 needled?中庭（CV16）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.3–0.5 cun.平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at CV16?中庭（CV16）最要注意的安全问题？

Bone is thin at the xiphisternal junction with the pericardium and lower heart behind; off the midline lie the intercostal space and pleura, risking pneumothorax. Needle subcutaneously only.胸剑结合处骨板薄，后方即心包与心脏下缘；偏离中线则近肋间、胸膜，有气胸风险。只沿皮下平刺。
