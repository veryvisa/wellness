Superficial venous injury and local haematoma at the wrist腕部浅静脉损伤及局部血肿

Superficial veins cross the HT5 region, with considerable individual variation in their course and depth. Accidental venous puncture may cause local bleeding, bruising or haematoma. The risk may be increased in patients receiving anticoagulants, those with coagulation disorders or those with fragile vessels. Inspect the skin and visible veins before needling, avoid prominent vessels, and stop needling for assessment if a significant haematoma develops.通里所在区域有浅静脉经过，个体静脉走行和深浅差异较大。针刺误入浅静脉可引起局部出血、青紫或血肿，抗凝治疗、凝血功能异常及血管脆弱者风险可能增加。针刺前必须观察皮肤和血管走行，避免可见静脉；出现明显血肿应停止针刺并进行必要的医疗评估。

Wrist fracture and post-traumatic wrist pain腕部骨折及创伤后疼痛

Falls onto an outstretched hand, direct impact or sports injuries can cause ulnar-sided or volar wrist pain. Differential diagnoses include injury to the ulnar styloid, pisiform, hook of the hamate and distal radioulnar joint. Tenderness around HT5 cannot exclude fracture. Swelling, bruising, reduced grip strength or pain with forearm rotation warrants clinical examination and imaging when indicated.跌倒时手掌撑地、直接撞击或运动损伤后，腕部可出现尺侧或掌侧疼痛。需要鉴别尺骨茎突、豌豆骨、钩骨及远端桡尺关节损伤。通里局部压痛不能排除骨折，尤其是伴随肿胀、瘀斑、握力下降或旋转前臂疼痛时，应先接受临床检查，必要时影像学评估。
