ST35

犊鼻 Dúbí

Calf Nose · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

On the anterior knee, in the depression lateral to the patellar ligament.膝前区，髌韧带外侧凹陷中。

💡 With the knee flexed about 90 degrees, take the hollow below the patella lateral to the patellar ligament (lateral knee eye); the matching medial hollow is the extra point medial knee eye, not ST35.屈膝约 90 度，髌骨下方、髌韧带外侧出现的凹陷（外膝眼）；内侧对应的凹陷是内膝眼（经外奇穴），不是本穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Knee flexed, oblique posteromedially屈膝，向后内斜刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

🛡 Safety安全

The needle points toward the joint cavity: strict asepsis to prevent septic arthritis; a hot swollen knee or post-injury effusion needs assessment, not needling.针向关节腔方向，必须严格消毒，防关节感染；膝红肿热痛或外伤后积液先就医，不针。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the knee flexed about 90 degrees so the hollows beside the patellar tendon appear.坐位或仰卧，屈膝约90度，使髌韧带两旁凹陷显出。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    With the knee flexed, find the hollow lateral to the patellar tendon between the lower patella and lateral tibial condyle; the matching medial hollow is EX-LE4, not ST35.屈膝，在髌韧带外侧、髌骨下缘与胫骨外侧髁之间的凹陷中；内侧同名凹陷是内膝眼（EX-LE4），不是本穴。

  2. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    It has no fixed cun distance: hold the patellar tendon with the patient's thumb and slide laterally to the first clear bony gap; finger breadth is only a directional check.它不靠固定寸数：用患者拇指按住髌韧带，另一指沿韧带向外滑到第一个明显骨间隙复核；指宽仅作方向校核。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The patellar tendon is the reliable palpable structure; ST35 is in the anterolateral knee recess. Synovium and the joint lie deep, so avoid the tendon centre and visible veins and never force a deep intra-articular insertion.髌韧带是最可靠的可触结构，穴在其外侧的膝关节前外侧隐窝；深部是滑膜和关节腔，避开韧带中央及明显浅静脉，不向关节内强行深刺。
