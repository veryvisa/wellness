Yijinjing (Sinew-changing exercises)易筋经

Anyone who has read martial-arts novels knows the Yijinjing: a secret Shaolin manual that gives superhuman inner strength. The real thing is neither so magical nor so secret. Today it is most often a twelve-movement exercise set that, compared with Baduanjin, adds squats, deep forward bends and twisting turns: bigger stretches and more work for the legs. It suits people who already get through Baduanjin easily and want a little more strength and flexibility work; people with high blood pressure, raised eye pressure or fragile bones need to make several forms smaller.看过武侠小说的人都知道易筋经：少林秘传、练成内力大增。真实的易筋经没有那么神，也没有那么秘：今天最常见的是十二个动作的一套健身功，比八段锦多了下蹲、深弯腰和转身拧转，伸展的幅度更大，也更费腿力。它适合已经能轻松做完八段锦、想多练一点力量和柔韧的人；血压高、眼压高、骨头脆的人，有几式一定要改小。

🚩 Before you start开始之前

  • With heart disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure, recent surgery or a fracture, or pregnancy, check with your doctor before starting有心脏病、未控制的高血压、近期手术或骨折、怀孕，开始前先问医生
  • With high blood pressure, glaucoma, retinal disease or recent eye surgery: in forms 9 to 12 (Green Dragon, Crouching Tiger, Bowing, Swinging the Tail) do not let the head drop below the heart; stop when the upper body is level with the floor. Head-down positions raise eye pressure markedly within a minute高血压、青光眼、视网膜病变或做过眼科手术：第九至第十二式（青龙探爪、卧虎扑食、打躬、掉尾）头不要低过心脏，弯到上身与地面平行就停；头低位会让眼压在一分钟内明显升高
  • With osteoporosis: bend from the hips with a flat back instead of rounding the spine, never fold all the way down, and never bend and twist at the same time; if you have had a spinal compression fracture, have a physiotherapist look at forms 7 to 12 with you first骨质疏松：弯腰一律改成屈髋、背保持平直，不弓背弯到底，不在弯腰的同时拧转；有过脊椎压缩性骨折的，先请物理治疗师看过再练第七至第十二式
  • With knee pain or a hip replacement: in form 8 squat only as low as is pain-free, knees pointing the same way as the toes膝盖痛、换过髋关节：第八式三盘落地只蹲到不痛的高度，膝盖与脚尖同向
  • Do not hold your breath or grit your teeth through it; stop at once and sit down with chest pain, dizziness, dimming vision, eye pain or marked breathlessness in any form不憋气、不咬牙硬撑；任何一式出现胸痛、头晕、眼前发黑、眼痛或明显气短，马上停下，坐下休息

📜 Where it comes from从哪里来

The Yijinjing is credited to Bodhidharma, the Indian monk said to have come to China in the fifth or sixth century, but that attribution was added later, and the book gives itself away. The late-Qing compilation by Zhou Shuguan carries two often-quoted old prefaces. One, signed 'second year of Zhenguan' (628) in the name of the Tang general Li Jing, says Bodhidharma reached Shaolin 'in the Taihe years of Emperor Xiaoming of the Later Wei', yet Taihe (477 to 499) was the reign title of Emperor Xiaowen, not Xiaoming; it also has the secret passed on to the Curly-Bearded Hero, a character invented in a Tang tale. The other, signed 1142 in the name of Niu Gao, a general under Yue Fei, opens with 'I am a soldier and cannot read a single character'. The study of surviving editions is even plainer: the medical historian Zhang Zhibin of the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences examined 48 extant versions and found none from the Ming; the earliest is a manuscript from the early Kangxi years, and the earliest printed edition dates only from 1823. The familiar set of twelve illustrations, she found, comes from a health manual of 1858, the Weisheng Yaoshu, not from the early Yijinjing. The Health Qigong Yijinjing practised in parks today was compiled from those twelve postures and promoted by China's General Administration of Sport in 2003; the names and order below follow that version.易筋经托名南北朝时来华的达摩所传，这是后人的附会，书里自己就露了破绽。清末周述官《增演易筋洗髓内功图说》收有两篇常被转引的古序：一篇落款「唐贞观二载」、托名李靖，开头说达摩在「后魏孝明帝太和年间」来到少林，可太和是北魏孝文帝的年号（477—499），不是孝明帝；序里还说这套秘法由徐鸿客「授于虬髯客」，虬髯客是唐人传奇里虚构的人物。另一篇落款「宋绍兴十二年」、托名岳飞部将牛皋，开口却是「予武人也，目不识一字」。版本学的考察更直接：中国中医科学院张志斌查看现存 48 种版本，没有找到明代的本子，最早的是康熙初年的抄本，最早的刻本到道光三年（1823）才出现；今天流传的「易筋经十二势」图，她考证出自咸丰八年（1858）的《卫生要术》，并不在早期易筋经里。现在公园里练的「健身气功·易筋经」由国家体育总局健身气功管理中心在十二势的基础上整理推广（2003 年），下面的名称和顺序按这一版。

🗓 How to practise怎么练

Start by learning the first four forms (the three Wei Tuo forms and Plucking a Star), once a day, then add the later forms one at a time. One full round takes about 12–15 minutes. Studies commonly used 3–5 sessions a week of about 30 minutes, for 5–12 weeks or longer. Always start the squats and forward bends shallow, and go deeper only after a few weeks without pain or dizziness.起步：先学会前四式（三个韦驮献杵加摘星换斗），每天一遍，熟了再逐式往后加。整套一遍约 12–15 分钟。研究里常见的练法是每周 3–5 次、每次约 30 分钟、连续 5–12 周以上。下蹲和弯腰的深度一律从浅开始，几周后不痛不晕再加深。

Opening stance预备势

  1. Stand with feet together, hands hanging naturally at the sides两脚并拢站立，两手自然垂在身体两侧
  2. Tuck the chin slightly and let the crown of the head lift gently; lips closed, tongue resting lightly on the roof of the mouth下颏微收，头顶向上轻轻领起，嘴唇合拢，舌尖轻贴上腭
  3. Look straight ahead and breathe naturally for a few breaths目视前方，自然呼吸，静站几次呼吸

Closing收势

  1. Let go of the hands, rotate the arms outward and slowly straighten up两手松开，两臂外旋，上身慢慢直立
  2. Open the arms out to the sides and raise them overhead, turning the palms down两臂向两侧伸展、上举过头，掌心转向下
  3. Guide the palms slowly down past the head, face and chest to the lower belly; raise and lower like this 3 times in all两掌经头、面、胸前慢慢下引到小腹前；这样上抱、下引共 3 遍
  4. Let the arms fall, bring the left foot back beside the right两臂自然垂落，左脚收回，并步站立

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 A meta-analysis of 10 randomised trials with 590 older adults reported clear gains with Yijinjing on chair-stand and squat-to-stand tests, and small to moderate gains in grip strength and knee muscle strength; shoulder flexibility and the sit-and-reach test did not differ significantly from control. The authors rated the evidence low to moderate.一篇汇总 10 项随机试验、590 名老年人的荟萃分析报告，练易筋经的人在起坐测试和蹲起测试上进步明显，握力和膝部肌力有小到中等的提高；肩部柔韧和坐位体前屈与对照没有显著差别。作者评价证据质量低到中等。 [1]
  • E2 A randomised trial in chronic neck pain (102 people): tuina plus Yijinjing (3 times a week for 8 weeks) lowered pain scores 1.2 points more than tuina alone at week 8, and the difference was still there at week 12. The trial was open-label: participants knew which group they were in.慢性颈痛的随机试验（102 人）：推拿加易筋经（每周 3 次、8 周）比单做推拿，第 8 周疼痛评分多降 1.2 分，第 12 周差别仍在。试验是开放标签，受试者知道自己在哪一组。 [2]
  • E2 A randomised trial in stroke rehabilitation (70 people): adding 5 weeks of Yijinjing to usual rehabilitation gave bigger gains in balance, gait and daily-living scores than the same time spent stretching, with no serious adverse events. These are short-term results only.卒中康复的随机试验（70 人）：在常规康复之外加练易筋经 5 周，平衡、步态和日常活动评分的进步比做同样时长拉伸的对照组多，没有严重不良事件。这只是短期结果。 [3]
  • E2 A small trial in postmenopausal women (40 people): six months of Yijinjing plus elastic-band exercise gave better bone density than no exercise; but the two were done together, so their effects cannot be separated, and it does not show that Yijinjing on its own protects against osteoporosis.绝经后女性的小型试验（40 人）：易筋经加弹力带练 6 个月，骨密度比不运动的对照组好；但易筋经和弹力带一起做，分不出各自的作用，不能据此说易筋经本身能防骨质疏松。 [4]
  • E3 Why the head-down forms need extra care: in one study, people with and without glaucoma who held head-down yoga poses had a marked rise in eye pressure within a minute, falling back within two minutes of sitting up; whether this affects glaucoma progression is not known.为什么低头的几式要格外小心：一项研究让青光眼患者和正常人做头低位的瑜伽动作，眼压都在一分钟内明显升高，坐起后两分钟内回落；这对青光眼进展有没有影响，目前不清楚。 [5]
  • E5 Retraction check: a PubMed search in November 2026 found no retractions or expressions of concern for Yijinjing studies. Most trials were run in China with small samples, and few have been independently repeated.撤稿检查：2026 年 11 月在 PubMed 检索易筋经相关研究，没有查到撤稿或期刊关注声明。易筋经的试验大多在中国完成、样本不大，独立重复的还很少。 [6]
  • E5 The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) regards tai chi and qigong as generally safe mind-body practices; people who are pregnant should talk with their health care providers before starting.美国国家补充与整合健康中心（NCCIH）把太极、气功视为一般比较安全的身心练习；怀孕的人开始前先和医生商量。 [7]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Yijin means changing the sinews, making them supple and strong; jing, classic, is a way of claiming ancient authority. The old texts pair the twelve postures with images such as the guardian Wei Tuo, a green dragon, a crouching tiger and nine ghosts, as cues for each posture and the direction of effort. The old texts also give counts, such as stamping the heels twenty-one times, that differ from today's Health Qigong version; the names and verses are traditional records, not promises.「易筋」的意思是把筋骨换过、练得柔韧有力，「经」是托古的说法。旧本把十二势配上韦驮、青龙、卧虎、九鬼等形象，用来提示每一式的姿态和用力方向。旧本里还有「起而顿足，二十一次」之类的次数，和今天健身气功版不同；名字和口诀是传统记载，不是疗效承诺。
