The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing notes GB42 for internal injury with spitting of blood and loss of bodily moisture and luster, reflecting classical use of the Gallbladder channel to regulate qi-blood and clear deficient heat.《针灸甲乙经》载地五会可用于内伤唾血、津液亏耗、肌肤失润等虚损证候，体现足少阳经气血调节与清泄虚火的古法应用。

「地五會足小指次指本節後陷中，去俠谿一寸，銅人針一分，禁灸」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu Dacheng: GB42 is in the hollow behind the 4th MTP joint, 1 cun from GB43, needled shallow and not moxibusted; this matches the standard 'hollow proximal to the 4th MTP joint'.《针灸大成》：地五会在第 4 跖趾关节后陷中，距侠溪一寸，浅刺、禁灸；与国标「第 4 跖趾关节近端凹陷」一致。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

4th or 5th metatarsal stress fracture or dorsal interosseous strain第4、5跖骨应力性骨折或骨间肌劳损

Common after distance running, prolonged marching, or wearing stiff-soled shoes, presenting with localized lateral dorsal tenderness worse with weight-bearing. Early X-rays can miss stress fractures; if percussion pain or weight-bearing pain is marked, refer for imaging before local needling.常见于长跑、军训、穿硬底鞋长时间行走后足背外侧局限性压痛、负重痛加重。应力性骨折早期 X 光可能阴性，若局部叩击痛明显或负重剧痛，须先转诊影像学排查骨折，禁直接局部强刺激针刺。

Superficial fibular nerve dorsal branch entrapment / shoelace neuropathy腓浅神经足背支卡压 / 鞋带压迫性神经痛

Tight shoelaces, high-top boots, or a dorsal ganglion cyst compressing branches of the superficial fibular nerve cause burning pain or numbness over the lateral dorsum and 4th–5th toes, with a positive Tinel sign near GB42. Relieve footwear compression first and needle very superficially away from the nerve trunk.紧鞋带、高帮靴或足背腱鞘囊肿压迫腓浅神经分支，引起足背外侧及第4、5趾背烧灼痛、麻木，叩击地五会附近可出现 Tinel 征放射麻感。应先解除鞋具压迫，针刺宜极浅且避开神经干。

Residual pain after lateral midfoot sprain / lateral tarsometatarsal arthritis足跗关节扭伤后遗痛 / 跖跗关节炎（Lisfranc 区域外侧）

Inversion ankle sprains often strain the 4th–5th tarsometatarsal joint capsules in addition to the lateral ankle ligaments, leaving tenderness between GB42 and GB40. In acute swelling and bruising, rule out a 5th metatarsal base avulsion or Jones fracture first.足内翻扭伤后除外踝韧带外，常合并第4、5跖骨基底关节囊拉伤，痛点常落在地五会至丘墟（GB40）之间。急性期红肿热痛明显时应先排除第5跖骨基底部撕脱骨折（Jones 骨折区）。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Marked swelling, bruising, or inability to bear weight after trauma or sprain (obtain X-rays first to rule out a 5th metatarsal base fracture or Lisfranc injury)外伤或扭伤后足背外侧明显肿胀、瘀斑、无法负重行走（须先拍片排除第5跖骨基底部骨折或 Lisfranc 损伤）
  • Local redness, heat, swelling with fever, or any skin breakdown, coldness, or dusky discoloration in a diabetic foot (seek immediate medical care to rule out infection or limb ischemia)足背局部红肿热痛伴发热、或糖尿病患者足背出现破溃、发凉、色泽发紫（须立即就医排查感染或下肢缺血）

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is GB42 Dìwǔhuì?地五会（GB42）在哪里？

On the dorsum of the foot, between the 4th and 5th metatarsals, in the depression proximal to the 4th metatarsophalangeal joint.足背，第 4、5 跖骨间，第 4 跖趾关节近端凹陷中。

How is GB42 needled?地五会（GB42）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at GB42?地五会（GB42）最要注意的安全问题？

The Jiayi Jing forbids moxibustion; the thin dorsal skin burns easily with direct moxa.《甲乙经》载不可灸；足背皮薄，直接灸易烫伤。
