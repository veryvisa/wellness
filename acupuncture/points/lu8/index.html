LU8

经渠 Jīngqú

Channel Ditch · Take care需谨慎

★Jing-river point (five transport points; metal)经穴（五输穴，五行属金）

📍 Location定位★

Front-outer forearm, 1 cun above the wrist crease, in the hollow between the radial styloid and the radial artery.前臂前外侧，腕横纹上 1 寸，桡骨茎突与桡动脉之间的凹陷中。

💡 Palm up, 1 cun above the wrist crease, feel the radial pulse; the hollow between the pulse and the radial styloid is LU8. This stretch of the wrist is where TCM pulse-taking (cunkou) is done.仰掌，在腕横纹上 1 寸摸到桡动脉的跳动，跳动与桡骨茎突之间的凹陷就是经渠；这一段正是中医切脉的「寸口」区域。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular, keeping clear of the radial artery避开桡动脉，直刺 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5 寸

Classical texts say this point should not be treated with moxibustion.古籍记载此穴不宜灸。

🛡 Safety安全

The radial artery is right next to it; hitting it causes bleeding and a haematoma. Locate the artery with a finger and keep clear of it. The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing says LU8 must not be moxibusted.紧邻桡动脉，刺中会出血、血肿；进针前先用手指把动脉定位并避开。《针灸甲乙经》记载经渠不可灸。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Cough, wheezing, chest pain咳嗽、气喘、胸痛 ⚖️Sore swollen throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️Wrist pain手腕疼痛

「不可灸，灸之傷人神明。」——《针灸甲乙经》卷三论经渠

Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing, vol. 3, on LU8: no moxibustion; moxa here harms the spirit.

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The elbow crease to distal palmar wrist crease is 12 cun; LU8 is on the Lung line 1 cun above the wrist crease.肘横纹至腕掌侧远端横纹为 12 寸；经渠在腕横纹上 1 寸的肺经线上。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    After finding the radial pulse, LU8 is in the hollow between radial styloid and artery, just ulnar to brachioradialis tendon; the artery is adjacent, so avoid its pulse and do not needle medially deep. The superficial radial nerve is also nearby.触到桡动脉搏动后，经渠在桡骨茎突与动脉之间的凹陷，靠肱桡肌腱尺侧；动脉紧邻，避开搏动、不可向内侧深刺，桡神经浅支亦在附近。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    LU8 is 1 cun above LU9 and about half a cun below LU7; identify the hard radial styloid first, then the artery on its palmar side.经渠在太渊上 1 寸、列缺下约半寸；先辨桡骨茎突的硬突，再在其掌侧找到动脉。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Map the artery with a fingertip, then move into the soft hollow between it and the bony edge; a strong pulse under the finger means you remain on the artery.用指腹轻压动脉确认其走向，再移到动脉与骨缘之间的软凹；若指下仍是强搏动，尚未离开动脉。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Needling directly at the radial pulse causes haematoma; locate the hollow beside, not on, the artery.把桡动脉搏动点当穴位正中直刺：可造成血肿；应在动脉旁的凹陷取穴。

Where is LU8 Jīngqú?经渠（LU8）在哪里？

Front-outer forearm, 1 cun above the wrist crease, in the hollow between the radial styloid and the radial artery.前臂前外侧，腕横纹上 1 寸，桡骨茎突与桡动脉之间的凹陷中。

How is LU8 needled?经渠（LU8）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, keeping clear of the radial artery; 0.3–0.5 cun.避开桡动脉，直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。
