Where is ST5 Dàyíng?大迎（ST5）在哪里？

On the face, anterior to the angle of the mandible, in the depression anterior to the masseter attachment, over the facial artery pulse.面部，下颌角前方，咬肌附着部的前缘凹陷中，面动脉搏动处。

How is ST5 needled?大迎（ST5）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse, avoiding the artery; 0.3–0.5 cun.避开动脉，斜刺或平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at ST5?大迎（ST5）最要注意的安全问题？

The facial artery lies under the point: feel the pulse and move away from it before insertion, never into it, and press after withdrawal.面动脉在穴下，进针前先摸到搏动并推开或避开，不可刺入搏动处；出针按压防血肿。
