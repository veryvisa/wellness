💡 BL59 is above the malleolus, BL60 behind it, and GB40 anteroinferior to it; select by the distinct superior, posterior and anterior locations.跗阳在外踝上，昆仑在外踝后，丘墟在外踝前下方；按前后上下的局部解剖区别选穴。

Xi-cleft point of the Yang Qiao vessel; used in xi-cleft methods and pairing with BL62, not the Bladder Luo point (that is BL58).阳跷脉郄穴；可用于郄穴体系及阳跷与申脉配伍，不是足太阳络穴（络穴为飞扬 BL58）。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「附陽 外踝上三寸，太陽前，少陽後，筋骨之間，陽蹻脈」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

English version coming《针灸大成》以「附阳」异体名记载该穴在外踝上三寸、太阳经前、少阳经后、筋骨之间，并属阳跷脉；本站穴名采用标准名跗阳。

「在足外踝上三寸，太陽前，少陽後，筋骨間，刺入六分」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Elsewhere in the same Jiayi Jing passage, this sentence records the point 3 cun above the lateral malleolus between the Taiyang and Shaoyang regions, confirming the level and inter-tissue location.《针灸甲乙经》：在外踝上三寸，太阳经之前、少阳经之后，筋骨之间。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Peroneal tendon or posterolateral ankle soft-tissue pain腓骨肌腱或外踝后方软组织痛

Tenderness behind the lateral malleolus, worsened by resisted eversion or plantar flexion, may indicate peroneal tendon disease. BL59 is posterosuperior to the malleolus; distinguish it from GB40 anteroinferiorly and BL60 in the posterior hollow.外踝后方压痛、抗阻外翻或跖屈加重可提示腓骨肌腱问题；跗阳在外踝上后方，需与外踝前下的丘墟和外踝后凹陷的昆仑区分。

Lateral ankle sprain or distal fibular injury外踝扭伤或腓骨远端损伤

Swelling, bruising and weight-bearing pain after a sprain require assessment; bony tenderness or inability to take four steps warrants fracture evaluation.扭伤后肿胀、瘀青和负重痛需按损伤程度评估；骨性压痛或不能走四步应先排骨折。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Marked ankle deformity, bony tenderness or inability to bear weight needs prompt imaging to exclude fracture.踝外侧明显畸形、骨性压痛或无法负重，尽快影像检查排除骨折。
  • A cold or discoloured foot, weak pulse or rapidly worsening numbness requires urgent care.足部发凉、颜色改变、脉搏减弱或麻木快速加重，立即就医。

Where is BL59 Fūyáng?跗阳（BL59）在哪里？

Posterior leg, 3 cun directly above BL60, between the fibula and the calcaneal tendon.小腿后区，昆仑直上 3 寸，腓骨与跟腱之间。

How is BL59 needled?跗阳（BL59）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.8–1.2 cun.直刺；0.8–1.2 寸。

Special-point roles of BL59?跗阳（BL59）是哪类特定穴？

Xi-cleft point of the Yang Qiao vessel阳跷脉郄穴

Main safety point at BL59?跗阳（BL59）最要注意的安全问题？

Sural nerve and small saphenous vein run superficially; redirect at tingling to the lateral foot.腓肠神经与小隐静脉浅行；出现足外侧放射麻感即调整。
