📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the forearm pronated, shoulder relaxed, and elbow slightly flexed; identify the posterior triceps.坐位或仰卧，前臂旋前、肩放松，肘微屈；沿上臂后侧辨认肱三头肌。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    On the posterior upper arm along the olecranon-to-acromion line, 5 cun above the olecranon, over the posterolateral triceps rather than the acromion or humeral surface.在肘尖至肩峰连线的上臂后侧，肘尖上 5 寸处；以肱三头肌后外侧肌腹为所在组织，不在肩峰或肱骨骨面取穴。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Slight elbow flexion reveals the posterior triceps belly; palpate its lateral margin. The radial nerve and profunda brachii vessels lie deep in the humeral radial groove; keep any insertion superficial to muscle and do not probe deeply toward the humerus or medially. Stop for radiating paresthesia.轻屈肘使肱三头肌后侧肌腹显露，触诊其外侧缘。深部靠近肱骨桡神经沟内的桡神经和肱深血管；针向肌层浅入，不向肱骨深面或内侧深探，放射麻感应停止。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Measure 5 cun from the olecranon toward the acromion along the triceps, palpating the posterolateral muscle belly; confirm against both the line and posterior muscle contour.从肘尖沿肱三头肌向肩峰方向量取 5 寸，触诊上臂后外侧肌腹；与肩峰至肘尖连线及后侧肌肉轮廓同时核对。

  4. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The posterior axillary fold to elbow crease is 9 cun; 5 cun proximally from the elbow is a supporting measure. Recheck on the olecranon-acromion line to avoid a purely proportional medial error.腋后纹头至肘横纹按 9 寸折量，肘端向近端 5 寸为辅助位置；再回到肘尖—肩峰连线校准，避免只按比例落到内侧。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Measuring 5 cun without maintaining the posterolateral channel line can shift medially; deep needling toward the humerus may reach the radial nerve or profunda brachii vessels, so stop immediately for radiation into the forearm or hand.仅按肘上五寸而横向偏到内侧，会离开三焦经上臂后外侧线；偏深向肱骨可能刺激桡神经及肱深血管，出现向前臂或手背放射感应立即停止。
