Diarrhoea泄泻

Many points list 'diarrhoea'. In clinic you first sort acute from chronic, cold from heat, deficiency from excess, and decide whether the root lies in the spleen and stomach, the liver or the kidney — only then do you choose points, method and whether to use moxa. This page compares the common anti-diarrhoea points side by side and gives the main and supporting points for each pattern, with reasons.很多穴的主治里都写着「泄泻」。临床上要先分急性还是慢性、寒热虚实、病位在脾胃、肝还是肾，再决定用哪几个穴、用补还是泻、要不要灸。这一页把常用的止泻穴放在同一张表里比较，并给出每个证型的主穴、配穴和理由。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Bloody, black tarry or pus-containing stools with abdominal pain or fever: same-day care to rule out bacterial enteritis, inflammatory bowel disease or GI bleeding大便带血、黑便（柏油样）或脓血便，伴腹痛、发热：当天就医，排除细菌性肠炎、炎症性肠病、消化道出血
  • Signs of severe dehydration: intense thirst, much less urine or none for 8 hours, dizziness on standing, fast heartbeat, confusion; in infants no tears when crying, sunken fontanelle, lethargy: seek urgent care or call 911严重脱水表现：口干极渴、尿量明显减少或 8 小时无尿、头晕站立发黑、心跳快、意识模糊；婴幼儿哭时无泪、囟门凹陷、精神萎靡：立即就医或拨 911
  • Diarrhoea beyond 2 weeks, waking you at night, with weight loss, anaemia or a family history of bowel cancer: see a doctor to rule out inflammatory bowel disease, coeliac disease, tumours and others腹泻超过 2 周（慢性）、夜间也腹泻把人弄醒、体重下降、贫血或家族有大肠癌史：看医生做检查，排除炎症性肠病、乳糜泻、肿瘤等
  • Watery diarrhoea with cramps or fever after recent antibiotics, hospital stay or travel: get tested for C. difficile and other infections近期服过抗生素后水样泻、腹痛、发热，或近期去过旅行地、住院后腹泻：就医检查艰难梭菌等感染
  • Severe abdominal pain, distension, vomiting, no gas or stool passed, or tenderness with rebound: call 911 to rule out obstruction, perforation or appendicitis剧烈腹痛、腹胀、呕吐、停止排气排便，或腹部压痛反跳痛：拨 911，排除肠梗阻、穿孔、阑尾炎

「泄瀉肚腹諸般疾，（足）三里內庭功無比」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zabing Xuefa verse in Dacheng, vol. 4: for all kinds of diarrhoea and abdominal disorders, ST36 with ST44 is unrivalled. ST36, Stomach he-sea and lower he-sea point, regulates gut qi; ST44, the Stomach ying-spring point, clears gut heat. Later practice uses the pair mainly for diarrhoea with gut heat or food stagnation; chronic cold-deficient diarrhoea relies on moxa instead.《针灸大成》卷四所收《杂病穴法歌》：各种泄泻和肚腹病，足三里配内庭效果最好。足三里是胃经合穴、胃的下合穴，调胃肠气机；内庭是胃经荥穴，清胃肠之热。后世多用于胃肠有热、饮食积滞的泄泻；虚寒久泻则以灸法为主，不照搬此对。

「關元 主諸虛積，及虛老人泄瀉」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Dacheng: CV4 (front-mu of the Small Intestine, meeting point of the Ren vessel with the three leg yin channels) governs all kinds of deficiency and accumulated cold, especially diarrhoea in the elderly. The basis for generous moxa on CV4 in chronic spleen–kidney yang-deficiency diarrhoea.《针灸大成》：关元（小肠募、任脉与三阴交会）主各种虚损积冷，尤其老年人虚证泄泻。这是脾肾阳虚久泻重灸关元的依据。

「腹中寒痛，泄瀉不止，天樞、中脘、關元、三陰交」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Dacheng, vol. 7, treatment entries: for cold pain in the abdomen with unceasing diarrhoea, use ST25, CV12, CV4 and SP6 — one for the large intestine (its front-mu), one for the stomach (its front-mu), one to warm the lower origin (front-mu of the small intestine) and one for the spleen; moxa may be added in cold patterns.《针灸大成》卷七治症条：腹中寒痛、泄泻不止，取天枢、中脘、关元、三阴交。四穴一调肠（大肠募）、一调胃（胃募）、一温下元（小肠募）、一健脾，寒证可配灸。

「大腸病者，腸中切痛而鳴濯濯，冬日重感於寒即泄，當臍而痛，不能久立，與胃同候，取巨虛上廉」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.4: in large-intestine disease there is cutting pain and gurgling in the gut, cold in winter brings on diarrhoea, the pain sits around the navel and the patient cannot stand for long — needle ST37. This is the original source for using the Large Intestine lower he-sea point ST37 in acute diarrhoea.《灵枢·邪气脏腑病形》：大肠有病，肠中切痛、肠鸣辘辘，冬天再受寒就泄泻，痛在脐周，不能久站，取上巨虚。这是「合治内腑」、急性泄泻取大肠下合穴上巨虚的原始出处。

「清氣在下，則生飧泄」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.5: when clear qi sinks instead of rising, there is diarrhoea with undigested food. This is the basis for treating chronic spleen-deficient diarrhoea by strengthening the spleen and raising the clear, with ST36 and moxa, rather than simply stopping the stool.《素问·阴阳应象大论》：清阳之气不升反陷于下，就会出现飧泄（完谷不化的泄泻）。这是脾虚久泻要健脾升清、重用足三里与灸法，而不是一味止泻的理论根据。

Acute急性

Cold-damp encumbering the spleen寒湿困脾

How it presents辨证要点 Thin, even watery stools with abdominal pain and borborygmi, better for warmth and pressure; aversion to cold, heaviness, poor appetite, or blocked nose and headache; sudden onset after chill or raw cold food; white greasy coating, soggy slow pulse.泻下清稀，甚至如水样，腹痛肠鸣，喜温喜按；恶寒、身重、食少，或兼鼻塞头痛；多在受凉、吃生冷后突发；舌苔白腻，脉濡缓。

Principle治法 Dispel cold, transform damp, strengthen the spleen, stop diarrhoea (needle with generous moxa)散寒化湿，健脾止泻（针灸并用，重灸）

Main主穴ST25 Tiānshū天枢ST37 Shàngjùxū上巨虚SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉CV9 Shuǐfēn水分
Add配穴CV8 Shénquè神阙CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘

💡 Core for acute diarrhoea: ST25, front-mu of the Large Intestine, acts locally on the bowel; ST37 is its lower he-sea point ('he-sea points treat the fu organs'); SP9, the Spleen he-sea (water) point, strengthens the spleen and drains damp; CV9, 1 cun above the navel, was held to sit at the lower opening of the small intestine where clear and turbid separate, so it promotes urine to firm the stool. For cold-damp add salt moxa on CV8 to warm yang and disperse cold, and CV12 (Stomach front-mu, influential point of the fu organs) with moxa to warm the middle.急性泄泻主穴：天枢是大肠募穴，近治肠腑；上巨虚是大肠下合穴，「合治内腑」；阴陵泉是脾经合穴（水），健脾利湿；水分在脐上 1 寸，古称当小肠下口、泌别清浊，利小便以实大便。寒湿加神阙隔盐灸，温阳散寒；中脘是胃募、腑会，配灸温运中焦。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern medicine groups diarrhoea by duration: acute (under 2 weeks, mostly viral or bacterial gastroenteritis, food poisoning, or drugs such as antibiotics), persistent (2–4 weeks) and chronic (over 4 weeks). Common causes of chronic diarrhoea include irritable bowel syndrome (diarrhoea-predominant), inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's, ulcerative colitis), coeliac disease, lactose intolerance, chronic pancreatitis, hyperthyroidism, drugs (metformin, PPIs and others) and colorectal tumours. Prompt investigation is needed with blood in stool, black stool, weight loss, nocturnal diarrhoea, fever, recent antibiotics or hospital stay, persistence, or a family history of bowel cancer. TCM patterns do not map one-to-one onto these causes but overlap: liver overacting on spleen often coincides with diarrhoea-predominant IBS (stress-triggered, relieved after stool); spleen–kidney yang deficiency appears in chronic enteritis and functional diarrhoea in the elderly; damp-heat is typical of acute infectious enteritis, where rehydration comes first. In acute diarrhoea preventing dehydration matters most; acupuncture is adjunctive and does not replace rehydration or medical care.现代医学按病程把腹泻分为急性（<2 周，多为病毒或细菌性胃肠炎、食物中毒、药物如抗生素）、持续性（2–4 周）和慢性（>4 周）。慢性腹泻常见原因包括肠易激综合征（腹泻型）、炎症性肠病（克罗恩病、溃疡性结肠炎）、乳糜泻、乳糖不耐受、慢性胰腺炎、甲状腺功能亢进、药物（二甲双胍、PPI 等）和大肠肿瘤。必须尽快检查的情况：便血、黑便、体重下降、夜间腹泻、发热、近期抗生素或住院史、长期不愈或家族有大肠癌史。中医证型与这些病因并不一一对应，但大致有交集：肝郁乘脾型常与肠易激综合征（腹泻型）重叠，情绪诱发、泻后痛减；脾肾阳虚久泻可见于慢性肠炎、老年功能性腹泻；湿热型多见于急性感染性肠炎，需注意补液。急性腹泻最重要的是防脱水，针灸只作辅助，不替代补液和就医。
