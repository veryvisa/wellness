Headache头痛

Triage first, then pattern: a sudden explosive headache, or one with neurological signs or fever and a stiff neck, goes to emergency. Once dangerous causes are excluded, the textbook works in two steps — first locate the channel (forehead is Yangming, the sides Shaoyang, the occiput and neck Taiyang, the vertex Jueyin) to choose the main points, then sort external (wind-cold, wind-heat, wind-damp) from internal (liver yang, phlegm, blood stasis, blood deficiency, kidney deficiency) to add points. The main points on this page are the textbook's set for whole-head pain; the additions for each region are in the comparison table.头痛先分流、后辨证：突然炸裂样的痛、伴神经症状或发热颈硬的痛，先去急诊。排除危险头痛后，教材的思路分两步——先看痛在哪条经（前额属阳明、两侧属少阳、后枕连项属太阳、巅顶属厥阴）定主穴，再分外感（风寒、风热、风湿）与内伤（肝阳、痰浊、瘀血、血虚、肾虚）加配穴。本页的主穴取教材「全头痛」一组，各部位的加穴写在比较表里。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Thunderclap headache that reaches its worst within seconds to a minute ('the worst headache of my life'), or headache with collapse, seizure or confusion: call 911 now; it may be a subarachnoid or other brain haemorrhage.雷击样头痛：几秒到一分钟内就痛到最重（「这辈子最痛的一次」），或头痛伴昏倒、抽搐、意识不清：立即拨 911，可能是蛛网膜下腔出血或脑出血。
  • Headache with any FAST sign — facial droop (Face), one-sided arm weakness (Arm), slurred or muddled speech (Speech) — or sudden loss of vision, double vision or unsteady walking: call 911 now and note the time the person was last well (Time).头痛伴 FAST 任一表现——脸歪（Face）、一侧手臂无力（Arm）、说话含糊或听不懂（Speech）——或突然视力丧失、复视、走路不稳：立即拨 911，记下最后正常的时间。
  • Headache with fever, a stiff neck, a rash, light sensitivity or drowsiness: emergency department today to rule out meningitis; call 911 if a purple rash does not fade when pressed.头痛伴发热、颈部僵硬、皮疹、畏光或嗜睡：当天急诊，排除脑膜炎；出现紫红色不褪色皮疹时拨 911。
  • Headache getting worse after a head injury, with repeated vomiting or increasing drowsiness, or any headache after a knock to the head in someone on blood thinners: emergency department today.头部外伤后头痛加重、反复呕吐、越来越困；或服抗凝药的人头部受撞后头痛：当天急诊。
  • A new headache starting after age 50, especially with a tender temple, jaw pain on chewing or blurred vision (possible giant cell arteritis, which can cause blindness): see a doctor the same day.50 岁以后第一次出现的新头痛，尤其伴太阳穴处触痛、咀嚼时下巴痛或视物模糊（警惕巨细胞动脉炎，可致失明）：当天就医。
  • A new severe headache late in pregnancy or after birth with blurred vision or sudden swelling of the face, hands or feet (possible pre-eclampsia), or headache with a red painful eye and halos around lights (possible acute glaucoma): emergency department today.孕晚期或产后新发剧烈头痛伴视物模糊、面部手脚突然水肿（警惕子痫前期）；头痛伴眼红眼痛、看灯有彩虹圈（警惕急性青光眼）：当天急诊。
  • A headache that worsens week by week, is worst on waking with vomiting, or is made worse by coughing or straining, or any headache in someone with cancer or a weak immune system: see a doctor soon for imaging. Taking painkillers on more than 10–15 days a month can itself cause headaches; review this with a doctor.头痛一周周加重、晨起明显伴呕吐、咳嗽用力时加剧，或有癌症、免疫低下病史：尽快就医做影像检查。每月服止痛药超过 10–15 天者，头痛可能由止痛药本身引起，要与医生调整用药。

「厥頭痛，項先痛，腰脊為應，先取天柱，後取足太陽」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.24: a headache that starts in the back of the neck and is felt down the spine is treated first at BL10 locally, then at distal points of the foot Taiyang channel. This is the origin of the textbook's Taiyang headache treatment — BL10 nearby, BL62 or BL60 far away: find the channel by location, then combine local and distal points.《灵枢·厥病》：先从后项痛起、牵连腰背的头痛，先取局部的天柱，再取足太阳经的远端穴。这正是教材「太阳头痛」近取天柱、远取申脉昆仑的来历——按痛的部位找经、局部加远端。

「真頭痛，頭痛甚，腦盡痛，手足寒至節，死不治」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

The same chapter: a 'true headache' — extremely severe, the whole brain aching, hands and feet cold up to the elbows and knees — was judged fatal and was not treated as a headache for needling. Today it corresponds to the dangerous headache that needs a 911 call at once.同篇：头痛极剧、整个脑袋都痛、手脚冷到肘膝的「真头痛」，古人直接判为不治之症，并不当作可以针刺调理的头痛。今天它对应的正是必须立即拨 911 的危险头痛。

「頭項尋列缺，面口合谷收」——高武《针灸聚英》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Four Command Points song in Zhenjiu Juying: for the head and neck seek LU7, for the face and mouth LI4. External headaches often use the two together — LU7, the Lung luo point, releases the exterior, and LI4, the Large Intestine yuan point, is strongly analgesic; together they form a source–connecting pair.《针灸聚英》四总穴歌：头项的病找列缺，面口的病找合谷。外感头痛常把这两穴合用——列缺是肺经络穴、宣肺解表，合谷是大肠原穴、止痛力强，两穴原络相配。

External外感

Wind-cold headache风寒头痛

How it presents辨证要点 Fairly sudden onset; pain spreads to the neck and upper back and worsens in wind or cold, and the person likes to cover the head; chills, no sweating, blocked nose with clear discharge; no thirst; thin white tongue coating, floating tight pulse.起病较急，痛连项背，遇风受寒加重，喜裹头；怕冷、无汗、鼻塞流清涕；口不渴；苔薄白，脉浮紧。

Principle治法 Dispel wind and cold, free the channels, stop pain (drain; moxa allowed)疏风散寒，通络止痛（泻法，可灸）

Main主穴GB20 Fēngchí风池GV20 Bǎihuì百会ST8 Tóuwéi头维GB8 Shuàigǔ率谷LI4 Hégǔ合谷
Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门LU7 Lièquē列缺

💡 The main set — GB20, GV20, ST8, GB8 and LI4 — covers the occiput, vertex, forehead and sides, with LI4 as the distal pain point; BL12, the 'wind gate', expels wind, and LU7 frees the lung and releases the exterior, pairing with LI4 as source and connecting points for wind-cold.主穴取风池、百会、头维、率谷、合谷，覆盖后枕、巅顶、前额、两侧，合谷远端止痛；风门是风邪出入之门，列缺宣肺解表，与合谷原络相配，专治外感风寒。

Wind-heat headache风热头痛

How it presents辨证要点 Distending headache, at worst as if splitting; fever, slight aversion to wind, red face and eyes, thirst, sore throat; dark urine, dry stools; red tongue tip, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse.头胀痛，甚则如裂；发热、微怕风、面红目赤、口渴、咽痛；尿黄、便干；舌尖红、苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat, free the channels, stop pain (drain; GV14 may be pricked to bleed)疏风清热，通络止痛（泻法，大椎可点刺出血）

Wind-damp headache风湿头痛

How it presents辨证要点 Headache like a tight wrapping, foggy and heavy, worse in damp rainy weather; heavy limbs, chest fullness, poor appetite, sometimes loose stools; greasy white coating, soggy pulse.头痛如裹、昏沉发重，阴雨天加重；肢体困重、胸闷纳呆，大便或溏；苔白腻，脉濡。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, overcome damp, free the channels, stop pain (drain; moxa allowed)祛风胜湿，通络止痛（泻法，可灸）

Liver yang rising肝阳上亢

How it presents辨证要点 Distending or throbbing headache, mainly at the sides or vertex; dizziness, irritability, red face and eyes, bitter taste, poor sleep; worse with emotional upset; red tongue, yellow coating, wiry rapid pulse.头胀痛或跳痛，以两侧、巅顶为主；眩晕，心烦易怒，面红目赤，口苦，失眠；情绪激动时加重；舌红苔黄，脉弦数。

Principle治法 Calm the liver, anchor the yang, settle wind, stop pain (drain LR3 and GB43, tonify KI3)平肝潜阳，熄风止痛（泻太冲、侠溪，补太溪）

Acupuncture does not replace blood-pressure medicine when the pressure is very high.太冲是肝经原穴，泻之平肝；侠溪是胆经荥穴，清少阳之火，正对两侧胀痛；太溪是肾经原穴，补肾水以涵肝木——上实下虚，上泻下补。血压很高时针灸不能代替降压药。

CV12 is on the upper abdomen and is not needled in pregnancy.丰隆是胃经络穴、化痰要穴；中脘是胃的募穴、腑会，健运中焦——痰生于脾胃，治痰先治中焦。中脘在上腹，孕妇禁针。

Blood-stasis headache瘀血头痛

How it presents辨证要点 Long-standing headache fixed in one place, stabbing like an awl, worse at night; sometimes after an old head injury; purple or dusky tongue or stasis spots, thin choppy pulse.头痛经久不愈，痛处固定不移，痛如锥刺，夜间加重；或有头部外伤史；舌紫暗或有瘀斑，脉细涩。

Principle治法 Move blood, dispel stasis, free the channels, stop pain (drain; ashi points may be pricked to bleed)活血化瘀，通络止痛（泻法，阿是穴可点刺出血）

GB20 Fēngchí风池

Best for最适合 Occipital or lateral headache, in both external and liver-yang patterns后枕痛、两侧痛、外感头痛与肝阳头痛都用

Why依据 Meeting point of the Gallbladder channel and Yang Wei vessel; a key wind point and the shared main point for every headache pattern足少阳与阳维脉交会穴；祛风要穴，各型头痛的共用主穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Covers the sides and both external and internal wind, whereas BL10 is for the midline back of the head and neck; needle 0.8–1.2 cun toward the tip of the nose, never deep upward and inward, where the medulla and vertebral artery lie.比天柱偏两侧和外风内风都治，天柱只管正后方连项；针尖朝鼻尖方向刺 0.8–1.2 寸，不可向内上方深刺，深部是延髓和椎动脉。

GV20 Bǎihuì百会

Best for最适合 Vertex (Jueyin) headache, or headache with dizziness or an empty feeling巅顶痛（厥阴头痛）、头痛伴眩晕或头空

Why依据 The Governor vessel meets the Liver channel at the vertex, the 'meeting of all yang'督脉与足厥阴交会于巅顶，「诸阳之会」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the vertex and empty deficiency headaches than GB20, which leans to the occiput and expelling wind; insert transversely 0.5–0.8 cun, and do not needle infants whose fontanelle has not closed.比风池更偏头顶和虚证头空，风池偏后枕与祛风；平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，婴儿囟门未闭时不针。

ST8 Tóuwéi头维

Best for最适合 Pain at the corner of the forehead, heavy wrapped head (Yangming, wind-damp or phlegm)前额角痛、头重如裹（阳明头痛、风湿痰浊）

Why依据 Meeting point of the Stomach and Gallbladder channels at the corner of the hairline足阳明与足少阳交会穴，位于额角发际

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the forehead and heavy damp headaches than GB8, which is for temporal migraine; the scalp is thin, so insert transversely backward 0.5–1 cun.比率谷更偏前额和湿浊头重，率谷偏颞侧偏头痛；头皮薄，向后平刺 0.5–1 寸。

GB8 Shuàigǔ率谷

Best for最适合 Temporal one-sided headache (Shaoyang), headache after drinking颞侧偏头痛（少阳头痛）、酒后头痛

Why依据 Meeting point of the Gallbladder and Bladder channels, 1.5 cun into the hairline above the ear apex足少阳与足太阳交会穴，耳尖直上入发际 1.5 寸

Versus others与其他穴的区别 The first local choice for one-sided headache and more temporal than ST8; often paired with TE5 and GB41 above and below; insert transversely 0.5–0.8 cun.偏头痛局部首选，比头维偏颞侧；常配外关、足临泣上下呼应，平刺 0.5–0.8 寸。

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

Best for最适合 Frontal headache, external headache with fever and blocked nose, general pain relief前额痛、外感头痛伴发热鼻塞，全身止痛

Why依据 Large Intestine yuan point; 'face and mouth, seek LI4', the distal point for Yangming headache大肠经原穴；「面口合谷收」，阳明头痛远端穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More analgesic and frontal than LU7, which leans to releasing the exterior and the back of the neck; forbidden in pregnancy.比列缺更偏止痛和前额，列缺偏解表和后项；孕妇禁针。

LU7 Lièquē列缺

Best for最适合 External headache running into a stiff neck, blocked nose外感头痛连项、项强、鼻塞

Why依据 Lung luo point, confluent point of the Ren vessel; 'head and neck, seek LU7'肺经络穴，八脉交会通任脉；「头项寻列缺」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 For external headaches only, especially wind-cold, not a first choice in internal patterns; works better paired with LI4 as source and connecting points than alone.只用于外感（尤其风寒），内伤头痛不作主选；与合谷原络相配比单用效果好。

BL10 Tiānzhù天柱

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern medicine divides headache into primary and secondary. The main primary types are tension-type headache (both sides, a band or pressure, not worse with activity), migraine (usually one-sided and throbbing, with nausea and sensitivity to light and sound, worse with activity, sometimes with aura) and cluster headache (severe pain around one eye with tearing and a blocked nostril); medication-overuse headache is also common. Secondary headaches are picked up by the red flags above: bleeding, stroke, meningitis, a space-occupying lesion, giant cell arteritis, glaucoma, pre-eclampsia and others. Acupuncture has a reasonable body of trial evidence for migraine prevention and tension-type headache, usually as an addition to medicine and lifestyle measures. The channel regions (Yangming, Shaoyang, Taiyang, Jueyin) help describe the pain and choose points but cannot rule out neurological disease.现代医学把头痛分原发和继发。原发的主要是紧张型头痛（双侧、箍紧或压迫感、不随活动加重）、偏头痛（多为单侧搏动性痛，伴恶心、怕光怕声，活动加重，部分有先兆）和丛集性头痛（一侧眼眶周围剧痛，伴流泪鼻塞）；药物过度使用性头痛也很常见。继发头痛要靠上面的红旗信号筛出来：出血、卒中、脑膜炎、颅内占位、巨细胞动脉炎、青光眼、子痫前期等。针刺对偏头痛预防和紧张型头痛有较多临床研究支持，通常作为药物和生活方式管理的补充。经络分区（阳明、少阳、太阳、厥阴）能帮助描述和选穴，但不能用来排除神经系统疾病。
