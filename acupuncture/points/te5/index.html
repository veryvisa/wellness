⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

TE5 is a stronger fit for temporal headache with ear symptoms along the Shaoyang pathway because it is a luo and Yang-Wei confluent point; headache alone without Shaoyang features is not enough to select it.偏头侧痛、耳周症状并沿少阳经分布时，外关兼具络穴及通阳维属性；外感头项痛而少阳特征不明显时不应仅凭头痛选它。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

A distal option when tinnitus comes with Shaoyang temporal, neck, or forearm signs; sudden hearing loss, vertigo, or ear discharge needs cause assessment first.耳鸣兼少阳头侧、颈项或前臂经脉症状时可作远端穴；突发听力下降、眩晕或耳流脓须先查病因。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

Where is TE5 Wàiguān?外关（TE5）在哪里？

Posterior forearm, 2 cun above the dorsal wrist crease, between ulna and radius.前臂后面，腕背横纹上2寸，尺骨与桡骨之间。

How is TE5 needled?外关（TE5）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1寸。

Special-point roles of TE5?外关（TE5）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting point; Eight-confluent point, opens the Yang Wei vessel络穴；八脉交会穴，通阳维脉

Main safety point at TE5?外关（TE5）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid superficial veins; withdraw and redirect for marked radiating sensation or extensor weakness.避开浅静脉；出现伸指无力或明显放射感应退针调整。
