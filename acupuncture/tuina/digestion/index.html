Bloated after meals: belly rubbing and a few common points吃完总是胀：摩腹与几个常用穴

Bloating after meals, or days between bowel movements, is a minor nuisance many people simply live with. Belly rubbing is one of the few self-massage techniques that has had a randomised trial, but that trial's conclusion was modest: it can go alongside what your doctor prescribes, not replace it.饭后肚子发胀、几天才去一次厕所，是很多人习以为常的小毛病。摩腹是少数有过随机试验的自我按摩之一，但那项试验的结论很克制：它可以和医生开的处理一起用，不能代替。

⏱ About 8 minutes约 8 分钟 · About an hour after a meal, or in bed before getting up; empty your bladder first.饭后一小时左右，或早上起床前躺在床上做；膀胱先排空。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Vomiting blood or what looks like coffee grounds, or black or bloody stools: go to emergency呕血、吐出咖啡渣样的东西，或黑便、便血：去急诊
  • Sudden severe belly pain, a rigid belly, or pain that is worse when pressure is released: go to emergency突然剧烈腹痛、肚子发硬，或按下去松手时更痛：去急诊
  • Swallowing that keeps getting harder, unexplained weight loss, anaemia吞咽困难越来越重、不明原因体重下降、贫血
  • A new change in bowel habits after age 50: see your family doctor and ask about BC's colon cancer screening50 岁以后新出现的排便习惯改变：去看家庭医生，并了解 BC 的大肠癌筛查
  • Vomiting that does not stop, or pain that wakes you at night持续呕吐，或夜里痛醒

✋ When not to press这些情况先别按

  • In pregnancy, leave the belly alone (including ST25 Tianshu), and skip LI4 and SP6 as well: traditional pregnancy cautions孕期不按腹部（包括天枢 ST25），也不按合谷、三阴交：这些是传统的孕期禁忌
  • Within an hour after eating, rub lightly only; no point pressure饭后一小时内只轻摩，不按穴
  • With an abdominal hernia, an unhealed abdominal wound or a known abdominal aortic aneurysm, do not massage the belly有腹部疝、腹部手术伤口未愈、已知腹主动脉瘤的人不按腹部
  • Not during sudden belly pain急性腹痛时不按

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1
    Circular rubbing摩法

    Lie on your back with knees bent, palm around the navel, and circle clockwise: up the right side of the belly, across the top, down the left side, following the path of the large bowel.仰卧屈膝，手掌放在肚脐周围，顺时针画大圈：从右下腹往上，横过上腹，再从左侧往下——这是大肠走的方向。

    How much力度与时间 3–5 minutes, light and steady3–5 分钟，轻而平稳

  2. 2
    CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘Kneading揉法

    CV12 Zhongwan is 4 cun above the navel, about halfway between the navel and the bottom of the breastbone; knead gently with three finger pads.肚脐上 4 寸（约在肚脐和胸骨下端的正中间）是中脘，用三指指腹轻轻揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

  3. 3
    ST25 Tiānshū天枢Kneading揉法

    ST25 Tianshu lies 2 cun (about three finger-widths) to each side of the navel; knead both sides gently at once.肚脐两旁 2 寸（约三横指）是天枢，两手同时轻揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute; skip in pregnancy约 1 分钟；孕期跳过

  4. 4
    PC6 Nèiguān内关Pressing按法

    When you feel queasy, press PC6 Neiguan, 2 cun above the wrist crease between the two tendons.觉得反胃、恶心时，按压手腕横纹上 2 寸、两筋之间的内关。

    How much力度与时间 1–2 minutes each side每侧 1–2 分钟

  5. 5
    ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里Kneading揉法

    Sit up. Below and outside the knee, 3 cun (about four finger-widths) below the outer knee eye and one finger-width out from the shinbone, is ST36 Zusanli; knead it with your thumb.坐起来，膝盖外下方：从膝眼往下 3 寸（约四横指）、胫骨外侧一横指处是足三里，用拇指按揉。

    How much力度与时间 1–2 minutes each side每侧 1–2 分钟

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 In a randomised trial, people with constipation who had about 15 minutes of abdominal massage a day for 8 weeks had milder gut symptoms and more bowel movements, but they used no fewer laxatives; the authors framed belly massage as a complement to laxatives, not a substitute.一项随机试验里，便秘的人每天做约 15 分钟腹部按摩，连续 8 周，胃肠症状减轻、排便次数增加；但他们用的泻药并没有减少，作者把腹部按摩定位为泻药的补充，而不是替代。 [1]
  • E1 A Cochrane review of 59 trials found that stimulating PC6 was followed by less nausea and vomiting after surgery than sham stimulation, though the evidence quality was low; that is the post-operative setting, and everyday after-meal queasiness has not been studied the same way.Cochrane 系统综述（59 项试验）发现，刺激内关（PC6）比假刺激更少发生手术后的恶心和呕吐，但证据质量低；这是手术后的场景，日常饭后反胃没有同样的研究。 [2]
  • E5 We found no reliable controlled studies of pressing CV12, ST25 or ST36 for everyday bloating.中脘、天枢、足三里用于日常腹胀的自我按揉，没有找到可靠的对照研究。

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 The Four Command Points rhyme says 'belly and abdomen, keep to ST36', and ST36 is the point tradition uses most for digestion; CV12 is the front-mu point of the stomach and ST25 that of the large intestine. Rubbing the belly and strolling after meals is a long-standing habit in traditional self-care. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.四总穴歌有「肚腹三里留」，足三里是传统上消化问题最常用的穴；中脘是胃的募穴，天枢是大肠的募穴。饭后摩腹、缓步走，是传统养生里流传很久的习惯。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。

  • E1 Systematic reviews / guidelines系统综述或指南
  • E2 Small or few randomised trials少量或小样本随机试验
  • E3 Observational studies (association)观察性研究（相关）
  • E4 Lab, animal or mechanism studies机制、动物或实验室研究
