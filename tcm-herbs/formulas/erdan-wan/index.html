⚠️ Cinnabar Toxicity and Mercury Exposure Warning朱砂毒性与汞暴露警示 Cinnabar is primarily composed of mercury sulfide. Mercury-containing medicines carry risks of cumulative exposure and kidney and nervous system toxicity. Traditional formula records do not establish modern safety. Do not independently purchase ingredients, prepare this formula, or take it long term. Suspected mercury exposure requires prompt medical assessment and avoidance of further exposure.丹砂即朱砂，主要成分为硫化汞。含汞药物存在累积暴露及肾脏、神经系统毒性风险。传统方书记载不等于现代安全性证明；不得自行购买原料配制或长期服用。出现疑似汞暴露症状，应停止进一步暴露并及时接受医疗评估。

Original text原文

「丹參熟地天冬（兩半）麥冬茯神甘草（一兩）丹砂人參菖蒲遠志（五錢。蜜丸）。」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

The formula contains one and a half liang each of Danshen, prepared Rehmannia root, and Asparagus root; one liang each of Ophiopogon root, Poria with hostwood, and licorice root; and five qian each of cinnabar, ginseng, Acorus rhizome, and Polygala root. The ingredients are made into honey pills.组成：丹参、熟地、天冬各一两半；麦冬、茯神、甘草各一两；丹砂、人参、菖蒲、远志各五钱，制成蜜丸。

「安神養血，清熱息風，服之得睡。」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

The traditional description states that the formula nourishes the blood, calms the spirit, clears heat, extinguishes internal wind, and is associated with sleep.功用为安神养血、清热息风，原文记载服后得睡。

Cautions使用注意

This formula contains cinnabar, which carries a risk of mercury exposure. It should not be prepared or taken without qualified medical oversight. Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals, children, people with impaired liver or kidney function, and those using other mercury-containing products should particularly avoid unsupervised use. Seek emergency medical attention for palpitations with chest pain, breathing difficulty, fainting, altered consciousness, sudden speech loss, or one-sided weakness. Sudden speech loss may indicate a stroke and must not be treated as ordinary insomnia or heart yin deficiency. Persistent insomnia or substantial emotional or cognitive changes also warrant professional assessment.本方含朱砂（丹砂），具有汞暴露风险，不宜自行配制或服用；孕妇、哺乳期人士、儿童、肝肾功能异常者及正在使用其他含汞产品者尤其应避免自行使用。心悸、胸痛、呼吸困难、晕厥、意识改变、突然失语或单侧肢体无力，应立即就医；突然失语可能是卒中表现，不能按普通失眠或心阴不足自行处理。长期失眠、明显情绪或认知改变，也应接受专业评估。

Contraindicated in pregnancy孕妇禁用 This formula contains cinnabar, a toxic mineral medicine with mercury exposure risks. This site classifies the formula as contraindicated during pregnancy at the strictest level. This is more restrictive than the pregnancy cautions associated with Danshen, licorice, and ginseng.本方含丹砂（朱砂），属于有毒矿物药；考虑汞暴露风险，本站按最严格级别列为孕期禁用。此级别严于方中丹参、甘草、人参的孕期慎用要求。
