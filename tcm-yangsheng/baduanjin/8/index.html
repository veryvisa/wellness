One rise and drop is one repetition; 7 in all一起一落为一遍，共 7 遍

Bèi hòu qī diān bǎi bìng xiāo

Qigong养生功法 › Baduanjin (Eight Pieces of Brocade)八段锦 › Form 8: Rising on the toes and dropping第8式 · 背后七颠百病消

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Osteoporosis or a past spinal compression fracture: lower the heels softly, no tap骨质疏松、脊柱有压缩骨折史：脚跟轻放，不震地
  • Knee, ankle or heel pain: rise less and land more gently膝、踝、足跟疼痛：提踵低一些、落下轻一些
  • Older adults with poor balance or a history of falls: always hold a chair back or the wall平衡差、容易跌倒的老人：一定扶着椅背或墙

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Stand with feet together, arms hanging at the sides两脚并拢站立，两臂自然垂在体侧
  2. Raise both heels slowly, lifting the crown of the head; hold briefly两脚跟慢慢提起，头向上顶，略停
  3. Drop the heels so they tap the floor lightly脚跟下落，轻轻震一下地面
  4. Repeat 7 times, then go into the closing form重复 7 次，然后接收势

🌬 Breathe in as you rise, out as you drop.提踵时吸气，落下时呼气。

✅ Key points要领

  • As you rise, grip the floor with the toes and lift the whole body, as if drawn upward提踵时五趾抓地、身体向上拔，像被提起来
  • The drop is a light tap, not a stamp: let the heels fall almost to the floor, then set them down落地是「轻震」，不是砸地：先落到离地一点点，再放下
  • Stay relaxed, teeth lightly together as you land全身放松，落下时牙齿轻合

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Landing so hard it jars the head落地太重，震得头疼
  • Leaning forward or back on the rise提踵时身体前倾或后仰
  • Refusing to hold on when your balance is shaky站不稳还勉强不扶

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Hold the back of a chair; or sit and simply raise and lower the heels without the tap.扶着椅背做；或坐着只做抬脚跟、放下（不震）。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 The Governor Vessel runs up the midline of the back, and the traditional reading is that the gentle jolt of the heels 'shakes' the back, hence 'seven jolts behind the back'; 'a hundred illnesses melt away' is the rhyme's traditional saying, not a promise.督脉沿背后正中上行，传统解释说脚跟落地的轻震「颠」动背后，所以叫「背后七颠」；「百病消」是口诀的传统说法，不是疗效承诺。
