📍 Location定位★

In the anterior neck, above the laryngeal prominence, in the depression above the hyoid bone, on the anterior midline.颈前区，喉结上方，舌骨上缘凹陷中，前正中线上。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique toward the root of the tongue向舌根方向斜刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

🛡 Safety安全

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Aphasia after stroke, sudden loss of voice, swallowing difficulty, flaccid tongue with drooling, swelling under the tongue, mouth ulcers, sore throat中风失语、暴喑、吞咽困难、舌缓流涎、舌下肿痛、口舌生疮、喉痹等咽喉口舌病证 ⚖️

    CV23 lies in the anterior midline hollow above the hyoid body and below the mandible, above the laryngeal prominence; gentle tongue elevation or swallowing helps identify hyoid movement.廉泉在舌骨体上缘与下颌下方之间的前正中凹陷，结喉上方；轻抬舌或吞咽可帮助辨认舌骨活动。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The hyoid, tongue base and suprahyoid muscles are nearby; laterally lie the carotid sheath, hypoglossal nerve and salivary structures. Localize superficially and avoid deep lateral pressure or blind procedures toward the floor of mouth or airway.舌骨、舌根及舌骨上肌群位于附近，偏外侧有颈动脉鞘、舌下神经及唾液腺结构；仅作浅表定位，避免偏侧深压或向口底、气道盲目操作。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Use the slight movement of the hyoid to identify the hollow above it, with very light touch; stop for painful swallowing, a neck pulse or a mass.以舌骨轻微上下移动辨识上缘凹陷，触压要轻；出现吞咽痛、颈部搏动或肿块时停止。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Cross-check its Ren-channel sequence between CV22 at the suprasternal notch and CV24 below the lower lip; CV23 is near the hyoid, not the laryngeal prominence itself.与天突（胸骨上窝）和承浆（下唇下）按颈前任脉次序核对；廉泉在舌骨附近，不是结喉本身。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Mistaking the laryngeal prominence or thyroid cartilage for the hyoid shifts the point too low; deep lateral pressure approaches the carotid sheath and neurovascular structures and may cause injury or a vagal response.把结喉或甲状软骨当舌骨会定位过低；偏侧深压接近颈动脉鞘和神经血管结构，可能引发损伤或晕厥反射。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

More fitting than CV22 when tongue-base, swallowing or tongue-movement symptoms dominate; acute throat swelling, stridor or inability to swallow is urgent.舌根、吞咽或舌运动异常突出时比天突更相符；咽喉急性肿胀、喘鸣或不能吞咽属急症。

Stroke (sequelae)中风 ⚖️

Only an adjunct for stable post-stroke swallowing or tongue-movement impairment; sudden facial droop, weakness or aphasia must never delay emergency stroke care.仅作卒中后稳定期吞咽或舌运动障碍的辅助定位；突发面歪、肢体无力或失语绝不可延误卒中急救。

Where is CV23 Liánquán?廉泉（CV23）在哪里？

In the anterior neck, above the laryngeal prominence, in the depression above the hyoid bone, on the anterior midline.颈前区，喉结上方，舌骨上缘凹陷中，前正中线上。

How is CV23 needled?廉泉（CV23）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the root of the tongue; 0.5–0.8 cun.向舌根方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of CV23?廉泉（CV23）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Conception and Yin Linking vessels任脉、阴维脉交会穴

Main safety point at CV23?廉泉（CV23）最要注意的安全问题？

Angle only along the midline toward the tongue root: laterally and deep lie the submandibular gland, lingual artery and carotid sheath; never enter the larynx or airway. Sudden aphasia or dysphagia needs stroke assessment first.针只在正中向舌根斜刺：偏外深部有颌下腺、舌动脉与颈动脉鞘；不刺入喉部与气道。突发失语、吞咽困难先按卒中急症就医。
