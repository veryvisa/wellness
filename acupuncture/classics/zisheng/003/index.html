《针灸资生经》卷二。

Illustrated Classic of Acumoxibustion as a Vital Aid, Volume 2.

💡 书名与卷次。南宋王执中撰，成书于南宋嘉定十三年（1220年）。卷二集中讨论针灸与药物配合、针刺禁忌、骨度与同身寸、穴名异同、灸法及灸疮处理等基础操作规范。

鍼灸須藥

针灸必须配合药物。

Acupuncture and Moxibustion Require Medicinal Herbs.

💡 篇目标题。强调针药并重，反对单用针、单用灸或只用药物而排斥针灸的片面做法。

千金云病有須鍼者即鍼刺以補瀉之不宜鍼者直爾灸之然灸之大法其孔穴與鍼無忌即下白鍼或温鍼訖乃灸之此為良醫其脚氣一病最宜鍼若鍼而不灸灸而不鍼非良醫也鍼灸而藥藥不鍼灸亦非良醫也但恨下里間知鍼者鮮爾所以學者須解用鍼燔鍼白鍼皆須妙解知鍼知藥固是良醫此言鍼灸與藥之相須也今人或但知鍼而不灸灸而不鍼或惟用藥而不知鍼灸者皆犯孫真人所戒也而世所謂醫者則但知有藥而已鍼灸則未嘗過而問焉人或詰之則曰是外科也業貴精不貴雜也否則曰富貴之家未必肯鍼灸也皆自文其過爾吾故詳著千金之説以示人云

《千金方》说：病症中应当用针刺的，就用针刺来补虚泻实；不适宜用针刺的，就直接用艾灸。然而施灸的大法是，凡孔穴没有针灸禁忌的，可以先下毫针（白针）或用温针，然后再施灸，这样才算良医。其中‘脚气’这种病最适宜用针刺。如果只针不灸，或者只灸不针，都不是良医；只针灸不用药，或只用药不针灸，也都不是良医。只是遗憾民间懂得针灸的人很少，所以学者必须懂得运用各种针具，对火针（燔针）、毫针（白针）都要深刻领悟；既懂针灸又懂药物，固然是良医。这就是说明针灸与药物应当相互配合。如今的人，有的只知针不知灸，有的只知灸不知针，或者只用药不知针灸，这些都违背了孙思邈（孙真人）的告诫。而世上所谓的医生，却只知道用药罢了，对针灸从来不过问。有人质问他们，就推说这是‘外科’技术，学业贵在专精不贵驳杂；或者说富贵人家未必肯接受针灸。这些都是自己掩饰过错罢了。所以我详细录出《千金方》的说法来昭示世人。

The Qianjin Fang states: When a disease calls for needling, puncture with a needle to tonify or drain; when it is unsuitable for needling, apply moxibustion directly. However, the general rule of moxibustion is that where an acupoint has no contraindications to needling, one may insert a fine needle (white needle) or use a warm needle first and then apply moxibustion—this makes a good physician. Among illnesses, beriberi / leg qi (jiaoqi) is especially suited to needling. To needle without moxibustion or moxibustion without needling does not make a good physician; to use acupuncture and moxibustion without medicinals, or medicinals without acupuncture and moxibustion, also fails to make a good physician. It is only regrettable that few in the countryside understand needling, so students must know how to use needles and master both fire needles (fan zhen) and fine needles (bai zhen). Knowing both needling and medicinals certainly makes a good physician; this explains how acupuncture, moxibustion, and medicinals complement one another. Today, some know needling without moxibustion, some moxibustion without needling, or some use only medicinals without knowing acupuncture and moxibustion—all violate Sun Simiao's warning. Yet so-called physicians of the world only know medicinals and never inquire into acupuncture and moxibustion. If questioned, they claim it is 'external medicine' and that study values specialization rather than mixing, or they claim wealthy households may not be willing to accept acupuncture and moxibustion. These are all excuses to cover up their own shortcomings. Therefore, I record the teachings of the Qianjin Fang in detail to show people.

💡 ‘白针’指单纯针刺、不加热的针法，与温针、燔针相对；‘燔针’即火针，烧红后速刺速出；‘温针’指针刺后在针柄加艾绒施灸。古代‘脚气’多指湿邪下注导致的下肢痿弱、麻木、肿痛，部分可对应现代维生素B1缺乏性脚气病、周围神经病变或下肢水肿/静脉循环障碍。‘先针后灸’或‘针药并用’在现代临床仍具实用价值：如风寒湿痹、寒凝血瘀或慢性疼痛，中医仍有先针后灸、针药并用的做法；急性感染、高热、出血倾向等情况须先由医生评估。古代医家将针灸归为‘外科’或以‘富贵不喜针灸’推诿，反映了宋代医籍中针药分流、重药轻针的社会现象。

针刺禁忌。

Contraindications and Cautions of Needling.

千金云夫用鍼者先明其孔穴補虚瀉實而失其理鍼毛皮腠理勿傷肌肉鍼肌肉勿傷筋脉鍼筋脉勿傷骨髓鍼骨髓勿傷諸絡傷筋膜者愕視失魂傷血脉者煩亂失神傷皮毛者上氣失魄傷骨髓者呻吟失志傷肌肉者四支不收失智此為五亂因鍼所生若更失度有死之憂也素問亦云刺骨無傷筋刺筋無傷肉刺肉無傷脉刺脉無傷皮刺皮無傷肉刺肉無傷筋刺筋無傷骨刺中心一日死中肝五日死中腎六日死中肺三日死中脾十日死中膽一日半死刺跗上中大脉血出不止死刺頭中腦户入腦立死又無刺大醉大怒大勞大飢大渴大驚新飽云云詳見素問

《千金方》说：凡是用针的人，首先要明确穴位所在，补虚泻实，不可违背法理；针刺皮毛腠理时不要伤及肌肉，针刺肌肉时不要伤及筋脉，针刺筋脉时不要伤及骨髓，针刺骨髓时不要伤及各种络脉。如果伤及筋膜，会出现瞪眼惊视、失魂的表现；伤及血脉，会出现心烦意乱、失神的表现；伤及皮毛，会出现气喘上逆、失魄的表现；伤及骨髓，会出现呻吟不止、失志的表现；伤及肌肉，会出现四肢不能收持、失智的表现。这就是因针刺不当而产生的‘五乱’；如果再失于调治法度，就有死亡的隐忧。《素问》也说：刺骨不要伤筋，刺筋不要伤肉，刺肉不要伤脉，刺脉不要伤皮；刺皮不要伤肉，刺肉不要伤筋，刺筋不要伤骨。针刺中了心脏，一日内死亡；中了肝脏，五日内死亡；中了肾脏，六日内死亡；中了肺脏，三日内死亡；中了脾脏，十日内死亡；中了胆腑，一日半死亡。针刺足背（跗上）伤及大血管导致出血不止，会死亡；针刺头部脑户穴深刺入脑，会立即死亡。此外，不要在醉酒、大怒、过度劳累、极度饥饿、极度口渴、大惊、刚吃饱等状态下针刺，详细内容见《素问》。

The Qianjin Fang states: A practitioner using needles must first be clear about the acupoints. If tonifying deficiency and draining excess violate proper principles: when needling skin, hair, and striae (couli), do not injure the muscles; when needling muscles, do not injure the sinews and vessels; when needling sinews and vessels, do not injure the bone marrow; when needling bone marrow, do not injure the various collateral vessels. Injuring the fascia/sinew membranes causes startled staring and loss of hun (ethereal soul); injuring the blood vessels causes restlessness, agitation, and loss of shen (spirit); injuring the skin and hair causes upward-rushing qi (dyspnea) and loss of po (corporeal soul); injuring the bone marrow causes groaning and loss of zhi (will); injuring the muscles causes inability to draw in/control the four limbs and loss of zhi (intellect/wisdom). These are the 'Five Disorders' arising from needling; if regulation is further lost, there is risk of death. The Suwen also states: When needling bone, do not injure sinew; when needling sinew, do not injure flesh; when needling flesh, do not injure vessels; when needling vessels, do not injure skin; when needling skin, do not injure flesh; when needling flesh, do not injure sinew; when needling sinew, do not injure bone. Puncture striking the heart causes death in one day; striking the liver, in five days; striking the kidney, in six days; striking the lung, in three days; striking the spleen, in ten days; striking the gallbladder, in one and a half days. Puncture on the dorsum of the foot (fushang) striking a major vessel with unstoppable bleeding causes death; puncture on the head at Naohu (GV17) penetrating into the brain causes immediate death. Furthermore, do not needle during severe intoxication, extreme anger, overexertion, extreme hunger, extreme thirst, severe fright, or right after a full meal; details are in the Suwen.

💡 涉及穴位：脑户 GV17。‘五乱’在《灵枢·五乱》中本指气乱于心、肺、肠胃、臂胫、头，此处引《千金方》将深浅失度与魂、神、魄、志、智相配，体现古人重形神合一的思想；‘刺五脏中胆死期’出自《素问·刺禁论》，属于古代医理推演，并非现代临床精确死亡时间表，但其核心警示极具临床价值：胸腹部深刺可伤及心、肺、肝、脾、胆、肾等重要脏器，足背深刺可伤及足背动脉（如冲阳 ST42 附近大血管）导致难以控制的出血，枕部深刺（如脑户 GV17）可伤及延髓或颅内结构。‘醉、怒、劳、饥、渴、惊、饱’等禁忌至今仍适用：醉酒、大怒、饥饿、惊恐状态下针刺更易诱发晕针、血压波动或不良反应，临床操作前需评估患者状态。

孔穴相去

各孔穴之间的距离与测量方法。

Distances Between Acupoints and Measurement Methods.

💡 篇目标题。讨论脊柱椎体长度折量、胸腹部骨度分寸及古代文献记载的矛盾之处。

甲乙經云自大椎下至尾2585骨二十一椎長三尺折量取俞穴或云第一椎上更有大椎在宛宛陷中非有骨也有骨處即是第一椎若以大椎至尾骶二十一椎長三尺法校之則上節云椎每傾一寸四分惟第七椎下至於膂骨多分之七故上七節共九寸八分分之七下節十四椎每椎一寸四分分之五有竒故下七節共二尺一分分之三此亦是一説也但第一椎有骨乃骨節之收大椎雖無骨實是穴名既曰自大椎下至十一椎豈可不量大椎以下或者之説於是不通矣

《针灸甲乙经》说：从大椎往下到尾骶骨，共二十一椎，长三尺，按比例折量来取俞穴。有人说：第一椎上面另有大椎，在凹陷处，那里没有骨，有骨的地方才是第一椎。如果用'大椎到尾骶二十一椎长三尺'的算法来核对，那么上段每椎一寸四分，只有第七椎下到膂骨多出七厘，所以上七节共九寸八分七厘；下段十四椎，每椎一寸四分五厘有余，所以下面各节共二尺一分三厘。这也是一种说法。但是第一椎有骨，是骨节的开端；大椎虽然不是骨，却实实在在是穴名。既然说'从大椎往下'到某椎（原文作'十一椎'），怎么可以不从大椎量起呢？那种说法在这里就讲不通了。（原文'尾2585骨'即尾骶骨，底本缺字。）

The Zhenjiu Jiayijing states: from Dazhui (GV14) down to the coccyx (weidi bone) there are twenty-one vertebrae, three chi long, and the back transport (shu) points are located by proportional measurement. Some say that above the first vertebra there is a separate Dazhui in a depression where there is no bone, and that where the bone is, is the first vertebra. Checked against the rule that the twenty-one vertebrae from Dazhui to the coccyx measure three chi, each upper vertebra is 1 cun 4 fen, with an extra 7 li from below the seventh vertebra to the spinal bone, so the upper seven segments total 9 cun 8 fen 7 li; each of the lower fourteen vertebrae is a little over 1 cun 4 fen 5 li, so the lower segments total 2 chi 1 fen 3 li. This is one view. But the first vertebra with bone is the beginning of the bony segments, while Dazhui, though not a bone, is truly a point name. Since the text says 'from Dazhui downward' (the source reads 'to the eleventh vertebra'), how can one not measure from Dazhui? That view therefore does not hold.

💡 涉及穴位：大椎 GV14（第七颈椎棘突下凹陷中）。古代以‘尺’为骨度单位，不同时期尺长不同，且将颈胸腰骶椎体折算为固定数值属于古典骨度理论。现代临床定位脊柱穴位（如大椎 GV14、肺俞 BL13、心俞 BL15、肝俞 BL18、脾俞 BL20、肾俞 BL23 等）主要依靠体表骨性标志（如第7颈椎棘突、肩胛冈内侧端平第3胸椎棘突、肩胛骨下角平第7胸椎棘突、髂嵴最高点平第4腰椎棘突）结合骨度分寸或指寸法，不再机械套用三尺总长折算。文中厘数为原文所记，与《甲乙经》通行说法（上七节每节一寸四分一厘）略有出入，属古人折算法的不同版本。

自蔽骨下至臍八寸而中管居其中上下各四十氣穴論注云中管居心蔽骨與臍之中是也按明堂下經云鳩尾在臆前蔽骨下五分人無蔽骨者從岐骨際下行一寸則是欲定中管之中又當詳有蔽骨無蔽骨也當準人長短肥瘠量自臍下寸半為氣海三寸為丹田至屈骨凡五寸千金云屈骨在臍下五寸明堂下經亦云屈骨在横骨上中極下一寸當準人長短肥瘠量之銅人云幽門夾巨闕旁各五分肓俞夾臍各五分明堂云在巨闕旁各寸半通谷夾上管旁相去三寸不容在幽門旁各寸半天樞去肓俞寸半夾臍期門在不容旁寸半大横直臍旁不容天樞期門既各寸半則幽門育俞各五分誤矣銅人云腎俞在十四椎下兩旁各寸半與臍平肓門在十三椎下相去各三寸與鳩尾相直腎俞既與臍平肓門乃與鳩尾相直亦可疑也

从蔽骨（剑突）往下到肚脐是八寸，中脘穴居于正中，上下各有四寸。《气穴论》的注说：中脘居于心蔽骨与肚脐的中间，就是这个意思。按《明堂下经》说：鸠尾在胸前蔽骨下五分；没有蔽骨的人，从胸骨下端分叉处往下量一寸。这就是说，要想确定中脘的中点，还应当详细分辨是有蔽骨还是没有蔽骨。应当根据人的长短胖瘦来测量：从肚脐往下量一寸半是气海穴，三寸是丹田穴（相当于关元一带），到屈骨（耻骨联合上缘）共五寸。《千金方》说：屈骨在肚脐下五寸。《明堂下经》也说：屈骨在横骨上、中极下一寸，应当根据人的长短胖瘦来测量。《铜人腧穴针灸图经》说：幽门在巨阙旁开各五分，肓俞在脐旁开各五分；《明堂经》说在巨阙旁开各一寸半，通谷在上脘旁开、两穴相距三寸，不容在幽门旁开各一寸半，天枢离肓俞一寸半、夹脐两旁，期门在不容旁开一寸半，大横正对肚脐两旁。既然不容、天枢、期门各自旁开一寸半，那么幽门、肓俞各自旁开五分就是错误的了。《铜人》说：肾俞在第十四椎下两旁各一寸半，与肚脐平齐；肓门在第十三椎下，两旁相距各三寸，与鸠尾平齐。肾俞既然与肚脐平齐，肓门却与鸠尾平齐，这也是可疑的。

From the xiphoid process (bi gu) down to the umbilicus is eight cun, with Zhongwan (CV12) at the midpoint—four cun above and four cun below. The commentary to Qixue Lun states that Zhongwan lies midway between the xiphoid process and the umbilicus, which matches this. According to the Mingtang Xiajing, Jiuwei (CV15) is five fen below the xiphoid process in front of the chest; for those without a distinct xiphoid process, measure one cun down from the xiphisternal junction. Thus, to pinpoint the midpoint of Zhongwan, one must also distinguish whether a xiphoid process is present or not. Measure according to a person's height, length, and build: 1.5 cun below the umbilicus is Qihai (CV6), 3 cun is Dantian (corresponding to the Guanyuan CV4 region), and to the qu gu (superior border of the pubic symphysis) is five cun in total. The Qianjin Fang states that qu gu is five cun below the umbilicus; the Mingtang Xiajing also states that qu gu is above the transverse bone (heng gu, pubic bone) and one cun below Zhongji (CV3), to be measured according to body build. The Tongren says Youmen (KI21) is 0.5 cun lateral to Juque (CV14) on each side, and Huangshu (KI16) is 0.5 cun lateral to the umbilicus on each side; the Mingtang places it 1.5 cun lateral to Juque, Tonggu (KI20) lateral to Shangwan (CV13) with three cun between the two points, Burong (ST19) 1.5 cun lateral to Youmen, Tianshu (ST25) 1.5 cun from Huangshu flanking the umbilicus, Qimen (LR14) 1.5 cun lateral to Burong, and Daheng (SP15) directly lateral to the umbilicus. Since Burong, Tianshu, and Qimen are each 1.5 cun lateral to the midline, Youmen and Huangshu being 0.5 cun lateral is an error. The Tongren says Shenshu (BL23) is 1.5 cun lateral on each side below the fourteenth vertebra, level with the umbilicus, while Huangmen (BL51) is below the thirteenth vertebra, 3 cun apart laterally, level with Jiuwei (CV15). Since Shenshu is level with the umbilicus, Huangmen being level with Jiuwei is also doubtful.

論同身寸

论述同身寸。

Discussion of Proportional Finger Measurement (Tongshen Cun).

💡 篇目标题。对比古代不同医家关于同身寸的取法与量具优缺点。

下經曰岐伯以八分為一寸縁人有長短肥瘠不同取穴不凖扁鵲以手中指第一節為一寸縁人有身長手短身短手長取穴亦不準孫真人取大拇指節横文為一寸亦有差互今取男左女右手中指第二節内庭兩横文相去為一寸若屈指即旁取指則中節上下兩文角角相去逺近為一寸謂同身寸自依此寸法與人著灸療病多愈今以為準銅人亦曰取中指内文為一寸素問云同身寸是也又多用繩度量繩多出縮不準今以薄竹片3295量分寸療病凖的亦有用蠟紙條量者但薄篾易折蠟紙亦粘手難取稻稈心量却易為勝於用繩之信縮也

《明堂下经》说：岐伯以八分为一寸，因为人的长短胖瘦不同，这样取穴不准；扁鹊以手中指第一节为一寸，因为有人身长手短、身短手长，这样取穴也不准；孙思邈（孙真人）取大拇指节横纹为一寸，也存在差异。如今取男左女右手的中指第二节（中节）掌侧两横纹之间的距离为一寸；如果屈指即从侧面取指，则中节上下两横纹两端角点之间的距离为一寸，这叫做‘同身寸’。依照这个寸法给人施灸治病，大多痊愈，如今以此作为标准。《铜人》也说取中指内侧横纹为一寸，《素问》所说的‘同身寸’就是这个意思。此外，过去多用绳子来度量，但绳子容易拉伸或收缩不准；如今用薄竹片来量取分寸，治疗疾病很准确；也有用蜡纸条来量的，但薄竹篾容易折断，蜡纸也容易粘手难用，改用稻草芯（稻秆心）来量却容易操作，胜过用绳子那样伸缩不定。（原文‘3295’是底本缺字，本书多处出现，按《千金方》“凡点灸法”等用例当为‘点’字，这里指用薄竹片点量分寸。）

The Mingtang Xiajing says: Qibo took eight fen as one cun, which is inaccurate because people differ in height, length, and build; Bian Que took the first phalanx of the middle finger as one cun, which is also inaccurate because some people have long bodies and short hands or short bodies and long hands; Sun Simiao took the transverse crease of the thumb joint as one cun, which also varies. Today, we take the distance between the two palmar creases of the middle phalanx (second segment) of the middle finger—left hand for men, right hand for women—as one cun; if bending the finger to measure laterally, the distance between the corresponding corners of the upper and lower creases of the middle segment is one cun. This is called 'tongsheng cun' (proportional finger cun). Following this measurement to apply moxibustion has cured most illnesses I treated, and we now take it as the standard. The Tongren also states to take the inner crease of the middle finger as one cun, which is what the Suwen calls 'tongsheng cun'. In addition, cords/strings were often used for measurement, but cords stretch or shrink and are inaccurate. Now using thin bamboo strips to measure cun is accurate for treating illness; some also use wax-paper strips, but thin bamboo splints snap easily and wax paper sticks to the hand and is awkward, whereas using pith of rice stalks (daogang xin) is easy and superior to stretchy cords.

💡 现代针灸学中，中指同身寸是指将食指、中指、无名指、小指微屈，以中指中节桡侧两端纹头之间的距离作为1寸（不区分男左女右）；拇指同身寸是以拇指指间关节的宽度作为1寸；一夫法是以食指、中指、无名指和小指四指并拢，以中指近侧指间关节横纹水平的四指宽度作为3寸。临床上指寸法主要用于四肢取穴，躯干部仍以骨度分寸为主，两者结合使用更准。古代用竹片、稻草芯代替易伸缩的绳线，体现了古人注重测量工具稳定性的实践经验。

審方書

审察医方典籍中的定位与用语细节。

Examining Medical Formularies and Texts Carefully.

💡 篇目标题。提示阅读医书时须留心方位、缝隙、凹陷及分寸归属的精确含义。

經云爪甲與爪甲角内與外間内側與外側與夫陷中宛宛中要精審如某穴去某處幾寸與其穴去處同者自各有經絡

医经上说：‘爪甲’与‘爪甲角’，‘内’与‘外’，‘间’、‘内侧’与‘外侧’，以及‘陷中’、‘宛宛中’（明显凹陷处），都要精细审察。如果说某穴距离某处几寸，而它与另一穴位所在之处相同，那各自有不同的经络循行归属。

The medical classics speak of 'nail root/corner (zhua jia)' versus 'corner of the nail (zhua jia jiao)', 'inner (nei)' versus 'outer (wai)', 'space/cleft between (jian)', 'medial side (neice)' versus 'lateral side (waice)', as well as 'in a depression (xianzhong)' and 'in a clear hollow (wawan zhong)'—all require careful scrutiny. If a point is stated to be a certain number of cun from some place, and that location coincides with another point, each belongs to its own meridian/channel system.

💡 精准定位的核心基本功。例如井穴常在指甲角旁约0.1寸处（如少商 LU11 在拇指桡侧，距指甲角0.1寸；少泽 SI1 在小指尺侧，距指甲角0.1寸；至阴 BL67 在足小趾外侧，距指甲角0.1寸），‘陷中’与‘宛宛中’在现代解剖上多对应肌腱间隙、骨缘凹陷或关节窝。同一解剖位置若分属不同经脉，其主治侧重点与循经远治作用也不同，现代临床与针灸考试均高度重视定位精度与经脉归属。

灸膏肓云其間當有四肋三閒灸中間者謂四肋必有三閒當中間灸不灸邊兩閒也

关于灸膏肓穴的说法：其间应当有四条肋骨、三个肋间隙，在中间施灸的意思是，四肋之间必有三个肋间隙，应当在中间那个肋间隙施灸，不在上下边缘的两个肋间隙施灸。

Regarding moxibustion on Gaohuang (BL43): between them there should be four ribs and three intercostal spaces; moxibusting the middle one means that where four ribs enclose three intercostal spaces, you moxibust in the middle intercostal space rather than the two outer spaces.

💡 涉及穴位：膏肓 BL43。现代针灸学标准定位中，膏肓位于背部，当第4胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开3寸（大致相当于第4、5肋间隙或肩胛骨内侧缘水平，具体以现行国标骨度为准）。施灸时注意避开肩胛骨与肋骨缘，且背部施灸需防烫伤起疱。古文强调‘中间一间’，提示定位时需理清相邻肋间隙的层次关系，避免错位。

千金曰經云横三閒寸者則是三灸兩閒一寸有三灸灸有三分三壮之處即為一寸也

《千金方》说：经文上讲‘横三间寸’，就是说三壮灸、两间距为一寸，一寸有三次灸，每灸宽三分，三壮所占的地方就是一寸。

The Qianjin Fang says: When the classic speaks of 'transverse three-jian cun', it means three moxa cones across two intervals equal one cun, with three moxa applications—each three fen wide—so the area covered by three cones equals one cun.

💡 古代灸法分寸与艾炷大小换算记载。属于历史文献中对灸灼范围与分寸关系的解释，现代针灸临床不再用艾炷排列宽度作为人体长度单位，但‘三分’等大小概念仍用于提示艾炷规格对热力渗透范围的影响。

又曰凡量一夫之法覆手并舒四指對度四指上下節横過為一夫夫有兩種有三指為一夫者若灸脚弱以四指為一夫也見脚氣

又说：凡是测量‘一夫’的方法，是将手掌覆下、并拢伸展四指横向比量，四指上下指节横过的宽度为一夫。‘夫’有两种：有以三指为一夫的；如果是灸治‘脚弱’（下肢痿弱无力），则以四指为一夫，详情见《脚气》篇。

It also states: To measure one fu, place the palm facing down with the four fingers extended and joined, and measure transversely across the upper and lower finger joints of the four fingers for one fu. There are two kinds of fu: one takes three fingers as one fu; when moxibusting 'weakness of the legs (jiaoruo)', four fingers are taken as one fu, as detailed in the section on Beriberi/Leg Qi.

💡 ‘一夫’在古籍中既可指四指横宽（现代多称四指横量/一夫法为3寸），有时也见三指用法。现代标准中，将食指、中指、无名指、小指并拢，以中指近侧指间关节横纹水平的四指宽度定为3寸（如足三里 ST36 在犊鼻下3寸、三阴交 SP6 在内踝尖上3寸）。临床上需统一按现行标准指寸法操作，避免因指宽差异导致取穴偏差。

《千金方》说：痈疽、疔肿、喉痹、客忤尤为紧急，凡是配制汤药不能避开所谓的凶日，刚察觉生病片刻之间就应当立刻治疗。又说：凡是人突然中风或感受时气（疫疠时邪），各种痛苦都必须抓紧急救治疗，拖延日久之后就都难以治愈了。这个说法非常恰当。在危急关头，生命就在呼吸之间，如果一定要等到吉日再治疗，人就已经列入死亡名单了。这就是为什么不能拘泥于避忌的原因。只有在平时治病、防病于未发之前，选用天德、月德等吉日来服药、针灸是可以的。

The Qian Jin Fang says: carbuncles and deep abscesses (yongju), furuncles (dingzhong), throat obstruction (houbi), and sudden fright/evil-qi intrusion (kewu) are especially urgent; whenever preparing decoctions, one must not avoid so-called inauspicious days, and as soon as illness is noticed, one should treat it immediately. It also says: whenever a person is suddenly struck by wind or contracts seasonal pestilential qi, whatever suffering arises must be treated with urgent first aid, for once it drags on for a long time it becomes hard to cure. This argument is very sound. In moments of acute crisis, life hangs by a thread; if one waits for an auspicious day to treat later, the patient will already be on the register of the dead. That is why one cannot be bound by taboos. Only when treating illness in peaceful times or intervening before disease takes clear shape may one choose auspicious days such as Tian De or Yue De to take medicine or practice acupuncture and moxibustion.

The Qian Jin Fang says: moxibustion may be performed after midday, meaning that before yin qi arrives, moxibustion never fails to take effect. During the morning and especially at early dawn (pingdan), grain/digestive qi is still deficient and can easily cause seizure-like or syncopal reactions, so acupuncture and moxibustion should not be performed; however, sudden and urgent conditions are exempt from this rule.

💡 术语解释：'日正午以后'指午后阳气渐转、气血相对充盈之时；'平旦'指黎明刚亮、晨起空腹阶段；'榖气虚'指清晨未进饮食、脾胃水谷精气不足；'癫'在此语境多指突发的昏仆、抽搐、眩晕或晕厥类惊厥/厥逆表现（原文“癫3392”的3392为底本缺字，本书他处“目3392”通行本作“目眩”，此处当为“癫眩”）。医理：认为清晨空腹状态下人体气血生化不足，耐受针刺或温热刺激的能力较弱，易出现气血逆乱或晕厥。涉及穴位：无特定穴位代码。今天是否适用及原因：临床上避免让患者在过度饥饿、清晨空腹状态下接受针灸或艾灸非常实用，能显著降低晕针、晕灸发生率；但'必须等到正午以后才能艾灸'在现代临床并不严格执行，白天常规时段只要患者进食适度、状态平稳即可施治，急症更是随时可治。与现代医学对照：清晨空腹低血糖、交感神经张力变化及焦虑紧张，是针刺/艾灸过程中发生血管迷走神经性晕厥（晕针/晕灸）的重要诱因；进食少量食物后再治疗有助于维持血糖与血流动力学稳定。

The Xia Jing states: if during moxibustion one encounters heavy gloomy fog, fierce wind and snow, extreme scorching heat, thunder and lightning, or rainbows/halos, pause temporarily, and resume moxibustion once the weather clears up. Urgent and critical conditions are not bound by this either.

💡 术语解释：'阴雾大风雪''猛炎暑''雷电虹霓'指恶劣气象或剧烈天气变化；'1699''1974'为底本缺字：1974按本书他处用例当为“候”（暂停，候晴明再灸），1699疑为“寒”。医理：传统认为剧烈风雨寒暑或雷电等极端气候会扰动自然之气与人体气血，且施灸时环境通风、温度与安全条件受影响，故宜暂避；但遇急症则不拘泥。涉及穴位：无特定穴位代码。今天是否适用及原因：极端酷暑、严寒大风或雷雨天气下，室内外温湿度剧烈变化、空气流通受限或烟雾不易消散，暂停非急症艾灸有现实合理性；现代诊室通常配有空调、排烟与恒温设施，只要环境安全舒适、通风良好即可正常施灸，急症更不受天气限制。与现代医学对照：极端高温可增加热应激或中暑风险，严寒大风易使体表血管收缩、增加受凉不适；现代医疗环境通过温控与排烟系统缓冲了外部气象对治疗操作的直接影响。
