經脈第十 Chapter 10: The Meridians.

雷公問於黃帝曰︰禁脈之言，凡刺之理，經脈為始，營其所行，制其度量，內次五藏，外別六府，願盡聞其道。

雷公向黄帝请教说：关于经脉的学说，凡是针刺治疗的原理，都应当从经脉开始。希望了解经脉运行的路线、测量和规律，以及它们在体内如何联系五脏、在体表如何区分和联系六腑。请您完整地讲述其中的道理。

Lei Gong asked the Yellow Emperor: Regarding the theory of the meridians, the principles of acupuncture begin with the meridians. I wish to understand their courses, their measurements and proportions, their internal connections with the five zang organs, and their external relationships with the six fu organs. Please explain this entire system.

💡 五藏即五脏：心、肝、脾、肺、肾；六府即六腑：胆、胃、小肠、大肠、膀胱、三焦。这里的藏府是古代中医的功能系统概念，不应直接等同于现代解剖学中的单一器官。经脉理论将人体内部脏腑功能与体表肢体、感觉及病候联系起来。

黃帝曰︰人始生，先成精，精成而腦髓生，骨為幹，脈為營，筋為剛，肉為牆，皮膚堅而毛髮長，穀入於胃，脈道以通，血氣乃行。

黄帝说：人在开始形成时，首先形成精，精形成后，脑髓逐渐生成。骨骼构成人体的支架，经脉承载和运行营养物质，筋使身体具有强健的力量，肌肉构成人体的屏障，皮肤逐渐坚实，毛发也逐渐生长。饮食进入胃后，经脉通道逐渐畅通，气血便能够运行。

The Yellow Emperor said: When a human being first develops, essence forms first. From essence, the brain and marrow develop. The bones form the body's framework, the vessels provide nourishment, the sinews provide strength, and the muscles form a protective wall. The skin becomes firm and the hair grows. When food enters the stomach, the pathways of the vessels become open, allowing qi and blood to circulate.

💡 精是中医生命物质和生长发育理论中的重要概念；脑髓、骨、筋、肉、皮毛构成古代对人体发育的整体认识。营在此有营养、运行之意。谷入于胃、气血乃行，体现脾胃为气血生化之源的理论。现代医学可将部分内容与胚胎发育、骨骼肌肉形成、营养代谢及血液循环作概念性对照，但不能将精直接等同于DNA、干细胞或某种单一生物物质。

雷公曰︰願卒聞經脈之始生。

雷公说：我希望进一步听您完整讲解经脉是如何形成的。

Lei Gong said: I would like to hear a complete explanation of how the meridians originate.

💡 卒在此有完整、全部之意。这是由人体发育的整体论述转入经脉形成与功能的提问。

黃帝曰︰經脈者，所以能決死生，處百病，調虛實，不可不通。

黄帝说：经脉具有关系生命存亡、诊察和处理各种疾病、调节人体虚实状态的重要作用，因此医者不能不通晓经脉理论。

The Yellow Emperor said: The meridians are essential for determining life and death, managing a hundred diseases, and regulating deficiency and excess. Their theory must therefore be thoroughly understood.

💡 决死生、处百病、调虚实，概括了经脉在传统中医诊疗中的重要地位。虚实是辨证论治的基本纲领：虚证通常涉及正气不足，实证通常涉及邪气亢盛或病理产物壅盛。经脉诊察与针刺选穴在传统理论中相互联系。现代临床不能仅凭经脉诊断判断生死，亦不能把经脉系统直接视为已获现代解剖学证实的独立管道。

肺手太陰之脈，起於中焦，下絡大腸，還循胃口，上膈，屬肺，從肺系橫出腋下，下循臑內，行少陰、心主之前，下肘中，循臂內上骨下廉，入寸口，上魚，循魚際，出大指之端。其支者，從腕後直出次指內廉，出其端。是動則病肺脹滿，膨膨而喘欬，缺盆中痛，甚則交兩手而瞀，此為臂厥。是主肺所生病者，欬，上氣喘喝，煩心胸滿，臑臂內前廉痛厥，掌中熱。氣盛有餘，則肩背痛風寒，汗出中風，小便數而欠。氣虛則肩背痛寒，少氣不足以息，溺色變。為此諸病，盛則寫之，虛則補之，熱則疾之，寒則留之，陷下則灸之，不盛不虛，以經取之。盛者寸口大三倍於人迎，虛者則寸口反小於人迎也。

手太阴肺经起于中焦，向下联络大肠，再返回沿着胃的入口上行，穿过膈肌，归属于肺。从肺系横向出来到腋下，沿上臂内侧下行，经过手少阴心经和手厥阴心包经的前方，到达肘窝，再沿前臂内侧桡骨下方的边缘下行，进入寸口脉搏处，上行至大鱼际，沿鱼际到达拇指尖端。它的分支从腕后直接走向食指内侧，并到达食指末端。 本经经气异常活动时，可出现肺部胀满、胸部膨胀、喘息和咳嗽、锁骨上窝疼痛；严重时会双手交叉抱持胸前、神志昏蒙，这种情况称为臂厥。 本经所主的肺脏相关病证包括咳嗽、气逆、喘息、心烦、胸满、上臂及前臂内侧前缘疼痛或厥冷，以及掌心发热。 肺经之气亢盛有余时，可出现肩背疼痛、感受风寒后出汗、容易受到风邪侵袭、小便频数而且量少。肺经之气虚弱时，可出现肩背寒痛、气短、呼吸不足以及尿液颜色改变。 治疗这些疾病，邪气盛时用泻法，正气虚时用补法，热证用疾刺法，寒证用留针法，经气下陷时用灸法；没有明显虚实时，按照本经的情况选取穴位。经气盛时，寸口脉比人迎脉大三倍；经气虚时，寸口脉反而比人迎脉小。

The Lung meridian of Hand Taiyin begins in the middle jiao, descends to connect with the Large Intestine, returns along the entrance of the stomach, ascends through the diaphragm, and belongs to the Lung. From the Lung system, it emerges transversely below the axilla and descends along the medial aspect of the upper arm, passing anterior to the Heart and Pericardium meridians. It reaches the elbow, follows the inferior border of the radius along the medial forearm, enters the cun pulse region, ascends to the thenar eminence, follows the thenar region, and emerges at the tip of the thumb. Its branch travels from the back of the wrist directly to the radial side of the index finger and emerges at its tip. When the meridian's qi becomes disturbed, symptoms may include pulmonary distension, chest fullness, wheezing, coughing, pain in the supraclavicular fossa, and, in severe cases, crossing the arms with clouded consciousness. This is called arm reversal. Diseases associated with the Lung include coughing, upward surging of qi, wheezing, vexation of the heart, chest fullness, pain or reversal along the anterior medial aspect of the upper arm and forearm, and heat in the palm. When qi is excessive, there may be shoulder and back pain, sweating after exposure to wind and cold, susceptibility to wind invasion, and frequent urination with scanty output. When qi is deficient, there may be cold pain in the shoulders and back, shortness of breath, insufficient respiratory capacity, and changes in urine color. For these disorders, excess is treated by draining, deficiency by supplementing, heat by rapid needling, cold by retaining the needle, and sinking by moxibustion. When neither excess nor deficiency is evident, treatment is selected according to the meridian. In excess, the cun pulse is three times greater than the renying pulse; in deficiency, the cun pulse is smaller than the renying pulse.

💡 经脉：手太阴肺经，WHO标准代码 LU。主要循行：中焦—肺—腋下—上肢内侧—拇指。分支通向食指，与手阳明大肠经相接。重要定位：寸口为腕部桡动脉搏动处；鱼际 LU10；鱼际穴附近为肺经掌侧循行区。臂厥是传统经脉病候名称，不是现代医学的独立诊断。肺胀、喘、欬与现代呼吸系统疾病症状有表面相似性，但不能据此直接诊断肺部疾病。人迎与寸口脉比较是古代脉诊方法，三倍的比例属于古典理论，不能作为现代循证诊断标准。治疗原则：盛泻、虚补、热疾、寒留、陷下灸。疾是快速进针或出针的传统操作原则，不等同于现代所有热证都应快速针刺。

大腸手陽明之脈，起於大指次指之端，循指上廉，出合谷兩骨之間，上入兩筋之中，循臂上廉，入肘外廉，上臑外前廉，上肩，出髃骨之前廉，上出於柱骨之會上，下入缺盆，絡肺，下膈，屬大腸。其支者，從缺盆上頸，貫頰，入下齒中，還出挾口，交人中，左之右，右之左，上挾鼻孔。是動則病齒痛，頸腫。是主津液所生病者，目黃，口乾，鼽衄，喉痹，肩前臑痛，大指次指痛不用。氣有餘，則當脈所過者熱腫，虛則寒慄不復。為此諸病，盛則寫之，虛則補之，熱則疾之，寒則留之，陷下則灸之，不盛不虛，以經取之。盛者人迎大三倍於寸口，虛者人迎反小於寸口也。

手阳明大肠经起于食指末端，沿食指上侧行走，经过合谷穴所在的第一、第二掌骨之间，再进入两筋之间，沿前臂外侧上行，经过肘外侧、上臂外侧前缘，到达肩部，经过肩峰骨前方，上行至颈椎骨相会之处，再向下进入缺盆，联络肺，穿过膈肌，归属于大肠。它的分支从缺盆上行至颈部，穿过面颊，进入下齿，再返回口旁，交会于人中，左侧经脉走向右侧，右侧经脉走向左侧，最后沿鼻孔两旁上行。 本经经气异常活动时，可出现牙齿疼痛和颈部肿胀。 本经所主的津液相关病证包括眼睛发黄、口干、鼻塞流涕、鼻出血、喉部痹阻疼痛、肩前部和上臂疼痛，以及食指疼痛、活动不利。 经气有余时，经脉经过的部位会出现发热、肿胀；经气虚弱时，会出现寒冷、战栗而不能恢复正常。 治疗原则是盛则泻、虚则补、热则疾、寒则留、陷下则灸；没有明显虚实时，按照本经取穴。经气盛时，人迎脉比寸口脉大三倍；经气虚时，人迎脉反而比寸口脉小。

The Large Intestine meridian of Hand Yangming begins at the tip of the index finger, follows its superior border, passes through the space between the two metacarpal bones at Hegu, and enters the space between the tendons. It ascends along the lateral forearm, passes the lateral elbow and the anterolateral upper arm, reaches the shoulder, passes anterior to the acromial region, and ascends to the meeting point of the cervical spine. It then descends into the supraclavicular fossa, connects with the Lung, passes through the diaphragm, and belongs to the Large Intestine. Its branch ascends from the supraclavicular fossa to the neck, crosses the cheek, enters the lower teeth, returns to the sides of the mouth, crosses at the philtrum, with the left pathway crossing to the right and the right pathway crossing to the left, and ascends alongside the nostrils. When the meridian's qi becomes disturbed, toothache and neck swelling may occur. Disorders associated with the body's fluids include yellowing of the eyes, dry mouth, nasal obstruction or discharge, nosebleeds, throat obstruction, pain in the anterior shoulder and upper arm, and pain or impaired function of the index finger. When qi is excessive, the areas traversed by the meridian may become hot and swollen. When qi is deficient, there may be chills and persistent coldness. Excess is treated by draining, deficiency by supplementing, heat by rapid needling, cold by needle retention, and sinking by moxibustion. When neither excess nor deficiency is evident, treatment follows the meridian. In excess, the renying pulse is three times greater than the cun pulse; in deficiency, the renying pulse is smaller than the cun pulse.

💡 经脉：手阳明大肠经，WHO代码 LI。循行由食指至上肢外侧、肩、颈、面部和鼻旁。重要穴位：合谷 LI4，位于手背第一、第二掌骨之间；迎香 LI20，位于鼻翼外缘中点旁；曲池 LI11，位于肘横纹外侧端。原文提及合谷所在位置，并非直接列出穴名。合谷是常用针灸穴位，传统上用于头面部疼痛等病证；现代研究对针刺疼痛调节有一定研究，但不能据此认定其能治疗所有牙病、鼻病或喉部疾病。津液是中医对体液生成、输布和代谢的功能性概念。气有余的局部热肿与炎症性表现有部分症状相似性，不能直接画等号。

胃足陽明之脈，起於鼻，之交頞中，旁納太陽之脈，下循鼻外，入上齒中，還出挾口，環唇，下交承漿，卻循頤後下廉，出大迎，循頰車，上耳前，過客主人，循髮際，至額顱。其支者，從大迎前下人迎，循喉嚨，入缺盆，下膈，屬胃，絡脾。其直者，從缺盆下乳內廉，下挾臍，入氣街中。其支者，起於胃口，下循腹裏，下至氣街中而合，以下髀關，抵伏兔，下膝臏中，下循脛外廉，下足跗，入中指內間。其支者，下廉三寸而別，下入中指外間。其支者，別跗上，入大指間，出其端。是動則病洒洒振寒，善呻數欠，顏黑，病至則惡人與火，聞木聲則惕然而驚，心欲動，獨閉戶塞牖而處，甚則欲上高而歌，棄衣而走，賁響腹脹，是為骭厥。是主血所生病者，狂瘧溫淫，汗出鼽衄，口喎唇胗，頸腫喉痹，大腹水腫，膝臏腫痛，循膺乳、氣街、股、伏兔、骭外廉、足跗上皆痛，中指不用。氣盛則身以前皆熱，其有餘於胃，則消穀善飢，溺色黃，氣不足則身以前皆寒栗，胃中寒則脹滿，為此諸病，盛則寫之，虛則補之，熱則疾之，寒則留之，陷下則灸之，不盛不虛，以經取之。盛者人迎大三倍於寸口，虛者人迎反小於寸口也。

足阳明胃经起于鼻部，在鼻根部交会，旁边接纳足太阳经的脉气，沿鼻外侧下行，进入上齿，再返回面颊两旁，环绕嘴唇，向下交会于承浆穴。随后沿下颌后下缘行走，出于大迎穴，沿颊车部上行，经过耳前的客主人穴，沿发际到达额头。 它的一条分支从大迎穴前方下行至人迎，沿喉咙进入缺盆，向下穿过膈肌，归属于胃，联络脾。另一条直行支脉从缺盆向下，沿乳房内侧下行，经脐旁进入气街部位。另一分支从胃的入口开始，沿腹腔内部向下，到达气街与前述支脉会合，再向下经髀关，抵达伏兔穴，经过膝盖中央，沿小腿外侧下行，经过足背，进入第三趾内侧的趾缝。它的一条分支在小腿外侧下方三寸处分出，进入第三趾外侧的趾缝。还有一条分支从足背分出，进入大趾与第二趾之间，最后到达大趾末端。 本经经气异常活动时，可出现阵阵寒冷、战栗、经常呻吟和频繁打哈欠、面色晦暗。疾病发作时，患者可能厌恶见人和火光，听到木器敲击声便惊恐不安，心中悸动，喜欢独自关门闭窗待在室内。病情严重时，可能登高唱歌、脱掉衣服奔跑，腹中有鸣响并胀满，这种情况称为骭厥。 本经所主的血分病证包括狂病、疟疾、温热类疾病、出汗、鼻塞流涕和鼻出血、口角歪斜、唇部疮疹、颈部肿胀、喉痹、腹部高度胀大及水肿、膝盖肿痛，以及胸部、乳房、气街、大腿、伏兔部位、小腿外侧和足背疼痛；第三趾也可能活动不利。 胃经之气亢盛时，身体前面各部位都会发热；胃中有余时，消化食物过快而容易饥饿，小便颜色发黄。胃经之气不足时，身体前面各部位会发冷、战栗；胃中寒冷时，会出现胀满。 治疗原则是盛则泻、虚则补、热则疾、寒则留、陷下则灸；没有明显虚实时，按照本经取穴。经气盛时，人迎脉比寸口脉大三倍；经气虚时，人迎脉反而比寸口脉小。

The Stomach meridian of Foot Yangming begins at the nose, meets at the root of the nose, and receives the pathway of the Taiyang meridian alongside it. It descends along the outer nose, enters the upper teeth, returns along both sides of the mouth, encircles the lips, and descends to meet at Chengjiang. It then follows the lower border of the jaw, emerges at Daying, follows Jiache, ascends anterior to the ear, passes the point known as Kerenzhu, follows the hairline, and reaches the forehead. One branch descends from the anterior region of Daying to Renying, follows the throat, enters the supraclavicular fossa, passes through the diaphragm, belongs to the Stomach, and connects with the Spleen. Its direct pathway descends from the supraclavicular fossa along the medial side of the breast, passes beside the umbilicus, and enters the Qijie region. Another branch begins at the entrance of the stomach, descends through the abdomen, and joins the pathway at Qijie. It then descends through Biguan, reaches Futu, passes through the center of the kneecap, follows the lateral lower leg, travels over the dorsum of the foot, and enters the space on the medial side of the third toe. One branch separates three cun below the knee and enters the space on the lateral side of the third toe. Another branch separates on the dorsum of the foot, enters the space between the great toe and second toe, and emerges at the tip of the great toe. When the meridian's qi becomes disturbed, symptoms may include recurrent chills, shivering, frequent groaning and yawning, and a darkened complexion. During an attack, the person may dislike people and firelight, become startled by the sound of wood, experience agitation and palpitations, and prefer to remain alone with doors and windows closed. In severe cases, the person may climb to high places and sing, discard clothing and run about, and experience abdominal rumbling and distension. This is called leg-bone reversal. Disorders associated with the blood include mania, malaria, febrile disorders, sweating, nasal discharge and nosebleeds, deviation of the mouth, sores of the lips, neck swelling, throat obstruction, marked abdominal distension, edema, swelling and pain of the kneecap, and pain along the chest, breasts, Qijie region, thighs, Futu region, lateral lower leg, and dorsum of the foot. The third toe may also become functionally impaired. When Stomach qi is excessive, the entire anterior aspect of the body becomes hot. Excess within the Stomach may cause rapid digestion and excessive hunger, with yellow urine. When qi is deficient, the anterior aspect of the body may become cold and shivery. Cold within the Stomach causes distension and fullness. Excess is treated by draining, deficiency by supplementing, heat by rapid needling, cold by retaining the needle, and sinking by moxibustion.
