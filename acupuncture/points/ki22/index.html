Walking Corridor · High risk高危

Point of the Kidney channel足少阴肾经穴
⚠️ Pneumothorax-risk point气胸风险穴 The pleura and lung lie directly beneath Bùláng, very shallow in thin people. Use only 0.5–0.8 cun oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space; never perpendicular or deep, never medially. Chest pain, breathlessness, worsening pain on breathing in or persistent cough after needling needs urgent care (in BC call 911).步廊深面就是胸膜和肺，胸壁薄者尤其浅。只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不可直刺、深刺，不可向内侧进针。针后胸痛、气短、吸气痛加重或持续咳嗽，立即就医（BC 拨 911）。

📍 Location定位★

On the chest, in the 5th intercostal space, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 5 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

💡 Count down the intercostal spaces from the sternal angle (level of the 2nd rib) to the 5th space, then go 2 cun lateral to the midline (half the 4 cun to the midclavicular line); do not use the nipple in women.从胸骨角（平第 2 肋）向下数肋间隙到第 5 肋间隙，再从前正中线向外量 2 寸（锁骨中线距中线 4 寸的一半）；女性不用乳头定位。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse, laterally along the intercostal space斜刺或平刺（沿肋间隙向外） · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never perpendicular or deep, to avoid the heart and lungs.不可直刺、深刺，以免伤及心、肺。

🛡 Safety安全

The pleura and lung lie directly beneath the intercostal muscles: oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space only, never perpendicular or deep; do not insert hugging the lower border of the rib above (intercostal vessels and nerve).肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺：只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺，不直刺、不深刺；不贴上位肋骨下缘进针（肋间血管神经）。

    Anatomy解剖结构

    At the fifth intercostal space 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline, superficial thoracoabdominal veins lie subcutaneously, with pectoralis major and external and internal intercostal muscles beneath; the intercostal vessels and nerve run along the inferior border of the fifth rib, and parietal pleura and lung lie directly deep. Insert obliquely or transversely laterally with the tip along the chest wall and away from the thorax; never needle deeply or medially.在前正中线旁开 2 寸、第 5 肋间隙处，皮下有胸腹壁浅静脉，深面依次为胸大肌、肋间外肌与肋间内肌；第 5 肋骨下缘有肋间血管和肋间神经走行，肌层深面即为壁胸膜与肺。进针必须向外斜刺或平刺，针尖沿胸壁走行、远离胸腔，严禁向内侧或深部直刺。

  4. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    For 2 cun lateral to the midline use the patient's index, middle and ring fingers together (about 2 cun), or half the distance to the midclavicular line (4 cun); the vertical level comes only from counting intercostal spaces, never from finger measurement.前正中线旁开 2 寸可用患者食指、中指、无名指三指并拢（约 2 寸）自前正中线向外量取，或取锁骨中线（距中线 4 寸）的一半；纵向高度只看肋间隙，不靠指量。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently outward along the fifth intercostal space from the parasternal region to feel a wider soft-tissue interval near the lateral costal cartilage margin, often with soreness or tenderness; keep palpation gentle and avoid heavy pressure on the chest wall.沿第 5 肋间隙自胸骨旁向外轻触，在肋软骨外侧缘附近可触及较宽的软组织间隙，局部常有酸胀或压痛；触诊及按压力度宜轻柔，避免重压胸壁。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Mistaking the fourth or sixth intercostal space: counting without starting at the sternal angle or being misled by breast contour shifts the level up or down, where improper needle angle still risks injuring the pleura and lung.误取第 4 肋间隙或第 6 肋间隙：数肋骨时若未从胸骨角起算或受乳房形态干扰，易错数肋间，导致偏上或偏下，进针角度不当同样有伤及胸膜与肺的风险。
  • Using nipple level instead of counting ribs: the nipple approximates the fourth intercostal space in men, whereas breast position and size vary widely in women, causing location errors and confusion with adjacent points.误取乳头外侧或仅按乳头水平定第 4 肋间隙：男性乳头通常平第 4 肋间隙，女性乳房位置与大小个体差异大，不可用乳头代替肋间数法，否则定位偏差明显且易误入邻近经穴。
  • Needling deeply or medially: parietal pleura and lung lie directly deep to the intercostals, so deep or medial insertion risks pneumothorax, while hugging the inferior border of the fifth rib risks injuring the intercostal vessels and nerve.向内侧或深部直刺：肋间肌深面紧邻壁胸膜与肺，向内深刺极易刺伤胸膜引发气胸，贴第 5 肋骨下缘进针则易损伤肋间血管和肋间神经。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

Suited as a supporting point when cough comes with anterior chest tightness at the fifth intercostal space and reversed qi, rather than a lead point for early external wind or chronic deficient cough; pair with BL13 or LU7.偏于前胸第 5 肋间隙局部胸闷气逆伴咳嗽时可配伍宣通，不作为外感表证或慢性虚咳的首选主穴，宜配肺俞、列缺等穴。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Used to help free chest qi when wheezing is accompanied by chest fullness and shortness of breath; for acute or pronounced wheezing, evaluate clinical severity first and pair with EX-B1 or BL13 rather than replacing acute bronchodilator care.用于气喘伴胸胁满闷、呼吸气短时协同宽胸理气，急性发作或喘憋明显时须先评估病情并配定喘、肺俞等穴，不替代平喘急救。

Hiccups呃逆

Classically noted for diaphragmatic qi obstruction as a secondary reference, though clinical hiccup management typically leads with PC6, CV12, ST36, or BL17 rather than KI22.古籍载膈逆不通可作辅助调理思路，但临床治呃逆常首选内关、中脘、足三里或膈俞等穴，步廊较少作为呃逆首选。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「步廊，在神封下一寸六分陷者中，足少陰脈氣所發，仰而取之，刺入四分，灸五壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing places Bulang (KI22) in the depression 1.6 cun below Shenfeng (KI23) as a site where Kidney channel qi emerges, taken supine; while the classic notes needling 0.4 cun and moxibustion with five cones, modern textbooks restrict depth to 0.5–0.8 cun with lateral oblique or transverse insertion to protect the chest wall.《针灸甲乙经》明确步廊位于神封下一寸六分凹陷中，属足少阴肾经脉气所发，采用仰卧位取穴，古籍记载刺四分、灸五壮；现代临床与教材严格控制深度（0.5–0.8 寸）并采用向外斜刺或平刺以保障胸壁安全。

「胸脅 滿，鬲逆不通，呼吸少氣，喘息不得舉臂，步廊主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing indicates Bulang (KI22) for fullness of the chest and hypochondrium, reversed diaphragm qi obstruction, shortness of breath, and wheezing with inability to raise the arm, reflecting its traditional role in freeing chest-diaphragm qi and easing chest fullness and wheezing.《针灸甲乙经》概括步廊主治胸胁胀满、膈气上逆不通、呼吸气短少气、喘息甚至难以抬臂，体现了前胸足少阴经穴宣通胸膈气机、缓解胸满气喘的传统主治特点。

「步廊 神封下一寸六分陷中，去胸中行各二寸，仰而取之」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Daceng specifies that Bulang (KI22) lies in the depression 1.6 cun below Shenfeng (KI23) and 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline, taken supine, matching the modern standard location.《针灸大成》补充说明步廊在神封下一寸六分凹陷中，距前胸正中线各 2 寸，仰卧位取穴，与现代标准定位高度一致。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Costochondritis or intercostal neuralgia肋软骨炎或肋间神经痛

Presents as localized sharp or aching anterior chest pain that may worsen with deep breath, cough, or trunk rotation, with tenderness near the costochondral junction or intercostal space; acute coronary ischemia, pulmonary embolism, pneumothorax, and prodromal herpes zoster must be ruled out first, and radiating chest pain or dyspnea requires immediate medical evaluation.表现为前胸局部刺痛或酸痛，深呼吸、咳嗽或转身时可加重，肋软骨交界处或肋间隙可有压痛；需先排除急性冠脉缺血、肺栓塞、气胸及带状疱疹前期等器质性急症，若伴胸痛放射或呼吸困难须立即就医。

Chest-wall muscle strain and local soft-tissue discomfort胸壁肌肉劳损与局部软组织不适

Often follows forceful coughing, heavy lifting, or sustained poor posture with local aching that changes with trunk stretch or palpation; after trauma, evaluate for rib fracture with imaging before local needling or pressure.多与剧烈咳嗽、搬重物或长期姿势不良相关，局部酸胀明显，随躯干牵拉或按压而变；外伤后若出现明显压痛、肿胀或骨擦感，应先拍片排查肋骨骨折。

Chronic cough or wheezing with anterior chest tightness慢性咳嗽或气喘伴前胸闷胀

May accompany bronchitis, asthma, or gastroesophageal reflux-related discomfort with cough, wheezing, or chest tightness; new-onset wheezing, persistent cough, or nocturnal dyspnea requires medical diagnosis and guideline treatment.可见于支气管炎、哮喘或胃食管反流相关不适，常伴咳嗽、气喘或胸闷；新发喘息、持续咳嗽或夜间憋醒应先由医生评估病因并规范用药。

Where is KI22 Bùláng?步廊（KI22）在哪里？

Main safety point at KI22?步廊（KI22）最要注意的安全问题？
