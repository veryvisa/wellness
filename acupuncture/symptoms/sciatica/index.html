Sciatica坐骨神经痛

The textbook sorts sciatica by channel first and pattern second. Pain down the back of the buttock, thigh and calf is the Foot Taiyang type (BL54, BL40, BL57, BL60); pain down the outer side is the Foot Shaoyang type (GB30, GB34, GB39, GB40). Both add lumbar Jiaji (extra points with no code here) and ashi points, then points for cold-damp, blood stasis or qi and blood deficiency. The sciatic nerve lies right beneath GB30 and BL54, so stop as soon as a radiating sensation appears and do not keep lifting and thrusting. Ask three things before starting: bladder, bowel and saddle sensation; any growing weakness of the leg or foot; any fever or cancer history.坐骨神经痛的教材处方先分经络、后分证候：痛沿臀和大腿、小腿后侧走的是足太阳经证，取秩边、委中、承山、昆仑；沿臀和大腿、小腿外侧走的是足少阳经证，取环跳、阳陵泉、悬钟、丘墟；两组都加腰夹脊（经外奇穴，本站无代码）和阿是穴，再按寒湿、瘀血、气血不足加穴。环跳、秩边深部就是坐骨神经，有放射感即止，不反复提插。开始之前先问三件事：大小便和会阴感觉、腿脚有没有越来越没力、有没有发热或肿瘤史。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Foot drop, inability to stand on toes or heels, or leg weakness worsening over days: same-day emergency assessment足下垂、脚尖或脚跟站不起来、腿部无力在数天内加重：当天急诊评估
  • With fever or chills, IV drug use or weak immunity, or a cancer history, unexplained weight loss or night pain at rest: same-day care for spinal infection or metastasis伴发热寒战、静脉用药、免疫低下，或有癌症史、不明原因消瘦、夜间静息痛不缓解：当天就医，排除脊柱感染和转移瘤
  • Back and leg pain after major trauma, or after a minor fall with osteoporosis or long-term steroids: emergency imaging严重外伤后，或骨质疏松、长期服激素者轻微跌倒后出现腰腿痛：急诊拍片
  • One calf swollen, warm and painful, or a cold pale leg with no pulse on the foot: emergency care today for venous clot or arterial blockage; sudden tearing back or abdominal pain with dizziness: call 911一侧小腿肿、热、痛，或腿发凉发白、足背摸不到脉搏：当天急诊，排除静脉血栓和动脉缺血；突发撕裂样腰腹痛伴头晕：拨 911

「髀不可以曲，膕如結，踹如裂，是為踝厥」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.10 on the Foot Taiyang: the thigh cannot bend, the back of the knee feels tied up and the calf as if splitting; this is called ankle reversal. Pain through the back of the thigh, the popliteal fossa and calf is exactly the Foot Taiyang path.《灵枢·经脉》足太阳经病候：大腿不能弯曲，腘窝像被捆住，小腿肚像要裂开，叫踝厥。痛在大腿后、腘窝、小腿肚，正是足太阳经证的路线。

「髀痺引膝股外廉痛，不仁，筋急，陽陵泉主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi jing: hip pain drawing down the outer knee and thigh with numbness and tight sinews, GB34 governs it — the source of GB34 for the Foot Shaoyang type with outer leg pain.《针灸甲乙经》：髋部痹痛牵引膝和大腿外侧痛、麻木、筋脉拘急，以阳陵泉主治。痛在腿外侧，正是足少阳经证取阳陵泉的来源。

「邪客於足少陽之絡，令人留於樞中痛，髀不可舉」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.63: a pathogen lodged in the Foot Shaoyang collateral leaves pain in the hip so that the thigh cannot be raised; the text goes on to needle the hip joint area with a fine needle, the region of GB30.《素问·缪刺论》：邪气停在足少阳的络脉，使人髋部（髀枢）疼痛留着不去，大腿抬不起来；下文说「刺枢中以毫针」，即刺环跳一带。

Foot Taiyang type足太阳经证

How it presents辨证要点 Pain from the back and buttock down the back of the thigh and calf to the heel or outer foot, worse on bending, coughing or straight-leg raise (often the S1 root).痛从腰臀沿大腿后侧、小腿后侧放射到足跟或足外侧，弯腰、咳嗽、直腿抬高时加重（常与骶 1 神经根相应）。

Principle治法 Free the Foot Taiyang channel, stop pain (drain)疏通足太阳经，通络止痛（泻法）

Main主穴BL54 Zhìbiān秩边BL40 Weizhong委中BL57 Chéngshān承山BL60 Kūnlún昆仑
Add配穴BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞BL36 Chengfu承扶

💡 Textbook Foot Taiyang set: lumbar Jiaji (no code), BL54, BL40, BL57, BL60 and ashi points, following the channel from buttock to foot. Add BL25 for marked low-back pain, at the L4–5 level where disc herniation is common, and BL36 for pain at the gluteal fold. BL60 and lumbosacral points are not used in pregnancy.教材足太阳经证：腰夹脊（无代码）、秩边、委中、承山、昆仑、阿是穴，从臀到足循经取穴。下腰痛明显加大肠俞，正当腰椎间盘突出常见的第 4、5 腰椎水平；痛在臀横纹加承扶。孕妇不取昆仑和腰骶部穴。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Sciatica names a symptom. The commonest cause is a lumbar disc herniation pressing on a root: L5 pain runs to the outer calf and top of the foot with a weak big-toe lift; S1 pain to the back of the calf and outer foot with weak tiptoe and a reduced ankle reflex; the straight-leg raise is positive. An older person whose legs ache and go numb after walking and ease on sitting or bending usually has lumbar spinal stenosis, to be told apart from arterial calf pain that eases simply on standing still. Hip disease, sacroiliac pain, deep gluteal (piriformis) syndrome and early shingles are other possibilities. Leg pain from a disc herniation mostly improves markedly in 6–12 weeks, needs no early imaging without red flags, and does better with activity than bed rest. Acupuncture can help with pain as an adjunct; pain beyond six weeks or falling strength needs referral.坐骨神经痛是症状名。最常见的是腰椎间盘突出压迫神经根：腰 5 根痛到小腿外侧和足背、大脚趾背伸无力；骶 1 根痛到小腿后和足外侧、踮脚无力、跟腱反射减弱；直腿抬高试验阳性。老年人走一段路就腿痛麻、坐下或弯腰缓解，多为腰椎管狭窄，要与走路小腿痛、站着就缓解的动脉缺血（间歇性跛行）区分。还要鉴别髋关节病、骶髂关节痛、深臀综合征（梨状肌综合征）、带状疱疹早期。多数腰椎间盘突出的腿痛在 6–12 周内明显减轻，没有红旗时早期不需要影像；保持活动优于卧床。针刺可作减痛辅助，持续 6 周以上不缓解或肌力下降须转诊。
