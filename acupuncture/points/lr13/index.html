LR13

章门 Zhāngmén

Completion Gate · High risk高危

★Front-mu point of the Spleen脾之募穴Influential point of the zang organs八会穴之脏会Crossing point of the Liver and Gallbladder channels足厥阴、足少阳经交会穴
⚠️ Liver and spleen injury risk point肝脾损伤风险穴 LR13 sits below the free end of the 11th rib with the liver deep on the right and the spleen on the left, closer still when enlarged. Insert obliquely 0.5–0.8 cun only, with no deep perpendicular insertion or wide lifting-thrusting; never needle deeply over an enlarged liver or spleen. Worsening abdominal pain, dizziness, pallor or left-shoulder pain (signs of splenic injury) after needling needs emergency care.章门在第 11 肋游离端下际，深面右为肝、左为脾，肝脾肿大时更近。只可斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不深直刺、不大幅提插；肝脾肿大者禁深刺。针后持续加重的腹痛、头晕、面色苍白或左肩痛（脾损伤征象），立即急诊。

📍 Location定位★

On the lateral abdomen, just inferior to the free extremity of the 11th rib.侧腹部，在第 11 肋游离端的下际。

💡 Lie on the side with the arm raised and follow the costal margin back to the free end of the 11th rib (roughly where the tip of the flexed elbow rests against the flank); the point is at its lower edge.侧卧举臂，沿肋弓向外后摸到第 11 肋游离端（屈肘贴胁时肘尖所及处约在此），在其下缘取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique斜刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Some textbooks give perpendicular 0.8–1 cun; with an enlarged liver or spleen, or in thin people, use only shallow oblique insertion. Use caution in pregnancy.教材亦作直刺 0.8–1 寸；肝脾肿大、消瘦者只可浅斜刺。孕期慎针。

🛡 Safety安全

The liver lies deep on the right and the spleen on the left, and an enlarged organ can reach beneath the point: use shallow oblique insertion only, no deep perpendicular insertion or wide lifting-thrusting, and never deep needling over an enlarged liver or spleen.深面右为肝、左为脾，肝脾肿大时脏器下缘可达穴下：只作浅斜刺，不深直刺、不大幅提插；肝脾肿大者禁深刺。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Abdominal pain and distension, borborygmus, diarrhoea, vomiting腹痛、腹胀、肠鸣、泄泻、呕吐 ⚖️Hypochondriac pain, jaundice, abdominal masses (enlarged liver or spleen)胁痛、黄疸、痞块（肝脾肿大）

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Mistaking the 12th rib or the superior margin/intercostal space for the 11th rib tip: shifting superiorly or posteriorly risks contacting ribs or intercostal neurovascular bundles, while shifting inferiorly lowers the point away from the 11th rib tip landmark.误取第 12 肋或肋弓上缘/肋间隙：偏上或偏后易触及肋骨或肋间血管神经，偏下则位置过低，偏离脾募与肋端下缘定位。
  • Deep or perpendicular insertion (especially in thin people or with an enlarged liver or spleen): the wall is thin and the needle can pass the peritoneum into the spleen (left) or liver and colon (right), causing internal bleeding; insert obliquely 0.5–0.8 cun only, never deep perpendicular.深刺或直刺（尤其瘦人或肝脾肿大者）：腹壁较薄，深刺可穿透腹膜伤及左侧脾脏或右侧肝脏、结肠，引起内出血；只可斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸，切忌深直刺。
  • Treating lateral abdominal myofascial trigger points or intercostal neuralgia with deep needling without clinical screening and red-flag checks can mask organic intra-abdominal or thoracic disease.误将侧腹壁深层肌筋膜激痛点或肋间神经痛当作单纯常规深刺位：未结合体格检查与红旗征排查急腹症，可能掩盖器质性病变。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Consider LR13 with CV12 and ST36 for lateral or mid-to-lower abdominal distending pain with poor appetite or bloating; do not rely on acupuncture for acute abdomen, severe tenderness, or rebound pain (seek emergency care first), and compared with lower-abdomen or local points, LR13 emphasizes zang-organ and Spleen front-mu middle-jiao regulation.侧腹或中下腹气滞胀痛、伴纳差腹胀时可选章门配中脘、足三里调理气机；急腹症、剧烈压痛或反跳痛不选针灸，须先急诊就医，且与以疏理下焦或局部痛点为主的穴位相比侧重脏会与脾募调中。

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Useful as a supportive point when epigastric fullness coexists with Spleen-deficiency poor transformation, low appetite, or loose stools; not a first-line standalone choice for acute severe epigastric pain, hematemesis, melena, or high fever, and typically paired with CV12, ST36, and PC6 rather than used alone in place of primary Stomach points.胃脘胀满伴脾虚运化不健、食少便溏时可作配伍穴协助调中；胃痛暴作、呕血黑便或伴高热时不作首选，临床常与中脘、足三里、内关配合，而非单用章门替代胃经核心穴。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Suitable alongside BL20 and ST36 for Spleen-deficiency diarrhea with bloating, poor appetite, and fatigue; acute infectious diarrhea with high fever, bloody or purulent stools, or dehydration requires medical evaluation and rehydration first, differing from combinations focused on acute cold-damp or damp-heat presentations.脾虚泄泻伴腹胀、纳呆、倦怠时可配脾俞、足三里健脾止泻；急性感染性腹泻伴高热、脓血便或明显脱水须先就医补液与诊治，区别于侧重寒湿或湿热急象的其他穴位组合。

Where is LR13 Zhāngmén?章门（LR13）在哪里？

On the lateral abdomen, just inferior to the free extremity of the 11th rib.侧腹部，在第 11 肋游离端的下际。

How is LR13 needled?章门（LR13）怎么刺？

Oblique; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of LR13?章门（LR13）是哪类特定穴？

Front-mu point of the Spleen; Influential point of the zang organs; Crossing point of the Liver and Gallbladder channels脾之募穴；八会穴之脏会；足厥阴、足少阳经交会穴

Main safety point at LR13?章门（LR13）最要注意的安全问题？

The liver lies deep on the right and the spleen on the left, and an enlarged organ can reach beneath the point: use shallow oblique insertion only, no deep perpendicular insertion or wide lifting-thrusting, and never deep needling over an enlarged liver or spleen.深面右为肝、左为脾，肝脾肿大时脏器下缘可达穴下：只作浅斜刺，不深直刺、不大幅提插；肝脾肿大者禁深刺。
