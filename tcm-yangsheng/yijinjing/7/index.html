✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ Twist拧转

  • Neck problems or dizziness on turning the head: do not look at the heel; let the head turn only a little with the body颈椎病、转头就晕：不看脚跟，头随身体小幅转动即可
  • Frozen shoulder: leave the back hand on the waist without pushing up肩周炎：背后的手放在腰上，不向上推
  • Osteoporosis: do only the standing turn and opening, never twisting while rounding and bending骨质疏松：只做站立的转身展臂，不在含胸弯腰的同时拧转

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Turn to the right; raise the right hand in front, bend the elbow over and behind the head, and rest the palm lightly over the left ear; swing the left hand down and round behind the back, the back of the hand against the spine, pushing upward身体右转，右手经体前上举，屈肘绕到头后，手掌轻按住左耳；左手经体侧下摆到背后，手背贴在脊柱上，向上推
  2. Turn right, open the arms and chest, and look up to the right身体右转、展臂扩胸，目视右上方
  3. Bend the knees and turn the upper body to the left, bringing the right arm in and rounding the chest; push the left hand a little further up the spine; look at the right heel屈膝，上身左转，右臂向内合、含胸，左手沿脊柱再向上推一点，目视右脚跟
  4. Straighten the knees, face forward and open the chest; close and open like this 3 times, then switch to left hand up, right hand down for 3 more直膝，身体转正，展臂扩胸；如此合、开共 3 次，然后换左手上、右手下做 3 次

🌬 Breathe in as the chest opens, out as it closes.展臂扩胸时吸气，合臂含胸时呼气。

✅ Key points要领

  • The hand on the head rests lightly and never pulls the neck down抱头的手只是轻按，不往下压脖子
  • Turn and round the chest slowly, stopping at a comfortable angle转身和含胸要慢，转到舒服的角度就停
  • The hand behind the back goes only as high as is pain-free背后的手只推到不痛的高度

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Pulling the head down with the hand用手把头往下压
  • Twisting too hard or dropping the head too low转身过猛、低头太深
  • Hunching the shoulders耸肩

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Skip the knee bend and the look at the heel; do only the standing turn and chest opening; or sit and rest the raised hand on top of the head.不屈膝、不看脚跟，只做站立的转身展臂；或者坐着，抱头的手放在头顶即可。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Nine ghosts stands for great strength, and drawing the sabre is the pose of pulling a blade from behind the back: one hand over the head, the other against the back. In the late-Qing text we checked, the form is called seven ghosts; its verse speaks of turning the head and bending the arm to hold the crown and neck, alternating left and right with the body upright and the breath quiet.「九鬼」形容力大，「拔马刀」是从背后拔刀的姿态，一手抱头、一手贴背，正是拔刀的样子。本机清末底本里这一式写作「七鬼拔马刀势」，歌诀：「侧首弯肱，抱顶及颈；自头收回，弗嫌力猛；左右相轮，身直气静。」
