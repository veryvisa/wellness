📍 Location定位★

On the medial knee, at the medial end of the popliteal crease, in the depression medial to the tendon of semitendinosus.膝部，腘横纹内侧端，半腱肌肌腱内缘凹陷中。

Posture体位 · Supine with the knee flexed to about 90° (foot flat on the table or a bolster under the knee), or seated with the knee flexed, exposing the medial knee.仰卧位，患侧膝关节屈曲约 90°（足底平放床面或膝下垫枕），或正坐屈膝位，暴露膝内侧。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    With the knee flexed to 90°, the popliteal crease appears; at its medial end feel the taut semitendinosus tendon—the point is in the hollow at its front (medial) edge, level with the medial end of the crease (national standard).屈膝 90° 时，膝内侧后方出现腘横纹；在腘横纹内侧端摸到绷起的半腱肌腱，穴在腱的前缘（内缘）凹陷中，与横纹内侧端平齐（国标）。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Have the patient flex the knee and press the heel lightly against resistance: the semitendinosus (superficial, thin, round) and semimembranosus (deep, broad—the classical 'great sinew') tendons stand out at the back of the medial knee. The national standard places LR8 at the medial end of the popliteal crease, in the hollow medial to the semitendinosus tendon; the older wording 'in front of the semitendinosus and semimembranosus insertions, above the medial end of the knee crease' describes the same hollow.   3. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Greater trochanter to popliteal crease (level with apex of patella) = 19 cun.   4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Superior to the medial end of the popliteal crease with the knee flexed, press the thumb pad into the narrow cleft between the anterior edge of the semitendinosus tendon and the medial femoral epicondyle to feel a distinct hollow and deep dull ache. ⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Locating with the knee extended: when straight, the semitendinosus and semimembranosus tendons relax against surrounding tissues and the popliteal crease vanishes, making the hollow hard to define and raising the risk of hitting fascia or superficial veins.伸膝取穴：伸直膝关节时半腱肌、半膜肌腱松弛且与周围软组织贴紧，腘横纹消失，极易找不准凹陷并误入周围筋膜或浅静脉。
  • Drifting posteriorly or angling posterolaterally toward the popliteal centre: crossing behind the hamstring tendons enters the popliteal space, where deep insertion risks injuring the tibial nerve or popliteal vessels.取穴偏后或针尖向后外侧（腘窝中央）斜刺：越过半腱肌、半膜肌腱后缘即进入腘窝区域，深刺可能损伤胫神经或腘动静脉。
  • Drifting inferiorly below the medial tibial condyle into the SP9 (Yinlingquan) area: LR8 sits above the medial popliteal crease end (femoral side), whereas SP9 lies in the depression under the inferior border of the medial tibial condyle — roughly 2–3 cun apart, often confused when located without knee flexion.取穴偏下至胫骨内侧髁下方（阴陵泉 SP9 区域）：曲泉在膝关节横纹内侧端上方（股骨侧），阴陵泉在胫骨内侧髁下缘凹陷中，二者相距约 2–3 寸，临床常因未屈膝定位而混淆。
  • Failing to avoid superficial great saphenous vein tributaries: the medial knee has dense superficial veins; piercing one causes local subcutaneous hematoma and bruising.未避开皮下大隐静脉属支：膝内侧浅静脉丰富，直刺穿破易引起局部皮下血肿与青紫。

Knee pain膝痛 ⚖️

Irregular periods月经不调

Where is LR8 Qūquán?曲泉（LR8）在哪里？

On the medial knee, at the medial end of the popliteal crease, in the depression medial to the tendon of semitendinosus.膝部，腘横纹内侧端，半腱肌肌腱内缘凹陷中。

How is LR8 needled?曲泉（LR8）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of LR8?曲泉（LR8）是哪类特定穴？

He-sea (water)合穴（水）

Main safety point at LR8?曲泉（LR8）最要注意的安全问题？

Locate with the knee flexed; do not aim toward the centre of the popliteal fossa (popliteal vessels and tibial nerve), and avoid the great saphenous vein.屈膝取穴；针不朝腘窝中央，以免接近腘动静脉与胫神经；避开大隐静脉。
