BL10

天柱 Tiānzhù

Celestial Pillar · High risk高危

⚠️ High-risk anatomy上颈部深层神经血管风险 BL10 must not be deeply directed inferomedially. Keep the tip away from the midline and deep cervical vascular region; stop and assess immediately for dizziness, severe pain, or neurologic symptoms.天柱不应向内下方深刺。针尖须离开中线和颈深部血管区；出现眩晕、剧痛或神经症状应立即停针并评估。

📍 Location定位★

Posterior neck, level with the upper border of the C2 spinous process, in the depression at the lateral border of trapezius (about 1.3 cun lateral to the midpoint of the posterior hairline).颈后区，横平第 2 颈椎棘突上际，斜方肌外缘凹陷中（约后发际正中旁开 1.3 寸）。

💡 Flex the head and find the first prominent spinous process below the occiput (C2); at its upper border move laterally to the hollow at trapezius' lateral edge, about 1.3 cun from GV15.低头摸到枕骨下第一个明显棘突（C2），平其上缘向外到斜方肌外缘的凹陷；哑门旁开约 1.3 寸。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular or oblique直刺或斜刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never insert deeply superomedially: aiming at the foramen magnum risks the medulla; the suboccipital triangle and vertebral artery lie deep and medial.不可向内上方深刺：针尖朝枕骨大孔可伤延髓；深部内侧为枕下三角与椎动脉。

🛡 Safety安全★

Deep and medial lie the third part of the vertebral artery, the foramen magnum and medulla; never needle deeply upward-medially. Withdraw and seek care for severe pain, vertigo, nausea or limb numbness.深部内侧有椎动脉第三段与枕骨大孔、延髓；严禁向内上方深刺。针下出现剧痛、眩晕、恶心或肢体麻木立即出针并就医。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Occipital headache, stiff neck, shoulder, back and low-back pain后头痛、项强、肩背腰痛 ⚖️Nasal obstruction鼻塞 ⚖️Mania and epilepsy癫狂痫Febrile disease热病

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Appropriate for occipital headache, cervicogenic headache, or headaches starting with neck pain (often paired with GB20, LI4, and BL60); migraine or internal headaches often use points such as GB20, EX-HN5, LR3, or GV20 per pattern, while new severe headache or neurologic signs require prompt medical care.后头痛、颈源性头痛或外感/厥逆头痛先见项痛者可选用（常配 GB20、LI4、BL60）；偏头痛或内伤头痛常按证型配风池、太阳、太冲、百会等穴，若出现新发剧烈头痛或神经体征则先就医。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Where is BL10 Tiānzhù?天柱（BL10）在哪里？

Posterior neck, level with the upper border of the C2 spinous process, in the depression at the lateral border of trapezius (about 1.3 cun lateral to the midpoint of the posterior hairline).颈后区，横平第 2 颈椎棘突上际，斜方肌外缘凹陷中（约后发际正中旁开 1.3 寸）。

How is BL10 needled?天柱（BL10）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at BL10?天柱（BL10）最要注意的安全问题？

Deep and medial lie the third part of the vertebral artery, the foramen magnum and medulla; never needle deeply upward-medially. Withdraw and seek care for severe pain, vertigo, nausea or limb numbness.深部内侧有椎动脉第三段与枕骨大孔、延髓；严禁向内上方深刺。针下出现剧痛、眩晕、恶心或肢体麻木立即出针并就医。
