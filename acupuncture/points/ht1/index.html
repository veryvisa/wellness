Posture体位 · Seat the patient or place them supine with the axilla exposed. Abduct the upper arm approximately 60–90 degrees, flex the elbow comfortably, and keep the forearm relaxed with the palm facing upward or forward. Avoid excessive abduction, external rotation, or sustained muscular effort, which can tighten the axillary tissues and alter the position of the neurovascular bundle.坐位或仰卧位，充分暴露腋窝。上臂轻度外展约60–90°，肘关节自然屈曲，前臂放松，掌心向上或朝向身体前方。避免上臂过度外展、外旋或持续用力，以免腋窝软组织紧张、血管神经束位置改变。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First identify the four walls of the axilla. The anterior wall is formed by the pectoralis major and its fascia; the posterior wall is formed mainly by the latissimus dorsi, teres major, and subscapularis; the medial wall is the thoracic wall; and the lateral wall lies near the proximal humerus. HT1 is in the central axillary hollow, not on the anterior or posterior axillary fold. With the arm comfortably abducted, inspect the central skin depression and axillary creases to identify the approximate region.先确定腋窝的四壁：前壁由胸大肌及其筋膜构成，后壁主要由背阔肌、大圆肌及肩胛下肌构成，内侧壁为胸壁，外侧壁邻近肱骨上端。极泉位于腋窝中央凹陷区，不在腋前纹或腋后纹本身。患者上臂自然外展时，观察腋窝中央的皮肤凹陷及皱襞纹理，初步确定取穴区域。

    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate the anterior axillary fold, formed by the inferior border of pectoralis major, and the posterior axillary fold, formed by the inferior borders of latissimus dorsi and teres major. The standard location of HT1 is at the center of the axilla, at the axillary arterial pulse. However, the axillary artery, accompanying axillary vein, and brachial plexus lie deep in this region. The cords of the brachial plexus are arranged around the axillary artery, so the palpable pulse must not be interpreted as a safe needle target. Also recognize the proximal humerus and the lateral region occupied by the coracobrachialis and short head of biceps brachii. Avoid deep lateral insertion. Before needling, establish the approximate positions of the pulsating artery and neurovascular bundle. If pain is mainly over the lateral acromion or anterior shoulder and is provoked by active abduction or resisted external rotation, distinguish it from rotator cuff disorders and compare with local shoulder points such as LI15 Jianyu and TE14 Jianliao. Inability to raise the arm or marked weakness after trauma requires evaluation for rotator cuff tear, dislocation, or fracture.肩痛主要位于腋窝中央或上臂内侧，并符合心经循行区域不适时，可考虑极泉作为局部配穴；若疼痛集中在肩峰外侧、肩前部，主动外展或抗阻外旋诱发疼痛，应优先与肩髃（LI15）、肩髎（TE14）等肩部局部穴及肩袖病变鉴别。外伤后不能抬臂或出现明显肌力下降，应先排除肩袖撕裂、脱位及骨折。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

When medial elbow and arm pain follows the Heart channel and is accompanied by axillary or medial upper-arm discomfort, HT1 may be considered with HT3 as a proximal-distal combination. If pain is localized to the medial elbow and worsens with resisted wrist flexion or pronation, distinguish it from pronator syndrome, common flexor tendinopathy, and ulnar neuropathy. HT3 is the local elbow point and is generally more directly relevant to local treatment. Persistent finger numbness or weakness requires neurological assessment.肘臂内侧疼痛沿心经循行，并伴腋窝或上臂内侧牵涉不适时，可将极泉与少海（HT3）作近远端配伍考虑；若疼痛集中在肘内侧、屈腕或旋前抗阻时加重，应与旋前圆肌综合征、屈肌总腱病及尺神经病变鉴别。少海是肘部局部穴，通常比极泉更适合局部处理；出现手指持续麻木或肌力下降应先检查神经。

Stroke (sequelae)中风 ⚖️

HT1 may be considered within a traditional local channel-based approach to upper-limb dysfunction after stroke, but it should not be selected automatically simply because a patient has post-stroke symptoms. First establish the stroke type, stage, muscle tone, joint range of motion, and sensory deficits. Rehabilitation generally requires functional training and an appropriately selected multi-point protocol. Sudden facial drooping, unilateral weakness, speech disturbance, or visual changes require immediate activation of the stroke emergency pathway rather than acupuncture.中风患者出现上肢功能障碍时，极泉在传统针灸中可作为上肢局部经脉配穴思路的一部分，但不能因患者有中风后遗症就直接选择极泉。应先明确脑卒中类型、病程、肌张力、关节活动度及感觉障碍；康复方案通常需结合功能训练与多穴配伍。突然出现口角歪斜、单侧肢体无力、言语不清或视力异常，应立即启动卒中急救流程，而不是针刺。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Brachial plexus injury or cervical radiculopathy臂丛神经损伤或神经根病变

Symptoms may include pain in the axilla, shoulder, upper arm, or forearm, accompanied by numbness, altered sensation, or reduced muscle strength. Brachial plexopathy may involve several peripheral nerve territories, whereas cervical radiculopathy may be associated with neck pain and radiation in a corresponding dermatome. Assess sensation, muscle strength, and reflexes, with nerve conduction studies, electromyography, or cervical imaging when indicated. If needling produces electric-shock-like sensations radiating into the forearm or fingers, stop advancing immediately rather than interpreting this as deqi.可表现为腋窝、肩部、上臂或前臂疼痛，伴麻木、感觉异常、肌力下降。臂丛神经病变常涉及多个周围神经支配区域；颈神经根病变可能伴颈痛，并按相应皮节出现放射症状。应进行感觉、肌力、反射检查，必要时进行神经传导检查、肌电图或颈椎影像学检查。针刺时若出现向前臂、手指放射的电击样感觉，必须立即停止推进，不能将其当作得气。

Axillary lymphadenopathy腋窝淋巴结肿大

A palpable axillary mass may be tender or painless. Possible causes include infection and inflammation of the skin or upper limb, but lymph-node involvement from breast or other malignancies must also be considered. A progressively enlarging, hard, fixed mass, a mass without an apparent infectious cause, or a mass accompanied by a breast lump, nipple changes, weight loss, or night sweats requires prompt medical assessment and appropriate ultrasound or further investigation. Do not needle or repeatedly compress a mass of uncertain origin.可触及腋窝肿块，可能伴局部压痛，也可能无痛。常见相关原因包括感染、皮肤或上肢炎症，也需要排查乳腺及其他恶性肿瘤相关淋巴结转移。持续增大、质硬、固定、无明显感染原因的肿块，或伴乳房肿块、乳头异常、体重下降、盗汗者，应尽快就医并根据临床判断进行超声及进一步检查。不得在原因不明的肿块上针刺或反复按压。

Axillary skin and sweat-gland disorders: hidradenitis suppurativa, folliculitis, and skin abscess腋窝皮肤及汗腺感染：化脓性汗腺炎、毛囊炎及皮肤脓肿

Medical assessment is needed to determine whether antimicrobial treatment or drainage is required.可表现为腋窝反复出现疼痛性结节、红肿、脓液、窦道或瘢痕。化脓性汗腺炎常反复发作，不能简单理解为局部经络不通。存在红肿、发热、波动感或流脓时，不应在病灶附近针刺；必要时由医生评估是否需要抗感染治疗或引流。

Axillary arterial or venous disorders腋动脉或腋静脉相关血管病变

Important vascular structures lie deep within the axilla. Axillary arterial injury, thrombosis, or upper-extremity deep venous thrombosis may cause pain, swelling, altered skin color, temperature changes, or abnormal pulses. Sudden unilateral arm swelling, cyanosis, marked temperature change, or diminished distal pulses requires urgent vascular assessment rather than treatment as ordinary channel pain.腋窝深部有重要血管结构。腋动脉损伤、血栓或上肢深静脉血栓可能出现疼痛、肿胀、皮肤颜色改变、温度异常或脉搏异常。单侧上肢突然肿胀、发绀、明显温度改变，或远端脉搏减弱，不能按普通经脉疼痛处理，应立即进行血管评估。

Rotator cuff injury and subacromial pain syndrome肩袖损伤及肩峰下疼痛综合征

Typical symptoms include lateral shoulder or proximal upper-arm pain, aggravated by active abduction, resisted external rotation, or lying on the affected side at night. Unlike localized tenderness in the central axilla around HT1, rotator cuff disorders are commonly provoked by shoulder movement or specific resisted tests. Assess shoulder range of motion and strength, with ultrasound or MRI when indicated. Inability to raise the arm after significant trauma requires evaluation for tendon rupture or fracture.主要表现为肩外侧或上臂近端疼痛，主动外展、抗阻外旋或夜间侧卧时加重。与极泉所在腋窝中央的局部压痛不同，肩袖病变通常在肩关节活动或特定抗阻动作中诱发症状。应结合肩关节活动度、肌力及必要的超声或磁共振检查鉴别。明显外伤后不能抬臂，应先排除肌腱断裂或骨折。

Breast disorders and associated referred axillary pain乳腺疾病及相关腋窝牵涉痛

Mastitis, breast masses, and lymph-node involvement associated with breast malignancy may cause axillary pain or swelling. Cervicoscapular myofascial pain and peri-axillary muscle strain颈肩肌筋膜疼痛及腋窝周围肌肉劳损

Strain involving pectoralis major, latissimus dorsi, teres major, or subscapularis may cause tenderness along the anterior or posterior axillary wall, aggravated by shoulder movement.
