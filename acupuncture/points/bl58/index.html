Luo-connecting point of the Foot-Taiyang Bladder channel, connecting separately to Foot-Shaoyin; used in Luo–source or host–guest pairing.足太阳膀胱经络穴，别走足少阴；可用于原络配穴及主客配穴。One of the Luo points; it is not an eight-confluent or five-transport point.络穴之一，见于络穴体系；不属八脉交会穴或五输穴。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「飛揚，一名厥陽，在足外踝上七寸，足太陽絡，別走少陰者，刺入三分，灸三壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Jiayi Jing calls Feiyang Jueyang, places it 7 cun above the lateral malleolus, and identifies it as the Bladder Luo point connecting separately to the Kidney channel. Its historical needling depth is quoted only as a record.《针灸甲乙经》记飞扬又名厥阳，在外踝上七寸，是足太阳络穴并别走少阴；原文针灸深度仅作古籍记载。

「足太陽之別，名曰飛揚，去踝七寸，別走少陰」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu, Channel Vessels, says the Foot-Taiyang connecting vessel is called Feiyang, lies 7 cun above the ankle and connects separately with Shaoyin, establishing its Luo-point role.《灵枢·经脉》说足太阳络脉名飞扬，距踝七寸，别走少阴；这也是飞扬作为络穴的经典依据。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Posterolateral calf myofascial pain小腿后外侧肌筋膜痛

Lateral gastrocnemius or soleus aching after exercise may be reproduced by resisted plantar flexion; persistent numb pain along the lateral foot needs assessment for sural nerve irritation.运动后腓肠肌外侧或比目鱼肌酸痛，踝跖屈抗阻可诱发；若沿足背外侧持续麻痛，需鉴别腓肠神经受刺激。

Small saphenous varicosity or superficial thrombophlebitis小隐静脉曲张或浅表静脉炎

Visible varicosities or a red tender cord on the posterior calf should not be pressed or needled; sudden swelling and warmth requires exclusion of DVT.小腿后外侧可见曲张静脉或红痛索条，不在静脉上按压或针刺；突然肿热需排除深静脉血栓。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Sudden unilateral calf swelling, redness, warmth or venous tenderness needs prompt assessment for thrombosis.小腿单侧突发肿胀、红热或沿静脉压痛，应尽快就医排查血栓。
  • Progressive lateral-foot numbness, foot drop or calf weakness needs timely neurological assessment.足背外侧麻木进行性加重、足下垂或小腿无力，及时做神经评估。

Where is BL58 Fēiyáng?飞扬（BL58）在哪里？

Posterior leg, 7 cun directly above BL60, where the lower lateral border of gastrocnemius meets the calcaneal tendon.小腿后区，昆仑直上 7 寸，腓肠肌外下缘与跟腱移行处。

How is BL58 needled?飞扬（BL58）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of BL58?飞扬（BL58）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting point络穴

Main safety point at BL58?飞扬（BL58）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid a visible small saphenous vein; generally safe.浅层小隐静脉可见时避开；一般安全。
