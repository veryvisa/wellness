Iliotibial band syndrome (ITBS / runner's knee)髂胫束摩擦综合征（ITBS / 跑步膝）

Lateral femoral cutaneous nerve entrapment (meralgia paresthetica)股外侧皮神经炎（感觉异常性股痛 / Meralgia Paresthetica）

Burning, tingling, numbness, or hypersensitivity over the anterolateral thigh triggered by tight clothing, obesity, pregnancy, or entrapment beneath the inguinal ligament. Evaluate the pelvis/inguinal region to rule out mass compression; in pregnant patients (where it often resolves postpartum), use gentle stimulation and safe lateral positioning.大腿前外侧出现烧灼感、针刺感、麻木或痛觉过敏，紧身裤、肥胖、妊娠或腹股沟韧带下方卡压诱发。需检查骨盆与腹股沟区排除占位性病变压迫；注意妊娠期患者需谨慎评估（孕期虽非传统绝对禁针穴，但妊娠期感觉异常性股痛多在产后自愈，针刺时体位与刺激量需温和安全）。
