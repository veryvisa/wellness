Mulberry Leaf and Chrysanthemum Drink桑菊饮

Sāng Jú Yǐn · Wen Bing Tiao Bian by Wu Jutong (Qing dynasty, Upper Burner section); the textual anchor is the original formula and commentary on Sang Ju Yin in the Wen Bing Tiao Bian.清·吴鞠通《温病条辨》（上焦篇）；原文锚点取《温病条辨》卷首辛凉轻剂桑菊饮方及方论。

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Mulberry Leaf and Chrysanthemum Drink桑菊饮
⚠️ Bitter Apricot Kernel Safety Warning苦杏仁安全警示 Bitter apricot kernel contains amygdalin and other cyanogenic glycosides that may release hydrogen cyanide after hydrolysis. Excessive ingestion or improper processing may cause cyanide poisoning. Possible manifestations include nausea, vomiting, dizziness, headache, difficulty breathing, and impaired consciousness. Severe poisoning can be life-threatening. Do not increase the dose independently or consume large quantities of improperly processed bitter apricot kernels as food. Suspected poisoning requires immediate emergency medical attention.苦杏仁含苦杏仁苷等氰苷类成分，水解后可能释放氢氰酸，过量摄入或炮制不当可能导致氰化物中毒。可能出现恶心、呕吐、头晕、头痛、呼吸困难、意识障碍等表现，严重时可危及生命。不得自行增加剂量，不得将未经规范炮制的苦杏仁作为日常食品大量食用。疑似中毒应立即寻求急诊医疗帮助。

Original text原文

「六、太陰風溫，但咳，身不甚熱，微渴者，辛涼輕劑桑菊飲主之。」——温病条辨

Wenbing Tiaobian (Systematic Differentiation of Warm Diseases)

At the onset of wind-warmth affecting the greater yin, the main manifestations are cough, mild or absent fever, and slight thirst. This is a relatively mild wind-warmth pattern for which Sang Ju Yin is traditionally indicated.太阴风温初起，主要表现为咳嗽、身热不明显、稍有口渴，属于病情较轻的风温证，可用辛凉轻剂桑菊饮。

「杏仁（二錢）連翹（一錢五分）薄荷（八分）桑葉（二錢五分）菊花（一錢）苦梗（二錢）甘草（八分）葦根（二錢）」——温病条辨

Wenbing Tiaobian (Systematic Differentiation of Warm Diseases)

The original formula contains eight ingredients: apricot kernel, forsythia fruit, mint, mulberry leaf, chrysanthemum flower, platycodon root, licorice root, and reed rhizome. The original preparation uses two cups of water, reduced to one cup, taken twice daily.原方由杏仁、连翘、薄荷、桑叶、菊花、苦梗、甘草、苇根八味组成。苦梗即桔梗，苇根即芦根。原文另载煎服法：水二杯，煮取一杯，日二服。

「咳，熱傷肺絡也。身不甚熱，病不重也。渴而微，熱不甚也。恐病輕藥重，故另立輕劑方。」——温病条辨

Wenbing Tiaobian (Systematic Differentiation of Warm Diseases)

Cough indicates that heat has affected the lung collaterals. Mild fever and slight thirst indicate that the illness and heat are not severe. Wu Jutong therefore established a lighter formula for milder presentations.咳嗽说明热邪损伤肺络；身热不明显、口渴轻微，说明病情及热势尚轻。因此吴鞠通另立轻剂，体现轻证用轻方的原则。

Cautions使用注意

This formula is traditionally associated with early wind-warmth, wind-heat affecting the defensive level, and impaired lung dispersing and descending functions, with manifestations such as cough, mild fever, and slight thirst. It is not appropriate to apply this formula independently to wind-cold cough characterized by pronounced chills, absence of sweating, and thin white sputum. Persistent dry cough due to yin deficiency, lung and kidney yin deficiency, or illness that has progressed into the qi, nutritive, or blood levels requires a different clinical assessment. Seek prompt medical attention for difficulty breathing, marked shortness of breath, chest pain, coughing up blood, persistent high fever, altered consciousness, or rapidly worsening symptoms. Persistent cough also requires medical evaluation. Bitter apricot kernel contains cyanogenic glycosides, so its dosage and processing require particular care. Do not increase the dose independently or use improperly processed bitter apricot kernels. This is educational information about a traditional formula, not an individualized diagnosis or prescription.本方适用于风温初起、风热犯卫、肺失宣降所见的咳嗽、身热不甚、微渴等表现。风寒咳嗽、恶寒明显、无汗、痰白清稀者，不宜按本方证自行使用。阴虚燥咳、肺肾阴虚或病情已经深入气分、营分、血分者，不应仅凭咳嗽自行套用本方。若出现呼吸困难、气促明显、胸痛、咯血、持续高热、意识异常或症状迅速加重，应及时就医；持续咳嗽也应接受医学评估。苦杏仁含氰苷，必须注意剂量与炮制，不可自行加量或使用未经规范处理的苦杏仁。本方为传统方剂知识，不构成个人诊疗或用药建议。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 This formula contains bitter apricot kernel, which is classified as requiring pregnancy caution and carries minor toxicity in the existing herb record. Mint, Platycodon root, and licorice root also carry pregnancy cautions. The entire formula is therefore classified at the strictest pregnancy caution level among its ingredients. Pregnant individuals should not use this formula independently and should obtain an assessment from a qualified healthcare professional.本方含苦杏仁，已有药档标注孕期慎用且具有轻度毒性；薄荷、桔梗、甘草亦标注孕期慎用。因此全方按最严格的药味安全等级列为孕期慎用。孕期不应自行使用，应由具备资质的医疗专业人员评估风险与必要性。
