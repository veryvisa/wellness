ST44

内庭 Nèitíng

Inner Courtyard · Lower risk较安全

★Ying-spring (water) point荥穴（水）

📍 Location定位★

On the dorsum of the foot, between the 2nd and 3rd toes, proximal to the web margin, at the border between the red and white flesh.足背，第 2、3 趾间，趾蹼缘后方赤白肉际处。

💡 Separate the 2nd and 3rd toes and take the point just behind the web margin where the dorsal and plantar skin meet.分开第 2、3 趾，在趾蹼缘稍后、足背皮肤与趾底皮肤交界处取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular or oblique直刺或斜刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

🛡 Safety安全

Avoid dorsal veins; do not needle broken skin, fungal infection, cellulitis or a diabetic foot; the foot is sensitive, so keep shallow.避开足背浅静脉，足部皮损、真菌感染、蜂窝织炎或糖尿病足不针；足部针感较痛，宜浅刺。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Toothache, sore swollen throat, nosebleed齿痛、咽喉肿痛、鼻衄 ⚖️Febrile disease热病Stomach disorders with acid regurgitation, diarrhoea, dysentery, constipation胃病吐酸、腹泻、痢疾、便秘 ⚖️Swollen painful dorsum of the foot足背肿痛

Where is ST44 Nèitíng?内庭（ST44）在哪里？

On the dorsum of the foot, between the 2nd and 3rd toes, proximal to the web margin, at the border between the red and white flesh.足背，第 2、3 趾间，趾蹼缘后方赤白肉际处。

How is ST44 needled?内庭（ST44）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of ST44?内庭（ST44）是哪类特定穴？

Ying-spring (water) point荥穴（水）

Main safety point at ST44?内庭（ST44）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid dorsal veins; do not needle broken skin, fungal infection, cellulitis or a diabetic foot; the foot is sensitive, so keep shallow.避开足背浅静脉，足部皮损、真菌感染、蜂窝织炎或糖尿病足不针；足部针感较痛，宜浅刺。
