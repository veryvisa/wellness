GB1

瞳子髎 Tóngzǐliáo

Pupil Crevice · High risk高危

★Crossing point of the Hand-Taiyang and Hand/Foot-Shaoyang channels手太阳、手足少阳交会穴
⚠️ Orbital region: aim toward the temple眼区：针尖朝颞侧 High-risk orbital region: aim only posteriorly toward the temple, never medially toward the globe or deep; press after withdrawal to prevent haematoma.眼区高风险：针尖只朝颞侧后方，绝不向内、向眼球或深刺；出针按压防血肿。

📍 Location定位★

On the face, in the depression 0.5 cun lateral to the outer canthus.面部，目外眦外侧 0.5 寸凹陷中。

💡 With the eyes closed, feel the lateral orbital rim beside the outer canthus; the point is the small hollow just outside the bony rim, not inside the eye slit.闭目，在外眦外侧摸到眶外侧缘，骨缘外侧的小凹陷即是，不在眼裂内。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Transverse, directed posteriorly along the lateral orbital rim向后平刺（沿眶外缘向颞侧） · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5 寸

May be pricked to bleed with a three-edged needle for red, swollen eyes.也可三棱针点刺出血（目赤肿痛）。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the head level, eyes looking straight ahead, and the face and orbicularis oculi relaxed.坐位或仰卧位，头部端正，双眼平视前方，面部与眼轮匝肌放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate the outer canthus first, then feel about 0.5 cun laterally for the bony depression on the lateral orbital margin; GB1 lies inside this depression.先找到外眦（眼外角），再沿外眦向外侧约 0.5 寸处摸到眶外侧缘的骨性凹陷，瞳子髎即在此凹陷中。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Estimate half the width of the patient's own interphalangeal joint (about 0.5 cun) laterally from the outer canthus, but confirm placement inside the lateral orbital margin hollow and clear of the outer palpebral commissure.以患者拇指指间关节宽度（1 寸）的一半（约 0.5 寸）作横向粗估，从外眦向外量取，最后仍须落入眶外侧缘凹陷并避开眼裂外侧角。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    In the hollow just outside the lateral orbital rim; zygomaticofacial and zygomaticotemporal nerves and the temporal branch of the facial nerve lie superficially, temporal fascia, temporalis and zygomatico-orbital vessels deeper. The globe and orbital contents lie medially, so thread only posteriorly toward the temple, never medially or deep.位于眶外侧缘外侧的凹陷，浅层有颧神经颧面支、颧颞支和面神经颞支，深层为颞筋膜与颞肌、颧眶动静脉；向内侧即眼球及眶内结构，行针必须沿眶缘向颞侧后方平刺，严禁向内或向深处进针。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Light fingertip palpation along the lateral orbital margin reveals a small bony notch or depression with mild local tenderness; keep pressure gentle and never press onto the eyeball.指腹轻触眶外侧缘可感骨缘转折处的小凹陷，局部可伴轻微压痛；因紧邻眼眶，按压和进针都需轻柔，不可重压眼球。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Directing the needle inward or deeply toward the globe and orbital contents: this high-risk orbital zone contains sensitive ocular and intraorbital structures, so inward or deep insertion risks ocular injury, extraocular muscle damage, or orbital hemorrhage; always needle transversely posterolaterally along the orbital margin for 0.3–0.5 cun.向内侧或向深处刺入眼球及眶内结构：眼区高危结构密集，直刺或向内刺可伤及眼球壁、眼外肌或眶内血管神经，造成视力受损或眼眶血肿，必须沿眶缘向后外方平刺 0.3–0.5 寸。
  • Drifting too close to the outer palpebral fissure or eyelid margin: causes sharp pain, tearing, or conjunctival irritation; keep the point in the depression at the lateral orbital margin.偏入外眦裂隙或眼睑游离缘过近：易引起明显刺痛、流泪或结膜刺激，应退至眶外侧缘骨性凹陷处。
  • Drifting too far posteriorly toward preauricular points such as TE21 or GB2: moves away from the lateral orbital margin and loses local orbital targeting; re-verify the outer canthus and orbital rim first.误取为耳门或听会等耳周穴位：偏后下方过远会离开眶外缘，失去眼区局部近治作用，需先核对外眦与眶外缘。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

Can be included for red, swollen, painful eyes, tearing, or discomfort leaning toward the outer canthus and Shaoyang region using transverse posterolateral insertion along the orbital rim; do not use alone for acute severe pain with vision changes or urgent ocular emergencies, which require medical care first.目赤肿痛、流泪或偏重外眦与少阳经循行部位不适时可纳入配穴，沿眶缘向后外方平刺；急性剧痛伴视力改变或眼底急症应先就医，不选作单一急症处理。

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Can serve as a local point for lateral or temporal headache and migraine radiating toward the lateral orbit or Shaoyang region, often paired with GB20; not a primary choice for frontal, vertex, or internal-pattern headaches.侧头痛、偏头痛牵连眼眶外侧或头面少阳经不适时可作局部配穴，常配风池等穴；前额、巅顶或内伤头痛不以它为主。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Conjunctivitis, keratitis, or periorbital soft-tissue discomfort结膜炎、角膜炎或眼眶周缘软组织不适

When eye redness, eye pain, discharge, or vision changes occur, an ophthalmic exam is needed first to evaluate infection, inflammation, or corneal disease rather than relying on needling or acupressure, especially if infection or trauma is present.出现眼红、眼痛、分泌物增多或视力变化时，需先由眼科检查明确感染、炎症或角膜病变，不能仅靠针灸或按揉处理，眼部有活动性感染或外伤时更需谨慎。

Temporal or lateral periorbital pain, or migraine with periocular discomfort颞部或眼眶外侧放射痛、偏头痛伴眼周不适

Differentiate from acute angle-closure glaucoma, optic neuritis, or giant cell arteritis; seek urgent medical evaluation if severe eye pain, nausea or vomiting, sudden vision loss, or diplopia occurs.需与急性闭角型青光眼、视神经炎或颞动脉炎等急症鉴别，若伴剧烈眼痛、恶心呕吐、视力骤降或复视，应立即就医。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is GB1 Tóngzǐliáo?瞳子髎（GB1）在哪里？

On the face, in the depression 0.5 cun lateral to the outer canthus.面部，目外眦外侧 0.5 寸凹陷中。

How is GB1 needled?瞳子髎（GB1）怎么刺？

Transverse, directed posteriorly along the lateral orbital rim; 0.3–0.5 cun.向后平刺（沿眶外缘向颞侧）；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of GB1?瞳子髎（GB1）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Hand-Taiyang and Hand/Foot-Shaoyang channels手太阳、手足少阳交会穴

Main safety point at GB1?瞳子髎（GB1）最要注意的安全问题？

High-risk orbital region: aim only posteriorly toward the temple, never medially toward the globe or deep; press after withdrawal to prevent haematoma.眼区高风险：针尖只朝颞侧后方，绝不向内、向眼球或深刺；出针按压防血肿。
