GV2

腰俞 Yāoshū

Lumbar Transport · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

In the sacral region, directly over the sacral hiatus, on the posterior midline.骶区，正对骶管裂孔，后正中线上。

💡 Run down the median sacral crest to its end; the hollow between the two sacral cornua is the sacral hiatus.沿骶正中嵴向下摸到嵴的末端，两侧骶角之间的凹陷即骶管裂孔。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique upward向上斜刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

🛡 Safety安全

The sacral canal lies under the hiatus; deep needling can enter the epidural space and irritate sacral nerves, so withdraw on any electric sensation into the legs or perineum. Avoid the lumbosacral region after 3 months of pregnancy.裂孔下即骶管，深刺可进入硬膜外腔刺激骶神经；出现下肢或会阴放射麻电感即退针。妊娠 3 个月以上腰骶部不宜针。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Diarrhoea, dysentery, rectal bleeding, constipation, haemorrhoids, prolapse腹泻、痢疾、便血、便秘、痔疮、脱肛 ⚖️Irregular menstruation月经不调Stiff painful lower back, weakness or numbness of the legs腰脊强痛、下肢痿痹Epilepsy癫痫

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone, optionally with a thin support under the abdomen to relax the lumbosacral region.俯卧，腹下可垫薄枕以放松腰骶部；臀部放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the sacral hiatus on the posterior sacral midline, below the sacral median crest; GV2 is in the depression at the hiatus.在骶骨后正中线上寻找骶管裂孔；其上方可触到骶正中嵴，穴在裂孔处的凹陷中。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Trace the sacral median crest caudally to its end, where the hiatus forms a shallow inferior opening. The sacrococcygeal ligament and sacral canal lie here; the hollow is not a safe passage for deep needling.沿骶骨正中嵴向尾侧触诊至嵴末端，裂孔呈下端开口的浅凹陷；周围是骶尾韧带与骶管入口，不能把凹陷当作可向深部进针的通道。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Use light fingertip pressure to confirm the midline and hiatus margins, comparing both sides. With obesity, scarring or sacral variation, reassess landmarks clinically rather than guessing by deep pressure.以指腹轻压确认骶骨中线及裂孔边界，左右对称触摸；肥胖、瘢痕或骶骨变异时以骨性标志和影像/临床评估复核，勿凭深压猜点。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Confusing GV2 with GV1 below the coccyx, or directing a procedure deeply into the hiatus, risks sacral neural structures; refer for assessment with neurologic symptoms or sacrococcygeal deformity.误取到尾骨尖下方的长强，或在裂孔内向深部操作，可能刺激骶管内神经结构；疑有神经症状或骶尾畸形时先转诊评估。

Where is GV2 Yāoshū?腰俞（GV2）在哪里？

In the sacral region, directly over the sacral hiatus, on the posterior midline.骶区，正对骶管裂孔，后正中线上。

How is GV2 needled?腰俞（GV2）怎么刺？

Oblique upward; 0.5–1 cun.向上斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Main safety point at GV2?腰俞（GV2）最要注意的安全问题？

The sacral canal lies under the hiatus; deep needling can enter the epidural space and irritate sacral nerves, so withdraw on any electric sensation into the legs or perineum. Avoid the lumbosacral region after 3 months of pregnancy.裂孔下即骶管，深刺可进入硬膜外腔刺激骶神经；出现下肢或会阴放射麻电感即退针。妊娠 3 个月以上腰骶部不宜针。
