🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「少衝（一名經始）手小指內側，去爪甲角如韭葉，手少陰心脈所出，為井木，」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Dacheng records Shaochong, also called Jing Shi, on the inner side of the little finger near the nail corner, at a distance described by the width of a chive leaf. It identifies HT9 as the Jing-Well point of the Hand-Shaoyin Heart channel and assigns it to the Wood phase. The historical term for the inner side should be interpreted in conjunction with the classical channel description and the modern standardized location on the radial side of the little finger. Jing-Well points traditionally address disorders involving the emergence and movement of channel qi, including certain acute Heart-channel and consciousness-related presentations.《针灸大成》记载少冲又名经始，位于小指内侧，距离指甲角如韭叶宽度，并明确指出少冲是手少阴心经脉气所出的井穴，五行属木。这里的「内侧」应结合古代经脉定位和现代标准穴位定位理解，临床应按小指桡侧、指甲角旁的标准位置取穴。井穴主经气初出，传统上用于心神急症、神志异常及经气郁闭等证候。

「少衝 主心虛膽寒，怔忡癲狂」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Dacheng lists Heart deficiency, Gallbladder cold, palpitations, and manic or psychotic presentations among the traditional indications of HT9. These terms describe historical Chinese medical patterns and symptom categories. The classical term dian-kuang should not be equated directly with a modern psychiatric diagnosis or used as a substitute for emergency psychiatric or neurological assessment.《针灸大成》列出少冲主治心虚胆寒、怔忡及癫狂，反映其传统应用涉及心神不宁、心悸和神志异常。这里的癫狂属于古代中医病证术语，不能直接等同于现代精神科诊断，也不能据此替代急诊精神科或神经科评估。

「取本經少衝以補之，少衝者，井木也」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Paronychia and periungual soft-tissue infection甲沟炎及甲周软组织感染

HT9 is close to the nail corner, so its location may overlap with the tender area of paronychia. Typical findings include redness, swelling, increased local temperature, and tenderness around the nail fold. Severe cases may develop pus or fluctuance. Mild mechanical irritation must be distinguished from infection. An abscess, spreading redness, or substantial pain requires medical assessment before any needling or blood-pricking procedure.少冲位于小指甲角附近，临床容易与甲沟炎的压痛区域重叠。甲沟炎常见甲襞红肿、局部温度升高、触痛，严重时出现脓液或波动感。早期轻度甲周刺激与感染应加以区别；出现脓肿、红肿扩散或明显疼痛时，应先接受医疗评估，不应在感染区域针刺或点刺出血。

Ingrown nail and mechanical irritation of the nail fold嵌甲及甲沟机械性刺激

A nail edge growing into the lateral nail fold or excessively short nail trimming may cause tenderness, redness, swelling, and a foreign-body sensation. Examine whether the nail edge is embedded in the fold and look for granulation tissue. Although the painful region may overlap with HT9, the underlying problem is a local nail abnormality and should not be treated simply as an acupuncture-point complaint.指甲边缘向甲沟内生长或甲缘修剪过短，可造成局部压痛、红肿及异物感。检查甲板侧缘是否嵌入甲襞，并观察是否有肉芽组织。此类疼痛以甲缘及甲沟为中心，与少冲的标准定位可能重叠，但病因属于局部甲结构异常，不能简单按穴处理。

Irritation or injury of the proper palmar digital nerve of the little finger小指固有掌侧指神经刺激或损伤

The proper palmar digital nerves provide sensation to the little finger. Compression, laceration, contusion, or a postoperative neuroma may cause localized stabbing pain, burning, numbness, or electric pain radiating toward the fingertip. Assess sensory distribution, two-point discrimination, and whether gentle percussion produces radiating symptoms. Persistent sensory loss, significant neuropathic pain, or motor abnormalities require referral and avoidance of repeated stimulation along the nerve.小指两侧的固有掌侧指神经负责指端感觉。神经受压、切割伤、挫伤或术后神经瘤可引起局部刺痛、烧灼感、麻木及沿指端放射的电击样疼痛。应检查感觉分布、两点辨别觉及轻叩是否诱发放射痛。若存在持续感觉缺失、明显神经性疼痛或运动功能异常，应转诊评估，避免在神经走行处反复刺激。

Contusion or fracture of the distal phalanx of the little finger小指远节指骨挫伤或骨折

Crushing injuries, heavy objects, or direct impact may fracture the distal phalanx of the little finger. Typical findings include localized swelling, bruising, marked tenderness, and restricted fingertip movement. Examine skin integrity, subungual hematoma, distal circulation, and sensation, and obtain radiographs when indicated. Persistent pain after trauma must not be interpreted solely as channel obstruction based on tenderness at an acupuncture point.夹伤、重物砸伤或直接撞击可造成小指远节指骨骨折，常见局部肿胀、瘀血、明显压痛及指端活动受限。应检查皮肤完整性、甲下血肿、指端循环和感觉，必要时进行 X 线检查。外伤后持续明显疼痛不能仅依据穴位压痛判断为经络不通。

Subungual hematoma甲下血肿

After fingertip compression or impact, blood may accumulate beneath the nail, producing a dark red, purple, or black discoloration with throbbing pain. The pain results from increased pressure beneath the nail plate and may occur close to HT9. Assess the severity of the injury and the possibility of an associated distal phalanx fracture. A large hematoma or severe pain requires prompt medical evaluation; do not attempt self-needling or bloodletting through the hematoma.指端受到挤压或撞击后，甲板下可出现暗红、紫黑色血肿，并伴明显搏动性疼痛。疼痛来自甲板下压力增加，位置可能接近少冲。需要评估外伤程度及是否合并远节指骨骨折；较大血肿或剧烈疼痛应及时就医，不可在血肿处自行针刺放血。

Distal little-finger ischemia and microvascular impairment小指远端缺血及微循环障碍

Vasospasm, thrombosis, or other circulatory disorders may cause pallor, cyanosis, coldness, numbness, and pain in the fingertip. Compare the color and temperature of both hands, capillary refill, and relevant pulses. Sudden pallor, blue discoloration, coldness, or persistent severe pain requires immediate assessment for acute vascular compromise. Local needling or bloodletting must be avoided.指端血管痉挛、血栓或其他血流障碍可能造成小指苍白、发绀、发凉、麻木及疼痛。应比较双手指端颜色、皮温、毛细血管再充盈时间及脉搏情况。若指端突然苍白、发紫、冰冷或出现持续剧痛，应立即就医排查急性血管问题，禁止在局部进行针刺或放血。
