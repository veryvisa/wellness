📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the forearm on its side, thumb up, wrist relaxed.坐位或仰卧，前臂立起、拇指在上（侧掌位），腕放松。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Elbow crease to distal wrist crease = 12 cun. LU7 is 1.5 cun above the wrist crease: one eighth of the way from wrist to elbow.肘横纹到腕掌侧远端横纹折作 12 寸。列缺在腕横纹上 1.5 寸，即从腕横纹往肘方向量 1/8 段。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    One of the patient's own thumb-widths plus half again; or the index and middle fingers held together (about 1.5 cun) laid across from the wrist crease. Finger measures are rough; the bony landmark decides.患者拇指同身寸 1 寸再加半寸；或食指、中指并拢（约 1.5 寸）横放在腕横纹上，上缘即是。指宽只作粗量，最后以骨性标志为准。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Feel the most prominent bump on the thumb side just above the wrist: the radial styloid. LU7 sits just proximal to it, in a small hollow at its upper end.沿拇指一侧摸到腕上方最突出的骨头——桡骨茎突。列缺在茎突的上方（近肘侧）一点，茎突顶端的小凹陷里。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Ask the patient to abduct and extend the thumb hard: two tendons stand out on the radial side above the wrist — abductor pollicis longus on the palm side and the brachioradialis insertion. LU7 lies in the cleft between them at the upper edge of the radial styloid. The radial pulse beats about a finger-width toward the palm (the LU8–LU9 line); do not drift onto the pulse.让患者拇指用力外展、伸直：手腕上方桡侧会绷出两条肌腱——掌侧的拇长展肌腱和更靠肘侧斜过的肱桡肌腱止点。列缺在这两条腱之间的裂隙中，正好在桡骨茎突上缘。桡动脉在它掌侧约一横指处搏动（那里是太渊、经渠一线），不要把穴偏到搏动处。

  5. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Interlocked-hands method: cross the webs of both hands; the index fingertip of one hand resting on the other's radial styloid falls into a small cleft — that is LU7. Hand sizes differ, so treat this as a beginner's shortcut only.虎口交叉法：两手虎口自然交叉，一手食指压在另一手桡骨茎突上，食指尖下的小裂隙即是。手大小差别大，交叉深浅会偏，只作入门找法。

  6. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Stroke across with a fingertip: a narrow groove on the bone (the classical 'parting gap') can be felt, usually tender. Pressing too hard compresses the superficial radial nerve and sends tingling to the back of the thumb — right spot, too much pressure.指腹横向轻拨，能感到一道细长的骨面裂隙（古人称「分间」），按下多有酸胀；按得过深会压到桡神经浅支，出现向拇指背放射的麻感——说明位置对、但压力太大。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting onto the radial pulse (the LU8–LU9 line): too far toward the palm; a perpendicular needle can hit the artery.取到桡动脉搏动处（经渠、太渊一线）：太靠掌侧，直刺易伤动脉。
  • Sliding dorsally into the snuffbox (toward LI5): that is the Large Intestine channel, beyond the abductor pollicis longus tendon.取到桡骨茎突背侧的鼻烟窝（阳溪方向）：那是大肠经，已过了拇长展肌腱。
  • Needling perpendicularly: periosteum lies right under the skin; it is painful and shallow. Textbooks use oblique or transverse insertion toward the elbow.直刺：皮下即骨膜，直刺既扎不进也很痛，教材用向肘斜刺或平刺。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

Headache头痛 ⚖️

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

Lingshu ch.10, on the connecting vessels: the Hand-Taiyin connecting vessel is called Lieque; it starts in the 'parting gap' above the wrist, runs with the main channel into the palm and disperses over the thenar eminence — the classical basis for LU7 as the Luo-connecting point.《灵枢·经脉》讲络脉：手太阴的络脉叫列缺，从腕上的「分间」（骨与肌腱间的裂隙）起，与本经并行进入掌中，散布在鱼际。这就是列缺是络穴、能联络手阳明的出处。

Inflamed sheath of abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis; pain at the radial styloid, sharply provoked by fist-plus-ulnar-deviation (Finkelstein test); common in new parents and heavy phone users. Where is LU7 Lièquē?列缺（LU7）在哪里？

Radial side of the forearm, just above the styloid process of the radius, 1.5 cun above the wrist crease, between the tendons of brachioradialis and abductor pollicis longus.前臂桡侧，桡骨茎突上方，腕横纹上 1.5 寸，肱桡肌腱与拇长展肌腱之间。

How is LU7 needled?列缺（LU7）怎么刺？

Oblique, pointing up the arm toward the elbow; 0.5–0.8 cun.向上（朝肘）斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of LU7?列缺（LU7）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting point; One of the eight confluent points; opens the Ren vessel; Four Command Points rhyme: "for head and neck, seek LU7"络穴；八脉交会穴之一，通任脉；四总穴歌：「头项寻列缺」

Main safety point at LU7?列缺（LU7）最要注意的安全问题？

The superficial radial nerve runs here; redirect if a shooting tingling appears. Low risk.桡神经浅支在此，出现放射样麻感就调整方向；风险较低。
