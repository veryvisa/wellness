Form 8: Grasping the sparrow's tail, right第8式 · 右揽雀尾

after the turn, ward off, roll back, press and push make one round; 1 round转身后，掤、捋、挤、按为一遍，做 1 遍

Yòu lǎn quèwěi

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚

  • Knee pain: stand high, bending the knee no more than about 30 degrees, and take small steps; keep the knee in line with the toes and not past them; stop if it hurts膝盖痛：架子站高，弯膝到约 30 度为止，步子迈小；膝盖对着脚尖方向，不超过脚尖；痛就停
  • After a hip or knee replacement, or with a meniscus problem: skip the turn, set yourself facing right, and then do the form髋、膝换过关节或半月板有问题：不做转身那一步，直接换到右边站好再做
  • The moment the front toes lift puts most of the weight on one leg. If you fall easily or your balance is poor: hold the back of a chair or a counter, or do the arms only后坐时右脚尖翘起那一下单腿受力多：容易跌倒、平衡不好：扶着椅背或台面做，或只做手的动作

🧘 The movement动作

  1. From the push of the left side: sit back onto the right leg with the left toes lifted, turn right and swing the left toes inward; the right hand arcs to the right with the turn接左揽雀尾的按：重心后坐到右腿，左脚尖翘起，身体向右转，左脚尖向里扣；右手随转体向右划弧
  2. Shift the weight back onto the left leg, 'hold a ball' at the left front and draw the right foot in beside the left, toes touching重心移回左腿，两手在左前「抱球」，右脚收到左脚内侧，脚尖点地
  3. Ward off: step the right foot forward and right, heel first, and shift into a right bow stance; the right arm rises in a curve from the belly to shoulder height, palm inward, while the left hand presses down beside the left hip掤：右脚向右前方迈出，脚跟先着地，重心前移成右弓步；右臂弯成弧，从腹前向前上方掤起，到肩高，掌心向内，左手按到左胯旁
  4. Roll back: turn slightly right and reach the right hand forward, the left hand under the right forearm, palm up; sit back onto the left leg, turning slightly left, and draw both hands back past the belly to the left捋：上身稍向右转，右手向前，左手到右前臂下方、掌心向上；重心后坐到左腿，身体稍左转，两手经腹前向左后方捋回
  5. Press: turn back to the front and rest the left hand against the inside of the right wrist; shift into a right bow stance and press forward with both hands, the right arm staying curved挤：上身转回正前方，左手搭在右手腕内侧；重心前移成右弓步，两手一起向前挤出，右臂保持弧形
  6. Push: separate the hands to shoulder width, palms down; sit back, drawing the hands to the chest and down to the belly, right toes lifting; shift into a right bow stance and push forward, palms forward, wrists at shoulder height按：两手分开与肩同宽、掌心向下；重心后坐，两手收到胸前再落到腹前，右脚尖翘起；重心前移成右弓步，两手向前推按，掌心向前，手腕与肩同高

🌬 Breathe in on the turn, roll back and sit back, out on ward off, press and push; or breathe naturally.转身、捋、后坐时吸气，掤、挤、按时呼气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • In the turn, swing the left toes in first and then move the weight; never twist the knee with the foot planted转身时先扣左脚尖、再移重心，不要脚钉在地上拧膝
  • Keep the four parts clear: ward off, roll back, press, push; finish one before the next掤、捋、挤、按四个劲要分清，一个做完再做下一个
  • Moving forward and sitting back is the weight travelling; the trunk stays upright前进、后坐都是重心在走，上身始终竖直

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Turning with the foot stuck, so the knee twists转身时脚不动，膝盖跟着拧
  • Leaning back or sticking the hips out as you sit back后坐时上身后仰、臀部撅起
  • Front knee past the toes in the bow前弓时膝盖超过脚尖

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Skip the turn and do the right side in place; or do it seated.不转身，原地站着做右边的掤、捋、挤、按；或坐着做。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 The mirror of the left side, with an added turn. The name pictures the hands lightly holding a bird's tail, neither gripping nor letting go; ward off, roll back, press and push are the four basic hand methods of taiji push-hands. It is a traditional image, not a research finding.和左揽雀尾对称，多一个转身扣脚。「揽雀尾」说的是两手像轻轻揽住鸟的尾巴，不夹紧也不放走；掤、捋、挤、按四个字，是太极拳推手里的四种手法。这是传统的比喻，不是研究结论。
