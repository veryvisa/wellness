The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The xiphisternal junction-to-umbilicus span is 8 cun; CV11 is three cun above the umbilicus and five cun below the xiphisternal junction.胸剑联合中点至脐中为 8 寸；建里在脐上 3 寸，距胸剑联合下端 5 寸。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate three cun above the navel on the midline, one cun above CV10 and one cun below CV12; keep posture constant during measurement.沿前正中线定位于脐上三寸、下脘上方一寸、中脘下方一寸；同一人量寸时保持体位不变。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The linea alba and abdominal wall overlie the cavity, with stomach and bowel deeper. Avoid lateral abdominal-wall vessels, surgical scars and visible hernia bulges; do not press deeply.腹白线和腹壁组织覆盖腹腔，深部邻胃及肠管；避开腹壁外侧血管、手术瘢痕及可见疝包块，不作深压。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently confirm three cun above the navel and check the sequence of CV13, CV12 and CV10; marked tenderness needs gastrointestinal causes assessed first.轻触脐上三寸处并核对上脘、中脘、下脘的连续次序；局部显著压痛应先排查消化系统病因。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

An adjacent point for middle-jiao fullness around three cun above the navel, below CV12; CV12 is preferred when the Stomach front-mu role is central.脐上三寸附近、较中脘偏下的中焦胀满可选作邻近穴；以胃募穴辨治时优先比较中脘。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

An adjunct for vomiting with mid-to-lower epigastric fullness; severe vomiting, dehydration, blood or obstruction signs need medical care.呕吐伴中下上腹胀满时辅助；热性剧吐、脱水、呕血或梗阻表现需先就医。

Hiccups呃逆

May pair with CV12 for hiccups with middle-jiao fullness; persistent hiccups or accompanying swallowing or breathing symptoms need assessment.呃逆兼中焦胀满时可与中脘等配用；持续超过数日或伴吞咽、呼吸症状应评估病因。
