⚠️ Pneumothorax-risk point气胸风险穴 Pleura and lung are deep to PC1. Use only lateral oblique or transverse insertion, 0.3–0.5 cun; never needle deeply medially. Chest pain, breathlessness, pain on inhalation, or persistent cough after needling needs urgent care (call 911 in BC).天池深面接近胸膜和肺，只可向外斜刺或平刺0.3–0.5寸，绝不可向内深刺。针后胸痛、气短、吸气痛或持续咳嗽应立即就医（BC拨911）。

📍 Location定位★

On the chest, in the 4th intercostal space, 5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 4 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 5 寸。

💡 Count down to the 4th intercostal space, then measure 5 cun lateral to the anterior midline; in men this is about 1 cun lateral to the nipple, in women do not use the nipple.先数清第 4 肋间隙，再由前正中线向外量 5 寸；男性约在乳头外 1 寸，女性不以乳头定位。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique laterally or transverse, away from the thorax向外斜刺或平刺，避开胸腔 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5寸

Never insert deeply toward the midline.不可向内深刺。

🛡 Safety安全

Pleura and lung lie deep; medial or deep insertion can cause pneumothorax. Avoid the neurovascular bundle below each rib.深面为胸膜和肺，向内或过深可致气胸；避开肋下缘的血管神经。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine with the arm slightly abducted and the chest and shoulder relaxed. Confirm the ribs and intercostal space rather than using breast contour as the landmark.仰卧，上臂稍外展，胸肩放松；取穴前先确认肋骨与肋间隙，不以乳房轮廓代替骨性定位。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the 2nd rib beside the sternal angle, count down to the 4th rib and find the 4th intercostal space below it. The standard location is the 4th intercostal space, 5 cun lateral to the anterior midline; in men this is about 1 cun lateral to the nipple, in women use the 5-cun measurement instead of the nipple.先在胸骨角两侧摸到第 2 肋，从此向下数到第 4 肋，再找它下方的第 4 肋间隙。国标写法是第 4 肋间隙、前正中线旁开 5 寸；男性约在乳头外 1 寸，女性不用乳头，按旁开 5 寸量。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The sternal angle meets the 2nd costal cartilage; count down along the ribs to identify the 4th rib and the space below it. The point is on the chest wall beneath pectoralis major. Intercostal vessels and nerves run along the lower rib margin, with intercostal muscle, pleura and lung deeper; this is a danger point for qualified practitioners only, never for deep medial or intrathoracic insertion.胸骨角连接第 2 肋软骨；沿肋骨逐根向下确认第 4 肋，再在其下缘找到肋间隙。穴在胸大肌覆盖的胸壁，肋间血管神经沿肋骨下缘走行，深面为肋间肌、胸膜和肺；危险穴，只能由合格专业人员评估操作，绝不可向内或向胸腔深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently trace the bony curve of the 4th rib and confirm the soft-tissue gap between adjacent ribs. Do not press deeply to seek a hollow or use breast tissue to define the point.沿第 4 肋的骨性弧面轻触，在相邻肋骨之间确认软组织间隙；勿用深压寻找所谓凹陷，也不要按压乳房组织来确定穴位。

  4. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Use the patient's own finger breadth as a rough 1-cun lateral check from the nipple or midclavicular line. Finger width is only an estimate; the 4th intercostal space and channel line take priority.以患者本人横指作约 1 寸的辅助横量，由乳头或锁骨中线附近向外复核；手指宽仅作粗估，最后以第 4 肋间隙和标准经线为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Miscounting the ribs places the point in the 3rd or 5th intercostal space. Do not correct by nipple height; recount from the sternal angle.数错肋骨而落入第 3 或第 5 肋间：穴位上下偏移，不能凭乳头高度纠正；应回到胸骨角重新数肋。
  • Medial, perpendicular or deep insertion risks the pleura and lung and can cause pneumothorax. PC1 is high risk; do not self-needle and follow professional training and safety limits.向内、垂直或过深操作：胸膜与肺紧邻深面，可能造成气胸；PC1 为高风险穴，不可自行针刺，必须遵守受训范围与安全规范。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Intercostal strain or chest-wall myofascial pain肋间肌劳损或胸壁肌筋膜痛

Pain may be reproduced by local pressure or trunk rotation, but tenderness does not exclude cardiopulmonary disease; new, severe or unexplained chest pain needs assessment first.局部按压或躯干转动可复现疼痛；但压痛不能排除心肺病因，首次、剧烈或原因不明胸痛须先评估。

Breast or anterior-axillary discomfort乳房或腋前组织不适

A breast lump, skin dimpling or change, or unusual discharge should not be treated as point tenderness and needs timely medical assessment.乳房肿块、皮肤凹陷或改变、异常分泌物不应当作穴位压痛处理，应及时接受医疗检查。

  • Chest pain with breathlessness, cold sweat, nausea or radiation to the arm, back or jaw: call emergency services now.胸痛伴气短、冷汗、恶心或放射至手臂、背部、下颌：立即呼叫急救。

Where is PC1 Tiānchí?天池（PC1）在哪里？

On the chest, in the 4th intercostal space, 5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 4 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 5 寸。

How is PC1 needled?天池（PC1）怎么刺？

Oblique laterally or transverse, away from the thorax; 0.3–0.5 cun.向外斜刺或平刺，避开胸腔；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of PC1?天池（PC1）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Pericardium channel手厥阴心包经穴

Main safety point at PC1?天池（PC1）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung lie deep; medial or deep insertion can cause pneumothorax. Avoid the neurovascular bundle below each rib.深面为胸膜和肺，向内或过深可致气胸；避开肋下缘的血管神经。
