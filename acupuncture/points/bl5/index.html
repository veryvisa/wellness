    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Anterior midline hairline to posterior midline hairline = 12 cun, and distance between the two anterior hairline corners (ST8) = 9 cun.     Measure 1 cun posterior to the anterior midline hairline using the patient's own thumb-width, and use the index plus middle fingers together (about 1.5 cun) for a rough lateral check, then confirm against the midline and the 9-cun inter-corner proportion.先以患者拇指同身寸（1 寸）自前发际正中向后量取入发际 1 寸；横向旁开 1.5 寸可先以食指、中指并拢（约 1.5 寸）作粗略参照，最终需结合前正中线与两额角发际骨度比例核定。

Typically presents as bilateral or frontal band-like or pressing dull pain often linked to sleep loss, eye strain, or stress; seek medical evaluation first if accompanied by fever, severe headache, or visual changes.多表现为双侧或前额紧箍样、压迫样钝痛，常与熬夜、用眼疲劳、精神紧张相关；若伴发热、剧烈头痛或视力改变，需先由医生评估排查器质性病变。

Frontal sensory irritation involving terminal branches of the supraorbital or supratrochlear nerves眶上神经或滑车上神经末梢刺激所致前额局部感觉异常

May cause localized prickling, numbness, or tenderness near the anterior hairline; differentiate from migraine, sinusitis-related headache, and intracranial causes, and obtain medical care if symptoms worsen or neurological signs appear.可表现为前额发际附近局部刺痛、麻木或触痛，需与偏头痛、鼻窦炎性头痛及颅内病变鉴别；症状持续加重或有神经缺损时应先就医。

Post-traumatic scalp contusion or subgaleal hematoma头部外伤后局部软组织挫伤或帽状腱膜下血肿

After head trauma with local swelling, fluctuance, or marked tenderness, rule out skull fracture or intracranial bleeding medically first and avoid deep needling or heavy pressure.外伤后局部隆起、波动感或压痛明显时，应先由医学影像或临床检查排除颅骨骨折或颅内出血，急性期不宜盲目深刺或重压。

Where is BL5 Wǔchù?五处（BL5）在哪里？

On the head, 1.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline, 1 cun behind the anterior hairline.头部，前发际正中旁开 1.5 寸，入发际 1 寸。

How is BL5 needled?五处（BL5）怎么刺？

Transverse in the scalp; 0.3–0.5 cun.沿头皮平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at BL5?五处（BL5）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid deep insertion and puncturing superficial vessels.避免深刺和穿刺表浅血管。
