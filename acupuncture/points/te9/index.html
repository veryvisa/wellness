📍 Location定位★

Posterior forearm, 5 cun below the olecranon, between ulna and radius.前臂后面，肘尖下5寸，尺骨与桡骨之间。

💡 Measure 5 cun toward the wrist from the olecranon, keeping within the interosseous groove.由肘尖向腕侧量5寸，保持在两骨间沟内。

Avoid superficial veins; avoid repeated deep stimulation toward the posterior interosseous nerve.避开浅静脉；深刺避免向骨间后神经反复刺激。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the forearm pronated, dorsum upward, elbow slightly flexed, and forearm muscles relaxed.坐位或仰卧，前臂旋前、手背向上，肘微屈，前臂肌肉放松。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The elbow crease to the distal dorsal wrist crease is 12 cun. TE9 is 7 cun proximal to the wrist, or 5 cun distal to the elbow, between the bones.肘横纹至腕背侧远端横纹为 12 寸；四渎在腕上 7 寸（即肘横纹下 5 寸），位于两骨之间。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Measure 5 cun distally from the elbow crease along the posterior forearm, staying in the longitudinal interval between ulna and radius. Use the olecranon to orient the elbow, not as a substitute starting point for the crease.在前臂背侧由肘横纹向腕量 5 寸，沿尺骨与桡骨之间的纵向间隙定位；肘尖用于确认肘部高度，不从肘尖骨突直接量偏。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently extend the wrist to identify the extensor masses and palpate the soft groove between radius and ulna. Posterior cutaneous nerves and superficial veins are shallow; extensors and posterior interosseous neurovascular structures are deeper, so avoid deep probing.轻伸腕辨认两侧伸肌群，在桡、尺骨之间触及软组织沟；浅层有前臂后皮神经和浅静脉，深层邻伸肌及骨间后神经血管，避免向骨间深部探刺。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    The palpated site should feel like a soft intermuscular interval. If a distinct bone edge is felt, adjust into the gap and recalibrate at 7 cun above the wrist.按压时在两骨之间应为肌间隙的柔软触感；如明显触到骨缘，横向微调回间隙并以腕上 7 寸校准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • The standard says '5 cun below the olecranon' and the bone-measure table's elbow crease is level with the olecranon, so both starting points give the same height. Real errors come from stacking finger-widths instead of the 12-cun forearm measure, or drifting onto the ulna or radius. Deep needling between the bones can irritate the posterior interosseous nerve.国标写「肘尖下 5 寸」，骨度表的肘横纹平鹰嘴，两种起点是同一高度；真正的误差来自不用 12 寸前臂骨度、改用手指叠量，或横移到尺骨、桡骨骨面。深刺两骨之间可刺激骨间后神经。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

TE9 has traditional ear indications but is better considered a distal adjunct for Shaoyang signs; sudden unilateral hearing loss is not a point-selection problem and needs immediate care.四渎有传统耳病主治，但更适合作为少阳经症状的远端配穴；突发单侧听力下降不选穴等待，应立即就医。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

TE9 may be considered for mid-forearm interosseous pain; pain centred at the elbow joint or accompanied by swelling needs local elbow assessment first.前臂中段两骨间痛可取四渎附近配穴；若疼痛集中肘关节或伴肿胀，则应先检查肘部局部病变。

Where is TE9 Sìdú?四渎（TE9）在哪里？

Posterior forearm, 5 cun below the olecranon, between ulna and radius.前臂后面，肘尖下5寸，尺骨与桡骨之间。

How is TE9 needled?四渎（TE9）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1寸。

Special-point roles of TE9?四渎（TE9）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Triple Energizer channel手少阳三焦经穴

Main safety point at TE9?四渎（TE9）最要注意的安全问题？
