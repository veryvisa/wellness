Points and what lies under them穴位与针下要点

LI15Jiānyú肩髃 — Beneath deltoid lie the subdeltoid bursa and supraspinatus tendon, and deeper still the shoulder joint: a deep insertion enters the joint, so skin preparation must meet joint-puncture standards.穿过三角肌就是三角肌下囊与冈上肌腱，再深便进入肩关节：深刺等于进关节腔，消毒要按关节穿刺的标准。Take care需谨慎LI16Jùgǔ巨骨 — Trapezius and supraspinatus lie under the needle with the suprascapular nerve and vessels deep; going too deep, or angling medially and down toward the root of the neck, risks entering the chest.针下是斜方肌与冈上肌，肩胛上神经血管在深处；直刺过深或朝内下方（颈根）走，就有刺入胸腔的风险。High risk高危
