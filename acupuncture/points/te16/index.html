TE16

天牖 Tiānyǒu

Heavenly Window · High risk高危

One of the 'five regions of Tianyou' (Lingshu, Benshu)「天牖五部」之一（《灵枢·本输》）
⚠️ Major-neck-vessel risk point颈部大血管风险穴 The carotid sheath lies anteromedial to TE16. Use only shallow posterolateral oblique insertion; never advance toward a pulse, anteromedially, or deeply. Progressive neck swelling or breathing or swallowing difficulty needs emergency assessment now.天牖前内侧是颈动脉鞘。只作向后外方的浅斜刺；不可朝搏动处、前内侧或深部推进。出现进行性颈部肿胀、呼吸或吞咽困难应立即急诊评估。

📍 Location定位★

On the neck, level with the angle of the mandible, in the hollow at the posterior border of sternocleidomastoid.颈部，横平下颌角，胸锁乳突肌后缘凹陷中。

💡 Turn the head to make sternocleidomastoid prominent; lightly palpate the hollow at its posterior border level with the mandibular angle.转头使胸锁乳突肌显露，在下颌角同高的肌后缘轻触凹陷取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique posterolaterally向后外方斜刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8寸

🛡 Safety安全

The carotid sheath is anteromedial, with external jugular vein and accessory nerve nearby; never needle deeply anteromedially or through a palpable pulse.颈动脉鞘在前内侧，另有颈外静脉和副神经；不可向前内侧深刺，不可在搏动处进针。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the head neutral, then turn it slightly to the opposite side to outline the posterior sternocleidomastoid border; avoid neck hyperextension.坐位或仰卧，头正中后轻转向对侧，使胸锁乳突肌后缘显露；避免过伸颈部。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Approximately level with the mandibular angle, in the hollow behind SI17 at the posterior border of sternocleidomastoid. Turn the head slightly to identify the border and avoid any palpable carotid pulse.与下颌角大致同高，在胸锁乳突肌后缘、天容（SI17）后方凹陷中；轻转头后沿肌肉后缘确认，不取颈侧可触及的动脉搏动处。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Turn the head to outline the sternocleidomastoid and gently palpate its posterior edge. The external jugular vein and cervical plexus branches are superficial; the accessory nerve and transverse cervical vessels are nearby, while the carotid sheath lies anteromedially. Only a trained acupuncturist should use a shallow posterolateral oblique approach; never direct a needle anteromedially, toward a pulse, or deeply.转头使胸锁乳突肌轮廓显出，浅触其后缘；颈外静脉及颈丛皮支浅行，副神经与颈横血管在邻近区域，颈动脉鞘位于前内侧。只由受训针灸人员作浅而向后外的斜刺，绝不向前内侧、搏动处或深部进针。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate the hollow where the posterior sternocleidomastoid border meets the mandibular-angle level. If a pulse, cord-like vessel, or electric radiating sensation is felt, stop pressing and reassess the location.以指腹轻触胸锁乳突肌后缘与下颌角水平的交界凹陷；若触到搏动、条索状血管或患者有电击样放射感，停止按压并重新定位。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Find the mandibular angle, then trace posteroinferiorly to the posterior sternocleidomastoid border. The hollow is only a rough guide and must remain away from the carotid pulse and deep anteromedial neck.先找下颌角，再向后下方寻找胸锁乳突肌后缘；凹陷位置只作粗略提示，必须远离颈动脉搏动和颈前内侧深部。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting anteromedially approaches the carotid sheath and internal jugular vein; deep insertion toward a pulse can cause serious neurovascular injury. Keep to a shallow posterolateral direction and stop for a pulse, abnormal radiation, or neck swelling.偏向前内侧会靠近颈动脉鞘及颈内静脉，向搏动处或深部直刺可能造成严重血管神经损伤；保持浅层向后外，出现搏动、异常放射感或颈部肿胀立即停止并评估。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

Consider TE16 for pain at the posterior sternocleidomastoid border tracking behind the ear or toward the temple; midline neck pain or radicular symptoms are not indications, and vascular risk is high.疼痛在胸锁乳突肌后缘、伴耳后或侧头牵扯时才考虑天牖；正中颈痛或神经根症状不能因邻近而选本穴，且其血管风险高。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

May be a local adjunct for tinnitus with lateral neck tension and Shaoyang signs; sudden hearing loss or abnormal neck pulsation needs medical care first.耳鸣兼颈侧筋肉紧张和少阳经循行不适时可作局部配穴；突发听力下降或颈部搏动异常先就医。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Sternocleidomastoid strain or cervicogenic pain胸锁乳突肌劳损或颈源性疼痛

Lateral neck pain after rotation or prolonged sitting may reflect myofascial strain; post-traumatic pain, arm numbness or weakness, or marked movement restriction requires cervical assessment first.转头或久坐后颈侧痛可见肌筋膜劳损；外伤后颈痛、上肢麻木无力或明显活动受限须先评估颈椎。

Carotid or jugular vascular disorder颈动脉或颈静脉相关病变
