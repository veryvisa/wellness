Chuanxiong Powder to be Taken with Green Tea川芎茶调散

Chuān Xiōng Chá Tiáo Sǎn · Taiping Huimin Heji Jufang (Song); anchor text from Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie宋《太平惠民和剂局方》；原文锚点取清·汪昂《医方集解》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Chuanxiong Powder to be Taken with Green Tea川芎茶调散
⚠️ Safety Notice Regarding Minor-Toxicity Herb (Asarum)含小毒药材提示（细辛） This formula contains asarum (traditionally classified as having minor toxicity, containing volatile oils and trace aristolochic acid analogs, with modern medicinal use restricted strictly to the root and rhizome). Direct ingestion as a powder without prolonged boiling carries higher toxicity risks than a decoction, and it is strictly incompatible with veratrum (Lilu). Individuals with liver or kidney impairment, pregnant patients, and children must only use it under the strict supervision of a licensed TCM practitioner; do not self-prepare or self-administer.本方含有细辛（传统标定「有小毒」，含挥发油及微量马兜铃酸类成分，现代药用仅限根及根茎）。作散剂直接冲服时成分不经久煎挥发，毒性风险较汤剂更高，且严禁与藜芦同用；肝肾功能不全者、孕妇及儿童须在执业中医师严格指导下权衡使用，切勿自行配制服用。

Original text原文

「治諸風上攻，正偏頭痛，恶風有汗，憎寒壯熱，鼻塞痰盛，頭晕目眩」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

For upward attack of wind pathogens causing bilateral or unilateral headache, aversion to wind with sweating, chills and high fever, nasal congestion with profuse phlegm, and dizziness.治各种风邪上攻引起的正头痛或偏头痛、怕风有汗、怕冷发热、鼻塞痰多、头晕目眩。

「薄荷（八錢）川芎荆介（四錢）羌活白芷甘草（炙。一錢）防風（錢半）細辛（一錢）」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie records the formula composition and dosages (in the original Jufang and today's textbook, Angelica dahurica is two qian and Asarum is adjusted to maintain a lower ratio relative to licorice).汪昂《医方集解》载本方组成及剂量（其中白芷在《局方》原方与今教材中为二钱，细辛原方为去芦一两、与甘草配比为1:2，今教材折算用量亦有调整）。

「羌活治太陽頭痛，白芷治陽明頭痛，川芎治少陽頭痛，細辛治少陰頭痛」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Wang Ang explains how each herb targets specific channel headaches and designates mint and schizonepeta as monarchs; today's textbook instead designates Sichuan lovage as monarch, notopterygium, angelica dahurica, and asarum as ministers, schizonepeta, saposhnikovia, and mint as assistants, and licorice with green tea as envoys.汪昂解释诸药分经引药治头痛之理，并以薄荷、荆芥为君；今教材则以川芎（善治少阳、厥阴头痛）为君，羌活、白芷、细辛为臣，荆芥、防风、薄荷为佐，甘草与清茶为使。

Cautions使用注意

1. Pattern contraindications. This formula is acrid, warm, aromatic, and drying, and applies only to externally contracted wind headache; do not use for internal headaches due to qi deficiency, blood deficiency, ascendant liver yang, liver-kidney yin deficiency, or phlegm-stasis. 2. Medication safety. The formula contains asarum (minor toxicity), which carries higher toxicity risks when ingested directly as a powder rather than a decoction; avoid excessive doses or prolonged use. 3. When to seek urgent medical care. Seek immediate emergency medical attention for sudden severe thunderclap headache, headache with high fever and neck stiffness, confusion, double vision, limb numbness or weakness, persistent vomiting, or post-traumatic headache to rule out cerebrovascular events, intracranial infection, or mass lesions.1. 证候禁忌：本方辛温香燥，仅适用于外感风邪头痛；若属气虚、血虚、肝阳上亢、肝肾阴虚或痰浊瘀血所致之内伤头痛，均不宜使用。2. 用药安全：方中含细辛（有小毒），作散剂吞服时马兜铃酸及挥发油毒性风险高于汤剂，不可超量或久服。3. 必须就医情形：突发剧烈头痛（如雷击样头痛）、头痛伴随高热颈项强直、意识模糊、视物重影、肢体麻木无力、呕吐不止或外伤后头痛，须立即前往急诊就医，排除脑血管意外、颅内感染或占位性病变。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Sichuan lovage, schizonepeta, angelica dahurica, notopterygium, field mint, asarum, and licorice all carry a pregnancy caution on this site (with asarum having minor toxicity and Sichuan lovage strongly moving qi and blood), so the formula takes the strictest level of its herbs.方中川芎、荆芥、白芷、羌活、薄荷、细辛、甘草在本站均列为孕期慎用（且细辛有小毒，川芎活血行气、辛温走窜），方随其最严者定为孕期慎用。
