  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width for the back and loins); BL26 is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun out, level with the lower edge of the L5 spine.肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰部横寸通用），关元俞在中点，旁开 1.5 寸；纵向在第 5 腰椎棘突下。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    The line across the iliac crests is about level with the L4 spine (GV3, BL25); the next spine down is L5, with the top of the sacrum just below.   3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Skin, subcutaneous tissue, thoracolumbar fascia, erector spinae and multifidus; deep lie the L5–S1 facet joint, the L5 transverse process and the iliolumbar ligament. This level is below the kidney; the main risks of deep needling are the L5 nerve root (electric pain down the leg) and periosteum. The iliac vessels in front of psoas are far beyond normal depth.皮肤、皮下组织、胸腰筋膜、竖脊肌与多裂肌；深面是 L5–S1 关节突关节、L5 横突与髂腰韧带。L5 平面已在肾下方，主要风险是深刺触及 L5 神经根（向下肢放电）与骨膜；更深的腰大肌前方有髂血管，正常深度到不了。

    Palpation check触诊确认

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Deep insertion with vigorous lifting and thrusting, continuing despite electric pain down the leg: the L5 root has been reached — withdraw at once. Textbooks insert obliquely toward the spine 0.5–1 cun (or perpendicularly 0.8–1.2 cun with controlled depth).深刺并大幅提插，出现向腿放射的触电感仍继续：已触及 L5 神经根，应立即退针。教材向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–1 寸（亦可直刺 0.8–1.2 寸，须控制深度）。
  • Taking L4 for L5: the iliac-crest line sometimes falls at the L4–L5 space, and one level off gives BL25. Count up from the top of the sacrum to check.把 L4 当 L5：髂嵴连线有时平 L4–L5 间隙，差一节就成了大肠俞。从骶骨上缘往上数核对。
  • Needling in pregnancy: this is a lumbosacral point, forbidden by textbooks after the third month; do not needle it in pregnancy.孕期针刺：腰骶部腧穴，教材规定怀孕三个月以上禁针，孕期一律不针。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Local point for lumbosacral junction pain, with BL23, BL25 and BL40; upper-lumbar pain leads with BL23 and BL22.腰骶交界痛的局部穴，配肾俞、大肠俞、委中；痛在腰上段以肾俞、三焦俞为主。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Warmed with moxa for chronic deficiency-cold diarrhoea, with CV4 and ST25; not used for acute diarrhoea.虚寒久泻加灸，配关元、天枢；急性腹泻不以它为主。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Added for frequent night urination or enuresis from lower-jiao cold, with CV4 and BL23; urgency, pain or blood in the urine needs infection and stones checked first.夜尿多、遗尿属下元虚寒者加用，配关元、肾俞；尿急尿痛、血尿先查感染与结石。

Low-back pain (L5–S1 strain, facet-joint pain)下腰痛（L5–S1 劳损、关节突关节痛）
