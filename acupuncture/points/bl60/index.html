Posture体位 · Side-lying or supine with the ankle relaxed and lateral malleolus exposed; do not actively tense the Achilles tendon.侧卧或仰卧，足踝放松；外踝朝上，避免主动绷紧跟腱。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate the lateral malleolar tip and lateral edge of the Achilles tendon. BL60 lies in the hollow between them, behind the malleolus. Dorsiflexion can outline the tendon; relax the ankle and confirm the site afterward.摸清外踝尖与跟腱外缘；昆仑在两者之间、外踝后方的凹陷中。脚踝背屈可让跟腱边界更清楚，定位后放松再确认。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The point is in the soft-tissue groove between the malleolus and Achilles tendon, near sural nerve branches, the small saphenous vein and calcaneal structures. Identify the tendon and bone edge, avoid superficial veins and nerve-sensitive sites, and do not drift anteriorly onto the peroneal tendons or needle deeply toward bone or tendon.穴在外踝与跟腱之间的软组织沟，附近有腓肠神经分支、小隐静脉及跟骨周围结构；辨清肌腱和骨缘，避开浅表静脉及神经放射敏感点。勿向前移到腓骨肌腱，也勿朝跟骨或跟腱深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate the hollow between the posteroinferior malleolus and Achilles tendon; local tenderness may occur. Stop and reassess if there is a clear pulse, cord-like vein or electric radiating sensation.以指腹轻触外踝后下缘至跟腱之间，凹陷处常有局部压痛；若触及明显血管搏动、条索状静脉或电击样放射，停止并重新辨认。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Slide behind the lateral malleolar tip to the anterior edge of the Achilles tendon; the shallow groove is BL60. BL62 is directly below the malleolus and GB40 is anteroinferior, so the three lie in different directions.沿外踝尖向后滑到跟腱前缘，指腹落入的浅沟为昆仑；外踝正下方是申脉，外踝前下方是丘墟，三者方向不同。
