GV29

印堂 Yìntáng

Hall of Impression · Lower risk较安全

📍 Location定位★

On the head, in the depression midway between the medial ends of the eyebrows.头部，两眉毛内侧端中间的凹陷中。

💡 Midpoint of the line joining the inner ends of the eyebrows; moved from extra points into the Governor Vessel by GB/T 12346-2006.两眉头连线的中点；GB/T 12346-2006 起由经外奇穴归入督脉。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Pinch up the skin, transverse downward提捏局部皮肤，向下平刺 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5 寸

Or prick to bleed with a three-edged needle.或用三棱针点刺出血。

🛡 Safety安全

Pinch up and needle subcutaneously toward the nasal root, never deep or toward either orbit; eclampsia and postpartum collapse are emergencies needing hospital care first.提捏后沿皮下向鼻根方向平刺，不向深部或两侧眼眶方向刺；子痫、产后血晕属急症，先送医。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Dementia, epilepsy, insomnia, poor memory痴呆、痫证、失眠、健忘等神志病证 ⚖️Headache and dizziness头痛、眩晕 ⚖️Nosebleed and sinusitis鼻衄、鼻渊 ⚖️Infantile convulsions, postpartum fainting from blood loss, eclampsia (as an adjunct)小儿惊风、产后血晕、子痫

  • With LI20 and LI4 for sinusitis and nasal congestion配迎香、合谷：鼻渊、鼻塞
  • With GB20 and GV20 for headache and dizziness配风池、百会：头痛、眩晕

    Procerus lies under the skin, directly over the nasal part of the frontal bone and the frontal sinus, with supratrochlear nerves and vessels on each side; tissue is thin, so pinch up the skin and needle subcutaneously.皮下为降眉间肌，深面紧贴额骨鼻部与额窦前壁，两侧有滑车上神经与血管；软组织很薄，必须提捏皮肤沿皮下平刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Pressing lightly between the brows reveals a shallow hollow above the frontonasal junction; frowning makes procerus bulge over the point.指腹轻按两眉头之间，可触到额骨与鼻骨交界上方的浅凹；皱眉时降眉间肌隆起处即穴区。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Taking it at the nasal root or toward one brow misplaces it; needling perpendicular without pinching hits the frontal bone, and drifting toward the inner canthus risks the angular vessels.把穴取在鼻根（山根）或偏向一侧眉头会偏离；不提捏直接直刺会碰到额骨，偏向眼眶内角有刺伤内眦血管的风险。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Blocked nose and sinusitis鼻塞鼻渊 ⚖️

For nasal blockage with fullness at the nasal root use GV29 locally with LI20 and LI4; one-sided persistent blockage, bloody discharge or facial numbness needs examination for a nasal mass first.鼻塞、鼻根胀闷时印堂取局部，配迎香、合谷；单侧持续阻塞、血涕或面部麻木先查鼻腔肿物。

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Choose GV29 for frontal or glabellar pressure without red flags; migraine or occipital pain is matched by location, and sudden severe pain goes to emergency first.前额、眉间胀痛且无红旗时可选印堂；偏头痛、枕部痛另按部位选穴，突发剧痛先急诊。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is GV29 Yìntáng?印堂（GV29）在哪里？

On the head, in the depression midway between the medial ends of the eyebrows.头部，两眉毛内侧端中间的凹陷中。

How is GV29 needled?印堂（GV29）怎么刺？

Pinch up the skin, transverse downward; 0.3–0.5 cun.提捏局部皮肤，向下平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at GV29?印堂（GV29）最要注意的安全问题？

Pinch up and needle subcutaneously toward the nasal root, never deep or toward either orbit; eclampsia and postpartum collapse are emergencies needing hospital care first.提捏后沿皮下向鼻根方向平刺，不向深部或两侧眼眶方向刺；子痫、产后血晕属急症，先送医。
