Hives and itching瘾疹（皮肤瘙痒）

The textbook prescription for hives has a shared core — LI11, LI4, SP10, BL17 and SP6 — plus points by pattern: red wheals worse with heat are wind-heat (GV14, GB20); pale wheals worse with cold are wind-cold (BL12, BL13); with abdominal pain, constipation or food triggers it is stomach and intestinal heat (ST25, ST44); recurrent wheals worse at night are blood deficiency with wind-dryness (BL20, ST36). Itch with no rash is a separate condition that first needs liver, kidney, thyroid, blood and drug causes checked. Swollen lips or tongue, wheeze or faintness is anaphylaxis: epinephrine first, then 911.瘾疹（荨麻疹）的教材处方有一组共用主穴——曲池、合谷、血海、膈俞、三阴交，再按证加穴：风团红、遇热重是风热，加大椎、风池；风团白、遇冷重是风寒，加风门、肺俞；伴腹痛便秘、饮食诱发是胃肠积热，加天枢、内庭；反复发作、夜间痒重是血虚风燥，加脾俞、足三里。没有皮疹只是痒，另归风瘙痒，要先查肝胆肾、甲状腺、血液病和药物。嘴唇舌头肿、喘、晕，是过敏性休克，先用肾上腺素、打 911。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Hives with swollen lips, tongue or throat, hoarseness, wheeze, trouble breathing, faintness, or repeated vomiting and belly pain: anaphylaxis. Inject an epinephrine auto-injector in the outer thigh and call 911, lie flat with legs raised (half-sitting if breathing is hard); do not wait for acupuncture or rely on antihistamines alone风团伴嘴唇、舌头或咽喉肿、声音嘶哑、喘鸣、呼吸困难、头晕晕厥或反复呕吐腹痛：过敏性休克，立即大腿外侧注射肾上腺素笔并拨 911，平躺抬腿（呼吸困难者可半坐），不等针灸也不只吃抗过敏药
  • Purple or red spots that do not blanch under pressure, especially with fever, joint pain, abdominal pain or marked fatigue: seek same-day medical assessment for vasculitis, infection or systemic disease.皮疹呈紫红色或出血点，按压不褪色，伴发热、关节痛、腹痛或明显乏力：当天接受医疗评估，排查血管炎、感染或其他系统性疾病。
  • An individual wheal remaining fixed for more than 24 hours, with pain, burning or residual bruising or pigmentation: seek prompt medical assessment for conditions such as urticarial vasculitis.单个风团持续固定超过 24 小时，伴疼痛、灼痛或消退后留下瘀斑、色素沉着：尽快就医，排查荨麻疹性血管炎等疾病。
  • Recurrent hives lasting more than 6 weeks, or accompanied by weight loss, persistent fever, night sweats or enlarged lymph nodes: arrange medical evaluation for chronic urticaria and associated systemic conditions.荨麻疹反复超过 6 周，或伴体重下降、持续发热、夜间盗汗、淋巴结肿大：预约医生评估慢性荨麻疹及相关系统性疾病。
  • New generalized itching without an obvious rash, especially with jaundice, dark urine, excessive thirst and urination, or palm-and-sole itching during pregnancy: seek medical assessment for liver, biliary, kidney, glucose or pregnancy-related cholestatic conditions.新出现的全身瘙痒，没有明显皮疹，尤其伴黄疸、尿色加深、明显口渴多尿或妊娠期间掌跖瘙痒：及时就医，检查肝胆、肾脏、血糖或妊娠相关胆汁淤积。
  • Blisters, painful peeling skin or sores of the mouth, eyes or genitals after a medicine, or whole-body redness with fever: emergency now for a severe drug reaction用药后出现水疱、皮肤疼痛脱皮、口眼生殖器黏膜糜烂，或全身红皮伴发热：立刻急诊，警惕严重药疹

「搏於皮膚之間，其氣外發，腠理開，毫毛搖，氣往來行，則為癢」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.75: when a deficiency pathogen lodges in the skin and its qi moves outward, the pores open, the fine hairs stir, and the qi moving back and forth becomes itch. Itch is wind moving in the surface, which is why LI11 and LI4 dispel wind there.《灵枢·刺节真邪》：虚邪搏结在皮肤之间，气向外发散，腠理开、毫毛动，气来回游走就成了痒。痒是风气在肌表走窜，所以用曲池、合谷祛风走表。

「癢者，陽也，淺刺之」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.9: itch is yang and lies at the surface, so needle shallowly. Needling for itch is shallow, light and brisk rather than deep and strong.《灵枢·终始》：痒属阳，在表，要浅刺。治痒的针法以浅、轻、疾为主，不追求深刺强刺激。

「偏風癮疹，喉痺，胸填滿，筋緩，手臂無力，皮膚枯燥，曲池」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu dacheng: for hemiplegia, hives, throat obstruction, a full chest, slack sinews, weak arms and dry skin, take LI11, draining first and then tonifying. LI11 is the first core point for hives in the textbook, clearing external wind and treating the skin.《针灸大成》：偏风、瘾疹、喉痹、胸中胀满、筋缓、手臂无力、皮肤枯燥，取曲池，先泻后补。曲池是教材瘾疹第一主穴，既清外风又治皮肤。

「天井治瘰癮疹」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu dacheng: TE10 treats scrofula and hives (the base text drops one character after the first). TE10, the Hand Shaoyang he-sea point, is a support for hives, not a core point.《针灸大成》：天井治瘰疬、瘾疹（底本「瘰」后缺「疬」字）。天井是手少阳合穴，可作瘾疹配穴，不是主穴。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

External外感

Wind-heat invading the exterior风热犯表

How it presents辨证要点 Acute onset with bright-red wheals, burning heat and intense itching, aggravated by warmth or sweating after exercise and relieved somewhat by cooling; may accompany fever, sore throat, thirst and restlessness; red tongue, thin yellow coating and floating rapid pulse.发病急，风团颜色鲜红，灼热明显，瘙痒剧烈，遇热或运动出汗后加重，遇凉稍缓；可伴发热、咽喉肿痛、口渴、烦躁；舌红，苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat, cool the blood and relieve itching (draining method; avoid excessive warming stimulation).疏风清热，凉血止痒（针用泻法，避免过度温热刺激）

Main主穴LI11 Qūchí曲池LI4 Hégǔ合谷SP10 Xuèhǎi血海BL17 Géshū膈俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎GB20 Fēngchí风池

💡 Textbook core for hives: LI11 and LI4 on the Hand Yangming dispel wind and clear heat at the surface; SP10 and BL17, the influential point of blood, regulate blood, since wind is treated by treating the blood; SP6 nourishes blood and strengthens the spleen. Wind-heat adds GV14, meeting of all yang, which may be bled to reduce heat, and GB20 to dispel wind. LI4 and SP6 are contraindicated in pregnancy, so other points are chosen.教材瘾疹主穴：曲池、合谷同在手阳明，疏风清热、走肌表；血海、膈俞（血会）调血，「治风先治血，血行风自灭」；三阴交养血健脾。风热加大椎（诸阳之会，可点刺出血退热）、风池祛风。合谷、三阴交孕妇禁针，孕期另选穴。

External外感

Wind-cold constraining the exterior风寒束表

How it presents辨证要点 Pale or skin-coloured wheals appearing or worsening after cold exposure, wind or contact with cold water, and improving with warmth; prominent itching without burning; may accompany chills, headache, nasal congestion and clear discharge; pale or light-red tongue, thin white coating and floating tight pulse.风团颜色淡白或接近肤色，遇冷、吹风、接触冷水后明显出现或加重，得温稍缓；瘙痒明显，皮肤不灼热；可伴恶寒、头痛、鼻塞、流清涕；舌淡或淡红，苔薄白，脉浮紧。

Principle治法 Dispel wind and cold and harmonize the nutritive and defensive qi (even method; gentle moxibustion may be considered for deficiency-cold).疏风散寒，调和营卫（针用平补平泻，虚寒者可酌情温灸）

Main主穴LI11 Qūchí曲池LI4 Hégǔ合谷SP10 Xuèhǎi血海BL17 Géshū膈俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门BL13 Fèishū肺俞

💡 Same core (LI11, LI4, SP10, BL17, SP6). Wind-cold adds BL12 and BL13: the lung governs skin and hair, so wind and cold are dispelled and the exterior secured; moxa allowed.主穴同上（曲池、合谷、血海、膈俞、三阴交）。风寒加风门、肺俞，肺主皮毛，祛风散寒、宣肺固表，可灸。

Internal内伤

Stomach and intestinal heat (damp-heat)胃肠积热（湿热）

How it presents辨证要点 Bright-red or dark-red recurrent wheals with intense itching; may accompany abdominal fullness, nausea, bitter or sticky mouth, thirst without desire to drink much, sticky stools or constipation, and dark urine; red tongue, yellow greasy coating and slippery rapid pulse. Often aggravated after spicy or greasy food or alcohol.风团颜色鲜红或暗红，瘙痒剧烈，皮疹反复；可伴脘腹胀满、恶心、口苦口黏、口渴不欲多饮、大便黏滞或便秘、小便黄；舌红，苔黄腻，脉滑数。常在进食辛辣、油腻、饮酒后加重。

Principle治法 Clear heat, drain dampness, harmonize the middle and relieve itching (draining method).清热利湿，和中止痒（泻法）

Main主穴LI11 Qūchí曲池LI4 Hégǔ合谷SP10 Xuèhǎi血海BL17 Géshū膈俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴ST25 Tiānshū天枢ST44 Nèitíng内庭ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

💡 Same core (LI11, LI4, SP10, BL17, SP6). Stomach–intestine heat adds ST25, the Large Intestine front-mu, to clear the bowels, ST44, the Stomach ying-spring point, to clear stomach heat, and ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, to harmonise. Food-related attacks call for a food diary; ST25 is not needled in pregnancy.主穴同上（曲池、合谷、血海、膈俞、三阴交）。胃肠积热加天枢（大肠募穴，通腑泄热）、内庭（胃经荥穴，清胃热）、足三里（胃经合穴，和胃）。发作与饮食相关的要记录食物；天枢孕妇不针。

Internal内伤

Blood deficiency with wind-dryness血虚风燥

How it presents辨证要点 Long-standing recurrent wheals or predominantly dry, flaky skin with scratch marks; itching is worse at night and worsens with scratching. Skin may appear pale, with dizziness, palpitations, a dull complexion, insomnia or scanty menstruation; pale tongue, scanty or thin white coating and thin weak pulse.病程较长，风团反复发作或皮肤以干燥、脱屑、抓痕为主，瘙痒夜间明显，越抓越痒；皮肤色泽偏淡，伴头晕、心悸、面色少华、失眠、月经量少；舌淡，苔少或薄白，脉细弱。

Principle治法 Nourish blood, moisten dryness, dispel wind and relieve itching (primarily tonifying, with gentle wind-dispersing treatment).养血润燥，祛风止痒（补法为主，兼顾疏风）

Dry winter itch in older people starts with moisturising, and itch that persists needs internal causes checked.主穴同上（曲池、合谷、血海、膈俞、三阴交）。血虚风燥加脾俞、足三里健脾生血；手法以补为主，曲池、合谷轻刺。老人冬季皮肤干痒先做好保湿，久痒不愈要查内科病因。

Itch without a rash风瘙痒（无疹瘙痒）

Internal damp-heat湿热内蕴

How it presents辨证要点 Itching without a primary rash, with scratch marks, weeping or eczema-like change after scratching, often on the legs, genitals or around the anus, worse at night or with sweat; bitter sticky mouth, dark urine; red tongue, yellow greasy coating, slippery rapid pulse.皮肤本无皮疹，瘙痒不止，抓后起抓痕、渗液或湿疹样变，下肢、会阴、肛周多见，夜间或出汗后加重；口苦口黏，小便黄；舌红，苔黄腻，脉滑数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, drain damp, dispel wind, stop itch (drain)清热利湿，祛风止痒（泻法）

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Pruritus is a symptom rather than a single disease. The first distinction is whether primary skin lesions are present. Wheals, erythema, papules, vesicles or scaling may suggest urticaria, contact dermatitis, eczema, scabies, drug reactions or infection. Generalized itching without an obvious primary rash warrants consideration of hepatobiliary disease, chronic kidney disease, thyroid disorders, iron deficiency, diabetes, hematologic disease and medication adverse effects. Typical urticaria produces raised, blanching, transient wheals. Individual wheals usually disappear within 24 hours, although new ones may appear elsewhere. Urticaria lasting more than 6 weeks is classified as chronic and warrants medical assessment. Modern medicine distinguishes spontaneous urticaria, inducible urticaria triggered by factors such as cold, heat, pressure, exercise or sweating, and other pruritic skin diseases. Chronic spontaneous urticaria may involve immune mechanisms; contact dermatitis is associated with irritants or allergens; scabies results from mite infestation. Treatment depends on the cause. Second-generation H1 antihistamines are commonly used for urticaria; acupuncture alone is not an adequate treatment. TCM patterns do not correspond one-to-one with biomedical diagnoses. Cold-triggered pale wheals may be considered within wind-cold; red, hot wheals aggravated by warmth may fit wind-heat; abdominal symptoms and greasy yellow coating may suggest gastrointestinal damp-heat; long-standing itching with dry skin, night-time aggravation and pallor may suggest blood deficiency with wind-dryness. The same chronic urticaria condition may present with different TCM patterns over time. These patterns should not be equated directly with biomedical causes. For chronic generalized itching or itching without primary skin lesions, clinicians may consider a complete blood count, ferritin, liver and kidney function, thyroid function and glucose testing according to the history. Suspected scabies, contact allergy or drug reactions require targeted assessment. Acute allergic reactions with breathing difficulty or swelling of the mouth, tongue or throat are emergencies requiring immediate treatment. Acupuncture may be considered as an adjunct for selected patients, but it does not replace emergency treatment, investigation of underlying causes or evidence-based medication.现代医学中的皮肤瘙痒（pruritus）是一种症状，不是单一疾病。首先区分有原发皮疹与没有原发皮疹。有风团、红斑、丘疹、水疱、鳞屑时，应考虑荨麻疹、接触性皮炎、湿疹、疥疮、药物反应、感染等；没有明显原发皮疹而全身瘙痒时，需要考虑肝胆疾病、慢性肾病、甲状腺疾病、缺铁、糖尿病、血液系统疾病及药物副作用。荨麻疹的典型表现是隆起、可褪色、短暂出现的风团，单个风团通常在 24 小时内消退，其他部位可能继续出现新风团。持续超过 6 周属于慢性荨麻疹，应由医生评估。现代医学按病因区分自发性荨麻疹、诱发性荨麻疹（如冷、热、压力、运动或出汗诱发）及其他瘙痒性皮肤病。慢性自发性荨麻疹可能与免疫机制有关；接触性皮炎与刺激物或过敏原有关；疥疮与疥螨感染有关。治疗需根据病因决定，荨麻疹常使用第二代 H1 抗组胺药，不能仅依靠针灸处理。中医证型与现代疾病诊断并非一一对应：遇冷诱发、风团淡白可在辨证时参考风寒束表；红热风团、遇热加重可参考风热犯表；伴腹胀、便黏、苔黄腻可参考胃肠湿热；长期瘙痒、皮肤干燥、夜间加重、面色淡可参考血虚风燥。同一种慢性荨麻疹可能在不同时间呈现不同证候，不能将风热、风寒等证型直接等同于现代医学的病因。检查方面，慢性全身瘙痒或没有原发皮疹者，医生可能根据病史安排血常规、铁蛋白、肝肾功能、甲状腺功能及血糖等检查；怀疑疥疮、接触性过敏或药物反应时，应针对性检查。急性过敏反应伴呼吸困难或口舌咽喉肿胀属于急症，应立即急救。针灸可作为部分患者的辅助管理方式，不能替代过敏性休克急救、病因检查或规范药物治疗。
