May accompany tension-type headache or scalp sensitivity; progressive/night pain or neurologic signs require assessment.可与紧张型头痛或头皮敏感同见；持续恶化、夜间痛或神经体征需另行评估。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is GV22 Xìnhuì?囟会（GV22）在哪里？

On the head, 2 cun directly above the anterior hairline.头部，前发际正中直上 2 寸。

How is GV22 needled?囟会（GV22）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at GV22?囟会（GV22）最要注意的安全问题？

This is the anterior fontanelle: it closes at about 1–2 years, and before that only membrane separates the scalp from the brain, so never needle; in adults needle transversely beneath the galea.正当前囟：婴幼儿前囟约 1–2 岁闭合，未闭时下方无骨、只隔膜即是颅内，禁针；成人平刺于腱膜下层。
