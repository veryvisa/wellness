ST36

足三里 Zúsānlǐ

Leg Three Miles · Take care需谨慎

He-sea (earth); lower he-sea of the Stomach合穴（土）；胃下合穴

📍 Location定位★

On the anterolateral leg, 3 cun below ST35, on the line connecting ST35 with ST41.小腿外侧，犊鼻下 3 寸，犊鼻与解溪连线上。

💡 3 cun (about four patient finger-breadths) below ST35, one finger-breadth lateral to the tibial crest. Knee flexed: measure down from ST35 along the ST35–ST41 line using the 16-cun span from the popliteal crease (patellar apex level) to the lateral malleolus, about one middle-finger breadth lateral to the anterior tibial crest.犊鼻下 3 寸（约患者四横指），胫骨前嵴外一横指（中指）；屈膝，从犊鼻（外膝眼）沿犊鼻与解溪连线向下，按腘横纹（平髌尖）至外踝尖 16 寸折量；距胫骨前嵴外侧约一横指（中指）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–2 cun1–2 寸

Mild moxibustion is commonly used for health maintenance.强壮保健常用温和灸。

🛡 Safety安全

The anterior tibial vessels and deep fibular nerve lie deep: redirect for tingling to the dorsum of the foot, and do not drive along the tibial surface.深部有胫前动静脉与腓深神经，出现向足背放射的麻感退针调整；不要贴胫骨骨面直刺。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Epigastric pain, vomiting, dysphagia, distension, diarrhoea, dysentery, constipation胃痛、呕吐、噎膈、腹胀、腹泻、痢疾、便秘 ⚖️Leg weakness and numbness下肢痿痹Mania癫狂Breast and intestinal abscess乳痈、肠痈Deficiency and exhaustion (key tonifying and health-care point)虚劳诸证（强壮保健要穴）

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is ST36 Zúsānlǐ?足三里（ST36）在哪里？

On the anterolateral leg, 3 cun below ST35, on the line connecting ST35 with ST41.小腿外侧，犊鼻下 3 寸，犊鼻与解溪连线上。

How is ST36 needled?足三里（ST36）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–2 cun.直刺；1–2 寸。

Special-point roles of ST36?足三里（ST36）是哪类特定穴？

He-sea (earth); lower he-sea of the Stomach合穴（土）；胃下合穴

Main safety point at ST36?足三里（ST36）最要注意的安全问题？

The anterior tibial vessels and deep fibular nerve lie deep: redirect for tingling to the dorsum of the foot, and do not drive along the tibial surface.深部有胫前动静脉与腓深神经，出现向足背放射的麻感退针调整；不要贴胫骨骨面直刺。
