CV12

中脘 Zhōngwǎn

Middle Cavity · Take care需谨慎

★Front-mu point of the Stomach胃募穴Influential point of the fu organs八会穴之腑会Crossing point of the Conception Vessel with Hand-Taiyang, Hand-Shaoyang and Foot-Yangming任脉、手太阳、手少阳、足阳明交会穴

📍 Location定位★

On the upper abdomen, 4 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 4 寸，前正中线上。

💡 Midpoint between the xiphisternal junction and the umbilicus.剑胸结合与脐中连线的中点。

🪡 Needling刺法

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

🛡 Safety安全

The stomach lies beneath and an enlarged left liver lobe may reach here: do not needle deep after a meal or with hepatosplenomegaly; severe epigastric pain or bloody vomit/black stool needs medical care first.深面为胃，肝大者肝左叶可下达此处：饱餐后、肝脾大者不深刺；上腹剧痛、呕血黑便先就医。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Epigastric pain, distension, poor appetite, vomiting, acid reflux, hiccups, infantile malnutrition胃痛、腹胀、纳呆、呕吐、吞酸、呃逆、小儿疳积等脾胃病 ⚖️Jaundice黄疸Mania, visceral agitation (zang zao)癫狂、脏躁

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine with knees flexed and the abdomen relaxed; a semi-reclined position may be used to recheck the xiphisternal-junction-to-umbilicus span.仰卧屈膝，腹壁放松；可半坐位复核剑胸联合至脐中骨度段。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The xiphisternal junction-to-umbilicus span is 8 cun; CV12 is four cun above the umbilicus, at the midpoint of the span.胸剑联合中点至脐中为 8 寸；中脘在脐上 4 寸，正居该段中点。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Measure four cun above the navel on the anterior midline, one cun below CV13 and one cun above CV11; check both the navel and xiphisternal junction.沿前正中线量脐上四寸，位于上脘下一寸、建里上一寸；体表位置以脐和剑胸结合共同校核。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The linea alba and abdominal wall are superficial; stomach, transverse colon and peritoneum lie deeper. Deep pressure can compress viscera, and lateral drift approaches abdominal-wall vessels; avoid deep pressure after meals and over scars.浅层是腹白线及腹壁，深部邻胃、横结肠及腹膜；按压过深会压迫脏器，偏外侧可能接近腹壁血管，避免饱餐后深压及瘢痕处操作。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the abdomen relaxed, gently palpate four cun above the navel. Tenderness does not diagnose stomach disease; stop immediately for rebound tenderness or guarding.腹壁放松时轻触脐上四寸；可询问局部压痛但不得据此确诊胃病，出现反跳痛或肌紧张立即停止。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Confusing CV13 at five cun or CV11 at three cun with CV12 usually reflects poor endpoint identification; confirm both xiphisternal junction and navel.将上脘（脐上五寸）或建里（脐上三寸）误作中脘，常由骨度段两端定位不清造成；剑胸结合与脐都要确认。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

A leading point for midline epigastric fullness or post-meal discomfort because it is the Stomach front-mu and fu influential point; distinguish subxiphoid cardiac or right-upper-quadrant pain.脐上正中、胃脘痞满或食后不适时优先，因其胃募穴与腑会属性；剑突下心痛或偏肝胆区痛需鉴别。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

Choose for nausea and vomiting with epigastric fullness and Stomach counterflow, often with PC6 and ST36; blood or obstruction signs need care first.胃气上逆、恶心呕吐兼中脘痞满时选，常配内关、足三里；血性呕吐或梗阻表现先就医。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Pair for diarrhea with epigastric fullness or poor appetite in a Spleen–Stomach pattern; acute dehydration, blood or infection signs need cause-directed care.脾胃不和、腹泻兼胃脘胀或食少时配用；急性脱水、血便或感染征象先处理病因。

Hiccups呃逆

Choose with PC6 for hiccups with epigastric fullness or nausea; persistent hiccups or chest pain or dysphagia needs assessment.呃逆伴胃脘痞满或恶心时选并配内关；持续呃逆或伴胸痛、吞咽困难须评估。

Where is CV12 Zhōngwǎn?中脘（CV12）在哪里？

On the upper abdomen, 4 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 4 寸，前正中线上。

How is CV12 needled?中脘（CV12）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of CV12?中脘（CV12）是哪类特定穴？

Front-mu point of the Stomach; Influential point of the fu organs; Crossing point of the Conception Vessel with Hand-Taiyang, Hand-Shaoyang and Foot-Yangming胃募穴；八会穴之腑会；任脉、手太阳、手少阳、足阳明交会穴

Main safety point at CV12?中脘（CV12）最要注意的安全问题？

The stomach lies beneath and an enlarged left liver lobe may reach here: do not needle deep after a meal or with hepatosplenomegaly; severe epigastric pain or bloody vomit/black stool needs medical care first.深面为胃，肝大者肝左叶可下达此处：饱餐后、肝脾大者不深刺；上腹剧痛、呕血黑便先就医。
