Major Bupleurum Decoction大柴胡汤

Dà Chái Hú Tāng · Shanghan Lun by Zhang Zhongjing (Eastern Han dynasty); the textual anchors are the Major Bupleurum Decoction passage and its formula composition in the Shanghan Lun.东汉·张仲景《伤寒论》；原文锚点取《伤寒论》大柴胡汤条文及方剂组成。

⚠️ Pinellia Toxicity and Medication Safety半夏毒性与用药安全 Pinellia has moderate toxicity. Raw Pinellia is particularly irritating and potentially toxic and must not be collected, processed, or ingested independently. Only appropriately processed Pinellia should be considered, under the supervision of a qualified practitioner. Do not increase the dose, extend the duration of use, or combine this formula with other preparations without professional assessment. Significant oral or throat irritation, numbness, swelling, difficulty swallowing, or breathing difficulty requires immediate medical attention.半夏具有中等毒性，生半夏尤其具有刺激性和潜在毒性，不可自行采集、加工或内服。方中半夏须采用符合规范的炮制品，并由合格中医师判断是否适用。不得自行增加剂量、延长使用时间或与其他方药随意合用。出现口腔或咽喉明显刺痛、麻木、肿胀、吞咽困难或呼吸困难，应立即停止进一步接触并寻求紧急医疗帮助。

Original text原文

「太陽病，過經十餘日，反二三下之。後四五日，柴胡證仍在者，先與小柴胡湯；嘔不止，心下急，鬱鬱微煩者，為未解也，與大柴胡湯下之則愈。」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

When a Taiyang disorder persists for more than ten days and has been purged repeatedly, Shaoyang signs may remain. If vomiting continues with epigastric tightness and vexation, the Shanghan Lun indicates Major Bupleurum Decoction as the subsequent formula.太阳病超过十余日，曾经反复攻下二三次。之后四五日，若仍有柴胡证，先用小柴胡汤；若呕吐不止、心下拘急、烦闷不舒，说明病邪尚未解除，可用大柴胡汤攻下。

「柴胡半斤黃芩三兩芍藥三兩半夏半升（洗）生薑五兩（切）枳實四枚（炙）大棗十二枚（擘）」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

The original prescription lists seven ingredients: Bupleurum, Scutellaria, peony, Pinellia, fresh ginger, immature bitter orange, and jujube, with their respective quantities and processing instructions.原文列出七味药：柴胡、黄芩、芍药、半夏、生姜、枳实、大枣，并分别注明剂量及部分炮制方法。

「一方，加大黃二兩。若不加，恐不爲大柴胡湯。」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

An alternative version adds two liang of rhubarb. The original commentary emphasizes that omitting rhubarb may mean the prescription does not constitute the complete Major Bupleurum Decoction, reflecting a textual difference among historical versions.另一版本加入大黄二两。原文注语特别强调，不加大黄可能不足以构成完整的大柴胡汤。这反映了古代传本中关于大黄是否为方中组成的差异。

Cautions使用注意

This formula combines Shaoyang harmonization with purging of internal heat accumulation. It is traditionally considered for Shaoyang-Yangming concurrent patterns with signs of internal excess, and should not be used solely on the basis of an isolated symptom. People with weak digestion, deficient constitution, persistent diarrhea, significant dehydration, low blood pressure, or frailty require assessment by a qualified practitioner. Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals, children, and people taking other medications should consult a physician or qualified practitioner before use. Persistent or severe abdominal pain, repeated vomiting, bloody or black stools, high fever, altered consciousness, or significant dehydration require prompt medical assessment. Sudden severe abdominal pain, fainting, or altered consciousness requires emergency medical attention.本方具有和解少阳、内泻热结的配伍特点，适用于少阳阳明合病、兼见里实表现的辨证情形，不宜仅凭单一症状自行使用。脾胃虚弱、正气不足、持续腹泻、明显脱水、低血压或体质虚弱者，应由合格中医师评估。孕妇、哺乳期人士、儿童及正在服用其他药物者，应先咨询医生或合格中医师。出现持续或剧烈腹痛、反复呕吐、便血、黑便、高热、意识改变或明显脱水，应及时就医；突发剧烈腹痛、晕厥或意识障碍应立即寻求急诊帮助。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Scutellaria, Pinellia, immature bitter orange, and rhubarb carry pregnancy cautions. Pinellia also has moderate toxicity. Following the strictest individual-herb safety classification, this formula is classified as requiring caution during pregnancy and should not be self-administered. Suitability during pregnancy must be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.方中黄芩、半夏、枳实、大黄均列孕期慎用；半夏另有中等毒性。按方中最严格的单味药安全等级，本方列为孕期慎用，不应自行使用。孕期是否适用须由具备资质的医疗专业人员评估。
