📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or reclining with the head supported, eyes closed and the brow relaxed.仰卧或靠坐，头枕稳，闭目，眉部放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Follow the inner end of the eyebrow toward the root of the nose: under the head of the brow the upper orbital rim has a small notch, the frontal notch. BL2 lies in the hollow at the head of the eyebrow, over that notch (GB/T 12346-2021).沿眉毛内端往鼻根方向摸，眉头下方眶上缘有一个小凹陷——额切迹。攒竹就在眉头凹陷中、额切迹处（GB/T 12346-2021）。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Under the skin lie orbicularis oculi, corrugator supercilii and frontalis; the supratrochlear nerve and vessels emerge at the frontal notch, and about a finger-width lateral (on the pupil line) is the supraorbital notch with its own nerve and vessels. Directly under the point is the bony orbital rim, with the orbit below and behind it.皮下依次是眼轮匝肌、皱眉肌和额肌；额切迹处有滑车上神经和滑车上动、静脉穿出，更外侧约一横指（瞳孔线上）是眶上切迹和眶上神经血管。穴下一步就是眶上缘骨面，再往下深处是眼眶。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a fingertip up and down at the head of the brow and a small dip in the orbital rim can be felt, usually achy; pressing hard sends tingling up the forehead — that is the supratrochlear nerve: right spot, too much pressure.指尖在眉头轻轻上下滑动，能摸到眶上缘的一个小缺口，按之酸胀；压重了会有向前额放射的麻感——那是滑车上神经，位置对、但压力太大。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Frown: corrugator bunches at the head of the brow; the small dimple at its inner lower end, once relaxed, is the spot. A beginner's shortcut only; the frontal notch decides.皱眉时眉头隆起的肌肉（皱眉肌）内下端，放松后留下的小窝即是。只作入门找法，以额切迹为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Inserting deep and downward into the orbit: once past the rim the tip is in the orbit and can injure the globe or orbital vessels. BL2 is needled only transversely along the skin, never into the orbit.向下、向眶内深刺：针尖越过眶上缘就进入眼眶，有伤及眼球与眶内血管的危险；攒竹只沿皮平刺，不往眶里扎。
  • Not pressing after withdrawal: the notch is vascular, so bruising of the brow and upper lid is common. Press with dry cotton for about a minute after removing the needle.出针不按压：额切迹处血管丰富，易出现眉头、上眼睑青紫；出针后用干棉球按压 1 分钟左右。
  • Taking the middle of the brow (Yuyao) or the supraorbital notch: a finger-width too lateral — that is the extra point Yuyao and the supraorbital nerve exit, not BL2.取到眉中（鱼腰）或眶上切迹：偏外了一横指，那是经外奇穴鱼腰与眶上神经出口，不是攒竹。

Supratrochlear or supraorbital neuralgia滑车上神经痛、眶上神经痛

Sharp pain and tenderness at the inner or middle brow spreading up the forehead; common after tight goggles, helmet pressure or a blow to the forehead. Shingles of the ophthalmic branch can start as forehead nerve pain before the rash.眉头或眉中一点刺痛、触痛，可向前额放射；戴紧泳镜、头盔压迫或额部外伤后常见。眼支带状疱疹在出疹前也可只表现为额部神经痛。

Peripheral facial palsy (Bell palsy)周围性面瘫（贝尔麻痹）

One side loses its forehead lines, the eye will not close and the mouth droops. Loss of forehead lines points to a peripheral palsy; a drooping mouth with forehead lines spared raises concern for stroke.一侧额纹消失、闭眼不全、口角歪斜；额纹也消失的是周围性，额纹保留而口角歪斜要警惕中风。

Tension-type headache and eye strain with an aching inner brow紧张型头痛、视疲劳伴眉头酸胀

Tight inner brow and forehead after long screen use; rule out uncorrected refractive error and glaucoma first.长时间看屏幕后眉头、前额发紧；先排除屈光不正和青光眼。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Drooping mouth with normal forehead lines, or with limb weakness or slurred speech: possible stroke, call 911口角歪斜但额纹正常，或伴肢体无力、说话含糊：疑中风，立即拨 911
  • Eye pain with blurred vision, halos around lights and nausea: possible acute glaucoma, go to emergency now眼痛伴视力下降、看灯有彩虹圈、恶心：疑急性青光眼，立即急诊
  • Clustered blisters on the forehead or upper lid, or a rash on the tip of the nose: ophthalmic shingles can involve the eye, see a doctor the same day额部、上眼睑出现成簇水疱或鼻尖起疹：眼支带状疱疹可累及眼球，当天就医

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is BL2 Cuánzhú?攒竹（BL2）在哪里？

In the depression at the medial end of the eyebrow, near the supraorbital notch.眉头内侧端凹陷处，眶上切迹附近。

How is BL2 needled?攒竹（BL2）怎么刺？

Transverse laterally along the eyebrow; 0.3–0.5 cun.向外或向眉中平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at BL2?攒竹（BL2）最要注意的安全问题？

Supraorbital neurovascular structures and the orbit are adjacent; do not insert deeply downward into the orbit.眶上神经血管和眼眶邻近；不向下深刺入眶。
