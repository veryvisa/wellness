🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Superficial fibular nerve entrapment腓浅神经卡压综合征（Superficial fibular nerve entrapment）

Caused by fascial band compression where the nerve pierces the crural fascia or traction from recurrent ankle sprains; presents with numbness, burning, or tingling over the distal lateral leg and dorsum of the foot, worsened by plantarflexion-inversion, with a positive Tinel sign above GB39. Differentiate from L5 radiculopathy (which typically involves low back pain, positive straight-leg raise, and weak great-toe extension, whereas superficial fibular entrapment spares motor strength and has a focal Tinel sign).神经穿出小腿深筋膜处受筋膜束带卡压或踝关节反复扭伤牵拉所致，表现为小腿下 1/3 外侧及足背麻木、烧灼感或刺痛，足跖屈内翻时加重，叩击悬钟上方深筋膜穿出点可诱发向足背放射的 Tinel 征。需与腰 5（L5）神经根病鉴别（L5 神经根病常伴腰痛、直腿抬高试验阳性及踇背伸肌力减弱，而腓浅神经卡压无肌力减退且局部 Tinel 征阳性）。

Fibularis (peroneus) longus and brevis tendinopathy or subacute lateral ankle sprain pain腓骨长、短肌肌腱病或急性外踝扭伤恢复期疼痛

After overuse or inversion ankle sprain, aching and swelling develop along the posterolateral fibula and lateral malleolus, aggravated by resisted foot eversion. If acute trauma produces bony tenderness over the malleolus and inability to bear weight for four steps (positive Ottawa Ankle Rules), obtain an X-ray first to rule out distal fibular fracture before needling or massaging.运动过度或踝内翻扭伤后，沿腓骨外后侧及外踝后下方出现酸痛、肿胀，抗阻足外翻时疼痛加剧。若外伤后出现外踝骨性压痛且无法负重行走 4 步（Ottawa 踝关节规则阳性），须先拍 X 光排除腓骨远端骨折，不可直接针刺或推拿局部。

Chronic lateral compartment myofascial pain and trigger points小腿外侧筋膜室慢性劳损与肌筋膜触发点疼痛

Prolonged running, gait imbalance, or stiff footwear strains fibularis brevis and extensor digitorum longus, producing taut bands with referred lateral ankle pain. If severe tight bursting pain, shiny tense skin, and dorsal foot numbness develop after exercise, suspect compartment syndrome and refer urgently.长期跑步、步行姿势异常或穿硬底鞋导致腓骨短肌、趾长伸肌紧张，局部可触及条索状结节并向踝外侧牵涉痛。若出现运动后小腿外侧剧烈紧胀痛、皮肤发亮紧绷及足背麻木，需警惕慢性或急性骨筋膜室综合征，属急症指征。
