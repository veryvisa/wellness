Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋

First separate sudden from chronic: hearing that drops within 72 hours is an ear emergency, and early treatment gives the best chance of recovery, so do not wait on acupuncture. Chronic tinnitus is then sorted into excess — sudden, loud as a tide, not eased by pressing (external wind, liver-gallbladder fire, phlegm-fire) — and deficiency — long-standing, fine as cicadas, eased by pressing (kidney essence or spleen-stomach deficiency). The textbook uses GB2 and TE17 with the distal Shaoyang points TE3 and GB43 for excess, and SI19 and TE17 with KI3 and BL23 for deficiency. Both Shaoyang channels enter the ear from behind it, so distal points are mostly Shaoyang; "the kidney opens into the ear", so deficiency is treated through the kidney.耳鸣耳聋先分急缓：72 小时内突然听不见是耳科急症，越早用药越可能恢复，不能先针灸观察。慢性耳鸣再分虚实——暴起、声大如潮、按之不减的多实（外感风邪、肝胆火旺、痰火），久病、声细如蝉、按之可减的多虚（肾精亏虚、脾胃虚弱）。教材实证取听会、翳风加手足少阳远端的中渚、侠溪；虚证取听宫、翳风加太溪、肾俞。因为手足少阳经都「从耳后入耳中」，耳病远端多取少阳；因为「肾开窍于耳」，虚证多取肾。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Hearing that drops in one or both ears within 72 hours, often with fullness, tinnitus or vertigo: emergency or ENT the same day — sudden hearing loss needs early steroids, which work far less well after two weeks.72 小时内一侧或两侧听力突然下降（常伴耳闷、耳鸣、眩晕）：当天急诊或耳鼻喉科——突发性耳聋要尽早用激素，两周后效果明显变差。
  • Tinnitus or hearing loss with slurred speech, weakness or numbness, facial droop, unsteady walking or sudden severe headache: call 911 to exclude stroke.耳鸣或听力下降伴说话不清、肢体无力麻木、面歪、走路不稳或突发剧烈头痛：立即拨 911，排除卒中。
  • Tinnitus that beats with the pulse: prompt medical review with vascular imaging; with blurred vision or headache (raised pressure) the same day.耳鸣跟着心跳搏动：尽快就医做血管和影像检查；伴视物模糊、头痛（颅内压高）当天就医。
  • Hearing loss, bleeding or clear fluid from the ear after a head injury: emergency department now to exclude skull-base fracture.头部外伤后听力下降、耳内流血或流清水样液体：立即急诊，排除颅底骨折。
  • Ear pain with fever, redness and swelling behind the ear pushing it forward (mastoiditis), persistent discharge, or severe ear pain in a diabetic: see a doctor the same day.耳痛发热、耳后红肿把耳朵向前推开（乳突炎），或耳流脓不止、糖尿病人耳部剧痛：当天就医。
  • Persistent one-sided tinnitus or hearing loss, or with facial numbness or poor balance: see ENT to exclude an acoustic neuroma.持续的单侧耳鸣或单侧听力下降，或伴面部麻木、平衡差：看耳鼻喉科，排除听神经瘤。
  • Tinnitus that wrecks sleep or mood with thoughts of suicide: call 988 (Canada's suicide crisis line) or 911.耳鸣严重影响睡眠情绪、有轻生念头：拨 988（加拿大自杀危机热线）或 911。

「髓海不足，則腦轉耳鳴」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.33: when the sea of marrow is insufficient, the head spins and the ears ring (the text goes on to aching shins and dizziness). The kidney governs bone and makes marrow — the basis for tonifying kidney essence in this kind of tinnitus.《灵枢·海论》：脑髓不足，就头晕目转、耳鸣（后文还说小腿酸软、眩晕）。肾主骨生髓，这是肾精亏虚型耳鸣补肾填精的依据。

「耳者，宗脈之所聚也，故胃中空則宗脈虛，虛則下溜，脈有所竭者，故耳鳴」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.28: the ear is where many vessels gather; when the stomach is empty the vessels become deficient, qi sinks, and the vessels above are exhausted, so the ears ring. This is tinnitus from stomach-qi deficiency — the textbook's spleen-stomach deficiency pattern.《灵枢·口问》：耳朵是众多经脉汇聚的地方，胃里空虚、经脉就空虚，气向下流，上面的脉枯竭，所以耳鸣。这说的是脾胃气虚也会耳鸣，正是教材虚证中「脾胃虚弱」一型的依据。

「聾而不痛，取足少陽；聾而痛，取手陽明」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi, book 12: deafness without pain, use the foot Shaoyang; deafness with pain, use the hand Yangming — the channel is chosen by whether the ear hurts. Today ear pain first needs a check for infection.《针灸甲乙经》卷十二：耳聋不痛取足少阳经，耳聋又痛取手阳明经。说明古人已按有没有耳痛来分经；耳痛在今天首先要查感染。

「耳聾取手小指次指爪甲上與肉交者，先取手，後取足」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.24: for deafness use TE1 beside the ring-finger nail, first the hand channel and then the foot channel. The Triple Energizer channel enters the ear from behind, so the hand Shaoyang comes first; the textbook uses TE3 on the same channel.《灵枢·厥病》：耳聋取无名指指甲旁的关冲（三焦经井穴），先取手经，后取足经。三焦经「从耳后入耳中」，所以耳病远端先取手少阳；教材用的是同经的中渚。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Excess实证

External wind外感风邪

How it presents辨证要点 After a cold: sudden tinnitus, reduced hearing or a full blocked ear, stuffy nose, headache, fever or chills; thin coating, floating pulse. A sudden drop in hearing is treated as sudden deafness first.感冒后突然耳鸣、听力下降或耳胀闷；鼻塞，头痛，发热或怕冷；苔薄，脉浮。听力骤降须先按突发性耳聋就医。

Principle治法 Expel wind, free the collaterals and open hearing (drain)疏风散邪，通络聪耳（泻法）

Main主穴GB2 Tīnghuì听会TE17 Yìfēng翳风TE3 Zhōngzhǔ中渚GB43 Xiáxī侠溪
Add配穴GB20 Fēngchí风池TE5 Wàiguān外关

💡 Main set: GB2 in front of and TE17 behind the ear free local qi and blood; TE3 is the Triple Energizer shu-stream and GB43 the Gallbladder ying point; both Shaoyang channels enter the ear, so the two distal points are paired. For external wind add GB20 to expel it and TE5, opening the Yang Wei, to release the exterior.主穴：听会、翳风在耳前耳后，疏通耳周气血；中渚是三焦经输穴、侠溪是胆经荥穴，手足少阳经都入耳中，远端同名经相配。外感加风池祛风、外关解表（通阳维）。

Excess实证

Liver-gallbladder fire肝胆火旺

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden roaring tinnitus like tides or thunder, or sudden deafness; headache, red face, bitter mouth, dry throat, irritability (worse with anger), flank fullness, constipation; red tongue, yellow coating, wiry rapid pulse.突发耳鸣如潮声、风雷声，或暴聋；头痛面赤，口苦咽干，急躁易怒，怒则加重，胁胀，便秘；舌红苔黄，脉弦数。

Principle治法 Drain liver-gallbladder fire, free the collaterals and open the ear (drain)清泻肝胆，通络开窍（泻法）

Main主穴GB2 Tīnghuì听会TE17 Yìfēng翳风TE3 Zhōngzhǔ中渚GB43 Xiáxī侠溪
Add配穴LR2 Xíngjiān行间GB40 Qiūxū丘墟

💡 LR2, the Liver ying point, clears liver fire; GB40, the Gallbladder yuan point, drains fire from that channel.行间是肝经荥穴，清肝火；丘墟是胆经原穴，泻胆经之火。

Excess实证

Phlegm-fire痰火郁结

How it presents辨证要点 Constant tinnitus like cicadas or steam, a blocked ear, heavy dizzy head, chest fullness with phlegm, bitter mouth, unsatisfying stools; red tongue, greasy yellow coating, slippery rapid pulse.耳鸣不断、声如蝉或如蒸汽，耳闷如堵，头昏重，胸闷痰多，口苦，大便不爽；舌红苔黄腻，脉滑数。

Principle治法 Clear and transform phlegm-heat, free the collaterals and open the ear (drain)清化痰热，通络开窍（泻法）

Main主穴GB2 Tīnghuì听会TE17 Yìfēng翳风TE3 Zhōngzhǔ中渚GB43 Xiáxī侠溪
Add配穴ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉

💡 ST40 transforms phlegm and SP9 drains damp at their middle-burner source, while the Shaoyang main points open the ear.丰隆化痰，阴陵泉利湿，从中焦清痰湿之源；少阳主穴负责通耳。

Deficiency虚证

Kidney essence deficiency肾精亏虚

How it presents辨证要点 Fine cicada-like tinnitus, clearer at night and worse with fatigue, or gradual hearing loss, slightly eased by pressing the ear; sore back and knees, dizziness, insomnia, early ageing. Yin type: red tongue, little coating, thin rapid pulse; qi type: pale tongue, deep weak pulse.耳鸣细如蝉、入夜明显、劳累加重，或听力渐减，按压耳部可稍减；腰膝酸软，头晕，失眠，早衰。偏阴虚舌红少苔、脉细数；偏气虚舌淡、脉沉弱。

Principle治法 Tonify the kidney, fill essence and open the ear (tonify; moxa for the qi type)补肾填精，聪耳开窍（补法；气虚可灸）

Main主穴SI19 Tīnggōng听宫TE17 Yìfēng翳风KI3 Tàixī太溪BL23 Shènshū肾俞
Add配穴CV6 Qìhǎi气海BL18 Gānshū肝俞

💡 SI19, before the tragus, is the meeting point of the Small Intestine, hand and foot Shaoyang and the key local ear point; TE17 is local too; KI3, the Kidney yuan, with BL23, the Kidney back-shu, tonifies the kidney, which opens into the ear. Add CV6 (moxa) for kidney qi deficiency or BL18 for liver-kidney yin deficiency. CV6 and BL23 lie on the abdomen and lumbar region and are not needled in pregnancy.听宫在耳屏前、手足少阳与手太阳交会，是耳病局部要穴；翳风同为局部；太溪是肾经原穴，肾俞是肾背俞，俞原相配补肾，「肾开窍于耳」。肾气不足加气海（宜灸），肝肾阴虚加肝俞。气海、肾俞属腹部腰骶，孕妇不针。

Deficiency虚证

Spleen-stomach deficiency脾胃虚弱

How it presents辨证要点 Tinnitus that varies, worse with exertion and better with rest, or a hollow cold feeling in the ear on standing; fatigue, poor appetite, bloating, loose stools, sallow face; pale tongue, thin coating, weak pulse.耳鸣时轻时重，遇劳加重、休息减轻，或突然站起时耳内空虚发凉；神疲乏力，食少腹胀，便溏，面色萎黄；舌淡苔薄，脉弱。

Principle治法 Strengthen the spleen, lift yang qi and open the ear (tonify; moxa allowed)健脾益气，升阳聪耳（补法，可灸）

Main主穴SI19 Tīnggōng听宫TE17 Yìfēng翳风KI3 Tàixī太溪BL23 Shènshū肾俞
Add配穴CV6 Qìhǎi气海ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里BL20 Píshū脾俞

💡 The textbook adds CV6 and ST36 for spleen-stomach deficiency; BL20 is added here. CV6 tonifies qi and ST36 strengthens the spleen and stomach and helps lift clear qi, matching the Lingshu's "an empty stomach leaves the vessels deficient". CV6 is forbidden in pregnancy, and BL20 is needled obliquely toward the spine.教材脾胃虚弱加气海、足三里，这里再加脾俞：气海补气，足三里健脾胃、助升清，正应《灵枢》「胃中空则宗脉虚」。气海孕妇禁针，脾俞斜刺向脊柱。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Tinnitus most often goes with hearing loss (age, noise) and also with earwax, middle-ear infection, jaw-joint problems, medicines (high-dose aspirin, some antibiotics, diuretics, chemotherapy), anxiety and poor sleep. Persistent tinnitus needs an audiogram; one-sided tinnitus or asymmetric hearing loss needs an acoustic neuroma excluded, and pulsatile tinnitus a vascular check. Sudden sensorineural hearing loss is one of the few ear emergencies: guidelines advise early steroids, with salvage treatment possible for some weeks. What helps chronic tinnitus is hearing aids (when hearing is reduced), cognitive behavioural therapy, sound therapy, noise protection and better sleep. Randomised trials of acupuncture for tinnitus conflict, so never promise restored hearing; it may be an adjunct to ease the distress.耳鸣最常与听力下降相关（老年性、噪声性），也见于耳垢堵塞、中耳炎、颞下颌关节问题、药物（大剂量阿司匹林、某些抗生素、利尿剂、化疗药）和焦虑失眠。持续耳鸣要做听力图；单侧持续耳鸣或不对称听力下降要排除听神经瘤，搏动性耳鸣要查血管。突发性感音神经性聋是少数几个耳科急症之一，指南建议尽早用激素，补救治疗可到数周。慢性耳鸣的有效做法是助听器（合并听力下降时）、认知行为治疗、声音治疗、防噪声与改善睡眠。针灸治疗耳鸣的随机试验结论不一致，不宜承诺恢复听力；可以作为减轻困扰的辅助。
