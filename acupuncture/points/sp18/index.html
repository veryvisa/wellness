SP18

天溪 Tiānxī

Celestial Ravine · High risk高危

⚠️ Pneumothorax risk point气胸风险穴 SP18 lies in the 4th intercostal space 6 cun lateral where the chest wall is thin; pleura and lung are just beyond the intercostal muscles. Insert only obliquely or transversely outward 0.5–0.8 cun—never perpendicular, deep medially or with wide lifting-thrusting. Chest pain, breathlessness, worsening cough or stabbing pain on breathing after needling needs urgent assessment for pneumothorax.天溪在第 4 肋间隙旁开 6 寸，胸壁薄，肋间肌后即胸膜与肺。只斜刺或向外平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不直刺、不向内深刺、不大幅提插。针后出现胸痛、气短、咳嗽加重或呼吸时刺痛，立即就医排查气胸。

📍 Location定位★

On the lateral chest, in the 4th intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 4 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

💡 One intercostal space above SP17 on the same 6-cun line; in men roughly level with and 2 cun lateral to the nipple, in women rely on counting the spaces.在食窦上一肋间，同在旁开 6 寸线上；男性约平乳头水平外 2 寸，女性以肋间计数为准。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse outward斜刺或向外平刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never needle deeply.不可深刺。

🛡 Safety安全

Pleura and lung lie just deep to the intercostal muscles and the chest wall is thin here: insert only obliquely or transversely outward, never perpendicularly or deep medially—risk of pneumothorax; avoid the intercostal vessels and nerve.肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺，此处胸壁薄：只斜刺或向外平刺，不直刺、不向内深刺，有气胸风险；避开肋间血管神经。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Intercostal neuralgia and chest-wall myofascial pain肋间神经痛与胸壁肌筋膜痛

Stabbing or burning pain along the 4th intercostal space, worse with deep breaths, coughing or turning, with local tenderness; clustered blisters suggest shingles. Exclude cardiopulmonary emergencies first.沿第 4 肋间的刺痛、灼痛，深吸气、咳嗽或转身加重，局部压痛明显；若见成簇水疱考虑带状疱疹。胸痛须先排除心肺急症。

Lactational milk stasis and mastitis哺乳期乳汁淤积与乳腺炎

Local breast fullness and lumps; mastitis adds redness, heat, pain and fever. Fever, signs of abscess or no improvement in 24 hours needs medical care—abscesses need drainage, and the lesion itself is never needled.乳房局部胀痛、硬块，乳腺炎可伴红肿热痛与发热；发热、脓肿迹象或 24 小时不缓解须就医，脓肿需引流，不在病灶上针刺。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Chest pain, breathlessness, pain on inhaling or persistent dry cough after needling: suspect pneumothorax, seek care now (call 911 in BC)针后胸痛、气短、吸气痛或持续干咳：警惕气胸，立即就医（BC 拨 911）
  • A painless breast lump, skin dimpling or bloody nipple discharge: breast imaging first to exclude cancer乳房无痛肿块、皮肤凹陷、乳头血性溢液：先做乳腺影像检查排除肿瘤
  • Chest pain spreading to the left arm or jaw with sweating: treat as a cardiac emergency, call 911胸痛向左臂、下颌放射伴出汗：按心脏急症处理，拨 911

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is SP18 Tiānxī?天溪（SP18）在哪里？

On the lateral chest, in the 4th intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 4 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

How is SP18 needled?天溪（SP18）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse outward; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺或向外平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at SP18?天溪（SP18）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung lie just deep to the intercostal muscles and the chest wall is thin here: insert only obliquely or transversely outward, never perpendicularly or deep medially—risk of pneumothorax; avoid the intercostal vessels and nerve.肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺，此处胸壁薄：只斜刺或向外平刺，不直刺、不向内深刺，有气胸风险；避开肋间血管神经。
