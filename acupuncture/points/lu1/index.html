Where is LU1 Zhōngfǔ?中府（LU1）在哪里？

Upper outer chest. Find the hollow below the outer end of the collarbone (LU2), then go 1 cun down: level with the 1st intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部外上方。先找锁骨外端下方的凹陷（云门），再往下 1 寸，与第 1 肋间隙同高，距前正中线 6 寸。

How is LU1 needled?中府（LU1）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse, tip pointing laterally toward the armpit, away from the chest cavity; 0.5–0.8 cun.向外斜刺或平刺，针尖朝腋窝方向、远离胸腔；0.5–0.8 寸。
