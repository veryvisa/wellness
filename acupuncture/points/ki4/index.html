📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the foot relaxed and the medial aspect accessible for palpating the medial Achilles border.坐位或仰卧位，足部自然放松，内踝侧朝上或便于触诊跟腱内缘。

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Medial malleolus tip to sole = 3 cun. KI4 is posteroinferior to the malleolus, at the upper border of the calcaneus in the hollow in front of the Achilles attachment, about 0.5 cun below and slightly behind KI3; rely on the tendon attachment rather than measurement alone.内踝尖到足底折作 3 寸。大钟在内踝后下方、跟骨上缘、跟腱附着部前缘凹陷中，约当太溪下 0.5 寸稍后；以跟腱附着处为准，不单靠量寸。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Locate the region between the posterior medial malleolus and the sole, then use the patient's thumb width as a rough check for the position relative to the medial Achilles border and malleolus; use the hollow at the anteromedial Achilles insertion to confirm.先定位内踝后方与足底之间的区域，再以患者拇指指腹横指比量跟腱内缘与内踝后下缘的相对位置；指宽仅作辅助参考，须以跟腱附着部内侧前缘凹陷为准。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the tip of the medial malleolus and KI3 in the hollow between the malleolus and the Achilles tendon, then follow the medial Achilles edge down and back to its calcaneal insertion; KI4 sits in the hollow at its anteromedial edge.先找到内踝尖与太溪（内踝尖与跟腱之间的凹陷），再沿跟腱内缘向后下方摸到跟腱附着于跟骨的止点，其前缘凹陷中即是大钟。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With slight plantarflexion relaxing the tendon, palpate the hollow at the anteromedial edge of the Achilles insertion. Calcaneal sensory branches and small veins lie superficially; the Achilles insertion and calcaneus lie deep, with posterior tibial vessels and tibial nerve branches nearby anteriorly and deeply. Keep insertion shallow to avoid injuring the tendon insertion, calcaneal periosteum, or posterior tibial neurovascular structures.患者轻度跖屈跟腱放松时，可触及跟腱止点内侧前缘的凹陷。浅层有跟骨支感觉神经及小静脉；深面为跟腱附着部与跟骨，其深前方紧邻胫后动脉、静脉及胫神经分支。进针须控制深度，避免深刺伤及跟腱止点、跟骨骨膜或胫后血管神经。

  5. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Slide down along the medial edge of the Achilles tendon from KI3 to the hollow just in front of its bony insertion; beginners can use this to find the general area and verify against the tendon insertion edge.从太溪顺着跟腱内侧缘往下摸到骨头止点前方的凹陷，初学者可用此法快速找到大致区域，再用跟腱附着缘核对。

  6. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press gently in the hollow at the anteromedial edge of the Achilles insertion; local tenderness or dull ache is common. If a distinct arterial pulse is felt, shift slightly away from the vessel before needling.指腹在跟腱附着部内侧前缘凹陷处轻按，常可触及局部压痛或酸胀感；若触及明显动脉搏动，应避开血管走形处调整进针点。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Suitable as an adjunct when low-back or spinal pain accompanies heel pain or kidney-deficiency soreness along the channel; for acute severe pain or radicular signs, prioritize core points like BL40 or BL23 or medical assessment rather than KI4 alone.伴足跟痛或肾虚腰脊酸痛、络脉循行关联时可选大钟辅助通络；急性剧烈腰痛或伴神经根体征时常选委中、肾俞等主穴或先就医评估，不单靠大钟。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Useful as a supportive point when wheezing or shortness of breath co-occurs with kidney-deficiency signs, lumbar soreness, or heel discomfort; acute dyspnea typically favors points such as EX-B1, BL13, or CV17, and acute distress requires medical care first.适用于偏肾气不足、气短喘促且兼见腰脊酸痛或足跟不适者辅助调理；急性喘憋发作常选定喘、肺俞、膻中等穴，急性重症须先就医。

Knee pain膝痛 ⚖️

At most an adjunct when heel or lower-ankle channel links are prominent, but not a primary choice for knee pain, which typically favors local and regional points like EX-LE4/EX-LE5, GB34, SP10, or SP9.足跟或踝下循经关联明显时可作辅助，但膝痛一般不首选大钟，多选膝眼、阳陵泉、血海、阴陵泉等膝周及下肢主穴。
