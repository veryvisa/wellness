Gentian Liver-Draining Decoction龙胆泻肝汤

Lóngdǎn Xiègān Tāng · Attributed to the Heji Ju Fang; quoted from Wang Ang, Yifang Jijie汪昂引《局方》；本站原文取清·汪昂《医方集解》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Gentian Liver-Draining Decoction龙胆泻肝汤
⚠️ Contains Mu Tong (akebia stem; never the aristolochia species): licensed prescription only含木通：只能由持照医师处方 In the past 'Mu Tong' was often replaced by Guan Mu Tong, which contains aristolochic acid and caused kidney failure and urinary-tract cancer. Only an authenticated non-Aristolochia Mu Tong may be used; avoid products of unknown origin. This entry is for study only; do not make or take the formula yourself.历史上「木通」常被含马兜铃酸的关木通冒充或混用，曾造成肾衰竭与尿路癌；只能用经鉴定的非马兜铃科木通，来源不明的产品不要用。本方只作学习，不要自行配服。

Pattern证

  • liver-gall-damp-heat

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Drain liver and gallbladder fire清泻肝胆实火
  • clear damp-heat from the liver channel清利肝经湿热

Liver-gallbladder fire rising: flank pain, bitter mouth, headache, red eyes, deafness; or damp-heat sinking: genital swelling and itching, foul yellow discharge, cloudy painful urine.肝胆实火上扰：胁痛口苦、头痛目赤、耳聋耳肿；或湿热下注：阴肿阴痒、带下黄臭、小便淋浊。

How the formula is built方解

Gentian clears fire above and drains damp-heat below, scutellaria and gardenia help, alisma, akebia and plantago move it out in the urine, rehmannia and angelica protect liver blood, bupleurum guides and licorice settles, draining with support. In the past the akebia here was swapped for Guan Mu Tong with aristolochic acid, causing severe kidney damage and urinary cancers, a lesson in drug history.龙胆草为君上清肝火下利湿热，芩栀助清，泽泻木通车前从小便导湿热，生地当归养血护阴使苦寒不伤肝血，柴胡引经、甘草调中。「泻中有补」。历史上本方的木通曾以含马兜铃酸的关木通代用，国内外都发生过严重肾损害与尿路癌病例，是用药史上必须记住的教训。

Cautions使用注意

Very bitter and cold: not for a cold weak stomach or in pregnancy; stop once it works; check patent products hold no Guan Mu Tong; sudden vision change or deafness needs care at once. This page is for study and is not a prescription; see a doctor first if symptoms are severe, persistent or getting worse.大苦大寒，脾胃虚寒、孕妇禁用，中病即止；成药须核对成分不含关木通；视力改变、突发耳聋立即就医。本页供学习，不是处方；症状重、持续或加重时先就医。

Contraindicated in pregnancy孕妇禁用 Not to be used in pregnancy (this site grades by the strictest ingredient and the force of the formula); ingredients concerned: Mu Tong (akebia stem; never the aristolochia species), Che Qian Zi (plantago seed), Huang Qin (scutellaria root), Zhi Zi (gardenia fruit), Ze Xie (alisma rhizome), Dang Gui (Chinese angelica root), Sheng Di Huang (rehmannia root), Gan Cao (licorice root).孕期禁用（本站按方内最严药味与攻下破血之力从严定级）；涉及：木通、车前子、黄芩、栀子、泽泻、当归、生地黄、甘草。

Exam notes考点

Exam points: support inside draining; Mu Tong versus Guan Mu Tong.考点：泻中寓补；木通与关木通。
