Stomach pain胃痛

Stomach pain sits in the upper abdomen below the breastbone — exactly where a heart attack or an acute gallbladder or pancreas problem can pass for stomach pain, so excluding emergencies always comes first. The textbook set is tidy: CV12, ST36 and PC6 for every pattern — the Stomach front-mu and lower he-sea points, plus PC6 to descend qi and ease pain — with additions for cold, food, qi, stasis, deficiency cold and yin deficiency. Ask three things: better or worse with pressure (deficiency or excess), with warmth or cold (cold or heat), and on an empty or full stomach.胃痛的位置在上腹、两肋之间的「心下」，这里也是心梗、胆胰急症最容易「冒充」胃痛的地方，所以第一步永远是排除急症。教材对胃痛的取穴很整齐：各型都以中脘、足三里、内关为主——胃的募穴加下合穴，再加一个降逆止痛的内关——然后按寒、食、气、瘀、虚寒、阴虚加配穴。分辨的抓手是：喜按还是拒按（虚实）、得温还是得凉舒服（寒热）、饿时痛还是饱时痛。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Upper abdominal pain with chest pressure, breathlessness, cold sweat, or pain spreading to the left arm, jaw or back — especially over 40, with diabetes or heart risk: call 911 to exclude a heart attack.上腹痛伴胸闷胸压、气短、冷汗，或痛向左臂、下颌、后背放射——尤其 40 岁以上、糖尿病或有心脏病风险的人：立即拨 911，排除心梗。
  • Sudden knife-like pain with a board-hard abdomen that hurts to move: emergency department now for a perforated ulcer.突发刀割样剧痛、腹部硬如板、不敢动：立即急诊，警惕溃疡穿孔。
  • Vomiting blood or coffee-ground material, black tarry stools, or dizziness and palpitations with it: emergency care now; call 911 for fainting or heavy bleeding.呕血、呕吐物像咖啡渣、黑色柏油样便，或伴头晕心慌：立即急诊；晕厥或大量出血拨 911。
  • Severe pain boring to the back with persistent vomiting (pancreatitis), or right upper pain with fever or jaundice (biliary infection): emergency department.上腹剧痛向后背放射、持续呕吐（胰腺炎），或右上腹痛伴发热、黄疸（胆道感染）：急诊。
  • A pulsating abdominal mass, or sudden abdominal or back pain with collapse (aortic aneurysm): call 911.腹部可摸到搏动性肿块，或突发腹痛、背痛伴晕倒（腹主动脉瘤）：立即拨 911。
  • New persistent stomach pain with weight loss, trouble swallowing, anaemia, repeated vomiting or a family history of stomach cancer, or new dyspepsia at 60 or over: see a doctor soon, endoscopy may be needed.新出现的持续胃痛伴体重下降、吞咽困难、贫血、反复呕吐、胃癌家族史，或 60 岁以上新发消化不良：尽快就医，可能需要胃镜。

「胃脹者，腹滿，胃脘痛，鼻聞焦臭，妨於食，大便難」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.35: in stomach distension there is abdominal fullness, epigastric pain, a smell of burning, trouble eating and difficult stools — an early record that looks at epigastric pain together with eating and bowel habit, both of which should be asked about.《灵枢·胀论》：胃胀的人肚子胀满、胃脘痛、鼻中闻到焦臭气味、妨碍进食、大便困难。这是较早把「胃脘痛」和进食、大便放在一起观察的记载，也提示胃痛要问吃饭和大便。

「胃脘當心而痛，上兩脅，鬲咽不通，食飲不下，取三裡」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi, book 9, on stomach disorders: pain in the epigastrium at the heart pit reaching both flanks, a blocked diaphragm and throat and food that will not go down — use ST36. ST36 is the stomach's lower he-sea point ("he-sea points treat the fu organs"), the classical basis for its place in the main set.《针灸甲乙经》卷九论胃病：胃脘当心窝处作痛，连到两胁，膈咽不通、饮食不下，取足三里。足三里是胃的下合穴，「合治内腑」，这是教材以足三里为胃痛主穴的古籍依据。

「腹脹不通，寒中傷飽，食飲不化，中脘主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi, book 9: abdominal distension and blockage, cold in the middle with overeating, food not transformed — CV12 governs it. One line covers cold and food retention, two of today's patterns.《针灸甲乙经》卷九：肚子胀满不通、中焦受寒又吃得过饱、饮食不化，中脘主治。一句话里有寒、有食积，对应今天的寒邪客胃和饮食伤胃两型。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Excess实证

Cold invading the stomach寒邪客胃

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden pain with dislike of cold, eased by warmth and worse with cold, no thirst or a wish for hot drinks, usually after exposure to cold or cold food; thin white coating, wiry tight pulse.胃痛暴作，怕冷喜暖，得温痛减、遇寒加重，口淡不渴或喜热饮；多有受凉或吃生冷史；苔薄白，脉弦紧。

Principle治法 Warm the stomach, disperse cold, move qi and stop pain (drain, with moxa)温胃散寒，理气止痛（泻法，加灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴BL21 Wèishū胃俞ST34 Liángqiū梁丘

💡 Main set: CV12 is the Stomach front-mu and influential point of the fu; ST36 is the lower he-sea ("he-sea points treat the fu organs"); PC6 is the Pericardium luo point opening the Yin Wei ("in Yin Wei disorders the heart region hurts"), opening the chest, descending qi and easing pain. For cold add BL21, which pairs with CV12 as back-shu and front-mu and may be moxa'd, and ST34, the Stomach xi-cleft point, as cleft points treat acute pain.主穴：中脘是胃募、腑会，直接调胃；足三里是胃下合穴，「合治内腑」；内关是心包经络穴、通阴维脉，「阴维为病苦心痛」，宽胸降逆止痛。寒邪加胃俞（与中脘成俞募配穴，可灸）、梁丘（胃经郄穴，郄穴治急痛）。

Excess实证

Food injuring the stomach饮食伤胃

How it presents辨证要点 After overeating or bad food: fullness and pain that dislike pressure, rotten belching and acid, vomiting of undigested food with relief afterwards, unsatisfying stools; thick greasy coating, slippery pulse.有暴饮暴食或饮食不洁史；胃脘胀满疼痛、拒按，嗳腐吞酸，吐出不消化食物，吐后痛减，大便不爽；苔厚腻，脉滑。

Principle治法 Reduce food stagnation, harmonise the stomach and stop pain (drain)消食导滞，和胃止痛（泻法）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴ST21 Liángmén梁门CV10 Xiàwǎn下脘ST25 Tiānshū天枢

💡 ST21 beside CV12 and CV10 at the lower outlet of the stomach both move food held in the stomach; add ST25, the Large Intestine front-mu, for constipation or unsatisfying loose stools. Abdominal points are not needled deeply on a full stomach.梁门在中脘旁、胃经腹部穴，下脘在胃下口，两穴专消胃中停食；大便秘结或泻而不爽加天枢（大肠募穴）通腑。腹部穴饱食后不深刺。

Excess实证

Liver qi attacking the stomach肝气犯胃

How it presents辨证要点 Distending pain reaching the flanks, brought on or worsened by emotion, frequent belching and sighing, acid regurgitation, sluggish stools; thin white coating, wiry pulse.胃脘胀满，痛连两胁，情绪不好时发作或加重，嗳气频、喜叹息，吞酸，大便不畅；苔薄白，脉弦。

Principle治法 Soothe the liver, relieve constraint, move qi and harmonise the stomach (drain)疏肝解郁，理气和胃（泻法）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴LR3 Tàichōng太冲LR14 Qīmén期门

💡 LR3, the Liver yuan point, soothes the liver; LR14, the Liver front-mu, governs flank pain. LR14 is needled only obliquely or transversely outward along the rib space to avoid pneumothorax.太冲是肝经原穴、疏肝理气；期门是肝募穴，主胁肋胀痛。期门只沿肋间向外斜刺或平刺，防气胸。

Excess实证

Damp-heat in the middle (added from internal medicine)湿热中阻（内科辨证补充）

How it presents辨证要点 Burning urgent pain, bitter and foul sticky mouth, thirst without wanting to drink, nausea, constipated or sticky stools, dark urine; red tongue, greasy yellow coating, slippery rapid pulse.胃脘灼痛、痛势急迫，口苦口臭、口黏，渴不欲饮，恶心，便秘或黏滞，小便黄；舌红苔黄腻，脉滑数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, transform damp, harmonise the stomach and stop pain (drain; no moxa)清热化湿，和胃止痛（泻法；不灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴ST44 Nèitíng内庭SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉

💡 Internal medicine lists this pattern, while acupuncture textbooks often do not. ST44, the Stomach ying point, clears stomach fire; SP9, the Spleen he-sea, drains damp.《中医内科学》胃痛有此型，针灸教材多不单列：内庭是胃经荥穴清胃火；阴陵泉是脾经合穴利湿。

Excess实证

Blood stasis in the stomach瘀血停胃

How it presents辨证要点 Stabbing or cutting pain in a fixed spot that dislikes pressure, worse after eating or at night; dark-purple tongue or spots, choppy pulse. Vomiting blood or black stools is a bleeding emergency and goes to hospital first.胃痛如针刺或刀割、固定不移、拒按，食后或夜间加重；舌紫暗或有瘀斑，脉涩。若有呕血黑便，是出血急症，先送医。

Principle治法 Move blood, dissolve stasis, regulate qi and stop pain (drain)活血化瘀，理气止痛（泻法）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴BL17 Géshū膈俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交

💡 BL17 is the influential point of blood and SP6 regulates blood. BL17 is on the back: oblique toward the spine to avoid pneumothorax. SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy. No bleeding technique with active bleeding or anticoagulants.膈俞是血会，活血止痛；三阴交调血。膈俞在背部，斜刺向脊柱防气胸；三阴交孕妇禁针。有活动性出血或服抗凝药者不做刺络放血。

Deficiency虚证

Spleen-stomach deficiency cold脾胃虚寒

How it presents辨证要点 Dull pain that likes warmth and pressure, worse on an empty stomach and eased by food, watery regurgitation, fatigue, cold limbs, loose stools; pale tongue, white coating, weak pulse.胃痛隐隐、喜温喜按，空腹痛甚、得食则缓，泛吐清水，神疲乏力，手足不温，便溏；舌淡苔白，脉虚弱。

Principle治法 Warm the middle, strengthen the spleen and ease pain (tonify, with plenty of moxa)温中健脾，和胃止痛（补法，重灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴BL20 Píshū脾俞BL21 Wèishū胃俞CV6 Qìhǎi气海

💡 BL20 and BL21 are back-shu points paired front and back with CV12 (BL21 with CV12 is a shu–mu pair); CV6 tonifies qi and CV4 may be used instead, both with moxa. Needle back-shu points obliquely toward the spine; CV6 is forbidden in pregnancy.脾俞、胃俞是背俞穴，与中脘前后相配（胃俞配中脘为俞募配穴）；气海补气，可换关元，都宜灸。背俞斜刺向脊柱，气海孕妇禁针。

Deficiency虚证

Stomach yin deficiency胃阴不足

How it presents辨证要点 Dull burning pain, hungry without wanting to eat, dry mouth and throat, dry stools; red tongue with little moisture or coating, thin rapid pulse.胃脘隐隐灼痛，饥而不欲食，口燥咽干，大便干结；舌红少津或少苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin and benefit the stomach (tonify; no moxa)养阴益胃（补法；不灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴BL21 Wèishū胃俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交KI3 Tàixī太溪

💡 BL21 tonifies the stomach, SP6 nourishes yin and KI3 supplements kidney yin to help stomach yin. Although there is heat, it is deficiency heat, so it is not drained like an excess pattern. SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy.胃俞补胃，三阴交养阴，太溪滋肾阴以助胃阴。虽是「热」，却是虚热，不能照实热用泻法。三阴交孕妇禁针。

⚖️ The points that treat this symptom, compared同治此症的穴位比较 · 19

CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘

Best for最适合 Epigastric pain and fullness of any pattern各型胃脘痛、胀满

Why依据 Stomach front-mu and influential point of the fu胃募穴、腑会

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More local than ST36 and broader than ST21; perpendicular 1–1.5 cun, not deep on a full stomach or with an enlarged liver or spleen.比足三里更偏胃脘局部，比梁门作用更广；直刺 1–1.5 寸，饱餐后、肝脾大者不深刺。

ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

Best for最适合 Any stomach pain, especially deficiency各型胃痛，虚证尤宜

Why依据 Stomach he-sea and lower he-sea point; he-sea points treat the fu organs胃经合穴、胃下合穴，「合治内腑」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Regulates and tonifies where ST44 clears fire and ST34 treats acute pain; perpendicular 1–2 cun, moxa allowed.比内庭偏调补，内庭偏清火；比梁丘偏调理，梁丘偏急痛。直刺 1–2 寸，可灸。

PC6 Nèiguān内关

Best for最适合 Stomach pain with nausea, chest tightness, reflux胃痛兼恶心、胸闷、反酸

Why依据 Pericardium luo point, opens the Yin Wei; "chest and heart, PC6"心包经络穴，通阴维脉，「心胸内关谋」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 For nausea and counterflow, where LR3 soothes the liver; "stomach pain" with chest tightness and cold sweat is a possible heart attack first, not a PC6 case.比太冲偏降逆止呕，太冲偏疏肝理气；胸闷冷汗的「胃痛」先排除心梗，不靠内关观察。

ST34 Liángqiū梁丘

Best for最适合 Sudden or cramping stomach pain突发胃痛、痉挛样痛

Why依据 Stomach xi-cleft point; cleft points treat acute pain胃经郄穴，郄穴治急痛

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Quicker and more for pain than ST36, which regulates over time; perpendicular 1–1.5 cun.比足三里更急、更偏止痛，足三里偏长期调理；直刺 1–1.5 寸。

BL21 Wèishū胃俞

Best for最适合 Cold-type pain, stomach yin deficiency, chronic weakness受寒胃痛、胃阴不足、久病胃虚

Why依据 Stomach back-shu, forming a shu–mu pair with CV12胃之背俞穴，与中脘成俞募配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More about the stomach itself than BL20, which governs transformation; oblique toward the spine 0.5–0.8 cun against pneumothorax, moxa for cold.比脾俞更偏胃腑，脾俞偏运化；向脊柱斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸防气胸，寒证可灸。

ST21 Liángmén梁门

Best for最适合 Food retention with fullness that dislikes pressure食积胃胀、拒按

Why依据 Stomach channel point 4 cun above and 2 cun lateral to the navel胃经腹部穴，脐上 4 寸旁开 2 寸

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Higher and more for the stomach than ST25, which is for the bowel; avoided or used with care on an over-full stomach or enlarged liver, and not in mid or late pregnancy.比天枢偏上、偏胃，天枢偏肠；过饱、肝大者慎针或禁针，孕中晚期不针。

CV10 Xiàwǎn下脘

Best for最适合 Food held in the stomach, abdominal fullness食停中脘、腹胀

Why依据 Meeting point of the Ren and Spleen channel at the lower stomach outlet任脉与足太阴交会穴，在胃下口

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Leans to moving food down, where CV12 harmonises; caution in pregnancy and never deep with an unexplained mass.比中脘偏消导下行，中脘偏和胃；孕妇慎用，腹部包块不明者不深刺。

ST25 Tiānshū天枢

Best for最适合 Stomach pain with constipation or diarrhoea胃痛兼便秘或腹泻

Why依据 Large Intestine front-mu大肠募穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the bowel than ST21; forbidden in pregnancy.比梁门偏肠腑传导；孕期禁针。

LR3 Tàichōng太冲

Best for最适合 Emotion-triggered distending pain to the flanks情绪引发的胀痛连胁

Why依据 Liver yuan and shu-stream point肝经原穴、输穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Distal liver-soothing, where LR14 is the local flank and front-mu point; palpate the dorsal pedal artery first.比期门是远端疏肝，期门是胁部局部与募穴；进针前摸足背动脉。

LR14 Qīmén期门

Best for最适合 Marked flank distension and pain胁肋胀痛明显

Why依据 Liver front-mu肝募穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More local to the flank than LR3; only oblique or transverse outward along the rib space 0.5–0.8 cun, never perpendicular, to avoid pneumothorax and the liver.比太冲更偏胁肋局部；只沿肋间向外斜刺或平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不直刺，防气胸与伤肝。

GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉

Best for最适合 Stomach pain reaching the right flank, bitter mouth胃痛牵及右胁、口苦

Why依据 Gallbladder lower he-sea, influential point of sinews胆下合穴、筋会

Versus others与其他穴的区别 For the gallbladder and flank, where LR3 is for liver qi; persistent right upper pain, jaundice or fever needs a biliary work-up first.比太冲偏胆、偏胁肋，太冲偏肝气；右上腹持续痛、黄疸、发热先查胆道。

ST44 Nèitíng内庭

Best for最适合 Stomach fire with burning pain, foul breath, constipation胃火灼痛、口臭、便秘

Why依据 Stomach ying (water) point胃经荥（水）穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Clears fire where ST36 regulates and tonifies; not for dull deficiency-cold pain.比足三里偏清火，足三里偏调补；虚寒隐痛不用。

SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉

Best for最适合 Damp-heat with sticky mouth and greasy yellow coating湿热口黏、苔黄腻

Why依据 Spleen he-sea (water) point, drains damp脾经合（水）穴，利湿

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Drains damp where ST44 clears fire; used together in damp-heat.比内庭偏利湿，内庭偏清火，湿热型两穴同用。

BL17 Géshū膈俞

Best for最适合 Fixed stabbing pain worse at night刺痛固定、夜间加重

Why依据 Influential point of blood八会穴之血会

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Moves blood and stops pain, where SP6 regulates blood and nourishes yin; oblique on the back against pneumothorax.比三阴交偏活血止痛，三阴交偏调血养阴；背部斜刺防气胸。

SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Common causes are functional dyspepsia, reflux, gastritis and peptic ulcer, plus gallbladder, pancreas and heart disease that pass for "stomach pain". The two main causes of ulcers are Helicobacter pylori and anti-inflammatory painkillers such as ibuprofen, naproxen and aspirin; H. pylori is found by breath or stool antigen testing and, if positive, eradicated. New symptoms later in life or alarm signs (bleeding, weight loss, trouble swallowing, anaemia, persistent vomiting) need endoscopy. Trials in functional dyspepsia suggest acupuncture may ease post-meal fullness and early satiety, but it does not replace eradication, acid suppression or necessary tests; a TCM pattern is not an endoscopic diagnosis, and one gastritis may show several patterns.常见原因有功能性消化不良、胃食管反流、胃炎、消化性溃疡，以及胆囊、胰腺和心脏问题冒充的「胃痛」。溃疡的两大原因是幽门螺杆菌感染和布洛芬、萘普生、阿司匹林等消炎止痛药；幽门螺杆菌可用呼气或粪便抗原检测，阳性就根除。年龄较大的新发症状或有警讯（出血、消瘦、吞咽困难、贫血、持续呕吐）需要胃镜。针灸对功能性消化不良的研究提示可能减轻餐后饱胀和早饱，但不能代替根除治疗、抑酸药和必要检查；中医证型不等于胃镜诊断，同一种胃炎可以是不同的证。
