    Hold the patient's index, middle, and ring fingers together (about 2 cun) with the ulnar border of the ring finger at the hairline along the posterior border of the auricular root; the radial border of the index finger marks 2 cun within the hairline.   3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Over the posterosuperior squamous temporal bone near the parietal border (around the posterior margin of temporalis and superior auricular muscle), palpate the bony cranial surface directly superior to the posterior border of the auricular root. Beneath the skin lie the epicranial aponeurosis and loose areolar tissue; carefully identify and avoid branches of the lesser occipital nerve and ascending branches of the posterior auricular artery and vein — the posterior auricular pulse is palpable near the postauricular sulcus, and drifting posteroinferiorly risks hitting the lesser occipital nerve trunk or posterior auricular vessels, causing sharp electric pain or scalp hematoma.在颞骨鳞部后上方与顶骨交界区（颞肌后缘与耳上肌附近），沿耳根后缘直上触诊颅骨骨面。针下有帽状腱膜及疏松结缔组织，须特别识别并避开深层穿行的枕小神经分支及耳后动、静脉的升支——耳后动脉搏动多在耳廓后沟附近可触及，向后下方偏移易触及枕小神经干或耳后血管导致剧烈放电样痛或头皮血肿。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently across the cranial surface posterosuperior to the ear with a fingertip pad; a subtle bony depression (near the superior mastoid border and suture line) can often be felt at GB9, frequently presenting as a distinct tender spot during migraine or tinnitus episodes.用指腹在耳后上方骨面轻按滑摸，天冲处常可触及轻微骨面凹陷（近颞骨乳突部上缘的骨缝凹陷处），偏头痛或耳鸣发作期局部多有明显酸胀压痛点。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Shifting 0.5 cun anteriorly onto GB8 (1.5 cun directly above the auricular apex): too far anterior; GB8 emphasizes temporal migraine and vomiting, whereas GB9 targets the postauricular channel zone and classical indications like palpitations with fright.向前移 0.5 寸误取为率谷（GB8，耳尖直上入发际 1.5 寸）：位置偏前，偏重于治疗偏头痛与呕吐，而天冲偏重耳后循行区及惊悸癫痫等传统主治。
  • Drifting posteroinferiorly onto GB10 (posterosuperior to the mastoid process, at the upper third of the curved line between GB9 and GB12): approaches the lesser occipital nerve trunk and posterior auricular vessels, increasing the risk of radiating occipital pain or local bleeding.向后下方偏移误取为浮白（GB10，耳后乳突后上方，天冲与完骨弧形连线的上 1/3 处）：易靠近枕小神经主干与耳后血管，刺中易引发向枕部放射的刺痛或局部出血。
  • Perpendicular or deep insertion: the epicranial aponeurosis and cranial periosteum lie immediately beneath the thin scalp; perpendicular needling causes sharp periosteal pain or scalp bleeding, so transverse insertion of 0.3–0.5 cun along the scalp is mandatory.直刺或深刺：头皮下紧邻帽状腱膜与颅骨骨膜，直刺极易刺痛骨膜或刺破头皮血管出血，必须沿头皮平刺 0.3–0.5 寸。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Favored for Shaoyang-channel migraine centered posterosuperior to the ear and at the temporoparietal junction (especially with tinnitus or fright palpitations); if pain sits directly above the ear apex with marked nausea/vomiting, prefer GB8; if occipital pain radiates into a stiff neck, prefer GB20.偏重耳后上方及颞顶交界区的少阳经偏头痛（常伴耳鸣或惊悸）；若痛在耳尖直上颞部且伴明显恶心呕吐，优先选率谷（GB8）；若痛在后枕部连及颈项强痛，优先选风池（GB20）。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Used as a local periauricular Shaoyang adjunct for tinnitus accompanied by postauricular fullness from liver-gallbladder fire or Shaoyang qi stagnation; for excess tinnitus/deafness, TE21, SI19, and GB2 serve as primary points with GB9 as a supporting point, and it is not a primary choice for kidney-essence deficiency tinnitus.作为耳周少阳经近部配穴，用于肝胆火旺或少阳经气郁滞所致耳鸣伴耳后胀痛；实证耳鸣耳聋常以耳门（TE21）、听宫（SI19）、听会（GB2）为主穴，天冲多作辅助配穴，虚证肾精亏虚型不作为首选。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Selected when dizziness is accompanied by lateral head distending pain and irritability from ascendant liver yang or gallbladder fire disturbing the clear orifices; if dizziness involves marked neck stiffness, external wind, or heavy-headedness, prefer GB20 or GV20, as GB9 alone has limited scope for vertigo.用于肝阳上亢或胆火上扰清窍所致的侧头胀痛兼眩晕、心烦；若眩晕伴明显颈项僵硬、外感风邪或头重如裹，优先选风池（GB20）或百会（GV20），单用天冲治眩晕力度较局限。

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

Classically indicated for fright palpitations and emotional agitation, making it suitable when palpitations and startle are paired with migraine or tinnitus from liver-gallbladder heat; for routine palpitations due to heart-gallbladder or heart-spleen deficiency, lead with PC6, HT7, and BL15, using GB9 only as a pattern-specific adjunct.古籍载其治「惊悸怔忡、反张悲哭」，适用于肝胆郁热、痰火扰心所致的心悸易惊兼偏头痛、耳鸣；常规心虚胆怯或心脾两虚型心悸，应首选内关（PC6）、神门（HT7）及心俞（BL15），天冲仅作辨证配穴。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Migraine (throbbing temporal and postauricular headache)偏头痛（颞侧及耳后搏动性头痛）

Typically unilateral throbbing moderate-to-severe headache, often with photophobia, phonophobia, or nausea. Differentiate from intracranial mass, subarachnoid hemorrhage, or giant cell arteritis; sudden 'thunderclap' onset or first onset after age 50 requires medical evaluation first.多为单侧搏动性中重度头痛，可伴畏光、畏声或恶心。需与颅内占位性病变、蛛网膜下腔出血或巨细胞动脉炎鉴别；若为突发剧烈「雷击样」头痛或首次发作于 50 岁以后，应先就医排查。

Lesser occipital neuralgia / cervicogenic headache枕小神经痛 / 颈源性头痛

Paroxysmal electric or stabbing pain radiating from above the mastoid process to the lateral scalp, often triggered by cervical degeneration, myofascial entrapment along the posterior sternocleidomastoid border, or prolonged neck flexion; palpating along the GB9–GB12 line may reproduce radiating pain. Seek imaging and neurological evaluation first if preceded by neck trauma or accompanied by limb weakness or numbness.耳后乳突上方至侧头部阵发性电击样、刀割样痛，常因颈椎退变、胸锁乳突肌后缘筋膜卡压或长期低头诱发，局部按压天冲至完骨一线可复制放射痛。若伴随颈部外伤史或肢体无力麻木，需先进行影像学与神经科检查。

Temporomandibular disorder (TMD) related referred temporal pain颞下颌关节紊乱病（TMD）相关颞部牵涉痛

Myofascial trigger points in the posterior temporalis muscle can refer pain superior to the ear into the GB9 region, accompanied by pain on chewing, joint clicking, or limited jaw opening. Refer to a dentist/oral surgeon or physician first if acute jaw locking, local redness/warmth, or fever is present.颞肌后部肌筋膜触发点可向耳上及天冲区牵涉痛，伴咀嚼痛、张口弹响或受限。若出现下颌关节急性交锁、局部红肿热痛或发热，应先转诊口腔颌面外科或全科医生排除感染与急性关节盘移位。
