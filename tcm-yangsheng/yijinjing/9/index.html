✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ Head below heart头低于心脏 ⚠ Twist拧转

  • High blood pressure, glaucoma or retinal disease: do not let the head drop below the heart; stop when the upper body is level with the floor and stay no longer than a breath or two高血压、青光眼、视网膜病变：头不要低过心脏，上身弯到与地面平行就停，停留不超过一两次呼吸
  • Osteoporosis: do only the standing claw reach and turn, without the forward bend or the twisting sweep while bent; if you have had a spinal fracture, have a physiotherapist check it first骨质疏松：只做站立的探爪转身，不做弯腰下按和弯腰时的划弧拧转；有过脊椎骨折的先请物理治疗师看过
  • Disc herniation: keep the bend small and stop if the leg goes numb or painful腰椎间盘突出：弯腰幅度小，觉得腿麻腿痛就停

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Bring the left foot in half a step to about shoulder width; make fists and rest them at the waist左脚收回半步，约与肩同宽；两手握拳，放在腰间
  2. Open the right fist and extend the right arm out to the right side, eyes following the hand右拳变掌，右臂伸直向右侧展开，目随手走
  3. Bend the right elbow, curl the fingers into a 'dragon claw', and reach it level past the chin across to the left, turning the upper body to the left右臂屈肘，五指弯曲成「龙爪」，经下巴前向身体左侧水平伸出，上身随之左转
  4. Open the claw and bend forward to the front left, pressing the palm down near the outside of the left foot「龙爪」变掌，上身向左前方弯下，掌心向下按到左脚外侧附近
  5. Sweep the upper body from front left through the centre to front right, the right hand tracing an arc to the outside of the right foot; then make a fist, slowly rise and return it to the waist; repeat with the left hand上身由左前方经正前方划到右前方，右手随之划弧到右脚外侧，然后握拳，慢慢起身，拳收回腰间；换左手做一遍

🌬 Breathe out as the claw reaches and as you bend to press, in as you rise; or breathe naturally.探爪时呼气，弯腰下按时呼气，起身时吸气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • As the claw reaches, the waist turns first and the arm follows探爪时腰先转，手臂跟随
  • How low you press depends on your flexibility; reaching the shin or the outside of the knee is fine下按的深度由柔韧决定，手到小腿或膝外侧也可以
  • Rise slowly, with the head coming up last起身要慢，头最后抬起

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Forcing a twist while bent over弯腰同时用力拧转
  • Rounding the back and forcing down to reach the foot为了按到脚边而弓背硬压
  • Coming up too fast起身太快

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Do only the standing claw reach and turn, without bending down; or press only to the same-side thigh.只做站立的探爪和转身，不弯腰下按；或者手只按到同侧大腿。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 In tradition the dragon belongs to the east, and stretching out its claw pictures a dragon reaching for something: a cue for the arm reaching while the waist turns. The old verse: the green dragon's claw goes out to the left from the right, the effort runs round the shoulders and back and gathers past the knees, eyes level, breath even and mind quiet.龙在传统里是东方之象，「探爪」比作龙伸爪取物，提示手臂伸展与腰身转动相配合。旧本歌诀：「青龙探爪，左从右出」「力周肩背，围收过膝；两目注平，息调心谧。」
