Form 1: Holding up the sky with both hands第1式 · 两手托天理三焦

Qigong养生功法 › Baduanjin (Eight Pieces of Brocade)八段锦 › Form 1: Holding up the sky with both hands第1式 · 两手托天理三焦

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Shoulder pain or rotator-cuff injury: lift only to the pain-free point肩痛、肩袖损伤：举到不痛为止
  • High blood pressure: no breath-holding when looking up, and keep the hold short高血压：抬头看手时不憋气，略停时间可缩短
  • If you get dizzy: tip the head back less头晕的人：抬头的幅度小一些

🧘 The movement动作

  1. From the opening stance, interlace the fingers in front of the belly, palms up接预备势，两掌在腹前五指分开、交叉，掌心向上
  2. Straighten the legs slowly while lifting the hands to the chest, then rotate the arms and turn the palms up as you push them overhead, looking up at the hands两腿慢慢伸直，两掌上托到胸前，随后两臂内旋，翻掌向上托起，抬头看手
  3. Keep pushing until the elbows are straight, tuck the chin slightly and look ahead; hold two or three seconds, lengthening the whole body upward两臂继续上撑到肘伸直，下巴微收、目视前方，略停两三秒，全身向上拉长
  4. Bend the knees slightly, separate the hands and lower the arms slowly out to the sides, bringing the palms back in front of the belly两腿微屈，十指分开，两臂从身体两侧缓缓落下，两掌捧回腹前

🌬 Breathe in as the hands rise, out as they come down; if that is hard at first, just breathe naturally, never holding your breath.上托时吸气，下落时呼气；初学跟不上就自然呼吸，绝不憋气。

✅ Key points要领

  • As you push up, lengthen the spine one segment at a time, as if the crown were lifted by a string上撑时脊柱一节一节向上伸展，像有人轻轻提着头顶
  • Keep the hands on the midline of the body两掌始终在身体中线上
  • The two- or three-second hold is the heart of this form; do not drop straight down略停的两三秒是这一式的关键，不要一托就落

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Tipping the head too far back and pushing the belly forward, which compresses the lower back仰头过度、腰往前挺，腰椎被压
  • Hunching the shoulders up around the neck耸肩，脖子缩进肩膀里
  • Holding the breath as the arms go up两臂上举时憋住一口气

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Sit on the front half of a chair: lift only as high as is comfortable; with a shoulder problem, stop at shoulder height.坐在椅子前半部做：手只举到不痛的高度，肩关节有问题的人举到与肩平即可。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 In TCM the San Jiao (Triple Energizer) is the concept that gathers up the movement of qi and fluids through the upper, middle and lower body. The rhyme says this form 'regulates the San Jiao'; the traditional reading is that the whole-body stretch opens the chest and belly. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.「三焦」是中医里统括上、中、下三个部位气机与水液运行的概念。口诀说这一式「理三焦」，传统解释是全身上下伸展、带动胸腹。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
