Special-point roles of SI1?少泽（SI1）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-well (metal)井穴（金）

Main safety point at SI1?少泽（SI1）最要注意的安全问题？

The fingertip is very sensitive: shallow or pricking insertion only, strict asepsis, keep off the nail bed and do not squeeze repeatedly.指端痛觉敏感，只作浅刺或点刺；严格消毒，避开甲床，不反复挤压。
