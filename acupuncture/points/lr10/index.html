📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine with the lower leg slightly abducted, externally rotated, and mildly flexed at the knee, muscles relaxed, exposing the proximal medial thigh and groin region.仰卧位，下肢微外展、外旋并略屈膝，肌肉放松，充分暴露大腿内侧近端与腹股沟区。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Upper border of the pubic symphysis to the base of the patella = 18 cun. LR10 is 3 cun directly below ST30 (level with the symphysis, 2 cun lateral), i.e. 3/18 of the way down.耻骨联合上缘到髌底折作 18 寸。足五里在气冲（ST30，平耻骨联合上缘、旁开 2 寸）直下 3 寸，即从耻骨联合上缘水平向下量 3/18。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure with the patient's own four fingers held together (3 cun) downward from the level of ST30 Qichong, then verify the muscle border. Finger widths are only a rough guide and must be checked against muscular landmarks and arterial pulsation.以患者本人同身寸量取：食指、中指、无名指、小指四指并拢（一夫法为 3 寸），自气冲（ST30）水平向下量取 3 寸，再核对肌缘位置。指宽仅作粗量，必须结合肌腱体表投影与动脉搏动核对。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find ST30 at the level of the upper border of the symphysis, 2 cun lateral; go straight down 3 cun to the upper medial thigh at the lateral edge of adductor longus, where a pulse is often felt deep (the standard's 'over the pulsation').先定气冲：耻骨联合上缘水平、前正中线旁开 2 寸；由此垂直向下 3 寸，在大腿根内侧、长收肌外缘处取穴，深面常可触及动脉搏动（国标「动脉搏动处」）。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With the hip flexed and abducted, adductor longus stands out on the upper medial thigh; LR10 is at its lateral edge, 3 cun below ST30. Adductor longus and brevis lie deep, with the femoral artery and vein deep and lateral in the femoral triangle; the genitofemoral nerve, obturator cutaneous branches and great saphenous tributaries are under the skin. Feel the pulse, insert perpendicularly 0.5–0.8 cun medial to it, never deep superolaterally.屈髋外展时长收肌在大腿根内侧绷起，足五里在其外缘、气冲直下 3 寸；深层为长收肌、短收肌，外侧深处是股三角内的股动静脉。皮下有生殖股神经、闭孔神经皮支与大隐静脉属支。先摸清搏动，针从搏动内侧直刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不向外上方深刺。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press lightly at the lateral edge of adductor longus and identify the pulse; if it is strong, move the needle point medial to it. Tingling down the front-inner thigh on deep pressure means a nerve branch—redirect.指腹在长收肌外缘轻按并辨认搏动；若搏动明显，针点移到搏动内侧。深压出现向大腿前内侧放射的麻感，提示触及神经分支，应调整方向。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting superolaterally or going deep: risks the femoral vessels or femoral nerve branches in the femoral triangle (haematoma, bleeding, radiating pain); the textbook depth is 0.5–0.8 cun, perpendicular, never probing superolaterally.取偏外上或深刺：易伤股三角内的股动静脉或股神经分支，导致血肿、出血或放射痛；教材深度 0.5–0.8 寸，直刺不向外上深探。
  • Drifting onto the femoral artery pulsation line: sits right over major femoral vessels where needling can injure the vessel or cause prominent subcutaneous bruising; always palpate for a pulse and avoid it before inserting.误取到股动脉搏动线上：靠近股动脉主干，针刺易引起血管损伤或较明显的皮下瘀血，取穴前必须先摸清搏动并避开。
  • Entering a superficial vein or overlooking superficial varicosities: superficial veins such as tributaries of the great saphenous vein lie in the subcutaneous layer and can bleed or bruise if pierced; inspect and avoid visible superficial veins before needling.误入表浅静脉或忽略下肢静脉曲张：浅层有大隐静脉属支等浅静脉，刺破易致皮下血肿或局部渗血，进针前应先观察并避开可见浅静脉。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Traditionally used for lower-abdominal pain and Liver-channel discomfort, but acute abdominal pain or organic disease should not rely on a single point and requires diagnosis first; clinically paired with LR3, LR2, or SP6.传统用于少腹痛及肝经循行相关下腹不适，但急性腹痛、急腹症或器质性病变不以单穴为主，须先明确病因，临证常配太冲、行间、三阴交等穴协同调理。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Difficult urination appears in traditional indications, but it is not a first-line point for urinary retention or urinary symptoms; seek medical evaluation for retention, hematuria, or fever, and typically pair with CV3, BL28, SP6, or SP9.传统主治含小便不利，但癃闭或泌尿系症状不作为首选主穴，遇尿潴留、血尿或发热须先就医排查，临床多配中极、膀胱俞、三阴交、阴陵泉等穴。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Medial thigh muscle strain or overuse (sartorius, adductor longus, gracilis)大腿内侧肌群劳损或拉伤（缝匠肌、长收肌、股薄肌等）

Common after sudden direction changes, lunges, or adduction strain, worsening with resisted adduction or hip flexion/abduction; rule out significant adductor tear, severe pain, or swelling and seek medical evaluation for acute severe injury.多见于突然变向、跨步或内收发力后大腿内侧痛，抗阻内收或屈髋外展时痛感加重；需先排查内收肌明显撕裂、局部剧痛或肿胀，急性严重损伤先休息与医疗评估。

Inguinal hernia, femoral hernia, or inguinal lymphadenopathy腹股沟疝、股疝或腹股沟淋巴结肿大

A reducible or fixed bulge, heaviness, or pulling pain in the groin or upper inner thigh when standing, coughing, or straining differs from simple muscle strain; seek urgent care if a bulge is irreducible, severely painful, or accompanied by nausea or vomiting.站立、咳嗽或用力时腹股沟或大腿根部出现可复性或固定包块、坠胀或牵扯痛，与单纯肌肉痛不同；包块无法还纳、剧痛或伴恶心呕吐须立即就医。

Femoral or obturator nerve branch irritation causing medial or anteromedial thigh paresthesia股神经或闭孔神经分支受累所致大腿内侧/前内侧感觉异常

Presents as numbness, tingling, or burning over the medial or anteromedial thigh, potentially linked to entrapment near the hip, pelvis, or groin; obtain medical workup if accompanied by noticeable weakness or progressive numbness.表现为大腿内侧或前内侧麻木、刺痛或烧灼感，可能与髋部、骨盆或腹股沟区结构卡压相关；若伴明显肌力下降或进行性麻木，应先完善医学检查。

Where is LR10 Zúwǔlǐ?足五里（LR10）在哪里？
