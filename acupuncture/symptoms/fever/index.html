Fever发热

GV14, LI11, LI4 and TE5 are all used for fever, but fever is not a single TCM pattern. First distinguish external from internal causes, cold from heat, and excess from deficiency. Consider duration, sweating, chills, thirst, mental status, tongue and pulse. External excess heat calls for releasing the exterior and clearing heat; cold constraint calls for dispersing cold; internal fever requires differentiation of qi, blood, yin or yang deficiency. Do not automatically use heat-clearing and draining methods whenever body temperature rises. This page selects main and supporting points by pattern, then compares commonly used fever points and explains why one is preferred over another.大椎、曲池、合谷、外关等穴都能用于发热，但发热并不是一个单一证型。临床首先要分外感还是内伤、寒邪还是热邪、实热还是虚热，并结合病程、汗出、恶寒、口渴、精神状态和舌脉判断。外感实热重在疏风解表、清热；寒邪束表重在解表散寒；内伤发热则要辨气虚、血虚、阴虚、阳虚，不能一见体温升高就一律用清热泻法。本页按证型选择主穴和配穴，再横向比较常见发热穴，解释为什么选择某穴而不是另一个穴。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Temperature at or above 40°C, or fever with confusion, difficulty waking, seizures, neck stiffness, severe headache, difficulty breathing or blue lips: call 911 or go to the emergency department immediately; do not delay for acupuncture.体温达到或超过 40°C，或发热伴意识模糊、难以唤醒、抽搐、颈项强直、剧烈头痛、呼吸困难、嘴唇发紫：立即拨打 911 或前往急诊，不要先针灸观察。
  • An infant younger than 3 months with a rectal temperature of 38.0°C or higher needs immediate medical assessment, even if the infant appears otherwise well.3个月以下婴儿直肠温度达到 38.0°C 或以上：立即接受医疗评估，即使婴儿看起来精神尚可，也不能在家单纯观察或针灸。
  • Fever with a non-blanching purple-red rash, marked drowsiness, persistent vomiting or significant dehydration (markedly reduced urination, dry mouth or dizziness on standing) requires emergency assessment.发热伴皮肤出现按压不褪色的紫红色皮疹、明显嗜睡、持续呕吐、严重脱水（尿量显著减少、口干、站立头晕）：立即急诊评估。
  • Adult fever persisting beyond 3 days without improvement, or recurrent fever for more than a week, especially with weight loss, night sweats, persistent cough or focal pain, warrants prompt medical investigation for infection, tuberculosis, inflammatory disease or malignancy.成人发热持续超过 3 天仍未改善，或反复发热超过 1 周；伴体重下降、夜间盗汗、持续咳嗽或局部疼痛：尽快就医，检查感染、结核、炎症性疾病或肿瘤等原因。
  • Anyone receiving chemotherapy, taking immunosuppressants after an organ transplant, or with significant immunosuppression who develops a temperature of 38.0°C or higher should contact their treatment team or emergency service immediately rather than waiting for acupuncture.正在接受化疗、器官移植后使用免疫抑制剂，或已知严重免疫功能低下者，体温达到 38.0°C 或以上：立即联系治疗团队或急诊，不等待针灸处理。
  • Fever within a year of visiting a malaria area; fever in pregnancy; fever with painful urination, flank pain or shaking chills: same-day medical care.近 1 年内去过疟疾流行地区后发热；孕妇发热；发热伴尿痛、腰痛或寒战：当天就医

「今夫熱病者，皆傷寒之類也」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.31 opens: the febrile diseases of today all belong to the category of cold damage. Acute external fever was understood within the 'cold damage' frame — first the exterior, then its passage inward — which is why this page sorts external fever first and then its depth.《素问·热论》开篇：现在所说的热病，都属于外感伤寒一类。古人把急性外感发热放在「伤寒」框架下理解——先看表证、再看传里，这正是本页先分外感、再分卫气营血层次的来由。

「有所勞倦，形氣衰少，穀氣不盛，上焦不行，下脘不通，胃氣熱，熱氣熏胸中，故內熱」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.62, answering 'yin deficiency produces internal heat': overwork leaves body and qi depleted, grain qi from food is insufficient, the upper burner cannot spread it and the lower stomach is blocked, so stomach qi stagnates into heat that steams the chest. 'Yin deficiency' here means spleen qi worn down by fatigue — the root of later qi-deficiency fever treated with sweet warm tonics — not the yin-fluid deficiency the term means today.《素问·调经论》回答「阴虚生内热」：劳倦过度，形体与气都衰少，饮食化生的谷气不足，上焦不能布散，下脘不通，胃气郁而生热，熏蒸胸中，所以内热。这里的「阴虚」指劳倦伤脾气，是后世气虚发热、甘温除热的源头，不等于今天说的阴液亏虚。

「發熱 有寒潮熱，煩熱，往來熱」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Dacheng distinguishes fever with chills, tidal fever, vexing heat and alternating chills and fever. Each form points to a different mechanism and different points: fever with chills is usually exterior, afternoon tidal fever is yin deficiency or yangming excess, and alternating chills and fever belongs to shaoyang.《针灸大成》把发热分为恶寒发热、潮热、烦热、往来热（寒热交替）几种形式。形式不同，病机和选穴也不同：恶寒发热多在表，午后潮热多属阴虚或阳明腑实，寒热往来属少阳。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

External外感

Wind-cold constraining the exterior风寒束表

How it presents辨证要点 Mild fever with pronounced chills, relieved by warmth; no sweating or little sweating; headache, neck and upper-back stiffness, body aches, nasal obstruction and clear rhinorrhea; no thirst or preference for warm drinks; thin white tongue coating and floating tight pulse. Usually presents early in an acute external illness.发热较轻，恶寒明显，得温则舒，无汗或汗少；头痛、项背拘急、肢体酸痛，鼻塞、流清涕，口不渴或喜热饮；舌苔薄白，脉浮紧。多见于起病较急的外感初期。

Principle治法 Dispel wind and cold, release the exterior and diffuse lung qi (primarily draining, with warming methods when appropriate)疏风散寒，解表宣肺（以泻法为主，可酌情配合温法）

Main主穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎LI4 Hégǔ合谷TE5 Wàiguān外关
Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门LU7 Lièquē列缺

💡 GV14 is the meeting point of the yang channels and helps release exterior pathogens. LI4, the Large Intestine yuan-source point, with TE5 helps disperse wind and release the exterior. BL12, the Wind Gate, is added for exterior wind-cold with neck and upper-back stiffness. LU7, the Lung luo-connecting point linking with the Large Intestine channel, supports lung diffusion and nasal symptoms. The priority is releasing exterior constraint, not indiscriminate heat draining.大椎为诸阳之会，疏解表邪；合谷为大肠原穴，配外关疏风解表、调和表里；风门为风邪出入之门，风寒束表且项背拘急时配用；列缺为肺经络穴，联络大肠经并宣肺利鼻。此型重点是解除表闭，不应把清热泻火作为唯一治法。

External外感

Wind-heat attacking the exterior风热犯表

How it presents辨证要点 More prominent fever with mild aversion to wind or slight chills; sweating or impaired sweating; headache, red swollen sore throat, yellow nasal discharge and thirst for cold drinks; cough with yellow sputum may occur; red tongue tip, thin yellow coating and floating rapid pulse.发热较重，微恶风寒或恶寒较轻，有汗或汗出不畅；头痛、咽喉红肿疼痛、鼻流黄涕，口渴喜冷饮；可伴咳嗽、痰黄；舌尖红，苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat and release the exterior (draining method)疏风清热，解表泄热（泻法）

Main主穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎LI11 Qūchí曲池LI4 Hégǔ合谷TE5 Wàiguān外关
Add配穴LU10 Yújì鱼际LU5 Chǐzé尺泽LI20 Yíngxiāng迎香

💡 GV14 clears heat from the yang aspect. LI11, the Large Intestine he-sea point, together with its yuan-source point LI4, clears heat and releases the exterior. TE5, the San Jiao luo-connecting point, supports exterior regulation. LU10, the Lung ying-spring point, is added for a red sore throat; LU5, the Lung he-sea point, for pronounced lung heat and rebellious qi; LI20 for local nasal obstruction and discharge.大椎清解阳分之热；曲池为大肠经合穴、五行属土，配合谷原穴清热解表；外关为三焦经络穴，通表解郁。咽喉红肿加鱼际，利用肺经荥穴清肺热；痰黄、肺热明显加尺泽，取肺经合穴清肺降逆；鼻塞流黄涕可加迎香局部通鼻。

External外感

Summer-heat and dampness attacking the exterior暑湿袭表

How it presents辨证要点 Often occurs in hot, humid weather or after heat exposure; fever with mild chills, sweating without adequate relief, heavy-headed sensation, chest oppression, abdominal fullness, nausea, poor appetite, thirst without a desire to drink much and bodily heaviness; loose stools may occur; thick greasy tongue coating and soggy rapid pulse.多发生于炎热潮湿季节或暑热环境中；发热、微恶风寒，汗出而热不退，头重如裹、胸闷、脘腹胀满、恶心、食欲差，口渴但不欲多饮，身体困重；可伴大便溏薄；舌苔厚腻，脉濡数。

Principle治法 Clear summer-heat, transform dampness, release the exterior and harmonize the middle (even method, with more draining when dampness predominates)清暑化湿，解表和中（平补平泻，湿重可偏泻）

Main主穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎LI11 Qūchí曲池TE5 Wàiguān外关
Add配穴SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆

💡 GV14, LI11 and TE5 address exterior heat. SP9, the Spleen he-sea point, drains dampness and is important when heaviness predominates. ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, regulates the middle burner. CV12, the Stomach front-mu point, pairs with ST36 to harmonize the stomach and direct rebellious qi downward. ST40, the Stomach luo-connecting point, transforms phlegm-dampness when chest oppression or abundant phlegm is present. Treatment must address dampness and middle-burner dysfunction, not heat alone.大椎、曲池、外关处理表热；阴陵泉为脾经合穴，健脾利湿，是湿重身困的关键配穴；足三里为胃经合穴，调理中焦、和胃；中脘为胃募穴，配足三里加强和胃降逆；丰隆为胃经络穴，化痰湿，胸闷痰多时加入。此型不能只清热而忽视湿邪困脾、胃气不和。

External外感

Blazing heat in the qi aspect气分热盛

How it presents辨证要点 High fever without chills, aversion to heat, profuse sweating, great thirst for cold drinks, red face, heavy breathing, agitation; or abdominal fullness and constipation; red tongue with dry yellow coating, flooding large or slippery rapid pulse. The exterior has resolved and heat has entered the interior.高热不恶寒、反恶热，大汗出，口大渴喜冷饮，面红气粗、心烦；或腹胀便秘；舌红苔黄燥，脉洪大或滑数。表证已解，热入于里。

Principle治法 Clear and drain interior heat (drain; prick the finger tips or jing-well points to bleed; no moxa)清泄里热（泻法，十宣或井穴可点刺出血，不灸）

Main主穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎LI11 Qūchí曲池LI4 Hégǔ合谷TE5 Wàiguān外关
Add配穴ST44 Nèitíng内庭TE6 Zhīgōu支沟

💡 GV14, meeting point of the Du vessel with all six yang channels, is the key fever point; LI11 (Large Intestine he-sea) and LI4 (yuan) on the same channel clear yangming heat; TE5, the Triple Energizer luo point linked to the Yang Wei vessel, handles heat in exterior and interior. ST44, the Stomach ying-spring point ('ying points govern fever'), clears yangming qi-aspect heat; add TE6 to free the bowels and drain heat when there is constipation.大椎是督脉与手足三阳交会，退热要穴；曲池（大肠合穴）、合谷（大肠原穴）同经相配，清阳明之热；外关是三焦络穴、通阳维，主表里之热。内庭是胃经荥穴，「荥主身热」，清阳明气分热；腹胀便秘加支沟（三焦经穴）通腑泄热。

External外感

Heat entering the nutritive and blood aspects热入营血

How it presents辨证要点 High fever worse at night, agitation, even confusion and delirium; rashes or bleeding spots on the skin, dry mouth without much thirst; crimson tongue with little coating, thin rapid pulse. This is a critical emergency that needs hospital care.高热夜间尤甚，心烦躁扰，甚至神昏谵语；皮肤出现斑疹或出血点，口干而不甚渴；舌绛红少苔，脉细数。属急危重症，须住院诊治。

Principle治法 Clear the nutritive aspect and cool the blood (drain, bloodletting; acupuncture is only an adjunct to hospital treatment)清营凉血（泻法，点刺出血；针灸只作住院救治中的辅助）

Main主穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎LI11 Qūchí曲池LI4 Hégǔ合谷TE5 Wàiguān外关
Add配穴PC3 Qūzé曲泽BL40 Weizhong委中PC9 Zhōngchōng中冲

💡 The core points bring down fever; PC3 (Pericardium he-sea) and BL40 (Bladder he-sea, the 'blood cleft') are bled at their superficial veins to drain heat from the blood; PC9, the Pericardium jing-well point, is pricked to open the orifices in confusion. Rash with confusion must first be checked for meningococcal sepsis and other emergencies.主穴退热；曲泽是心包经合穴、委中是膀胱经合穴（「血郄」），二穴浅表静脉点刺出血以泄血分之热；中冲是心包经井穴，井穴点刺开窍醒神，用于神昏。出现斑疹、神昏时首先要排除脑膜炎球菌败血症等急症。

Internal内伤

Fever due to qi deficiency气虚发热

How it presents辨证要点 Usually low-grade or intermittent fever, sometimes worsening after exertion; spontaneous sweating, aversion to wind, shortness of breath, reluctance to speak, fatigue and poor appetite; worse with activity and somewhat relieved by rest; chills may be absent; pale tongue with thin white coating and weak pulse. Often chronic, with signs of lung and spleen qi deficiency.发热常为低热，或劳累后体温升高，时作时止；自汗、怕风、气短懒言、神疲乏力、食欲差，活动后加重，休息后稍缓；不一定明显恶寒；舌淡、苔薄白，脉虚弱。病程较长，常有脾肺气虚表现。

Principle治法 Tonify qi, strengthen the spleen and resolve deficiency heat through appropriate warming and tonification益气健脾，甘温除热（补法，酌情施灸）

💡 CV6 supports original qi. ST36, the Stomach he-sea and lower he-sea point, strengthens the spleen and replenishes qi. BL20 is the Spleen back-shu point; BL13 adds lung regulation for combined lung-spleen deficiency. SP6 coordinates the three yin channels and supports chronic qi and blood deficiency. GV20 raises clear yang and may be added for sinking qi and marked fatigue. This is a deficiency presentation; strong heat-draining treatment with LI11 or GV14 should not be applied indiscriminately.气海为元气之海，补益一身之气；足三里为胃经合穴、胃下合穴，健脾益气；脾俞为脾之背俞穴，直接调理脾气；肺俞配合脾俞，肺脾同调；三阴交兼顾脾肝肾三阴，适合久病气血不足；百会升提清阳，气虚下陷、精神疲乏时酌加。此型属于虚热，不宜照搬曲池、大椎强力泻热。

Internal内伤

Fever due to blood deficiency血虚发热

How it presents辨证要点 Low-grade or afternoon fever, generally not high; pale or sallow complexion, dizziness, blurred vision, palpitations, insomnia and pale lips and nails; menstruation may be scanty or delayed; worse with fatigue; pale tongue and thin weak pulse. May follow chronic illness, blood loss or inadequate nutrition.低热或午后发热，热势不高，面色苍白或萎黄，头晕眼花、心悸、失眠，唇甲色淡，女性可有月经量少或经期延后；劳累后加重；舌淡，脉细弱。常见于久病、失血或营养不足之后。

Principle治法 Replenish qi and nourish blood, harmonizing the nutritive and defensive qi (tonifying method)益气养血，调和营卫（补法）

💡 SP6 meets the three yin channels and supports blood nourishment and menstrual regulation. ST36 strengthens the spleen and qi, supporting blood production. BL17 is the influential point of blood. BL20 supports spleen transformation and blood production, while BL18 regulates the liver and its blood-storing function. CV6 replenishes original qi to support qi and blood generation. Blood nourishment is central; elevated temperature alone does not justify extensive use of bitter-cold heat-clearing points.三阴交为肝脾肾三阴交会穴，养血调经；足三里健脾益气，以气生血；膈俞为血会，直接调血；脾俞健脾生血，肝俞调肝藏血；气海补元气，使气血生化有源。血虚发热以养血为核心，不应因体温升高就大量使用苦寒清热穴。

Internal内伤

Fever due to yin deficiency阴虚发热

How it presents辨证要点 Low-grade fever in the afternoon or at night, tidal or steaming-bone heat, hot palms and soles, restlessness, night sweats, dry mouth and throat despite limited drinking, flushed cheeks, sometimes with sore lower back and knees or insomnia; red tongue with scanty or absent coating and thin rapid pulse. Usually chronic, often following prolonged illness or other yin-depleting states.午后或夜间低热，骨蒸潮热，手足心热、心烦、夜间盗汗，口干咽燥但饮水不多，颧红，可能伴腰膝酸软、失眠；舌红少苔或无苔，脉细数。病程多较长，常见于久病耗阴、产后或慢性消耗状态。

Principle治法 Nourish yin, clear deficiency heat and gently vent residual heat (primarily tonifying, with appropriate clearing)滋阴清热，养阴透热（补法为主，兼清虚热）

💡 KI3 is the Kidney yuan-source and shu-stream point, nourishing kidney yin. SP6 supports the liver, spleen and kidney yin. BL23 is the Kidney back-shu point and reinforces KI3. 🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

In modern medicine, fever is generally understood as an elevation in the body's thermoregulatory set point. A temperature of 38.0°C or above is commonly used as an adult fever reference threshold, although the interpretation depends on measurement site, method and individual factors. Fever is a symptom, not a diagnosis. Common causes include: 1. Infectious: viral upper respiratory infections, influenza, COVID-19, pneumonia, urinary tract infections, gastrointestinal infections, skin and soft-tissue infections, and tuberculosis. 2. Inflammatory and autoimmune: rheumatoid diseases, systemic lupus erythematosus and vasculitis. 3. Malignancy-related: persistent or recurrent fever may occur with lymphoma, leukemia and other malignancies. 4. Medication-related and other causes: drug fever, short-term post-vaccination reactions, thromboembolic disease and other non-infectious conditions. Investigations depend on duration and associated symptoms. They may include history and physical examination, complete blood count, CRP, urinalysis and cultures, respiratory pathogen testing and chest imaging. Persistent or unexplained fever should not be managed by TCM pattern differentiation alone. Immunocompromised patients, infants and people with severe systemic symptoms require prompt medical assessment. TCM patterns do not correspond one-to-one with modern medical causes. Wind-cold and wind-heat presentations may overlap symptomatically with some acute respiratory infections, but they cannot identify a specific pathogen. Summer-dampness may coexist with gastrointestinal symptoms or dehydration in hot, humid conditions, but it is not a modern medical diagnosis. Qi, blood, yin and yang deficiency are TCM pathophysiological categories and should not be equated respectively with anemia, endocrine disease or post-infectious conditions. Afternoon tidal fever, night sweats and persistent low-grade fever also warrant investigation for tuberculosis, malignancy and chronic inflammatory conditions. In clinical education and practice, dangerous causes should be assessed before pattern-based point selection. Acupuncture does not replace antimicrobial treatment, antipyretic medication, fluid replacement or necessary emergency care. During fever, assess dehydration, circulatory stability and the risk of transmissible infection; avoid inappropriate strong stimulation and moxibustion.现代医学将发热视为机体体温调定点升高的表现，成人通常以体温达到或超过 38.0°C 作为发热参考值，具体数值受测量部位、测量方法和个体因素影响。发热本身不是病名，而是多种疾病的症状。 常见病因可分为： 1. 感染性：病毒性上呼吸道感染、流感、COVID-19、肺炎、尿路感染、胃肠道感染、皮肤软组织感染，以及结核等。 2. 炎症及免疫性：类风湿性疾病、系统性红斑狼疮、血管炎等。 3. 肿瘤相关：部分淋巴瘤、白血病及其他恶性肿瘤可出现持续或反复发热。 4. 药物及其他原因：药物热、疫苗接种后的短期反应、血栓相关疾病及其他非感染性疾病。 临床检查根据病程和伴随症状选择，可能包括病史与体格检查、血常规、CRP、尿液检查及培养、呼吸道病原检测、胸部影像等。持续或不明原因发热不能只靠针灸辨证处理；免疫抑制患者、婴幼儿及出现严重全身症状者需要更及时的医学评估。 中医证型与现代医学病因并不一一对应。风寒、风热等外感证可与部分急性呼吸道感染的临床表现存在表面交集，但不能据此判断具体病原体；暑湿证与高温潮湿环境下的胃肠道症状或脱水可能同时出现，但不等于现代医学诊断；气虚、血虚、阴虚、阳虚属于中医病机分类，不能分别等同于贫血、内分泌疾病或感染后状态。午后潮热、夜间盗汗、长期低热等表现还需要排查结核、肿瘤及慢性炎症等原因。 针灸学习与临床应用应遵循先排除危险病因、再辨证选穴的原则。针灸不能替代抗感染治疗、退热药物、补液或必要的急诊处理。发热期间还应评估患者是否存在脱水、循环不稳定及传染性疾病风险，避免不适当的强刺激和艾灸。
