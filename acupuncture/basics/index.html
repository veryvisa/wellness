How to find a point accurately怎样把穴位找准

Whether a point is found accurately decides both effect and safety. Tradition gives four methods — proportional bone measurement, surface landmarks, finger measurement and simple methods — and modern practice adds a fifth: confirming with anatomy. They are not alternatives but a coarse-to-fine cross-check: proportional measurement sets the segment and line, landmarks and anatomy lock the spot, palpation confirms it.取穴准不准，决定了针刺有没有效、安不安全。传统上有四种方法：骨度分寸、体表标志、手指同身寸、简便取穴；现代再加上一条——用解剖结构去确认。它们不是四选一，而是由粗到细、互相校核：先用骨度定出大致的段和线，再用标志和解剖结构锁定，最后用触诊确认。

📐 What a 'cun' is「寸」是什么

A cun in acupuncture is not a centimetre on a ruler but a relative length taken from each person's own proportions: on the same limb a tall person's cun is longer, a short person's shorter. A cun therefore has no fixed metric value (roughly 2–3 cm in adults), and you must never measure 2.5 cm and call it one cun for everyone.针灸里的「寸」不是尺子上的厘米，而是按每个人自己身体比例算出来的相对长度：同一段肢体，高个子的 1 寸长，矮个子的 1 寸短。所以 1 寸没有固定厘米数（成人大致在 2–3 厘米之间浮动），绝不能拿尺子量 2.5 厘米当作所有人的 1 寸。

📏 Proportional bone measurement (the authoritative method)骨度分寸法（最权威的折量法）

The distance between two bony landmarks is fixed at a set number of cun regardless of height; divide that span evenly. The method goes back to Lingshu ch.14 and underlies both the Chinese national standard and the WHO standard. 'Vertical' spans measure up–down, 'horizontal' spans side to side.规定两个骨性标志之间的距离是固定的寸数，不管人高矮，把这段等分即可。这是《灵枢·骨度》以来的传统，今天国家标准与 WHO 标准都以它为基础。表中「直寸」用于纵向，「横寸」用于横向。

How to measure怎么量

  1. Feel both bony end-points precisely and mark them lightly.先摸准两端的骨性标志，用笔轻点两端。
  2. Divide the span into the set number of parts (e.g. 12 on the forearm) with fingers or a soft tape — usually halve it first (the 6-cun mark), then subdivide each half.用两手手指或软尺把这段分成规定的等份（如前臂 12 等份）；常用的是先对半分（6 寸处），再在半段里细分。
  3. Place the point at the computed mark, then correct it against nearby landmarks, tendons or pulses.穴位定在计算出的刻度上，再用体表标志、肌腱、动脉搏动等就近校正。
  4. Measure each side separately; limb lengths can differ between sides.左右两侧分别量；同一人两侧肢体长度可能不同。

✋ Finger measurement (a supporting method)手指同身寸（辅助方法）

The patient's own finger widths or segment lengths serve as the ruler. It is convenient but the least precise: finger thickness, swollen or deformed joints all distort it. The national standard treats it as a supplement to proportional bone measurement, never the sole decider — especially on the chest, back and neck. Use the patient's fingers; if you use your own, scale by the size difference.用患者本人手指的宽度或指节长度当尺子。方便，但精度最低：手指粗细、关节肿胀或变形都会让它失真。国家标准把它定为骨度分寸的补充，不能单独决定穴位——尤其在胸背、颈部这类有重要结构的部位。以患者自己的手指为准；用施术者手指时，要按两人手指大小比例换算。

Limits局限

  • Finger measures suit short distances only; beyond 3 cun the error accumulates, so switch to bone measurement.手指同身寸只适合短距离；跨度超过 3 寸时累计误差明显，改用骨度分寸。
  • Scale for size: an adult practitioner's fingers on a child will overshoot badly.比例换算：患者是小孩、施术者是成人时，直接用施术者的手指会严重量长。
  • Arthritis, injured or very plump fingers distort the measure.关节炎、手指外伤、肥胖手指都会让指宽失真。

🦴 Surface landmarks: bony ones are the most reliable体表标志：骨性标志最可靠

Landmarks are fixed (bony prominences, joints, tendons, nipple, navel, nail corners) or movable (hollows or bulges that appear only when the mouth opens, the elbow bends, the fist clenches or the jaw bites). In practice, counting vertebrae and counting ribs are the two commonest tasks — and the commonest sources of error.体表标志分固定标志（骨突、关节、肌腱、乳头、脐、指甲角）和活动标志（张口、屈肘、握拳、咬牙时才出现的凹陷或隆起）。实际取穴时，数椎骨和数肋骨是最常用、也最容易出错的两件事。

C7 spinous process (GV14)第 7 颈椎棘突（大椎）

The most prominent bump at the base of the neck when the head bends forward. Turn the head side to side: C7 moves with it, T1 does not (C6 slides in and out with flexion and extension).低头时颈后最突出的骨突。左右转头：随之转动的是 C7，不动的是 T1（C6 在低头仰头时会前后滑动）。

Medial end of the scapular spine ≈ T3 spinous process肩胛冈内侧端 ≈ 第 3 胸椎棘突

With arms hanging relaxed, the line joining the medial ends of the scapular spines is level with T3 (GV12, BL13).两臂自然下垂时，两肩胛冈内侧端连线平 T3（身柱、肺俞所在）。

Inferior angle of the scapula ≈ T7 spinous process肩胛下角 ≈ 第 7 胸椎棘突

The line joining the inferior angles is level with T7 (GV9, BL17). It fails once the arms are raised and the scapulae move.两肩胛下角连线平 T7（至阳、膈俞所在）。抬臂、肩胛移动时此对应失效。

Line across the tops of the iliac crests ≈ L4 spinous process髂嵴最高点连线 ≈ 第 4 腰椎棘突

The line across the iliac crests is level with L4 or the L4–L5 space (GV3, BL25); individuals vary by up to one vertebra.两髂嵴最高点连线平 L4 棘突或 L4–L5 间隙（腰阳关、大肠俞附近）；个体差异可达一个椎体。

Posterior superior iliac spine ≈ S2髂后上棘 ≈ 第 2 骶椎

Just below the dimples on either side of the sacrum lie the posterior superior iliac spines, level with the second sacral foramen (BL32).骶部两侧的小窝（腰窝）下方即髂后上棘，平第 2 骶后孔（次髎）。

Sternal angle = 2nd rib胸骨角 = 第 2 肋

The transverse ridge where manubrium meets the sternal body joins the 2nd costal cartilages; counting intercostal spaces from here is most reliable. In men the nipple is roughly at the 4th space (not used in women).胸骨柄与胸骨体交界处的横嵴，两侧连接第 2 肋软骨；从这里往下数肋间隙最可靠。男性乳头约平第 4 肋间隙（女性不用乳头定位）。

Movable landmarks, examples活动标志举例

Open the mouth for the hollow in front of the tragus (SI19); bend the elbow for the outer end of the crease (LI11); make a fist for the crease end behind the 5th knuckle (SI3); bite for the peak of masseter (ST6).张口取耳屏前凹陷（听宫）；屈肘取肘横纹外端（曲池）；握拳取第 5 掌指关节后横纹头（后溪）；咬牙时咬肌隆起最高点（颊车）。

🔬 Confirming with anatomy: turning 'feel' into checkable steps现代解剖确认：让「悟性」变成可检验的步骤

  • Find the tendons: have the patient perform the matching action (flex the wrist, extend the thumb, flex the elbow against resistance) and see which two tendons the point lies between.找肌腱：让患者做对应动作（屈腕、伸拇、屈肘抗阻），看清穴位在哪两条腱之间。
  • Find the arteries: LU9, ST42, ST9 and ST30 are marked by a pulse — which is exactly why the needle must avoid it.找动脉：太渊、冲阳、人迎、气冲等穴以动脉搏动为标志，也正因为动脉在那里，刺时要避开。
  • Find bone edges and gaps: many points lie in a hollow between bone and muscle or between two bones; a fingertip gliding along the bone edge drops into it.找骨面与间隙：很多穴在骨与肌之间、两骨之间的凹陷（「分间」「陷者中」），手指沿骨缘滑动能感到落空。
  • Know what lies under the needle: each point page lists the structures from skin to bone, with ⚠️ on those to avoid.想清楚针下有什么：每个穴页的「针下解剖」列出由浅入深的结构，⚠️ 标的是要避开的。
  • Posture moves structures: seated vs lying, elbow bent vs straight change where tendons and vessels sit — locate in the standard posture.体位决定结构位置：坐位与卧位、屈肘与伸肘，肌腱与血管的相对位置会变，定位时用标准体位。
