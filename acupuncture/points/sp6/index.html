🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Medial tibial stress syndrome (shin splints)内侧胫骨应力综合征（胫骨内侧缘骨膜炎 / 夹胫痛）

Common after rapid increases in running or jumping; diffuse tenderness along the posteromedial distal tibial border (often >5 cm) directly overlaps SP6. Focal pinpoint bone tenderness, night pain, or severe weight-bearing pain warrants medical imaging to rule out a tibial stress fracture.常见于跑步或跳跃增量过快者，沿胫骨中下段内侧缘出现弥漫性压痛（常跨度超过 5 cm），正好经过三阴交所在骨缘区。若压痛高度局限在单一骨点且夜间痛、负重即剧痛，需先就医排除胫骨应力性骨折。

Posterior tibial tendinopathy and proximal tarsal tunnel referral胫后肌腱病与踝管综合征近端牵涉痛

Posteromedial ankle pain radiating to the arch, aggravated by resisted inversion, points to posterior tibial tendinopathy; plantar burning or numbness with a positive Tinel sign behind the medial malleolus suggests tibial nerve entrapment (tarsal tunnel syndrome).内踝后上方疼痛并向足弓放射，抗阻足内翻诱发疼痛多提示胫后肌腱炎；若伴足底烧灼感、麻木且叩击内踝后方诱发放射（Tinel 征阳性），需鉴别胫神经卡压（踝管综合征）。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SP6 Sānyīnjiāo?三阴交（SP6）在哪里？

On the medial leg, 3 cun above the prominence of the medial malleolus, posterior to the medial border of the tibia.小腿内侧，内踝尖上 3 寸，胫骨内侧缘后际。

How is SP6 needled?三阴交（SP6）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SP6?三阴交（SP6）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Spleen, Kidney and Liver channels足太阴、足少阴、足厥阴经交会穴

Main safety point at SP6?三阴交（SP6）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy (classically, reducing SP6 with reinforcing LI4 was recorded to induce delivery). Avoid the great saphenous vein; do not angle deep posteromedially toward the posterior tibial vessels and tibial nerve, and withdraw at electric tingling into the sole.孕妇禁针（《铜人》以泻三阴交、补合谷下胎为戒）。避开大隐静脉；针不向后内侧深刺，以免伤胫后血管与胫神经，出现向足底放射的电麻感即退针。
