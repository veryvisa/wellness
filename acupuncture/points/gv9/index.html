📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone or seated leaning forward with the shoulder girdle relaxed and arms hanging naturally.俯卧或坐位前屈，放松肩胛带；双臂自然下垂以便使用肩胛骨标志。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the depression below T7 on the posterior midline. The line between scapular inferior angles is approximate and should be checked against the vertebrae with the shoulders relaxed.后正中线第 7 胸椎棘突下方凹陷；两肩胛下角连线约平 T7，可作初筛，需核对椎棘突而非按肩胛移动后的高度定点。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate T7 and the interspinous tissue below it, with erector-spinae muscles laterally. Ribs and pleura are further lateral and the canal is deep to the midline; avoid deep or lateral procedures.触认 T7 棘突及其下方棘间软组织，两侧为竖脊肌；更外侧逐渐接近肋骨与胸膜，正中深部为椎管，须避免深刺及偏外操作。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Move medially from the scapular-angle level and count the spinous processes, confirming the inferior margin in slight flexion. Arm elevation moves the scapula, so do not use its line in that posture.手指从肩胛下角水平移至脊柱后逐节核对，在轻度前屈时确认棘突下缘；抬臂会移动肩胛骨，不能在该姿势下依肩胛线取点。

Yellow sclera/skin or dark urine needs prompt evaluation for hepatobiliary, haemolytic and other causes; this point does not treat the cause.巩膜或皮肤黄染、尿色深需尽快检查肝胆、溶血等原因；本穴不能替代病因治疗。

Main safety point at GV9?至阳（GV9）最要注意的安全问题？

Thoracic spinous processes slope downward like roof tiles, so angle upward along the interspinous gap; a give (through the ligamentum flavum) or an electric sensation into the limbs means withdraw at once.胸段棘突呈叠瓦状向下，针尖须向上斜刺顺棘突间隙；有落空感（穿过黄韧带）或触电感向肢体放射即退针，不可再进。
