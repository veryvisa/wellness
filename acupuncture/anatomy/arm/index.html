Points and what lies under them穴位与针下要点

LU3Tiānfǔ天府 — Thick muscle and no major vessel or nerve close by: a relatively safe zone of the arm.肌肉厚、无大血管神经贴近，属上臂较安全区。Lower risk较安全LU4Xiábái侠白 — Same layers as LU3: between the lateral biceps and brachialis, relatively safe.与天府同一层次：肱二头肌外缘与肱肌之间，较安全。Lower risk较安全LI12Zhǒuliáo肘髎 — The lateral edge of the humerus is close; the radial nerve and radial collateral artery descend along the lateral intermuscular septum deep to brachioradialis and brachialis.肱骨外缘贴得很近，桡神经与桡侧副动脉在肱桡肌、肱肌深面沿外侧肌间隔下行。Take care需谨慎LI13Shǒuwǔlǐ手五里 — Here the radial nerve has just left the spiral groove and pierced the lateral intermuscular septum with the radial collateral artery: withdraw if you feel a pulse or an electric jolt.桡神经刚绕出桡神经沟、穿外侧肌间隔，伴桡侧副动脉就在这一段：针下有搏动或触电感要退针。Take care需谨慎LI14Bìnào臂臑 — The needle passes from the lower front of deltoid into brachialis, a thick and forgiving layer; the radial nerve lies behind the humerus in the spiral groove, so do not drive deep and backward.针下是三角肌前下部到肱肌，肌层厚而安全；桡神经在肱骨后方的桡神经沟里，方向别偏后深刺。Take care需谨慎
