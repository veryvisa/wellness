📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the head neutral and gently fold the auricle forward to expose the hairline opposite its apex.坐位或仰卧，头正中，耳廓向前轻折，使耳尖对应的颞侧发际清楚。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    At the temporal hairline directly opposite the auricular apex. Fold the auricle forward and locate the hairline opposite the top of the helix; if the hairline is unclear, use the vertical projection of the ear tip.耳尖正对的头侧发际处；将耳廓向前折叠，耳轮最高点正对的发际即为角孙，发际不清时以耳尖垂直投影校正。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently palpate the scalp above the ear apex. The auriculotemporal nerve and branches of superficial temporal vessels are superficial; temporal fascia, temporalis, and temporal bone lie deeper. Confirm there is no prominent pulse or visible vessel and remain superficial to the scalp.轻触耳尖上方头皮，浅层有耳颞神经和颞浅动静脉分支；深面为颞筋膜、颞肌及颞骨。先确认无明显动脉搏动或可见血管，只沿头皮浅层定位，不向颅骨深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Fold the auricle forward and gently palpate the scalp at the hairline opposite its apex. Avoid rather than press on any pulsation of the superficial temporal artery.耳廓向前折后以指腹轻触耳尖上方发际的头皮，寻找发际与耳尖投影交点；颞浅动脉有搏动处应避开而非按压定位。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Use the vertical projection of the ear apex to the hairline as a simple method; with sparse hair or a receding hairline, keep the auricular apex projection as the reference.以耳尖向上作垂直投影至发际作为简便取法；头发稀疏或发际后移时仍以耳廓最高点的投影为准。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Consider TE20 for local hairline or temporal tenderness, especially pain related to chewing; frontal or occipital pain and neurologic red flags require a different assessment.耳尖上方发际或颞区局部压痛、头痛与咀嚼相关时可考虑角孙；前额或枕部为主、伴神经红旗者不以本穴为主。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

May be an adjacent point for tinnitus with superior-ear scalp or temporal discomfort; postauricular tenderness is closer to TE17, and sudden hearing change needs urgent otologic care.耳上方头皮或颞侧不适兼耳鸣可作邻近配穴；耳后压痛更近翳风，突发听力改变须急诊耳科评估。

Toothache牙痛 ⚖️

May be an adjunct for jaw symptoms with chewing difficulty and temporal muscle tension; caries or gingival abscess requires dental treatment.咀嚼困难、颞部肌肉紧张伴牙颌症状可作辅助配穴；龋齿、牙龈脓肿等牙源性病因须牙科治疗。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Temporalis myofascial pain or tension-type headache颞肌筋膜痛或紧张型头痛

Temporal aching after chewing, clenching, or prolonged effort may reflect temporalis overuse; sudden worst-ever headache, neurologic deficits, or fever with neck stiffness needs emergency care.咀嚼、紧咬牙或长时间用力后颞部酸痛可与颞肌过劳有关；突然最严重头痛、神经缺损或发热颈强需急诊。

Localized superficial temporal artery tenderness颞浅动脉局部压痛

Where is TE20 Jiǎosūn?角孙（TE20）在哪里？

Head, at the hairline directly above the apex of the auricle.头部，耳尖正对发际处。

How is TE20 needled?角孙（TE20）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.3–0.5 cun.平刺；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of TE20?角孙（TE20）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Triple Energizer and Gallbladder channels手少阳、足少阳交会穴

Main safety point at TE20?角孙（TE20）最要注意的安全问题？

Superficial temporal vessels are superficial: avoid a pulse and visible vessels, needle only along the scalp, and do not needle deeply.颞浅血管表浅，先避开搏动和可见血管；仅沿头皮平刺，不可深刺。
