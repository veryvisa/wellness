Ten minutes before bed: a self-massage routine for restless nights睡前十分钟：给睡不着的夜晚一套按摩

The lights are off but your mind is still in a meeting. These ten minutes are not a sleeping pill; they give you something slow to do: a fixed sequence for the hands, and attention that moves from tomorrow's to-do list to the ache under your fingertips. When you finish, lie down and leave the phone alone.关了灯，脑子却还在开会。睡前这十分钟的按摩不是安眠药，它给你一件慢下来的事可做：手上有固定的顺序，注意力从明天的待办挪到指尖的酸胀上。按完直接躺下，不再看手机。

⏱ About 10 minutes约 10 分钟 · After washing up, sitting on the edge of the bed; lie down and turn off the light when you finish.洗漱之后、上床之前，坐在床边做；按完关灯躺下。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Loud snoring, and someone has seen you stop breathing at night打鼾很响，被人看到夜里憋气、停止呼吸
  • Nodding off while driving白天开车时打瞌睡
  • Mood that stays low, or thoughts of harming yourself: in danger call 911, or call or text 9-8-8情绪持续低落或有伤害自己的念头：危险时拨 911，或拨打、短信 9-8-8
  • Sleeplessness with fever, marked weight loss, chest pain or palpitations失眠伴发热、体重明显下降、胸痛或心悸
  • Bad nights three or more times a week for three months: ask your family doctor; the first-choice treatment is cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I)一周有三晚以上睡不好、已经持续三个月：请家庭医生评估，首选的是失眠认知行为疗法（CBT-I）

✋ When not to press这些情况先别按

  • In pregnancy, skip SP6 Sanyinjiao: a traditional pregnancy caution孕期不按三阴交（SP6），这是传统的孕期禁忌
  • Never press over broken, red or swollen skin or a rash皮肤破损、红肿、皮疹处不按
  • If you take warfarin or another blood thinner, press lightly and stop if you bruise在吃华法林等抗凝药的人力度放轻，容易淤青就停
  • With diabetes or numb soles, rub the feet gently and check the skin afterwards糖尿病或脚底麻木的人搓脚心要轻，搓完看一眼皮肤

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1
    GV20 Bǎihuì百会Pushing推法

    Spread your fingers and 'comb' with the finger pads from the front hairline back to the nape, then knead GV20 Baihui at the top of the head with the middle finger.十指张开，用指腹从前发际向后「梳」到后颈，再用中指指腹按揉头顶正中的百会。

    How much力度与时间 Comb 20–30 times; knead GV20 for about 1 minute梳 20–30 遍；百会按揉约 1 分钟

  2. 2
    Anmian Ānmián安眠Kneading揉法

    Place both middle fingers on Anmian, behind each ear lobe between the bony bump behind the ear and the hollow at the back of the neck, and knead both sides together.两手中指放在耳垂后方、耳后骨与颈后凹陷之间的安眠穴，同时按揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

  3. 3
    PC6 Nèiguān内关Kneading揉法

    Put one thumb on PC6 Neiguan of the other arm, 2 cun (about three finger-widths) above the wrist crease between the two tendons; knead, then switch hands.一手拇指放在另一手腕横纹上 2 寸（约三横指）、两条筋之间的内关，按揉后换手。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side每侧约 1 分钟

  4. 4
    HT7 Shénmén神门Pressing按法

    Find HT7 Shenmen in the hollow on the little-finger side of the wrist crease, just inside the tendon; press gently with the thumb, hold a few seconds, release.在腕横纹小指一侧、那条筋的内侧凹陷处找到神门，拇指轻按、停几秒再松。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side每侧约 1 分钟

    ⚠️ The ulnar artery and nerve lie right under HT7: if tingling shoots to the little finger, ease off.神门下面就是尺动脉和尺神经：按到麻感窜向小指就松一点。

  5. 5
    SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交Kneading揉法

    Sitting, rest one foot on the other knee; find SP6 Sanyinjiao 3 cun (about four finger-widths) above the tip of the inner ankle bone, just behind the edge of the shinbone; knead, then switch legs.坐着把一只脚放到另一侧膝上，在内踝尖上 3 寸（约四横指）、胫骨内侧缘后面找到三阴交，按揉后换腿。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side; skip this step in pregnancy每侧约 1 分钟；孕期跳过这一步

  6. 6
    KI1 Yǒngquán涌泉Friction rubbing擦法

    Rub KI1 Yongquan (the deepest hollow of the sole when you curl the toes) briskly with the opposite palm until it feels warm, then switch feet.用对侧手掌来回擦脚心的涌泉（卷起脚趾时脚底最凹的地方），擦到发热后换脚。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side, until warm每侧约 1 分钟，以温热为度

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E1 The first-line starting point for chronic insomnia is cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I); guidelines from the American College of Physicians and the American Academy of Sleep Medicine both put it ahead of medication. Massage can be part of winding down, but it is not a substitute for CBT-I.慢性失眠的首选起点是失眠认知行为疗法（CBT-I），美国内科医师学会和美国睡眠医学会的指南都把它排在吃药之前。按摩可以是睡前放松的一部分，但不能替代 CBT-I。 [1] [2]
  • E2 A systematic review of 40 randomised trials found acupressure on its own only marginally better than sham pressure, and every trial had at least one area at high risk of bias; the authors concluded the evidence does not allow a clear conclusion.一篇汇总 40 项随机试验的系统综述发现：穴位按压单独使用只比假按压略好一点，而且每项试验都至少有一处高偏倚风险，作者认为现有证据下不了明确结论。 [3]
  • E2 A systematic review of self-administered acupressure (10 studies) reported mostly favourable results, including stress, fatigue and sleep measures in healthy people, but the samples were small and the authors called for larger, better-designed trials.自我穴位按压的系统综述（10 项研究）报告的结果偏正面，其中包括健康人的压力、疲劳与睡眠指标，但样本都不大，作者要求更大、设计更好的试验。 [4]
  • E5 The massage itself carries very little risk; the most common problem is soreness or bruising from pressing too hard.按摩本身风险很低，最常见的不适是按得太重后的酸痛或淤青。

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 HT7, PC6, SP6, GV20 and Anmian are points traditionally used to 'calm the spirit', and rubbing the soles before bed is a common practice in traditional self-care books. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.神门、内关、三阴交、百会和安眠，都是传统上用于「安神」的常用穴；睡前擦脚心是传统养生书里常见的做法。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
