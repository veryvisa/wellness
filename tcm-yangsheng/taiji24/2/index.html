Form 2: Parting the wild horse's mane, left and right第2式 · 左右野马分鬃

left, right, left; 3 in all左、右、左，共 3 次

Zuǒyòu yěmǎ fēnzōng

Qigong养生功法 › Tai chi (simplified 24-form)太极拳（简化二十四式） › Form 2: Parting the wild horse's mane, left and right第2式 · 左右野马分鬃

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ Higher knee load膝部负荷较大 ⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚

  • Knee pain: stand high, bending the knee no more than about 30 degrees, and take small steps; keep the knee in line with the toes and not past them; stop if it hurts. In one laboratory study this form loaded the knee more than Cloud Hands or Grasping the Sparrow's Tail, so do fewer at first膝盖痛：架子站高，弯膝到约 30 度为止，步子迈小；膝盖对着脚尖方向，不超过脚尖；痛就停。一项实测里，这一式的膝部负荷比云手、揽雀尾大，初学先少做
  • If you fall easily or your balance is poor: hold the back of a chair or a counter, or do the arms only容易跌倒、平衡不好：扶着椅背或台面做，或只做手的动作
  • After a hip or knee replacement: turn the toes first, then shift the weight; never twist the knee with the foot planted髋、膝换过关节：转体时先动脚尖，再移重心，不要脚钉在地上拧膝

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Turn slightly right with the weight on the right leg; right hand in front of the chest, palm down, left hand in front of the belly, palm up, as if holding a ball; draw the left foot in beside the right, toes touching上身稍向右转，重心在右腿；右手在胸前、掌心向下，左手划到腹前、掌心向上，像抱一个球；左脚收到右脚内侧，脚尖点地
  2. Turn left and step the left foot forward and out, heel landing lightly first身体左转，左脚向左前方迈出，脚跟先轻轻着地
  3. Shift your weight slowly forward into a left bow stance; as the body turns, the hands part: the left rises to eye level, palm slanting up, the right sinks beside the right hip, palm down重心慢慢前移，成左弓步；两手随转体上下分开：左手到眼睛高度、掌心斜向上，右手落到右胯旁、掌心向下
  4. Sit back slowly, lift the left toes and turn them out, then move the weight onto the left leg; 'hold the ball' on the other side, draw the right foot in beside the left, and do the right side上身慢慢后坐，左脚尖翘起、向外撇，再把重心移到左腿；换手「抱球」，右脚收到左脚内侧，接着做右式
  5. After the right side, sit back, turn the foot out and draw the other foot in the same way, and do the left side once more右式做完，同样后坐、撇脚、收脚，再做一次左式

🌬 Breathe in as you hold the ball, out as the hands part and the weight moves forward; or breathe naturally.抱球时吸气，分手、前移时呼气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • Place the foot first, then move the weight; while you step, the weight stays on the back leg脚先站稳，重心再走；迈步时重心还在后腿
  • The waist leads the arms; hands and feet arrive together腰带着手走，手脚同时到位
  • In the bow stance keep a hip's width sideways between the feet; do not stand on one line弓步时两脚之间留一胯宽的横向距离，别踩在一条线上

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Feet on one line, so you wobble两脚踩成一条线，站不稳
  • Front knee past the toes or falling inward前膝超过脚尖或向内扣
  • Leaning back as you sit back后坐时上身后仰

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Stand without stepping and only do the ball-holding and parting of the hands; or take half your usual walking step and rest the toes on the floor each time the foot comes in.站着不迈步，只做抱球、分手；或步子迈到平时走路的一半，脚收回时脚尖点地歇一下。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 The name is an image: one hand rises and the other sinks as if parting the mane on a wild horse's head. It is a traditional image, not a research finding.名字是个比喻：两手一上一下分开，像拨开野马头顶的鬃毛。这是传统的比喻，不是研究结论。
