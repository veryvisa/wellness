Posture体位 · Supine or seated with the foot relaxed and its medial border facing the practitioner.仰卧或正坐位，足部自然放松，足内侧朝向医者。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Run a finger from the tip of the great toe back along its medial edge to the big joint at its base—the first metatarsophalangeal joint; SP2 is in the hollow just distal to it (toe side), on the red-white border (national standard).顺着足大趾内侧缘从趾尖往足跟方向摸，摸到大趾根部的大关节——第 1 跖趾关节；大都在关节远端（趾侧）内侧的赤白肉际凹陷中（国标）。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Passively flex and extend the great toe to confirm the first MTP joint; SP2 is just distal to it, in the hollow beside the base of the proximal phalanx on the red-white border. The proper plantar digital nerve and branches of the medial plantar vessels are under the skin—avoid visible veins; bone and joint capsule are just deep, so do not insert deeply.被动屈伸足大趾可确认第 1 跖趾关节的位置；大都在关节远端、近节趾骨底内侧的凹陷中，紧贴赤白肉际。皮下有足底内侧神经的趾底固有神经和足底内侧血管分支，避开可见浅静脉；深面即骨面与关节囊，不可直刺过深。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Proportional bone measurements and finger-cun measures are not used here; rely directly on the distal edge of the bony joint prominence and the medial dorsoventral skin-colour border.此处不用骨度折量，也无需手指同身寸量取，直接以大趾关节骨性突起前缘及足内侧肤色交界线（赤白肉际）为唯一准绳。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Look along the medial side of the great toe where the darker dorsal skin meets the paler, thicker plantar skin; the slight hollow just distal to the toe joint along this line is SP2, usually tender to pressure.观察足大趾内侧，背面肤色较深、足底皮肤较白且角质层较厚，两色交界线在趾关节前方略微凹陷处即是；按压时常有明显的酸胀或微痛感。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a thumb pad back and forth along the medial skin-colour border of the great toe to feel the small hollow where the bone slopes distal to the joint. Firm pressure can trigger sharp tingling radiating toward the toe tip (stimulating the digital branch of the medial plantar nerve), confirming location but indicating excessive pressure.用拇指指腹沿大趾内侧赤白肉际前后轻推，指下可触及关节囊前缘与骨面交界的小凹陷；用力按压易诱发向趾尖放射的针刺样麻感（触及足底内侧神经趾支），提示位置准确但按压过重。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Fever发热 ⚖️

Selected for fever with anhidrosis accompanied by epigastric/abdominal fullness ('Ying-spring treats body heat'); for high fever with delirium or severe sore throat, lead with GV14, LI11, or bloodletting at LU11 rather than SP2.外感或里热证见身热、无汗、兼有脾胃脘腹胀闷时选用（取「荥主身热」）；若属高热神昏或咽喉剧痛，首选大椎（GV14）、曲池（LI11）或少商（LU11）点刺出血，不以大都为主。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Gouty arthritis of the first metatarsophalangeal or interphalangeal joint第 1 跖趾关节/趾间关节痛风性关节炎（痛风急性期或慢性痛风石）

The medial great toe is the classic site for acute gout, presenting with sudden nocturnal severe pain, redness, swelling, and warmth. Never needle directly into acutely inflamed, hot, swollen tissue due to infection and pain-flare risk; refer for serum uric acid testing and anti-inflammatory care first.足大趾内侧是痛风最常见好发部位，表现为夜间突发剧痛、局部红肿热痛、皮肤发亮。急性红肿热痛期严禁在红肿中心直接针刺（防继发感染与加重疼痛），应先就医查血尿酸及抗炎治疗；间歇期可在周围取穴调理。

Hallux valgus (bunion) and first metatarsophalangeal osteoarthritis拇外翻（Bunion）及第 1 跖趾关节骨关节炎

Tight footwear or biomechanical stress causes lateral toe deviation, medial bony prominence, and bursitis right over the SP2–SP3 region. Differentiate via palpation and X-ray; avoid deep needling into an inflamed bunion bursa.穿窄鞋或长期负重导致大趾外偏、内侧骨赘增生与滑囊炎，局部压痛点与大都、太白区域高度重叠。需通过触诊骨性畸形与 X 线鉴别，避免将关节滑囊炎症误当普通穴位压痛而强力按压或深刺滑囊。

Entrapment of the medial plantar proper digital nerve (Joplin neuroma)足底内侧神经趾支卡压或摩顿神经瘤样前足痛（Joplin 神经瘤）

Where is SP2 Dàdū?大都（SP2）在哪里？

On the great toe, in the depression distal to the first metatarsophalangeal joint, at the border of the red and white flesh.足趾，第 1 跖趾关节远端赤白肉际凹陷中。

How is SP2 needled?大都（SP2）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SP2?大都（SP2）是哪类特定穴？

Ying-spring (fire)荥穴（火）

Main safety point at SP2?大都（SP2）最要注意的安全问题？

Thin skin over shallow bone: stay shallow and keep out of the metatarsophalangeal joint capsule.皮薄骨浅：浅刺，不刺入跖趾关节囊。
