    The distance between the two mastoid processes behind the ear is measured as 9 cun, and can also be cross-checked with the 3-cun span from below the seventh cervical spinous process to the midpoint of the posterior hairline before locating along the line between GB10 and GB12.耳后两乳突（完骨）之间骨度折作 9 寸，也可结合第 7 颈椎棘突下（大椎）至后发际正中 3 寸、耳后发际折量关系核对耳后横向与上下位置，再以浮白、完骨连线定位。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Superficial layers posterosuperior to the mastoid contain branches of the lesser occipital and great auricular nerves plus posterior auricular artery branches, overlying sternocleidomastoid and splenius capitis attachments and the mastoid bone; avoid the posterior auricular vessels and nerve branches during needling.乳突后上方浅层有枕小神经与耳大神经分支及耳后动脉分支走行，深层邻近胸锁乳突肌与头夹肌附着部及颞骨乳突部骨面；进针时注意避开耳后血管与神经分支。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a fingertip gently posterosuperior to the mastoid between GB10 and GB12 to find tenderness or a shallow depression; light pressure may produce local soreness or slight radiation toward the postauricular or occipital area without pressing hard on the bone edge.指腹在乳突后上方沿浮白至完骨方向轻柔滑动寻找压痛点或浅凹，轻压局部可能出现局部酸胀或向耳后、枕侧放散的轻微感觉，勿重压骨缘。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting anteroinferiorly onto the mastoid tip or sternocleidomastoid insertion edge: easily confused with GB12 near sensitive muscle attachments, where perpendicular or deep insertion causes sharp soreness.偏向前下方触及乳突尖或胸锁乳突肌止点边缘：易误入完骨 GB12 一带，且肌附着处较敏感，直刺或深刺易引起明显酸痛。
  • Drifting posteroinferiorly toward the suboccipital muscles or foramen magnum: this lies near sensitive neurovascular structures, and scalp points require transverse insertion of 0.3–0.5 cun rather than deep or inward-angled needling.偏向后下方深入枕下肌群或枕骨大孔方向：该处靠近枕下重要结构与血管神经，且头皮穴位规范为沿头皮平刺 0.3–0.5 寸，深刺或斜向深部均属不当。
  • Failing to press firmly after pricking a posterior auricular artery branch: the postauricular scalp has rich blood supply, so inadequate post-withdrawal pressure can cause a subcutaneous hematoma.误刺耳后动脉分支后未充分按压：头皮耳后血供丰富，出针后若不及时按压易形成皮下血肿。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

GB11 fits headaches involving the postauricular, posterosuperior mastoid, or temporal Shaoyang region; for holocephalic, frontal, or deficiency-related headaches it is not primary and should be compared with GB20, GV20, or distal points.头窍阴适合偏于耳后、乳突后上方或头侧少阳循行区的头痛与局部紧张；若为全头痛、前额痛或内伤虚损头痛，则不以头窍阴为主，可与风池、百会或远端穴位对比选用。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

GB11 can serve as a local adjunct for tinnitus related to the postauricular and Shaoyang region, though sudden hearing loss, middle ear infection, or tympanic perforation requires medical evaluation first, and selection is often compared with SI19, TE, or TE.头窍阴可作为耳后及少阳经循行相关耳鸣的局部辅助穴位，但急性突发耳聋、中耳感染或鼓膜穿孔等需先就医评估，临床上常与听宫、翳风、中渚等耳周及少阳穴位对比配伍。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

GB11 suits neck discomfort centered around the posterosuperior mastoid, posterior mastoid margin, and upper lateral neck; when suboccipital midline stiffness, broad neck-back tightness, or acute wry neck predominates, compare with GB20, SI3, or GB39 rather than relying on GB11 alone.头窍阴适用于偏重耳后上方、乳突后缘及颈侧上段牵拉感的颈项不适；若以枕下正中、项背广泛僵硬或落枕为主，则常以风池、后溪、悬钟等穴位对比权衡，不单靠头窍阴。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Lesser occipital neuralgia or postauricular nerve sensitivity枕小神经痛或耳后神经敏感

Presents as paroxysmal stabbing, burning, or hyperesthesia posterosuperior to the mastoid or lateral occiput, often provoked by local pressure; seek medical evaluation first if pain worsens or clear neurological deficits appear.表现为耳后、乳突后上方或枕外侧阵发性刺痛、烧灼感或触觉过敏，局部按压常可诱发放射痛；若疼痛持续加重或伴明显神经缺损，应先就医明确诊断。

Cervicogenic headache and head-neck myofascial tension颈源性头痛与头颈肌筋膜紧张

Often linked to poor neck posture or tension in sternocleidomastoid, splenius capitis, and suboccipital muscles radiating toward the temple or behind the ear; new severe headache or neurological symptoms require prompt medical evaluation.常由颈部姿势不良或胸锁乳突肌、头夹肌及枕部肌群紧张牵拉引起，可向颞部或耳后放射；新发剧烈头痛或伴有意识、视力、肢体异常时必须先就医。

Tinnitus and ear fullness discomfort耳鸣与耳部闷胀相关不适

Tinnitus involves auditory pathways, vascular factors, cerumen, or middle/inner ear conditions, so acupuncture is only an adjunct to ENT assessment, and full treatment plans require careful review during pregnancy.耳鸣涉及听觉通路、血管、耳垢或中耳/内耳病变，针灸仅可作为辅助调理，不可替代耳鼻喉科检查；妊娠期使用相关配穴时需结合整体方案审慎评估。
