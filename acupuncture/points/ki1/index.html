    Anatomy解剖结构

Headache头痛 ⚖️

KI1 may be selected as a distal adjunct when headache is associated with traditionally diagnosed rising liver yang or a Liver–Kidney pattern, particularly with a sensation of heaviness in the head and heat in the soles. It may be combined with LR3 and GV20. For external headache or marked neck stiffness, consider more directly indicated points such as LU7 and GB20 instead. Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

KI1 may be considered with KI3, LR3, or GV20 for dizziness traditionally attributed to rising liver yang or Liver–Kidney yin deficiency, particularly with a heavy-headed sensation, after acute neurological and cardiovascular causes have been excluded. Insomnia不寐（失眠） ⚖️

KI1 may be selected with KI6 and HT7 for insomnia accompanied by restlessness, dry throat, nocturnal plantar heat, and lower-back or knee weakness, traditionally interpreted as heart–Kidney disharmony or yin deficiency with fire hyperactivity. Insomnia alone does not justify selecting KI1.失眠伴心烦、咽干、夜间足心热、腰膝酸软等心肾不交或阴虚火旺表现时，可选涌泉配照海、神门；心脾两虚、气血不足而以健忘、乏力、食少为主时，应更多考虑神门、三阴交、足三里等辨证穴组，不能仅凭失眠症状使用涌泉。

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

KI1 may be considered with KI6 for dry throat and upward disturbance traditionally attributed to Kidney yin deficiency, especially when accompanied by plantar heat and lower-back or knee weakness. Fainting (first aid)晕厥（急救）

KI1 is frequently mentioned in traditional acupuncture discussions of emergency points, but actual syncope requires immediate assessment of consciousness, breathing, and circulation. Activate emergency services and provide CPR or use an automated external defibrillator when indicated. 🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Plantar fasciopathy足底筋膜病（Plantar fasciopathy）

Typical symptoms include pain at the medial heel or proximal plantar fascia, especially with the first steps in the morning, after prolonged sitting, or following extended weight-bearing. Pain may sometimes extend toward the anterior sole, but the usual tender site is not the standard KI1 location. Medial calcaneal tenderness and increased pain when the toes are dorsiflexed to tension the plantar fascia can assist differentiation. Tenderness from plantar fasciopathy must not automatically be interpreted as an acupoint response. Persistent pain, impaired weight-bearing, or diagnostic uncertainty warrants assessment by a family physician or foot and ankle specialist.常见表现为足跟内侧或足底筋膜近端疼痛，晨起下床第一步、久坐后起步或长时间负重后疼痛明显。虽然疼痛有时向足底前部延伸，但典型压痛点通常不在涌泉的标准位置。可通过足跟内侧结节压痛、足趾背伸牵拉足底筋膜时疼痛加重等表现辅助鉴别。不能把足底筋膜病的压痛直接当作涌泉穴位反应。若疼痛持续、影响负重或诊断不明确，应进行足踝专科或家庭医生评估。

Medial plantar nerve entrapment and plantar neuropathy足底内侧神经卡压及足底神经病变

Symptoms may include numbness, burning, electric-shock-like pain, or altered sensation along the medial sole, arch, and some toes. Unlike isolated myofascial tenderness, neuropathic pain often radiates along a nerve distribution and may be accompanied by reduced or abnormal sensation. Assess plantar sensation, muscle strength, and symptom distribution, with referral for neurological evaluation when appropriate. If palpation at KI1 produces electric-shock-like radiation toward the toes, do not repeatedly compress or needle the area.可出现足底内侧、足弓及部分足趾的麻木、烧灼感、电击样疼痛或感觉异常。与单纯肌筋膜压痛不同，神经性疼痛常沿神经分布区域放射，可伴感觉减退或异常。应检查足底感觉、肌力及症状分布，必要时转诊进行神经系统评估。若触诊涌泉时出现向足趾放射的电击感，不应反复加压或直接进针。

Lateral plantar nerve entrapment and tarsal tunnel syndrome足底外侧神经卡压及跗管综合征

Tarsal tunnel syndrome involves compression of the tibial nerve within the tunnel behind the medial malleolus and may cause plantar burning, numbness, and radiating pain. Lateral plantar nerve entrapment may produce lateral plantar pain or sensory disturbance. Symptoms are often not confined to KI1 but extend along plantar nerve distributions. Assess whether percussion behind the medial malleolus reproduces plantar radiation and examine plantar sensation and symptom distribution. Persistent numbness, weakness, or progressive symptoms require medical assessment rather than treating the condition solely with KI1 needling.跗管综合征涉及胫神经在内踝后方跗管内受压，可引起足底烧灼、麻木和放射性疼痛；足底外侧神经卡压可出现足底外侧疼痛或感觉异常。症状常不局限于涌泉，而沿足底神经分布区域扩展。应检查内踝后方叩击是否诱发足底放射感、足底感觉及症状分布。持续麻木、肌力下降或症状进行性加重，应先进行医学评估，不宜以涌泉针刺代替神经压迫诊断。

Metatarsal stress injury and metatarsal fracture跖骨应力性损伤及跖骨骨折

Metatarsal stress injuries and fractures may cause localized pain in the anterior sole or metatarsal region, worsening with weight-bearing, walking, and direct bony pressure. Risk is increased after long-distance running, sudden increases in exercise, osteoporosis, or trauma. Unlike mild tenderness during normal KI1 localization, bony injury often causes focal bone tenderness and pain with weight-bearing, sometimes accompanied by swelling or bruising. Clinical examination and appropriate imaging, including radiographs or other studies when indicated, are required to exclude bone injury. Do not needle a suspected fracture site.可出现足底前部或跖骨区域局限性疼痛，负重、行走及局部骨性按压时加重。长跑、突然增加运动量、骨质疏松或外伤后应提高警惕。与涌泉正常取穴时的轻度酸胀不同，骨性损伤常有明确局限性骨压痛及负重痛，部分患者出现肿胀、瘀斑。需要通过临床检查及必要的 X 线或其他影像学检查排除骨损伤，不能直接在疑似骨折处针刺。

Plantar soft-tissue contusion and foreign-body injury足底软组织挫伤及异物损伤

Stepping on a sharp object, glass, or a splinter, or sustaining a direct impact, may cause focal tenderness, swelling, bruising, or sharp pain near KI1. A foreign body may be embedded in the plantar fat pad or near the fascia even when the skin opening is not obvious. Ask about trauma and inspect the skin, local temperature, and possible entry wound. Suspected deep foreign bodies, puncture injuries, or infection require medical assessment. Do not attempt to remove a foreign body through acupuncture.踩到尖锐物、玻璃、木刺或外物撞击后，可出现涌泉附近局限性压痛、肿胀、瘀斑或刺痛。异物可能埋藏于足底脂肪垫或筋膜附近，表面伤口不一定明显。应询问外伤史，检查皮肤、局部温度和是否存在异物入口。怀疑深部异物、穿刺伤或感染时，应先就医评估，不能通过针刺尝试取出异物。
