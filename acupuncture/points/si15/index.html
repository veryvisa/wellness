Where is SI15 Jiānzhōngshū?肩中俞（SI15）在哪里？

In the upper back, at the level of the inferior border of the C7 spinous process, 2 cun lateral to the posterior midline.脊柱区，第 7 颈椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 2 寸。

How is SI15 needled?肩中俞（SI15）怎么刺？

Oblique; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of SI15?肩中俞（SI15）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Small Intestine channel手太阳小肠经穴

Main safety point at SI15?肩中俞（SI15）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleural dome and lung apex lie deep: shallow oblique insertion only, never deep perpendicular and never toward the chest cavity.深面是胸膜顶和肺尖：只作浅斜刺，不可直刺深刺，不朝胸腔方向。
