With the arm hanging and adducted, measure up from the end of the fold by one of the patient's own thumb-widths (the width at the thumb's interphalangeal joint).国标：肩关节后下方，腋后纹头直上 1 寸。上臂内收、自然下垂，在腋后纹头向上量患者本人拇指同身寸 1 寸（拇指指间关节宽度）。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Below the fold the point falls into the armpit; too high it lands on the posterior deltoid. The axillary nerve, posterior circumflex humeral vessels and radial nerve lie deep; needling deeply inward toward the armpit and chest wall is the main danger. Insert straight or slightly lateral, trained practitioners only.取在腋后纹头以下会进到腋窝皱襞内；过高则落在三角肌后束上。此处深面有腋神经、旋肱后血管、桡神经，向内（腋窝、胸壁方向）深刺尤其危险；直刺或略向外，限受训人员。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

Consider as a local Small Intestine point for posterior shoulder pain with limited elevation or extension; its deep risk is high, so suspected cuff tear, dislocation, or neurovascular injury needs assessment first.肩后痛并上举或后伸受限时可作局部小肠经点；深部风险高，疑肩袖撕裂、脱位或神经血管损伤时先就医。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

SI9 is at the posterior shoulder, not the elbow; select elbow points by local structures rather than treating this shoulder point as a local elbow point.肩贞在肩后而非肘部；肘痛应按压痛结构选择肘周穴，勿因经脉相连误将肩穴作为局部治疗。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Rotator-cuff tendinopathy or subacromial pain肩袖肌腱病或肩峰下疼痛

Pain with elevation, night pain when lying on the shoulder, and weak resisted external rotation warrant rotator-cuff assessment; tenderness near SI9 is not diagnostic.抬臂痛、夜间侧卧痛及抗阻外旋无力需评估肩袖；肩贞邻近肩后软组织，不应据穴位压痛作诊断。

Injury near the axillary nerve or posterior circumflex humeral artery腋神经或旋肱后动脉区域损伤

Where is SI9 Jiānzhēn?肩贞（SI9）在哪里？

In the scapular region, posteroinferior to the shoulder joint, 1 cun superior to the posterior axillary fold.肩胛区，肩关节后下方，腋后纹头直上 1 寸。

How is SI9 needled?肩贞（SI9）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SI9?肩贞（SI9）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Small Intestine channel手太阳小肠经穴

Main safety point at SI9?肩贞（SI9）最要注意的安全问题？
