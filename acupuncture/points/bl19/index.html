  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun; BL19 is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun out, level with the lower edge of the T10 spine.肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸，胆俞在中点，旁开 1.5 寸；纵向在第 10 胸椎棘突下。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Arms hanging, the inferior angles of the scapulae are about level with T7 (BL17); count down T8, T9, T10 and take the point level with the lower edge of T10, 1.5 cun out (beside GV7). Cross-check by counting up from the iliac crests (about L4).两臂下垂，肩胛下角连线约平 T7（膈俞所在）；往下数 T8、T9、T10，在 T10 棘突下缘水平旁开 1.5 寸（中枢 GV7 旁）。也可从髂嵴最高点（约 L4）往上数核对。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Skin, subcutaneous tissue, the lower trapezius and latissimus dorsi, erector spinae; beneath are the 10th and 11th ribs and the space between. Beside the spine the pleura reaches down to the 12th rib, so pleura and the lower lung edge (costodiaphragmatic recess) still lie deep here, with the liver further in on the right.皮肤、皮下组织、斜方肌下部与背阔肌、竖脊肌；其下为第 10、11 肋与肋间隙。胸膜后下界在脊柱旁可低到第 12 肋，所以这里深面仍是胸膜和肺下缘（肋膈隐窝），右侧再前方是肝。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    In gallbladder disease the right paraspinal area at T10–T12 is often tender or hypersensitive (the zone of Boas sign); press toward the spine.胆囊病的人右侧 T10–T12 脊旁常有压痛或皮肤过敏（Boas 征的区域）；按压时朝脊柱方向用力。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Assuming the lower chest is safe and needling straight in or laterally: the pleura has not ended here and pneumothorax is still possible. Insert only obliquely toward the spine, 0.5–0.8 cun.以为到了下胸段就不会气胸，垂直深刺或向外刺：胸膜在此仍未结束，照样可致气胸。只向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸。
  • Miscounting levels: counting down from the inferior angle drifts by one or two levels if the arms are raised or the back is rounded. Confirm from C7 or the iliac crests.数错椎节：从肩胛下角往下数时，抬臂或驼背会让起点漂一两节。最后从 C7 或髂嵴核对。
  • Taking it 3 cun out: that is BL48 on the second line.取在旁开 3 寸：那是阳纲（BL48），第二侧线。

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of BL19?胆俞（BL19）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Gallbladder胆之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL19?胆俞（BL19）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only shallow oblique insertion toward the spine; never insert deeply or laterally.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向浅斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
