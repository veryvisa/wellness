Calm the Stomach Powder平胃散

Píng Wèi Sǎn · Taiping Huimin Heji Jufang (Song dynasty); anchor text taken from Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie (Qing dynasty)宋《太平惠民和剂局方》；原文锚点取清·汪昂《医方集解》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Calm the Stomach Powder平胃散

Original text原文

「治脾有停溼痰飲，痞膈，宿食不消，滿闷呕瀉；及山岚瘴雾，不服水土」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Indicated for dampness and phlegm-fluid retention in the spleen and stomach, focal distention in the chest and diaphragm, undigested retained food, fullness, nausea, and diarrhea, as well as mountain miasma and unacclimation to local water and soil.主治脾胃停聚湿浊痰饮、胸膈痞塞、宿食不化、脘腹胀满闷乱、呕吐泄泻，以及山岚瘴气、水土不服。

「苍术（泔浸，二錢）厚朴（薑炒）陳皮（去白）甘草（炙，一錢。）加薑、棗煎。」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Composition and preparation using rice-water-soaked Atractylodes rhizome (two qian), ginger-fried Magnolia bark, white-pith-removed tangerine peel, and honey-fried licorice (one qian), decocted with fresh ginger and jujube.组成与用法：苍术（米泔水浸泡，二钱）、厚朴（姜汁炒）、陈皮（去白）、甘草（炙，一钱），加生姜、大枣水煎服。

「此足太陰、陽明藥也。苍术辛烈，燥溼而繩脾；厚朴苦溫，除溼而散滿」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Formula analysis noting its action on the Foot Taiyin and Yangming channels, where acrid and intense Atractylodes dries dampness and strengthens the spleen, while bitter and warm Magnolia bark eliminates dampness and disperses fullness.方义解析：本方入足太阴脾经与足阳明胃经，苍术辛烈燥湿健脾，厚朴苦温除湿消散胀满。

Cautions使用注意

This formula is acrid, warm, and drying, which can easily consume yin and blood; avoid in patterns of yin deficiency with fluid depletion or qi deficiency without dampness, and use with caution during pregnancy. Seek prompt medical evaluation if severe persistent abdominal pain, high fever, dehydration from frequent vomiting, or bloody stools occur.本方辛温燥烈，易耗伤阴血，阴虚津亏、气虚无湿者忌用；孕妇慎用。若出现持续剧烈腹痛、高热、频繁呕吐致脱水或便血等症状，须及时就医检查。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Cang Zhu and Gan Cao carry a pregnancy caution on this site, and the acrid-warm drying nature of the formula may consume qi and yin; therefore, the formula takes the strictest caution level of its herbs.苍术与甘草在本站按孕期慎用管理，全方辛温燥湿亦易耗气伤阴，方随其最严者定为慎用。
