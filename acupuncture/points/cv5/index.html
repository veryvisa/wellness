📍 Location定位★

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Oedema水肿 ⚖️

Only an adjunct for a traditional fluid-pattern assessment; new generalized edema, breathlessness or unilateral leg swelling needs cause evaluation first.仅作传统水液代谢证候的配穴参考；全身新发水肿、气短或单侧腿肿须先查病因。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Consider for infraumbilical midline pain with traditional Sanjiao or fluid features; compare CV9 for periumbilical and CV2 for suprapubic localization.脐下中线、伴传统三焦或水湿表现时考虑；疼痛偏脐周或耻骨上时分别比较水分、曲骨等局部穴。

Period pain痛经

Textbooks describe lower-abdominal use, but it is not the lead for menstrual pain; differentiate by pattern from CV4/CV3, and assess abnormal bleeding or possible pregnancy first.教材有下腹应用但非经期痛首选；按证候与关元、中极鉴别，异常出血或妊娠可能先就医。

💡 Both are Ren points; CV5 emphasizes the lower-jiao Sanjiao front-mu role and CV9 lies above the umbilicus for middle-jiao fluid patterns. This does not replace edema work-up.两穴同属任脉，石门取下焦三焦募穴属性，水分位于脐上以调中焦水液；不替代水肿病因评估。

Front-mu point of the Triple Burner, used in shu–mu pairing. The Jiayi Jing warns that needling or moxa here can cause infertility in women; use cautiously in women of reproductive age.三焦募穴；可用于俞募配穴。《甲乙经》记女子针灸此穴可致绝子，古代列为女子禁针；育龄女性慎用。No needling in pregnancy (textbook rule: no lower-abdominal needling in the first 3 months; after 3 months avoid the abdomen and lumbosacral region too); empty the bladder before needling.孕妇禁针（教材针刺注意事项：妊娠 3 个月内小腹部禁针，3 个月以上腹部、腰骶部亦不宜针）；针前排空膀胱。

Where is CV5 Shímén?石门（CV5）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, 2 cun below the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.下腹部，脐中下 2 寸，前正中线上。

How is CV5 needled?石门（CV5）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of CV5?石门（CV5）是哪类特定穴？

Front-mu point of the Triple Burner三焦募穴

Main safety point at CV5?石门（CV5）最要注意的安全问题？

No needling in pregnancy (textbook rule: no lower-abdominal needling in the first 3 months; after 3 months avoid the abdomen and lumbosacral region as well); empty the bladder first (a full bladder rises above the pubic symphysis against the abdominal wall). Classically forbidden for women; use cautiously in women of reproductive age.孕妇禁针（教材针刺注意事项：妊娠 3 个月内小腹部禁针，3 个月以上腹部、腰骶部亦不宜针）；针前排空膀胱（充盈膀胱可越过耻骨联合贴近腹壁）。古籍列女子禁针，育龄女性慎用。
