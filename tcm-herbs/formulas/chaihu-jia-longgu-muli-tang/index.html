Bupleurum plus Dragon Bone and Oyster Shell Decoction柴胡加龙骨牡蛎汤

Cháihú Jiā Lónggǔ Mǔlì Tāng · Zhang Zhongjing, Shanghan Lun (Eastern Han)东汉·张仲景《伤寒论》

Original text原文

「傷寒八九日，下之，胸滿煩驚，小便不利，譫語，一身盡重，不可轉側者，柴胡加龍骨牡蠣湯主之」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Eight or nine days in, after purging: full chest, agitated and easily startled, little urine, rambling speech, a heavy body that cannot turn; this decoction governs.伤寒八九天误下后，胸满心烦易惊、小便不利、胡言乱语、全身沉重翻身困难的，用柴胡加龙骨牡蛎汤。

「柴胡四兩龍骨黃芩生薑（切）鉛丹人參桂枝（去皮）茯苓各一兩半半夏二合半（洗）大黃二兩牡蠣一兩半（熬）大棗六枚（擘）」——伤寒论

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Harmonise the lesser yang和解少阳
  • open yang, drain heat and settle the spirit通阳泄热、重镇安神

After wrong purging: full chest, agitation and startling, little urine, muddled speech, heavy body that cannot turn.伤寒误下后胸满烦惊、小便不利、谵语、一身尽重不可转侧。

How the formula is built方解

Half-dose Xiao Chai Hu Tang plus dragon bone, oyster shell and red lead to weigh down, cinnamon twig and poria to open yang and move water, and rhubarb to drain heat. The signs involve several levels and the spirit, so the formula is mixed. Red lead is a lead compound and swallowing it is unacceptable today; clinics and textbooks drop or replace it. The original has no licorice.原方是小柴胡汤半量加龙骨、牡蛎、铅丹重镇，桂枝、茯苓通阳利水，大黄泄热。症状涉及表、半表半里、里与心神多个层面，所以药味也杂。原方的铅丹是铅化合物，今天内服不可接受，临床与教材多去之或以他药代替。条文原文没有甘草。

Cautions使用注意

Red lead must not be used today; the formula contains rhubarb root, a purgative, so people with diarrhoea or a weak constitution especially need a practitioner's judgement; muddled speech, altered mind or convulsions need emergency care; people with anxiety or insomnia should not take it on the strength of its name. This page is for study and is not a prescription; see a doctor first if symptoms are severe, persistent or getting worse.铅丹今天不得使用；方含大黄，有泻下作用，腹泻、体虚者尤须医师判断；谵语、神志改变、惊厥须立即急诊；焦虑、失眠的人不要因本方名而自行服用。本页供学习，不是处方；症状重、持续或加重时先就医。

Contraindicated in pregnancy孕妇禁用 Not to be used in pregnancy (this site grades by the strictest ingredient and the force of the formula); ingredients concerned: Qian Dan (red lead oxide), Ban Xia (pinellia tuber), Huang Qin (scutellaria root), Ren Shen (ginseng), Gui Zhi (cassia twig), Da Huang (rhubarb).孕期禁用（本站按方内最严药味与攻下破血之力从严定级）；涉及：铅丹、半夏、黄芩、人参、桂枝、大黄。
