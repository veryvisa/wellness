✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ Twist拧转

  • With osteoporosis, a disc problem or past back surgery: keep the turn small and slow, and do not bend forward while turning骨质疏松、腰椎间盘突出或做过腰椎手术：转身幅度要小、要慢，转身时不弯腰
  • With neck trouble: turn the head together with the body, not on its own颈椎不适：头跟着身体一起转，不单独扭头
  • If you feel dizzy or your hands tingle while breathing out: stop, breathe naturally, and make the sound softer吐气时头晕或手发麻：马上停下，自然呼吸，声音放轻

🗣 How to say it怎么念

xū IPA [ɕy]

Roughly 'shü': a soft 'sh' made with the tongue forward, gliding into the vowel of French 'tu' or German 'über'. Held long and quiet, like a long hushing sound.念「嘘」，声音轻而长，像让人小声一点时那一声「嘘——」；舌尖靠前，声音从槽牙两边的缝里出去。

👄 Mouth shape口型

  • Lips and teeth slightly apart, the corners of the mouth drawn back so the lips are gently pulled flat嘴唇和牙齿微微张开，嘴角向后拉，嘴唇微微用力拉「扁」
  • Upper and lower back teeth level with each other, a small gap between them上下槽牙平对，中间留一道缝
  • Tip of the tongue flat, the tongue drawn back slightly, with a little space between its sides and the back teeth舌尖放平，舌头微微往后缩，舌头两边和槽牙之间也留出空隙

✔ Check in a mirror: the mouth is flat, not pushed forward; as you breathe out you can feel the air leave through the gaps by the back teeth.对镜子看：嘴是扁的、不是噘起来的；吐气时能感觉到气从两边槽牙的缝里出去。

🔊 Aloud读出声

Beginners practise aloud: say one long 'xu' each time a hand reaches out, the sound lasting as long as the breath, loud at first and then quieter; the voice lets your ears check the mouth shape.初学出声练：每次穿掌时念一声「嘘——」，声音和呼气一样长，先大声、后小声，出声是为了让耳朵帮你校口型。

🤫 Mouth shape only, no voice只做口型、不出声

Once it is familiar, keep the mouth shape only, no voice: the shape and the movement stay the same, the air leaves through the same gaps, and all you hear is a faint rush of breath.熟练后只做口型、不出声：口型和动作不变，只让气从同样的缝里出去，自己只听到很轻的气流声。

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Open the hands, palms up, the little-finger edges resting lightly at the waist两手松开，掌心向上，小指一侧轻贴腰际，收到腰间
  2. Feet stay put; turn the body about 90 degrees to the left, and slowly reach the right palm out from the waist toward the left at shoulder height while breathing out xu, eyes gradually widening, looking at the right hand两脚不动，身体向左转约 90 度；右掌从腰间慢慢向左侧穿出，约与肩同高，同时吐「嘘」，眼睛慢慢睁大，看着右掌
  3. Draw the right palm back along the same path to the waist, turn back to the front and breathe in naturally右掌沿原路收回腰间，身体转回正前方，自然吸气
  4. Turn to the right and reach the left palm out to the right, breathing out xu; draw it back and turn to the front. Alternate sides身体向右转，左掌向右侧穿出，吐「嘘」；再收回、转正。左右交替

🌬 Breathe in through the nose and breathe out slowly through the mouth as you make the sound; when it is done, breathe in naturally. Never hold the breath or force it longer. Beginners can simply breathe naturally and get the mouth shape right first.吸气用鼻子，吐字时用嘴慢慢呼气；吐完自然吸气，不憋气，也不硬把气拉长。初学完全可以自然呼吸，先把口型做对。

✅ Key points要领

  • The turn comes from the waist; the feet and knees do not twist with it转身靠腰带动，两脚和膝盖不跟着拧
  • The reach and the sound start and finish together穿掌和吐字同时开始、同时结束
  • Widen the eyes naturally, without glaring or frowning眼睛睁大要自然，不瞪眼、不皱眉

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Pushing the lips forward instead of keeping them flat嘴唇噘起来念成「虚」的长音
  • Letting the knees twist with the turn转身时膝盖跟着拧
  • Straining to make the breath longer until the face goes red为了吐长憋着劲、脸涨红

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Seated: turn only as far as is comfortable and reach the arm halfway; or skip the turn and reach straight forward.坐在椅子上做：上身只转到舒服的角度，手臂伸到一半即可；或者不转身，只朝前方穿掌。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Tradition pairs xu with the liver and with spring; widening the eyes is the signature of this form, because tradition links the liver with the eyes. These are traditional ideas, not research findings.传统把嘘配肝，配春天；「两目圆睁」是这一式的标志，传统说肝开窍于目。这些是传统说法，不是研究结论。
