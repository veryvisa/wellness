Points and what lies under them穴位与针下要点

LI6Piānlì偏历 — The radius lies close beneath, with the superficial radial nerve and cephalic vein in the subcutaneous layer: angle the needle along the bone and withdraw at any shooting tingle.桡骨就在下面不深，桡神经浅支与头静脉在皮下：针沿骨面斜刺，出现放射麻感就退针。Lower risk较安全LI7Wēnliū温溜 — The radial wrist extensors lie under the needle; the superficial radial nerve runs just in front, deep to brachioradialis, so a shooting tingle means the tip has drifted forward.针下是桡侧腕伸肌群；桡神经浅支在前外侧的肱桡肌深面伴行，针尖偏前出现放射麻感要退针。Lower risk较安全LI8Xiàlián下廉 — A thick muscle layer: below brachioradialis and extensor carpi radialis brevis lies supinator, with the deep radial nerve just emerging from its lower edge.肌层较厚：肱桡肌、桡侧腕短伸肌之下是旋后肌，桡神经深支刚从它的下缘穿出。Lower risk较安全LI9Shànglián上廉 — Past the radial wrist extensors the needle enters supinator, through which the deep radial nerve is passing: an electric jolt means you have met it.针过桡侧腕伸肌群进入旋后肌，桡神经深支正在旋后肌里穿行：针下触电感就是碰到它。Lower risk较安全LI10Shǒusānlǐ手三里 — Beyond about 1 cun the needle enters supinator, where the deep radial nerve passes and the radial recurrent artery runs alongside: withdraw at once if tingling shoots down.1 寸以上的深度会进入旋后肌，桡神经深支在肌内穿行、桡返动脉在旁：有放射麻感立即退针。Take care需谨慎
