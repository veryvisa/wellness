📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the forearm supinated, palm up and wrist relaxed; lightly flex the wrist to show the anterior tendons.坐位或仰卧，前臂旋后、掌心向上，腕放松；轻屈腕使腕前肌腱显露。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The elbow crease to the distal palmar wrist crease is 12 cun. PC6 is 2 cun above the wrist crease, measured proximally along the anterior forearm midline.肘横纹至腕掌侧远端横纹为 12 寸；内关在腕横纹上 2 寸，沿前臂掌面中线向肘侧折量。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With wrist flexion and thumb-to-little-finger opposition, identify palmaris longus and flexor carpi radialis; PC6 lies between them. Palmaris longus is absent in a sizeable minority, so do not rely on it alone. The median nerve lies directly deep between the two tendons; a slightly deep perpendicular needle often sends an electric tingle to the middle finger. That means the nerve was touched: withdraw a little and change angle rather than lifting and thrusting for a stronger sensation.屈腕并拇指对小指辨认掌长肌腱与桡侧腕屈肌腱，内关在两腱之间；掌长肌腱约一成多的人缺如，不能单独依赖它。正中神经就在两腱之间的深面，直刺稍深常引出向中指放射的电麻感——那是碰到了神经，应退针少许、改变角度，不要反复提插追求针感。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Measure 2 cun proximally from the wrist crease and locate between the tendons on the anterior midline. If palmaris longus is absent, use the wrist crease, flexor carpi radialis and midline together.由腕横纹向肘侧量 2 寸，在前臂正中线两腱间落点；找不到掌长肌腱时以腕横纹、桡侧腕屈肌腱及中线共同定位。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Lightly palpate the shallow groove between the tendons; the tendon margins become clearer with gentle wrist flexion. Two finger breadths are only about 1.5 cun, not 2 cun, and cannot replace proportional measurement.轻触两腱间浅沟，腕轻屈时能感觉腱缘；两横指约 1.5 寸而非 2 寸，不能以两指宽替代骨度量。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Using two finger breadths often places PC6 only about 1.5 cun above the wrist; measure 2 cun from the wrist crease using the forearm span.以两横指直接量内关，常落在腕上约 1.5 寸而偏近腕；应从腕横纹按 12 寸前臂骨度量取 2 寸。
  • An absent palmaris longus can lead to misidentification or drifting onto flexor carpi radialis; cross-check the tendon interval with the cun measure and avoid median-nerve compression.掌长肌腱缺如时误以为找错位置，或落到桡侧腕屈肌腱上；以可触腱间隙和骨度刻度交叉确认，避免压迫正中神经。
  • Treating an electric tingle to the middle finger as 'arrival of qi' and lifting and thrusting repeatedly: the needle is on the median nerve, and repeated contact can leave lasting tingling and pain.把向中指放射的电麻感当作「得气」反复提插：这是针碰到正中神经，反复刺激可致持续麻痛。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

Often used distally for nausea or vomiting with rebellious stomach qi and chest oppression, sometimes with SP4; persistent vomiting, dehydration or blood needs medical care first.恶心呕吐、胃气上逆兼胸闷时常作远端主穴，并可配公孙；持续呕吐、脱水或呕血先就医。

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

Consider the luo point and Yin Wei connection for palpitations with chest oppression, anxiety or nausea; sudden chest pain, breathlessness or fainting needs urgent assessment.心悸伴胸闷、焦虑或恶心时可选络穴并通阴维；突发胸痛、气短或晕厥不以针灸替代急诊评估。

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Lead distal point for stomach pain with nausea or chest tightness, usually with CV12 and ST36; severe pain, vomiting blood or black stools need medical care first.胃痛伴恶心、胸闷时的远端主穴，常配中脘、足三里；剧痛、呕血或黑便先就医。

The median nerve is compressed in the carpal tunnel at and beyond the wrist crease (its entrance is at about PC7); PC6 lies 2 cun proximal to it. Numbness of the thumb, index and middle fingers or waking at night suggests the condition and needs clinical assessment; pressing PC6 neither diagnoses nor replaces treatment.正中神经在腕横纹附近及其远侧的腕管内受压（腕管入口约在大陵处），内关在其近侧 2 寸；拇、食、中指麻木或夜间麻醒提示此病，需临床检查，按压内关不能诊断或替代治疗。

Forearm flexor tendinopathy前臂屈肌腱劳损

Anterior wrist or forearm pain after repetitive flexion or gripping can overlap with tendon overuse; injury, marked swelling or persistent weakness needs care.反复屈腕、抓握后腕前或前臂掌侧疼痛可与肌腱劳损重叠；外伤、显著肿胀或持续无力需就医。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Persistent or worsening hand numbness or weakness, or weak thumb opposition, needs timely median-nerve assessment.手部麻木无力持续或加重、拇指对掌变弱：及时评估正中神经。
  • Visible deformity, severe pain or cold/discoloured fingers after wrist injury requires urgent care.腕部外伤后明显变形、剧痛或手指发凉变色：立即就医。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is PC6 Nèiguān?内关（PC6）在哪里？

Anterior forearm, 2 cun above the wrist crease, between palmaris longus and flexor carpi radialis tendons.前臂前面，腕横纹上2寸，掌长肌腱与桡侧腕屈肌腱之间。

How is PC6 needled?内关（PC6）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1寸。

Special-point roles of PC6?内关（PC6）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting point; Eight-confluent point, opens the Yin Wei vessel络穴；八脉交会穴，通阴维脉

Main safety point at PC6?内关（PC6）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid superficial veins; immediately withdraw and redirect for paresthesia radiating to the fingers.避开浅静脉；出现向手指放射的麻感应立即退针调整。
