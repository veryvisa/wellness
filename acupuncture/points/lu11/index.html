📍 Location定位★

Where is LU11 Shàoshāng?少商（LU11）在哪里？

On the thumb, 0.1 cun (finger-cun) from the radial corner of the nail: where a line along the radial edge of the nail meets a line along the nail base.拇指末节桡侧，距指甲根角 0.1 寸（指寸）：沿指甲桡侧缘和指甲根各画一条线，交点处。

How is LU11 needled?少商（LU11）怎么刺？

Shallow; or prick to bleed; 0.1 cun.浅刺；或点刺出血；0.1 寸。

Special-point roles of LU11?少商（LU11）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-well point (five transport points; wood)井穴（五输穴，五行属木）

Main safety point at LU11?少商（LU11）最要注意的安全问题？

Very painful at the fingertip; no bloodletting for people with a bleeding tendency, on anticoagulants, or with local infection. Unconsciousness and high fever are emergencies first: call 911.指端很痛；放血不用于有出血倾向、服抗凝药或局部感染的人。昏迷、高热首先是急症，先叫 911。
