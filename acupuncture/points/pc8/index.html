PC8

劳宫 Láogōng

Palace of Toil · Take care需谨慎

★Ying-spring point, fire荥穴，属火

📍 Location定位★

Centre of palm, between the 2nd and 3rd metacarpals nearer the 3rd; where the middle fingertip falls in a fist.掌心，第2、3掌骨之间偏第3掌骨，握拳时中指尖所指处。

💡 Make a relaxed fist and confirm where the middle fingertip touches.自然握拳，以中指尖触及处复核。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5寸

Palmar vessels and nerves are dense; avoid visible vessels and do not needle deeply to the palmar arch.掌部血管神经密集，避开可见血管；不可深刺至掌弓。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the forearm supinated, palm up and hand relaxed; make a gentle fist only for the simple locating check.坐位或仰卧，前臂旋后、掌心向上，手掌自然伸展；需要简便定位时再轻握拳。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Standard location: on the palm, level with the proximal side of the 3rd metacarpophalangeal joint, between the 2nd and 3rd metacarpals and closer to the 3rd. Where the middle fingertip lands in a relaxed fist is a helpful cross-check.国标：掌区，横平第 3 掌指关节近端，第 2、3 掌骨之间偏于第 3 掌骨。自然握拳时中指尖所落处可作辅助定位。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Trace the palmar edges of the 2nd and 3rd metacarpals distally and locate the soft tissue between them beneath the palmar aponeurosis; superficial flexor tendons become apparent with finger flexion. The superficial palmar arch and digital neurovascular structures are deeper or lateral; avoid visible vessels and deep probing.沿第 2、3 掌骨掌侧骨缘向远端触摸，在两骨间、掌腱膜覆盖的掌心软组织定位；屈指时可见浅层屈肌腱走向。掌浅弓及指神经血管较深或偏侧分布，避开可见血管，勿向深部刺探。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate the soft tissue near the radial side of the 3rd metacarpal and feel the interval between the 2nd and 3rd metacarpals. The fist method is only a check because hand size and fist shape vary.轻按掌心第 3 掌骨桡侧附近的软组织，横向摸清第 2、3 掌骨间隙；握拳中指尖落点只作复核，手掌大小或握拳方式会造成偏差。

  4. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Use the patient's middle-fingertip position as a simple palm reference without converting it into a fixed cun; confirm against the metacarpal interval and slight third-metacarpal bias.以患者本人中指指尖作掌心落点的简便参照，不折算固定寸数；最后回到掌骨间隙和第 3 掌骨偏侧确认。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • A tight fist or using the ring fingertip often shifts the location ulnarly; use the middle fingertip in a relaxed fist and correct against the metacarpal interval.握拳过紧或以无名指尖定位，常使位置偏向尺侧；应使用自然握拳的中指尖作参考，并以掌骨间隙校正。
  • Locating at the edge of the metacarpal interval or over a visible vessel and probing deeply can miss PC8 and approach palmar neurovascular structures; palpate lightly and leave procedures to trained practitioners.取在第 2、3 掌骨间隙边缘或可见血管上，再向深部操作：易偏离劳宫并接近掌部血管神经；只轻触定位，操作需由受训专业人员完成。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Palmar tendon or palmar-aponeurosis overuse手掌肌腱或掌腱膜劳损

Palmar tenderness after repetitive gripping or tool use may reflect soft-tissue overuse; triggering, catching or a firm palmar nodule needs assessment for tendon or aponeurosis disease.反复抓握、工具使用后掌心压痛可来自软组织劳损；掌指关节弹响、屈伸卡顿或掌心硬结需评估屈肌腱/掌腱膜病变。

Palmar neurovascular pain手掌神经血管性疼痛

Numbness or tingling radiating into the fingers differs from isolated tenderness; persistent sensory changes or altered hand circulation needs prompt care.麻木、针刺感沿手指分布不同于单纯局部压痛；持续感觉异常或手部循环改变应及时就医。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • A red, hot, swollen palm with fever, pus or rapid spread needs same-day care to exclude infection.手掌红肿热痛伴发热、脓液或迅速扩展：当天就医排除感染。
  • Suddenly pale, blue or cold fingers, or persistent numbness or weakness, require urgent assessment for circulation or nerve injury.手指突然苍白、发紫、冰冷或持续麻木无力：立即评估血流或神经损伤。

Where is PC8 Láogōng?劳宫（PC8）在哪里？

Centre of palm, between the 2nd and 3rd metacarpals nearer the 3rd; where the middle fingertip falls in a fist.掌心，第2、3掌骨之间偏第3掌骨，握拳时中指尖所指处。

How is PC8 needled?劳宫（PC8）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of PC8?劳宫（PC8）是哪类特定穴？

Ying-spring point, fire荥穴，属火

Main safety point at PC8?劳宫（PC8）最要注意的安全问题？

Palmar vessels and nerves are dense; avoid visible vessels and do not needle deeply to the palmar arch.掌部血管神经密集，避开可见血管；不可深刺至掌弓。
