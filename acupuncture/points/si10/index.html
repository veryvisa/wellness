    Gently alternate arm abduction and adduction to observe the posterior deltoid and axillary fold. Confirm from the scapular-spine edge, posterior fold, and intervening hollow; deep pulsation or radiating tingling is not a locating cue.上臂轻度外展、内收交替，观察三角肌后缘和腋后襞变化；以肩胛冈下缘、腋后纹头和两者之间的肩后凹陷互证，不以压到深部搏动或放射麻感作为确认。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Confusion with SI9 often places the point above the fold but outside the hollow below the scapular spine. Deep probing into the axilla or toward the chest wall risks the axillary nerve, posterior circumflex humeral vessels, and chest wall; trained practitioners must follow formal safety standards.与肩贞（SI9）混淆时常偏到腋后纹上方、但未落在肩胛冈下缘凹陷；向腋窝深处或向胸壁深探会危及腋神经、旋肱后血管及胸壁，危险穴应避免此类方向，由受训人员依规范操作。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

Consider for posterior-shoulder tenderness above the axillary fold near the scapular-spine edge; lateral numbness, post-traumatic weakness, or deformity needs assessment for nerve injury or dislocation first.肩后腋襞上方且靠肩胛冈下缘压痛时可考虑；若肩外侧麻木、外伤后无力或肩关节变形，应先排除神经血管损伤及脱位。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

SI10 is in the upper outer scapular region and is an adjunct only when neck stiffness includes posterior-shoulder symptoms; isolated neck pain needs neck-focused localization.臑俞定位于肩胛外上区域，项强仅在伴肩后局部症状时配用；单纯颈项痛更需颈部定位。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Rotator-cuff or posterior-deltoid strain肩袖或三角肌后束劳损

Pain with elevation, external rotation, or extension warrants rotator-cuff and deltoid assessment; tenderness alone cannot distinguish tendon tear from myofascial pain.抬臂、外旋或伸肩时痛需评估肩袖及三角肌；局部压痛不能区分肌腱撕裂与肌筋膜痛。

Injury near the axillary nerve and posterior circumflex humeral vessels腋神经及旋肱后血管周围损伤

Lateral-shoulder numbness or deltoid weakness after dislocation or proximal humeral fracture needs prompt neurovascular assessment.肩脱位或肱骨近端骨折后肩外侧麻木、三角肌无力应尽快检查神经血管。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SI10 Nàoshū?臑俞（SI10）在哪里？

In the scapular region, directly superior to the posterior axillary fold, in the depression inferior to the scapular spine.肩胛区，腋后纹头直上，肩胛冈下缘凹陷中。

How is SI10 needled?臑俞（SI10）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–1.5 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SI10?臑俞（SI10）是哪类特定穴？

Meeting point of the Hand-Taiyang channel with the Yang Wei and Yang Qiao vessels手太阳、阳维脉、阳跷脉交会穴

Main safety point at SI10?臑俞（SI10）最要注意的安全问题？

The suprascapular nerve and vessels lie deep: aim toward the scapula, not deep toward the axilla, and withdraw for shooting tingling.深面有肩胛上神经血管；针向肩胛骨面，不向腋窝方向深刺，出现窜麻退针。
