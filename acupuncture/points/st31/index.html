ST31

髀关 Bìguān

Thigh Gate · Lower risk较安全

📍 Location定位★

On the anterior thigh, in the depression between three muscles: the proximal rectus femoris, sartorius and tensor fasciae latae.股前区，股直肌近端、缝匠肌与阔筋膜张肌 3 条肌之间凹陷中。

💡 Supine, raise the leg against resistance: an inverted V formed by sartorius and tensor fasciae latae appears at the front of the upper thigh, with rectus femoris below its apex; roughly on the line from the ASIS to the lateral patellar base, level with the perineum.仰卧屈髋抗阻抬腿，大腿根前外侧出现缝匠肌与阔筋膜张肌夹成的倒「V」形凹陷，凹陷尖下方即股直肌近端；约在髂前上棘与髌底外侧端连线上、平会阴高度。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–2 cun1–2 寸

🛡 Safety安全★

Locating too medially approaches the femoral vessels and nerve in the femoral triangle; tingling down the lateral thigh is the lateral femoral cutaneous nerve — redirect.取穴偏内会接近股三角的股动静脉与股神经；出现向大腿外侧放射的麻痛为股外侧皮神经，调整针向。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Weakness and numbness of the leg下肢痿痹Low back pain, cold knees腰痛、膝冷 ⚖️

  • With ST32 and ST36: leg weakness (treat atrophy through Yangming)配伏兔（ST32）、足三里（ST36）：下肢痿痹（治痿独取阳明）

    Palpation check触诊确认

Where is ST31 Bìguān?髀关（ST31）在哪里？

How is ST31 needled?髀关（ST31）怎么刺？

Main safety point at ST31?髀关（ST31）最要注意的安全问题？

Locating too medially approaches the femoral vessels and nerve in the femoral triangle; tingling down the lateral thigh is the lateral femoral cutaneous nerve — redirect.取穴偏内会接近股三角的股动静脉与股神经；出现向大腿外侧放射的麻痛为股外侧皮神经，调整针向。
