Point of the Heart channel; listed among the classical forbidden-to-needle points手少阴心经穴；古代禁针穴之一

📍 Location定位★

On the anteromedial upper arm, 3 cun above the cubital crease, in the medial bicipital groove.臂前区，肘横纹上 3 寸，肱二头肌的内侧沟中。

💡 Flex the elbow and make a fist so biceps stands out, and find the longitudinal groove along its medial border; measure 3 cun up from the cubital crease (the lower third of the 9 cun from the axillary fold to the crease).屈肘握拳使肱二头肌隆起，沿其内侧缘找到纵行的沟；从肘横纹向上量 3 寸（腋前纹头至肘横纹 9 寸的下 1/3 处）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular, avoiding the brachial artery直刺，避开肱动脉 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

🛡 Safety安全★

The brachial artery and median nerve run in the groove: feel the pulse and insert away from it, never deep over it; withdraw for tingling radiating to the forearm or fingers. The Zhenjiu Dacheng lists Qingling as forbidden to needle; with modern filiform needles it may be needled away from the bundle, but carefully.肱动脉与正中神经就在沟内：先摸清搏动再避开进针，不在搏动处深刺；出现向前臂、手指放射的麻感即退针。《针灸大成》把青灵列入禁针穴，现代用毫针避开血管神经可刺，但不可大意。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Headache, shivering with cold头痛、振寒 ⚖️Hypochondriac pain, shoulder and arm pain胁痛、肩臂疼痛

🔗 Pairings配伍

  • With HT1 and HT3: pain along the medial arm and inability to raise the arm, combining near and distal Heart-channel points配极泉（HT1）、少海（HT3）：上肢内侧疼痛、肩臂不举，沿手少阴经近远取穴

  • Locating the point with the elbow flexed or arm adducted: skin and biceps belly shift relative to each other, skewing the level up or down and obscuring the medial bicipital groove.在屈肘或上臂内收位取穴：皮肤与肱二头肌肌腹相对移位，导致定穴偏高或偏低，且肌肉松弛难以分清内侧沟边界。

Causes medial upper-arm pain radiating with numbness or weakness into the medial forearm and radial digits (median nerve) or ulnar digits (ulnar nerve), often with a positive Tinel sign over the medial bicipital groove. Differentiate from cervical radiculopathy; refer for neurology/orthopedic evaluation and EMG if intrinsic hand muscle wasting or progressive weakness develops.表现为上臂内侧痛并向前臂内侧、拇食中指（正中神经）或环小指（尺神经）放射麻木无力，叩击肱二头肌内侧沟可诱发 Tinel 征。需与颈椎神经根型颈椎病鉴别；若出现手内在肌萎缩或持续肌力下降，应先转诊神经科或骨科行肌电图检查。

Cardiac ischemic pain (angina or myocardial infarction) radiating to the left medial shoulder and arm心脏缺血性疼痛（心绞痛或心肌梗死）向左肩臂内侧放射痛

Cardiac referred pain frequently radiates down the left shoulder, medial upper arm, and ulnar forearm, mimicking local musculoskeletal pain. If triggered by exertion or emotional stress, or accompanied by chest pressure, dyspnea, diaphoresis, or nausea, never treat it as simple arm strain — seek emergency medical care immediately.心源性牵涉痛常沿左肩、左上臂内侧及前臂尺侧放射，易被误认为单纯肌肉劳损或经筋痛；若疼痛与劳力、情绪激动相关，或伴胸闷压榨感、气短、出冷汗、恶心，严禁单纯按局部软组织痛处理，必须立即就医急诊排查。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is HT2 Qīnglíng?青灵（HT2）在哪里？

On the anteromedial upper arm, 3 cun above the cubital crease, in the medial bicipital groove.臂前区，肘横纹上 3 寸，肱二头肌的内侧沟中。

How is HT2 needled?青灵（HT2）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, avoiding the brachial artery; 0.5–1 cun.直刺，避开肱动脉；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of HT2?青灵（HT2）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Heart channel; listed among the classical forbidden-to-needle points手少阴心经穴；古代禁针穴之一

Main safety point at HT2?青灵（HT2）最要注意的安全问题？

The brachial artery and median nerve run in the groove: feel the pulse and insert away from it, never deep over it; withdraw for tingling radiating to the forearm or fingers. The Zhenjiu Dacheng lists Qingling as forbidden to needle; with modern filiform needles it may be needled away from the bundle, but carefully.肱动脉与正中神经就在沟内：先摸清搏动再避开进针，不在搏动处深刺；出现向前臂、手指放射的麻感即退针。《针灸大成》把青灵列入禁针穴，现代用毫针避开血管神经可刺，但不可大意。
