CV10

下脘 Xiàwǎn

Lower Cavity · Take care需谨慎

★Crossing point of the Conception Vessel and Foot-Taiyin channel任脉、足太阴交会穴

📍 Location定位★

On the upper abdomen, 2 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 2 寸，前正中线上。

💡 With xiphisternal junction to umbilicus as 8 cun, measure 2 cun up from the umbilicus.剑胸结合到脐中折 8 寸，从脐向上量 2 寸。

🪡 Needling刺法

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

🛡 Safety安全

Do not needle deep after a full meal, with bloating, or over an undiagnosed mass; caution in pregnancy.饱餐后、肠胀气或腹部包块未明者不深刺；孕妇慎用。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Abdominal pain, distension, diarrhoea, vomiting, undigested food, infantile malnutrition腹痛、腹胀、腹泻、呕吐、完谷不化、小儿疳积等脾胃病 ⚖️Abdominal masses (pi kuai)痞块

🔗 Pairings配伍★

  • With ST25 and ST36 for distension, diarrhoea and indigestion配天枢、足三里：腹胀、腹泻、消化不良
  • With CV12 and ST44 for food stagnation with pain配中脘、内庭：食积腹痛

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine with knees flexed and the abdominal wall relaxed; use the eight-cun xiphisternal-junction-to-umbilicus span.仰卧屈膝，腹壁放松；用剑胸联合至脐中八寸段量取上腹。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The xiphisternal junction-to-umbilicus span is 8 cun; CV10 is two cun above the umbilicus, six cun below the xiphisternal junction.胸剑联合中点至脐中为 8 寸；下脘在脐上 2 寸，即距胸剑联合下端 6 寸。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Measure two cun above the umbilicus on the anterior midline, one cun below CV11 and two cun below CV12.沿腹部前正中线从脐向上量两寸，位于建里下一寸、中脘下方两寸。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The linea alba and abdominal-wall tissue are superficial, with preperitoneal tissue and bowel deeper. Lateral drift can approach abdominal-wall vessels; palpate lightly at midline and avoid scars, stomas and hernia bulges.浅层为腹白线及腹壁组织，深部为腹膜前组织和肠管；偏外侧可接近腹壁血管，保持中线轻触，避开瘢痕、造口与疝包块。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Identify the umbilicus, measure two cun upward and check the sequence relative to CV12 and CV8; stop palpation if there is distension or marked tenderness.先触脐中，再向上量两寸并核对与中脘、神阙的位置次序；腹胀或明显压痛时停止按压。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Choose for fullness or food-stagnation discomfort around two cun above the navel; compare CV14 for subxiphoid pain and CV12 for Stomach front-mu patterns.脐上两寸附近胀满、食积或中下胃脘不适时选；心下剑突下痛或胃募证更比较巨阙、中脘。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

An adjunct for lower epigastric stagnation or post-meal regurgitation; persistent severe vomiting, dehydration or blood needs medical care.胃脘下部停滞、食后反出可作配穴；剧烈持续呕吐、脱水或呕血先就医。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

An adjunct when diarrhea accompanies upper periumbilical fullness or indigestion; blood, fever or dehydration needs cause-directed care first.伴脐上胀满或食物不化时辅助；急性血便、发热或脱水需先处理病因。
