Where is KI17 Shāngqū?商曲（KI17）在哪里？

On the upper abdomen, 2 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, 0.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 2 寸，前正中线旁开 0.5 寸。

How is KI17 needled?商曲（KI17）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of KI17?商曲（KI17）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Chong vessel and the Kidney channel冲脉与足少阴经交会穴

Main safety point at KI17?商曲（KI17）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. Beyond the peritoneum lie the stomach or transverse colon, and wall thickness varies: the textbook depth is a ceiling, go shallower in thin people, avoid large lifting-thrusting, and do not needle unexplained acute abdominal pain.孕期禁针。腹膜深面是胃或横结肠，腹壁厚薄因人而异：教材深度是上限，消瘦者减浅，不大幅提插；急性腹痛未明原因不针。
