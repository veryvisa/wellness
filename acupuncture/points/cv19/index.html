A qualified clinician must keep any procedure superficial and avoid deep perpendicular or chest-directed insertion.胸骨后邻纵隔及大血管，肋间外侧邻胸膜、肺；胸上部深刺可造成严重损伤或气胸。若由合格人员操作须严格浅表，避免深直刺及向胸腔方向。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Identify the second rib at the sternal angle, then its interspace, and locate gently on the midline; do not press firmly on the sternum or cartilage.从胸骨角第 2 肋辨认后确认第 2 肋间隙，在胸骨正中轻触定位；不需用力按压胸骨或肋软骨。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Cross-check the Ren-point sequence above CV20 and below CV18, using rib-space localization as the final reference.以紫宫在华盖上方、玉堂下方的任脉穴序列作交叉核对，最终以肋间定位为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Confusing the second rib with its interspace shifts the point vertically; lateral or deep perpendicular insertion may injure pleura, lung or mediastinum, so keep any procedure superficial.把第 2 肋骨本身或第 2 肋间隙误认会造成上下错位；偏外或深直刺可伤胸膜、肺和纵隔结构，必须浅表并避开胸腔方向。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

Only a neighbouring-point reference for cough with parasternal tightness; acute wheeze, fever or blood needs medical care.上胸部咳嗽伴胸骨旁紧痛时仅作邻近穴参考；气喘急、发热或咯血先就医。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

An adjunct for stable upper-chest tightness, not acute asthma or respiratory failure; its thoracic risk favours considering safer alternatives.稳定期上胸闷可作辅助，不以此处理急性哮喘或呼吸衰竭；胸部风险高，优先评估安全替代。

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

A supporting point when throat symptoms accompany upper-chest tightness; throat swelling, dysphagia or airway compromise needs prompt assessment.若咽喉不适兼上胸憋闷可作配穴参考；咽喉红肿、吞咽困难或气道受阻时需及时评估。

Where is CV19 Zǐgōng?紫宫（CV19）在哪里？

On the chest, level with the 2nd intercostal space, on the anterior midline.胸部，横平第 2 肋间隙，前正中线上。

How is CV19 needled?紫宫（CV19）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.3–0.5 cun.平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at CV19?紫宫（CV19）最要注意的安全问题？

Behind the sternum lie the great vessels, and laterally the intercostal space and pleura (pneumothorax): transverse only, never perpendicular.胸骨后为纵隔大血管，偏外即肋间与胸膜（气胸）：只平刺，不直刺。
