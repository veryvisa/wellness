Knee pain膝痛

The textbook treats knee pain within painful obstruction (bi): local points ST35, ST34, GB34 and GB33 first, then points for wandering, painful, fixed or heat bi. The blood-stasis and liver–kidney patterns come from TCM internal medicine and fit old injuries and the worn knees of middle and older age. Local points follow the site of pain: ST35 and the inner knee-eye (an extra point with no code here) below the kneecap, ST34 above and outside it, GB33 and GB34 on the outer side, LR8 and SP9 on the inner side, BL40 behind. A red, hot, swollen knee that cannot bear weight needs infection, fracture and gout ruled out first, and no needle goes toward the joint.教材把膝痛放在痹证里治：先取膝局部的犊鼻、梁丘、阳陵泉、膝阳关，再按行痹、痛痹、着痹、热痹配穴；瘀血和肝肾亏虚两型来自中医内科学的痹证辨证，正对旧伤和中老年的退行性膝痛。局部穴怎么选看痛在哪里：髌骨下方是犊鼻和内膝眼（经外奇穴，本站无代码），髌上外侧是梁丘，外侧是膝阳关、阳陵泉，内侧是曲泉、阴陵泉，后面是委中。红、热、肿、不能负重的膝，先排除感染、骨折和痛风，不向关节里进针。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • A suddenly red, hot, swollen, very painful knee with fever or chills or inability to bear weight, especially after a joint injection or surgery, or with diabetes or weak immunity: emergency care the same day for septic arthritis膝突然红、热、肿、剧痛，伴发热寒战或不能负重，尤其近期打过关节针、做过手术、糖尿病或免疫低下：当天急诊，排除化脓性关节炎
  • After injury: deformity, swelling within two hours, inability to take four steps or bend to 90 degrees, or tenderness over the fibular head or kneecap: same-day emergency imaging外伤后膝部变形、两小时内迅速肿大、不能走四步、不能屈到 90 度，或腓骨头、髌骨压痛：当天急诊拍片
  • After a violent blow or dislocation: a cold pale lower leg, no pulse on the foot, foot drop or numbness: call 911 now for possible popliteal artery or common fibular nerve injury膝部受强力撞击或脱位后，小腿发凉发白、足背无脉、足下垂或麻木：立即拨 911，警惕腘动脉和腓总神经损伤
  • Swelling, pain and warmth behind one knee or in one calf: same-day care for deep vein thrombosis; with breathlessness, chest pain or coughing blood, call 911 for possible pulmonary embolism膝后或小腿一侧肿、痛、发热：当天就医排除深静脉血栓；同时有气短、胸痛、咯血：拨 911，警惕肺栓塞
  • A knee that locks and will not straighten or keeps giving way; knee pain in a child or teenager with a limp or restricted hip; pain at rest at night or unexplained weight loss: see a doctor soon膝“卡住”伸不直、一用力就打软；儿童青少年膝痛伴跛行或髋动受限；夜间静息痛、不明原因消瘦：尽快就医

「膝中痛，取犢鼻，以員利鍼」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.26: for pain inside the knee, take ST35 with the round-sharp needle. This is the origin of ST35 as the core knee point. The round-sharp needle is one of the ancient nine; a filiform needle is used today, with strict asepsis because it points toward the joint.《灵枢·杂病》：膝关节里面痛，取犊鼻，用员利针刺治。犊鼻作膝痛主穴即由此而来。员利针是古代九针之一，今天改用毫针，并须严格消毒，因为这一针朝向关节腔。

「膝者筋之府，屈伸不能，行則僂附，筋將憊矣」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.17: the knee is the residence of the sinews; when it cannot bend or straighten and the person walks stooped and holding on, the sinews are failing. This is why GB34, the influential point of sinews, is used for the knee and why long-standing cases tonify the liver, which governs the sinews.《素问·脉要精微论》：膝是筋汇聚的地方；不能屈伸，走路要弯着身子扶着东西，说明筋快要衰败了。这是膝痛取筋会阳陵泉、久病补肝（肝主筋）的依据。

「兩膝紅腫疼痛，名曰鶴膝風。膝關 行間 風市 陽陵泉」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu dacheng: red, swollen, painful knees are called crane-knee wind, and four points are listed — LR7, LR2, GB31 and GB34 — all on the Liver and Gallbladder channels and none into the joint. Today red swollen knees first need rheumatoid arthritis, gout and infection checked.《针灸大成》：两膝红肿疼痛称鹤膝风，列膝关、行间、风市、阳陵泉四穴。四穴都在肝、胆两经，且都不进关节腔。双膝红肿今天须先查类风湿、痛风和感染。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Bi syndrome痹证

Wandering bi (wind predominant)行痹（风邪偏胜）

How it presents辨证要点 Pain that moves from knee to knee or to other joints, with restricted movement; at onset there may be aversion to wind or fever; thin white coating, floating pulse.膝痛游走，时左时右，或连及其他关节，屈伸不利；初起可有怕风、发热；苔薄白，脉浮。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, free the collaterals, move blood (drain or even method)祛风通络，活血止痛（泻法或平补平泻）

Main主穴ST35 Dúbí犊鼻ST34 Liángqiū梁丘GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉GB33 Xīyángguān膝阳关
Add配穴BL17 Géshū膈俞SP10 Xuèhǎi血海

💡 The core is the textbook local prescription for knee bi: ST35 and ST34 on the Foot Yangming, GB33 and GB34 on the Foot Shaoyang, GB34 being also the influential point of sinews. Wandering bi adds BL17, the influential point of blood, and SP10, on the principle that wind is treated by treating the blood.主穴是教材痹证膝部的局部处方：犊鼻、梁丘在足阳明，膝阳关、阳陵泉在足少阳，阳陵泉又是筋会，合起来疏通膝部筋脉。行痹配膈俞（血会）、血海，取「治风先治血，血行风自灭」之意。

Bi syndrome痹证

Painful bi (cold predominant)痛痹（寒邪偏胜）

How it presents辨证要点 Fairly severe, fixed knee pain, worse with cold and better with warmth, no redness or heat; thin white coating, wiry tight pulse.膝部疼痛较剧，痛处固定，遇寒加重、得热减轻，局部不红不热；苔薄白，脉弦紧。

Principle治法 Warm the channels, disperse cold, stop pain (drain or even method; warm needle and moxa)温经散寒，通络止痛（泻法或平补平泻，多用温针、艾灸）

Main主穴ST35 Dúbí犊鼻ST34 Liángqiū梁丘GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉GB33 Xīyángguān膝阳关
Add配穴BL23 Shènshū肾俞CV4 Guānyuán关元

💡 Same core; painful bi adds BL23 and CV4, which warm original yang so that cold can be dispersed. Neither point is needled in pregnancy.主穴同上；痛痹配肾俞、关元，二穴温补元阳，阳气足则寒邪易散。关元、肾俞在孕期都不针。

Bi syndrome痹证

Fixed bi (damp predominant)着痹（湿邪偏胜）

How it presents辨证要点 Heavy aching swollen knee with numb skin, worse in wet weather; white greasy coating, soggy slow pulse.膝部酸痛重着，肿胀，肌肤麻木，阴雨天加重；苔白腻，脉濡缓。

Principle治法 Eliminate damp, free the collaterals, stop pain (drain or even method; moxa allowed)除湿通络止痛（泻法或平补平泻，可灸）

Main主穴ST35 Dúbí犊鼻ST34 Liángqiū梁丘GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉GB33 Xīyángguān膝阳关
Add配穴SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

💡 Same core; fixed bi adds SP9, the Spleen he-sea point, to drain damp, and ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, to strengthen the middle where damp is produced. One sits inside and one outside, both just below the knee.主穴同上；着痹配阴陵泉、足三里：阴陵泉是脾经合穴，利水渗湿；足三里是胃经合穴，健运中焦以断湿源。两穴一内一外，也都在膝下局部。

Bi syndrome痹证

Heat bi (damp-heat)热痹（湿热痹阻）

How it presents辨证要点 Red, hot, swollen knee too painful to touch, slightly better with cold, perhaps with fever, thirst and restlessness; red tongue, yellow greasy coating, slippery or soggy rapid pulse.膝部红肿灼热疼痛，痛不可触，得冷稍舒，可伴发热、口渴、烦躁；舌红，苔黄腻，脉滑数或濡数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, drain damp, stop pain (drain; GV14 may be pricked to bleed; no moxa)清热利湿，通络止痛（泻法，大椎可点刺出血；不灸）

Main主穴GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉ST34 Liángqiū梁丘GB33 Xīyángguān膝阳关
Add配穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎LI11 Qūchí曲池

💡 A doctor first rules out septic arthritis, gout and fracture. ST35 is dropped because no needle goes toward the cavity of a hot swollen joint; only ST34, GB33 and GB34 outside the joint are used. Heat bi adds GV14, meeting of all yang channels, and LI11, the Hand Yangming he-sea point, to clear heat.先由医生排除化脓性关节炎、痛风和骨折。主穴去掉犊鼻，因为红肿积液的膝不朝关节腔进针；只取关节外的梁丘、膝阳关、阳陵泉。热痹配大椎（诸阳之会）、曲池（手阳明合穴）清热。

Blood stasis in the collaterals瘀血阻络

How it presents辨证要点 Stabbing fixed pain that resists pressure, worse at night or after sitting, usually after an old injury or overuse, the area dusky or stiff; dark tongue with stasis spots, choppy pulse.膝部刺痛，痛处固定拒按，夜间或久坐后加重，多有旧伤或劳损，局部暗紫或僵硬；舌暗有瘀点，脉涩。

💡 Same core; BL17, the influential point of blood, and SP10, a blood point that is also local above the knee, move stasis. Versus others与其他穴的区别 Stronger for fever than LI11 and may be bled and cupped; filiform needle obliquely upward 0.5–1 cun, with the cord beneath, so withdraw at once at an electric sensation比曲池退热力强，可点刺出血加拔罐；毫针向上斜刺 0.5–1 寸，深面为脊髓，有触电感立即退针

BL23 Shènshū肾俞

Best for最适合 Cold or long-standing knee pain with a sore back膝冷痛或久痛兼腰酸

Why依据 Back-shu point of the kidney肾之背俞穴，补肾

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Used in painful bi and in liver–kidney deficiency; warms and strengthens the back more than KI3, which nourishes yin; oblique toward the spine or perpendicular 0.5–1 cun, never deep outward toward the kidney, not in pregnancy痛痹与肝肾亏虚都用；比太溪偏温阳强腰，太溪偏滋肾阴；向脊柱方向斜刺或直刺 0.5–1 寸，不向外深刺伤肾，孕期不针

CV4 Guānyuán关元

Best for最适合 Cold painful bi with cold limbs膝冷痛、怕冷肢凉的痛痹

Why依据 Ren vessel, front-mu of the small intestine; warms original yang, usually moxaed任脉，小肠募穴；培元温阳，多灸

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Warms yang from the front while BL23 does so from the back; CV4 is mostly moxaed, BL23 strengthens back and knees; contraindicated in pregnancy, empty the bladder first与肾俞一前一后温补元阳，关元偏培元多灸，肾俞偏强腰膝；孕妇禁针，针前排尿

KI3 Tàixī太溪

Best for最适合 Long-standing pain with sore back and tinnitus久痛兼腰酸、耳鸣的肾虚

Why依据 Kidney yuan and shu-stream point肾经原穴、输穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More distal and yin-nourishing than BL23 and easy to reach; not the first choice in an acutely swollen knee or when weight cannot be borne比肾俞偏远端滋肾，体位方便；急性红肿、不能负重时不以补肾穴为先

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Think of causes by age and site: in teenagers, Osgood–Schlatter disease and patellofemoral pain, and hip disease referring to the knee; in younger active people, meniscus, anterior cruciate or collateral ligament injury and iliotibial band syndrome; over 50, most often osteoarthritis (pain on stairs, morning stiffness under 30 minutes, grating). Stiffness over 30 minutes with several swollen joints calls for rheumatoid testing; a single suddenly hot, swollen knee needs joint-fluid testing to separate gout, pseudogout and septic arthritis. A cystic lump behind the knee is usually a Baker cyst, which mimics a clot when it ruptures and needs ultrasound. The base of osteoarthritis care is quadriceps exercise, weight loss and topical anti-inflammatories; acupuncture can be an adjunct for pain, with inconsistent evidence, and does not restore worn cartilage. Imaging after injury follows the Ottawa knee rule.膝痛按年龄和位置想病因：青少年多为胫骨结节骨骺炎和髌股痛，也要想到髋部疾病牵涉到膝；年轻运动者多为半月板、前交叉韧带、侧副韧带损伤和髂胫束综合征；50 岁以上最常见的是骨关节炎（上下楼痛、晨僵少于 30 分钟、有摩擦感）。晨僵超过 30 分钟、多关节肿痛要查类风湿；单膝突然红肿剧痛要在痛风、假性痛风和化脓性关节炎之间由抽液化验来区分。膝后囊性肿块多为腘窝囊肿，破裂时像血栓，需超声鉴别。骨关节炎的基础治疗是股四头肌锻炼、减重和外用消炎药；针刺可作为减痛的辅助，证据不一致，不能恢复已磨损的软骨。外伤后是否拍片按渥太华膝关节规则判断。
