Where is GB17 Zhèngyíng?正营（GB17）在哪里？

On the head, 2.5 cun above the anterior hairline, directly above the pupil.头部，前发际上 2.5 寸，瞳孔直上。

How is GB17 needled?正营（GB17）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of GB17?正营（GB17）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Foot-Shaoyang channel and Yang Linking vessel足少阳、阳维脉交会穴

Main safety point at GB17?正营（GB17）最要注意的安全问题？

Thread under the galea; the scalp is vascular, so press after withdrawal to prevent haematoma.沿帽状腱膜下平刺；头皮血运丰富，出针按压防血肿。
