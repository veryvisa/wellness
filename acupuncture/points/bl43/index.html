📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone or seated with the head flexed and scapulae naturally lowered, without shoulder elevation.俯卧或端坐低头，肩胛自然下垂，避免耸肩。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    The medial end of the scapular spine ≈ T3; one level down is T4, measure 3 cun lateral. The classical method has the patient hug the opposite shoulders or lean on a table so the scapula slides out and uncovers the point; with arms hanging it may lie under the scapular border.肩胛冈内侧端约平 T3，向下一椎为 T4，在其下缘水平旁开 3 寸。古法令患者两手交抱对肩或伏案，使肩胛骨外移，穴才露在肩胛内缘外；两臂下垂时穴可被肩胛内缘覆盖。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    On the lateral Bladder line over trapezius, rhomboids and erector spinae. Intercostal structures, pleura and lung lie deep; the tip must be shallow and oblique toward the spine. At 3 cun lateral the needle is beyond erector spinae, the muscle cover is thinner than on the 1.5-cun line and the pleura is closer to the skin, so the same depth is more likely to puncture it.位于斜方肌、菱形肌和竖脊肌覆盖区第二侧线。深部为肋间神经血管束、胸膜和肺；针尖必须浅斜向脊柱。旁开 3 寸已到竖脊肌外缘，肌层比旁开 1.5 寸的背俞穴薄，胸膜离皮肤更近，同样的深度在这条线上更容易刺破胸膜。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure roughly 3 cun laterally with finger cun, then verify against the T4 spinous process and scapular-spine relationship.后正中线旁开约 3 寸，用横指或患者中指同身寸粗量，再以 T4棘突和肩胛冈标志核验。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    At T4 in the medial lower-scapular region, palpate muscle bands and tenderness; do not explore the deep chest.在肩胛内下区 T4水平触肌筋膜条索及压痛；深部落空不可朝胸腔探索。

Pleura and lung are reachable deeply; the needle must travel shallowly toward the spine. Chest pain, shortness of breath, inspiratory pain, or persistent cough after needling requires emergency care.深部可及胸膜和肺；针尖必须浅斜向脊柱。针后胸痛、气短、吸气痛或持续咳嗽须立即就医。
