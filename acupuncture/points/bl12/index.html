📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone with a thin pillow under the chest, or seated with the head bowed and the arms hanging. Raising or folding the arms moves the scapulae.俯卧，胸下可垫薄枕；或正坐低头、两臂自然下垂，肩放松。抬臂、抱胸会让肩胛骨移位。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun; BL12 is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by the spinous processes, not by cun: below the spine of T2.两臂下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸；风门在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。纵向不量寸，按棘突定：第 2 胸椎棘突下。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    The most prominent bump at the base of the bowed neck is C7 (it moves when the head turns; T1 below does not). Count down T1, T2, and take the point level with the lower edge of the T2 spine, 1.5 cun out. Cross-check by counting one spine up from the medial end of the scapular spine (about T3).低头时颈后最突出的骨突是第 7 颈椎（左右转头时它跟着动，下面的 T1 不动）。往下数：T1、T2，在 T2 棘突下缘水平、旁开 1.5 寸取穴。也可从肩胛冈内侧端（约平 T3）往上数一个棘突核对。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Skin and subcutaneous tissue, trapezius, rhomboids, serratus posterior superior, then erector spinae; beneath are the ribs and the 2nd–3rd intercostal space, with the intercostal vessels and nerve along the lower rib edge. The upper back wall is thin — in lean people skin to pleura may be under 3 cm — and the pleura and lung lie directly beneath.皮肤、皮下组织，斜方肌、菱形肌、上后锯肌，再是竖脊肌；其下为肋骨与第 2、3 肋间隙，肋间血管神经沿肋下缘走行。上胸背的胸壁薄，瘦人皮肤到胸膜可能不足 3 厘米，深面即胸膜和肺。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press on the crest of the erector spinae ridge beside the spine; early in a cold, with a stiff upper back, it is often tender or cool. Push toward the spine, not down into the rib space.在竖脊肌隆起的最高处（脊柱旁的肌肉条）按压，外感初起、项背发紧的人此处常有压痛或发凉感。按压时把手指朝脊柱方向推，不要往肋间隙里顶。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Needling laterally or straight in deep: the tip passes through the intercostal space into the pleura and lung, causing pneumothorax. Textbooks allow only oblique insertion toward the spine, 0.5–0.8 cun.向外侧或垂直深刺：针尖穿过肋间隙即达胸膜和肺，造成气胸。教材只许向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸。
  • Mistaking C6 or T1 for C7: one spine off moves every back-shu point by a level. Use the head-turning test to identify C7.把 C6 当成 C7、或把 T1 当成 C7：数错一个棘突，整排背俞穴都错一节。用转头法分辨 C7。
  • Measuring from the scapula with the arms raised or hugging a pillow: the scapula slides out, the '1.5 cun' lands too lateral and closer to the dangerous rib space.抬臂或趴着抱枕时量肩胛内侧缘：肩胛骨外移，量出来的「1.5 寸」偏外，更接近肋间隙危险区。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is BL12 Fēngmén?风门（BL12）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of T2, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 2 胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL12 needled?风门（BL12）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at BL12?风门（BL12）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only oblique insertion toward the spine, never a deep perpendicular or lateral direction.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向的斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
