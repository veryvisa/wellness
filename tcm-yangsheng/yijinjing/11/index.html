Form 11: Bowing第11式 · 打躬势

Bend forward and rise is one repetition; 3 in all, each a little deeper向前弯腰、起身为一遍，共 3 遍，一遍比一遍弯得深

Dǎ gōng shì

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ Head below heart头低于心脏 ⚠ Deep forward bend深弯腰

  • High blood pressure, glaucoma or retinal disease: do not let the head drop below the heart; stop when the upper body is level with the floor and stay no longer than a breath or two; keep all three bends shallow高血压、青光眼、视网膜病变：头不要低过心脏，上身弯到与地面平行就停，停留不超过一两次呼吸；三遍都按浅的做
  • Osteoporosis: do not roll the spine down section by section; instead rest the hands on the thighs and lean forward from the hips with a flat back; if you have had a spinal fracture, have a physiotherapist check it first骨质疏松：不做一节一节卷背向下，改为两手扶大腿、背平直、从髋部前倾；有过脊椎骨折的先请物理治疗师看过
  • Disc herniation: keep the bend small and stop if the leg goes numb or painful腰椎间盘突出：弯腰幅度小，腿麻腿痛就停
  • Dizzy on standing up: steady yourself or sit down before going on起身后头晕：先站稳或坐下再继续

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Stand up, turn the right toes in to face forward and bring the left foot in to a parallel stance; raise the arms from the sides, bend the elbows, cover the ears with the palms, fingers on the back of the head起身，右脚尖内扣转正，左脚收回成开立；两臂从两侧上举，屈肘，两手掌心捂住耳朵，十指放在后脑
  2. Tap the back of the head lightly a few times with the fingers (traditionally called 'beating the heavenly drum')两手手指轻轻弹击后脑数下（传统叫「鸣天鼓」）
  3. Keeping the legs straight, bend forward slowly, one section at a time: head, neck, chest, waist; look at the toes两腿伸直，从头、颈、胸、腰一节一节向前慢慢弯下，目视脚尖
  4. Uncurl slowly from the waist, chest and neck to the head, back to standing从腰、胸、颈到头一节一节慢慢伸直，回到直立
  5. 3 times in all: a shallow bend first, upper body about level with the floor the second time, and the third to your own comfortable limit共 3 遍：第一遍弯得浅，第二遍上身约与地面平行，第三遍到自己的舒适极限

🌬 Breathe out as you bend, in as you rise; or breathe naturally. Never hold the breath at the bottom.弯下时呼气，起身时吸气；或自然呼吸。弯到底时不憋气。

✅ Key points要领

  • Bend and uncurl one section at a time, slowly一节一节地弯、一节一节地起，速度慢
  • Knees straight but not locked; a stretch at the back of the thighs is enough膝盖伸直但不锁死，大腿后侧有牵拉感即可
  • The hands over the ears rest lightly and never push the head down捂耳的手只是轻贴，不往下压头

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Dropping down in one sudden movement一下子猛弯到底
  • Pushing the head down with the hands to go further用手压头加大幅度
  • Coming up too fast and getting dizzy起身太快导致头晕

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Keep all three bends at or above level with the floor; with osteoporosis, rest the hands on the thighs and lean forward from the hips with a flat back.三遍都只弯到上身与地面平行或更浅；骨质疏松者改为两手扶大腿、背保持平直的屈髋前倾。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Bowing is the posture of a formal bow. The old verse: both hands holding the back of the head, the waist lowered to between the knees, the head reaching below the hips and the teeth clenched. Today's teaching deepens the bend gradually according to your own flexibility instead; there is no need to follow the old text.「打躬」是弯腰作揖的礼节姿态。旧本歌诀：「两手齐持脑，垂腰至膝间；头惟探胯下，口更啮牙关」旧本要求头探到胯下、咬紧牙关，今天的教法已经改为按自己的柔韧逐遍加深，不必照旧本做。
