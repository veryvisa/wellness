Pressing按法 Àn fǎ

Press straight down on one spot with the pad or tip of the thumb or the heel of the palm, building from light to firm, hold a few seconds, then ease off slowly.用拇指指腹、指尖或掌根垂直向下按压一个点，由轻到重，停留几秒再慢慢放松。

Pressure力度 Stop at a mild ache; more pain is not better. Release as slowly as you press.以微酸胀为度，不是越痛越好；放松时同样要慢。

Used on常用部位 Points and well-muscled areas.穴位、肌肉较厚处。

On yourself?自己能做吗 Easy on yourself: use your thumb on points on the hands and legs; someone else does the back.自己就能做：拇指按手上、腿上的穴；够不着的背部由家人按。

Not on不用于

  • Never press hard on the spine or ribs of someone with osteoporosis骨质疏松者的脊柱与肋骨处不用力按
  • Broken, red, swollen skin or a rash皮肤破损、红肿、皮疹处
  • Fresh swelling within 48 hours of a sprain刚扭伤 48 小时内的肿胀处

🏛 Old massage texts paired pressing and rubbing; the Chinese word for massage, anmo, literally means 'press and rub'.古代推拿书把按、摩并称，「按摩」一词就由此而来。

Gentle traction拔伸法 Báshēn fǎ

Steady one side of a joint, hold the other side and draw it slowly and steadily outward along the line of the bone, hold a few seconds and ease back. At home, use it on fingers and toes only.固定关节的一端，握住另一端，沿着骨头的方向慢慢、持续地向外拉开一点，停几秒再慢慢放回。在家只用于手指和脚趾。

Pressure力度 A slow, gentle draw, never a yank. Do not chase a 'click'; a sound does not mean it was done right.是慢慢牵开，不是猛拉；不追求「咔」一声，有响声不代表做对了。

Used on常用部位 At home: fingers and toes. Traction of the neck, low back and large joints is for licensed practitioners only.在家：手指、脚趾。颈部、腰部和大关节的牵引只由持照人员做。

On yourself?自己能做吗 You can gently draw out your own fingers; do not pull your own neck or back, or have family do it.自己可以轻轻牵拉手指；不要自己或请家人拉脖子、拉腰。

Not on不用于

  • Nobody should do neck or low-back traction at home任何人都不在家做颈部、腰部牵拉
  • A joint that has just dislocated, broken or sprained刚脱位、骨折、扭伤的关节
  • People with rheumatoid arthritis or loose joints类风湿关节炎、关节松弛的人

🏛 In bone-setting, traction is used to put joints back in place, and the Golden Mirror of Medicine warns that technique 'must not be applied carelessly'; home self-care borrows only its gentlest corner.拔伸在正骨里用于复位，《医宗金鉴·正骨心法要旨》说「手法亦不可乱施」；家庭保健只取其中最轻的一小部分。

Friction rubbing擦法 Cā fǎ

Keep the palm, heel of the hand or the little-finger edge of the hand on the skin and rub briskly back and forth in a straight line until the area feels warm.手掌、掌根或小鱼际贴住皮肤，沿直线来回快速摩擦，直到局部发热。

Pressure力度 Little pressure, quick strokes; stop when the skin is warm, before it turns red or sore.压力不大、速度较快；以皮肤温热为止，不要擦到发红发痛。

Used on常用部位 Soles, lower back and sacrum, palms, back.脚心、腰骶、手掌、背部。

On yourself?自己能做吗 You can rub your own palms, soles and forearms; the back needs someone else.自己能擦手心、脚心、前臂；腰背由家人擦。

Not on不用于

  • With numb hands or feet (such as diabetic neuropathy) rub gently and check the skin afterwards脚或手有麻木（如糖尿病神经病变）时轻擦，擦后检查皮肤
  • Broken or infected skin, blisters皮肤破损、感染、水泡处

🏛 Tradition calls friction rubbing a 'warming' technique, used on cold hands, feet and the lower back.传统称擦法「温」，常用来让手脚、腰部暖起来。

Palm rolling搓法 Cuō fǎ

Hold an arm or calf between two facing palms, rub quickly back and forth with the palms moving in opposite directions, and travel slowly from top to bottom.两手掌心相对，夹住手臂或小腿，两掌相对用力、方向相反地快速来回搓动，同时慢慢从上往下移。

Pressure力度 Hold neither tight nor loose; rub fast and move slowly. Stop when the skin is warm and the muscle loosens; never rub the skin raw.夹得要松紧适中，搓得快、移得慢；皮肤发热、肌肉松下来就停，不磨红皮肤。

Used on常用部位 Upper arm, forearm, thigh, calf; often used to close a routine.上臂、前臂、大腿、小腿；常放在一段按摩的最后。

On yourself?自己能做吗 Usually done on someone else; on yourself, sit and roll one calf between both hands. Rubbing your palms together is the same motion.多给家人做；自己可以坐着，两手夹住一侧小腿搓，两掌对搓手心也是同一个动作。

Not on不用于

  • Calves with prominent varicose veins静脉曲张明显的小腿
  • Never on a calf that is suddenly swollen, painful and warm on one side (possible blood clot); see a doctor the same day一侧小腿突然肿、痛、发热（可能是血栓）时绝对不搓，要当天看医生
  • Broken skin or an eczema flare皮肤破损、湿疹发作处

🏛 Textbooks list palm rolling among the 'friction' techniques, often paired with shaking to finish.教材把搓法列为「摩擦类」手法，常和抖法一起用来收尾。

Point pressing点法 Diǎn fǎ

Press straight into a point with the tip of the thumb, or with the bony knuckle of a bent index or middle finger; more focused and deeper than ordinary pressing.用拇指指端，或屈起食指、中指用第一节指间关节的骨突，垂直按在穴位上，比按法更集中、更深。

Pressure力度 Strong: hold only a few seconds to a dozen or so, building up and easing off gradually; a sudden jab is how people get hurt.刺激强，只停留几秒到十几秒，逐渐加力、逐渐松开；突然用力戳最容易伤到人。

Used on常用部位 Points in well-muscled spots such as the buttocks, thighs and soles.肌肉较厚处的穴位，如臀部、大腿、足底。

On yourself?自己能做吗 You can use a knuckle on your own soles and calves; for the buttocks, sit on a tennis ball instead.自己可以用指关节点足底、小腿；臀部可坐在网球上代替。

Not on不用于

  • The front of the neck, around the eyes, the temples, anywhere the bone is thin or vessels and nerves lie close to the surface颈前、眼周、头部太阳穴这类骨薄或血管神经浅的地方
  • Over bony points in someone with osteoporosis骨质疏松者的骨突处
  • During pregnancy, not on LI4, SP6, GB21 or the other traditional pregnancy-caution points孕期不点合谷、三阴交、肩井等传统孕期禁忌穴
  • Not on the soles of someone with diabetes and reduced feeling in the feet糖尿病足底感觉减退的人不点足底

🏛 Textbooks regard point pressing as a sharper offshoot of pressing.教材把点法看作由按法演变而来、刺激更集中的一种。

Shaking抖法 Dǒu fǎ

Hold the other person's wrist (or let your own hand hang), have the arm go slack, and shake it up and down in small, quick movements so a wave travels up to the shoulder.握住对方手腕（或自己的手自然下垂），让手臂放松，用小幅度、快频率的上下抖动，让抖动像波浪一样传到肩膀。

Pressure力度 Small and fast; no pulling or flinging. Stop as soon as the person tenses or says it hurts.幅度小、频率快，不拉、不甩；对方一紧张或说疼就停。

Used on常用部位 Mainly the arms; less often the legs.上肢为主，下肢较少用。

On yourself?自己能做吗 On yourself, shake out your hands and wrists; shaking the whole arm needs someone holding your wrist.自己可以甩松双手、抖抖手腕；抖整条手臂要由家人握着手腕做。

Not on不用于

  • An arm whose shoulder has dislocated before, had surgery, or is acutely painful肩关节脱位过、做过肩部手术、肩周正痛得厉害的手臂
  • The weak arm after a stroke (the shoulder slips out of place easily); family should not shake it中风后无力的一侧手臂（肩关节容易半脱位）不由家人抖
  • People whose joints are habitually loose习惯性关节松弛的人

🏛 Textbooks class shaking among the 'vibration' techniques, usually last in an arm routine.教材把抖法归为「振动类」手法，多放在上肢按摩的最后。

Rolling滚法 Gǔn fǎ

Curl the hand loosely and rest the little-finger side of its back on the skin; rotate the forearm back and forth so the back of the hand rolls on one spot, pressing and releasing like a rolling pin, without dragging over the skin.手微握成空拳，用小指一侧的手背贴住皮肤，前臂来回旋转，带动手背在一处来回滚动，像擀面一样一压一松，手背不在皮肤上拖擦。

Pressure力度 The pressure comes from the weight of the hand as it rolls, not from pushing down at the wrist; textbooks give a rhythm of about 120 rolls a minute, slower is fine at home. Aim for a loosening ache in the muscle.压力来自手背滚过时的重量，不是手腕使劲往下顶；每分钟约 120 次上下是教材的通行节律，在家慢一些也可以，以肌肉酸胀放松为度。

Used on常用部位 Thick muscle: shoulders and upper back, low back and buttocks, thighs, calves.肩背、腰臀、大腿、小腿等肌肉厚实的地方。

On yourself?自己能做吗 Hard to do well on yourself; usually a family member does it on your shoulders, back or hips. To learn the motion, practise on your own thigh.很难在自己身上做好，多由家人在你的肩背、腰臀上做；想练手可以先在自己大腿上滚。

Not on不用于

  • Bony, thinly covered spots: the spine's bony bumps, the edge of the shoulder blade, the kneecap骨头突出、肌肉薄的地方（脊柱棘突、肩胛骨边、膝盖骨）
  • Over the spine of someone with osteoporosis骨质疏松者的脊柱上
  • The low back, sacrum and belly during pregnancy孕期的腰骶部和腹部
  • Fresh swelling within 48 hours of a sprain, or broken skin刚扭伤 48 小时内的肿胀处、皮肤破损处

🏛 Rolling is the signature move of a tuina school that took shape in modern Shanghai; textbooks class it among the 'swinging' techniques.滚法是近代上海推拿形成的一个流派的看家手法，教材把它归为「摆动类」手法。

Tapping击法 Jī fǎ

Tap lightly and quickly in rhythm with the fingertips, the little-finger edge of the hand or a loose fist; on the head use only the ten fingertips, like raindrops.用指尖、小指一侧的掌缘或松拳，有节律地轻快叩击；头部只用十指指尖像下雨点一样轻叩。

Pressure力度 Light, quick and springy, lifting at once; lightest of all on the head.轻、快、有弹性，一触即起；头部用力要最轻。

Used on常用部位 Top of the head (fingertips); shoulders, upper back and thighs (hand edge or loose fist).头顶（指尖）、肩背和大腿（掌缘或松拳）。

On yourself?自己能做吗 Fingertip tapping on the scalp you can do yourself; fist or hand-edge work on the back needs someone else.头部指尖叩击自己就能做；背部用拳或掌缘要由家人做。

Not on不用于

  • No fist tapping over the kidneys on either side of the low back后腰两侧（肾所在的位置）不用拳叩
  • No fist for people with osteoporosis or on blood thinners骨质疏松、在吃抗凝药的人不用拳
  • Any part of the head that has been injured or operated on头部受过伤、做过开颅手术的地方
  • The belly, low back and sacrum during pregnancy孕期的腹部和腰骶部

🏛 Traditional self-care has 'beating the heavenly drum': palms over the ears while the fingers tap the back of the head, an old example of tapping used on oneself.传统养生里有「鸣天鼓」——两手掌心捂耳、手指在后脑轻叩，是击法用于自我保健的一个老例子。

Circular rubbing摩法 Mó fǎ

Rest the palm or four fingers lightly on the skin and stroke in wide circles over the surface without pressing in.手掌或并拢的四指轻贴皮肤，在体表做较大的环形抚摩，只在皮肤表面走，不往深处压。

Pressure力度 Lighter than kneading, at a steady pace; on the belly go clockwise (up the right side, across, down the left).比揉法轻，速度平稳；腹部按顺时针方向（从右下腹往上、往左、往下）。

Used on常用部位 Belly, chest and flanks, face.腹部、胸胁、面部。

On yourself?自己能做吗 Easy on yourself; most often on the belly and face.自己就能做，摩腹、摩面最常用。

Not on不用于

  • Sudden belly pain, a rigid belly, or pain that is worse when pressure is released急性腹痛、腹部变硬或按下去松手更痛
  • The belly in pregnancy孕期腹部
  • An unhealed abdominal wound, a hernia or a known abdominal aortic aneurysm腹部手术伤口未愈、有疝或已知腹主动脉瘤

🏛 Rubbing the belly after meals is a long-standing habit in traditional self-care.饭后摩腹是传统养生里流传很久的做法。

Grasping拿法 Ná fǎ

Oppose the thumb and the other four fingers, gently lift and squeeze a band of muscle, then lower it slowly, in a steady lift-and-release rhythm.拇指与其余四指相对，把一块肌肉轻轻提起、捏住，再慢慢放下，一提一松，有节律地进行。

Pressure力度 Use the finger pads, not the nails, building from light to firm; aim for a comfortable ache, never pinching the skin.用指腹而不是指尖，力量由轻到重；以酸胀舒服为度，不掐皮肤。

Used on常用部位 Top of the shoulders (trapezius), back of the neck, upper arms, calves.肩上（斜方肌）、颈后、上臂、小腿。

On yourself?自己能做吗 You can grasp the opposite shoulder, the back of your neck and your calves; the back needs someone else.自己能拿对侧肩上、颈后、小腿；背部由家人拿。

Not on不用于

  • A freshly strained or sprained muscle刚拉伤或扭伤的肌肉
  • People on blood thinners bruise easily with firm grasping抗凝药使用者用力过大易淤青

🏛 Textbooks class grasping among the 'squeezing' techniques; it is a staple for the neck and shoulders.教材把拿法归为「挤压类」手法，常用于颈肩。

Used in用在💆 After an afternoon at the desk: self-massage for the neck and shoulders伏案一下午之后：颈肩的自我按摩

Pinching捏法 Niē fǎ

Oppose the thumb and index finger (or the other four fingers), gently lift the skin and the layer under it, roll it between them, let go and move on section by section.拇指与食指（或与其余四指）相对，把皮肤和皮下一层轻轻捏起、捻动，再放下，一段一段移动。

Pressure力度 Shallower and smaller than grasping; a slight full feeling is enough. Never use the nails.比拿法提得浅、范围小；捏起来有微微的胀感即可，不用指甲掐。

Used on常用部位 Fingers, toes, upper arms, back of the neck; along the spine it is called spine pinching (listed separately).手指、脚趾、上臂、颈后；脊柱两旁做的叫捏脊（单列）。

On yourself?自己能做吗 You can pinch your own fingers, toes and upper arms.自己可以捏手指、捏脚趾、捏上臂。

Not on不用于

  • Broken skin, a rash, eczema or sunburn皮肤破损、皮疹、湿疹、晒伤处
  • Lightest touch only for people on blood thinners or who bruise easily在吃抗凝药、容易淤青的人力度放到最轻

🏛 Textbooks put pinching and grasping together as 'squeezing' techniques: pinching is shallow, grasping is deep.教材把捏法、拿法同归「挤压类」，区别在捏得浅、拿得深。

Used in用在💆 Cold hands and feet: rub, roll, warm, then move手脚冰凉：搓、捻、擦，再动一动

Spine pinching捏脊法 Niējǐ fǎ

The person lies face down. Along both sides of the spine, lift a roll of skin between the thumb and the index and middle fingers and walk it forward hand over hand, from just above the tailbone to the bony bump at the base of the neck; 'three pinches, one lift' means lifting the skin once every three pinches.被按的人俯卧。两手沿脊柱两旁，用拇指与食指、中指把皮肤捏起，两手交替向前捻动推进，从尾骨上端一直捏到颈后大椎；俗称「捏三提一」是每捏三下把皮肤向上提一下。

Pressure力度 Only the skin is lifted; never press on the spine itself. Start light and short; stop if a child resists or cries.只捏皮肤，不按脊骨；第一次从轻、从少开始，孩子抗拒或哭闹就停。

Used on常用部位 Either side of the spine, from above the tailbone to the base of the neck.脊柱两旁，从尾骨上端到颈后。

On yourself?自己能做吗 Cannot be done on yourself: a parent does it for a child or family members for each other. For children, see the pediatric page on spine pinching.自己做不了，由家长给孩子做或家人之间做；小儿怎么做见小儿推拿「捏脊」页。

Not on不用于

  • Broken skin, a rash or an eczema flare on the back背部皮肤破损、皮疹、湿疹发作时
  • Known spinal disease, or after spinal surgery有已知脊柱疾病、脊柱手术后的人
  • During a fever or illness when the person looks unwell发烧且精神差、正生病时

🏛 In the Eastern Jin, Ge Hong's Handbook of Prescriptions for Emergencies, under sudden abdominal pain, says to pick up the skin over the spine, from the tailbone to the top, and repeat if needed. Patting拍法 Pāi fǎ

Close the fingers and cup the palm slightly, keep the wrist loose and pat in an even rhythm; a hollow sound means the cup is right.五指并拢、掌心微微拱起成空心掌，手腕放松，有节律地平稳拍打，拍下去能听到空空的声音。

Used on常用部位 Small points over bone, such as fingertips and beside the nails.手指尖、指甲旁等骨面上的小点。

Not on不用于

Used on常用部位 The sides of the head, from the temple hairline to above and behind the ear.头的两侧，额角到耳上、耳后。

Cross-fibre plucking弹拨法 Tánbō fǎ

Press the pad of the thumb or four fingers onto a tight band of muscle or tendon and slowly pluck across it, at right angles to its line, like strumming a string.拇指或四指指腹按住一条发紧的肌肉或筋，垂直于它走行的方向，像拨琴弦一样慢慢来回拨动。

Pressure力度 Press first, then pluck with small movements; an ache is fine, but a tingling electric feeling means a nerve: move off at once.先按住再拨，幅度小；酸胀可以，出现麻电感就是拨到神经了，马上移开。

Used on常用部位 Tight spots at the back of the neck, the top of the shoulders, inside the shoulder blades, the buttocks.颈后、肩上、肩胛骨内侧、臀部等肌肉发紧处。

Used on常用部位 Forehead, scalp, neck and back, limbs.额头、头皮、颈背、四肢。

On yourself?自己能做吗 You can push along your face and head, forearms and calves; the back needs someone else.自己能推头面、前臂、小腿；背部由家人推。

Joint circling摇法 Yáo fǎ

Let a joint draw slow circles within the range it already has: a few circles at the wrist, ankle and shoulder, clockwise then counter-clockwise. At home, move the joint yourself.让关节在自己本来能到的范围里慢慢画圈：手腕、脚踝、肩膀各转几圈，先顺时针再逆时针。在家以自己主动转为主。

Pressure力度 Slow and round, small to larger, only where it does not hurt; shrink the circle or stop at any catch or sharp pain.慢、圆、由小到大，只在不痛的范围里；感到卡住、刺痛就缩小圈或停。

Used on常用部位 Wrists, ankles, shoulders, hips. For the neck, only slow nodding and turning; no fast circles.手腕、脚踝、肩、髋。颈部只做慢慢的低头、抬头、左右转头，不快速绕圈。

On yourself?自己能做吗 Move the joints yourself; never let someone else forcibly turn your neck for you.自己主动转就行；不要让别人用力替你转脖子。

Not on不用于

  • If turning the head brings dizziness, dimming vision or numb hands, skip neck movements and see a doctor转头就头晕、眼前发黑、手麻的人不做颈部动作，先看医生
  • No neck circles with rheumatoid arthritis (the upper neck can be unstable) or osteoporosis类风湿关节炎（上颈椎可能不稳）、骨质疏松的人不做颈部绕圈
  • A freshly sprained joint or one just operated on刚扭伤、刚做完关节手术的关节

🏛 Textbooks list circling among the 'joint-moving' techniques, alongside manipulation and traction; home self-care keeps only the slow, self-moved part.教材把摇法列为「运动关节类」手法，和扳法、拔伸法同类；家庭保健只取主动、慢速的部分。

One-finger pushing一指禅推法 Yīzhǐchán tuī fǎ

Stand the tip or pad of the thumb on one spot, let the wrist hang loose, and swing the wrist side to side from the forearm so the force reaches that spot through the thumb in a steady rhythm; the thumb never leaves the skin.拇指指端或螺纹面立在一个点上，手腕放松下垂，前臂带动手腕左右摆动，力量通过拇指持续、有节律地送到这一点上，拇指不离开皮肤。

Pressure力度 The aim is steady, deep and soft, not heavy; a stiff wrist or thumb is how practitioners strain their own thumb joint.要的是持续、渗透、柔和，而不是重；手腕和拇指一僵就容易伤到自己的拇指关节。

Used on常用部位 Points and small areas that need fine work: face and head, neck, shoulders and upper back.穴位、头面、颈项、肩背等需要细致刺激的小范围。

On yourself?自己能做吗 A professional technique that takes long training; at home there is no need to master the swing. Pressing and kneading are enough for your own points.这是要长期练功的专业手法，在家不必追求标准摆动；自己按穴时用按法、揉法就够。

Not on不用于

  • The eyeball, and the front of the neck (the carotid arteries run either side of the throat)眼球、颈前（喉咙两侧有颈动脉）
  • Broken, red or swollen skin, or a rash皮肤破损、红肿、皮疹处
  • Do not keep practising once your own thumb joint aches自己的拇指关节已经疼痛时不要硬练

⛔ Manipulation (thrust)扳法 Bān fǎ · licensed practitioners only只能由持照人员操作

Manipulation takes a joint passively to the end of its range and adds a fast, small thrust, often with a 'click'; it is used on the neck, mid back and low back. This entry explains what it is and does not teach it.扳法是把关节被动带到活动范围的尽头，再加一个快速、小幅度的推扳，常伴有「咔」的一声；颈椎、胸椎、腰椎都有。这里只说明它是什么，不教做法。

Who谁能做 Only after an in-person assessment by a licensed practitioner (registered massage therapist, physiotherapist, chiropractor, licensed TCM practitioner and so on).只在持照人员（注册按摩治疗师、物理治疗师、脊骨神经科医生、持照中医师等）面诊评估后使用。

At home在家 Do not crack your own neck, do not let family do it, and do not let anyone unlicensed do it.不自己扳脖子、不让家人扳、也不让没有执照的人扳。

Why only licensed practitioners为什么只能由持照人员操作

  • The thrust is too fast for the body's own muscles to guard against; before it, a practitioner has to rule out fracture, osteoporosis, tumours, loose ligaments and disease of the neck arteries, which takes an in-person assessment and sometimes imaging.推扳的动作快，人体来不及用肌肉保护自己；施术前要先排除骨折、骨质疏松、肿瘤、韧带松弛、颈动脉或椎动脉病变等不能扳的情况，这要靠面诊和必要时的影像检查。
  • The neck matters most: studies link neck manipulation with vertebral artery dissection (a tear in the artery wall that can cause a stroke). Cause and effect is unproven and it is probably rare, but the outcome is severe.颈部最要紧：颈椎推扳与椎动脉夹层（会引起中风的血管内壁撕裂）之间在研究里有关联，因果没有定论，发生率可能很低，但后果重。
  • Licensed practitioners are trained, held to practice standards and answerable to a regulator; if something goes wrong, there is someone accountable.持照人员有培训、有执业标准、能被投诉和追责；出了问题知道找谁。

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E1 An American Heart Association / American Stroke Association scientific statement concluded that biomechanical evidence is not enough to show that neck manipulation causes arterial dissection, but most population-controlled studies found an association between neck manipulation and vertebral artery dissection stroke in younger patients; the rate is unknown and probably low, and practitioners should tell patients about this risk beforehand.美国心脏协会/卒中协会的科学声明综述后认为：现有生物力学证据不足以证明颈椎推扳会导致颈动脉夹层，但多数人群对照研究在年轻患者中见到颈椎推扳与椎动脉夹层卒中的关联；发生率未明、可能很低，施术者应在推扳前告知这项风险。 [1]
  • E3 A 2026 systematic review found 24 case reports of harm after people cracked their own necks: about half did it for neck pain or stiffness, most with their own hands; 9 were severe and 4 catastrophic (2 deaths), almost all within 2 days. Case reports show that harm can happen, not how often.一篇 2026 年系统综述找到 24 例「自己扳脖子」后出事的病例报告：约一半是因为颈痛或发僵，多数用自己的双手扳；其中 9 例重度、4 例灾难性（2 例死亡），几乎都在扳后 2 天内出现。病例报告只能说明可能出事，不能说明有多常见。 [2]
  • E3 For children, a literature review collected 15 serious adverse events (3 deaths), mostly after high-velocity, extension or rotation spinal manipulation, and most of the children had an underlying condition nobody had found; the authors stress a full history and examination first. That is why pediatric massage here uses only light techniques such as pressing, rubbing, pushing and pinching, never manipulation.儿童方面，一篇文献综述收集到 15 例严重不良事件（含 3 例死亡），多数涉及高速、伸展、旋转的脊柱推扳，且多数孩子本来就有未被发现的病变；作者强调施术前要充分问诊和检查。所以小儿推拿只用按、摩、推、捏这类轻手法，不用扳法。 [3]
