In the posterior neck, below the occipital bone, in the depression between the upper ends of sternocleidomastoid and trapezius (level with GV16).颈后区，枕骨之下，胸锁乳突肌上端与斜方肌上端之间的凹陷中（横平风府）。

How is GB20 needled?风池（GB20）怎么刺？

Oblique, tip slightly downward, toward the nose tip or the opposite eye; 0.8–1.2 cun.针尖微下，向鼻尖或对侧眼球方向斜刺；0.8–1.2 寸。

Special-point roles of GB20?风池（GB20）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Foot-Shaoyang channel and Yang Linking vessel足少阳、阳维脉交会穴

Main safety point at GB20?风池（GB20）最要注意的安全问题？

The medulla and vertebral artery lie deep: aim only toward the nose tip or opposite eye, never deep superomedially toward the foramen magnum; withdraw if an electric sensation or dizziness occurs.深部正对延髓与椎动脉：针尖只朝鼻尖或对侧眼球，绝不向内上方（枕骨大孔方向）深刺；进针中出现触电感或患者头晕即退针。
