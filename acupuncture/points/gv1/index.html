Luo-connecting point of the Governor Vessel督脉络穴Crossing point of the Governor Vessel with the Foot-Shaoyang and Foot-Shaoyin channels督脉、足少阳、足少阴交会穴

📍 Location定位★

In the perineal region, below the coccyx, midway between the tip of the coccyx and the anus.会阴区，尾骨下方，尾骨端与肛门连线的中点处。

💡 In the knee-chest or prone kneeling position, find the coccyx tip and take the midpoint between it and the centre of the anus.跪伏或胸膝位，先摸到尾骨尖，再取它与肛门中心连线的中点。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique upward, keeping close to the anterior surface of the coccyx紧靠尾骨前面向上斜刺 · 0.8–1 cun0.8–1 寸

Do not needle perpendicularly, to avoid the rectum.不宜直刺，以免伤及直肠。

🛡 Safety安全

The rectum lies directly in front: keep the needle against the front of the coccyx pointing upward, never perpendicular or angled forward; do not needle over a perianal abscess or fissure.直肠就在穴位前方：针体须贴尾骨前面向上，不可直刺或偏向前方；局部有肛周脓肿、肛裂时不刺。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治★

Diarrhoea, dysentery, blood in the stool, constipation, haemorrhoids, rectal prolapse腹泻、痢疾、便血、便秘、痔疮、脱肛 ⚖️Mania and epilepsy癫狂痫Pain of the lumbar spine, sacrum and coccyx腰脊、尾骶部疼痛

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Side-lying with hips and knees flexed, or prone; preserve privacy and relax the gluteal muscles.侧卧屈髋屈膝，或俯卧，充分遮盖并尊重隐私；放松臀部。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Identify the midline between the coccygeal tip and anal opening and locate its midpoint; do not substitute the anal margin for the opening.先触尾骨尖与肛门之间的正中线，按两者连线中点定位；不以肛缘代替肛门中心。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate the coccygeal tip and the anal opening; the point is on the skin midline between them. The anal sphincter, anal canal and rectum are immediately adjacent; assessment and any procedure require a qualified practitioner, with no deep direction toward the canal or rectum.沿尾骨末端向下触到尾骨尖，再向前下方确认肛门中心，穴在两者间的皮肤正中。肛门括约肌、肛管和直肠紧邻，定位及任何操作均须由合格针灸师谨慎评估，不得向肛管或直肠深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate the midline soft tissue below the coccygeal tip, avoiding drift into the gluteal cleft or anal margin. Stop and assess first if there is a mass, ulceration or marked inflammatory pain.轻触尾骨尖下方的正中软组织，确认没有偏入臀沟或肛缘；按压若有异常肿块、破溃或明显炎痛，应停止并先评估病因。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting to the anal margin or lateral perianal skin misses the midline point and increases local irritation; do not treat through infection, fissure or bleeding—seek assessment first.取在肛缘或肛門旁会偏離正中穴位並增加局部刺激；有肛周感染、裂口或出血时不可在此处操作，先就医。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Coccydynia and sacrococcygeal soft-tissue pain尾骨痛、骶尾部软组织痛

Pain after sitting may follow coccygeal contusion or soft-tissue strain; marked tenderness after a fall needs fracture assessment, and persistent night pain or a mass warrants prompt evaluation.久坐后痛可见于尾骨挫伤或软组织劳损；跌倒后明显压痛需排除骨折，持续夜间痛或肿块应及时检查。

Perianal pain肛周疼痛

Haemorrhoids, fissure and abscess require clinical differentiation; fever, redness or discharge must not be managed as a point problem.痔、肛裂、脓肿等病因需由医疗人员鉴别；发热、红肿或流脓不能按穴处理。

  • Severe coccygeal pain after a fall, saddle numbness or bowel/bladder dysfunction: emergency assessment.跌倒后尾骨剧痛、会阴麻木或大小便控制异常：急诊评估。

Where is GV1 Chángqiáng?长强（GV1）在哪里？

In the perineal region, below the coccyx, midway between the tip of the coccyx and the anus.会阴区，尾骨下方，尾骨端与肛门连线的中点处。

How is GV1 needled?长强（GV1）怎么刺？

Oblique upward, keeping close to the anterior surface of the coccyx; 0.8–1 cun.紧靠尾骨前面向上斜刺；0.8–1 寸。

Special-point roles of GV1?长强（GV1）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting point of the Governor Vessel; Crossing point of the Governor Vessel with the Foot-Shaoyang and Foot-Shaoyin channels督脉络穴；督脉、足少阳、足少阴交会穴

Main safety point at GV1?长强（GV1）最要注意的安全问题？

The rectum lies directly in front: keep the needle against the front of the coccyx pointing upward, never perpendicular or angled forward; do not needle over a perianal abscess or fissure.直肠就在穴位前方：针体须贴尾骨前面向上，不可直刺或偏向前方；局部有肛周脓肿、肛裂时不刺。
