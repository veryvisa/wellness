Palpitations心悸

The textbook treats palpitations with a shared core — PC6, PC4, HT7, BL15 and CV14 — and adds points by pattern: BL19 for heart–gallbladder timidity, BL20 and ST36 for heart–spleen deficiency, KI3 and BL23 for yin deficiency with fire, GV9 and CV4 for heart-yang deficiency, CV6 and SP9 for fluid harassing the heart, CV17 and BL17 for blood stasis; internal medicine adds phlegm-fire. Palpitations can also mean an arrhythmia, an overactive thyroid or anaemia: a first episode needs an ECG, and chest pain, fainting or breathlessness means 911 first.教材治心悸有一组共用主穴——内关、郄门、神门、心俞、巨阙，再按证加穴：心胆虚怯加胆俞，心脾两虚加脾俞、足三里，阴虚火旺加太溪、肾俞，心阳不振加至阳、关元，水饮凌心加气海、阴陵泉，心血瘀阻加膻中、膈俞；中医内科还有痰火扰心一型。心悸也可能是心律失常、甲亢、贫血的表现，第一次发作先做心电图，有胸痛、晕厥、气短先打 911。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Palpitations with pressure-like chest pain, difficulty breathing, cold sweats, nausea, fainting or near-fainting, especially if lasting several minutes or worsening: call 911. Do not attempt acupuncture or drive yourself to hospital.心悸同时出现胸部压榨感或胸痛、呼吸困难、冷汗、恶心、晕厥或濒临晕厥，尤其持续数分钟或正在加重：立即拨打 911，不要自行针灸或驾车就医。
  • A resting heart rate persistently above 120 or below 40 beats per minute with dizziness, marked weakness, breathlessness or chest discomfort requires urgent emergency assessment and an ECG, even if symptoms subsequently settle.静息状态下心跳持续超过 120 次/分钟或低于 40 次/分钟，且伴头晕、明显乏力、气短或胸部不适：立即接受急诊评估；即使症状缓解，也应尽快查心电图。
  • New recurrent palpitations, episodes lasting more than several minutes, or a marked increase in frequency, especially with reduced exercise tolerance, waking breathless at night or leg swelling: seek same-day medical assessment, with ECG and ambulatory monitoring as indicated.新出现的心悸反复发作、持续超过数分钟，或发作频率明显增加，尤其伴运动耐量下降、夜间憋醒、下肢水肿：当天联系医生评估，必要时做心电图及动态心电图。
  • Palpitations with fever, unexplained weight loss, tremor and heat intolerance, or heavy menstrual bleeding, black stools or marked pallor require prompt medical evaluation, including thyroid function and blood tests.心悸伴发热、明显消瘦、手抖、怕热，或近期出现大量月经出血、黑便、明显面色苍白：尽快就医，检查甲状腺功能、血常规及其他相关指标。

「胸脇支滿，心中憺憺大動」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.10, disorders of the Pericardium channel: fullness of the chest and flanks and the heart pounding strongly. Palpitations are placed on this channel, the basis for treating them with PC6, PC4 and PC7.《灵枢·经脉》手厥阴心包经病候：胸胁胀满，心里扑扑地大动。心悸归到心包经，这是内关、郄门、大陵这些心包经穴治心悸的依据。

「心中憺憺，恐人將捕之，邪在膽」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.19: the heart flutters and the person fears being seized; the pathogen is in the gallbladder. This is the root of the heart–gallbladder timidity type and of adding BL19.《灵枢·四时气》：心里悸动不安，害怕像有人要来抓自己，病邪在胆。心胆虚怯一型的惊悸善恐，配胆俞，渊源在此。

「心悸虛煩刺三里」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu dacheng: for palpitations with deficiency restlessness, needle ST36. ST36 builds qi and blood and suits heart–spleen deficiency with fatigue and poor appetite, not every type.《针灸大成》载：心悸而虚烦，刺足三里。足三里补后天气血，适合心脾两虚、乏力食少的心悸，不是各型通用。

「心膽諸虛，怔忡驚悸」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu dacheng: fluttering and fright from weak heart and gallbladder; the entry lists HT6, BL15 and HT5, all acting directly on the heart.《针灸大成》：心胆虚弱所致的怔忡惊悸，原条下列阴郄、心俞、通里三穴，都直接调心。

Internal内伤

Heart and gallbladder qi timidity心胆虚怯

How it presents辨证要点 Palpitations with easy fright, restlessness and excessive apprehension; easily startled by minor sounds or events. Often accompanied by insomnia, vivid dreams, timidity and shortness of breath. Episodes may be triggered or aggravated by emotional stress or fright. Pale or light-red tongue, thin white coating, wiry-thin or weak pulse.心悸易惊，坐卧不安，善恐，遇事容易紧张，稍有声响即惊跳；常伴失眠、多梦、胆怯、气短；症状常由惊吓、情绪刺激诱发或加重。舌淡或淡红，苔薄白，脉弦细或细弱。

Principle治法 Calm fright, settle the mind and nourish the heart (even method, with tonification when deficient).镇惊定志，养心安神（以平补平泻为主，虚者配补法）

Main主穴PC6 Nèiguān内关HT7 Shénmén神门PC4 Xìmén郄门BL15 Xīnshū心俞CV14 Jùquè巨阙
Add配穴BL19 Dǎnshū胆俞PC7 Dàlíng大陵

💡 Textbook core for palpitations: PC6 (Pericardium luo point, opens the Yin Linking vessel), PC4 (Pericardium xi-cleft, for acute bouts), HT7 (Heart yuan point), and BL15 with CV14 (the Heart back-shu and front-mu pair). Add BL19, the Gallbladder back-shu point, to steady the gallbladder and settle fright; if easily startled add PC7, the Pericardium yuan point. Lingshu assigns a fluttering heart with fear of being seized to the gallbladder, which is why BL19 is added.教材心悸主穴：内关（心包络穴，通阴维）、郄门（心包郄穴，主急症）、神门（心经原穴）、心俞与巨阙（心的背俞穴与募穴，俞募相配）。心胆虚怯配胆俞，胆的背俞穴，壮胆定惊；易惊明显加大陵，心包经原穴，宁心定惊。《灵枢》把「心中憺憺，恐人将捕之」归于胆，正是此型配胆俞的道理。

Internal内伤

Heart and spleen deficiency心脾两虚

How it presents辨证要点 Palpitations and shortness of breath, worse after exertion, with a weak heartbeat sensation. Associated insomnia, vivid dreams, poor memory, dizziness, sallow complexion, fatigue, poor appetite and abdominal bloating; women may have scanty, pale menstruation. Often follows chronic illness, overwork, excessive worry or blood loss. Pale tongue, thin white coating, thin weak pulse.心悸气短，活动后加重，心跳乏力；兼失眠、多梦、健忘、头晕、面色萎黄、神疲乏力、食欲不振、腹胀，女性可伴月经量少或色淡。多见于久病、劳倦、思虑过度或失血之后。舌淡，苔薄白，脉细弱。

Principle治法 Tonify the heart and spleen, nourish blood and calm the mind (tonifying method; moxa may be added).补益心脾，养血安神（补法，可配灸）

Severe cases may have spontaneous sweating, leg oedema, bluish lips or tongue, and cold chest pain. Often seen in older or chronically ill patients. Pale swollen tongue with white coating; deep, slow, weak or irregular pulse.心悸不安，动则加重，胸闷气短，面色苍白，畏寒肢冷，精神疲倦；严重者可见自汗、下肢浮肿、唇舌青紫、胸部冷痛。多见于年老体弱、久病或心阳受损者。舌淡胖、苔白，脉沉迟、弱或结代。

Internal内伤

Accompanied by cough or wheezing, thin watery phlegm, chest and flank fullness, epigastric distension and nausea; severe cases may have leg oedema and reduced urine output. 🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

In modern medicine, palpitations are an individual's awareness of an abnormal heartbeat. They may feel like a racing, slow, pounding, fluttering, skipped or irregular heartbeat. Palpitations are a symptom, not a diagnosis, and do not necessarily indicate an arrhythmia. Common causes include: 1. Cardiac causes: atrial fibrillation, supraventricular tachycardia, premature ventricular or atrial contractions, conduction disorders and other arrhythmias. Coronary artery disease, cardiomyopathy, valvular disease and heart failure may also cause palpitations. 2. Systemic causes: anaemia, hyperthyroidism, fever, infection, hypoglycaemia, dehydration and electrolyte disturbances. 3. Physiological and emotional factors: exercise, sleep deprivation, stress, anxiety, panic attacks, pregnancy and menopausal hormonal changes. 4. Medications and stimulants: caffeine, nicotine, alcohol, some cold remedies containing pseudoephedrine, bronchodilators, thyroid medication and certain prescription drugs. Assessment: New or recurrent palpitations warrant a medical history, vital signs and a 12-lead ECG. If an ECG is normal between episodes but symptoms recur, a clinician may arrange Holter monitoring (24–48 hours or longer), an event recorder or another form of rhythm monitoring. Depending on the presentation, investigations may include a complete blood count, thyroid function, electrolytes, glucose and kidney function. Echocardiography may be indicated if structural heart disease is suspected. Chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness or haemodynamic instability require emergency assessment, not a trial of acupuncture. TCM patterns do not map one-to-one onto modern medical diagnoses. Heart–gallbladder timidity may describe palpitations after emotional stress or fright, but this does not establish an anxiety disorder. Heart–spleen deficiency, yin deficiency with fire and heart yang deficiency are traditional pattern descriptions involving combinations of weakness, insomnia, cold intolerance and night sweats; they are not equivalent to anaemia, thyroid disease or heart failure. Fluid retention harassing the heart may overlap symptomatically with oedema, breathlessness and chest tightness in heart or kidney disease and must be medically investigated. Blood stasis obstructing the heart may include chest pain and a dusky tongue, but it is not synonymous with coronary artery disease; acute coronary syndrome must be excluded first. Acupuncture is a complementary symptom-pattern approach, not a substitute for the diagnosis or treatment of arrhythmias. Patients with diagnosed arrhythmia, coronary disease, heart failure or anticoagulant use require review of their diagnosis, medications and bleeding risk, with discussion with their treating clinician before acupuncture. Patients with pacemakers or implantable cardioverter-defibrillators should not have electroacupuncture at all, nor needling near the device. Stop treatment and activate emergency care if red flags occur.现代医学所说的心悸，是患者对心跳的异常感知，可以表现为心跳过快、过慢、漏跳、乱跳、搏动增强或胸中扑动感。心悸是一种症状，不是一个独立诊断，也不必然意味着存在心律失常。 常见病因可分为： 1. 心脏原因：房颤、室上性心动过速、室性早搏、房性早搏、传导阻滞及其他心律失常；也可能与冠心病、心肌病、心脏瓣膜病、心力衰竭有关。 2. 全身性原因：贫血、甲状腺功能亢进、发热、感染、低血糖、脱水、电解质紊乱等。 3. 生理及情绪因素：运动、睡眠不足、压力、焦虑、惊恐发作、妊娠及更年期激素变化。 4. 药物与刺激物：咖啡因、尼古丁、酒精、部分感冒药（如含伪麻黄碱制剂）、支气管扩张剂、甲状腺药物及某些处方药。 检查原则：首次出现或反复发作的心悸，应先进行病史询问、生命体征检查及十二导联心电图。发作时心电图正常但症状反复者，医生可能安排 Holter 动态心电图（24–48 小时或更长时间）、事件记录仪或其他心律监测。根据临床表现检查全血细胞计数、甲状腺功能、电解质、血糖及肾功能；怀疑结构性心脏病时考虑超声心动图。伴胸痛、晕厥、严重呼吸困难或血流动力学不稳定时，按急症处理，不等待针灸治疗。 中医证型与现代病因不能一一对应。心胆虚怯常见于情绪刺激或惊恐后心悸，但不能因此直接诊断为焦虑症；心脾两虚、阴虚火旺、心阳不振是传统对虚弱、失眠、畏寒、盗汗等表现的整体辨证，并不能直接等同于贫血、甲状腺疾病或心力衰竭；水饮凌心的水肿、气短、胸闷可能与现代医学的心衰或液体潴留表现重叠，必须排查心肾疾病；心血瘀阻的胸痛、舌紫等传统表现与现代冠心病也不能画等号，必须优先排除急性冠脉综合征。 针灸定位是症状辨证辅助，不是心律失常治疗的替代方案。对于已确诊的心律失常、冠心病、心衰或正在服用抗凝药物的患者，应先了解诊断、药物及出血风险，与主管医生沟通后再决定是否针灸。正在接受心脏起搏器或植入式除颤器治疗者，不用电针，也不在装置附近针刺。出现危险信号时立即停止针灸并启动急救。
