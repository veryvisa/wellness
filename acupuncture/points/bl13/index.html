⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

The main back point for every kind of cough, first choice in chronic and deficient cough; early external cough leads with LU7 and LI4, BL13 added.各型咳嗽的背部主穴，久咳、虚咳尤其首选；外感初起以列缺、合谷为主，肺俞配用。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Used in both excess and deficient wheeze, with Dingchuan and CV17 or BL43 and BL23; an acute attack needs medication first.哮喘实证、虚证都用，配定喘、膻中或膏肓、肾俞；急性发作先用药物控制。

Coughing up blood咯血 ⚖️

A back point for coughing blood, with LU6 and LU5; find the cause medically first — acupuncture does not replace treatment of bleeding.咳血的背部配穴，配孔最、尺泽；咳血先就医查原因，针灸不代替止血处理。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is BL13 Fèishū?肺俞（BL13）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of T3, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 3 胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL13 needled?肺俞（BL13）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of BL13?肺俞（BL13）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Lung肺之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL13?肺俞（BL13）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only oblique insertion toward the spine, never a deep perpendicular or lateral direction.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向的斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
