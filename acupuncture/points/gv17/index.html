Where is GV17 Nǎohù?脑户（GV17）在哪里？

On the head, in the depression above the external occipital protuberance.头部，枕外隆凸上缘凹陷中。

How is GV17 needled?脑户（GV17）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of GV17?脑户（GV17）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Governor Vessel and Foot-Taiyang channel督脉、足太阳交会穴

Main safety point at GV17?脑户（GV17）最要注意的安全问题？

The occipital bone lies just beneath, so needle transversely in the subaponeurotic layer only; classically no moxibustion. The scalp bleeds easily, so press after withdrawal.头皮下即枕骨，只沿骨面平刺于腱膜下层；古籍记载禁灸。头皮血管丰富，出针按压防血肿。
