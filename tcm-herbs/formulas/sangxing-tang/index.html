Mulberry Leaf and Apricot Kernel Decoction桑杏汤

Sāng Xìng Tāng · Wu Tang, Wenbing Tiaobian (Qing)清·吴瑭《温病条辨》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Mulberry Leaf and Apricot Kernel Decoction桑杏汤
⚠️ Contains Ku Xing Ren (bitter apricot kernel): licensed prescription only含苦杏仁：只能由持照医师处方 Bitter apricot kernel contains amygdalin; too much releases cyanide, and children are especially at risk. This entry is for study only; do not make or take the formula yourself.苦杏仁含苦杏仁苷，过量会放出氢氰酸中毒，儿童尤其危险。本方只作学习，不要自行配服。

Original text原文

「秋感燥氣，右脈數大，傷手太陰氣分者，桑杏湯主之」——温病条辨

Wenbing Tiaobian (Systematic Differentiation of Warm Diseases)

When autumn dryness harms the lung qi level and the right pulse is rapid and big, this decoction governs.秋天感受燥气，右脉数而大，伤到手太阴气分的，用桑杏汤。

「桑葉（一錢）杏仁（一錢五分）沙參（二錢）象貝（一錢）香豉（一錢）梔皮（一錢）梨皮（一錢）」——温病条辨

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Clear and disperse warm dryness清宣温燥
  • moisten the lungs and stop cough润肺止咳

External warm dryness: slight fever, thirst, dry throat and nose, dry cough with little sticky phlegm and a red tongue with dry thin coating.外感温燥：身热不甚、口渴、咽干鼻燥、干咳无痰或痰少而黏、舌红苔薄白而干。

How the formula is built方解

The acrid-cool method, light enough to act on the lung's surface. Wu Tang insists light medicines must not be taken heavy or they overshoot. It is close to Sang Ju Yin but aims at moistening.辛凉法，轻清走肺卫。吴瑭强调轻药不可重用、重用则过病所。与桑菊饮相近，但重在润燥。

Cautions使用注意

Not for cool dryness or wind-cold cough; high fever, rapid breathing or chest pain need pneumonia excluded. This page is for study and is not a prescription; see a doctor first if symptoms are severe, persistent or getting worse.凉燥、风寒咳嗽不宜；发热高、呼吸急促、胸痛须就医（排除肺炎）。本页供学习，不是处方；症状重、持续或加重时先就医。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Caution in pregnancy (this site grades by the strictest ingredient); only a licensed practitioner can decide; ingredients concerned: Ku Xing Ren (bitter apricot kernel), Zhi Zi (gardenia fruit), Dan Dou Chi (prepared soybean).孕期慎用（本站按方内最严药味从严定级），是否可用由执业医师决定；涉及：苦杏仁、栀子、淡豆豉。

Exam notes考点

Exam points: light medicines light dose; versus Qing Zao Jiu Fei Tang.考点：轻药轻取；与清燥救肺汤轻重之别。
