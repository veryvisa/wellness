On the great toe, lateral to the distal phalanx, 0.1 F-cun proximal-lateral to the lateral corner of the toenail.足趾，大趾末节外侧，趾甲根角侧后方 0.1 寸（指寸）。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or seated with the foot relaxed and the tip and nail margin of the great toe clearly exposed.仰卧位或坐位，足部自然放松，充分暴露足大趾末端及趾甲缘。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First identify the lateral side of the great toe facing the second toe; locate the point about 0.1 cun (roughly one Chinese-chive leaf width) beside the intersection of the lateral nail border and the base-of-nail line.先辨认足大趾朝向第二趾的一侧（外侧），沿趾甲外侧缘与趾甲根部水平线交界处向旁开约 0.1 寸（约一韭菜叶宽）定穴。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Clinically, a jing-well point is placed about 0.1 cun beside the nail corner; finger cun serves only as a rough proportion reference, while the nail corner and distal phalanx margin set the exact mark on the small toe tip.临床井穴多按约 0.1 寸定位于甲角旁侧；指同身寸仅作比例参考，趾端细小结构须以趾甲角与末节趾骨缘为基准。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located near the lateral nail corner of the terminal phalanx of the great toe, with dorsal digital nerve endings and dorsal digital venous branches subcutaneously and plantar digital nerve branches plus the lateral margin of the distal phalanx immediately deep. Avoid the nail bed, nail root, and prominent superficial veins during insertion.位于足大趾末节外侧缘近甲角处，皮下有趾背神经末梢及趾背静脉分支，深层紧邻足底数字神经末梢与拇趾末节趾骨外侧缘。进针需避开甲床、趾甲根部及较粗的浅表静脉。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Lightly touch the soft tissue beside the lateral great-toe nail corner with the index or thumb pad to feel the thin subcutaneous cushion right over the distal phalanx; mild tenderness may appear in acute states or liver qi stagnation, while heavy pressure causes sharp pain from compressing periosteum and nerve endings.以示指或拇指腹轻触大趾外侧甲角旁软组织，可感知紧贴末节趾骨的薄层皮下垫；急性期或肝经气滞明显时局部可有轻度压痛，按压过重会因骨膜与神经末梢受压产生明显刺痛。

Where is LR1 Dàdūn?大敦（LR1）在哪里？

On the great toe, lateral to the distal phalanx, 0.1 F-cun proximal-lateral to the lateral corner of the toenail.足趾，大趾末节外侧，趾甲根角侧后方 0.1 寸（指寸）。

How is LR1 needled?大敦（LR1）怎么刺？

Shallow insertion, or prick to bleed; 0.1–0.2 cun.浅刺，或点刺出血；0.1–0.2 寸。

Special-point roles of LR1?大敦（LR1）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-well (wood)井穴（木）

Main safety point at LR1?大敦（LR1）最要注意的安全问题？

The toe tip has thin, very sensitive skin with the distal phalanx immediately beneath: stay shallow and avoid the nail bed; disinfect carefully before pricking to bleed, and take care in diabetic feet or peripheral vascular disease.趾端皮薄、痛觉敏感，针下即是末节趾骨：浅刺即止，避开甲床；点刺出血前严格消毒，糖尿病足、周围血管病患者慎刺。
