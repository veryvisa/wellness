BL40

委中 Weizhong

Middle of the Bend · High risk高危

★He-sea (earth); lower he-sea of the Bladder合穴（土）；膀胱下合穴One of the Four Command Points: for the low back seek BL40四总穴之一，腰背委中求
⚠️ Popliteal neurovascular-risk point腘窝神经血管风险穴 The tibial nerve, popliteal vein, and popliteal artery can enter the needle path. Only trained personnel should needle with popliteal anatomy in mind.胫神经、腘静脉和腘动脉可进入针路。仅由受训人员依据腘窝解剖控制进针。

📍 Location定位★

At the midpoint of the popliteal crease.腘横纹中央。

💡 With the knee slightly flexed and the popliteal fossa relaxed, locate at the midpoint of the crease.膝轻屈、腘窝放松时，在腘横纹正中取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

Perpendicular 1–1.5 cun, or prick the visible superficial popliteal veins to bleed; never the pulsating centre.直刺 1–1.5 寸，或用三棱针点刺腘窝浅表显露的静脉出血；不刺腘窝中央搏动处。

🛡 Safety安全★

The tibial nerve and popliteal vein and artery lie deep (the classics warn that striking the great vessel here makes the patient collapse). No bloodletting with bleeding disorders or anticoagulants, anaemia or frailty, pregnancy, local infection or venous thrombosis. Stop and seek care for electric pain, persistent numbness or significant bleeding.胫神经、腘静脉、腘动脉在深部（古人已警告「刺郄中大脉，令人仆脱色」）。刺络放血禁用于凝血障碍或服抗凝药、贫血体虚、孕妇、局部感染或静脉血栓者。出现电击样放射痛、持续麻木或明显出血应停止并就医。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Low-back pain and leg weakness or numbness腰背痛、下肢痿痹 ⚖️Abdominal pain, acute vomiting and diarrhoea腹痛、急性吐泻 ⚖️Difficult urination and enuresis小便不利、遗尿Erysipelas, urticaria, itching丹毒、瘾疹、皮肤瘙痒 ⚖️

🔗 Pairings配伍

  • With BL23 and BL25 for low-back pain (textbook)配肾俞、大肠俞：腰痛（教材）
  • With PC3, pricked to bleed, for heatstroke and acute vomiting-diarrhoea配曲泽点刺出血：中暑、急性吐泻

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    With the knee slightly flexed, find the central popliteal hollow between the two gastrocnemius heads; BL40 is at the midpoint of the crease.膝轻屈，在腘横纹中央找到双侧腓肠肌头之间的纵向凹陷；委中在横纹正中。

  2. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Popliteal crease (level with the patellar apex) to the lateral malleolus = 16 cun; BL40 sits at its start, and BL55 (2 cun), BL56 (5 cun) and BL58 (7 cun above the malleolus) are measured on it.腘横纹（平髌尖）到外踝尖折作 16 寸，委中就在这把尺的起点；合阳（下 2 寸）、承筋（下 5 寸）、飞扬（外踝上 7 寸）都从这里量。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Posterior femoral cutaneous branches are superficial; deeply in the central fossa lie the tibial nerve, popliteal vein and popliteal artery, with gastrocnemius heads on either side. Do not advance blindly toward deep structures or a visible pulse.浅层为股后皮神经分支，腘窝中央深部依次邻近胫神经、腘静脉和腘动脉；腓肠肌内外侧头在中央凹陷两侧。针尖不可朝深部或任何血管搏动处盲刺。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the knee flexed, palpate across the central crease for a shallow hollow and posterior fascia; withdraw if electric tingling radiates downward or into the sole.膝轻屈后横向按腘横纹中央，可感浅凹及一条后侧肌筋膜；若向下或足底出现电击麻感应退针并改为浅刺。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting laterally: BL39 lies beside the biceps tendon; BL40 is the central point.偏到腘窝外侧：委阳在股二头肌腱内侧，委中是正中点。
  • Locating with the knee straight narrows the crease and makes deep structures harder to assess.膝伸直取穴：腘横纹变窄，深部结构相对位置更难辨，且肌肉紧张。
  • Perpendicular deep insertion risks the tibial nerve and popliteal vein or artery.朝深部直刺：胫神经、腘静脉、腘动脉在针路风险区，不宜盲目深刺。

Main safety point at BL40?委中（BL40）最要注意的安全问题？

The tibial nerve and popliteal vein and artery lie deep (the classics warn that striking the great vessel here makes the patient collapse). No bloodletting with bleeding disorders or anticoagulants, anaemia or frailty, pregnancy, local infection or venous thrombosis. Stop and seek care for electric pain, persistent numbness or significant bleeding.胫神经、腘静脉、腘动脉在深部（古人已警告「刺郄中大脉，令人仆脱色」）。刺络放血禁用于凝血障碍或服抗凝药、贫血体虚、孕妇、局部感染或静脉血栓者。出现电击样放射痛、持续麻木或明显出血应停止并就医。
