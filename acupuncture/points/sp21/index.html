  • Confusing the posterior axillary line with the midaxillary line shifts the point toward the anterior border of latissimus dorsi and places it too far posteriorly, near the thoracodorsal junction.把腋后线误认为腋中线：取穴偏向背阔肌前缘，位置过于靠后，容易把大包误定位到胸背交界区域。
  • Incorrect rib counting places the point in the fifth or seventh intercostal space instead of the sixth. Nipple height, the inframammary fold, and chest shape vary considerably between individuals and are unreliable substitutes for direct rib identification.肋骨计数错误：将第5肋间隙或第7肋间隙误认为第6肋间隙，造成上下偏移。乳头高度、乳房下缘和胸廓外形存在明显个体差异，不能作为可靠的第6肋间隙标志。应逐条触摸肋骨确认。

  • Perpendicular or deep medial insertion is particularly dangerous because the pleura and lung lie beneath the chest wall, which may be thin. Advancing vertically or toward the thoracic cavity can penetrate the intercostal muscles and pleura and cause pneumothorax. ⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

SP21 may be considered as a traditional supplementary point when coughing accompanies chest and hypochondriac fullness, chest-wall discomfort, and generalized aching, but it is not a routine primary point for cough. Compare it with LU7, LU5, and BL13 for external cough and select the appropriate point combination according to the pattern, such as phlegm-heat, Lung yin deficiency, or Lung qi deficiency. Cough accompanied by chest pain, hemoptysis, shortness of breath, or persistent fever requires investigation for pulmonary disease before acupuncture is considered.若咳嗽同时伴胸胁胀满、胸壁不适及全身酸痛，可将大包作为传统辨证配穴考虑，但它不是咳嗽的常规首选主穴。外感咳嗽常与列缺LU7、尺泽LU5、肺俞BL13等比较；痰热、肺阴虚或肺气虚应按证型选择相应穴组。若咳嗽伴胸痛、咯血、气短或持续发热，应先排查肺部疾病，不可因胸胁压痛而直接针刺大包。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

SP21 may be considered in a traditional pattern involving wheezing and chest or hypochondriac fullness, but it must not be used as treatment for an acute asthma attack. Compare it with EX-B1, BL13, CV17, and PC6: the former points are used in respiratory pattern differentiation, whereas CV17 and PC6 emphasize regulation of chest qi. Acute wheezing, inability to speak in full sentences, cyanosis, or difficulty breathing requires immediate use of the prescribed emergency plan and a call to 911. Do not wait for acupuncture to work.若喘息同时伴胸胁胀满，可从传统胸胁气机失调角度考虑大包配伍，但不应将大包作为哮喘急性发作的处理穴。与定喘EX-B1、肺俞BL13、膻中CV17、内关PC6比较，前者偏向喘息局部及肺系辨证，后者偏向胸中气机调节。急性喘息、不能完整说话、发绀或吸气困难，应立即使用既定急救方案并拨打911，不能等待针刺起效。

Coughing up blood咯血 ⚖️

Hemoptysis is not an indication for routinely selecting SP21. Even when chest-wall tenderness is present, the source of bleeding must first be established and infection, bronchiectasis, tuberculosis, lung malignancy, and pulmonary embolism considered. Compared with LU6, LU5, and LU10, which appear in traditional Lung-channel treatment combinations for bleeding, SP21 is more closely associated with the traditional theory of chest-wall collaterals. It cannot replace investigation of the cause or modern hemostatic treatment. Large-volume hemoptysis or bleeding accompanied by breathing difficulty, dizziness, or hypotension requires emergency care.咯血不是选择大包进行常规治疗的理由。即使患者同时存在胸胁压痛，也必须先明确咯血来源，排查感染、支气管扩张、肺结核、肺肿瘤及肺栓塞等。与孔最LU6、尺泽LU5、鱼际LU10等传统肺经止血配穴相比，大包更偏向胸胁局部络脉理论，不能替代病因检查和现代止血处理。大量咯血或伴呼吸困难、头晕、低血压时立即急诊。

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

SP21 may be considered as a traditional local supplementary point when shoulder pain accompanies discomfort near the midaxillary chest wall, tenderness over serratus anterior, or pain during scapular protraction, once a musculoskeletal cause has been established. If pain is primarily around the shoulder joint, with restricted abduction or pain when lying on the affected side, compare LI15, TE14, SI9, and local ashi points around the scapula. SP21 is not a routine primary point for shoulder-joint disorders. Shoulder pain accompanied by chest tightness, cold sweating, or exertional chest discomfort requires cardiac assessment first.若肩痛伴腋中线附近胸胁不适、前锯肌区域压痛或肩胛前伸时疼痛，可在明确肌肉骨骼病因后考虑大包的传统局部配穴意义。若疼痛主要位于肩关节周围、外展受限或夜间侧卧痛，应优先比较肩髃LI15、肩髎TE14、肩贞SI9及肩胛周围阿是穴。大包不是肩关节疾病的常规首选穴；肩痛伴胸闷、冷汗或活动后胸部不适时，应先排除心血管疾病。

Low mood and anxiety郁证（焦虑抑郁）

When emotional distress accompanies chest and hypochondriac fullness or oppression, SP21 may be considered within traditional qi-pattern differentiation alongside PC6, CV17, and LR3. However, SP21 is not a standard primary point for anxiety or depression, and chest discomfort alone does not establish a diagnosis of Liver qi stagnation or Spleen deficiency. Compared with HT7 and PC6, which are commonly used to regulate the mind, SP21 primarily serves a traditional local chest-wall and Great Luo function. Suicidal thoughts, self-harm risk, or severe psychiatric symptoms require professional mental health assessment first.若情志不畅伴胸胁胀满、胸中痞闷，可在传统气机辨证中考虑大包与内关PC6、膻中CV17、太冲LR3等配伍。但大包并非焦虑抑郁的标准主穴，不能仅因患者诉胸口不舒就认定属于肝郁或脾虚。与神门HT7、内关PC6等调神穴相比，大包主要承担胸胁局部及脾之大络的传统配穴作用。若存在自伤、自杀念头或严重精神症状，应优先进行专业精神卫生评估。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Intercostal neuralgia肋间神经痛（Intercostal neuralgia）

Intercostal neuralgia commonly presents as band-like pain following an intercostal nerve, with burning, stabbing, electric-shock-like sensations, or altered skin sensitivity. Coughing, deep breathing, and trunk rotation may aggravate symptoms. Lateral cutaneous branches of the intercostal nerves are present near SP21, so local tenderness may overlap with neuropathic pain. Assess whether the pain follows a rib distribution and whether numbness or allodynia is present. Consider herpes zoster, thoracic radiculopathy, and intrathoracic causes. Persistent radiating pain must not be mistaken for normal acupuncture sensation.肋间神经痛常表现为沿肋间走行的带状疼痛，可有烧灼、针刺、电击样疼痛或皮肤感觉异常，咳嗽、深呼吸、转身时可能加重。SP21附近存在肋间神经外侧皮支，因此局部疼痛可能与神经性疼痛重叠。应检查疼痛是否沿肋骨分布、是否伴麻木或感觉过敏，并排查带状疱疹、胸椎神经根病变及胸腔内病因。不能把持续性放射痛简单当作针刺得气。

Chest-wall myofascial pain and serratus anterior strain胸壁肌筋膜疼痛及前锯肌劳损

Serratus anterior lies deep to the SP21 region. Repeated pushing, pulling, overhead lifting, throwing, or carrying loads may cause muscle strain or myofascial pain. Symptoms often include localized tenderness near the midaxillary line, aggravated by scapular protraction, arm elevation, or resisted wall-pushing. Differentiate this condition from intercostal neuralgia and rib injury. Pain accompanied by significant shortness of breath, chest pressure, or systemic symptoms must not be treated as routine muscular strain.前锯肌位于大包区域深层，反复推、拉、举臂、投掷或负重可能造成肌肉劳损及肌筋膜疼痛。常见表现为腋中线附近局部压痛，肩胛前伸、上举或抵抗性推墙动作可能诱发疼痛。应与肋间神经痛及肋骨损伤鉴别。若疼痛伴明显呼吸困难、胸部压迫感或全身症状，不能按普通肌肉劳损处理。

Rib fracture and chest-wall contusion肋骨骨折及胸壁挫伤

Falls, impacts, motor vehicle collisions, and sports injuries can cause rib tenderness, swelling, and pain during deep breathing or coughing. A fracture may occur without visible deformity but still produce marked focal tenderness. Obtain a trauma history, assess breathing and chest-wall movement, and arrange imaging when indicated. Rib fractures may be complicated by pneumothorax, hemothorax, or pulmonary contusion. Do not needle or apply forceful pressure near an injured area.跌倒、撞击、交通事故或运动损伤后，局部可出现肋骨压痛、肿胀及深呼吸、咳嗽时疼痛。骨折患者有时没有明显畸形，但局部压痛十分明确。应询问外伤史，检查呼吸和胸廓活动，必要时进行影像学检查。肋骨骨折可能合并气胸、血胸或肺挫伤，受伤部位附近禁止自行针刺或强力按压。

Pleuritis can cause unilateral chest pain that worsens with inspiration, coughing, or sneezing and may be perceived along the lateral chest. Pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, and pulmonary embolism may also present with chest pain or breathing discomfort. Tenderness near SP21 does not exclude intrathoracic disease. Pain clearly related to breathing, especially when accompanied by fever, cough, hypoxia, or tachycardia, requires medical assessment first.胸膜炎可表现为单侧胸痛，吸气、咳嗽或打喷嚏时加重，疼痛可能投射到胸胁侧部。肺炎、胸腔积液、气胸及肺栓塞等疾病也可能表现为胸痛或呼吸不适。大包附近的压痛不能排除胸腔疾病。若疼痛与呼吸明显相关，或伴发热、咳嗽、低氧、心动过速等，应优先进行医学检查。

Referred pain from cardiovascular disease心血管疾病引起的牵涉痛

Myocardial ischemia and other cardiovascular disorders may cause chest pressure, tightness, or discomfort, sometimes radiating to the shoulder, back, arm, neck, or upper abdomen. Women, older adults, and people with diabetes may have atypical presentations. Local tenderness in the hypochondriac region does not rule out cardiac disease. New chest pressure, exertional chest pain, or symptoms accompanied by cold sweating, nausea, or shortness of breath require immediate emergency assessment.心肌缺血及其他心血管疾病可表现为胸部压迫、紧缩或不适，部分患者疼痛可向肩背、上肢、颈部或上腹部放射。女性、老年人及糖尿病患者可能表现不典型。胸胁侧部疼痛即使有局部压痛，也不能据此排除心脏疾病。新发胸部压迫感、活动诱发胸痛或伴冷汗、恶心、呼吸困难时，应立即进行急诊评估。
