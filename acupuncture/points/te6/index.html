    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently extend the wrist and identify the posterior forearm extensors, then palpate the interval between the bones. ⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Constipation便秘 ⚖️

Consider TE6 for constipation with qi constraint, abdominal distension, and Shaoyang signs; dry stools with fluid deficiency need a different pattern-based plan, while pain, vomiting, or inability to pass gas or stool is urgent.便秘兼气滞腹胀、少阳经不舒时可考虑支沟；干结伴阴液不足须辨证配伍，腹痛呕吐、停止排气排便先急诊。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

May be a channel-based distal point for pain between the posterior forearm bones; focal elbow-joint pain should not be treated as though TE6 replaces local pattern-based points such as TE10.前臂背侧两骨间疼痛可作循经远端穴；肘关节局部疼痛不应以支沟代替天井等局部辨证取穴。
