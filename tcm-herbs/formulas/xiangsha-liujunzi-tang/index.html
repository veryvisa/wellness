Original text原文

「加木香、砂仁行氣溫中，名香砂六君子湯。」——汤头歌诀

Tangtou Gejue (Formula Verses)

Adding Muxiang and Sharen to Liujunzi Tang (Renshen, Baizhu, Fuling, Gancao, Chenpi, and Banxia) moves qi and warms the middle, naming the formula Xiangsha Liujunzi Tang.在六君子汤（人参、白术、茯苓、甘草、陈皮、半夏）的基础上，加木香、砂仁以行气止痛、温中和胃，命名为香砂六君子汤。

Cautions使用注意

Not appropriate for stomach pain or vomiting caused by yin deficiency with dry heat, a red tongue with scant fluid, or internal excess heat; seek prompt medical evaluation to rule out gastrointestinal ulcers or organic disease if severe abdominal pain, hematemesis, melena, or rapid unexplained weight loss occurs.阴虚燥热、舌红少津或实热内积引起的胃痛呕吐不宜使用；若出现剧烈腹痛、呕血、黑便或体重短期明显下降，须及时就医检查以排除消化道溃疡及器质性病变。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Banxia carries moderate toxicity and a pregnancy caution in both the textbook and this site, while Renshen, Gancao, and Muxiang also carry a pregnancy caution on this site; the formula follows the strictest level of its herbs.方中半夏具中等毒性且在教材与本站均列孕期慎用，人参、甘草、木香在本站亦按孕期慎用管理，方随其最严者定为孕期慎用。
