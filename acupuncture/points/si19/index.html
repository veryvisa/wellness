📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the head neutral; slowly open the mouth so the mandibular condyle glides forward and reveals the pretragal hollow.坐位或仰卧，头正中，缓慢张口，使下颌髁突向前滑动并显出耳屏前凹陷。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Identify the area just anterior to the tragus and posterior to the mandibular condyle. A hollow appears between them on opening the mouth; SI19 lies there and can be rechecked as the condyle returns on closing.找到耳屏前方及下颌髁突后缘，张口时两者之间出现凹陷；听宫位于该凹陷中，闭口后髁突回位可再次核对。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With the mouth open, palpate the condyle moving forward and confirm the hollow anterior to the tragus and behind it. Superficial temporal arterial branches, the auriculotemporal nerve, and TMJ capsule are nearby; avoid deep pressure into the joint, visible pulses, or repeated forced opening of a painful jaw.张口时触摸髁突前移，在耳屏前、髁突后方的关节间凹陷确认。颞浅动脉分支、耳颞神经和颞下颌关节囊邻近；避免朝关节腔深压、避开可见搏动，并勿在疼痛关节上强行反复张口。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Alternate gentle opening and closing to feel the condyle's movement; the hollow becomes clearer on opening and shallower on closing. Use minimal movement if there is clicking or pain, and do not treat joint tenderness itself as the sole locating criterion.张口、闭口交替触诊髁突的移动边界；凹陷应在张口时清楚，闭口时变浅。若出现关节弹响或疼痛，应以轻柔动作定位，不把关节压痛本身误作取穴依据。

Where is SI19 Tīnggōng?听宫（SI19）在哪里？

On the face, in the depression between the middle of the tragus and the condylar process of the mandible.面部，耳屏正中与下颌骨髁突之间的凹陷中。

How is SI19 needled?听宫（SI19）怎么刺？

Mouth open, perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.张口，直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SI19?听宫（SI19）是哪类特定穴？

Meeting point of Hand- and Foot-Shaoyang with Hand-Taiyang手足少阳、手太阳交会穴

Main safety point at SI19?听宫（SI19）最要注意的安全问题？

Insert with the mouth open and keep it open during retention, or the returning condyle squeezes the needle; avoid the superficial temporal pulse. Sudden hearing loss needs medical assessment first.必须张口进针、留针期间不闭口，否则髁突回位会挤压针身；避开颞浅动脉搏动。突发听力下降须先就医。
