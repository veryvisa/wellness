  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Superior border of the pubic symphysis to the base of the patella = 18 cun.   2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find ST30 (level with the symphysis, 2 cun lateral) and measure 2 cun down, at the lateral edge of adductor longus; or find LR10 (3 cun below ST30)—LR11 is 1 cun above it.先定气冲（耻骨联合上缘水平、旁开 2 寸），向下量 2 寸，在长收肌外缘取穴；也可先定足五里（气冲下 3 寸），阴廉在其上方 1 寸。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With the hip flexed and abducted, adductor longus stands out; LR11 is at its lateral edge, 2 cun below ST30. Palpate and avoid the pulse, insert perpendicularly 0.8–1 cun, never deep superolaterally.屈髋外展时长收肌在大腿根内侧绷起，阴廉在其外缘、气冲直下 2 寸。皮下有生殖股神经、闭孔神经皮支与大隐静脉属支；深层为长收肌、短收肌；外侧深处为股动脉、股静脉。进针前先触诊搏动并避开，直刺 0.8–1 寸，不向外上方深刺。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a finger along the lateral edge of adductor longus to identify the deep femoral pulse; tingling down the front-inner thigh means a nerve branch—adjust depth and direction.指腹在长收肌外缘轻按滑动，辨认深部股动脉搏动；若按压出现向大腿前内侧放射的麻感，提示触及神经分支，应调整深度与方向。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Deep superolateral insertion: LR11 is near the groin with the femoral artery and vein deep and lateral; angling that way can puncture them and cause a deep haematoma.向外上方深刺误伤股血管：阴廉靠近腹股沟，外侧深处是股动脉、股静脉，向外上深刺易刺破大血管致深部血肿。
  • Mistaking LR10 or ST30: 1 cun lower is LR10, 2 cun higher (level with the symphysis) is ST30—check the vertical distance.误取到足五里（LR10）或气冲（ST30）：向下 1 寸即足五里，向上 2 寸（耻骨联合上缘水平）即气冲，需核对纵向距离。
  • Inserting without avoiding superficial veins or arterial pulsation: superficial veins are abundant near the groin and proximal medial thigh, causing bruising if punctured, while arterial pulsation carries high bleeding risk.未避开浅静脉或动脉搏动直接进针：腹股沟及大腿内侧近端浅静脉较丰富，针后易皮下瘀血，动脉搏动区误刺风险高。

Medial thigh muscle strain or adductor/sartorius overuse大腿内侧肌肉拉伤或内收肌群/缝匠肌劳损

Often presents with proximal or medial thigh ache after sudden stops, direction changes, or lunges, provoked by resisted adduction or hip flexion; if accompanied by a distinct tear sensation, extensive bruising, or marked movement loss, evaluate muscle/tendon injury first and avoid forceful stretching.常在急停、变向或跨步动作后出现大腿根部及内侧酸痛，抗阻内收或屈髋诱发；若伴明显撕裂感、皮下大面积瘀青或活动受限，先评估肌腱/肌肉损伤程度并休息，避免剧烈牵拉。
