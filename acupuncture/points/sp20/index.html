SP20

All-round Flourishing · High risk高危

⚠️ Pneumothorax risk point气胸风险穴 SP20 lies in the 2nd intercostal space 6 cun lateral where the chest wall is thin; pleura and lung are just beyond the intercostal muscles. Insert only obliquely or transversely outward 0.5–0.8 cun—never perpendicular, deep medially or with wide lifting-thrusting. Chest pain, breathlessness, worsening cough or stabbing pain on breathing after needling needs urgent assessment for pneumothorax.周荣在第 2 肋间隙旁开 6 寸，胸壁薄，肋间肌后即胸膜与肺。只斜刺或向外平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不直刺、不向内深刺、不大幅提插。针后出现胸痛、气短、咳嗽加重或呼吸时刺痛，立即就医排查气胸。

📍 Location定位★

On the lateral chest, in the 2nd intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 2 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse outward斜刺或向外平刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never needle deeply.不可深刺。

🛡 Safety安全

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Perpendicular or medial oblique insertion toward the thoracic cavity: the chest wall at the second intercostal space is thin, with the parietal pleura and lung directly beneath; deep medial needling easily punctures the lung and causes traumatic pneumothorax. Used only as an adjacent supportive point when cough and wheezing accompany lateral chest fullness; not a primary point for external or internal cough, where BL13, LU7, or LU9 lead.仅作为胸胁胀痛伴咳嗽气喘时的局部辅助配穴，偏于宽胸理气；外感或内伤咳嗽主症不首选，应选肺俞（BL13）、列缺（LU7）或太渊（LU9）。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Supports chest opening when phlegm and qi stagnate in the lateral chest, but cannot replace primary anti-wheezing points like EX-B1, CV17, or BL13; acute severe asthma requires immediate medical emergency care.用于痰气壅滞胸胁、呼吸满闷时的辅助宽胸，不能替代定喘、膻中（CV17）、肺俞（BL13）等平喘主穴；急性重症哮喘发作须先规范医疗急救。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Second intercostal neuralgia or referred costochondritis pain第 2 肋间神经痛或肋软骨炎牵涉痛

Band-like stabbing or burning pain along the second intercostal space worsened by coughing, deep breathing, or trunk rotation, with localized intercostal tenderness. Differentiate from prodromal herpes zoster (vesicles appear days later) and cardiopulmonary emergencies; sudden crushing chest pain with diaphoresis, dyspnea, or radiation to the left arm requires immediate emergency care.沿第 2 肋间隙呈条带状刺痛或灼痛，咳嗽、深呼吸或转身时加重，局部肋间隙压痛明显。需与带状疱疹前驱痛（数日后出现簇集水疱）及心肺急症鉴别；突发压榨性胸痛伴出汗、气促或向左臂放射必须立即急诊就医。

Myofascial pain of the lateral pectoralis major and upper serratus anterior胸大肌外侧缘及前锯肌上部肌筋膜痛（投掷、推举或搬运劳损）

Common after bench pressing, swimming, tennis serves, or repetitive pushing; a localized trigger point appears at the lateral second intercostal space, reproduced by resisted shoulder adduction or protraction. Never needle perpendicularly into deep taut bands — insert transversely within the muscle layer.常见于卧推、游泳、网球发球或重复推拉动作后，第 2 肋间隙外侧出现局限性激痛点，抗阻内收或前伸肩关节时疼痛再现。治疗时切勿因触及深部条索而垂直深扎，必须沿肌层平刺。

Referred pain from upper chest-wall and thoracic outlet soft-tissue tension胸廓出口区域软组织紧张牵涉痛

Chronic shortening of pectoralis minor and subclavius in rounded-shoulder posture often causes aching tension between SP20 and LU1. If accompanied by upper-limb numbness, coldness, or diminished radial pulse, refer for neurovascular evaluation first.圆肩驼背人群胸小肌及锁骨下肌群长期短缩，常在周荣至中府一带出现酸胀牵扯感；若伴随患侧上肢麻木、发凉或桡动脉搏动减弱，需先转诊评估神经血管受压情况。

🔬 Research现代研究

  • E3 There are currently no randomized controlled trials evaluating SP20 in isolation. Modern anatomical and acupuncture safety literature consistently notes that the skin-to-parietal-pleura depth at the 2nd intercostal space 6 cun lateral to the midline is shallow (often under 1.5 cm in thin patients), making lateral oblique or transverse insertion essential to prevent iatrogenic pneumothorax.目前缺乏针对周荣（SP20）单穴的随机对照临床试验；现代解剖与针刺安全研究一致指出，胸壁旁开 6 寸第 2 肋间隙处皮下至壁胸膜距离较浅（瘦弱者常不足 1.5 cm），向外斜刺或平刺是预防医源性气胸的关键操作规范。

Where is SP20 Zhōuróng?周荣（SP20）在哪里？

On the lateral chest, in the 2nd intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 2 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

How is SP20 needled?周荣（SP20）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse outward; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺或向外平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at SP20?周荣（SP20）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung lie just deep to the intercostal muscles and the chest wall is thin here: insert only obliquely or transversely outward, never perpendicularly or deep medially—risk of pneumothorax; avoid the intercostal vessels and nerve.肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺，此处胸壁薄：只斜刺或向外平刺，不直刺、不向内深刺，有气胸风险；避开肋间血管神经。
