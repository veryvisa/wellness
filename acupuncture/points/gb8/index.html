📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or side-lying with the affected side up, head neutral and relaxed, and the auricle naturally exposed.坐位或侧卧位，患侧朝上，头部正中、自然放松，耳廓向侧方自然展开。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Fold the auricle forward to find its highest point (the apex of the auricle), then measure 1.5 cun directly superior into the hairline; because hairline height varies, confirm with proportional landmarks and palpation during chewing.将耳廓向前折曲，耳轮最高点（耳尖）正上方入发际 1.5 寸处；注意「耳尖直上」是指头皮表面垂线向上，受头发疏密影响大，不能仅凭发际线高低判断，需结合骨度与咀嚼触诊复核。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Place the patient's index and middle fingers held together (about 1.5 cun) horizontally directly above the apex of the auricle from the hairline; the upper border marks the level of GB8, though finger measurement is only a rough guide to be confirmed by muscle palpation.以患者本人食指、中指并拢的宽度（约 1.5 寸），横置于耳尖正上方，从发际缘向上量取，指腹上缘即率谷所在水平；指宽仅为粗略参考，需配合颞肌收缩凹陷定准。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located in the temporal region over the squamous part of the temporal bone, covered by the temporalis muscle and epicranial aponeurosis. Ask the patient to clench and relax the jaw repeatedly; palpate the bulging temporalis fibers about 1.5 cun directly above the auricular apex to find the slight depression at the muscle belly. Before needling, palpate the parietal branch of the superficial temporal artery and accompanying auriculotemporal nerve, steering clear of visible or palpable pulses during transverse scalp insertion and avoiding perpendicular deep needling onto the periosteum.位于颞区、颞骨鳞部外侧面，浅层覆盖颞肌与帽状腱膜。嘱患者反复咬紧牙关（咀嚼动作），指腹在耳尖直上约 1.5 寸处可触及颞肌纤维隆起，其肌腹边缘微凹处即是率谷。进针前先触清颞浅动脉顶支（常在耳前上方皮下搏动）及伴行的耳颞神经，沿头皮平刺时避开明显血管搏动点，切忌垂直深刺触及颞骨骨膜。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate transversely about 1.5 cun directly above the apex of the auricle; patients with migraine often exhibit a tender cord-like nodule here whose soreness radiates toward the ipsilateral temple or periauricular area, confirming the exact point.在耳尖直上约 1.5 寸范围指腹横向按揉，偏头痛患者此处常有明显条索状结节或局限性压痛（阿是反应），按压时酸胀感可向同侧太阳穴或耳周放散，即为准穴。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting too far anteriorly and inferiorly (toward the zygomatic arch in front of the ear): likely to puncture the superficial temporal artery trunk or parietal branch and the auriculotemporal nerve, causing a local scalp hematoma, brisk bleeding, or sharp radiating pain.取穴偏前下方（靠近耳前颧弓上方）：易刺中颞浅动脉主干或顶支及耳颞神经，引起头皮局部血肿、明显出血或向耳颞部放射的刺痛。
  • Needling perpendicularly too deeply (beyond 0.5 cun): scalp soft tissue is thin, so perpendicular insertion hits the temporal periosteum, causing severe pain or a bent needle; standard technique is transverse insertion along the scalp to 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺过深（超过 0.5 寸）：头皮软组织较薄，直刺易触及颞骨骨膜引发剧痛，甚至造成弯针、滞针；教材规范为沿头皮平刺 0.3–0.5 寸。
  • Measuring 1.5 cun blindly from a receding hairline: in patients with frontal or temporal hair loss, measuring from the current hairline places the point far too superiorly, off the Gallbladder channel.仅凭发际线高低定 1.5 寸：发际后移或脱发患者若盲目从当前发际量 1.5 寸，会导致取穴严重偏高（接近头顶），偏离胆经循行。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

First-choice local point for unilateral throbbing Shaoyang headache in the temporal and supra-auricular region; use EX-HN3 or GB14 for frontal Yangming headache, and GV20 or EX-HN1 for vertex Jueyin headache.少阳经侧头部（颞部、耳上）搏动性偏头痛的首选局部穴；前额阳明头痛选印堂、阳白，巅顶厥阴头痛选百会、四神聪。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Select GB8 when dizziness accompanies temporal migraine and tinnitus due to ascendant liver yang or Shaoyang wind-heat; choose GV20 and ST36 for qi-blood deficiency dizziness, or ST40 and CV12 for phlegm-dampness.肝阳上亢或少阳风热上扰引起的眩晕伴偏头痛、耳鸣时选率谷；气血亏虚型头晕乏力首选百会、足三里，痰湿中阻眩晕选丰隆、中脘。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Migraine (with or without aura)偏头痛（无先兆或有先兆偏头痛）

Typically presents as unilateral throbbing headache with nausea, photophobia, and phonophobia, often with marked tenderness near GB8; first-ever severe attacks or changed headache patterns require medical evaluation first.典型表现为单侧搏动性头痛，常伴恶心、畏光、畏声，率谷附近常有明显压痛；首次剧烈发作或形态突变时需先就医排除颅内病变。

Auriculotemporal neuralgia and temporalis myofascial pain (often with TMD)耳颞神经痛与颞肌肌筋膜疼痛综合征（常伴颞下颌关节紊乱 TMD）

Bruxism and jaw clenching strain the temporalis muscle right beneath GB8, causing pain radiating to the temple or jaw; differentiate from trigeminal neuralgia and giant cell arteritis.夜间磨牙、紧咬牙关者易出现颞肌劳损，痛点正位于率谷附近，按压痛点可向太阳穴或下颌放射；需与三叉神经痛及颞动脉炎鉴别。

Giant cell arteritis (temporal arteritis) — critical differential巨细胞动脉炎（颞动脉炎）——高危鉴别

Occurs mainly in adults over 50 with new persistent temporal headache, scalp tenderness, jaw claudication, and a thickened or diminished superficial temporal pulse; if visual symptoms appear, it is an ophthalmic emergency — do not needle or massage, and refer immediately.多见于 50 岁以上人群，表现为新发颞部持续胀痛、头皮触痛（梳头痛）、咀嚼时下颌乏力疼痛（颌跛行），局部颞浅动脉增粗搏动减弱，伴视力模糊时属眼科急症，严禁直接针刺按揉，必须立即就医查血沉与 C 反应蛋白。
