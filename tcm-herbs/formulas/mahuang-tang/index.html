Ephedra Decoction麻黄汤

Máhuáng Tāng · Zhang Zhongjing, Shanghan Lun (Eastern Han), Taiyang disease, part two东汉·张仲景《伤寒论》太阳病中篇

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Ephedra Decoction麻黄汤
⚠️ A strong sweating formula: only for robust people with wind-cold and no sweating峻汗之剂：只用于身体壮实、无汗的风寒 Ma Huang Tang sweats strongly and contains ephedrine (raises blood pressure and heart rate) and bitter apricot kernel (amygdalin; cyanide poisoning in overdose). People with high blood pressure or heart disease, the weak or sweaty, the elderly, pregnant women and children should not use it on their own; stop once sweating comes and do not keep taking it.麻黄汤发汗力强，含麻黄碱（升压、加快心率）与苦杏仁（含苦杏仁苷，过量可致氰化物中毒）。高血压、心脏病、体虚多汗、老人、孕妇、儿童不要自行使用；汗出即停，不可连服。

Original text原文

「太陽病，頭痛，發熱，身疼，腰痛，骨節疼痛，惡風，無汗而喘者，麻黃湯主之」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Taiyang disease with headache, fever, body aches, low-back pain, joint pain, dislike of wind, no sweating and wheezing: Ma Huang Tang governs it. Later teachers called these 'the eight Ma Huang signs'.太阳病：头痛、发热、全身疼、腰痛、关节痛，怕风，不出汗而且喘——用麻黄汤。后世归纳为「麻黄八症」。

「麻黃三兩（去節）桂枝二兩（去皮）甘草一兩（炙）杏仁七十箇（去皮尖）」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Ingredients and original doses.组成与原书剂量。

「先煮麻黃，減二升，去上沫；內諸藥；煮取二升半，去滓，溫服八合。覆取微似汗，不須啜粥」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Boil Ma Huang first and skim the froth, then add the rest; take it warm and stay covered for a light sweat. Unlike Gui Zhi Tang no porridge is needed, because Ma Huang sweats strongly on its own.先煮麻黄并撇去浮沫，再下其他药；温服后盖被取微汗，不必像桂枝汤那样喝粥助汗——因为麻黄本身发汗力强。

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Induces sweating and releases the exterior发汗解表
  • Diffuses the lungs and calms wheezing宣肺平喘

Wind-cold exterior excess: chills and fever, head and body aches, no sweating with wheezing, thin white coating, a floating tight pulse.外感风寒表实证：恶寒发热，头身疼痛，无汗而喘，苔薄白，脉浮紧。

How the formula is built方解

'No sweating' is the key: cold binds the surface and closes the pores, so there is no sweat and the whole body aches; the lungs govern the skin, so when the surface is shut lung qi cannot spread and the person wheezes. Ma Huang opens the surface and diffuses the lungs; Gui Zhi warms and adds to the sweat, and together they make a strong diaphoretic. Xing Ren lowers lung qi against Ma Huang's diffusing; honey-fried licorice tempers the force. Zhang listed a whole string of people who must not be sweated (dry throat, chronic urinary dribbling, sores, nosebleeds, blood loss, habitual sweating), which shows he treated it as a risky medicine.「无汗」是用方的钥匙：寒邪束表、毛孔闭塞，所以不出汗、全身痛；肺合皮毛，表闭则肺气不宣而喘。麻黄开表发汗兼宣肺，桂枝温通助汗，二药相须为发汗峻剂；杏仁降肺，与麻黄一宣一降；炙甘草缓其峻烈。仲景对麻黄汤的禁例写了一整串（咽喉干燥、淋家、疮家、衄家、亡血家、汗家不可发汗），说明他把它当作有风险的药。

Modern research, graded现代研究（分级）

  • E4 The bronchodilating and sweating effects of ephedrine and pseudoephedrine are well established, but human randomised trials of the formula are few; modern care for colds and flu centres on symptom relief and antivirals.麻黄碱、伪麻黄碱的支气管扩张与发汗作用药理明确；但麻黄汤复方的人体随机试验少，现代感冒、流感的规范处理以对症与抗病毒药为主。

Cautions使用注意

Not for exterior deficiency with sweating, wind-heat, colds in weak people, new mothers or after blood loss; stop as soon as it works. See a doctor first for fever beyond three days, high fever, chest pain, breathing difficulty or confusion.表虚自汗、外感风热、体虚外感、新产妇、失血者均不宜；中病即止，不可过服。发热超过三天、高热、胸痛、呼吸困难、意识改变，先就医。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Contains Ma Huang and Gui Zhi; not for self-use in pregnancy.含麻黄、桂枝；孕期不建议自行使用。

Exam notes考点

Essentials: (1) the eight signs and the contrast with Gui Zhi Tang; (2) Ma Huang with Gui Zhi, Ma Huang with Xing Ren; (3) boil Ma Huang first and skim; (4) the people who must not be sweated.必考：①麻黄八症与桂枝汤鉴别；②麻黄桂枝相须、麻黄杏仁一宣一降；③先煮麻黄去上沫；④禁汗诸家。
