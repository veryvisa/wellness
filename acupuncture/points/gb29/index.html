📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Lie on the side with the affected side up, lower leg straight and upper leg slightly flexed so the ASIS and greater trochanter are easy to feel.侧卧位，患侧在上，下腿伸直、上腿微屈，使髂前上棘与股骨大转子都容易摸到。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Feel the ASIS at the front end of the iliac crest and the most prominent point of the greater trochanter on the upper lateral thigh; GB29 is the midpoint of the line joining them.先摸髂嵴前端最突出的髂前上棘，再摸大腿外上方最凸的股骨大转子，两点连线的中点即居髎。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    At the junction of tensor fasciae latae and gluteus medius over the outer iliac wing; the lateral femoral cutaneous nerve emerges below the ASIS superficially, and branches of the superior gluteal vessels and nerve lie deep.   3. 👆

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Thumb on the ASIS, middle finger on the greater trochanter; the point is midway between them.一手拇指按髂前上棘、中指按大转子，两指连线正中即是。
