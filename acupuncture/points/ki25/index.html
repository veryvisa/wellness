Spirit Storehouse · High risk高危

Point of the Kidney channel足少阴肾经穴
⚠️ Pneumothorax-risk point气胸风险穴 The pleura and lung lie directly beneath Shéncáng, very shallow in thin people. Use only 0.5–0.8 cun oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space; never perpendicular or deep, never medially. Chest pain, breathlessness, worsening pain on breathing in or persistent cough after needling needs urgent care (in BC call 911).神藏深面就是胸膜和肺，胸壁薄者尤其浅。只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不可直刺、深刺，不可向内侧进针。针后胸痛、气短、吸气痛加重或持续咳嗽，立即就医（BC 拨 911）。

📍 Location定位★

On the chest, in the 2nd intercostal space, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 2 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

💡 Count down the intercostal spaces from the sternal angle (level of the 2nd rib) to the 2nd space, then go 2 cun lateral to the midline (half the 4 cun to the midclavicular line); do not use the nipple in women.从胸骨角（平第 2 肋）向下数肋间隙到第 2 肋间隙，再从前正中线向外量 2 寸（锁骨中线距中线 4 寸的一半）；女性不用乳头定位。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse, laterally along the intercostal space斜刺或平刺（沿肋间隙向外） · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never perpendicular or deep, to avoid the heart and lungs.不可直刺、深刺，以免伤及心、肺。

🛡 Safety安全

The pleura and lung lie directly beneath the intercostal muscles: oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space only, never perpendicular or deep; do not insert hugging the lower border of the rib above (intercostal vessels and nerve).肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺：只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺，不直刺、不深刺；不贴上位肋骨下缘进针（肋间血管神经）。

    For 2 cun lateral to the midline use the patient's index, middle and ring fingers together (about 2 cun), or half the distance to the midclavicular line (4 cun); the vertical level comes only from counting intercostal spaces, never from finger measurement.前正中线旁开 2 寸可用患者食指、中指、无名指三指并拢（约 2 寸）自前正中线向外量取，或取锁骨中线（距中线 4 寸）的一半；纵向高度只看肋间隙，不靠指量。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located in the second intercostal space on the anterior chest wall, about 2 cun lateral to the sternal border. Superficially lie skin, subcutaneous tissue (with superficial thoracoabdominal veins), pectoralis major, and external and internal intercostal muscles; deep lie the intercostal neurovascular bundle (running near the inferior border of the upper rib), parietal pleura, and lung. Never insert deeply or medially; needle obliquely or transversely laterally along the chest wall to avoid injuring intercostal vessels, pleura, or lung.位于前胸壁第 2 肋间隙、胸骨外侧缘旁开约 2 寸处。浅层依次为皮肤、皮下组织（含胸腹壁浅静脉）、胸大肌及肋间外肌、肋间内肌；深面紧邻肋间血管神经束（多靠近上位肋骨下缘走行）、壁胸膜与肺。进针严禁向内侧或深层直刺，须沿胸壁方向向外斜刺或平刺，避免刺伤肋间血管、胸膜及肺。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide the index or middle fingertip gently outward along the second intercostal space from beside the sternum to feel the soft-tissue groove between the two ribs, checking for local tenderness or soreness 2 cun lateral to the midline; keep both palpation and needling gentle to avoid compressing or injuring the deep pleura.用食指或中指指腹沿第 2 肋间隙自胸骨旁向外轻轻滑动，体会上下两肋之间的软组织凹陷，并在前正中线旁开 2 寸处感受局部压痛或酸胀；触诊与进针均须轻柔，防止压迫或损伤深层胸膜。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Taking the 1st or 3rd space: without fixing the sternal angle at the 2nd rib, one ends up at KI26 (1st space) or KI24 (3rd space).误取第 1 或第 3 肋间隙：未先认清胸骨角平第 2 肋，易取高到彧中（KI26，第 1 肋间隙）或取低到灵墟（KI24，第 3 肋间隙）。
  • Inserting deeply or medially: parietal pleura and lung lie directly deep to KI25, so deep or medial needling carries a high risk of pneumothorax, hemothorax, or intercostal neuralgia.向内侧或深层直刺、进针过深：神藏深面即为壁胸膜与肺，向内或深刺极易引发气胸、血胸或肋间神经痛。
  • Drifting too far laterally or medially: going too far laterally enters thicker pectoralis major or other chest-wall musculature, while going too far medially approaches the sternal margin, reducing accuracy and safety margin.旁开距离偏移过大：过偏外侧可能误入胸大肌较厚处或胸壁其他肌层，过偏内侧则靠近胸骨缘，影响取穴准确度与局部安全评估。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

Consider KI25 when cough accompanies chest fullness, chest tightness, or wheezing to help free chest qi; for straightforward external cough or itchy throat, points such as LU7, LI4, and BL13 are more commonly prioritized, chronic deficient cough requires pattern differentiation, and pregnancy requires careful point selection.前胸胀满、胸闷或气喘并见咳嗽时可参用神藏宣通胸气；单纯外感咳嗽或咽痒咳嗽多先选列缺、合谷、肺俞等更常用穴，久咳虚证需辨证调补，孕期针刺相关配穴需谨慎评估。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Use KI25 alongside points such as BL13 and EX-B1 as an adjunct when wheezing occurs with chest tightness or fullness; acute severe asthma or respiratory distress requires prompt medical emergency care and standard medication rather than acupuncture alone.喘息伴胸闷、胸部胀满时可用神藏配合肺俞、定喘等辅助宽胸平喘；急性重症哮喘或呼吸窘迫必须优先西医急救与规范用药，不可单靠针灸处理。

Coughing up blood咯血 ⚖️

Hemoptysis is a serious warning sign requiring immediate medical evaluation to identify the cause (such as bronchiectasis, tuberculosis, pulmonary embolism, or malignancy); KI25 and acupuncture are not first-line or emergency treatment and must never delay medical care.咯血属于急危重信号，须立即就医查明病因（如支气管扩张、结核、肺栓塞或肿瘤），神藏与一般针灸均非咯血首选或急救手段，严禁延误医疗。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Costochondritis (Tietze syndrome or non-suppurative costal cartilage inflammation)肋软骨炎（Tietze 综合征或非化脓性肋软骨炎）

Often presents with localized tenderness and chest pain near the second to fifth costochondral junctions, worsening with deep inspiration, cough, or arm movement; differentiate from the KI25 intercostal site, and seek urgent medical evaluation first if pain is severe, radiates to the arm or jaw, or comes with diaphoresis or dyspnea to rule out cardiac chest pain.常见于前胸第 2–5 肋软骨交界处局部压痛、胸痛，深呼吸、咳嗽或上肢动作时可加重；神藏位于第 2 肋间隙旁开 2 寸，需与肋软骨炎压痛点鉴别，若胸痛剧烈、放射至左臂/下颌或伴冷汗、呼吸困难，应先就医排除心源性胸痛。

Chest-wall myofascial pain and intercostal neuralgia胸壁肌筋膜痛与肋间神经痛

Prolonged desk posture or strain can cause pectoralis major or intercostal soreness or pain along an intercostal space; distinguish superficial myofascial tenderness from deeper pleural pathology, and rule out pneumothorax promptly if sudden chest pain occurs with shortness of breath.长期伏案、姿势不良或劳损可引起胸大肌、肋间肌酸痛或沿肋间隙放射痛；触诊与针刺需区分浅层肌筋膜激痛点与深部胸膜病变，出现突发胸痛伴气短须立即排查气胸。

Chest tightness and wheezing related to airway hyperreactivity, asthma, or COPD气道高反应、哮喘或慢阻肺相关的胸闷与气喘

Presents with chest tightness, wheezing, and cough; acupuncture is only an adjunctive measure and does not replace bronchodilators or inhaled corticosteroids, while acute wheezing, difficulty speaking in full sentences, low oxygen saturation, or cyanosis requires immediate medical care.表现为胸闷、喘息、咳嗽，针灸仅可作为辅助调理，不可替代支气管扩张剂或吸入糖皮质激素；急性喘息发作、说话费力、血氧下降或口唇发绀须立即就医。

Where is KI25 Shéncáng?神藏（KI25）在哪里？

On the chest, in the 2nd intercostal space, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 2 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

How is KI25 needled?神藏（KI25）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse, laterally along the intercostal space; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺或平刺（沿肋间隙向外）；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of KI25?神藏（KI25）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Kidney channel足少阴肾经穴

Main safety point at KI25?神藏（KI25）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung lie directly beneath the intercostal muscles: oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space only, never perpendicular or deep; do not insert hugging the lower border of the rib above (intercostal vessels and nerve).肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺：只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺，不直刺、不深刺；不贴上位肋骨下缘进针（肋间血管神经）。
