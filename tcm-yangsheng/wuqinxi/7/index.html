✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚

  • Poor balance or a history of falls: hold the back of a chair or a counter with one hand and keep the heels down容易跌倒、平衡不好：一只手扶椅背做或台面，脚跟不提起
  • Neck problems or vertigo: turn the head only a little, slowly颈椎病、眩晕：转头幅度很小，慢慢转
  • High blood pressure: keep breathing in as you lift; never hold the breath高血压：上提时照常吸气、不憋气
  • Late pregnancy: skip the belly and pelvic-floor squeeze and keep the heels down怀孕后期：不做提肛收腹的用力，脚跟不提

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Hands in front of the body, bunch the fingertips together into a 'monkey hook' (all five fingertips pinched together, wrists bent)两手在体前，手指撮拢成「猿钩」（五指指尖捏在一起，屈腕）
  2. Draw the hooks up to the chest while shrugging the shoulders, drawing in the belly, gently lifting the pelvic floor and rising onto the toes; turn the head to the left and look left两手猿钩上提到胸前，同时耸肩、收腹、提肛，脚跟提起，头向左转，目视左方
  3. Turn the head back to the front, drop the shoulders, release the belly and pelvic floor, and lower the heels; open the hooks into palms头转回正前方，肩放下，腹和肛门放松，脚跟落地；猿钩变掌
  4. Press the palms down in front of the body to the belly; next time turn the head to the right两掌沿体前下按到腹前；下一次头向右转

🌬 Breathe in as you lift, out as you come down.上提时吸气，下落时呼气。

✅ Key points要领

  • Shrug, draw in the belly, lift the pelvic floor and rise onto the toes almost together, the whole body gathering inward耸肩、收腹、提肛、提踵几乎同时，全身向中间收紧
  • Rise only as high as you can stay steady提踵的高度以站得稳为准
  • Turn the head slowly and let the eyes lead转头慢，眼睛跟着走

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Rising too high on the toes and wobbling提踵太高、站不稳
  • Turning the head too fast转头太快
  • Holding the breath at the top憋着气不放

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Chair version: hold a chair back or counter with one hand, keep the heels down, and only shrug, draw in the belly and turn the head; it can also be done seated.扶椅版：一只手扶椅背或台面，脚跟不提，只做耸肩、收腹、转头；也可以坐着做。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Traditional teaching says Monkey Lifting imitates a wary monkey drawing itself in and glancing about; the whole body gathers and releases. Tradition pairs the monkey with the heart and says the gather and release brings a calm mind. This is a traditional view.传统教法说猿提学猴子警觉时缩身、东张西望；全身一收一放。传统上把猿配心，说这一收一放让人「心静」。这是传统说法。
