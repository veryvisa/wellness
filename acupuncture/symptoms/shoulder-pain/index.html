Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风）

The acupuncture prescription for frozen shoulder ('leaking-shoulder wind', 'fifty-year shoulder') has three layers: a fixed set of shoulder points; a distal point on the Hand Yangming, Shaoyang, Taiyang or Taiyin channel depending on whether pain sits at the front-outer, outer, back or front-armpit part of the shoulder; and pattern points for external invasion, qi and blood stagnation, or qi and blood deficiency. The channel is judged by two things: where the tenderness is and which movement hurts most. Moving the shoulder while a distal point is being needled is the signature technique for this condition.漏肩风（五十肩、冻结肩）的针灸处方由三层组成：一组固定的肩部主穴；按疼痛在肩前、肩外、肩后还是腋前选手阳明、手少阳、手太阳或手太阴经的远端配穴；再按外邪内侵、气滞血瘀、气血虚弱加证候配穴。判断经络靠两样：压痛在哪里，哪个方向的动作最痛。远端穴针刺时让患者活动患肩，是本病最有特色的操作。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Left shoulder, arm or jaw pain with chest tightness or pain, breathlessness, sweating or nausea, or brought on by exertion: call 911, this may be angina or a heart attack左肩、左臂或下颌痛同时有胸闷、胸痛、气短、出汗、恶心，或活动后诱发：拨 911，可能是心绞痛或心肌梗死
  • Shoulder deformity, inability to lift the arm, or numbness and weakness after a fall or impact: same-day emergency care for dislocation, fracture or acute rotator-cuff tear跌倒或撞击后肩部变形、不能抬臂、手臂麻木无力：当天急诊，排除脱位、骨折和急性肩袖撕裂
  • A red, hot, swollen shoulder with fever or feeling unwell, especially after a joint injection or broken skin: same-day assessment for septic arthritis肩关节红肿发热、伴发烧或全身不适，近期有关节注射或皮肤破损：当天就医，排除化脓性关节炎
  • Right shoulder pain with right upper abdominal pain, fever or jaundice; left shoulder-tip pain with abdominal pain or dizziness, especially if pregnancy is possible or after a blow to the abdomen: emergency now for referred pain from the gallbladder, below the diaphragm, an ectopic pregnancy or a ruptured spleen右肩痛伴右上腹痛、发热、黄疸；左肩尖痛伴腹痛、头晕，尤其可能怀孕或腹部受过撞击：立即急诊，排除胆囊、膈下病变、宫外孕或脾破裂的牵涉痛
  • In a long-term smoker, shoulder pain with numbness on the little-finger side, wasting of hand muscles, a drooping eyelid on the same side or coughing blood; constant night pain or unexplained weight loss: see a doctor soon to rule out a lung-apex tumour or bone metastasis长期吸烟者肩痛伴手掌尺侧麻木、手部肌肉萎缩、同侧眼睑下垂或咳血；夜间静息痛、不明原因消瘦：尽快就医，排除肺尖肿瘤或骨转移

「不可以顧，肩似拔，臑似折」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.10, the Hand Taiyang Small Intestine channel's disorders: unable to look round, the shoulder feels as if being pulled out and the upper arm as if broken. The ancients already assigned severe pain of the shoulder and back of the upper arm to the Hand Taiyang, the channel basis for treating posterior shoulder pain with SI3 and SI9.《灵枢·经脉》手太阳小肠经的「是动病」：不能回头，肩痛得像要被拔起来，上臂痛得像要折断。古人已把肩背、上臂后侧的剧痛归到手太阳经，这是肩后痛（手太阳经证）配后溪、取肩贞的经络依据。

「邪客於足太陽之絡，令人頭項肩痛」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.63: when a pathogen lodges in the collateral of the Foot Taiyang, the head, neck and shoulder hurt. Shoulder pain often travels with the neck, so first check whether the shoulder pain actually comes from the neck (cervical radiculopathy often presents as shoulder pain today).《素问·缪刺论》：邪气侵入足太阳经的络脉，会出现头、颈项和肩部疼痛。说明肩痛常与颈项同病，临床要先看是不是颈部问题牵连到肩（现代的颈神经根病常表现为肩痛）。

Principle治法 Free the channel, relax the sinews, stop pain (drain; move the shoulder while the distal point is needled)通经活络，舒筋止痛（泻法；远端穴针刺时嘱患者活动肩关节）

💡 Textbook core: LI15, TE14, SI9, Jianqian (an extra point with no code here) and ashi points to free the shoulder locally, plus GB34, the influential point of sinews, as a distal sinew point. Pain on the Hand Yangming adds its yuan point LI4, distal along the channel. Some textbook editions also list LI11 as a core point.教材主穴：肩髃、肩髎、肩贞、肩前（经外奇穴，本站无代码）、阿是穴，局部疏通肩部经气；阳陵泉是筋会，远端舒筋。痛在手阳明经，加本经原穴合谷，循经远取。有的教材版本主穴另列曲池。

Pattern证候

External invasion (wind-cold-damp)外邪内侵（风寒湿）

How it presents辨证要点 Shifting or cold shoulder pain after wind, cold or damp living, worse with cold and better with warmth, the shoulder feels cold; shorter course; thin white or greasy coating, floating or tight pulse.受风受凉或久居潮湿后肩部窜痛或冷痛，遇风寒加重、得温减轻，肩部怕冷；病程较短；苔薄白或白腻，脉浮或紧。

Principle治法 Expel wind-cold, free the collaterals, stop pain (drain; moxa, warm needle or cupping may be added)祛风散寒，通络止痛（泻法，可加灸、温针、拔罐）

Main主穴LI15 Jiānyú肩髃TE14 Jiānjiáo肩髎SI9 Jiānzhēn肩贞GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉
Add配穴LI4 Hégǔ合谷GB20 Fēngchí风池

💡 Core points free the shoulder; for external invasion the textbook adds LI4 and GB20 to expel wind and release the exterior. When cold-damp dominates, warm-needle moxa on the shoulder points is common.主穴疏通肩部；教材外邪内侵配合谷、风池，祛风解表。寒湿重的肩部穴位多用温针灸。

Pattern证候

Qi and blood stagnation气滞血瘀

How it presents辨证要点 Stabbing fixed shoulder pain that resists pressure, worst at night and disturbing sleep, or after injury or overuse; clearly restricted movement; dark tongue or stasis marks, choppy pulse.肩部刺痛、痛处固定拒按，夜间痛甚影响睡眠，或有外伤劳损史；活动明显受限；舌暗或有瘀斑，脉涩。

Principle治法 Move qi and blood, free the collaterals, stop pain (drain; ashi points may be bled and cupped)行气活血，通络止痛（泻法，阿是穴可刺络拔罐）

Main主穴LI15 Jiānyú肩髃TE14 Jiānjiáo肩髎SI9 Jiānzhēn肩贞GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉
Add配穴PC6 Nèiguān内关BL17 Géshū膈俞

💡 Same core; the textbook adds PC6 to move qi and BL17, the influential point of blood, to move blood. Severe night pain is typical of the early painful phase of frozen shoulder, but can also be rotator-cuff disease or a tumour, so persistent cases need investigation.主穴同上；教材气滞血瘀配内关、膈俞：内关行气，膈俞是血会、活血。夜间痛甚是冻结肩早期（疼痛期）的特点，也可能是肩袖病变或肿瘤，持续不缓解要检查。

Pattern证候

Qi and blood deficiency气血虚弱

How it presents辨证要点 Dull aching shoulder worse with fatigue, weak arm, muscle wasting over time; tiredness, shortness of breath, a lustreless complexion; pale tongue, thin weak pulse. Common in older people with a long course.肩部酸痛隐隐、劳累后加重，肩臂无力，日久肌肉萎缩；伴乏力、气短、面色无华；舌淡，脉细弱。多见于病程长的中老年人。

Principle治法 Tonify qi and blood, relax the sinews, free the collaterals (tonify or even method; moxa allowed)益气养血，舒筋通络（补法或平补平泻，可灸）

Main主穴LI15 Jiānyú肩髃TE14 Jiānjiáo肩髎SI9 Jiānzhēn肩贞GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉
Add配穴ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里CV6 Qìhǎi气海

💡 Same core; the textbook adds ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, to build qi and blood, and CV6 to tonify original qi. In the stiff phase of frozen shoulder, exercise is the mainstay and acupuncture supports it.主穴同上；教材气血虚弱配足三里、气海：足三里是胃经合穴，补后天以生气血；气海培补元气。冻结肩僵硬期以功能锻炼为主，针灸配合。

LI15 Jiānyú肩髃

Best for最适合 Front-outer shoulder pain, restricted abduction and raising肩前外侧痛、外展上举受限

Why依据 Meeting point of the Hand Yangming and Yang Heel vessel; in the hollow in front of and below the acromion, located with the arm abducted手阳明与阳跷脉交会；肩峰前下方凹陷，臂外展时取

Versus others与其他穴的区别 In front of TE14 and more for abduction pain, the two are used as a front–back pair; insert 0.8–1.5 cun perpendicular or obliquely downward with strict asepsis toward the joint比肩髎靠前、偏外展痛，两穴前后同用；直刺或向下斜刺 0.8–1.5 寸，关节腔方向要严格消毒

TE14 Jiānjiáo肩髎

Best for最适合 Outer shoulder pain behind and below the acromion, restricted abduction肩外侧、肩峰后下方痛，外展受限

Why依据 Local Hand Shaoyang shoulder point in the hollow between the acromial angle and the greater tuberosity手少阳经肩部局部穴，肩峰角与肱骨大结节之间的凹陷

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Behind LI15; with TE5 for the Hand Shaoyang type; insert 1–1.5 cun toward the joint without repeated strong stimulation比肩髃靠后，配外关治手少阳经证；向肩关节直刺 1–1.5 寸，不必为止痛反复强刺激

SI9 Jiānzhēn肩贞

Best for最适合 Posterior shoulder pain, restricted adduction, tenderness above the posterior axillary fold肩后侧痛、内收受限，腋后皱襞上方压痛

Why依据 Hand Taiyang point, 1 cun above the posterior axillary crease手太阳经，腋后纹头上 1 寸

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Lower and further back than TE14, paired with SI3 for the Hand Taiyang type; the radial and axillary nerves lie deep, so needle perpendicular or slightly outward, never deep toward the armpit比肩髎更靠后下，配后溪治手太阳经证；深部有桡神经、腋神经，直刺或略向外，不向腋窝内侧深刺

GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉

Best for最适合 Distal support for tight sinews and restricted shoulder movement肩部筋脉拘急、活动受限的远端配合

Why依据 Influential point of sinews, Foot Shaoyang he-sea point; one of the core frozen-shoulder points筋会，足少阳合穴；漏肩风主穴之一

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Not on the shoulder, it relaxes sinews as their influential point; more for sinew tightness, while ST38 threaded to BL57 is a distal movement technique; the shoulder is also moved while it is needled不在肩上，靠「筋会」舒筋；比条口偏于筋脉拘挛，条口透承山偏远端运动针法；针时同样让患者活动肩部

ST38 Tiáokǒu条口

Best for最适合 Distal 'movement needling' for painful restricted shoulder肩痛活动受限的远端「运动针法」

Why依据 Tiaokou, 8 cun below ST35; threading ST38 toward BL57 is an empirical technique for frozen shoulder条口，犊鼻下 8 寸；条口透承山是治肩凝的经验穴法

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Depends more on moving while needling than GB34: strong stimulation while the patient raises and abducts the arm, stopping once it helps; only for patients who can move actively, not in the acute injury phase比阳陵泉更依赖边针边活动：强刺激同时嘱患者抬臂、外展，见效即止；只适合能主动活动的患者，不用于外伤急性期

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

Best for最适合 Hand Yangming type; external invasion with exterior signs手阳明经证；外邪内侵兼表证

Why依据 Hand Yangming yuan point; distal along the channel, expels wind and releases the exterior手阳明原穴；循经远取、祛风解表

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the front-outer shoulder (Hand Yangming) and exterior signs, TE5 more for the outer shoulder (Hand Shaoyang); contraindicated in pregnancy比外关偏肩前外侧（手阳明）和表证，外关偏肩外侧（手少阳）；孕妇禁针

TE5 Wàiguān外关

Best for最适合 Hand Shaoyang type: outer shoulder pain, painful abduction手少阳经证：肩外侧痛、外展痛

Why依据 Hand Shaoyang luo point, confluent point of the Yang Linking vessel手少阳络穴，八脉交会通阳维脉

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More lateral than LI4, and lateral rather than posterior compared with SI3; have the patient slowly abduct the arm while it is needled比合谷偏肩外侧，比后溪偏外不偏后；针外关时嘱患者缓慢外展患臂

SI3 Hòuxī后溪

Best for最适合 Hand Taiyang type: posterior shoulder pain, painful adduction, with a stiff neck手太阳经证：肩后痛、内收痛，兼颈项强

Why依据 Hand Taiyang shu-stream point, confluent point of the Governor vessel; 'shu points govern heavy painful joints'手太阳输穴，八脉交会通督脉；「输主体重节痛」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More posterior than TE5 and also helps the neck; first choice when shoulder pain joins neck pain, while ruling out shoulder pain caused by a cervical nerve root比外关偏肩后，又能兼治颈项；肩痛连颈时优先，同时要排除颈神经根病引起的肩痛

LU7 Lièquē列缺

Best for最适合 Hand Taiyin type: front of shoulder near the armpit, painful reaching behind the back手太阴经证：肩前近腋痛、后伸背手痛

Why依据 Hand Taiyin luo point, confluent point of the Ren vessel手太阴络穴，八脉交会通任脉

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the front-inner shoulder (Taiyin) than LI4, which covers the front-outer (Yangming); above the radial styloid, insert 0.5–0.8 cun transversely upward比合谷偏肩前内侧（太阴），合谷偏前外侧（阳明）；在桡骨茎突上方，向上平刺 0.5–0.8 寸

PC6 Nèiguān内关

Best for最适合 Qi-moving support point in shoulder pain from stagnation气滞血瘀型肩痛的行气配穴

Why依据 Hand Jueyin luo point, confluent point of the Yin Linking vessel; moves qi手厥阴络穴，八脉交会通阴维脉；行气

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Moves qi while BL17 moves blood, and the two are used together; left shoulder pain with chest tightness or palpitations needs a cardiac cause ruled out first比膈俞偏行气，膈俞偏活血，两穴同用；左肩痛伴胸闷心慌时先排除心脏原因，不按肩病处理

BL17 Géshū膈俞

Best for最适合 Blood stasis with fixed stabbing shoulder pain worse at night肩部刺痛固定、夜间痛甚的瘀血证

Why依据 Influential point of blood; moves stasis血会；活血化瘀

Versus others与其他穴的区别 A whole-body stasis point, not on the shoulder; more for the blood level than PC6; beside T7 over the pleura, so oblique toward the spine only, 0.5–0.8 cun治瘀的全身配穴，不在肩部；比内关偏血分；第 7 胸椎旁，深部是胸膜，只向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸

ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

Best for最适合 Long-standing weak shoulder with wasting from qi and blood deficiency久病肩痛无力、肌肉萎缩的气血虚弱

Why依据 Stomach he-sea point; tonifies qi and blood胃经合穴；补益气血

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Builds qi and blood through the spleen and stomach, CV6 tonifies original qi; added only for deficiency, not in the acute painful phase比气海偏补后天脾胃、生气血，气海偏元气；只在虚证加，不用于急性疼痛期

CV6 Qìhǎi气海

Best for最适合 Qi and blood deficiency with fatigue and breathlessness气血虚弱兼乏力、气短

Why依据 Qihai on the Ren vessel; tonifies original qi, moxa preferred气海，任脉；培补元气，宜灸

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for original qi than ST36 and usually moxaed; on the lower abdomen, so contraindicated in pregnancy and not needled deep with a full bladder比足三里偏元气，常用灸；位于下腹部，孕妇禁针，膀胱充盈时不深刺

SI11 Tiānzōng天宗

Best for最适合 Shoulder pain spreading over the scapula with tenderness in the infraspinous fossa肩痛牵连肩胛区、肩胛冈下窝压痛

Why依据 Hand Taiyang point in the centre of the infraspinous fossa手太阳经，肩胛冈下窝中央

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

'Frozen shoulder' in everyday use is a loose label that needs unpacking: (1) true frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), common at 40–60 and more likely with diabetes or thyroid disease, with both active and passive movement restricted, especially external rotation, passing through painful, stiff and thawing phases over one to three years; (2) rotator-cuff tendinopathy or tear, most painful between 60° and 120° of elevation (painful arc), with passive range near normal but active strength reduced, and a tear to suspect when an older person cannot lift the arm after a fall; (3) subacromial bursitis and calcific tendinitis; (4) acromioclavicular or glenohumeral osteoarthritis; (5) cervical radiculopathy referring to the shoulder, often with neck pain and hand numbness; (6) referred visceral pain (heart, gallbladder, below the diaphragm, lung apex). The key test is whether passive movement is also restricted: in frozen shoulder it is, in rotator-cuff problems it is largely preserved. Frozen shoulder is managed mainly with exercise and sometimes an intra-articular injection, with acupuncture plus movement helping pain; full-thickness cuff tears and recurrent dislocation need orthopaedic assessment.现代医学里「肩周炎」是笼统叫法，要拆开看：①冻结肩（粘连性关节囊炎），40–60 岁多见，糖尿病、甲状腺病患者风险高，主动和被动活动都受限，尤其外旋，病程分疼痛期、僵硬期、恢复期，常持续 1–3 年；②肩袖腱病或撕裂，抬臂 60°–120° 之间最痛（疼痛弧），被动活动范围基本正常、主动力弱，老年人跌倒后不能抬臂要想到撕裂；③肩峰下滑囊炎、钙化性肌腱炎；④肩锁关节、盂肱关节骨关节炎；⑤颈椎神经根病牵涉到肩，常伴颈痛和手麻；⑥内脏牵涉痛（心、胆、膈下、肺尖）。区分要点是「被动活动是否也受限」：冻结肩被动也动不了，肩袖问题被动尚可。冻结肩以功能锻炼、必要时关节内注射为主，针灸配合运动可缓解疼痛；肩袖完全撕裂、反复脱位需骨科评估。
