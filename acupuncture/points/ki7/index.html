  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate KI3 between the medial malleolus tip and the Achilles tendon first, then measure 2 cun upward along the anterior border of the Achilles tendon in the soft-tissue groove anterior to the tendon.先找到内踝尖与跟腱之间的太溪（KI3），沿跟腱前缘向上量 2 寸，在跟腱前缘与胫骨后缘之间的软组织凹陷处取穴。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    KI7 lies at the front edge of the Achilles tendon, with saphenous nerve branches and great saphenous tributaries superficially; flexor digitorum longus and the lower edge of soleus are deeper, and the posterior tibial artery and tibial nerve descend deep and anteromedially. Insert perpendicular 0.5–1 cun and do not advance deep anteromedially.复溜位于跟腱前缘，浅层有隐神经分支和大隐静脉属支；深层为趾长屈肌和比目鱼肌下缘，胫后动脉与胫神经在前内侧深面下行。直刺 0.5–1 寸，不向深部前内侧推进。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently upward along the anterior Achilles border to feel the fascial groove in front of the tendon; if pressure or insertion triggers tingling radiating toward the sole, withdraw and redirect away from tibial nerve branches.指腹沿跟腱前缘由下向上轻按滑动，可触及跟腱前方的肌腱间隙与筋膜凹陷；若按压或进针引起沿足底放射的麻电感，提示触及或邻近胫神经分支，应立即退针调整方向。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Placing the point inside or posterior to the Achilles tendon: the tendon is dense and posterior, so needling it causes sharp pain and misses the point.取到跟腱内部或偏后方：跟腱纤维致密且位于后方，扎入腱内疼痛明显且偏离穴位。
  • Inserting deeply posteromedially: risks injuring the posterior tibial artery and tibial nerve, causing bleeding or radiating paresthesia into the leg and sole.向后内侧深刺：易伤及深部后内侧走行的胫后动脉和胫神经，引发出血或下肢/足底神经放射痛。
  • Too high or too low (not 2 cun above the malleolus): KI8 is at the same level 0.5 cun in front, so do not confuse them front-to-back; either deviation misses the point.位置偏高或偏低（未在内踝尖上 2 寸）：交信与复溜同高、在其前方 0.5 寸，不要前后混淆；偏高偏低都离开经穴定位。

Oedema水肿 ⚖️

Used in pattern-based care for oedema linked to kidney deficiency or impaired fluid transformation; do not substitute for medical care in acute cardiac, renal, or hepatic oedema or sudden unilateral swelling, and compare/pair with SP9 or CV9.偏重肾虚或下肢气化不利所致水肿的辨证配穴；急性心、肾、肝源性水肿或突发单侧下肢肿胀须先就医，不以单穴替代诊疗，与阴陵泉、水分等利水穴配伍使用。

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Suited as a distal point for kidney-deficiency low-back and leg pain or pain along the Kidney pathway; rule out acute trauma or progressive neurological deficit medically first, and compare/pair with BL23, BL40, and KI3.适用于肾虚型腰腿痛或沿足少阴经循行牵涉痛的远端配穴；急性外伤、神经根压迫伴马尾或进行性肌力减退须先就医，常与肾俞、委中、太溪等穴对比配伍。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Used mainly as an adjunct for deficiency-cold diarrhoea such as spleen-kidney yang deficiency or early-morning diarrhoea; seek medical care for acute infectious diarrhoea, fever, bloody stools, or dehydration, and compare/pair with ST36, BL20, and CV4.多用于脾肾阳虚或久泻、五更泻等虚寒证候的辅助调理；急性感染性腹泻、高热、血便或严重脱水须先就医，与足三里、脾俞、关元等穴辨证配伍。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Achilles tendinitis or peritendinitis跟腱炎或跟腱周围炎（Achilles tendinitis / peritendinitis）

Common in runners, jumpers, or after sudden load increases, causing morning stiffness or exercise-related pain; differentiate from posterior tibial tendinitis. Seek prompt medical evaluation if sudden severe pain, a tendon gap, or inability to heel-raise suggests Achilles rupture.常见于跑步、跳跃或突然增加运动量者，跟腱及周围晨僵或活动后疼痛；需与胫后肌腱炎鉴别。若跟腱突发剧痛、局部凹陷或无法踮脚站立，应先就医排查跟腱断裂。

Posterior tibial tendon dysfunction or medial ankle tendinopathy胫后肌腱功能不全或内侧踝部肌腱病

Distal medial leg cutaneous nerve irritation or mild venous congestion小腿内侧远端皮神经卡压或静脉回流不畅

Where is KI7 Fùliū?复溜（KI7）在哪里？

On the medial lower leg, 2 cun above the tip of the medial malleolus, at the anterior border of the calcaneal tendon.小腿内侧，内踝尖上 2 寸，跟腱的前缘。

How is KI7 needled?复溜（KI7）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of KI7?复溜（KI7）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-river (metal)经穴（金）

Main safety point at KI7?复溜（KI7）最要注意的安全问题？

Do not insert into the tendon or advance deep and anteromedially toward the posterior tibial bundle; withdraw at once for electric tingling into the sole.不刺入跟腱；不向深部前内侧推进（胫后血管神经束）；出现沿足底放射的电麻感立即退针。
