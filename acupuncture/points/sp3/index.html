    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate proximally along the medial border of the great toe until you feel the prominent bulge of the first metatarsal head (medial prominence of the first metatarsophalangeal joint). Just posteroinferior to this prominence (toward the heel and plantar border) lies a distinct bony hollow — that is SP3.沿足大趾内侧缘向足跟方向推摸，先摸到最明显膨大的骨性突起——第 1 跖骨头（即第 1 跖趾关节内侧突）。越过此突起后，在其后下方（近足跟、偏足底侧）可触及一明显骨性凹陷，即是太白。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Have the patient slightly flex the toes to palpate the longitudinal belly of abductor hallucis along the medial arch; SP3 sits in the hollow posteroinferior to the first metatarsal head, between the upper border of abductor hallucis and the first metatarsal shaft, with flexor hallucis brevis and the first metatarsal neck-head junction deep to it.     Simple method简便取穴

    Palpation check触诊确认

Medial swelling and tenderness over the first metatarsal head, restricted joint motion, or lateral deviation of the great toe directly overlaps SP3. 🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SP3 Tàibái?太白（SP3）在哪里？

On the medial foot, in the depression proximal to the first metatarsophalangeal joint, at the border of the red and white flesh.跖区，第 1 跖趾关节近端赤白肉际凹陷中。

How is SP3 needled?太白（SP3）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of SP3?太白（SP3）是哪类特定穴？

Shu-stream (earth), Yuan-source point输穴（土）、原穴

Main safety point at SP3?太白（SP3）最要注意的安全问题？

Tissue along the medial foot is thin: stop at the bone without forcing, and avoid visible veins.足内侧缘皮下组织薄，遇骨面即止，不强行抵骨；避开可见静脉。
