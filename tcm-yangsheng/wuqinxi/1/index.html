Form 1: Tiger lifting第1式 · 虎举

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Shoulder pain or frozen shoulder: lift only as high as is pain-free肩痛、肩周炎：上举到不痛的高度即可
  • High blood pressure: keep breathing in as you lift; do not clench the jaw or hold the breath高血压：上举时照常吸气，不咬牙、不憋气
  • A flare of finger arthritis: form the claw without force手指关节炎发作：成爪时不用力

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Palms facing down, spread the fingers wide, then bend them into a 'tiger claw' (the first two joints of each finger bent firmly)两手掌心向下，十指张开，再弯曲成「虎爪」（手指第一、二节用力弯曲）
  2. Rotate the hands outward and close them into fists, little finger first; raise the fists slowly up the front of the body to the chest两手外旋，由小指起依次屈指握拳，两拳沿体前慢慢上提到胸前
  3. Keep lifting; above the head open the fists into claws, palms up as if lifting something heavy, arms straight两拳继续上举，到头顶上方时松开成虎爪，掌心向上，像托起重物，两臂伸直
  4. Turn the hands, close them into fists facing each other and draw them slowly down to the chest, then open the hands and press them down to the belly两掌外旋握拳，拳心相对，慢慢下拉到胸前，再松开成掌，下按到腹前

🌬 Breathe in as you lift, out as you press down; never hold the breath.上举时吸气，下按时呼气；不憋气。

✅ Key points要领

  • Make the hand shapes distinct: spread, claw, fist手型变化要清楚：张开、成爪、握拳
  • Stretch the whole body up as you lift; let the shoulders drop as you press down上举时身体向上拔伸，下按时肩松下来
  • Let the eyes follow the hands眼睛跟着手走

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Hunching the shoulders and holding the breath耸肩、憋气
  • Arching the lower back and throwing the head back腰向前挺、仰头
  • Vague hand shapes, fingers not separated手型含糊，五指不分

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Do it seated, lifting only as high as is pain-free; or skip the fists and simply open and close the hands.坐在椅子上做，手只举到不痛的高度；或者不握拳，只做张开、合拢。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Traditional teaching says Tiger Lifting borrows the tiger's power: the hands push up as if lifting a great weight, and the firm, spread tiger claw is the signature of this animal.传统教法说虎举要学老虎的威猛，两手上托像举起千斤；「虎爪」用力、十指撑开，是这一戏的标志。
