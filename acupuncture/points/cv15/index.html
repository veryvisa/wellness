  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    CV15 is about one cun below the xiphoid tip on the anterior midline; identify both the tip and xiphisternal junction rather than the bone itself.鸠尾在剑突下方、前正中线上约一寸；先辨认剑突尖及胸剑联合，不把剑突骨面当穴位。

  2. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The xiphisternal junction-to-umbilicus span is 8 cun; CV15 is about seven cun above the navel, one cun below the xiphoid.胸剑联合中点至脐中为 8 寸；鸠尾约在脐上 7 寸，即剑突下方一寸处。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The xiphoid varies in shape and length; beneath lie the peritoneum and left liver lobe, and above, beyond the diaphragm, the pericardium and heart; angle downward only, never upward or deep.剑突形态和长度差异明显，深面为腹膜与肝左叶，上方隔膈肌即心包与心脏；只向下斜刺，绝不向上或深刺。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Visual localization from the xiphoid margin and midline is sufficient; avoid repeated palpation with a prominent xiphoid, tenderness or recent trauma.以剑突下缘和正中线作视觉定位即可；明显骨性突起、压痛或近期外伤时不做反复触诊。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Only a traditional location reference for subxiphoid pain after cardiac and acute abdominal causes are excluded; its Luo status does not justify needling the xiphoid.剑突下局部疼痛且已排除心脏及急腹症时仅作传统定位参考；不因鸠尾属络穴而在骨突处进针。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

For upper-abdominal discomfort with nausea, prefer safer points such as CV12 and PC6; CV15's classical needling prohibition makes it unsuitable as a routine lead point.上腹不适伴恶心时优先选更安全的中脘、内关等；鸠尾古籍有禁刺记载，不作为常规针刺首选。

Where is CV15 Jiūwěi?鸠尾（CV15）在哪里？

On the upper abdomen, 1 cun below the xiphisternal junction, on the anterior midline.上腹部，剑胸结合下 1 寸，前正中线上。

How is CV15 needled?鸠尾（CV15）怎么刺？

Oblique downward; 0.5–1 cun.向下斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of CV15?鸠尾（CV15）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting point of the Conception Vessel; Source point of the gao (fatty tissue)任脉络穴；膏之原

Main safety point at CV15?鸠尾（CV15）最要注意的安全问题？

Below the xiphoid an upward tip nears the diaphragm, pericardium and heart, and the left liver lobe lies beneath: angle downward only, never deep or upward. The Jiayi Jing once listed it as forbidden.剑突下针尖向上即近膈肌、心包与心脏，深面又有肝左叶：只向下斜刺，不深刺、不向上；《甲乙经》曾列禁刺。
