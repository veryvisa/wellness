🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Lateral dorsal foot strain and 4th–5th intermetatarsal bursitis / stress reaction足背外侧软组织劳损与第 4、5 跖骨间滑囊炎 / 应力性反应

Common in distance runners, tight-shoe wearers, and high-arched feet; localized tenderness at the 4th–5th metatarsal bases worsens on push-off. Differentiate from a 5th metatarsal base fracture (Jones or avulsion fracture): if there is an inversion injury, marked swelling/bruising, or inability to bear weight, obtain a foot X-ray before needling.常见于长跑者、穿窄头鞋或高足弓人群，足背外侧第 4、5 跖骨基底部局部压痛、负重蹬地时加重。需与第 5 跖骨基底部骨折（Jones 骨折或撕脱骨折）鉴别：若有明确崴脚外伤史、局部明显肿胀淤青及不能负重，必须先拍足部 X 光排除骨折再考虑针灸。

Superficial fibular nerve dorsal branch entrapment (lace bite / anterior tarsal irritation)腓浅神经足背分支卡压（鞋带综合征）

Caused by tight shoelaces, ski boots, or repetitive dorsal compression; produces burning, numbness, or tingling over the lateral dorsum into the 3rd–5th toes, with a positive Tinel sign near GB41. Avoid direct heavy stimulation of the nerve trunk; needle adjacent points instead.鞋带系得过紧、滑雪靴或足背反复受压后，出现足背外侧至第 3–5 趾背侧烧灼感、麻木或针刺感，叩击足临泣附近可诱发向足趾放射的 Tinel 征。局部选穴应避开神经干直接强刺激，改用周边疏通或近端穴位。

Where is GB41 Zúlínqì?足临泣（GB41）在哪里？

On the dorsum of the foot, distal to the junction of the bases of the 4th and 5th metatarsals, in the depression lateral to the extensor tendon of the 5th toe.足背，第 4、5 跖骨底结合部的前方，第 5 趾长伸肌腱外侧凹陷中。

How is GB41 needled?足临泣（GB41）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of GB41?足临泣（GB41）是哪类特定穴？

Shu-stream point (wood); Confluent point of the eight extraordinary vessels (Dai vessel)输穴（木）；八脉交会穴（通带脉）

Main safety point at GB41?足临泣（GB41）最要注意的安全问题？

Thin skin with superficial veins on the dorsum; avoid them and press after withdrawal.足背皮薄、静脉表浅，避开血管；出针按压。
