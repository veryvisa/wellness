Cold hands and feet: rub, roll, warm, then move手脚冰凉：搓、捻、擦，再动一动

A Vancouver winter is wet and cold, and some people have icy fingers all day and feet that never warm up at night. This is an everyday routine for warming hands and feet; first, check which kinds of 'cold' are not a matter of constitution but something to get looked at.温哥华的冬天又湿又冷，有人整天手指冰凉、晚上脚暖不过来。下面这套是让手脚暖起来的日常做法，先看清楚哪些「凉」不是体质问题，而是要查的病。

⏱ About 8 minutes约 8 分钟 · Before going out in the morning, after long sitting, or at bedtime.早上出门前、久坐之后，或睡前。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • One leg or foot suddenly turns cold, pale and very painful: call 911一条腿或一只脚突然变冷、发白、剧痛：拨 911
  • Fingers that go white, then blue, then red in the cold, especially if it is new, one-sided, or there are sores on the fingertips: see a doctor (possible Raynaud's or a condition behind it)手指遇冷先变白、再发紫、再变红，尤其是新出现的、只在一侧，或指尖有破口：去看医生（可能是雷诺现象或其背后的病）
  • Cold hands or feet with numbness, tingling, or foot wounds that will not heal: get your blood sugar, nerves and circulation checked手脚冰凉同时麻木、刺痛，或脚上的伤口很久不好：查血糖与神经、血管
  • Feeling cold together with tiredness, weight gain, constipation and dry skin, or with paleness and a racing heart: get your thyroid and blood count checked怕冷同时乏力、体重增加、便秘、皮肤干，或面色苍白、心慌：查甲状腺和有没有贫血
  • Calf pain that comes on with walking and goes away when you stop走一段路小腿就痛、停下来就好

✋ When not to press这些情况先别按

  • With diabetes or numb feet: no hot-water bottles or electric blankets, and test foot-bath water with your elbow first; numb feet may not feel a burn有糖尿病或脚有麻木的人：不用热水袋、电热毯，泡脚前先用手肘试水温——脚感觉不到烫，可能烫伤了也不知道
  • In pregnancy, skip SP6 Sanyinjiao: a traditional pregnancy caution孕期不按三阴交（SP6），这是传统的孕期禁忌
  • Do not rub over wounds, infection or blisters on the feet脚上有伤口、感染、水泡时不擦

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1
    PC8 Láogōng劳宫Friction rubbing擦法

    Rub your palms together until warm; then knead PC8 Laogong in the centre of the other palm (where the tip of the middle finger lands when you make a fist) with one thumb.两掌对搓到发热；再用一手拇指按揉另一手掌心的劳宫（握拳时中指尖所指的地方）。

    How much力度与时间 Rub for 30 seconds; knead about 1 minute each side搓 30 秒；每侧按揉约 1 分钟

  2. 2
    Pinching捏法

    Pinch each finger of the other hand between thumb and index finger and roll from the base to the tip, one finger after another.一手拇指和食指捏住另一手的手指，从指根捻到指尖，十个手指逐一做。

    How much力度与时间 3–5 times per finger每指 3–5 次

  3. 3
    ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里Kneading揉法

    Sitting, knead ST36 Zusanli, 3 cun below the outer knee eye and one finger-width out from the shinbone, with your thumb.坐着，拇指按揉膝眼下 3 寸、胫骨外侧一横指的足三里。

    How much力度与时间 1–2 minutes each side每侧 1–2 分钟

  4. 4
    SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交Kneading揉法

    SP6 Sanyinjiao is 3 cun above the tip of the inner ankle bone, just behind the edge of the shinbone; knead it, then switch legs.内踝尖上 3 寸、胫骨内侧缘后面是三阴交，按揉后换腿。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side; skip in pregnancy每侧约 1 分钟；孕期跳过

  5. 5
    KI1 Yǒngquán涌泉Friction rubbing擦法

    Rub KI1 Yongquan on the sole briskly with the opposite palm until it feels warm.用对侧手掌来回擦脚心的涌泉，擦到发热。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side每侧约 1 分钟

  6. 6

    Stand holding the back of a chair and raise and lower your heels, so the calf muscles squeeze and relax.站起来扶着椅背，脚跟抬起再放下，让小腿肌肉一缩一放。

    How much力度与时间 30 times, in two sets if you like30 次，可以分两组

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E5 We found no reliable studies testing massage for cold hands and feet. The warmth from rubbing palms and soles is local and short-lived; working the calf muscles and getting up to walk around are more direct ways to warm up.我们没有找到检验按摩对手脚冰凉作用的可靠研究。搓手、擦脚心带来的暖意是局部、暂时的；小腿一缩一放和起来走动，是让手脚暖和的更直接的办法。
  • E5 The UK NHS self-care advice for Raynaud's is to keep your whole body warm (not just hands and feet), wear gloves, avoid smoking, exercise regularly and find ways to manage stress.英国 NHS 对雷诺现象的自我照顾建议是：全身保暖（不只是手脚）、戴手套、不吸烟、规律运动、学会应对压力。 [1]
  • E5 The US NIDDK foot advice for people with diabetes: look at your feet every day, test the water before washing them, and do not warm them with hot-water bottles or electric blankets.美国 NIDDK 对糖尿病人的足部建议：每天看一遍脚，洗脚前先试水温，不用热水袋和电热毯暖脚。 [2]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 TCM sorts cold hands and feet into different patterns, such as 'yang deficiency', 'qi and blood deficiency' or 'constrained qi', each handled differently, which is why a TCM practitioner asks many other questions about the same complaint. PC8, KI1 and ST36 are points tradition uses to warm the hands and feet. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.中医常把手脚凉归到「阳虚」「气血不足」或「气机郁滞」等不同的证型，处理方向各不相同，这也是为什么同一个「手脚凉」中医师会问很多别的问题。劳宫、涌泉、足三里是传统上温养手足常用的穴。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
