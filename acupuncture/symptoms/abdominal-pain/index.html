Abdominal pain腹痛

Many points list 'abdominal pain', yet the cause may be cold, food stagnation, qi stagnation, blood stasis or spleen yang deficiency, and each needs different points and technique. In clinic you ask: sudden or slow, likes or refuses pressure, better or worse with warmth, where exactly it hurts, and how it relates to meals, bowel movements and emotions, and only then choose needle or moxa, tonify or drain. Abdominal pain has one extra requirement: surgical emergencies must be ruled out before any pattern-based point selection. This page sorts the patterns first, gives main and supporting points with reasons for each, and then compares the common abdominal-pain points in one table.很多穴的主治里都写着「腹痛」，但腹痛背后可能是受凉、食积、气滞、血瘀，也可能是脾阳不足，用穴和手法各不相同。临床上要先问：痛得急还是慢、喜按还是拒按、得热缓解还是得热加重、痛在哪个部位、跟吃饭、排便、情绪有什么关系，再决定针还是灸、补还是泻。腹痛还有一个特别的要求：必须先排除外科急腹症，再谈辨证选穴。这一页先分证型，给出每型的主穴、配穴和理由，再把常用腹痛穴放在同一张表里比较。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Sudden severe abdominal pain with a rigid board-like abdomen, unwillingness to move, vomiting, fever, cold sweats or shock-like signs (pallor, fast heartbeat, faintness): call 911; possible perforated appendix, perforated ulcer or bowel obstruction突然剧烈腹痛、腹部硬如板状、不敢动，伴呕吐、发热、冷汗或休克样表现（面色苍白、心跳快、头晕）：拨 911，可能是阑尾炎穿孔、消化道穿孔、肠梗阻等急腹症
  • Lower abdominal pain in a woman of childbearing age with a missed period, vaginal bleeding, shoulder-tip pain or dizziness: same-day emergency care to rule out ruptured ectopic pregnancy育龄女性下腹痛，伴停经、阴道出血或肩部放射痛、头晕：当天急诊，排除宫外孕破裂
  • Abdominal pain with vomiting blood, black or bloody stools, persistent vomiting with no fluid kept down, or bloating with no gas or stool passed: same-day care腹痛伴呕血、黑便或便血、持续呕吐不能进水、腹胀且停止排气排便：当天就医
  • Upper abdominal pain with chest tightness, cold sweats, breathlessness or pain spreading to the left arm or jaw (especially in older adults or those with hypertension or diabetes): call 911; rule out heart attack first上腹痛伴胸闷、出冷汗、气短、疼痛放射到左臂或下颌（尤其是中老年、有高血压糖尿病者）：拨 911，先排除心肌梗死
  • Right upper abdominal pain with fever, chills, or yellow skin or eyes: same-day care for possible cholangitis or cholecystitis; sudden severe pain in an older adult with a pulsating abdominal mass: call 911 to rule out aortic aneurysm右上腹痛伴发热寒战、皮肤或眼白发黄：当天就医，可能是胆管炎、胆囊炎；老年人突发剧烈腹痛、腹部可摸到搏动性肿块：拨 911，排除腹主动脉瘤
  • Infant with episodic screaming, drawn-up legs, vomiting and currant-jelly stools (intussusception); or abdominal pain beyond 3 weeks with weight loss, night waking or ongoing fever: prompt medical assessment婴幼儿阵发性哭闹、蜷腿、呕吐，排果酱样大便（肠套叠）；或腹痛持续超过 3 周、伴体重下降、夜间痛醒、持续发热：尽快就医检查
  • Significant abdominal pain in pregnancy, or with vaginal bleeding, leaking fluid or regular contractions: same-day obstetric emergency care. A teenage or young man with sudden lower abdominal or groin pain and a painful swollen scrotum: go to emergency at once to rule out testicular torsion, where hours decide whether the testis can be saved.孕妇出现明显腹痛，或伴阴道流血、流液、规律宫缩：当天产科急诊；青少年或年轻男性突发下腹或腹股沟痛伴阴囊肿痛：立即急诊，排除睾丸扭转（时间就是睾丸）
  • A person with diabetes who has abdominal pain with vomiting, thirst and frequent urination, deep rapid breathing or fruity breath: same-day emergency care to rule out diabetic ketoacidosis.糖尿病患者腹痛伴呕吐、口渴多尿、呼吸深快或呼气有烂苹果味：当天急诊，排除糖尿病酮症酸中毒

「胃中熱，腸中寒，則疾饑小腹痛脹」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.29: heat in the stomach with cold in the intestines gives rapid hunger together with lower abdominal pain and distension. Clinical meaning: one abdomen can hold mixed cold and heat, hot above and cold below, so the pain cannot be treated by simply warming or simply clearing — points are chosen separately above and below, for example ST44 to clear stomach heat and moxa at CV4 and CV8 to warm the lower burner.《灵枢·师传》：胃里有热、肠里有寒，会出现容易饥饿，同时小腹胀痛。临床意义：同一个人的腹部可以寒热错杂，上热下寒；这类痛不能单纯温中或单纯清热，选穴要上下分开，例如胃经清热用内庭，温下焦用关元、神阙灸。

「寒氣客於小腸，小腸不得成聚，故後泄腹痛矣」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.39: cold lodging in the small intestine stops it from holding and transforming food, so contents rush downward and pain comes with diarrhoea. This is the typical cold-pain picture: sudden, eased by warmth, often with loose stools. Clinical meaning: moxa is the first choice for cold pain, at CV8, CV4 and CV12, to warm yang and disperse cold.《素问·举痛论》：寒邪侵入小肠，小肠不能容留、化物，水谷不得停聚就往下走，所以腹痛同时腹泻。这是寒邪腹痛的典型：痛得急、得温则减、常伴泄泻。临床意义：寒痛首选灸，神阙、关元、中脘温阳散寒。

「厥氣客於陰股，寒氣上及少腹，血泣在下相引，故腹痛引陰股」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.39: cold counterflow qi lodges in the inner thigh and rises to the lower abdomen; blood congeals below and the two pull on each other, so the pain reaches into the inner thigh. The passage is about cold congealing the blood: cold congeals blood, and congealed blood hurts. Clinical meaning: fixed stabbing pain reaching the groin or inner thigh points to blood stasis, treated with SP10 and BL17; add LR3 and LR14 when qi stagnation is also present.《素问·举痛论》：厥逆的寒气留在大腿内侧，向上波及小腹，下部血液凝涩，上下相互牵引，所以腹痛连及大腿内侧。原文讲的是寒凝血涩：寒则血凝，凝则痛。临床意义：固定刺痛、痛连腹股沟或大腿内侧的腹痛要想到血瘀，取血海、膈俞活血；兼气滞的加太冲、期门行气。

Excess实证

Internal accumulation of cold寒邪内积

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden cramping cold pain, severe, relieved by warmth and worse in the cold; bland taste without thirst, cold hands and feet; clear copious urine, loose stools; white greasy coating, deep tight pulse. Often follows chilling or cold raw food.腹痛急起，拘急冷痛、疼痛剧烈，得热则减、遇冷加重；口淡不渴、手足不温；小便清长、大便稀溏；苔白腻，脉沉紧。常有受凉或生冷饮食史。

Principle治法 Warm the middle, disperse cold, regulate qi, stop pain (drain; generous moxa)温中散寒，理气止痛（针用泻法，重用灸）

Main主穴ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST25 Tiānshū天枢SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴CV8 Shénquè神阙CV4 Guānyuán关元

💡 CV12 is the front-mu point of the stomach and influential point of the fu organs, regulating the middle burner directly; ST25 is the front-mu point of the large intestine and regulates intestinal qi; ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, treats the fu organs and frees stomach and intestine qi and blood; SP6 strengthens the spleen and regulates lower abdominal qi and blood. CV8 with salt or ginger moxa and CV4 moxa warm yang and disperse cold.中脘是胃之募穴、腑会，直接调中焦；天枢是大肠募穴，调肠腑气机；足三里是胃经合穴，合治内腑，疏通胃肠气血；三阴交健脾、调下腹气血。神阙隔盐或隔姜灸、关元灸，温阳散寒。

Damp-heat stagnation湿热壅滞

How it presents辨证要点 Distending pain that refuses pressure with a burning feel; thirst, constipation or sticky unsatisfying loose stool, scanty dark urine; fever, sweating; yellow greasy coating, slippery rapid pulse.腹痛拒按，胀满，痛处有灼热感；烦渴引饮、大便秘结或溏滞不爽、小便短赤；身热、出汗；苔黄腻，脉滑数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, drain damp, free the fu organs, stop pain (drain; no moxa)清热利湿，通腑止痛（泻法，不灸）

ST25 acts locally on the large intestine where damp-heat stagnates.曲池是大肠经合穴，清热通腑；内庭是胃经荥穴（水），荥主身热，清胃肠湿热；阴陵泉是脾经合穴，利湿。天枢是大肠募穴，对大肠湿热壅滞局部通调。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern medicine sorts abdominal pain first by acuity and location. Acute pain must first be screened for causes needing surgery or emergency care (appendicitis, cholecystitis, bowel obstruction, perforation, pancreatitis, ectopic pregnancy, aortic aneurysm, heart attack and others). Common causes of chronic or recurrent pain include functional dyspepsia, irritable bowel syndrome, reflux, peptic ulcer, chronic gastritis, constipation, gallstones, celiac disease, inflammatory bowel disease, and in women dysmenorrhea, endometriosis and pelvic inflammatory disease. By location: right upper quadrant suggests biliary or liver causes; epigastric suggests stomach or pancreas (and always consider the heart); periumbilical early on suggests appendicitis or enteritis; right lower quadrant suggests appendicitis or gynecologic causes; left lower quadrant suggests diverticulitis or constipation; suprapubic suggests urinary infection or gynecologic causes. Any acute, severe or progressive abdominal pain, or pain with fever, vomiting, bloody stool or weight loss, needs medical assessment before acupuncture. TCM patterns do not map one-to-one onto these diagnoses, but they overlap roughly: cold accumulation resembles acute gastroenteritis or intestinal spasm; damp-heat may involve infection and must be investigated first; food stagnation resembles functional dyspepsia; liver qi stagnation overlaps closely with irritable bowel syndrome; blood stasis relates to adhesions, endometriosis and other chronic conditions; middle deficiency with cold is common in functional pain, chronic gastritis and diarrhea-predominant IBS. For menstrual abdominal pain see the dysmenorrhea symptom page.现代医学把腹痛先按急缓和部位分：急性腹痛要先排除需要手术或急救的原因（阑尾炎、胆囊炎、肠梗阻、消化道穿孔、胰腺炎、宫外孕、腹主动脉瘤、心肌梗死等）；慢性或反复腹痛常见原因包括功能性消化不良、肠易激综合征、胃食管反流、消化性溃疡、慢性胃炎、便秘、胆石症、乳糜泻、炎症性肠病，女性还要考虑痛经、子宫内膜异位症、盆腔炎。按部位看：右上腹多想胆道、肝；上腹想胃、胰腺（也要想心脏）；脐周早期想阑尾、肠炎；右下腹想阑尾、妇科；左下腹想憩室炎、便秘；耻骨上想泌尿系感染、妇科。任何急性、剧烈、进行性加重，或伴发热、呕吐、血便、体重下降的腹痛，都应先就医检查，再谈针灸。中医证型与这些病因不一一对应，大致交集是：寒邪内积常见于急性胃肠炎、胃肠痉挛；湿热壅滞可能涉及感染性疾病，必须先查；食积类似功能性消化不良；肝郁气滞与肠易激综合征关系较密切；瘀血与粘连、内异症等慢性病相关；中虚脏寒多见于功能性腹痛、慢性胃炎、腹泻型肠易激。女性经行腹痛可参考痛经症状页。
