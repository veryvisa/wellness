📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the forearm supinated, palm up and wrist relaxed; lightly flex the wrist and oppose thumb to little finger to identify palmaris longus.坐位或仰卧，前臂旋后、掌心向上，腕放松；轻屈腕并拇指对小指以辨认掌长肌腱。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The elbow crease to the distal palmar wrist crease is 12 cun. PC5 is 3 cun above the wrist crease, measured proximally along the anterior forearm midline.肘横纹至腕掌侧远端横纹为 12 寸；间使在腕横纹上 3 寸，沿前臂掌面中线向肘方向折量。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    PC5 lies between palmaris longus and flexor carpi radialis. Gentle wrist flexion shows the tendons; if palmaris longus is absent, use flexor carpi radialis and the midline bone measure. Cutaneous nerves and veins are superficial, with flexors and the median nerve deeper; do not probe blindly.间使在掌长肌腱与桡侧腕屈肌腱之间。轻屈腕可显肌腱；若掌长肌腱缺如，以桡侧腕屈肌腱及正中线骨度量定位。浅层有皮神经、静脉，深部为指屈肌群及正中神经，勿向深部盲探。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Identify the wrist crease and measure 3 cun toward the elbow. The hollow between the tendons at that level is the reference; do not shift onto a visibly pulsating vessel.先定腕横纹，再向肘侧量 3 寸；该高度两腱间的凹隙为定位参照，不能把量到的点移至明显血管搏动处。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate between the tendons while alternately flexing and relaxing the wrist. Ease pressure immediately if electric tingling reaches the fingers; paresthesia is not confirmation of the point.指腹轻触两腱之间，在腕轻屈与放松时确认腱间隙；压迫出现电击样手指麻感时立即减压，不能把麻感当作取穴确认。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Confusing PC5 with PC6 at 2 cun or PC4 at 5 cun shifts the point; remeasure 3 cun from the wrist crease rather than estimating from the tendon groove alone.把腕横纹上 2 寸的内关（PC6）或 5 寸的郄门（PC4）误作间使：需从腕横纹重折 3 寸，不能以掌长肌腱沟单独估距。

Where is PC5 Jiānshǐ?间使（PC5）在哪里？

Anterior forearm, 3 cun above the wrist crease, between palmaris longus and flexor carpi radialis tendons.前臂前面，腕横纹上3寸，掌长肌腱与桡侧腕屈肌腱之间。

How is PC5 needled?间使（PC5）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1寸。

Special-point roles of PC5?间使（PC5）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-river point, metal经穴，属金

Main safety point at PC5?间使（PC5）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid superficial veins; withdraw for electric paresthesia and do not repeatedly manipulate toward the median nerve.避开浅静脉；有电击样麻感时退针，不在正中神经方向反复操作。
