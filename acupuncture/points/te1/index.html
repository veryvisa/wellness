Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

Traditionally used for sore throat associated with the Hand-Shaoyang channel; high fever, drooling, or breathing or swallowing difficulty requires urgent assessment rather than point selection.传统用于手少阳相关咽喉肿痛；急性高热、流涎、呼吸或吞咽困难不是选穴问题，应先急诊评估。
