Where is SP8 Dìjī?地机（SP8）在哪里？

On the medial leg, 3 cun below SP9, posterior to the medial border of the tibia.小腿内侧，阴陵泉下 3 寸，胫骨内侧缘后际。

How is SP8 needled?地机（SP8）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SP8?地机（SP8）是哪类特定穴？

Xi-cleft point郄穴

Avoid the great saphenous vein; do not angle deep posteromedially.避开大隐静脉；不向后内侧深刺。
