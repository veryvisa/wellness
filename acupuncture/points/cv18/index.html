    Behind the sternum lie the mediastinum and pericardium, with pleura and lungs on each side; needle only transversely under the skin 0.3–0.5 cun, never perpendicular or toward the chest cavity.胸骨后方为纵隔及心包区域，两侧邻胸膜、肺；胸前高风险，针刺只沿皮下平刺 0.3–0.5 寸，避免直刺与朝胸腔方向。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Count gently from the second rib at the sternal angle to the third interspace and confirm the midline; local sternal tenderness is not a substitute for rib localization.从胸骨角处的第 2 肋轻数至第 3 肋间并确认正中线；不以胸骨局部压痛代替肋间定位。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Cross-check its position above CV17 at the fourth space and below CV19 at the second; use rib counting despite variation in body habitus.与膻中（第 4 肋间）及紫宫（第 2 肋间）上下顺序互校；体型变化时始终以肋间计数为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Confusing the third rib with the third interspace shifts the point by one level; nipple height is not fixed. Deep insertion can injure pleura, lung or mediastinum, so keep any procedure superficial.错把第 3 肋当第 3 肋间会整体偏一格；乳头水平并非固定标志。偏深可伤胸膜、肺或纵隔，必须浅表并避开胸腔方向。

Transverse; 0.3–0.5 cun.平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at CV18?玉堂（CV18）最要注意的安全问题？

Behind the sternum lie the pericardium and mediastinum, and laterally the intercostal space and pleura (pneumothorax): transverse subcutaneous needling only.胸骨后为心包与纵隔，偏外即肋间与胸膜（气胸）：只沿皮下平刺，不直刺、不偏外。
