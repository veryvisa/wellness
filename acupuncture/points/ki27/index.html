Point of the Kidney channel足少阴肾经穴Last point of the Kidney channel足少阴经终止穴
⚠️ Pneumothorax and vascular-risk point气胸与血管风险穴 The pleura and lung apex lie directly beneath Shūfǔ, very shallow in thin people. Use only 0.5–0.8 cun oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the infraclavicular chest wall; never perpendicular or deep, never medially, never behind the clavicle. Oblique or transverse, laterally along the infraclavicular chest wall斜刺或平刺（沿锁骨下胸壁向外） · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never perpendicular or deep, to avoid the heart and lungs.不可直刺、深刺，以免伤及心、肺。

    Across the anterior chest, the inter-nipple distance equals 8 cun for proportional reference, and KI27 lies at the inferior clavicular border, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline; verify against the inferior clavicular border and first intercostal space rather than relying on soft-tissue estimation alone.前胸壁横向以两乳头之间折作 8 寸（或以胸骨中线旁开骨度折算），俞府位于锁骨下缘、前正中线旁开 2 寸处；临床常结合第 1 肋间隙与锁骨下缘骨性标志核对，不单独依赖软组织横向估量。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    For 2 cun lateral to the midline use the patient's index, middle and ring fingers together (about 2 cun), or half the distance to the midclavicular line (4 cun); the vertical level comes only from counting intercostal spaces, never from finger measurement.前正中线旁开 2 寸可用患者食指、中指、无名指三指并拢（约 2 寸）自前正中线向外量取，或取锁骨中线（距中线 4 寸）的一半；纵向高度只看肋间隙，不靠指量。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    KI27 is under the clavicle: skin and subcutaneous tissue (anterior supraclavicular nerves), the clavicular head of pectoralis major and subclavius; the subclavian vein lies deep behind the clavicle, and the cervical pleura and lung apex deep inferomedially. Insert only oblique or transverse 0.5–0.8 cun laterally along the infraclavicular chest wall; never perpendicular or deep, never inferomedially or behind the clavicle.俞府在锁骨下缘，浅层为皮肤、皮下组织（有锁骨上神经前支）、胸大肌锁骨部和锁骨下肌；锁骨后方深处有锁骨下静脉，内下方深面为胸膜顶与肺尖。只沿锁骨下胸壁向外斜刺或平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，严禁直刺、深刺，不向内下方或锁骨后方探针。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently just inferior to the clavicle about 2 cun lateral to the midline for a slight hollow or aching tenderness; if a clear pulse or radiating paresthesia is felt, shift to avoid vessels or nerve branches and keep pressure light.用指腹在锁骨下缘外侧、距前正中线约 2 寸处轻按，多可触及微凹或局部酸胀感；若触及明显搏动或放射麻电感，提示靠近血管或神经分支，应调整避开并控制按压力度。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Inserting perpendicularly, deeply, or inferomedially: the needle tip can injure the pleura and lung apex inferomedially, risking pneumothorax or hemopneumothorax.直刺或深刺、向内下方刺入：针尖可刺伤内下方的胸膜与肺尖，引发气胸或血气胸，临床上属于高危操作失误。
  • Probing behind the clavicle or deep: can injure the subclavian vein behind the clavicle, causing a deep haematoma that is hard to compress.向锁骨后方或深部探针：可伤及锁骨后方的锁骨下静脉，形成难以压迫止血的深部血肿。
  • Drifting too far medially or laterally: moving medially nears the sternal margin and pleura/lung apex, while moving laterally approaches the lateral pectoralis major, coracoid process, and LU2, changing both target anatomy and safety margins.取穴过偏内侧或过偏外侧：偏内侧易误入胸骨缘或更靠近胸膜肺尖区域，偏外侧则接近胸大肌外侧缘与喙突、云门（LU2）一带，主治与刺法安全边界都会改变。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

Suited when cough accompanies lung-kidney qi deficiency, lingering shortness of breath, or chest tightness; for early external wind-cold/wind-heat or acute heat cough, prioritize points such as LU7, LI4, or BL13 rather than relying on KI27.偏肺肾气虚、久咳气短或伴胸闷者可酌情选用；急性外感风寒风热初起或实热咳喘多先选列缺、合谷、肺俞等，不以俞府单穴为主。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Useful as a supportive point for deficient wheezing, shortness of breath, or chest fullness, but acute wheezing with marked dyspnea requires prompt medical bronchodilator and emergency care rather than KI27 alone.虚喘、气短纳气不足或胸膺闷胀者可配伍调理；急性发作期呼吸困难明显者应先规范西医急救与平喘治疗，不可单靠俞府缓解。

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

Can pair with KI6 or KI3 when mild throat dryness accompanies lung-kidney yin or qi-yin deficiency; for acute red, swollen, painful throat, choose points such as LU11 or LI4 instead of KI27.偏肺肾阴虚或气阴两虚伴咽干不适、气短者可配照海、太溪调理；咽喉急性红肿热痛明显者多选少商、合谷等，不首选俞府。

Coughing up blood咯血 ⚖️

Hemoptysis is a potentially serious clinical sign requiring immediate medical evaluation and hemostatic care; never rely on KI27 or acupuncture alone as a primary or standalone measure for hemoptysis.咯血属急危重症征象，必须先就医明确病因并止血抢救，绝不可仅靠俞府或针灸处理，临床上不作为咯血首选或独立疗法。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Thoracic outlet syndrome or subclavian neurovascular compression胸廓出口综合征（TOS）或臂丛、锁骨下血管受压相关不适

Pectoralis major strain or anterior chest-wall myofascial pain胸大肌劳损或前胸壁肌筋膜痛

Cough, wheezing, or chest tightness related to airway infection, asthma, or COPD呼吸道感染、哮喘或慢阻肺相关的咳嗽、气喘与胸闷

Where is KI27 Shūfǔ?俞府（KI27）在哪里？

On the chest, inferior to the clavicle, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，锁骨下缘，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

How is KI27 needled?俞府（KI27）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse, laterally along the infraclavicular chest wall; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺或平刺（沿锁骨下胸壁向外）；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of KI27?俞府（KI27）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Kidney channel; Last point of the Kidney channel足少阴肾经穴；足少阴经终止穴

Main safety point at KI27?俞府（KI27）最要注意的安全问题？

The cervical pleura and lung apex lie deep inferomedially and the subclavian vein deep behind the clavicle: oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the chest wall only — never perpendicular or deep, never inferomedially or behind the clavicle.胸膜顶与肺尖在内下方深面，锁骨后方深处有锁骨下静脉：只沿胸壁向外斜刺或平刺，绝不直刺、深刺，不向内下方或锁骨后方探针。
