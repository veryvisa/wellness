On the lateral upper arm, 7 cun superior to LI11, just anterior to the border of the deltoid muscle.上臂外侧，曲池上 7 寸，三角肌前缘紧前方。

Perpendicular; 0.8–1.5 cun.直刺；0.8–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of LI14?臂臑（LI14）是哪类特定穴？
