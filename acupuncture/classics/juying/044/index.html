必欲治病。莫如用鍼。巧運神機之妙。工開聖理之深。外取砭鍼。能蠲邪而輔正。中含水火。善回陽而倒陰。原夫絡別支殊。經交錯綜。或溝渠谿谷以岐異。或山海丘陵而隙共。斯流派以難揆。在條綱而有統。理繁而昧。縱補瀉以何功。法捷而明。自隨而得用。且如行步難移。太衝最奇。人中除脊膂之強痛。神門去心性之呆癡。風傷項急。始求於風府。頭暈目眩。要覓於風池。耳閉須聽會而治也。眼痛則合谷以推之。胸結身黃。取湧泉而即可。腦昏目赤。瀉攢竹以便宜。但見兩肘之拘攣。仗曲池而平掃。四肢之懈惰。憑照海以消除。牙齒痛。呂細堪治。頭項強。承漿可保。太白宣導於氣衝。陰陵開通於水道。腹(左月右真)而脹。奪內庭以休遲。筋轉而疼。瀉承山而在早。大抵腳腕痛。崐崙解愈。股膝疼。陰市能醫。癇發癲狂兮。憑後谿而療理。瘧生寒熱兮。仗間使以扶持。期門罷胸滿血膨而可已。勞宮退胃翻心痛亦何疑。稽夫大敦去七疝之偏墜。王公謂此。三里卻五勞之羸瘦。華佗言斯。固知腕骨祛黃。然谷瀉腎。行間治膝腫腰疼。尺澤去肘疼筋緊。目昏不見。二間宜取。鼻窒無聞。迎香可引。肩井除兩胛難任。絲竹空療偏頭疼不忍。咳嗽寒痰。列缺堪憑。眵(左目右蔑)冷淚。臨泣尤准。髖骨將腿疼以祛殘。腎俞把腰疼而瀉盡。以見越人治尸厥於維會。隨手而甦。文伯瀉死胎於三陰。應鍼而隕。所謂諸痛為實。但麻曰虛。實則自外而入也。虛則自內而出歟。是故濟母而裨其不足。奪子而平其有餘。觀二十七之經絡。一一明辨。據四百四之疾證。件件皆除。故得夭枉都無。躋斯民於壽域。幾微已判。彰往古之玄書。抑又聞心胸病。求掌後之大陵。肩背疼。責肘前之三里。冷痹腎敗。取足陽明之土。連臍腹痛。瀉足少陰之水。脊間心後者。鍼中渚而立痊。脅下肋邊者。刺陽陵而即止。頭項痛。擬後谿以安然。腰腳疼。在委中而已矣。夫用鍼之士。於此理茍能明焉。收祛邪之功。而在乎撚指。

如果一定要治疗疾病，没有比使用针刺更好的方法了。巧妙地运用神妙的气机，精巧地阐发圣贤医理的深奥。在体外运用砭石与针具，能够祛除病邪并扶助正气；针法之中含有水火（凉与热）之用，善于回复阳气、扭转阴盛。推究经络的原本，络脉分支各异，经脉交错纵横，有的像沟渠溪谷那样分岔不同，有的像山海丘陵那样在空隙处相通。这些流派虽然难以逐一揣测，但在经脉纲领上却有统属。如果道理繁杂却昏昧不清，即使施行补泻又有什么功效？唯有方法简捷明了，才能随手取用而得心应手。譬如行走困难、步履难移，选太冲穴最为奇效；脊柱强直疼痛，可用水沟（人中）解除；心神呆滞痴傻，可用神门祛除。风邪伤及导致项背拘急，首先寻求风府；头晕目眩，要寻找风池；耳聋耳闭，必须用听会来治疗；眼痛，则用合谷来推导疏通；胸中痞结、全身发黄，取涌泉即可缓解；头脑昏沉、眼睛红赤，泻攒竹最为合适；只要见到双肘拘挛，就依靠曲池来平复清除；四肢困倦懈怠，凭借照海来消除；牙齿疼痛，吕细可以治疗（吕细所指见篇末按语）；头项强痛，承浆可以稳固缓解；太白能够宣导上冲之气；阴陵能够开通疏理水道；腹部䐜胀，应当及早泻内庭不可迟缓；抽筋疼痛，应当尽早泻承山；大凡脚踝疼痛，昆仑可以解除治愈；大腿与膝盖疼痛，阴市能够医治；癫痫发作、癫狂失常，凭借后溪来调理；疟疾发作寒热往来，依靠间使来扶持正气；胸满、血膨（腹部胀大或血瘀胀满），取期门可以停止消除；胃中翻腾、心痛，取劳宫也毫无怀疑；考究大敦能够祛除七疝偏坠，这是王公所言；足三里能够退除五劳导致的羸瘦，这是华佗所说。固然知道腕骨可以祛除黄疸，然谷可以泻肾邪，行间可以治疗膝肿与腰痛，尺泽可以祛除肘痛与筋紧；视力昏花看不清，宜取二间；鼻塞不通闻不到气味，可用迎香引导；肩井可以消除双肩胛难以承受的沉重疼痛；丝竹空可以治疗难以忍受的偏头痛；咳嗽伴有寒痰，可以凭借列缺；眼屎多、迎风冷泪，临泣尤为准确；髋骨穴可以把腿痛祛除；肾俞可以把腰痛泻除干净。由此可见扁鹊（越人）针维会穴治疗尸厥，随手就苏醒；徐文伯在三阴交穴泻死胎，针到胎下。所谓'诸痛为实，但麻曰虚'，实邪大多是从外侵入的，虚则是由内而生的吧。因此'补母以助其不足，泻子以平其有余'。观察二十七经络（十二经加十五络），一一明辨；依据四百零四种病证，件件都能祛除。所以能够使夭折横死都不发生，把百姓提升到长寿的境界。精微之处已经分辨清楚，彰显了往古的玄妙医书。再听闻心胸疾病，寻求手掌后的大陵；肩背疼痛，责之于肘前的三里（手三里）；寒痹导致肾气败坏，取足阳明胃经的土穴（足三里）；连及脐腹的疼痛，泻足少阴肾经的水穴（阴谷）；脊柱之间、心脏后方疼痛，针刺中渚就能立刻痊愈；胁下肋缘疼痛，针刺阳陵泉就能立即停止；头项疼痛，选用后溪就能安然缓解；腰腿疼痛，只取委中就可以了。凡是使用针刺的医士，对于这些道理如果能够明白，那么取得祛邪功效，全在于捻转手指之间。

If one is determined to treat disease, nothing surpasses the use of acupuncture needles. Skillfully mobilizing the subtlety of spirit-qi and adeptly unfolding the depth of sage doctrines, one externally employs stone and metal needles to expel pathogens and support the upright qi, while within the needle holding both water and fire (cooling and warming) to restore yang and reverse yin excesses. Tracing the origin of collaterals and branches, meridians intersect and weave together—sometimes diverging like channels, streams, and valleys, and sometimes communicating through gaps like mountains, seas, hills, and mounds. These streams and branches are difficult to fathom on their own, yet they are unified under overarching principles. If the principles are complex yet obscure, what effect can tonification and sedation have even if applied? When the method is swift and clear, one can adaptively put it to use. For instance, when walking is difficult and steps can barely move, Taichong (LR3) is most extraordinary. Renzhong / Shuigou (GV26) removes stiffness and pain along the spine. Shenmen (HT7) dispels dullness and confusion of the heart-mind. When wind injures the neck causing acute stiffness, first seek Fengfu (GV16). For dizziness and vertigo, look for Fengchi (GB20). Deafness and ear blockage must be treated with Tinghui (GB2). Eye pain is guided and relieved with Hegu (LI4). Chest binding and body jaundice are addressed by taking Yongquan (KI1). Mental fog and red eyes are appropriately treated by sedating Cuanzhu (BL2). Whenever stiffness and contracture of both elbows appear, rely on Quchi (LI11) to sweep them away. Fatigue and lassitude of the four limbs are eliminated through Zhaohai (KI6). Toothache can be treated with Lüxi (identified with Taixi, KI3, by some; see the closing comment). Stiffness of the head and neck can be stabilized with Chengjiang (CV24). Taibai (SP3) conducts and settles qi that surges upward. Yinlingquan (SP9) opens and clears the water passageways. Abdominal distension should be sedated at Neiting (ST44) without delay. Muscle cramps and spasms with pain should be sedated at Chengshan (BL57) early on. In general, ankle pain is resolved and cured by Kunlun (BL60). Pain of the thigh and knee can be treated by Yinshi (ST33). When epilepsy or mania and madness erupt, rely on Houxi (SI3) to treat and regulate it. When malaria generates alternating chills and fever, rely on Jianshi (PC5) to support the body. Chest fullness and blood distension can be stopped and resolved by Qimen (LR14). Laogong (PC8) reduces stomach churning and heart pain without doubt. Examining that Dadun (LR1) removes unilateral descent in the seven hernias is what Wang Gong stated; that Zusanli (ST36) dispels emaciation from the five overstrains is what Hua Tuo said. It is certainly known that Wangu (SI4) dispels jaundice, Rangu (KI2) sedates kidney excess, Xingjian (LR2) treats knee swelling and lower back pain, and Chize (LU5) removes elbow pain and tight sinews. For dim vision and inability to see, Erjian (LI2) should be taken. For nasal congestion with loss of smell, Yingxiang (LI20) can draw it open. Jianjing (GB21) removes unbearable heaviness and pain across both scapulae. Sizhukong (TE23) treats intolerable unilateral headache. For cough with cold phlegm, Lieque (LU7) can be relied upon. For purulent eye discharge and cold tearing, Linqi (Toulinqi, GB15) is especially accurate. The point Kuangu (“hip-bone”) expels leg pain, and Shenshu (BL23) sedates and clears lower back pain. Thus we see how Bian Que (Yue Ren) treated “corpse reversal” (sudden deathlike collapse) at Weihui, reviving the patient at a touch of the hand, and how Xu Wenbo purged a dead fetus at Sanyinjiao (SP6), causing expulsion right as the needle took effect. The saying goes: 'All pains are excess, whereas numbness indicates deficiency.' Excess generally enters from without, while deficiency, it seems, arises from within. Therefore, one tonifies the mother to supplement its deficiency and drains the child to level its excess. Observing the twenty-seven meridians and collaterals, one clearly distinguishes each one; based on the four hundred and four disease patterns, each and every one is eliminated. Thus premature and unnatural death does not occur, and the people are raised to the realm of longevity. Subtle nuances are already clarified, illuminating the abstruse medical classics of antiquity. Furthermore, we hear that for heart and chest diseases, seek Daling (PC7) behind the palm; for shoulder and back pain, look to Sanli before the elbow (Hand Sanli, LI10, distinct from Foot Sanli, ST36); for cold bi-impediment with kidney decline, take the earth point of Foot Yangming (Stomach meridian, ST36, Zusanli); for pain extending to the umbilicus and abdomen, drain the water point of Foot Shaoyin (Yingu, KI10); for pain between the spine and behind the heart, needle Zhongzhu (TE3) for prompt recovery; for pain along the lateral ribs and hypochondrium, puncture Yanglingquan (GB34) for immediate cessation; for head and neck pain, select Houxi (SI3) for calm relief; for lower back and leg pain, rely simply on Weizhong (BL40). For an acupuncture practitioner, if one can clearly understand these principles, achieving the efficacy of expelling pathogens lies right between the twisting fingers.

💡 本段为《通玄指要赋》（又名《流注指要赋》）全文，金元窦汉卿撰，高武据《卫生宝鉴》收录并附按语。1. 术语与医理：'砭鍼'指古代石针与金属针；'回陽而倒陰'指温阳散寒以纠正阴盛；'諸痛為實，但麻曰虛'概括痛多属实邪阻滞、麻木多属气血不足或经气虚馁的辨证经验，但临床需结合寒热虚实综合判断，不可机械套用；'濟母而裨其不足，奪子而平其有餘'即五行生克补泻法（虚则补其母，实则泻其子）；'二十七之經絡'即十二经加十五络；'四百四之疾證'即“四百四病”，是源于佛教医学“四大各生一百零一病”的约数，《千金方》已用。2. 涉及穴位（WHO代码）：太冲 LR3、人中/水沟 GV26、神门 HT7、风府 GV16、风池 GB20、听会 GB2、合谷 LI4、涌泉 KI1、攒竹 BL2、曲池 LI11、照海 KI6、太白 SP3、阴陵泉 SP9、内庭 ST44、承山 BL57、昆仑 BL60、阴市 ST33、后溪 SI3、间使 PC5、期门 LR14、劳宫 PC8、大敦 LR1、足三里 ST36、腕骨 SI4、然谷 KI2、行间 LR2、尺泽 LU5、二间 LI2、迎香 LI20、肩井 GB21、丝竹空 TE23、列缺 LU7、头临泣 GB15（《针灸大成》本注明“头临泣穴”；也有人解作足临泣 GB41）、髋骨（《针灸大成》注有委中上三寸、膝髌上一寸即梁丘等异说，经外奇穴髋骨在梁丘两旁，不是环跳）、肾俞 BL23、三阴交 SP6、大陵 PC7、手三里 LI10（文中'肘前之三里'应结合上肢'肩背疼'辨析，与足三里 ST36 区分）、中渚 TE3、阳陵泉 GB34、委中 BL40、阴谷 KI10。“維會”所指各书不同：《史记》记扁鹊取“三阳五会”（多解作百会 GV20），《针灸大成》注作外踝上三寸、又说即玉泉，不宜定于一说；“呂細”见下一段按语。3. 当代适用与现代医学对照：所列多为经络穴位主治歌诀，具有较强的临床经验总结价值与考试记忆意义（如'面口合谷收''头项寻列缺''腰背委中求'等歌诀思想的源流之一）。现代医学可将其对应为躯体与内脏反射、节段神经支配调节、局部软组织肌张力与微循环改善、自主神经功能调节及中枢镇痛机制。需注意古代'尸厥''死胎'等描述在现代急危重症与产科中必须优先采用现代急救与产科规范处理，针灸仅可作为特定情境下的辅助或历史文献参考，不可延误抢救。

右流注指要賦。羅謙甫謂竇子聲裁就。今自衛生寶鑒表錄於此。所可疑者。舊注。牙齒痛。呂細堪治-{云}-。膀胱經。一名太谿。今按資生經、千金、銅人俱無太谿為呂細別名。而太谿為足少陰腎經。非膀胱經也。

以上是《流注指要赋》。罗谦甫（罗天益）说是窦子声（窦默，字子声，又字汉卿）裁定编成的。现在从《卫生宝鉴》中摘录抄录在这里。其中值得怀疑的是，旧注说'牙齿痛，吕细堪治'，并注云属于膀胱经，又名太溪。如今考证《针灸资生经》《备急千金要方》《新铸铜人腧穴针灸图经》，都没有记载太溪是吕细的别名，而且太溪（KI3）是足少阴肾经的穴位，不是足太阳膀胱经的穴位。

The above is the Rhapsody on the Essentials of Flow and Pouring (Liuzhu Zhiyao Fu). Luo Qianfu (Luo Tianyi) stated that it was composed by Dou Zisheng (Dou Mo, also known as Dou Hanqing), and it is here excerpted and recorded from the Weisheng Baojian (Treasured Mirror of Health). What is doubtful is an old commentary on 'Toothache can be treated with Lüxi,' which states that it belongs to the Bladder meridian and is also called Taixi. Upon examining the Zhenjiu Zisheng Jing, Beiji Qianjin Yaofang, and Xinzhu Tongren Shuxue Zhenjiu Tujing, none records Taixi as an alias for Lüxi; moreover, Taixi (KI3) is an acupoint of the Foot Shaoyin Kidney meridian, not the Bladder meridian.

💡 本段为高武按语，体现严谨的文献考据精神。1. 文献与穴位（WHO代码）：涉及太溪 KI3（属足少阴肾经，非足太阳膀胱经）。高武通过核对《针灸资生经》《备急千金要方》《铜人腧穴针灸图经》三部重要古籍，指出旧注把'吕细'说成太溪、又归入膀胱经，查无依据且归经有误。不过后世通行的读法仍以吕细为太溪别名，《针灸大成》所载此赋径作“太谿堪治”。2. 当代适用与现代医学对照：在针灸古籍学习、学术考据及执业医师/考研中，准确辨析经络归属（如太溪 KI3 属肾经而非膀胱经）是基本功。现代解剖与神经节段理论虽不以'膀胱经/肾经'划分结构，但在临床精确定位与循经取穴时，必须依据权威经穴标准（如 WHO 编码与国家标准经穴部位）以避免误取误刺。
