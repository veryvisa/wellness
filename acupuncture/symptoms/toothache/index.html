Toothache牙痛

Two things about toothache: acupuncture can ease the pain but cannot fill a cavity or drain pus — once the pain eases, see a dentist. The textbook main set for every pattern is LI4, ST6 and ST7: LI4 is the distal first choice ("face and mouth, LI4"), ST6 leans to the lower teeth and ST7 to the upper. Then add points for wind-fire, stomach fire or deficiency fire. The hand Yangming enters the lower teeth and the foot Yangming the upper, which guides distal choice. The wind-cold and caries patterns come from dental pattern theory and the Lingshu, as labelled.牙痛要记住两条：针灸能止痛，但补不了蛀洞、引不了脓——止住痛之后必须看牙医。教材主穴是合谷、颊车、下关，各型都用：合谷是「面口合谷收」的远端首选，颊车偏下牙、下关偏上牙。再按风火、胃火、虚火加配穴。手阳明经「入下齿中」、足阳明经「入上齿中」，这是分上牙下牙选远端穴的依据。风寒与龋齿两型取自口腔科辨证和《灵枢》，页内注明。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Rapidly spreading swelling of the face, jaw or neck, inability to open the mouth, trouble swallowing, drooling, laboured breathing or a raised tongue: call 911 for a deep jaw infection (Ludwig angina).脸、下颌或颈部肿胀迅速扩大，张不开口，吞咽困难、流口水、呼吸费力或舌头被顶起：立即拨 911，警惕深部颌面感染（Ludwig 咽峡炎）。
  • Jaw or tooth pain with chest pressure, breathlessness or cold sweat, or that worsens with exertion: call 911 — a heart attack can show only as lower jaw and tooth pain.下颌或牙痛伴胸闷、气短、冷汗，或活动时加重：立即拨 911，心梗可以只表现为下牙和下颌痛。
  • Toothache with fever, facial swelling, swelling around the eye or vision change: emergency the same day; infection may be spreading to the orbit.牙痛伴发热、脸肿、眼周肿胀或视力改变：当天急诊，感染可能向眼眶扩散。
  • A knocked-out adult tooth: hold the crown, not the root, rinse gently, put it back or keep it in milk, and see a dentist within 30–60 minutes; do not replant a baby tooth, but still see a dentist the same day.外伤撞掉恒牙：捏牙冠不捏牙根，清水轻冲后尽快放回原位或泡在牛奶里，30–60 分钟内到牙科；乳牙不要塞回去，但也要当天看牙医。
  • Spontaneous pain, pain that wakes you, lasting hot or cold pain, or a gum boil: see a dentist promptly — this is pulpitis or an abscess that needs root canal treatment or drainage and will not settle on its own.自发痛、夜间痛醒、冷热刺激痛持续不退，或牙龈有脓包：尽快看牙医，这是牙髓炎或脓肿，需要根管治疗或引流，不是忍一忍会好的。
  • Over 50 with jaw pain on chewing, new headache or blurred vision: see a doctor the same day for giant cell arteritis.50 岁以上咀嚼时下颌痛、伴新头痛或视物模糊：当天就医，警惕巨细胞动脉炎。

「齒動痛，不惡清飲，取足陽明；惡清飲，取手陽明」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi, book 12: for toothache that does not mind cold drinks, use the foot Yangming; for pain that dislikes cold drinks, use the hand Yangming. Channels were chosen by reaction to cold; today pain with cold also suggests a pulp problem.《针灸甲乙经》卷十二：牙痛，不怕冷饮的取足阳明经，怕冷饮的取手阳明经。这是古人按怕不怕冷来分经取穴；冷刺激痛在今天还要想到牙髓问题。

「失欠，下齒齲，下牙痛，腫，下關主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi, book 12: ST7 governs the jaw slipping out on yawning, decay of the lower teeth and lower toothache with swelling. The textbook's ST7 comes straight from here; decay and swelling still need a dentist.《针灸甲乙经》卷十二：打哈欠时下颌脱开（失欠）、下齿龋、下牙痛肿，下关主治。教材主穴里的下关直接出自这里；龋齿和肿胀仍要牙科处理病因。

「虛則齒寒痹膈，取之所別也」——《灵枢·经脉第十》（《黄帝内经》，公版）

Lingshu ch.10, on LI6, the luo point of the hand Yangming: excess gives decay and deafness, deficiency gives cold teeth, and the luo point treats both — the basis for adding LI6 in decay or cold-sensitive teeth.《灵枢·经脉》手阳明络穴偏历：「实则龋聋，虚则齿寒」——实证蛀牙、耳聋，虚证牙齿怕冷，都取偏历。这是龋齿和牙齿怕冷加偏历的依据。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Excess实证

Wind-fire toothache风火牙痛

How it presents辨证要点 Paroxysmal toothache with red swollen gums, worse with wind and heat and eased by cold; fever, aversion to wind, thirst; red tongue, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse.牙痛阵发、牙龈红肿，遇风、遇热加重，得凉则减；伴发热、恶风、口渴；舌红苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat, reduce swelling and stop pain (drain)疏风清热，消肿止痛（泻法）

Main主穴LI4 Hégǔ合谷ST6 Jiáchē颊车ST7 Xiàguān下关
Add配穴TE5 Wàiguān外关GB20 Fēngchí风池

💡 Main set: LI4 is the Large Intestine yuan point and the hand Yangming enters the lower teeth — the distal first choice; ST6 and ST7 free local qi in the cheek. For wind-fire add TE5, which opens the Yang Wei and disperses wind, and GB20 to expel wind. LI4 is forbidden in pregnancy.主穴：合谷是大肠经原穴，手阳明经入下齿中，是牙痛远端首选；颊车、下关在局部，疏通面颊经气。风火加外关（通阳维）疏风、风池祛风。合谷孕妇禁针。

Excess实证

Stomach-fire toothache胃火牙痛

How it presents辨证要点 Severe toothache with red swollen gums, perhaps pus or bleeding, hot swollen cheek, foul breath, thirst, constipation, dark urine; red tongue, yellow coating, surging rapid pulse.牙痛剧烈，牙龈红肿甚至流脓出血，颊腮肿热，口臭口渴，便秘尿黄；舌红苔黄，脉洪数。

Principle治法 Clear the stomach, drain fire, reduce swelling and stop pain (drain)清胃泻火，消肿止痛（泻法）

Main主穴LI4 Hégǔ合谷ST6 Jiáchē颊车ST7 Xiàguān下关
Add配穴ST44 Nèitíng内庭LI2 Èrjiān二间

💡 ST44 and LI2 are the ying-spring points of the Stomach and Large Intestine; "ying points govern fever", and as the foot Yangming enters the upper teeth and the hand Yangming the lower, they clear both Yangming channels.内庭是胃经荥穴、二间是大肠经荥穴，「荥主身热」，足阳明入上齿、手阳明入下齿，一上一下两条阳明同清。

Deficiency虚证

Deficiency-fire toothache (kidney yin)虚火牙痛（肾阴不足）

How it presents辨证要点 Dull intermittent toothache, worse at night, slightly red and swollen gums, loose teeth weak for biting; sore back and knees, hot palms and soles, dry mouth; red tongue, little coating, thin rapid pulse.牙痛隐隐、时作时止，夜间较重，牙龈微红微肿，牙齿松动、咬物无力；腰膝酸软，手足心热，口干；舌红少苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin, bring down fire and stop pain (tonify KI3, drain LR2, even method locally)滋阴降火止痛（太溪补，行间泻，局部平补平泻）

Main主穴LI4 Hégǔ合谷ST6 Jiáchē颊车ST7 Xiàguān下关
Add配穴KI3 Tàixī太溪LR2 Xíngjiān行间

💡 KI3, the Kidney yuan point, nourishes kidney yin ("the kidney governs bone, and teeth are the surplus of bone"); LR2, the Liver ying point, drains fire. Loose teeth also need a check for gum disease, not a kidney-deficiency label alone.太溪是肾经原穴，补肾阴（「肾主骨，齿为骨之余」）；行间是肝经荥穴，泻虚火。牙齿松动还要查牙周病，不能只按肾虚。

Excess实证

Wind-cold toothache (added from dental pattern theory)风寒牙痛（口腔科辨证补充）

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden pulling toothache worse with cold wind or drinks and slightly better with warmth, gums not red or swollen, chills; pale tongue, white coating, tight pulse. Cold sensitivity can also be early pulpitis or a cracked tooth, which a dentist must tell apart.牙痛突作、抽掣感，遇冷风冷饮加重、得热稍减，牙龈不红不肿，怕冷；舌淡苔白，脉紧。怕冷也可能是牙髓炎早期或牙裂，需牙科鉴别。

Principle治法 Disperse wind-cold and stop pain (even method; gentle local warmth or moxa)疏风散寒止痛（平补平泻；局部可温灸或热敷）

Main主穴LI4 Hégǔ合谷ST6 Jiáchē颊车ST7 Xiàguān下关
Add配穴GB20 Fēngchí风池LU7 Lièquē列缺

💡 Acupuncture textbooks usually list wind-fire, stomach fire and deficiency fire; wind-cold is added from dental theory. GB20 expels wind and LU7 releases the exterior, pairing with LI4 as luo and yuan points. Do not warm an acutely infected mouth.针灸教材多列风火、胃火、虚火三型，风寒型按口腔科补充：风池祛风，列缺宣散解表并与合谷原络相配。口内急性化脓时不加热。

Branch标证

Toothache from decay龋齿牙痛

How it presents辨证要点 A visible cavity, pain when food lodges or with sweet, hot or cold; gums usually not swollen; tongue and pulse vary with constitution.有蛀洞，食物嵌入、吃甜或冷热时痛，牙龈多无红肿；舌脉随体质而异。

Principle治法 Free the collaterals and stop pain (drain); the real treatment is a filling or root canal通络止痛（泻法）；根本在补牙或根管治疗

Main主穴LI4 Hégǔ合谷ST6 Jiáchē颊车ST7 Xiàguān下关
Add配穴LI6 Piānlì偏历

💡 LI6 is the Large Intestine luo point; the Lingshu says excess gives decay and deafness. Make clear that needling only eases the pain for a while.偏历是大肠经络穴，《灵枢》「实则龋聋」。针灸只能暂时止痛，要说清楚。

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

Best for最适合 Any toothache, especially lower teeth各型牙痛，下牙尤宜

Why依据 Large Intestine yuan; the hand Yangming enters the lower teeth; "face and mouth, LI4"大肠经原穴，手阳明入下齿中，「面口合谷收」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Hand Yangming and lower teeth with the fastest pain relief, where ST44 serves the foot Yangming, upper teeth and stomach fire; forbidden in pregnancy.比内庭偏手阳明下牙、止痛最快，内庭偏足阳明上牙、清胃火；孕妇禁针。

ST6 Jiáchē颊车

Best for最适合 Lower toothache, tight masseter下牙痛、咬肌紧

Why依据 Stomach channel point on the cheek胃经颊部穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Lower jaw and masseter, below ST7; perpendicular 0.3–0.5 cun and stop at the bone.比下关偏下颌、偏咬肌；直刺 0.3–0.5 寸，抵下颌骨即止。

ST7 Xiàguān下关

Best for最适合 Upper toothache, pain opening the mouth上牙痛、张口痛

Why依据 Meeting point of the Stomach and Gallbladder channels足阳明足少阳交会穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Upper jaw and the jaw joint more than ST6; closed mouth, perpendicular 0.5–1 cun, do not open the mouth, withdraw on sharp radiating pain.比颊车偏上颌与颞下颌关节；闭口直刺 0.5–1 寸，留针勿张口，放射剧痛即退。

ST44 Nèitíng内庭

Versus others与其他穴的区别 The foot partner of LI2: ST44 for upper teeth and stomach fire, LI2 for lower teeth.与二间一足一手两荥穴，内庭偏上牙胃火，二间偏下牙。

LI2 Èrjiān二间

Best for最适合 Stomach-fire toothache with lower gum swelling胃火牙痛、下牙龈肿

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Clears heat where LI4 stops pain; perpendicular or oblique 0.2–0.3 cun.比合谷偏清热，合谷偏止痛；直刺或斜刺 0.2–0.3 寸。

GB20 Fēngchí风池

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Toothache usually comes from decay reaching the pulp, a root-tip abscess, gum disease or an inflamed wisdom tooth; it can also be a cracked tooth, jaw-joint disorder, sinusitis (upper teeth) or trigeminal neuralgia (brief electric pain). Spontaneous pain, night pain, pain with heat or pain that lingers suggest the pulp is beyond recovery; swelling, warmth and pus suggest infection. Antibiotics do not replace drainage or root canal treatment, and pulpitis without spread or general illness usually needs none. Dentists start with ibuprofen and acetaminophen as labelled (ask first in pregnancy or with gut or kidney disease). Trials suggest acupuncture eases pain after tooth extraction; other dental pain is little studied, and a TCM pattern cannot show which layer of the tooth is affected.牙痛多来自龋齿引起的牙髓炎、根尖脓肿、牙周病和智齿冠周炎，也可能是牙裂、颞下颌关节紊乱、鼻窦炎（上牙痛）或三叉神经痛（短暂触电样痛）。判断要不要急看：自发痛、夜间痛、热刺激痛或痛持续不退提示牙髓不可逆损伤；肿、热、流脓提示感染。抗生素不能代替引流和根管治疗，没有扩散和全身症状的牙髓炎一般不需要抗生素。牙医止痛首选布洛芬和对乙酰氨基酚（按说明，孕妇、胃肠道或肾病患者先问医生）。针灸对拔牙后痛的研究提示可减轻疼痛，对其他牙痛研究较少；中医证型不能判断病变在牙的哪一层。
