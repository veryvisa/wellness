    Measure the elbow crease to the dorsal distal wrist crease as 12 cun. SI7 is 5 cun above the wrist crease, or 7 cun from the elbow crease, on the longitudinal ulnar forearm line.肘横纹至腕背远端横纹按 12 寸折量，支正在腕背横纹上 5 寸，即由肘横纹向腕量 7 寸，沿前臂尺侧纵线取点。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Standard location: on the back of the forearm, 5 cun above the dorsal wrist crease, between the medial border of the ulna and flexor carpi ulnaris. Follow the subcutaneous edge of the ulna and slide slightly toward the palm into the groove between the ulna and flexor carpi ulnaris. Branches of the medial antebrachial cutaneous nerve and basilic vein tributaries lie superficially; the bone is directly beneath.国标：前臂后区，腕背横纹上 5 寸，尺骨尺侧与尺侧腕屈肌之间。沿尺骨可触及的皮下骨缘向掌侧轻移，进入尺骨与尺侧腕屈肌之间的沟即是。浅层有前臂内侧皮神经分支和贵要静脉属支，深面紧贴尺骨。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Mark 5 cun proximal to the wrist crease using the patient's own elbow-to-wrist 12-cun segment. Finger widths are only a short-span aid, not a substitute for proportional measurement; confirm against the ulna and tendon groove.以患者自身 12 寸肘腕段量出腕横纹近端 5 寸，手指宽仅作短段辅助，不可连续叠指代替骨度折量；用尺骨缘和肌腱沟复核。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Measure the 5 cun up from the dorsal wrist crease; measuring from the elbow crease lands too high. A commoner error is placing it on the dorsal side of the ulna beside extensor carpi ulnaris: the standard location is on the ulna's medial side, next to flexor carpi ulnaris.5 寸要从腕背横纹往肘量，从肘横纹量会偏到前臂中上段。更常见的错是把穴取到尺骨背侧、尺侧腕伸肌一边——国标在尺骨的尺侧（掌侧方向），与尺侧腕屈肌之间。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

The base text says 'five cun behind the elbow', with a note that another edition reads 'behind the wrist'; the modern standard, 5 cun above the wrist crease, follows the latter.《针灸甲乙经》卷三：支正是手太阳经络穴，别走手少阴心经。底本作「肘后五寸」，校注说另一本作「腕后」；今国标取腕背横纹上 5 寸，与「腕后五寸」一致。

Lingshu ch.10 on the connecting vessels: the Hand-Taiyang connecting vessel is Zhizheng, five cun above the wrist, pouring inward to the Heart channel; a branch runs up to the elbow and the shoulder. 🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Flexor carpi ulnaris strain, myofascial pain on the ulnar forearm尺侧腕屈肌劳损、前臂尺侧肌筋膜痛

A cord-like tender band on the ulnar forearm after repeated gripping and ulnar flexion usually comes from flexor carpi ulnaris; pinpoint tenderness on the ulnar shaft with pain on loading needs a stress fracture ruled out.反复握持、屈腕尺偏后前臂尺侧条索样压痛常来自尺侧腕屈肌；尺骨干局限压痛伴负重痛要排除应力性骨折。

Irritation of the dorsal cutaneous branch of the ulnar nerve尺神经背侧皮支刺激

Tingling from the distal forearm to the dorsoulnar hand should be distinguished from tendon pain; persistent sensory changes need nerve assessment.前臂远端至手背尺侧麻刺应与局部肌腱痛区分，持续感觉异常需检查神经。

Common warts寻常疣

SI7 for warts follows the Lingshu's 'deficiency brings warts'; warts are a papillomavirus infection, and a lesion that grows fast, bleeds or is unevenly coloured needs a dermatologist to rule out other conditions.支正治疣是《灵枢》络脉「虚则生肬」的传统用法；疣是人乳头瘤病毒感染，迅速增大、出血、颜色不均的皮损要请皮肤科排除其他病变。

Where is SI7 Zhīzhèng?支正（SI7）在哪里？

On the posteromedial forearm, 5 cun proximal to the dorsal wrist crease, between the medial border of the ulna and the flexor carpi ulnaris.前臂后区，腕背横纹上 5 寸，尺骨尺侧与尺侧腕屈肌之间。

How is SI7 needled?支正（SI7）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of SI7?支正（SI7）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting络穴

Main safety point at SI7?支正（SI7）最要注意的安全问题？

Keep the needle along the ulnar border: angling deep toward the palm side nears the ulnar nerve and artery; withdraw for tingling into the little finger.针贴尺骨缘进针；向掌侧深刺会接近尺神经与尺动脉，出现向小指放射的麻感即退针。
