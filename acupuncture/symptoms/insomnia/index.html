Insomnia不寐（失眠）

HT7, SP6, GV20, KI6, BL62 and PC6 all list insomnia, but point selection cannot rest on a vague "calms the mind" label. The textbook builds the core along two lines — the heart and the qiao vessels: HT7 and SP6 settle the spirit and nourish yin and blood, KI6 and BL62 regulate the yin and yang qiao vessels that open and close sleep, and GV20 calms. Supporting points then follow the pattern: irritability and a bitter taste point to liver fire; chest oppression, phlegm and a greasy yellow coating to phlegm-heat; palpitations, poor memory and loose stools to heart–spleen deficiency; timidity and easy fright to heart–gallbladder qi deficiency; agitation with a sore back, tinnitus and night sweats to heart–kidney disharmony; fullness and sour belching to stomach disharmony. This page gives the points and reasons for each pattern and then compares the common insomnia points side by side.神门、三阴交、百会、照海、申脉、内关的主治里都有失眠，但选穴不能只看有没有「安神」功效。教材把不寐的主穴放在「心」与「跷脉」两条线上：神门、三阴交调心神与阴血，照海、申脉调阴阳跷脉的寤寐开合，百会镇静。再分证型加配穴：急躁易怒、口苦属肝火扰心；胸闷痰多、苔黄腻属痰热扰心；心悸健忘、食少便溏属心脾两虚；胆怯易惊属心胆气虚；心烦、腰酸耳鸣、潮热盗汗属心肾不交；脘腹胀满、嗳腐属胃不和。本页给出每型主配穴与理由，再把常用失眠穴放在同一张表里比较。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Suicidal thoughts with a plan, inability to stay safe, hallucinations or confusion associated with severe sleep loss: seek emergency mental-health assessment immediately; call 911 if there is immediate danger.出现自杀想法、具体自伤计划、无法保证自身安全，或因严重失眠出现幻觉、意识混乱：立即寻求急诊精神卫生评估；有即时危险拨打 911。
  • Almost no sleep for several nights accompanied by unusually high energy, excessive talking, racing thoughts, impulsive spending or risky behaviour: obtain same-day psychiatric assessment for possible mania rather than treating it as ordinary insomnia.连续数晚几乎不睡，同时出现精力异常旺盛、话多、思维奔逸、冲动消费或冒险行为：当天接受精神科评估，排查躁狂发作，而非简单按普通失眠处理。
  • Repeated gasping during sleep, witnessed breathing pauses, loud snoring, morning headaches or uncontrollable daytime sleepiness: seek prompt assessment for obstructive sleep apnoea. Persistent breathing difficulty, cyanosis or chest pain requires emergency care.睡眠中反复憋醒、目击呼吸暂停、严重打鼾，伴晨起头痛或白天无法控制的嗜睡：尽快就医评估阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停；若出现持续呼吸困难、发绀或胸痛，立即拨打 911。
  • Insomnia accompanied by new one-sided weakness, facial drooping, slurred speech or a sudden severe headache: call 911 immediately to rule out stroke or another emergency.失眠伴新发单侧肢体无力、口角歪斜、言语不清或突发剧烈头痛：立即拨打 911，排查卒中等急症。
  • Insomnia occurring at least three nights per week for three months or longer, with impaired daytime functioning, work, study or driving safety: arrange a medical assessment rather than escalating sleeping medication or alcohol independently.失眠每周至少 3 晚、持续 3 个月以上并影响日间工作、学习或驾驶安全：预约家庭医生评估，不应长期自行增加安眠药或酒精剂量。

「老人臥而不寐，少壯寐而不寤者，何也?」——《难经》（公版，维基文库底本）

Nanjing, Difficulty 46, raises the question of differences in sleep between older and younger people, introducing the relationship between sleep and the abundance or deficiency of nutritive and defensive qi and blood. Assessment should consider age, constitution, qi and blood status, and duration, not merely difficulty falling asleep. Repeated early awakening with palpitations, poor memory and a pale complexion in an older patient may suggest deficient qi and blood failing to nourish the mind rather than excess fire.《难经·四十六难》提出老人和年轻人的睡眠差异问题，引出营卫气血盛衰与睡眠的关系。临床辨证不能只看入睡困难，还要结合年龄、体质、气血状态和病程。老年患者反复早醒、心悸健忘、面色少华，应考虑气血不足、心神失养，而不是一律按实火论治。

「衛氣不得入於陰，常留於陽，留於陽則陽氣滿，陽氣滿則陽蹻盛，不得入於陰則陰氣虛，故目不瞑矣」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.80: defensive qi cannot enter yin and stays in yang; staying in yang makes yang qi full, full yang qi makes the Yang Qiao vessel exuberant, and with qi kept out of yin, yin is relatively deficient — so the eyes will not close. This is the basis for using KI6 (Yin Qiao) and BL62 (Yang Qiao) in insomnia.《灵枢·大惑论》：卫气不能进入阴分，一直停留在阳分；留在阳分则阳气满，阳气满则阳跷脉盛，卫气不入阴则阴气相对不足，所以闭不上眼、睡不着。这是失眠取照海（通阴跷）、申脉（通阳跷）的理论依据。

「陽明逆不得從其道，故不得臥也。下經曰：胃不和則臥不安」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.34: when yangming stomach qi rebels and cannot follow its downward path, the patient cannot lie in peace; an older text says 'when the stomach is not in harmony, sleep is not peaceful'. Poor sleep after a heavy supper with abdominal fullness is treated by harmonising the stomach and sending qi down (CV12, ST36, PC6).《素问·逆调论》：阳明胃气上逆、不能顺其下行之道，所以不能安卧；古书说「胃不和则卧不安」。晚饭过饱、脘腹胀满而睡不好，要从和胃降逆入手（中脘、足三里、内关）。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern insomnia disorder is not simply short sleep. It involves difficulty initiating sleep, maintaining sleep or waking too early, together with daytime consequences such as fatigue, impaired concentration, mood changes, sleepiness or impaired occupational and social functioning. Chronic insomnia generally requires symptoms at least three nights per week for at least three months, despite adequate opportunity and appropriate conditions for sleep. Common causes and contributing factors include: (1) psychophysiological factors such as stress, anxiety, depression, trauma and excessive worry about sleep; (2) psychiatric conditions including depressive disorders, bipolar disorder and mania; (3) sleep disorders such as obstructive sleep apnoea, restless legs syndrome, periodic limb movement disorder and circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders; (4) medical conditions such as chronic pain, gastro-oesophageal reflux, cardiopulmonary disease, thyroid dysfunction, nocturia and menopausal symptoms; (5) medications and substances including caffeine, nicotine, alcohol, stimulants, some antidepressants, corticosteroids and certain bronchodilators; and (6) lifestyle and environmental factors such as shift work, jet lag, light exposure, noise and irregular schedules. Assessment should document sleep latency, awakenings, total sleep time, wake time and daytime functioning. A sleep diary for at least two weeks is useful, together with review of medications, caffeine, alcohol and comorbidities. Loud snoring, witnessed apnoeas, nocturnal gasping or severe daytime sleepiness warrant assessment for obstructive sleep apnoea. Polysomnography (PSG) may be considered when another sleep disorder is suspected or treatment is unsuccessful; a multiple sleep latency test (MSLT) may be added when indicated. Significant depression, suicidal risk or manic symptoms require psychiatric assessment. Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is the first-line non-pharmacological treatment for chronic insomnia; sleep-hygiene education alone is insufficient. TCM patterns do not map one-to-one onto modern medical causes. Anxiety- or stress-related insomnia may present as liver constraint transforming into fire or heart–gallbladder qi deficiency. Depression-associated insomnia may coexist with heart–spleen deficiency or Liver constraint, but this does not establish a TCM diagnosis. Obstructive sleep apnoea may coexist with phlegm-damp or phlegm-heat features, but acupuncture points such as ST40 and SP9 cannot replace airway assessment or standard treatment such as continuous positive airway pressure. Sleep disruption from chronic pain, reflux or nocturia requires management of the underlying condition. Acupuncture may be considered an adjunct in selected patients, with outcomes assessed through sleep diaries and daytime functioning. Its effect should not be guaranteed, and it is not a substitute for necessary medical evaluation or CBT-I.现代医学中的失眠障碍（Insomnia Disorder）不只是睡眠时间短，而是存在入睡困难、睡眠维持困难或过早醒来，同时造成疲劳、注意力下降、情绪变化、日间思睡或工作学习功能受损。慢性失眠通常要求每周至少 3 晚、持续至少 3 个月，并且有充分的睡眠机会和适宜的睡眠环境。常见病因和相关因素包括：①心理生理因素：压力、焦虑、抑郁、创伤经历、对睡眠过度担忧；②精神疾病：抑郁障碍、双相情感障碍、躁狂发作等；③睡眠疾病：阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停、不宁腿综合征、周期性肢体运动障碍、昼夜节律睡眠觉醒障碍；④躯体疾病：慢性疼痛、胃食管反流、心肺疾病、甲状腺功能异常、夜尿及更年期症状；⑤药物与物质：咖啡因、尼古丁、酒精、兴奋剂、部分抗抑郁药、糖皮质激素及某些支气管扩张药；⑥生活与环境：轮班工作、跨时区旅行、光照、噪声及不规律作息。评估时应记录入睡潜伏期、夜间觉醒次数、总睡眠时间、起床时间和日间功能，建议使用至少 2 周睡眠日记，并核查药物、咖啡因、酒精及共病。存在明显打鼾、呼吸暂停、夜间憋醒或日间严重嗜睡时，应评估阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停；怀疑其他睡眠障碍或常规治疗效果不佳时，医生可考虑多导睡眠监测（PSG），必要时结合多次睡眠潜伏期试验（MSLT）。失眠伴明显抑郁、自杀风险或躁狂表现时，需要精神科评估。慢性失眠的一线非药物治疗是失眠认知行为治疗（CBT-I），不是单纯依赖睡眠卫生教育。中医证型与现代医学病因并不一一对应：焦虑和压力相关失眠可能表现为肝郁化火，也可能表现为心胆气虚；抑郁伴失眠可能兼有心脾两虚或肝郁，但不能据此诊断中医证型；阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停患者可能同时出现痰湿、痰热表现，但不能用丰隆、阴陵泉等针灸治疗替代呼吸道评估和持续气道正压通气等规范治疗；慢性疼痛、胃食管反流和夜尿导致的睡眠中断，也必须处理原发因素。针灸可作为部分患者综合管理的辅助措施，疗效应结合睡眠日记及日间功能评估，不应对疗效作保证，也不能替代必要的医学检查和 CBT-I。
