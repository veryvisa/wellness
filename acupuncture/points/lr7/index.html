Where is LR7 Xīguān?膝关（LR7）在哪里？

On the medial knee, inferior to the medial condyle of the tibia, 1 cun posterior to SP9.膝部，胫骨内侧髁的下方，阴陵泉后 1 寸。

How is LR7 needled?膝关（LR7）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at LR7?膝关（LR7）最要注意的安全问题？

Driving deep posterolaterally approaches the posterior tibial vessels and tibial nerve: keep perpendicular, do not angle toward the centre of the popliteal fossa, and withdraw at electric tingling into the sole.针向外后深进会接近胫后血管与胫神经：保持直刺、不偏向腘窝中央；出现向足底放射的电麻感即退针。
