Blocked nose and sinusitis鼻塞鼻渊

Textbooks split a blocked, runny nose into two sections. Recurrent clear watery discharge with sneezing and itch is biqiu (mostly allergic rhinitis), rooted in weak lung, spleen or kidney, treated with points such as LI20, BL12 and ST36 to support and secure the exterior. Thick yellow discharge with blockage, head pressure and poor smell is biyuan (mostly sinusitis), with heat in the lung, gallbladder or spleen and stomach, treated with LI20, LU7 and LI4 to clear heat and open the nose. A nose freshly blocked by a chill is handled as wind-cold. Look first at whether discharge is clear or turbid, then at one side or both: long one-sided blockage, blood or a foul smell goes to ENT first.鼻塞流涕在教材里分属两节：清水样鼻涕、喷嚏、鼻痒反复发作的是鼻鼽（多为过敏性鼻炎），病在肺脾肾阳气不足，取迎香、风门、足三里等扶正固表；黄浊涕、鼻塞、头胀、嗅觉差的是鼻渊（多为鼻窦炎），病在肺、胆、脾胃有热，取迎香、列缺、合谷等清热通窍。刚受凉的鼻塞按感冒风寒处理。先看鼻涕是清是浊，再看是一侧还是两侧：单侧长期鼻塞、带血或恶臭，一定先看耳鼻喉科。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Red swollen eyelids or skin around the eye, eye pain, pain on moving the eye, double or reduced vision, high fever: emergency now for orbital cellulitis眼睑或眼周红肿、眼球痛、转眼痛、复视或视力下降、高热：立刻急诊，排除眶蜂窝织炎
  • Severe headache, stiff neck, vomiting, confusion, seizures or a soft swelling of the forehead: call 911 for intracranial infection剧烈头痛、颈硬、呕吐、意识改变、抽搐，或前额软性肿胀：拨 911，排除颅内感染
  • In weak immunity or poorly controlled diabetes, facial pain or swelling, black tissue in the nose or fever: emergency now for invasive fungal infection免疫低下或血糖控制差者面部肿痛、鼻内发黑、发热：立刻急诊，警惕侵袭性真菌感染
  • Clear watery discharge from one side after head injury or surgery, worse on bending: emergency care today for a cerebrospinal fluid leak头部受伤后或手术后一侧流清水样鼻涕，低头时明显：当天急诊，排除脑脊液鼻漏
  • One-sided blockage for weeks, bloody or foul discharge, recurrent nosebleeds, facial numbness or a neck lump: see ENT soon; in a small child, one-sided foul discharge means a foreign body until shown otherwise单侧鼻塞数周、血性或恶臭分泌物、反复鼻出血、面部麻木、颈部肿块：尽快看耳鼻喉科；幼儿单侧恶臭涕先查鼻腔异物
  • Sneezing and runny nose with widespread hives, swollen lips or tongue, wheeze or faintness: call 911 and use an epinephrine auto-injector first喷嚏流涕同时出现全身风团、嘴唇舌头肿、喘鸣、头晕：拨 911，先用肾上腺素笔

「膽移熱於腦，則辛頞鼻淵，鼻淵者，濁涕下不止也」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.37: when gallbladder heat moves up to the brain, the nose bridge stings and biyuan follows, which means turbid discharge that does not stop. This is the source of the name and of the gallbladder-heat type treated with GB34, GB43 and GB20.《素问·气厥论》：胆热上移到脑，就鼻梁里发辛辣感、得鼻渊；鼻渊就是浊涕流个不停。这是「鼻渊」病名和胆热一型的出处，取阳陵泉、侠溪、风池由此而来。

「鼻塞不知香臭。迎香 上星 風門」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu dacheng: for a blocked nose that cannot smell, LI20, GV23 and BL12 — one beside the nose, one at the front of the head, one on the back — opening the nose locally and securing the exterior, the same thinking as the modern textbook.《针灸大成》：鼻塞闻不出香臭，取迎香、上星、风门。一穴在鼻旁、一穴在头前、一穴在背，局部通窍加祛风固表，与今天教材的取穴思路一致。

「鼻流清涕，腠理不密，噴嚏不止。神庭 肺俞 太淵 三里」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu dacheng: clear discharge, loose pores and endless sneezing take GV24, BL13, LU9 and ST36. This is today's biqiu (allergic rhinitis): the root is an insecure lung and exterior, so lung (BL13, LU9) and spleen–stomach (ST36) are tonified.《针灸大成》：流清涕、腠理不密（肌表不固）、喷嚏不止，取神庭、肺俞、太渊、三里。这正是今天的鼻鼽（过敏性鼻炎）：病根在肺卫不固，所以补肺（肺俞、太渊）、补脾胃（足三里）。

Principle治法 Dispel wind-cold, free the lung, open the nose (drain; moxa allowed)疏风散寒，宣肺通窍（泻法，可灸）

Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门BL13 Fèishū肺俞

💡 As for a cold: LU7, the Lung luo point, LI4, the Large Intestine yuan point, and GB20 release the exterior and dispel wind, with LI20 beside the nose; wind-cold adds BL12 and BL13 to free the lung, which can be cupped or moxaed.按感冒取列缺（肺经络穴）、合谷（大肠经原穴）、风池解表祛风，加鼻旁迎香通窍；风寒配风门、肺俞宣肺散寒，背部穴可拔罐或灸。

Biyuan鼻渊

Lung-channel wind-heat肺经风热

How it presents辨证要点 Blocked nose, plentiful yellow or sticky white discharge, reduced smell, headache; or fever, cough and sore throat; red tongue, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse.鼻塞，涕黄或黏白而量多，嗅觉减退，头痛；或发热、咳嗽、咽痛；舌红，苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat, free the lung, open the nose (drain; prick LU11 to bleed)疏风清热，宣肺通窍（泻法，少商点刺出血）

Main主穴LI20 Yíngxiāng迎香LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷
Add配穴LU5 Chǐzé尺泽LU11 Shàoshāng少商

💡 Textbook core for biyuan: Yintang (on the Governor vessel, no card here), LI20, LU7 and LI4 — Yintang and LI20 open the nose locally, and LU7 with LI4, one Lung and one Large Intestine, free the lung and clear heat. Wind-heat adds LU5, the Lung he-sea point ("in excess, drain the child"), and LU11, the Lung jing-well point, pricked to drain heat.教材鼻渊主穴：印堂（督脉，本站无卡）、迎香、列缺、合谷：印堂、迎香在鼻根鼻旁通窍，列缺与合谷一肺一大肠，宣肺清热。风热配尺泽（肺经合穴，实则泻子）、少商（肺经井穴，点刺泄热）。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

The four commonest causes: (1) a cold, settling in 7–10 days; (2) allergic rhinitis — sneezing, watery discharge, itchy nose and eyes, by season or with dust mites and pets, for which steroid nasal sprays are first-line; (3) acute sinusitis, mostly viral, with three clues to a bacterial cause: no improvement after 10 days, worsening after improving, or high fever with purulent discharge and facial pain for three to four days from the start; (4) polyps, a deviated septum or chronic sinusitis, which need nasal endoscopy. Decongestant sprays should not be used beyond 3–5 days or blockage rebounds. Pregnancy and medicines can also block the nose. One-sided symptoms always go to ENT. Acupuncture has some randomised-trial support for allergic rhinitis symptoms and fits alongside avoiding triggers, saline rinses and medication.鼻塞流涕最常见的四类：①普通感冒，7–10 天自愈；②过敏性鼻炎，喷嚏、清水涕、鼻痒、眼痒，季节性或接触尘螨宠物时发作，鼻用激素喷雾是一线治疗；③急性鼻窦炎，多数是病毒性，提示细菌性的三种情况是超过 10 天不好转、好转后又加重、或起病即高热伴脓涕面痛三四天；④鼻息肉、鼻中隔偏曲、慢性鼻窦炎，要鼻内镜看。血管收缩型喷鼻剂连用不超过 3–5 天，否则反弹性鼻塞。妊娠期鼻塞、药物性鼻塞也要想到。单侧症状一律先看耳鼻喉科。针刺对过敏性鼻炎症状有一些随机试验支持，适合与环境控制、洗鼻和药物同时使用。
