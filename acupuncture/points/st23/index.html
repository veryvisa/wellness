📍 Location定位★

On the upper abdomen, 2 cun above the umbilicus, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 2 寸，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

💡 Lie supine with the knees bent; set the level by proportional measure (xiphisternal junction to umbilicus 8 cun, umbilicus to pubic symphysis 5 cun), then measure 2 cun from the midline (the Kidney channel is at 0.5 cun).仰卧屈膝放松腹壁；按骨度（胸剑联合至脐 8 寸、脐至耻骨联合上缘 5 寸）定纵向位置，再从前正中线横量 2 寸（任脉旁开 2 寸，肾经在 0.5 寸）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.8–1.2 cun0.8–1.2 寸

Do not needle abdominal points in mid–late pregnancy.孕中晚期子宫上升，腹部穴不针。

🛡 Safety安全

Deep insertion through the peritoneum can injure the transverse colon and small intestine; go shallower with a thin wall, distension or surgical scars. Acute abdominal pain or rebound tenderness needs medical care, not needling.深刺穿过腹膜可伤横结肠与小肠；腹壁薄、腹胀或有腹部手术瘢痕时减浅。急性腹痛、反跳痛先就医，不取穴。

Where is ST23 Tàiyǐ?太乙（ST23）在哪里？

On the upper abdomen, 2 cun above the umbilicus, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 2 寸，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

How is ST23 needled?太乙（ST23）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.8–1.2 cun.直刺；0.8–1.2 寸。

Main safety point at ST23?太乙（ST23）最要注意的安全问题？

Deep insertion through the peritoneum can injure the transverse colon and small intestine; go shallower with a thin wall, distension or surgical scars. Acute abdominal pain or rebound tenderness needs medical care, not needling.深刺穿过腹膜可伤横结肠与小肠；腹壁薄、腹胀或有腹部手术瘢痕时减浅。急性腹痛、反跳痛先就医，不取穴。
