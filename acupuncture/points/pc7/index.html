📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the forearm supinated, palm up and wrist relaxed; lightly flex the wrist to define the crease and tendons.坐位或仰卧，前臂旋后、掌心向上，腕自然放松；轻屈腕使腕横纹和肌腱清楚。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the centre of the distal palmar wrist crease; PC7 lies on the crease between palmaris longus and flexor carpi radialis.先找腕掌侧远端横纹中央；大陵位于横纹上、掌长肌腱与桡侧腕屈肌腱之间。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With the wrist slightly flexed, identify palmaris longus and flexor carpi radialis; PC7 is in the hollow on the wrist crease between them (palmaris longus may be absent). PC7 sits over the entrance of the carpal tunnel: deep to it is the proximal edge of the flexor retinaculum, under which the median nerve lies at its most superficial between the two tendons, with the finger flexor tendons deeper. Palpate gently and needle shallowly (0.3–0.5 cun).腕轻屈辨认掌长肌腱与桡侧腕屈肌腱，大陵在两腱之间的腕横纹凹陷中；掌长肌腱可能缺如。大陵正对腕管入口：深面是屈肌支持带近缘，其下正中神经在两腱之间位置最浅，再深是指屈肌腱。触诊要轻，针刺宜浅（0.3–0.5 寸）。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently feel the soft-tissue hollow between the tendons on the crease and cross-check against the proximal carpal row; ease pressure and stop repeated compression if tingling reaches the thumb or fingers.沿腕横纹轻触两腱之间的软组织凹陷，并与腕骨近端边缘核对；麻刺向拇指或手指出现时，减轻压力并停止反复压迫。

  4. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    PC7 is on the wrist crease and does not require finger-cun measurement. A fingertip may estimate the crease midpoint, but confirm with the crease, tendon interval and carpal margin.此穴在腕横纹，不以手指同身寸折量；可用指腹宽度粗略找横纹中点，最终以横纹、两腱间隙和腕骨边缘确认。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting onto flexor carpi radialis leaves the gap between the tendons. More importantly, the point lies right over the carpal tunnel entrance where the median nerve is most superficial; deep or repeated needling can injure it, so withdraw at once if an electric tingle runs into the fingers.偏到桡侧腕屈肌腱上就离开了两腱之间；更要紧的是，穴位本身就在腕管入口上方，正中神经在此最浅，深刺或反复提插可伤神经，出现向手指的电麻感立即退针。
  • Using only the crease midpoint can misplace the point when palmaris longus is absent; cross-check with the other tendon and carpal margin.只凭腕横纹中点、不辨两腱，会因掌长肌腱缺如而偏位；应以其他肌腱和腕骨边缘共同校核。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Carpal tunnel syndrome腕管综合征

Numbness in the thumb, index and middle fingers or waking at night may indicate median-nerve compression; tenderness near PC7 is not diagnostic and persistent symptoms need clinical assessment.拇指、食指、中指麻木或夜间麻醒提示正中神经受压可能；大陵附近触痛不是诊断依据，持续症状需临床评估。

Wrist flexor tendon or joint overuse腕屈肌腱或腕关节劳损

Anterior wrist pain after repetitive flexion, loading or gripping may arise from tendon or joint; swelling, restricted motion or marked tenderness after injury needs bone and ligament assessment.重复屈腕、支撑或抓握后腕前痛可能来自肌腱或关节；外伤后肿胀、活动受限或压痛明显需检查骨与韧带。

Where is PC7 Dàlíng?大陵（PC7）在哪里？

Anterior wrist, at the centre of the wrist crease, between palmaris longus and flexor carpi radialis tendons.腕前区，腕横纹中央，掌长肌腱与桡侧腕屈肌腱之间。

How is PC7 needled?大陵（PC7）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of PC7?大陵（PC7）是哪类特定穴？

Shu-stream and yuan-source point, earth输穴、原穴，属土

Main safety point at PC7?大陵（PC7）最要注意的安全问题？

The point lies over the entrance of the carpal tunnel where the median nerve is most superficial: shallow insertion of 0.3–0.5 cun only, no deep insertion or lifting-thrusting, and withdraw at an electric tingle.穴位就在腕管入口上方，正中神经在此最浅：只浅刺 0.3–0.5 寸，不深刺、不提插，针下电麻即退针。
