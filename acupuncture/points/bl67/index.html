📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the lateral foot exposed and little toe relaxed; inspect the nail, skin and circulation before locating.坐位或仰卧，足外侧朝上，小趾自然放松；取穴前检查趾甲、皮肤和循环。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Identify the lateral nail plate and nail-root corner. BL67 is on the skin about 0.1 cun lateral to that corner; do not locate on the nail itself or an inflamed nail fold.看小趾外侧甲板与甲根角，至阴在甲根外侧角旁约 0.1 寸的皮肤交界处；不要把指甲本身或甲沟炎处当作取穴点。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The point is at the lateral tip of the little toe, where proper digital nerves and dorsal digital arterial branches are superficial and the nail bed lies distally. Only superficial insertion or a properly supervised prick is appropriate; avoid visible vessels, infection, ulceration or sensory loss.穴在小趾外侧末端，趾固有神经和趾背动脉分支浅行，远端邻甲床；位置仅适合浅刺或按规范点刺，避开可见血管、感染、溃疡及感觉异常区域。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate the lateral nail-root corner and inspect the skin–nail border and toe perfusion. Do not repeatedly probe with a needle; assess abnormal temperature, colour or sensation first.以指腹轻触甲根外侧角，确认皮肤与甲板交界及趾端血流；不可用针尖反复探刺寻找点位，皮温、颜色或感觉异常时先评估。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Trace proximally from the lateral tip of the little-toe nail to its outer root corner, then locate a small distance beyond it. BL66 is more proximal at the web border, distinctly separate from BL67.从小趾外侧趾甲尖向近端找到甲根外角，再在角外约一小段处定位；足通谷在更近端的趾蹼缘，二穴相距明显。
