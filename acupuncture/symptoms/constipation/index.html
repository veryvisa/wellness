Constipation便秘

Many points list 'constipation'. In clinic you first sort excess from deficiency. Excess splits into heat, qi stagnation and cold accumulation; deficiency splits into qi, blood and yin deficiency. You also ask whether the stool is dry and hard or soft but hard to pass, and whether there is bloating, pain or foul breath, and check tongue and pulse. Only then do you choose the points and whether to drain, tonify or use moxa. This page compares the common constipation points side by side and gives the main and supporting points for each pattern, with reasons.大肠俞、天枢、支沟、上巨虚、足三里、三阴交的主治里都写着「便秘」。临床上要先分实秘还是虚秘：实秘再分热、气滞、寒凝，虚秘再分气虚、血虚、阴虚。同时看大便是干结还是不干却排不出，有没有腹胀、腹痛、口臭，舌脉如何，然后决定用泻法、补法还是灸法。这一页把能通便的穴放在同一张表里比较，并给出每个证型的主穴、配穴和理由。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Sudden severe abdominal pain, marked distension and repeated vomiting, with no stool and no gas: possible bowel obstruction, call 911 or go to the ER突然剧烈腹痛、腹胀明显、反复呕吐，同时完全不排便也不排气：可能是肠梗阻，拨 911 或立即去急诊
  • Large amounts of fresh blood in the stool, black tarry stool, or dizziness, palpitations and pallor: same-day care to rule out GI bleeding大便带鲜血量多、黑便（柏油样），或伴头晕、心慌、面色苍白：当天就医，排除消化道出血
  • New constipation after age 50, or a bowel-habit change lasting weeks, with weight loss, anaemia, narrow stools or a family history of colorectal cancer: see a doctor for blood tests and colonoscopy to rule out colorectal tumour50 岁以上新出现的便秘，或持续数周的排便习惯改变，伴体重下降、贫血、大便变细、有大肠癌家族史：看医生，需要查血和结肠镜，排除结直肠肿瘤
  • Constipation with fever and persistent left lower abdominal pain and tenderness: possible diverticulitis or other infection, seek care promptly便秘伴发热、左下腹持续疼痛压痛：可能是憩室炎等感染，尽快就医
  • A newborn who has not passed meconium within 48 hours, or an infant with constipation plus bloating, vomiting or poor growth: same-day care to rule out Hirschsprung disease and others新生儿出生后 48 小时内未排胎便，或婴幼儿便秘伴腹胀、呕吐、生长迟缓：当天就医，排除先天性巨结肠等
  • Constipation after starting opioids, iron, anticholinergics, some antidepressants or calcium-channel blockers: ask your doctor or pharmacist, it may be a side effect; do not stop the drug on your own近期开始服用阿片类止痛药、铁剂、抗胆碱药、某些抗抑郁药或钙通道阻滞剂后出现便秘：问医生或药剂师，可能是药物副作用，不要自行停药

「照海支溝通大便之秘」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

A verse collected in Dacheng, vol. 3: KI6 with TE6 opens constipation. TE6 on the Triple Energizer channel frees triple-burner qi and drains heat; KI6 on the Kidney channel connects with the Yin Qiao vessel and replenishes fluids. One moves qi and the other moistens, which is why the pair suits heat constipation with injured fluids and yin-deficient dryness; draining TE6 and tonifying KI6 is the usual later practice.《针灸大成》卷三所收歌赋：照海、支沟相配，能通大便秘结。支沟属三焦经，通调三焦气机、泄热；照海属肾经，通阴跷脉，滋阴增液。一个行气、一个润燥，所以热秘伤津、阴虚肠燥最常用这一对；「泻支沟、补照海」是后世常用的操作。

Excess实秘

Heat constipation (accumulated heat in the stomach and intestines)热秘（肠胃积热）

How it presents辨证要点 Dry hard pellet-like stools every few days, hard to pass; abdominal distension and pain, tender to pressure; dry mouth, foul breath, red face, feeling hot, irritability, scanty dark urine; red tongue with dry yellow coating, slippery rapid pulse.大便干结如羊屎，数日一行，排便困难；腹胀腹痛、按之痛；口干口臭、面红身热、心烦、小便短赤；舌红苔黄燥，脉滑数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, drain fire, free the bowels and move stagnation (drain)清热泻火，通腑导滞（泻法）

Main主穴BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞ST25 Tiānshū天枢TE6 Zhīgōu支沟ST37 Shàngjùxū上巨虚
Add配穴LI11 Qūchí曲池LI4 Hégǔ合谷KI6 Zhàohǎi照海

💡 BL25 and ST25 are the shu–mu pair of the Large Intestine, one on the back and one on the front, regulating its transport directly; ST37 is the Large Intestine lower he-sea point ('he-sea points treat the fu organs') and sends fu qi downward; TE6 is the Triple Energizer jing-river (fire) point that clears triple-burner heat and frees qi movement, a classic constipation point. LI11 (he-sea) and LI4 (yuan) clear yangming heat; add KI6 with TE6 when fluids are clearly injured, to drain heat without further damaging yin.大肠俞与天枢是大肠的俞募配穴，一前一后直接调大肠传导；上巨虚是大肠下合穴（「合治六腑」），通降腑气；支沟是三焦经经穴（火），清三焦之热、通调气机，是便秘经验要穴。曲池是大肠合穴，合谷是大肠原穴，两者清阳明热；津伤明显时加照海，与支沟配对，「泻热而不伤阴」。

Excess实秘

Qi constipation (liver qi stagnation)气秘（肝郁气滞）

How it presents辨证要点 Stool dry or not, with urge but difficult passage; abdominal bloating, borborygmus, much flatus, flank fullness and belching; worse with stress or low mood; thin greasy coating, wiry pulse.大便干或不干，想排却排不出，排便不畅；腹胀、肠鸣矢气多，胁肋胀满、嗳气；情绪紧张或抑郁时加重；舌苔薄腻，脉弦。

Principle治法 Soothe the liver, move qi, relieve stagnation and open the bowels (drain)疏肝理气，行滞通便（泻法）

Main主穴BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞ST25 Tiānshū天枢TE6 Zhīgōu支沟ST37 Shàngjùxū上巨虚
Add配穴LR3 Tàichōng太冲CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘SP15 Dàhéng大横

💡 The core points free the fu organs; LR3, the Liver yuan and shu-stream point, soothes the liver and with LI4 forms the 'four gates' that move qi; CV12, front-mu of the stomach and influential point of the fu organs, restores middle-burner ascent and descent so bowel qi can move down; SP15, 4 cun beside the umbilicus and a meeting point of the Spleen channel and Yin Wei vessel, moves qi and relieves bloating locally. TE6 frees triple-burner qi and suits this pattern particularly.主穴通腑；太冲是肝经原穴、输穴，疏肝解郁，与合谷合称「四关」，行全身气滞；中脘是胃募、腑会，调中焦升降、助腑气下行；大横在脐旁 4 寸，是脾经与阴维脉交会穴，局部行气消胀。支沟善通三焦气机，对气滞型尤宜。

Excess实秘

Cold constipation (yin-cold accumulation, with yang deficiency)冷秘（阴寒积滞，兼阳虚）

How it presents辨证要点 Stool not necessarily dry, but hard to pass; cold abdominal pain better with warmth and pressure, cold limbs, aversion to cold; clear copious urine; pale or bluish face; pale tongue with moist white coating, deep slow pulse.大便不一定干硬，艰涩难排；腹中冷痛、喜温喜按，四肢不温、畏寒；小便清长；面色白或青；舌淡苔白润，脉沉迟。

Principle治法 Warm yang, dispel cold, open the bowels and ease pain (needle with generous moxa)温阳散寒，通便止痛（针灸并用，重灸）

Add配穴CV8 Shénquè神阙CV4 Guānyuán关元BL23 Shènshū肾俞

💡 The core points open the fu organs, but cold patterns need moxa rather than pure draining. CV8 with salt or ginger moxa warms the centre and disperses cold (needling forbidden); CV4, the Small Intestine front-mu point and meeting of the Ren vessel with the three leg yin channels, warms lower-burner original yang; BL23 tonifies kidney yang — the kidney governs the two lower orifices, and enough yang can push the stool along.主穴通腑，但寒证宜加灸，不用单纯泻法。神阙隔盐或隔姜灸温中散寒（神阙禁针）；关元是小肠募穴、任脉与足三阴交会，温下焦元阳；肾俞补肾阳，肾主二阴，阳气足则能推动大便。

Deficiency虚秘

Qi deficiency constipation (spleen and lung qi)气虚秘（脾肺气虚）

How it presents辨证要点 Stool not dry or hard, yet passed without force; straining causes sweating and shortness of breath, with fatigue afterward; pale face, tiredness, little desire to talk; pale tongue with white coating, weak pulse.大便不干也不硬，却排便无力，临厕努挣则汗出气短；便后疲乏；面色白、神疲懒言、容易累；舌淡苔白，脉弱。

Principle治法 Tonify spleen and lung, moisten the intestines and open the bowels (tonify; moxa may be added)补益脾肺，润肠通便（补法，可加灸）

Main主穴BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞ST25 Tiānshū天枢TE6 Zhīgōu支沟ST37 Shàngjùxū上巨虚
Add配穴ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里CV6 Qìhǎi气海BL20 Píshū脾俞

💡 Here the problem is not an accumulation but too little propulsive force. ST36, the Stomach he-sea point and a major tonifying point, supports middle qi; BL20 strengthens the spleen and CV6 tonifies original qi, together raising the bowel's transport strength; in the core points use TE6 and ST37 lightly with an even method, not heavy draining.虚秘不是肠里有「积」，而是推动力不足。足三里是胃经合穴、强壮要穴，补中气；脾俞健脾益气，气海补元气，三穴合用提升传导之力；主穴中的支沟、上巨虚可轻刺平补平泻，不宜重泻。

Deficiency虚秘

Blood deficiency constipation血虚秘

How it presents辨证要点 Dry stool that is hard to pass, without marked abdominal pain or distension; sallow face, pale lips and nails, dizziness, palpitations; common after childbirth, blood loss or long illness; pale tongue with thin white coating, thin pulse.大便干结，排出困难，但腹部无明显胀痛；面色萎黄无华、唇甲色淡、头晕心悸；产后、失血或久病后多见；舌淡苔薄白，脉细。

Principle治法 Nourish blood, moisten dryness and the intestines (tonify)养血润燥，润肠通便（补法）

Main主穴BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞ST25 Tiānshū天枢TE6 Zhīgōu支沟ST37 Shàngjùxū上巨虚
Add配穴SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交BL17 Géshū膈俞BL20 Píshū脾俞

💡 Without blood to moisten the bowel the stool dries. SP6, where the Liver, Spleen and Kidney channels meet, nourishes yin and blood; BL17, influential point of blood, regulates and tonifies the blood aspect; BL20 strengthens the spleen to generate blood (spleen and stomach are the source of qi and blood). SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy.肠道失血液濡润则便干。三阴交是肝脾肾三阴经交会穴，滋阴养血；膈俞是血会，调补血分；脾俞健脾以生化气血（脾胃为气血生化之源）。孕妇禁针三阴交。

Deficiency虚秘

Yin deficiency with intestinal dryness (kidney yin deficiency)阴虚肠燥（肾阴亏虚）

How it presents辨证要点 Dry pellet-like stools every few days; dry mouth and throat, thin body; afternoon low fever, hot palms and soles, night sweats, sore back and knees; common in the elderly or after febrile illness; red tongue with little or no coating, thin rapid pulse.大便干结如羊屎，数日一行；口干咽燥、形体消瘦；午后潮热、手足心热、盗汗、腰膝酸软；多见于老年人或热病之后；舌红少苔或无苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin and fluids, moisten the intestines and open the bowels (tonify; little moxa)滋阴增液，润肠通便（补法，少灸）

Main主穴BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞ST25 Tiānshū天枢TE6 Zhīgōu支沟ST37 Shàngjùxū上巨虚
Add配穴KI6 Zhàohǎi照海KI3 Tàixī太溪SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交

💡 The kidney governs the two lower orifices; when kidney yin is short, the intestines lose moisture. KI3, the Kidney yuan point, tonifies kidney yin; KI6, confluent point of the Yin Qiao vessel, moistens dryness, and its pairing with TE6 (drain TE6, tonify KI6) is the constipation pair recorded in Dacheng; SP6 supports nourishing yin. SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy.肾主二阴、司二便，肾阴不足则肠道失润。太溪是肾经原穴，滋补肾阴；照海是八脉交会穴、通阴跷脉，滋阴润燥，与支沟相配是《针灸大成》所载通便对穴（泻支沟、补照海）；三阴交助养阴。孕妇禁针三阴交。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern medicine defines functional constipation by the Rome IV criteria: fewer than 3 spontaneous bowel movements a week, plus at least two of straining, hard stools, a sense of incomplete evacuation or the need for manual assistance, for 3 months or more. By mechanism it divides into normal-transit, slow-transit and outlet-obstruction types (pelvic floor dyssynergia); the treatments differ, so stubborn cases need anorectal function testing. Secondary causes include drugs (opioids, iron, anticholinergics, calcium-channel blockers, antidepressants), hypothyroidism, hypercalcaemia, diabetes, Parkinson disease, spinal cord disease and colorectal tumour; IBS with constipation is also common. Anyone over 50 with new constipation, or with alarm features (blood in the stool, weight loss, anaemia), needs investigation first, not a trial of acupuncture. TCM patterns do not map one-to-one onto these causes, but they overlap: heat and qi-stagnation types are common with low fluid and fibre intake and with stress; qi-deficient and yang-deficient types are common in older adults and slow-transit constipation; yin- and blood-deficient types are common in the elderly, postpartum or after chronic illness; pelvic floor dyssynergia has no TCM equivalent and needs biofeedback training. Lifestyle (enough fluid, dietary fibre, regular exercise, a fixed toilet routine) is the foundation; acupuncture can be an adjunct, and some studies suggest it increases spontaneous bowel movements in chronic functional constipation, but the evidence quality varies and it does not replace medical care.现代医学以罗马 IV 标准定义功能性便秘：每周自发排便少于 3 次，并伴有用力排便、硬便、排不尽感或需手助排便等症状中的至少两项，持续 3 个月以上。按机制可分为正常传输型、慢传输型和出口梗阻型（盆底肌协调障碍），三者治疗不同，所以顽固便秘需要做肛门直肠功能检查。继发性原因包括药物（阿片类、铁剂、抗胆碱药、钙通道阻滞剂、抗抑郁药）、甲状腺功能减退、高钙血症、糖尿病、帕金森病、脊髓病变和结直肠肿瘤；肠易激综合征便秘型也很常见。任何 50 岁以上新发便秘、伴报警症状（便血、体重下降、贫血）者，必须先做检查，不要先试针灸。中医证型与这些原因并不一一对应，但有大致的交集：热秘、气秘常见于饮水少、纤维摄入不足和精神紧张者；气虚秘、阳虚秘多见于老年人和慢传输型；阴虚、血虚秘常见于老年、产后或慢性病后；盆底协调障碍与中医证型没有对应关系，需要做生物反馈训练。生活方式（足量饮水、膳食纤维、规律运动、固定排便时间）是基础，针灸可作为辅助，部分研究提示对慢性功能性便秘的自发排便次数有改善，但证据质量不一，不能代替就医。
