CV17

膻中 Dànzhōng

Chest Centre · High risk高危

★Front-mu point of the Pericardium心包募穴Influential point of qi八会穴之气会
⚠️ Sternal foramen: never perpendicular (tamponade, pneumothorax)胸骨孔变异：禁直刺（心包压塞、气胸风险） A congenital foramen in the lower sternum is not rare (autopsy and CT studies report about 4–13%) and cannot be felt: a perpendicular needle can pass through it into the pericardium and heart, and fatal tamponade has been reported. Needle only transversely under the skin; drifting laterally into the intercostal space risks pneumothorax.胸骨下段先天性胸骨孔并不少见（尸检与 CT 研究报道约 4%–13%），体表摸不出：直刺可穿孔直达心包与心脏，曾有心脏压塞致死报道。只沿皮下平刺；偏外进入肋间有气胸风险。

📍 Location定位★

On the chest, level with the 4th intercostal space, on the anterior midline.胸部，横平第 4 肋间隙，前正中线上。

💡 Count down from the sternal angle (2nd rib) to the 4th intercostal space on the midline; in men roughly midway between the nipples, in women rely on rib counting.从胸骨角（平第 2 肋）向下数到第 4 肋间隙，在正中线取穴；男性约在两乳头连线中点，女性以肋间计数为准。

🪡 Needling刺法

Transverse平刺 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5 寸

🛡 Safety安全★

A congenital foramen in the lower sternum is not rare (autopsy and CT studies report about 4–13%) and cannot be felt: a perpendicular needle can pass through it into the pericardium and heart, and fatal tamponade has been reported. Needle only transversely under the skin; drifting laterally into the intercostal space risks pneumothorax.胸骨下段先天性胸骨孔并不少见（尸检与 CT 研究报道约 4%–13%），体表摸不出：直刺可穿孔直达心包与心脏，曾有心脏压塞致死报道。只沿皮下平刺；偏外进入肋间有气胸风险。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Cough, wheezing, chest tightness, heart pain, dysphagia, hiccups (impeded chest qi)咳嗽、气喘、胸闷、心痛、噎膈、呃逆等胸中气机不畅的病证 ⚖️Insufficient lactation, mastitis, breast lumps产后乳少、乳痈、乳癖等胸乳病证

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or semi-reclined with shoulders and chest relaxed; chest localization is for qualified practitioners, and acute chest pain is not treated by needling first.仰卧或半坐位，肩胸放松；胸前定位需由合格人员操作，急性胸痛不先针刺。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    CV17 lies on the anterior midline at the fourth intercostal space; locate the second rib at the sternal angle and count down.膻中位于前正中线上、平第 4 肋间隙；先由胸骨角定位第 2 肋，再向下数至第 4 肋间。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Behind the sternal body lie the pericardium and heart; some people have an impalpable congenital foramen in the lower sternum, so a perpendicular needle can reach the pericardium; drifting into an intercostal space risks the pleura and pneumothorax. Transverse subcutaneous needling only.胸骨体后为心包与心脏，部分人胸骨下段有先天性胸骨孔（体表摸不出），直刺可穿孔伤及心包；向两侧偏入肋间则有胸膜、肺与气胸风险。只沿皮下平刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Use the sternal angle for the second rib, count gently to the fourth interspace and confirm the midline; nipple level varies with sex, age and body habitus and is only secondary.以胸骨角为第 2 肋标志，轻数至第 4 肋间后确认正中线；乳头连线受性别、年龄及体型影响，仅作辅助。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    The midpoint between the nipples is only a rough check; use rib counting when breast position varies or in women.两乳头连线中点仅供粗略核对，女性及乳房位置改变者以肋间计数为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Nipple-line-only localization may be too high or low with body habitus, age or breast position; deep midline insertion risks cardiac, pulmonary and mediastinal injury or pneumothorax. Keep any procedure superficial.只按乳头连线取穴会因体型、年龄或乳房位置而偏高偏低；胸骨中线深刺会有心肺及纵隔损伤、气胸风险，必须严格浅表并避开深直刺。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

A core adjunct for cough with chest tightness or restricted breathing; acute infection, hemoptysis or respiratory distress needs medical care first.咳嗽伴胸闷、气短或胸中气机不畅时可作核心配穴；急性感染、咯血或呼吸窘迫先诊治。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Consider for stable wheeze with chest tightness and qi counterflow, with its qi-influential status; an acute attack or hypoxia needs emergency care.胸闷气逆、稳定期气喘时考虑，兼气会属性；急性发作或低氧不能以针灸延误急救。

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

A traditional chest adjunct for palpitations with tightness; new palpitations with fainting, chest pain or breathlessness need cardiac assessment first.传统心悸兼胸闷时作为胸部配穴；新发心悸伴晕厥、胸痛或气短应先作心电评估。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Chest-wall pain, breast-related pain or palpitations胸壁痛、乳房相关疼痛或心悸

Chest-wall, breast and cardiopulmonary pain can overlap. A breast mass, skin dimpling or bloody discharge needs breast assessment; palpitations with fainting or chest pain need urgent care.胸壁、乳房与心肺疼痛可能重叠；乳房肿块、皮肤凹陷或血性溢液需乳腺评估，心悸伴晕厥或胸痛需急诊。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Crushing chest pain, breathlessness, cold sweat, fainting or palpitations with chest pain requires emergency care; pain on inhalation after needling needs pneumothorax exclusion.胸部压榨痛、呼吸困难、冷汗、晕厥或心悸伴胸痛：立即急诊；针后吸气痛也须排除气胸。

Where is CV17 Dànzhōng?膻中（CV17）在哪里？

On the chest, level with the 4th intercostal space, on the anterior midline.胸部，横平第 4 肋间隙，前正中线上。

How is CV17 needled?膻中（CV17）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.3–0.5 cun.平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of CV17?膻中（CV17）是哪类特定穴？

Front-mu point of the Pericardium; Influential point of qi心包募穴；八会穴之气会

Main safety point at CV17?膻中（CV17）最要注意的安全问题？

A congenital foramen in the lower sternum is not rare (autopsy and CT studies report about 4–13%) and cannot be felt: a perpendicular needle can pass through it into the pericardium and heart, and fatal tamponade has been reported. Needle only transversely under the skin; drifting laterally into the intercostal space risks pneumothorax.胸骨下段先天性胸骨孔并不少见（尸检与 CT 研究报道约 4%–13%），体表摸不出：直刺可穿孔直达心包与心脏，曾有心脏压塞致死报道。只沿皮下平刺；偏外进入肋间有气胸风险。
