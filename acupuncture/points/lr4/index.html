Where is LR4 Zhōngfēng?中封（LR4）在哪里？

On the anteromedial ankle, anterior to the medial malleolus, in the depression medial to the tendon of tibialis anterior.踝区，内踝前，胫骨前肌腱的内侧缘凹陷中。

How is LR4 needled?中封（LR4）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of LR4?中封（LR4）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-river (metal)经穴（金）

Main safety point at LR4?中封（LR4）最要注意的安全问题？

The great saphenous vein and saphenous nerve pass in front of the medial malleolus: locate the vein first and keep the needle out of the ankle joint capsule.大隐静脉与隐神经从内踝前方经过：先看清静脉；针尖不进踝关节囊。
