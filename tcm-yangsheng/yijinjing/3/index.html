✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Poor balance or a history of falls: keep the heels down, or hold a chair back平衡不好或容易跌倒：脚跟不提，或扶着椅背做
  • High blood pressure: do not clench the jaw or hold the breath while pushing up, and shorten the hold高血压：上托时不咬紧牙关、不憋气，静站时间缩短
  • Shoulder pain: push up only as far as is pain-free肩痛：上托到不痛的高度即可

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Bring the arms in front of the chest, palms down, about a fist's width from the chest两臂向前收到胸前，掌心向下，掌与胸相距约一拳
  2. Turn the hands over to just below the earlobes, palms up, elbows out at shoulder height两掌翻转到耳垂下方，掌心向上，两肘向外与肩平
  3. Shift the weight onto the balls of the feet and slowly lift the heels; at the same time push the palms up above the head, palms up, elbows straightening重心移到前脚掌，脚跟慢慢提起；同时两掌向上托到头顶上方，掌心向上，伸肘
  4. Tuck the chin slightly and hold下颏微收，静站
  5. Make fists, lower the heels slowly, and bring the fists down past the sides两掌握拳，脚跟慢慢落地，两拳经体侧下落

🌬 Breathe in as the hands push up, breathe naturally while holding, out as you come down. Do not hold the breath while pushing.上托时吸气，静站时自然呼吸，落下时呼气。不要憋着气托住不放。

✅ Key points要领

  • Feel the push run from the soles of the feet to the heels of the hands上托的力从脚底一直传到掌根
  • Lift the heels only as high as you can stay steady提踵高度以站得稳为准
  • Shoulders relaxed, not hunched两肩放松不耸

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Throwing the head back and arching the lower back仰头看天、腰向前挺
  • Lifting the heels so high that you wobble提踵太高站不稳
  • Holding the breath and clenching the jaw憋气咬牙

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Keep the heels down; or hold a chair back with one hand and push up with the other.脚跟不提起，两脚平站；或者一只手扶椅背，另一只手上托。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 The old verse begins with the palms pushing up to the gate of heaven, eyes looking up, standing upright on the toes. Today's Health Qigong version drops the upward gaze and asks for a slightly tucked chin; the verse also says to clench the teeth and not let go, which beginners and people with high blood pressure need not follow.旧本歌诀开头：「掌托天门目上观，足尖着地立身端。」今天健身气功版不要求仰头上看，改为下颏微收；歌诀里「咬紧牙关不放宽」，初学者和血压高的人不必照做。
