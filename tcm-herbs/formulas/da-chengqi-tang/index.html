Major Order the Qi Decoction大承气汤

Dà Chéng Qì Tāng · The earliest source is Zhang Zhongjing's Treatise on Cold Damage from the Han dynasty. The textual anchors are the Yangming disease passages 209, 212, 238, and 252–255, together with the Major Order the Qi Decoction formula in the Treatise on Cold Damage.汉·张仲景《伤寒论》；原文锚点取《伤寒论》阳明病篇第二〇九、二一二、二三八、二五二至二五五条及大承气汤方。

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Major Order the Qi Decoction大承气汤

Original text原文

「大黃四兩（酒洗）厚朴半斤（炙，去皮）枳實五枚（炙）芒硝三合」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

The formula consists of rhubarb, magnolia bark, immature bitter orange, and mirabilite. Rhubarb and mirabilite purge accumulation and drain heat, while magnolia bark and immature bitter orange move qi and relieve fullness.方由大黄、厚朴、枳实、芒硝四味组成。大黄与芒硝攻下泄热，厚朴与枳实行气除满。

「陽明病，潮熱，大便微鞕者，可與大承氣湯，不鞕者，不可與之。」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

In Yangming disease, tidal fever with slightly hard stools is an indication to consider this formula. If the stools are not hard, it should not be administered, emphasizing the need to establish bowel excess.阳明病出现潮热、大便微硬，是考虑使用本方的重要原文锚点；若大便不硬，则不可使用，强调必须辨明腑实。

「陽明病，發熱，汗多者，急下之，宜大承氣湯。」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

In Yangming disease with fever and profuse sweating, the original text describes urgent purgation and names this formula as appropriate. This passage illustrates its strong purgative character for heat accumulation.阳明病发热、汗出较多，属于原文所述需要急下的情形之一，体现本方峻下热结的特点。

Cautions使用注意

This is a powerful cold purgative intended for specific patterns of Yangming bowel excess with dry stool accumulation. It must not be used independently merely because of constipation or abdominal distension. People with spleen and stomach deficiency-cold, qi and blood deficiency, insufficient body fluids, pregnancy, or breastfeeding should not use it without professional assessment. Older or frail individuals and those with dehydration, impaired kidney function, or serious gastrointestinal disease require evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional. The original text explicitly states that the formula should not be given when the stool is not hard and that further doses should be withheld once purgation occurs. Severe persistent abdominal pain, marked abdominal distension, vomiting, inability to pass stool or gas, bloody stool, altered consciousness, or fever accompanied by significant dehydration or collapse requires immediate medical assessment to exclude bowel obstruction, gastrointestinal bleeding, and other acute abdominal emergencies. This educational material is not an individual diagnosis or prescription.本方为峻下寒下之剂，适用于阳明腑实、燥屎内结等特定证候，不可仅凭便秘或腹胀自行使用。脾胃虚寒、气血亏虚、津液不足、孕期及哺乳期人群应避免自行使用；年老体弱、脱水、肾功能异常或有严重胃肠疾病者须由合格医疗人员评估。原文明确指出大便不硬不可与本方，并强调服药后得下应停止后续服用。若出现持续剧烈腹痛、腹部明显膨隆、呕吐、停止排气排便、便血、意识改变、发热伴明显脱水或虚脱，应立即就医，以排除肠梗阻、消化道出血及急腹症等情况。本资料用于学习，不构成个人诊断或处方。

Contraindicated in pregnancy孕妇禁用 This site applies a strict precautionary classification. Major Order the Qi Decoction is a powerful purgative containing rhubarb and mirabilite. Although the existing herb record classifies rhubarb as requiring pregnancy caution, the formula's strong purgative action creates additional potential risks during pregnancy. The complete formula is therefore classified as contraindicated during pregnancy and should not be self-administered.本站从严：大承气汤属于峻下剂，含大黄及芒硝，具有强烈攻下作用；即使现有药档将大黄标记为孕期慎用，方剂整体仍须考虑峻下攻积对妊娠的潜在风险，因此列为孕期禁用，不应自行使用。
