Ginseng Toxin-Expelling Powder败毒散（人参败毒散）

Rén Shēn Bài Dú Sǎn · The formula is recorded in Qian Yi's Key to Therapeutics of Children's Diseases from the Song dynasty and later appears in the Taiping Huimin Heji Jufang; the textual anchor used here is Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie from the Qing dynasty.宋·钱乙《小儿药证直诀》所载方，后见于《太平惠民和剂局方》；原文锚点取清·汪昂《医方集解》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Ginseng Toxin-Expelling Powder败毒散（人参败毒散）

Original text原文

「人參羌活獨活柴胡前胡川芎枳壳桔梗茯苓（一兩）甘草（五錢。）」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

The source lists ginseng, Qiang Huo, Du Huo, Chai Hu, Qian Hu, Chuan Xiong, Zhi Ke, Jie Geng, and Fu Ling at one liang each, with Gan Cao at five qian. The original punctuation is ambiguous, but the traditional formula composition assigns one liang to each of the first nine ingredients and five qian to Gan Cao.原文列出人参、羌活、独活、柴胡、前胡、川芎、枳壳、桔梗、茯苓各一两，甘草五钱。原文括号的剂量标注存在断句歧义，按传统方剂组成理解为前九味各一两、甘草五钱。

「治傷寒頭痛，憎寒壯熱，项繩睛暗，鼻塞声重，風痰咳嗽；及時氣疫疠，岚瘴鬼疟」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

The passage describes headache, aversion to cold, fever, nasal obstruction, a heavy nasal voice, and cough with wind-phlegm associated with external pathogenic influences. It also mentions epidemic seasonal disorders and miasmatic febrile illnesses. Some characters in the passage are textually uncertain.主治外感风寒湿邪所致头痛、恶寒发热、鼻塞声重及风痰咳嗽等表现，亦记载用于时行疫疠、山岚瘴疟一类病证。原文部分字句存在版本及识读问题。

「此足太陽、少陽、手太陰藥也。羌活入太陽而理游風；獨活入少陰而理伏風，兼能去溼」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Wang Ang explains the traditional channel associations of Qiang Huo and Du Huo, assigning them to the Taiyang and Shaoyin systems respectively, with both addressing wind-damp patterns. This is a traditional theoretical explanation rather than modern pharmacological evidence.汪昂从经脉归经角度说明方中羌活偏入太阳、独活偏入少阴，并指出二药兼顾风湿。本段是传统方义解释，不应等同于现代药理证据。

Cautions使用注意

This is an acrid-warm exterior-releasing and qi-supporting formula traditionally associated with external wind-cold-damp patterns accompanied by insufficient upright qi. It should not be self-selected based on isolated symptoms such as headache or fever. People with yin deficiency and internal heat, pronounced excess heat, or marked thirst and irritability without aversion to cold require professional pattern assessment. Seek prompt medical attention for persistent or high fever, breathing difficulty, chest pain, altered consciousness, severe dehydration, persistent vomiting, or rapidly worsening symptoms. Suspected influenza, COVID-19, or another communicable illness warrants appropriate testing and medical assessment. Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals, children, people with chronic conditions, and those taking other medicines should consult a physician or qualified practitioner before use.本方为辛温解表、益气祛邪之剂，适用于外感风寒湿邪兼正气不足的相应证候，不宜仅凭头痛、发热等单一症状自行使用。阴虚内热、实热炽盛、明显口渴烦热而无恶寒等表现，应由合格医疗人员辨证评估。发热持续或高热、呼吸困难、胸痛、意识改变、严重脱水、持续呕吐或症状迅速加重时，应及时就医；疑似流感、新冠或其他传染性疾病时，应进行适当检测与医疗评估。孕期、哺乳期、儿童、慢性病患者及正在服用其他药物者，使用前应咨询医生或合格中医师。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Qiang Huo, Chuan Xiong, Ren Shen, Jie Geng, and Gan Cao are classified as requiring pregnancy caution in the existing herb records. The entire formula therefore carries a caution classification. It should not be self-administered during pregnancy; assessment by an obstetric clinician and a qualified Chinese medicine practitioner is recommended.方中羌活、川芎、人参、桔梗、甘草均按现有药档标记为孕期慎用；因此整方采用慎用级别。孕期不宜自行服用，应由产科医生及具备资质的中医师共同评估。
