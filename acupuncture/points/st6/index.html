🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is ST6 Jiáchē?颊车（ST6）在哪里？

On the face, one finger-breadth (middle finger) anterosuperior to the angle of the mandible.面部，下颌角前上方一横指（中指）。

How is ST6 needled?颊车（ST6）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, or transverse threading toward ST4; 0.3–0.5 cun perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun transverse.直刺，或向地仓平刺透刺；直刺 0.3–0.5 寸；平刺 0.5–1 寸。

Main safety point at ST6?颊车（ST6）最要注意的安全问题？

Stop at the mandible when needling perpendicularly; avoid opening the mouth wide while needles are retained.直刺抵下颌骨即止；留针时不要大张口。
