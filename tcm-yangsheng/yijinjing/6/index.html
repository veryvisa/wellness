Form 6: Showing the claws and spreading the wings第6式 · 出爪亮翅势

Push out and draw back is one repetition; 3 in all推出、收回为一遍，共 3 遍

Chū zhǎo liàng chì shì

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • High blood pressure: no sudden force, no breath-holding, no glaring or clenched teeth高血压：推掌不用猛力，不憋气，不瞪眼咬牙
  • Shoulder or wrist pain: push gently and do not force the wrists upright肩痛、腕痛：推掌用力小，手腕不强行立起

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Bring the right foot back to a parallel stance; stand the palms up in front of the chest, facing forward右脚收回成开立，两手在胸前立掌，掌心向前
  2. Push the palms slowly forward until the arms are straight at shoulder height, fingers spread, heels of the hands leading两掌向前慢慢推出，推到两臂伸直、与肩同高，手指张开、掌根向前
  3. Relax the wrists and draw the hands slowly back to the front of the shoulders, opening the chest手腕放松，两掌慢慢收回到肩前，同时扩胸展肩
  4. Push out again; 3 times in all再推出，共 3 遍

🌬 Breathe out as you push, in as you draw back.推出时呼气，收回时吸气。

✅ Key points要领

  • Push light at first, then firmer, like opening a heavy door推出时先轻后重，像推开一扇门
  • As you draw back, let the shoulder blades move together and the chest open收回时肩胛向后靠拢、胸打开
  • Push at shoulder height推掌高度与肩平

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Hunching the shoulders as you push推掌时耸肩
  • Glaring and clenching the teeth with sudden force瞪眼咬牙用猛力
  • Pushing too fast推得太快

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Seated; push without force, just the movement out and back.坐着做；推掌不用力，只做推出和收回的动作。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Showing the claws is the pushing palm and spreading the wings is the opening of the chest and arms, like a bird spreading its wings. The old verse says to stand tall with fierce eyes, push the hands forward, draw back with effort, and do it seven times; the common Health Qigong teaching today does 3.「出爪」是推掌、「亮翅」是扩胸展臂，比作鸟展翅。旧本歌诀：「挺身兼怒目，推手向当前；用力收回处，功须七次全。」旧本要做 7 次，今天健身气功版通行教法做 3 遍。
