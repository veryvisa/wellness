🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is BL53 Bāohuāng?胞肓（BL53）在哪里？

Sacral region, level with the 2nd posterior sacral foramen, 3 cun lateral to the median sacral crest.骶区，横平第 2 骶后孔，骶正中嵴旁开 3 寸。

How is BL53 needled?胞肓（BL53）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at BL53?胞肓（BL53）最要注意的安全问题？

Superior gluteal vessels and nerve lie deep; withdraw at electric radiation. No sacral needling in pregnancy.深部有臀上血管神经；出现放射麻电感即退针。孕妇骶部不针。
