📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the head neutral and face relaxed, eyes gently closed or looking ahead; do not press on the globe.坐位或仰卧，头正中，面部放松，双眼自然闭合或平视；不得压迫眼球。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    In the hollow at the lateral end of the eyebrow near the superolateral orbital rim. Trace the brow outward and confirm the point lies by the bony rim, not on the eyelid.在眉梢末端凹陷中、眼眶外上缘附近；沿眉弓由内向外摸至眉毛外端，确认穴位在骨性眶缘外上方而非眼睑。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently palpate the lateral brow end and superolateral orbital rim. Lateral supraorbital nerve branches and frontal branches of superficial temporal vessels are superficial; orbicularis oculi lies beneath, with the orbital septum and orbit deep. Only a qualified practitioner may use a shallow tangential brow-line approach; never direct a needle downward, into the orbit, or toward the globe.轻触眉梢末端及眶外上缘；浅层有眶上神经外侧支、颞浅血管额支，针下为眼轮匝肌，深面邻眶隔和眼眶。只能由专业人员沿眉弓切向浅刺，绝不向下、向眶内或朝眼球深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the eyes relaxed, gently palpate the hollow at the lateral brow end and feel the orbital rim; do not press the globe or slide a fingertip into the orbit.闭眼放松，以指腹轻触眉梢末端凹陷并感知眶缘骨面；不可按压眼球或将指腹滑入眶内。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Follow the eyebrow laterally to its end and locate the adjacent hollow at the superolateral orbital rim; with tattooed, shaped, or sparse brows, confirm against the bony rim.沿眉毛外侧走向找到眉梢，在眉梢与眶外上缘相邻的凹陷定位；眉尾纹身、修眉或缺眉者以眶缘骨点复核。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Consider TE23 for focal lateral-brow or superolateral orbital pain with eyelid twitching; posterior-neck or temporal-predominant pain calls for other locations, and visual changes require care first.眉梢或眼眶外上缘局部痛、伴眼睑抽动时可考虑丝竹空；后颈或头侧为主则优先其他部位穴，视力异常先就医。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

Use only as an adjunct for superficial lateral-brow tenderness without visual change, red eye, or pain on eye movement; acute red painful eye or visual change needs urgent ophthalmic care.仅在无视力改变、红眼或眼球活动痛的眉梢浅表压痛中作辅助穴；急性红痛眼或视力变化须先眼科急诊。

Facial palsy面瘫（口眼歪斜） ⚖️

May be an adjunct for eyelid twitching or local brow tension; new incomplete eye closure or facial droop requires urgent stroke and corneal-risk assessment first.眼睑抽动或眉梢局部肌肉紧张可作配穴；新发闭眼不全、口角歪斜应优先急诊排除卒中和角膜暴露风险。

Used for用于 A proximodistal pairing for lateral brow headache with Hand-Shaoyang symptoms; not for acute eye pain or visual change.眉梢头痛伴手少阳经头侧症状时作远近配伍；急性眼痛、视力变化不适用。

💡 A local lateral-brow point is paired with the same-channel forearm luo point; visual symptoms need ophthalmic assessment first.眉梢局部穴与同经络穴相配，兼顾局部与前臂远端；视力症状须先由眼科评估。

Lateral-brow point of the Hand-Shaoyang Triple Energizer; classical texts advise against moxibustion. Only a trained acupuncturist should use a shallow brow-line approach, never toward the orbit.手少阳三焦经眉梢穴；古籍明确不宜灸。仅由受训针灸人员作浅层沿眉弓处理，避免眶内方向。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「絲竹空，一名巨，在眉後陷者中，足少陽脈氣所發，刺入三分，留三呼，不宜灸，灸之不幸令人目小及盲」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing locates Sizhukong in the hollow behind the eyebrow and explicitly advises against moxibustion, warning of eye injury; modern practice must also prevent needle entry into the orbit.《针灸甲乙经》将丝竹空定位于眉后凹陷，并明确不宜灸，记载误灸可能伤目；现代操作更须防止针尖进入眶内。

「眩，頭痛，刺絲竹空主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing lists Sizhukong for dizziness and headache; acute eye disease and neurologic red flags must first be excluded.《针灸甲乙经》记眩晕、头痛可取丝竹空；须先排除急性眼病和神经系统红旗。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Pain at the superolateral orbital rim or orbicularis oculi眶外上缘或眼轮匝肌局部痛

Local tenderness may relate to brow soft-tissue strain or orbicularis tension; red eye, photophobia, visual change, or pain with eye movement needs ophthalmic assessment first.局部压痛可与眉弓软组织劳损或眼轮匝肌紧张有关；红眼、畏光、视力改变或眼球活动痛需先眼科评估。

Brow-orbital neuralgia or eyelid spasm眉眶神经痛或眼睑痉挛

Unilateral electric pain or persistent eyelid twitching needs assessment of nerve, ocular-surface, and medication causes; numbness or ptosis should not be managed with point pressure alone.单侧电击样痛或持续眼睑抽动需评估神经、眼表及药物因素；麻木或眼睑下垂不应只按穴。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is TE23 Sīzhúkōng?丝竹空（TE23）在哪里？

Face, in the depression at the lateral end of the eyebrow.面部，眉梢凹陷中。

How is TE23 needled?丝竹空（TE23）怎么刺？

Transverse along the eyebrow laterally or medially; 0.3–0.5 cun.沿眉弓向外或向内平刺；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of TE23?丝竹空（TE23）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Triple Energizer channel手少阳三焦经穴

Main safety point at TE23?丝竹空（TE23）最要注意的安全问题？

This point lies next to the orbit and direction of the globe; needle only along the eyebrow, never into the orbit, downward, or deeply. Visual change, persistent eye pain, or rapidly increasing periorbital swelling needs emergency assessment now.紧邻眼眶和眼球方向；只沿眉弓平刺，绝不可向眶内、向下或深刺。出现视力改变、持续眼痛或眼周迅速肿胀应立即急诊评估。
