Minor Bupleurum Decoction小柴胡汤

Xiǎo Cháihú Tāng · Zhang Zhongjing, Shanghan Lun (Eastern Han), Taiyang part two and the Shaoyang chapter东汉·张仲景《伤寒论》太阳病中篇、少阳病篇

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Minor Bupleurum Decoction小柴胡汤
⚠️ Interstitial pneumonia when combined with interferon与干扰素合用曾致间质性肺炎 In the 1990s Japan reported interstitial pneumonia, some fatal, in chronic hepatitis patients taking Xiao Chai Hu Tang, especially with interferon; Japan then banned the combination and added warnings. If a dry cough, breathlessness or fever appears while taking it, stop and see a doctor at once. The current Japanese prescribing insert lists three contraindications: interferon treatment; cirrhosis or liver cancer; and chronic hepatitis with abnormal liver function and platelets of 100,000/mm³ or less. This site also advises against it with a history of interstitial lung disease such as pulmonary fibrosis.1990 年代日本报告：慢性肝炎病人服用小柴胡汤（尤其与干扰素合用）后发生间质性肺炎，部分死亡；日本随后禁止二者合用并加警示。服用中出现干咳、气促、发热，立即停服就医。日本现行处方药说明书列三条禁忌：正在用干扰素、肝硬化或肝癌、慢性肝炎肝功能异常且血小板 10 万/mm³ 以下。另外本站从严建议：有肺纤维化等间质性肺病史者不要使用。

Original text原文

「傷寒五六日，中風，往來寒熱，胸脅苦滿，嘿嘿不欲飲食，心煩喜嘔」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Five or six days into a cold: chills and fever taking turns, fullness in the chest and flanks, a quiet gloom with no appetite, irritability and a constant urge to vomit. Later teachers called these 'the four Chai Hu signs'.外感五六天，冷一阵热一阵，胸胁胀满，闷闷不乐、不想吃东西，心烦、老想吐——这四个主症后世称「柴胡四证」。

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Harmonises the lesser yang和解少阳

Lesser-yang pattern of cold damage: alternating chills and fever, fullness in the chest and flanks, gloom without appetite, irritability and urge to vomit, bitter mouth, dry throat and dizziness, thin white coating, a wiry pulse. Also for 'heat entering the blood chamber', malaria-like and jaundice conditions showing the lesser-yang pattern.伤寒少阳证：往来寒热，胸胁苦满，默默不欲饮食，心烦喜呕，口苦咽干目眩，苔薄白，脉弦。也用于妇人热入血室、疟疾、黄疸等见少阳证者。

How the formula is built方解

The lesser yang lies between exterior and interior: the pathogen is neither on the surface (so sweating is wrong) nor inside (so purging is wrong), so the method is to harmonise. Chai Hu vents outward and Huang Qin clears inside; Ban Xia and ginger stop vomiting; ginseng, jujube and licorice support the upright qi so the pathogen cannot pass inward, the formula's distinctive touch. The line 'if just one sign is seen, that is enough; not all are needed' is among the most quoted in clinical practice.少阳在表里之间，邪既不在表（不能汗）、也不在里（不能下），只能「和解」。柴胡透邪外出、黄芩清里热，是和解的主体；半夏、生姜止呕；人参、大枣、甘草扶正，防邪内传太阴——这一组扶正药是本方的独到之处。原文说「但见一证便是，不必悉具」，是临床最常被引用的一句。

Cautions使用注意

Chai Hu rises and disperses and Huang Qin is bitter-cold: caution with yin and blood deficiency or rising liver yang; not for upper excess with lower deficiency or vomiting blood. Stop and see a doctor if cough, breathlessness or fever appear.柴胡升散、黄芩苦寒，阴虚血少、肝阳上亢者慎用；上实下虚、吐血者不宜。出现咳嗽气促发热立即停服就医。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Contains ginseng and licorice (both caution on this site); in pregnancy leave it to a licensed practitioner.含人参、甘草（本站均按慎用）；孕期由持照中医师判断。
