Friction tenosynovitis where abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis cross the radial wrist extensors, about 4–8 cm above the wrist — right around LI6; painful on wrist extension, sometimes with crepitus, common in rowers and weightlifters.拇长展肌、拇短伸肌腱跨过桡侧腕伸肌腱处的摩擦性腱鞘炎，在腕上约 4–8 厘米（正是偏历附近），伸腕时痛并可有捻发感，常见于划船、举重。
