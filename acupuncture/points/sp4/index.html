🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Medial plantar nerve entrapment (jogger's foot)足底内侧神经卡压（慢跑者足 / Jogger's foot）

Common in distance runners or individuals with pes planus; pain sits along the medial longitudinal arch (under the navicular and first metatarsal base) radiating to the medial sole and toes 1–3, with a positive Tinel sign posterior-inferior to SP4. Avoid heavy needle manipulation directly on the nerve trunk; refer if intrinsic foot muscle atrophy or persistent sensory loss occurs.长跑或足外翻扁平足人群常见，疼痛位于足内侧纵弓（第 1 跖骨基底与舟骨下方）并向足底内侧及第 1–3 趾放射，叩击公孙后下方可出现 Tinel 征放射麻感；需避免在神经干上强刺激针刺，若伴有足内在肌萎缩或持续感觉丧失应转诊骨科或神经科。

Abductor hallucis strain and medial arch myofascial pain拇展肌劳损与内侧纵弓筋膜痛

Prolonged standing, rigid footwear, or arch collapse causes traction pain at the abductor hallucis origin overlapping SP4; differentiate from plantar fasciitis (tender more posteriorly at the medial calcaneal tuberosity) and first metatarsal base stress fracture.长时间站立、穿硬底鞋或足弓塌陷导致拇展肌起点牵拉痛，局部压痛点常与公孙重合；需与足底筋膜炎（痛点更偏跟骨内侧结节）及第 1 跖骨基底应力性骨折鉴别。

Where is SP4 Gōngsūn?公孙（SP4）在哪里？

On the medial foot, anteroinferior to the base of the first metatarsal bone, at the border of the red and white flesh.跖区，第 1 跖骨底的前下缘赤白肉际处。

How is SP4 needled?公孙（SP4）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.6–1.2 cun.直刺；0.6–1.2 寸。

Special-point roles of SP4?公孙（SP4）是哪类特定穴？

Luo-connecting point; Confluent point of the eight extraordinary vessels (Chong vessel)络穴；八脉交会穴（通冲脉）

Main safety point at SP4?公孙（SP4）最要注意的安全问题？

Insert along the lower edge of the metatarsal base; back off and redirect on striking bone. Branches of the medial plantar nerve are deep—withdraw at electric tingling.沿跖骨底下缘直刺，遇骨即稍退调整方向；足底内侧神经分支在深部，出现放射样电麻感即退针。
