TE10

Heavenly Well · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

Posterior elbow, in the hollow 1 cun superior to the olecranon when the elbow is flexed.肘后区，屈肘时肘尖上1寸凹陷中。

💡 Flex the elbow and locate in the triceps-tendon hollow proximal to the olecranon.屈肘，在肘尖近端的三头肌腱凹陷取穴。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the elbow flexed about 90 degrees and forearm relaxed; identify the olecranon and both epicondyles first.坐位或仰卧，屈肘约 90°，前臂放松；先辨认肘尖与内、外上髁。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    With the elbow flexed, locate the hollow about 1 cun proximal to the olecranon. Flexion clarifies the olecranon and posterior humeral contours; do not use the tip itself as the point.屈肘时在肘尖（尺骨鹰嘴）近端约 1 寸的凹陷中；屈肘可使鹰嘴与肱骨后方轮廓清楚，定位不以肘尖本身代替凹陷。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With the elbow flexed, identify the triceps tendon and hollow above the olecranon. The ulnar nerve runs behind the medial epicondyle in its groove; deep medial or posterior probing is hazardous. Keep location in the superficial posterior hollow and do not probe medially or deeply.屈肘辨认肱三头肌腱及鹰嘴上方凹陷；尺神经走在内上髁后方的尺神经沟，明显偏内、偏后深刺会有风险。定位于肘后骨性标志旁的浅层凹陷，不朝内侧或深部探刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate the hollow above the olecranon as the elbow flexes and extends. Confirm its relation to the olecranon; if the ulnar groove is reached or tingling radiates to the little finger, move away immediately.手指轻触肘尖上方的凹陷并屈伸肘关节；以凹陷和肘尖近端关系复核，若触到尺神经沟或出现小指放射麻电感，立即离开该处。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Flex the elbow and measure about 1 cun proximally from the olecranon into the palpable posterior soft-tissue hollow; not every area above the elbow tip is the point.患者屈肘，沿尺骨鹰嘴向近端轻量约 1 寸，停在肘后可触及的软组织凹陷；不可把肘尖上方所有软组织都当作穴位。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting posteromedially approaches the ulnar groove behind the medial epicondyle and may provoke tingling into the little finger and ulnar ring finger; deep probing may irritate the capsule or nerve, so remain superficial and away from the nerve course.偏向内侧后方会靠近内上髁后的尺神经沟，可诱发小指、环指尺侧放射麻痛；向深部直探也可能刺激关节囊或神经，宜保持浅层并避开尺神经走向。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

Consider TE10 for posterior-elbow hollow pain and restricted flexion without ulnar sensory symptoms; pain at the lateral epicondyle with resisted wrist extension suggests a different problem and point strategy.后肘凹陷痛、屈肘受限而无尺神经感觉异常时可考虑天井；肘外上髁抗阻伸腕痛更像网球肘，选穴重点不同。

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

TE10 may be used for posterior-elbow pain tracking proximally along the Hand-Shaoyang channel; focal shoulder pain needs shoulder assessment first.疼痛主要在肘后并沿手少阳向上牵扯时，天井可作远端肘部穴；肩关节局部疼痛应优先评估肩部。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Olecranon bursitis肘后滑囊炎

A localized tender swelling over the olecranon can occur with bursitis; redness, warmth, fever, or rapidly enlarging swelling needs evaluation for infection.肘尖后方局限肿包、压痛可见于滑囊炎；红热、发热或迅速增大的肿胀需排除感染。

Ulnar nerve entrapment at the elbow肘尺神经卡压

Numbness in the little finger and ulnar ring finger that worsens with elbow flexion or at night suggests ulnar nerve compression; avoid repeated pressure in the groove and assess nerve function.屈肘时小指和无名指尺侧麻木、夜间加重提示尺神经受压，应避免在尺神经沟反复按压并评估神经功能。

Posterior elbow, in the hollow 1 cun superior to the olecranon when the elbow is flexed.肘后区，屈肘时肘尖上1寸凹陷中。

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1寸。

Special-point roles of TE10?天井（TE10）是哪类特定穴？

He-sea point, earth合穴，属土

Main safety point at TE10?天井（TE10）最要注意的安全问题？
