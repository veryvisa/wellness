SP17

食窦 Shídòu

Food Cavity · High risk高危

⚠️ Pneumothorax risk point气胸风险穴 SP17 lies in the 5th intercostal space 6 cun lateral where the chest wall is thin; pleura and lung are just beyond the intercostal muscles. Insert only obliquely or transversely outward 0.5–0.8 cun—never perpendicular, deep medially or with wide lifting-thrusting. Chest pain, breathlessness, worsening cough or stabbing pain on breathing after needling needs urgent assessment for pneumothorax.食窦在第 5 肋间隙旁开 6 寸，胸壁薄，肋间肌后即胸膜与肺。只斜刺或向外平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不直刺、不向内深刺、不大幅提插。针后出现胸痛、气短、咳嗽加重或呼吸时刺痛，立即就医排查气胸。

📍 Location定位★

On the lateral chest, in the 5th intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 5 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

💡 The nipples are 8 cun apart, so 6 cun lateral is 2 cun outside the midclavicular line; count down from the sternal angle (2nd rib) to the 5th intercostal space. In women do not use the nipple.两乳头间为 8 寸，旁开 6 寸在锁骨中线外 2 寸；从胸骨角（平第 2 肋）向下数到第 5 肋间隙取穴，女性不以乳头定位。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse outward斜刺或向外平刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never needle deeply.不可深刺。

🛡 Safety安全

Pleura and lung lie just deep to the intercostal muscles and the chest wall is thin here: insert only obliquely or transversely outward, never perpendicularly or deep medially—risk of pneumothorax; avoid the intercostal vessels and nerve.肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺，此处胸壁薄：只斜刺或向外平刺，不直刺、不向内深刺，有气胸风险；避开肋间血管神经。

  • Inserting deeply or perpendicularly toward the medial thoracic cavity: the chest wall is thin here, and the pleura and lung lie directly beneath the intercostals; deep or perpendicular needling readily punctures the pleura and causes traumatic pneumothorax or lung injury.深刺或向内侧（胸腔方向）直刺：该处胸壁薄，肋间肌深面紧邻胸膜和肺，直刺或深刺极易刺破胸膜导致创伤性气胸，甚至损伤肺实质。
  • Needling right along the inferior border of the fifth rib: the intercostal vessels and nerve run in the costal groove along the lower rib margin; angling too high risks puncturing the intercostal artery (causing chest-wall hematoma) or irritating the intercostal nerve (causing sharp radiating pain).沿第 5 肋骨下缘进针：肋间血管和肋间神经贴第 5 肋下缘肋沟走行，针尖偏向上方肋骨下缘易刺破肋间动脉引起胸壁血肿，或刺激肋间神经引发剧烈电击样放射痛。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

Used only as an adjunct when cough is accompanied by lateral chest-hypochondriac distension, not as a primary antitussive point; BL13, LU7, or LU9 are preferred first-line points for cough, and SP17 is avoided unless lateral chest symptoms warrant it due to pneumothorax risk.仅作为咳嗽兼见胸胁胀痛、气机不利时的局部配穴，不作止咳主穴；外感及内伤咳嗽多首选肺俞（BL13）、列缺（LU7）或太渊（LU9），且食窦有气胸风险，无胸胁痛时不优先选用。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

May serve as an adjunct for lateral chest tightness in asthma, but does not replace core dyspnea points such as EX-B1, BL13, or CV17; acute severe asthma exacerbations require immediate medical intervention.哮喘发作期胸胁满闷、呼吸不畅时可作辅助配穴以宽胸理气，但不替代定喘、肺俞（BL13）、膻中（CV17）等核心平喘穴；重症哮喘急性发作须先就医急救。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「食竇，在天溪下一寸六分陷者中，足太陰脈氣所發，仰而取之，刺入四分，灸五壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing places SP17 1.6 cun below SP18 (one intercostal space lower, in the fifth intercostal space) within a depression where Foot-Taiyin channel qi emerges, advising supine positioning and shallow insertion of 0.4 cun — reflecting classical awareness of thoracic needling safety.《针灸甲乙经》指出食窦位于天溪（第 4 肋间隙）下一寸六分（即下一肋间隙，第 5 肋间隙）的凹陷中，为足太阴脾经脉气所发之处，主张仰卧取穴并严格限制浅刺四分，体现了古人对胸壁危险穴浅刺防伤脏腑的安全意识。

「食竇 天谿下一寸六分，去胸中行各六寸，舉臂取之」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Dacheng specifies the lateral measurement of 6 cun from the anterior midline, matching modern standards; its note to 'locate with the arm raised' helps expose the lateral intercostal spaces, though in modern practice the arm should be lowered before needling to prevent skin traction and needle bending.《针灸大成》明确了食窦距离前正中线（胸中行）六寸的横向定位标准，与现代国标一致；其中「举臂取之」提示抬臂可使胸外侧肋间隙充分展露，但现代临床在抬臂定位后需放下手臂再进针，防止皮肤回缩引起滞针或弯针。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Intercostal neuralgia and thoracic herpes zoster neuralgia肋间神经痛与胸壁带状疱疹神经痛

Stabbing or burning pain tracking along the fifth intercostal space, worsened by deep breathing, coughing, or trunk rotation. Inspect the skin closely for grouped vesicles or post-herpetic scarring; if pain is accompanied by fever, dyspnea, or radiation to the left shoulder/back, seek immediate medical evaluation to rule out cardiopulmonary emergencies.沿第 5 肋间隙呈带状分布的针刺样或烧灼样疼痛，深呼吸、咳嗽或转身时加重。需仔细检查局部皮肤有无成簇水疱或色素沉着以排查带状疱疹；若疼痛伴随发热、呼吸困难或向左肩背放射，必须先就医排除心肺及胸膜急症。

Serratus anterior myofascial pain syndrome (lateral chest wall strain)前锯肌肌筋膜疼痛综合征（胸外侧壁劳损）

Common after severe coughing, push-up/rowing workouts, or heavy lifting; taut trigger points in the serratus anterior near SP17 reproduce lateral chest and inferior scapular pain on palpation. Needle only superficially into the muscle belly; if focal bony tenderness or crepitus follows trauma, obtain imaging to rule out rib fracture.常见于剧烈咳嗽后、俯卧撑/划船训练或搬运重物后，在食窦附近前锯肌肌腹处可触及痛性激痛点，按压可复制胸外侧及肩胛下角牵涉痛。针刺仅限肌腹浅层平刺，严禁深刺；若外伤后局部骨擦感或剧烈压痛，需拍片排除肋骨骨折。

Costochondritis and non-cardiac chest wall pain肋软骨炎及非心源性胸壁痛

SP17 lies lateral to the fifth costochondral junction, where intercostal strain or rib periosteal irritation can cause focal tenderness. Reproducibility upon direct palpation suggests musculoskeletal origin, yet any new-onset chest pain (especially left-sided) requires medical clearance first to exclude acute coronary syndrome, pulmonary embolism, and spontaneous pneumothorax.食窦位于第 5 肋骨与肋软骨交界外侧，局部肋间肌劳损或肋骨骨膜刺激可引起局限性压痛。鉴别要点为按压局部可精准重现疼痛；但任何新发胸痛（尤其左侧）必须先通过医学评估排除急性冠脉综合征、肺栓塞及自发性气胸。

🚩 See a doctor先就医

  • Progressive dyspnea, pleuritic chest pain, dry cough, or diminished breath sounds after needling or trauma (suspect pneumothorax or hemothorax — seek emergency care immediately)针刺后或外伤后出现进行性呼吸困难、吸气性胸痛、刺激性干咳或患侧呼吸音减弱（警惕气胸或血气胸，立即就医）
  • Chest wall pain accompanied by high fever, rust-colored or purulent sputum, or pleural friction rub (suspect pneumonia, pleurisy, or empyema)胸壁痛伴随高热、咳铁锈色/脓性痰或胸膜摩擦音（警惕肺炎、胸膜炎或脓胸）
  • Unexplained focal mass, peau d'orange breast skin changes, bloody nipple discharge, or axillary lymphadenopathy (requires specialist evaluation to rule out breast or chest-wall neoplasm)局部出现不明原因肿块、乳房皮肤橘皮样改变、乳头溢血或腋窝淋巴结肿大（需专科排查乳腺或胸壁肿瘤）

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is SP17 Shídòu?食窦（SP17）在哪里？

On the lateral chest, in the 5th intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 5 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

How is SP17 needled?食窦（SP17）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse outward; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺或向外平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at SP17?食窦（SP17）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung lie just deep to the intercostal muscles and the chest wall is thin here: insert only obliquely or transversely outward, never perpendicularly or deep medially—risk of pneumothorax; avoid the intercostal vessels and nerve.肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺，此处胸壁薄：只斜刺或向外平刺，不直刺、不向内深刺，有气胸风险；避开肋间血管神经。
