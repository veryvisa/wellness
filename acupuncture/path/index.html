Studying TCM in BC: school choice and the licence path在 BC 读中医：选校与执照路径

TCM and acupuncture in BC are regulated by CCHPBC (College of Complementary Health Professionals of BC) under a new Act since April 2026. If you want acupuncture, herbs and dietary therapy together, aim for R.TCM.P. (TCM Practitioner) and treat R.Ac. (Acupuncturist) as an early stop on the way. Below: three first-choice schools, the traps to avoid, and a timeline from now to licence.BC 的中医针灸由 CCHPBC（BC 补充健康专业学院）监管，2026 年 4 月起按新法执行。想把针灸、中药和饮食调理都做起来，目标执照应该定在 R.TCM.P.（中医师），R.Ac.（针灸师）只当中途先执业的一站。下面是三所首选学校、要避开的坑、从现在到拿照的时间表。

1 · Pick the licence first1 · 先定执照目标

R.Ac.Acupuncturist: acupuncture (including acupressure and tuina). May not prescribe or dispense herbal or dietary formulas, and may not recommend Health Canada-approved natural health products such as vitamins or minerals; dietary advice only as an adjunct to acupuncture.针灸师：针灸（含指压、推拿）。不得开、配中药方或食疗方，也不得推荐维生素、矿物质等加拿大卫生部批准的天然保健品；饮食指导只能作为针灸的辅助。
PCE Acupuncturist exam (ACU) + safety modules 1–3 + jurisprudence examPCE 针灸师考试（ACU）＋安全课程 1–3＋法规考试
R.TCM.H.TCM Herbalist: prescribes and dispenses herbal and dietary formulas; may recommend natural health products.中药师：开、配、发中药与食疗方，可推荐天然保健品。
PCE Herbalist exam (HER) + safety modules 1, 2, 4 + jurisprudence examPCE 中药师考试（HER）＋安全课程 1、2、4＋法规考试
R.TCM.P.TCM Practitioner: acupuncture + herbs + dietary therapy + natural health product advice; the full TCM scope.中医师：针灸＋中药＋食疗＋推荐天然保健品，全中医范围。
PCE Practitioner exam (PRA), or ACU + HER together; safety modules 1–4 + jurisprudence examPCE 中医师考试（PRA），或 ACU＋HER 两科；安全课程 1–4＋法规考试
Dr.TCMDoctor of TCM: requires a BC R.TCM.P. licence first, then BC's own written and clinical exams; may use the title doctor of TCM.高级中医师：须先持 BC 的 R.TCM.P.，再考 BC 自办的笔试与临床考试；可用 doctor of TCM 头衔。
Dr.TCM written + clinical exams ($1,110 each)Dr.TCM 笔试＋临床考试（各 1,110 加元）

Recommended: enrol directly in an R.TCM.P. program that is built as a ladder (acupuncture years first, herbal year last), sit the ACU when the acupuncture part is done and practise as R.Ac., then add the HER to become R.TCM.P. Same school, one contract, no credit-transfer losses.建议：直接读阶梯式的 R.TCM.P. 课程（先针灸、后中药），针灸部分读完就考 ACU 先以 R.Ac. 执业，最后一年考 HER 升为 R.TCM.P.。同一所学校、同一份合同，没有转学分的损失。

2 · Three first-choice schools2 · 三所首选学校

1

Tzu Chi International College of TCM慈济国际中医学院（Tzu Chi International College of TCM）

W Broadway, Vancouver; private, PTIB-designated (to 2027-03-31)温哥华 W Broadway；私立，PTIB 指定（至 2027-03-31）

Program课程
R.TCM.P. four years, 2,676 hours; finishing year three completes the acupuncturist program, so R.Ac. can come firstR.TCM.P. 四年 2,676 小时；读完第三年即算针灸师课程完成，可先考 R.Ac.
Cost费用
About $44,000 tuition over four years, about $49,900 with books and fees (June 2023 fee sheet; 2027 will be higher)四年学费约 4.4 万，加书本等约 4.99 万（2023-06 收费表，2027 年会更高）
Why理由
BC's oldest and largest school (founded 1986), with the widest alumni network; course-by-course registration, with weekday 6–8 p.m. and Saturday classes and three intakes a year, the easiest fit for someone working; on-site teaching clinic with 3,000+ visits a year.BC 历史最久、规模最大（1986 年建校），同行网络最广；按课程注册，课表有工作日晚 6–8 点和周六时段，一年三次开学，最容易配合在职；校内诊所每年 3,000 多人次。
Risks风险
Published fees date from 2023; many core classes are weekday daytime, so working students may need five years; whether a Chinese-language stream exists was not found; the "over 90% pass rate" is the school's own claim.公开学费是 2023 版；主课多在工作日白天，在职可能要拉长到 5 年；是否有中文授课班未查到；通过率 90% 以上是学校自述。
2

Vancouver Beijing College of Chinese Medicine (VBCCM)温哥华北京中医学院（VBCCM）

Odlin Cres, Richmond; private, PTIB-designated (to 2027-03-31)列治文 Odlin Cres；私立，PTIB 指定（至 2027-03-31）

Program课程
Three years to R.TCM.P.; a fourth year for Dr.TCM三年到 R.TCM.P.，第四年 Dr.TCM
Cost费用
$46,640 for three years; $59,360 for four三年合计 46,640；四年 59,360
Why理由
The only recognised school teaching entirely in Chinese (HSK 5 required); day and evening classes, 9:00–22:30; CCHPBC allows theory classes online in Metro Vancouver; three years to R.TCM.P. at a below-average total price.唯一全中文授课的认可学校（入学要求 HSK 5 级）；日班夜班都有，上课时间 9:00–22:30；理论课获 CCHPBC 准许在大温网授；三年即到中医师，总价在同类中偏低。
Risks风险
Small, with little public information; training only in Chinese affects later English charting and patient communication (CCHPBC's English requirement is not yet published); the website's "3 academic years for acupuncture" does not match the fee sheet, so get the program and fees in writing before signing.规模小、公开信息少；全中文训练对日后用英文接诊、写病历有影响（CCHPBC 的英语要求尚未公布）；网页上「针灸 3 学年」与收费表口径对不上，签约前要拿书面课程与学费合同核对。
3

KPU Bachelor of Traditional Chinese Medicine (BTCM)KPU 中医学士（Bachelor of Traditional Chinese Medicine）

Richmond (Lansdowne); public university列治文 Lansdowne 校区；公立大学

Program课程
135 credits, 10 semesters; after 6 semesters the acupuncture diploma allows R.Ac., after 10 semesters R.TCM.P.135 学分、10 学期；读满 6 学期可拿针灸文凭先考 R.Ac.，读满 10 学期到 R.TCM.P.
Cost费用
135 credits × $390.74 ≈ $52,750 (2026/27 tuition + mandatory fees)135 学分 × 390.74 ≈ 52,750（2026/27 学费＋必缴费）
Why理由
The only public university degree; the only route exempt from the "two years / 60 credits of arts and sciences" registration requirement; the program includes nutrition, Western imaging and labs, pharmacology and practice management.唯一的公立大学学位；唯一能豁免「2 年 / 60 学分文理课」注册要求的路径；课程含营养、西医影像化验、药理、执业管理。
Risks风险
Full-time, cohort-based semester by semester: very hard while working; one fall intake only, limited seats, admitted by the date you meet requirements; taught in English.全日制、按学期整班推进，在职基本做不到；只有秋季一个入学口、名额有限、按资格达成日期先到先录；英文授课。

3 · Ruled out, and why3 · 排除项与原因

  • PCU College of Holistic Medicine: still on CCHPBC's recognised list, but PTIB shows a voluntary closure; the same address now enrols through Vancouver Career College. Do not apply under the PCU name.PCU College of Holistic Medicine：仍在 CCHPBC 认可清单上，但 PTIB 显示已自愿关闭，课程现由同址的 Vancouver Career College 招生。不要按 PCU 名义报名。
  • Vancouver International College of Health and Wellness (Richmond): claims recognition, but is not on the April 2026 recognised list (Table 4). Treat it as not recognised until CCHPBC confirms in writing.Vancouver International College of Health and Wellness（列治文）：官网称获认可，但不在 2026-04-01 版认可清单（Table 4）上。CCHPBC 书面确认之前按不认可处理。
  • Oriental Medicine Institute: not on the recognised list and not found in the PTIB directory.Oriental Medicine Institute：不在认可清单，PTIB 名录查无此校。
  • Pacific Rim College and Oshio College: recognised, but in Victoria; not a realistic commute from the Lower Mainland.Pacific Rim College、Oshio College：认可，但在维多利亚，低陆平原通勤不现实。
  • Vancouver Career College (R.TCM.P. $52,698, full-time career-college chain) and Kootenay Columbia College (R.TCM.P. $67,234, the most expensive): recognised, but poorer value than the top three.Vancouver Career College（R.TCM.P. 52,698，全日制连锁职业学院）与 Kootenay Columbia College（R.TCM.P. 67,234，最贵）：可读，但不如前三所划算。
  • Backup: Central College (New Westminster): Chinese-language stream, the lowest R.TCM.P. price at $42,075, January/May/September intakes; class times and pass rates not found, so fourth.备选 Central College（New Westminster）：有中文班，R.TCM.P. 42,075 全场最低，1/5/9 月开学；上课时段与通过率未查到，列第四。

5 · What registration requires5 · 注册的全部条件

  • Graduate from a recognised program for the licence class认可课程毕业（与所申请的执照类别对应）
  • Plus two years / at least 60 credits of university arts and sciences (KPU BTCM graduates exempt; foreign credentials need an evaluation)另有 2 年 / 至少 60 学分的大学文理课（KPU 中医学士毕业生豁免；国外学历需做学历认证）
  • Pass the national PCE; results valid for 3 years通过 PCE 全国考试，成绩 3 年内有效
  • CCHPBC jurisprudence exam ($197, online) + interactive safety courseCCHPBC 法规考试（197 加元，线上）＋互动安全课程
  • Current Standard First Aid + CPR-C有效的 Standard First Aid＋CPR-C
  • Professional liability insurance of at least $5 million per claim执业责任险每宗不低于 500 万加元
  • English proficiency: CCHPBC says the policy is "coming soon"; not published as of 2026-11-19英语能力：CCHPBC 写「细则即将公布」，截至 2026-11-19 尚未公布
  • Criminal record check (4–12 weeks) and good-character declaration犯罪记录查核（4–12 周）与品行声明

Regulator and exam costs alone for R.Ac. come to about $1,849: application $225 + jurisprudence $197 + PCE application $410 + ACU $942 + safety course $75; plus the first-year licence fee ($1,130 for 2027–2028, prorated by month).只算监管与考试，拿 R.Ac. 约 1,849 加元：申请 225＋法规考 197＋PCE 申请 410＋ACU 942＋安全课 75；另加首年执照费（2027–2028 年度 1,130，按月折算）。

6 · The PCE exam6 · PCE 全国考试

  • The PCE is run by CARB-TCMPA, registered through CCHPBC; twice a year (April and October), computer-based, with multiple-choice and clinical-case parts.PCE 由 CARB-TCMPA 主办，经 CCHPBC 报名；每年 4 月、10 月两次，机考，分选择题和临床案例两部分。
  • BC candidates can view questions in English, Simplified or Traditional Chinese during the exam.BC 考生在考场可以选看英文、简体或繁体中文题目。
  • ACU (acupuncturist): 125 multiple-choice questions in 2.5 hours + 40 clinical cases in 2.5 hours; exam fee $942 plus CCHPBC's $410 application fee.ACU（针灸师）：选择题 125 题 2.5 小时＋临床案例 40 题 2.5 小时；考试费 942 加元，另有 CCHPBC 申请费 410。
  • April 2027 sitting: ACU on April 29–30, registration closes 2027-01-15. Dates from 2028 on are not yet published.2027 年 4 月场：ACU 4 月 29–30 日，报名截止 2027-01-15。2028 年以后的日期尚未公布。
  • After a first fail, a 50-hour approved review course is required before retaking; three fails on the same exam ends eligibility for it.第一次不过，须先上 50 小时获批复习课才能再考；同一考试三次不过不得再考。
