📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine, side-lying, or sitting upright with the foot relaxed and the medial malleolus clearly exposed.仰卧位、侧卧位或正坐位，足部自然放松，内踝充分显露。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    On the medial ankle, in the hollow between the medial malleolus and the calcaneal tendon, level with the tip of the medial malleolus. Locate the posterior edge of the medial malleolus and the anterior edge of the calcaneal tendon first; the groove between them is the target area.在足内侧，内踝尖与跟腱之间的凹陷中，平内踝尖高度。先摸清内踝后缘与跟腱前缘，两者之间的沟槽即取穴区。

  2. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The distance from the inferior border of the medial condyle of the tibia (SP9) to the tip of the medial malleolus equals 13 cun, and from the tip of the medial malleolus to the sole equals 3 cun. KI3 sits level with the medial malleolar tip in the posterior hollow rather than measured along the vertical bone length.胫骨内侧髁下方（阴陵泉）到内踝尖折作 13 寸，内踝尖到足底折作 3 寸。太溪平内踝尖高度，位于内踝尖后方凹陷中，不沿纵轴量寸，以横平内踝尖为准。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    When checking the relative position of KI5 (1 cun below KI3), a patient's own thumb interphalangeal width (1 cun) helps gauge the downward offset, while KI3 itself is fixed by the horizontal level of the medial malleolar tip within the hollow between the malleolus and tendon.若需核对下方水泉（KI5，太溪下一寸）的相对位置，可用患者拇指指间关节横度（1 寸）向下比量；太溪本身则以平内踝尖横线与跟腱前缘、内踝后缘围成的凹陷定位。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Branches of the medial crural cutaneous nerve are superficial, with the Achilles tendon behind. Deeper lie the tendons of tibialis posterior, flexor digitorum longus and flexor hallucis longus, and the posterior tibial artery, veins and tibial nerve pass through the tarsal tunnel directly deep to the point; avoid the arterial pulse and never lift-thrust repeatedly at depth.皮下有小腿内侧皮神经分支，后方为跟腱。深层邻胫骨后肌腱、趾长屈肌腱和拇长屈肌腱，胫后动、静脉与胫神经在此经过踝管，就在穴的深面；进针需避开动脉搏动处，切勿向深处反复提插。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press gently with a fingertip in the posteromedial ankle groove; KI3 typically yields a pronounced aching or distending sensation in the soft-tissue hollow between the tendon and malleolus. Feeling a strong arterial pulse or triggering electric-like tingling radiating toward the sole indicates proximity to the posterior tibial vessels and nerve.指腹在踝后内侧沟槽内轻压并体会搏动，太溪多在跟腱前缘与内踝后缘之间的软组织凹陷处有明显酸胀感；若触及较强动脉搏动或诱发向足底放射的触电样麻感，提示靠近胫后血管神经。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Choose KI3 for chronic kidney-deficiency low-back ache with weak knees, tinnitus, or nocturia; for acute sprain or myofascial pain do not rely on it alone—pair with BL40, BL23, or local ashi points.肾虚久痛、腰膝酸软无力伴耳鸣或夜尿时选太溪加强补肾强腰；急性扭伤或局部肌筋膜痛不单靠它，常配委中、肾俞或局部阿是穴。

Insomnia不寐（失眠） ⚖️

Select KI3 for insomnia with vivid dreams, restlessness, dry throat, or five-center heat from heart-kidney disharmony and yin deficiency; avoid making it primary for liver-fire or food-stagnation insomnia, which call for HT7, SP6, or targeted points.心肾不交、阴虚火旺致入睡困难、多梦心烦伴咽干或五心烦热时可选太溪；肝郁化火或食滞所致失眠不以此为主，需改配神门、三阴交或相应调理穴。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Use KI3 for gradual tinnitus and reduced hearing with sore low back/knees from kidney-essence deficiency or rising deficient fire; do not rely on KI3 alone for wind-heat, acute ear canal disease, or sudden hearing loss, which requires prompt medical care.肾精亏虚、虚火上扰致耳鸣渐起、听力减退伴腰膝酸软时选太溪；风热外感或耳道急性病变、突发性耳聋不以单用太溪为主，突聋须及时就医。

Irregular periods月经不调

Include KI3 to nourish liver and kidney for delayed, scanty, pale menstruation or menstrual low-back ache from liver-kidney insufficiency; do not rely on KI3 alone for pronounced heat or blood stasis—pair with SP10, SP6, or CV6 per pattern.肝肾不足、冲任失调致月经后期、量少色淡或经期腰酸时可配太溪调补肝肾；实热或血瘀明显者不单靠太溪，需结合血海、三阴交或气海等辨证选穴。

Where is KI3 Tàixī?太溪（KI3）在哪里？

In the ankle region, in the depression between the tip of the medial malleolus and the calcaneal tendon.踝区，内踝尖与跟腱之间的凹陷中。

How is KI3 needled?太溪（KI3）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of KI3?太溪（KI3）是哪类特定穴？

Shu-stream (earth), Yuan-source point输穴（土）、原穴

Main safety point at KI3?太溪（KI3）最要注意的安全问题？

The posterior tibial artery and tibial nerve run deep through the tarsal tunnel: feel the pulse and avoid it, no repeated deep lifting-thrusting, and withdraw at once for electric tingling into the sole.胫后动脉、胫神经在深面经过踝管：先摸搏动并避开，不向深处反复提插；出现向足底放射的电麻感立即退针。
