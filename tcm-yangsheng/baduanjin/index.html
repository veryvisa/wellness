Baduanjin (Eight Pieces of Brocade)八段锦

The early-morning group in the park slowly raising their arms, drawing a bow and turning to look behind them are most likely doing Baduanjin. Eight movements, about ten to twelve minutes, no space or equipment, and a seventy-year-old can do it start to finish. It is the easiest door into the TCM idea of body and mind working as one: get the movements right first, then read what the old rhymes are saying.公园里清晨那群慢慢抬手、拉弓、转头的人，多半在练八段锦。八个动作，十来分钟，不需要场地和器械，七十岁也能从头做到尾。它是了解中医「形神合一」最容易上手的一扇门：先把动作做对，再去读那些口诀在讲什么。

🚩 Before you start开始之前

  • With heart disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure, recent surgery or a fracture, or pregnancy, check with your doctor before starting有心脏病、未控制的高血压、近期手术或骨折、怀孕，开始前先问医生
  • With osteoporosis: in form 6 do not bend all the way down, and in form 8 lower the heels softly without jarring有骨质疏松：第六式不要弯腰到底，第八式脚跟轻放、不震地
  • With glaucoma, retinal disease or high blood pressure: in forms 5 and 6 do not keep the head below the heart for long有青光眼、视网膜病变或高血压：第五、第六式头不要长时间低于心脏
  • With knee pain: stand higher in the horse stance, knees pointing the same way as the toes膝盖痛：马步站高一些，膝盖与脚尖同一方向
  • Stop at once with chest pain, dizziness, dimming vision or marked breathlessness in any form任何一式出现胸痛、头晕、眼前发黑或明显气短，马上停下

📜 Where it comes from从哪里来

The name Baduanjin goes back at least to the Southern Song: Hong Mai's Yijian Zhi records that in 1117 the court diarist Li Siju would sit up at midnight to breathe and self-massage, 'doing what is called the Eight Pieces of Brocade'. Early versions came both seated and standing, and the seven-character rhyme for each movement settled only in the Qing dynasty. The version most often seen in parks today is Health Qigong Baduanjin, standardised and promoted by China's General Administration of Sport in 2003; the names, order and repetitions below follow that version.「八段锦」这个名字至少可以追到南宋：洪迈《夷坚志》记北宋政和七年（1117）起居郎李似矩「尝以夜半时起坐，嘘吸按摩，行所谓八段锦者」。早期的八段锦有坐式和站式多种版本，七字一句的口诀到清代才定型。今天公园里最常见的是 2003 年国家体育总局健身气功管理中心整理推广的「健身气功·八段锦」，下面的动作名称、顺序和遍数都按这一版。

🗓 How to practise怎么练

Start with one round a day, about 10–12 minutes, aiming only for the right order and no breath-holding. Once it is familiar, one or two rounds a day. Studies typically used several sessions a week for 12 weeks or more: keeping it up matters more than doing a lot.起步：每天一遍，约 10–12 分钟，先只求动作顺序对、不憋气。熟练后可以每天一到两遍。研究里常见的是每周练几次、连续 12 周以上；练得勤不如练得久。

Opening stance预备势

  1. Stand with feet together, arms hanging naturally, eyes forward两脚并拢站立，两臂自然垂在身体两侧，目视前方
  2. Step the left foot out to shoulder width左脚向左开一步，与肩同宽
  3. Rotate the arms inward and swing them out to the sides to hip height两臂内旋，向两侧摆起到与髋同高
  4. Bend the knees slightly while rotating the arms outward and bringing them round in front of the belly as if holding a ball, palms facing in, fingertips about 10 cm apart; stand still for a few breaths两腿微屈膝，同时两臂外旋向前合抱于腹前，掌心向内，两掌指尖相距约 10 厘米，静站几次呼吸

Closing收势

  1. Rotate the arms inward and swing them out to hip height两臂内旋，向两侧摆起到与髋同高
  2. Bend the elbows and rest one palm over the other on the lower belly屈肘，两掌相叠放在小腹前
  3. Stand still for a few breaths静站几次呼吸
  4. Let the arms fall to the sides and bring the left foot back beside the right两臂自然垂落，左脚收回，并步站立

Swaying the head and swinging the tail摇头摆尾去心火

Reaching down to the feet两手攀足固肾腰

Rising on the toes and dropping背后七颠百病消

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 A meta-analysis of 19 randomised trials reported better results for Baduanjin than control on quality of life, sleep quality, balance, grip strength, trunk flexibility and blood pressure; but most trials were small and hard to blind, and the findings need confirming in better trials.一篇汇总 19 项随机试验的荟萃分析报告，练八段锦的人在生活质量、睡眠质量、平衡、握力、躯干柔韧和血压上的结果都比对照好；但这些试验多数样本小、难以设盲，结论还需要更好的试验来确认。 [1]
  • E2 A meta-analysis on insomnia in older adults (10 studies, 789 people) found Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index scores about 2.2 points lower with Baduanjin, with a clearer effect at 12 weeks or more than at under 12; a randomised trial in Taiwanese community-dwelling older adults also saw better sleep quality than control after 12 weeks. Both sets of authors call for higher-quality studies.针对老年人失眠的荟萃分析（10 项研究、789 人）发现，练八段锦组的匹兹堡睡眠质量指数平均低约 2.2 分，练满 12 周的比不到 12 周的效果明显；台湾社区老人的随机试验也看到 12 周后睡眠质量好于对照。作者都表示还需要高质量研究。 [2] [3]
  • E2 Worth knowing: a frequently cited 2017 meta-analysis on Baduanjin for insomnia was retracted by the journal in 2023. An article that leans on it is leaning on a source that no longer stands.要留意：一篇 2017 年发表、常被转引的「八段锦治疗失眠」荟萃分析，已在 2023 年被期刊撤稿。看到养生文章引它，那条依据已经不成立。 [4]
  • E2 A meta-analysis on low back pain (9 studies, 519 people) found a small drop in pain scores with Baduanjin compared with general exercise.腰痛的荟萃分析（9 项研究、519 人）发现，与一般锻炼相比，八段锦组的疼痛评分小幅下降。 [5]
  • E5 The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) regards tai chi and qigong as generally safe mind-body practices; people who are pregnant should talk with their health care providers before starting.美国国家补充与整合健康中心（NCCIH）把太极、气功视为一般比较安全的身心练习；怀孕的人开始前先和医生商量。 [6]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Each Baduanjin rhyme ties a movement to an organ system or function: regulating the San Jiao, the spleen and stomach, clearing heart fire, firming the kidneys and waist. It is the traditional way of saying what each form trains; phrases such as 'a hundred illnesses melt away' are traditional sayings, not promises.八段锦的每句口诀都把一个动作和一个脏腑或功能连在一起：理三焦、调脾胃、去心火、固肾腰。古人用它来说明「这一式在练什么」，是传统的解释框架；口诀里的「百病消」「增气力」是传统说法，不是疗效承诺。

What E1–E5 meanE1–E5 是什么意思
  • E1 Systematic reviews / guidelines系统综述或指南
  • E2 Small or few randomised trials少量或小样本随机试验
  • E3 Observational studies (association)观察性研究（相关）
  • E4 Lab, animal or mechanism studies机制、动物或实验室研究
  • E5 Tradition, experience or case reports传统、经验或个案

E4, E5 and 🏛 lines describe tradition, experience or lab findings only; they never claim that something works.E4、E5 和 🏛 只描述传统、经验或实验室发现，不作疗效主张。
