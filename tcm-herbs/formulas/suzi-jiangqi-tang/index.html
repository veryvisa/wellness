Perilla Fruit Qi-Descending Decoction苏子降气汤

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Direct qi downward to calm wheezing降气平喘
  • clear phlegm and stop cough祛痰止咳

Excess above and weakness below: much phlegm, wheezing cough, short breath and a full chest, sometimes weak back and legs.上实下虚：痰涎壅盛、喘咳短气、胸膈满闷，或腰脚无力。

How the formula is built方解

Phlegm blocks above while the kidneys are weak below, so the herbs that lower qi and clear phlegm lead, with cinnamon bark to help the kidneys hold the breath and angelica to moisten against over-drying. The source text here gives amounts only for angelica and cinnamon. Against Ding Chuan Tang, this one is warmer and supports below, while that one clears lung heat and releases the exterior.上有痰壅、下有肾虚，所以降气化痰的一组药为主，再用肉桂温肾纳气、当归养血润燥，防辛燥太过。汪昂书中的组成与分量以底本为准；本页原文只给当归和肉桂注了量。与定喘汤相比，本方偏温、兼顾下元，定喘汤偏于清肺热散表寒。

Cautions使用注意

Not for lung heat with yellow phlegm, dry cough from yin deficiency or weak qi and yin; worsening wheeze, blue lips or inability to speak in sentences means calling 911; never stop prescribed inhalers. This page is for study and is not a prescription; see a doctor first if symptoms are severe, persistent or getting worse.肺热痰黄、阴虚干咳、气阴两虚者不宜；喘息加重、口唇发紫、说话不成句须立即拨 911；哮喘的吸入药不可停。本页供学习，不是处方；症状重、持续或加重时先就医。

Exam notes考点

Exam points: excess above and deficiency below; why cinnamon and angelica are there; compare with Ding Chuan Tang and Xiao Qing Long Tang.考点：上实下虚；肉桂、当归的作用；与定喘汤、小青龙汤鉴别。
