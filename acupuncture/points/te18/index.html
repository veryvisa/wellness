📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the head turned to the opposite side and gently draw the auricle forward to expose the mastoid region.坐位或仰卧，头转向对侧，耳廓轻向前牵开，露出耳后乳突区。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Behind the ear on the curved line along the helix root between TE20 and TE17, in the lower segment about one third of the way upward from TE17, near the centre of the mastoid.在耳后沿耳轮根部弧线、角孙（TE20）与翳风（TE17）连线的下段，约从翳风向上 1/3 处，乳突中央附近。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently palpate the postauricular helix curve over the mastoid. Great auricular and lesser occipital nerve branches and posterior auricular vessels are superficial; the mastoid lies deep. Avoid visible vessels, stop at bone, and do not probe into the mastoid.沿乳突表面轻触耳轮后弧线，浅层有耳大神经、枕小神经分支及耳后血管；深面为乳突骨面，避免可见血管，抵骨即止，不向乳突深探。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Trace upward from TE17 along the postauricular groove to a shallow hollow on the lower segment of the helix curve near the mastoid centre. Tenderness is only a check; do not press hard to provoke pain.由翳风向上沿耳后沟触诊，至耳廓后缘弧线下段、乳突中央附近出现浅凹时复核；压痛只作辅助，不以强压诱发痛。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Use the postauricular curve from TE17 to TE20 and locate about one third of the way upward from TE17; distinguish it from the higher TE19.以TE17至TE20耳后弧线为参照，从翳风向上约三分之一处定位；避免和更上方的颅息（TE19）混淆。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Moving higher along the curve misidentifies TE19, while moving too low approaches TE17; needling a visible postauricular vessel risks bleeding, and deep insertion toward the mastoid can cause severe periosteal pain.沿耳后弧线取到更上方会误作颅息（TE19），过低则接近翳风（TE17）；偏向耳后可见血管会增加出血，向乳突深刺可造成明显骨膜痛。

「瘈脈（一名資脈）耳本後雞足青絡脈，銅人刺出血如豆汁，不宜多出，針一分」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Postauricular mastoid-region tenderness or soft-tissue strain耳后乳突区压痛或软组织劳损

Muscle tension or local pressure can cause tenderness; redness, warmth, outward displacement of the ear, or fever may indicate mastoiditis and needs urgent assessment.耳后肌肉紧张或局部压迫可致压痛；耳后红肿发热、耳廓向外推或发热可能提示乳突炎，应紧急评估。

Great auricular or lesser occipital neuralgia耳大神经或枕小神经痛

Burning or tingling radiating from behind the ear into the scalp may arise from sensory nerves; persistent altered sensation needs neurologic assessment, and repeated pressure should be avoided.耳后至头皮放射的烧灼痛或麻刺可源于感觉神经；持续感觉异常应作神经评估，避免反复压迫。

Where is TE18 Chìmài?瘈脉（TE18）在哪里？

Head, at the centre of mastoid region, on the curved line along the ear helix: at the junction of upper two-thirds and lower third from TE20 to TE17.头部，乳突中央，沿耳轮弧形连线，在角孙（TE20）与翳风（TE17）连线的上2/3与下1/3交点。

How is TE18 needled?瘈脉（TE18）怎么刺？

Transverse or oblique; 0.3–0.5 cun.平刺或斜刺；0.3–0.5寸。
