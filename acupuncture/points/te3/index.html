  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Between the fourth and fifth metacarpals, in the hollow proximal to the fourth metacarpophalangeal joint. With a relaxed fist, identify the metacarpal heads and move proximally from the fourth knuckle.在第 4、5 掌骨之间，第 4 掌指关节近端的凹陷；握拳使掌骨头显出后，从第 4 指关节向腕侧回退定位。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate the soft groove between the dorsal borders of the metacarpals. Dorsal veins and the dorsal ulnar nerve branch are superficial; dorsal interosseous muscle and metacarpal vessels lie deeper, so avoid deep insertion toward the palm.沿两掌骨背侧缘触摸，定位两骨间可触及的软组织沟；浅层有手背静脉及尺神经手背支，深层为骨间背侧肌和掌背血管，勿向掌侧深刺。

  3. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    With a fist, trace the fourth metacarpal proximally from its knuckle, then enter the hollow between the fourth and fifth metacarpals. Confirm that it is just proximal to the fourth MCP joint and between the bones.握拳后，沿第 4 掌骨向近端摸到掌指关节后方，再滑入第 4、5 掌骨间凹陷；以第 4 掌指关节近端和两骨间隙双重核对。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

May be chosen as a distal dorsal-hand shu-stream point for temporal symptoms or tinnitus with Hand-Shaoyang channel signs; acute hearing loss needs urgent otologic care.偏头侧痛或耳鸣伴手少阳经循行症状时可选为手背远端输穴；急性耳聋优先耳科急诊。

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Better suited to temporal headache along the Shaoyang pathway with ear or arm channel signs; consider points such as LU7 instead for externally contracted head-and-neck pain.偏头痛沿少阳经、并见耳侧或手臂少阳经症状时较合适；外感头项痛优先考虑列缺等不同经穴。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

An adjunct for distal dorsal-hand or forearm pain with impaired finger movement; focal elbow pain still needs local assessment and point selection.手背至前臂远端疼痛、掌指活动不利时作远端配穴；肘部局限痛仍需局部检查与取穴。

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing states that Zhongzhu belongs to wood, matching its five-phase shu-stream classification.《针灸甲乙经》记中渚属木，与三焦经输穴的五行属性相应。

Where is TE3 Zhōngzhǔ?中渚（TE3）在哪里？

Dorsum of hand, between the 4th and 5th metacarpals, in the hollow proximal to the 4th metacarpophalangeal joint.手背，第4、5掌骨间，第4掌指关节近端凹陷中。

How is TE3 needled?中渚（TE3）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of TE3?中渚（TE3）是哪类特定穴？

Shu-stream point, wood输穴，属木

Main safety point at TE3?中渚（TE3）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid dorsal veins and dorsal ulnar nerve; do not needle deeply toward the palm.避开手背静脉和尺神经手背支；不可向掌侧深刺。
