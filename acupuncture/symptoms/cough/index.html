Cough咳嗽

Dozens of points list 'cough'. In clinic you first sort external from internal, cold from heat, phlegm or none, new or chronic, deficient or excess — only then do you choose the points and whether to tonify or drain. This page compares the common cough points side by side and gives the main and supporting points for each pattern, with reasons.几十个穴的主治里都写着「咳嗽」。临床上要先分外感还是内伤、寒还是热、有痰无痰、新病久病、虚还是实，再决定用哪几个穴、用补还是用泻。这一页把常用的咳嗽穴放在同一张表里比较，并给出每个证型的主穴、配穴和理由。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Coughing up a lot of blood, difficulty breathing, chest pain, blue lips: call 911咳血量多、呼吸困难、胸痛、嘴唇发紫：拨 911
  • Cough beyond 3 weeks (adults), or still there at 8 weeks, with weight loss, night sweats or ongoing fever: see a doctor for a chest X-ray to rule out TB, lung cancer and others咳嗽超过 3 周（成人）或 8 周仍不好、体重下降、夜间盗汗、持续发热：看医生查胸片，排除结核、肺癌等
  • Infant cough with fast breathing, chest indrawing or poor feeding; a whooping sound on breathing in after coughing fits: same-day care婴幼儿咳嗽伴呼吸急促、肋下凹陷、喂食困难；咳嗽后吸气有「鸡鸣样」回声（百日咳）：当天就医
  • Dry cough after starting an ACE-inhibitor blood-pressure drug (names ending in -pril): ask your doctor or pharmacist, it may be a side effect服用 ACE 抑制剂类降压药（如 -pril 结尾的药）后出现干咳：问医生或药剂师，可能是药物副作用

「五藏六府皆令人欬，非獨肺也」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.38: all the organs can cause cough, not only the lung. So cough point selection cannot stop at the Lung channel — heavy phlegm points to spleen and stomach (ST40, SP9), flank pain and irritability to the liver (LR2), chronic cough with a sore lower back to the kidney (KI3).《素问·咳论》：五脏六腑的病都能引起咳嗽，不只是肺。所以咳嗽选穴不能只盯肺经——痰多看脾胃（丰隆、阴陵泉），胁痛易怒看肝（行间），久咳腰酸看肾（太溪）。

「此皆聚於胃，關於肺，使人多涕唾而面浮腫氣逆也」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

The same chapter: every cough 'gathers in the stomach and concerns the lung' — phlegm is made in the middle burner and expelled through the lung, the classical basis for adding ST40 in phlegm cough.同篇总结：各种咳嗽最终都「聚于胃、关于肺」——痰的来源在中焦，出口在肺。这是痰咳要配胃经络穴丰隆的理论根据。

「經主喘咳寒熱」——《难经》（公版，维基文库底本）

Nanjing 68, on the five transport points: jing-river points govern wheeze, cough, chills and fever. The Lung's jing-river point is LU8 — an underused choice for external cough with chills and fever.《难经·六十八难》五输穴主病：经穴主喘、咳、寒热。肺经的经穴是经渠（LU8），所以外感咳嗽伴寒热时，经渠是被低估的选择。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

External外感

Wind-cold invading the lung风寒袭肺

How it presents辨证要点 Heavy cough with thin white sputum; marked chills, mild fever, no sweating, headache and body aches, blocked or runny clear nose; thin white tongue coating, floating tight pulse.咳声重浊，痰稀色白；怕冷明显、发热轻、无汗，头痛身痛，鼻塞流清涕；舌苔薄白，脉浮紧。

Principle治法 Dispel wind and cold, free the lung, stop cough (drain; moxa or cupping may be added)疏风散寒，宣肺止咳（针用泻法，可加灸或拔罐）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷BL13 Fèishū肺俞
Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门

💡 LU7 (Lung luo) with LI4 (Large Intestine yuan) is a source–connecting pair releasing the exterior; BL13 regulates lung qi directly; BL12, the 'wind gate', is added early in wind-cold and can be cupped.列缺（肺络）配合谷（大肠原）是原络配穴，表里同治、解表宣肺；肺俞直接调肺气；风门是风邪出入之门，风寒初起加用，背部可拔罐。

External外感

Wind-heat invading the lung风热犯肺

How it presents辨证要点 Unproductive cough with thick yellow sputum; sore swollen throat, thirst, prominent fever, slight aversion to wind, sweating; red tongue tip, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse.咳嗽不爽，痰黄稠；咽喉肿痛、口渴，发热重、微怕风、有汗；舌尖红、苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat, free the lung, stop cough (drain; bloodletting allowed)疏风清热，宣肺止咳（泻法，可点刺出血）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷BL13 Fèishū肺俞
Add配穴GV14 Dàzhuī大椎LI11 Qūchí曲池LU5 Chǐzé尺泽LU11 Shàoshāng少商

💡 Same core as external cough; GV14 and LI11 clear heat and bring down fever; LU5, the Lung's he-sea and child point, drains lung heat; prick LU11 to bleed for a swollen throat.主穴同外感；大椎、曲池清热退热；尺泽是肺经合穴、子穴，泻肺热；咽喉肿痛加少商点刺出血。

Internal内伤

Phlegm-damp obstructing the lung痰湿蕴肺

How it presents辨证要点 Recurrent cough with copious white sticky sputum, easily brought up, worse in the morning or after meals; chest tightness, bloating, poor appetite, heaviness; thick greasy white coating, slippery pulse.反复咳嗽，痰多、色白、黏稠易咳出，早晨或饭后加重；胸闷、胃脘胀、食欲差、身体困重；苔白腻，脉滑。

Principle治法 Strengthen the spleen, dry damp, transform phlegm (even method; moxa allowed)健脾燥湿，化痰止咳（平补平泻，可灸）

Main主穴LU9 Tàiyuān太渊BL13 Fèishū肺俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆SP9 Yīnlíngquán阴陵泉

💡 Core for internal cough: LU9 (Lung yuan and earth point, 'banking earth to generate metal'), BL13, SP6 (spleen); ST40, the Stomach luo point, is the key phlegm point and SP9 drains damp — matching 'gathers in the stomach, concerns the lung'.内伤咳嗽主穴：太渊（肺原穴、输穴，「土」穴，培土生金）、肺俞、三阴交（健脾）；丰隆是胃经络穴、化痰要穴，阴陵泉利湿——对应「聚于胃、关于肺」。

Internal内伤

Liver fire attacking the lung肝火犯肺

How it presents辨证要点 Fits of choking cough with qi rushing upward, pulling pain in the flanks when coughing; red face, bitter mouth, dry throat, scanty sticky sputum hard to bring up; worse with emotional upset; red tongue edges, dry thin yellow coating, wiry rapid pulse.阵发性呛咳、气往上冲，咳时牵引两胁痛；面红、口苦咽干，痰少质黏难咳；随情绪波动加重；舌边红、苔薄黄少津，脉弦数。

Principle治法 Clear liver fire, send rebellious qi down, stop cough (drain)清肝泻火，降逆止咳（泻法）

Main主穴LU9 Tàiyuān太渊BL13 Fèishū肺俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴LR2 Xíngjiān行间LU10 Yújì鱼际

💡 LR2, the Liver ying-spring (fire) point, drains liver fire; LU10, the Lung ying-spring (fire) point, clears lung heat and soothes the throat — two fire points treating both ends of 'wood-fire scorching metal'.行间是肝经荥穴（火），泻肝火；鱼际是肺经荥穴（火），清肺热、利咽——两个荥穴一清肝一清肺，「木火刑金」两头治。

Internal内伤

Lung yin deficiency肺阴亏虚

How it presents辨证要点 Dry cough with little sputum, sometimes streaked with blood; dry throat and mouth, hoarse voice; afternoon low fever, flushed cheeks, hot palms and soles, night sweats; long course; red tongue with little coating, thin rapid pulse.干咳少痰，或痰中带血丝；咽干口燥、声音嘶哑；午后潮热、颧红、手足心热、夜间盗汗；病程长；舌红少苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin, moisten the lung, stop cough (tonify)滋阴润肺，止咳（补法）

Main主穴LU9 Tàiyuān太渊BL13 Fèishū肺俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴BL43 Gaohuangshu膏肓俞KI3 Tàixī太溪LU6 Kǒngzuì孔最

💡 BL43 governs chronic consumptive cough; KI3, the Kidney yuan point, nourishes kidney yin to moisten the lung ('metal and water generate each other'); add LU6 (Lung xi-cleft; yin-channel cleft points treat bleeding) for blood-streaked sputum. LU7 with KI6 is the classic pair for dry throat and cough from yin deficiency.膏肓主虚劳久咳；太溪是肾经原穴，金水相生、滋肾阴以润肺；痰中带血加孔最（肺经郄穴，阴经郄穴治血证）。列缺配照海也是阴虚咽干咳的经典对穴。

Internal内伤

Lung qi deficiency (lung and spleen)肺气虚（肺脾气虚）

How it presents辨证要点 Weak chronic cough, low voice, shortness of breath worse on exertion; thin clear sputum; dislikes wind, catches colds easily, sweats spontaneously; pale face, fatigue; pale tongue, weak pulse.久咳无力、声低气短，动则加重；痰清稀；怕风、容易感冒、自汗；面色淡白、乏力；舌淡苔白，脉弱。

Principle治法 Tonify lung qi, bank earth to generate metal (tonify, generous moxa)补益肺气，培土生金（补法，重灸）

Main主穴LU9 Tàiyuān太渊BL13 Fèishū肺俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里CV6 Qìhǎi气海BL20 Píshū脾俞

💡 LU9 (earth) is the Lung's mother point — 'tonify the mother in deficiency'; ST36 and BL20 strengthen the spleen to feed lung qi, CV6 tonifies original qi; summer-solstice back-shu moxa or plasters are traditionally used to prevent relapses in this pattern.太渊（土）是肺经母穴，「虚则补其母」；足三里、脾俞补脾以生肺气，气海补元气；三伏天背俞穴贴敷/灸法常用于这一型的预防。

LU7 Lièquē列缺

Best for最适合 Early external cough: itchy throat, blocked nose, headache or stiff neck外感初起：咽痒、鼻塞、头项痛

Why依据 Lung luo point linking the Large Intestine channel; confluent point of the Ren vessel; 'for head and neck seek LU7'肺经络穴，联络大肠经；八脉交会通任脉；四总穴「头项寻列缺」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More dispersing than LU9 (treats the exterior); more for cold and early stages than LU5比太渊偏散（治表）；比尺泽偏寒证、初起

LU5 Chǐzé尺泽

Best for最适合 Heat cough, thick yellow sputum, cough with chest fullness and rising qi, coughing blood热咳、痰黄稠、咳而胸满气逆、咯血

Why依据 He-sea (water) point, the Lung's child point — drain it in excess; he-sea points treat the organs合穴（水），肺经子穴「实则泻其子」；合治内府

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Treats phlegm-heat in the lung itself, LU10 leans to the throat; not for chronic cold-deficient cough比鱼际偏治肺本脏的痰热，鱼际偏咽喉；虚寒久咳不用

LU9 Tàiyuān太渊

Best for最适合 Chronic internal cough, weak cough, low voice, breathlessness内伤久咳、气虚无力、声低气短

Why依据 Yuan and shu-stream (earth) point, the Lung's mother point — tonify it in deficiency; influential point of the vessels原穴、输穴（土），肺经母穴「虚则补其母」；脉会太渊

Versus others与其他穴的区别 One of the three core internal-cough points; the opposite of LU7: tonifies rather than disperses内伤三主穴之一；与列缺相反，偏补不偏散

LU6 Kǒngzuì孔最

Best for最适合 Coughing blood, acute violent cough咳血、急性剧咳

Why依据 Xi-cleft point: yin-channel cleft points treat bleeding; cleft points treat acute conditions郄穴：阴经郄穴治血证，郄穴治急症

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Distinctive for bleeding and acute cases; paired with LU5 for heat-type bleeding以止血、治急为特点；肺热咯血常与尺泽同用

LU8 Jīngqú经渠

Best for最适合 External cough with chills and fever外感咳嗽伴恶寒发热

Why依据 Jing-river (metal) point; Nanjing: 'jing-river points govern wheeze, cough, chills and fever'经穴（金）；《难经》「经主喘咳寒热」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Little used in clinic (beside the radial artery; shallow, avoiding the artery) but its five-phase role is a common exam item临床少用（贴近桡动脉，宜避开动脉浅刺），考试常考五输穴属性

LU10 Yújì鱼际

Best for最适合 Cough with dry sore throat, hoarseness, blood-streaked sputum (lung heat, liver fire)咳嗽伴咽喉干痛、声嘶、痰中带血（肺热、肝火）

Why依据 Ying-spring (fire) point, 'ying points govern fever'; clears lung heat, benefits the throat荥穴（火），「荥主身热」，清肺热、利咽

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More focused on the throat than LU5; with LR2 for liver fire attacking the lung比尺泽更偏咽喉；肝火犯肺配行间

LU11 Shàoshāng少商

Best for最适合 Acute red, swollen, very sore throat with cough急性咽喉红肿剧痛伴咳

Why依据 Jing-well point; pricked to bleed to drain heat; well points for emergencies井穴，点刺出血泄热；井穴急救

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Bloodletting for acute excess-heat throat pain; not a general cough point以放血用于急性实热咽痛，不作一般咳嗽主穴

LU1 Zhōngfǔ中府

Best for最适合 Cough or wheeze with chest tightness or pain; chronic cases with tenderness on the chest咳喘伴胸闷胸痛、慢性咳喘胸前压痛

Why依据 Front-mu point of the lung; shu–mu pairing with BL13肺之募穴，俞募配穴配肺俞

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Danger point (pneumothorax); needling is restricted, so moxa, pressure or cupping is often used危险穴（气胸），刺法受限；常以灸、按、拔罐代替

BL13 Fèishū肺俞

Best for最适合 Any cough, external or internal一切咳嗽（外感、内伤都用）

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

Best for最适合 External cough with fever, headache, sore throat外感咳嗽伴发热、头痛、咽痛

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Alone it releases the exterior and eases pain; for cough pair it with Lung points; forbidden in pregnancy单用偏解表止痛，治咳须配肺经穴；孕妇禁针

ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆

Best for最适合 Heavy phlegm, cold or hot痰多（无论寒热）

BL43 Gaohuangshu膏肓俞

Best for最适合 Chronic consumptive cough, lung yin or qi deficiency虚劳久咳、肺阴虚或气虚

Why依据 Traditional key point for consumptive conditions; moxa preferred传统虚劳要穴，宜灸

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Only for chronic deficiency; medial to the scapula, deep needling risks pneumothorax只用于久病虚证；位于肩胛内侧，深刺有气胸风险

CV22 Tiāntū天突

Best for最适合 Cough triggered by an itchy throat, phlegm stuck in the throat, sudden rebellious cough喉痒即咳、痰阻咽喉、气逆暴咳

CV17 Dànzhōng膻中

Best for最适合 Cough or wheeze with chest tightness and breathlessness咳喘伴胸闷气短

Why依据 Influential point of qi, front-mu of the pericardium; opens the chest气会，心包募穴，宽胸理气

Versus others与其他穴的区别 For chest tightness and qi stagnation rather than phlegm; transverse insertion偏治胸闷气滞而非痰；平刺

LR2 Xíngjiān行间

Best for最适合 Liver fire attacking the lung: cough pulling at the flanks, red face, bitter taste, triggered by emotion肝火犯肺：咳引胁痛、面红口苦、情绪诱发

Why依据 Liver ying-spring (fire) point, drains liver fire肝经荥穴（火），泻肝火

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Only with clear signs of liver fire; pairs with LU10, one clearing liver, one lung只用于有肝火证据时；与鱼际一清肝一清肺

KI3 Tàixī太溪

Best for最适合 Chronic cough with sore back and knees, afternoon fever (lung–kidney yin deficiency)久咳伴腰膝酸软、潮热（肺肾阴虚）

Why依据 Kidney yuan point; metal and water generate each other肾经原穴，金水相生

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Treats the kidney root rather than stopping cough directly; with BL13 and LU9治本于肾，不直接止咳；配肺俞、太渊

ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

Best for最适合 Chronic cough from lung–spleen qi deficiency, frequent colds肺脾气虚久咳、容易感冒

Why依据 Stomach he-sea point; banks earth to generate metal胃经合穴，培土生金

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Tonifying and preventive; not a lead point in excess phlegm cases偏补、偏预防，痰多实证不作主穴

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern medicine sorts cough by duration: acute (under 3 weeks, mostly viral upper-respiratory infection), subacute (3–8 weeks, often post-infectious) and chronic (over 8 weeks). The three commonest causes of chronic cough in adults are upper-airway cough syndrome (postnasal drip), cough-variant asthma and gastro-oesophageal reflux, plus ACE inhibitors, smoking and COPD; every chronic cough needs a chest X-ray first. TCM patterns do not map one-to-one onto these causes, but they overlap: postnasal drip often presents as wind or phlegm-damp; a dry cough worse at night or with cold air or smoke suggests cough-variant asthma; cough after meals or lying down with heartburn suggests reflux (in TCM often liver–stomach disharmony with rebellious stomach qi; PC6, CV12 and ST36 are reference points).现代医学把咳嗽按病程分：急性（<3 周，多为病毒性上呼吸道感染）、亚急性（3–8 周，常是感染后咳嗽）、慢性（>8 周）。成人慢性咳嗽最常见的三类原因是上气道咳嗽综合征（鼻后滴流）、咳嗽变异性哮喘和胃食管反流，另有 ACE 抑制剂、吸烟、慢阻肺；任何慢性咳嗽都要先有胸片。中医证型与这些病因并不一一对应，但有大致的交集：鼻后滴流常见风邪、痰湿；夜间、遇冷遇烟加重的干咳要想到咳嗽变异性哮喘；餐后、平卧、伴烧心的咳嗽要想到反流（中医常辨为肝胃不和、胃气上逆，选穴可参考内关、中脘、足三里）。
