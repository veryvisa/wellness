    Anatomy解剖结构

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently with the fingertip pad about two finger-widths lateral to the lower border of the T9 spinous process to feel the erector spinae bundle or local tenderness and nodularity; in lean patients or during deep breathing, note the rib contour and do not press forcefully into the intercostal space.用指腹在 T9 棘突下缘旁开约两横指处轻按，常可触及竖脊肌束及局部压痛或筋结；瘦削者或深呼吸时注意感知肋骨轮廓，切勿用力向肋间顶压。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Inserting too deeply or angling laterally or inferiorly into an intercostal space: the needle tip can pass through the chest wall and parietal pleura into the pleural cavity or lung, risking pneumothorax (high-risk hazard; treat chest pain, tightness, shortness of breath, or pain on inspiration after needling as an emergency).进针过深或方向偏外侧、偏下刺入肋间隙：针尖穿过胸壁与壁层胸膜进入胸膜腔或肺实质，可导致气胸（高危风险，针后若出现胸痛、胸闷、气短或吸气加重需立即急诊处理）。
  • Miscounting thoracic levels (such as mistaking T7 at the inferior scapular angle or T8 for T9): shifting by one or two levels alters segmental alignment for regulating liver and gallbladder qi.椎数算错（如将 T7 肩胛下角或 T8 误认为 T9）：定位偏差一至两个椎节，影响针对肝胆经气调节的节段对应。
  • Measuring too far laterally (well beyond 1.5 cun): this moves toward the medial scapular border or lateral latissimus dorsi region, missing the first lateral line of the Bladder channel and aiming more directly toward an intercostal space.旁开距离过宽（远超 1.5 寸）：更靠近肩胛骨内侧缘或外侧背阔肌区，既偏离足太阳膀胱经第一侧线，又更易直接对向肋间隙。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low mood and anxiety郁证（焦虑抑郁）

Choose BL18 with LR3 and LR14 when low mood and hypochondriac distension involve liver qi stagnation; if palpitations, insomnia, or heart-spleen deficiency predominate, BL18 is not primary and points such as BL15, HT7, and SP6 are often favored instead.情志抑郁、胁肋胀闷兼见肝气不舒时可选用 BL18 配太冲、期门疏肝解郁；若以心悸失眠、心脾两虚为主则不以 BL18 为先，多选心俞、神门、三阴交等。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

BL18 may serve as an adjunctive point to regulate the liver when eye redness, pain, or blurred vision relates to liver fire or wind-heat, but it is not a first choice for acute severe eye pain, sudden vision loss, or ocular trauma, which require prompt ophthalmic assessment and often points like LR2, GB20, and BL1.肝经风热或肝火上炎致目赤肿痛、视物昏花时可作辅助配穴调肝，但急性剧烈眼痛、视力骤降或眼外伤不首选 BL18，应先由眼科检查并常配行间、风池、睛明等。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Useful with LR3 and GB20 for dizziness accompanied by irritability and hypochondriac fullness from ascending liver yang or liver blood deficiency, whereas sudden severe vertigo with diplopia, dysarthria, or ataxia requires prompt medical evaluation rather than relying on acupuncture alone.肝阳上亢或肝血不足引起的头晕目眩、易怒胁痛时可配合太冲、风池调理，而突发性剧烈眩晕伴复视、构音障碍或共济失调时切勿仅靠针刺延误，应先就医排查中枢神经问题。

Where is BL18 Gānshū?肝俞（BL18）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of T9, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 9 胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL18 needled?肝俞（BL18）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of BL18?肝俞（BL18）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Liver肝之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL18?肝俞（BL18）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only shallow oblique insertion toward the spine; never insert deeply or laterally.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向浅斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
