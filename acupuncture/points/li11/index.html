Where is LI11 Qūchí?曲池（LI11）在哪里？

At the lateral elbow, midpoint between the lateral end of the elbow crease and the lateral epicondyle of the humerus when the elbow is flexed.肘外侧，屈肘时肘横纹外侧端与肱骨外上髁连线的中点。

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

He-sea point (five transport points; earth)合穴（五输穴，五行属土）

Avoid superficial veins at the lateral elbow; do not force a deep insertion toward the joint cavity.避开肘外侧浅静脉；不向关节腔强行深刺。
