⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Consider GB7 as a regional adjunct when pain is localized to the lateral head along the Shaoyang pathway and tenderness corresponds to the curved preauricular temple hairline. If pain is mainly occipital or posterior cervical, compare GB20 and BL10; for externally contracted head and neck pain, compare LU7. Do not select an acupoint first for thunderclap headache or headache with neurological symptoms; arrange medical assessment.头侧少阳经循行区域出现局限性颞痛，且耳前鬓角发际弯曲处有对应压痛时，可将曲鬓作为局部配穴；若疼痛以枕部、后颈为主，应优先比较风池GB20、天柱BL10；若为外感头项痛，应比较列缺LU7。突发剧痛或伴神经系统症状时不取穴，先就医。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

GB7 may be considered as a regional adjunct when dizziness accompanies lateral-head discomfort and temporal distension consistent with a Shaoyang pattern. If the main presentation is rotational vertigo, tinnitus, hearing loss, or position-triggered symptoms, first assess vestibular and otologic causes and compare traditional points such as GB20 and GV20. Sudden neurological deficits, double vision, or inability to stand require emergency care.若眩晕同时伴少阳经头侧不适、颞部胀痛，可在辨证后考虑曲鬓作为局部辅助穴；若主要表现为旋转性眩晕、耳鸣、听力下降或体位变化诱发，应优先鉴别前庭系统及耳科疾病，并比较风池GB20、百会GV20等传统配穴。伴突发神经功能异常、复视或不能站立时立即就医。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

GB7 may be used as a regional adjunct when tinnitus accompanies pain above the preauricular region or discomfort along the Shaoyang pathway. If the main symptoms are intrameatal tinnitus, hearing loss, or ear fullness, investigate external, middle, and inner ear disorders first and compare peri-auricular points SI19, GB2, and TE. Sudden unilateral hearing loss is an otologic emergency and should not be managed by waiting to observe the effects of acupuncture.耳鸣伴耳前上方颞部疼痛或少阳经循行区域不适时，可考虑曲鬓作为局部配穴；若以耳内鸣响、听力下降或耳闷为主，应优先检查外耳、中耳及内耳疾病，并比较听宫SI19、听会GB2、耳门TE等耳周穴。突发单侧听力下降属于耳科急症，应尽快就医，不能等待针灸观察。

Toothache牙痛 ⚖️

GB7 may be considered as an adjunct when dental pain is accompanied by temporalis tension and referred preauricular or temporal pain, after the dental cause has been assessed. If pain is localized to a tooth or gingiva and is clearly triggered by temperature or biting, prioritize dental examination and compare traditional points ST6, ST7, and LI4. Gingival swelling with fever, facial swelling, or difficulty swallowing requires prompt medical care.若牙痛同时伴颞肌紧张、耳前及颞部牵涉痛，可在明确牙源性病因后考虑曲鬓作为辅助穴；若疼痛集中于牙体、牙龈，冷热刺激或咬合明显诱发，应优先进行牙科检查，并比较颊车ST6、下关ST7、合谷LI4等传统配穴。牙龈肿胀伴发热、面部肿胀或吞咽困难须及时就医。

Facial palsy面瘫（口眼歪斜） ⚖️

GB7 is not a primary regional point for facial palsy. It may be considered as an adjunct when temporal pain is also present, but it cannot replace facial nerve examination or assessment of the underlying cause. Compare GB14, EX-HN5, ST4, ST6, and TE for traditional facial palsy point selection. New-onset facial palsy requires medical assessment to exclude stroke; associated limb weakness, slurred speech, or double vision requires emergency care.曲鬓不是面瘫的首选局部穴。若患者同时有颞部疼痛，可根据局部表现考虑辅助使用，但不能替代面神经功能检查及病因评估。面瘫取穴应比较阳白GB14、太阳EX-HN5、地仓ST4、颊车ST6、翳风TE等。新发面瘫必须先排除卒中；伴肢体无力、言语不清或复视时立即急诊。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

GB7 may serve as a regional adjunct when neck stiffness is accompanied by pulling pain toward the lateral head and symptoms along the Shaoyang pathway. If tenderness is mainly suboccipital or in the posterior cervical muscles, prioritize GB20 and BL10. If restricted cervical movement or radiating arm pain predominates, investigate cervical spondylosis and nerve-root compression. Progressive weakness, gait disturbance, or bowel or bladder dysfunction requires urgent medical assessment.颈项强痛同时伴头侧牵扯痛、少阳经循行区域不适时，可将曲鬓作为头侧局部辅助穴；若主要压痛在枕下、颈后肌群，应优先比较风池GB20、天柱BL10；若以颈椎活动受限或上肢放射痛为主，应进一步鉴别颈椎病及神经根受压。进行性肌力下降、步态异常或大小便功能改变须立即就医。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

GB7 may be considered as an adjunct when eye discomfort accompanies temporal headache and lateral Shaoyang-pattern symptoms. If red eye, ocular pain, photophobia, or reduced vision predominates, first exclude acute angle-closure glaucoma, keratitis, and uveitis, and compare traditional periocular points such as BL2 and EX-HN5. Sudden visual loss or severe ocular pain requires emergency assessment.眼痛伴颞侧头痛、少阳经头侧症状时，可考虑曲鬓作为远近配穴中的辅助局部穴；若主要表现为眼红、眼球疼痛、畏光、视力下降，应优先排查急性闭角型青光眼、角膜炎及葡萄膜炎，并比较攒竹BL2、太阳EX-HN5等传统眼周穴。突发视力下降或剧烈眼痛必须立即就医。

Insomnia不寐（失眠） ⚖️

GB7 may be considered as a regional adjunct when insomnia is accompanied by temporal headache or head tension. If the main presentation is restlessness, difficulty falling asleep, or palpitations, prioritize traditional points such as HT7, PC6, and GV20. GB7 is not a primary calming point and should not be selected routinely for insomnia alone. Severe emotional crisis or risk of self-harm requires prompt professional support.若不寐伴明显颞侧头痛、头部紧张，可在辨证配穴中考虑曲鬓作为局部辅助穴；若以心神不宁、入睡困难、心悸为主，应优先比较神门HT7、内关PC6、百会GV20等。曲鬓不是安神要穴，不能仅因患者失眠便常规选用；伴严重情绪危机或自伤风险应及时寻求专业帮助。

Migraine偏头痛（Migraine）

Migraine commonly presents as recurrent unilateral or bilateral pulsating headache, sometimes accompanied by nausea, vomiting, photophobia, and phonophobia, with worsening during activity in some patients. Pain may involve the temporal region, but tenderness at GB7 alone cannot diagnose migraine. Assess attack duration, frequency, aura, family history, and neurological symptoms. A first severe headache, a substantial change in headache pattern, or neurological deficits requires medical assessment first.常表现为反复发作的单侧或双侧搏动性头痛，可伴恶心、呕吐、畏光、畏声，部分患者活动后加重。疼痛可累及颞部，但曲鬓区域压痛不能单独确诊偏头痛。应询问发作持续时间、频率、先兆、家族史及神经系统症状。首次出现剧烈头痛、头痛模式明显改变或伴神经功能缺损时，先进行医学评估。

Temporalis myofascial pain syndrome颞肌肌筋膜疼痛综合征

The temporalis occupies the temporal fossa and contributes to jaw closure and mastication. Myofascial pain may cause aching and tenderness in the temple, radiating toward the forehead, teeth, or temporomandibular region. Ask the patient to clench gently, observe temporalis contraction, and assess muscle tenderness and pain during chewing. Differentiate muscular tenderness from superficial skin tenderness at GB7 and pain over the superficial temporal artery. Restricted mouth opening, altered occlusion, or progressively worsening pain warrants assessment by an oral and maxillofacial or relevant specialist.颞肌位于颞窝，参与下颌闭合及咀嚼。肌筋膜疼痛可表现为颞部酸胀、压痛，并向额部、牙齿或颞下颌关节附近放射。让患者轻咬牙观察颞肌收缩，结合肌肉触诊和咀嚼诱发痛判断；与曲鬓局部皮肤压痛及颞浅动脉搏动区疼痛区分。若张口受限、咬合改变或疼痛持续加重，应转口腔颌面外科或相关专科评估。

Temporomandibular disorder颞下颌关节紊乱（TMD）

Pain is usually located anterior to the tragus or around the joint and may worsen with opening the mouth, chewing, or lateral jaw movement. Clicking, crepitus, restricted opening, or altered occlusion may occur. Ask the patient to open and close the mouth slowly while palpating joint movement. GB7 lies above the preauricular region at the temple hairline and is not the joint space. Marked restriction, jaw locking, altered occlusion after trauma, or persistent pain requires medical assessment.疼痛通常位于耳屏前方或关节周围，张口、咀嚼及下颌侧向运动时加重，可伴弹响、摩擦音、张口受限或咬合不适。让患者缓慢张闭口并触诊关节活动，注意曲鬓位于耳前上方鬓角发际，不等于关节间隙。若存在明显张口受限、下颌锁闭、外伤后咬合改变或持续疼痛，应就医评估。

Auriculotemporal neuralgia or nerve irritation耳颞神经痛或神经刺激

The auriculotemporal nerve is a branch of the mandibular division of the trigeminal nerve and supplies the temporal, preauricular, and parts of the auricular region. Symptoms may include unilateral brief electric-shock or burning pain, sometimes with altered sensation, triggered by chewing or touching the preauricular area. Differentiate it from migraine, temporalis myofascial pain, and ear disorders. Marked radiating tingling or electric sensations during needling require stopping and reassessment; neural radiation must not be deliberately sought.耳颞神经为三叉神经下颌神经分支，支配颞部、耳前及部分耳廓区域。可表现为单侧短暂电击样、烧灼样疼痛，或伴局部感觉异常，可能在咀嚼、触碰耳前区域时诱发。应与偏头痛、颞肌肌筋膜疼痛及耳部疾病鉴别。针刺过程中出现沿耳前或颞部放射的明显麻电感，应停止进针并重新评估，不应追求神经放射感。

Temporal arteritis (giant cell arteritis)颞浅动脉炎（巨细胞动脉炎）

Giant cell arteritis primarily affects people aged over 50 and may cause new temporal headache, scalp tenderness, a tender or enlarged superficial temporal artery, jaw fatigue or pain during chewing, and systemic or visual symptoms. Permanent visual loss is a major risk. Temporal arterial tenderness must not be dismissed as acupoint sensitivity. Suspected cases require urgent medical assessment rather than acupuncture or massage.主要发生于50岁以上人群，可能出现新发颞部头痛、头皮触痛、颞浅动脉压痛或增粗、咀嚼时下颌疲劳或疼痛，并可伴全身症状及视觉异常。此病存在视力永久损害风险。不能将颞部动脉压痛简单当作经穴敏感；出现疑似表现必须紧急医学评估，不能先针刺或按摩。

Herpes zoster and postherpetic neuralgia带状疱疹及带状疱疹后神经痛

Unilateral burning or stabbing pain with grouped vesicles over the temple or forehead may indicate herpes zoster involving a trigeminal branch. Periocular involvement can cause corneal and ocular complications and requires prompt medical care. Persistent neuralgic pain after the rash resolves may represent postherpetic neuralgia. Do not needle directly through active lesions.颞部或额部出现单侧灼痛、针刺痛及成簇水疱时，应考虑三叉神经分支受累的带状疱疹。若累及眼周，可能发生角膜及眼部并发症，应及时就医。皮疹消退后仍持续神经痛属于带状疱疹后神经痛。活动期皮损区域不应直接针刺。

Otologic disorders and referred ear pain耳部疾病及耳源性疼痛

Otitis media, otitis externa, auricular lesions, and other otologic disorders may produce referred pain around the preauricular or temporal region. Assess for earache, discharge, hearing changes, fever, and peri-auricular swelling. GB7 cannot replace otoscopy or hearing assessment. Ear discharge, significant hearing loss, vertigo, or fever requires medical evaluation first.中耳炎、外耳道炎、耳廓病变及其他耳科疾病可引起耳前或颞部牵涉痛。应检查有无耳痛、耳漏、听力变化、发热及耳周红肿。曲鬓不能替代耳镜检查及听力评估。伴耳道流脓、明显听力下降、眩晕或发热时，应先就医。
