📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or seated with the foot relaxed and positioned so the toe web and dorsal-plantar skin border are clearly visible.仰卧位或坐位，足部自然放松，足底平放或微抬便于观察趾蹼与足背皮色交界。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Gently separate the first and second toes and locate the small depression just proximal to the web margin at the junction of dorsal and plantar skin.将第 1 趾（足大趾）与第 2 趾轻轻分开，在趾蹼缘后方、足背与足底皮肤交界处（赤白肉际）摸到小凹陷即是。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located on the proximal dorsal aspect between the first and second toes, with dorsal digital nerves and veins subcutaneously and the first dorsal interosseous muscle and first and second metatarsophalangeal joints deeper. Avoid visible superficial dorsal or digital veins during insertion or pressure, and keep in mind that Taichong (LR3) lies more proximally in the first intermetatarsal space.位于第 1、2 趾间近端足背侧，皮下有趾背神经与趾背静脉，深面为第一骨间背侧肌及第 1、2 跖趾关节附近。针刺或按压时应避开可见足背/趾背浅静脉，并注意近端为太冲（LR3）所在的第一跖骨间隙。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press gently with a fingertip along the dorsal-plantar skin margin just behind the web to feel a slight hollow or mild tenderness; soft tissue here is thin, so keep angle and depth controlled to avoid the joint capsule or bone.用指腹在趾蹼后方赤白肉际处轻轻按压，常可触及轻微压痛或凹陷；此处软组织较薄，进针角度与深度需控制，避免直抵关节囊或骨面。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Locating too far distally onto the web margin or lateral toe edge: the tissue is thinner near the toe, producing sharp local sensation rather than the standard ying-spring location.取到趾蹼缘前方或第 1、2 趾侧缘偏远端处：位置偏远，软组织更薄且靠近趾端，针感偏局部刺痛而离荥穴标准定位点有偏差。
  • Drifting too far proximally into the first intermetatarsal space toward Taichong (LR3): this leaves the border behind the web and blurs the distinction between LR2 and LR3.向近端偏入第一跖骨间隙过深处（接近太冲 LR3）：偏离趾蹼后方赤白肉际，易混淆行间与太冲的定位与主治侧重。
  • Advancing too deeply or angling directly into the joint: the first dorsal interosseous muscle, first and second metatarsophalangeal joint capsules, and metatarsal heads lie deep here, risking sharp pain or injury to the joint capsule or superficial veins.过深刺入或向关节方向顶刺：深面接近第一骨间背侧肌与第 1、2 跖趾关节囊及跖骨头，易引起明显疼痛或损伤关节囊、浅静脉。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

Where is LR2 Xíngjiān?行间（LR2）在哪里？

On the dorsum of the foot, between the first and second toes, proximal to the web margin, at the border of the red and white flesh.足背，第 1、2 趾间，趾蹼缘后方赤白肉际处。

Perpendicular; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of LR2?行间（LR2）是哪类特定穴？

Ying-spring (fire)荥穴（火）

Main safety point at LR2?行间（LR2）最要注意的安全问题？

Soft tissue behind the web is thin with dorsal digital veins under the skin: avoid visible veins and do not drive into the metatarsophalangeal joint capsules.趾蹼后软组织薄，皮下有趾背静脉：避开可见静脉，不向跖趾关节囊深刺。
