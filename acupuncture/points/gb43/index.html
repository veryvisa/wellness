Interdigital nerve irritation or interdigital neuroma第4、5趾间神经刺激或趾间神经瘤

Metatarsalgia前足跖痛症

Metatarsalgia causes pain beneath the weight-bearing forefoot, often aggravated by walking, running or narrow footwear, and may be accompanied by callus formation or focal tenderness. Its typical location is plantar to the metatarsal heads, whereas GB43 lies on the distal dorsal foot between the fourth and fifth toes. Distinguish plantar weight-bearing pain from dorsal web-space pain by examining metatarsal-head tenderness, loading patterns and footwear. Not all forefoot pain originates at GB43.前足跖侧负重区疼痛，常在行走、跑步或穿窄鞋时加重，可能伴胼胝或局部压痛。疼痛通常位于跖骨头跖侧，而侠溪位于足背远端第4、5趾间。应比较疼痛发生在负重面还是趾蹼背侧，并检查跖骨头压痛、足部负重及鞋具因素。不能把所有前足疼痛都定位为侠溪局部病变。

Where is GB43 Xiáxī?侠溪（GB43）在哪里？

On the dorsum of the foot, between the 4th and 5th toes, proximal to the web margin, at the border of the red and white skin.足背，第 4、5 趾间，趾蹼缘后方赤白肉际处。

Ying-spring point (water)荥穴（水）
