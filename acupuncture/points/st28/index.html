ST28

水道 Shuǐdào

Water Passage · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

On the lower abdomen, 3 cun below the umbilicus, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.下腹部，脐中下 3 寸，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

💡 Lie supine with the knees bent; set the level by proportional measure (xiphisternal junction to umbilicus 8 cun, umbilicus to pubic symphysis 5 cun), then measure 2 cun from the midline (the Kidney channel is at 0.5 cun).仰卧屈膝放松腹壁；按骨度（胸剑联合至脐 8 寸、脐至耻骨联合上缘 5 寸）定纵向位置，再从前正中线横量 2 寸（任脉旁开 2 寸，肾经在 0.5 寸）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

Forbidden in pregnancy. Empty the bladder before needling the lower abdomen.孕期禁针。排空膀胱后再针下腹部。

🛡 Safety安全

Deep insertion through the peritoneum can injure the small intestine, a full bladder and the uterus; go shallower with a thin wall, distension or surgical scars. Forbidden in pregnancy. Acute abdominal pain or rebound tenderness needs medical care, not needling.深刺穿过腹膜可伤小肠、膀胱（充盈时）与子宫；腹壁薄、腹胀或有腹部手术瘢痕时减浅。孕期禁针。急性腹痛、反跳痛先就医，不取穴。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is ST28 Shuǐdào?水道（ST28）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, 3 cun below the umbilicus, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.下腹部，脐中下 3 寸，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

How is ST28 needled?水道（ST28）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at ST28?水道（ST28）最要注意的安全问题？

Deep insertion through the peritoneum can injure the small intestine, a full bladder and the uterus; go shallower with a thin wall, distension or surgical scars. Forbidden in pregnancy. Acute abdominal pain or rebound tenderness needs medical care, not needling.深刺穿过腹膜可伤小肠、膀胱（充盈时）与子宫；腹壁薄、腹胀或有腹部手术瘢痕时减浅。孕期禁针。急性腹痛、反跳痛先就医，不取穴。
