Too long at the screen: five minutes for tired eyes盯屏幕太久：五分钟的眼周放松

At a screen people blink far less, and the eyes turn dry, gritty and heavy, slow to refocus on anything far away. This routine starts by taking your eyes off the screen, then works around the eye sockets. It is a break, not a way to prevent short-sightedness; the research on that is below.对着屏幕时，人眨眼的次数会少很多，眼睛干、涩、发胀，看远处一时对不上焦。下面这套从「先让眼睛离开屏幕」开始，再做眼周按揉。它是一次休息，不是防近视的办法——这一点后面有研究交代。

⏱ About 5 minutes约 5 分钟 · Once every hour or two of screen work; wash your hands first.每工作 1–2 小时做一次；先洗手。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Sudden loss of vision, a missing patch in your field of view, or sudden flashes and a shower of floaters: go to emergency the same day视力突然下降、视野缺了一块，或突然出现闪光和大量黑影飘动：当天去急诊
  • Eye pain with redness, headache, nausea and coloured halos around lights may be acute glaucoma: get care at once眼痛伴眼红、头痛、恶心，看灯周围有彩色光圈：可能是急性青光眼，马上就医
  • A red eye with pus, light sensitivity, or double vision眼红伴脓性分泌物、怕光，或看东西重影
  • Dry or sore eyes that last for weeks: see an optometrist or eye doctor眼干、眼痛持续几周不好：去看验光师或眼科

✋ When not to press这些情况先别按

  • Never press on the eyeball itself, only on the bony rim of the socket永远不压眼球本身，只按眼眶骨的边缘
  • Take contact lenses out first戴隐形眼镜的先摘下
  • After eye surgery (including laser vision correction), leave the area around the eye alone until your doctor says it is fine眼部手术（包括激光近视手术）后，医生说可以之前不按眼周
  • Not with a red, swollen eyelid or a stye眼睑红肿、长针眼时不按

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1

    Leave the screen first: look at something at least 6 metres (20 feet) away for 20 seconds and blink slowly a dozen times on purpose. Doing this every 20 minutes is the familiar 20-20-20 habit.先离开屏幕：看向 6 米（20 英尺）以外的地方 20 秒，同时有意识地慢慢眨眼十几次。平时每 20 分钟这样做一次，就是常说的 20-20-20。

    How much力度与时间 20 seconds20 秒

  2. 2
    BL2 Cuánzhú攒竹Kneading揉法

    Close your eyes and rest both thumb pads in the small hollows at the inner ends of the eyebrows (BL2 Cuanzhu); knead gently.闭眼，用两手拇指指腹按在眉头内侧的小凹陷（攒竹），轻轻揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

  3. 3
    ST2 Sìbái四白Kneading揉法

    Put the index-finger pads about one finger-width below the middle of the lower rim of each eye socket (ST2 Sibai) and knead gently.食指指腹放在眼眶下缘正中再往下约一横指的地方（四白），轻轻揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

    ⚠️ Stay on the bone; do not press up into the eye socket.只在骨面上揉，不要往眼眶里面按。

  4. 4
    EX-HN5 Tàiyáng太阳Kneading揉法

    Put the middle-finger pads in the hollow one finger-width behind the space between the outer end of the eyebrow and the outer corner of the eye (EX-HN5 Taiyang), and knead with light to moderate pressure.中指指腹放在眉梢和外眼角之间往后一横指的凹陷（太阳），轻到中度地揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

  5. 5
    Pushing推法

    Bend the index fingers and, with the side of the middle segment, stroke lightly along the upper bony rim from the inside out, then along the lower rim.食指弯曲，用第二节的侧面沿上眼眶骨从内向外轻刮，再刮下眼眶骨。

    How much力度与时间 5–10 strokes above and below上下各 5–10 次

  6. 6
    Friction rubbing擦法

    Rub your palms together until warm and cup them over your closed eyes, hollow of the palm over the eye, without touching the eyeball; stay still for a moment.两掌对搓到发热，轻轻盖在闭着的眼睛上，掌心空着、不碰眼球，静静停一会儿。

    How much力度与时间 About 30 seconds, 2–3 times每次约 30 秒，做 2–3 次

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E5 For screen-related eye strain the American Academy of Ophthalmology advises looking 20 feet away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes, blinking more, keeping the screen about an arm's length away, cutting glare, and using artificial tears for dryness.美国眼科学会对屏幕眼疲劳的建议是：每 20 分钟看 20 英尺外 20 秒、多眨眼、屏幕放在约一臂远、调低屏幕眩光，眼干时可以用人工泪液。 [1]
  • E3 Chinese eye exercises have been done in schools for over fifty years, but a study that followed 201 junior high students for two years found no statistical link between doing them and either the onset or the worsening of short-sightedness. There is no evidence for eye-area massage as a way to prevent myopia.眼保健操在中国校园做了五十多年，但一项跟踪 201 名初中生两年的研究发现，做不做眼保健操与近视的发生、加深都没有统计学关联。把眼周按摩当作防近视的办法，没有证据支持。 [2]
  • E5 We found no reliable trials of eye-area massage for adult screen eye strain; what it offers is a few minutes with the eyes closed and away from the screen.眼周按揉没有找到针对成人屏幕眼疲劳的可靠试验；它的价值是让眼睛有几分钟离开屏幕、闭上休息。
