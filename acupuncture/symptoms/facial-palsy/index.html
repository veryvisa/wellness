Facial palsy面瘫（口眼歪斜）

With any new facial droop, the first job is to rule out stroke. The forehead is the clue: peripheral palsy such as Bell palsy paralyses the whole half of the face, so the brow cannot lift and the eye cannot close, whereas the central palsy of a stroke usually spares the forehead and often comes with weak limbs or unclear speech — but laypeople should not rely on this rule; when in doubt, call 911. Once peripheral palsy is confirmed and medical treatment has started early, acupuncture patterns are wind-cold, wind-heat, wind-phlegm and, in recovery, qi and blood deficiency. The textbook main points are local facial points with LI4 as the distal point; in the acute stage use few facial points, light stimulation and shallow needling.新出现的脸歪，第一件事是排除卒中。区分要点是额头：周围性面瘫（如 Bell 麻痹）整半边脸都瘫，抬不起眉、闭不上眼；卒中引起的中枢性面瘫多半额头还能皱，而且常伴手脚无力或说话不清——但这个规律不能让外行自己判，拿不准一律拨 911。确认是周围性面瘫并尽早接受医疗处理后，针灸辨证分风寒外袭、风热侵袭、风痰阻络和恢复期的气血不足。教材主穴以面部局部穴为主，配合谷远端；急性期面部取穴宜少、手法宜轻、刺得浅。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Facial droop with any FAST sign — weakness or numbness of an arm or leg, slurred or muddled speech, sudden vision or balance problems: call 911 now and note when the person was last well; a crooked mouth with a forehead that still wrinkles is especially suspicious for stroke.脸歪伴 FAST 任一表现——一侧手臂或腿无力麻木、说话含糊或听不懂、突然视力或平衡问题：立即拨 911，记下最后正常的时间；额头能皱而嘴歪时尤其要警惕卒中。
  • Bell palsy should be seen by a doctor within 72 hours: steroids (sometimes with antivirals) help most when started early, so do not wait on acupuncture first.Bell 麻痹最好在发病 72 小时内就医：医生会评估是否用激素（必要时加抗病毒药），越早越有用，不要先针灸观察。
  • Severe pain in or behind the ear, blisters in the ear canal or on the ear, or hearing loss or vertigo: see a doctor today for possible Ramsay Hunt syndrome (shingles), which needs antiviral treatment.耳朵或耳后剧痛，耳道、耳廓出现水疱，或伴听力下降、眩晕：当天就医，警惕 Ramsay Hunt 综合征（带状疱疹），需要抗病毒治疗。
  • An eye that will not close: use artificial tears by day and ointment with the lid taped shut at night to protect the cornea; eye pain, redness or blurred vision needs an eye doctor the same day.眼睛闭不上：白天用人工泪液、夜间用眼膏并贴合眼睑保护角膜；出现眼痛、眼红、视力下降时当天看眼科。
  • Palsy on both sides, weakness that creeps on over weeks, repeated attacks on the same side, facial numbness or other cranial-nerve symptoms, or no recovery at all after 3–4 months: see a doctor for further tests to rule out a tumour, Lyme disease (after a tick bite), Guillain–Barré syndrome and others.两侧同时面瘫、几周内逐渐加重、同侧反复发作、面部麻木或伴其他脑神经症状、3–4 个月仍无任何恢复：就医做进一步检查，排除肿瘤、莱姆病（有蜱叮咬史）、吉兰-巴雷综合征等。

「卒口僻，急者，目不合，熱則筋縱，目不開。頰筋有寒，則急引頰移口，有熱則筋弛縱緩不勝收，故僻」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.13, on the sinew channel of the foot Yangming: a sudden crooked mouth; on the tense side the eye will not close; cold in the cheek sinews tightens them and pulls the mouth across, heat slackens them so they cannot hold, hence the deviation. The text explains the deviation as one cheek's sinews tightening and the other's slackening; the foot Yangming sinew channel runs past the mouth and binds at the cheek, which is the sinew-channel basis for using the Stomach points ST4, ST6 and ST2 locally. In modern practice the mouth is pulled toward the healthy side, and the slack, weak side is the affected one.《灵枢·经筋》足阳明之筋病：突然口歪，筋拘急的一侧眼睛闭不上；面颊受寒则筋拘急把口角拉过去，受热则筋松弛收不住，所以歪斜。原文把口歪解释为面颊经筋一侧拘急、一侧弛缓；足阳明经筋上挟口、结于颊，这是面瘫局部取足阳明经地仓、颊车、四白的经筋依据。现代临床的判断是：口角被拉向健侧，松弛无力的一侧才是病侧。

「口眼喎斜最可嗟，地倉妙穴連頰車，喎左瀉右依師正，喎右瀉左莫令斜」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Yulong song in Zhenjiu Dacheng: for a crooked mouth and eye, thread ST4 to ST6; if the mouth pulls left, drain the right, and if it pulls right, drain the left — the classical crossed method. Modern textbooks mainly needle the affected side, adding the healthy side in long-standing cases; students should know both.《针灸大成·玉龙歌》：口眼歪斜取地仓透颊车；嘴歪向左泻右侧、歪向右泻左侧。这是古人的「交叉取穴」法。现代教材以患侧取穴为主，病程久的可以加刺健侧，两者都要知道。

「面口合谷收」——高武《针灸聚英》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Four Command Points song in Zhenjiu Juying: for the face and mouth, use LI4. LI4 is the only distal point in the facial-palsy main set, usually needled on the healthy side or both sides; it is forbidden in pregnancy.《针灸聚英》四总穴歌：面部和口的病取合谷。合谷是面瘫主穴中唯一的远端穴，一般取健侧或双侧；孕妇禁针。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Acute stage急性期

External wind-cold风寒外袭

How it presents辨证要点 Often after wind or cold on the face (air conditioning, cycling into the wind); sudden crooked mouth and eye, incomplete eye closure, loss of forehead lines; tight face or mild ache behind the ear; pale tongue, thin white coating, floating tight pulse.多有面部受风受凉史（如吹空调、迎风骑车），突然口眼歪斜，眼睑闭合不全，额纹消失；面部发紧，或耳后隐痛；舌淡苔薄白，脉浮紧。

Principle治法 Dispel wind and cold, warm and free the channels (acute stage: shallow, light facial needling, distal points drained; moxa or warm compresses allowed)祛风散寒，温经通络（急性期面部穴浅刺轻刺激，远端穴泻法；可灸、热敷）

💡 Main set: GB14 for the forehead, SI18 for the cheek, ST4 and ST6 for the mouth corner, TE17 behind the ear near where the facial nerve leaves the skull, and LI4 distally ('face and mouth, LI4'); for wind-cold add GB20 to expel wind and LU7 to release the exterior, pairing with LI4 as source and connecting points.主穴：阳白管额、颧髎管面颊、地仓颊车管口角，翳风在耳后面神经出颅处附近，合谷远端「面口合谷收」；风寒加风池祛风、列缺宣肺解表，并与合谷原络相配。

Acute stage急性期

Wind-heat invasion风热侵袭

How it presents辨证要点 Often after a cold with fever or sore throat; sudden crooked mouth and eye with marked pain or tenderness behind the ear over the mastoid; thirst; red tongue, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse. Blisters on the ear mean Ramsay Hunt syndrome, which needs antivirals.多继发于感冒发热、咽痛之后，突然口眼歪斜，耳后、乳突部疼痛或压痛明显；口渴，舌红苔薄黄，脉浮数。耳部有疱疹者属 Ramsay Hunt，须抗病毒治疗。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat, move blood and free the channels (acute stage: shallow, light facial needling, distal points drained; no moxa)疏风清热，活血通络（急性期面部穴浅刺轻刺激，远端穴泻法；不灸）

LI11 may be added when fever and sore throat are marked. No moxa in heat patterns, and facial needling should be especially light.教材风热配外关、关冲：外关是三焦经络穴、通阳维，疏散少阳风热，正对耳后痛；关冲是三焦经井穴，点刺出血泄热。发热咽痛明显时也可加曲池。热证不灸，面部穴更要轻刺。

Wind-phlegm obstructing the collaterals风痰阻络

How it presents辨证要点 Crooked mouth and eye with marked facial numbness and slackness, a heavy wrapped head, chest fullness or much drooling from the mouth corner; heavier build; greasy white coating, wiry slippery pulse.口眼歪斜，面部麻木、肌肉松弛感明显，头重如裹，胸闷，或口角流涎多；形体偏胖；苔白腻，脉弦滑。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, transform phlegm, free the collaterals and correct the deviation (even method)祛风化痰，通络牵正（平补平泻）

Acupuncture textbooks often list only wind-cold, wind-heat and qi-blood deficiency; this pattern adds the internal-medicine view.这是《中医内科学》口僻用牵正散的证型：丰隆是胃经络穴，化痰并通足阳明面部经筋；风池熄风。针灸教材多只列风寒、风热、气血不足三型，这一型补充内科辨证。

Recovery stage恢复期

Qi and blood deficiency气血不足

How it presents辨证要点 Longer course (usually beyond 2–3 weeks) with slow recovery, slack weak facial muscles, sometimes reversed pull or synkinesis; fatigue, pale face, dizziness; pale tongue, thin weak pulse.病程较长（一般超过 2–3 周），口眼歪斜恢复缓慢，面肌松弛无力，或出现面肌倒错、联动；神疲乏力，面色淡白，头晕；舌淡，脉细弱。

Principle治法 Tonify qi and blood, nourish the sinews (tonify; moxa allowed; facial threading and needling of the healthy side may be added)益气养血，濡养筋脉（补法，可灸；面部可透刺、加刺健侧）

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

The commonest peripheral facial palsy is Bell palsy (idiopathic facial nerve palsy), which usually peaks within a day or two, often with pain behind the ear, altered taste and sensitivity to sound. Guidelines recommend oral steroids within 72 hours and protection of the cornea when the eye cannot close; about seven to eight in ten people recover well over weeks to months. Conditions that must be distinguished include stroke (central palsy, usually sparing the forehead, often with limb or speech symptoms), Ramsay Hunt syndrome (shingles of the ear, with a poorer outlook and a need for antivirals), Lyme disease, middle-ear infection, tumours of the parotid gland or skull base, injury, and Guillain–Barré syndrome when both sides are affected. Acupuncture is widely used for Bell palsy in China; trials suggest it may help but the evidence quality is limited, so it belongs after medical treatment as an addition. Facial stimulation should be light in the acute stage, and once synkinesis appears strong stimulation or prolonged electro-acupuncture is also best avoided.周围性面瘫最常见的是 Bell 麻痹（特发性面神经麻痹），多在一两天内达高峰，常有耳后痛、味觉改变、听觉过敏。指南推荐发病 72 小时内口服激素，眼睛闭不上时要保护角膜；约七到八成患者数周到数月内明显恢复。必须鉴别的有：卒中（中枢性面瘫，额肌多不受累，常伴肢体或言语症状）、Ramsay Hunt 综合征（耳部带状疱疹，预后较差，需抗病毒）、莱姆病、中耳炎、腮腺或颅底肿瘤、外伤，以及双侧面瘫的吉兰-巴雷综合征。针灸在中国广泛用于 Bell 麻痹，临床研究提示可能有帮助但证据质量有限，应在医疗处理之后作为补充；急性期面部刺激宜轻，恢复期出现联动时也不宜过强刺激或长期电针。
