📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the head turned to the opposite side and gently draw the auricle forward to expose the posterosuperior scalp.坐位或仰卧，头转向对侧，耳廓轻向前牵开，露出耳后上方头皮。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    On the postauricular curve from TE20 to TE17, locate about one third of the way from TE20 toward TE17, on the scalp behind and above the auricle; this is superior to TE18.沿角孙（TE20）至翳风（TE17）的耳后弧线，在从角孙向翳风约 1/3 处取穴，位于耳廓后上方头皮；比瘈脉（TE18）更靠上。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently palpate the posterosuperior scalp behind the auricular root. Posterior auricular vessels and branches of the great auricular and lesser occipital nerves are superficial; the mastoid part of the temporal bone lies deep. Stop at bone, avoid visible vessels, and do not needle deeply toward it.轻触耳廓根后上方头皮，浅层有耳后动静脉及耳大神经、枕小神经分支；深面为颞骨乳突部，触及硬骨即止，避开可见血管且不向骨面深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Trace upward from TE17 along the posterior mastoid curve to the upper-third junction, distinguishing scalp from mastoid bone; do not press hard on postauricular vessels to seek tenderness.从翳风沿乳突后缘向上触摸耳后弧线至上段三分之一交界，辨别头皮与乳突骨面；不可为寻找压痛而强压耳后血管。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Find TE17 and TE20, then move about one third down the postauricular curve from TE20; confirm that the point is higher than TE18.先分别找到翳风和角孙，再沿耳后弧线从角孙向下约三分之一处定位；以其高于瘈脉的关系复核。

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing places Luxi among bluish vessels behind the ear and warns that excessive bleeding is dangerous; this historical description is not a modern bloodletting instruction.《针灸甲乙经》将颅息记在耳后青络脉间，并警告出血过多有严重危险；现代临床不把古代放血描述作为操作指令。

「顱息刺不可多出血」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing separately gives a concise warning not to cause excessive bleeding at Luxi.《针灸甲乙经》另有简明提示，明确不可在颅息过量出血。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Mastoid-region soft-tissue pain or occipital neuralgia乳突区软组织痛或枕神经痛

Sharp pain radiating from behind the ear into the scalp may involve sensory nerves such as the lesser occipital nerve; rash, postauricular swelling, or fever needs assessment for infection or shingles.耳后向头皮放射的刺痛可涉及枕小神经等感觉神经；皮疹、耳后红肿或发热需排查感染或带状疱疹。

Scalp bleeding risk from postauricular vessels耳后血管性头皮出血风险

Postauricular vessels are superficial; people taking anticoagulants, with bleeding disorders, or with a local wound should not self-needle here.耳后血管浅表，抗凝药使用者、凝血障碍或局部伤口者不应在此处自行针刺。

Where is TE19 Lúxī?颅息（TE19）在哪里？

Head, on the curved line along the ear helix: at the junction of upper third and lower two-thirds from TE20 to TE17.头部，沿耳轮弧形连线，在角孙（TE20）与翳风（TE17）连线的上1/3与下2/3交点。

How is TE19 needled?颅息（TE19）怎么刺？

Transverse or oblique; 0.3–0.5 cun.平刺或斜刺；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of TE19?颅息（TE19）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Triple Energizer channel手少阳三焦经穴

Main safety point at TE19?颅息（TE19）最要注意的安全问题？

Scalp vessels and nerves are superficial; avoid visible vessels, stop at bone, and do not needle deeply.头皮血管神经表浅，避开可见血管；抵骨即止，不作深刺。
