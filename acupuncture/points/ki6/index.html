  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    A patient's thumb-width or combined index and middle fingers can serve as a rough check, but the hollow just below the medial malleolar border is the definitive guide.可用患者拇指横指约 1 寸或食指中指并拢比量参考，但以内踝尖下缘凹陷的骨性解剖标志为准。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate the prominent bony projection of the medial malleolus first, then move about 1 cun inferiorly toward the sole into the hollow between the lower border of the medial malleolus and the medial talus.先摸到足内侧最突出的骨性隆起——内踝尖，沿其下缘向足底方向移动约 1 寸，在距骨内侧与内踝下缘之间的凹陷处即是。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    In the hollow below the medial malleolus, with the medial crural cutaneous nerve under the skin; the deltoid ligament, tibialis posterior tendon and medial talus are deeper; the posterior tibial artery and veins lie posteroinferiorly with the tibial nerve trunk deep. Do not probe into the ankle joint, and withdraw if angling posteroinferiorly causes electric tingling.位于内踝下缘凹陷处，皮下有小腿内侧皮神经；深层邻三角韧带、胫骨后肌腱和距骨内侧；后下方有胫后动、静脉，深部为胫神经本干。不可向踝关节深处探刺，针尖偏后下方出现电麻感即退针。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gentle fingertip pressure between the medial talus and the lower margin of the medial malleolus reveals a distinct hollow and ligamentous tension, often with a distending ache; if tenderness shifts anterosuperiorly or posteroinferiorly, recheck its position relative to KI3, BL62, or KI8.指腹在距骨内侧与内踝下缘间轻按，可触及明显凹陷及韧带张力，局部常有酸胀感；若压痛偏前上或偏后下，需核对与太溪、申脉或交信的相对位置。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Insomnia不寐（失眠） ⚖️

Consider KI6 with HT7 and SP6 when insomnia leans toward heart–kidney disharmony or yin deficiency with effulgent fire, especially with dry throat or nocturnal heat; for liver constraint turning to fire or phlegm-heat disturbing the heart, do not rely on KI6 alone and combine points such as LR3 or PC6 based on pattern differentiation.偏心肾不交、阴虚火旺或伴咽干、夜间烦热者可重点考虑照海配神门、三阴交；若属肝郁化火或痰热扰心，则不以照海为唯一主穴，常与太冲、内关等辨证合用。

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

Use KI6 with LU7 for yin deficiency with effulgent fire, chronic dry throat with mild pain, or accompanying insomnia and palmar-plantar heat; for acute wind-heat with marked throat redness, swelling, and severe pain, do not lead with KI6 and favor points such as LU11, LU5, or LI4.偏阴虚火旺、慢性咽干微痛或伴失眠、手足心热者可用照海配列缺；若为急性风热外感、咽喉红肿剧痛，则不以照海为主，多选用少商、尺泽、合谷等清热利咽穴。

Irregular periods月经不调

Consider KI6 with SP6 and SP10 for delayed menses, scanty flow, or amenorrhea with yin and blood deficiency, dry throat, or restlessness; for qi stagnation with blood stasis, excess heat, or pregnancy, differentiate carefully and strictly avoid points contraindicated or risky in pregnancy.月经后期、量少或闭经而见阴虚血少、伴咽干或心烦失眠者可参考照海配三阴交、血海；若属气滞血瘀、实热或妊娠期，则需谨慎辨证与选穴，孕期禁针或慎用穴位须严格规避。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is KI6 Zhàohǎi?照海（KI6）在哪里？

In the ankle region, 1 cun below the tip of the medial malleolus, in the depression at the lower border of the malleolus.踝区，内踝尖下 1 寸，内踝下缘边际凹陷中。

How is KI6 needled?照海（KI6）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of KI6?照海（KI6）是哪类特定穴？

Eight-confluent point (opens the Yin Qiao vessel)八脉交会穴（通阴跷脉）

Main safety point at KI6?照海（KI6）最要注意的安全问题？

Bone and ligament are close: do not probe into the ankle joint; angling posteroinferiorly approaches the posterior tibial vessels and tibial nerve, so withdraw for electric tingling.骨面与韧带很近：不向踝关节深处探刺；针尖偏后下方会接近胫后血管与胫神经，有电麻感即退针。
