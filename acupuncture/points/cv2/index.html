Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is CV2 Qūgǔ?曲骨（CV2）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, at the upper border of the pubic symphysis, on the anterior midline.下腹部，耻骨联合上缘，前正中线上。

How is CV2 needled?曲骨（CV2）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of CV2?曲骨（CV2）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Conception Vessel and Foot-Jueyin channel任脉、足厥阴交会穴

Main safety point at CV2?曲骨（CV2）最要注意的安全问题？

No needling in pregnancy (textbook rule: no lower-abdominal needling in the first 3 months; after 3 months avoid the abdomen and lumbosacral region as well); empty the bladder first (a full bladder rises above the pubic symphysis against the abdominal wall).孕妇禁针（教材针刺注意事项：妊娠 3 个月内小腹部禁针，3 个月以上腹部、腰骶部亦不宜针）；针前排空膀胱（充盈膀胱可越过耻骨联合贴近腹壁）。
