    Anterior hairline midline to posterior hairline midline equals 12 cun. GB15 is 0.5 cun posterior to the anterior hairline, or one twenty-fourth of that span measured posteriorly from the hairline.前发际正中到后发际正中折作 12 寸。头临泣在前发际后 0.5 寸，即从前发际线向后量 1/24 段（前发际不清时，可由眉间印堂向上 3 寸定前发际正中后再向后量 0.5 寸）。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Use half the patient's thumb-width to go 0.5 cun behind the anterior hairline, aligned over the pupil; if the hairline is irregular or receding, place it 3 cun above the glabella.以患者拇指同身寸的一半量取前发际后 0.5 寸，再对准瞳孔直上；发际不齐或后退者以眉间上 3 寸定前发际。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Have the patient look straight ahead and locate the point directly superior to the pupil, 0.5 cun posterior to the anterior hairline, roughly aligned vertically with GB14 above the brow and the supraorbital region.嘱患者平视前方，取瞳孔正上方直线向上，与前发际后 0.5 寸的交点处；向下可与同侧阳白（眉上 1 寸，瞳孔直上）及眶上切迹或眶上孔大体在一条纵线上。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located just behind the anterior hairline on the forehead, the superficial layer contains cutaneous sensory nerve branches and scalp arterial branches above the epicranial aponeurosis, loose areolar tissue, and frontal calvarial periosteum, with branches of the supraorbital nerve and vessels nearby. Insert transversely along the scalp in the loose areolar plane, avoiding perpendicular needling against the periosteum or deep inferomedial probing near supraorbital neurovascular branches.局部位于前额发际后缘，浅层有头皮感觉神经分支与头皮动脉分支，深层为帽状腱膜、疏松结缔组织层及颅骨额骨骨膜，下方及内侧附近有眶上神经、眶上血管分支走行。行针时宜沿帽状腱膜下疏松结缔组织层平刺，避免直刺伤及颅骨骨膜或向内下深刺损伤眶上神经、血管分支。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently with the fingertip directly above the pupil, 0.5 cun behind the anterior hairline, where mild soreness or distension is common; moving inferiorly toward the orbital rim meets the supraorbital margin or can trigger tingling radiating across the forehead or brow.指腹在瞳孔直上、前发际后 0.5 寸处轻按或沿额骨表面滑动，局部常有轻微酸胀感；若按压偏下接近眶缘，可触及眶上缘骨缘或诱发向额部、眉弓放射的酸麻感。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting medially or inferiorly toward the brow or supraorbital margin moves closer to the supraorbital region; deep or misdirected insertion can irritate supraorbital nerve and vessel branches, causing sharp frontal pain or local hematoma.取偏靠内侧或偏下接近眉弓、眶上缘：易误入前额正中或眶上切迹附近，进针过深或方向不当易刺激眶上神经、眶上血管分支，引起额部剧痛或局部血肿。
  • Drifting laterally toward the temporal hairline leaves the vertical line above the pupil and nears branches of the superficial temporal vessels and auriculotemporal nerve, raising the risk of subcutaneous bruising.取偏外侧接近颞部发际：易偏离瞳孔直上纵线，向颞浅动静脉及耳颞神经分支走行区靠近，增加皮下出血风险。
  • Inserting perpendicularly or at too steep an angle: the scalp here is thin over calvarial periosteum, so perpendicular insertion is painful, shallow, and risks irritating the periosteum.直刺或角度过陡：额部头皮较薄，骨膜紧贴颅骨，直刺不仅疼痛明显、进针受限，还可能损伤骨膜。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Suited to headache and dizziness along the line above the pupil and Shaoyang pathway, often paired with GB41 or GB20; not the sole anchor for generalized, predominantly occipital, or secondary organic headache.偏重前额发际后、瞳孔直上及少阳经循行部位的头痛、眩晕，常配足临泣或风池；全头痛、后枕为主或器质性头痛不以其为唯一核心。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Used as an adjunct for dizziness accompanied by headache, visual blurring, or Shaoyang disharmony; vertigo with severe vomiting or suspected central causes requires medical workup rather than relying on a single point.适用于伴头痛、目眩或少阳经气不和的眩晕辅助调理；前庭周围性眩晕伴剧烈呕吐或中枢性眩晕需先明确病因，不以单穴替代诊疗。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

Helpful for red, sore, or strained eyes involving the Shaoyang pathway or head qi stagnation, often with distal points; acute ocular trauma, chemical exposure, or sudden vision loss requires immediate eye care.适用于目赤肿痛、眼部胀痛属少阳经循行或头面气机不畅者，可配合远端穴位；急性眼外伤、化学伤或视力急降须立即眼科就诊。

Blocked nose and sinusitis鼻塞鼻渊 ⚖️

Can serve as a supporting point for nasal congestion with frontal fullness and head qi stagnation; acute purulent sinusitis with high fever or periorbital swelling requires medical evaluation first.可作为鼻塞伴前额胀痛、头面经气不畅时的配穴之一；急性化脓性鼻窦炎伴高热或眶周红肿需先就医评估。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Frontotemporal discomfort in tension-type headache or migraine紧张型头痛与偏头痛前驱或发作期额颞部不适

Often presents as aching or throbbing pain across the forehead or temple with photophobia, nausea, or neck tension; sudden severe thunderclap headache, fever with neck stiffness, or new focal neurological deficits require immediate medical evaluation.多表现为前额、双侧或单侧太阳及颞部胀痛、搏动性痛，常伴畏光、恶心或颈肩紧张；若出现突发剧烈炸裂样头痛、发热颈项强直或新发神经缺损，须立即就医排除蛛网膜下腔出血或颅内病变。

Supraorbital neuralgia or localized frontal cutaneous nerve sensitivity眶上神经痛或前额局部浅表神经敏感

Causes paroxysmal shooting, burning, or tenderness over the forehead and supraorbital area, sometimes triggered by light touch; differentiate from prodromal or active herpes zoster, which requires prompt medical care if clustered vesicles or severe pain appear.前额及眶上区出现阵发性刺痛、烧灼感或触痛，局部按压或叩击可诱发不适；需与带状疱疹前驱期鉴别，若发疹前或发疹后出现成簇水疱及剧痛，应及时就医抗病毒与疼痛评估。

Eye strain, conjunctival redness, or sinus-related frontal fullness眼疲劳、结膜充血或鼻窦炎相关额部胀痛

Prolonged screen work, asthenopia, or impaired sinus drainage can cause frontal heaviness or periorbital ache; acute vision loss, features of raised intraocular pressure, high fever, purulent discharge, or marked facial swelling require prompt ophthalmologic or ENT evaluation.长时间用眼、视疲劳或鼻窦引流不畅可引起额部沉重感或眼眶周围酸胀；若伴有眼痛伴视力急剧下降、眼压升高症状或高热、脓涕及面部明显肿胀，需先由眼科或耳鼻喉科诊治。
