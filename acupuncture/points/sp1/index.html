📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or seated with the foot resting flat on the table or a stool, exposing the medial border of the great toe with the toes relaxed.仰卧位或正坐位，足平放于床面或凳上，充分暴露足大趾内侧缘，足趾自然放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Identify the medial border of the great toenail (the side closer to the midline of the body), locate the proximal medial corner where the nail root meets the medial nail groove, and move about 0.1 cun medially and slightly toward the toe tip to find SP1.观察足大趾趾甲的内侧缘（靠近身体中线的一侧），找到趾甲内侧底角（甲根与内侧甲沟交界处），向内侧及趾尖方向退开约 0.1 寸即隐白。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Clinically estimated using the classical 'width of a chive leaf': mark the skin about 0.1 cun (roughly 2–3 mm) beside the medial corner of the great toenail. Because the distal phalanx is tiny, standard finger-breadth rulers are not used here.临床常以古籍所称「如韭叶」估量：在足大趾内侧趾甲角旁开相当于一片细韭菜叶宽度（约 0.1 寸、约 2～3 毫米）的皮肤处定穴；因末节趾体极小，不用常规一横指法量取。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located subcutaneously on the medial side of the distal great toe along the medial margin of the distal phalanx tuberosity, with the flexor hallucis longus tendon insertion on the plantar aspect and the dorsal digital venous network plus digital branches of the medial plantar nerve superficially. Avoid the nail bed, nail matrix, and larger dorsal digital vein tributaries along the nail fold, and do not insert deeply onto the periosteum of the distal phalanx.位于足大趾末节内侧、大趾末节趾骨粗隆内侧缘的皮下；深面趾底侧有拇长屈肌腱末端附着，浅层分布有趾背静脉网与足底内侧神经的趾背/趾底固有神经分支。进针时需避开甲床、甲根生发区及甲沟内侧较粗的趾背静脉属支，切勿直刺触及末节趾骨骨膜。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Intersecting-lines shortcut: trace a longitudinal line proximally along the medial edge of the great toenail and a transverse line medially along the nail base; their intersection 0.1 cun medial to the nail corner locates SP1.交叉垂线法：沿足大趾趾甲内侧缘向近端作一条纵线，再沿趾甲根部（甲半月后缘）向内侧作一条横线，两线在甲角内侧 0.1 寸处的交点即是。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press lightly 0.1 cun beside the nail corner with a fingernail edge or blunt probe: the skin is thin with minimal subcutaneous fat and typically elicits sharp tenderness. Feeling hard nail keratin or the sharp ridge of the distal phalanx means you are too dorsal or pressing too deep.用指甲缘或钝头探针轻压甲角旁 0.1 寸处，局部皮薄肉少，多有明显锐痛或酸胀感；若摸到硬韧的甲缘角质或趾骨外缘棱线，说明位置过于偏背侧或偏深。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Locating at the lateral corner of the great toenail (facing the second toe): that is LR1 (Dadun), the Jing-well point of the Liver channel, resulting in a wrong-channel error.取到足大趾外侧甲角（靠第 2 趾侧）：那是足厥阴肝经井穴大敦（LR1），经脉完全取错。
