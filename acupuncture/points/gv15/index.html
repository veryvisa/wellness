GV15

哑门 Yǎmén

Mute Gate · High risk高危

★Crossing point of the Governor and Yang Linking vessels督脉、阳维脉交会穴
⚠️ Medulla zone: slow insertion toward the mandible only延髓区：只向下颌方向缓刺 The medulla and upper cervical cord lie deep: advance slowly toward the mandible only, never upward toward the foramen magnum or deep; withdraw at once on an electric sensation, limb jerk or dizziness.深面为延髓与上颈髓：针尖只朝下颌方向缓慢进针，禁向上（枕骨大孔方向）斜刺或深刺；有触电感、患者四肢抽动或头晕立即退针。

📍 Location定位★

In the posterior neck, in the depression above the spinous process of C2, on the posterior midline (about 0.5 cun above the posterior hairline).颈后区，第 2 颈椎棘突上际凹陷中，后正中线上（约后发际正中直上 0.5 寸）。

💡 Seated with the head slightly forward, slide down from the occiput to the first distinct spinous process (C2); the point is in the hollow at its upper edge.正坐头微前倾，从枕骨下向下摸到第一个明显的棘突（C2），穴在其上缘凹陷。

🪡 Needling刺法

Seated, head slightly forward and neck relaxed; insert slowly toward the mandible正坐头微前倾、项部放松，向下颌方向缓慢刺入 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Never angle upward or go deep; no lift-thrust or wide rotation.不可向上斜刺或深刺；不提插、不捻转大幅度。

🛡 Safety安全★

The medulla and upper cervical cord lie deep: advance slowly toward the mandible only, never upward toward the foramen magnum or deep; withdraw at once on an electric sensation, limb jerk or dizziness.深面为延髓与上颈髓：针尖只朝下颌方向缓慢进针，禁向上（枕骨大孔方向）斜刺或深刺；有触电感、患者四肢抽动或头晕立即退针。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Cervicogenic occipital pain颈源性枕后痛

May accompany restricted neck movement; occipital neuralgia has electric radiation and must be distinguished from headache, cervical disease and vascular causes.可伴颈部活动受限；枕神经痛呈放电样放射，需与头痛、颈椎病及血管性病因鉴别。

Speech or swallowing difficulty言语或吞咽障碍

New symptoms may reflect stroke, brainstem or neuromuscular disease and need urgent evaluation, not point-area treatment alone.新发症状可能为脑卒中、脑干或神经肌肉疾病，需紧急检查，不能以穴区治疗代替。

  • Sudden slurred speech, difficulty swallowing, one-sided weakness, double vision or altered consciousness: call 911.突发口齿不清、吞咽困难、单侧无力、复视或意识改变：立即拨 911。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is GV15 Yǎmén?哑门（GV15）在哪里？

In the posterior neck, in the depression above the spinous process of C2, on the posterior midline (about 0.5 cun above the posterior hairline).颈后区，第 2 颈椎棘突上际凹陷中，后正中线上（约后发际正中直上 0.5 寸）。

How is GV15 needled?哑门（GV15）怎么刺？

Seated, head slightly forward and neck relaxed; insert slowly toward the mandible; 0.5–1 cun.正坐头微前倾、项部放松，向下颌方向缓慢刺入；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of GV15?哑门（GV15）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Governor and Yang Linking vessels督脉、阳维脉交会穴

Main safety point at GV15?哑门（GV15）最要注意的安全问题？

The medulla and upper cervical cord lie deep: advance slowly toward the mandible only, never upward toward the foramen magnum or deep; withdraw at once on an electric sensation, limb jerk or dizziness.深面为延髓与上颈髓：针尖只朝下颌方向缓慢进针，禁向上（枕骨大孔方向）斜刺或深刺；有触电感、患者四肢抽动或头晕立即退针。
