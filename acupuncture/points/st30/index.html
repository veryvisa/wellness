ST30

气冲 Qìchōng

Rushing Qi · High risk高危

Traditional origin of the Chong vessel冲脉所起（传统）
⚠️ Groin vessel risk point腹股沟血管风险穴 Avoid any pulse: injury to the inferior epigastric or femoral vessels causes a groin haematoma; do not needle deeply into a hernia sac or the spermatic cord. Forbidden in pregnancy. A painful irreducible groin lump needs emergency care.避开搏动，刺伤腹壁下或股血管可致腹股沟血肿；疝囊或精索区不可深刺。孕期禁针。腹股沟包块剧痛不能回纳须急诊。

📍 Location定位★

In the groin region, level with the superior border of the pubic symphysis, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline, over the pulsating artery.腹股沟区，耻骨联合上缘，前正中线旁开 2 寸，动脉搏动处。

💡 Find the upper border of the pubic symphysis, measure 2 cun laterally at that level, just above the groin; if a pulse is felt, insert away from it.先摸耻骨联合上缘，平此高度从前正中线横量 2 寸，在腹股沟上方；指下若有搏动，进针时避开。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular, away from the artery避开动脉直刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Moxibustion not advised (Jiayi Jing). Forbidden in pregnancy.不宜灸（《甲乙经》「灸之不幸，使人不得息」）。孕期禁针。

🛡 Safety安全

Avoid any pulse: injury to the inferior epigastric or femoral vessels causes a groin haematoma; do not needle deeply into a hernia sac or the spermatic cord. Forbidden in pregnancy. A painful irreducible groin lump needs emergency care.避开搏动，刺伤腹壁下或股血管可致腹股沟血肿；疝囊或精索区不可深刺。孕期禁针。腹股沟包块剧痛不能回纳须急诊。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine with hip slightly abducted; locate only by trained clinicians.仰卧，髋轻外展；仅由受训临床人员定位。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Level with the upper border of pubic symphysis, 2 cun lateral to midline, just above inguinal ligament.耻骨联合上缘同高、前正中线旁开 2 寸，位于腹股沟韧带上方。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Under the external oblique aponeurosis lie the lower internal oblique and transversus; the superficial epigastric vessels are superficial, the inferior epigastric vessels lateral, and the femoral vessels further lateral near the mid-inguinal point; the ilioinguinal and genitofemoral nerves supply the area, with the spermatic cord (round ligament in women) deep. Identify and avoid any pulse. Forbidden in pregnancy.腹外斜肌腱膜下为腹内斜肌、腹横肌下部；浅层有腹壁浅动静脉，外侧有腹壁下动静脉，股动静脉在更外侧的腹股沟中点附近；有髂腹股沟神经、生殖股神经，深面为精索（女性子宫圆韧带）。指下搏动先确认并避开。孕期禁针。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Use pubic symphysis and inguinal ligament as references; touch lightly rather than compress vessels.以耻骨联合上缘与腹股沟韧带为参照，轻触而非持续压迫血管。

Where is ST30 Qìchōng?气冲（ST30）在哪里？

In the groin region, level with the superior border of the pubic symphysis, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline, over the pulsating artery.腹股沟区，耻骨联合上缘，前正中线旁开 2 寸，动脉搏动处。

How is ST30 needled?气冲（ST30）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, away from the artery; 0.5–1 cun.避开动脉直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of ST30?气冲（ST30）是哪类特定穴？

Traditional origin of the Chong vessel冲脉所起（传统）

Main safety point at ST30?气冲（ST30）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid any pulse: injury to the inferior epigastric or femoral vessels causes a groin haematoma; do not needle deeply into a hernia sac or the spermatic cord. Forbidden in pregnancy. A painful irreducible groin lump needs emergency care.避开搏动，刺伤腹壁下或股血管可致腹股沟血肿；疝囊或精索区不可深刺。孕期禁针。腹股沟包块剧痛不能回纳须急诊。
