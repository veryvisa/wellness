Newly Augmented Mosla Drink新加香薷饮

Xīnjiā Xiāngrú Yǐn · Wu Jutong's Wenbing Tiaobian (Qing; modified from Xiangru Yin in Taiping Huimin Heji Jufang); anchor text from Wenbing Tiaobian清·吴鞠通《温病条辨》（由宋《太平惠民和剂局方》香薷饮化裁而成）；原文锚点取《温病条辨》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Newly Augmented Mosla Drink新加香薷饮

Original text原文

「【新加香薷飲方】（辛溫複辛涼法）香薷（二錢）銀花（三錢）鮮扁豆花（三錢）濃朴（二錢）連翹（二錢）水五杯，煮取二杯。先服一杯，得汗止後服；不汗再服；服盡不汗，再作服。」——温病条辨

Wenbing Tiaobian (Systematic Differentiation of Warm Diseases)

States the composition and administration of the formula, using mosla herb, honeysuckle flower, fresh hyacinth bean flower, magnolia bark, and forsythia fruit decocted from five cups of water down to two, taking one cup first and stopping once mild sweating occurs.载明本方药物组成与煎服法：香薷二钱、金银花三钱、鲜扁豆花三钱、厚朴二钱、连翘二钱，加水五杯煎取二杯；先服一杯，得微汗即停服余药，未汗再服，若两杯服尽仍无汗则重新煎服。

「二四、手太陰暑溫，如上條證，但汗不出者，新加香薷飲主之。」——温病条辨

Wenbing Tiaobian (Systematic Differentiation of Warm Diseases)

Indicates this formula for hand taiyin summerheat-warmth presenting with anhidrosis due to exterior excess alongside fever, chills, flushed face, thirst, a surging large right pulse, and a relatively small left pulse.指明本方主治手太阴暑温兼感寒湿、表实无汗之证，症见恶寒发热形似伤寒、面赤口渴、右脉洪大、左手反小而汗不出。

「如夏月小兒身熱頭痛，項強無汗，此暑兼風寒者也，宜新加香薷飲」——温病条辨

Wenbing Tiaobian (Systematic Differentiation of Warm Diseases)

Notes that summer pediatric cases presenting with fever, headache, neck stiffness, and absence of sweating due to summerheat complicated by wind-cold exterior excess are also suited to this formula.补充说明夏季小儿身热头痛、项强无汗、属暑温兼夹风寒表实者，亦宜用新加香薷饮辛温解表、辛凉清暑。

Cautions使用注意

Combining acrid-warm diaphoretics with acrid-cool heat-venting herbs, this formula should be stopped as soon as mild sweating appears to prevent depleting qi and fluids; it is contraindicated in exterior deficiency with spontaneous sweating, pure summerheat without cold, or profuse sweating from yangming qi-level heat. Seek immediate medical care if high fever persists, profuse sweating continues, breathing becomes rapid, mental confusion develops, or pediatric convulsions occur.本方辛温发散与辛凉透热并用，得微汗即应停服，不可过汗以防耗气伤津（吴鞠通明言「得汗止後服」）；表虚自汗、暑热无寒或纯属阳明气分大热大汗者忌用。若服药后高热持续不退、大汗不止、呼吸急促、烦躁神昏或小儿出现惊厥抽搐，必须立即就医。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Honeysuckle flower carries a pregnancy caution on this site, and acrid-warm aromatic herbs such as mosla and magnolia bark also require clinical caution during pregnancy, so the formula takes the strictest level among its ingredients.方中金银花在本站按孕期慎用（香薷、厚朴含芳香辛温燥湿行气成分，临床孕期亦需审慎使用），方随最严者定为孕期慎用。
