KI20

腹通谷 Fùtōnggǔ

Abdominal Open Valley · Take care需谨慎

Crossing point of the Chong vessel and the Kidney channel冲脉与足少阴经交会穴

📍 Location定位★

On the upper abdomen, 5 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, 0.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 5 寸，前正中线旁开 0.5 寸。

💡 Locate CV13 (5 cun above the umbilicus) and go 0.5 cun lateral; called Abdominal Tonggu to distinguish it from BL66 (level with CV13).先定上脘（脐上 5 寸），旁开 0.5 寸；名「腹通谷」，以别于膀胱经足通谷（BL66）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Never insert deeply upward, to avoid the liver; contraindicated in pregnancy.不可向上深刺，以免伤及肝脏；孕期禁针。

🛡 Safety安全★

Contraindicated in pregnancy. Deep lie the stomach and left lobe of the liver: perpendicular 0.5–1 cun only, never deep upward under the costal margin; take care with an enlarged liver.孕期禁针。深面是胃、肝左叶：只按教材 0.5–1 寸直刺，不向上（肋弓下）深刺；肝大者慎针。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Abdominal pain and distension, epigastric pain, vomiting腹痛、腹胀、胃痛、呕吐 ⚖️Heart pain, palpitations心痛、心悸 ⚖️

🔗 Pairings配伍

  • With PC6 and ST36: vomiting and epigastric pain配内关（PC6）、足三里（ST36）：呕吐、胃痛

    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The distance from the midpoint of the xiphisternal junction to the umbilicus equals 8 cun. KI20 lies 5 cun above the umbilicus—five-eighths of the way up from the umbilicus—and 0.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸剑联合中点到脐中折作 8 寸。腹通谷在脐中上 5 寸，即从脐中向上量 5/8 段，再向前正中线旁开 0.5 寸。

    Surface landmark体表标志

    First locate CV13 on the anterior midline at 5 cun above the umbilicus, then measure 0.5 cun laterally; alternatively, measure 5 cun above the umbilicus first and move half a cun lateral.先定前正中线上的上脘（CV13，脐中上 5 寸），再向旁开 0.5 寸处取穴；也可由脐中向上量 5 寸定位后旁开半寸。

    Anatomy解剖结构

    Situated over the medial portion of the rectus abdominis on the upper abdominal wall, 0.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline, roughly at the level of the sixth or seventh costal cartilage deep plane.     Along the level 5 cun above the umbilicus, measure roughly half a middle-finger cun—the distance between the creases of the flexed middle finger—lateral to the anterior midline. Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

Appropriate for vomiting from rebellious stomach or Chong qi requiring upper epigastric regulation in non-pregnant patients; seek medical care first for severe persistent vomiting with dehydration or hyperemesis gravidarum, and compare or combine with PC6 and ST36.适用于胃气上逆、冲气上逆所致呕吐且需调理上腹气机者（非孕期）；剧烈频繁呕吐伴脱水或妊娠剧吐先就医，临床常与内关、足三里对比与配合。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Used only for mild-to-moderate epigastric distension or pain above the umbilicus from qi stagnation or cold in non-pregnant patients; not used for generalized abdominal pain, acute abdomen, or lower abdominal pain, and differentiated from CV12, ST25, and ST36 by pain location and pathogenesis.仅适用于脐上脘腹区域气滞或寒凝所致轻中度胀痛（非孕期）；全腹痛、急腹症或下腹痛不选，常与中脘、天枢、足三里按疼痛部位与病机区分选用。

Where is KI20 Fùtōnggǔ?腹通谷（KI20）在哪里？

On the upper abdomen, 5 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, 0.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 5 寸，前正中线旁开 0.5 寸。

How is KI20 needled?腹通谷（KI20）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of KI20?腹通谷（KI20）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Chong vessel and the Kidney channel冲脉与足少阴经交会穴

Main safety point at KI20?腹通谷（KI20）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. Deep lie the stomach and left lobe of the liver: perpendicular 0.5–1 cun only, never deep upward under the costal margin; take care with an enlarged liver.孕期禁针。深面是胃、肝左叶：只按教材 0.5–1 寸直刺，不向上（肋弓下）深刺；肝大者慎针。
