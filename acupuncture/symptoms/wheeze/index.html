Wheezing and asthma哮喘

In an attack use the rescue inhaler first and follow the asthma action plan; acupuncture belongs to the remission phase and severe acute asthma is not needled. Then separate excess from deficiency: attacks are mostly excess (wind-cold, phlegm-heat, phlegm-turbidity), treated with LU7, LU5, BL13 and LU1 to free the lung and descend qi; remission is mostly deficiency (lung or kidney qi), treated with BL13, BL43, BL23, LU9, KI3 and ST36. Both textbook sets also use the extra point Dingchuan (0.5 cun lateral to GV14), which has no card here. The main safety issue on this page is the chest and back: asthmatic lungs are often over-inflated, so even slight excess depth at LU1, BL43 or BL13 can cause a pneumothorax.哮喘发作时先用急救吸入器，按哮喘行动计划走；针灸是缓解期的事，急性重症不针。辨证先分虚实：发作期多实（风寒、痰热、痰浊），以列缺、尺泽、肺俞、中府宣肺降气；缓解期多虚（肺气虚、肾气虚），以肺俞、膏肓、肾俞、太渊、太溪、足三里补肺益肾。教材两组都还有经外奇穴定喘（大椎旁开 0.5 寸），本库没有它的穴卡，列在这里供查。这一页的安全重点是胸背穴：哮喘病人的肺常过度充气，中府、膏肓、肺俞稍深就可能气胸。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Unable to finish a sentence, blue lips or nails, sucking in above the collarbones or between ribs, sitting forward to breathe, growing drowsy, or the wheeze going silent while getting worse: call 911 and keep using the reliever as the action plan says.说不完整句子、嘴唇或指甲发青、锁骨上和肋间凹陷、坐着前倾喘、越来越困倦，或喘鸣音突然消失而人更差：立即拨 911，同时按行动计划反复使用急救吸入器。
  • Needing the reliever again within 4 hours, repeated attacks in 24 hours, or peak flow under half your best: emergency department the same day.急救吸入器用完 4 小时内又需要，或 24 小时内反复发作、峰流速低于个人最佳值一半：当天急诊。
  • Wheeze or breathlessness with chest pain, coughing blood, one swollen leg, recent surgery or a long flight: call 911 for pulmonary embolism.喘伴胸痛、咯血、一侧腿肿、近期手术或久坐飞行：拨 911，排除肺栓塞。
  • Wheeze after food, medicine or a sting with hives, facial swelling or vomiting: use an epinephrine auto-injector and call 911 (anaphylaxis).进食、服药或虫蜇后喘，伴风团、面唇肿、呕吐：立即用肾上腺素笔并拨 911（过敏性休克）。
  • A child who starts wheezing suddenly after choking (foreign body), or a baby breathing fast with flared nostrils and not feeding: emergency now.儿童突然喘、呛咳后一侧呼吸音不对（异物），或婴幼儿呼吸快、鼻翼扇动、吃不下奶：立即急诊。
  • Breathlessness lying flat at night that eases on sitting up, leg swelling, or a heart history: see a doctor the same day for possible heart failure.夜里平躺就喘、坐起好转，下肢水肿，或有心脏病史：当天就医，可能是心力衰竭。

「哮喘之症最難當，夜間不睡氣遑遑，天突妙穴宜尋得，亶中著艾便宜康」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Yulong song in Zhenjiu Dacheng, book 4: asthma is hard to bear, sleepless nights and hurried breath; find CV22 and use moxa on CV17. The textbook likewise adds CV22 for severe wheeze; both are high-risk points, needled shallowly or warmed only by trained practitioners.《针灸大成》卷四玉龙歌：哮喘最难熬，夜里睡不着、气急慌张，取天突，膻中用灸。教材「喘甚加天突」与此相合；天突、膻中都是高风险穴，只能由受训者浅刺或用灸。

「哮喘氣促，痰氣壅盛。豐隆 俞府 膻中 三里」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu Dacheng, book 7, under LU7: for asthma with hurried breath and obstructing phlegm use ST40, KI27, CV17 and ST36 — ST40 transforms phlegm, ST36 strengthens the spleen at its source and CV17 opens the chest, which is the logic of the phlegm pattern.《针灸大成》卷七八脉交会穴「列缺」条下：哮喘气促、痰气壅盛，取丰隆、俞府、膻中、足三里。丰隆化痰、足三里健脾治痰之源、膻中气会宽胸，正是痰浊型的配穴思路。

「肺脹滿，膨膨而喘欬」——《灵枢·经脉第十》（《黄帝内经》，公版）

Lingshu ch.10, disorders of the hand Taiyin: a lung swollen full, with puffing wheeze and cough — the channel basis for LU7, LU5, LU1 and LU9.《灵枢·经脉》手太阴肺经「是动」病：肺部胀满、气鼓鼓地喘咳。这是哮喘取手太阴列缺、尺泽、中府、太渊的经脉依据。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Excess (attack)实证（发作期）

External wind-cold风寒外袭

How it presents辨证要点 Triggered by cold: wheeze and cough with thin white frothy sputum, chills without sweat, headache, clear runny nose, no thirst; thin white coating, floating tight pulse.遇寒触发，咳喘气促，痰稀白有泡沫，怕冷无汗，头痛，鼻塞流清涕，口不渴；苔薄白，脉浮紧。

Principle治法 Dispel wind-cold, free the lung and settle wheeze (drain; moxa or cupping on the back)祛风散寒，宣肺平喘（泻法，背部可灸或拔罐）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LU5 Chǐzé尺泽BL13 Fèishū肺俞LU1 Zhōngfǔ中府
Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门LI4 Hégǔ合谷

💡 Main set: LU7, the Lung luo point, frees the lung and releases the exterior; LU5, the Lung he-sea, descends counterflow ("he-sea points govern counterflow qi"); BL13 and LU1 are the lung's back-shu and front-mu, working front and back; the textbook also adds Dingchuan. For wind-cold add BL12 to expel wind and LI4 to release the exterior, pairing with LU7 as yuan and luo. LI4 is forbidden in pregnancy.主穴：列缺是肺经络穴，宣肺解表；尺泽是肺经合穴，「合主逆气而泄」，肃肺降逆；肺俞与中府是肺的俞募配穴，前后调肺气；教材另加定喘止喘。风寒加风门祛风、合谷解表并与列缺原络相配。合谷孕妇禁针。

Excess (attack)实证（发作期）

Phlegm-heat obstructing the lung痰热阻肺

How it presents辨证要点 Urgent wheeze with chest fullness and phlegm rattle, thick yellow sputum hard to clear, thirst, fever or restlessness, dry stools; red tongue, greasy yellow coating, slippery rapid pulse.喘急胸闷，喉中痰鸣，痰黄稠难咯，口渴，发热或烦热，大便干；舌红苔黄腻，脉滑数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, transform phlegm, descend counterflow and settle wheeze (drain; no moxa)清热化痰，降逆平喘（泻法；不灸）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LU5 Chǐzé尺泽BL13 Fèishū肺俞LU1 Zhōngfǔ中府
Add配穴ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆LI11 Qūchí曲池

💡 ST40 transforms phlegm; LI11, the Large Intestine he-sea, clears heat through the channel paired with the lung; add CV22 for severe wheeze. Fever with purulent sputum needs pneumonia excluded.丰隆化痰，曲池是大肠经合穴清热，肺与大肠相表里；喘甚加天突降气利咽。发热、脓痰须排除肺炎。

Excess (attack)实证（发作期）

Phlegm-turbidity obstructing the lung (added from internal medicine)痰浊阻肺（内科辨证补充）

How it presents辨证要点 Wheeze with a stuffed full chest, abundant sticky white sputum, nausea, poor appetite, sticky mouth without thirst; thick greasy white coating, slippery pulse.喘而胸满闷塞，咳嗽痰多黏腻色白，恶心，食少，口黏不渴；苔白厚腻，脉滑。

Principle治法 Expel phlegm, descend counterflow and settle wheeze (even method)祛痰降逆，宣肺平喘（平补平泻）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LU5 Chǐzé尺泽BL13 Fèishū肺俞LU1 Zhōngfǔ中府
Add配穴ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆CV17 Dànzhōng膻中ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

💡 Dacheng treats "hurried wheezing with phlegm" with ST40, CV17 and ST36: ST40 transforms phlegm, CV17, the influential point of qi, opens the chest, and ST36 strengthens the spleen where phlegm forms. CV17 is needled only transversely under the skin.《大成》以丰隆、膻中、三里治「哮喘气促，痰气壅盛」：丰隆化痰，膻中为气会宽胸理气，足三里健脾治生痰之源。膻中只沿皮下平刺。

Deficiency (remission)虚证（缓解期）

Lung qi deficiency (with the spleen)肺气虚（兼脾虚）

How it presents辨证要点 Shortness of breath worse on exertion, weak low voice, spontaneous sweating, aversion to wind, frequent colds, thin sputum; poor appetite and loose stools if the spleen is weak; pale tongue, white coating, thin weak pulse.喘促气短、动则加重，语声低弱，自汗怕风，易感冒，咳痰清稀；兼脾虚见食少便溏；舌淡苔白，脉细弱。

Principle治法 Tonify lung and spleen qi and settle wheeze (tonify, plenty of moxa)补肺健脾，益气平喘（补法，多灸）

Main主穴BL13 Fèishū肺俞BL43 Gaohuangshu膏肓俞BL23 Shènshū肾俞LU9 Tàiyuān太渊KI3 Tàixī太溪ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里
Add配穴CV6 Qìhǎi气海CV17 Dànzhōng膻中

💡 Deficiency main set: BL13, BL43 and BL23 are back-shu and tonifying points; LU9 and KI3 are the yuan points of the lung and kidney; ST36 "banks up earth to generate metal". For lung qi add CV6 to tonify qi and CV17 to regulate it. BL13 and BL43 are oblique toward the spine and mostly moxa'd; CV17 is transverse; CV6 is forbidden in pregnancy.虚证主穴：肺俞、膏肓、肾俞是背俞和补虚要穴，太渊、太溪是肺肾原穴，足三里培土生金；肺气虚加气海补气、膻中调气。膏肓、肺俞斜刺向脊柱，多用灸；膻中平刺；气海孕妇禁针。

Deficiency (remission)虚证（缓解期）

Kidney qi deficiency (kidney failing to grasp qi)肾气虚（肾不纳气）

How it presents辨证要点 Long-standing wheeze with more exhaling than inhaling, breath that will not connect, much worse with movement, sore back and knees, cold limbs or night sweats, copious clear urine; pale tongue, deep thin pulse.久病喘促，呼多吸少、气不接续，动则喘甚，腰膝酸软，畏寒肢冷或盗汗，小便清长；舌淡，脉沉细。

Principle治法 Tonify the kidney to grasp qi and settle wheeze (tonify, plenty of moxa)补肾纳气平喘（补法，多灸）

Main主穴BL13 Fèishū肺俞BL43 Gaohuangshu膏肓俞BL23 Shènshū肾俞LU9 Tàiyuān太渊KI3 Tàixī太溪ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里
Add配穴CV4 Guānyuán关元KI10 Yīngǔ阴谷

💡 CV4 supports original qi and suits moxa; KI10 is the Kidney he-sea (water) point, tonifying the kidney to grasp qi. CV4 is forbidden in pregnancy; empty the bladder first.关元培补元气、宜灸；阴谷是肾经合穴（水），补肾纳气。关元孕妇禁针、针前排尿。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Asthma is chronic airway inflammation and hyper-reactivity, diagnosed from recurrent wheeze, chest tightness and cough (often at night or after exercise) with lung function showing reversible airflow limitation. The foundation of treatment is inhaled steroids to control inflammation, not the reliever alone: people who rely only on short-acting relievers have a higher risk of death and guidelines no longer recommend it; whether a combined low-dose inhaled steroid and formoterol serves as maintenance and reliever is the doctor's decision. Everyone should have a written action plan, correct inhaler technique, avoidance of triggers (viral infections, smoke, allergens, wildfire smoke) and the vaccines their doctor advises, such as flu, COVID-19 and pneumococcal. Not all wheeze is asthma: COPD, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, a foreign body and vocal cord dysfunction also wheeze. Systematic reviews have not shown that acupuncture reliably improves lung function, and inhaled steroids must not be cut or stopped for it; TCM patterns organise symptoms but are no reason to change medicines.哮喘是气道慢性炎症和高反应性，诊断靠反复喘鸣、胸闷、咳嗽（夜间和运动后多见）加肺功能证明可逆的气流受限。现代治疗的根本是控制炎症的吸入激素，而不是只用急救药：只靠短效扩张剂的人死亡风险更高，指南已不建议单用；推荐低剂量吸入激素加福莫特罗既维持又急救的方案由医生决定。每个人都应有一份书面哮喘行动计划，学会正确吸入法，避开诱因（病毒感染、烟尘、过敏原、山火烟），按医生建议打流感、新冠、肺炎等疫苗。喘不都是哮喘：慢阻肺、心衰、肺栓塞、异物、声带功能障碍都会喘。针灸对哮喘的系统综述没有显示能稳定改善肺功能，不能减停吸入激素；中医证型帮助整理症状，不是停药依据。
