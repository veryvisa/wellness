BL51

肓门 Huángmén

Yellow Gate · High risk高危

⚠️ Deep kidney-risk point深部肾脏风险穴 At 3 cun lateral on the lumbar second line the kidney lies just beneath erector spinae's lateral edge and quadratus lumborum. Insert only shallowly toward the spine, never deep or lateral. Severe flank pain or blood in the urine after needling needs care now.第二侧线腰段旁开 3 寸，竖脊肌外缘与腰方肌下面就是肾。只浅斜向脊柱，不直刺深入、不向外。针后腰侧剧痛或血尿立即就医。

📍 Location定位★

Lumbar region, 3 cun lateral to the lower border of the L1 spinous process.腰区，第 1 腰椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 3 寸。

💡 Find the L1 spinous process (C7 is the most prominent on neck flexion; scapular-spine roots ≈ T3, inferior angles ≈ T7, iliac-crest line ≈ L4) and measure 3 cun lateral at its lower border (medial scapular border to midline = 3 cun).先定 L1 棘突（C7 低头最突出、随转头微动；肩胛冈内端平 T3、肩胛下角平 T7、髂嵴最高点连线平 L4），在其下缘水平旁开 3 寸（肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique toward the spine向脊柱方向斜刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never deep perpendicular or lateral.不直刺深入、不向外。

🛡 Safety安全

The kidney lies deep: shallow oblique toward the spine only; no needling of the lower back in pregnancy. Severe flank pain or haematuria afterwards needs care.深部为肾，只浅斜向脊柱；孕妇腰部不针。针后腰侧剧痛或血尿立即就医。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Abdominal pain, constipation, abdominal masses腹痛、便秘、痞块 ⚖️Breast disorders乳疾

    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Anatomy解剖结构
