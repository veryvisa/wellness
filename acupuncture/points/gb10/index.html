    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Fix GB9 2 cun into the hairline above the back of the ear root and GB12 behind the mastoid, divide the curve that follows the ear into thirds, and take the division nearest GB9; hairlines vary, so rely on the mastoid and the proportion.先在耳根后缘直上入发际 2 寸处定天冲，再在乳突后下方定完骨，沿耳郭弧度的弧线三等分，靠天冲端的第一个分点即浮白；发际高低因人而异，以乳突与弧线比例为准。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    GB10 lies posterosuperior to the mastoid part of the temporal bone, with branches of the lesser occipital nerve, great auricular nerve, and posterior auricular artery superficially, and sternocleidomastoid and splenius capitis attachments plus deep mastoid fascia deeper; insert transversely along the scalp to avoid the posterior auricular artery and branches of the lesser occipital and great auricular nerves.浮白位于颞骨乳突后上方，浅层有枕小神经分支、耳大神经分支及耳后动脉分支走行，深层邻近胸锁乳突肌与头夹肌附着部及乳突部深筋膜；进针需沿头皮平刺，避开耳后动脉及枕小神经、耳大神经分支。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a fingertip gently along the curved line posterosuperior to the mastoid process to feel tenderness or dull ache near the bone-tendon junction; tingling radiating toward the pinna or occiput indicates contact with a great auricular or lesser occipital nerve branch.指腹在耳后乳突后上方循弧形线轻按滑动，可触及乳突骨面与肌腱附着缘交界处的压痛或酸胀感；若按压引起向耳廓或枕部放射的麻感，提示触及耳大神经或枕小神经分支。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting into the hollow posteroinferior to the mastoid process (GB12): too low, landing directly in the GB12 depression instead of the upper curved arc between GB12 and GB9.取偏至乳突后下方凹陷（完骨 GB12 处）：位置过低，直接落入完骨所在凹陷，偏离完骨与天冲的弧形连线上部。
  • Drifting anteriorly or superiorly inside the hairline above the ear (toward GB9 or GB8): too far forward or high, confusing temporal Shaoyang points rather than the posterosuperior mastoid arc.取偏至耳廓上方发际内（天冲 GB9 或率谷 GB8 方向）：位置偏前或偏上，易混淆少阳经颞部诸穴，无法准确对应乳突后上方的弧形连线。
  • Inserting perpendicularly or deeply over the mastoid: scalp tissue is thin over the mastoid bone and neurovascular structures, so deep or perpendicular insertion causes sharp pain, hematoma, or injury to the posterior auricular artery and lesser occipital or great auricular nerve branches; textbooks specify transverse insertion along the scalp for 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺或深刺乳突部：头皮软组织较薄且下方为颞骨乳突与神经血管，直刺或深刺易引起剧痛、皮下血肿或损伤耳后动脉与枕小、耳大神经分支，教材规定沿头皮平刺 0.3–0.5 寸。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Suitable as a supporting or paired point for unilateral headache or temporal-postauricular discomfort along the Shaoyang pathway; not a primary choice for generalized headache, elevated intracranial pressure, or internal deficiency headache, which require etiological evaluation first.偏侧头痛、颞部或耳后牵扯痛伴少阳经循行不适时可配伍选用；全头痛、颅内高压或内伤虚损头痛不以此穴为主，需先辨明病因。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Appropriate as an adjunct for tinnitus or hearing reduction accompanied by Shaoyang qi stagnation around the ear or neck; sudden sensorineural hearing loss, otitis media, or structural ear disease requires medical assessment first rather than relying on a single point.耳鸣耳聋伴耳周或颈项少阳经气阻滞时可作辅助配穴；突发性耳聋、中耳炎或器质性耳疾须优先医学评估，不以单穴替代专科治疗。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

Helpful alongside points such as GB20 for posterolateral neck and upper mastoid stiffness or restricted rotation; if cervical fracture, dislocation, cord compression, or significant trauma is suspected, do not needle blindly and seek medical care first.颈项侧后方及乳突上方牵拉强痛、转头不利时可配合风池等穴选用；若为颈椎骨折脱位、脊髓压迫或严重外伤后疼痛，严禁盲目针刺，须先就医。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Occipital neuralgia and great auricular neuralgia枕神经痛与耳大神经痛（耳后及枕部刺痛、电击样痛）

Presents as paroxysmal sharp, burning pain or numbness behind the ear, near the mastoid, or over the occiput, sometimes triggered by percussion or pressure along the nerve course; rule out intracranial pathology, cervical spine disease, or local masses first before considering acupuncture or referral.表现为耳后、乳突旁或枕部阵发性刺痛、灼痛或麻木，叩击或按压神经走行区可诱发放射痛；须先排查颅内病变、颈椎病或局部占位，明确神经分布后再决定是否配合针刺或转诊。

Myofascial pain syndrome of sternocleidomastoid and splenius capitis attachments胸锁乳突肌及头夹肌附着处肌筋膜疼痛综合征

Causes aching, tightness, or referred discomfort posterosuperior to the mastoid and along the lateral neck after sustained head postures or rotation, often with taut bands or tender spots; seek medical evaluation first if fever, local redness and warmth, or post-traumatic symptoms are present.长期低头、转头受限或伏案工作后出现乳突后上方及颈侧牵拉痛、紧绷感，可触及条索状结节或局限压痛点；若伴随发热、局部红肿热痛或外伤后症状，应先就医排查感染或骨关节损伤。

Referred pain from outer/middle ear conditions or temporomandibular disorder外耳/中耳病变或颞下颌关节紊乱牵涉痛

Ear inflammation, canal obstruction, or temporomandibular disorders can refer discomfort to the periauricular and temporal region; examine the ear and jaw first rather than masking symptoms with acupressure or needling alone.耳部炎症、耳道阻塞或颞下颌关节紊乱均可引起耳周及颞部不适，临床需先做耳镜及颌面检查明确原发病因，切勿仅靠按揉或针刺掩盖病情。

Where is GB10 Fúbái?浮白（GB10）在哪里？

On the head, posterosuperior to the mastoid process, at the junction of the upper third and lower two thirds of the curved line from GB9 to GB12 (parallel to the ear curve).头部，耳后乳突的后上方，天冲与完骨弧形连线（其弧度与耳郭弧度相应）的上 1/3 与下 2/3 交点处。

How is GB10 needled?浮白（GB10）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of GB10?浮白（GB10）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Foot-Taiyang and Foot-Shaoyang channels足太阳、少阳交会穴
