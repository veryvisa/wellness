📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or lying on the side with the head relaxed and neutral; ask the patient to open the mouth slightly so the space behind the condyle opens, and keep mouth opening steady during insertion and retention.坐位或侧卧位，头部端正放松，嘱患者微张口（闭口时髁突前移可能遮挡深部间隙，进针与留针期间按临床规范维持张口度）。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    In front of the auricle, find the intertragic notch between the tragus and antitragus first, then feel the posterior margin of the mandibular condyle just ahead; when the mouth opens, the hollow anterior to the notch and posterior to the condyle is GB2.在耳廓前方，先找到耳屏间切迹（耳屏与对耳屏之间的凹陷），再向前摸到下颌骨髁突后缘；让患者张口，切迹前方、髁突后缘出现的凹陷即是听会。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Opening the mouth glides the mandibular condyle forward; GB2 sits in the hollow anterior to the intertragic notch and posterior to the mandibular condyle, deep near the posterior temporomandibular joint capsule and posterior border of the parotid gland, with auriculotemporal nerve twigs, facial nerve branches, and superficial temporal vessels nearby that must be avoided.张口时下颌骨髁突向前滑动，听会位于耳屏间切迹前方、下颌骨髁突后缘的凹陷处，深面近颞下颌关节囊后份及腮腺后缘；浅层及周围有耳颞神经分支、面神经分支与颞浅血管走行，操作时须避开动脉搏动处及神经干。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate lightly anterior to the tragus and behind the condyle while the patient opens and closes the mouth to feel the condyle glide forward and back; a hollow deepens on opening, and if radiating tingling toward the temple or cheek or a strong pulse is felt, adjust angle or avoid the vessel.以指腹轻触耳屏前方及髁突后缘，嘱患者张口、闭口，可体会髁突向前滑动与后退；张口时凹陷加深，触诊或进针时若出现向耳颞部或面颊的放射麻痛或明显血管搏动，应调整角度或避开血管。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Locate in the open-mouth hollow anterior to the intertragic notch and behind the mandibular condyle, then cross-check vertically against TE21 in front of the supratragic notch and SI19 anterior to the mid-tragus as a rough orientation check only.耳屏间切迹向前微偏下、下颌骨髁突后缘张口凹陷处找穴，再与耳门（耳屏上切迹前方、髁突后缘凹陷）和听宫（耳屏中点前、下颌骨髁突后缘张口凹陷）上下对照，仅作初步定位核对。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting superiorly toward TE21 or the root of the zygomatic arch: the point shifts off the target for ear and jaw symptoms and sits closer to main superficial temporal vessels and ascending auriculotemporal twigs, raising bleeding or sharp discomfort risk.取偏靠上（误入耳门 TE21 或过近颧弓根）：位置偏上，不仅主治耳部与颞颌关节的局部对位偏差，且更靠近颞浅动静脉主干及耳颞神经上行支，易致出血或局部酸胀过强。
  • Drifting inferiorly or advancing too deeply into the parotid gland or temporomandibular joint space: going too low enters parotid tissue, and excessive depth or a poor angle can irritate the joint capsule, hit the mandibular condyle, or affect facial nerve branches, causing sharp pain, joint irritation, or facial twitching or numbness.取偏靠下或过深刺入腮腺深部／颞下颌关节腔：偏下易入腮腺组织，过深或角度不当可触及颞下颌关节囊、下颌骨髁突骨面或面神经分支，引起剧痛、关节囊刺激或面肌抽动／麻木。
  • Inserting deeply with insufficient mouth opening or with the mouth closed: the deep space narrows before the condyle advances forward, making perpendicular advancement more likely to meet the condyle or risk nearby vessels and nerves.张口不足或闭口状态下强行深刺：髁突未充分前移时深部空间狭窄，直刺易抵触髁突或增加关节及周围血管神经损伤风险。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Useful as a local pre-auricular point for tinnitus or reduced hearing with peri-auricular or Shaoyang discomfort, often paired with SI19, TE17, and TE5; sudden hearing loss or acute ear infection requires medical assessment first, and chronic tinnitus warrants audiologic evaluation.耳鸣、听力下降伴耳周或少阳经循行不适时可作为耳前局部穴选用，常配听宫、翳风、外关；若为突发耳聋或耳道急性感染应先就医评估，慢性耳鸣亦需结合听力学检查。

Toothache牙痛 ⚖️

Can serve as an adjunct for toothache related to upper posterior teeth or the Shaoyang pathway, though LI4, ST6, and ST7 are typically prioritized for toothache and GB2 alone is rarely the first choice.偏上颌后牙或少阳经循行相关牙痛可作辅助配穴，但远端循经与局部牙痛常优先考虑合谷、颊车、下关等牙痛常用穴，单用听会较少作为牙痛首选。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「聽會，在耳前陷者中，張口得之，動脈應手，少陽脈氣所發，刺入四分，灸三壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing states that GB2 lies in a depression anterior to the ear found on opening the mouth, where arterial pulsation is felt and Gallbladder channel qi arises, with classical needling of 4 fen and moxibustion of 3 cones — a reminder to respect nearby vessels and joint structures and keep depth controlled.《针灸甲乙经》明确听会在耳前凹陷中，须张口取穴，局部可感动脉搏动，为足少阳胆经脉气所发，古法刺四分、灸三壮。临床上提示该区邻近血管与关节结构，须注意避让与控制深度。

「聽會兼之與聽宮，七分針瀉耳中聾，耳門又瀉三分許，更加七壯灸聽宮」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Dacheng records pairing GB2 with SI19 using draining needling for deafness, alongside TE21 and moxibustion at SI19; in modern practice, GB2 is often grouped with SI19, TE21, and TE17 for tinnitus, hearing loss, and temporomandibular disorders.《针灸大成》记载听会与听宫相配泻法治疗耳聋，并可配合耳门及听宫艾灸。现代临床常将听会、听宫、耳门与翳风等耳周穴位组合用于耳鸣耳聋及颞下颌关节紊乱。

「少阳足经瞳子髎，四十四穴行迢迢，听会上关颔厌集，悬颅悬厘曲鬓翘」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Dacheng verse for the Foot-Shaoyang Gallbladder channel lists GB2 among lateral head and face points, reflecting its course along the side of the head and its role in regulating Shaoyang qi for ear, lateral head, and jaw complaints.《针灸大成》足少阳胆经歌诀列出听会等头面侧部经穴，体现其循行头侧、通调少阳经气的特点，故可用于耳窍及头面侧部、颞下颌关节相关不适。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Temporomandibular disorders (TMD)颞下颌关节紊乱病（TMD）

Commonly causes pre-auricular or joint pain, clicking, or limited mouth opening during chewing or opening, often linked to masseter tension, bruxism, or disc displacement; GB2 sits right behind the joint, so if joint locking, catching clicks, or marked bite changes occur, evaluate with dentistry or oral-maxillofacial care first.常见于张口或咀嚼时耳前、关节区疼痛、弹响或张口受限，常与咬肌紧张、夜磨牙或关节盘移位相关；听会紧邻关节后缘，若伴明显关节交锁、弹响卡顿或严重咬合改变，应先由牙科／颌面专科评估。

Tinnitus, conductive or sensorineural hearing loss, and outer or middle ear conditions耳鸣、传导性或感音神经性听力下降与外耳／中耳病变

Tinnitus and hearing loss have diverse causes including cerumen impaction, otitis media, tympanic membrane perforation, sudden hearing loss, or auditory nerve issues; sudden unilateral hearing loss, ear discharge, or vertigo requires prompt otologic and audiologic evaluation first.耳鸣与耳聋原因复杂，可能是耵聍栓塞、中耳炎、鼓膜穿孔、突发性聋或听神经问题；出现单侧突发听力下降、耳流脓或眩晕时，必须先做耳科检查与听力评估。

Auriculotemporal neuralgia or parotid gland inflammatory or mass lesions耳颞神经痛或腮腺区炎性／肿块性病变

Paroxysmal shock-like pain in the pre-auricular and temporal area or a pre-auricular lump or tenderness should be distinguished from joint pain; if a fixed mass, warmth, redness, swelling, or fever is present, seek medical diagnosis first rather than needling or pressing blindly.耳前及颞部阵发性电击样痛或耳屏前肿块、压痛需与颞下颌关节痛区分；若触及固定包块、红肿热痛或伴发热，应先就医明确诊断，避免盲目针刺或按压。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is GB2 Tīnghuì?听会（GB2）在哪里？

On the face, in the depression between the intertragic notch and the condylar process of the mandible.面部，耳屏间切迹与下颌骨髁突之间的凹陷中。

How is GB2 needled?听会（GB2）怎么刺？

Perpendicular with the mouth slightly open; 0.5–0.8 cun.微张口，直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at GB2?听会（GB2）最要注意的安全问题？

Insert with the mouth open; avoid the pulsating superficial temporal artery; deeper needling enters the parotid gland and back of the jaw joint.须张口进针；触到动脉搏动（颞浅动脉）要避开，过深会入腮腺与颞下颌关节后份。
