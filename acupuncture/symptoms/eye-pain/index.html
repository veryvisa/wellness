Red painful eyes目赤肿痛

Most red eyes are conjunctivitis, dry eye or allergy and settle within days to two weeks. What matters are three signals: real pain (not just itch or grittiness), light sensitivity and reduced vision. Any one of them means an eye doctor first, to rule out acute glaucoma, keratitis or uveitis; acupuncture cannot wait in line ahead of that. Once those are excluded, the textbook divides red painful eyes into external wind-heat and liver–gallbladder fire, with the eye-area points BL1 and Taiyang as main points plus GB20, LI4 and LR3. Severe, highly contagious epidemic red eye is the heat-toxin pattern, and long-standing dry, reddened eyes are yin deficiency with fire; these two come from TCM ophthalmology.红眼本身多半是结膜炎、干眼或过敏，几天到两周会好；真正要紧的是三个信号：眼睛痛（不只是痒或沙）、怕光、视力下降。有任何一个，先看眼科排除急性青光眼、角膜炎、葡萄膜炎，针灸不能等。排除之后，教材的目赤肿痛分外感风热和肝胆火盛两型，主穴是眼区的睛明、太阳，配风池、合谷、太冲；传染性强、红肿剧烈的天行赤眼重证属热毒炽盛，久病干涩发红的属阴虚火旺，这两型来自中医眼科的辨证。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Chemicals in the eye, especially alkalis such as drain cleaner, lime or cement: rinse with running water for at least 15–20 minutes straight away, and call 911 or go to emergency while rinsing; do not wait to see how it feels.化学品（尤其碱性：通渠剂、石灰、水泥）溅入眼睛：立刻用清水连续冲洗至少 15–20 分钟，边冲边拨 911 或直奔急诊，不要先等症状。
  • Sudden severe eye pain with blurred vision and halos around lights, plus headache, nausea or vomiting: emergency now for possible acute angle-closure glaucoma, which can damage the optic nerve within hours.眼睛突然剧痛、视物模糊、看灯有彩虹圈，伴头痛、恶心呕吐：立即急诊，警惕急性闭角型青光眼，几小时内可能损伤视神经。
  • Eye pain with light sensitivity, reduced vision or a white spot on the cornea, or a red painful eye in a contact-lens wearer: take the lenses out and see an eye doctor the same day to rule out keratitis or a corneal ulcer, which can threaten sight within a day or two.眼痛伴怕光、视力下降、角膜上有白点，或戴隐形眼镜的人眼睛红痛：立即摘镜，当天看眼科，排除角膜炎和角膜溃疡（棘阿米巴、绿脓杆菌可在一两天内致盲）。
  • Redness, pain, reduced vision or an irregular pupil after a blow, a puncture or something flying into the eye, or a sudden shower of floaters, flashes or a curtain over the vision: emergency now.眼睛被击中、扎伤或有异物飞入后红痛、视力下降、瞳孔变形，或眼前突然出现大量飞蚊、闪光、黑幕遮挡：立即急诊。
  • Red, hot, swollen eyelids and surrounding skin, a bulging eye, pain on moving the eye or double vision, with fever: emergency today to rule out orbital cellulitis.眼睑和眼周红肿发热、眼球突出、转动眼球疼痛或复视，伴发热：当天急诊，排除眼眶蜂窝织炎。
  • Red, discharging eyes in a baby under 4 weeks old, or a red eye with shingles blisters on the forehead or scalp: see a doctor the same day.出生 4 周内的婴儿眼红有分泌物，或红眼伴前额头皮疱疹（带状疱疹）：当天就医。

「目不明，惡風日，淚出憎寒，目痛目眩，內眥赤痛，目𥆨𥆨無所見，癢痛，淫膚白翳，睛明主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi jing: dim vision, aversion to wind and light, tearing with chills, eye pain and blurring, a red sore inner corner, hazy sight, itching and pain, white film over the eye — BL1 governs it. This is why the textbook makes BL1 a main point for red painful eyes. BL1 sits right against the inner side of the eyeball, so only trained practitioners needle it, slowly along the orbital wall and without lifting, thrusting or twirling.《针灸甲乙经》：视物不清，怕风怕光，流泪怕冷，眼痛眼花，内眼角红痛，看东西模模糊糊，眼痒眼痛，眼内生白翳，以睛明为主治穴。这就是教材把睛明列为目赤肿痛主穴的源头。睛明紧贴眼球内侧，只能由受训者沿眶缘缓慢刺入，不提插捻转。

「診目痛，赤脈從上下者太陽病，從下上者陽明病，從外走內者少陽病」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.74: in examining eye pain, look at the direction of the red vessels on the white of the eye — running from above downward is a Taiyang disorder, from below upward Yangming, from the outer corner inward Shaoyang. It is the classical way of assigning eye pain to a channel to choose distal points; it cannot tell conjunctivitis from glaucoma.《灵枢·论疾诊尺》：诊察眼痛，看眼白上的红血丝走向——从上往下的属太阳经病，从下往上的属阳明经病，从外眼角往内走的属少阳经病。这是按经辨眼痛的古法，用于选择远端穴所在的经；它不能判断是结膜炎还是青光眼。

「胸中滿，耳前痛，齒痛，目赤痛，頸腫，寒熱，渴飲輒汗出，不飲則皮乾熱，曲池主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi jing: fullness in the chest, pain in front of the ear, toothache, red painful eyes, a swollen neck, chills and fever, sweating whenever one drinks and dry hot skin otherwise — LI11 governs it. All of these are signs of exuberant Yangming heat, which is why LI11 is added in the heat-toxin pattern.《针灸甲乙经》：胸中胀满、耳前痛、牙痛、眼红痛、颈部肿、发冷发热、喝水就出汗、不喝水皮肤干热，以曲池为主治穴。这一串都是阳明热盛的表现，所以热毒炽盛型加曲池清热。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

External外感

External wind-heat外感风热

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden onset with red whites of the eyes, itchy, gritty, painful eyes, tearing, sticky discharge and slightly swollen lids; sometimes headache, fever, aversion to wind or sore throat; red tongue tip, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse.起病急，白睛红赤，眼痒涩痛，流泪，眵多黏稠，眼睑微肿；或伴头痛、发热、恶风、咽痛；舌尖红，苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Disperse wind and heat, reduce swelling and stop pain (drain; prick LU11 to bleed)疏风散热，消肿止痛（泻法；少商点刺出血）

Main主穴BL1 Jīngmíng睛明GB20 Fēngchí风池LI4 Hégǔ合谷LR3 Tàichōng太冲
Add配穴LU11 Shàoshāng少商GV23 Shàngxīng上星

💡 Main set: BL1 is the local eye point, Taiyang is pricked to bleed to drain heat (no card here), GB20 expels wind and clears the head and eyes, LI4 ('face and mouth, LI4') clears Yangming and stops pain, and LR3 is the Liver yuan point, the liver opening into the eyes; for wind-heat add LU11, the Lung jing-well point, pricked to bleed to drain lung heat (the white of the eye belongs to the lung), and GV23, a Governor vessel point that clears heat from the head and face.主穴：睛明是眼区局部穴，太阳点刺出血泄热（本库无卡），风池祛风清头目，合谷「面口合谷收」清阳明、止痛，太冲是肝经原穴、肝开窍于目；风热加少商——肺经井穴，点刺出血泄肺热（白睛属肺）；上星是督脉穴，清头面之热。

Excess实证

Liver and gallbladder fire肝胆火盛

How it presents辨证要点 More severe red, swollen, painful eyes with dislike of heat and light and much discharge; bitter taste and dry throat, irritability, tinnitus, flank pain, sometimes constipation and dark urine, often worse after anger, late nights or alcohol; red tongue, yellow coating, wiry rapid pulse.目赤肿痛较重，怕热羞明，眵多；口苦咽干，烦躁易怒，耳鸣，胁痛，或便秘尿黄，常因生气、熬夜、饮酒加重；舌红苔黄，脉弦数。

Principle治法 Drain liver and gallbladder fire, brighten the eyes, stop pain (drain)清泻肝胆，明目止痛（泻法）

Main主穴BL1 Jīngmíng睛明GB20 Fēngchí风池LI4 Hégǔ合谷LR3 Tàichōng太冲
Add配穴LR2 Xíngjiān行间GB43 Xiáxī侠溪

💡 LR2, the Liver ying-spring (fire) point, and GB43, the Gallbladder ying-spring point ('ying points govern heat'), clear the liver and the gallbladder respectively; with LR3 and GB20 in the main set they clear fire along both channels. Severe pain, reduced vision or halos around lights do not belong here: rule out glaucoma first.行间是肝经荥穴（火），侠溪是胆经荥穴，「荥主身热」，一清肝一清胆；与主穴里的太冲、风池配合，从肝胆经上下清火。剧痛、视力下降、看灯有彩虹圈的不属此型，先排除青光眼。

Epidemic toxin (ophthalmology pattern)疫毒（眼科辨证）

Blazing heat-toxin (severe epidemic red eye)热毒炽盛（天行赤眼重证）

How it presents辨证要点 Usually in an outbreak season or after contact with someone with red eye, one eye then the other; red, puffy whites with dot or patch haemorrhages, red swollen lids, much discharge, hot streaming tears, gritty pain that makes the eyes hard to open; sometimes fever, headache or constipation; red tongue, yellow coating, rapid pulse.多在流行季节、接触红眼病人后发病，双眼先后受累；白睛红赤浮肿、可见点状或片状出血，眼睑红肿，眵多，热泪如汤，涩痛难睁；可伴发热、头痛、便秘；舌红苔黄，脉数。

Principle治法 Drain fire, resolve toxin, cool the blood and reduce swelling (drain; GV14 and Taiyang may be pricked to bleed)泻火解毒，凉血消肿（泻法；大椎、太阳可刺络出血）

Main主穴BL1 Jīngmíng睛明GB20 Fēngchí风池LI4 Hégǔ合谷LR3 Tàichōng太冲
Add配穴LI11 Qūchí曲池GV14 Dàzhuī大椎

💡 This pattern comes from TCM ophthalmology's treatment of epidemic red eye; the acupuncture textbook lists only two patterns. LI11, the Large Intestine he-sea point, clears Yangming heat-toxin; GV14, where all yang channels meet, is pricked and cupped to drain heat. In the acute stage the eye area is mainly for draining heat, and bleeding at Taiyang may replace BL1. Viral conjunctivitis is highly contagious, so wash hands and sterilise instruments strictly; symptoms beyond one or two weeks or any drop in vision need an eye doctor.这一型来自《中医眼科学》天行赤眼的辨证，针灸教材目赤肿痛节只分两型。曲池是大肠经合穴、清阳明热毒；大椎是诸阳之会，刺络拔罐泄热。急性期眼区以泻热为主，睛明可改用太阳放血；病毒性结膜炎传染性强，接诊前后洗手、器械严格消毒，症状超过一两周或出现视力下降要看眼科。

Deficiency (ophthalmology pattern)虚证（眼科辨证）

Yin deficiency with flaring fire阴虚火旺

How it presents辨证要点 A long course with pale-red whites that redden again and again, dry, burning, easily tired eyes that are worse in the afternoon or after screen work, little discharge; dry mouth, hot palms, soles and chest, sore lower back and knees, poor sleep; red tongue with little coating, thin rapid pulse.病程长，白睛淡红、反复发红，眼干涩、灼热、易疲劳，午后或用眼久后加重，眵少；口干，五心烦热，腰膝酸软，失眠；舌红少苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin, bring down fire, nourish the liver and brighten the eyes (tonify or even method; no bleeding, no moxa near the eye)滋阴降火，养肝明目（补法或平补平泻；不放血、不灸眼区）

Main主穴BL1 Jīngmíng睛明GB20 Fēngchí风池KI3 Tàixī太溪SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交
Add配穴GB37 Guāngmíng光明BL18 Gānshū肝俞

💡 This pattern comes from TCM ophthalmology's dry-eye and chronic red-eye differentiation rather than the textbook's two patterns, so the main set shifts to tonifying: BL1 and GB20 stay, KI3, the Kidney yuan point, nourishes kidney water, and SP6 nourishes the yin of the liver, spleen and kidney; GB37, the Gallbladder luo point connecting to the Liver channel, is the traditional point for brightening the eyes; BL18 nourishes liver blood. Modern dry-eye care — artificial tears, warm compresses, eyelid-gland care and less screen time — is the foundation, with acupuncture as an addition. SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy; BL18 is beside T9 and is needled obliquely to avoid pneumothorax.这一型来自《中医眼科学》白涩症、目赤久病的辨证，不在针灸教材目赤肿痛节的两型里，主穴相应换成补虚：睛明、风池照用，太溪是肾经原穴滋肾水，三阴交滋肝脾肾之阴；光明是胆经络穴、别走厥阴，传统明目要穴；肝俞补肝血。干眼的现代处理（人工泪液、热敷、睑板腺护理、减少屏幕时间）是基础，针灸是补充。三阴交孕妇禁针，肝俞在 T9 旁斜刺防气胸。

BL1 Jīngmíng睛明

Best for最适合 The eye-area main point in every pattern: red sore inner corner, tearing, blurred vision各型目赤肿痛的眼区主穴：内眼角红痛、流泪、视物模糊

Why依据 First point of the Bladder channel, meeting of the hand and foot Taiyang and foot Yangming; Jiayi: 'red sore inner corner… BL1 governs it'足太阳经起点，手足太阳、足阳明之会；《甲乙经》「内眥赤痛……睛明主之」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Closer to the eyeball than BL2, so more direct and more dangerous: with the eye closed, push the eyeball gently outward and hold it, insert slowly along the orbital wall 0.5–1 cun without lifting, thrusting or twirling, and press 1–2 minutes after withdrawal to prevent a haematoma; no moxa; specially trained practitioners only.比攒竹更近眼球、作用更直接也更危险：嘱患者闭目，押手把眼球轻推向外侧固定，沿眶缘缓慢直刺 0.5–1 寸，不提插、不捻转，出针后按压 1–2 分钟防血肿；禁灸；只限受过专门训练者。

BL2 Cuánzhú攒竹

Best for最适合 Eye pain running into the brow ridge, twitching lids, or as the eye-area substitute when BL1 is not suitable眼痛连眉棱骨、眼睑跳动，或不便刺睛明时的眼区替代穴

Why依据 Bladder channel point in the hollow at the inner end of the brow足太阳经眉头凹陷中

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Farther from the eyeball than BL1 and lower risk, acting more on the brow; insert transversely downward or outward 0.3–0.5 cun, no moxa, press after withdrawal to prevent bleeding.比睛明离眼球远、风险低，作用偏眉部；向下或向外平刺 0.3–0.5 寸，禁灸，出针后按压防出血。

GB20 Fēngchí风池

Best for最适合 Red eyes with headache, a stiff nape or aversion to wind目赤伴头痛、后项强、怕风

Why依据 Meeting point of the Gallbladder channel and Yang Wei vessel; expels wind and clears the head and eyes足少阳与阳维脉交会穴；祛风清头目

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the head, eyes and wind than LI4, which leans to the face and pain; 0.8–1.2 cun toward the tip of the nose, never deep upward and inward (medulla, vertebral artery).比合谷更偏头目和风邪，合谷偏面口止痛；针尖朝鼻尖 0.8–1.2 寸，不可向内上方深刺（延髓、椎动脉）。

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

Best for最适合 Wind-heat or heat-toxin red eyes with face or head pain and fever风热、热毒目赤伴头面痛、发热

Why依据 Large Intestine yuan point; 'face and mouth, LI4'大肠经原穴；「面口合谷收」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for external and Yangming heat than LR3, which is for liver fire; together they are the Four Gates, often paired in excess red eye; forbidden in pregnancy.比太冲偏外感和阳明热，太冲偏肝火；两穴合称「四关」，实证目赤常同用；孕妇禁针。

LR3 Tàichōng太冲

Best for最适合 Red distending eye pain with irritability, bitter taste and a full head目赤胀痛伴急躁、口苦、头胀

Why依据 Liver yuan and shu-stream point; the liver opens into the eyes肝经原穴、输穴；肝开窍于目

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Calms and regulates the liver as a yuan point, whereas LR2, the ying-spring point, clears liver fire; not drained as the lead point in yin deficiency.比行间偏平肝调气（原穴），行间是荥穴、偏清肝火；阴虚火旺型不以泻太冲为主。

LU11 Shàoshāng少商

Best for最适合 Early wind-heat red eye with red whites and a sore throat风热目赤初起，白睛红、咽痛

Why依据 Lung jing-well point; the white of the eye belongs to the lung, so it is pricked to bleed to drain heat肺经井穴；白睛属肺，点刺出血泄热

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Quicker and more confined to lung-level heat than LI11, which clears interior Yangming heat; for bleeding in excess heat only, pricked 0.1 cun from the radial corner of the thumbnail.比曲池更急更局限于肺卫热，曲池偏阳明里热；只用于实热放血，拇指桡侧指甲角旁 0.1 寸点刺。

GV23 Shàngxīng上星

Best for最适合 Wind-heat red eye with frontal headache or a blocked nose风热目赤伴前额痛、鼻塞

Why依据 Governor vessel point 1 cun into the anterior hairline at the midline; the textbook's added point for wind-heat督脉穴，前发际正中直上 1 寸；教材风热配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the forehead, nose and eyes than GB20, which leans to the back of the head and wind; insert transversely backward 0.5–0.8 cun, or prick to bleed.比风池偏前额和鼻目，风池偏后头与祛风；向后平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，可点刺出血。

LR2 Xíngjiān行间

Best for最适合 Liver-fire red eye: burning, bitter taste, irritability, insomnia肝火目赤：灼热、口苦、烦躁、失眠

Why依据 Liver ying-spring (fire) point, 'ying points govern heat'; the textbook's added point for liver–gallbladder fire肝经荥穴（火），「荥主身热」；教材肝胆火盛配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Clears fire more than LR3 and works on the Liver channel itself rather than GB43; not added without fire signs.比太冲更偏清火，比侠溪偏肝本经；没有火象时不加。

GB43 Xiáxī侠溪

Best for最适合 Red eye with pain at the outer corner, one-sided headache or tinnitus目赤伴外眼角痛、偏头痛、耳鸣

Why依据 Gallbladder ying-spring point; the textbook's added point for liver–gallbladder fire胆经荥穴；教材肝胆火盛配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the Shaoyang course (outer eye corner, temple, ear) than LR2, which acts on the Liver channel; when red vessels run in from the outer corner (Shaoyang, per Lingshu) it is a natural first choice.比行间偏少阳循行（外眼角、颞侧、耳），行间偏肝本经；《灵枢》红丝从外眼角向内走的属少阳，可优先取。

LI11 Qūchí曲池

Best for最适合 Heat-toxin red eye with fever, constipation and thirst热毒目赤伴发热、便秘、口渴

Why依据 Large Intestine he-sea point; Jiayi: 'red painful eyes… LI11 governs it'大肠经合穴；《甲乙经》「目赤痛……曲池主之」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for general interior heat than LI4, which acts on head and face pain; not needed in a mild wind-heat case.比合谷更偏全身里热，合谷偏头面止痛；单纯风热轻证不必加。

GV14 Dàzhuī大椎

Best for最适合 Blazing heat-toxin with high fever and head and neck pain热毒炽盛伴高热、头项痛

Why依据 Meeting point of the Governor vessel and all six yang channels; pricked to bleed to drain heat督脉与手足三阳经交会，「诸阳之会」，刺络泄热

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for bringing down fever and draining the yang channels than LI11, which leans to Yangming; prick and cup, or needle obliquely upward 0.5–1 cun, never deep (spinal canal).比曲池更偏退热、泄阳经之热，曲池偏阳明；刺络拔罐或向上斜刺 0.5–1 寸，不可深刺（椎管）。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

The commonest causes of a red eye are conjunctivitis (mostly viral, often one eye then the other, with watery discharge, a swollen node in front of the ear, highly contagious and usually gone in one to three weeks; bacterial with thick yellow discharge; allergic with itching above all), dry eye, blepharitis and subconjunctival haemorrhage (a bright red patch that is painless, leaves vision intact and clears within about two weeks). Signs of serious eye disease are real pain, light sensitivity, reduced vision, a cloudy cornea or white spot, an abnormal pupil, and a ring of redness around the cornea (ciliary flush), which point to acute angle-closure glaucoma, keratitis, uveitis or scleritis and need slit-lamp and eye-pressure examination. A red painful eye in a contact-lens wearer is treated as corneal infection until proved otherwise. TCM patterns such as wind-heat, liver fire, heat-toxin and yin deficiency are clusters of symptoms used to choose treatment; they do not map to particular eye diseases and cannot replace an eye examination. Modern research on acupuncture for red painful eyes is sparse, and its role is mainly symptom relief.红眼最常见的原因是结膜炎（病毒性最多，常先一眼后两眼、水样分泌物、耳前淋巴结肿，传染性强，一般一到三周自愈；细菌性分泌物黄稠；过敏性以痒为主）、干眼、睑缘炎和结膜下出血（一片鲜红、不痛、不影响视力，一般两周内自行吸收）。提示严重眼病的是：明显眼痛、怕光、视力下降、角膜混浊或白点、瞳孔异常、角膜周围一圈充血（睫状充血），对应急性闭角型青光眼、角膜炎、葡萄膜炎、巩膜炎等，需要裂隙灯和眼压检查。戴隐形眼镜的人红眼疼痛要先当角膜感染处理。中医的风热、肝火、热毒、阴虚是症状组合，用于选择治法，不能对应某一种眼病，也不能代替眼科检查；针灸对目赤肿痛的现代研究很少，主要用于缓解症状。
