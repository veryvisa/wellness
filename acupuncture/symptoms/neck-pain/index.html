Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹）

The textbook treats neck pain in two sections. Acute wry neck (luozhen), waking unable to turn the head, is an acute sinew strain that often eases on the spot when distal points are needled while the patient moves the neck. Recurrent neck pain spreading to the shoulder, arm or fingers (xiangbi, cervical spondylosis) involves the spine and nerve roots and is sorted into wind-cold, strain with blood stasis, and liver–kidney deficiency. The two have different core points: decide which one it is, then whether pain is on bending forward and back (Foot Taiyang) or on turning (Hand Taiyang, Foot Shaoyang), and only then choose points and technique.颈痛在教材里分两节：一觉醒来转不了头的「落枕」，是急性筋伤，远端穴配合活动颈部往往当场见效；反复发作、牵连肩臂手麻的「项痹」（颈椎病），病在颈椎与神经根，要分风寒、劳伤血瘀、肝肾亏虚。两者主穴不同，先分清是哪一种，再看是低头仰头痛（足太阳）还是左右转头痛（手太阳、足少阳），最后才是选穴和手法。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Neck pain after a car crash, fall or diving injury, or tenderness over the midline bones: keep the neck still and go to emergency for imaging; do not turn or manipulate the neck车祸、跌倒、跳水等外伤后颈痛，或颈部中线骨头压痛：先固定颈部、急诊拍片，不要转动或推拿颈部
  • Clumsy hands (buttons and handwriting getting harder), walking as if on cotton, stiff or weak legs, bladder or bowel change: possible spinal cord compression (cervical myelopathy); see a doctor promptly and avoid neck manipulation双手笨拙（扣扣子、写字变难）、走路像踩棉花、腿发紧发软、大小便异常：可能是脊髓受压（脊髓型颈椎病），尽快看医生，禁止颈部推拿扳动
  • Sudden severe neck pain with headache plus vertigo, double vision, slurred speech, one-sided face or limb numbness or weakness, or unequal pupils, especially after neck manipulation, injury or a violent head turn: call 911 for possible carotid or vertebral artery dissection or stroke突发剧烈颈痛伴头痛，并有眩晕、复视、口齿不清、一侧面部或肢体麻木无力、瞳孔一大一小，尤其在颈部推拿、外伤或剧烈转头之后：拨 911，警惕颈动脉或椎动脉夹层、卒中
  • Fever, severe headache, a stiff neck that will not bend forward, light sensitivity, rash or confusion: emergency now to rule out meningitis发热、剧烈头痛、颈部发硬不能低头、畏光、皮疹或意识改变：立即急诊，排除脑膜炎
  • Neck, jaw or left shoulder pain with chest tightness, breathlessness, sweating or nausea, worse on exertion: call 911, this can be referred pain from angina or a heart attack颈部、下颌或左肩痛同时有胸闷、气短、出汗、恶心，活动后加重：拨 911，可能是心绞痛或心肌梗死的牵涉痛
  • Arm pain with progressive weakness or muscle wasting, a history of cancer, unexplained weight loss, pain at rest at night or fever; a wry neck not improving after a week: see a doctor soon手臂放射痛伴进行性无力或肌肉萎缩、癌症史、不明原因消瘦、夜间静息痛或发热；落枕一周以上不见好转：尽快就医检查

「項痛不可俛仰，刺足太陽。不可以顧，刺手太陽也」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.26: neck pain that stops the patient bending the head forward or back is needled on the Foot Taiyang; inability to look round is needled on the Hand Taiyang. This is the origin of choosing the channel by the restricted movement: flexion–extension pain takes the BL10–BL60 line, pain on turning takes SI3, and pulling along the side of the neck on turning adds the Foot Shaoyang points GB20 and GB39.《灵枢·杂病》：颈痛以致不能低头仰头的，刺足太阳经；不能回头左右看的，刺手太阳经。这是颈痛按活动受限方向选经的源头：前后屈伸痛取天柱、昆仑一线，左右转头痛取后溪；转头时颈侧牵痛则再加足少阳的风池、悬钟。

「大風頸項痛，刺風府，風府在上椎」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.60: neck pain from strong wind is needled at GV16, which lies above the first vertebra. The ancients treated externally caused neck stiffness at this suboccipital Governor vessel point. The medulla lies straight behind GV16, so today only trained practitioners needle it, slowly and shallowly toward the chin and never upward; beginners are safer with GB20 and BL10.《素问·骨空论》：外感大风而颈项痛，刺风府，风府在第一颈椎之上。说明外感颈项强痛古人取督脉枕下穴。风府（GV16）深部正对延髓，现代只许受训者向下颌方向缓慢浅刺，不可向上深刺；初学者用风池、天柱更安全。

Acute wry neck落枕

Wry neck: wind-cold in the collaterals落枕·风寒袭络

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden one-sided neck stiffness and pain on waking or after a draught, with restricted turning or bending and the head often tilted to the sore side; tight tender muscles, perhaps mild chills or headache; thin white coating, floating tight pulse. Lasts days, with no arm pain or numbness.睡醒或吹风受凉后突然一侧颈项僵痛，转头或低头受限，头常歪向患侧；局部肌肉紧张压痛，可伴轻度怕冷、头痛；苔薄白，脉浮紧。病程以天计，没有手臂麻痛。

Principle治法 Relax the sinews, free the collaterals, dispel wind-cold (drain; needle the distal points first while the patient slowly moves the neck, then the local points; local moxa or cupping may be added)舒筋活络，祛风散寒（泻法；先刺远端穴并嘱患者缓慢活动颈部，再取局部，局部可加灸、拔罐）

Main主穴BL10 Tiānzhù天柱SI3 Hòuxī后溪GB39 Xuánzhōng悬钟
Add配穴GB20 Fēngchí风池LI4 Hégǔ合谷

💡 The textbook core for wry neck is Wailaogong (the 'stiff-neck point', an extra point with no standard code on this site), BL10, ashi points, SI3 and GB39. SI3 is the Hand Taiyang shu-stream point and confluent point of the Governor vessel, matching 'inability to look round: needle the Hand Taiyang'; GB39 on the Foot Shaoyang treats pulling along the side of the neck; BL10 and ashi points are local. Turning the needle at distal points while the patient rotates the neck is the usual 'movement needling'. For wind-cold add GB20 to dispel wind and LI4 to release the exterior.教材落枕主穴是外劳宫（落枕穴，经外奇穴，本站无标准代码）、天柱、阿是穴、后溪、悬钟。后溪是手太阳输穴、八脉交会通督脉，正合「不可以顾，刺手太阳」；悬钟在足少阳，治颈侧牵痛；天柱、阿是穴是局部。远端穴边捻转边让患者转头，是落枕常用的「运动针法」。风寒加风池祛风、合谷解表。

Acute wry neck落枕

Wry neck: qi and blood stagnation落枕·气血瘀滞

How it presents辨证要点 Starts after an awkward sleeping position, a high pillow or a twist; stabbing fixed neck pain that resists pressure, sometimes with a palpable taut band; no exterior signs; dark tongue or stasis spots, wiry choppy pulse.睡姿不当、枕头过高或颈部扭挫后发病，颈项刺痛、痛处固定拒按，局部可摸到条索；无明显外感表现；舌暗或有瘀点，脉弦涩。

Principle治法 Move blood, transform stasis, relax the sinews and stop pain (drain; ashi points may be bled and cupped)活血化瘀，舒筋止痛（泻法；局部阿是穴可刺络拔罐）

Main主穴BL10 Tiānzhù天柱SI3 Hòuxī后溪GB39 Xuánzhōng悬钟
Add配穴PC6 Nèiguān内关

💡 Same core as wry neck. For stagnation the textbook adds PC6 and local ashi points: PC6, the Pericardium luo point, moves qi and blood; bleeding and cupping the ashi points removes local stasis directly. Add LI15 if pain spreads to the shoulder and SI11 if it spreads over the shoulder blade.主穴同落枕。教材气血瘀滞配内关和局部阿是穴：内关是心包络穴，行气活血、宽胸；阿是穴刺络拔罐直接祛局部瘀血。痛连肩部加肩髃，连肩胛加天宗。

Cervical spondylosis项痹（颈椎病）

Cervical spondylosis: wind-cold obstruction项痹·风寒痹阻

How it presents辨证要点 Cold, stiff pain in the neck, shoulders and upper back, worse in cold or wet weather and better with warmth; there may be aching, numbness or heaviness of one arm; thin white or greasy coating, wiry tight pulse. Recurs over months or years.颈、肩、上背冷痛僵硬，遇寒、阴雨加重，得温减轻；可有一侧上肢酸麻沉重；苔薄白或白腻，脉弦紧。病程反复，多年发作。

Principle治法 Dispel wind-cold, free the collaterals, stop pain (drain or even method; moxa or warm needle may be added)祛风散寒，通络止痛（泻法或平补平泻，可加灸、温针）

Main主穴GB20 Fēngchí风池BL10 Tiānzhù天柱LI11 Qūchí曲池GB39 Xuánzhōng悬钟
Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门GV14 Dàzhuī大椎

💡 Textbook core for cervical spondylosis: cervical Jiaji (extra points, no code here), BL10, GB20, LI11, GB39 and ashi points. Jiaji, BL10 and GB20 free the local neck channels; LI11, the Hand Yangming he-sea point, frees the arm channels for arm pain and hand numbness; GB39, the influential point of marrow, supports the bones. For wind-cold add BL12 and GV14 to expel wind-cold and rouse Governor-vessel yang.教材项痹主穴：颈夹脊（经外奇穴，本站无代码）、天柱、风池、曲池、悬钟、阿是穴。颈夹脊、天柱、风池疏通颈部局部经气；曲池是手阳明合穴，通上肢经络、治臂痛手麻；悬钟是髓会，补髓壮骨。风寒加风门、大椎祛风散寒、通督振阳。

Cervical spondylosis项痹（颈椎病）

Cervical spondylosis: strain with blood stasis项痹·劳伤血瘀

How it presents辨证要点 Long hours at a desk or phone, or a past neck injury; stabbing fixed neck and shoulder pain, worse at night or after sitting, with arm pain or tingling; dark purple tongue or stasis marks, choppy pulse.长期伏案、低头看手机或有颈部外伤史；颈肩刺痛、痛处固定，夜间或久坐后加重，手臂麻痛；舌暗紫或有瘀斑，脉涩。

Principle治法 Move blood, transform stasis, free the collaterals and stop pain (drain; local bleeding and cupping allowed)活血化瘀，通络止痛（泻法，局部可刺络拔罐）

Main主穴GB20 Fēngchí风池BL10 Tiānzhù天柱LI11 Qūchí曲池GB39 Xuánzhōng悬钟
Add配穴BL17 Géshū膈俞LI4 Hégǔ合谷

💡 Same core as cervical spondylosis. BL17, the influential point of blood, moves stasis; LI4, the Hand Yangming yuan point on the same channel as LI11, moves qi, stops pain and frees the arm. Add TE5 for arm numbness and local hand points for numb fingers.主穴同项痹。膈俞是血会，活血化瘀；合谷是手阳明原穴，与曲池同经，行气止痛、通上肢。上肢麻木再加外关，手指麻加合谷、八邪一类局部穴。

Cervical spondylosis项痹（颈椎病）

Cervical spondylosis: liver and kidney deficiency项痹·肝肾亏虚

How it presents辨证要点 Common in middle and older age: dull aching neck worse with fatigue and better with rest; often dizziness, tinnitus, sore lower back and knees, blurred vision; pale tongue, or red with little coating, deep thin or weak pulse.中老年多见，颈项酸痛隐隐、劳累后加重，休息减轻；常伴头晕、耳鸣、腰膝酸软、视物模糊；舌淡或红少苔，脉沉细或细弱。

Principle治法 Tonify liver and kidney, free the collaterals, stop pain (tonify or even method; moxa allowed)补益肝肾，通络止痛（补法或平补平泻，可灸）

Main主穴GB20 Fēngchí风池BL10 Tiānzhù天柱LI11 Qūchí曲池GB39 Xuánzhōng悬钟
Add配穴BL18 Gānshū肝俞BL23 Shènshū肾俞

💡 Same core as cervical spondylosis; BL18 and BL23, the back-shu points of liver and kidney, tonify them and strengthen sinew and bone, treating the root together with GB39. Add GV20 for dizziness and PC6 for nausea, but dizziness first needs vertebral-artery and posterior-circulation problems ruled out.主穴同项痹；肝俞、肾俞是肝、肾的背俞穴，补肝肾、强筋骨，与悬钟（髓会）同用治本。头晕加百会，恶心加内关；头晕要先排除椎动脉与后循环问题。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

In modern terms acute wry neck is usually mechanical neck pain: muscle spasm or a jammed facet joint from an awkward sleeping position, resolving in one to two weeks, and gentle movement speeds recovery more than immobilisation. Cervical spondylosis is degeneration of discs and facet joints compressing nearby structures, and is classified clinically: neck-type (neck and shoulder pain and stiffness only), radiculopathy (arm pain and numbness in a dermatome — C6 to the thumb, C7 to the middle finger, C8 to the little finger — with a positive Spurling test) and myelopathy (clumsy hands, unsteady gait, brisk reflexes, positive Hoffmann sign; this needs spinal surgical assessment and no manipulation). Hand numbness must also be told apart from carpal tunnel syndrome (thumb to the radial ring finger, worse at night), cubital tunnel syndrome (little and ulnar ring finger) and thoracic outlet syndrome. Whether to image after trauma follows the Canadian C-spine rule. High-velocity neck manipulation can trigger vertebral artery dissection, which is why manual techniques beyond acupuncture need particular caution. Acupuncture has some evidence for chronic non-specific neck pain and fits alongside exercise therapy and workstation changes.落枕在现代医学里多属急性机械性颈痛：睡姿不良导致颈部肌肉痉挛或小关节卡压，一般 1–2 周自愈，保持温和活动比制动恢复更快。颈椎病是颈椎间盘与小关节退变压迫周围结构，临床分几型：颈型（只有颈肩痛僵）、神经根型（手臂放射痛与麻木，按皮节分布，C6 到拇指、C7 到中指、C8 到小指，椎间孔挤压试验阳性）、脊髓型（手笨拙、步态不稳、腱反射亢进、Hoffmann 征阳性，须脊柱外科评估，禁推拿扳动）。手麻还要和腕管综合征、肘管综合征、胸廓出口综合征鉴别：腕管麻在拇指到环指桡侧、夜间加重，肘管麻在小指和环指尺侧。外伤后是否拍片按加拿大颈椎规则判断。颈部快速扳动手法可能诱发椎动脉夹层，这也是针灸以外的手法要特别谨慎的原因。针刺对慢性非特异性颈痛有一定证据，适合与运动治疗、工作姿势调整一起用。
