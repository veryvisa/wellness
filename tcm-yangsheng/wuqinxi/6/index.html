✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚

  • Poor balance or a history of falls: hold the back of a chair with one hand and keep both feet on the floor容易跌倒、平衡不好：一只手扶椅背做，脚不离地
  • Knee pain or a hip or knee replacement: take small steps and land softly, knees pointing the same way as the toes膝盖痛、换过髋或膝关节：步子小，落脚轻，膝盖与脚尖同向
  • Back pain: keep the waist turn small腰痛：转腰幅度小

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Shift your weight onto the right foot and hitch the left hip up so that the left foot lifts off the floor, left knee slightly bent重心移到右脚，左髋向上提，带动左脚离地，左膝微屈
  2. Set the left foot down forward and left with the whole sole, and shift your weight forward; the left arm rotates in and swings forward, fist above the left knee, the right fist swinging behind左脚向左前方落地，全脚掌踏实，重心前移；左臂内旋前摆，拳在左膝前上方，右拳摆到体后
  3. Turn the body left and sit back, bending the right knee; the arms swing forward and back with the waist, the shoulders swaying along身体左转，重心后坐，右腿屈膝；两臂随腰的转动前后摆动，肩跟着晃
  4. Shift forward again with the left arm swinging forward; bring the left foot back and repeat on the right重心再前移，左臂前摆；收回左脚，换右侧做

🌬 Breathe naturally.自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • The hip hitch lifts the leg; the knee does not lift on its own是提髋带腿，不是抬膝盖
  • Land heavily with the whole sole, like a bear's step落脚要沉，全脚掌着地，像熊踩下去
  • The waist and shoulders sway; the head stays steady晃的是腰和肩，头保持稳

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Lifting the knee instead of hitching the hip抬膝盖代替提髋
  • Landing toes first落脚时脚尖先着地
  • Swaying too much and losing balance晃得太大、站不稳

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Hold a chair back with one hand and keep both feet on the floor, doing only the forward and back weight shift and the arm swing.一只手扶椅背，脚不离地，只做重心的前后移动和两臂摆动。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Traditional teaching says Bear Swaying imitates a bear's rolling walk; hitching the hip and stepping down heavily give the bear its steadiness. This is a traditional teaching cue.传统教法说熊晃学熊左右摇晃着走路；提髋落步、沉稳踏地，是熊戏的「稳」。这是传统的教学提示。
