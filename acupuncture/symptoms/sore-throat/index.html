Sore throat咽喉肿痛

With a sore throat check three things first: can the person breathe, swallow saliva, and is the neck swollen? Any problem means the emergency department. Once danger is excluded the textbook has two classes: excess (external wind-heat, lung-stomach heat), treated with LU11, LI4, LU5, ST43 and TE1 to clear heat and reduce swelling; and deficiency (yin deficiency with fire), treated with KI3, KI6 and LU10. Sudden, red, swollen and very painful with a rapid pulse is excess; dry, mildly red, recurrent, worse at night with a red tongue and little coating is deficiency. The phlegm-heat pattern comes from internal medicine and is labelled as such.咽喉肿痛先看三件事：喘不喘得过气、咽不咽得下口水、脖子肿不肿；任何一项不对都先去急诊。排除危险后，教材只分两大类：实证（外感风热、肺胃实热）用少商、合谷、尺泽、陷谷、关冲清热消肿，虚证（阴虚火旺）用太溪、照海、鱼际滋阴降火。分辨的抓手是：红肿剧痛、起病急、脉数的是实；干痛微红、反复、入夜加重、舌红少苔的是虚。痰热一型取自内科辨证，页内注明。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Trouble breathing, noisy breathing in, leaning forward and drooling, unable to swallow saliva, or a muffled voice: call 911 now for possible epiglottitis or deep neck infection; do not press the tongue to look, and do not needle first.呼吸困难、吸气有喘鸣声、坐着前倾流口水、咽不下口水、声音含混像含着东西：立即拨 911，警惕会厌炎或深颈部感染；不要压舌检查，也不要先针刺。
  • Severe one-sided throat pain, difficulty opening the mouth, neck swelling or stiffness, or high fever: emergency department to exclude peritonsillar or deep neck abscess.一侧咽痛特别剧烈、张口困难、颈部肿胀或僵硬、高热：急诊，排除扁桃体周围脓肿或深颈部脓肿。
  • Sudden throat tightness with face or lip swelling, hives or wheeze after food, medicine or a sting: call 911, this may be anaphylaxis.吃药、吃食物或被虫蜇后突然喉头发紧、面唇肿、起风团、喘：立即拨 911，这是过敏性休克的信号。
  • Sore throat with fever in someone immunocompromised, on chemotherapy, or taking medicines that can lower white cells (such as antithyroid drugs): see a doctor the same day.免疫低下、正在化疗、服用可能抑制白细胞的药物（如抗甲状腺药）时咽痛发热：当天就医。
  • Sore throat with tender neck nodes, pus on the tonsils, a scarlet-fever-like rash, or strep contact: get a strep test; confirmed strep needs antibiotics to prevent rheumatic fever.咽痛伴颈前淋巴结肿痛、扁桃体有脓、猩红热样皮疹，或接触过链球菌患者：就医做链球菌检测；确诊后需要抗生素，防风湿热。
  • Hoarseness or sore throat beyond 3 weeks, one enlarged tonsil, a neck lump, trouble swallowing or weight loss: see ENT to exclude a tumour.声音嘶哑或咽痛超过 3 周、一侧扁桃体肿大、颈部肿块、吞咽困难、体重下降：看耳鼻喉科排除肿瘤。

「喉痺不能言，取足陽明；能言，取手陽明」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi, book 12: in throat obstruction, choose the foot Yangming if the patient cannot speak and the hand Yangming if they can. Both Yangming channels reach the neck and throat, which is why the textbook excess set includes LI4 of the hand Yangming and ST43 of the foot Yangming.《针灸甲乙经》卷十二：喉痹说不出话的取足阳明经，还能说话的取手阳明经。手、足阳明经都循行到颈喉部位，所以教材实证主穴里既有手阳明的合谷，也有足阳明的陷谷。

「口熱舌乾，咽腫上氣，嗌乾及痛」——《灵枢·经脉第十》（《黄帝内经》，公版）

Lingshu ch.10, disorders of the foot Shaoyin: hot mouth, dry tongue, swollen throat, rising qi, dry and painful throat. The Kidney channel runs along the throat to the root of the tongue — the channel basis for KI3 and KI6 in deficiency-type sore throat.《灵枢·经脉》足少阴肾经病候：口中发热、舌干、咽肿、气上逆、咽喉干痛。肾经循喉咙、挟舌本，这是虚证咽痛取太溪、照海的经脉依据。

「咽喉閉塞。少商 風池 照海」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu Dacheng, book 7, under the confluent point KI6, Yang's cases: for a blocked throat use LU11, GB20 and KI6. LU11 drains excess heat, GB20 expels wind, and KI6 is the confluent point of the Yin Qiao that benefits the throat. Today a "blocked" throat is an airway emergency first.《针灸大成》卷七八脉交会穴「照海」条下的杨氏治症：咽喉闭塞取少商、风池、照海。少商泻实热，风池散风，照海是通阴跷、利咽喉的八脉交会穴。「闭塞」在今天首先是气道急症，要先送急诊。

📋 Pattern by pattern逐个证型

Excess实证

External wind-heat外感风热

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden red, swollen, painful throat, worse on swallowing; fever with slight aversion to wind, headache, cough, yellow sputum; red tongue tip and edges, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse.起病急，咽部红肿疼痛、吞咽时明显，发热微恶风，头痛，咳嗽，痰黄；舌边尖红、苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, clear heat, reduce swelling and benefit the throat (drain; prick LU11 and TE1 to bleed)疏风清热，消肿利咽（泻法；少商、关冲点刺出血）

Main主穴LU11 Shàoshāng少商LI4 Hégǔ合谷LU5 Chǐzé尺泽ST43 Xiàngǔ陷谷TE1 Guānchōng关冲
Add配穴GB20 Fēngchí风池TE5 Wàiguān外关

💡 Main set: LU11, the Lung jing-well point, is pricked to drain lung heat and is the key point for acute sore throat; LI4, the Large Intestine yuan point, belongs to the channel paired with the lung and vents Yangming heat; LU5, the Lung he-sea and child point, drains the child in excess; ST43, the Stomach shu-stream point, drains foot-Yangming heat; TE1, the Triple Energizer well point, is pricked to clear its fire. For wind-heat add GB20 to expel wind and TE5, which opens the Yang Wei, to release the exterior. LI4 is forbidden in pregnancy.主穴：少商是肺经井穴，点刺出血泄肺热，是急性咽痛的要穴；合谷是大肠经原穴，与肺经相表里，疏泄阳明郁热；尺泽是肺经合穴、子穴，「实则泻其子」；陷谷是胃经输穴，泻足阳明之热；关冲是三焦经井穴，点刺清三焦火。风热加风池祛风、外关（通阳维）解表。合谷孕妇禁针。

Excess实证

Lung-stomach excess heat肺胃实热

How it presents辨证要点 Severe red swelling and pain with difficulty swallowing, swollen tonsils that may show pus, high fever, thirst, foul breath, constipation, dark urine; red tongue, yellow coating, surging rapid pulse.咽部红肿剧痛、吞咽困难，喉核红肿甚至有脓点，高热，口渴喜饮，口臭，大便秘结，小便黄；舌红苔黄，脉洪数。

Principle治法 Drain lung and stomach heat, reduce swelling and stop pain (drain; LU11 and LI1 may be pricked)清泻肺胃，消肿止痛（泻法；少商、商阳可点刺出血）

Main主穴LU11 Shàoshāng少商LI4 Hégǔ合谷LU5 Chǐzé尺泽ST43 Xiàngǔ陷谷TE1 Guānchōng关冲
Add配穴ST44 Nèitíng内庭LU10 Yújì鱼际LI1 Shāngyáng商阳

💡 ST44 and LU10 are the ying-spring points of the Stomach and Lung; "ying points govern fever", so one clears stomach fire and the other lung heat. LI1, the Large Intestine well point, may be pricked together with LU11. Pus on the tonsils or high fever needs medical assessment for strep and abscess first; needling only helps the pain.内庭是胃经荥穴、鱼际是肺经荥穴，「荥主身热」，一清胃火一清肺热，正对「肺胃」两字；商阳是大肠经井穴，可与少商同时点刺。扁桃体有脓、高热须先就医做链球菌和脓肿评估，针刺只是辅助止痛。

Excess实证

Phlegm-heat in the throat (added from internal medicine)痰热壅咽（内科辨证补充）

How it presents辨证要点 Red swollen painful throat with sticky phlegm that is hard to clear, cough, chest fullness, thick yellow sputum, sticky bitter mouth; red tongue, greasy yellow coating, slippery rapid pulse — the opposite of the dry pain, scant sputum and little coating of yin deficiency.咽痛红肿，喉中黏痰难咯，咳嗽胸闷，痰黄稠，口黏口苦；舌红苔黄腻，脉滑数。与阴虚的干痛少痰、舌红少苔相反。

Principle治法 Clear heat, transform phlegm, benefit the throat and reduce swelling (drain)清热化痰，利咽消肿（泻法）

Main主穴LU11 Shàoshāng少商LI4 Hégǔ合谷LU5 Chǐzé尺泽ST43 Xiàngǔ陷谷TE1 Guānchōng关冲
Add配穴ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆LI11 Qūchí曲池

💡 The textbook does not list this pattern separately; it is added from internal medicine. ST40, the Stomach luo point, is the key phlegm point and the Stomach channel runs along the throat; LI11, the Large Intestine he-sea, clears Yangming heat. LU5 in the main set remains the core for lung phlegm-heat.教材咽喉肿痛没有单列此型，这里按内科痰热补充：丰隆是胃经络穴、化痰要穴，胃经循喉咙；曲池是大肠经合穴，清阳明热。清痰热的核心仍是主穴里的尺泽。

Deficiency虚证

Yin deficiency with fire阴虚火旺

How it presents辨证要点 Dry sore throat, slightly red with little swelling, or a foreign-body feeling; recurrent and worse at night; hot palms and soles, afternoon flushed cheeks, hoarseness, dry mouth without much thirst; red tongue, little coating, thin rapid pulse.咽喉干痛、微红不甚肿，或有异物感，反复发作，入夜加重；手足心热，午后颧红，声音嘶哑，口干不欲多饮；舌红少苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin, bring down fire, moisten the throat (tonify or even method; no moxa)滋阴降火，养阴利咽（补法或平补平泻；不灸）

Main主穴KI3 Tàixī太溪KI6 Zhàohǎi照海LU10 Yújì鱼际
Add配穴LU7 Lièquē列缺KI7 Fùliū复溜SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交

💡 KI3, the Kidney yuan point, nourishes kidney yin to control deficiency fire; KI6 opens the Yin Qiao, and both the Kidney channel and the Yin Qiao rise to the throat; LU10, the Lung ying (fire) point, clears deficiency heat in the lung. LU7, which opens the Ren, pairs with KI6 — the confluent pair for the lung system, throat and diaphragm. For night fever add KI7 and SP6 to nourish yin. SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy.太溪是肾经原穴，滋肾阴以制虚火；照海通阴跷脉，肾经与阴跷都上循咽喉；鱼际是肺经荥火穴，清肺中虚热利咽。列缺通任脉，与照海是一对八脉交会配穴，合于肺系、咽喉、胸膈；入夜发热加复溜、三阴交养阴。三阴交孕妇禁针。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Most acute sore throats are viral and settle in about a week. Strep causes roughly one in ten adult and two to three in ten childhood cases; fever, tender front neck nodes, tonsil exudate and no cough raise the odds, and a rapid test or culture confirms it before antibiotics. In a teenager with extreme fatigue, swollen nodes at the back of the neck and a large spleen, think of infectious mononucleosis and avoid contact sport until recovered. Chronic discomfort often comes from acid reflux, postnasal drip, voice overuse, dryness or smoking. Peritonsillar abscess and epiglottitis are emergencies. Acupuncture trials for sore throat are small and uneven, so it is only an adjunct for pain; TCM patterns describe symptom groups and cannot separate viral from bacterial infection.急性咽痛多数是病毒感染，一周左右自愈。链球菌性咽炎约占成人急性咽痛的一成、儿童的两三成，发热、颈前淋巴结肿痛、扁桃体渗出、没有咳嗽提示可能性较高，要靠快速检测或培养确认，确诊后用抗生素。青少年咽痛伴极度疲劳、颈后淋巴结和脾大要想到传染性单核细胞增多症，恢复前避免撞击腹部的运动。慢性咽部不适常见于胃酸反流、过敏性鼻炎后滴漏、用声过度、干燥与吸烟。扁桃体周围脓肿、会厌炎是急症。针灸对咽痛的研究规模小、质量参差，只宜作止痛的辅助；中医证型描述的是症状组合，不能区分病毒和细菌。
