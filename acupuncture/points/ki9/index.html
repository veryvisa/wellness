On the medial lower leg, 5 cun directly above KI3, between soleus and the calcaneal tendon.小腿内侧，太溪直上 5 寸，比目鱼肌与跟腱之间。

How is KI9 needled?筑宾（KI9）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of KI9?筑宾（KI9）是哪类特定穴？

Xi-cleft point of the Yin Wei vessel阴维脉郄穴

Main safety point at KI9?筑宾（KI9）最要注意的安全问题？

The muscle is thick and perpendicular insertion is safe; do not advance deep and anteromedially toward the posterior tibial bundle, and withdraw for electric tingling.肌肉厚，直刺无大碍；不向深部前内侧推进（胫后血管神经束），有电麻感即退针。
