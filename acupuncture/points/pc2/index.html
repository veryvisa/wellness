📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the upper arm abducted about 90 degrees, palm up and shoulder relaxed.坐位或仰卧，上臂外展约 90°，掌心向上，肩部放松。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Divide the 9-cun span from the anterior axillary fold to the elbow crease; PC2 is 2 cun below the fold. Measure along the anterior midline of the upper arm, not diagonally over the skin.从腋前纹头至肘横纹按上臂 9 寸折量，天泉在腋前纹头下 2 寸；尺量时沿上臂前面中线，不沿皮肤斜量。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    From the anterior axillary fold, measure 2 cun distally toward the elbow and confirm the point is over the biceps region, not in the axilla or elbow crease.确定腋前纹头后沿上臂前面向肘方向量 2 寸，再确认穴位落在肱二头肌区域，而非腋窝或肘窝。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With gentle resisted elbow flexion, identify the interval between the long and short heads of biceps brachii; PC2 lies between them. The cephalic vein and cutaneous nerves may be superficial, with brachialis, brachial artery and median nerve deeper; palpate superficially and do not probe medially.让患者轻度屈肘抗阻，辨出肱二头肌长头与短头形成的肌腹间沟；天泉在两头之间。浅层可见头静脉及皮神经，深面近肱肌、肱动脉和正中神经；只作表浅触诊，不向内侧深探。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate across the muscle belly to find the shallow interval between the heads and cross-check it against the 2-cun measurement. A strong pulse or radiating numbness is not a locating target.以指腹横向轻触肌腹，找到两头间较浅的沟隙，并与腋前纹下 2 寸的位置互相核对；明显搏动或放射麻感不是取穴目标。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Measuring from the axillary apex instead of the anterior fold, or leaving the arm adducted, obscures the starting landmark and can shift the point proximomedially.从腋窝顶点而非腋前纹头起量，或上臂未外展：起点和肌肉轮廓不清，容易使穴位偏上、偏内。
  • Placing the point medially on biceps and probing deeply can approach the brachial artery and median nerve. Locate the shallow interval; stop and reassess if a pulse or electric paresthesia is felt.将穴误取到肱二头肌内侧并向深部探刺：可能接近肱动脉、正中神经；按肌间沟浅触定位，出现搏动或电击样麻感即停止并重新评估。
