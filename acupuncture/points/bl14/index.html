📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit upright with the head slightly forward and shoulders relaxed, or lie prone with the back muscles relaxed; avoid trunk rotation or hyperextension that shifts vertebral and rib landmarks.正坐位，头微前倾、含胸沉肩，或俯卧位使背部肌肉放松；切忌躯干扭转或过度后仰导致椎体与肋骨关系错位。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: below the T4 spine.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），厥阴俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 4 胸椎棘突下定。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate the spinous process of C7 (most prominent and mobile on neck flexion), count down to the spinous process of T4, and measure 1.5 cun lateral below its lower border; cross-check vertically against the medial end of the scapular spine (near T3) and the inferior angle of the scapular spine/scapula (inferior angle is near T7).先摸第 7 颈椎棘突（大椎，低头时转动最明显），向下数至第 4 胸椎棘突，在其下缘旁开 1.5 寸处定穴；亦可参考肩胛冈内侧端约平第 3 胸椎棘突、肩胛骨下角约平第 7 胸椎棘突作上下校对。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure 1.5 cun laterally from the posterior midline below the T4 spinous process using the patient's index and middle fingers together (about 1.5 cun) or proportional cun; in clinical work and exams, confirm against the spinous process and muscle planes rather than a fixed centimeter rule.以患者本人食指、中指并拢（约 1.5 寸）自后正中线第 4 胸椎棘突下向外平量，或按同身寸 1.5 寸定取；临床与备考仍以骨性棘突与肌层触诊核准为准，瘦弱者或体型偏大者不可机械套用固定厘米数。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located 1.5 cun lateral to the lower border of the T4 spinous process, overlying posterior cutaneous branches of thoracic spinal nerves and dorsal branch vessels superficially, trapezius, rhomboids or erector spinae, then intercostal muscles, intercostal neurovascular bundles in the costal groove, and parietal pleura and lung deeply.   5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently with the fingertip 1.5 cun lateral to the lower border of the T4 spinous process; mild tenderness, a taut band, or muscle tension is often felt over the erector spinae or adjacent paraspinal tissue. ⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting to T3 (BL13 Feishu) or T5 (BL15 Xinshu): shifting one vertebral level up or down alters the target between lung, pericardium, and heart regulation; accounting for the downward-posterior slope of thoracic spinous processes prevents level errors.误取第 3 胸椎棘突下旁开 1.5 寸（肺俞 BL13）或第 5 胸椎棘突下旁开 1.5 寸（心俞 BL15）：上下错位一个椎节会导致主治偏离心包与胸中气机调理，临床核对棘突斜度（胸椎棘突向后下方倾斜）尤为关键。
  • Needling deeply perpendicular or angling laterally away from the spine: advancing toward the intercostal space or deep thoracic wall risks injuring the intercostal neurovascular bundle or puncturing pleura and lung to cause pneumothorax; use only oblique insertion toward the spine for 0.5–0.8 cun.向侧方或深部直刺、向外展方向进针：针尖背离脊柱并向肋间隙或胸壁深层推进，极易伤及肋间神经血管束或刺破胸膜与肺引起气胸；必须严格向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸。

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

Useful for palpitations with chest tightness, shortness of breath, or emotional stress and thoracic qi stagnation, often paired with PC6, BL15, or CV17; if palpitations are sudden and severe, accompanied by chest pain, or hemodynamically unstable, seek emergency care immediately rather than relying on acupuncture.心悸伴胸闷、气短或情志不遂、胸中气滞时可选用，常配内关、心俞或膻中；若突发剧烈心悸、胸痛或血流动力学不稳，须立即就医，不选单靠针灸延误急救。

Low mood and anxiety郁证（焦虑抑郁）

Consider when anxiety or low mood is accompanied by chest oppression, frequent sighing, restlessness, or insomnia linked to pericardial qi constraint, often with PC6, HT7, or LR3; refer for professional psychiatric evaluation first in severe depression, self-harm risk, or acute crisis.情志抑郁、胸闷善太息、心烦不寐而偏于心包气郁或胸中气机不畅时可参用，常配内关、神门或太冲；重度抑郁、自伤风险或急性精神危机须先转介专业医疗评估。

Insomnia不寐（失眠） ⚖️

Helpful when insomnia with dream-disturbed sleep or easy waking is accompanied by chest vexation, restlessness, or heart-pericardium disharmony, often combined with BL15 and HT7; evaluate underlying causes such as sleep apnea or major mood disorder rather than relying on BL14 alone.因心胸烦闷、情志不宁或心包与心气失调导致入睡困难、多梦易醒时可配合心俞、神门调理；若失眠长期严重或伴呼吸暂停、重度抑郁，需先评估原发病因，不单靠此穴。

Where is BL14 Juéyīnshū?厥阴俞（BL14）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of T4, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 4 胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL14 needled?厥阴俞（BL14）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of BL14?厥阴俞（BL14）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Pericardium心包之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL14?厥阴俞（BL14）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only oblique insertion toward the spine, never a deep perpendicular or lateral direction.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向的斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
