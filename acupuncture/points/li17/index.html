📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or seated, head turned slightly to the opposite side so sternocleidomastoid stands out.仰卧或坐位，头稍转向对侧，使胸锁乳突肌显出。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    On the side of the neck, level with the cricoid cartilage (the first firm ring below the Adam's apple), at the posterior border of sternocleidomastoid; directly below LI18.颈外侧，横平环状软骨（喉结下方第一个硬环），胸锁乳突肌后缘；在扶突正下方。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Deep to the posterior border of sternocleidomastoid is the interscalene gap: the brachial plexus emerges here, the phrenic nerve lies on scalenus anterior, the carotid sheath is anteromedial, the external jugular vein crosses the muscle, and the cervical pleura and lung apex are deep below. Textbook depth 0.5–0.8 cun perpendicular, never deep anteromedially or downward, and no forceful manipulation.胸锁乳突肌后缘深面就是斜角肌间隙：臂丛从这里穿出，膈神经贴在前斜角肌表面，前内侧是颈动脉鞘，颈外静脉斜跨肌表面，下方深处是胸膜顶与肺尖。教材直刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不向前内侧或向下深刺，不做强烈提插。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the head turned, follow the back edge of sternocleidomastoid down to the cricoid level; avoid the visible external jugular vein and any pulse.转头时沿胸锁乳突肌后缘向下摸到环状软骨水平；避开可见的颈外静脉与任何搏动。

Where is LI17 Tiāndǐng?天鼎（LI17）在哪里？

On the anterolateral neck, level with the cricoid cartilage, just posterior to the border of sternocleidomastoid; directly inferior to LI18.颈前外侧，平环状软骨，胸锁乳突肌后缘紧后方；位于扶突（LI18）正下方。

How is LI17 needled?天鼎（LI17）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at LI17?天鼎（LI17）最要注意的安全问题？

The carotid sheath lies anteromedially; the cervical plexus and phrenic nerve are deeper, and deep inferior insertion can approach the lung apex and cause pneumothorax. Avoid pulsation and strictly control direction and depth.前内侧邻颈动脉鞘；深部还有颈丛与膈神经，向下过深可接近肺尖并造成气胸。避开搏动，严格控制方向和深度。
