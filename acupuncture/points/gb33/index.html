Knee pain膝痛 ⚖️

Consider GB33 when tenderness is localized posterosuperior to the lateral femoral epicondyle, between the biceps femoris tendon and iliotibial tract, without significant effusion, locking, or neurological symptoms. Tenderness around the fibular head requires assessment of the common fibular nerve. Anterior patellar or joint-line pain should be differentiated from ST35, the medial and lateral Xiyan points, and intra-articular pathology.膝外侧股骨外上髁后上方、股二头肌腱与髂胫束之间局部压痛，且无明显关节积液、卡锁或神经症状时，可考虑膝阳关；若压痛集中在腓骨头周围，优先鉴别腓总神经；若在膝前髌下或关节线，则分别与犊鼻、内外膝眼及膝关节病变鉴别。

Sciatica坐骨神经痛 ⚖️

Neuropathic pain radiating from the buttock or posterior thigh into the leg must be distinguished from localized tendon pain around GB33. Dorsal foot numbness, ankle dorsiflexion weakness, or foot drop requires assessment for common fibular nerve injury or lumbosacral radiculopathy. Radiating nerve pain must not be treated as ordinary lateral knee pain.臀部或大腿后侧放射至小腿的神经性疼痛，应先与膝阳关所在区域的局部筋腱痛区分。出现足背麻木、踝背伸无力或足下垂时，优先排除腓总神经损伤或腰骶神经根病变，不应将放射性神经痛当作普通膝外侧痛。

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Iliotibial band syndrome髂胫束综合征

Pain commonly occurs over the lateral femoral epicondyle and worsens with running, downhill activity, or repetitive knee flexion and extension. Local tenderness is common. The symptomatic region is close to GB33, but pain is often centered over the lateral epicondyle rather than precisely in the interval between the biceps femoris tendon and iliotibial tract. Differentiate using running load, knee movement, and local examination. Tenderness alone does not establish a channel-related diagnosis. Persistent pain, swelling, or reduced function warrants sports medicine or orthopedic assessment.常表现为膝外侧股骨外上髁附近疼痛，跑步、下坡或反复屈伸膝关节时加重，常有局部压痛。与膝阳关所在区域接近，但疼痛常集中在股骨外上髁附近，不一定位于股二头肌腱与髂胫束之间的穴位间隙。需结合跑步负荷、膝关节活动和局部触诊鉴别。不能仅凭压痛点判定为经筋病；持续疼痛、肿胀或运动能力下降应接受运动医学或骨科评估。

Distal biceps femoris tendinopathy or tendon injury股二头肌远端肌腱病或肌腱损伤

Pain is usually located posterolaterally near the distal biceps femoris tendon insertion and may worsen with resisted knee flexion or external rotation of the lower leg. Because the tendon lies close to GB33, differentiate using active and resisted knee flexion, tendon palpation, and the injury history. Acute sports injuries with a pop, substantial swelling, bruising, or inability to bear weight require assessment for tendon rupture and other structural damage before any needling is considered.疼痛多位于膝后外侧、股二头肌腱附着区，抵抗屈膝或小腿外旋时可能加重。与膝阳关附近的股二头肌腱关系密切，应通过主动与抗阻屈膝、肌腱触诊以及受伤经过鉴别。急性运动损伤伴弹响、明显肿胀、瘀斑或无法负重时，先排除肌腱撕裂及其他结构损伤，不应直接在疼痛处深刺。

Common fibular nerve entrapment or injury腓总神经卡压或损伤

The common fibular nerve is relatively superficial around the fibular head and neck and may be injured by trauma, prolonged compression, tight braces, or surgery. Typical findings include numbness or tingling over the lateral leg and dorsum of the foot. More severe injury may cause weakness of ankle dorsiflexion and eversion or foot drop. Unlike isolated lateral knee tendon pain, neuropathy commonly produces sensory changes, altered muscle strength, or radiating symptoms on percussion. Suspected nerve entrapment requires neurological assessment before needling near the nerve.腓总神经在腓骨头及腓骨颈附近较表浅，可能因外伤、长期压迫、紧绷支具或手术等受损。典型表现包括小腿外侧及足背麻木、刺痛，严重时踝背伸和足外翻无力，甚至足下垂。与单纯膝外侧肌腱痛不同，神经病变常有感觉异常、肌力变化或叩击诱发放射感。怀疑神经卡压时先做神经系统检查，避免在神经走行附近深刺。

Lateral meniscal injury外侧半月板损伤

This commonly follows twisting, sudden stopping, or loaded knee flexion and may cause joint-line pain, swelling, clicking, locking, or difficulty squatting. Pain can spread across the lateral knee, but tenderness is generally closer to the joint line than the posterosuperior lateral femoral epicondyle location of GB33. True locking, substantial effusion, or inability to fully extend the knee requires orthopedic assessment and imaging when indicated.常见于膝关节扭转、急停或负重屈膝后，表现为关节线附近疼痛、肿胀、弹响、卡锁或深蹲困难。疼痛可能向膝外侧扩散，但压痛位置通常更靠近关节间隙，而非膝阳关的股骨外上髁后上方。出现关节卡锁、明显积液或不能完全伸膝，应优先进行骨科评估，必要时影像检查。

Lateral collateral ligament injury膝关节外侧副韧带损伤

This commonly follows a varus force or direct impact. Pain occurs along the lateral collateral ligament between the lateral femoral epicondyle and fibular head and may be accompanied by tenderness and joint instability. Differentiate it from localized pain in the GB33 tendon interval. Marked instability, difficulty bearing weight, or rapid swelling after trauma requires assessment of the ligament, bone, and neurovascular status before any deep needling.常由膝内翻暴力或直接撞击引起，疼痛位于股骨外上髁至腓骨头之间的外侧副韧带走行区，可能伴局部压痛和关节不稳。与膝阳关局部肌腱间隙痛应鉴别。若有外伤后明显不稳、负重困难或快速肿胀，应先检查韧带、骨性结构及神经血管状态，不可在急性损伤区域直接深刺。

Knee osteoarthritis膝关节骨关节炎

Symptoms may include activity-related pain, brief stiffness after rest or in the morning, restricted movement, and recurrent swelling. Pain may involve the medial compartment, lateral compartment, or patellofemoral joint, so not all knee pain is an indication for GB33. Assessment should consider age, history, joint examination, and imaging when appropriate. Sudden redness, warmth, severe swelling, fever, or inability to bear weight requires evaluation for septic arthritis, crystal arthritis, or another acute cause.可表现为活动后疼痛、晨起或久坐后的短暂僵硬、关节活动受限及反复肿胀。疼痛可涉及内侧、外侧或髌股关节，不应将所有膝痛均定位为膝阳关适应证。需结合年龄、病史、关节检查和必要的影像检查判断；若出现突然红肿热痛、发热或不能负重，应先排除感染性关节炎、晶体性关节炎或其他急性病因。
