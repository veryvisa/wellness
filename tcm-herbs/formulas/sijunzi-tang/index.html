Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Four Gentlemen Decoction四君子汤

Original text原文

「治一切陽虛氣弱，脾衰肺損，飲食少思，体瘦面黃，皮聚毛落，脈来細软」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

For all yang and qi weakness, a failing spleen and damaged lungs: poor appetite, thinness and a sallow face, shrunken skin and falling hair, a thin soft pulse.治一切阳气虚弱、脾衰肺损：食欲差、消瘦面黄、皮肤皱缩毛发脱落、脉细软。

「人參甘溫，大補元氣，爲君；白术苦溫，燥脾補氣，爲臣」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Wang Ang's analysis: ginseng, sweet and warm, greatly supplements source qi as monarch; Bai Zhu, bitter and warm, dries the spleen and supplements qi as minister.汪昂的方解：人参甘温大补元气为君，白术苦温燥脾补气为臣。

Spleen-stomach qi deficiency: pale sallow face, weak voice, shortness of breath and fatigue, poor appetite and loose stools, pale tongue with white coating, a weak pulse.脾胃气虚证：面色萎白，语声低微，气短乏力，食少便溏，舌淡苔白，脉虚弱。

How the formula is built方解

All four herbs are mild, neither hot nor drying, hence 'gentlemen'. Ginseng supplements qi; Bai Zhu dries and Poria drains damp, because the spleen likes dryness and dislikes damp, so the tonic does not clog; licorice harmonises. It is the ancestor of qi formulas: add Chen Pi for Yi Gong San, then Ban Xia for Liu Jun Zi Tang, then Mu Xiang and Sha Ren for Xiang Sha Liu Jun Zi Tang; combined with Si Wu Tang it becomes Ba Zhen Tang.四味药都平和，不热不燥，所以称「君子」。人参补气为主，白术健脾燥湿、茯苓渗湿，脾喜燥恶湿，二药让补而不滞；甘草调和。它是补气方的祖方：加陈皮为异功散，再加半夏为六君子汤，再加木香、砂仁为香砂六君子汤，与四物汤合为八珍汤。

Cautions使用注意

Not for yin deficiency with internal heat or for excess patterns; long-standing fatigue, poor appetite with weight loss, or black stools need a doctor first.阴虚内热、实证者不宜；长期乏力、食少消瘦、黑便或体重下降，先就医排查器质性疾病。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Contains ginseng (caution on this site); in pregnancy leave it to a licensed practitioner.含人参（本站按慎用）；孕期由持照中医师判断。

Exam notes考点

Essentials: (1) the base qi formula and why each herb is there; (2) the lineage of Yi Gong San, Liu Jun Zi, Xiang Sha Liu Jun Zi and Shen Ling Bai Zhu San; (3) versus Li Zhong Wan (warming the middle).必考：①补气基础方与组成意义；②异功散、六君子、香砂六君子、参苓白术散的加减脉络；③与理中丸（温中散寒）的区别。
