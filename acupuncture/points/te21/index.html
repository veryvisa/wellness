📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the mouth gently open to move the mandibular condyle forward; relax the head and neck and avoid clenching.坐位或仰卧，轻张口，使下颌髁突前移；头颈放松，避免用力咬紧。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    In the hollow anterior to the supratragic notch and posterior to the mandibular condyle, located with the mouth open. Avoid any pulse anterior to the tragus.在耳屏上切迹前方、下颌髁突后缘的凹陷中，张口时取穴；耳屏前方的动脉搏动须避开。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    With the mouth open, palpate the condyle moving forward and identify the hollow just anterosuperior to the tragus. The auriculotemporal nerve and superficial temporal vessels are superficial; the TMJ capsule, parotid, and facial nerve branches are deeper. Only a trained practitioner should use a shallow approach, avoiding pulses and deep insertion toward the joint or parotid.张口触摸髁突前移，在耳屏前上方寻找凹陷；浅层有耳颞神经及颞浅血管分支，深部邻颞下颌关节囊、腮腺和面神经分支。只由受训人员浅层操作，避开血管搏动，不向关节或腮腺深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Rest a fingertip lightly in front of the tragus while the patient slowly opens and closes the mouth. Locate the hollow behind the moving condyle and confirm its change with jaw movement.手指轻放耳屏前方，患者缓慢张口、闭口时触及髁突移动；在张口形成的髁突后缘凹陷中定位，闭口后复核其动态变化。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Find the supratragic notch, ask the patient to open the mouth, and gently palpate the hollow between the notch and posterior condyle; the hollow becomes shallower when the mouth closes.先找耳屏上切迹，再让患者张口，在切迹前方与髁突后缘之间轻触凹陷；闭口时该凹陷变浅是交叉核对。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Choose TE21 for preauricular condylar-hollow tenderness with chewing or jaw-opening symptoms; postauricular tenderness is closer to TE17, and sudden hearing loss is urgent.耳前髁突后缘凹陷压痛、伴咀嚼或张口相关不适时选耳门；耳后压痛更近翳风，突发听力下降先急诊。

Toothache牙痛 ⚖️

May be an adjacent point for jaw pain with restricted opening and preauricular condylar tenderness; clear dental-source pain, swelling, or fever needs dental care first.牙颌痛兼张口受限、耳前髁突区压痛可作邻近配穴；明确牙源性痛、肿胀或发热应先牙科处理。

Facial palsy面瘫（口眼歪斜） ⚖️

Use only as an adjacent point for local preauricular discomfort after acute neurologic causes are excluded; new facial droop requires urgent stroke assessment.仅在耳前局部不适并已排除急性神经事件时作为邻近穴；新发口眼歪斜应优先急诊排除卒中。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Temporomandibular disorder颞下颌关节紊乱

Pain, clicking, or restricted opening suggests a TMJ or masticatory-muscle problem; locking, trauma, or progressive bite change needs dental or medical assessment.张口疼痛、弹响或受限提示颞下颌关节或咀嚼肌问题；锁闭、外伤或进行性咬合变化需牙科或医疗评估。

External-ear infection or ear disease外耳道感染或耳部疾病

Ear discharge, fever, sudden hearing change, or severe ear pain should not be managed with point pressure instead of otologic diagnosis.耳流脓、发热、突发听力改变或剧烈耳痛不应按穴代替耳科诊断。

Main safety point at TE21?耳门（TE21）最要注意的安全问题？

Near superficial temporal vessels, auriculotemporal nerve, TMJ, and parotid; locate with mouth open, avoid a pulse, and do not needle deeply.邻近颞浅血管、耳颞神经、颞下颌关节和腮腺；张口定位，避开搏动，勿深刺。
