Posture体位 · Side-lying or seated with the lateral foot exposed and relaxed; identify the malleolus, cuboid and lateral border.侧卧或坐位，足外侧朝上并放松；显露外踝、骰骨和足外侧缘。

    Surface landmark体表标志

    Drop vertically from the front of the lateral malleolus to the hollow below the cuboid, behind the 5th metatarsal tuberosity — anteroinferior to BL62, posterior to BL64.从外踝前缘垂直向下，到第 5 跖骨粗隆后方、骰骨下缘的凹陷；在申脉前下方、京骨后方。

    Anatomy解剖结构
