ST13

气户 Qìhù

Qi Door · High risk高危

⚠️ Chest · pneumothorax danger point胸部·气胸危险穴 Only 1–2 cm separate skin from pleura (less in thin people): perpendicular or deep insertion can cause pneumothorax; chest pain, breathlessness or worsening cough after needling needs emergency care.胸壁到胸膜只有 1–2 厘米（瘦人更薄），直刺或深刺可致气胸；针后胸痛、气短、咳嗽加重须立即急诊。

📍 Location定位★

On the chest, at the inferior border of the clavicle, 4 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，锁骨下缘，前正中线旁开 4 寸。

💡 Follow the lower edge of the clavicle to where it meets the nipple line, 4 cun from the midline, right under the bone.沿锁骨下缘找到乳中线（前正中线旁开 4 寸）与之相交处，紧贴锁骨下缘。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse (laterally along the intercostal space)斜刺或平刺（沿肋间隙向外） · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

No perpendicular or deep insertion.不可直刺、深刺。

🛡 Safety安全

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治
