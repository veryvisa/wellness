Common cold感冒

The common cold is the most frequent external illness and mostly clears in about a week. Acupuncture can ease the headache, blocked nose, aches and fever, and pattern diagnosis sorts cold from heat: wind-cold has marked chills and clear mucus, wind-heat has more fever, a sore throat and yellow mucus, summer colds often carry summer-damp, and people who catch colds over and over have a deficiency-type cold. The textbook main points are LU7, LI4, GB20, GV14 and Taiyang, with additions for each pattern. Sort first: a high fever that will not settle, breathlessness or chest pain, or symptoms in someone at high risk from flu or COVID-19 are not ordinary colds.感冒是最常见的外感病，大多一周左右自愈。针灸的价值在于减轻头痛、鼻塞、身痛、发热，并通过辨证分清寒热：风寒怕冷重、流清涕，风热发热重、咽痛、涕黄，夏季多夹暑湿，体质虚弱的人反复感冒属体虚感冒。教材主穴是列缺、合谷、风池、大椎、太阳，各型只在此基础上加配穴。先要分辨：高热不退、气短胸痛，或流感、新冠高风险人群出现症状，都不是普通感冒的处理范围。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Shortness of breath, laboured breathing, chest pain, blue lips or face, confusion or being hard to wake: call 911 now.气短、呼吸费力、胸痛、嘴唇或脸色发青、意识混乱或叫不醒：立即拨 911。
  • Fever above 39°C that will not come down, fever lasting more than 3 days, or fever and worse cough with pus-like sputum after initial improvement (possible pneumonia or sinusitis): see a doctor today.高热 39°C 以上持续不退、发热超过 3 天，或症状好转后又发热、咳嗽加重、咳脓痰（警惕继发肺炎或鼻窦炎）：当天就医。
  • Fever with a stiff neck, severe headache, a rash or drowsiness: emergency department to rule out meningitis.发热伴颈部僵硬、剧烈头痛、皮疹或嗜睡：急诊，排除脑膜炎。
  • Flu-like illness (sudden high fever, marked body aches, extreme tiredness) in someone over 65, pregnant, a baby or young child, immunocompromised, or with chronic heart, lung or kidney disease: contact a doctor within 48 hours, as antivirals work best early.65 岁以上、孕妇、婴幼儿、免疫低下或有心肺肾慢性病的人出现流感样症状（突发高热、明显全身痛、极度乏力）：48 小时内联系医生，抗病毒药越早越有效。
  • A baby under 3 months with a temperature of 38°C or more, or a young child breathing fast, with chest indrawing, refusing feeds or passing much less urine: get medical care immediately.婴儿 3 个月以下发热 38°C 以上，或幼儿呼吸急促、胸壁凹陷、不吃奶、尿明显减少：立即就医。

「風從外入，令人振寒，汗出、頭痛、身重、惡寒。治在風府，調其陰陽，不足則補，有餘則瀉」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.60: when wind enters from outside, the person shivers, sweats, has a headache, feels heavy and dislikes cold; treat at GV16, tonifying deficiency and draining excess. It is almost a portrait of the common cold; GV16, GB20 and GV14 all lie on the nape, the region of the 'wind' points used first for external illness. The medulla lies deep to GV16, so only trained practitioners needle it, slowly and shallowly toward the chin, never deep and upward.《素问·骨空论》：风邪从外侵入，人就寒战、出汗、头痛、身体沉重、怕冷，治疗取风府，按虚实或补或泻。这几乎就是一幅感冒的画像；风府与风池、大椎都在颈项部，「风」字穴是外感病的首选部位。风府深部是延髓，只能由受训者向下颌方向缓慢浅刺，不可向上深刺。

「傷於風者，上先受之；傷於濕者，下先受之」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.29: wind injures the upper body first, damp the lower body first. A cold begins with headache, blocked nose and sore throat — 'the upper body first' — so the main points cluster on the head and neck and the hand Taiyin and Yangming channels; when damp joins in with heaviness, poor appetite and loose stools, CV12 and ST36 are added for the middle burner.《素问·太阴阳明论》：伤于风邪，上部先受病；伤于湿邪，下部先受病。感冒先见头痛、鼻塞、咽痛，正是「上先受之」，所以主穴集中在头项和手太阴、手阳明经；夹湿时身重、纳呆、便溏，才加中脘、足三里调中焦。

「頭項尋列缺，面口合谷收」——高武《针灸聚英》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Four Command Points song in Zhenjiu Juying: head and neck, LU7; face and mouth, LI4. LU7 is the Lung luo point and LI4 the Large Intestine yuan point; the lung and large intestine are paired, so this source–connecting pair frees the lung and releases the exterior — the core of the cold's main set.《针灸聚英》四总穴歌：头项病取列缺，面口病取合谷。列缺是肺经络穴，合谷是大肠经原穴，肺与大肠相表里，两穴原络相配，宣肺解表，是感冒主穴的核心。

External外感

Wind-cold风寒感冒

How it presents辨证要点 Marked chills with mild fever and no sweating; headache and aching limbs; blocked nose with a heavy voice, clear runny nose, itchy throat, thin white sputum; no thirst; thin white coating, floating tight pulse.恶寒重、发热轻，无汗，头痛，肢体酸痛；鼻塞声重，流清涕，喉痒，咳痰稀白；口不渴；苔薄白，脉浮紧。

Principle治法 Expel wind and cold, release the exterior, free the lung (drain; GV14, BL12 and BL13 may be moxa'd or cupped)祛风散寒，解表宣肺（泻法，大椎、风门、肺俞可灸或拔罐）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷GB20 Fēngchí风池GV14 Dàzhuī大椎
Add配穴BL12 Fēngmén风门BL13 Fèishū肺俞

💡 Main set: LU7 and LI4 as source and connecting points release the exterior, GB20 expels wind and eases headache, GV14 rouses the yang and brings down fever (moxa for wind-cold, drain or bleed for wind-heat); for wind-cold add BL12, the gate of wind, and BL13 for lung qi, with moxa or cupping on the back. Both are on the upper back, so needle obliquely to avoid pneumothorax.主穴：列缺、合谷原络相配解表，风池祛风止头痛，大椎通督振阳、退热（风寒用灸，风热用泻或刺络）；风寒加风门（风邪出入之门）、肺俞（调肺气），背部可加灸或拔罐。两穴在上背，斜刺防气胸。

External外感

Wind-heat风热感冒

How it presents辨证要点 Prominent fever, slight aversion to wind, sweating; distending headache, red swollen sore throat, blocked nose with thick yellow mucus, thick yellow sputum; thirst; red tongue tip and edges, thin yellow coating, floating rapid pulse.发热重、微恶风，有汗；头胀痛，咽喉红肿疼痛，鼻塞流黄浊涕，咳痰黄稠；口渴；舌尖边红、苔薄黄，脉浮数。

Principle治法 Disperse wind-heat, clear the lung (drain; GV14 may be pricked and cupped, LU11 pricked to bleed)疏散风热，清利肺气（泻法，大椎可刺络拔罐，少商点刺出血）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷GB20 Fēngchí风池GV14 Dàzhuī大椎
Add配穴LI11 Qūchí曲池LU5 Chǐzé尺泽LU11 Shàoshāng少商

💡 LI11, the Large Intestine he-sea point, is the key heat-clearing point; LU5, the Lung he-sea and child point, drains lung heat ('drain the child in excess'); for a swollen sore throat prick LU11 to bleed. No moxa in heat patterns.曲池是大肠经合穴、清热要穴；尺泽是肺经合穴、子穴，「实则泻其子」，泻肺热；咽喉肿痛加少商点刺出血。热证不灸。

Summer-damp暑湿感冒

How it presents辨证要点 Mostly in summer: a fever that does not rise clearly, slight aversion to wind, little or unrelieved sweating; a heavy, swollen, wrapped head, heavy limbs, chest and epigastric fullness, nausea, poor appetite, sticky mouth, sometimes loose stools; greasy white or yellow coating, soggy rapid pulse.多见于夏季，身热不扬、微恶风，汗少或汗出不畅；头昏重胀如裹，肢体困重，胸闷脘痞，恶心，纳呆，口黏，大便或溏；苔白腻或黄腻，脉濡数。

Principle治法 Clear summerheat, transform damp, release the exterior and settle the middle (drain or even method)清暑化湿，解表和中（泻法或平补平泻）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷GB20 Fēngchí风池GV14 Dàzhuī大椎
Add配穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

💡 Damp burdens the middle burner: CV12, the stomach's front-mu and influential point of the fu organs, settles the stomach, transforms damp and stops nausea; ST36 strengthens digestion. High fever with collapse after heavy sweating in summer is heatstroke, not this page. CV12 is on the upper abdomen and is not needled in pregnancy.湿困中焦：中脘是胃募、腑会，和胃化湿止呕；足三里健脾胃、助运化。夏季高热、大量出汗后虚脱的属中暑，不在本页范围。中脘在上腹，孕妇禁针。

Deficient constitution体虚感冒

Qi-deficiency cold气虚感冒

How it presents辨证要点 Repeated colds triggered by the slightest wind or cold; fairly marked chills, low fever, spontaneous sweating, tiredness, shortness of breath, reluctance to talk, weak cough; pale tongue, white coating, floating weak pulse.反复感冒、稍遇风寒即发，恶寒较重，发热不高，自汗，倦怠乏力，气短懒言，咳痰无力；舌淡苔白，脉浮无力。

Principle治法 Tonify qi and release the exterior (mixed: even method on the main points, tonify ST36; moxa allowed)益气解表（补泻兼施：主穴平补平泻，足三里补法，可灸）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷GB20 Fēngchí风池GV14 Dàzhuī大椎
Add配穴ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

💡 A weak constitution does not tolerate strong drainage: use lighter technique on the main set and add ST36 to tonify the spleen and stomach so as to strengthen lung qi ('bank earth to generate metal'); between colds, regular moxa on ST36, GV14 and BL13 is used for prevention. Repeated or stubborn infections need checking for immune problems and chronic illness rather than being put down to 'weakness'.体虚之人不耐强泻：主穴手法放轻，加足三里补脾胃以益肺气（培土生金）；不发病时常灸足三里、大椎、肺俞以预防。反复感染、久治不愈要查免疫和慢性病，不能只归因于「体虚」。

Deficient constitution体虚感冒

Yin-deficiency cold阴虚感冒

How it presents辨证要点 Fever with slight aversion to wind and cold, little sweating or night sweats, a foggy head, restlessness, dry mouth and throat, dry cough with little sputum; red tongue with little coating, thin rapid pulse.身热，微恶风寒，少汗或盗汗，头昏，心烦，口干咽燥，干咳少痰；舌红少苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin and release the exterior (even method on the main points, tonify KI3; no moxa)滋阴解表（主穴平补平泻，太溪补法；不灸）

Main主穴LU7 Lièquē列缺LI4 Hégǔ合谷GB20 Fēngchí风池GV14 Dàzhuī大椎
Add配穴KI3 Tàixī太溪LU10 Yújì鱼际

💡 KI3, the Kidney yuan point, nourishes kidney yin to moisten the lung ('metal and water generate each other'); LU10, the Lung ying-spring point, clears deficiency heat and soothes a dry throat and cough. Heavy sweating harms yin, so GV14 is needled lightly without moxa.太溪是肾经原穴，滋肾阴以润肺（金水相生）；鱼际是肺经荥穴，清虚热、利咽止干咳。阴虚者忌大汗，大椎宜轻刺不灸。

LU7 Lièquē列缺

Best for最适合 Early cold: blocked nose, itchy throat, stiff head and neck感冒初起：鼻塞、喉痒、头项强痛

Why依据 Lung luo point, confluent point of the Ren vessel; 'head and neck, seek LU7'肺经络穴，八脉交会通任脉；「头项寻列缺」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Shared by every pattern; more for freeing the lung and a stiff neck than LI4, which leans to pain and fever; oblique toward the elbow 0.5–0.8 cun.寒热各型共用；比合谷偏宣肺、项强，合谷偏止痛退热；向肘部斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸。

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

Best for最适合 Cold with headache, fever, blocked nose or sore throat感冒头痛、发热、鼻塞、咽痛

Why依据 Large Intestine yuan point; 'face and mouth, LI4'; source–connecting pair with LU7大肠经原穴；「面口合谷收」；与列缺原络相配

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More analgesic and clears Yangming heat more than LU7; light technique in weak patients; forbidden in pregnancy.比列缺偏止痛和清阳明热；体虚者手法宜轻；孕妇禁针。

GB20 Fēngchí风池

Best for最适合 Cold with headache, stiff nape, foggy head感冒头痛、后项强、头昏

Why依据 Meeting point of the Gallbladder channel and Yang Wei vessel; key wind point足少阳与阳维脉交会穴；祛风要穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for head symptoms than GV14, which leans to fever; 0.8–1.2 cun toward the tip of the nose, never deep upward and inward (medulla, vertebral artery).比大椎偏头部症状，大椎偏退热；针尖朝鼻尖 0.8–1.2 寸，不可向内上方深刺（延髓、椎动脉）。

GV14 Dàzhuī大椎

Best for最适合 Fever, chills, aching neck and upper back发热、恶寒、项背酸痛

Why依据 Meeting point of the Governor vessel and all six yang channels督脉与手足三阳经交会穴，「诸阳之会」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Moxa for wind-cold, pricking and cupping for wind-heat, so it serves both; more about releasing the exterior through the yang than LI11; oblique upward 0.5–1 cun, never deep (spinal canal).风寒用灸、风热刺络拔罐，同一穴寒热两用，比曲池更偏通阳解表；向上斜刺 0.5–1 寸，不可深刺（椎管）。

GV16 Fēngfǔ风府

Best for最适合 External wind with headache and stiff neck (classical point)风邪外感头痛、项强（古典取穴）

Why依据 Meeting point of the Governor and Yang Wei vessels; Suwen: 'when wind enters from outside… treat at GV16'督脉与阳维交会穴；《素问》「风从外入……治在风府」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 The classical first choice, but the medulla lies beneath, so GB20 usually replaces it; only trained practitioners needle it, slowly 0.5–1 cun toward the chin, never deep and upward.古典首选，但深部是延髓，临床多以风池代替；只许受训者向下颌方向缓慢刺 0.5–1 寸，禁向上深刺。

BL12 Fēngmén风门

Best for最适合 Early wind-cold: chills, a tight neck and upper back风寒初起：恶寒、项背发紧

Why依据 Bladder channel point, the 'wind gate' through which wind enters and leaves足太阳经穴，「风门」为风邪出入之门

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for expelling wind than BL13, which regulates the lung and cough; beside T2, needle obliquely 0.5–0.8 cun to avoid pneumothorax; moxa or cupping suit it.比肺俞偏祛风解表，肺俞偏调肺止咳；T2 旁，斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸防气胸，宜灸或拔罐。

BL13 Fèishū肺俞

Best for最适合 Cold with cough, phlegm or chest tightness感冒咳嗽、痰多、胸闷

Why依据 Back-shu point of the lung肺之背俞穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Added when cough dominates, treating the lung itself more than BL12; beside T3, needle obliquely to avoid pneumothorax; moxa for the deficient.感冒以咳为主时加，比风门偏治肺本身；T3 旁，斜刺防气胸；体虚者宜灸。

LI11 Qūchí曲池

Best for最适合 Wind-heat cold with fever and sore throat风热感冒发热、咽痛

Why依据 Large Intestine he-sea point; key heat-clearing point大肠经合穴，清热要穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Clears interior heat more than GV14, which releases the exterior; not used in wind-cold without heat.比大椎更偏清里热，大椎偏解表通阳；风寒无热者不用。

LU5 Chǐzé尺泽

Best for最适合 Wind-heat cold with cough, yellow sputum and sore throat风热感冒咳嗽、痰黄、咽痛

Why依据 Lung he-sea (water) and child point; 'drain the child in excess'肺经合穴（水）、子穴，「实则泻其子」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for lung-heat cough than LI11, which is for general fever; not drained in deficient colds.比曲池更偏肺热咳嗽，曲池偏全身发热；体虚感冒不泻。

LU11 Shàoshāng少商

Best for最适合 A red, swollen, very sore throat咽喉红肿剧痛

Why依据 Lung jing-well point; pricked to bleed to drain heat肺经井穴，点刺出血泄热

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Only for bleeding in excess-heat sore throats, stronger and quicker than LU10, which suits dry deficiency-heat pain; not a general cold point.只用于实热咽痛的放血，比鱼际更急更猛，鱼际偏虚热干痛；不作一般感冒主穴。

LU10 Yújì鱼际

Best for最适合 Yin-deficiency cold with dry throat and dry cough; wind-heat sore throat阴虚感冒咽干、干咳；风热咽痛

Why依据 Lung ying-spring (fire) point; 'ying points govern heat'肺经荥穴（火），「荥主身热」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Gentler than LU11 and suited to dry pain and deficiency heat; more for the throat than LU5, which leans to cough with phlegm-heat.比少商温和，适合干痛和虚热；比尺泽更偏咽喉，尺泽偏咳嗽痰热。

CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘

Best for最适合 Summer-damp cold with nausea, epigastric fullness, poor appetite暑湿感冒恶心、脘闷、纳呆

Why依据 Front-mu point of the stomach, influential point of the fu organs胃之募穴，八会穴之腑会

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Added only when damp and digestive symptoms are prominent, more for settling the stomach and nausea than ST36; on the upper abdomen, forbidden in pregnancy.只在夹湿、胃肠症状明显时加，比足三里偏和胃止呕；位于上腹，孕妇禁针。

ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

Best for最适合 Repeated colds from qi deficiency; summer-damp burdening the spleen气虚反复感冒；暑湿困脾

Why依据 Stomach he-sea point; tonifies the spleen and stomach, banks earth to generate metal胃经合穴，补益脾胃，培土生金

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Tonifying and preventive, and unlike GV14 it does not release the exterior; regular moxa may reduce colds, but it is not a lead point during high fever.偏补、偏预防，比大椎不解表；平时可灸以减少感冒，急性高热期不作主穴。

LI20 Yíngxiāng迎香

Best for最适合 Blocked nose, runny nose, loss of smell鼻塞不通、流涕、不闻香臭

Why依据 Meeting point of the hand and foot Yangming beside the nose手足阳明交会穴，鼻旁局部

Versus others与其他穴的区别 The local addition for a blocked nose, more direct for the nose than LI4; oblique toward the root of the nose 0.3–0.5 cun, no moxa.鼻塞的局部加穴，比合谷更直接通鼻窍；向鼻根斜刺 0.3–0.5 寸，不灸。

GV12 Shēnzhù身柱

Best for最适合 Cold with aching all over and a stiff painful upper back感冒全身酸楚、项背强痛

Why依据 Governor vessel point below the spinous process of T3督脉穴，第 3 胸椎棘突下

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

The common cold is caused by more than 200 viruses, rhinoviruses above all; the main symptoms are a blocked or runny nose, sore throat and cough, fever is usually low, and it clears in 7–10 days, though the cough can linger two or three weeks; antibiotics do not help. Influenza starts suddenly with high fever, marked aches and exhaustion, and high-risk people benefit most from antivirals started within 48 hours; COVID-19 can look like a cold, so test according to local guidance. The things to watch for are secondary bacterial infection (sinusitis, ear infection, pneumonia) and flare-ups of chronic illness. Acupuncture can ease headache, blocked nose and aches, but evidence that it shortens the illness is limited, and wind-heat versus wind-cold cannot tell one virus from another.普通感冒由鼻病毒等 200 多种病毒引起，症状以鼻塞流涕、咽痛、咳嗽为主，发热一般不高，7–10 天自愈，咳嗽可拖两三周；抗生素无效。流感起病急、高热、全身痛和乏力明显，高危人群发病 48 小时内用抗病毒药获益最大；新冠症状可与感冒重叠，按当地指引检测。需要警惕的是继发细菌感染（鼻窦炎、中耳炎、肺炎）和基础病加重。针灸可缓解头痛、鼻塞、身痛等症状，但对缩短病程的研究证据有限；风热、风寒的辨证也不能用来区分病毒种类。
