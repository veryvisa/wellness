    Surface landmark体表标志

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    BL56 is over the gastrocnemius belly. The tibial nerve and posterior tibial vessels lie deeper and medially; the small saphenous vein and sural branches may be superficial. Use the muscle belly and cun measurement, remain shallow without deep medial advancement, and avoid visible veins; assess sensory changes, anticoagulation or bruising first.承筋落在腓肠肌肌腹区；胫神经和胫后血管位于更深层、偏内侧，浅表还可能见小隐静脉及腓肠神经分支。以肌腹和骨度定位，针向保持浅而不朝内深进，避开可见静脉；既往感觉异常、抗凝或局部瘀青者先评估。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate from both sides of the calf belly toward the midline to feel the interval between the gastrocnemius heads and distinguish the Achilles tendon below. Release immediately if deep pressure causes electric or numb sensation into the sole.用指腹从肌腹两侧向中央轻按，确认腓肠肌两头之间的肌间沟，并与下方跟腱连续性区分；深压出现向足底放射的麻电感时立即减压，不继续探按。

「承筋，一名腸，一名直腸，在腸中央陷者中，足太陽脈氣所發，禁不可刺，灸三壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Jiayi Jing calls Chengjin Chang or Zhichang, places it in a hollow at the centre of the calf, and explicitly says needling is forbidden. This historical prohibition differs from shallow needling in some modern textbooks; it is recorded here as historical text, not an instruction, and current regulation, supervised training and individual risk assessment govern practice.《针灸甲乙经》记承筋又名肠、直肠，位于腨肠中央凹陷处，并明确记「禁不可刺」。这条历史禁刺说法与现代常见教材的浅刺应用并不一致；本条只作原文记录，临床必须遵循现行法规、受训教材与个体风险评估，不据此自行操作。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Gastrocnemius strain or exercise-related calf cramp腓肠肌劳损或运动性小腿痉挛

Tightness or tenderness after exercise, worsened by tiptoe push-off, can reflect muscle strain; a sudden pop, bruising or inability to rise on tiptoe suggests a tear and needs care.运动后局部紧绷或压痛，踮脚、推蹬时加重可见于肌肉劳损；突然「啪」声、瘀青、无法踮脚提示撕裂，需就医。

Differential diagnosis of deep-vein thrombosis深静脉血栓鉴别

Sudden unexplained one-sided calf swelling, warmth and pain, especially after surgery, prolonged travel or previous thrombosis, requires urgent exclusion of DVT; do not massage or needle.单侧小腿无明显诱因突然肿热痛，尤其近期手术、久坐旅行或既往血栓，应尽快排除血栓，不按摩、不针刺。
