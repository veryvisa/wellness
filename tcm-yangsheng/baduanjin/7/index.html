✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • High blood pressure or heart disease: use only half to two-thirds effort, never holding the breath高血压、心脏病：用力只到五六成，绝不憋气
  • Wrist or elbow pain: make the wrist circle smaller手腕、肘部疼痛：旋腕幅度小一些
  • Knee pain: stand higher in the horse stance膝盖痛：马步站高

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Shift your weight right and step the left foot out into a horse stance; make 'firm-grip' fists (thumb tucked against the base of the ring finger, other fingers closed over it) and hold them at the waist, thumb side up重心右移，左脚向左开步成马步；两手「握固」（拇指扣在无名指根部，其余四指握拢），抱在腰侧，拳眼向上
  2. Punch the left fist slowly and firmly forward to shoulder height, thumb side up; widen the eyes and look at the fist左拳缓缓用力向前冲出，与肩同高，拳眼向上；瞪眼，看着左拳
  3. Open the left fist, circle the wrist once and close it into a fist again左拳变掌，手腕旋转一圈、抓握成拳
  4. Bend the elbow and draw the left fist back to the waist; then punch with the right屈肘把左拳收回腰侧；然后换右拳

🌬 Breathe out as you punch, in as you draw back; never hold your breath while exerting.冲拳时呼气，收拳时吸气；用力时不憋气。

✅ Key points要领

  • Power starts at the feet, passes through the waist and reaches the fist; slow but firm力从脚下起、经腰到拳，拳慢而有力
  • The wide-eyed stare is the signature of this form: eyes open wide, fixed on the fist瞪眼是这一式的特点：眼睛睁大盯着拳
  • Complete the full wrist circle and grip旋腕抓握要做完整

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Holding the breath and going red in the face as you punch冲拳时憋气、面红耳赤
  • Hunching the shoulders耸肩
  • Leaning the trunk forward after the fist上身前倾追着拳

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Stand higher or sit, and just punch and circle the wrist at half the effort.站高一点或坐着做，只出拳、旋腕，力量减半。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 TCM says 'the liver governs the sinews' and 'the liver opens into the eyes'; the clenched fists and wide stare of this form are traditionally read as training the sinews and freeing liver qi. 'Firm grip' (wogu) is a fist often mentioned in old texts as a way of holding the spirit steady. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.中医说「肝主筋」「肝开窍于目」，这一式握拳用力、怒目圆睁，传统解释为练筋、舒肝气；「握固」一词古籍里常见，指固守精神的握拳法。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
