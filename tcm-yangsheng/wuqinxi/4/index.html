Form 4: Deer running第4式 · 鹿奔

Left then right is one round; 2 rounds in all先左后右各做一次为一遍，共 2 遍

Lù bēn

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Osteoporosis or a past spinal compression fracture: skip the rounding; keep the back flat and only reach the arms forward骨质疏松、有过脊椎压缩性骨折：不弓背，改为背平直、只做两臂前伸
  • Disc problems in the lower back: round only a little, and stop if it hurts腰椎间盘突出：弓背幅度小，痛就停
  • Poor balance or a history of falls: take a short step and hold a chair back with one hand平衡不好、容易跌倒：步子迈小，一只手扶椅背做

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Step the left foot forward into a left lunge stance; loose fists arc up and forward to shoulder height in front of you左脚向前迈一步成左弓步，两手握空拳向上、向前划弧到体前，与肩同高
  2. Shift your weight back, straightening the left knee and bending the right; at the same time drop the head, round the back and draw in the belly, rotating the arms inward and reaching forward, backs of the hands facing, hands in deer antlers重心后移，左膝伸直，右腿屈膝；同时低头、弓背、收腹，两臂内旋前伸，手背相对，两手变鹿角
  3. Shift your weight forward again, lift the upper body, rotate the arms outward and return to loose fists in the lunge重心前移，上身抬起，两臂外旋，两手变回空拳，回到弓步
  4. Bring the left foot back and lower the fists to the sides; repeat with the right foot左脚收回，两拳下落到体侧；换右脚做

🌬 Breathe out as you round and sit back, in as you lift the upper body; or breathe naturally.弓背后坐时呼气，上身抬起时吸气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • As you round, reach the arms forward and press the back out behind, like a drawn bow弓背时两臂向前伸、背向后撑，像一张拉开的弓
  • Shift the weight forward and back smoothly without moving the feet重心前后移动要稳，脚不挪
  • Keep the shoulders relaxed肩放松

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Dropping the head without rounding the back只低头不弓背
  • Lifting the front toes as you sit back and wobbling后坐时前脚尖翘起、站不稳
  • Holding the breath憋气

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Take a shorter step and round the back half as much; or stay standing in place and only reach the arms forward while pressing the back out.步子迈小一点，弓背的幅度减半；或者站在原地，只做两臂前伸、背向后撑。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Traditional teaching says Deer Running imitates a startled deer gathering itself to bolt, the back pressing out and the belly drawn in; tradition says this 'gathers' energy into the lower back. This is a traditional view.传统教法说鹿奔学鹿受惊后回身蓄势、准备奔跑，背向后撑、腹向内收；传统上说这样能把气「收」到腰背。这是传统说法。
