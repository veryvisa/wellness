🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Superficial fibular nerve entrapment or irritation腓浅神经卡压或刺激

The superficial fibular nerve travels deep to the lateral crural fascia and emerges distally to supply the dorsum of the foot. Entrapment may cause numbness, burning, pins-and-needles, or electric pain over the distal lateral leg, dorsum of the foot, and some toes. Symptoms may worsen with activity, ankle inversion, or local compression. Unlike ordinary muscular tenderness, nerve symptoms often radiate along a recognizable distribution, and gentle tapping over the nerve may produce a Tinel-like response. Differentiate this condition from common fibular nerve injury and lumbosacral radiculopathy. Persistent sensory changes, muscle weakness, or foot drop require neurological or orthopedic assessment and should not be dismissed as an acupuncture-point response.腓浅神经在小腿外侧筋膜深面走行，远端穿出深筋膜后分布于足背。卡压可表现为小腿远端外侧、足背及部分足趾的麻木、烧灼感、针刺感或电击样疼痛，症状可能在活动、踝关节内翻或局部受压后加重。与普通肌肉压痛相比，神经症状常有沿神经分布的放射特点，轻叩神经走行处可能诱发Tinel样感觉。应与腓总神经损伤、腰骶神经根病鉴别。持续感觉异常、肌力下降或足下垂应接受神经科或骨科评估，不应直接将症状解释为穴位反应。

Fibularis tendinopathy or tenosynovitis腓骨肌腱病或腓骨肌腱鞘炎

The fibularis longus and brevis muscles lie along the lateral and posterior fibula, and their tendons pass behind the lateral malleolus distally. Pain is usually concentrated behind the fibular border or posterior to the lateral malleolus and may worsen with resisted ankle eversion, running, or jumping. GB37 lies near the anterior fibular border. Pain centred behind the lateral malleolus should raise suspicion of fibularis tendon pathology rather than being automatically attributed to GB37. Sudden snapping, substantial swelling, difficulty bearing weight, or a sensation of tendon displacement behind the malleolus warrants prompt medical assessment.腓骨长肌和腓骨短肌位于小腿外侧及腓骨后方，其肌腱向远端经过外踝后方。疼痛通常偏向腓骨后缘、外踝后方，可能伴踝关节外翻抗阻疼痛、跑跳后加重及局部肌腱压痛。光明位于腓骨前缘附近，若疼痛中心在外踝后方，更应考虑腓骨肌腱病，而非将后方压痛误认为光明区域病变。若有突然弹响、明显肿胀、负重困难或外踝后方肌腱滑脱感，应尽快就医。

Extensor digitorum longus strain or tendinopathy趾长伸肌劳损或肌腱病

The extensor digitorum longus lies in the anterolateral leg and contributes to ankle dorsiflexion and extension of toes two through five. Strain or tendinopathy may cause aching in the anterolateral leg, aggravated by resisted dorsiflexion or toe extension. The painful muscle belly may become tender during contraction. This region is adjacent to GB37 but is often more anterior. Assessment should include resisted testing, tendon tracking, and local swelling. Sudden marked weakness, inability to actively dorsiflex the foot, or pain following trauma requires assessment for tendon injury or fracture.趾长伸肌位于小腿前外侧，参与足背伸和第2至第5趾伸展。劳损可表现为小腿前外侧酸痛，抗阻背伸或伸趾时疼痛加重，局部肌肉收缩时可触及疼痛肌腹。与光明所在的腓骨前缘区域相邻，但疼痛中心常偏前方。应结合抗阻测试、肌腱走行和局部肿胀判断。突然出现明显肌力下降、无法主动背伸或足部外伤后疼痛，应先排除肌腱损伤及骨折。

Lateral leg myofascial pain小腿外侧肌筋膜疼痛

Prolonged running, hill climbing, compensatory gait, or a sudden increase in exercise load may cause pain in the lateral leg muscles and fascia. Typical symptoms include localized aching and tenderness that worsen with activity or sustained contraction and may improve with rest. Increasingly severe tightness or pain during exercise, numbness, or weakness that persists after stopping activity requires prompt assessment for a possible compartment-pressure disorder.长时间跑步、爬坡、步态代偿或运动负荷突然增加，可引起小腿外侧肌群及筋膜疼痛。常见表现为局部酸胀、压痛，活动或肌肉持续收缩后加重，休息后可能缓解。应区分单纯肌筋膜疼痛与腓浅神经卡压、腓骨肌腱病及慢性劳力性筋膜室综合征。若运动中出现越来越强烈的胀痛、麻木、肌力下降，停止运动后仍不缓解，需及时评估筋膜室压力相关问题。

Fibular injury or stress fracture腓骨骨损伤或应力性骨折

Focal bony tenderness over the lateral leg may result from a fibular contusion, fracture, or stress fracture caused by repetitive loading. Stress fractures are associated with sudden increases in running volume or prolonged repetitive impact and typically produce localized bony tenderness and pain during weight-bearing activity. Compared with muscular tenderness, bony tenderness is more concentrated over the shaft. Significant pain or swelling after trauma, inability to bear weight, or progressively worsening focal bone pain after exercise requires clinical and imaging assessment. Do not needle a suspected fracture site.小腿外侧局限性骨性压痛可能来自腓骨挫伤、骨折或反复负荷造成的应力性骨折。应力性骨折常与跑步量骤增、长期重复冲击有关，特点是骨面局限性压痛及负重活动疼痛。与肌肉压痛相比，骨性压痛通常更集中于骨干。外伤后明显疼痛、局部肿胀、不能负重，或运动后持续加重的局限性骨痛，应先接受影像学和临床评估，不宜在疑似骨折区域针刺。
