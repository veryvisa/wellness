Small Intestine Shu · Take care需谨慎

★Back-shu point of the Small Intestine小肠之背俞穴

📍 Location定位★

At the level of the first posterior sacral foramen, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 1 后骶孔同一水平，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

💡 The lower edge of the posterior superior iliac spine is roughly level with the 2nd posterior sacral foramen; palpate foramen by foramen to the 1 and measure 1.5 cun from the median sacral crest at that level.髂后上棘下缘约平第 2 骶后孔；由此上下逐孔触摸找到第 1 骶后孔，平该孔、距骶正中嵴 1.5 寸取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular or oblique medially直刺或向内斜刺 · 0.8–1.2 cun0.8–1.2 寸

Perpendicular or oblique medially; contraindicated in pregnancy.直刺或向内斜刺；孕妇禁针。

🛡 Safety安全

Posterior sacral foramina and sacral posterior rami are nearby; use bony landmarks to control depth and avoid overly deep or foraminal direction. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.邻近后骶孔及骶神经后支；以骨性标志确认层次，避免过深或偏向骶孔。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone position with the lumbosacral region relaxed and fully exposed; a thin pillow may be placed under the abdomen when needed to reduce lumbosacral lordotic tension.俯卧位，腰骶部放松，充分暴露骶区；必要时腹部可垫薄枕以减轻腰骶前凸张力。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: level with the first posterior sacral foramen.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），小肠俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 1 骶后孔水平定。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure laterally from the posterior midline using the width of the patient's index and middle fingers held together (roughly 1.5 cun) or check lateral distance with finger-cun; finger widths serve only as a rough reference and must be confirmed by sacral bony landmarks and palpable depressions.以患者本人食指、中指并拢（约 1.5 寸横径）自后正中线向外比量，或以同身寸横指核对旁开宽度；手指横径仅作辅助参考，最终须结合骶骨骨性标志与触诊凹陷核准。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First palpate the median sacral crest and lateral sacral crests on each side, locate the shallow depression or tender point near the first posterior sacral foramen along the posterolateral sacrum, and take the intersection 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline as BL27. This lumbosacral point is contraindicated for needling during pregnancy.先摸清骶正中嵴及两侧的骶外侧嵴，沿骶骨后外侧寻找第 1 后骶孔附近的浅陷或压痛点，与后正中线旁开 1.5 寸交汇处即为小肠俞。腰骶部穴位，孕期禁针。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Needling passes through skin, subcutaneous tissue containing posterior cutaneous branches of sacral nerves and lateral sacral venous branches, and gluteus maximus, reaching deeper near the posterior rami of sacral nerves and lateral sacral vessels over the sacrum. Avoid entering the posterior sacral foramen (to prevent entering the sacral canal or injuring anterior rami or pelvic structures) and lateral sacral vessels by controlling depth carefully.针刺依次经过皮肤、皮下组织（含骶神经后支皮支及骶外侧静脉分支）、臀大肌，深层邻近骶神经后支及骶外侧动、静脉，底层为骶骨骨面。进针须避开后骶孔（避免误入骶管或伤及骶神经前支/盆腔结构）及骶外侧血管，严格控制深度。

  5. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    In the prone position, locate the line joining the posterior superior iliac spines (roughly level with the second posterior sacral foramen), move up one level toward the first posterior sacral foramen region, and take the point about two finger-widths laterally.   6. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gentle pressing and sliding palpation 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline at the S1 level reveals firm resistance over the fascial and muscular planes above the sacrum with local soreness or distension; sharp pain or strong radiating tingling along a nerve distribution indicates contact with a nerve branch and calls for withdrawing slightly and adjusting angle and depth.指腹在 S1 水平后正中线旁开 1.5 寸处轻柔按压与滑动触诊，可感知骶骨后方筋膜与肌层下的韧性阻力及局部酸胀感；若出现沿神经分布的锐痛或剧烈放射麻感，提示触及神经分支，应退针调整角度与深度。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting inferiorly to the S2 or S3 posterior sacral foramen level (closer to BL28 or BL29): mistaking the line between the posterior superior iliac spines for the S1 foramen level shifts the point downward and alters its specific channel point correspondence and clinical focus.取偏至第 2 或第 3 后骶孔水平（偏下，接近膀胱俞 BL28 或中膂俞 BL29）：误将髂后上棘连线直接当作第 1 后骶孔水平会导致定位下移，影响小肠俞与膀胱俞、中膂俞的经穴归属与主治侧重。
  • Measuring too far medially or laterally from 1.5 cun off the posterior midline: going too medially approaches the median sacral crest, while going too laterally approaches the lateral sacral margin and thicker gluteal tissue, reducing accuracy for the S1-level Back-Shu region.旁开过宽或过窄（偏离后正中线 1.5 寸）：偏内易靠近骶正中嵴或骶管正中线，偏外则靠近骶外侧缘与臀肌较厚区，减弱对 S1 水平背俞穴局部结构的针对性。
  • Inserting too deeply or angling toward the posterior sacral foramen: perpendicular or deep insertion into a foramen risks injuring sacral nerve roots, sacral canal structures, or lateral sacral vessels, causing sharp radiating pain, hematoma, or nerve injury; needling the lumbosacral region during pregnancy is contraindicated.进针过深或方向偏向后骶孔：直刺或向孔内深刺可能穿入后骶孔，伤及骶神经根、骶管内结构或骶外侧血管，引起剧烈放射痛、血肿或神经损伤；孕期误针腰骶部更属禁忌。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Consider when low-back pain involves the lumbosacral region around the S1 level or lower-jiao referral; acute lumbar sprain or clear discogenic pain often prioritizes tender points, BL54, BL40, or EX-B2 rather than relying on BL27 alone.偏重腰骶部、第 1 骶椎水平及少腹下焦相关牵涉的腰骶痛时可酌情选用；急性腰扭伤或明显椎间盘源性痛常优先结合局部压痛点、秩边、委中或腰夹脊穴，不作为唯一主穴。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Suited to abdominal pain involving the lower abdomen, lower-jiao tension, or disturbed intestinal transit, often paired with CV4, ST36, or ST39; acute abdomen, appendicitis, or perforation requires immediate medical care rather than reliance on this point.适用于偏下焦、少腹拘急或肠道传导失调相关的腹痛，常配关元、足三里或下巨虚；急性急腹症、阑尾炎或穿孔等器质性急症须先就医，不以本穴延误。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Used for diarrhea, yellowish-red stools, or adjunctive care of bloody stools linked to disturbed bowel transit, damp-heat, or lower-jiao dysfunction, often paired with ST39, ST25, or CV4; severe dehydration, high fever, or hematochezia requires medical assessment first.适用于肠道传导失常、湿热或下焦气化失调相关的泄泻、便黄赤或便血辅助调理，常配下巨虚、天枢、关元；严重脱水、高热或血便须先就医评估。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Where is BL27 Xiǎochángshū?小肠俞（BL27）在哪里？

At the level of the first posterior sacral foramen, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 1 后骶孔同一水平，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL27 needled?小肠俞（BL27）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique medially; 0.8–1.2 cun.直刺或向内斜刺；0.8–1.2 寸。

Special-point roles of BL27?小肠俞（BL27）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Small Intestine小肠之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL27?小肠俞（BL27）最要注意的安全问题？

Posterior sacral foramina and sacral posterior rami are nearby; use bony landmarks to control depth and avoid overly deep or foraminal direction. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.邻近后骶孔及骶神经后支；以骨性标志确认层次，避免过深或偏向骶孔。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
