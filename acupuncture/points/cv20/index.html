    The manubrium lies beneath the midline, with the left brachiocephalic vein and aortic arch behind it; laterally the 1st intercostal space approaches the pleural dome and lung apex. Transverse subcutaneous needling only.正中深面为胸骨柄，柄后是左头臂静脉与主动脉弓；偏外进入第 1 肋间则近胸膜顶、肺尖。只沿皮下平刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Find the sternal angle and second rib, then identify the first interspace and midline; do not probe forcefully in the supra- or infraclavicular fossae.先触胸骨角并确认第 2 肋，再向上辨认第 1 肋间隙及胸骨正中线；不要在锁骨上窝或锁骨下窝用力探压。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Cross-check the sequence with adjacent CV21 and CV19; rib counting takes precedence over judging a surface hollow.与璇玑、紫宫上下相邻穴位序列核对；第一肋间的肋骨计数优先于表面凹陷判断。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Mistaking an infraclavicular or sternocleidomastoid hollow for CV20 shifts laterally toward the lung apex and great vessels; deep downward insertion risks pneumothorax and mediastinal injury, so remain superficial.将锁骨下窝或胸锁乳突肌间隙当作华盖会偏离正中并靠近肺尖、大血管；向下深直刺有气胸及纵隔损伤风险，必须严格浅表。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

A local reference for stable upper-chest cough, tightness or itchy throat; CV21 is closer to the suprasternal notch, and lung-apex risk rules out deep insertion.上胸咳嗽、胸闷或咽痒的稳定期可作局部参考；CV21更靠近胸骨上窝，肺尖风险使本穴不宜深刺。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Consider only for assessed non-acute chest discomfort; acute breathlessness, cyanosis or asthma attack requires emergency care.非急性、已评估的胸部不适才考虑；急性气促、发绀或哮喘发作立即急救。

Sore throat咽喉肿痛 ⚖️

A local-distal adjunct when chest tightness accompanies throat discomfort; throat swelling, stridor or inability to swallow needs airway care first.胸闷兼咽部不适时远近配穴参考；咽喉肿胀、喘鸣或不能吞咽须先处理气道。

Main safety point at CV20?华盖（CV20）最要注意的安全问题？
