Escape Restraint Pill越鞠丸

Yuè Jū Wán · Danxi Xinfa by Zhu Zhenheng (Yuan); anchor texts from Danxi Xinfa and Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie元·朱震亨《丹溪心法》；原文锚点取元·朱震亨《丹溪心法》及清·汪昂《医方集解》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Escape Restraint Pill越鞠丸

Original text原文

「越鞠丸解诸郁。又名芎术丸。苍术香附抚芎神曲栀子（各等分）上为末，水丸如绿豆大。」——丹溪心法

Danxi Xinfa

Yueju Wan disperses various constraints and is also known as Xiong Zhu Wan; it comprises equal parts Cang Zhu, Xiang Fu, Fu Xiong (Chuan Xiong), Shen Qu, and Zhi Zi ground into powder and formed into mung-bean-sized water pills.越鞠丸用来散解各种郁结，又名芎术丸。组成是苍术、香附、抚芎（川芎）、神曲、栀子各等分，研成细末，用水泛丸如绿豆大小。

「统治六郁，胸膈痞闷，吞酸呕吐，飲食不消」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Governs all six constraints (qi, blood, phlegm, fire, dampness, and food), characterized by focal distension and stuffiness in the chest and diaphragm, acid regurgitation, vomiting, and indigestion.统辖所有六郁（气、血、痰、火、湿、食郁）证候，主要表现为胸膈部痞塞满闷、泛酸呕吐、饮食不消化。

「香附開氣郁，苍术燥溼郁，抚芎調血郁，栀子解火郁，神曲消食郁。」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Explains the division of roles among the five herbs—Xiang Fu opens qi constraint, Cang Zhu dries dampness constraint, Fu Xiong regulates blood constraint, Zhi Zi resolves fire constraint, and Shen Qu disperses food constraint.阐明五味药的分工：香附行气解气郁，苍术燥湿除湿郁，川芎活血调血郁，栀子清热解火郁，神曲消食化食郁。

Cautions使用注意

Because this formula is aromatic, drying, and dispersing, it is contraindicated in constraint patterns arising from qi, yin, or blood deficiency (such as fatigue, a red tongue with scant coating, or a thready, rapid, weak pulse); seek immediate medical evaluation if severe chest pain radiating to the back, persistent hematemesis or melena, jaundice, or high fever occurs.本方药性偏于香燥行散，凡属气虚、阴虚、血虚所致之郁证（如神疲乏力、舌红少苔、脉细数无力）忌用；若出现剧烈胸痛彻背、持续呕血黑便、黄疸或高热不退，须立即就医排查心肺及消化道急症。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Cang Zhu, Chuan Xiong, and Zhi Zi all carry a pregnancy caution on this site (Chuan Xiong invigorates blood and moves qi, which may disturb the fetus, while Cang Zhu is drying and Zhi Zi is bitter-cold), so the formula takes the strictest level of its herbs.方中苍术、川芎、栀子在本站均列为孕期慎用（川芎活血行气易动胎气，苍术辛燥、栀子苦寒亦需谨慎），方随其最严者。
