Jade Windscreen Powder玉屏风散

Yù Píng Fēng Sǎn · Shiyi Dexiao Fang (Yuan, Wei Yilin; also recorded in Danxi Xinfa); anchor text from Wang Ang's Yifang Jijie元·危亦林《世医得效方》（亦见于元·朱震亨《丹溪心法》）；原文锚点取清·汪昂《医方集解》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Jade Windscreen Powder玉屏风散

Original text原文

「治自汗不止，氣虛表弱，易感風寒」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

For incessant spontaneous sweating, qi deficiency with exterior weakness, and susceptibility to wind-cold.治自汗不止、卫气虚弱、肌表不固而容易感受风寒。

「黃（炙，四兩）防風（一兩）白术（炒，二兩。）爲末。每服三錢。」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Four liang of honey-fried astragalus root, one liang of saposhnikovia root, and two liang of stir-fried atractylodes rhizome, ground into fine powder and taken at three qian per dose.炙黄芪四两、防风一两、炒白术二两，共研细末，每次服用三钱。

「黃補氣，專固肌表，故以爲君；白术益脾，脾主肌肉，故以爲臣；防風去風，爲風藥卒徒，而黃畏之，故以爲使，以其益衛固表，故曰玉屏風」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Astragalus supplements qi and secures the exterior as monarch, atractylodes strengthens the spleen to support the exterior as minister, and saposhnikovia disperses wind while mutually enhancing astragalus as assistant and envoy, together fortifying defensive qi like a jade windscreen.黄芪补气固表为君，白术健脾益气以助固表为臣，防风走表散风并与黄芪相畏相使为佐使，合而益卫固表，故名玉屏风。

Cautions使用注意

Not suitable for exterior excess wind-cold or night sweats from yin deficiency; prompt medical evaluation is required if spontaneous sweating is accompanied by low-grade fever, weight loss, palpitations, shortness of breath, or recurrent infections to rule out underlying infectious, thyroid, or immune conditions.外感风寒表实证或阴虚盗汗者不宜使用；若自汗伴低热、消瘦、心悸、气促或反复感染，应及时就医排查感染、甲状腺或免疫等基础疾病。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Astragalus carries a pregnancy caution on this site (atractylodes and saposhnikovia have no pregnancy restrictions listed), so the formula follows the strictest level of its constituent herbs.方中黄芪在本站按孕期慎用（白术、防风未列孕期禁忌），方随最严一味定为孕期慎用。
