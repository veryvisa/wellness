⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Consider its bladder front-mu role for midline lower-abdominal urinary patterns; infection or acute retention needs medical care first.下腹正中排尿不利且传统膀胱证候明显时优先考虑其募穴属性；尿路感染或急性潴留需先就医。

Period pain痛经

An adjunct for menstrual midline pain with pelvic or urinary symptoms; sudden atypical pain needs urgent causes excluded.经期下腹正中痛伴盆腔或排尿症状可作辅助；纯寒凝痛可结合辨证，突发异常痛需排急症。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Choose for localized infraumbilical midline pain related to bladder or pelvis, not primarily epigastric or periumbilical pain.局限脐下正中、与膀胱或盆腔相关时选；胃脘或脐周疼痛不以中极为首选。

No needling in pregnancy (textbook rule: no lower-abdominal needling in the first 3 months; after 3 months avoid the abdomen and lumbosacral region as well); empty the bladder first (a full bladder rises above the pubic symphysis against the abdominal wall). In urinary retention the bladder lies against the wall and deep needling can puncture it.孕妇禁针（教材针刺注意事项：妊娠 3 个月内小腹部禁针，3 个月以上腹部、腰骶部亦不宜针）；针前排空膀胱（充盈膀胱可越过耻骨联合贴近腹壁），尿潴留时膀胱紧贴腹壁，深刺可刺破膀胱。
