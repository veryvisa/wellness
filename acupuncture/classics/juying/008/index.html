手太陰肺中焦生。下絡大腸出賁門。上鬲屬肺從肺系。系橫出腋臑中行。肘臂寸口生魚際。大指內側爪甲根。支絡還從腕內出。接次指屬陽明經。此經多氣而少血。是動則病欬與嗽。肺脹膨膨缺盆痛。兩手交瞀為臂厥。所生病者為氣嗽。喘欬煩心胸滿結。臑臂之外前廉痛。小便頻數掌中熱。氣虛肩背痛而寒。氣盛亦疼風汗出。欠伸少氣不足息。遺失無度溺色赤。

手太阴肺经起源于中焦。向下联络大肠，经过贲门附近；向上穿过膈膜，归属于肺，并沿着肺系上行。肺系横向延伸，出于腋下，沿上臂内侧下行，经过肘部、前臂，到达寸口，再到鱼际。沿拇指内侧到达指甲根部。另有一条支脉从腕部内侧分出，连接食指，与手阳明大肠经相接。本经气多而血少。经气异常活动时，可出现咳嗽、肺部胀满、缺盆疼痛，严重时咳得两手交叉按着胸口、心烦眼花，叫做臂厥。本经所主的病症包括气逆咳嗽、气喘、咳嗽、心烦、胸部满闷、上臂及前臂外侧前缘疼痛、小便频数、掌心发热。肺气虚时，肩背疼痛而畏寒；肺气盛时，肩背也会疼痛，并因感受风邪而出汗。还可能出现呵欠、伸懒腰、气短、呼吸不足，以及小便失禁、尿色赤等症状。

The Hand Taiyin Lung channel originates in the middle burner. It descends to connect with the Large Intestine and passes near the cardia. It ascends through the diaphragm, pertains to the Lung, and follows the Lung system. The Lung system extends transversely and emerges in the axilla, then travels along the medial aspect of the upper arm, passing the elbow and forearm to reach the cun pulse region and the thenar eminence. It continues along the medial side of the thumb to the root of its nail. A collateral branch emerges from the medial wrist and connects with the index finger, joining the Hand Yangming Large Intestine channel. This channel has abundant qi and relatively little blood. When its qi is disturbed, symptoms may include coughing, pulmonary distension, pain in the supraclavicular fossa, and, in severe cases, clutching the chest with both hands crossed while the vision blurs, which is called arm reversal (bi jue). Its associated disorders include cough due to rebellious qi, dyspnea, coughing, vexation of the Heart, fullness and oppression of the chest, pain along the anterolateral aspect of the upper arm and forearm, frequent urination, and heat in the palms. Lung qi deficiency may cause shoulder and back pain accompanied by coldness. Excess Lung qi may also produce pain, and sweating from invading wind. Other symptoms include yawning, stretching, shortness of breath, inadequate respiration, urinary incontinence, and reddish urine.

陽明之脈手大腸。次指內側起商陽。循指上連出合谷。兩筋岐骨循臂肪。入肘外廉循臑外。肩端前廉柱骨旁。從肩下入缺盆內。絡肺下鬲屬大腸。支從缺盆直上頸。斜貫頰前下齒當。環出人中交左右。上夾鼻孔注迎香。此經氣盛血亦盛。是動(左出右頁)腫并齒痛。所生病者為鼽衂。目黃口乾喉痹生。大指次指難為用。肩前臑外痛相仍。氣有餘兮脈熱腫。虛則寒慄病偏增。

手阳明大肠经起于食指内侧末端的商阳穴，沿食指上行，经过合谷穴，从第一、二掌骨之间、两筋之中穿过，沿前臂外侧上行，进入肘部外侧，再沿上臂外侧上行至肩端前侧、柱骨（颈椎大椎）旁，向下进入缺盆。由此联络肺，穿过膈膜，归属大肠。另有支脉从缺盆直上颈部，斜行穿过面颊，进入下齿；绕行口唇，左右两脉在人中交叉，左脉走右、右脉走左，再向上夹鼻孔两侧，终止于迎香穴。本经气血具盛。经气异常活动时，可出现面颊肿胀及牙痛。本经所主病症包括鼻塞、鼻流血、眼睛发黄、口干、咽喉肿痛，以及食指（大指旁的次指）活动不利、肩前及上臂外侧疼痛。经气有余时，可出现经脉部位发热、肿胀；虚弱时则可能出现畏寒、战栗，病情加重。

The Hand Yangming channel is the Large Intestine channel. It begins at Shangyang on the medial side of the index finger. It travels along the finger, passes through the region of Hegu, and ascends between the metacarpal bones along the forearm. It enters the lateral aspect of the elbow and continues along the lateral upper arm to the anterior aspect of the shoulder, beside the column bone (the cervical spine at Dazhui). It descends into the supraclavicular fossa, connects with the Lung, passes through the diaphragm, and pertains to the Large Intestine. A branch ascends from the supraclavicular fossa to the neck, crosses the cheek obliquely, and reaches the lower teeth. It circles the lips, crosses its counterpart at the philtrum, left going right and right going left, and ascends alongside the nostrils to Yingxiang. This channel has abundant qi and blood. When its qi is disturbed, cheek swelling and toothache may occur. Its associated disorders include nasal obstruction, nosebleeds, yellow discoloration of the eyes, dry mouth, throat obstruction, difficulty using the index finger (the finger next to the thumb), and pain in the anterior shoulder and lateral upper arm. When channel qi is excessive, heat and swelling may occur along the channel. When it is deficient, chills and shivering may predominate.

胃足陽明交鼻起。下循鼻外下入齒。還出夾口繞承漿。頤後大迎頰車裏。耳前髮際至額顱。支下人迎缺盆底。下鬲入胃絡脾宮。直者缺盆下乳內。一支幽門循腹中。下行直合氣衝逢。遂由髀關抵膝臏。(左骨右行)跗中指內關同。一支下膝注三里。前出中指外關通。一支別走足跗指。大指之端經盡矣。此經多氣復多血。是動欠伸面顏黑。凄凄惡寒畏見人。忽聞木音心驚惕。登高而歌棄衣走。甚則腹脹乃賁響。凡此諸疾皆骭厥。所生病者為狂瘧。溫淫汗出鼻流血。口喎唇裂又喉痹。膝臏疼痛腹脹結。氣膺伏兔(左骨右行)外廉。足跗中指俱痛徹。有餘消穀溺色黃。不足身前寒振慄。胃房脹滿食不消。氣盛身前皆有熱。

足阳明胃经起于鼻部，与手阳明大肠经交接。沿鼻外侧下行，进入上齿；再出来环绕口唇，经过承浆穴附近，沿下颌后方的大迎、颊车一带上行，经耳前、发际，到达额部。另有支脉向下经人迎穴，进入缺盆，再向下穿过膈膜，进入胃并联络脾。另一条直行支脉从缺盆沿胸部乳房内侧下行；一支从幽门附近沿腹部中线旁下行，与气冲穴会合，再经髀关到达膝盖。之后沿小腿前外侧下行，经足背，进入中趾内侧。另有支脉从膝下分出，经足三里，向前到达中趾外侧。又有一支从足背分出，走向足大趾末端，与足太阴脾经相接。本经气血具盛。经气异常活动时，可出现呵欠、伸懒腰、面色发黑、畏寒、怕见人、听到木声便惊恐，以及登高唱歌、脱衣奔走等精神行为异常；严重时腹部胀满并出现肠鸣。这些病候古代统称为骭厥。本经所主病症包括精神错乱、疟疾、发热出汗、鼻出血、口角歪斜、嘴唇干裂、咽喉肿痛、膝盖疼痛、腹部胀满，以及气街、胸膺、伏兔、小腿外侧、足背和中趾疼痛。经气有余时，食欲旺盛、消化较快，尿色发黄；不足时，身体前面畏寒、战栗，胃脘胀满、食物难以消化。胃气盛时，身体前面可出现发热。

The Foot Yangming Stomach channel begins around the nose, where it connects with the Hand Yangming channel. It descends along the outer sides of the nose, enters the upper teeth, emerges again, circles the mouth, and passes around the Chengjiang region. It travels behind the jaw through the regions of Daying and Jiache, ascends past the ear and anterior hairline, and reaches the forehead. A branch descends through Renying into the supraclavicular fossa, then passes through the diaphragm, enters the Stomach, and connects with the Spleen. Another branch descends from the supraclavicular region along the chest, medial to the breast. A branch travels from the pyloric region along the abdomen, joins at Qichong, and continues through Biguan to the kneecap. It then descends along the lower leg and dorsum of the foot to the medial side of the middle toe. Another branch descends from below the knee through Zusanli and reaches the lateral side of the middle toe. A further branch travels along the dorsum of the foot to the great toe, where the channel ends and connects with the Foot Taiyin Spleen channel. This channel has abundant qi and blood. When its qi is disturbed, symptoms may include yawning, stretching, a dark facial complexion, aversion to cold, social withdrawal, fright at the sound of wood, and manic behavior such as singing and running about while removing clothing. Severe cases may involve abdominal distension and intestinal rumbling. These disorders are called shin reversal (gan jue). Its associated disorders include mania, malaria, fever with sweating, epistaxis, facial deviation, chapped lips, throat obstruction, knee pain, abdominal distension, and pain along the qi street in the groin, the chest, the thigh, the lateral leg, the dorsum of the foot, and the middle toe. Excess may be associated with rapid digestion and yellow urine. Deficiency may cause chills and shivering along the anterior body, gastric distension, and poor digestion. Excessive Stomach qi may produce heat along the anterior body.

太陰脾起足大指。上循內側白肉際。核骨之後內髁前。上腨循(左骨右行)脛膝裏。股內前廉入腹中。屬脾絡胃與鬲通。俠喉連舌散舌下。支絡從胃注心宮。此經氣盛而血衰。是動其病氣所為。食入即吐胃脘痛。更兼身體痛難移。腹脹善噫舌本強。得後與氣快然衰。所生病者舌亦痛。體重不食亦如之。煩心心下仍急痛。泄水溏瘕寒瘧隨。不臥強立股膝腫。疸發身黃大指痿。

足太阴脾经起于足大趾内侧，沿足内侧赤白肉际上行，经过第一跖骨后方及内踝前方，再沿小腿内侧上行，经膝内侧、大腿内侧前缘进入腹部。归属于脾，联络胃，并穿过膈膜。经过咽喉，连接舌根，散布于舌下。另有支脉从胃部分出，注入心。此经气盛而血相对不足。经气异常活动时，可能出现进食后呕吐、胃脘疼痛、全身疼痛且难以活动、腹胀、频繁嗳气、舌根僵硬等症状；排便或排气后，腹部胀满可能减轻。本经所主病症包括舌痛、身体沉重、食欲不振、心烦、心下急迫疼痛、腹泻、水样便、腹部积聚及寒性疟疾。还可能出现不能安睡、勉强站立、大腿及膝部肿胀、黄疸、身体发黄及大趾萎弱。

The Foot Taiyin Spleen channel begins at the medial side of the great toe. It ascends along the border between the red and white skin of the foot, passes behind the first metatarsal prominence and in front of the medial malleolus, and continues along the medial lower leg, passing the medial knee and anterior aspect of the inner thigh before entering the abdomen. It pertains to the Spleen, connects with the Stomach, and communicates through the diaphragm. It passes alongside the throat, connects with the root of the tongue, and spreads beneath the tongue. A branch emerges from the Stomach region and enters the Heart. This channel is traditionally described as having abundant qi and relatively little blood. When its qi is disturbed, symptoms may include vomiting immediately after eating, epigastric pain, generalized pain with difficulty moving, abdominal distension, frequent belching, and stiffness of the tongue root. Defecation or passage of gas may relieve the distension. Its associated disorders include tongue pain, heaviness of the body, loss of appetite, vexation of the Heart, acute pain below the Heart, watery diarrhea, loose stools, abdominal masses, and cold-type malaria. Other manifestations include inability to sleep, difficulty standing, swelling of the thighs and knees, jaundice, yellow discoloration of the body, and weakness or wasting of the great toe.

The Hand Shaoyin Heart channel originates in the Heart. It descends through the diaphragm and communicates with the Small Intestine. A branch travels from the Lung system, ascends directly to the throat, and connects with the eyes. The main channel ascends toward the Lung, emerges below the axilla, and descends along the posteromedial aspect of the upper arm. It passes through Shaohai at the medial elbow, travels along the posteromedial forearm, reaches the palm, and terminates at Shaochong near the ulnar side of the little finger. This channel has abundant qi and relatively little blood. When its qi is disturbed, severe pain in the Heart and epigastric region may occur, together with thirst, a desire to drink water, and dryness of the throat. 足少阳胆经起于眼外眦，向上到达头部，沿头角下行至耳后，经过脑空、风池等部位。之后行于手少阳三焦经之前到达肩上，再交叉到手少阳之后，进入缺盆。另有支脉从耳后进入耳内，再从耳前出来，沿眼外眦循行。另一支从眼外眦向下经大迎附近，与手少阳三焦经相合，抵达颈根，再经颊车附近与缺盆会合，进入胸中，穿过膈膜，联络肝，归属胆，并沿胁肋内侧下行，进入气冲、绕过阴毛边缘，横行进入髋关节环跳处。直行的一支从缺盆向下经腋下及胸侧，过季胁，下行与前一支会合于髋部，再向外到达膝外侧的阳陵泉，再沿小腿外侧、绝骨附近及外踝前方下行，经足背到达第四趾与第五趾之间。另有支脉从足背分出，走向大趾，于足大趾爪甲根部附近与肝经相接。本经气多而血少。经气异常活动时，可出现口苦、频繁叹息、心胁疼痛且难以转动身体、面色灰暗如蒙尘、足部发热及身体缺乏光泽。本经所主病症包括头痛并延伸至眼外眦、缺盆肿痛、两腋肿胀、腋下及颈侧出现类似马刀、瘿瘤的肿块，以及汗出、寒战、疟疾。还可能出现胸胁、大腿、膝部、小腿骨、外踝及全身关节疼痛。

The Foot Shaoyang Gallbladder channel begins at the outer canthus. It reaches the head, travels around the temporal region, and descends behind the ear, passing through Naokong and Fengchi. It proceeds toward the shoulder, anterior to the Hand Shaoyang channel, then crosses behind it and enters the supraclavicular fossa. A branch travels from behind the ear through the inner ear, emerges in front of the ear, and follows the outer canthus. Another branch descends from the outer canthus toward the Daying region, joins the Hand Shaoyang channel, and reaches the root of the neck. It passes through the Jiache region, joins the supraclavicular fossa, enters the chest, crosses the diaphragm, connects with the Liver, and pertains to the Gallbladder. It continues along the inner hypochondrium and descends to Qichong, skirts the pubic hair, and runs transversely into the hip joint at Huantiao. The straight branch descends from the supraclavicular region through the axilla and lateral chest, passes the lower ribs, joins the former branch at the hip, and reaches Yanglingquan at the lateral knee. It continues along the lateral lower leg, passes the region of Juegu and the anterior aspect of the lateral malleolus, and travels across the dorsum of the foot to the space between the fourth and fifth toes. Another branch leaves the dorsum of the foot and travels toward the great toe, joining the Liver channel near the base of its nail. This channel has abundant qi and relatively little blood. When its qi is disturbed, symptoms may include bitter taste in the mouth, frequent sighing, pain in the Heart and hypochondrium with difficulty turning the body, a dusty or dull complexion, heat in the feet, and lack of bodily luster. Its associated disorders include headache extending to the outer canthus, swelling and pain in the supraclavicular fossa and both axillae, masses resembling saber-like swellings or goiters on the sides of the neck, sweating, chills, and malaria. Pain may also occur in the chest, hypochondrium, thigh, knee, lower-leg bones, lateral malleolus, and other joints.

厥陰足脈肝所終。大指之端毛際叢。足跗上廉太衝分。踝前一寸入中封。上踝交出太陰後。循膕內廉陰股衝。環繞陰器抵小腹。夾胃屬肝絡膽逢。上貫膈裏布脇肋。俠喉頏顙目系同。脈上巔會督脈出。支者還生目系中。下絡頰裏還唇內。支者便從膈肺通。是經血多氣少焉。是動腰疼俛仰難。男疝女人小腹腫。面塵脫色及咽乾。所生病者為胸滿。嘔吐洞泄小便難。或時遺溺并狐疝。臨證還須仔細看。

足厥阴肝经是十二经脉循行的最后一经。起于足大趾末端爪甲附近，沿足背上缘经过太冲穴，向前到达内踝前一寸的中封穴。再向上经过内踝，交叉至足太阴脾经之后，沿膝窝内侧及大腿内侧上行，环绕生殖器，抵达小腹。经过胃部附近，归属肝，联络胆。向上穿过膈膜，分布于胁肋，沿喉咙到达鼻咽部，并联系眼部。经脉向上到达头顶，与督脉交会。另有支脉进入眼部，向下联络面颊内侧，再回到口唇内部；又有一支从膈膜出发，与肺相通。本经血多而气少。经气异常活动时，可出现腰痛、俯仰困难，男性疝气，女性小腹肿胀，面色灰暗或失去光泽，以及咽喉干燥。本经所主病症包括胸部胀满、呕吐、剧烈腹泻、小便不通或排尿困难，有时还会出现尿失禁及狐疝。临床诊察时仍须仔细辨别。

The Foot Jueyin Liver channel is the final channel in the twelve-channel sequence described here. It begins at the tip of the great toe near the nail region. It travels along the upper aspect of the dorsum of the foot through the region of Taichong and reaches Zhongfeng, located one cun anterior to the medial malleolus. It ascends past the ankle and crosses behind the Foot Taiyin Spleen channel. It continues along the medial popliteal region and inner thigh, circles the external genitalia, and reaches the lower abdomen. It passes near the Stomach, pertains to the Liver, and connects with the Gallbladder. It ascends through the diaphragm, spreads through the hypochondrium, follows the throat to the nasopharyngeal region, and connects with the eyes. The channel reaches the crown of the head, where it meets the Governing Vessel. A branch enters the eye region, descends to connect with the inner cheek, and returns to the interior of the lips. Another branch travels from the diaphragm and communicates with the Lung. This channel has abundant blood and relatively little qi. When its qi is disturbed, symptoms may include lower-back pain and difficulty bending forward or backward, hernia in men, lower-abdominal swelling in women, a dull or pale complexion, and dryness of the throat. Its associated disorders include chest fullness, vomiting, severe diarrhea, difficulty urinating, occasional urinary incontinence, and fox-type hernia. Careful clinical differentiation is required.
