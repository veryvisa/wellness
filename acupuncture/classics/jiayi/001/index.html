針灸甲乙經

《针灸甲乙经》

The Systematic Classic of Acupuncture and Moxibustion (Zhenjiu Jiayijing).

💡 全书总题。晋·皇甫谧撰，成书于西晋泰始至太康年间（约265—282年，序中言及魏甘露年间患病，后撰集），原名《黄帝三部针灸甲乙经》，简称《甲乙经》，是我国现存最早的一部针灸学专著。以'甲、乙'等十天干编排卷次，故名'甲乙'。在针灸史上具有承前启后、奠定体系的划时代地位。

林序宋校本 Preface by Lin Yi and Colleagues to the Song Dynasty Collated Edition.

臣聞通天地人曰儒，通天地不通人曰技。斯醫者雖曰方技，其實儒者之事乎。班固序《藝文志》稱，儒者助人君，順陰陽，明教化。此亦通天地人之理也。又雲：“方技者，蓋論病以及國，原診以知政。”非能通三才之奧，安能及國之政哉？晉·皇甫謐博綜典籍百家之言，沉靜寡欲，有高尚之志。得風痹，因而學醫，習覽經方，遂臻至妙。

臣听说贯通天、地、人之道的人叫'儒'，贯通天、地却不通达人事的人叫'技'。医学虽然被称为'方技'，实际上却是儒者所应当关心的事啊。班固在《汉书·艺文志》的序言中说，儒家辅佐君主，顺应阴阳规律，阐明教化。这同样是贯通天、地、人道理的表现。又说：'方技这门学问，大概是推论疾病进而推及国家，探求诊察进而了解政事。'如果不是能够通晓天、地、人'三才'奥妙的人，怎么能够推及国家的政事呢？晋代的皇甫谧广泛涉猎古籍和诸子百家的学说，性情沉静、寡欲，有高尚的志向。后来得了'风痹'（风湿痹证），因此开始学习医学，熟读研习经方，于是达到了极高的造诣。

Your servant has heard that one who comprehends Heaven, Earth, and Humanity is called a Confucian scholar (Ru), whereas one who comprehends Heaven and Earth yet fails to comprehend humanity is called a technician (Ji). Although medicine is categorized as a 'skill and technique' (fangji), it is in truth the domain of a Confucian scholar. In his preface to the Treatise on Arts and Letters, Ban Gu stated that Confucians assist the ruler, follow yin and yang, and clarify moral instruction—which likewise embodies the principles of Heaven, Earth, and Humanity. He also wrote: 'Those skilled in medical techniques extrapolate from illness to statecraft and from diagnosis to governance.' Without grasping the profound mysteries of the Three Powers (Heaven, Earth, and Humanity), how could one extend this to the governance of a state? Huangfu Mi of the Jin dynasty extensively mastered the classics and writings of the hundred schools; serene and free of excessive desires, he possessed lofty aspirations. Stricken with wind-impediment (fengbi), he turned to studying medicine, thoroughly read classical formulas, and eventually attained supreme mastery.

💡 1. 术语与医理：'三才'指天、地、人，中医强调'人与天地相参'；'风痹'多指感受风寒湿邪导致的肢体关节疼痛、麻木、重着或屈伸不利。2. 历史与现代对照：将医道升华为'通天地人'的治国安民之道，体现了中国古代'不为良相，便为良医'的文化传统。现代医学虽不再将疾病直接类比国家政事，但公共卫生、社会医学与流行病学同样关注群体健康与社会治理的关系。皇甫谧因病学医，属于典型的'久病成良医'、'病中求医'。3. 适用性：医德修养与整体思维至今适用；将疾病直接等同于政治兴衰属于古代哲学类比，今作文化与医史理解。

取黃帝《素問》、《針經》、《明堂》三部之書，撰為《針灸經》十二卷，曆古儒者之不能及也。或曰：《素問》、《針經》、《明堂》三部之書非黃帝書，似出於戰國。曰：人生天地之間，八尺之軀，髒之堅脆，腑之大小，穀之多少，脈之長短，血之清濁，十二經之血氣大數，皮膚包絡其外，可剖而視之乎？非大聖上智，孰能知之？戰國之人何與焉。大哉，《黃帝內經》十八卷，《針經》三卷最出遠古，皇甫士安能撰而集之。惜簡編脫落者已多，是使文字錯亂，義理顛倒，世失其傳，學之者鮮矣。唐·甄權但修《明堂圖》，孫思邈從而和之，其餘篇第亦不能盡言。

他选取了黄帝时代的《素问》《针经》《明堂》三部书，编撰成《针灸经》（即后世所传《针灸甲乙经》）十二卷，这是历代儒者所不能达到的成就。有人说：《素问》《针经》《明堂》这三部书不是黄帝所作，似乎出于战国时期。答辩说：人生于天地之间，这八尺之躯，五脏的坚韧与脆弱，六腑的大小，水谷容纳的多少，经脉的长短，血液的清浊，十二经脉气血的大致数量，都被皮肤包裹在外面，难道可以剖开身体直接看见吗？如果不是大圣、上智之人，谁能知道这些？战国时期的人怎么能做到这些呢！伟大啊，《黄帝内经》十八卷，《针经》三卷最早出于远古，皇甫士安（皇甫谧，字士安）能够编撰并汇集它们。可惜竹简木牍脱落散失的已经很多，致使文字错乱、义理颠倒，世人失去了完整的传承，学习的人很少了。唐代的甄权只是修定了《明堂图》，孙思邈接着加以附和补充，其余的篇章次序也不能完全说明。

He selected three works—the Suwen (Basic Questions), Zhenjing (Needle Classic), and Mingtang (Bright Hall)—and compiled them into the twelve-fascicle Classic of Acupuncture and Moxibustion, an achievement unmatched by past scholars. Some say these three texts were not written by the Yellow Emperor, appearing instead to originate in the Warring States period. The reply is: Humans live between Heaven and Earth with a frame of eight chi; the firmness or fragility of the viscera, the size of the bowels, the volume of food held, the length of vessels, the clarity or turbidity of blood, and the general quantities of qi and blood in the twelve channels are all enclosed within the skin—could one simply cut open a body and observe them? Without supreme sagacity and highest wisdom, who could know such things? What could people of the Warring States period have to do with this? How vast and great! The eighteen fascicles of the Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic and the three fascicles of the Needle Classic originated in remote antiquity, and Huangfu Shi'an was able to compile and assemble them. Sadly, many bamboo slips had already fallen away and been lost, causing characters to become disordered and meanings inverted, so the world lost its complete transmission and few studied it. Zhen Quan of the Tang dynasty merely revised the Mingtang illustrations, and Sun Simiao followed in echoing this, while the remaining chapters and sequence could not be fully explained.

💡 1. 文献考证与医理：林亿在此记录了关于《内经》《针经》（即《灵枢》）和《明堂》成书年代的争议，以及皇甫谧'类编三部'的贡献。2. 现代考据与医学对照：现代医史学界普遍认为《素问》《灵枢》成书于战国至秦汉时期，并非黄帝亲著，而是托名古圣以尊其道。文中提到'脏之坚脆，腑之大小，谷之多少，脉之长短'，在《灵枢·肠胃》《平人绝谷》《本脏》等篇中确有解剖测量记载（如食道、胃、肠的长度与容量），说明古人并非完全靠玄想，也有朴素的解剖观察与推理。现代医学通过解剖学、影像学、内镜与血液检验直接观察脏器形态、容量与血液理化指标。3. 针灸史与考试意义：了解《甲乙经》源于《素问》《针经》《明堂》三书，是针灸学史高频考点；甄权、孙思邈对明堂孔穴与针灸文献的整理亦为重要医史线索。

國家詔儒臣校正醫書，令取《素問》、《九墟》、《靈樞》、《太素經》、《千金方》及《翼》、《外台秘要》諸家善書校對，玉成繕寫，將備親覽。恭惟主上聖哲文明，光輝上下，孝慈仁德，蒙被眾庶，大頒岐黃，遠及方外，使皇化兆於無窮，和氣浹而充塞。此亦助人靈，順陰陽，明教化之一端云。

朝廷下诏命儒臣校正医书，令调取《素问》《九墟》《灵枢》《太素经》《千金方》以及《千金翼方》《外台秘要》等各家善本进行校对，完成抄写，准备呈送皇上亲自阅览。恭敬地想到当今皇上圣明智慧、文教昌明，光耀上下，孝顺慈爱、仁厚有德，恩泽覆盖普罗大众，广泛颁行岐黄医道，远及四方边远之地，使皇家的教化兆示于无穷，和谐之气普遍充盈。这也是辅助生灵、顺应阴阳、阐明教化的一方面啊。

The state decreed that Confucian officials collate medical texts, ordering them to gather fine editions of the Suwen, Jiuxu, Lingxu, Taisu Jing, Qianjin Fang, as well as Qianjin Yifang and Waitai Miyao for textual collation and fair copying so they may be presented for imperial inspection. Reverently considering our sovereign's sagely wisdom and enlightened civilization, shining above and below with filial piety, compassion, and benevolence covering the masses, widely promulgating the teachings of Qi and Huang even to distant regions beyond the center, so that imperial culture heralds infinity and harmonious qi pervades everywhere—this too is one aspect of aiding living beings, following yin and yang, and clarifying moral instruction.

💡 1. 文献线索：'九墟'、'灵枢'在历史上常与《针经》相关或指代不同传本；《太素经》为隋·杨上善撰；《千金方》《千金翼方》为唐·孙思邈撰；《外台秘要》为唐·王焘撰。宋代校正医书局参校多部典籍，有助于还原《甲乙经》引文原貌并纠正错讹。2. 适用性：赞颂皇恩与教化属于宋代官修序言的常规文体与政治语境，今作历史文献背景了解。

皇甫謐序 Preface by Huangfu Mi.

夫醫道所興，其來久矣。上古神農始嘗草木而知百藥。黃帝咨訪岐伯、伯高、少俞之徒，內考五臟六腑，外綜經絡血氣色候，參之天地，驗之人物，本性命，窮神極變，而針道生焉。其論至妙，雷公受業傳之於後。伊尹以亞聖之才，撰用《神農本草》，以為《湯液》。

医道的兴起，由来已经很久了。上古时期神农氏开始亲尝草木，从而认识了百药。黄帝向岐伯、伯高、少俞等人请教咨询，向内推究五脏六腑的规律，向外综合分析经络、气血与面色形候的变化，参照天地自然的法则，验证于人与万物的实际，以生命本性为根本，穷尽精微变化，于是针刺之道便产生了。这些论述极其精妙，雷公拜师受业并传给了后世。伊尹凭借'亚圣'般的才智，编撰运用《神农本草》的知识，创制了《汤液》。

The rise of the medical Way has a long history. In high antiquity, Shennong first tasted herbs and plants and came to know the hundred medicinals. The Yellow Emperor consulted Qi Bo, Bo Gao, and Shao Yu, examining the five viscera and six bowels within, synthesizing the channels, network vessels, qi, blood, and complexion signs without, correlating them with Heaven and Earth, verifying them against human beings and things, grounding them in life's nature, and exhausting subtle transformations—whereupon the Way of needling was born. These teachings were supremely subtle, and Lei Gong studied them and passed them down to later generations. Yi Yin, possessing the talent of a 'lesser sage,' compiled and applied the knowledge of Shennong's Materia Medica to create decoction therapy (Tangye).

💡 1. 术语与医理：'五脏六腑'为藏象核心；'经络血气色候'指循行通道、气血运行及望诊所见色泽形态；'参之天地，验之人物'体现了天人相应与临床验证相结合的思维。雷公、伯高、少俞、岐伯均为《内经》中重要传道与问答人物；伊尹传有制《汤液经法》之说。2. 现代医学对照：神农尝百草与伊尹制汤液反映了药物经验积累与方剂配伍的形成；黄帝与岐伯等探讨脏腑经络与色候，反映了早期系统化医学理论的建构。现代医学则通过药理学、生理学、解剖学与循证医学验证干预措施。3. 适用性：'参之天地，验之人物'的整体观察与实践验证精神对临床思维仍有启发；神话传说人物（神农、黄帝、伊尹等）属于医学起源的文化叙事。

中古名醫有俞跗，醫緩，扁鵲，秦有醫和，漢有倉公。其論皆經理識本，非徒診病而已。漢有華佗，張仲景，華佗奇方異治，施世者多，亦不能盡記其本末。若知直祭酒劉季琰病發於畏惡，治之而瘥。雲：“後九年季琰病應發，發當有感，仍本於畏惡，病動必死。”終如其言。仲景見侍中王仲宣時年二十余。謂曰：“君有病，四十當眉落，眉落半年而死。”令含服五石湯可免。仲宣嫌其言忤，受湯勿服。居三日，仲景見仲宣謂曰：“服湯否？”仲宣曰：“已服。”仲景曰：“色候固非服湯之診，君何輕命也！”仲宣猶不信。後二十年果眉落，後一百八十七日而死，終如其言。此二事雖扁鵲，倉公無以加也。華佗性惡矜技，終以戮死。仲景論廣伊尹《湯液》為十數卷，用之多驗。近代太醫令王叔和撰次仲景遺論甚精，皆可施用。按《七略》藝文誌，《黃帝內經》十八卷。今有《針經》九卷，《素問》九卷，二九十八卷，即《內經》也。亦有所亡失，其論遐遠，然稱述多而切事少，有不編次。比按倉公傳，其學皆出於《素問》，《素問》論病精微，《九卷》是原本經脈，其義深奧，不易覽也。又有《明堂孔穴針灸治要》，皆黃帝岐伯遺事也。三部同歸，文多重複，錯互非一。

中古时期的名医有俞跗、医缓、扁鹊，秦国有医和，汉代有仓公（淳于意）。他们的医论都注重条理经络、把握疾病根本，不只是单纯诊断疾病而已。汉代有华佗、张仲景，华佗奇方异治很多，施治于世人甚广，但也无法把每件事的始末都完全记录下来。比如他诊知直祭酒刘季琰的病起于畏惧厌恶之情（一说指恶寒），治疗后痊愈了，却说：'九年之后季琰的病应当发作，发作时必有所触动，仍然源于畏惧厌恶，一旦病发必定死亡。'最终果然如他所言。张仲景见到侍中王仲宣时，王仲宣二十多岁。仲景对他说：'您有病，四十岁时应当眉毛脱落，眉毛脱落半年后就会死。'让他含服五石汤可以免除这一灾祸。王仲宣嫌他的话冲撞不吉利，接过汤药却没有服用。过了三天，仲景见到王仲宣问道：'服汤了吗？'王仲宣说：'已经服用了。'仲景说：'从气色形候来看，本来就不是服了汤药的征象，您为什么轻视自己的生命呢？'王仲宣还是不信。二十年后果然眉毛脱落，一百八十七天后去世，最终果然如他所言。这两件事即使扁鹊、仓公在世也无法超越。华佗性情乖戾、自恃医技，最终因此被杀。张仲景推演扩充伊尹的《汤液》成为十几卷书，应用起来多有灵验。近世太医令王叔和编次整理张仲景的遗论非常精审，都可以付诸临床应用。按照《七略》和《艺文志》的记载，《黄帝内经》有十八卷。如今有《针经》九卷、《素问》九卷，二九共十八卷，这就是《内经》。但也有散失，它的论述宏大深远，然而引述铺陈多而切合具体实务少，有的还没有编排次序。对比考察《仓公传》，仓公的医学大多出于《素问》，《素问》论述疾病精深微妙，《九卷》是阐述经脉本源的，其义理深奥，不容易一览而懂。另外还有《明堂孔穴针灸治要》，都是黄帝、岐伯留下的事迹与医理。这三部书旨归相同，但文字多有重复，错乱交叉之处不止一处。

Notable physicians of antiquity included Yu Fu, Yi Huan, and Bian Que; Qin had Yi He, and Han had Cang Gong (Chunyu Yi). Their theories all grasped fundamental principles and roots rather than merely diagnosing illness. The Han dynasty had Hua Tuo and Zhang Zhongjing. Hua Tuo had many extraordinary formulas and unusual treatments and treated many people in the world, though the full beginnings and endings of all cases could not be recorded. For instance, he knew that Direct Libationer Liu Jiyan's illness arose from dread and aversion (some read: aversion to cold); after treatment he recovered, yet Hua Tuo said: 'Nine years later Jiyan's illness should recur, triggered by an event and still rooted in dread and aversion; once aroused, it will certainly be fatal.' In the end it happened just as he said. Zhang Zhongjing met Palace Attendant Wang Zhongxuan when Wang was in his twenties and told him: 'You have a latent illness; at forty your eyebrows will fall out, and half a year after your eyebrows fall out you will die.' He instructed him to hold and swallow Wushi Decoction (Five-Stone Decoction) to avoid this. Wang Zhongxuan disliked the blunt, offending remark, took the medicine, and did not drink it. Three days later, Zhongjing saw him and asked, 'Did you take the decoction?' Zhongxuan replied, 'I have taken it.' Zhongjing said, 'Your complexion and signs are certainly not those of one who has taken the decoction—why do you treat your life so lightly?' Wang Zhongxuan still did not believe him. Twenty years later his eyebrows indeed fell out, and 187 days after that he died, fulfilling the prediction. Even Bian Que and Cang Gong could not surpass these two instances. Hua Tuo was ill-tempered and proud of his skill, and was executed in the end. Zhang Zhongjing expanded upon Yi Yin's Tangye into more than ten fascicles, and its clinical use proved effective in many cases. In recent times, Imperial Medical Director Wang Shuhe compiled Zhang Zhongjing's surviving writings with great precision, and all can be put into practice. According to the Seven Summaries (Qilue) and the Treatise on Arts and Letters, the Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic comprised eighteen fascicles. Today there are nine fascicles of the Needle Classic and nine fascicles of the Basic Questions—two times nine makes eighteen fascicles—which constitute the Inner Classic. Some parts have also been lost; its discussions are vast and distant, with abundant exposition but less that is immediately practical, and some sections lack systematic ordering. Comparing this with Cang Gong's biography, his learning all derived from the Suwen; the Suwen discusses illness subtly and minutely, while the Nine Fascicles are foundational to the channels and vessels, with profound meaning not easily grasped at a glance. There is also the Essential Acupuncture and Moxibustion Therapeutics of the Mingtang Points, all being legacies of the Yellow Emperor and Qi Bo. These three texts share the same ultimate aim, yet contain much repetition and numerous misplaced or overlapping passages.

💡 1. 医史与文献价值：本段串联了俞跗、医缓、扁鹊、医和、仓公、华佗、张仲景、王叔和等历代名医，并明确指出皇甫谧编撰《甲乙经》的直接动机——《素问》《针经》（九卷）、《明堂孔穴针灸治要》三书旨归一致但文字重复错乱，故需加以汇整。2. 病例与预后叙事的现代理解：刘季琰、王仲宣的案例带有浓厚的古代医案与预后判断色彩。王仲宣案例中'眉落'及慢性消耗性或严重全身性疾病预后，在现代医学中可能与某些慢性消耗性疾病、内分泌/代谢或肿瘤/麻风等晚期表现相关，但古人关于'百八十七日而死'的精确时间预测属于历史叙事与经验预估，不能作为现代临床精确生存期判断依据。文中提及'五石汤'，需注意古代含矿物药（石类）的方剂若不当使用可能存在重金属或毒性风险，现代临床已极少盲目使用。3. 针灸与中医基础考点：《仓公传》学出《素问》；《素问》重病机理论，《九卷》（即《灵枢》）重经脉本源，《明堂》重孔穴治要。'三部同归，文多重复，错互非一'是理解《甲乙经》编纂体例的关键。

甘露中，吾病風加苦聾百日，方治要皆淺近。乃撰集三部，使事類相從，刪其浮辭，除其重複，論其精要，至為十二卷。《易》曰：“觀其所聚，而天地之情事見矣。”況物理乎。事類相從，聚之義也。夫受先人之體，有八尺之軀，而不知醫事，此所謂遊魂耳！若不精通於於醫道，雖有忠孝之心，仁慈之性，君父危困，赤子塗地，無以濟之，此固聖賢所以精思極論盡其理也。由此言之，焉可忽乎？其本論其文有理，雖不切於近事，不甚刪也。若必精要，俟其閑暇，當撰核以為教經云爾。

曹魏甘露年间，我患了'风'病（中风或风邪所致病证），加上耳聋痛苦长达一百天，当时所见的方药治疗要诀都很浅近。于是我编撰汇集了《素问》《针经》《明堂》三部书，让同类事物相互归聚，删去浮泛的辞藻，剔除重复的内容，阐发其中的精要，最终编成十二卷。《周易》说：'观察它们所汇聚之处，天地间的情状事理就可以显现出来了。'何况是事物的道理呢？同类相从，就是'聚'的意思。承受父母先人赐予的身体，拥有八尺之躯，却不懂得医学常识，这就是所说的'游魂'罢了！如果不精通医道，即使有忠孝之心、仁慈之性，当君主、父母处于危难困境，百姓流离陷入水深火热时，也无法挽救他们，这正是圣贤之所以深入思考、极力推论、穷尽其理的原因。由此说来，怎么可以忽视呢？对于那些基础理论、文辞有道理的内容，即使不切合眼前的具体实务，也不做过多的删削。如果一定要追求极致精要，等日后有了空闲，应当再作编核作为教学范本（'教经'）罢了。

During the Ganlu era, I suffered from a wind affliction compounded by a hundred days of distressing deafness, and the treatment essentials then available were all rather superficial. Thus I compiled and assembled the three works, grouping related topics together, deleting superfluous wording, removing repetitions, and expounding their essence, resulting in twelve fascicles. The Book of Changes says: 'Observe what is gathered together, and the affairs and principles of Heaven and Earth become visible.' How much more so the principles of physical things! Grouping related matters together is the meaning of 'gathering.' To receive a body from one's ancestors and possess an eight-chi frame yet know nothing of medical matters is what is called a mere 'wandering soul'! If one is not thoroughly versed in the medical Way, even with a loyal and filial heart and a compassionate nature, when ruler or parents are in grave distress or the common people are cast into misery, one has no way to save them; this is precisely why sages and worthies thought deeply, reasoned thoroughly, and exhausted the underlying principles. From this perspective, how could it be neglected? Where the foundational theories and text have sound rationale, even if not directly tied to immediate practical affairs, I did not delete them heavily. If extreme conciseness is desired, when leisure permits I shall compile and examine them to serve as a teaching canon.

序例 Introductory Rules and Editorial Conventions.

諸問，黃帝及雷公皆曰“問”。其對也，黃帝曰“答”，岐伯之徒皆曰“對”。上章問及對已有名字者，則下章但言“問”言“對”，亦不更說名字也。若人異則重複更名字。此則其例也。諸言“主之”者可灸可刺，其言“刺之”者不可灸，言“灸之”者不可刺，亦其例也。

凡是提问，黄帝和雷公都称为'问'。至于回答，黄帝称为'答'，岐伯等人（岐伯之徒）都称为'对'。如果上一章的提问和回答已经写明人名，那么下一章就只写'问'、写'对'，不再重复写明人名。如果说话的人物不同，就再次写明人名。这就是关于称谓的体例。凡是条文中说'主之'的穴位或病证，既可以艾灸也可以针刺；凡是说'刺之'的，就不宜（不可）艾灸；说'灸之'的，就不宜（不可）针刺。这也是本书的体例。

Whenever a question is asked, both the Yellow Emperor and Lei Gong are designated as 'asks' (wen). As for replies, the Yellow Emperor is designated as 'answers' (da), whereas Qi Bo and his associates are all designated as 'replies' (dui). If the questioner and respondent already have their names stated in a preceding section, the following section simply writes 'asks' and 'replies' without repeating their names. If the speaker changes, the name is stated again. Such is the convention for dialogue. Whenever a passage states 'governs it' (zhu zhi), both moxibustion and needling may be applied; where it states 'needle it' (ci zhi), moxibustion should not be applied; and where it states 'moxibust it' (jiu zhi), needling should not be applied. This is also a convention of the text.

💡 1. 针灸核心体例与临床意义：'主之'可刺可灸、'刺之'不可灸、'灸之'不可刺，是《针灸甲乙经》极具实用价值的施治规范。'不可灸'常见于热证、阴虚阳亢、颜面五官或重要血管/危险三角区及特定禁灸穴；'不可刺'常见于深部重要脏器毗邻处、大血管、孕妇禁针穴或虚弱/特定禁忌部位。2. 现代针灸临床与考试对照：在现代针灸学和执业医师考试中，'主之'、'刺之'、'灸之'的区分体现了辨证施治与刺灸宜忌。现代临床中，是否施灸或针刺需结合患者体质、证候寒热虚实、局部解剖安全性（如避免损伤大血管、神经、胸膜、脏器及局部感染/破损）综合判断，并非绝对僵化，但古人的安全警示意识非常值得借鉴。

7經筋第六

7 经筋第六

7. The Sinew Channels (Jingjin).

💡 十二经筋是十二经脉之气濡养筋肉骨节的体系，不入脏腑，结聚于关节、骨骼周围，主司运动与约束骨骼。常见病候为转筋、拘挛、掣痛等。经筋是古人对肌肉、筋腱分布与牵拉关系的描述模型；现代有人拿它与肌筋膜、肌腱、韧带及扳机点作对照理解，分布上有相似处，但二者不是同一概念，不能互相替代。

8骨度腸度腸胃所受第七

8 骨度肠度肠胃所受第七

8. Measurements of Bones, Intestines, and Capacities of the Stomach and Intestines.

💡 '骨度'即骨度分寸法，规定了人体各部骨骼之间的折量长度，是现代针灸取穴'骨度分寸定位法'的源头（如前发际至后发际12寸、胸骨剑突至耻骨联合上缘9寸、肘横纹至腕横纹12寸、腋前纹头至肘横纹9寸、股骨大转子至腘横纹19寸、腘横纹至外踝尖16寸、内踝尖至足底3寸等）。'肠度肠胃所受'则记载消化道长度与容纳量，体现古代解剖测量的早期成果。
