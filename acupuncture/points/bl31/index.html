上髎 Shàngliáo

Upper Crevice · High risk高危

⚠️ Sacral-foramen and neural-risk point骶孔与神经风险穴 Neural structures lie in the posterior sacral foramen and vessels are nearby. Only trained practitioners should use anatomical landmarks; electric-shock pain, persistent numbness, or weakness requires immediate cessation and medical assessment.后骶孔内有神经结构，邻近血管。只可由受训人员按解剖标志操作；若出现电击样痛、持续麻木或无力，应立即停止并寻求医疗评估。

📍 Location定位★

In the sacral region, at the first posterior sacral foramen.骶区，第 1 后骶孔。

💡 The 1st posterior sacral foramen lies between the posterior superior iliac spine and the midline; the foramina descend about a finger-breadth apart; count down to foramen 1 — the point is in the foramen.髂后上棘与后正中线之间约是第 1 骶后孔（上髎），向下约每隔一横指一个孔；逐孔数到第 1 骶后孔，穴就在孔中。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique inferomedially向内下方斜刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

Angle slightly inferomedially to follow the foramen; too deep can exit the anterior sacral foramen into the pelvis. Contraindicated in pregnancy.针尖稍向内下，顺孔刺入；过深可穿出骶前孔进入盆腔。孕妇禁针。

🛡 Safety安全

The needle path targets a posterior sacral foramen and sacral neural structures; only trained practitioners should control direction and depth from bony landmarks to avoid neurovascular injury. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.针路直指后骶孔与骶神经结构；必须由受训人员按骨性标志控制方向和深度，避免神经血管损伤。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone or upright sitting slightly leaning forward with gluteal muscles relaxed; prone is safest for clinical needling to palpate the posterior sacral foramen and control the inferomedial angle. Lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.俯卧位或正坐微前倾位，臀部肌肉放松；临床施针以俯卧位最稳妥，便于触及骶后孔与控制向内下方斜刺角度。腰骶部穴，孕期禁针。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the posterior superior iliac spine (the bump below the low-back dimple); the hollow medial to it at the same level is the second posterior sacral foramen (BL32). Move up and slightly out from BL32 to the next hollow: the first foramen, where BL31 lies, roughly between the top of the iliac spine and the midline. The foramina lie between the midline and the 1.5-cun line, not on it (that line is BL27).先摸髂后上棘（腰窝下的骨突），其内侧平髂后上棘的凹陷是第 2 骶后孔（次髎）；从次髎向上、略向外摸到的凹陷就是第 1 骶后孔——上髎在孔中，约当髂后上棘上缘与后正中线之间。骶后孔在后正中线与 1.5 寸线之间，不在 1.5 寸线上（那是小肠俞）。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Skin, subcutaneous tissue, the origin of gluteus maximus and the erector spinae aponeurosis, then into the posterior sacral foramen. It carries the dorsal ramus of S1 and its vessels and opens into the sacral canal, which leads through the anterior foramina into the pelvis. The needle is meant to enter the foramen (spread of sensation to the sacrum, perineum or lower abdomen is the intended response); the real danger is excessive depth — in a thin person a needle passing out of the anterior foramen can injure the rectum or pelvic venous plexus. The first foramen is the largest and most lateral of the four.皮肤、皮下组织、臀大肌起始部与竖脊肌腱膜，进入第 1 骶后孔。孔内有第 1 骶神经后支与伴行血管；孔通向骶管，骶管再经骶前孔通盆腔。刺法本来就是针入孔中（针感向骶部、会阴、小腹放散为得气），真正要防的是过深——瘦人针穿出骶前孔可伤及直肠、盆腔静脉丛。第 1 骶后孔是四孔中最大、最外的一个。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a fingertip gently over the posterolateral sacrum to feel the bony depression of the first posterior sacral foramen, where local tenderness or soreness is common. If pressure or needling triggers an electric-shock tingling radiating toward the buttock, perineum, or lower limb, a sacral nerve branch is stimulated—release pressure or withdraw and adjust immediately, never force deep insertion.指腹在骶骨后外侧方由外向内或由上向下轻按滑动，可摸到第 1 后骶孔的骨凹陷，局部常有明显压痛或酸胀感；若指压或针刺引起向臀部、会阴或下肢放射的触电样麻痛，提示刺激到骶神经分支，应立即减压或退针调整，切忌强行深刺。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting laterally onto the iliac wing or deep gluteus maximus belly: missing the first posterior sacral foramen depression throws off angle and depth, risking deep gluteal hematoma or sharp pain with poor effect, plus pregnancy safety risks on lumbosacral points.取偏外侧（误入髂骨翼或臀肌深部肌腹）：未能摸准第 1 后骶孔骨凹陷，斜刺方向与深度失准，易造成臀大肌深部血肿或刺痛明显而疗效不足；孕期误刺腰骶部更有安全风险。
  • Pushing on after entering the foramen: 1–1.5 cun in the foramen is enough, and deeper can exit the anterior foramen into the pelvis. If sharp electric pain shoots to the perineum or leg, withdraw slightly and do not lift and thrust. Not needled in pregnancy (a lumbosacral point, forbidden after the third month).进孔后继续深推：针在孔内 1–1.5 寸已足，再深可穿出骶前孔入盆腔；出现向会阴、下肢的剧烈触电感时退针少许，不要提插。孕妇禁针（腰骶部，怀孕三个月以上禁针，孕期一律不针）。
  • Misidentifying the sacral foramen level (confusing BL32 at the second or BL33 at the third foramen with BL31): shifts the segmental target and reduces specificity for conditions involving S1 innervation of the lumbosacral region, pelvic floor, and lower limb.取错骶孔节段（误将第 2 后骶孔次髎或第 3 后骶孔中髎当作上髎）：导致节段错位，对骶 1 神经节段支配的腰骶、盆底及下肢症状针对性下降。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Suited when aching centers on the lumbosacral region near the sacral foramina with buttock or pelvic referral; for simple lumbar strain or paraspinal lumbar pain, BL23, BL25, and BL40 are often chosen first, and lumbosacral points are avoided in pregnancy.偏重腰骶部及骶孔附近酸胀痛、伴臀部或盆腔牵涉感时可选；单纯腰肌劳损或腰椎棘突旁痛多先选肾俞、大肠俞、委中，腰骶部孕期不针。

Sciatica坐骨神经痛 ⚖️

Often paired in the Baliao group or locally with GB30, BL40, and GB34 for buttock-sacral and posterior leg radiating pain; distal or sciatic-trunk points such as GB30, BL40, and BL57 are more commonly primary for lower-limb radiation.常作为八髎组穴或局部穴配合环跳、委中、阳陵泉用于臀骶部及下肢后侧放射痛；单用远端穴或坐骨神经干穴位如环跳、委中、承山更常作为下肢循经主治。

Period pain痛经

Used for dysmenorrhea with marked lumbosacral cold ache or heaviness, often paired with BL32, BL33, SP6, and CV4; isolated lower abdominal cramping often relies more on CV4, CV6, SP8, and SP6, with lumbosacral needling contraindicated in pregnancy.用于痛经伴明显腰骶冷痛或坠胀者，常与次髎、中髎、三阴交、关元配伍；单纯下腹绞痛或寒凝血瘀常以关元、气海、地机、三阴交为主，孕期禁针。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Can be used for urinary retention or enuresis with lumbosacral discomfort alongside BL33, BL34, BL28, CV3, and SP6; acute/chronic retention or urinary infection requires medical evaluation first, and lumbosacral needling is contraindicated in pregnancy.可用于癃闭或遗尿伴腰骶部不适者，多与中髎、下髎、膀胱俞、中极、三阴交合用；急慢性尿潴留或泌尿系感染须先医疗评估，且腰骶部孕期禁针。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is BL31 Shàngliáo?上髎（BL31）在哪里？

In the sacral region, at the first posterior sacral foramen.骶区，第 1 后骶孔。

How is BL31 needled?上髎（BL31）怎么刺？

Oblique inferomedially; 1–1.5 cun.向内下方斜刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at BL31?上髎（BL31）最要注意的安全问题？

The needle path targets a posterior sacral foramen and sacral neural structures; only trained practitioners should control direction and depth from bony landmarks to avoid neurovascular injury. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.针路直指后骶孔与骶神经结构；必须由受训人员按骨性标志控制方向和深度，避免神经血管损伤。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
