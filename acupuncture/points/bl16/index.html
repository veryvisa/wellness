📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated upright with the head slightly bowed and shoulders relaxed, arms resting naturally or on a table so the upper back muscles stay loose.正坐位，低头微含胸，双臂自然下垂或置于桌上，背部肌肉放松。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: below the T6 spine.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），督俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 6 胸椎棘突下定。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate the C7 spinous process (the most prominent cervical-thoracic spinous process that moves slightly with neck rotation), count down to below the T6 spinous process, and measure 1.5 cun laterally; cross-check against the inferior angle of the scapula (near T7), while relying on spinous-process counting.先定位第 7 颈椎棘突（大椎，低头时颈胸交界最隆起且随转颈微动者），向下数至第 6 胸椎棘突下缘，再向外侧旁开 1.5 寸即是；可结合肩胛骨下角平第 7 胸椎棘突作上下对照，但以棘突计数为准。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure about two finger-widths (index and middle fingers held together, roughly 1.5 cun) laterally from just below the T6 spinous process. Finger widths are only a rough estimate and must be checked against the midline spinous process and body build.以患者食指、中指并拢（约 1.5 寸）横量，从第 6 胸椎棘突下缘向患侧旁开约两横指宽处。指宽仅作辅助估算，需结合棘突中线与体格体型核对。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located 1.5 cun lateral to the lower border of the T6 spinous process, passing through posterior cutaneous branches of thoracic spinal nerves and dorsal branch vessels superficially, then trapezius, rhomboids or erector spinae, followed by intercostal muscles, the sixth or seventh rib and the intercostal neurovascular bundle, with pleura and lung deeper still. Always angle obliquely toward the spine and never needle deeply outward or straight in, which risks injuring the pleura and lung and causing pneumothorax.位于第 6 胸椎棘突下旁开 1.5 寸处，浅层有胸脊神经后支皮支与背侧支血管，深层依次为斜方肌、菱形肌或竖脊肌，再深为肋间肌、第 6 或第 7 肋骨及肋间神经血管束，最深处邻近胸膜腔与肺。进针必须向脊柱方向斜刺，严禁向外侧深刺或直刺深扎，以免伤及胸膜和肺引发气胸。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press gently with the fingertip about 1.5 cun lateral to the inferior border of the T6 spinous process to feel soreness or distension over the erector spinae; keep palpation and needling controlled rather than pressing deeply into an intercostal space, which can cause sharp pain or risk pneumothorax.指腹在 T6 棘突下旁开约 1.5 寸处轻柔按压，常可触及竖脊肌外缘或肌腹间的酸胀感；触诊与进针均需控制力度，避免重按或向肋间隙深压引起锐痛或气胸风险。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Mistaking it for BL15 (Xinshu) or BL17 (Geshu): miscounting thoracic spinous processes by one level up or down lands at T5 or T7 instead of T6, shifting the segmental level.误取为 BL15（心俞）或 BL17（膈俞）：胸椎棘突计数偏上或偏下 1 节，分别落在 T5 或 T7 棘突下旁开 1.5 寸，导致对应背俞节段错位。
  • Needling deeply outward or too deeply perpendicularly: angling away from the spine or exceeding the 0.5–0.8 cun depth can injure the intercostal neurovascular bundle or pierce the pleura and lung, causing pneumothorax; always angle obliquely toward the spine.向外侧深刺或直刺过深：针尖偏离脊柱方向或超过 0.5–0.8 寸安全深度，易穿过肋间肌伤及肋间血管神经束或胸膜与肺，诱发气胸；须严格向脊柱方向斜刺。
  • Estimating level blindly from the medial border or inferior angle of the scapula: raising or shrugging the shoulder shifts the scapula upward, so relying on the inferior angle alone can misplace the level by a segment or more.按肩胛骨内侧缘或下角盲目定高矮：举臂或耸肩时肩胛骨会上移，若单凭肩胛骨下角推算胸椎节段易出现 1 节以上误差。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

May serve as a secondary adjunct when cough accompanies upper-back tightness or chest stuffiness, whereas BL16 is not a primary first-choice point for acute external cough or chronic deficiency cough.咳嗽兼见上胸背拘急或胸闷时可作配伍参考；外感咳嗽或久咳虚证一般不以 BL16 为首选主穴。

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

Can be an adjunct for chest-back qi regulation in palpitations, but clinicians more commonly rely on points such as PC6, HT7, or BL15, and structural arrhythmias or chest pain require medical evaluation first.心悸不适时可作为胸背气机调理的辅助穴位，但临床更常首选内关 PC6、神门 HT7、心俞 BL15 等常用穴，且器质性心律失常或胸痛须先就医评估。
