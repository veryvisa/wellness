Hou's Black Powder侯氏黑散

Hóu Shì Hēi Sàn · Zhang Zhongjing, Essentials from the Golden Cabinet (Eastern Han), chapter on wind-stroke and joint-running wind; the Waitai Miyao cites it for wind epilepsy东汉·张仲景《金匮要略·中风历节病脉证并治》（《外台秘要》引作治风癫）

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Hou's Black Powder侯氏黑散
⚠️ Alum is poisonous礬石有毒 Alum is poisonous in the formula; must follow medical advice and not self-medicate to avoid poisoning.方中礬石为有毒之品，必须遵医嘱，不可自行服用，以免中毒。

Original text原文

「治大風四肢煩重，心中惡寒不足者。《外臺》治風癲。」——金匮要略

Jingui Yaolue (Essentials from the Golden Cabinet)

Treats wind-stroke with heaviness and discomfort in the four limbs and heart coldness with deficiency. Wai Tai treats wind-stroke dementia.治疗大风病四肢烦重，心中恶寒不足之人。《外臺秘要》用于治疗风癫。

「寸口脈遲而緩，遲則為寒，緩則為虛；營緩則為亡血，衛緩則為中風。邪氣中經，則身癢而癮疹；心氣不足，邪氣入中，則胸滿而短氣。」——金匮要略

Jingui Yaolue (Essentials from the Golden Cabinet)

The pulse at the cun position is slow and moderate. Slowness indicates cold, moderation indicates deficiency. Moderate qi in the ying indicates blood loss, moderate qi in the wei indicates wind-stroke. When evil qi enters the channels, there is itching and urticaria on the body; if heart qi is deficient and evil qi enters the interior, there is fullness in the chest and shortness of breath.寸口脉迟而缓，迟则为寒，缓则为虚；营缓则为亡血，卫缓则为中风。邪气中经，则身痒而瘾疹；心气不足，邪气入中，则胸满而短气。

Cautions使用注意

Alum in this formula is toxic: study only, never make or take it yourself. The original text forbids fish, meat and garlic during the course and asks for cold food. A sudden drooping face, one-sided weakness or numbness, or slurred speech is a stroke sign: call 911 at once; do not wait for any medicine.方中矾石有毒，只作学习，不可自行配服；原书嘱服药期间忌鱼肉、大蒜，宜冷食。突发口眼歪斜、一侧肢体无力麻木、言语不清，是中风信号，立即拨 911，不要等服药。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Contains ginseng, guizhi, ganjiang, danggui, chuanxiong; following the site rule of minimum dose in formulas, the strictest level in the formula is taken (pregnancy caution).本方含人参、桂枝、干姜、当归、川芎，按本站按最小剂量使用规则，在方中取最严者（妊娠期慎用）。
