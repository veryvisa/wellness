Form 5: Bear circling第5式 · 熊运

2 circles clockwise, then 2 anticlockwise顺时针 2 圈、逆时针 2 圈

Xióng yùn

Qigong养生功法 › Wuqinxi (Five Animals)五禽戏 › Form 5: Bear circling第5式 · 熊运

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Straight after a meal: wait an hour, or keep the circles very small刚吃完饭：饭后一小时内先不做，或圈很小
  • Disc problems or a flare of back pain: keep the circles small enough to be pain-free腰椎间盘突出、腰痛发作：圈小到不痛
  • Vertigo: keep the head still and move only the waist and belly眩晕：头保持不动，只动腰腹
  • Pregnancy: do not press on the belly, keep the circles small, and check with your doctor first怀孕：不压迫腹部，圈小，先问医生

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Shape the hands into 'bear paws' (four fingers curled, thumb pressed on the index finger, the web of the thumb rounded) in front of the lower belly, knees slightly soft两手握成「熊掌」（四指弯曲、拇指压在食指上，虎口撑圆），放在下腹前，两腿微屈
  2. With the waist and belly as the axis, circle the upper body slowly clockwise: right, forward, left, back; the hands trace a circle in front of the belly, past the right ribs, upper belly, left ribs and back to the lower belly以腰腹为轴，上身顺时针慢慢转圈：向右、向前、向左、向后；两手跟着在腹前划圆，经右肋、上腹、左肋回到下腹
  3. After 2 clockwise circles, do 2 anticlockwise顺时针做 2 圈后，换逆时针 2 圈
  4. At the end, open the hands and let them hang at the sides结束时两手松开，自然垂在体侧

🌬 Breathe in as the trunk rises and moves back, out as it leans forward and down; or breathe naturally.上身向上提、向后时吸气，向前俯、向下时呼气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • The movement is in the waist and belly, not the hips and knees; the lower body barely moves动的是腰腹，不是胯和膝；下半身基本不动
  • The body carries the hands round; the hands do not circle on their own两手是被身体带着划圆，不是自己转
  • Small, slow circles: the slower, the steadier圈小而慢，越慢越稳

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Swinging the hips and knees as if hula-hooping转胯摆膝，像扭腰舞
  • Circling the head widely and getting dizzy头跟着大幅度转圈，转晕
  • Circling the hands while the body stays still两手自己划圈，身体不动

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Sit on a chair and make small circles with the upper body only, the hands following in front of the belly.坐在椅子上，只做上身的小圈，两手在腹前跟着划。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Traditional teaching says Bear Circling imitates the slow, heavy steadiness of a bear rolling its body; tradition pairs the bear with the spleen and stomach and describes the circling of the waist as 'kneading' the middle of the body. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.传统教法说熊运学熊晃着身子慢慢走动的笨拙沉稳；传统上把熊配脾胃，说腰腹的圆转是在「揉」中焦。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
