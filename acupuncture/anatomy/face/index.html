Points and what lies under them穴位与针下要点

LI19Kǒuhéliáo口禾髎 — The upper lip is thin: the superior labial artery lies just deep to orbicularis oris with the maxillary alveolar bone behind, so bleeding is common and the needle must not pierce into the mouth.上唇很薄：口轮匝肌深面就是上唇动脉，再深是上颌骨牙槽突，出血多见、刺穿到口腔黏膜要避免。Take care需谨慎LI20Yíngxiāng迎香 — The facial artery and vein climb right beside the ala; the facial vein connects through the angular vein to the cavernous sinus inside the skull, so this is the 'danger triangle' — never needle through infected skin.面动静脉就在鼻翼旁上行；面静脉经内眦静脉与颅内海绵窦相通，这里属「危险三角」，皮肤有感染就不针。Take care需谨慎
