Where is GB27 Wǔshū?五枢（GB27）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, level with 3 cun below the umbilicus, medial to the anterior superior iliac spine.下腹部，横平脐下 3 寸，髂前上棘内侧。

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of GB27?五枢（GB27）是哪类特定穴？

Main safety point at GB27?五枢（GB27）最要注意的安全问题？
