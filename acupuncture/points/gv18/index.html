Scalp and neck/shoulder tenderness can occur, but diagnosis depends on headache features; frequent or new persistent headache needs assessment.可见头皮和颈肩肌压痛，但诊断取决于头痛特征；发作频繁或新发持续头痛应评估。

Where is GV18 Qiángjiān?强间（GV18）在哪里？

On the head, 4 cun above the posterior hairline (1.5 cun above GV17).头部，后发际正中直上 4 寸（脑户上 1.5 寸）。

How is GV18 needled?强间（GV18）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at GV18?强间（GV18）最要注意的安全问题？

The skull lies directly beneath; needle transversely beneath the galea only, never perpendicular, and press after withdrawal.头皮下即颅骨，只平刺于腱膜下层，不可垂直进针；出针按压防血肿。
