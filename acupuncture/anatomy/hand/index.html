Points and what lies under them穴位与针下要点

LU10Yújì鱼际 — The thenar muscles are thick and sensitive, with the 1st metacarpal right beneath.鱼际肌群厚而敏感，深面贴着第 1 掌骨。Lower risk较安全LU11Shàoshāng少商 — Periosteum and nail root lie just under the skin amid dense vessel and nerve endings: hence pricking to bleed, and it hurts.皮下即骨膜与甲根，血管神经末梢密集，所以点刺出血、痛感明显。Lower risk较安全LI1Shāngyáng商阳 — The nail bed and distal phalanx lie a millimetre or two beneath the skin, with almost no soft tissue to needle: hence a shallow insertion or a bleeding prick.皮下一两毫米就是甲床与末节指骨，几乎没有可刺的软组织：所以只浅刺或点刺出血。Lower risk较安全LI2Èrjiān二间 — Just distal to the joint the needle soon meets tendon and the base of the proximal phalanx, with digital nerves and vessels along the side: stop at 0.2–0.3 cun.关节远侧皮下很快到肌腱和近节指骨底，指神经血管走在两侧：浅刺 0.2–0.3 寸即止。Lower risk较安全LI3Sānjiān三间 — The needle runs along the radial side of the 2nd metacarpal head through the 1st dorsal interosseous toward the flexor tendons, where the proper palmar digital artery and nerve lie deep.针贴第 2 掌骨头桡侧进入，经第 1 骨间背侧肌到屈肌腱旁，深处走着指掌侧固有动脉和神经。Lower risk较安全LI4Hégǔ合谷 — The needle passes through the 1st dorsal interosseous into adductor pollicis; the radial artery dives into the palm at the proximal end of the web, so go carefully the deeper and more proximal you are.针穿第 1 骨间背侧肌到拇收肌；虎口近端是桡动脉穿入手掌之处，越往近端越深越要小心。Take care需谨慎
