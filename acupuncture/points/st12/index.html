Where is ST12 Quēpén?缺盆（ST12）在哪里？

In the lateral neck region, in the greater supraclavicular fossa, in the depression above the clavicle, 4 cun lateral to the anterior midline.颈外侧区，锁骨上大窝，锁骨上缘凹陷中，前正中线旁开 4 寸。

How is ST12 needled?缺盆（ST12）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique, never downward; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺或斜刺，不向下；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at ST12?缺盆（ST12）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleural dome lies shallow under the fossa: deep or downward insertion can cause pneumothorax and injure the subclavian artery or brachial plexus — the Jiayi Jing warns that deep needling makes the breath rebel. Forbidden in pregnancy.胸膜顶在锁骨上窝深面很浅，深刺或向下刺可致气胸，还可伤锁骨下动脉与臂丛；《甲乙经》警告「刺太深，令人逆息」。孕期禁针。
