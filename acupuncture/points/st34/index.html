ST34

梁丘 Liángqiū

Beam Mound · Lower risk较安全

Xi-cleft point郄穴

📍 Location定位★

On the anterior thigh, 2 cun above the base of the patella, between vastus lateralis and the rectus femoris tendon.股前区，髌底上 2 寸，股外侧肌与股直肌肌腱之间。

💡 Straighten the knee forcefully: a groove between the two muscles appears 2 cun above the lateral patellar base; take the point in it once relaxed.伸膝用力，髌底外上方 2 寸处两肌之间显出一条沟，放松后在沟中取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

🛡 Safety安全

Avoid visible veins; the thigh is muscular, so set depth by build and redirect for radiating tingling.避开可见浅静脉；大腿肌层厚，按体型控制深度，出现放射性麻痛退针调整。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治★

Acute epigastric pain急性胃痛 ⚖️Swollen painful knee, paralysis of the leg膝肿痛、下肢不遂 ⚖️Breast abscess乳痈

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Knee pain膝痛 ⚖️

Where is ST34 Liángqiū?梁丘（ST34）在哪里？

On the anterior thigh, 2 cun above the base of the patella, between vastus lateralis and the rectus femoris tendon.股前区，髌底上 2 寸，股外侧肌与股直肌肌腱之间。

How is ST34 needled?梁丘（ST34）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of ST34?梁丘（ST34）是哪类特定穴？

Xi-cleft point郄穴

Main safety point at ST34?梁丘（ST34）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid visible veins; the thigh is muscular, so set depth by build and redirect for radiating tingling.避开可见浅静脉；大腿肌层厚，按体型控制深度，出现放射性麻痛退针调整。
