White Tiger Decoction白虎汤

Bái Hǔ Tāng · Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage) by Zhang Zhongjing (Eastern Han dynasty; also recorded in Jingui Yaolue)东汉·张仲景《伤寒论》（兼载于《金匮要略》）

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › White Tiger Decoction白虎汤

Original text原文

「傷寒，脈浮滑，此以表有熱，裏有寒，白虎湯主之。」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

In cold damage with a floating, slippery pulse indicating intense interior heat manifesting externally, White Tiger Decoction governs.外感伤寒，脉象浮滑，这是里热炽盛而外现热象，用白虎汤主治。

「知母六兩石膏一斤（碎）甘草二兩（炙）粳米六合」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Composition of White Tiger Decoction: Zhimu 6 liang, Shigao 1 jin (crushed), prepared Gancao 2 liang, and Jingmi 6 ge.白虎汤组成：知母六两，石膏一斤（打碎），炙甘草二两，粳米六合。

「三陽合病，腹滿，身重，難以轉側，口不仁，面垢，譫語，遺尿。發汗則譫語，下之則額上生汗，手足厥冷。若自汗出者，白虎湯主之。」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

In three-yang combination disease presenting with abdominal fullness, heavy body difficult to turn, numb mouth, dirty-looking face, delirious speech, and enuresis, sweating worsens delirium and purgation causes forehead sweating with cold extremities; if spontaneous sweating is present, White Tiger Decoction governs.太阳、阳明、少阳三阳合病，出现腹部胀满、身体沉重难以翻身、口中麻木不辨滋味、面部污垢、说胡话、遗尿。若误用发汗则会加重胡话，误用攻下则会导致额头出冷汗、手足厥冷；如果表现为自然汗出的，用白虎汤主治。

Cautions使用注意

This formula is extremely cold in nature and is indicated solely for excess heat in the yangming qi level. It is contraindicated in unresolved exterior patterns with anhidrotic fever, fever due to blood deficiency, true cold with false heat, and spleen-stomach deficiency cold. Seek immediate emergency medical attention rather than relying solely on this formula if persistent high fever, worsening delirium or altered consciousness, cold extremities, rapid breathing, or falling blood pressure occurs.本方药性大寒，仅适用于阳明气分实热证。表证未解的无汗发热、血虚发热、真寒假热（阴盛格阳）以及脾胃虚寒者禁用。若出现高热持续不退、神昏谵语加重、四肢厥冷、呼吸急促或血压下降等急重症表现，必须立即前往医院急诊就医，不可仅依赖本方处理。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Zhimu, Shigao, and Gancao are all categorized under pregnancy caution on this site (extremely cold heat-clearing herbs may injure fetal qi and spleen-stomach yang), so the formula follows the strictest level among its ingredients.知母、石膏、甘草在本站均列为孕期慎用（大寒清热易伤胎元及脾胃阳气），方随其最严者。
