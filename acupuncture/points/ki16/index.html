  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The distance between the two nipples (or midclavicular lines) is 8 cun, making 4 cun from the anterior midline (umbilicus) to one nipple line.   2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure half of the patient's own thumb-width (1 cun at the interphalangeal joint) horizontally lateral from the midline at the centre of the umbilicus. Because abdominal subcutaneous fat varies, finger measurement is only a rough estimate and must be verified against the midline and medial border of the rectus abdominis.取患者自身拇指同身寸（指间关节横纹宽度为 1 寸）的一半，从脐中正中线水平向外量取 0.5 寸。因腹部皮下脂肪厚薄差异大，指宽仅作粗估，须结合前正中线与腹直肌内侧缘校核。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    On the horizontal level of CV8 (centre of the umbilicus), 0.5 cun lateral to the linea alba (anterior midline).   4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    At the umbilical level, about half a finger-width lateral to the linea alba over the medial belly of the rectus abdominis. Subcutaneously lie superficial abdominal veins and anterior cutaneous branches of the T10 intercostal nerve; deeper layers pass through the anterior rectus sheath, medial rectus abdominis, transversalis fascia, and parietal peritoneum, directly overlying small bowel loops or the transverse colon (the wall can be very thin in lean patients).     Palpation check触诊确认

    Ask the patient to slightly lift the head while supine to gently tense the rectus abdominis, palpating the central groove of the linea alba and the medial border of the muscle. Where is KI16 Huāngshū?肓俞（KI16）在哪里？

On the abdomen, 0.5 cun lateral to the centre of the umbilicus.腹部，脐中旁开 0.5 寸。

How is KI16 needled?肓俞（KI16）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of KI16?肓俞（KI16）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Chong vessel and the Kidney channel冲脉与足少阴经交会穴

Main safety point at KI16?肓俞（KI16）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. Beyond the peritoneum lie the small intestine or transverse colon, and wall thickness varies: the textbook depth is a ceiling, go shallower in thin people, avoid large lifting-thrusting, and do not needle unexplained acute abdominal pain.孕期禁针。腹膜深面是小肠或横结肠，腹壁厚薄因人而异：教材深度是上限，消瘦者减浅，不大幅提插；急性腹痛未明原因不针。
