The dorsal cutaneous branch of the ulnar nerve and dorsal wrist veins are superficial nearby; avoid drifting toward the little-finger side or styloid tip.沿腕背尺侧触诊，尺骨茎突下方可摸到三角骨隆起，二者之间的腕关节凹隙即阳谷。尺神经背侧皮支及背侧腕静脉邻近浅层；不要把针位向小指侧、尺骨茎突尖端偏移。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With gentle wrist flexion and extension, slide from the ulnar styloid distally; SI5 is the clearest soft hollow between the bony prominences. A distinct tendon cord or the fifth-metacarpal base indicates drift toward SI4.腕部轻屈伸并沿尺骨茎突向远端滑动指腹，骨突间最明显的软凹处为阳谷；若触到明显肌腱条索或位于第 5 掌骨基底，已偏向腕骨方向。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • A point distal to the styloid below the triquetrum drifts toward SI4; the styloid tip or excessive ulnar drift risks the dorsal ulnar nerve branch and superficial veins.取在尺骨茎突远端、三角骨下方会偏向腕骨（SI4）；取在尺骨茎突顶端或向尺侧过偏则可能刺激尺神经背支和浅静脉。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

SI5 is a local point when symptoms are truly at the dorsoulnar wrist; elbow-motion pain needs elbow localization and assessment for tendon or nerve causes.若主诉实际位于腕后尺侧，阳谷是局部腕点；肘部屈伸痛应选肘周穴并查肌腱或神经原因。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

Use as a distal partner when ulnar wrist pain accompanies channel-related neck symptoms; SI3 is more directly chosen for isolated neck stiffness via the Governing vessel.腕部尺侧痛兼经脉项背症状时作远近配穴；单纯项强更直接考虑后溪通督。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Extensor carpi ulnaris tendinopathy or dorsoulnar wrist strain尺侧腕伸肌腱病或腕背尺侧劳损

Pain with forearm rotation, ulnar deviation, or resisted wrist extension should be distinguished from carpal and triangular fibrocartilage complex injury.旋转前臂、尺偏或抗阻伸腕诱发痛，需与腕骨、三角纤维软骨复合体损伤鉴别。

Trauma around the ulnar styloid or triquetrum尺骨茎突或三角骨周围创伤

Tenderness and swelling after a fall on the hand may indicate fracture or ligament injury and should not be managed by pressure alone.跌倒撑地后局部压痛和肿胀可能提示骨折或韧带损伤，不能仅按压观察。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SI5 Yánggǔ?阳谷（SI5）在哪里？

On the posteromedial wrist, in the depression between the ulnar styloid process and the triquetrum.腕后区，尺骨茎突与三角骨之间的凹陷中。

How is SI5 needled?阳谷（SI5）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SI5?阳谷（SI5）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-river (fire)经穴（火）

Main safety point at SI5?阳谷（SI5）最要注意的安全问题？

The dorsal branch of the ulnar nerve winds superficially to the back of the hand here: withdraw for tingling into the back of the little finger, and do not enter the joint.尺神经手背支在此处浅出绕至手背，出现向小指背侧放射的麻感即退针；不刺入关节腔。
