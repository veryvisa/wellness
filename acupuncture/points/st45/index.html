ST45

厉兑 Lìduì

Strict Exchange · Lower risk较安全

★Jing-well (metal) point; child point of the Stomach channel井穴（金）；胃经子穴

📍 Location定位★

On the toe, on the lateral side of the distal phalanx of the 2nd toe, 0.1 cun (finger cun) proximal-lateral to the corner of the toenail.足趾，第 2 趾末节外侧，趾甲根角侧后方 0.1 寸（指寸）。

💡 Draw a line along the lateral nail border (the side away from the big toe) and one along the nail base; the point is where they meet.沿第 2 趾甲外侧缘（远离大趾一侧）与甲根缘各画一线，两线交点即是。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Shallow insertion, or prick to bleed浅刺；或点刺出血 · 0.1 cun0.1 寸

🛡 Safety安全

Strict asepsis before pricking; do not needle paronychia, an ingrown nail, foot infection or a diabetic foot; no bloodletting with a bleeding tendency.点刺前严格消毒；甲沟炎、嵌甲、足部感染或糖尿病足不针；出血倾向者不点刺放血。

Where is ST45 Lìduì?厉兑（ST45）在哪里？

On the toe, on the lateral side of the distal phalanx of the 2nd toe, 0.1 cun (finger cun) proximal-lateral to the corner of the toenail.足趾，第 2 趾末节外侧，趾甲根角侧后方 0.1 寸（指寸）。

How is ST45 needled?厉兑（ST45）怎么刺？

Shallow insertion, or prick to bleed; 0.1 cun.浅刺；或点刺出血；0.1 寸。

Special-point roles of ST45?厉兑（ST45）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-well (metal) point; child point of the Stomach channel井穴（金）；胃经子穴

Main safety point at ST45?厉兑（ST45）最要注意的安全问题？

Strict asepsis before pricking; do not needle paronychia, an ingrown nail, foot infection or a diabetic foot; no bloodletting with a bleeding tendency.点刺前严格消毒；甲沟炎、嵌甲、足部感染或糖尿病足不针；出血倾向者不点刺放血。
