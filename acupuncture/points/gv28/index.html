    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently retract the lip with a fingertip or swab to identify the frenulum and gum margin; do not probe with a nail or instrument. Bleeding on light touch, loose teeth or pus needs dental review.以棉签或指腹轻翻唇、辨认系带和牙龈缘，不用指甲或器械探压；若一触即出血、明显松牙或脓液，应先看牙医。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Gingivitis and periodontitis牙龈炎、牙周炎
