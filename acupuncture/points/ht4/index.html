📍 Location定位★

On the anterior forearm, 1.5 cun above the distal palmar wrist crease, radial to the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris.前臂前区，腕掌侧远端横纹上 1.5 寸，尺侧腕屈肌腱的桡侧缘。

💡 Make a fist and flex the wrist toward the little finger: the thick tendon that stands out on the inner wrist is flexor carpi ulnaris; measure 1.5 cun (the width of the index and middle fingers together) up its radial edge from the wrist crease.握拳屈腕并向小指侧用力，腕内侧绷起的粗腱就是尺侧腕屈肌腱；从腕横纹沿腱的桡侧缘向上量 1.5 寸（食、中两指并拢的宽度）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5 寸

Do not insert deeply, to avoid the ulnar artery and nerve.不可深刺，以免伤及尺动脉、尺神经。

🛡 Safety安全★

The ulnar artery and nerve lie directly beneath, deep to the radial edge of the tendon: insert shallowly, avoid the pulse, and withdraw at once for electric tingling into the little and ring fingers.尺动脉、尺神经在腱桡侧深面就在穴下：只浅刺，避开搏动；出现向小指、环指放射的电麻感立即退针。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is HT4 Língdào?灵道（HT4）在哪里？

On the anterior forearm, 1.5 cun above the distal palmar wrist crease, radial to the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris.前臂前区，腕掌侧远端横纹上 1.5 寸，尺侧腕屈肌腱的桡侧缘。

How is HT4 needled?灵道（HT4）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of HT4?灵道（HT4）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-river (metal)经穴（金）

Main safety point at HT4?灵道（HT4）最要注意的安全问题？

The ulnar artery and nerve lie directly beneath, deep to the radial edge of the tendon: insert shallowly, avoid the pulse, and withdraw at once for electric tingling into the little and ring fingers.尺动脉、尺神经在腱桡侧深面就在穴下：只浅刺，避开搏动；出现向小指、环指放射的电麻感立即退针。
