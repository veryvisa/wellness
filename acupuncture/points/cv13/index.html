📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine or semi-reclined with the abdomen relaxed; identify the navel and xiphisternal junction before proportional measurement.仰卧或半坐位，腹部放松；确认脐中与胸剑联合后按骨度比例定位。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The xiphisternal junction-to-umbilicus span is 8 cun; CV13 is five cun above the navel, three cun below the xiphisternal junction.胸剑联合中点至脐中为 8 寸；上脘在脐上 5 寸，即胸剑联合下端下方 3 寸。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate five cun above the umbilicus on the anterior midline, one cun above CV12; the xiphisternal junction provides an upper check.沿前正中线定位于脐上五寸、中脘上方一寸；剑胸结合可作为上端校验标志。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The linea alba and abdominal wall overlie the cavity, with stomach, left liver lobe and peritoneum deeper. Avoid deep procedures during pregnancy or after a meal, and stay clear of lateral abdominal-wall vessels.腹白线及腹壁覆盖腹腔，深部邻胃、左肝叶和腹膜；孕期及饱餐后不要在此作深部操作，偏外侧还须避开腹壁血管。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the abdomen relaxed, gently confirm five cun above the navel and check the sequence relative to CV12 and CV14; stop for marked or rebound tenderness.腹壁放松后轻触脐上五寸处，核对与中脘、巨阙的上下顺序；明显压痛或反跳痛时停止。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Consider for upper-abdominal or Stomach-counterflow symptoms around five cun above the navel; compare CV12 when the Stomach front-mu role or typical epigastric fullness dominates.脐上五寸、偏上腹和胃气上逆时考虑；胃募穴属性和较典型胃脘痞痛优先比较中脘。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

An adjunct for vomiting with upper-abdominal fullness in a traditional Stomach pattern; blood, obstruction signs or dehydration needs care first.呕吐伴上腹胀、传统胃脘不和时作局部辅助；呕血、梗阻表现或脱水先就医。

Hiccups呃逆

May pair with CV12 and PC6 for hiccups with epigastric fullness; persistent hiccups require evaluation for gastrointestinal, neurologic or medication causes.呃逆兼上腹痞满时可与中脘、内关配用；持续呃逆需辨查胃肠、神经或药物原因。

On the upper abdomen, 5 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 5 寸，前正中线上。

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。
