⚠️ Sacral-foramen and neural-risk point骶孔与神经风险穴 Neural structures lie in the posterior sacral foramen and vessels are nearby. Only trained practitioners should use anatomical landmarks; electric-shock pain, persistent numbness, or weakness requires immediate cessation and medical assessment.后骶孔内有神经结构，邻近血管。只可由受训人员按解剖标志操作；若出现电击样痛、持续麻木或无力，应立即停止并寻求医疗评估。

📍 Location定位★

In the sacral region, at the second posterior sacral foramen.骶区，第 2 后骶孔。

💡 The 1st posterior sacral foramen lies between the posterior superior iliac spine and the midline; the foramina descend about a finger-breadth apart; count down to foramen 2 — the point is in the foramen.髂后上棘与后正中线之间约是第 1 骶后孔（上髎），向下约每隔一横指一个孔；逐孔数到第 2 骶后孔，穴就在孔中。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique inferomedially向内下方斜刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

Angle slightly inferomedially to follow the foramen; too deep can exit the anterior sacral foramen into the pelvis. Contraindicated in pregnancy.针尖稍向内下，顺孔刺入；过深可穿出骶前孔进入盆腔。孕妇禁针。

🛡 Safety安全

The needle path targets a posterior sacral foramen and sacral neural structures; only trained practitioners should control direction and depth from bony landmarks to avoid neurovascular injury. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.针路直指后骶孔与骶神经结构；必须由受训人员按骨性标志控制方向和深度，避免神经血管损伤。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone position with the gluteal and lumbosacral regions fully relaxed, optionally with a thin pillow under the abdomen to reduce lumbosacral lordosis.俯卧位，臀部与腰骶部充分放松，必要时腹部可垫薄枕以减轻腰骶前凸。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the posterior superior iliac spine (the bump below the low-back dimple, about level with S2) and the median sacral crest (the row of bumps in the midline). BL32 is in the hollow medial and slightly below the iliac spine, at about the same height, over the second posterior sacral foramen.先摸髂后上棘（腰窝下的骨突，约平第 2 骶椎），再找骶正中嵴（正中一列骨突）。次髎在髂后上棘内下方、与它大致同高的凹陷中，正对第 2 骶后孔。

  2. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently over the dorsal sacrum in the BL32 region to locate the bony depression of the posterior sacral foramen; pressing near the foramen margin often elicits soreness or radiating tingling toward the perineum or posterior thigh, but avoid heavy pressure or deep probing into the foramen.在次髎定位区沿骶骨背面缓慢按压寻找骨性凹陷（后骶孔）；触及孔缘时局部可有明显酸胀或向会阴、下肢后侧放散的酸麻感，但切忌重压或向孔内深戳。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Skin, subcutaneous tissue, the origin of gluteus maximus and the erector spinae aponeurosis, then into the posterior sacral foramen. It carries the dorsal ramus of S2 and its vessels and opens into the sacral canal, which leads through the anterior foramina into the pelvis. The needle is meant to enter the foramen (spread of sensation to the sacrum, perineum or lower abdomen is the intended response); the real danger is excessive depth — in a thin person a needle passing out of the anterior foramen can injure the rectum or pelvic venous plexus.皮肤、皮下组织、臀大肌起始部与竖脊肌腱膜，进入第 2 骶后孔。孔内有第 2 骶神经后支与伴行血管；孔通向骶管，骶管再经骶前孔通盆腔。刺法本来就是针入孔中（针感向骶部、会阴、小腹放散为得气），真正要防的是过深——瘦人针穿出骶前孔可伤及直肠、盆腔静脉丛。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting to the first (BL31) or third (BL33) posterior sacral foramen: the high-risk hazard is injuring nearby posterior rami of sacral nerves or lateral sacral vessels if angle and depth lose control, while also confusing the segmental levels of the Baliao points.取偏到第 1 后骶孔（上髎）或第 3 后骶孔（中髎）：高危风险在于进针方向和深度失控时误伤邻近骶神经后支或骶外侧血管，且易混淆八髎穴对应节段。
  • Drifting onto the median sacral crest or laterally into deeper gluteus maximus: medially you hit bony prominence or midline ligamentous tissue, while laterally you miss the sacral foramen zone and fail to target the posterior sacral rami cleanly, with risk of vascular injury or hematoma if inserted deeply.取偏到骶正中嵴或向外侧偏入臀大肌深部：偏内会触及骨突或棘上韧带结构，偏外则脱离骶孔区域、难以达到对骶神经后支的温和刺激，且盲目深刺易致血管损伤或血肿。
  • Pushing on after entering the foramen: 1–1.5 cun in the foramen is enough, and deeper can exit the anterior foramen into the pelvis. If sharp electric pain shoots to the perineum or leg, withdraw slightly and do not lift and thrust. Not needled in pregnancy (a lumbosacral point, forbidden after the third month).进孔后继续深推：针在孔内 1–1.5 寸已足，再深可穿出骶前孔入盆腔；出现向会阴、下肢的剧烈触电感时退针少许，不要提插。孕妇禁针（腰骶部，怀孕三个月以上禁针，孕期一律不针）。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Period pain痛经

Often chosen for menstrual lower abdominal cramping or heaviness with lumbosacral ache and clots (frequently paired with other Baliao points or SP6); rule out organic disease first if severe pain comes with fever, vomiting, or acute abdomen signs, do not needle in pregnancy, and avoid substituting for acute abdominal points.经期小腹冷痛或坠痛伴腰骶酸痛、经血有块时常选次髎（常配八髎或三阴交）；若剧痛伴发热、呕吐或器质性病变征象须先就医，孕期禁针，不与急腹症对症穴位混用。

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Selected for lumbosacral pain centered over the sacrum or near the posterior superior iliac spine, especially when linked to uterine or lower-burner patterns; for lumbar paraspinal or sciatic-course pain, BL23, BL40, BL54, or GB30 are commonly chosen instead, and red flags require medical care first.偏于骶区或髂后上棘附近腰骶痛、尤与胞宫或下焦相关的腰骶酸痛可选次髎；若偏于腰椎旁或坐骨神经走行，常选肾俞、委中或秩边、环跳，伴神经赤旗征先就医。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

May be considered as an adjunctive point for lower-burner qi transformation issues or pelvic-related dysuria or enuresis; acute urinary retention, hematuria, or urinary tract infection requires medical evaluation first rather than relying on BL32 alone.涉及下焦气化不利或盆底相关小便不利、遗尿时可作配穴参考；急性尿潴留、血尿或泌尿系感染须先就医，不单靠次髎延误诊疗。

Where is BL32 Cìliáo?次髎（BL32）在哪里？

In the sacral region, at the second posterior sacral foramen.骶区，第 2 后骶孔。

How is BL32 needled?次髎（BL32）怎么刺？

Oblique inferomedially; 1–1.5 cun.向内下方斜刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at BL32?次髎（BL32）最要注意的安全问题？

The needle path targets a posterior sacral foramen and sacral neural structures; only trained practitioners should control direction and depth from bony landmarks to avoid neurovascular injury. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.针路直指后骶孔与骶神经结构；必须由受训人员按骨性标志控制方向和深度，避免神经血管损伤。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
