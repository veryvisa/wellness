📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Palm up, wrist relaxed or slightly extended, with the radial wrist crease and pulse palpable.仰掌，腕放松或微背伸；让桡侧腕横纹和桡动脉搏动清楚可触。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    At the radial end of the palmar wrist crease, locate LU9 in the hollow between radial styloid and scaphoid; it is on the palmar Lung line, not dorsal LI5.在腕掌侧横纹桡侧端，桡骨茎突与舟骨之间的凹陷取太渊；它位于肺经线上而非手背的阳溪。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The radial artery pulses in the area, with abductor pollicis longus and flexor carpi radialis tendons and superficial radial nerve nearby; displace the artery with the guiding hand and needle shallowly beside, never into, the pulse.桡动脉就在穴区搏动，周围有拇长展肌腱、桡侧腕屈肌腱及桡神经浅支；先以押手将动脉推向一侧，从旁浅刺，切勿直刺搏动。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide palmward from radial styloid from hard bone into the soft crease hollow; feel the artery lightly and take the point beside, not directly over, it.从桡骨茎突向掌侧滑，先触到硬骨缘再落入腕横纹软凹；轻压可感觉动脉，取穴在其旁而不是其正上。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    The radial end of the cunkou pulse area is the LU9 region; use it only to confirm the region, then check bone, tendons and artery together.中医切脉的寸口桡侧端即太渊区；此法只能确认区域，仍须以骨缘、肌腱与动脉共同核对。

Where is LU9 Tàiyuān?太渊（LU9）在哪里？

Front of the wrist, at the radial end of the wrist crease, between the radial styloid and the scaphoid, in the hollow just ulnar to the abductor pollicis longus tendon, where the radial pulse can be felt.腕前区，腕掌侧横纹的桡侧端，桡骨茎突与舟骨之间，拇长展肌腱尺侧的凹陷中，可摸到桡动脉搏动。

How is LU9 needled?太渊（LU9）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, keeping clear of the radial artery; 0.3–0.5 cun.避开桡动脉，直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of LU9?太渊（LU9）是哪类特定穴？

Shu-stream point (five transport points; earth); Yuan-source point of the Lung; Influential (meeting) point of the vessels输穴（五输穴，五行属土）；肺经原穴；八会穴之脉会

Main safety point at LU9?太渊（LU9）最要注意的安全问题？

The point sits on the radial pulse; hitting the artery causes bleeding and a haematoma, more so in people on anticoagulants.穴位就在桡动脉搏动处，刺中会出血、血肿；服抗凝药的人更要小心。
