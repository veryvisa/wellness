📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seat the patient or place them supine, with the head in a neutral position and the neck relaxed. Part the hair to expose the temporal hairline. Identify ST8, GB7 and the temporal arterial pulse before proportional measurement and palpation.坐位或仰卧位，头部保持正中，颈部放松，头发向两侧分开，充分暴露颞部鬓发区。先观察头维、曲鬓及颞部动脉搏动，再进行比例定位和触诊。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First identify ST8 at the temporal corner of the forehead, near the junction of the frontal and temporal hairlines. Then identify GB7 within the temporal hairline above and anterior to the ear. Use the curved line connecting these points as the reference. GB4 is at the junction of the upper quarter and lower three quarters, measured from ST8 toward GB7. The upper quarter is measured from ST8, not from GB7.先找到头维（ST8）：位于额角发际处，额部发际与颞部发际交界附近。再找到曲鬓（GB7）：耳前上方，鬓发内，沿颞部发际向上可触及其所在区域。以两穴之间的弧形连线为基准，颔厌位于该弧线自头维向曲鬓方向上四分之一与下四分之三的交界处，即靠近头维的一侧。注意这里的上四分之一是指从头维端起算，不是从曲鬓端起算。

  2. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The distance between the two ST8 points is standardized as 9 cun for transverse head measurement. This 9-cun measurement must not be transferred directly to the curved ST8–GB7 line. The official GB4 location remains the junction at one quarter of that curved line, measured from ST8.两侧头维之间的头维骨度折作9寸。此寸法用于横向头面部骨度测量，不能直接把9寸全部套用到头维至曲鬓的弧形连线上。GB4的正式定位仍以头维与曲鬓之间连线的上四分之一交点为准。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    The patient's thumb interphalangeal-joint width may provide an approximate 1-cun reference, but finger measurement alone is unsuitable for locating GB4 in the temporal hairline. Identify ST8 and GB7 first, divide their curved connecting line into four equal parts, and select the first division from ST8. Finger measurement is only a rough cross-check.可用患者自身拇指指间关节宽度作约1寸的辅助比例参照，但头部鬓发区不宜单靠手指宽度确定GB4。先确定头维、曲鬓两个端点，再按四等分取靠头维的第一个分界点。手指测量仅供粗略复核。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate the temporal skin gently with the fingertip and identify the pulse and course of the superficial temporal artery. This artery usually ascends anterior to the tragus and divides into frontal and parietal branches in the temporal region. The auriculotemporal nerve, a sensory branch of the mandibular division of the trigeminal nerve, runs near the superficial temporal vessels. These structures may be close to GB4. Before needling, assess the local pulse and avoid any palpable arterial trunk or prominent branch. The point location does not authorize penetration through a vessel or nerve. Superficial layers include skin, subcutaneous tissue, and cutaneous neurovascular branches; deeper structures include the temporal fascia and temporalis muscle. Tissue thickness varies between individuals. Follow the specified posterior transverse scalp technique, keeping the needle within superficial scalp tissue. Do not insert perpendicularly into deeper tissues or direct the needle toward the deep temporal region or orbit.触诊时先用指腹轻触颞部皮肤，识别颞浅动脉的搏动及其分支走向。颞浅动脉通常经耳屏前方上行，进入颞部后分为额支和顶支；耳颞神经伴随颞浅血管附近走行，为三叉神经下颌神经的感觉分支。GB4所在区域可能与这些血管、神经分支存在空间邻近关系。应在针刺前检查局部搏动，避开可触及的血管干及明显分支，不应将穴位定位误解为可以穿过血管或神经。该区域浅层为皮肤、皮下组织及头皮血管神经，深层涉及颞筋膜、颞肌及其深面结构；具体深度随个体头皮厚度而异。按本穴卡采用沿头皮向后平刺，保持针体处于浅层头皮组织内，不向深部垂直进针，不朝颞部深层或眼眶方向进针。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Glide the index fingertip gently along the curved line from ST8 to GB7 to confirm the temporal hairline pathway. GB4 should be at the first quarter-point from ST8, not at any arbitrary tender spot in the temple. Mild local tenderness may occur, but a prominent pulse, electric-shock sensation, or persistent numbness is not evidence of correct point location. If a clear arterial pulse is encountered, reassess the vessel course and adjust the location.用食指指腹沿头维至曲鬓的弧线轻轻滑动，确认鬓发内的定位路径。GB4应处于弧线靠头维端的四分之一交点，而不是颞部任意压痛点。触诊时可出现局部轻度酸胀，但不应以明显搏动、触电样放射感或持续麻木作为取穴正确的依据。若触及明显动脉搏动，应重新辨认血管走行并调整定位。

  6. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Proportional confirmation: divide the curved distance from ST8 to GB7 into four equal portions and measure the first quarter from ST8 toward GB7. Because the temporal contour is curved, measure along the skin surface rather than using the straight-line distance between the two points.比例复核法：将头维至曲鬓的弧形距离想象为四等份，先从头维向曲鬓方向量出四分之一，所得位置即GB4。头部曲线不规则，应沿皮肤表面弧线测量，不要用两点间直线距离替代。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Measuring one quarter from GB7 toward ST8 reverses the reference and places the point too close to GB7. This may confuse GB4 with the neighboring GB5–GB6 region. Always measure the first quarter from ST8.从曲鬓向头维量取四分之一，误将GB4放在靠近曲鬓的一侧：实际位置会偏向GB7，可能混淆颔厌与悬颅、悬厘一带的穴位分布。应始终从头维端起算四分之一。
  • Treating the ST8–GB7 reference as a straight line causes anterior or posterior displacement because the temporal contour is curved. Measure along the actual scalp surface.把头维至曲鬓的弧线当作直线：由于颞部轮廓呈弧形，直线量取会使定位向前或向后偏移。应沿头皮表面实际弧线取比例点。
  • Treating any tender temporal spot as GB4 is incorrect. Migraine, temporalis muscle overload, and temporomandibular disorders can all cause local tenderness. Establish the standard location from ST8 and GB7 first, then document whether the tender area overlaps GB4.把颞部压痛点直接当作颔厌：偏头痛、颞肌劳损、颞下颌关节相关疼痛均可产生局部压痛，疼痛点不等于标准穴位。应先按头维、曲鬓确定标准定位，再记录压痛点是否与穴位重合。
  • Needling without checking the superficial temporal arterial pulse may injure an arterial branch and cause bleeding, hematoma, and local pain. Contact with the auriculotemporal nerve may provoke radiating paresthesia. Palpate first, avoid prominent vessels and nerve pathways, and use only shallow posterior transverse insertion. Do not needle deeply, perpendicularly, or obliquely toward the orbit.忽略颞浅动脉搏动而直接进针：针尖可能损伤动脉分支，引起出血、血肿及局部疼痛。若误触耳颞神经，也可能诱发放射性麻痛。应先触诊血管，避开明显搏动及神经走行区域，采用沿头皮向后的浅层平刺，不作深刺、直刺或朝眼眶方向斜刺。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Consider GB4 as a local adjunct for temporal headache following the Shaoyang distribution, particularly with lateral canthal radiation or periauricular discomfort. Compare GV20 when pain predominates at the vertex, GB20 when occipital pain and neck stiffness predominate, and LU7 when external headache is accompanied by posterior neck and upper-back tightness. Do not manage sudden severe headache, neurological deficits, or new temporal headache after age 50 by point selection; obtain medical assessment first.颞侧、沿少阳经区域分布的偏头痛，尤其伴外眦牵引痛或耳周不适时，可考虑颔厌作为局部配穴；若以头顶部为主，比较百会；若以后枕部、颈项强痛为主，比较风池；若为外感头痛伴项背拘急，可考虑列缺。突发剧烈头痛、神经功能异常或50岁以上新发颞痛不选穴处理，先就医。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Consider GB4 with GB20 and TE when dizziness accompanies temporal headache and Shaoyang-region discomfort. Compare GV20 for predominant vertex heaviness and ST40 for a phlegm-dampness pattern with a heavy-headed sensation. Suspected vestibular disorders require assessment of ear symptoms and vestibular function; the traditional indication for dizziness alone is insufficient to select GB4. Sudden severe dizziness with double vision, dysarthria, limb weakness, or inability to walk independently requires emergency evaluation for a central cause.眩晕伴颞侧头痛、少阳经区域不适时，可考虑颔厌配风池、外关；若以头顶昏沉为主，比较百会；痰湿中阻、头重如裹时可比较丰隆；耳源性眩晕则须结合耳部症状和前庭检查，不能单凭颔厌主治眩晕便直接选用。伴复视、构音障碍、肢体无力、不能独立行走或突发严重眩晕者先急诊排查中枢性病因。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

GB4 may be considered as an adjunct local point when tinnitus accompanies temporal headache and Shaoyang-region symptoms. For tinnitus or hearing loss itself, compare SI19, GB2, and TE and determine the cause through hearing assessment. Sudden unilateral hearing loss developing over hours to days requires immediate otolaryngology assessment for sudden sensorineural hearing loss; do not rely on acupuncture alone.颞部头痛伴耳周不适、少阳经证候时，颔厌可作辅助局部穴；耳鸣耳聋本身应比较听宫、听会、翳风等耳周穴，并根据听力检查判断病因。若为突发单侧听力下降，尤其数小时至数日内发生，应立即转耳鼻喉科评估突发性感音神经性听力损失，不可仅针刺观察。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

Consider GB4 with GB1 and GB14 for headache radiating between the lateral canthus and temple after urgent ophthalmic conditions have been excluded. For symptoms centered on the medial canthus or other specific periocular regions, compare BL1 and BL2 according to the anatomical site. GB4 is not a substitute for emergency ophthalmic treatment. Redness with severe pain, reduced vision, halos, or nausea and vomiting requires emergency eye assessment.眼外眦牵引至颞部的头痛，且眼科急症已排除时，可考虑颔厌配瞳子髎、阳白；若以眼内眦或眼周局部症状为主，应按具体部位比较睛明、攒竹等穴。颔厌不是眼科急症的替代治疗。眼红伴剧痛、视力下降、虹视或恶心呕吐时，必须先眼科急诊。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

GB4 may be combined with GB20 and TE when temporal headache coexists with neck tension. For predominantly posterior neck pain, acute torticollis, or restricted rotation, compare GB20, SI3, and local ashi points. For external headache with neck stiffness, compare LU7. GB4 is not a diagnostic or therapeutic substitute for assessment of cervical radiculopathy or myelopathy. Progressive limb weakness, gait disturbance, bowel or bladder dysfunction, or neck pain after trauma requires medical assessment.颞侧头痛兼颈项紧张时，颔厌可与风池、外关配合；若主要是后颈项部落枕、旋转受限，优先比较风池、后溪及局部阿是穴；若为外感头项强痛，可比较列缺。颔厌不是颈椎神经根病或脊髓病的诊断、治疗替代方案。伴进行性肢体无力、步态异常、大小便功能改变或外伤后颈痛时先就医。

Facial palsy面瘫（口眼歪斜） ⚖️

GB4 is a temporal head point rather than a principal local point for facial palsy. It may be considered as an adjunct for accompanying temporal headache, while GB14, ST2, ST4, ST6, and TE are more directly relevant to facial motor symptoms. Sudden facial droop requires exclusion of stroke before it is treated as peripheral facial palsy.颔厌位于颞侧头部，不是面瘫的主要局部穴。若面瘫患者同时有颞侧头痛，可根据兼症考虑局部配穴；面瘫本身应比较阳白、四白、地仓、颊车、翳风等穴。突发面瘫必须先排除卒中，不能仅凭口眼歪斜直接按周围性面瘫针刺。

Insomnia不寐（失眠） ⚖️

GB4 is not a routine primary point for insomnia. Consider it only as an adjunct when insomnia coexists with significant temporal headache or Shaoyang-region discomfort. Compare HT7 and PC6 for predominant restlessness, LR3 and GB20 for liver-yang-related headache and dizziness, and ST40 for phlegm-heat patterns. New severe headache or neurological abnormalities must not be attributed to ordinary insomnia.颔厌不是不寐的常规主穴。只有在失眠同时伴明显颞侧头痛或少阳经头部不适时，才可考虑作为兼症局部配穴；以心神不宁为主比较神门、内关，以肝阳上扰、头痛眩晕为主比较太冲、风池，以痰热扰心为主比较丰隆。新发严重头痛或伴神经系统异常时不能将其归为普通失眠。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Migraine偏头痛（Migraine）

Migraine may present as unilateral or bilateral pulsating temporal headache, with nausea, photophobia, or phonophobia, often worsened by activity. Some patients experience visual aura. The GB4 region may be tender, but local tenderness alone cannot diagnose migraine. Assessment should include attack duration, associated symptoms, previous attack patterns, and neurological examination. A first severe headache, a headache unlike previous episodes, or headache with neurological abnormalities requires medical assessment.可表现为单侧或双侧颞部搏动性头痛，伴恶心、畏光、畏声，活动时加重。部分患者有视觉先兆。颔厌区域可能是头痛敏感区，但局部压痛不能单独确诊偏头痛。需结合发作持续时间、伴随症状、既往发作模式及神经系统检查鉴别。首次出现的剧烈头痛、与既往不同的头痛或伴神经功能异常，应先就医。

Tension-type headache紧张型头痛（Tension-type headache）

Tension-type headache commonly presents as bilateral frontal or temporal pressure or a band-like tightening sensation, usually without prominent pulsation, and may coexist with neck and shoulder muscle tension. Temporalis and scalp myofascial tenderness may overlap the GB4 region. Unlike migraine, nausea and marked worsening with physical activity are generally less prominent. Examine the temporalis, masseter, suboccipital muscles, and cervical range of motion.常表现为双侧额颞部或头部束带样压迫感、紧箍感，通常不是明显搏动性疼痛，可伴颈肩肌紧张。颞肌及头皮肌筋膜压痛可能与GB4附近重叠。与偏头痛相比，恶心和明显活动加重通常不突出。应同时检查颞肌、咬肌、枕下肌群及颈部活动度。

Temporalis myofascial pain颞肌肌筋膜疼痛

The temporalis occupies the temporal fossa and contributes to jaw closure. Prolonged chewing, bruxism, clenching, or temporomandibular dysfunction may produce temporal aching that worsens during chewing. Palpation of the temporalis muscle belly and attachments may reproduce the patient's familiar pain. Assess jaw opening and closing, pain provoked by chewing, and clenching. Do not automatically classify myofascial pain as a Gallbladder-channel headache.颞肌位于颞窝内，参与下颌闭合。长时间咀嚼、磨牙、紧咬牙关或颞下颌关节功能异常，可导致颞部酸痛，咀嚼时加重。触诊颞肌肌腹及其附着区域可能复制患者熟悉的疼痛。应检查下颌开闭口、咀嚼诱发痛及牙关紧闭情况，不能将肌筋膜痛直接归为胆经头痛。

Temporomandibular disorder颞下颌关节紊乱（Temporomandibular disorder）

Pain may radiate to the preauricular region, temple, and lateral head, often with restricted mouth opening, joint clicking, chewing pain, or mandibular deviation. The pain distribution may overlap the traditional GB4 indication, but the underlying problem involves the temporomandibular joint and masticatory muscles. Examine joint movement, occlusion, and masticatory muscle tenderness. Significant restriction, jaw locking, or altered occlusion after trauma requires referral.疼痛可向耳前、颞部及头侧放射，常伴张口受限、关节弹响、咀嚼痛或下颌偏斜。其疼痛区域可能与颔厌主治区域重叠，但病因来自颞下颌关节及相关咀嚼肌系统。应检查关节活动、咬合和咀嚼肌压痛；若出现明显张口困难、下颌锁闭或外伤后咬合改变，应转诊评估。

Auriculotemporal neuralgia or sensory disturbance耳颞神经痛或感觉异常

The auriculotemporal nerve is a sensory branch of the mandibular division of the trigeminal nerve and supplies parts of the temple and preauricular skin. Irritation may cause stabbing, burning, or electric-shock pain radiating from the preauricular region to the temple, sometimes with altered sensation. Compared with ordinary muscle tenderness, neuralgia more often follows a nerve distribution and may be provoked by touch or chewing. Persistent numbness, progressive sensory changes, or unexplained neuralgic pain warrants neurological or specialist assessment.耳颞神经是三叉神经下颌支的感觉分支，支配颞部及耳前部分皮肤。受刺激时可能出现耳前至颞部的刺痛、灼痛或电击样放射痛，部分患者伴局部感觉异常。与普通肌肉压痛相比，神经痛更常呈沿神经分布的放射性疼痛，触碰或咀嚼可能诱发。若出现持续麻木、进行性感觉异常或不明原因神经痛，应先进行神经系统及相关专科评估。

Superficial temporal artery disorders and giant cell arteritis颞浅动脉病变及颞动脉炎（巨细胞动脉炎）

The temporal region contains a palpable arterial pulse. Giant cell arteritis may present with new temporal headache, scalp tenderness, jaw fatigue or pain during chewing, and sometimes visual changes. It primarily affects people over 50 and requires prompt medical assessment rather than acupuncture or massage. Ask about age, systemic symptoms, visual changes, and jaw claudication, and arrange urgent referral.颞部可触及动脉搏动。血管炎尤其巨细胞动脉炎可表现为新发颞部头痛、头皮触痛、咀嚼时下颌疲劳或疼痛，部分患者伴视力改变。主要见于50岁以上人群，属于需要及时医学评估的疾病，不能通过针刺或按揉处理。应询问年龄、全身症状、视觉变化及咀嚼跛行，并尽快转诊。

Where is GB4 Hànyàn?颔厌（GB4）在哪里？

On the head, at the junction of the upper quarter and lower three quarters of the curved line from ST8 to GB7 (following the curve of the temple hairline).头部，从头维至曲鬓的弧形连线（其弧度与鬓发弧度相应）的上 1/4 与下 3/4 的交点处。

How is GB4 needled?颔厌（GB4）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of GB4?颔厌（GB4）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Hand/Foot-Shaoyang and Foot-Yangming channels手足少阳、足阳明交会穴

Main safety point at GB4?颔厌（GB4）最要注意的安全问题？

Thread transversely under the scalp aponeurosis; feel for the superficial temporal pulse first and press after withdrawal.沿头皮帽状腱膜下平刺；先触颞浅动脉搏动再进针，出针按压防血肿。
