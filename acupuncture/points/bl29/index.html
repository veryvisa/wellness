Posture体位 · Prone position with lumbosacral muscles relaxed and legs extended or slightly flexed to reduce gluteal tension.俯卧位，腰骶部肌肉放松，双下肢自然伸直或微屈以减轻臀肌张力。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: level with the third posterior sacral foramen.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），中膂俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 3 骶后孔水平定。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First locate the sacrum and posterior superior iliac spines (roughly at the S2 level), then palpate inferiorly to the third sacral spinous process or third posterior sacral foramen; BL29 is at the level of the third posterior sacral foramen, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.先定骶骨与髂后上棘（约平第 2 骶椎水平），再向下摸第 3 骶椎棘突或第 3 后骶孔；中膂俞在第 3 后骶孔同一水平，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    At the level of the third posterior sacral foramen, measure laterally from the posterior midline using the patient's own index and middle fingers together (about 1.5 cun) or 1.5 thumb-cun. In larger or leaner patients, verify lateral spacing against the median sacral crest and posterior sacral foramina.在对应第 3 后骶孔水平，由后正中线向外量取患者本人食指、中指并拢（约 1.5 寸）或拇指同身寸 1.5 寸。体形偏胖或偏瘦者，仍需结合骶骨正中嵴与后骶孔骨性标志校正横向距离。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located on the posterior sacrum at the level of the third posterior sacral foramen, 1.5 cun lateral to the midline; superficially lie posterior cutaneous branches of sacral nerves and lateral sacral venous branches, and deeply lie the gluteus maximus, posterior rami of sacral nerves, and lateral sacral artery. Keep the needle clear of the posterior sacral foramen and lateral sacral vessels, controlling depth so it does not enter the foramen or injure vessels.位于骶骨后面，第 3 后骶孔水平，正中嵴旁开 1.5 寸处；浅层有骶神经后支皮支与骶外侧静脉分支，深层为臀大肌及骶神经后支、骶外侧动脉。进针时需注意避开后骶孔及骶外侧血管，控制深度勿穿入孔内或伤及血管。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the patient prone and relaxed, slide the fingertip laterally at the third sacral level along the posterior sacrum to feel the bony surface and gluteus maximus origin; if lumbosacral or gluteal referred pain is present, local tenderness or a taut muscle band may be felt.俯卧放松后，指腹沿骶骨后缘第 3 骶椎水平向外侧滑动触诊，可感知骶骨骨面及臀大肌起始部肌束；若该侧有腰骶部或臀部牵涉痛，局部常可触及压痛或肌紧张带。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Locating at a higher or lower sacral foramen level (such as S2 or S4): segment alignment is off, reducing specificity for lumbosacral, lower-limb, or pelvic elimination complaints.误取偏上或偏下骶孔水平（如第 2 或第 4 后骶孔）：节段对应错误，治腰骶及下肢、二便相关不适的针对性下降。
  • Measuring too far laterally or medially: drifting medially hits the median sacral crest or sacral hiatus margin, while drifting laterally moves off the third posterior sacral foramen plane.旁开过宽或过窄：偏内侧易触及骶骨正中嵴或骶管裂孔周边骨缘，偏外侧则偏离第 3 后骶孔对应平面。
  • Needling too deeply or angling into a posterior sacral foramen: risks injuring sacral posterior rami or lateral sacral vessels, causing sharp radiating pain or local hematoma; needling this lumbosacral point is contraindicated during pregnancy and must be avoided.进针过深或方向偏入后骶孔：易刺伤骶神经后支或骶外侧血管，引起剧烈放射痛或局部血肿；孕期针刺此腰骶部穴位属禁针范畴，须严格规避。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Suitable when aching or stiffness centers on the posterior sacrum or lumbosacral segment; avoid in acute trauma fractures or severe neurological deficits, and compare with BL40, BL23, or Jiaji points.偏于骶后区或腰骶部局部酸胀僵硬、需结合骶部节段定位时可选，不选用于急性外伤骨折或伴神经根重度缺损者；临床常与委中、肾俞或腰夹脊穴对比选用。

Sciatica坐骨神经痛 ⚖️

Used only as a local and channel adjunct for associated sacrogluteal muscle tension rather than a standalone lead point for sciatica; compare with GB30, BL40, and GB34.仅可作为腰骶部局部与循经辅助配穴之一，缓解伴随的骶臀部肌筋膜张力，不作为坐骨神经痛唯一主穴；临床常与环跳、委中、阳陵泉对比搭配。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Used only as a non-pregnant adjunct for mild voiding or elimination sluggishness linked to lumbosacral channel qi, not acute urinary retention or obstruction; compare with CV3, CV4, and SP6.仅用于非孕期伴腰骶部经气不畅之轻浅排尿或排便调理辅助，不用于急性尿潴留或器质性梗阻急症；临床常与中极、关元、三阴交等前后配穴对比。

How is BL29 needled?中膂俞（BL29）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique medially; 0.8–1.2 cun.直刺或向内斜刺；0.8–1.2 寸。

Main safety point at BL29?中膂俞（BL29）最要注意的安全问题？

Posterior sacral foramina and sacral posterior rami are nearby; use bony landmarks to control depth and avoid overly deep or foraminal direction. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.邻近后骶孔及骶神经后支；以骨性标志确认层次，避免过深或偏向骶孔。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
