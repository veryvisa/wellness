GB3

上关 Shàngguān

Upper Gate · Take care需谨慎

★Crossing point of the Hand/Foot-Shaoyang and Foot-Yangming channels手足少阳、足阳明交会穴

📍 Location定位★

On the face, directly above ST7, in the depression at the midpoint of the upper border of the zygomatic arch.面部，下关直上，颧弓上缘中央凹陷中。

💡 Find ST7 below the zygomatic arch, then cross the arch to the hollow at the middle of its upper border.先找颧弓下缘的下关，再越过颧弓到其上缘正中的凹陷。

🪡 Needling刺法

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Jiayi Jing: needling too deep causes loss of hearing.《甲乙经》：刺太深令人耳无闻。

🛡 Safety安全★

Do not needle deep; the superficial temporal artery ascends in front of the ear, so feel the pulse first.不可深刺；颞浅动脉在耳前上行，先触搏动再进针。

Tinnitus, deafness, otorrhoea耳鸣、耳聋、聤耳 ⚖️Toothache, facial pain, facial deviation, lockjaw齿痛、面痛、口眼㖞斜、口噤 ⚖️

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated upright or side-lying with the affected preauricular area facing up, mouth gently closed and masseter relaxed.正坐位或侧卧位，患侧耳前向上，自然合口、咬肌放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate the horizontal zygomatic arch anterior to the tragus and locate ST7 (in the depression between the zygomatic arch and the mandibular notch, open when the mouth is closed and closed when open). Move superiorly across the zygomatic arch to the hollow on its upper border directly above ST7 — that is GB3.先在耳屏前方摸到横行的颧弓，找到下关穴（颧弓与下颌切迹所形成的凹陷中，闭口有孔、张口即闭）；再向上越过颧弓，在颧弓上缘正对下关的凹陷处即为上关。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    GB3 lies in the hollow at the middle of the upper border of the zygomatic arch over temporal fascia and temporalis; the superficial temporal artery rises just behind it in front of the ear with the auriculotemporal nerve. Feel the pulse first and avoid it; classically one must not needle deep, so stop at 0.8 cun.上关在颧弓上缘中央的凹陷中，深层为颞筋膜与颞肌，颞浅动脉在其后方经耳前上行，耳颞神经伴行。先触到搏动再进针，避开动脉；古训刺不可深，直刺以 0.8 寸为限。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the mouth closed, glide a fingertip along the upper border of the zygomatic arch to feel a distinct shallow hollow; ask the patient to clench the teeth lightly to feel the temporalis fibers contract beneath your finger. Pressing this hollow typically elicits a clear dull ache that may radiate toward the temple or inside the ear.闭口时用指腹在颧弓上缘上下滑动按压，可摸到一处明显的浅凹陷；让患者做轻微咬牙动作，可感到指下颞肌纤维收缩隆起，按压该凹陷处常有明显的酸胀感，有时向颞部或耳内放射。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Remember its pairing with ST7: 'Xiaguan is below the zygomatic arch, Shangguan is above it.' First find ST7 below the arch (hollow when closed, filled when open), then move straight up across the bony bridge of the arch to the hollow just behind the arterial pulse.与下关穴上下相对记忆：「下关在颧弓下，上关在颧弓上」。先用「闭口有孔、张口即闭」找到颧弓下缘的下关，垂直向上跨过颧弓骨梁约一横指宽，在动脉搏动后方凹陷处即是。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Locating in the hollow below the zygomatic arch: that is ST7 (Xiaguan) on the Stomach channel — vertically inverted.取到颧弓下缘的凹陷：那是胃经的下关穴（ST7），上下位置颠倒。
  • Needling directly over the superficial temporal artery pulse: the vessel lies superficially here, so direct puncture easily causes a hematoma or brisk bleeding.直接扎在颞浅动脉搏动点上：局部颞浅动脉位置表浅且管壁较脆，直刺易刺破动脉导致血肿或明显出血。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Chosen for tinnitus or ear pain with preauricular and temporal fullness from Shaoyang qi stagnation; often combined with TE17 and GB2, and not the lead point for chronic kidney-deficiency tinnitus.偏于耳前及颞部胀痛、少阳经气郁闭引起的耳鸣耳痛时选用；若伴耳后乳突压痛或胆火上逆明显多配翳风、听会，肾虚久鸣不以本穴为主。

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Targets temporal headache right above the zygomatic arch near the ear (often radiating to the jaw or upper teeth); choose GB8 if pain centers 1.5 cun directly above the ear apex, or EX-HN5 in the hollow lateral to the eyebrow and outer canthus.主治耳前紧靠颧弓上缘的颞侧偏头痛（常连及下颌或上牙）；若痛点在耳尖直上 1.5 寸选率谷（GB8），在眉梢与目外眦之间向后凹陷处选太阳（EX-HN5）。

Toothache牙痛 ⚖️

Used for upper toothache radiating toward the temple and preauricular area; prefer ST7 below the arch for upper toothache with cheek swelling, ST6 for lower toothache, and LI4 as the primary distal point.用于上颌牙痛连及颞部及耳前者；若上牙痛伴面颊肿胀首选颧弓下缘的下关（ST7），下牙痛首选颊车（ST6），远端通调首选合谷（LI4）。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Temporomandibular disorder (TMD) and temporalis myofascial pain颞下颌关节紊乱病（TMD）与颞肌肌筋膜疼痛

Bruxism, clenching, or unilateral chewing strains the anterior temporalis, creating trigger points at the upper zygomatic arch (GB3) that refer pain to the temple and upper teeth. Differentiate from pulpitis and trigeminal neuralgia; jaw locking or hot, swollen joint pain requires dental or imaging evaluation first.夜磨牙、紧咬牙或咀嚼偏侧引起颞肌前束劳损，痛点常位于颧弓上缘（上关区）并向颞部及上牙放射；需与牙源性牙髓炎、三叉神经痛鉴别，若出现下颌交锁无法张口或关节红肿热痛应先口腔科或医学影像评估。

Giant cell arteritis (temporal arteritis)巨细胞动脉炎（颞动脉炎）

In patients over 50, new-onset unilateral throbbing temporal headache, scalp tenderness, and jaw claudication when chewing — with a thickened, nodular, or diminished superficial temporal artery pulse just anterior to GB3 — is a medical/ophthalmic emergency carrying a risk of permanent blindness. Do not treat as routine migraine; refer immediately.50 岁以上患者新发一侧颞部跳痛、头皮触痛、咀嚼时下颌跛行（咬肌无力疼痛），触诊上关前方颞浅动脉可感变硬、结节状或搏动减弱/消失；属眼科与内科急症（有不可逆失明风险），严禁直接当普通偏头痛针刺，必须立即转诊急诊排查。

Auriculotemporal nerve entrapment or neuralgia耳颞神经卡压或耳颞神经痛

Paroxysmal burning or lancinating pain in the preauricular and temporal region caused by compression of the auriculotemporal nerve from TMJ inflammation, trauma, or scarring; palpating GB3 can reproduce shooting pain radiating upward.耳前及颞部阵发性烧灼样或刺痛，常因颞下颌关节炎症、外伤或局部瘢痕卡压耳颞神经所致，按压上关区可诱发沿耳前向头顶放射的电击样痛。
