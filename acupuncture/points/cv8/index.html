⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Consider only a non-needling adjunct for traditional cold-deficiency or chronic diarrhea; blood, high fever, dehydration or persistent vomiting needs medical care.仅在传统虚寒或久泻思路中考虑非针刺辅助；血便、高热、脱水或持续呕吐时立即按现代医疗处理。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Localized umbilical discomfort first requires exclusion of hernia, infection and acute abdominal disease; CV8 is not a routine needling point for nonspecific pain.脐部局限不适需先排除脐疝、感染和腹腔急症；不是一般腹痛的常规针刺点。

Oedema水肿 ⚖️

Historical use for edema does not replace etiologic diagnosis; new edema or breathlessness needs assessment for cardiac, renal and other causes.古籍用于水肿的传统记载不能替代病因诊断；新发水肿或气短先评估心肾等原因。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is CV8 Shénquè?神阙（CV8）在哪里？

In the umbilical region, at the centre of the umbilicus.脐区，脐中央。

How is CV8 needled?神阙（CV8）怎么刺？

No needling (moxa only); Not needled; moxa stick or moxa cones on salt.禁针（宜灸）；不针刺；多用艾条灸或艾炷隔盐灸。

Main safety point at CV8?神阙（CV8）最要注意的安全问题？

No needling: the navel has no fat under the skin, lies directly on the peritoneum and cannot be fully disinfected, so needling risks infection and peritoneal injury. Guard against burns with salt moxa; do not use over an umbilical hernia or infection.禁针：脐窝皮下无脂肪、直贴腹膜，且难以彻底消毒，针刺易致感染与腹膜损伤；隔盐灸防烫伤，脐疝、脐部感染不灸。
