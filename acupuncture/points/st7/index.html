Where is ST7 Xiàguān?下关（ST7）在哪里？

On the face, in the depression between the midpoint of the inferior border of the zygomatic arch and the mandibular notch.面部，颧弓下缘中央与下颌切迹之间凹陷中。

How is ST7 needled?下关（ST7）怎么刺？

Perpendicular with the mouth closed; 0.5–1 cun.闭口直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of ST7?下关（ST7）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Stomach and Gallbladder channels足阳明、足少阳交会穴

Main safety point at ST7?下关（ST7）最要注意的安全问题？
