The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing records KI12's alternate names (Yinwei, Yinguan), its position 1 cun below KI13, and its status as a crossing point of the Kidney channel and Chong Vessel — explaining its classical role in regulating Chong and Ren for gynecological and reproductive disorders.《针灸甲乙经》载明了大赫的别名（阴维、阴关）、与上方气穴（KI13）相距一寸的定位关系，以及它是足少阴肾经与冲脉的交会穴，解释了其调理冲任、治疗妇科及男科下焦病证的理论基础。

「女子赤淫，大赫主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Lower abdominal wall myofascial pain syndrome / distal rectus abdominis strain下腹壁肌筋膜疼痛综合征 / 腹直肌下段劳损

Localized tenderness beside the lower midline after core workouts or chronic coughing; pain worsens when tensing the rectus abdominis by lifting the head (positive Carnett sign), distinguishing it from visceral pain. KI12 often overlaps with local trigger points.仰卧起坐、核心训练过度或慢性咳嗽后出现下腹中线旁固定压痛，抬头绷紧腹直肌时疼痛加重（Carnett 征阳性），可与内脏痛鉴别；大赫常与局部激痛点重合。

Anterior cutaneous nerve entrapment syndrome (ACNES, T11–T12 anterior cutaneous branches)前皮神经卡压综合征（ACNES，胸11–12肋间神经前皮支）

Entrapment where cutaneous branches pierce the anterior rectus sheath, causing sharp or burning pain in a spot under 2 cm wide below the umbilicus with a positive pinch test. Acute abdomen (appendicitis, ovarian torsion, ureteral calculi) must be ruled out first.神经穿出腹直肌前鞘处受卡压，表现为脐下外侧直径不足 2 cm 的锐痛或烧灼感，捏起皮肤即痛（Pinch 征阳性）。须先排除阑尾炎、卵巢囊肿扭转或泌尿系结石等急腹症。
