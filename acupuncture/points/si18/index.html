Meeting point of Hand-Shaoyang and Hand-Taiyang手少阳、手太阳交会穴

📍 Location定位★

On the face, inferior to the zygomatic bone, directly below the outer canthus, in the depression.面部，颧骨下缘，目外眦直下凹陷中。

💡 From the outer canthus go straight down to the lower edge of the cheekbone; the point is in the hollow just below the bone, roughly level with the lower border of the nostril wing.由目外眦垂直向下摸到颧骨下缘，骨缘下方的凹陷即是，约与鼻翼下缘同高。

Perpendicular, or oblique / transverse直刺，或斜刺、平刺 · Perpendicular 0.3–0.5 cun; oblique or transverse 0.5–1 cun直刺 0.3–0.5 寸；斜刺或平刺 0.5–1 寸

🛡 Safety安全

The face is vascular: press after withdrawal to prevent bruising, and do not angle deep upward toward the orbit.面部血管丰富，出针后按压防血肿；不向上朝眼眶方向深刺。
