📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone with a pillow under the abdomen so the sacrum lies flat, buttocks relaxed.俯卧，腹下垫枕使骶部平展，臀部放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the posterior superior iliac spine (the bump below the low-back dimple); the hollow medial and inferior to it, level with the spine, is the second posterior sacral foramen (BL32). Move down and slightly in along the back of the sacrum to the next hollow: the third foramen, where BL33 lies. The four foramina converge toward the midline from top to bottom, so each lower one is a little more medial.先摸髂后上棘（腰窝下方的骨突），其内下方、平髂后上棘的凹陷是第 2 骶后孔（次髎）。沿骶骨背面向内下方摸下一个凹陷，就是第 3 骶后孔——中髎在孔中。四个骶后孔自上而下逐渐向内靠拢，所以每往下一孔都偏内一点。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Skin, subcutaneous tissue, the origin of gluteus maximus and the erector spinae aponeurosis, then into the third posterior sacral foramen. The foramen carries the dorsal ramus of S3 and its vessels; it opens into the sacral canal, which leads through the anterior foramina into the pelvis — in a thin person an over-deep needle can pass through the sacrum into the pelvis and injure the rectum or the presacral venous plexus.皮肤、皮下组织、臀大肌起始部与竖脊肌腱膜，进入第 3 骶后孔。孔内有第 3 骶神经后支与伴行血管；孔通向骶管，骶管前方经骶前孔通盆腔——瘦人针太深可穿过骶骨进入盆腔，伤及直肠或骶前静脉丛。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide the thumb up and down the back of the sacrum: each foramen is a fingertip-sized hollow that aches on pressure. The ridge in the midline is the median sacral crest; the point is not on the crest.拇指在骶骨背面上下滑动，孔处是一个手指尖大小的凹陷，按之酸胀；骶骨中线隆起是骶正中嵴，不在嵴上取。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Rest the index finger on BL32 and the little finger just above the sacral hiatus (between the two sacral horns); the middle two fingers fall roughly on BL33 and BL34. A first estimate only; the hollow of the foramen decides.把食指放在次髎、小指放在骶管裂孔上方（两骶角之间），中间两指自然分开落下的两处凹陷约是中髎、下髎。只作初筛，以孔的凹陷为准。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Needling in pregnancy: the eight liao lie over the pelvis; textbooks forbid lumbosacral points after the third month. In pregnancy do not needle, press hard or apply moxa.孕期针刺：八髎位于骶部，正对盆腔，教材规定怀孕三个月以上腰骶部禁针；孕期一律不针、不重按、不艾灸。
  • Pushing on after entering the foramen: 1–1.5 cun in the foramen is enough; deeper can exit the anterior foramen into the pelvis. If sharp electric pain shoots to the perineum or leg, withdraw slightly.进孔后继续深推：针在孔内 1–1.5 寸已足，再深可穿出骶前孔入盆腔。出现向会阴、下肢的剧烈触电感时退针少许。
  • Looking for the foramina in a straight vertical line: they converge downward, so following BL32's line straight down lands too lateral, on bone instead of in the foramen.按同一条直线往下找孔：骶后孔向下逐渐内收，按次髎的直线下找会偏外，扎到骨面进不了孔。

Where is BL33 Zhōngliáo?中髎（BL33）在哪里？

In the sacral region, at the third posterior sacral foramen.骶区，第 3 后骶孔。

How is BL33 needled?中髎（BL33）怎么刺？

Oblique inferomedially; 1–1.5 cun.向内下方斜刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at BL33?中髎（BL33）最要注意的安全问题？

The needle path targets a posterior sacral foramen and sacral neural structures; only trained practitioners should control direction and depth from bony landmarks to avoid neurovascular injury. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.针路直指后骶孔与骶神经结构；必须由受训人员按骨性标志控制方向和深度，避免神经血管损伤。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
