「筋會陽陵泉」——《难经》（公版，维基文库底本）

Nanjing (Classic of Difficult Issues) ch.45 establishes the Eight Influential Points: 'tendons meet at Yanglingquan.' 「膽合入於陽陵泉」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu states that the Gallbladder channel unites at Yanglingquan — making GB34 both the He-Sea point of the Gallbladder channel and the Lower He-Sea point of the Gallbladder ('He-Sea points treat the internal Fu organs'). It courses the Liver, benefits the Gallbladder, and clears Shao-Yang damp-heat for bitter taste, hypochondriac pain, jaundice, and vomiting.《灵枢·本输》及《邪气脏腑病形》指出足少阳胆经五输穴之合穴为阳陵泉，也是胆的下合穴（「合治内腑」），故能疏肝利胆、清泄少阳湿热，主治口苦、胁痛、黄疸、呕吐等胆腑实证。

「髀痺引膝股外廉痛，不仁，筋急，陽陵泉主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing records its use for bi-pain radiating down the lateral thigh and knee, numbness, and sinew spasm — matching the Foot Shao-Yang sinew pathway and grounding its modern use for lateral lower-limb pain and sciatica.《针灸甲乙经》载其主治下肢外侧痹痛、麻木不仁、筋脉拘急，与足少阳经筋循行高度吻合，是治疗下肢外侧痛与坐骨神经痛的核心远近兼顾穴。
