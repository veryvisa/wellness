Posture体位 · Seated or prone with arms relaxed and scapulae resting; avoid sustained arm elevation during location because it moves the scapula.坐位或俯卧，双臂自然下垂、肩胛放松；取穴时不要持续抬臂，以免肩胛骨移位。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Standard location: in the scapular region, in the hollow at the junction of the upper third and lower two-thirds of the line from the midpoint of the scapular spine to the inferior angle. Find both landmarks, divide the line in three and take the upper division point, roughly level with T4.国标：肩胛区，肩胛冈中点与肩胛骨下角连线上 1/3 与下 2/3 交点凹陷中。先摸到肩胛冈下缘的中点和肩胛下角，把两点连线三等分，靠上的分点即天宗，约平第 4 胸椎。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The center of the infraspinous fossa is a broad scapular surface covered by infraspinatus. Trace down from the spine or up from the inferior angle to confirm the middle of the bone; intercostal muscles and pleura lie deep to the lateral scapular edge, so stay on the palpable bone and never probe beyond its edge or into the chest.冈下窝中央可触及冈下肌覆盖的宽阔肩胛骨骨面。由肩胛冈向下、由下角向上滑动至骨面中部确认；肩胛骨外缘深面邻近肋间肌和胸膜，必须贴着骨面辨位，不能越过外缘或向胸腔深探。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    A gentle scapular retraction helps identify infraspinatus tension; with the muscle relaxed, find the central bony surface and any shallow hollow or tenderness. The scapula moves with arm elevation, so locate with the arms resting.轻收肩胛后可分辨冈下肌张力，放松时在肩胛骨面中央寻找压痛或浅凹；肩胛骨随抬臂移动，因此以双臂自然下垂的体位定位。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Miscounting from the inferior angle or needling beyond the lateral scapular edge can cross the bone into intercostal tissues and risk pneumothorax. This high-risk point is for trained practitioners following standards, staying on the scapular surface and never directing deeply beyond its edge or toward the chest.按肩胛下角连线误数到骨缘之外，或在肩胛外侧缘深刺，会越过骨面进入肋间组织并有气胸风险；高风险点仅可由受训人员沿肩胛骨表面按规范操作，不能向外缘、内侧胸壁或胸腔方向深刺。
  • Taking the midpoint of the spine–angle line: lower than the standard. SI11 is at the upper third, where tenderness is usually marked.取在肩胛冈中点与下角连线的中点：比国标低，偏到冈下窝下部。天宗在上 1/3 处，压痛常很明显。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

Choose for central infraspinous-fossa tenderness that changes with scapular movement; acromial or greater-tuberosity pain needs assessment of the cuff and subacromial structures.压痛位于肩胛冈下方冈下窝中央、随肩胛运动变化时选；肩峰或肱骨大结节痛需优先评估肩袖和肩峰下结构。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

SI11 suits shoulder-back symptoms with periscapular tenderness but does not replace cervical or nerve-root assessment; chest or breathing symptoms need medical care first.天宗适合伴肩胛肌群压痛的肩背症状，不代替颈椎或神经根评估；胸痛、呼吸症状先就医。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Infraspinatus tendinopathy or rotator-cuff injury冈下肌肌腱病或肩袖损伤

Weak external rotation, night pain, or restricted elevation warrants rotator-cuff assessment; tenderness near SI11 alone cannot diagnose tendinopathy.肩外旋无力、夜间痛或抬臂受限需评估肩袖；天宗附近压痛不能单独诊断肌腱病。

Periscapular myofascial pain肩胛周围肌筋膜痛

Infraspinatus tenderness can follow prolonged sitting or repetitive arm work; breathing-related pain, chest pain, or severe post-traumatic pain requires chest and bone assessment first.久坐、反复上肢工作后冈下肌压痛常见；若伴呼吸痛、胸痛或外伤后剧痛应先排除胸肺或骨性损伤。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Scapular pain with breathlessness, pain on inspiration, or chest pain—or these symptoms after needling—requires urgent evaluation for pneumothorax and other chest emergencies.肩胛区疼痛伴气短、吸气加重、胸痛，或针刺后出现上述症状：立即排查气胸等胸肺急症。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SI11 Tiānzōng?天宗（SI11）在哪里？

In the scapular region, in the depression at the junction of the upper one third and lower two thirds of the line connecting the midpoint of the scapular spine with the inferior angle of the scapula.肩胛区，肩胛冈中点与肩胛骨下角连线上 1/3 与下 2/3 交点凹陷中。

How is SI11 needled?天宗（SI11）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–1 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of SI11?天宗（SI11）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Small Intestine channel手太阳小肠经穴

Main safety point at SI11?天宗（SI11）最要注意的安全问题？

Bone lies beneath: stop on bone and never force. If the point drifts beyond the lateral border of the scapula, deep insertion can enter the intercostal space and puncture the pleura (pneumothorax).针下是肩胛骨，碰骨即止，不可强行进针；取穴偏到肩胛骨外缘之外再深刺，会进入肋间、刺破胸膜引起气胸。
