    Identify the medial end of the scapular spine and the supraspinous fossa above it. SI13 lies in the hollow above the medial end, in the curved central scapular region.摸到肩胛冈内侧端及其上方的肩胛冈上窝，曲垣在肩胛冈内侧端上方、肩胛骨中央曲陷处。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Trace the scapular spine medially to its inner end and feel the hollow just above it in the supraspinous fossa. Trapezius and levator scapulae cover the area; chest wall and pleura are nearby medial and deep to the scapula. Keep the scapular boundary clear and do not probe deeply toward the thorax.沿肩胛冈由外向内滑至内侧端，向上进入冈上窝的局部凹陷即曲垣。斜方肌、肩胛提肌覆盖其表面，胸壁与胸膜在肩胛骨内侧及深面附近；保持肩胛骨边界清晰，勿向胸腔深探。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Cross-check the medial scapular-spine end, lateral edge of the paraspinal muscles, and supraspinous hollow. The scapula moves with the arm, so confirm with the arm resting and do not substitute a paraspinal tender point.放松肩部，以肩胛冈内侧端、脊柱旁肌外缘和冈上窝凹陷三者互证；肩胛随手臂移动，需在自然下垂体位确认，勿将脊柱旁压痛点当作曲垣。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • A point below the medial scapular-spine end drifts into the infraspinous region; excessive medial drift reaches the paraspinal area. Pleura and lung lie deep nearby. Do not needle perpendicularly into the chest; trained practitioners must follow standards using a shallow oblique direction along the scapular plane.取到肩胛冈内侧端下方会落入冈下区域，过内则偏到棘突旁；该高风险区胸膜、肺位于深部邻近，不能垂直朝胸腔深刺，方向应由受训人员按规范沿肩胛面浅斜处理。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

Consider for tenderness above the medial scapular spine with periscapular myofascial tightness; breathing-related or post-traumatic pain needs chest-wall and lung assessment first.压痛位于肩胛冈内侧端上方、肩胛周围肌筋膜紧张时可考虑；如疼痛随呼吸加重或有外伤，应先排除胸壁及肺部损伤。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

Use only as a local adjunct when neck tension includes upper-medial scapular tenderness; isolated stiffness calls for neck or distal points, and chest warning signs take priority.仅在颈肩紧张伴肩胛上内侧压痛时作局部辅助；单纯项强不如颈部/远端穴直接，胸肺警讯必须先处理。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Levator scapulae or upper-trapezius myofascial pain肩胛提肌或斜方肌上束肌筋膜痛

Tenderness at the upper medial scapula can follow neck-shoulder tension, shrugging, or prolonged sitting; arm numbness or weakness warrants cervical nerve-root assessment.颈肩紧张、耸肩或久坐后内上肩胛压痛常见；若伴上肢麻木无力，应另行评估颈神经根。

Periscapular tendon or chest-wall injury肩胛周围肌腱或胸壁损伤

Pain with deep breathing, chest-wall tenderness, or marked bruising after trauma warrants evaluation for rib and chest injury, not treatment as simple scapular aching.外伤后深呼吸痛、胸壁压痛或明显瘀肿需排除肋骨及胸肺损伤，不应当作单纯肩胛酸痛。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SI13 Qūyuán?曲垣（SI13）在哪里？

In the scapular region, in the depression superior to the medial end of the scapular spine.肩胛区，肩胛冈内侧端上缘凹陷中。

How is SI13 needled?曲垣（SI13）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–1 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of SI13?曲垣（SI13）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Small Intestine channel手太阳小肠经穴

Main safety point at SI13?曲垣（SI13）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung apex lie deep superomedially: aim laterally and downward onto the supraspinous fossa, never deep medially, downward or toward the supraclavicular fossa.内上方深面是胸膜和肺尖：针向外下、贴冈上窝骨面，不可向内下或锁骨上窝方向深刺。
