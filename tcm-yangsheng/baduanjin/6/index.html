Form 6: Reaching down to the feet第6式 · 两手攀足固肾腰

One fold down and up is one repetition; 6 in all一下一上为一遍，共 6 遍

Liǎng shǒu pān zú gù shèn yāo

Qigong养生功法 › Baduanjin (Eight Pieces of Brocade)八段锦 › Form 6: Reaching down to the feet第6式 · 两手攀足固肾腰

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Osteoporosis: do not bend all the way down and do not round the back (loaded spinal flexion carries a risk of compression fracture)骨质疏松：不弯腰到底，弯腰时背不拱圆（脊柱屈曲加重压有压缩骨折风险）
  • High blood pressure, glaucoma or retinal disease: do not keep the head below the heart for long, and come up slowly高血压、青光眼、视网膜病变：头不长时间低于心脏，起身要慢
  • During an acute disc prolapse or a sciatica flare: skip it, or go only to the knees腰椎间盘突出急性期、坐骨神经痛发作期：先不做或只做到膝盖
  • If you get dizzy on standing: hold on to something as you come up容易体位性头晕的人：起身时扶着东西

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Stand with legs straight and raise the arms forward and up, palms forward两腿伸直站立，两臂向前、向上举起，掌心向前
  2. Rotate the arms, bend the elbows and press the hands down in front of the chest, then turn them and slide them back under the armpits两臂外旋、屈肘，两掌下按到胸前，再翻掌经腋下向后插
  3. Run the palms down either side of the spine to the buttocks, then fold forward and keep sliding them down the backs of the legs, round the sides of the feet, to rest on top of the feet; lift the head and hold briefly两掌心贴着脊柱两侧向下摩运到臀部，随即上身前俯，两掌沿腿后继续向下摩运，经脚两侧放到脚面；抬头，略停
  4. Slide the hands forward along the floor and let the arms lead the trunk slowly back up, arms overhead两掌沿地面向前伸，用手臂带动上身慢慢起立，两臂上举

🌬 Breathe out as you fold, in as you rise; or breathe naturally. Come up slowly.俯身时呼气，起身时吸气；或自然呼吸。起身要慢。

✅ Key points要领

  • Fold from the hips, knees straight but not locked俯身靠髋关节折叠，膝盖伸直但不锁死
  • Keep the palms on the body all the way down the spine and the backs of the legs两掌沿脊柱两侧、腿后一路摩下去，手不离身体
  • On the way up the arms lead and draw the trunk with them; no sudden effort from the lower back起身时手臂先走、带着上身起来，腰不突然发力

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Forcing the back to round to reach the feet, or bending the knees a lot为了摸到脚背硬弯腰、膝盖弯得很厉害
  • Coming up too fast and seeing stars起身太快，眼前发黑
  • Dropping the head and holding the breath低头憋气

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Slide the hands only to the knees or shins; there is no need to reach the feet. Or sit and slide the hands down the thighs to the knees.手只摩到膝盖或小腿，不必摸到脚；或坐在椅子上，两手沿大腿摩到膝盖。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 TCM calls the lower back 'the house of the kidneys'; in this form the palms run down past the areas of BL23 Shenshu and BL40 Weizhong, which is why the rhyme says it 'firms the kidneys and waist'. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.中医说「腰为肾之府」，这一式两掌沿腰背摩下，经过的正是肾俞（BL23）、委中（BL40）一带，口诀因此说它「固肾腰」。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
