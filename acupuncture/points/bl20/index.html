📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: below the T11 spine.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），脾俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 11 胸椎棘突下定。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Arms hanging, the inferior angles of the scapulae are about level with T7; count down T8, T9, T10, T11. Cross-check by counting up from the iliac crests (about L4): L3, L2, L1, T12, T11.   3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline using the width of the patient's index and middle fingers held together (or thumb-cun reference). Because body builds vary, finger measurement is only a rough guide and must be checked against the T11 spinous process and erector spinae.以患者本人食指、中指并拢的横径约 1.5 寸（或拇指同身寸折算）自后正中线向外量取；临床中不同患者体型差异大，手指同身寸仅作辅助参考，须结合棘突与竖脊肌外缘定位。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located 1.5 cun lateral to the lower border of the T11 spinous process over the erector spinae beneath trapezius, latissimus dorsi, or rhomboids, near the 11th rib and intercostal muscles. Needle shallowly obliquely toward the spine (0.5–0.8 cun); the intercostal neurovascular bundle lies laterally/deeply and the pleura and lung lie deeper, so never insert deeply or angle laterally.位于第 11 胸椎棘突下旁开 1.5 寸，约当竖脊肌肌腹与外侧缘内侧交界偏内区域，浅层有斜方肌、背阔肌或菱形肌覆盖，深层为竖脊肌、肋间肌及第 11 肋骨附近。进针必须朝脊柱方向浅斜刺（0.5–0.8 寸）；其深部外侧与下方紧邻肋间神经血管束，再深即胸膜腔与肺组织，切忌深刺或向外侧刺入。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate just below the T11 spinous process and 1.5 cun laterally; tenderness, ropy bands, or heightened muscle tone often appear in spleen-stomach disorders. Heavy pressure or pressing into an intercostal space causes sharp or radiating pain, so ease off immediately.顺棘突间隙轻压并向旁开 1.5 寸处按压，脾胃病变时此处常有明显压痛、条索感或肌张力增高；按压过重或向肋间隙顶压会引起局部锐痛或放射痛，应立即减轻压力。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Needling deeply or angling laterally: pleura and lung lie deep to the intercostal muscles and ribs, so deep or lateral insertion risks pneumothorax and can injure intercostal vessels and nerves.向外侧或深部直刺：背俞穴深面为肋间肌、肋骨、胸膜与肺，向外或深刺极易穿透肋间隙伤及胸膜和肺引发气胸，也可能损伤肋间血管神经。
  • Miscounting thoracic spinous processes (such as confusing T10 or T12 with T11): counting down from the T7 inferior scapular angle can slip by a level, shifting the point and changing its relation to the ribs.误取错椎节（如误认 T10 或 T12 为 T11）：肩胛下角平 T7，向下数椎节容易错位，导致定位偏上或偏下，影响背俞穴针对性与深部肋骨对应关系。
  • Drifting too far laterally (toward 2–3 cun from the midline): moving laterally approaches the lateral erector spinae and rib angles where soft tissue thins near the intercostal space, raising pneumothorax risk and missing the first lateral bladder line.旁开距离过大（偏向外侧至 2–3 寸一线）：偏外侧更接近竖脊肌外缘与肋角区域，局部软组织变薄、更靠近肋间隙与胸膜侧缘，气胸风险升高且偏离足太阳膀胱经第一侧线。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Often paired with BL21 and ST36 for dull postprandial pain with spleen deficiency and loose stools; acute severe abdominal pain needs medical evaluation first, while acute cold-qi pain may pair with points such as CV12 and PC6.脾胃虚弱、食少便溏、餐后隐痛绵绵时常与胃俞、足三里同用；急性剧烈腹痛或急腹症先就医，寒凝气滞急痛可酌配中脘、内关等。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Chosen for chronic spleen-deficiency diarrhea with undigested food and fatigue to warm and fortify the spleen; acute infectious diarrhea with high fever or dehydration needs medical care first, and acute damp-heat diarrhea is not treated primarily with warming tonification.脾虚久泻、完谷不化、神疲乏力时选它温运脾阳、健脾止泻；急性感染性腹泻伴高热脱水需先就医补液，湿热暴泻不以温补为主。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Used as a supportive point for cold-deficiency abdominal pain and distension relieved by warmth and pressure; do not delay medical care for acute abdomen or unexplained pain, and pair with points such as ST25 and ST36 for acute qi stagnation or damp-heat.虚寒腹痛、腹胀喜温喜按时可用作调理配穴；急腹症、外科痛或器质性病变未明时不可延误就医，实热或气滞剧痛常配天枢、足三里等通调腑气。

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

May offer mild reference for lower thoracic or thoracolumbar aching, but true low-back pain in the lumbar or sacroiliac region is treated primarily with points such as BL23, BL40, and BL25 rather than BL20.胸腰段交界或下胸背偏上酸痛可作局部与邻近调理参考，但真正腰部（腰椎及骶髂区）痛首选肾俞、委中、大肠俞等腰部及远端穴，不以此穴为主。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is BL20 Píshū?脾俞（BL20）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of T11, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 11 胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL20 needled?脾俞（BL20）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of BL20?脾俞（BL20）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Spleen脾之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL20?脾俞（BL20）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only shallow oblique insertion toward the spine; never insert deeply or laterally.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向浅斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
