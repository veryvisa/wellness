🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「漏谷，在內踝上六寸骨下陷者中，足太陰絡，刺入三分，留七呼，灸三壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing specifies SP7 as lying 6 cun above the medial malleolus in a depression below the bone and provides early needling and moxibustion parameters; modern textbooks and national standards still anchor it at 6 cun above the medial malleolus, posterior to the medial border of the tibia.《针灸甲乙经》明确记载漏谷位于内踝上六寸胫骨下方的凹陷中，并给出早期刺灸参数；现代教材与国家标准仍以“内踝尖上 6 寸，胫骨内侧缘后际”为定位基准。

「少腹脹急，小便不利，厥氣上頭巔，漏谷主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing links SP7 to lower abdominal fullness and urgency, difficult urination, and qi surging upward to the vertex, reflecting the Spleen channel ascending along the medial leg to regulate lower-burner qi movement and fluid distribution.《针灸甲乙经》指出漏谷可用于少腹胀急、小便不利及气逆上冲等症，体现足太阴脾经经气循下肢内侧上行、调理下焦气机与水液输布的思路。

「漏谷（一名太陰絡）內踝上六寸，胻骨下陷中，銅人針三分，禁灸」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Dacheng records SP7 at 6 cun above the medial malleolus in the depression below the tibia and includes a note against moxibustion, reminding clinicians to evaluate local heat, swelling, varicose veins, or edema carefully rather than applying moxibustion blindly.《针灸大成》记载漏谷内踝上六寸、胫骨（胻骨）下陷中的定位与“禁灸”一说，提示临床上对局部红肿热痛或静脉曲张、水肿等特殊情况须审慎评估，不可盲目施灸。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Medial lower-leg myofascial strain or soleus / flexor digitorum longus strain小腿内侧肌筋膜劳损或比目鱼肌、趾长屈肌劳损

Common after distance running, prolonged standing, or sudden training increases, causing medial-leg aching and tenderness that may be provoked by resisted ankle plantarflexion or toe flexion; seek medical evaluation first if marked local swelling or worsening night/rest pain occurs to rule out stress fracture or muscle tear.常见于长跑、久站或突然增加运动量后，小腿内侧酸痛、压痛，抗阻踝跖屈或足趾屈曲可诱发不适；若伴有明显局部肿胀或夜间静息痛加重，需先就医排除应力性骨折或肌肉撕裂。

Saphenous nerve or tibial nerve branch entrapment / peripheral neuropathic pain隐神经或胫神经分支卡压／周围神经痛

May present as burning, tingling, or numbness along the medial leg, medial malleolus, or sole, sometimes with radiating paresthesia on percussion or palpation; persistent sensory loss or weakness warrants medical neurological assessment first.可表现为小腿内侧、内踝或足底方向的烧灼感、刺痛或麻木，叩击或按压神经走形处可能出现放射感；出现持续性感觉减退或肌力下降应先由医生评估神经情况。

Chronic venous insufficiency, superficial phlebitis, or deep vein thrombosis (DVT) screening慢性静脉功能不全、浅静脉炎或深静脉血栓（DVT）风险排查

If unilateral leg swelling, warmth, redness, or worsening varicosities appear, avoid deep needling or heat/moxibustion and seek medical evaluation with vascular ultrasound first to rule out deep vein thrombosis or phlebitis.若小腿内侧出现单侧肿胀、皮温升高、发红或静脉曲张加重，切勿贸然深刺或热敷/艾灸，应先就医做血管超声排查静脉血栓或静脉炎。

Where is SP7 Lòugǔ?漏谷（SP7）在哪里？

On the medial leg, 6 cun above the prominence of the medial malleolus, posterior to the medial border of the tibia.小腿内侧，内踝尖上 6 寸，胫骨内侧缘后际。

How is SP7 needled?漏谷（SP7）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at SP7?漏谷（SP7）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid the great saphenous vein; do not angle deep posteromedially into the posterior tibial neurovascular bundle.避开大隐静脉；针不向后内侧深刺至胫后血管神经束。
