GV4

命门 Mìngmén

Gate of Life · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

In the lumbar region, in the depression below the spinous process of L2, on the posterior midline.脊柱区，第 2 腰椎棘突下凹陷中，后正中线上。

💡 Count two spinous processes up from the iliac crest line (about L4); roughly opposite the umbilicus.从髂嵴连线（约 L4）向上数两个棘突；约与脐相对。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

May angle slightly upward; moxibustion is preferred for warming Kidney yang.可略向上斜刺；温补肾阳多用灸法。

🛡 Safety安全★

In adults the conus ends around the lower L1 to L2 level, so the conus tip and upper cauda equina lie beneath; never needle through the ligamentum flavum. Avoid the lumbosacral region after 3 months of pregnancy.成人脊髓圆锥多止于第 1 腰椎下缘至第 2 腰椎，命门深面为圆锥末端与马尾起始；不可深刺透黄韧带。妊娠 3 个月以上腰骶部不宜针。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone or seated leaning forward with the back relaxed; identify L2 before locating the space below it.俯卧或坐位前屈，腰背放松；先辨认 L2 棘突，再定位其下方间隙。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    On the lumbar midline, find the depression below L2. The iliac-crest line is roughly at L4 and can help count upward, but does not replace vertebral identification.在腰部后正中线，找到第 2 腰椎棘突下方凹陷；髂嵴线大致平 L4，可由此向上逐节计数，不可直接按固定寸数替代椎骨辨认。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate the L2 spinous process and the interspinous tissue below it; erector-spinae muscle lies laterally. The canal is deep to the midline, so postsurgical anatomy, deformity or focal bony pain needs assessment; avoid deep canal-directed procedures.触认 L2 棘突及其下方棘间软组织；旁开可摸到竖脊肌。中线深部邻近椎管，腰椎术后、畸形或局部骨痛需先评估，不可向椎管深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Use slight flexion to reveal the interspinous space, then recheck the midline at rest. Count against GV5 above and GV3 below to avoid a one-level error.令患者轻度前屈以显出棘突间隙，再放松复核正中凹陷；与上方 GV5、下方 GV3 逐节核对，避免把相邻节段当作命门。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Confusing L1–L2 with L2–L3 shifts the point by one level; deep work in the interspinous space may approach the canal and neural structures.把 L1–L2 间隙误作 L2–L3 会上下错一节；在棘突间隙向深部操作可能接近椎管及神经结构。
