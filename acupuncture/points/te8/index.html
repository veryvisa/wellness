Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Where is TE8 Sānyángluò?三阳络（TE8）在哪里？

Posterior forearm, 4 cun above the dorsal wrist crease, between ulna and radius.前臂后面，腕背横纹上4寸，尺骨与桡骨之间。

How is TE8 needled?三阳络（TE8）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1寸。

Point of the Triple Energizer channel手少阳三焦经穴
