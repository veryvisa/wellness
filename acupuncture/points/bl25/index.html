Large Intestine Shu · Take care需谨慎

★Back-shu point of the Large Intestine大肠之背俞穴

📍 Location定位★

Below the spinous process of L4, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 4 腰椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

💡 Find the L4 spinous process (C7 is the most prominent on neck flexion; scapular-spine roots ≈ T3, inferior angles ≈ T7, iliac-crest line ≈ L4) and measure 1.5 cun lateral at its lower border (medial scapular border to midline = 3 cun).先定 L4 棘突（C7 低头最突出、随转头微动；肩胛冈内端平 T3、肩胛下角平 T7、髂嵴最高点连线平 L4），在其下缘水平旁开 1.5 寸（肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸）。

🪡 Needling刺法

Oblique toward the spine向脊柱方向斜刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Perpendicular 0.8–1.2 cun or oblique toward the spine; no needling of the lumbosacral area in pregnancy.直刺 0.8–1.2 寸或向脊柱斜刺；孕妇腰骶部不针。

🛡 Safety安全

Deep lumbar tissues include muscle, bony structures, and neurovascular branches; avoid forceful deep insertion. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.腰部深层有肌肉、骨性结构和神经血管分支；避免用力深刺。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: below the L4 spine.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），大肠俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 4 腰椎棘突下定。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure 1.5 thumb-cun of the patient's own thumb laterally from the posterior midline, or use combined index and middle fingers as a rough check, relying finally on the 1.5-cun lateral proportion from the spinous process.由后正中线（督脉循行线）向患侧或双侧旁开患者本人拇指同身寸 1.5 寸（约 1 寸半宽），或用食指、中指并拢横量作粗略参考，最终以棘突旁开 1.5 寸骨性比例为准。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Place hands on the iliac crests or trace across from the highest points of both iliac crests toward the posterior midline, which roughly aligns with the L4 spinous process or L4–L5 space; palpate adjacent spinous processes to confirm L4, then go 1.5 cun laterally.双手叉腰或由两侧髂嵴最高点向后正中线作连线，连线多与第 4 腰椎棘突或 L4–L5 棘突间隙相平；顺棘突向上摸第 3、第 2 腰椎棘突，向下摸第 5 腰椎棘突与骶正中嵴，定准 L4 棘突下缘后旁开 1.5 寸。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Beneath the skin, subcutaneous tissue, and thoracolumbar fascia lie the erector spinae and multifidus, with lumbar transverse processes, laminae, posterior rami of lumbar nerves (cutaneous and deep branches), and dorsal branches of lumbar vessels deeper.   5. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    With the patient prone or seated leaning slightly forward, locate the spinal level aligned with the highest points of the iliac crests, press about two finger-widths lateral below the spinous process for initial tenderness or muscle tension, and verify the spinous level.患者俯卧或端坐前倾，先找两侧髂嵴最高点连线对应的腰椎棘突水平，再在棘突下缘旁开约两横指处试压，寻找局部压痛或肌紧张点作初筛，随后核对棘突序数。

  6. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently at 1.5 cun lateral below the L4 spinous process over the erector spinae and thoracolumbar fascia to check for tenderness or taut bands; if pressure triggers radiating tingling down the buttock or posterior/lateral leg, lumbar posterior rami or radicular referred pain may be involved, so ease pressure and assess.拇指指腹在 L4 棘突下缘旁开 1.5 寸处沿肌纤维方向轻按与滑动，常可触及竖脊肌外侧缘或肌腹筋膜结节及局部压痛；若按压引起沿臀部或下肢后外侧放射麻痛，提示可能刺激腰神经后支或邻近神经根牵涉痛，应减轻压力并评估。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Choose BL25 with ashi points when lumbar stiffness or myofascial tension coexists with intestinal qi stagnation; compare with BL40 or BL23 for broader posterior-chain or kidney-deficiency presentations, and seek medical care first for acute severe pain or red flags.偏重腰部经气不畅、筋膜紧绷或伴肠道气滞者可选大肠俞与阿是穴；若以足太阳循行及腰背远端联动为主，可与委中 BL40 或肾俞 BL23 对比选用，急性剧痛或红旗征先就医。

Sciatica坐骨神经痛 ⚖️

BL25 may serve as an adjunct when sciatica coincides with lumbar muscle tension or bowel sluggishness, but it does not replace lower-limb channel points like GB30 or BL40 or medical nerve-root assessment when radiating pain or weakness is prominent.若坐骨神经痛伴腰部局部肌张力增高或排便不畅，可作辅助配穴之一，但单靠大肠俞不足以覆盖下肢循行与神经根评估；放射痛明显或肌力减退时需先就医并结合环跳 GB30、委中 BL40 等下肢穴位对比。

Constipation便秘 ⚖️

Suited to constipation involving impaired qi movement or fluid deficiency, often paired with ST25 and ST37; compare with TE6 or KI6 depending on heat or yin-deficiency features, and rule out organic causes or bleeding with a doctor first.适用于气机不畅、津液不足或虚实夹杂之便秘，常与天枢 ST25、上巨虚 ST37 相配；热结实秘偏重清泻传导时可对比支沟 TE6、照海 KI6 等侧重不同，器质性梗阻或便血须先就医。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Useful for diarrhea linked to disordered large-intestine qi alongside ST25 and ST37; compare with BL20 and ST36 when spleen-qi deficiency predominates, and seek medical care first for high fever, bloody stools, or dehydration.适用于大肠气机失调、腑气不和之泄泻，常与天枢 ST25、上巨虚 ST37 合用；脾虚久泻偏重健脾益气时需对比脾俞 BL20、足三里 ST36，伴高热、脓血便或脱水须先就医。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Applicable to abdominal pain along the large-intestine distribution from qi stagnation or sluggish bowel transit, often paired with ST25; never rely on acupuncture alone for acute abdomen, severe rebound tenderness, fever, or vomiting—seek immediate medical care.适用于脐周或下腹偏大肠循行之气滞、腑气不通所致腹痛，常配天枢 ST25；急腹症、剧烈压痛反跳痛或伴发热呕吐时绝不可仅靠针灸，须立即就医。

Where is BL25 Dàchángshū?大肠俞（BL25）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of L4, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 4 腰椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL25 needled?大肠俞（BL25）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–1 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of BL25?大肠俞（BL25）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Large Intestine大肠之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL25?大肠俞（BL25）最要注意的安全问题？

Deep lumbar tissues include muscle, bony structures, and neurovascular branches; avoid forceful deep insertion. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.腰部深层有肌肉、骨性结构和神经血管分支；避免用力深刺。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
