📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or prone with the head upright or slightly flexed and the neck and occipital muscles relaxed for clear landmark palpation and safe insertion.坐位或俯卧位，头部端正微低，颈部及枕部肌肉放松，便于触诊枕骨标志与安全进针。

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Older textbooks put it 2.5 cun above the middle of the posterior hairline and 1.3 cun lateral; GB/T 12346-2021 uses the bony landmark instead: level with the upper border of the external occipital protuberance, 1.3 cun from the midline (level with GV17). Hairlines vary widely, so the protuberance decides.旧教材写作后发际正中直上 2.5 寸、旁开 1.3 寸；GB/T 12346-2021 改以骨性标志定：横平枕外隆凸上缘，后正中线旁开 1.3 寸（与脑户 GV17 同高）。发际高低差别大，以枕外隆凸为准，不画骨度尺。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate the most prominent midline bump on the back of the head—the external occipital protuberance—find its superior border line, and measure 1.3 cun laterally to locate BL9.先在枕后正中线摸到最突出的骨性隆起——枕外隆凸，确定其上缘水平线，再向患侧（外侧）量 1.3 寸处即得玉枕。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Superficial layers carry the greater occipital nerve and occipital artery, underlain by the trapezius attachment and occipitalis muscle over the occipital pericranium and occipital bone. Needle obliquely upward or laterally along the shallow plane rather than deeply inferomedially to avoid injuring the greater occipital nerve, occipital artery, or deep tissues.穴区表浅层有枕大神经与枕动脉穿行，深层为斜方肌附着部与枕肌，下接枕骨骨膜及枕骨。针刺须向上或向外斜刺（沿头皮/枕骨浅层方向），避开向内下方深刺，以免损伤枕大神经、枕动脉或刺伤深层软组织与骨膜。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Lightly palpate or stroke with a fingertip about 1.3 cun lateral to the superior border of the external occipital protuberance; increased muscle tension or tenderness is often felt, sometimes with aching or mild radiation toward the occiput or vertex.指腹在枕外隆凸上缘旁开约 1.3 寸处轻按或轻拨，常可触及浅层肌纤维张力增高或压痛，按压时可能出现向枕部或头顶方向的酸胀感或轻微放射感。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Inserting deeply inferomedially: this risks injuring the greater occipital nerve and occipital artery, causing sharp pain or hematoma, or hitting the occipital pericranium and causing severe pain or a stuck needle.向内下方深刺：易刺伤枕大神经与枕动脉引起明显疼痛、血肿，或触碰枕骨骨膜导致剧痛与滞针。
  • Drifting out to GB19: it is at the same height but on the Shaoyang line, about 2.25 cun from the midline.偏外取到脑空（GB19）：脑空与玉枕同高，但在旁开约 2.25 寸的少阳线上。
  • Needling straight in too deeply: soft tissue over the occiput is relatively thin, so deep perpendicular insertion easily causes discomfort or neurovascular injury.直刺过深：枕部皮下软组织较薄，过深直刺易致患者不适甚至损伤血管神经。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Useful as a local or channel-guided point for posterior or unilateral head discomfort with occipital tenderness or eye strain; for sudden severe pain, neurological signs, or acute migraine, pair appropriately with points such as GB20, EX-HN5, or LI4 or seek medical evaluation first without delaying care.枕后或半侧头部不适、伴后枕压痛或视疲劳时可作局部/循经配穴；突发剧痛、伴神经症状或偏头痛急性发作常需结合风池、太阳、合谷等穴或先就医排查，不作急症延误。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Appropriate as an adjunct when head fogginess accompanies posterior occipital tightness or neck discomfort; if dizziness comes with severe vomiting, sudden hearing change, gait instability, or central signs, seek medical assessment first rather than relying on this point.头部昏沉、伴后枕紧绷或颈项不适时可作辅助配穴；眩晕伴剧烈呕吐、耳鸣听力骤变、步态不稳或中枢神经体征时须先就医评估，单靠此穴不合适。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

May be paired with points such as BL2 and GB20 as an adjunct for periorbital aching and visual fatigue; acute redness, severe pain, elevated intraocular pressure, vision drop, or post-trauma eye pain requires ophthalmologic evaluation first.眼眶周酸胀、视疲劳相关目不适可与攒竹、风池等配用辅助调理；急性红肿热痛、眼压升高、视力下降或外伤后眼痛必须先由眼科检查，不能单凭穴位处理。

Where is BL9 Yùzhěn?玉枕（BL9）在哪里？

On the occiput, 1.3 cun lateral to the posterior midline, level with the superior border of the external occipital protuberance.枕部，后发际正中旁开 1.3 寸，平枕外隆凸上缘。

How is BL9 needled?玉枕（BL9）怎么刺？

Oblique upward or laterally; 0.3–0.5 cun.向上或向外斜刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Main safety point at BL9?玉枕（BL9）最要注意的安全问题？

The greater occipital nerve and occipital vessels are superficial; thread within the scalp and never drive downward-medially toward the foramen magnum.枕大神经和枕动静脉在浅层；只在头皮层内平刺，不向下内方朝枕骨大孔方向深刺。
