The dorsal ulnar nerve branch and wrist veins are superficial nearby; avoid heavy pressure on the ulnar prominence and distinguish it from SI5 distal to the styloid.轻旋前臂时触诊桡尺远端关节，尺骨头桡侧与桡骨之间出现相对凹隙，即养老。尺神经背侧支和腕背静脉在邻近浅层；避免向尺侧突起处过压，并与阳谷（茎突远端）分开。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slowly pronate and supinate the forearm to find the cleft that becomes apparent with rotation. Measuring only from the wrist crease or pressing on the ulnar-styloid tip can misplace the point.前臂缓慢旋前、旋后，找到骨缝随旋转显露的位置；若只在腕横纹上量寸或固定压在尺骨茎突顶端，容易取错。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • A point distal to the ulnar styloid is SI5; a point on the ulnar side of the ulnar head misses the cleft and may irritate the dorsal ulnar nerve branch. Recheck the hollow while rotating the forearm.误取尺骨茎突远端会落到阳谷（SI5），误取尺骨头尺侧则失去骨缝并可能刺激尺神经背支；旋转前臂复核凹隙可减少偏差。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

Consider this distal xi-cleft point for shoulder pain with Small Intestine-channel wrist/arm symptoms or limited elevation; isolated cuff weakness or painful abduction needs shoulder assessment.肩痛兼手太阳经腕臂症状或上举受限时可作远端郄穴；单纯肩袖无力、外展痛需先评估肩部病因。

Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳） ⚖️

SI6 is at the distal wrist and does not replace local elbow assessment; use only as a distal adjunct when pain follows the Small Intestine channel through the arm.养老定位在远端腕部，不用于代替肘部局部评估；仅在疼痛沿手太阳经贯穿腕臂时作为远端配穴。

Where is SI6 Yǎnglǎo?养老（SI6）在哪里？

On the posterior forearm, 1 cun proximal to the dorsal wrist crease, in the depression radial to the head of the ulna.前臂后区，腕背横纹上 1 寸，尺骨头桡侧凹陷中。

How is SI6 needled?养老（SI6）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺或斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of SI6?养老（SI6）是哪类特定穴？

Main safety point at SI6?养老（SI6）最要注意的安全问题？

Find the cleft in supination first; needling palm-down hits the ulnar head. Do not enter the distal radioulnar joint.要在旋后位找到骨缝再进针，掌心向下直接扎会顶在尺骨头上；不刺入桡尺远侧关节腔。
