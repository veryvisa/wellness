Spine pinching: a back massage parents do for children捏脊：家长给孩子做的背部按摩

Your child lies across your lap while you lift the skin of the back a little at a time and walk it upward, from just above the bottom to the back of the neck. That is spine pinching, and many families learned it from grandparents. First, when not to do it and when to stop; then how to do it and how many passes.孩子趴在你腿上，背上的皮肤被一小段一小段捏起来、往上推，从屁股上面一直走到脖子后面——这就是捏脊，很多家庭从老人那里学来。下面先讲什么情况不做、什么时候该停手，再讲怎么捏、捏几遍。

⏱ 3–5 minutes3–5 分钟 · In the morning or before meals, or an hour after; once a day早上或饭前、饭后 1 小时以后；一天 1 次

👶 Ages 6 months to 12 years适合 6 个月到 12 岁

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • A baby under 3 months with a fever (rectal 38°C or higher) needs a doctor urgently, with no massage first; any fever under 6 months needs a doctor3 个月以下的宝宝发烧（肛温 38°C 或以上）要立即就医，不做推拿；6 个月以下发烧都要看医生
  • Seizure, struggling to breathe or blue lips, cannot be woken or is floppy and unresponsive: call 911抽搐、呼吸费力或嘴唇发青、叫不醒或软绵绵没反应：打 911
  • Signs of dehydration: fewer than 4 wet diapers in 24 hours (fewer than 3 for older children), no tears when crying, dry mouth and tongue, sunken eyes or soft spot, refusing to drink: see a doctor the same day脱水的样子：24 小时湿尿布少于 4 片（大孩子小便少于 3 次）、哭时没有眼泪、嘴和舌头干、眼窝或囟门凹下去、不肯喝水：当天看医生
  • Not gaining or losing weight, or poor appetite for more than a week or two: see the family doctor or a paediatrician; do not rely on spine pinching孩子最近体重不长或往下掉、总是不想吃东西超过一两周：先看家庭医生或儿科，不要只靠捏脊

🛑 When to stop and see a doctor什么时候停手去看医生

  • The child cries harder as you go, or flinches or cries out when you touch one spot: stop, and have a doctor look at that spot捏的时候孩子哭得越来越厉害，或碰背上某一处就躲、就喊痛：停手，那一处请医生看
  • Bruising or broken skin where you pinched, or redness still there the next day: pause for a few days; see a doctor if bruising keeps appearing背上捏过的地方出现淤青、破皮，或第二天还红：停几天，淤青反复出现要看医生
  • After a week or two nothing has changed with eating, sleep or stools, or new problems such as fever, vomiting or diarrhoea appear: see a doctor rather than doing more做了一两周，孩子吃饭、睡觉、大便的问题没有变化，或出现发烧、呕吐、腹泻等新情况：去看医生，不要继续加量

🤲 Who does it, and how to start谁来做、开始之前

Ages 6 months to 12 years适合 6 个月到 12 岁

Done by a parent or the adult who usually cares for the child. Trim and smooth your nails, warm your hands, keep the room warm; a little baby oil is fine, but no talc (babies breathe it in). Do it when the child is calm. Crying, squirming or pushing your hand away is the signal to stop; never hold a child down to finish.由家长或固定照顾孩子的大人做。先剪短磨平指甲、把手搓热，房间暖和；可以用一点婴儿润肤油，不用爽身粉（宝宝会吸进去）。孩子情绪平稳时做，哭闹、扭动、推开你的手，就是停手的信号——这时候不要按住孩子硬做。

✋ When not to do it这些情况先别做

  • Broken skin, an eczema flare, a rash or a boil on the back背部皮肤破损、湿疹或皮疹发作、有疖子时
  • During a fever, or when the child is unwell or listless发烧、精神不好、正在生病的时候
  • Children with a known spinal condition (spina bifida, after spinal surgery and so on) or a bleeding or clotting disorder有已知脊柱问题（脊柱裂、脊柱手术后等）、出血或凝血问题的孩子
  • Babies under 6 months: skin and spine are too delicate; ask the doctor first6 个月以下的宝宝：皮肤和脊柱都太嫩，先问医生

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1

    Lay the child face down on the bed or across your lap with the back bare and the head turned to one side so breathing is easy. Rub your hands warm first.孩子趴在床上或你腿上，背部露出来，头侧向一边、呼吸顺畅。你先把手搓热。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute to settle约 1 分钟准备

  2. 2
    Circular rubbing摩法

    Lay your palm flat on the child's back and stroke in gentle circles so the child gets used to your hand.手掌平放在孩子背上，轻轻画圈抚摸，让孩子习惯你的手。

    How much力度与时间 10–20 circles10–20 圈

  3. 3
    GV14 Dàzhuī大椎Spine pinching捏脊法

    Along both sides of the spine, lift a small roll of skin between your thumbs and your index and middle fingers and walk it forward hand over hand, from above the tailbone to GV14 Dazhui, just below the most prominent bone at the base of the neck when the head is bent.两手沿脊柱两旁，用拇指和食指、中指把皮肤轻轻捏起一小卷，两手交替向前捻动，从尾骨上方一直捏到颈后的大椎（低头时颈后最突出的骨头下面）。

    How much力度与时间 3–5 passes; only 3 the first time3–5 遍；第一次只做 3 遍

    ⚠️ Lift only skin; never press the spine. On the last pass you may give a gentle upward lift every third pinch ('three pinches, one lift'); a faint pop is the skin, not a bone. Lifting is optional.只捏皮肤，不按脊骨；最后一遍可以每捏三下轻轻往上提一下（「捏三提一」），听到皮肤轻微的「啪」声是皮肤离开皮下的声音，不是骨头。不用每次都提。

  4. 4
    Pushing推法

    Finish by stroking down the back with your palm, from the neck to the waist.用手掌从颈后往下顺着背轻轻抚到腰，结束。

    How much力度与时间 3–5 strokes3–5 次

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 A 2025 meta-analysis pooled 10 randomised trials of pediatric tuina for functional dyspepsia (1,336 children): symptom scores were better with tuina, mostly given alongside usual care, than with usual care alone, and no adverse events were reported. But the trials were methodologically weak and used different protocols, so they say nothing about spine pinching on its own.一篇 2025 年的荟萃分析合并了 10 项小儿推拿治功能性消化不良的随机试验（1336 名儿童）：推拿组（多数和常规治疗一起用）症状评分好于只用常规治疗，没有报告不良事件；但试验多在中国做、方法学弱，推拿方案各不相同，不能单独说明捏脊的作用。 [1]
  • E2 A pilot randomised trial in Hong Kong taught parents to give pediatric tuina at home to preschool children: parents mostly kept it up and there were no serious adverse events. Differences between groups on the main scale were minimal; parents said appetite and sleep were better in interviews, which is their impression, not a measurement.一项香港的先导随机试验教家长在家给学龄前孩子做小儿推拿：家长基本能坚持、没有严重不良事件；主要量表的组间差别很小，家长访谈里说孩子胃口和睡眠好了，这属于家长感受，不是测量结果。 [2]
  • E5 We found no high-quality trial that tests spine pinching on its own.单独检验捏脊的高质量试验，我们没有找到。

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 In the Eastern Jin, Ge Hong's Handbook of Prescriptions for Emergencies, under sudden abdominal pain, describes picking up the skin over the spine from the tailbone to the top; later writers on spine pinching quote it, though it was about an adult emergency. Pediatric massage books from the Ming and Qing onward used spine pinching for children who ate poorly and looked thin and sallow, so folk usage also calls it 'pinching out the food stagnation'. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.东晋葛洪《肘后备急方》治突然腹痛的一条写「拈取其脊骨皮，深取痛引之，从龟尾至顶乃止」，后世讲捏脊常引这句，原文说的是成人急症。明清以后的小儿推拿书把捏脊用在孩子吃饭不好、面黄消瘦（旧称「疳积」）上，民间又叫「捏积」。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
