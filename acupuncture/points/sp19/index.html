SP19

胸乡 Xiōngxiāng

Chest Village · High risk高危

⚠️ Pneumothorax risk point气胸风险穴 SP19 lies in the 3rd intercostal space 6 cun lateral where the chest wall is thin; pleura and lung are just beyond the intercostal muscles. Insert only obliquely or transversely outward 0.5–0.8 cun—never perpendicular, deep medially or with wide lifting-thrusting. Chest pain, breathlessness, worsening cough or stabbing pain on breathing after needling needs urgent assessment for pneumothorax.胸乡在第 3 肋间隙旁开 6 寸，胸壁薄，肋间肌后即胸膜与肺。只斜刺或向外平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不直刺、不向内深刺、不大幅提插。针后出现胸痛、气短、咳嗽加重或呼吸时刺痛，立即就医排查气胸。

📍 Location定位★

On the lateral chest, in the 3rd intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 3 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

💡 The sternal angle marks the 2nd rib; the space below is the 2nd (SP20) and the next is the 3rd intercostal space—take it on the 6-cun line.从胸骨角平第 2 肋，下一肋间是第 2 肋间隙（周荣），再下一肋间即第 3 肋间隙，在旁开 6 寸线上取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse outward斜刺或向外平刺 · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never needle deeply.不可深刺。

🛡 Safety安全

Pleura and lung lie just deep to the intercostal muscles and the chest wall is thin here: insert only obliquely or transversely outward, never perpendicularly or deep medially—risk of pneumothorax; avoid the intercostal vessels and nerve.肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺，此处胸壁薄：只斜刺或向外平刺，不直刺、不向内深刺，有气胸风险；避开肋间血管神经。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Distending pain of the chest and hypochondrium胸胁胀痛Chest pain radiating to the back preventing lying down胸痛引背不得卧

🔗 Pairings配伍

  • With CV17 and PC6: distending chest and hypochondriac pain配膻中（CV17）、内关（PC6）：胸胁胀痛
  • With SP21 and GB34: chest and flank pain reaching the back配大包（SP21）、阳陵泉（GB34）：胸胁痛连背

★ Also examined另考

💡 The Jiayi Jing assigns 'propping fullness of the chest and flank, pain drawing to the back, unable to turn in bed' to SP19; the great luo SP21 and GB34 complete it.《甲乙经》胸乡主「胸胁支满，却引背痛，卧不得转侧」；配脾之大络大包与胆经阳陵泉，疏通胸胁。

Spleen-channel chest point with no special category; used mainly for local chest, flank and breast problems.足太阴脾经胸部腧穴，非特定穴；以局部近治胸胁、乳房为主。Chest-wall safety: oblique or transverse outward along the space 0.5–0.8 cun; no perpendicular, deep or wide lifting-thrusting insertion.胸壁安全刺法：斜刺或沿肋间向外平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，禁直刺、深刺及大幅提插。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「胸鄉，在周榮下一寸六分陷者中，足太陰脈氣所發，仰而取之，刺入四分，灸五壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing (3rd century): SP19 lies one inch six fen below SP20 in the depression; it is the point where the Foot-Taiyin vessel qi is emitted. Take it in supine position, insert four fen, moxa five zhuang.《针灸甲乙经》记载胸乡穴位于周荣下一寸六分，在陷者中，是足太阴脉气所发，仰卧位取穴，刺入四分，灸五壮。

「胸脅 滿，卻引背痛，臥不得轉側，胸鄉主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing: SP19 treats chest and hypochondriac fullness, radiating back pain, and inability to turn side to side while lying down.《针灸甲乙经》载胸乡穴主治胸胁满闷、引胁背痛、卧不能转侧。

「胸鄉 周榮下一寸六分，去胸中行各六寸，仰而取之」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Dacheng (Ming dynasty): SP19 lies one inch six fen below SP20, six cun lateral to the anterior midline; take it in supine position.《针灸大成》记载胸乡穴位于周荣下一寸六分，去胸中行各六寸，仰卧取之。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Intercostal neuralgia and chest-wall myofascial pain肋间神经痛与胸壁肌筋膜痛

Stabbing or burning pain along the 3rd intercostal space, worse with coughing or turning, with local tenderness; exclude cardiopulmonary emergencies first—early shingles can also cause one-sided chest pain.沿第 3 肋间的刺痛、灼痛，咳嗽、转体加重，局部压痛；须先排除心肺急症，带状疱疹前驱期也可表现为单侧胸痛。

Pectoralis major or serratus anterior strain胸大肌、前锯肌劳损

Lateral chest pain after pressing, throwing or violent coughing, worse on resisted adduction or reaching forward; severe pain or restricted breathing after trauma needs a rib-fracture check.推举、投掷或剧烈咳嗽后侧胸痛，抗阻内收或前伸手臂时加重；外伤后剧痛或呼吸受限先排除肋骨骨折。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Chest pain, breathlessness, pain on inhaling or persistent dry cough after needling: suspect pneumothorax, seek care now (call 911 in BC)针后胸痛、气短、吸气痛或持续干咳：警惕气胸，立即就医（BC 拨 911）
  • Chest pain spreading to the left arm, jaw or back with sweating or breathlessness: treat as a cardiac emergency, call 911胸痛向左臂、下颌或背部放射伴出汗、气促：按心脏急症处理，拨 911

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is SP19 Xiōngxiāng?胸乡（SP19）在哪里？

On the lateral chest, in the 3rd intercostal space, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 3 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 6 寸。

How is SP19 needled?胸乡（SP19）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse outward; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺或向外平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at SP19?胸乡（SP19）最要注意的安全问题？

Pleura and lung lie just deep to the intercostal muscles and the chest wall is thin here: insert only obliquely or transversely outward, never perpendicularly or deep medially—risk of pneumothorax; avoid the intercostal vessels and nerve.肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺，此处胸壁薄：只斜刺或向外平刺，不直刺、不向内深刺，有气胸风险；避开肋间血管神经。
