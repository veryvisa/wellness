📍 Location定位★

In the posterior knee region, on the popliteal crease, lateral to the tendon of semitendinosus.膝后区，腘横纹上，半腱肌肌腱外侧缘。

💡 With the knee slightly flexed, feel two tendons on the inner side of the popliteal fossa; the thinner, more prominent one is semitendinosus. 🪡 Needling刺法★

Where is KI10 Yīngǔ?阴谷（KI10）在哪里？

In the posterior knee region, on the popliteal crease, lateral to the tendon of semitendinosus.膝后区，腘横纹上，半腱肌肌腱外侧缘。

How is KI10 needled?阴谷（KI10）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of KI10?阴谷（KI10）是哪类特定穴？

He-sea (water)合穴（水）

Main safety point at KI10?阴谷（KI10）最要注意的安全问题？

The popliteal artery, vein and tibial nerve lie deep in the centre of the fossa: insert beside the medial tendons, never angling laterally toward the centre, and withdraw for electric tingling.腘动、静脉和胫神经在腘窝中央深处：只在内侧肌腱旁进针，针尖不向外侧的腘窝中央偏，有电麻感即退针。
