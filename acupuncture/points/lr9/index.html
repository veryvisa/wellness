    Anatomy解剖结构

    The point lies between sartorius and gracilis over adductor magnus, with the medial femoral cutaneous nerve, obturator cutaneous branches and great saphenous vein under the skin. ⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Knee pain膝痛 ⚖️

Consider LR9 when medial knee pain is accompanied by proximal medial thigh pulling and tenderness in the muscular interval approximately 4 cun above the medial femoral epicondyle. If tenderness is concentrated at the medial joint line, medial collateral ligament, or peripatellar region, first assess local structural injury and compare LR9 with LR8 and SP9.膝痛以大腿内侧向上牵扯、压痛位于股骨内上髁上方约 4 寸的肌间沟时，可考虑阴包；若压痛集中在膝内侧关节间隙、内侧副韧带或髌周，应优先鉴别局部结构损伤，并与曲泉 LR8、阴陵泉 SP9 比较。

Irregular periods月经不调

LR9 may be considered as an adjunct when irregular menstruation is differentiated as involving the Foot Jueyin Liver channel and is accompanied by medial thigh symptoms. If Spleen-Kidney deficiency or dysfunction of the Chong and Ren vessels predominates, select points such as SP6 and CV4 according to the pattern rather than relying on LR9 alone because of its traditional gynecological indications.月经不调经辨证属于足厥阴肝经相关证候，兼有大腿内侧症状时，可将阴包作为配穴；若以脾肾亏虚、冲任失调为主，应结合三阴交 SP6、关元 CV4 等辨证选穴，不能因阴包有传统妇科主治便单独使用。

Period pain痛经

LR9 may be considered for dysmenorrhea accompanied by medial thigh pulling pain when Liver-channel qi and blood stagnation is identified. If pain is predominantly lower abdominal and progressively worsening, or is accompanied by dyspareunia or abnormal bleeding, investigate gynecological conditions such as endometriosis or adenomyosis first. Compare its pattern-based use with SP6 and SP8.痛经伴大腿内侧牵扯痛、辨证涉及肝经气血运行不畅时，可考虑阴包配合其他调经穴；若疼痛主要集中在下腹部并伴进行性加重、性交痛或异常出血，应优先排查子宫内膜异位症、腺肌病等妇科疾病，并与三阴交 SP6、地机 SP8 比较其辨证配伍。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

LR9 may be considered as an adjunct when enuresis or urinary symptoms are traditionally differentiated as involving the Liver channel, but it is not a first-line intervention for urinary retention, urinary tract infection, or neurogenic bladder. Dysuria, fever, hematuria, or sudden inability to urinate requires medical assessment first. Compare with CV3 and SP6 according to the underlying cause and pattern.遗尿或排尿异常在传统辨证属于肝经相关证候时，阴包可作为配穴参考，但不作为尿潴留、尿路感染或神经源性膀胱的首选处理。若伴尿痛、发热、血尿或突然不能排尿，应优先就医，并与中极 CV3、三阴交 SP6 等按病因和证候鉴别。

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

When lumbar pain is accompanied by lower abdominal pain, LR9 may be considered as a distal adjunct based on its traditional indication in the Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing. If back pain is accompanied by radicular symptoms, leg weakness, or bowel or bladder dysfunction, assess for lumbar spine disease and cauda equina involvement first. Compare with BL23 and BL40 according to the pain source and channel-pattern differentiation.腰痛兼少腹痛属于《针灸甲乙经》记载的阴包传统主治，可在辨证后作为远端配穴；若腰痛伴神经根放射痛、下肢无力或大小便功能异常，应优先排查腰椎及马尾神经病变，并与肾俞 BL23、委中 BL40 等按疼痛来源及经脉辨证比较。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Medial thigh muscle strain and adductor tendinopathy大腿内侧肌肉拉伤与内收肌肌腱病

Medial thigh pain may arise from injury to the adductor longus, adductor brevis, or adductor magnus, or from soft-tissue structures near sartorius. Adductor injuries typically worsen with sports, striding, changes of direction, or resisted hip adduction. Tenderness often occurs near the pubis or along the medial thigh muscle and tendon attachments. LR9 lies approximately 4 cun above the knee, and local tenderness alone does not establish a Liver-channel disorder. Differentiate using the injury history, resisted testing, and imaging when indicated.大腿内侧疼痛可能来自长收肌、短收肌或大收肌损伤，也可能涉及缝匠肌附近软组织。内收肌损伤通常在运动、跨步、变向或抗阻髋内收时加重，压痛常位于耻骨附近或大腿内侧肌肉、肌腱附着区；阴包位于膝上约 4 寸，局部压痛不能单独证明是肝经病变。应通过运动损伤史、抗阻检查及必要的影像学评估鉴别。

Saphenous nerve entrapment and adductor canal syndrome隐神经卡压及内收肌管综合征

The saphenous nerve travels with the femoral vessels in the adductor canal and subsequently supplies sensation to the medial knee and medial lower leg. Entrapment may cause burning pain, numbness, or tingling around the medial knee or medial lower leg, sometimes with radiating symptoms on percussion. Compared with ordinary muscular tenderness, neuropathic symptoms follow the saphenous sensory distribution and generally do not primarily present as motor weakness. Differentiate from lumbar radiculopathy, knee disorders, and local soft-tissue injuries.隐神经在内收肌管内与股血管伴行，随后向远端分布至膝内侧及小腿内侧。卡压可表现为膝内侧或小腿内侧烧灼痛、麻木、针刺感，部分患者有局部叩击放射感。与普通肌肉压痛相比，神经症状常呈沿隐神经分布的感觉异常，通常不以肌力下降为主要表现。需要与腰椎神经根病、膝关节病变及局部软组织损伤鉴别。

Medial collateral ligament injury膝内侧副韧带损伤

After a lateral impact, twisting injury, or sudden change of direction, medial knee pain and swelling, pain with valgus stress, or joint laxity may indicate a medial collateral ligament injury. Tenderness usually follows the ligament along the medial knee, whereas LR9 lies approximately 4 cun proximal to the medial femoral epicondyle. These are different anatomical locations. Assess knee stability and obtain imaging when indicated.膝关节受到外侧撞击、扭转或运动中突然变向后，膝内侧疼痛、肿胀，抗外翻应力时疼痛或出现关节松弛，应考虑内侧副韧带损伤。疼痛通常集中在膝关节内侧副韧带走行区，而阴包在股骨内上髁上方约 4 寸，二者不是同一个解剖位置。应进行膝关节稳定性检查，必要时接受影像学评估。

Pain around the medial femoral epicondyle involving bursae or tendon attachments股骨内上髁周围滑囊或肌腱附着区疼痛

Localized tenderness, swelling, or movement-related pain near the medial femoral epicondyle may involve tendon attachments, bursae, or adjacent soft tissues. LR9 is approximately 4 cun proximal to this bony prominence, so tenderness directly over the epicondyle should not automatically be interpreted as an LR9 reaction. Differentiate by the precise location of tenderness and symptoms during knee movement and weight-bearing.膝内侧骨性隆起附近的局限性压痛、肿胀或活动诱发痛，可能与肌腱附着区、滑囊及邻近软组织有关。阴包在骨性隆起近端约 4 寸，不能将骨性隆起本身的压痛直接认定为阴包反应。应结合压痛位置、膝关节活动及负重诱发情况鉴别。

Vascular injury risk involving the femoral vessels and adductor canal股动脉、股静脉及内收肌管邻近区域的血管损伤风险

The deep anatomy of LR9 requires particular attention during needling. The femoral artery and vein lie within the adductor canal, and excessive depth or posterior deviation may cause vascular injury and hematoma. A pulsatile mass, rapidly enlarging swelling, persistent bleeding, or progressively worsening pain requires immediate medical assessment. These findings must not be interpreted as normal needling sensation or deqi.阴包针刺时必须认识其深层解剖风险。股动脉、股静脉位于内收肌管内，过深或向后偏斜可能造成血管损伤及血肿。出现搏动性肿块、局部迅速肿胀、持续出血或进行性疼痛，应考虑血管损伤并立即就医。此类表现不能当作正常针感或得气反应。

Where is LR9 Yīnbāo?阴包（LR9）在哪里？

On the medial thigh, 4 cun above the base of the patella, between gracilis and sartorius.股前区，髌底上 4 寸，股薄肌与缝匠肌之间。

How is LR9 needled?阴包（LR9）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.8–1 cun.直刺；0.8–1 寸。

Main safety point at LR9?阴包（LR9）最要注意的安全问题？

The femoral vessels lie deep to sartorius, anterolateral to the needle path: do not angle deep anterolaterally, and avoid the great saphenous vein.股动静脉在缝匠肌深面、针路前外侧：针不斜向前外深刺；避开大隐静脉。
