📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the arm abducted about 90 degrees or the elbow flexed and arm raised to expose the lateral acromial hollows; avoid shrugging.坐位或仰卧，上臂外展约 90°或屈肘抬臂，使肩峰外侧凹陷显露；避免耸肩。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Between the acromial angle and greater tubercle. Raising the arm reveals hollows anterior and posterior to the acromion; TE14 is the posterior hollow. With the arm down it lies posterior to LI15.肩峰角与肱骨大结节之间，抬臂时肩峰前后各见凹陷，肩髎在后方凹陷；垂臂时约在肩髃（LI15）后方。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Slowly abduct the arm and palpate the deltoid hollow between the lateral acromion and greater tubercle. Deltoid overlies the joint capsule, axillary nerve, and posterior circumflex humeral vessels; keep localization and any insertion within standard limits, never probe deeply posteromedially or pursue radiating sensation.缓慢外展上臂，触诊肩峰外侧缘及肱骨大结节之间的三角肌凹陷。针下为三角肌，深部近肩关节囊、腋神经和旋肱后血管；只按标准方向浅层取穴，勿向后内深探或追逐放射感。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the arm raised, palpate the two hollows below the acromion. Gently identify the posterior one between the greater tubercle and acromial angle, not on the acromial prominence itself.抬臂可在肩峰下触到前后两个凹陷；轻触后侧凹陷并确认其位于肱骨大结节与肩峰角之间，不以肩峰本身的骨突作穴。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    With the arm down, find LI15 and move about 1 cun posteriorly to the hollow below the acromion; raise the arm to confirm it is the posterior of the two hollows.垂臂先找肩髃（LI15），再向后约 1 寸寻找肩峰下后方凹陷；抬臂后复核为两个凹陷中的后一个。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Choosing the anterior hollow shifts to LI15; the indications overlap but the hollows differ. Blind deep posteromedial needling may reach the capsule, axillary nerve, or posterior circumflex humeral vessels; follow standard direction and depth and avoid radiating paresthesia.取前方凹陷会偏到肩髃（LI15），两穴主治相近但所在凹陷不同；盲目向内后深刺可能接近关节囊、腋神经和旋肱后血管，必须按标准方向与深度并避开放射麻痛。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

Choose TE14 for pain in the posterior subacromial hollow confirmed during abduction; use LI15 for the anterior hollow, and assess cuff weakness or trauma first.肩峰下后方凹陷痛、外展时可触及后侧凹陷者选肩髎；前方凹陷痛选肩髃（LI15），肩袖无力或创伤先评估。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

May be a local adjunct when neck pain comes with posterior acromial-hollow tenderness and pain on arm elevation; isolated cervical pain or radicular signs are not primarily a TE14 indication.颈项痛兼肩峰后凹陷、抬臂牵扯痛时可作局部肩部配穴；单纯颈椎痛或神经根症状不以肩髎为主。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Rotator-cuff-related subacromial pain肩袖相关肩峰下疼痛

Pain with abduction or overhead movement and subacromial tenderness are common; marked weakness, night pain, or inability to raise the arm after injury needs assessment for cuff tear.外展或过头动作痛、肩峰下压痛可见；明显无力、夜间痛或外伤后不能抬臂需评估肩袖撕裂。

Deltoid strain or shoulder-capsule disorder三角肌劳损或肩关节囊病变

Tenderness in the hollows should be distinguished from subacromial bursal, greater-tubercle, and capsular disorders; restricted range warrants clinical examination.前后凹陷压痛应与肩峰下滑囊、肱骨大结节和关节囊病变鉴别，活动范围受限需专业检查。
