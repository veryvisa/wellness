🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「合陽，在膝約文中央下二寸，刺入六分，灸五壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Jiayi Jing places Heyang 2 cun below the central knee crease and records needling and moxibustion; the location agrees with the commonly used modern location. The historical needling depth is not an instruction for individual modern practice.《针灸甲乙经》记合阳在膝约纹中央下二寸，并记有针灸方法；古籍定位方向与现代常用定位一致。针刺深度是历史记载，不作为现代个人操作指导。

「跟厥膝急，腰脊痛引腹，篡陰股熱，陰暴痛，寒熱膝酸重，合陽主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Jiayi Jing lists heel or knee tightness, back pain referring to the abdomen, and cold, heat or heaviness in the legs among Heyang's traditional indications; modern causes still require assessment.《针灸甲乙经》记载跟部、膝部拘急、腰脊痛牵引腹部及下肢寒热酸重等可取合阳；这些古代描述需结合现代检查辨别病因。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Distal hamstring or biceps femoris tendon strain腘绳肌远端或股二头肌腱劳损

Lateral popliteal pain after exercise, worsened by resisted knee flexion, may reflect tendon strain; a sudden pop, extensive bruising or inability to flex needs assessment for tear.运动后腘窝外侧疼痛、抗阻屈膝加重可提示肌腱劳损；突然弹响、大片瘀青或不能屈膝应评估撕裂。

Popliteal cyst or vascular disorder腘窝囊肿或血管性病变

A popliteal lump or tightness may be a cyst; sudden unilateral calf swelling, warmth and pain requires evaluation for deep-vein thrombosis, not point pressure or massage.腘窝肿块、膝后胀紧可能为囊肿；单侧小腿突然肿热痛需排除深静脉血栓，不能按穴或按摩后观察。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Sudden one-sided calf swelling, warmth and tenderness, especially with chest pain or breathlessness, requires emergency assessment for thrombosis or pulmonary embolism.单侧小腿突然肿胀、发热、压痛，或伴胸痛气短，立即急诊排查血栓和肺栓塞。
  • A pale, cold foot, weakened pulse or new calf weakness requires urgent medical care.足部苍白冰冷、脉搏减弱或新发小腿无力，立即就医。

Where is BL55 Héyáng?合阳（BL55）在哪里？

Posterior leg, 2 cun below the popliteal crease, between the medial and lateral heads of gastrocnemius.小腿后区，腘横纹下 2 寸，腓肠肌内、外侧头之间。

How is BL55 needled?合阳（BL55）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–2 cun.直刺；1–2 寸。

Main safety point at BL55?合阳（BL55）最要注意的安全问题？

The tibial nerve and popliteal vessels lie deep; control depth and stop for electric radiation or bleeding.深部为胫神经与腘动静脉；控制深度，出现放射电击感或明显出血即停止。
