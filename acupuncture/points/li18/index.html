  • Taking the front border of sternocleidomastoid: just inside it is the carotid pulse (ST9); hitting it can bleed or stimulate the carotid sinus, slowing the heart and causing fainting.取在胸锁乳突肌前缘：前缘内侧就是颈动脉搏动处（人迎），刺中可出血、刺激颈动脉窦致心率骤降、晕厥。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Thyroid nodules and anterior neck lumps甲状腺结节、颈前肿块

The lateral lobe of the thyroid lies in front and medial to LI18; a lump that moves with swallowing is usually thyroid and needs ultrasound — never needle over it.扶突内前方是甲状腺侧叶；吞咽时随喉上下移动的肿块多来自甲状腺，需要超声，不在肿块上针刺。

Carotid sinus hypersensitivity and fainting颈动脉窦过敏、晕厥

Dizziness or fainting in an older person after turning the head, a tight collar or pressure on the neck may be carotid sinus hypersensitivity; in such people neck points are touched only lightly.老人转头、系紧衣领或按压颈部后头晕、晕倒，可能是颈动脉窦过敏；这类人颈部穴位只轻触不重按。

How is LI18 needled?扶突（LI18）怎么刺？
