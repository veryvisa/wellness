Qigong养生功法

Standing post (zhan zhuang)站桩（浑元桩）

Standing post looks like doing nothing: knees slightly bent, arms round as if hugging a tree, standing still. Two minutes is enough to find out that it is not easy: the thighs ache, the shoulders creep up, and you start counting seconds. Those unnoticed tensions are what it works on. People with bad knees or unsteady blood pressure need not avoid it, but should stand high and short: start with two minutes, with a chair behind you.站桩看上去什么也没做：两膝微屈，两臂像抱着一棵树，站着不动。试两分钟就知道它并不轻松：大腿发酸、肩膀偷偷往上耸、心里开始数秒。它练的正是这些不知不觉的紧。膝盖不好、血压不稳的人不用躲着它，但要站得高、站得短，从两分钟开始，身后放一把椅子。

Zhànzhuāng (húnyuánzhuāng) · ⏱ start with 2 minutes, once or twice a day一次 2 分钟起，一天 1–2 次

Qigong养生功法 › Standing post (zhan zhuang)站桩（浑元桩）

🚩 Before you start开始之前

  • Knee pain, knee osteoarthritis, or a hip or knee replacement: read 'Looking after your knees' first and start from 2 minutes; see a doctor first if the knee is swollen, hot, locks or gives way膝盖痛、膝骨关节炎、换过髋或膝关节：先看「膝盖怎么护」，从 2 分钟起；膝盖肿、热、卡住或打软，先看医生
  • If standing makes you dizzy, you have fainted recently, or you have heart disease or uncontrolled blood pressure: ask your doctor first, and begin seated站着会晕、最近晕倒过、心脏病、血压没控制好：先问医生，先坐着练
  • If you have fallen in the past year or your balance is poor: keep a wall behind you and someone nearby近一年跌倒过、平衡不好：背靠墙站，身边有人
  • Pregnancy, recent surgery or a fracture, a blood clot in the leg, or a calf that suddenly swells and hurts on one side: do not practise; see a doctor first怀孕、近期手术或骨折、腿上有血栓或突然一侧小腿肿痛：不练，先看医生
  • Call 911 for chest pain, marked breathlessness, fainting, trouble speaking or weakness on one side站的时候出现胸痛、明显气短、晕倒、说不出话或一侧手脚无力，拨 911

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Knee pain: high stance, from 2 minutes, knees bent no more than about 30 degrees; stop as soon as it hurts膝盖痛：站高架，从 2 分钟起，膝弯不超过约 30 度；一痛就停
  • Dizziness, a tendency to faint, low blood pressure, or a change in blood pressure medication: standing still can itself make you faint; practise seated or hold a chair back, and sit down with your head low at once if your vision dims头晕、容易晕倒、血压低或正在调降压药：站着不动本身就可能让人晕倒；改坐着练，或扶着椅背，觉得眼前发黑马上坐下、把头放低
  • High blood pressure: no breath-holding or gritting the teeth, hands low; if it is not under control, ask your doctor first高血压：不憋气、不咬牙，手放低；血压没控制好先问医生
  • If you fall easily or your balance is poor: keep a wall behind you or hold a chair back, with someone beside you容易跌倒、平衡不好：背靠墙或扶着椅背，旁边有人
  • Pregnancy, marked varicose veins or swollen legs: no long standing; practise seated or stand 1–2 minutes, and check with your doctor first怀孕、静脉曲张明显、腿脚水肿：不久站，坐着练或只站 1–2 分钟，先问医生

🦵 Looking after your knees膝盖怎么护

  • With knee osteoarthritis or knee pain: start at 2 minutes, high stance only; add 1 minute only when pain-free, and at most once a week有膝骨关节炎或膝盖痛：从 2 分钟起，只站高架；不痛才加 1 分钟，一周最多加一次
  • Three checks on the bend: you can see your big toe, the knee points to the second toe, and the shin does not lean forward膝弯的限度看三处：能看见大脚趾、膝盖对着第二脚趾、小腿不往前倒
  • If squatting or stairs hurt more afterwards, or the knee swells: stop for a week and see a doctor or physiotherapist first站完蹲不下、上下楼更痛、膝盖肿：停一周，先看医生或物理治疗师
  • If the knee locks, gives way or swells suddenly, or you have a knee replacement your surgeon has not cleared: do not stand; practise seated膝盖卡住、打软、突然肿大，或换过膝关节不到医生放行的时间：不站，坐着练
  • With a higher body weight or weak thighs: stand half a step from a wall, hands low, and halve the time体重较大或大腿无力：背靠墙半步站，手放低，时间减半

🧘 The posture姿势

Getting ready站之前

  1. Level, non-slip floor; a sturdy chair one step behind you, or stand half a step from a wall地面平、不滑，身后一步放一把稳当的椅子，或背靠墙半步站
  2. Flat shoes; a room that is not hot; not within an hour of a meal穿平底鞋；屋里不要太热，饭后一小时内不站
  3. Set a timer so you do not watch the clock定一个计时器，站的时候不看钟
  1. Feet parallel, shoulder-width apart, toes forward, weight spread over the whole foot两脚平行，与肩同宽，脚尖向前，重量铺满整个脚掌
  2. Let the knees unlock as if about to sit on a high stool, bending no more than about 30 degrees; knees point to the second toe, never falling inward两膝微微松开，像站着准备坐高凳，弯到约 30 度以内；膝盖对着第二脚趾，不向里扣
  3. Let the hips drop lightly; neither arch nor slump the lower back; crown lifted as if by a thread, chin slightly in臀部轻轻往下松，腰不挺、不塌；头顶像有一根线轻轻往上提，下巴微收
  4. Raise the arms slowly to the front of the chest as if hugging a tree or a large ball: elbows below the shoulders, fingers slightly open, hands one to two fists apart, palms inward; the hands may be as low as the navel两臂从体侧慢慢抬到胸前，像抱一棵树或一个大球：两肘低于肩，手指微张，两手相距一到两拳，掌心向内；手可以放低到肚脐前
  5. Shoulders down, lips lightly closed or parted, eyes level or slightly lowered; scan from head to foot and let go wherever you find tension肩放下，嘴轻闭或微张，眼睛平视或微垂；从头到脚扫一遍，哪里紧就松哪里
  6. Leave the breath alone: no counting, no holding; when the mind wanders, come back to the soles of the feet呼吸随它自然，不数、不憋；心思跑开了就回到脚底
  7. When the timer sounds, lower the hands slowly, straighten the knees slowly, and walk in place for half a minute before sitting到时间，两手慢慢落下，膝盖慢慢伸直，原地走半分钟再坐

🌬 Breathe naturally; no breath-holding and no forced deep breaths. If the breath tightens, lower the hands, stand higher, or stop.自然呼吸，不憋气、不刻意深呼吸；一旦呼吸发紧，就把手放低、站高，或停下。

🗓 How to practise怎么练

For the first two weeks, once or twice a day for two minutes; add time only when it feels comfortable. These steps are a cautious way in, not a prescription from research: one current trial protocol has university students stand for thirty minutes under a coach's eye, with ten minutes of warm-up and ten of cooling down, which is not a place to start. Standing leads naturally into the commencing form of tai chi.头两周每天一到两次、每次两分钟，站得舒服再往上加。这个分档是稳妥的起步办法，不是研究定下的处方：目前的站桩试验方案里，大学生是在教练看着的情况下练三十分钟，前后各有十分钟热身和放松，不适合一开始就照搬。站桩之后接练太极前八式里的起势，顺得很。

✅ Key points要领

  • Ache is fine, pain is not: tired, slightly trembling thighs are common; pain inside the knee means stop酸可以，痛不行：大腿发酸、微微发抖是常见的；膝盖里面痛就停
  • Standing high matters more than standing long; steady first, longer later站高比站久重要，先稳再加时间
  • You should finish more comfortable than you began, with no knee soreness the next day每次结束都应该比开始时舒服，第二天膝盖不酸痛

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Knees past the toes or falling inward膝盖超过脚尖、向内扣
  • Shoulders hunched, elbows propped up, lower back arched耸肩、架肘、腰往前挺
  • Gritting the teeth and holding the breath咬牙硬撑、憋气
  • Locking the knees straight, which makes dizziness more likely in a long stand膝盖伸直锁死：站久了更容易头晕

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Sit on the front half of a chair with both feet planted and the arms round, for 2 minutes; or stand with your back against a wall and the hands in front of the lower belly.坐在椅子前半边，两脚踏实，两臂抱球，坐桩 2 分钟；或背靠墙站，两手放在小腹前。

Closing收势

  1. Lower the hands to the sides and straighten the knees slowly两手慢慢落到体侧，膝盖慢慢伸直
  2. Step in place for half a minute to get the calves moving原地踏步半分钟，让小腿动起来
  3. Sit, have some water, and note where you were tightest; let go of that place first next time坐下喝口水，想一想哪里最紧，下次先松那里

📜 Where it comes from从哪里来

People often quote a line from the Suwen as the origin of standing practice: the 'true people' of high antiquity 'stood alone guarding the spirit, flesh and muscle as one'. The full sentence is about legendary sages who lived as long as heaven and earth; it does not say how to stand or for how long, so later practitioners borrowed it. The person who took standing out of boxing and made it something anyone could practise was the Republican-era boxer Wang Xiangzhai. In The Pivot of the Way of Boxing he writes that 'the basic practice of this boxing is standing post' and that 'standing post means standing steady, an even standing'; he asks for 'the head straight, the eyes level, the body upright', 'the joints slightly bent', and warns 'never use force in body or mind' and 'the moment breathing turns irregular or the diaphragm tightens, it is wrong'. The book also makes claims about blood and blood pressure; those are his opinions, not trial results. Teachers differ over the height and hand position of the round-arm post; Chinese medical-school qigong textbooks call it the 'three-circle post' (the hands, the arms and the feet each make a circle) and divide it into high, middle and low stances by how far the knees bend. What follows is the high stance.常有人拿《素问·上古天真论》的「独立守神，肌肉若一」当站桩的出处。原文是：「余闻上古有真人者，提挈天地，把握阴阳，呼吸精气，独立守神，肌肉若一，故能寿敝天地，无有终时」——讲的是传说中的「真人」，没有写怎么站、站多久，只能说后人借了这句话。把站桩单独拿出来、人人可练的，是民国拳师王芗斋。他在《拳道中枢》里写「本拳之基础练习，即为站桩」，「站桩即立稳，平均之站立也」；要求「头直目正，身端顶立」，「身体关节似有微曲之意」；还说「切记身心不可用力」，「只要呼吸一失常，或横隔膜一发紧，便是错误」。书里也有桩法对气血、血压如何如何的话，那是他的主张，不是试验结果。「浑元桩」抱球站的样子，各家高低、手位不完全一样；中医院校的气功教材叫「三圆桩」：两手之间圆、两臂圆、两脚圆，按膝盖弯多少分高、中、低三种架子。下面写的是高架。

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E5 Standing post itself has no finished outcome trials we could find: a PubMed search in November 2026 turned up only published trial protocols (students' well-being, students' insomnia, Parkinson's disease), no results yet. Findings on tai chi or qigong cannot simply be carried over.站桩本身还没有像样的疗效试验：2026 年 11 月在 PubMed 检索，查到的随机试验都只是已发表的研究方案（大学生身心状况、大学生失眠、帕金森病），还没看到结果论文；太极和气功的研究不能直接搬到站桩上。 [1]
  • E3 There are small mechanism studies: in 24 male tai chi practitioners, oxygenation signals in several prefrontal areas were higher during standing meditation than during ordinary standing. It does not say what that means for health.有的是机制方面的小研究：24 名男性太极拳练习者站桩时，前额叶几处的血氧信号比普通站着时高。没有说明这对健康意味着什么。 [2]
  • E3 Another exploratory study measured foot pressure while 23 people stood in the posture; experienced practitioners swayed in a different pattern from beginners. The sample is small, and the authors urge caution.另一项 23 人的探索性研究用压力垫测站桩时脚底的用力，熟练者和初学者的晃动模式不一样；样本小，作者自己也说要谨慎解读。 [3]
  • E3 Why stand high: in laboratory measurements of tai chi movements, a low stance with the knees bent about 60 degrees loaded the knee clearly more than a high one of about 30 degrees (14 professionals). The study was of tai chi movements, not standing, but the principle carries over.为什么要站高：太极动作的实验室测量里，膝弯约 60 度的低架，膝部力矩明显大于约 30 度的高架（14 名专业练习者）。测的是太极动作，不是站桩，但道理相通。 [4]
  • E5 Why guard against fainting: the US National Library of Medicine's patient information lists standing in one place for too long among the causes of fainting.为什么要防晕：美国国家医学图书馆的患者资料把「在一个地方站太久」列为晕倒的原因之一。 [5]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Tradition speaks of 'seeking movement in stillness': outside nothing moves, inside qi and blood go their own way; of 'holding the one' to gather a scattered mind; of feet like roots and a crown drawn up by a thread. Wang Xiangzhai wrote of 'sweeping away ten thousand thoughts and facing the open sky in silence'. These are traditional images for practice, not research findings.传统说站桩是「静中求动」，外面不动，里面气血自己走；「抱元守一」，把散的心收回来；两脚像树根，头顶像被提着。王芗斋说站桩要「扫除万虑，默对长空」。这些是传统的说法和练习时的意象，不是研究结论。
