A band around the head: self-massage for tension headaches头像被箍住：紧张型头痛的自我按摩

The most common headache feels like a band pulled tight across the forehead and sides, often late in the day when the neck and shoulders are tight too. This routine is for that kind, the tension-type headache. Read the red flags first: some headaches cannot wait a minute.最常见的头痛像一条带子勒在额头和两侧，往往在一天快结束、肩颈也很紧的时候出现。这套按摩针对的就是这种紧张型头痛。先看下面的红旗：有些头痛一分钟都不能等。

⏱ About 6 minutes约 6 分钟 · Early, while the headache is still mild; done sitting.头痛刚开始、还不重的时候；坐着做。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • The worst headache of your life, peaking within seconds to a minute: call 911一生中最剧烈的头痛，几秒到一分钟内就到顶峰：拨 911
  • Headache with slurred speech, weakness on one side, blurred or double vision, or confusion: call 911头痛伴说话不清、一侧手脚无力、视物模糊或重影、意识改变：拨 911
  • Headache with fever, a stiff neck or a rash头痛伴发热、脖子发硬或皮疹
  • Headache after a head injury头部受伤之后出现的头痛
  • A new headache after age 50, or a clear change in your usual pattern50 岁以后新出现的头痛，或一向的头痛方式明显变了
  • Worse with coughing or straining, or morning headaches with vomiting咳嗽、用力时加重，或早上起来头痛伴呕吐
  • A new severe headache in pregnancy or after giving birth孕期或产后新出现的严重头痛
  • Painkillers on more than 10 to 15 days a month: the medicine itself may be causing headaches; ask your doctor一个月里有 10–15 天以上在吃止痛药：可能是止痛药本身引起的头痛，去问医生

✋ When not to press这些情况先别按

  • In pregnancy, skip LI4 Hegu: a traditional pregnancy caution孕期不按合谷（LI4），这是传统的孕期禁忌
  • Do not press any area of a head injury头部有外伤的地方不按
  • During a migraine, if touch makes it worse, stop and lie down somewhere dark and quiet偏头痛发作时如果一碰就更痛，停下来，到暗、静的地方躺着

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1
    EX-HN5 Tàiyáng太阳Kneading揉法

    Place the middle-finger pads on both Taiyang points and knead slowly with light to moderate pressure.两手中指指腹放在两侧太阳穴，轻到中度地慢慢揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

  2. 2
    GV29 Yìntáng印堂Pushing推法

    Starting at GV29 Yintang between the eyebrows, push upward to the front hairline with the thumb pads, one after the other.两拇指指腹从两眉中间的印堂开始，交替向上推到前发际。

    How much力度与时间 20–30 strokes20–30 次

  3. 3
    Temple sweeping扫散法

    Curve your fingers and 'comb' with the pads from the corners of the forehead along the sides of the head toward the back of the ears.十指微屈，用指腹从额角沿头的两侧往耳后方向「梳」过去。

    How much力度与时间 20 passes20 遍

  4. 4
    GB20 Fēngchí风池Kneading揉法

    Put both thumbs in the hollows under the base of the skull (GB20 Fengchi) and knead upward.两拇指放在后脑勺下缘两侧凹陷的风池，向上按揉。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute约 1 分钟

    ⚠️ Light to moderate pressure; stop if you feel dizzy.轻到中等力度；头晕就停。

  5. 5
    LI4 Hégǔ合谷Pressing按法

    Press LI4 Hegu, in the web between thumb and index finger beside the middle of the second metacarpal, with the other thumb; hold a few seconds, release, then switch hands.一手拇指按在另一手虎口、第二掌骨中点旁边的合谷，按压停几秒再松，然后换手。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side; skip in pregnancy每侧约 1 分钟；孕期跳过

  6. 6
    LR3 Tàichōng太冲Kneading揉法

    Shoes off; put a thumb in the hollow on top of the foot just in front of where the bones of the big and second toes meet (LR3 Taichong); knead, then switch feet.脱鞋，拇指放在脚背大脚趾和第二趾的骨头交汇处前方凹陷（太冲），按揉后换脚。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute each side每侧约 1 分钟

    ⚠️ The artery on top of the foot runs under LR3: if you feel a pulse, shift slightly off it.太冲下面有足背动脉，摸到搏动就稍微偏开一点按。

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 A systematic review found 6 randomised trials in chronic tension-type headache (1 of massage, 5 of physiotherapy) with favourable results, but the controls were mostly usual care or nothing and most trials did not rule out other treatments, leaving real room for bias.一篇系统综述找到 6 项慢性紧张型头痛的随机试验（1 项按摩、5 项理疗），结果偏正面，但对照多是常规处理或不处理、多数试验没有避免合并治疗，偏倚风险不小。 [1]
  • E1 The UK NICE guideline warns that taking painkillers on too many days a month can itself cause headaches (medication-overuse headache); how to deal with it should be worked out with a doctor.英国 NICE 指南提醒：每月吃止痛药太多天，止痛药本身就会引起头痛（药物过度使用性头痛），处理方法要和医生一起定。 [2]
  • E5 Kneading the head and face carries very little risk; the real danger is not the massage but treating a headache that needs emergency care as an ordinary one.按揉头面部的风险很低；最需要防的不是按摩本身，而是把需要急诊的头痛当成普通头痛来按。

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 The Four Command Points rhyme says 'face and mouth, gather at LI4'; Taiyang, GB20, Yintang and LR3 are the points tradition uses most for headache, and LR3 with LI4 is called the 'Four Gates'. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.四总穴歌有「面口合谷收」；太阳、风池、印堂和太冲是传统上头痛最常用的穴，太冲与合谷合称「四关」。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
