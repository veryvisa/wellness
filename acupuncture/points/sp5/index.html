    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpation check触诊确认

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

For diarrhoea associated with impaired Spleen transformation and dampness, especially with abdominal distension, poor appetite and heaviness, SP5 may be combined with SP9 and ST36. If Spleen deficiency predominates, SP3 is often more directly selected as the source and shu-stream point for Spleen regulation. Acute diarrhoea with high fever, bloody stools, significant dehydration or severe abdominal pain requires medical assessment rather than acupuncture as first-line management.泄泻辨证属脾失健运、湿困中焦，兼有腹胀、食少、肢体困重时，可将商丘作为脾经经穴配阴陵泉（SP9）、足三里（ST36）；若以脾虚为主，太白（SP3）更适合从原穴、输穴角度健脾。急性腹泻伴高热、血便、明显脱水或剧烈腹痛时不以针灸作为首要处理。

Oedema水肿 ⚖️

For limb heaviness and oedema attributed to impaired Spleen transformation and dampness retention, SP5 may be combined with SP9 and SP6 to support Spleen function and transform dampness. SP9 is the more representative he-sea point for dampness-related patterns. Sudden unilateral ankle swelling with redness, warmth and pain, or swelling extending into the calf, requires exclusion of thrombosis, infection and trauma before considering acupuncture.水湿内停、脾失运化所致的肢体困重及水肿，可选商丘配阴陵泉（SP9）、三阴交（SP6）等健脾利湿；阴陵泉是水湿病证更具代表性的合穴。若为单侧足踝突然肿胀、红热疼痛，或伴小腿肿胀，应先排除血栓、感染和外伤，不选商丘直接针刺处理。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

For abdominal pain associated with Spleen–Stomach deficiency and dampness, especially with distension, loose stools and poor appetite, SP5 may be combined with ST36, SP3 and CV12. Acute localized severe pain, abdominal guarding, rebound tenderness, persistent vomiting or bloody stools requires evaluation for an acute surgical abdomen. SP5 is neither an emergency treatment nor a diagnostic test.腹痛辨证属于脾胃虚弱、湿困中焦，伴腹胀、便溏及食欲不振时，可选商丘配足三里（ST36）、太白（SP3）及中脘（CV12）。若为急性局限性剧痛、腹肌紧张、反跳痛、持续呕吐或便血，应先排除外科急腹症，不能以商丘作为急救或诊断手段。

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

For dull epigastric pain with poor appetite and loose stools attributed to Spleen–Stomach deficiency or dampness, SP5 may be used as an auxiliary Spleen-channel point with ST36, CV12 and PC6. Burning epigastric pain, acid regurgitation or prominent hunger-related pain may indicate gastritis, peptic ulcer disease or gastro-oesophageal reflux and should not automatically be classified as Spleen deficiency for SP5 treatment.胃脘隐痛、食少、便溏，辨证属脾胃虚弱或湿困中焦时，商丘可作为调理脾经的辅助穴，常配足三里（ST36）、中脘（CV12）、内关（PC6）。若胃脘灼痛、反酸、明显饥饿痛，需考虑胃炎、消化性溃疡及胃食管反流等现代疾病，不应仅按脾虚取商丘。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

When vomiting accompanies impaired Spleen–Stomach function, abdominal distension, poor appetite and diarrhoea, SP5 may be combined with ST36, PC6 and CV12 to regulate digestion and direct rebellious qi downward. Frequent vomiting, inability to retain fluids, dehydration, haematemesis, bilious vomiting or severe abdominal pain requires medical assessment first. When rebellious Stomach qi is the predominant presentation, PC6 and ST36 are generally more directly selected than SP5 alone.呕吐辨证属脾胃运化失常，伴腹胀、纳差、泄泻时，商丘可配足三里（ST36）、内关（PC6）、中脘（CV12），从调理脾胃和降逆角度使用。若呕吐频繁、不能进水、出现脱水、呕血、胆汁样呕吐或剧烈腹痛，应先就医。以胃气上逆为主要表现时，内关和足三里通常比单用商丘更直接。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「行於商丘，商丘，內踝之下，陷者之中也，為經」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

The Lingshu describes the Spleen channel's five-transport sequence and identifies SP5 as the jing-river point in a hollow below the medial malleolus. The term jing here refers specifically to the jing-river category of the five-transport points, not merely to the channel itself. Later anatomical descriptions clarify the anteroinferior location, preventing confusion with KI6 directly inferior to the medial malleolus.《灵枢》在描述足太阴经五输穴循行时，指出商丘位于内踝下方的凹陷中，并明确其为经穴。这里的「經」指五输穴中的经穴，不是泛指经络。结合后世定位，商丘应在内踝前下方的凹陷中，不能仅凭「内踝之下」误取内踝正下方的照海。

「商丘者，金也」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing identifies SP5 as belonging to metal. The Spleen channel belongs to earth, making its metal jing-river point the child point in the five-phase framework. Traditional five-phase theory therefore classifies SP5 as a point for reducing excess in the Spleen channel. Clinical selection and tonification or reduction must still be based on the actual pattern rather than point classification alone.《针灸甲乙经》明确商丘五行属金。足太阴脾经属土，商丘为经（金）穴，因此在五行子母补泻理论中属于脾经的子穴，传统上用于脾经实证的泻法取穴体系。临床应用必须结合证候，不能单凭穴性决定补泻。

「寒熱善嘔，商丘主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing records SP5 for alternating chills and fever with recurrent vomiting. This reflects the classical understanding of disturbed Spleen and Stomach function and mixed cold-heat presentations. Modern clinicians must not attribute every episode of vomiting to a Spleen-channel disorder; gastrointestinal infection, medication reactions, pregnancy, obstruction and other medical causes must first be considered.《针灸甲乙经》记载商丘主治寒热、容易呕吐，体现古代对脾胃升降失常及寒热错杂表现的认识。现代临床不能将所有呕吐直接归为脾经病，应先辨别胃肠感染、药物反应、妊娠、梗阻及其他需要医学评估的原因。

「小兒咳而泄，不欲食者，商丘主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing records SP5 for children with cough, diarrhoea and poor appetite. This reflects the classical association between Spleen and Stomach dysfunction, digestion and diarrhoeal symptoms. It is a historical indication, not evidence that SP5 alone treats childhood cough or diarrhoea. Persistent diarrhoea, dehydration or breathing difficulty in a child requires paediatric assessment first.《针灸甲乙经》记载小儿咳嗽、泄泻、不欲进食时可取商丘，反映古代将脾胃运化与饮食、泄泻等表现联系起来的治疗思路。此条是历史文献记载，不代表商丘能够单穴治疗儿童咳嗽或腹泻。儿童持续腹泻、脱水或呼吸困难应先接受儿科评估。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Tibialis posterior tendinopathy and tibialis posterior tendon dysfunction胫后肌腱病及胫后肌腱功能障碍

The tibialis posterior tendon passes behind the medial malleolus and inserts into the navicular and other medial-foot structures. Tendinopathy may cause pain around the medial ankle and medial foot, worsening with weight bearing, difficulty performing a single-leg heel raise, and progressive arch collapse or hindfoot valgus in advanced cases. SP5 is near this insertion region but lies anteroinferiorly. Tenderness at SP5 does not establish an acupuncture response. Assess tendon continuity, resisted inversion and single-leg heel-raise ability, and distinguish tendon disease from joint pathology.胫后肌腱经过内踝后方并止于舟骨等足内侧结构，病变可表现为内踝周围及足内侧疼痛、负重时加重、单脚提踵困难，严重时出现足弓逐渐塌陷及后足外翻。商丘位于其止点附近但偏前下方，触诊疼痛不能直接认定为穴位反应。应检查胫后肌腱连续性、抗阻足内翻及单脚提踵能力，并与局部关节病变鉴别。

Medial ankle ligament injury (deltoid ligament injury)内侧踝韧带损伤（三角韧带损伤）

The deltoid ligament lies deep to the medial malleolus and stabilizes the medial ankle. After an ankle sprain, swelling, tenderness around the medial malleolus and pain with weight bearing warrant assessment of ligament integrity and ankle stability. SP5 is adjacent to this anatomical region, but ligament tenderness must not be mistaken for point sensitivity. Significant trauma, inability to bear weight or suspected fracture requires medical assessment and imaging when indicated.三角韧带位于内踝深面，维持踝关节内侧稳定。踝关节扭伤后出现内踝周围肿胀、压痛、负重疼痛，应检查韧带及踝关节稳定性。商丘穴区与三角韧带邻近，但不能把韧带损伤的压痛直接当作穴位敏感。明显外伤、不能负重或怀疑骨折时先做医学评估，必要时影像检查。

Talonavicular joint pathology and perinavicular pain距舟关节病变及舟骨周围疼痛

The talonavicular joint lies between the head of the talus and the navicular, adjacent to the deeper SP5 region. Arthritis, trauma or local osteochondral pathology may cause anteromedial foot pain, restricted movement and weight-bearing discomfort. Pain associated with foot movement, restricted joint range or loading should be distinguished from isolated soft-tissue tenderness. Do not needle deeply toward the joint or treat joint-line tenderness as an automatic indication for acupuncture.距舟关节位于距骨头与舟骨之间，商丘深部邻近该关节。关节炎、创伤或局部骨软骨病变可能引起足内侧前部疼痛、活动受限及负重不适。若疼痛与足部活动、关节活动范围和负重密切相关，应与单纯软组织压痛区分。不要向关节方向深刺，也不可把关节线压痛直接作为针刺适应证。

Varicosities or superficial thrombophlebitis of great saphenous vein tributaries大隐静脉属支曲张或浅静脉炎

Tributaries of the great saphenous vein may be visible around the medial foot and ankle. Varicosities appear as dilated, tortuous superficial veins, whereas superficial thrombophlebitis may cause linear redness, warmth, swelling, tenderness and a palpable cord. These findings are not anatomical criteria for locating SP5. Do not needle directly through visible varicosities, inflamed veins or suspected thrombosed areas.足内侧和内踝周围可见大隐静脉属支。浅静脉曲张可表现为可见扩张、迂曲血管；浅静脉炎则可能出现沿血管走行的红、肿、热、痛及条索状硬结。此类疼痛不是商丘穴本身的定位依据。可见曲张静脉、红肿血管或疑似血栓区域禁止直接针刺。

Irritation or injury of the medial dorsal cutaneous nerve足背内侧皮神经刺激或损伤

The medial dorsal cutaneous nerve is a branch of the superficial fibular nerve and supplies sensation to part of the medial dorsum of the foot. Compression, trauma or surgery may cause numbness, tingling, burning pain or altered sensation over its distribution. Unlike localized musculoskeletal tenderness at SP5, neuropathic symptoms often radiate along a sensory nerve territory. Persistent numbness, sensory loss or progressive pain requires neurological and local examination.足背内侧皮神经为腓浅神经分支，负责足背内侧部分皮肤感觉。局部压迫、外伤或手术后可出现足背内侧麻木、刺痛、灼痛或触觉异常。与局限于商丘凹陷的肌肉骨骼压痛不同，神经性症状常沿感觉神经分布放射。出现持续麻木、感觉缺失或进行性疼痛时，应先进行神经系统及局部检查。

Medial malleolar and medial-foot fractures内踝及足内侧骨折

Following a fall, ankle twist or direct impact, marked swelling, focal bony tenderness, bruising or difficulty bearing weight around the medial malleolus or navicular requires exclusion of a medial malleolar or navicular fracture and other foot injuries. Do not needle or apply forceful pressure simply because the painful area coincides with SP5. Radiographs may be required, and further imaging may be considered if clinical suspicion remains despite negative initial radiographs.跌倒、扭伤或直接撞击后，若内踝或舟骨附近出现明显肿胀、骨性压痛、瘀青、负重困难，应排除内踝骨折、舟骨骨折及其他足部骨损伤。不能因疼痛点恰好位于商丘附近而直接进行针刺或强力按压。必要时进行X线检查，若临床高度怀疑而X线阴性，医生可能进一步安排其他影像检查。

Where is SP5 Shāngqiū?商丘（SP5）在哪里？

On the medial ankle, anteroinferior to the medial malleolus, in the depression midway between the tuberosity of the navicular and the prominence of the medial malleolus.踝区，内踝前下方，舟骨粗隆与内踝尖连线中点凹陷中。

How is SP5 needled?商丘（SP5）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of SP5?商丘（SP5）是哪类特定穴？

Jing-river (metal)经穴（金）

Main safety point at SP5?商丘（SP5）最要注意的安全问题？

Superficial veins are plentiful in front of and below the malleolus: identify them first, and keep the needle out of the talonavicular joint.内踝前下方浅静脉多：先看清静脉；针不刺入距舟关节腔。
