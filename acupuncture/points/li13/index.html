📍 Location定位★

On the lateral upper arm, 3 cun above LI11, on the line from LI11 to LI15.上臂外侧，曲池与肩髃连线上，曲池上 3 寸。

💡 Measure 3 cun superiorly from LI11 along the line toward LI15, below the deltoid insertion. Do not mistake LI14's 7-cun distance from LI11 for the total LI11-to-LI15 distance.从曲池沿通向肩髃的连线向上量 3 寸，在三角肌止点下方取穴。不要把臂臑距曲池 7 寸误当成曲池到肩髃的总长。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular, clear of the artery避开动脉，直刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Where is LI13 Shǒuwǔlǐ?手五里（LI13）在哪里？

How is LI13 needled?手五里（LI13）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, clear of the artery; 0.5–1 cun.避开动脉，直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Main safety point at LI13?手五里（LI13）最要注意的安全问题？
