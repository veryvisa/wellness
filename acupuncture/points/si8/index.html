Needle shallowly (0.3–0.5 cun); withdraw at once at any electric sensation and do not lift and thrust.小海所在的凹陷——尺骨鹰嘴与肱骨内上髁之间——正是尺神经沟（肘管）：尺神经紧贴骨面从这里通过，皮下很浅。按压稍重就会麻到无名指、小指，说明位置对、但压力太大。针刺宜浅（0.3–0.5 寸），出现电击样麻感立即退针，不可提插。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the elbow bent, feel the olecranon and medial epicondyle; the groove between them is SI8. A gentle sideways stroke may roll a thin round cord (the ulnar nerve): touch lightly and do not keep strumming it.屈肘，分别摸清鹰嘴和内上髁，两骨之间的沟即小海；轻轻横拨可触到一条细圆的条索（尺神经），定位只需轻触，不要反复拨压。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Taking tingling in the little finger as a mislocation and probing deeper: SI8 lies over the ulnar groove, so tingling means the nerve is being pressed — ease off and needle shallowly rather than going deeper.把按压时的小指麻感当成取错位置而去找更深处：小海就在尺神经沟上，麻感说明压到了神经，应减轻压力、浅刺，而不是往深处探。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Ulnar-groove irritation or cubital tunnel syndrome尺神经沟刺激或肘管综合征

Numbness in the little and ring fingers after prolonged elbow flexion, or radiating tingling when tapping behind the medial epicondyle, suggests ulnar-nerve irritation; persistent numbness or intrinsic-hand weakness needs assessment.肘屈曲时间长后小指、无名指麻痛，敲击内上髁后放射可提示尺神经受刺激；持续麻木、手内在肌无力需临床评估。

Elbow bursal, tendon, or ligament injury肘关节滑囊、肌腱或韧带损伤

Where is SI8 Xiǎohǎi?小海（SI8）在哪里？

On the posteromedial elbow, in the depression between the olecranon and the medial epicondyle of the humerus.肘后区，尺骨鹰嘴与肱骨内上髁之间凹陷中。

How is SI8 needled?小海（SI8）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SI8?小海（SI8）是哪类特定穴？

He-sea (earth)合穴（土）

Main safety point at SI8?小海（SI8）最要注意的安全问题？

The ulnar nerve lies directly beneath the point: shallow insertion only, no lifting-thrusting, and withdraw at once for an electric-shock sensation.尺神经就在穴位正下方：只可浅刺，不提插；出现电击样麻感立即退针。
