📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone with the calf relaxed; briefly plantar-flex to outline gastrocnemius, then relax before locating.俯卧，小腿后侧放松；可短暂跖屈使腓肠肌轮廓显出，再回到放松位取穴。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    With a gentle heel raise, the lower gastrocnemius heads meet the Achilles tendon in a visible V-shaped junction. Relax the leg and locate the hollow at the lower end of that split for BL57.让患者轻轻踮足，腓肠肌两肌腹下端与跟腱交界处形成明显尖角；放松后取两肌腹分叉下缘的凹陷，即承山。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    BL57 lies in the intermuscular angle formed by the lower medial and lateral gastrocnemius heads, with soleus deep to it. The tibial nerve and posterior tibial vessels lie deeper and medially; the sural nerve or small saphenous vein may be superficial. Locate gently in the cleft and avoid deep medial insertion and visible veins.承山位于腓肠肌内、外侧头下端形成的肌间角，深面为比目鱼肌；胫神经和胫后血管更深、偏内侧，浅表可见腓肠神经或小隐静脉。沿肌间凹陷轻触定位，避免向内深刺及可见血管。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently palpate the apex of the calf: the two gastrocnemius heads divide here and taper into the Achilles tendon. Local tenderness may be present, but does not replace the palpable muscle boundary.以指腹轻按小腿肚尖端，腓肠肌两头在此分开并向下收束为跟腱；可有局部酸胀，但压痛不能替代可见的肌肉边界。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    With the calf relaxed, find the lower ends of the two gastrocnemius heads and follow the midline to their dividing hollow. A heel raise is only an aid; do not needle while the muscle is contracted.小腿放松时先找两条腓肠肌肌腹的下端，再沿中线向下至肌腹分叉处凹陷；用踮足观察只作辅助，不要在绷紧状态下针刺。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Knee pain膝痛 ⚖️

A distal point for calf-belly pain or cramp rather than joint-line pain; pain at the popliteal crease calls for distinguishing BL40, and an acutely swollen warm calf needs thrombosis assessment.小腿后侧肌腹痛、痉挛而非膝关节线痛时可作远端穴；若疼痛集中腘横纹则辨委中，急性肿热小腿先排血栓。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Gastrocnemius strain or cramp腓肠肌劳损或抽筋

Tight calf pain after exercise, dehydration or a sudden load increase is common; a tearing sound, bruising or inability to rise onto the toes suggests muscle or Achilles injury and needs care.运动、脱水或负荷骤增后小腿肌肉紧痛常见；突发撕裂声、瘀青、无法踮足提示肌肉撕裂或跟腱损伤，应就医。

Differential diagnosis of deep-vein thrombosis深静脉血栓鉴别

Sudden unilateral calf swelling, warmth and pain, especially with immobility, recent surgery or prior thrombosis, needs urgent assessment rather than massage or acupressure.单侧小腿突然肿热痛，尤其久坐、近期手术或既往血栓者，不能按摩或按穴观察，应紧急评估。
