📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated or supine with the forearm supinated, palm up and middle finger extended but relaxed.坐位或仰卧，前臂旋后，手掌向上，中指自然伸展并放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    PC9 is in the shallow centre at the very tip of the middle finger, on the distal pulp rather than at a nail corner; locate each side separately.中冲在中指指尖中央的浅凹处，位于指腹最远端，不在指甲角；双侧分别确认。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Identify the central fingertip pulp. Proper palmar digital arteries and nerves run along both sides of the middle finger, so the midline is away from the lateral neurovascular bundles; any insertion must be superficial, never deep or directed laterally.从指腹正中确认指尖软组织，掌侧固有指动脉和指神经沿中指两侧走行，正中线相对避开两侧神经血管束；只作极浅定位，禁止深刺或朝侧方探寻。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently feel the central fingertip pulp without squeezing the sides; do not needle or prick through a wound, infection or area of reduced sensation.以指腹轻触指尖中央的软组织隆起，避免横向挤压两侧；有伤口、感染或感觉减退时不作针刺或点刺。

  4. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Use the centre of the patient's own distal middle-finger pulp rather than the practitioner's finger proportions; keep the point equidistant from both nail-side margins.以患者中指指腹远端的中央作目测定位，不使用施术者手指比例；中指尖端与指甲两侧缘保持等距。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • A nail corner or finger edge misses the central point and approaches a digital artery or nerve; relocate to the pulp midline and keep any procedure superficial.取到指甲角或中指侧缘会偏离正中冲穴，并接近指动脉、指神经；重新在指腹正中找点，保持极浅操作。
  • Treating blood-pricking as routine self-care risks infection, prolonged bleeding and delayed emergency care; PC9 is not self-administered first aid, and fainting requires emergency services first.把点刺出血当作日常自我处理，可能导致感染、持续出血或延误急症；中冲不用于自行急救，昏厥等情况应先呼叫急救。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Fainting (first aid)晕厥（急救）

PC9 is a traditional jing-well adjunct for acute patterns, but real syncope requires fall protection, emergency services and cause assessment; pricking never replaces first aid.中冲是传统井穴急症配穴之一，但真实晕厥先防跌伤、呼叫急救并排查病因；不得以点刺代替急救。

Fever发热 ⚖️

May serve as a jing-well point in traditional heat-pattern combinations; fever with altered awareness, breathing difficulty, neck stiffness or dehydration needs urgent care, not self-pricking.传统热证配穴中可作井穴角色；发热伴意识改变、呼吸困难、颈强或脱水征应立即就医，不作自行点刺。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Fingertip contusion or distal phalanx injury指尖挫伤或末节指骨损伤

Swelling, pain, a subungual haematoma or limited motion after a crush or impact needs assessment for fracture or nail-bed injury; do not needle or squeeze an open wound.夹挤、撞击后肿痛、甲下血肿或不能屈伸需评估骨折、甲床损伤；有明显伤口时避免针刺或挤压。

Fingertip sensory or circulation changes指尖感觉或循环异常

Persistent numbness or colour/temperature change may involve nerve or blood flow and should not be treated as simple point sensitivity.持续麻木、颜色或温度改变可能涉及神经或血流，不应当作单纯穴位反应。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • A pale/blue, cold fingertip, worsening numbness or severe pain requires urgent assessment of circulation and nerves.指尖发白发紫、冰冷、麻木加重或剧痛：立即就医评估血流和神经。
  • Fainting, altered consciousness, chest pain or abnormal breathing requires emergency services; do not try to needle the person awake.昏厥、意识改变、胸痛或呼吸异常：呼叫急救，不要尝试针刺唤醒。
