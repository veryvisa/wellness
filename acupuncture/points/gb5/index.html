📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Seated upright or side-lying with the head tilted slightly toward the unaffected side to fully expose the temporal hairline.正坐位或侧卧位，头微微向健侧偏斜，充分暴露患侧颞部鬓发区。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First establish the two end landmarks: superiorly, ST8 (Touwei) at the corner of the forehead hairline (4.5 cun lateral to GV24); inferiorly, GB7 (Qubin) at the posterior border of the temporal hairline level with the ear apex. Trace a gentle anteriorly convex arc within the hairline between them; GB5 lies at the exact midpoint (1/2).先定两端骨性与发际标志：上端找额角发际顶点的头维（ST8，前发际正中神庭旁开 4.5 寸）；下端找耳前鬓发后缘、平耳尖的曲鬓（GB7）。沿鬓发内侧作微向前凸的弧形连线，悬颅就在这条弧线的中点（1/2 处）。

  2. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The span between the two frontal hairline corners (ST8) is 9 cun; use it to fix ST8 (4.5 cun from the midline). The ST8–GB7 segment is a curve with no straight cun count in the standard; GB5 is its midpoint.两额角发际（头维）之间折作 9 寸，用来先定准头维（前正中线旁开 4.5 寸）；头维到曲鬓之间是弧形段，国标不设直寸，悬颅取这条弧线的中点。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located over the anterosuperior temporal fossa superficial to the temporalis muscle and fascia, at the transition between the epicranial aponeurosis and superficial temporal fascia, overlying the junction of the squamous temporal and anteroinferior parietal bones. Posteroinferiorly, pulses of the frontal and parietal branches of the superficial temporal artery run alongside the auriculotemporal nerve; insert transversely posteriorly in the loose subaponeurotic layer while avoiding palpable arterial pulses.位于颞窝前上方、颞肌及其深层颞筋膜浅面，帽状腱膜与颞浅筋膜移行区，深层对应颞骨鳞部与顶骨前下角交界区。穴位后下方（耳屏前上方至颞部）可触及颞浅动脉额支与顶支搏动，并有耳颞神经伴行；进针时须在皮下疏松结缔组织层（帽状腱膜下）沿头皮向后平刺，避开搏动的颞浅血管主干及分支。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Ask the patient to clench and relax the jaw to palpate the contraction of the anterosuperior temporalis belly within the hairline; press gently around the midpoint of the ST8–GB7 arc, where patients with migraine or temporalis tension often exhibit a taut band or distinct tender spot.让患者做用力咬牙、松开动作，手指在鬓发内可清楚摸到颞肌前上部肌腹的隆起与收缩；在头维与曲鬓弧线中点处用指腹上下轻按，偏头痛或颞肌紧张者常在此处触及条索状筋结或明显酸胀痛点。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting too superiorly (to the upper quarter of the ST8–GB7 arc): lands on GB4 (Hanyan), which targets frontotemporal corner pain; drifting too inferiorly (lower quarter) lands on GB6 (Xuanli), closer to the preauricular ST7 area.取穴偏上（靠近头维与曲鬓弧线上 1/4 处）：误取为颔厌（GB4），偏重前额颞角痛；偏下（靠近弧线下 1/4 处）则误取为悬厘（GB6），更近耳前上关。
  • Connecting ST8 and GB7 with a straight line instead of a curve: because the temporal hairline curves anteriorly, a straight midpoint shifts anterior to the hairline into the hairless temple above Taiyang (EX-HN5).未沿弧线取点而作直线连线：鬓发呈前凸弧形，若在头维与曲鬓之间拉直线取中点，穴位会向前偏出鬓发进入无毛颞区（靠近太阳穴上方）。
  • Needling too deeply through the temporal fascia or hitting superficial temporal artery branches: the temporal scalp is highly vascular and thin over bone, so deep or perpendicular needling readily causes subcutaneous hematoma or sharp radiating pain from the auriculotemporal nerve; use shallow transverse posterior insertion (0.3–0.5 cun).进针过深穿破颞筋膜或刺中颞浅动脉分支：颞区血管丰富且紧贴骨面，深刺或直刺极易刺破小动脉引起皮下血肿（青紫包块），或刺激耳颞神经引发剧烈电击样放射痛；必须沿头皮向后平刺 0.3–0.5 寸。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Specific for Shaoyang temporal migraine (especially with marked tenderness at the midpoint of the ST8–GB7 arc) and migraine accompanied by upper toothache or cheek pain; not chosen for vertex or occipital headache, where GV20 or GB20 are preferred.专治少阳经颞部偏头痛（头维至曲鬓弧线中点压痛明显者）及合并上齿痛、面颊痛；全头痛、巅顶痛或后枕痛不选此穴，应分别选百会、风池等。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Migraine (with or without aura, acute or preventive management)偏头痛（无先兆或有先兆偏头痛，发作期与间歇期）

Typically presents as unilateral throbbing moderate-to-severe temporal headache with nausea, photophobia, and phonophobia aggravated by routine activity. Sudden-onset 'thunderclap' headache or headache with fever, neck stiffness, or focal neurological deficits requires immediate emergency referral to rule out subarachnoid hemorrhage or intracranial pathology.典型表现为单侧颞部搏动性中重度头痛，常伴恶心、畏光、畏声，日常活动加重。若为首次突发「雷击样」剧痛、或伴发热颈强、神经功能缺损，须立即转急诊排查蛛网膜下腔出血或颅内病变。

Temporalis myofascial pain syndrome and temporomandibular disorders (TMD)颞肌肌筋膜疼痛综合征与颞下颌关节紊乱病（TMD）

Clenching, bruxism, or stress-induced temporalis overactivity creates taut tender bands at GB5 referring pain to the orbit and upper teeth, often mistaken for dental pain or migraine; check jaw opening/closing clicks and masseter tenderness to differentiate.紧咬牙、夜磨牙或压力大时颞肌过度收缩，可在悬颅处摸到条索状压痛点，疼痛可放射至眼眶与上牙，常被误认为牙痛或偏头痛；检查下颌开闭口弹响与咬肌压痛可助鉴别。

Auriculotemporal neuralgia and differential diagnosis of giant cell arteritis (temporal arteritis)耳颞神经痛与巨细胞动脉炎（颞动脉炎）鉴别

Auriculotemporal nerve entrapment causes paroxysmal stabbing or burning pain and scalp allodynia from the preauricular area to the temple. In patients over 50 with new-onset persistent temporal headache, scalp tenderness when combing hair, jaw claudication, or visual changes, suspect giant cell arteritis—do not needle blindly; refer urgently for ESR, CRP, and medical evaluation.耳颞神经卡压表现为耳前至颞部阵发性刺痛、灼痛或头皮触痛。尤其注意 50 岁以上患者新发颞部持续疼痛、头皮触痛（梳头痛）、咀嚼痛（颌跛行）或视力模糊时，高度警惕巨细胞动脉炎，严禁盲目针刺，须立即就医查血沉（ESR）、CRP 并转诊。

Where is GB5 Xuánlú?悬颅（GB5）在哪里？

On the head, at the midpoint of the curved line from ST8 to GB7 (following the curve of the temple hairline).头部，从头维至曲鬓的弧形连线（其弧度与鬓发弧度相应）的中点。

How is GB5 needled?悬颅（GB5）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at GB5?悬颅（GB5）最要注意的安全问题？

Thread transversely under the scalp aponeurosis; feel for the superficial temporal pulse first and press after withdrawal.沿头皮帽状腱膜下平刺；先触颞浅动脉搏动再进针，出针按压防血肿。
