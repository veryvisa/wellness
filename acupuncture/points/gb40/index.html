Sinus tarsi syndrome跗骨窦综合征（Sinus Tarsi Syndrome）

Persistent deep pain anteroinferior to the lateral malleolus following an ankle sprain, worse on uneven ground; deep palpation into GB40 (lateral sinus tarsi) reproduces sharp focal pain, differentiated from simple tendinitis via diagnostic imaging or injection.外踝扭伤后遗留的外踝前下方深部持续疼痛，走不平路面时加重，按压丘墟深处（跗骨窦外口）出现典型深部锐痛，局部封闭或超声影像可协助与单纯肌腱炎鉴别。

Superficial fibular nerve entrapment or post-traumatic neuralgia腓浅神经卡压或外踝术/伤后神经痛

Burning, tingling, or numbness from the anterolateral ankle to the lateral dorsum of the foot, provoked by plantarflexion-inversion and a positive Tinel sign anteroinferior to the malleolus; avoid direct nerve puncture.外踝前上方至足背外侧烧灼感、刺痛或麻木，足跖屈内翻时牵拉诱发症状，叩击外踝前下方可出现向足背放射的 Tinel 征；进针需避开神经干。
