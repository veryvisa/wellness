幽门 Yōumén

Hidden Gate · Take care需谨慎

Crossing point of the Chong vessel and the Kidney channel冲脉与足少阴经交会穴

📍 Location定位★

On the upper abdomen, 6 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, 0.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 6 寸，前正中线旁开 0.5 寸。

💡 Locate CV14 (6 cun above the umbilicus, about 2 cun below the xiphoid) and go 0.5 cun lateral (level with CV14).先定巨阙（脐上 6 寸，剑突下约 2 寸），旁开 0.5 寸。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Never insert deeply upward, to avoid the liver; contraindicated in pregnancy.不可向上深刺，以免伤及肝脏；孕期禁针。

🛡 Safety安全★

Contraindicated in pregnancy. Deep lie the left lobe of the liver and the stomach: perpendicular 0.5–1 cun only, never deep upward under the costal margin; take care with an enlarged liver.孕期禁针。深面是肝左叶、胃：只按教材 0.5–1 寸直刺，不向上（肋弓下）深刺；肝大者慎针。

  • Drifting onto the anterior midline along the Ren vessel (such as CV14 or the CV12 level): misses the 0.5-cun lateral Kidney and Chong vessel line, and deep perpendicular needling risks injuring the peritoneum and underlying gastric body, cardia, or left liver lobe.误取到前正中线任脉（如巨阙 CV14 或中脘 CV12 水平线正中）：偏入正中线，失去足少阴肾经与冲脉旁开 0.5 寸的循行定位；若向深处直刺过深，可伤及腹膜及深面胃体、贲门或肝左叶。
  • Drifting too far laterally (toward 1.5–2 cun along the Stomach channel such as ST19 or ST20): leaves the 0.5-cun Kidney line and moves closer to the costal margin and deeper projections of the left liver lobe or transverse colon, increasing visceral risk if needled deeply.误向外偏过远（如偏开 1.5–2 寸走向足阳明胃经不容 ST19 或承满 ST20 一线）：超出肾经 0.5 寸旁开距离，更靠近肋弓下缘与肝左叶、横结肠投影区，盲目深刺易伤及肝或胃肠。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

Useful with vexation, acid regurgitation, poor appetite, or rebellious qi; seek medical care first for intractable vomiting, dehydration, or peritoneal signs, and combine with PC6, CV12, and ST36 rather than using KI21 alone.呕吐伴心下烦闷、反酸、纳呆或偏脾胃气滞、冲气上逆时可配用；剧烈频繁呕吐、脱水或伴腹膜刺激征时须先就医，止呕常配内关、中脘、足三里，比单用幽门更全面。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

May be paired for aching or distension in the upper epigastric and Chong-Ren region; not a primary choice for lower abdominal pain, acute abdomen, or organic pain, and is typically combined with CV12, ST36, or ST25 by region and pattern.偏上腹部胃脘与冲任气滞所致的隐痛、胀痛可酌情配伍；下腹痛、急腹症或器质性腹痛不选作主力，临床多与中脘、足三里、天枢等按部位与病机配穴。

Where is KI21 Yōumén?幽门（KI21）在哪里？

On the upper abdomen, 6 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, 0.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 6 寸，前正中线旁开 0.5 寸。

How is KI21 needled?幽门（KI21）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of KI21?幽门（KI21）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Chong vessel and the Kidney channel冲脉与足少阴经交会穴

Main safety point at KI21?幽门（KI21）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. Deep lie the left lobe of the liver and the stomach: perpendicular 0.5–1 cun only, never deep upward under the costal margin; take care with an enlarged liver.孕期禁针。深面是肝左叶、胃：只按教材 0.5–1 寸直刺，不向上（肋弓下）深刺；肝大者慎针。
