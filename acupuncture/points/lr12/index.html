⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Consider only as a local adjunct for mild lower-abdominal qi-stagnation pain after ruling out acute abdomen, incarcerated hernia, and pregnancy contraindications; do not choose it for acute severe pain, rebound tenderness, or fever, where urgent medical evaluation comes first, and compare with local or distal points such as CV3 or ST36.仅在少腹局部气滞痛且已排除急腹症、疝气嵌顿及妊娠禁忌时可作局部配穴参考；急性剧烈腹痛、压痛反跳痛或发热时不选此穴，优先排查急腹症，常与中极、足三里等辨证配用。

Period pain痛经

Consider only as a local adjunct for lower-abdominal cramping or qi stagnation/cold when pregnancy is ruled out; dysmenorrhea protocols more commonly prioritize CV6, CV4, SP6, SP8, or LR3, whereas LR12 is generally not a routine first choice due to intimate location and deep-needling risk.仅在少腹拘急、寒凝或气滞偏下腹局部不适且明确排除妊娠时可作局部配穴参考；痛经调理常优先选关元、气海、三阴交、地机、太冲等穴，急脉因部位私密且有深刺风险通常不作常规首选。

Irregular periods月经不调

Traditionally linked to lower-abdominal and Chong/Ren qi dynamics, yet irregular menstruation is clinically managed more commonly with SP6, CV4, LR3, SP10, or ST36 rather than relying on LR12; rule out pregnancy or organic pathology first if amenorrhea, abnormal bleeding, or severe pain is present, and avoid needling in pregnancy.传统上与少腹及冲任气机相关，但临床调理月经不调较少单独依赖急脉，多选三阴交、关元、太冲、血海、足三里等穴；若伴停经、异常出血或剧痛，须先就医排查妊娠或器质性病变，孕期禁针。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

May serve as a local adjunct for lower-jiao urinary discomfort traditionally, but dysuria, urinary retention, or frequency/urgency more often prioritizes CV4, CV3, BL28, SP6, or SP9; seek medical care first for retention, hematuria, or fever with flank pain, as LR12 is not a first-line choice.传统用于下焦少腹相关泌尿不适时可作局部配穴参考，但排尿困难、尿潴留或尿频尿急常优先选关元、中极、膀胱俞、三阴交、阴陵泉等穴；出现尿潴留、血尿或发热腰痛时须先就医，急脉不作首选。

Where is LR12 Jímài?急脉（LR12）在哪里？

In the groin region, level with the upper border of the pubic symphysis, 2.5 cun lateral to the anterior midline.腹股沟区，横平耻骨联合上缘，前正中线旁开 2.5 寸。

How is LR12 needled?急脉（LR12）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, avoiding the artery; 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺，避开动脉；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at LR12?急脉（LR12）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. The femoral vein is lateral and the spermatic cord or round ligament and inguinal canal deep: avoid the pulse, stay shallow without lifting-thrusting and never aim into the abdomen; obtain consent and drape professionally.孕期禁针。外侧有股静脉、深面有精索或子宫圆韧带与腹股沟管：避开搏动，浅刺不提插，不向腹腔方向进针；须先取得同意并专业遮盖。
