Suwen ch.61 lists the fifty-nine needling sites for heat disease; Yunmen, the shoulder bone (LI15 area), Weizhong and Suikong, left and right, make eight sites used to drain heat from the four limbs. Where is LU2 Yúnmén?云门（LU2）在哪里？

In the hollow below the collarbone (infraclavicular fossa), just medial to the coracoid process of the scapula, 6 cun lateral to the anterior midline.锁骨下窝的凹陷中，肩胛骨喙突内侧，距前正中线 6 寸。

How is LU2 needled?云门（LU2）怎么刺？

Oblique, tip pointing laterally toward the shoulder; 0.5–0.8 cun.向外斜刺，针尖朝肩外侧；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at LU2?云门（LU2）最要注意的安全问题？

Deep medial insertion can injure the axillary vessels and brachial plexus, or puncture the pleura and cause a pneumothorax.向内深刺可伤及腋动脉、腋静脉和臂丛，或刺破胸膜造成气胸。
