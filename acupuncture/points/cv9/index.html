CV9

水分 Shuǐfēn

Water Division · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

On the upper abdomen, 1 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 1 寸，前正中线上。

💡 With xiphisternal junction to umbilicus as 8 cun, measure 1 cun up from the umbilicus.剑胸结合到脐中折 8 寸，从脐向上量 1 寸。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

Moxibustion is preferred for fluid disorders.水病多用灸法。

🛡 Safety安全

With marked ascites the wall is tense and bowel lies close, so prefer moxa over deep needling; caution in pregnancy.腹水明显时腹壁张力高、肠管贴近，宜灸不宜深刺；孕妇慎用。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Oedema, urinary difficulty (impaired fluid distribution)水肿、小便不利等水液输布失常病证 ⚖️Abdominal pain, diarrhoea, regurgitation of food腹痛、腹泻、反胃吐食等胃肠病证 ⚖️

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine with knees flexed and the abdomen relaxed; identify the xiphisternal junction and umbilicus before measuring the upper abdomen.仰卧屈膝，腹壁放松；先触剑胸结合与脐中，再量上腹正中距离。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The xiphisternal junction-to-umbilicus span is 8 cun; CV9 is one cun above the navel, seven cun below the xiphisternal junction.胸剑联合中点至脐中为 8 寸；水分在脐上一寸，即距胸剑联合下端七寸。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Measure one cun above the umbilicus on the anterior midline, one cun below CV10; use the umbilicus with the abdominal wall relaxed.沿腹部前正中线从脐向上量一寸，位于下脘下方一寸；腹壁松弛时以脐中为准。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The linea alba and abdominal-wall tissue are superficial, with preperitoneal tissue and bowel deeper. Lateral drift may approach abdominal-wall vessels; palpate superficially at midline and avoid scars, stomas or hernia bulges.浅层为腹白线和腹壁组织，深部为腹膜前组织与肠管；偏外侧可能接近腹壁血管，须在正中浅触并避开瘢痕、造口或疝包块。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently confirm one cun above the navel and cross-check its relation to CV10 and CV8; stop palpating if distension or marked tenderness is present.轻触脐上一寸处并与下脘、神阙位置关系复核；腹胀或压痛明显时停止按压，先评估病因。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Two cun above the navel is CV10 and the navel itself is CV8; lateral drift may approach abdominal-wall vessels and must not be corrected by deeper insertion.脐上量成两寸会落到下脘，量到脐平则是神阙；向一侧偏移可能靠近腹壁血管，不应加深进针纠偏。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Oedema水肿 ⚖️

An adjunct for traditional fluid-related abdominal fullness; generalized edema or ascites needs cardiac, renal and hepatic evaluation rather than local point treatment.腹部水湿胀满的传统辨证时可作辅助；全身水肿或腹水不以局部取穴代替心肾肝评估。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Consider for localized periumbilical upper-abdominal discomfort with distension; compare CV12/CV14 for epigastric pain and assess persistent pain for organic disease.脐上局限不适并有腹胀时考虑；上腹心下痛更比较中脘或巨阙，持续痛先排查器质病。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

An adjunct for periumbilical distension in a traditional fluid pattern; acute infectious diarrhea, blood or dehydration requires medical care first.水湿证伴脐周腹胀时作为辅助；急性感染性腹泻、血便或脱水须先医疗处理。

Where is CV9 Shuǐfēn?水分（CV9）在哪里？

On the upper abdomen, 1 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 1 寸，前正中线上。

How is CV9 needled?水分（CV9）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at CV9?水分（CV9）最要注意的安全问题？

With marked ascites the wall is tense and bowel lies close, so prefer moxa over deep needling; caution in pregnancy.腹水明显时腹壁张力高、肠管贴近，宜灸不宜深刺；孕妇慎用。
