Form 6: Step back and whirl the arms, left and right第6式 · 左右倒卷肱

alternating sides, 4 in all左右交替，共 4 次

Zuǒyòu dào juǎngōng

Qigong养生功法 › Tai chi (simplified 24-form)太极拳（简化二十四式） › Form 6: Step back and whirl the arms, left and right第6式 · 左右倒卷肱

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ Stepping backward倒退走 ⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚 ⚠ Higher knee load膝部负荷较大

  • If you fall easily or your balance is poor: leave out the stepping at first and do the arms only; practise the step back holding a wall or a counter, with someone beside you容易跌倒、平衡不好：这一式先不退步，只做手；要练退步就扶着墙或台面做，旁边有人
  • Keep at least three steps of clear space behind you: check for rug edges, cords, children and pets first身后要有至少三步的空地：先看清身后没有地毯边、电线、小孩和宠物
  • Knee pain: stand high, bending the knee no more than about 30 degrees, and take small steps; keep the knee in line with the toes and not past them; stop if it hurts. In one laboratory study this form loaded the knee more than Cloud Hands or Grasping the Sparrow's Tail, so do fewer at first膝盖痛：架子站高，弯膝到约 30 度为止，步子迈小；膝盖对着脚尖方向，不超过脚尖；痛就停。一项实测里，这一式的膝部负荷比云手、揽雀尾大，初学先少做
  • Dizziness or vertigo: keep the head still, turn only the trunk, and go slower头晕、眩晕：头保持不动，只转上身，动作更慢

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Turn right; turn the right palm up and arc it past the belly, back and up to ear height; turn the left palm up too; look at the right hand, then the left上身右转，右手翻掌向上，经腹前向后上方划弧，举到耳朵高度；左手也翻掌向上；眼睛看右手，再看左手
  2. Bend the right arm and push the right hand forward from beside the ear; draw the left hand back beside the waist, palm up; at the same time lift the left foot lightly and step back (slightly left), ball of the foot first, then the whole foot右臂屈肘，右手从耳旁向前推出；左手收到左腰旁、掌心向上；同时左脚轻轻提起，向后（稍偏左）退一步，脚前掌先落地，再踏实
  3. Shift the weight back onto the left leg and straighten the right foot, an empty stance重心后移到左腿，右脚脚尖转正，成右虚步
  4. Turn left, arc the left hand back and up, and step back with the right foot in the same way; 4 steps back in all, alternating sides上身左转，左手向后上方划弧举起，换右脚后退，动作相同；左右交替共退 4 步

🌬 Breathe in as the hand arcs back, out as you push and step back.手向后上举时吸气，推掌退步时呼气。

✅ Key points要领

  • Stepping back, the ball of the foot lands first; load it slowly退步时前脚掌先落，慢慢放重心
  • Step back slightly outward so the feet are not on one line退的脚偏一点外侧，两脚不踩一条线
  • The push and the step arrive together推掌和退步一起到位

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Rushing the step and leaning back急着退，身体后仰
  • Stepping back onto one line两脚退成一条线
  • Looking down at your feet as you step退步时低头看脚

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Stand in place and do the arms only, twice each side; or do it seated.原地站着，只做手的动作，左右各两次；或坐着做。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 The name means whirling the upper arm back and out again while stepping back; the common English name, repulse monkey, comes from an older name, 'driving the monkey back'. It is a traditional image, not a research finding.「倒卷肱」说的是一边退步、一边把上臂卷回来再推出；英文俗名「赶走猴子」（repulse monkey）来自传统的「倒撵猴」。这是传统的比喻，不是研究结论。
