Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Main safety point at LI8?下廉（LI8）最要注意的安全问题？

Insert along the muscular plane and avoid visible superficial veins; withdraw and redirect if severe radiating pain occurs.沿肌间进针，避开明显浅静脉；强烈放射痛时退针调整。
