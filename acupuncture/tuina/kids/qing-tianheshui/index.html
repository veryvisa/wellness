Pushing the Heavenly River: one small thing parents can do during a fever清天河水：孩子发烧时家长能做的一件小事

When a child has a fever in the middle of the night, the first worry is whether to go to hospital. Check the red flags below and how your child looks, which matters more than the number on the thermometer. Once you are sure it is fine to watch at home, pushing the Heavenly River is something to do with your child for comfort; it is not a fever medicine.半夜孩子发烧，家长最着急的是「要不要去医院」。先看下面的红旗和孩子的样子，这比体温计上的数字更要紧；确认可以在家观察，再给孩子做清天河水——它是让孩子舒服一点、让你有事可做的陪伴，不是退烧药。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • A baby under 3 months with a fever (rectal 38°C or higher) needs a doctor urgently, with no massage first; any fever under 6 months needs a doctor3 个月以下的宝宝发烧（肛温 38°C 或以上）要立即就医，不做推拿；6 个月以下发烧都要看医生
  • Seizure, struggling to breathe or blue lips, cannot be woken or is floppy and unresponsive: call 911抽搐、呼吸费力或嘴唇发青、叫不醒或软绵绵没反应：打 911
  • Signs of dehydration: fewer than 4 wet diapers in 24 hours (fewer than 3 for older children), no tears when crying, dry mouth and tongue, sunken eyes or soft spot, refusing to drink: see a doctor the same day脱水的样子：24 小时湿尿布少于 4 片（大孩子小便少于 3 次）、哭时没有眼泪、嘴和舌头干、眼窝或囟门凹下去、不肯喝水：当天看医生
  • Unusually sleepy or listless, unusually cranky and cannot be settled, or a fever with a rash or anything else that worries you: contact a doctor精神特别差、特别嗜睡、异常烦躁哄不好，或发烧伴皮疹、伴其他让你担心的情况：联系医生
  • A fever lasting more than 48–72 hours: see a doctor发烧超过 48–72 小时：看医生

🛑 When to stop and see a doctor什么时候停手去看医生

  • The child becomes more drowsy or harder to rouse while you work, or any red flag above appears: stop and act on the red flag推的过程中孩子精神越来越差、叫不太醒，或出现上面任何一条红旗：停手，按红旗去就医或打 911
  • The forearm skin turns raw or breaks: stop, switch arms or leave it for another day前臂皮肤推红、推破：停，换另一只手或改天再做
  • The child will not drink or is passing much less urine: that matters more than the temperature; see a doctor孩子不肯喝水、小便明显变少：这比体温更要紧，去看医生

🤲 Who does it, and how to start谁来做、开始之前

Ages 6 months to 12 years适合 6 个月到 12 岁

Done by a parent or the adult who usually cares for the child. Trim and smooth your nails, warm your hands, keep the room warm; a little baby oil is fine, but no talc (babies breathe it in). Do it when the child is calm. Crying, squirming or pushing your hand away is the signal to stop; never hold a child down to finish.由家长或固定照顾孩子的大人做。先剪短磨平指甲、把手搓热，房间暖和；可以用一点婴儿润肤油，不用爽身粉（宝宝会吸进去）。孩子情绪平稳时做，哭闹、扭动、推开你的手，就是停手的信号——这时候不要按住孩子硬做。

✋ When not to do it这些情况先别做

  • Babies under 6 months with a fever: see a doctor directly, no massage6 个月以下的宝宝发烧：直接看医生，不做推拿
  • Broken skin, eczema or a rash on the forearm前臂皮肤破损、湿疹、皮疹处
  • Not when the child resists or cries. If fever medicine is needed, give it as the label, pharmacist or doctor says; massage does not conflict with it孩子抗拒、哭闹时不做；需要退烧药时照常按说明书或药师、医生说的剂量用，推拿和用药不冲突

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E1 The Canadian Paediatric Society tells parents that the height of a fever does not show how serious the illness is, and how the child looks and acts is usually a better sign; babies under 6 months with a fever should see a doctor and under 3 months urgently; older children can be cared for at home if they drink enough and seem well, but should see a doctor if the fever lasts more than 48–72 hours.加拿大儿科学会给家长的说明：发烧的高低不能告诉你病有多重，孩子的样子和表现通常是更好的信号；6 个月以下发烧要看医生，3 个月以下要紧急就医；大一点的孩子只要喝得够、精神好可以在家照顾，烧超过 48–72 小时要看医生。 [1]
  • E2 A 2025 meta-analysis pooled 15 randomised trials of massage for fever in children (1,661 children): massage alone was not significantly different from fever medicine, and medicine plus massage lowered temperature more than medicine alone; the authors say trial quality needs to improve. Massage belongs alongside medicine and medical judgement, never in their place.一篇 2025 年荟萃分析合并了 15 项按摩用于儿童发烧的随机试验（1661 名儿童）：单用按摩和用退烧药相比没有显著差别，退烧药加按摩比单用药体温下降更多；作者认为试验质量需要提高。这说明按摩只能放在用药和就医判断之外做，不能拿来换掉它们。 [2]
  • E5 We found no reliable study of this one technique on its own; the Heavenly River is a point specific to pediatric massage and is not on the WHO list of standard points.单独检验「清天河水」这一个手法的可靠研究，我们没有找到；天河水是小儿推拿的特定穴，不在 WHO 标准经穴表里。

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Pediatric massage calls the line down the centre of the inner forearm the 'Heavenly River' and regards it as cooling; pushing from wrist to elbow is called 'clearing the Heavenly River' and is the method it uses most when a child is feverish. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.小儿推拿把前臂内侧正中这条线叫「天河水」，认为它性凉，从腕往肘推叫「清天河水」，是小儿发热时最常用的一法。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
