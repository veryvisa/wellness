GV7

中枢 Zhōngshū

Central Pivot · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

On the back, in the depression below the spinous process of T10, on the posterior midline.脊柱区，第 10 胸椎棘突下凹陷中，后正中线上。

💡 Count three spinous processes down from the inferior-angle level (about T7).肩胛下角平面（约 T7）向下数 3 个棘突。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique upward向上斜刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

🛡 Safety安全

Thoracic spinous processes slope downward like roof tiles, so angle upward along the interspinous gap; a give (through the ligamentum flavum) or an electric sensation into the limbs means withdraw at once.胸段棘突呈叠瓦状向下，针尖须向上斜刺顺棘突间隙；有落空感（穿过黄韧带）或触电感向肢体放射即退针，不可再进。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone or seated leaning forward; relax the shoulders and count the vertebral levels.俯卧或坐位前屈，放松肩背后逐节计数。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Find the depression below T10 on the posterior midline. The scapular inferior angle is roughly at T7; count down rather than estimating five levels.沿后正中线定位第 10 胸椎棘突下方凹陷；肩胛下角约平 T7，须向下逐节核对，不能直接估算五节。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate T10 and its interspinous tissue with erector-spinae muscles on either side. The thoracic canal is deep; ribs and pleura are lateral, so avoid deep or chest-directed procedures.触及 T10 棘突及棘间软组织，两侧为竖脊肌；胸椎管在深部，偏外有肋骨及胸膜，避免深刺或朝外侧胸腔方向操作。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slight flexion reveals the interspinous space; confirm the midline by counting from both adjacent levels. Spinal rotation or unusual focal tenderness makes a single surface line unreliable.轻度屈背使棘突间隙显出，正中线由上、下相邻椎节交叉确认；脊柱旋转或局部压痛异常时不要以单一体表线定穴。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Estimating from the scapular angle without counting can land at T9 or T11; deep work approaches the canal and lateral work risks the pleura.从肩胛下角估算而未逐节计数，容易误取 T9 或 T11；向深部操作会接近椎管，偏外则有胸膜风险。

Where is GV7 Zhōngshū?中枢（GV7）在哪里？

On the back, in the depression below the spinous process of T10, on the posterior midline.脊柱区，第 10 胸椎棘突下凹陷中，后正中线上。

How is GV7 needled?中枢（GV7）怎么刺？

Oblique upward; 0.5–1 cun.向上斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Main safety point at GV7?中枢（GV7）最要注意的安全问题？

Thoracic spinous processes slope downward like roof tiles, so angle upward along the interspinous gap; a give (through the ligamentum flavum) or an electric sensation into the limbs means withdraw at once.胸段棘突呈叠瓦状向下，针尖须向上斜刺顺棘突间隙；有落空感（穿过黄韧带）或触电感向肢体放射即退针，不可再进。
