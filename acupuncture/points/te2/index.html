Where is TE2 Yèmén?液门（TE2）在哪里？

Dorsum of hand, between the 4th and 5th fingers, just proximal to the web margin at the border of dorsal and palmar skin.手背，第4、5指缝间，指蹼缘后方赤白肉际处。

How is TE2 needled?液门（TE2）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique proximally; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺或向近端斜刺；0.3–0.5寸。

Special-point roles of TE2?液门（TE2）是哪类特定穴？

Ying-spring point, water荥穴，属水

Main safety point at TE2?液门（TE2）最要注意的安全问题？

Avoid dorsal veins and the dorsal branch of ulnar nerve; withdraw if marked paresthesia occurs.避开手背静脉及尺神经手背支；麻感明显应退针。
