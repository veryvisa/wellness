Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Main safety point at KI11?横骨（KI11）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. A full bladder rises above the pubis, so the bladder must be emptied first; insert to textbook depth, shallower in a thin abdominal wall, never angled down into the pelvis.孕期禁针。膀胱充盈时会升到耻骨上方，针前必须排空小便；按教材深度直刺，腹壁薄者减浅，不向下方盆腔斜刺。
