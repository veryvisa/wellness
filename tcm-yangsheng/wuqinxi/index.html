Wuqinxi (Five Animals)五禽戏

Wuqinxi is the oldest Chinese exercise set with a named author: about eighteen hundred years ago the physician Hua Tuo taught it to his students. The version practised in parks today imitates five animals, the tiger, deer, bear, monkey and bird, with two movements each, ten in all. Compared with Baduanjin it adds tiptoe work, standing on one leg and squatting, so it asks more of balance and leg strength, and it is more playful. If your balance is poor or you worry about falling, do Monkey Lifting, Monkey Picking, Bird Stretching and Bird Flying holding the back of a chair; that is not cheating, it is the right way in.五禽戏是中国最早有名有姓的一套健身操：一千八百年前，华佗把它教给弟子。今天公园里练的版本模仿虎、鹿、熊、猿、鸟五种动物，每种两个动作，共十个。和八段锦比，它多了踮脚、单腿站和下蹲，更考验平衡和腿力，也更有趣。平衡不好、怕跌倒的人，猿提、猿摘、鸟伸、鸟飞几式要扶着椅背做；这不是偷懒，是这一套的正确入门。

🚩 Before you start开始之前

  • If you fall easily, have poor balance or dizziness, or have fallen in the past year: do Monkey Lifting, Monkey Picking, Bird Stretching and Bird Flying holding the back of a chair or a counter, heels down and both feet on the floor; keep clear space around you and never practise on a slippery floor容易跌倒、平衡不好、头晕、近一年跌倒过：猿提、猿摘、鸟伸、鸟飞四式一律扶着椅背或台面做，脚跟不提、腿不离地；旁边留出空地，不在湿滑地面上练
  • With heart disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure, recent surgery or a fracture, or pregnancy, check with your doctor before starting有心脏病、未控制的高血压、近期手术或骨折、怀孕，开始前先问医生
  • With high blood pressure: do not hold your breath or clench your jaw; keep breathing as you rise onto your toes or lift your arms高血压：不憋气、不咬牙；踮脚、举臂的几式发力时照常呼吸
  • With osteoporosis: make the forward lean of Tiger Pouncing and the rounded back of Deer Running smaller, keep the back flat, and never bend and twist at the same time; if you have had a spinal compression fracture, have a physiotherapist look at the set with you first骨质疏松：虎扑、鹿奔的前俯和弓背改小，背保持平直，不在弯腰的同时拧转；有过脊椎压缩性骨折的，先请物理治疗师看过
  • With knee pain or a hip or knee replacement: in Monkey Picking squat and in Bird Stretching stand on one leg only as far as is pain-free, knees pointing the same way as the toes膝盖痛、换过髋或膝关节：猿摘的下蹲、鸟伸的单腿只做到不痛的高度，膝盖与脚尖同向
  • Stop at once and sit down with chest pain, dizziness, dimming vision or marked breathlessness in any form任何一式出现胸痛、头晕、眼前发黑或明显气短，马上停下，坐下休息

📜 Where it comes from从哪里来

The earliest record of the Five Animals is in Chen Shou's Records of the Three Kingdoms, in the biography of Hua Tuo. Hua Tuo tells his student Wu Pu that the body needs to work, but should never be pushed to exhaustion, and goes on: 'I have a method called the frolic of the five animals: the tiger, the deer, the bear, the monkey and the bird. It also serves to rid you of illness and makes the legs and feet nimble, and it can stand in for guiding exercises. When you feel out of sorts, get up and do one animal until you are damp with sweat, dust yourself with powder, and the body feels light and the belly hungry.' The history says Wu Pu practised it and at over ninety still had sharp eyes and ears and sound teeth; that is a biographer's account, not a record anyone can check. The Records give only the five names, not how the movements were done. The oldest surviving description, in the Yangxing Yanming Lu traditionally credited to the sixth-century Daoist Tao Hongjing, looks nothing like today's version: the tiger goes on all fours, leaping three times forward and twice back; the monkey hangs from something; the bear lies on its back hugging its knees; only the bird, standing with one foot raised, resembles the modern one-leg stand. The same text says to do as much as your strength allows and to stop once you sweat. Over the following centuries many different versions circulated. The Health Qigong Wuqinxi most often practised today was compiled and promoted by China's General Administration of Sport in 2003: everything is done standing, slowly, with two movements per animal, still in the order tiger, deer, bear, monkey, bird; the names, order and repetitions below follow that version. In other words, today's Wuqinxi is a modern set rebuilt for modern bodies under Hua Tuo's name, not Hua Tuo's original movements.五禽戏最早的记载在陈寿《三国志·华佗传》。华佗对弟子吴普说：「人体欲得劳动，但不当使极尔。」又说：「吾有一术，名五禽之戏，一曰虎，二曰鹿，三曰熊，四曰猿，五曰鸟，亦以除疾，并利蹄足，以当导引。体中不快，起作一禽之戏，沾濡汗出，因上著粉，身体轻便，腹中欲食。」史书说吴普照着练，「年九十余，耳目聪明，齿牙完坚」——这是传记里的记述，不是可以复核的记录。《三国志》只留下五种动物的名字，没有写动作怎么做。现存最早的动作描述在旧题南朝陶弘景的《养性延命录》里，和今天的样子很不一样：「虎戏者，四肢距地，前三踯，却二踯」，是四肢着地扑跳；猿戏要「攀物自悬」，吊在东西上；熊戏是仰躺抱膝；鸟戏「双立手，翘一足」，才有今天单腿站的影子。书里还说「任力为之，以汗出为度」，量力而行、出汗就停。此后一千多年各地流传多种五禽戏，动作都不统一。今天最常见的「健身气功·五禽戏」由国家体育总局健身气功管理中心在 2003 年整理推广，全部改成站着做、动作放慢，五禽各两戏，顺序仍是虎、鹿、熊、猿、鸟；下面的名称、顺序和遍数按这一版。所以，今天的五禽戏是借华佗之名、按现代人身体重编的一套操，不是华佗原样的动作。

🗓 How to practise怎么练

Start with the tiger and the deer (the first four forms), once a day, then add the bear, monkey and bird as you learn them. One full round takes about 12–15 minutes. Studies commonly used 4–6 sessions a week of 30–60 minutes, for 12 weeks to 6 months. Hua Tuo's warning not to push to exhaustion still holds: a light sweat while you can still talk comfortably is the right amount; start the one-leg and squatting forms holding a chair and standing tall.起步：先学虎、鹿两戏（前四式），每天一遍，熟了再加熊、猿、鸟。整套一遍约 12–15 分钟。研究里常见的练法是每周 4–6 次、每次 30–60 分钟、连续 12 周到 6 个月。华佗那句「不当使极」今天仍然适用：微微出汗、说话不喘就是合适的量，单腿和下蹲的几式先扶椅、先站高。

Opening stance预备势

  1. Stand with feet together, hands hanging at the sides, looking ahead两脚并拢站立，两手自然垂在体侧，目视前方
  2. Step the left foot out to shoulder width, knees slightly soft, the whole body relaxed左脚向左开步，与肩同宽，两膝微屈，全身放松
  3. Slowly raise the palms in front of you to chest height, palms up两掌在体前慢慢上提到胸前，掌心向上
  4. Turn the palms down and slowly press them to the front of the belly; one raise and press is one round, 2 rounds in all (opening and settling the breath)两掌翻转向下，慢慢按到腹前；一提一按为一遍，做 2 遍（起势调息）

Closing收势

  1. Raise the palms slowly out to the sides and overhead, then press them slowly down in front of the body to the belly; 2 rounds (drawing the energy home)两掌从体侧慢慢举到头顶上方，再经体前慢慢下按到腹前；做 2 遍（引气归元）
  2. Hold the hands lightly in front of the belly for a few breaths两手在腹前虚合，静站几次呼吸
  3. Rub the palms together until warm and stroke the face gently upward a few times两掌合拢搓热，轻轻从下往上抹脸几遍
  4. Let the hands fall and bring the left foot back beside the right两手垂落，左脚收回，并步站立

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 In a meta-analysis of 31 randomised trials with 3,622 older adults (tai chi, Baduanjin, Wuqinxi and others), people doing Wuqinxi scored on average 4.8 points higher than controls on the Berg Balance Scale. The authors rated the evidence low-certainty and stressed that better balance-test scores do not yet show fewer actual falls.一篇汇总 31 项随机试验、3622 名老人的荟萃分析（太极、八段锦、五禽戏等）里，练五禽戏的人在 Berg 平衡量表上比对照组平均高 4.8 分。作者评为低确定性证据，并特别说明：平衡测试分数变好，不等于真实跌倒次数减少，这一点还没有证据。 [1]
  • E2 Middle-aged and older adults with primary hypertension: a meta-analysis of 11 randomised trials with 770 people found that adding Wuqinxi to usual care for 3–6 months lowered systolic pressure by a further 5.3 mmHg and diastolic by 6.7 mmHg on average, with moderate-certainty evidence for blood pressure; the lipid results were low to very low certainty. This is an add-on to medication and lifestyle care, not a substitute.原发性高血压的中老年人：一篇汇总 11 项随机试验、770 人的荟萃分析，在常规治疗之外加练五禽戏 3–6 个月，收缩压平均多降 5.3 mmHg、舒张压多降 6.7 mmHg，血压证据评为中等；血脂的结果证据低到极低。这是加在降压药和生活方式之外的辅助，不是替代。 [2]
  • E2 Knee osteoarthritis: a meta-analysis of 9 trials with 809 people reported bigger gains in knee function scores and knee muscle strength with Wuqinxi; pain scores and two balance measures did not differ significantly from control. Almost all the studies were done in China, and several results are low-certainty.膝骨关节炎：一篇汇总 9 项试验、809 人的荟萃分析报告，五禽戏组的膝关节功能评分和膝部肌力进步更多；但疼痛评分和两项平衡指标与对照组没有显著差别。研究几乎都在中国完成，部分结果确定性低。 [3]
  • E3 Osteoporosis: a 2015 meta-analysis saw improvements in pain scores, but judged the effect of Wuqinxi on bone density uncertain because the studies were poorly designed and inconsistent. Do not count on it to prevent fractures; keep up your osteoporosis treatment.骨质疏松：一篇 2015 年的荟萃分析看到疼痛评分有改善，但五禽戏对骨密度的作用，因为研究设计差、结果互相矛盾，结论是不确定。不能指望靠它防骨折，抗骨松治疗照常。 [4]
  • E3 COPD: a 2018 meta-analysis reported gains in exercise capacity and several lung-function measures, and the included trials reported no adverse events; but the studies differed widely. It can be part of pulmonary rehabilitation, not a replacement for proper rehab and medication.慢阻肺：2018 年的一篇荟萃分析报告运动能力和几项肺功能指标改善，纳入的试验没有报告不良事件；但各研究之间差别很大，作为肺康复的一部分可以考虑，不能代替正规的肺康复和用药。 [5]
  • E5 Retraction check: a PubMed search in November 2026 turned up 1 retracted paper, a 2022 article on Wuqinxi and spinal shape in teenagers, retracted by the journal in 2023; this page does not use its conclusions. Most other studies were run in China with small samples, and participants knew which group they were in.撤稿检查：2026 年 11 月在 PubMed 检索五禽戏相关研究，查到 1 篇被撤稿的论文：2022 年一篇讲五禽戏与青少年脊柱形态的文章，2023 年被期刊撤稿，本页不引用它的结论。其余研究大多在中国完成、样本不大、受试者知道自己在哪一组。 [6] [7]
  • E5 The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) regards tai chi and qigong as generally safe mind-body practices; people who are pregnant should talk with their health care providers before starting.美国国家补充与整合健康中心（NCCIH）把太极、气功视为一般比较安全的身心练习；怀孕的人开始前先和医生商量。 [8]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Traditional teaching pairs the five animals with five organ systems: the tiger with the liver, the deer with the kidneys, the bear with the spleen, the monkey with the heart and the bird with the lungs, and says that each animal tends its organ. It also asks you to capture the spirit of each animal as you move: the tiger's power, the deer's lightness, the bear's steadiness, the monkey's quickness and the bird's openness. These are traditional ideas and teaching cues, not research findings.传统教法把五禽配五脏：虎配肝，鹿配肾，熊配脾，猿配心，鸟配肺，并说练哪一禽就是在调哪一脏；还要求练的时候「神似」，心里想着那种动物的样子：虎的威猛、鹿的轻灵、熊的沉稳、猿的机敏、鸟的舒展。这些是传统的说法和教学提示，不是研究结论。

What E1–E5 meanE1–E5 是什么意思
  • E1 Systematic reviews / guidelines系统综述或指南
  • E2 Small or few randomised trials少量或小样本随机试验
  • E3 Observational studies (association)观察性研究（相关）
  • E4 Lab, animal or mechanism studies机制、动物或实验室研究
  • E5 Tradition, experience or case reports传统、经验或个案

E4, E5 and 🏛 lines describe tradition, experience or lab findings only; they never claim that something works.E4、E5 和 🏛 只描述传统、经验或实验室发现，不作疗效主张。
