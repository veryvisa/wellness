📍 Location定位★

Side of head, at posterior margin of temple hairline, anterior to root of auricle and posterior to superficial temporal artery.头侧部，鬓发后缘、耳廓根前方，颞浅动脉后方。

💡 First palpate the superficial temporal pulse in front of the auricular root, then move posterior to it at the temple-hairline margin.先摸到耳廓根前的颞浅动脉搏动，再向后避开搏动，在鬓发后缘取穴。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Avoiding the artery, oblique or transverse避开动脉，斜刺或平刺 · 0.3–0.5 cun0.3–0.5 寸

🛡 Safety安全

The superficial temporal artery is immediately anterior; palpate and avoid its pulse, use only shallow oblique or transverse insertion, and never puncture the artery.颞浅动脉就在前方，先触诊避开搏动；只作浅斜刺或平刺，不可刺入动脉。

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the head neutral and auricle relaxed; palpate the superficial temporal artery before locating the point.坐位或仰卧，头正中，耳廓放松；先触诊颞浅动脉再定位。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    At the posterior margin of the temple hairline, anterior to the auricular root and posterior to the superficial temporal artery. Find the auricular root and hairline, then move behind the arterial pulse.在鬓发后缘、耳廓根前方，颞浅动脉搏动后方；先找到耳廓根和发际，再向后避开动脉搏动。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently palpate anterior to the auricular root to identify the superficial temporal pulse; TE22 lies posterior to it. The auriculotemporal nerve is also superficial, with temporalis and temporal bone deeper. Never needle on the pulse or deeply into temporalis or bone.用指腹轻触耳廓根前方以辨认颞浅动脉搏动；耳和髎位于搏动后方，浅层另有耳颞神经，深面为颞肌和颞骨。不可在搏动处进针，不向深部刺入颞肌或骨面。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Identify the superficial temporal pulse, then move posteriorly to the shallow hollow where the posterior temple hairline meets the anterior auricular root. If the pulse remains under the fingertip, the vessel has not been avoided.先确认颞浅动脉搏动，再将指腹向后移至鬓发后缘与耳根前方交界的浅凹；若搏动仍在指下，尚未避开血管。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    After palpating the artery anterior to the auricular root, move about a finger-width posteriorly to the temple-hairline margin. Finger width is only a rough guide; confirm the point is behind the artery and anterior to the ear root.沿耳廓根前方触诊动脉后，向后约一指宽到鬓发后缘取穴；指宽仅作粗略提示，最终按动脉后方与耳根前方复核。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting anteriorly onto the superficial temporal pulse can injure the artery and cause bleeding; drifting posteriorly reaches the auricular root or postauricular area. Head shape makes finger-width estimates unreliable, so palpate and avoid the artery first.偏前落在颞浅动脉搏动处可能刺伤血管并出血，偏后则到耳廓根或耳后区域；仅凭指宽可能随头型误差，必须先摸清动脉再避开。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Consider TE22 for tenderness at the temple-hairline margin anterior to the ear and posterior to the artery; never needle the pulse, and visual change or new severe headache needs urgent care.鬓发后缘、耳根前且动脉后方压痛时可考虑和髎；动脉搏动处不可刺，视力改变或新发剧烈头痛需急诊。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

May be a local adjunct for tinnitus with preauricular temporal discomfort behind the superficial temporal artery; postauricular tenderness is closer to TE17, and sudden hearing change needs urgent otologic care.耳前颞侧、颞浅动脉后方不适兼耳鸣时可作局部配穴；耳后压痛更近翳风，突发听力改变优先耳科急诊。

The Hand-Shaoyang point verse in Zhenjiu Dacheng places Heliao and Ermen among the ear-region points; this mnemonic does not provide detailed localization for TE22.《针灸大成》手少阳穴歌以和髎、耳门记耳部经穴次序；这条歌诀不提供和髎的精细定位。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Superficial temporal artery pain or injury颞浅动脉局部疼痛或损伤

Pulsatile pain, a local mass, or bleeding with pressure is not an acupoint issue; new temporal pain in an older adult with visual or jaw symptoms needs assessment for giant-cell arteritis.搏动性痛、局部肿块或按压出血不应按穴处理；老年新发颞痛伴视力或咀嚼异常需排除巨细胞动脉炎。

Temporalis myofascial pain颞肌筋膜痛

Temporal aching after chewing or clenching may reflect muscle overuse; condylar pain, dental pain, and headache red flags need separate assessment.咀嚼或緊咬牙后颞部酸痛可与肌肉过劳有关；耳前髁突痛、牙痛或头痛红旗需分别评估。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Immediate care is needed for a pulsatile temporal mass, persistent bleeding, visual change, jaw fatigue while chewing, or sudden severe headache.颞部搏动性包块、持续出血、视力变化、咀嚼时下颌疲劳或突发剧烈头痛，应立即就医。
🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is TE22 Ěrhéliáo?耳和髎（TE22）在哪里？

Side of head, at posterior margin of temple hairline, anterior to root of auricle and posterior to superficial temporal artery.头侧部，鬓发后缘、耳廓根前方，颞浅动脉后方。

How is TE22 needled?耳和髎（TE22）怎么刺？

Avoiding the artery, oblique or transverse; 0.3–0.5 cun.避开动脉，斜刺或平刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of TE22?耳和髎（TE22）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Triple Energizer and Gallbladder channels手少阳、足少阳交会穴

Main safety point at TE22?耳和髎（TE22）最要注意的安全问题？

The superficial temporal artery is immediately anterior; palpate and avoid its pulse, use only shallow oblique or transverse insertion, and never puncture the artery.颞浅动脉就在前方，先触诊避开搏动；只作浅斜刺或平刺，不可刺入动脉。
