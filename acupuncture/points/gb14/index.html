    Palpate the supraorbital margin and the frontal bone surface above the orbit first, have the patient gaze straight ahead, and locate the point directly above the pupil and 1 cun above the eyebrow; if the hairline is irregular, rely on the brow-to-hairline proportion and the vertical line through the pupil.先摸清眶上缘及眼眶上方额骨面，让患者平视前方，取瞳孔正上方、眉弓上缘向上 1 寸处；发际线不规则时以眉上比例与瞳孔垂线为准。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    GB14 sits on the forehead with cutaneous sensory and arterial scalp branches superficially and the epicranial aponeurosis and loose areolar tissue deeper; the supraorbital nerve and vessels run near the supraorbital margin below. Needle transversely along the forehead (downward or upward), and avoid deep insertion that could injure the supraorbital nerve and vessels or the underlying calvaria and orbital structures.阳白位于前额部，浅层有头皮感觉神经分支与动脉分支，深层为帽状腱膜及疏松结缔组织层；下方近眶上缘处有眶上神经、眶上血管走行。进针需沿额部平刺（向下或向上），避免深刺伤及眶上神经、眶上血管及下方的颅骨骨膜与眼眶结构。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Press lightly with the fingertip on the frontal bone directly above the pupil and 1 cun above the eyebrow, where mild soreness or distension is common; pressing heavily toward the orbital rim or near the supraorbital notch or foramen can provoke radiating sensation or tenderness along the supraorbital nerve.指腹在瞳孔直上、眉上 1 寸额骨表面轻按，局部常有轻微酸胀感；若向眶下缘重压或触及眶上切迹/孔附近，可能诱发眶上神经分布区放射感或局部压痛。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting medially toward the supraorbital notch or needling deeply toward the orbit: easily irritates or injures the supraorbital nerve and vessels, causing radiating forehead pain, numbness, or a subcutaneous hematoma.取位偏内侧靠近眶上切迹或误向眼眶方向深刺：易刺激或损伤眶上神经、眶上血管，引起前额放射痛、麻木或皮下血肿。
  • Drifting laterally or too high: easily misses the standard position directly above the pupil and 1 cun above the eyebrow, landing near the frontotemporal junction or higher scalp points.取位偏外侧或过高：容易偏离瞳孔直上、眉上 1 寸的标准定位，误入额颞交界或发际上方其他经穴区域。
  • Needling perpendicularly or at too steep an angle: soft tissue on the forehead is thin over the calvaria and near the orbit, so perpendicular insertion hits periosteum painfully or risks advancing toward the orbit.直刺或角度过陡：额部软组织较薄且下邻颅骨与眼眶，直刺易抵触骨膜引起剧痛，或误向眶内方向推进带来安全隐患。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Choose GB14 as a local point for frontal headache or supraorbital distending pain; it is not the primary point for migraine, occipital, or holocephalic headache, which typically pair with points such as GB20 or LI4.前额部头痛或伴眼眶上方胀痛时可选阳白作局部取穴；偏头痛或后枕、全头痛不以阳白为主，常与风池、合谷等穴配伍。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

GB14 can be used to regulate local channel qi in eye distension, red painful eyes, or eyelid heaviness, though acute severe eye pain or vision changes require medical evaluation first and distal pairing often includes LI4 or LR3.眼部胀痛、目赤肿痛或眼睑沉重感可配合阳白调理局部经气；急性剧烈眼痛或视力变化需先就医，且远端配穴常结合合谷、太冲等穴。

Facial palsy面瘫（口眼歪斜） ⚖️

GB14 may be considered when facial muscle dysfunction involves the forehead or periocular region, but facial palsy protocols primarily rely on Yangming channel point combinations and require syndrome differentiation based on stage and cause.面部表情肌功能异常涉及额部或眼周时可酌情参考阳白，但面瘫临床常以阳明经穴位为主穴组合，需结合病程与病因综合辨证。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Supraorbital neuralgia or localized frontal myofascial tension pain眶上神经痛或前额局部肌筋膜紧张痛

Presents as sharp, throbbing, or tight pain over the forehead or supraorbital rim, often linked to prolonged eye strain, forward head posture, or cold exposure; differentiate from herpes zoster prodrome, intracranial pathology, and acute eye disease, and seek medical care first if severe sudden headache, abrupt vision loss, or fever occurs.表现为前额或眶上缘区域刺痛、跳痛或发紧，常与长时间用眼、低头伏案或局部受凉有关；需与带状疱疹前驱痛、颅内病变及急性眼病鉴别，若出现剧烈突发头痛、视力骤降或发热需先就医。

Eyelid drooping or symptoms related to extraocular muscle fatigue眼睑下垂或眼肌疲劳相关症状

Mild eye strain or functional eyelid heaviness warrants rest and visual ergonomics adjustment, whereas progressive ptosis, diplopia, or anisocoria requires prompt medical evaluation to rule out oculomotor nerve palsy or myasthenia gravis.轻度视疲劳或功能性眼睑沉重需结合休息与用眼习惯调整；若出现进行性眼睑下垂、复视或瞳孔不等大，应先排查动眼神经麻痹或重症肌无力等神经科问题。

Soft-tissue contusion after blunt forehead trauma前额撞击伤后软组织挫伤

After a bump to the forehead causing local swelling and tenderness, assess for calvarial injury or concussion first; handle acute trauma cautiously so bleeding is not worsened or head injury missed.前额磕碰后局部肿胀压痛，先评估是否有颅骨损伤或脑震荡表现；急性外伤早期局部处理需谨慎，避免加重出血或漏诊颅脑损伤。

How is GB14 needled?阳白（GB14）怎么刺？

Transverse; 0.5–0.8 cun.平刺；0.5–0.8 寸。
