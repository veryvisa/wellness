Huángqí Jiànzhōng Tāng · Zhang Zhongjing, Jingui Yaolue (Eastern Han)东汉·张仲景《金匮要略》

Original text原文

「虛勞裏急、諸不足，黃耆建中湯主之」——金匮要略

Jingui Yaolue (Essentials from the Golden Cabinet)

In consumptive weakness with abdominal tightness and general insufficiency, this decoction governs.虚劳腹中拘急、各方面都不足的，用黄芪建中汤。

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Warm the middle and supplement qi温中补气
  • ease cramping和里缓急

Consumptive weakness with abdominal tightness: pain eased by pressure, short breath, sweating, fatigue and poor appetite.虚劳里急、诸不足：腹痛喜按、气短自汗、倦怠少食。

How the formula is built方解

Xiao Jian Zhong Tang plus astragalus: more qi support and a firmer surface, for those whose qi is clearly weak and who sweat easily. The source gives changes for breathlessness and fullness in the same passage.小建中汤加黄芪，补气固表的力量增强，适合虚寒之外气虚更明显、容易出汗的人。原书同条给出气短胸满、腹满的加减。

Cautions使用注意

Not for excess, damp-heat or deficiency fire; a sudden worsening of chest tightness or breathlessness needs heart and lung emergencies ruled out; it contains sugar, which matters in diabetes. This page is for study and is not a prescription; see a doctor first if symptoms are severe, persistent or getting worse.实证、湿热、阴虚火旺不宜；胸闷气短突然加重要先排除心肺急症；含饴糖，糖尿病患者注意。本页供学习，不是处方；症状重、持续或加重时先就医。

Exam notes考点

Exam points: adding astragalus aims at qi deficiency with sweating; contrast with Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang for sinking qi.考点：小建中汤加黄芪，偏于气虚自汗；与补中益气汤（气虚下陷）区别。
