Stroke (sequelae)中风

Sudden stroke symptoms are a race against time: call 911, and do not needle, bleed, or give food, drink or medicine by mouth. This page is about the recovery and sequelae stages after medical stabilisation, and the pattern diagnosis examinations ask for. The textbook first separates channel stroke (clear mind) from zang-fu stroke (disturbed consciousness), and the latter into closed and collapse patterns. All channel patterns share one main set — GV26, PC6, SP6, HT1, LU5 and BL40, the "awaken the brain, open the orifices" set — with additions for liver yang, wind-phlegm, phlegm-heat, qi deficiency or yin deficiency, then local points for where the deficit is (arm, leg, mouth, speech, swallowing); these are in the comparison table.突然出现的中风症状是分秒必争的急症：立即拨 911，不针刺、不放血、不喂水喂药。本页讲的是医疗稳定之后的恢复期和后遗症期，以及考试要求的辨证。教材先分中经络（神志清楚）与中脏腑（神志障碍），中脏腑再分闭证与脱证。中经络各型主穴相同：水沟、内关、三阴交、极泉、尺泽、委中，即「醒脑开窍」法的一组，按肝阳、风痰、痰热、气虚、阴虚加配穴，再按瘫在哪里（上肢、下肢、口角、语言、吞咽）加局部穴；这些对症加穴都列在比较表里。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Sudden facial drooping, one-sided arm or leg weakness or numbness, slurred speech or difficulty understanding speech requires an immediate 911 call, even if symptoms resolve within minutes. Record the exact last-known-well time; do not drive yourself to hospital. Do not take aspirin on your own before hospital: it worsens a haemorrhagic stroke.突然出现一侧面部下垂、单侧手臂或腿无力/麻木、言语含糊或听不懂话，即使几分钟后恢复，也要立即拨打 911；记录最后一次正常的准确时间，不要自行开车去医院。到急诊前不要自行吃阿司匹林：出血性卒中会因此加重。
  • Sudden loss of vision in one or both eyes, double vision, severe dizziness with inability to walk, difficulty swallowing or loss of coordination, developing over minutes to hours, requires 911. Consider posterior-circulation stroke even without typical FAST signs.突然出现单眼或双眼视力丧失、复视、严重眩晕伴走路不稳、吞咽困难或协调障碍，尤其在数分钟至数小时内发生：立即拨打 911，警惕后循环卒中，不能因没有典型 FAST 表现而排除。
  • A sudden thunderclap headache, especially with vomiting, neck stiffness, altered consciousness or fainting, requires 911 to exclude intracranial or subarachnoid haemorrhage. Do not needle, bleed or wait for symptoms to resolve.突然发生雷击样剧烈头痛，尤其伴呕吐、颈项强直、意识改变或晕厥：立即拨打 911，排除脑出血或蛛网膜下腔出血；不得针刺、放血或等待症状自行缓解。
  • Sudden reduced consciousness, seizures, repeated vomiting, abnormal breathing or new or worsening limb weakness after stroke requires 911 because of possible cerebral oedema, haemorrhagic transformation, recurrent stroke or seizure.中风后突然意识下降、抽搐、反复呕吐、呼吸异常或新出现/加重的肢体无力：立即拨打 911，警惕脑水肿、出血转化、再卒中或癫痫发作。
  • Coughing or choking when swallowing, a wet voice, breathing difficulty after meals, or recurrent fever with sputum after stroke requires prompt swallowing assessment. Stop oral feeding pending assessment; call 911 for breathing difficulty, cyanosis or altered consciousness.中风后吞咽时呛咳、湿性嗓音、进食后呼吸困难，或反复发热、咳痰：暂停经口喂食并尽快接受吞咽评估；若出现呼吸困难、发绀或意识改变，立即拨打 911。

「偏枯，身偏不用而痛，言不變，志不亂，病在分腠之間」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.23: in hemiplegia one side of the body does not work and hurts, but speech is unchanged and the mind is clear — the illness lies in the muscles and interstices; the text goes on to say it is needled, "adding to what is insufficient and reducing what is excessive, and it can recover". Clear mind and a shallow site: the later division into channel and zang-fu stroke follows the same line, and the treatment is still to tonify deficiency and drain excess.《灵枢·热病》：偏枯的人半身不能用、又有疼痛，但说话没有改变、神志不乱，病在肌肉腠理之间；原文接着说用大针取之，「益其不足，损其有余，乃可复也」。这是「神志清楚、病位较浅」的描述，后世中经络与中脏腑的分法与此一脉相承；原文的治法仍是补虚泻实。

「營衛稍衰，則真氣去，邪氣獨留，發為偏枯」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.75: when pathogens sink into one half of the body and the nutritive and defensive qi weaken, true qi leaves, the pathogen remains alone and hemiplegia results — putting weakened upright qi at the centre, a classical basis for tonifying in qi deficiency with blood stasis.《灵枢·刺节真邪》：邪气侵入半身较深，营卫逐渐衰弱，真气离去，邪气独留，就成了偏枯。这里把「正气虚」放在发病的关键位置，是气虚血瘀型扶正的古籍依据之一。

「凡初中風跌倒，卒暴昏沉，痰涎壅滯，不省人事，牙關緊閉，藥水不下，急以三稜針，刺手十指十二井穴」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu Dacheng, book 10, on first aid for sudden stroke: for the person who falls unconscious with phlegm and clenched teeth, prick the twelve well points with a three-edged needle — LU11, LI1, PC9, TE1, HT9 and SI1 on both sides. This is the source of the twelve well points in the textbook's closed pattern. Today the same patient needs 911 and an open airway at once; bloodletting must never delay clot-dissolving or clot-removing treatment.《针灸大成》卷十「初中风急救针法」：刚中风跌倒、昏沉、痰涎壅盛、不省人事、牙关紧闭，急用三棱针刺十二井穴（少商、商阳、中冲、关冲、少冲、少泽，左右共十二）。这就是教材闭证用十二井的出处。在今天，这样的病人要做的是立即拨 911、保持气道通畅，放血绝不能耽误溶栓和取栓。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Modern medicine classifies stroke mainly into ischaemic and haemorrhagic stroke. Ischaemic stroke results from arterial thrombosis or embolism that deprives brain tissue of blood supply. Haemorrhagic stroke includes intracerebral and subarachnoid haemorrhage and may involve hypertension, vascular malformations or aneurysms. A transient ischaemic attack (TIA) can produce similar focal neurological symptoms; even when symptoms resolve completely, it requires urgent assessment. Common manifestations include unilateral weakness or numbness, facial palsy, aphasia or dysarthria, visual disturbance, ataxia and altered consciousness. Acute management requires immediate activation of the stroke emergency pathway, brain CT or MRI, blood glucose and blood-pressure assessment, and vascular imaging when indicated to distinguish ischaemia from haemorrhage. Some patients with ischaemic stroke qualify for intravenous thrombolysis or endovascular thrombectomy, subject to strict time windows and imaging criteria. Acupuncture cannot replace these treatments or delay transport. Post-stroke rehabilitation is coordinated by a stroke team and may include physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, swallowing assessment, nutrition management, falls prevention and secondary prevention. Acupuncture may be considered as an adjunct after appropriate assessment, but cannot promise neurological recovery or replace evidence-based rehabilitation. TCM patterns do not correspond one-to-one with modern diagnoses. Liver yang rising may coexist with hypertension or emotional stress but cannot determine stroke subtype. Wind-phlegm obstruction may accompany swallowing difficulties, motor impairment or phlegm-damp features. Qi deficiency with blood stasis and yin deficiency generating wind describe constitutional and symptomatic combinations during recovery; they are not equivalent to ischaemic stroke, chronic cerebral hypoperfusion or a particular imaging finding. Stroke severity, lesion location, pathological subtype, muscle tone, cognition, swallowing and cardiovascular risk must be assessed independently. Before acupuncture, confirm clinical stability and review anticoagulants, antiplatelet medication, bleeding risk, skin condition, sensation and communication ability. Patients with shoulder subluxation, spasticity, osteoporosis or sensory loss require particular care to avoid inappropriate traction, excessive stimulation and deep needling. Acupuncture must be coordinated with an individualised rehabilitation plan and must not replace repetitive, task-oriented motor training.现代医学中的中风（stroke）主要分为缺血性脑卒中和出血性脑卒中。缺血性卒中通常由脑动脉血栓形成或栓塞造成脑组织缺血；出血性卒中包括脑实质出血和蛛网膜下腔出血，常涉及高血压、脑血管畸形、动脉瘤等因素。短暂性脑缺血发作（TIA）可出现类似卒中的局灶性神经症状，即使症状完全消失，也属于必须紧急评估的警告事件。常见表现包括单侧肢体无力或麻木、面瘫、失语或构音障碍、视力异常、共济失调及意识改变。急性期须立即启动卒中急救流程，进行脑部 CT 或 MRI、血糖、血压及必要的血管影像检查，区分缺血与出血。部分缺血性卒中患者符合静脉溶栓或血管内取栓条件，治疗存在严格时间窗及影像学筛选要求；针灸不能替代这些治疗，也不能延误转运。中风后的康复应由卒中团队制定，包括物理治疗、作业治疗、言语语言治疗、吞咽评估、营养管理、跌倒预防及二级预防。针灸可作为经过评估后的辅助干预，但不能承诺恢复神经功能或替代循证康复。中医证型与现代病因并非一一对应：肝阳暴亢可能与部分高血压、情绪激动等背景同时出现，但不能据此判断卒中类型；风痰阻络可能伴随吞咽、肢体运动障碍或痰湿表现；气虚血瘀、阴虚风动常用于描述恢复期的体质和症状组合，但不能分别等同于缺血性卒中、慢性脑供血不足或某一种影像学改变。现代卒中严重程度、病灶位置、病理类型、肌张力、认知、吞咽及心血管风险，均须独立评估。临床针灸前应确认患者生命体征和病情稳定，了解抗凝药、抗血小板药及出血风险，检查皮肤、感觉和沟通能力；对肩关节半脱位、痉挛、骨质疏松及感觉缺失者，须避免不当牵拉、过强刺激和深刺。针灸应与个体化康复计划协调，不能取代重复性、任务导向的运动训练。
