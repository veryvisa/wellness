Liuzijue (Six Healing Sounds)六字诀

Form 3: Hu (the spleen sound)第3式 · 呼字诀

Closing in and opening out is one round; 6 exhalations of hu in all合拢、展开为一次，共吐「呼」6 次

Hū zì jué

Qigong养生功法 › Liuzijue (Six Healing Sounds)六字诀 › Form 3: Hu (the spleen sound)第3式 · 呼字诀

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • With knee pain: bend the knees only slightly, or sit膝盖痛：只微微屈膝，或坐着做
  • After recent abdominal surgery or with a hernia: do not push from the belly, keep the breath light, and check with your doctor first腹部近期做过手术或有疝气：不要用腹部发力，吐气放轻，先问医生
  • If you feel dizzy or your hands tingle while breathing out: stop, breathe naturally, and make the sound softer吐气时头晕或手发麻：马上停下，自然呼吸，声音放轻

🗣 How to say it怎么念

hū IPA [xu]

Roughly 'hoo', with the same throaty h as the he sound, lips rounded as if about to cool a spoonful of hot soup.念「呼」，嘴唇撮圆，像吹凉一口热汤之前那一声「hū——」。

👄 Mouth shape口型

  • Lips and teeth apart嘴唇和牙齿张开
  • Lips rounded and pushed slightly forward, the lips themselves relaxed嘴唇撮圆、向前伸，嘴唇本身放松
  • The sides of the tongue curled upward舌头两边向上卷

✔ The air forms a single stream down the middle of the mouth and out through the rounded lips; a hand held in front of the mouth feels one warm, focused stream.气从口腔中间成一股，从撮圆的嘴唇出去；手放在嘴前能感到一股集中的暖气。

🔊 Aloud读出声

Beginners practise aloud: say one long 'hu' as the hands open outward, loud at first and then quieter.初学出声练：两掌向外展开时念「呼——」，一次一声，先大声、后小声。

🤫 Mouth shape only, no voice只做口型、不出声

Once it is familiar, keep the mouth shape only, no voice: the lips stay rounded and the air leaves gently through the middle.熟练后只做口型、不出声：嘴唇保持撮圆，只让气流从中间轻轻出去。

🧘 The movement动作

  1. Carrying on from the last form: turn the palms to face the navel, fingertips angled toward each other, fingers loosely spread; the space between the hands equals the distance from the palms to the navel接上一式：两掌转为掌心向内对着肚脐，指尖斜相对，五指自然张开；两掌之间的距离和掌心到肚脐的距离相等
  2. Slowly straighten the knees and draw the hands in toward the navel to about 10 centimetres away, breathing in naturally两膝慢慢伸直，两掌慢慢向肚脐合拢，到肚脐前约 10 厘米，自然吸气
  3. Bend the knees slightly and breathe out hu as the hands open outward, back to the same spacing, the arms making a circle微微屈膝，同时吐「呼」，两掌向外展开，回到两掌间距与掌心到肚脐距离相等，两臂成圆
  4. Slowly straighten the knees and draw the hands in again to begin the next round两膝慢慢伸直，两掌再向肚脐合拢，开始下一次

🌬 Breathe in through the nose and breathe out slowly through the mouth as you make the sound; when it is done, breathe in naturally. Never hold the breath or force it longer. Beginners can simply breathe naturally and get the mouth shape right first.吸气用鼻子，吐字时用嘴慢慢呼气；吐完自然吸气，不憋气，也不硬把气拉长。初学完全可以自然呼吸，先把口型做对。

✅ Key points要领

  • Open and close slowly, as if holding a ball in front of the belly that grows and shrinks开合要慢，像在腹前抱一个会变大变小的球
  • Breathe out the sound as the hands open吐字与两掌外开同时
  • Shoulders and elbows soft, the arms always rounded肩、肘放松，两臂始终圆

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Tightening the lips and blowing hard嘴唇绷紧用力吹
  • Opening the hands too wide, away from the belly两掌开合太大，离开了腹前
  • Pushing the belly out with effort腹部用力往外顶

🪑 Easier version简化做法

Seated: skip the knee bend and only open and close the hands; they can rest low, just above the thighs.坐着做：不屈膝，只做两掌开合；手也可以放低，搭在大腿上方开合。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 Tradition pairs hu with the spleen, in every season; the hands open and close in front of the belly because tradition places the spleen and stomach in the middle of the body. These are traditional ideas, not research findings.传统把呼配脾，四季皆可；两掌在腹前开合，传统教法说脾胃在中焦，所以手在腹前。这些是传统说法，不是研究结论。
