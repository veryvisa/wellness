  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: below the T7 spine.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），膈俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 7 胸椎棘突下定。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Identify the C7 spinous process (vertebra prominens, prominent and moving during neck flexion/rotation) and count down to below the T7 spinous process; the line connecting the inferior angles of the scapulae approximates the T7 level as a quick guide, though posture and build shift scapular position so spinous counting must confirm it.先摸清第 7 颈椎棘突（大椎，低头转动最明显且较长），向下计数至第 7 胸椎棘突下；临床亦常以两侧肩胛骨下角连线约平第 7 胸椎棘突作快速参考，但体型与体位会改变肩胛骨位置，须以椎骨计数核对。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure 1.5 cun laterally from the posterior midline below the T7 spinous process (roughly the width of index and middle fingers held together, or 1.5 times the patient's thumb width). Finger widths are a rough estimate and must be checked against vertebral offset and local tenderness.在后正中线第 7 胸椎棘突下，向患侧或健侧旁开 1.5 寸（约食指、中指并拢横指的宽度，或患者拇指同身寸 1.5 倍）。指寸仅作粗估，须结合椎体旁开距离与局部压痛核实。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Skin and subcutaneous tissue contain posterior cutaneous branches of thoracic spinal nerves and dorsal branch vessels; deeper layers pass through trapezius, rhomboids or erector spinae, then intercostal muscles, the corresponding rib, and the intercostal neurovascular bundle along the costal groove, with parietal pleura, pleural cavity, and lung deepest. Insert obliquely toward the spine only, and never insert deeply or laterally through an intercostal space where pleura and lung can be punctured.皮肤与皮下组织含胸脊神经后支皮支及背侧支血管；深层依次为斜方肌、菱形肌或竖脊肌，再深为肋间肌、相应肋骨及沿肋沟走行的肋间神经血管束，最深处为壁层胸膜、胸膜腔与肺。针刺方向必须向脊柱方向斜刺，绝不可向外侧或深部直刺穿入肋间隙伤及胸膜与肺。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently with the fingertip 1.5 cun lateral to the T7 spinous process to feel superficial tension or tenderness in erector spinae or rhomboids; if pressing into an intercostal space causes a sudden drop in resistance or deep aching, stop pressing immediately.在 T7 棘突下旁开约 1.5 寸处用指腹轻按揉拨，常可触及竖脊肌或菱形肌浅层束状紧张或压痛；若向肋间隙深压，局部阻力突然减小且可能诱发深部酸胀或肋间不适，须立即停止加压。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Treating the inferior scapular angle line as an exact T7 landmark without counting spinous processes: arm elevation, slouching, or body habitus can shift the point by one or two levels, causing inaccurate localization and adding pneumothorax risk if inserted deeply or laterally.误将肩胛骨下角连线绝对等同于 T7 棘突下而不数椎骨：抬臂、含胸或体型差异会使穴位偏高或偏低 1–2 个椎节，导致定位失准与配穴偏差；深部直刺或外刺还会增加穿入胸膜腔引发气胸的风险。
  • Angling laterally away from the spine or inserting deeply perpendicular beyond 0.5–0.8 cun: the needle tip can pass ribs or enter an intercostal space and injure the intercostal neurovascular bundle, parietal pleura, and lung, risking pneumothorax, hemothorax, or bleeding with dyspnea and chest pain.向脊柱外侧或垂直深刺（超过 0.5–0.8 寸）：针尖易越过肋骨或进入肋间隙刺伤肋间血管神经、壁层胸膜与肺组织，可致气胸、血胸或局部出血，严重者出现呼吸困难、胸痛与休克征象。
  • Miscounting thoracic spinous processes (such as taking T6 or T8 for T7): unclear cervicothoracic palpation shifts back-shu levels and reduces clinical precision for blood-influential and organ-shu selection.椎节计数错位（如将 T6 或 T8 棘突下误作 T7）：常因颈胸交界辨认不清导致整组背俞穴错位，影响血会与脏腑俞穴的临床针对性。

Period pain痛经

Where is BL17 Géshū?膈俞（BL17）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of T7, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 7 胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL17 needled?膈俞（BL17）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of BL17?膈俞（BL17）是哪类特定穴？

Influential point of blood八会穴之血会

Main safety point at BL17?膈俞（BL17）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only oblique insertion toward the spine, never a deep perpendicular or lateral direction.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向的斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
