🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SP12 Chōngmén?冲门（SP12）在哪里？

In the groin region, at the inguinal crease, lateral to the pulsation of the external iliac (femoral) artery.腹股沟区，腹股沟斜纹中，髂外动脉搏动处的外侧。

How is SP12 needled?冲门（SP12）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, avoiding the artery; 0.5–1 cun.直刺，避开动脉；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of SP12?冲门（SP12）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Spleen and Liver channels足太阴、足厥阴经交会穴

Main safety point at SP12?冲门（SP12）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. The femoral artery is medial and the femoral nerve beneath: palpate the pulse, insert perpendicularly lateral to it, never angle medially, go deep or lift-thrust, and withdraw at electric tingling down the front of the thigh.孕期禁针。股动脉在内侧、股神经在穴下：先摸清搏动，针在其外侧直刺，不向内侧斜刺、不深刺不提插；出现向大腿前面放射的电麻感即退针。
