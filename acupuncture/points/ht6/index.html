  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Elbow crease (level with the olecranon) to distal wrist crease = 12 cun. HT6 lies 0.5 cun above the wrist crease: one twenty-fourth of the way from wrist to elbow.肘横纹（平尺骨鹰嘴）到腕掌（背）侧远端横纹折作 12 寸。阴郄在腕横纹上 0.5 寸，即从腕横纹往肘侧量 1/24 段。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    The width of the patient's thumb at the interphalangeal joint is about 1 cun; HT6 is only half a thumb-cun (about 0.5 cun) above the wrist crease.   3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Near the ulnar end of the distal wrist crease, palpate the cord-like flexor carpi ulnaris tendon and the pisiform bone distally.     Anatomy解剖结构

    Have the patient flex the wrist toward the palm and ulnar side so flexor carpi ulnaris stands out; HT6 is at its radial edge, 0.5 cun above the wrist crease. Where is HT6 Yīnxì?阴郄（HT6）在哪里？

On the anterior forearm, 0.5 cun above the distal palmar wrist crease, radial to the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris.前臂前区，腕掌侧远端横纹上 0.5 寸，尺侧腕屈肌腱的桡侧缘。

How is HT6 needled?阴郄（HT6）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺；0.3–0.5 寸。

Special-point roles of HT6?阴郄（HT6）是哪类特定穴？

Xi-cleft point郄穴

Main safety point at HT6?阴郄（HT6）最要注意的安全问题？

The ulnar artery and nerve are shallow here: feel the pulse, insert shallowly away from it, withdraw for electric tingling, and press after withdrawal to prevent a haematoma.尺动脉、尺神经在此很浅：先摸清搏动，避开后浅刺；出现电麻感即退针，出针后按压防血肿。
