彧中 Yùzhōng

Elegant Centre · High risk高危

Point of the Kidney channel足少阴肾经穴
⚠️ Pneumothorax-risk point气胸风险穴 The pleura and lung apex lie directly beneath Yùzhōng, very shallow in thin people. Use only 0.5–0.8 cun oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space; never perpendicular or deep, never medially. Chest pain, breathlessness, worsening pain on breathing in or persistent cough after needling needs urgent care (in BC call 911).彧中深面就是胸膜和肺尖，胸壁薄者尤其浅。只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺 0.5–0.8 寸，不可直刺、深刺，不可向内侧进针。针后胸痛、气短、吸气痛加重或持续咳嗽，立即就医（BC 拨 911）。

📍 Location定位★

On the chest, in the 1st intercostal space, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 1 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

💡 The space above the 2nd rib is the 1st intercostal space: go one space up from the sternal angle (level of the 2nd rib), then 2 cun lateral to the midline.第 2 肋上方的肋间隙就是第 1 肋间隙：从胸骨角（平第 2 肋）向上一格，再从前正中线向外量 2 寸。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Oblique or transverse, laterally along the intercostal space斜刺或平刺（沿肋间隙向外） · 0.5–0.8 cun0.5–0.8 寸

Never perpendicular or deep, to avoid the heart and lungs.不可直刺、深刺，以免伤及心、肺。

🛡 Safety安全

The pleura and lung lie directly beneath the intercostal muscles: oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space only, never perpendicular or deep; do not insert hugging the lower border of the rib above (intercostal vessels and nerve).肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺：只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺，不直刺、不深刺；不贴上位肋骨下缘进针（肋间血管神经）。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Cough, wheezing, phlegm obstruction咳嗽、气喘、痰壅 ⚖️Chest and hypochondriac fullness胸胁胀满

    For 2 cun lateral to the midline use the patient's index, middle and ring fingers together (about 2 cun), or half the distance to the midclavicular line (4 cun); the vertical level comes only from counting intercostal spaces, never from finger measurement.前正中线旁开 2 寸可用患者食指、中指、无名指三指并拢（约 2 寸）自前正中线向外量取，或取锁骨中线（距中线 4 寸）的一半；纵向高度只看肋间隙，不靠指量。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located in the first intercostal space, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline. Superficial layers are skin, subcutaneous tissue (with superficial thoracic veins), pectoralis major, and the first intercostal muscles; deep to it lie the parietal pleura and lung apex, with subclavian vessels and intercostal vessels superolaterally. Never insert deeply or probe beneath the clavicle or superolaterally.位于第 1 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 2 寸。浅层依次为皮肤、皮下组织（含胸壁浅静脉）、胸大肌及第 1 肋间肌；深面紧邻壁层胸膜与肺尖，外上方还邻近锁骨下动静脉及肋间血管。进针严禁深刺，也严禁向锁骨下方或外上方探针。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a fingertip gently along the first intercostal space at 2 cun lateral to the midline to feel the soft-tissue gap between the adjacent rib borders, often tender or distended; keep palpation and needling gentle without pressing hard inward.指腹沿第 1 肋间隙在旁开 2 寸处轻柔滑动，可触及上下肋骨缘之间的软组织间隙，局部常有酸胀感；触诊与行针均须轻柔，避免重压胸壁或向深处顶撞。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Dropping into the second or a lower intercostal space: mistaking the second rib at the sternal angle for the first rib shifts the point downward, altering local layer correspondence while still carrying pneumothorax risk if needled deeply.误入第 2 肋间隙或更低肋间隙：把胸骨角平对的第 2 肋骨误认为第 1 肋骨，导致定位下移；虽仍在前胸壁，但主治与局部层次偏离，且低位肋间仍需防气胸。
  • Drifting medially toward the sternal border or laterally off the 2-cun line: medial drift approaches costal cartilages or internal thoracic branches, while lateral drift nears the infraclavicular region and outer pectoralis major, raising vascular or nerve-branch risk.向内侧偏近胸骨缘或向外侧偏离 2 寸线：内偏易抵触肋软骨或胸廓内血管分支，外偏靠近锁骨下区及胸大肌外侧部，增加血管或神经分支刺激风险。
  • Perpendicular insertion, deep insertion, or superolateral angulation: the pleura and lung apex lie deep and subclavian vessels lie superolateral, so deep or superolateral needling risks pneumothorax or vascular injury; textbooks specify lateral oblique or transverse insertion of 0.5–0.8 cun.直刺、深刺或向外上方斜刺：深面为胸膜与肺尖，外上方邻锁骨下血管，直刺或深刺极易引发气胸（pneumothorax）或血管损伤出血，教材为斜刺或平刺 0.5–0.8 寸。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Cough咳嗽 ⚖️

Useful when cough is accompanied by chest tightness, wheezing, or anterior chest fullness; for early external wind cough with headache or sore throat, points like LU7 and LI4 are typically prioritized, while chronic deficient cough often pairs BL13 and LU9.咳嗽兼见胸闷、气喘或前胸胀满时可参用；外感风寒风热初起伴头痛咽痒多先选列缺、合谷等宣散解表穴，久咳虚损则常配肺俞、太渊。

Wheezing and asthma哮喘 ⚖️

Applicable as a local chest-loosening point when wheezing involves chest tightness or upward qi counterflow; severe acute wheezing is not managed by acupuncture alone and is typically paired with EX-B1, BL13, CV17, and standard medical care.哮喘或气喘见胸闷、气逆喘促时可作局部宽胸理气配穴；急性发作重度喘憋不选单靠针灸处理，临床常与定喘、肺俞、膻中及规范平喘治疗相配合。

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

Used only adjunctively if palpitations co-occur with chest tightness and qi stagnation, not as a primary point for palpitations; PC6, HT7, and BL15 are more typical choices after ruling out arrhythmia or structural heart disease.仅在心悸兼见胸闷、气机不畅时作辅助参用，并非治疗心悸的首选主穴；心悸常选内关、神门、心俞，且须先排查心律失常或器质性心脏病。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Chest wall myofascial pain or intercostal neuralgia胸壁肌筋膜痛或肋间神经痛

Presents as localized anterior chest ache or sharp/shooting pain along an intercostal space that may worsen with deep breath or cough; sudden chest pain with dyspnea requires ruling out pneumothorax, coronary disease, or pulmonary embolism first.表现为前胸壁局部酸痛或沿肋间隙走行的刺痛、窜痛，深呼吸或咳嗽时可加重；若突发胸痛伴呼吸困难，须先排除气胸、冠心病或肺栓塞等急症后再行针灸评估。

Chest tightness, cough, or wheezing related to bronchitis or asthma支气管炎或哮喘相关的胸闷、咳嗽、气喘

Often accompanied by wheezing, sputum, or exertional shortness of breath as an adjunctive care area; acute respiratory distress, hypoxemia, or unrelieved asthma attacks require immediate medical care.常伴喘息、痰鸣或活动后气促，可作为综合调理的辅助方向；出现明显呼吸窘迫、血氧下降或哮喘急性发作未缓解时，应立即按医疗规范处置，不可延误就医。

Infraclavicular or thoracic outlet discomfort锁骨下或胸廓出口区不适

Aching or numbness near the shoulder-chest junction during arm elevation or traction requires differentiating vascular compression, cervical spine issues, and shoulder pathology without deep needling.上肢抬举或牵拉时出现肩胸交界处酸胀或麻木，须与血管压迫、颈椎病变及肩关节病变鉴别，切勿在该区盲目深刺。

Where is KI26 Yùzhōng?彧中（KI26）在哪里？

On the chest, in the 1st intercostal space, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.胸部，第 1 肋间隙，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

How is KI26 needled?彧中（KI26）怎么刺？

Oblique or transverse, laterally along the intercostal space; 0.5–0.8 cun.斜刺或平刺（沿肋间隙向外）；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of KI26?彧中（KI26）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Kidney channel足少阴肾经穴

Main safety point at KI26?彧中（KI26）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung lie directly beneath the intercostal muscles: oblique or transverse insertion laterally along the intercostal space only, never perpendicular or deep; do not insert hugging the lower border of the rib above (intercostal vessels and nerve).肋间肌深面就是胸膜和肺：只沿肋间隙向外斜刺或平刺，不直刺、不深刺；不贴上位肋骨下缘进针（肋间血管神经）。
