📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Supine with the legs extended or slightly flexed to relax the abdominal wall and expose the upper abdomen.仰卧位，双下肢自然伸直或微屈使腹壁放松，充分暴露上腹部。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Xiphisternal junction to the center of the umbilicus = 8 cun. SP16 is 3 cun above the umbilicus (3/8 of the way up toward the xiphisternal junction) and 4 cun lateral to the anterior midline, aligned with the midclavicular (nipple) line.胸剑联合中点（歧骨）到脐中折作 8 寸。腹哀在脐中上 3 寸（即脐中至胸剑联合下 3/8 处），前正中线旁开 4 寸。横向 4 寸可参考前正中线至乳头线（或锁骨中线）折作 4 寸。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Measure 3 cun above the umbilicus using the breadth of the patient's four fingers held together, then measure 4 cun laterally from that midline point (one four-finger breadth plus one thumb-width). In patients with abdominal obesity or laxity, verify against the midclavicular line.先定脐中上 3 寸（患者食指、中指、无名指、小指四指并拢一横掌宽，即「一夫法」），再由此水平线向外量一横掌（3 寸）加 1 拇指同身寸（1 寸）共 4 寸。肥胖或腹壁松弛者易偏，须结合乳头线校正。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate CV11 on the anterior midline (3 cun above the umbilicus), then drop a vertical line down from the midclavicular (nipple) line; SP16 lies where these two lines intersect just inferomedial to the costal margin. In narrow-chested individuals, it sits close to the lower costal border.先找到前正中线上的建里（脐中上 3 寸），再沿锁骨中线（男性约平乳头纵线）向下垂直引线，两线在肋弓内下方相交处即是腹哀。注意胸廓狭窄者该点贴近肋弓下缘。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located in the lateral abdominal wall lateral to the semilunar line (lateral border of rectus abdominis), with superficial abdominal vessels and lateral cutaneous branches of intercostal nerves superficially, and external oblique, internal oblique, transversus abdominis and peritoneum deeper. Two cun medially (2 cun from midline) lies ST22 inside the rectus sheath with the inferior epigastric vessels deep to rectus abdominis; superolaterally lies the costal arch. Deep to the peritoneum lie the stomach or transverse colon on the left and the left hepatic lobe or hepatic flexure on the right — avoid deep insertion and the costal cartilage.位于腹直肌外侧缘（半月线）外侧的侧腹肌群区，浅层有肋间神经外侧皮支及腹壁浅动静脉，深层依次穿过腹外斜肌、腹内斜肌、腹横肌及腹膜。内侧 2 寸处（旁开 2 寸）为腹直肌鞘内的足阳明胃经关门（ST22），腹壁下动脉在腹直肌深面走行；外上方紧邻肋弓，左侧深面可邻胃体或横结肠，右侧深面可邻肝左叶边缘或结肠肝曲，进针不可过深并需避开肋缘软骨。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently at the level 3 cun above the umbilicus from the lateral edge of rectus abdominis toward the midclavicular line; a subtle depression or taut muscular band is often felt inferomedial to the costal margin, frequently tender or guarded in patients with dyspepsia, bloating, or chronic abdominal pain.以指腹在脐上 3 寸水平由腹直肌外侧缘向锁骨中线轻按，在肋弓内下方肌肉较丰厚处可触及轻微凹陷或条索感；消化不良、胃肠胀气或慢性腹痛患者此处常有明显压痛或肌紧张。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Relying solely on finger-breadth measures in patients with thick abdominal adiposity: drifts medially onto ST22 (2 cun lateral) or laterally onto the costal margin, hitting costal cartilage and causing sharp pain.未用骨度折量、仅按「一横掌」在腹壁脂肪厚者取穴：易偏内成足阳明胃经关门（旁开 2 寸）或偏外至肋弓下缘，刺到肋软骨引起剧痛。
  • Going beyond the 1–1.5-cun ceiling in a thin wall, after a meal, with an enlarged liver or upper-abdominal adhesions: the needle can pass the peritoneum into the stomach, transverse colon, liver edge or gallbladder.腹壁薄弱、饱食后、肝大或有上腹部手术粘连时直刺过深（超过 1–1.5 寸的上限）：可能穿透腹膜刺伤胃壁、横结肠或肝下缘、胆囊，引发内出血或腹膜刺激征。

Select SP16 for upper-abdominal (3 cun above umbilicus, 4 cun lateral) deficiency-cold dull pain relieved by warmth and pressure with undigested food in stools; for periumbilical/lower abdominal pain or constipation, prefer SP15 and ST25, and rule out acute abdomen first.上腹部脐上 3 寸旁开 4 寸的虚寒隐痛、喜温喜按且兼见完谷不化时选腹哀；脐周及下腹寒痛或便秘首选脐旁的大横（SP15）与天枢（ST25），急性剧痛先排外急腹症。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

Use SP16 (often with moxibustion) for chronic diarrhea or dysentery driven by spleen deficiency-cold with undigested food and cold abdominal pain; for acute damp-heat diarrhea with tenesmus, lead with ST25 and ST37 instead.脾虚寒湿、食不化、腹中冷痛伴泄泻或久痢时选腹哀（常配合艾灸）；急性湿热暴泻、里急后重首选天枢（ST25）与上巨虚（ST37），不以腹哀为主。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

Add SP16 when vomiting and acid regurgitation accompany cold epigastric-abdominal pain from spleen-stomach deficiency-cold; for acute severe vomiting from rebellious stomach qi, lead with PC6, ST36, and CV12, using SP16 only as an adjunct.脾胃虚寒、饮食不化所致的脘腹冷痛兼呕吐反酸时可配用腹哀；胃气上逆剧烈呕吐首选内关（PC6）、足三里（ST36）与中脘（CV12），腹哀仅作辅助配穴。

How is SP16 needled?腹哀（SP16）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of SP16?腹哀（SP16）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Spleen channel and the Yin Linking vessel足太阴经与阴维脉交会穴

Main safety point at SP16?腹哀（SP16）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. The lower liver edge and gallbladder lie deep on the right and the stomach on the left: go shallower with an enlarged liver, after a full meal or in thin people, avoid wide lifting-thrusting, and do not needle unexplained acute abdominal pain.孕期禁针。深面右侧邻肝下缘与胆囊、左侧邻胃：肝大、饱食或腹壁薄者减浅，不大幅提插；急性腹痛原因未明不针。
