Form 17: Low stance and stand on one leg, right第17式 · 右下势独立

Yòu xiàshì dúlì

Qigong养生功法 › Tai chi (simplified 24-form)太极拳（简化二十四式） › Form 17: Low stance and stand on one leg, right第17式 · 右下势独立

✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

⚠ Deep squat深蹲 ⚠ Higher knee load膝部负荷较大 ⚠ One leg or tiptoe单腿或踮脚

  • Knee pain or knee osteoarthritis: start with the high version only, no low stance and no sliding out; lower it bit by bit only when your knees are not sore the next day. The full low stance is a deep one-legged squat, the heaviest load on the knee in the whole set; stand high膝盖痛、膝骨关节炎：先只做「不下势、只做高架独立」的做法，不蹲低、不仆步；练到第二天膝盖不酸痛，再一点点放低；完整的下势要单腿蹲很低，是整套里膝部负荷最大的一段
  • If you fall easily or your balance is poor: hold the back of a chair or a counter with one hand while you stand on one leg; the knee can stay low, or the toes can stay on the floor; ideally have someone beside you容易跌倒、平衡不好：独立时一手扶着椅背或台面，提起的膝盖低一点也行，脚尖点地不离地也行；旁边最好有人
  • High blood pressure or glaucoma: in the low stance the head drops with the body, so do only the high version; stop if you feel dizzy when you come up高血压、青光眼：下势时头会跟着往下低，有这两种情况的不做低的下势，只做高架；起身后头晕就停下
  • After a hip replacement, or with osteoporosis: no deep squat or slide-out, and no bending to reach the foot; if your surgeon gave you movement limits, follow them换过髋关节、骨质疏松：不做深蹲仆步，也不弯腰去够脚；有手术医生的动作限制，照医嘱

🧘 The movement动作

  1. From the left one-leg stand: set the right foot down in front of the left, on the ball of the foot; pivot on the ball of the left foot, turning the heel in, and turn the body left; the left hand swings back to shoulder height and becomes a hook, the right hand arcs with the turn to the front of the left shoulder, palm angled back; look at the hook接左独立式：右脚落在左脚前方，脚掌着地；以左脚掌为轴，脚跟向里转，身体随着左转；左手向后平举变勾手，右手随转体划到左肩前，掌心斜向后；眼看勾手
  2. Bend the left knee slowly and slide the right foot out to the right, sole brushing the floor, until the right leg is straight: a right low stance. High version: bend the left knee only about 30 degrees and stop after a short slide, no low squat左腿慢慢屈膝下蹲，右脚向右侧伸出，脚掌擦着地面向外，伸直成右仆步。高架做法：左膝只弯到约 30 度，右脚伸出去一小步就停，不蹲低
  3. The right hand moves down and threads forward along the inside of the right leg, the thumb side up右手向下，顺着右腿内侧向前穿出，虎口向上
  4. Pivot on the right heel to turn its toes out and swing the left toes in; shift the weight forward, bending the right knee and straightening the left leg as the body rises; the right hand keeps moving forward and up, while the left hook drops behind you, its fingertips turning up以右脚跟为轴，脚尖向外撇，左脚尖向里扣；重心前移，右腿屈膝前弓，左腿伸直，身体慢慢起来；右手继续向前、向上挑起，左勾手落到身后，勾尖转向上
  5. Lift the left knee slowly to stand on the right leg; the left hook opens and the palm rises forward and up past the outside of the left leg, palm facing right, fingers up, the left elbow above the left knee; the right hand lowers beside the right hip, palm down; the right knee stays slightly bent左腿慢慢屈膝提起，成右独立式；左勾手变掌，经左腿外侧向前、向上挑起，掌心向右、指尖向上，左肘和左膝上下相对；右手落到右胯旁，掌心向下；右腿微屈站稳

🌬 Breathe in as you go down, out as you rise and lift the hand; breathe naturally while you stand on one leg.下势时吸气，起身挑掌时呼气，独立定住时自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • At the start, as the right foot lands and the left heel turns, make sure both feet are settled before you go down开头右脚落地、左脚转脚跟的那一下，两脚都要先站稳再下势
  • As on the left side, your knees decide how low you go; the high version is fine下势高低按膝盖定，和左式一样，高架就好
  • Standing on one leg, keep the standing knee slightly bent and fix your eyes on a point ahead独立时支撑腿微屈，眼看前方一点

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Turning so fast that you squat before both feet are settled落脚转身太急，两脚没站稳就下蹲
  • Folding forward and looking at the floor to get low为了蹲低，上身前趴、低头看地
  • Locking the standing knee and swaying on one leg独立时支撑腿锁直、身体晃

🪑 Easier version简化做法

High version, no low stance: stand with the right foot ahead, move your weight slowly onto it, lift the left knee to a comfortable height as the left hand rises, hold for two or three seconds and lower. Chair version: do the same with the right hand on a chair back; the left toes can stay touching the floor throughout.不下势、只做高架独立：两脚前后站好，重心慢慢移到前面的右腿，左膝抬到舒服的高度，左手随着挑起，停两三秒再放下。扶椅版：右手扶椅背做同样的动作，左脚尖可以一直点地。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 The mirror of the left side, with a landing and turn at the start. That is how the set is put together, not a research finding.和左下势独立左右对称，开头多一个落脚转身。这是套路编排的说法，不是研究结论。
