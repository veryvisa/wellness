Headache头痛 ⚖️

Suitable for unilateral, retroauricular, or temporal headache with Shaoyang pathway features, though external headache often prioritizes LI4, GB20, or LU7 and internal headache requires differentiating liver yang, phlegm-turbidity, or blood deficiency rather than relying on GB12 alone.偏侧或耳后、颞部伴少阳经循行特点的头痛可选用，但外感头痛常优先合谷、风池、列缺等，内伤头痛需辨肝阳、痰浊或血虚，不单靠完骨。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️
