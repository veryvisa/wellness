Agastache Qi-Correcting Powder藿香正气散

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Release the exterior and transform damp解表化湿
  • regulate qi and harmonise the middle理气和中

Wind-cold outside with damp stuck inside: chills, fever, headache, a stuffy chest, vomiting, diarrhoea and a greasy white coating.外感风寒、内伤湿滞：恶寒发热、头痛、胸膈满闷、呕吐泄泻、苔白腻。

How the formula is built方解

Agastache treats outside and inside, perilla and dahurica release the surface, magnolia bark and areca peel relieve fullness, tangerine and pinellia clear damp, and atractylodes, poria and licorice support the spleen. Wang Ang quotes Wu Shou that it does not fit taiyang cold damage or weak qi. The bottled liquid often contains much alcohol.藿香为君表里兼治，苏芷发表，朴腹行气消满，陈夏化湿降逆，术苓甘益脾。汪昂引吴绶说太阳伤寒脉浮或元气虚者此方并不相干，不可乱用。市售藿香正气水常含大量酒精。

Cautions使用注意

Not with yin deficiency, damp-heat or heat-type vomiting; the alcoholic liquid must not be taken with cephalosporins or metronidazole (disulfiram reaction), and take care with children, pregnancy and driving; dehydration, bloody stool or high fever need a doctor. This page is for study and is not a prescription; see a doctor first if symptoms are severe, persistent or getting worse.阴虚、湿热、热证呕泻不宜；含酒精的藿香正气水禁与头孢类、甲硝唑同用（双硫仑样反应），儿童、孕妇、驾车者慎用；脱水、血便、高热须就医。本页供学习，不是处方；症状重、持续或加重时先就医。
