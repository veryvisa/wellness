    Have the patient look straight ahead to mark the pupil line, palpate the anterior hairline, and measure 1.5 cun posteriorly along the line above the pupil; if the hairline is indistinct, estimate it from the 3-cun span between the glabella and anterior hairline.先令患者向前平视定位瞳孔，再触及前发际线，向上引垂线至前发际后 1.5 寸处即为目窗；若前发际不明显，可先量眉间（印堂）至前发际正中为 3 寸推算发际线。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Situated in the frontoparietal scalp lateral to the midline, with cutaneous sensory nerves and frontal vessel branches (noting supraorbital nerve and frontal vessel branches) superficially, over the epicranial aponeurosis, loose areolar tissue, periosteum, and calvaria; insert transversely in the loose areolar plane while avoiding vessel branches and periosteum.位于额顶交界偏外侧头皮区，浅层有头皮感觉神经及额部血管分支（需注意眶上神经、额部血管分支走行），深层依次为帽状腱膜、疏松结缔组织层、颅骨骨膜与颅骨；进针宜沿帽状腱膜下疏松结缔组织层平刺，避开血管分支与骨膜。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently with the fingertip 1.5 cun behind the anterior hairline directly above the pupil over the smooth outer table of the calvaria; mild distension or tenderness may be felt without pressing hard against the bone.以指腹轻按头皮前发际后 1.5 寸、瞳孔直上处，颅骨外板表面较平缓，按压时常见轻度胀感或压痛，注意勿向颅骨表面用力顶压。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Taking GB15 in front or GB17 behind instead: GB15 is 0.5 cun into the hairline and GB17 is 1 cun behind GB16, so a forward or backward slip lands on the wrong point.误取到前方的头临泣（GB15）或后方的正营（GB17）：前发际后 0.5 寸为头临泣，目窗后 1 寸为正营，前后偏离即错位。
  • Shifting medially toward the frontal midline or laterally into thicker temporal tissue misses the landmark directly superior to the pupil.误向内侧偏近发际正中线或向外侧偏入颞部深层：内侧偏近额中线，外侧偏入颞肌较厚区域，均不符合瞳孔直上的定位标准。
  • Needling perpendicularly or too deeply risks pain, hematoma, or hitting the periosteum beneath the thin scalp layers; insert transversely along the scalp to 0.3–0.5 cun.直刺或深刺：头皮下即帽状腱膜、疏松结缔组织层与颅骨，直刺易致疼痛、血肿或触及骨膜，须按规范沿头皮平刺 0.3–0.5 寸。

👉 The front of the crown has thick tight scalp over the galea; a 0.5–0.8 cun thread stays off the periosteum.头顶前部头皮厚而紧，针下帽状腱膜，平刺 0.5–0.8 寸不触骨膜。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Suitable when headache has a lateral or frontoparietal distending quality along the Shaoyang pathway; avoid relying on a single point for sudden severe or organic headache before medical evaluation.偏于头侧、前额发际后方胀痛或少阳经循行头痛时可选用；突发剧痛或器质性头痛不首选单穴处理，需先明确病因。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Can be used as an adjunct when dizziness accompanies head fullness, visual haze, or Shaoyang qi stagnation; seek medical care first if accompanied by severe vomiting, diplopia, hearing loss, or ataxia.伴头胀、目眩或少阳气机不畅者可作辅助配穴；眩晕伴剧烈呕吐、复视、耳聋或共济失调时应先就医。

Red painful eyes目赤肿痛 ⚖️

Appropriate as a regional channel adjunct for mild eye strain or fullness after ruling out acute ocular disease; seek ophthalmic care first for acute redness, severe pain, or vision loss.偏于轻度视疲劳、目胀不适且经评估无急重眼病者可作局部循经配穴；急性红肿热痛或视力下降应先眼科就医。

Blocked nose and sinusitis鼻塞鼻渊 ⚖️

Can serve as a supportive point when mild nasal congestion coexists with frontal heaviness; seek medical care first for acute purulent sinusitis with fever or severe facial pain.伴前额头昏、鼻塞轻微者可作辅助取穴；急性化脓性鼻窦炎高热或剧烈颜面痛时应先就医。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「目窗，一名至營，在臨泣後一寸，足少陽、陽維之會，刺入三分，灸五壯」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing records GB16, also called Zhiying, one cun behind GB15 as a meeting point of the Gallbladder channel and Yang Wei vessel, with a classical depth of three fen and five cones of moxibustion.《针灸甲乙经》记载目窗别名至营，位于临泣后一寸，是足少阳胆经与阳维脉的交会穴，传统刺入三分、灸五壮，说明其具有通调少阳与阳维、疏风清头目的作用。

「頭痛，目窗及天沖、風池主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

The Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing lists GB16 for headache together with GB9 and GB20, reflecting regional treatment along the Shaoyang pathway.《针灸甲乙经》明确将目窗列为治疗头痛的主治穴之一，常与天冲、风池同用，体现头面少阳经脉循行部位同治的思路。

「目窗 在臨泣後寸半，灸五壯，針三分，三度刺，目大明」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Da Cheng notes GB16 at 1.5 cun behind GB15 and associates needling and moxibustion with clearer vision, reflecting traditional use for eye and head symptoms.《针灸大成》提及目窗位于临泣后寸半，施灸或浅刺后可使目视清明，体现了该穴用于眼目疾患与头目不适的传统经验。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Tension-type headache and migraine-related scalp discomfort紧张型头痛与偏头痛前驱或发作期头皮不适

Presents as tightening, pressure, or throbbing pain over the forehead, temple, or lateral crown, often with stress or poor sleep; sudden severe thunderclap headache or focal neurological signs require prompt medical evaluation.表现为前额、颞部或头顶侧方紧箍感、胀痛或搏动性痛，常伴睡眠不足或精神紧张；若突发剧烈雷击样头痛或伴神经体征，应先就医排查。

Digital eye strain and referred fronto-orbital discomfort视疲劳及眶周、额部牵涉性不适

Causes eye heaviness, visual fatigue, and aching near the frontal hairline after prolonged near work; differentiate from refractive error, dry eye, or elevated intraocular pressure, and seek medical care first for acute vision loss or severe eye pain.长时间近距离用眼后出现眼胀、视物易疲倦及前额发际附近酸胀；需先区分屈光不正、干眼或眼压升高，视力骤降或眼痛明显者先就医。

Superficial scalp soft-tissue tenderness or post-traumatic discomfort头皮浅表软组织压痛或局部外伤后不适

Local tenderness often reflects superficial fascial or cutaneous nerve irritation; after head impact, persistent dizziness, nausea, altered consciousness, or significant hematoma requires medical assessment first.局部压痛多为浅层筋膜或皮神经刺激所致；头部受撞击后若出现持续头晕、恶心、意识改变或局部明显血肿，须先就医评估。

🚩 See a doctor先就医

  • Persistent drowsiness, worsening headache, or unsteady gait after head trauma: seek medical evaluation first for concussion or intracranial bleeding头部外伤后出现持续嗜睡、头痛加重或步态不稳：先就医评估脑震荡或颅内出血
