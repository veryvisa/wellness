CV14

巨阙 Jùquè

Great Gateway · High risk高危

★Front-mu point of the Heart心募穴
⚠️ Left liver lobe and heart: downward oblique, never deep肝左叶与心：只向下斜刺、不深刺 The left liver lobe lies beneath, and an upward tip points at the diaphragm and heart: angle downward only and stay shallow, with extra care in hepatomegaly or cardiomegaly. 📍 Location定位★

On the upper abdomen, 6 cun above the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.上腹部，脐中上 6 寸，前正中线上。

💡 2 cun below the xiphisternal junction; 1 cun below CV15.剑胸结合下 2 寸；鸠尾下 1 寸。

Do not needle deep, to avoid the liver.不可深刺，以免伤及肝脏。

    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Surface landmark体表标志

    Measure six cun above the navel on the anterior midline, one cun below CV15; do not mistake the xiphoid tip itself for CV14.沿前正中线从脐向上量六寸，位于鸠尾下一寸；不要把剑突尖本身当成巨阙。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The stomach, left liver lobe and peritoneum lie deep to the abdominal wall, with the xiphoid above. Avoid upward or deep pressure, stay clear of lateral vessels and avoid deep procedures in pregnancy.腹壁深面邻胃、肝左叶及腹膜；剑突在其上方，避免朝上或深部按压，偏外还要避开腹壁血管，孕期不作深部操作。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently confirm six cun above the navel and its relation to the epigastrium, CV15 and CV12; a pulse or marked guarding calls for reassessment.轻触脐上六寸处，复核与心窝、鸠尾及中脘的相对位置；脉搏感或明显腹肌防御提示需重新判断。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Palpitations心悸 ⚖️

Use its Heart front-mu role for traditional palpitations with chest/epigastric discomfort; sudden palpitations with fainting, chest pain or breathlessness need cardiac evaluation.传统心悸、胸脘不适时按心募穴属性配用；突发心悸伴晕厥、胸痛或呼吸困难先排心律失常。

Stomach pain胃痛 ⚖️

Compare CV14 for subxiphoid pain with palpitations; CV12 is more fitting for central epigastric fullness or pain above the navel.心下剑突附近痛或伴心悸时比较巨阙；脐上中部胃脘胀痛优先中脘。

Vomiting呕吐 ⚖️

An adjunct for vomiting with subxiphoid discomfort or traditional chest/Heart patterns; persistent vomiting, blood or chest pain needs care.呕吐伴心下不适或传统心胸证候时辅助；持续呕吐、呕血或胸痛须就医。
