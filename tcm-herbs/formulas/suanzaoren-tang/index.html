Sour Jujube Seed Decoction酸枣仁汤

Suān Zǎo Rén Tāng · First recorded in Zhang Zhongjing's Jin Gui Yao Lue of the Han dynasty; the textual anchor is the chapter on blood impediment, consumptive disease, and related patterns.东汉·张仲景《金匮要略》；原文锚点取《金匮要略·血痹虚劳病脉证并治》

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Sour Jujube Seed Decoction酸枣仁汤

Original text原文

「虛勞虛煩不得眠，酸棗仁湯主之。」——金匮要略

Jingui Yaolue (Essentials from the Golden Cabinet)

Sour Jujube Seed Decoction is indicated for insomnia accompanied by vexation in consumptive deficiency.虚劳所致的虚烦、不得安眠，以酸枣仁汤为主方。

「酸棗仁二升甘草一兩知母二兩茯苓二兩芎藭二兩」——金匮要略

Jingui Yaolue (Essentials from the Golden Cabinet)

The formula contains two sheng of sour jujube seed, one liang of licorice, two liang each of Anemarrhena, poria, and Chuan Xiong. The historical name Qiong Qiong refers to Chuan Xiong.方由酸枣仁二升、甘草一两、知母二两、茯苓二两、芎藭二两组成。原文中的芎藭即今名川芎。

「上五味，以水八升，煮酸棗仁，得六升，內諸藥，煮取三升，分溫三服。」——金匮要略

Jingui Yaolue (Essentials from the Golden Cabinet)

The five ingredients are prepared by first decocting sour jujube seed in eight sheng of water until six sheng remain, then adding the other herbs and reducing the decoction to three sheng for three warm doses.五味药先煎酸枣仁，取六升药液后再加入其余药物，煎至三升，分三次温服。

Cautions使用注意

This formula is intended for the pattern of consumptive deficiency with vexation and insomnia, and should not be selected solely on the basis of insomnia symptoms. Patients with excess-pattern insomnia, phlegm-heat disturbance, or marked Yin deficiency with fire should receive individualized assessment by a qualified practitioner. Persistent or worsening insomnia, chest pain, breathing difficulty, altered consciousness, severe mood disturbance, or thoughts of self-harm require prompt medical attention. Suspected allergic reactions or other significant adverse effects warrant discontinuing unsupervised use and seeking medical assessment.本方适用于虚劳虚烦、不得眠的证候，不宜仅凭失眠症状自行判断使用。实证失眠、痰热内扰或明显阴虚火旺者，应由中医师辨证评估。若失眠持续或加重，或伴胸痛、呼吸困难、意识异常、严重情绪障碍及自伤念头，应及时就医。服用期间如出现过敏或其他明显不适，应停止自行继续使用并寻求医疗评估。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Zhi Mu, Chuan Xiong, and Gan Cao are classified as requiring pregnancy caution in this site's herb safety records. The formula therefore adopts the strictest applicable level, caution. Assessment by a qualified healthcare professional is recommended before use during pregnancy.方中知母、川芎、甘草在本站按孕期慎用管理。依据已有药档的孕期安全参照，本方从严采用 caution 级别；孕期使用前应由具备资质的医疗专业人员评估。
