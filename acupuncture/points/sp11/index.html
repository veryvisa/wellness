The great saphenous vein runs directly subcutaneously over SP11. Local redness, warmth, a tender cord-like induration, or sudden diffuse thigh/calf swelling suggests superficial thrombophlebitis or deep vein thrombosis (DVT); local needling and deep massage are strictly contraindicated, and immediate medical evaluation is required.箕门皮下即大隐静脉主干。若局部皮肤红热、条索状硬结压痛明显，或整个小腿/大腿突然肿胀发紧，需高度警惕血栓性浅静脉炎或深静脉血栓（DVT），严禁在局部针刺或重按，应立即转诊就医。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is SP11 Jīmén?箕门（SP11）在哪里？

On the medial thigh, at the junction of the upper third and lower two thirds of the line from the medial end of the patellar base to SP12, at the angle between adductor longus and sartorius, over the femoral artery.股前区，髌底内侧端与冲门的连线上 1/3 与下 2/3 交点，长收肌和缝匠肌交角的动脉搏动处。

How is SP11 needled?箕门（SP11）怎么刺？

Perpendicular, avoiding the artery; 0.5–1 cun.直刺，避开动脉；0.5–1 寸。

Main safety point at SP11?箕门（SP11）最要注意的安全问题？

The femoral artery and vein lie directly beneath: palpate the pulse, enter beside it, stay shallow without lifting-thrusting, and apply sustained pressure if the artery is punctured.股动静脉就在穴下：先摸清搏动，针从搏动旁避开进入，浅刺不提插；刺破动脉须持续压迫。
