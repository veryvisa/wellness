Where is BL15 Xīnshū?心俞（BL15）在哪里？

Below the spinous process of T5, 1.5 cun lateral to the posterior midline.第 5 胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开 1.5 寸。

How is BL15 needled?心俞（BL15）怎么刺？

Oblique toward the spine; 0.5–0.8 cun.向脊柱方向斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of BL15?心俞（BL15）是哪类特定穴？

Back-shu point of the Heart心之背俞穴

Main safety point at BL15?心俞（BL15）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleura and lung are reachable deep to this point, creating pneumothorax risk. Use only oblique insertion toward the spine, never a deep perpendicular or lateral direction.深部可及胸膜和肺，有气胸风险。只作向脊柱方向的斜刺，绝不向外侧或深部直刺。
