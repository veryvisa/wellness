📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Side-lying with the lateral foot up and ankle relaxed; expose the lateral calcaneus and malleolus.侧卧，足外侧朝上，踝关节放松；显露跟骨外侧面和外踝。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    From BL60 drop vertically onto the lateral calcaneus to the red-white skin border.从昆仑垂直向下到跟骨外侧，在足背与足底皮色交界（赤白肉际）处取仆参。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The point lies at the posterolateral calcaneal/plantar soft-tissue junction near terminal sural branches, the small saphenous vein and abductor digiti minimi. Palpate gently along the bone edge, avoid superficial veins and nerve-sensitive areas, and do not needle deeply toward the calcaneus.穴位于跟骨后外侧与足底外侧软组织交界，邻近腓肠神经末支、小隐静脉及小趾展肌；按骨缘轻触凹陷，避开浅静脉和麻电敏感区，不向跟骨深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Slide a fingertip down the lateral calcaneal edge to find the shallow hollow between bone and plantar soft tissue. Stop and release if a cord-like vein is felt or pressure sends numbness toward the little toe.指腹沿跟骨外侧缘向下滑，寻找骨缘与足底软组织之间的浅凹；若触到条索状静脉或压迫出现向小趾放射的麻感，停止并移开。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    From BL60 go down onto the calcaneus at the skin-colour border; BL62 is directly under the malleolus, GB40 anteroinferior.从昆仑往下摸到跟骨外侧，在皮色交界处；外踝正下方是申脉，前下方是丘墟。
