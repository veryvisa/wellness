Oedema水肿

Point selection for oedema is not simply a matter of needling where the swelling appears. The key is why fluid is retained: impaired lung diffusion from an external pathogen, impaired spleen transformation, damp-heat obstructing the triple burner, or spleen and kidney yang deficiency with poor fluid metabolism. Facial swelling, pitting, tight shiny skin, reduced urination and cold painful knees may indicate quite different mechanisms. This page distinguishes external from internal patterns and yang-type from yin-type oedema, then explains the main and supporting points for each. Oedema can also signal cardiac, renal or hepatic disease or venous thrombosis; TCM assessment does not replace medical investigation.水肿的穴位选择，关键不是哪里肿就只取哪里，而是判断水液为什么停留：外感风邪影响肺的宣发肃降，脾失健运导致水湿停聚，湿热阻滞三焦，还是脾肾阳虚、气化无力。面部先肿、按之凹陷、皮肤紧亮、伴尿少或腰膝冷痛，提示的病机并不相同。本页先区分外感与内伤、阳水与阴水，再给出各型主穴、配穴及穴位之间的取舍。水肿也可能是心、肾、肝疾病或静脉血栓的表现，针灸辨证不能代替病因检查。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Sudden breathlessness, inability to lie flat, chest pain, pink frothy sputum or blue lips, especially with leg swelling, may indicate acute heart failure or pulmonary oedema. Call 911 immediately; do not wait for acupuncture.突然出现呼吸困难、不能平卧、胸痛、咳粉红色泡沫痰、嘴唇发紫，尤其伴双腿水肿：可能是急性心衰或肺水肿，立即拨打 911，不要等待针灸治疗。
  • Sudden swelling of one calf or thigh with pain, warmth, redness or tenderness requires same-day emergency assessment for deep-vein thrombosis. If chest pain or breathlessness occurs as well, call 911 immediately to rule out pulmonary embolism.一侧小腿或大腿突然肿胀，伴疼痛、发热、发红或明显压痛：当天急诊评估深静脉血栓；若同时胸痛或气短，立即拨打 911，排除肺栓塞。
  • Oedema with markedly reduced urine output or no urine, visible blood in the urine, persistent vomiting, altered consciousness or rapid weight gain requires urgent same-day assessment for acute kidney injury and serious fluid retention.水肿伴尿量明显减少或突然无尿、肉眼血尿、持续恶心呕吐、意识改变，或短时间体重快速增加：当天紧急就医，评估急性肾损伤及严重液体潴留。
  • Sudden swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat with difficulty swallowing, voice changes or stridor may indicate angioedema or a severe allergic reaction. Call 911 immediately.面部、嘴唇、舌头或喉咙突然肿胀，伴吞咽困难、声音改变、喘鸣：可能是血管性水肿或严重过敏反应，立即拨打 911。
  • Oedema persisting for several days or progressively worsening, particularly with abdominal distension, jaundice, foamy urine, waking breathless at night or exertional breathlessness, requires prompt medical assessment of heart, kidney and liver function and urinalysis. Do not treat it solely as a TCM dampness pattern.水肿持续超过数日、逐渐加重，或同时出现腹胀、黄疸、泡沫尿、夜间憋醒、活动后气短：尽快由医生检查心、肾、肝功能及尿液，不应仅按中医水湿证处理。
  • New facial or hand swelling in pregnancy, especially with headache, blurred vision, upper abdominal pain or raised blood pressure: same-day obstetric emergency care to rule out pre-eclampsia.孕期新出现面部或手部水肿，尤其伴头痛、视物模糊、上腹痛或血压升高：当天产科急诊，排除先兆子痫

「故水病，下為胕腫大腹，上為喘呼，不得臥者，標本俱病，故肺為喘呼，腎為水腫，肺為逆不得臥，分為相輸俱受者，水氣之所留也」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen, Discussion of Water and Heat Points: water disorders may cause lower-body swelling and abdominal distension, as well as breathlessness and inability to lie flat. The lung governs diffusion and descent, while the kidney governs water metabolism. This passage supports assessing respiration, urine output and kidney-related symptoms rather than focusing only on swollen limbs. BL13 and BL23 may be considered as pattern-based supporting points, but acupuncture must not substitute for assessment of possible acute cardiopulmonary or renal failure.《素问·水热穴论》：水病可以表现为下部浮肿、腹大，也可以出现喘促、不能平卧。肺主宣发肃降，肾主水液气化，肺肾功能失调可使水液停留。这段文字提示水肿不能只看下肢局部，应同时检查呼吸、尿量及腰肾症状。肺俞、肾俞可作为辨证配穴，但不能用针灸处理疑似急性心肺或肾功能衰竭。

「水腫，水氣行皮中，陰交主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing: when water qi moves through the skin and produces oedema, CV7 is indicated. CV7 is located on the lower abdomen and is traditionally used to regulate fluid metabolism and lower-burner function. It is selected according to the abdominal and fluid-retention presentation, not automatically for every patient with oedema.《针灸甲乙经》：水气运行于皮肤之中而形成水肿，可取阴交（CV7）。阴交位于下腹部，传统上用于调理水液与下焦气化，适合结合腹部水湿表现辨证选用。它不是所有水肿患者的通用主穴。

「水腫，人中盡滿，唇反者死，水溝主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Zhenjiu Jiayi Jing: this passage records severe oedema with swelling around the philtrum and abnormal lip appearance, and mentions GV26. It reflects historical recognition of critical presentations; GV26 must not be interpreted as a modern emergency treatment for oedema. Respiratory, circulatory or consciousness abnormalities require emergency medical care.《针灸甲乙经》：古籍记载严重水肿伴人中部位肿满、唇反等危重表现，并列水沟（GV26）为相关穴位。此条反映古人对重症水肿及危候的观察，不应将GV26理解为现代医学意义上的水肿急救措施。出现呼吸、循环或意识异常必须优先急救。

「陰陵水分去水腫之臍盈」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

A verse collected in Dacheng, vol. 2: SP9 with CV9 removes oedema that swells the belly. SP9 drains damp from a distance by strengthening the spleen, CV9 separates fluid locally — one distal, one local, the backbone of the yang-type core points on this page.《针灸大成》卷二所收歌赋：阴陵泉、水分相配，能消腹部胀满的水肿。阴陵泉健脾利湿在远端，水分分利水液在局部，一远一近，正是本页阳水主穴的骨架。

Yang-type oedema阳水

Wind attacking the lung, with wind-water flooding风邪袭肺，风水泛滥

How it presents辨证要点 Acute onset, with eyelid and facial swelling first, followed by limb or generalized oedema; skin feels tight and pitting may be slight. Often accompanied by aversion to wind, fever, nasal congestion, sore throat, cough, breathlessness and reduced urination; thin white coating and floating pulse. A red swollen throat, thirst and prominent fever suggest a wind-heat component.发病较急，眼睑、面部先肿，继而四肢或全身浮肿；皮肤紧张，按之凹陷不明显；常伴恶风、发热、鼻塞、咽痛、咳嗽、气短，小便不利；舌苔薄白，脉浮。若兼咽喉红肿、口渴、发热明显，则偏风热。

Principle治法 Dispel wind, diffuse the lung, regulate water passages and reduce oedema (primarily draining for excess; moxa may be considered for wind-cold, but avoid warming treatment in wind-heat).疏风宣肺，通调水道，利水消肿（实证以泻法为主，风寒可配合灸法，风热不宜温灸）

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

In modern medicine, oedema is an abnormal accumulation of fluid in the interstitial tissues. It is a clinical sign rather than a diagnosis. Initial classification distinguishes localized from generalized oedema, unilateral from bilateral swelling, acute from chronic onset, and pitting from non-pitting oedema. Common causes include: (1) cardiovascular disease, such as heart failure, venous insufficiency and deep-vein thrombosis; (2) kidney disease, including acute kidney injury, chronic kidney disease, nephrotic syndrome and glomerulonephritis; (3) liver disease, including cirrhosis, hypoalbuminaemia and portal hypertension; (4) endocrine and metabolic conditions, such as hypothyroidism and malnutrition; (5) local causes, including lymphoedema, impaired venous return, infection and trauma; and (6) medications, including some calcium-channel blockers such as amlodipine, NSAIDs, corticosteroids and thiazolidinediones. During pregnancy, pre-eclampsia and other complications must also be excluded. Initial assessment should document changes in body weight, blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, urine output, distribution and symmetry of swelling, skin temperature, tenderness and degree of pitting. Depending on the presentation, investigations may include urinalysis, urine protein, renal function (creatinine and eGFR), electrolytes, liver function, serum albumin, thyroid function, BNP or NT-proBNP, ECG and echocardiography. Acute unilateral leg swelling should be assessed for deep-vein thrombosis according to clinical probability, with venous ultrasound when indicated. The clinician determines which investigations are appropriate; not every patient requires every test. In TCM, yang-type oedema generally has a relatively acute onset and an exterior or excess presentation. Patterns include wind-water flooding, water-dampness flooding, damp-heat obstruction and damp-toxin invasion. Yin-type oedema generally has a longer course and an interior or deficiency presentation, commonly involving spleen yang deficiency, kidney yang deficiency or combined spleen-kidney yang deficiency. These are TCM pathophysiological classifications, not equivalents of acute and chronic oedema in modern medicine, and they do not identify a specific diseased organ. Heart failure may produce bilateral pitting leg oedema without necessarily indicating kidney yang deficiency. Nephrotic syndrome may cause facial and generalized oedema without necessarily corresponding to spleen deficiency. There is some clinical overlap between modern causes and TCM presentations, but no one-to-one correspondence. Acupuncture may be considered as adjunctive symptom management, but it cannot replace evidence-based treatment of heart failure, kidney or liver disease, thrombosis or infection. Acute unexplained oedema, suspected thrombosis, infection-associated oedema, severe generalized oedema and respiratory or circulatory instability require medical assessment first. Needling risks must be reassessed in patients taking anticoagulants, with bleeding disorders, infected skin or severe circulatory instability. Patients should not independently restrict fluids or start diuretics; sodium and fluid intake must be individualized by a clinician according to the cause, kidney function and electrolyte status.
