Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

A local adjunct for focal sacrogluteal discomfort at the fourth-foramen level; urinary dysfunction, saddle numbness or leg weakness requires urgent neurological or urinary assessment first.骶骨第 4 后骶孔水平的局部骶臀不适可作辅助；伴排尿障碍、会阴麻木或腿无力时先排查神经和泌尿急症。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Sacrogluteal myofascial pain骶臀肌筋膜痛

Pressure on gluteal soft tissue may reproduce local pain, but distinguish sacroiliac, hip and lumbosacral radicular causes; restricted movement or worsening pain needs assessment.臀部软组织按压可复现局部痛，但应和骶髂关节、髋关节及腰骶神经根病鉴别；活动受限或持续加重需评估。

Lumbar radicular pain坐骨神经根性痛

Pain radiating down the leg with numbness, tingling or strength change may indicate nerve-root involvement; sacral tenderness alone is not diagnostic and clinical assessment is appropriate.沿腿放射并伴麻木、针刺感或肌力改变提示神经根受累；不能仅凭骶部压痛定位，宜由临床人员评估。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Saddle numbness, urinary retention or incontinence, or progressive weakness in both legs requires emergency assessment for cauda equina syndrome.会阴麻木、尿潴留或失禁、双腿进行性无力，立即急诊排查马尾综合征。
  • Sacrogluteal pain with fever, redness, swelling or inability to bear weight after major trauma needs prompt medical care.骶臀痛伴发热、红肿或重大外伤后无法负重，尽快就医。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is BL54 Zhìbiān?秩边（BL54）在哪里？

Sacral region, level with the 4th posterior sacral foramen, 3 cun lateral to the median sacral crest.骶区，横平第 4 骶后孔，骶正中嵴旁开 3 寸。

How is BL54 needled?秩边（BL54）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1.5–2 cun.直刺；1.5–2 寸。

Main safety point at BL54?秩边（BL54）最要注意的安全问题？

The sciatic nerve and inferior gluteal vessels lie deep; repeated strong stimulation can injure the nerve. Persistent numbness or foot weakness afterwards needs assessment. No sacral needling in pregnancy.深部为坐骨神经与臀下血管；反复强刺可致神经损伤。针后持续麻木、足无力需评估。孕妇骶部不针。
