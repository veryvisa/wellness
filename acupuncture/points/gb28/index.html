🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Ilioinguinal neuralgia or entrapment髂腹股沟神经痛或卡压

The ilioinguinal nerve arises predominantly from L1, travels through the abdominal wall, and enters the groin. Entrapment or injury may cause burning pain, pins-and-needles, or numbness in the groin, suprapubic region, or near the external genitalia, sometimes radiating into the proximal medial thigh. Consider this diagnosis particularly after inguinal hernia repair, lower abdominal surgery, or local trauma. Electric radiating pain triggered by light touch or pressure should not simply be interpreted as point sensitivity.髂腹股沟神经主要来自L1，经过腹壁肌层后进入腹股沟区域。神经损伤或卡压可引起腹股沟、耻骨上区及外生殖器附近烧灼痛、针刺感或麻木，部分患者可向大腿近端内侧放射。腹股沟疝修补术、下腹部手术及局部外伤后尤其需要考虑。若轻触或压迫即出现沿神经分布的放射性电击感，不应简单当作穴位敏感。

Iliohypogastric neuralgia髂腹下神经痛

The iliohypogastric nerve arises from T12-L1 and supplies the lower abdominal wall and part of the upper lateral gluteal region. Irritation may produce pain or altered sensation near the anterior iliac crest, lower abdominal wall, or suprapubic region. Compared with ilioinguinal neuralgia, its sensory distribution is generally more abdominal and upper lateral gluteal rather than predominantly inguinal or genital.髂腹下神经来自T12-L1，分布于下腹壁及臀外侧上部附近。受刺激时可表现为髂嵴前部、下腹壁或耻骨上方疼痛、感觉异常。与髂腹股沟神经痛相比，其感觉异常分布通常更偏下腹壁及臀外侧上部，而非典型腹股沟及外生殖器区域。

Inguinal hernia腹股沟疝

A bulge in the groin that becomes more prominent when standing, coughing, or straining and may decrease when lying down suggests an inguinal hernia. Tenderness around GB28 does not exclude a hernia. An irreducible bulge, sudden severe pain, or vomiting raises concern for incarceration or strangulation and requires urgent assessment rather than needling or massage.腹股沟区出现站立、咳嗽或用力时明显的隆起，平卧后可能减小，伴局部坠胀或疼痛，应考虑腹股沟疝。维道附近压痛不能排除疝。若包块不能回纳、突然剧痛或伴呕吐，须警惕嵌顿或绞窄，不得继续针刺或按摩。

Pain involving the inguinal ligament, tendons, or fascia around the anterior superior iliac spine腹股沟韧带及髂前上棘周围肌腱、筋膜疼痛

Pain aggravated by exercise, running, kicking, or hip movement may involve the abdominal aponeurosis, iliopsoas, sartorius origin, or surrounding fascia. Clinical assessment may compare symptoms during resisted trunk movement and hip flexion, abduction, and rotation. Overlap between a tender spot and GB28 does not establish a channel-tendon disorder or justify local needling.运动后、跑步、踢腿或髋关节活动时疼痛加重，可能涉及腹壁肌腱膜、髂腰肌、缝匠肌起点或周围筋膜。通过髋关节屈曲、外展、旋转及躯干抗阻动作进行临床鉴别。不能仅凭压痛点与维道位置重合，就认定为经筋病或适合局部针刺。

Hip-joint and iliopsoas-related pain髋关节及髂腰肌相关疼痛

Pain may be felt deep in the groin and accompanied by restricted hip flexion, internal rotation, or weight-bearing. Hip disorders can be confused with superficial abdominal wall pain because patients may describe similar locations. Persistent limping, substantial restriction of hip movement, or difficulty bearing weight warrants musculoskeletal assessment first.疼痛可能位于腹股沟深部，伴髋关节屈曲、内旋或负重活动受限。髋关节病变与腹壁局部疼痛在主观位置上容易混淆。若出现持续跛行、髋关节活动明显受限或负重困难，应先进行肌骨系统评估。

Inguinal lymphadenopathy腹股沟淋巴结肿大

A palpable nodule or lump in the groin may be tender or painless. Infection, skin conditions, and systemic diseases can all cause enlarged lymph nodes. A hard, fixed, progressively enlarging node or one associated with unexplained fever, night sweats, or weight loss requires medical assessment and must not be treated as an acupuncture-point nodule.腹股沟区可触及结节或肿块，可能伴局部压痛，也可能无痛。感染、皮肤病变及其他系统性疾病均可能导致淋巴结肿大。硬、固定、持续增大或伴不明原因发热、盗汗、体重下降的结节，应由医生评估，不能当作穴位结节处理。

Vascular disorders in the groin腹股沟区血管性病变

A pulsatile groin mass, marked local pulsation, or sudden painful swelling may indicate a vascular disorder such as a femoral artery pseudoaneurysm. This is particularly important after catheterization, vascular puncture, or trauma. Do not needle or apply deep pressure near a pulsatile mass.腹股沟区出现搏动性肿块、局部明显搏动或突然出现的疼痛肿胀，应考虑血管性病变，包括股动脉假性动脉瘤等。尤其是有导管介入、血管穿刺或外伤史者，不可在搏动性包块附近针刺或深压。

Lower abdominal pain associated with gynecological or pelvic disorders妇科及盆腔疾病引起的下腹痛

Lower abdominal pain may arise from ovarian cysts, pelvic inflammatory disease, endometriosis, or pregnancy-related emergencies. Assessment should consider menstrual history, the possibility of pregnancy, vaginal bleeding, discharge, and the onset and pattern of pain. GB28 cannot diagnose or exclude gynecological disease. Needling is contraindicated during pregnancy.下腹痛可能来自卵巢囊肿、盆腔炎、子宫内膜异位症或妊娠相关急症。需结合月经史、妊娠可能性、阴道出血、分泌物及疼痛发生方式鉴别。维道不是诊断或排除妇科疾病的依据；孕期禁针。

Where is GB28 Wéidào?维道（GB28）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, 0.5 cun medioinferior to the anterior superior iliac spine.下腹部，髂前上棘内下 0.5 寸。

How is GB28 needled?维道（GB28）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique anteroinferiorly; 1–1.5 cun.直刺或向前下方斜刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of GB28?维道（GB28）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Foot-Shaoyang channel and Dai (Girdling) vessel足少阳、带脉交会穴

Main safety point at GB28?维道（GB28）最要注意的安全问题？

Do not needle deep medially into the abdominal cavity. Caution in pregnancy: textbooks prohibit needling lower-abdominal points in the first 3 months and abdominal or lumbosacral points after 3 months.不向腹腔内侧深刺；孕妇慎用：教材规定妊娠 3 个月以内小腹部穴禁针，3 个月以上腹部与腰骶部穴均不宜针刺。
