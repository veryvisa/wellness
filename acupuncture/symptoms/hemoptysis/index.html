Coughing up blood咯血

When someone coughs up blood, the first step is not pattern diagnosis but three questions: is the blood really coming from the lungs (a nosebleed running backward, bleeding gums and vomited blood are all mistaken for it); how much is there; and is there breathlessness, chest pain or light-headedness? A few streaks in the sputum in someone who is otherwise well is usually bronchitis or hard coughing, but a first episode still needs a doctor that day; mouthfuls of fresh blood, or blood with breathlessness or chest pain, mean calling 911. Once the cause is known, acupuncture follows the TCM bleeding patterns — dryness-heat injuring the lung, liver fire attacking the lung, yin deficiency with lung heat, and qi failing to hold the blood — built on LU6, the Lung xi-cleft point, LU5, the Lung he-sea point, and BL13, with points added for each pattern.咳出血来，第一步不是辨证，而是判断三件事：血是不是真的从肺里咳出来的（鼻血倒流、牙龈出血、呕血都会被误认）；量有多少；有没有气短、胸痛、头晕。痰里几丝血、人很平稳，多是支气管炎或剧烈咳嗽，但首次出现仍要当天看医生；一口一口的鲜血、或伴气短胸痛，直接拨 911。查明原因后，针灸按中医血证辨证：燥热伤肺、肝火犯肺、阴虚肺热和气不摄血四型，取穴以肺经郄穴孔最、合穴尺泽和肺俞为主，再按证加穴。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • A mouthful or more of fresh blood at once, repeated bleeding over a short time, clots, or blood with breathlessness, chest pain, light-headedness, a fast heartbeat or blue lips: call 911 now. While waiting, have the person lie on their side, bleeding side down if known, so blood does not run into the healthy lung; do not lie them flat on their back, and give nothing to eat or drink.一次咳出满满一口以上的鲜血、短时间内反复咳血、血块，或伴气短、胸痛、头晕、心跳快、嘴唇发青：立即拨 911。等待时让病人侧卧、知道哪侧出血就让出血侧在下，以免血流进好的一侧肺；不要躺平仰卧，不要给吃喝。
  • Coughing blood with sudden chest pain or breathlessness, especially after recent surgery, a long flight, bed rest, a fracture, pregnancy or childbirth, or the contraceptive pill, or with a swollen painful calf: call 911 now for possible pulmonary embolism.咯血伴突然胸痛、气短，尤其近期手术、长途飞行、卧床、骨折、怀孕产后或服避孕药后，或一侧小腿肿痛：立即拨 911，警惕肺栓塞。
  • Coughing blood while taking a blood thinner or antiplatelet drug (warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, clopidogrel and others): emergency department today, and do not stop or change the medicine on your own.正在服用抗凝药或抗血小板药（华法林、阿哌沙班、利伐沙班、氯吡格雷等）的人咯血：当天急诊，不要自行停药或加药。
  • Blood-streaked sputum for the first time, or recurrent streaks for more than a week, or with fever, night sweats, weight loss or hoarseness, or in a smoker over 40: see a doctor the same day or within the week to rule out pneumonia, tuberculosis, bronchiectasis and lung cancer; wear a mask if TB is possible.第一次出现血痰，或反复血痰超过一周、伴发热、盗汗、体重下降、声音嘶哑，或 40 岁以上吸烟者：当天至一周内就医，排除肺炎、结核、支气管扩张和肺癌；疑似结核者就医时戴口罩。
  • Dark red or coffee-ground material with nausea, vomiting or black stools (usually vomited blood), or blood swallowed after a nosebleed and coughed back up: still see a doctor, but by a different route — same-day emergency care for gut bleeding, an ear, nose and throat doctor for recurrent nosebleeds.血是暗红或咖啡渣样、伴恶心呕吐或黑便（多为呕血），或鼻血后咽下再咳出：同样要就医，但去向不同——消化道出血当天急诊，反复鼻出血看耳鼻喉科。

「肺欬之狀，欬而喘息有音，甚則唾血」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.38: lung cough shows as coughing and wheezing with a noise in the throat, and in severe cases blood is coughed up. The ancients saw coughing blood as lung cough at its worst, so the points centre on the Lung channel and BL13; today the same picture of a cough that worsens until it brings up blood first calls for a search for the cause in the lungs.《素问·咳论》：肺咳的表现是咳嗽喘息、喉中有声，严重时咳唾出血。古人把咯血看作肺咳发展到重的一步，所以取穴以肺经和肺俞为主；今天同样的「越咳越重、咳出血来」首先要查肺部病因。

「凡唾血，瀉魚際，補尺澤」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi jing: whenever blood is coughed up, drain LU10 and tonify LU5. LU10 is the Lung ying-spring (fire) point and LU5 the Lung he-sea (water) point, so this drains fire and tonifies water, letting water control fire — which is why both points run through the dryness-heat and yin-deficiency patterns on this page. In excess heat LU5 is now also often drained to clear the lung; the method follows the pattern.《针灸甲乙经》：凡是咳唾出血，泻鱼际、补尺泽。鱼际是肺经荥穴（火），尺泽是肺经合穴（水），泻火补水，以水制火——这是本页燥热和阴虚两型都离不开这两穴的来历。今天实热证中尺泽也常用泻法清肺，补泻以证为准。

「唾血振寒嗌乾，太淵主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi jing: coughing blood with shivering and a dry throat — LU9 governs it. LU9 is the Lung yuan and shu-stream (earth) point, and earth generates metal, so it tonifies the mother of the lung and suits coughing blood after long illness has worn down qi and yin; shivering with fever today first calls for pneumonia and TB to be ruled out.《针灸甲乙经》：咳唾出血、寒战、咽喉干燥，以太渊为主治穴。太渊是肺经原穴、输穴（土），土生金，补肺之母，所以用于久病气阴受损的咯血；寒战发热同时出现时今天要先排除肺炎和结核。

Excess heat实热

Dryness-heat injuring the lung燥热伤肺

How it presents辨证要点 Often in dry autumn or winter weather or after a cold: an itchy throat and cough, scanty sticky sputum streaked with blood or fresh blood coughed up, dry mouth, nose and throat, sometimes fever or headache; red dry tongue, thin yellow coating, rapid pulse.多见于秋冬干燥或外感之后：喉痒咳嗽，痰少而黏、痰中带血或咳出鲜血，口鼻咽干，或有发热、头痛；舌红少津，苔薄黄，脉数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, moisten the lung, calm the vessels and stop bleeding (drain; drain LU10, tonify or drain LU5)清热润肺，宁络止血（泻法；鱼际泻、尺泽可补可泻）

Main主穴LU6 Kǒngzuì孔最LU5 Chǐzé尺泽BL13 Fèishū肺俞
Add配穴LU10 Yújì鱼际LI11 Qūchí曲池

💡 Main set: LU6, the Lung xi-cleft point (yin-channel xi-cleft points are used for bleeding); LU5, the Lung he-sea (water) point, clears lung heat; BL13, the back-shu point of the lung, regulates lung qi. For dryness-heat add LU10, the Lung ying-spring (fire) point, to drain lung fire and soothe the throat, pairing with LU5 exactly as Jiayi's 'drain LU10, tonify LU5'; with marked fever add LI11 to clear Yangming heat. BL13 is beside T3 and is needled obliquely to avoid pneumothorax.主穴：孔最是肺经郄穴，阴经郄穴善治血证；尺泽是肺经合穴（水），清肺热；肺俞是肺的背俞穴，调肺气。燥热加鱼际——肺经荥穴（火），泻肺火、利咽，与尺泽一泻一补正合《甲乙经》「泻鱼际，补尺泽」；发热明显加曲池清阳明热。肺俞在 T3 旁，斜刺防气胸。

Excess heat实热

Liver fire attacking the lung肝火犯肺

How it presents辨证要点 Bouts of choking cough that pull at the chest and flanks, sputum streaked with or full of bright red blood; irritability, bitter taste, red face and eyes, often set off or made worse by anger or upset; red tongue, thin yellow coating, wiry rapid pulse.阵阵呛咳，咳时牵引胸胁作痛，痰中带血或纯血鲜红；烦躁易怒，口苦，面红目赤，常因生气、激动而发作或加重；舌红苔薄黄，脉弦数。

Principle治法 Clear the liver, drain the lung, cool the blood and stop bleeding (drain)清肝泻肺，凉血止血（泻法）

Main主穴LU6 Kǒngzuì孔最LU5 Chǐzé尺泽BL13 Fèishū肺俞
Add配穴LR2 Xíngjiān行间BL18 Gānshū肝俞

💡 LR2, the Liver ying-spring (fire) point, drains liver fire; BL18, the back-shu point of the liver, frees liver qi. Liver fire rising to attack the lung is called 'wood fire scorching metal', so the liver is drained while the main set clears the lung and stops bleeding. Being set off by emotion is no reason to skip a lung work-up; BL18 is beside T9 and is needled obliquely to avoid pneumothorax.行间是肝经荥穴（火），泻肝火；肝俞是肝的背俞穴，疏泄肝气。肝火上逆犯肺称「木火刑金」，所以一面泻肝、一面用主穴清肺止血。情绪诱发不能当作排除肺病的理由；肝俞在 T9 旁，斜刺防气胸。

Deficiency heat虚热

Yin deficiency with lung heat阴虚肺热

How it presents辨证要点 A long dry cough with little sputum and recurrent bright red streaks; dry mouth and throat, afternoon fever, flushed cheeks, night sweats, hot palms and soles, sometimes hoarseness or weight loss; red tongue with little coating, thin rapid pulse.久咳干咳，痰少，反复痰中带血丝、血色鲜红；口干咽燥，午后潮热，颧红，盗汗，手足心热，或声音嘶哑、消瘦；舌红少苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish yin, moisten the lung, calm the vessels and stop bleeding (tonify or even method; no moxa)滋阴润肺，宁络止血（补法或平补平泻；不灸）

Main主穴LU6 Kǒngzuì孔最LU9 Tàiyuān太渊BL13 Fèishū肺俞
Add配穴KI3 Tàixī太溪KI6 Zhàohǎi照海BL43 Gaohuangshu膏肓俞

💡 Yin deficiency does not tolerate heavy draining, so LU9 replaces LU5 in the main set: LU9, the Lung yuan and shu-stream (earth) point, tonifies the mother of the lung; KI3, the Kidney yuan point, and KI6, linked to the Yin Qiao vessel, nourish kidney yin to moisten the lung ('metal and water generate each other'); BL43 is the classical point for chronic exhausting cough. Afternoon fever, night sweats, weight loss and recurrent streaks are the textbook picture of pulmonary tuberculosis, which must be checked first. BL43 lies 3 cun lateral to T4 directly over the lung, so needle only obliquely 0.5–0.8 cun.阴虚不宜重泻，主穴把尺泽换成太渊：太渊是肺经原穴、输穴（土），补肺之母；太溪是肾经原穴、照海通阴跷，两穴滋肾阴以润肺（金水相生）；膏肓是传统治虚劳久咳的要穴。潮热盗汗、消瘦、反复血丝正是肺结核的典型表现，一定要先查。膏肓在 T4 旁开 3 寸，深部即肺，只可斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸。

Deficiency虚证

Qi failing to hold the blood气不摄血

How it presents辨证要点 Long illness and weakness with recurrent small amounts of pale red blood; dull complexion, tiredness, shortness of breath, a weak voice, poor appetite and loose stools, sometimes easy bleeding or bruising elsewhere; pale tongue, thin weak pulse.久病体弱，反复少量咳血、血色淡红；面色无华，神疲乏力，气短声低，食少便溏，或兼其他部位易出血、皮下瘀斑；舌淡，脉细弱。

Principle治法 Tonify qi, strengthen the spleen, hold the blood and stop bleeding (tonify; moxa allowed)益气健脾，摄血止血（补法，可灸）

Main主穴LU6 Kǒngzuì孔最BL13 Fèishū肺俞BL20 Píshū脾俞ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里
Add配穴CV6 Qìhǎi气海SP1 Yǐnbái隐白

💡 The spleen holds the blood in the vessels: BL20, the back-shu point of the spleen, and ST36, the Stomach he-sea point, strengthen the spleen and qi so the blood is held; CV6 tonifies original qi; SP1, the Spleen jing-well point, is traditionally moxa'd for bleeding when the spleen fails to hold blood. Easy bleeding or bruising elsewhere needs platelet and clotting tests rather than being treated as 'qi deficiency'. BL20 is beside T11 and needled obliquely; CV6 is on the lower abdomen and is not needled in pregnancy.脾统血：脾俞是脾的背俞穴，足三里是胃经合穴，健脾益气以摄血；气海补元气；隐白是脾经井穴，传统用灸治脾不统血的各种出血。别处也容易出血、出现瘀斑的，要查血小板和凝血功能，不能只按「气虚」处理。脾俞在 T11 旁斜刺；气海在小腹，孕妇禁针。

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Haemoptysis is blood coughed up from the airways below the vocal cords. In primary care the commonest causes are acute bronchitis and hard coughing; other important causes include bronchiectasis, pneumonia, tuberculosis, lung cancer (especially in smokers over 40), pulmonary embolism, heart failure or mitral stenosis, and bleeding on blood thinners. 'False haemoptysis' must be excluded first: blood from a nosebleed or the gums running backward, and vomited blood (dark red or coffee-coloured, mixed with food, acidic). Doctors decide on a chest X-ray or CT, sputum tests including for TB, blood counts and clotting, and bronchoscopy according to the amount of blood, vital signs and risk factors. The main danger of heavy bleeding is suffocation from blood blocking the airway rather than blood loss itself, so first aid is to lie the person on their side, bleeding side down, keep the airway clear and get them to hospital quickly. The TCM patterns of dryness-heat, liver fire, yin deficiency and qi deficiency guide the choice of treatment but cannot locate the bleeding or rule out tuberculosis or cancer; acupuncture has little modern research for haemoptysis and is only a supporting treatment once the cause is known and the bleeding is controlled.咯血是从声门以下的呼吸道咳出的血。门诊最常见的原因是急性支气管炎和剧烈咳嗽；其他重要原因有支气管扩张、肺炎、肺结核、肺癌（尤其 40 岁以上吸烟者）、肺栓塞、心衰或二尖瓣狭窄，以及抗凝药相关出血。要先排除「假咯血」：鼻出血或牙龈出血倒流、呕血（暗红或咖啡色、混有食物、偏酸）。医生会按出血量、生命体征和危险因素决定做胸片或 CT、痰检（含结核）、血常规与凝血、支气管镜。大量咯血的主要危险是血堵住气道而窒息，而不是失血本身，所以院前处理以侧卧、出血侧在下、保持呼吸道通畅并尽快送医为要。中医的燥热、肝火、阴虚、气虚分型用于选择治法，不能判断出血来源，也不能排除结核和肿瘤；针灸对咯血缺乏现代研究，只在原因已明、出血已控制后作为辅助。
