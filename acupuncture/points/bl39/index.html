Where is BL39 Weiyang?委阳（BL39）在哪里？

At the lateral end of the popliteal crease, medial to the biceps femoris tendon.腘横纹外侧端，股二头肌腱内侧。

How is BL39 needled?委阳（BL39）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of BL39?委阳（BL39）是哪类特定穴？

Lower He-sea point of the Sanjiao三焦下合穴

Main safety point at BL39?委阳（BL39）最要注意的安全问题？

The common fibular nerve is nearby, with popliteal vessels and tibial nerve deeper in the fossa; control direction and depth. Stop and seek medical assessment for numbness, dorsiflexion weakness, radiating pain, or significant bleeding.腓总神经邻近，腘窝深部有腘血管和胫神经；控制针向与深度。出现麻木、足背无力、放射痛或明显出血应停止并医疗评估。
