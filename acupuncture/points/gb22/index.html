Intercostal muscle strain and chest-wall myofascial pain肋间肌拉伤及胸壁肌筋膜疼痛

This commonly follows forceful coughing, trunk rotation, lifting, or exercise. Pain is localized to the lateral chest and may worsen with trunk rotation, deep breathing, or muscle contraction. It overlaps with the traditional description of chest and hypochondriac pain, but modern assessment requires the history of injury, muscular loading, and physical examination. Intercostal neuralgia肋间神经痛

Symptoms include band-like or linear burning pain, stabbing pain, numbness, or altered sensation along an intercostal space. Causes include herpes zoster, trauma, and postsurgical nerve injury. Rib fracture or costal cartilage injury肋骨骨折或肋软骨损伤

These injuries often follow a fall, impact, chest compression, or severe coughing. Focal bony tenderness is prominent, and pain worsens with deep breathing, coughing, or turning in bed. 🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 3

Where is GB22 Yuānyè?渊腋（GB22）在哪里？

On the lateral chest, in the 4th intercostal space on the mid-axillary line.胸外侧区，第 4 肋间隙中，在腋中线上。

How is GB22 needled?渊腋（GB22）怎么刺？

Transverse or oblique along the intercostal space; 0.5–0.8 cun.沿肋间隙平刺或斜刺；0.5–0.8 寸。

Main safety point at GB22?渊腋（GB22）最要注意的安全问题？

Thin chest wall with pleura and lung beneath: thread along the space only, never perpendicular or deep. The Jiayi Jing forbids moxibustion here.胸壁薄、深面即胸膜和肺：只沿肋间隙平刺，不直刺、不深刺。《甲乙经》载本穴不可灸。
