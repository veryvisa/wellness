The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    Distance between the two nipples = 8 cun (or between the medial borders of the coracoid processes = 12 cun), making it 4 cun from the anterior midline (umbilicus) to the nipple line. SP15 lies on the horizontal line of the umbilicus, 4 cun lateral to the anterior midline.两乳头之间折作 8 寸（或两肩胛骨喙突内侧缘之间折作 12 寸），前正中线（脐中）至乳头水平线为 4 寸。大横在脐中水平线上，前正中线旁开 4 寸。

  2. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Using the patient's own four fingers held together at the middle phalangeal crease of the middle finger (3 cun) measured laterally from the umbilicus, plus 1 patient thumb-width (1 cun) for a total of 4 cun; in patients with excess abdominal fat, verify with proportional bone measurements as skin shifts easily.以患者本人食指、中指、无名指、小指四指并拢（中指中节横纹处）为一夫法，即 3 寸，自脐中水平向外量取 3 寸后再加 1 拇指同身寸（1 寸），共 4 寸；肥胖者腹壁脂肪易移位，须以骨度分寸复核。

  3. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Draw a horizontal line through the center of the umbilicus to intersect the ipsilateral midclavicular line (vertical line descending from the nipple). Note that in obese or elderly patients with lax abdominal walls, the umbilicus may droop inferiorly; confirm the true anatomical umbilical level first.平脐中作一水平线，与同侧锁骨中线（或乳头垂直向下延长线）相交处即是。注意肥胖或老年腹部松弛者脐孔常向下移位，需先确认正常解剖平脐水平。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located over the three flat lateral abdominal muscles (external oblique, internal oblique, transversus abdominis) lateral to the lateral border of rectus abdominis (linea semilunaris), innervated by the T10 anterior ramus. Two cun medially lies ST25 within the rectus abdominis (where the inferior epigastric vessels run); deep to the transversalis fascia and parietal peritoneum lies the peritoneal cavity containing the ascending colon on the right and descending colon or small bowel loops on the left — deep needling in thin patients risks perforating the peritoneum and bowel.位于腹直肌外侧缘（半月线）外侧的腹外斜肌、腹内斜肌及腹横肌三层扁肌处，平对第 10 胸神经前支分布区。向内侧 2 寸为腹直肌中份的天枢（ST25，内有腹壁下动静脉走行）；深层穿腹横筋膜与壁腹膜即为腹膜腔，右侧深部多对应升结肠，左侧深部多对应降结肠或小肠袢，体瘦者深刺极易穿透腹膜伤及肠管。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Palpate gently and press deeply 4 cun lateral to the umbilicus; cord-like nodules or deep tender spots are often felt, particularly in patients with chronic constipation or irritable bowel syndrome, frequently accompanied by soreness or active borborygmi.指腹在脐旁开 4 寸处轻按深揉，常可触及条索状硬结或深部压痛点，尤其在慢性便秘、肠易激综合征患者局部多有明显的酸胀感或肠鸣音活跃。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Drifting 2 cun medially onto ST25: this lies within the rectus sheath where the inferior epigastric vessels run; deep perpendicular insertion can pierce the vessels and cause a rectus sheath hematoma.取穴偏内侧 2 寸误入天枢（ST25）：该处位于腹直肌鞘内，深层有腹壁下动静脉走行，直刺过深易刺破血管引起腹直肌鞘血肿。
  • Inserting too deeply (beyond 1–1.5 cun) in thin patients, those with thin abdominal walls, or post-abdominal surgery: risks piercing the transversalis fascia and parietal peritoneum, injuring the ascending/descending colon or small bowel and causing peritonitis or bowel perforation; surgical adhesions tether bowel to the abdominal wall, further increasing risk.体瘦、腹壁薄或有腹部手术史者直刺过深（超过 1–1.5 寸）：易穿透腹横筋膜和壁腹膜，刺伤升结肠、降结肠或小肠，引发腹膜炎或肠穿孔；术史者肠管可能粘连于腹壁，风险更高。
  • Relying on abdominal skin folds or a drooping umbilicus: lateral fat shift in the supine position stretches the surface measurement, pushing the point too far laterally toward the midaxillary line (near GB26).依赖腹部皮肤褶皱或下垂的肚脐定位：卧位时脂肪向两侧堆积会使标尺拉宽，导致定位偏外侧接近腋中线（带脉方向）。

Perpendicular; 1–2 cun.直刺；1–2 寸。

Main safety point at SP15?大横（SP15）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy. The bowel lies beyond the peritoneum and wall thickness varies: the textbook depth is a ceiling, go shallower in thin people, avoid wide lifting-thrusting, and do not needle unexplained acute abdominal pain.孕期禁针。腹膜深面是肠管，腹壁厚薄因人而异：教材深度是上限，消瘦者减浅，不大幅提插；急性腹痛原因未明不针。
