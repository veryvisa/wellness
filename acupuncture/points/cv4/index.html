    Anatomy解剖结构

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Consider for infraumbilical midline pain with a traditional lower-jiao or cold-deficiency pattern; periumbilical or post-meal epigastric pain needs a different focus.脐下正中、偏传统下焦或虚寒表现时考虑；脐周或上腹餐后痛优先按相应部位与病因评估。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Consider its small-intestine front-mu role for lower-abdominal bladder-region discomfort; CV3 is the bladder front-mu point, and acute retention needs care.下腹部膀胱区相关不适可考虑其小肠募穴属性；中极更直接对应膀胱募穴，急性潴留须就医。

Period pain痛经

An adjunct for midline menstrual pain with traditional cold-deficiency or fatigue features; sudden severe pain, abnormal bleeding or possible pregnancy needs assessment.下腹正中痛兼传统虚寒或乏力表现时作为辅助；突发剧痛、异常出血或疑妊娠先评估。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Lower abdominal or pelvic pain下腹或盆腔疼痛

Pain may be gastrointestinal, urinary, gynecologic or abdominal-wall in origin. Tenderness at CV4 cannot establish the cause; persistent or worsening pain or abnormal bleeding needs assessment.疼痛可来自肠道、泌尿、妇科或腹壁病因；关元区压痛不能判断病因，持续、加重或伴异常出血需临床评估。

Where is CV4 Guānyuán?关元（CV4）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, 3 cun below the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.下腹部，脐中下 3 寸，前正中线上。

How is CV4 needled?关元（CV4）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of CV4?关元（CV4）是哪类特定穴？

Front-mu point of the Small Intestine; Crossing point of the Conception Vessel and the three foot yin channels小肠募穴；任脉、足三阴交会穴

Main safety point at CV4?关元（CV4）最要注意的安全问题？

No needling in pregnancy (textbook rule: no lower-abdominal needling in the first 3 months; after 3 months avoid the abdomen and lumbosacral region as well); by mid-pregnancy the uterus rises above this level. Also empty the bladder first (a full bladder rises above the pubic symphysis against the abdominal wall).孕妇禁针（教材针刺注意事项：妊娠 3 个月内小腹部禁针，3 个月以上腹部、腰骶部亦不宜针），妊娠子宫中期即可达脐下 3 寸以上；针前排空膀胱（充盈膀胱可越过耻骨联合贴近腹壁）。
