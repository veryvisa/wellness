✋ Make it smaller or skip it if…这些情况改小或先不做

  • Poor balance or a tendency to fall: hold a chair back or counter with one hand during the empty stance; the left foot may rest flat平衡不好、容易跌倒：虚步时一手扶椅背或台面，左脚可以全脚着地
  • Knee pain: stand high, bending the knee no more than about 30 degrees, and take small steps; keep the knee in line with the toes and not past them; stop if it hurts膝盖痛：架子站高，弯膝到约 30 度为止，步子迈小；膝盖对着脚尖方向，不超过脚尖；痛就停

🧘 The movement动作

  1. From the single whip: bring the right foot half a step forward, ball of the foot first; shift the weight slowly back onto the right leg接单鞭：右脚向前跟进半步，脚掌先落地；重心慢慢后移到右腿
  2. Open the right hook into a palm and turn both palms up, elbows slightly bent; turn slightly right and let the left heel slowly lift右勾手松开变掌，两手翻掌向上，两肘微屈；身体稍右转，左脚跟慢慢离地
  3. Turn left to face ahead; bend the right elbow and push the right hand forward past the right ear, palm forward, fingertips at eye level; draw the left hand back to the front of the left waist, palm up; move the left foot slightly forward, toes touching, a left empty stance身体左转，面向前方；右手屈肘经右耳旁向前推出，掌心向前、指尖与眼同高；左手收到左腰前，掌心向上；左脚稍向前移，脚尖点地，成左虚步

🌬 Breathe in as you follow-step and turn the palms, out as you push; or breathe naturally.跟步、翻掌时吸气，推掌时呼气；或自然呼吸。

✅ Key points要领

  • In the empty stance nearly all the weight is on the right leg; the left foot only rests虚步时重心几乎全在右腿，左脚只是轻点
  • Keep the trunk upright; do not lean forward to reach上身竖直，不向前探
  • Let the right shoulder relax and sink as you push推掌时右肩放松下沉

❌ Common mistakes常见错误

  • Landing the follow step heel first and dropping the weight onto it跟步时脚跟先落、重心一下砸下去
  • Putting the left foot down fully so the weight is shared左脚踩实，变成两脚平均受力
  • Leaning forward as you push推掌时上身前倾

🪑 Easier version简化做法

No follow step: stand with one foot ahead and hold a chair back while the left toes rest, doing only the palm turn and the push; or do the arms seated.不跟步：两脚前后站好，左脚尖点地时手扶椅背，只做翻掌和推掌；或坐着做手的动作。

🏛 What the name means名字在讲什么

🏛 'High pat on horse' pictures one hand reaching high and forward, as if standing beside a horse and patting its back. It is a traditional image, not a research finding.「高探马」说的是一手高高地向前探出，像站在马旁去拍马背。这是传统的比喻，不是研究结论。
