Vomiting呕吐

Vomiting is stomach qi rising the wrong way. The textbook set is the same for every pattern — CV12 (Stomach front-mu, influential point of the fu), ST36 (lower he-sea of the stomach) and PC6 (the key antiemetic point) — plus additions by cause: cold, internal heat, food retention, phlegm-fluid, liver qi, spleen-stomach deficiency cold and stomach yin deficiency. Look first at what comes up: clear fluid or phlegm suggests cold or fluid, sour-bitter hot foul material suggests heat, rotten food is retention, dry retching suggests yin deficiency; then at the pace: sudden and violent is mostly excess, long and recurrent mostly deficiency. Vomiting can also be a heart attack, brain bleed, bowel obstruction, pregnancy or ketoacidosis, so read the warning signs first.呕吐是胃气上逆，教材取穴以和胃降逆为主：各型都用中脘（胃募、腑会）、足三里（胃下合穴）、内关（降逆止呕要穴），再按原因加配穴——寒邪客胃、热邪内蕴、饮食停滞、痰饮内阻、肝气犯胃、脾胃虚寒、胃阴不足。分辨先看吐什么：清水痰涎多寒多饮，酸苦热臭多热，酸腐食物是食积，干呕无物多阴虚；再看急缓：新病暴吐多实，久病反复多虚。呕吐也可能是心梗、脑出血、肠梗阻、妊娠、酮症的表现，先看红旗信号。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • Fresh blood, dark red clots or coffee-ground material in vomit, especially with dizziness, palpitations, cold sweats or black stools: seek emergency care. Call 911 for heavy bleeding, fainting or altered consciousness.呕吐物带鲜血、暗红血块或咖啡渣样物，尤其伴头晕、心慌、冷汗、黑便：立即急诊；大量出血、晕厥或意识不清拨打 911。
  • Persistent vomiting with inability to retain fluids for 8–12 hours, markedly reduced or dark urine, dizziness on standing or significant dry mouth: seek same-day assessment for dehydration. Infants, older adults and people with chronic illness need earlier assessment.持续呕吐，连续 8–12 小时无法保留液体，或明显少尿、尿色很深、站立头晕、口干明显：当天就医评估脱水；婴幼儿、老人及慢性病患者应更早评估。
  • Vomiting with sudden severe abdominal pain, a rigid or markedly distended abdomen, especially with inability to pass gas or stool: emergency assessment for obstruction, perforation or another acute abdominal condition.呕吐伴突发剧烈腹痛、腹部僵硬或明显腹胀，尤其停止排气排便：立即急诊，排除肠梗阻、穿孔等急腹症。
  • Repeated green, bile-stained vomit requires urgent assessment. Green bilious vomiting in an infant is an emergency.呕吐物呈绿色胆汁样，反复出现，尤其婴儿出现绿色呕吐：立即就医；婴儿胆汁性呕吐须按急症处理。
  • Vomiting with severe headache, neck stiffness, altered consciousness, seizures, limb weakness or speech disturbance: call 911 to exclude meningitis, raised intracranial pressure or stroke.呕吐伴剧烈头痛、颈项强直、意识改变、抽搐、肢体无力或言语异常：立即拨打 911，排除脑膜炎、颅内压升高或卒中。
  • If pregnancy is possible and vomiting persists, prevents eating or drinking, or causes weight loss: contact an obstetric provider or doctor the same day to assess hyperemesis gravidarum, electrolyte disturbance and dehydration.有妊娠可能，呕吐持续、无法进食饮水或体重下降：当天联系产科或医生，评估妊娠剧吐、电解质紊乱及脱水。
  • Vomiting with chest pressure, cold sweat or breathlessness (in diabetics and older women a heart attack may show mainly as nausea and vomiting): call 911.呕吐伴胸闷胸压、冷汗、气短（尤其糖尿病、年长女性，心梗可以主要表现为恶心呕吐）：立即拨 911。
  • A diabetic who vomits with deep rapid breathing, fruity breath or drowsiness: emergency now (ketoacidosis); repeated vomiting after a head injury: emergency now.糖尿病人呕吐伴深快呼吸、呼气有烂苹果味、嗜睡：立即急诊（酮症酸中毒）；头部受伤后反复呕吐：立即急诊。

「善嘔，嘔有苦，長太息，心中憺憺，恐人將捕之，邪在膽，逆在胃」——《黄帝内经·灵枢》（公版，维基文库底本）

Lingshu ch.19: frequent vomiting with bitter material, sighing, an unsettled heart and the fear of being caught — the pathogen is in the gallbladder and the counterflow in the stomach; the text then uses ST36 and below. An early account of liver-gallbladder qi attacking the stomach and the classical basis for ST36 in vomiting.《灵枢·四时气》：常呕、呕吐物发苦、爱叹气、心里发慌、怕人来捉，病在胆、逆在胃；原文接着说取足三里以下。这是肝胆之气犯胃而呕的早期描述，也是呕吐取足三里的古籍依据。

「食則嘔，胃脘痛，腹脹善噫」——《灵枢·经脉第十》（《黄帝内经》，公版）

Lingshu ch.10, disorders of the foot Taiyin Spleen: vomiting on eating, epigastric pain, bloating and frequent belching — vomiting belongs to spleen and stomach together, so chronic vomiting is treated through the spleen with BL20, BL21 and ST36.《灵枢·经脉》脾足太阴经「是动」病：一吃就呕，胃脘痛，腹胀，爱嗳气。说明呕吐与脾胃同病，久呕要从健脾论治，取脾俞、胃俞、足三里。

「嘔吐痰涎，眩暈不已。膻中 中魁 豐隆」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu Dacheng, book 7, under SP4: for vomiting of phlegm with constant dizziness use CV17, the extra point Zhongkui and ST40, with SP4 as the lead. ST40 transforms phlegm and SP4 opens the Chong and descends — the phlegm-fluid pattern; today dizziness with vomiting first needs stroke and vestibular causes excluded.《针灸大成》卷七八脉交会穴「公孙」条下：吐痰涎而头晕不止，取膻中、中魁（奇穴）、丰隆，并以公孙为主穴。丰隆化痰、公孙通冲脉降逆，正是痰饮内阻型的取穴；但眩晕呕吐今天要先排除中风和前庭病。

Excess实证

Cold attacking the stomach寒邪客胃

How it presents辨证要点 After cold or cold food: sudden vomiting of thin or undigested material, cold epigastric pain eased by warmth, no thirst, sometimes chills, stuffy nose or aches; white coating, wiry-tight or floating-tight pulse.多有受寒或吃生冷史，突然呕吐，吐物清稀或为未消化食物，胃脘冷痛、得温则舒，口不渴，或伴怕冷、鼻塞身痛；苔白，脉弦紧或浮紧。

Principle治法 Warm the stomach, disperse cold and stop vomiting (drain, with moxa)温胃散寒，降逆止呕（泻法，加灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴CV13 Shàngwǎn上脘BL21 Wèishū胃俞

💡 Main set: CV12 is the Stomach front-mu and influential point of the fu; ST36 is the lower he-sea ("he-sea points treat the fu organs"); PC6, the Pericardium luo point opening the Yin Wei, opens the chest and descends qi. For cold add CV13 to warm and descend and BL21, which pairs with CV12 as back-shu and front-mu, with moxa. Beneath CV13 lies the liver and under BL21 the pleura, so do not needle deeply.主穴：中脘是胃募、腑会，足三里是胃下合穴（「合治内腑」），内关是心包经络穴、通阴维脉，宽胸降逆止呕。寒邪加上脘温胃降逆、胃俞（与中脘俞募相配），可灸。上脘、胃俞深部有肝、肺胸膜，勿深刺。

Excess实证

Heat in the interior热邪内蕴

How it presents辨证要点 Vomit is sour, bitter, hot and foul, immediately after eating; dry bitter mouth, foul breath, burning epigastrium, constipation, dark urine, restlessness; red tongue, yellow coating, rapid pulse.吐物酸苦热臭、食入即吐，口干口苦、口臭，胃脘灼热，便秘尿黄，烦热；舌红苔黄，脉数。

Principle治法 Clear heat, harmonise the stomach and stop vomiting (drain; no moxa)清热和胃，降逆止呕（泻法；不灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴LI4 Hégǔ合谷ST44 Nèitíng内庭

💡 LI4, the Large Intestine yuan point, clears Yangming and regulates qi; ST44, the Stomach ying point, clears stomach heat ("ying points govern fever"). The textbook also pricks the sublingual extra points Jinjin and Yuye, which have no card here. LI4 is forbidden in pregnancy.合谷是大肠经原穴，清阳明、调气机；内庭是胃经荥穴，清胃热，「荥主身热」。教材还加金津、玉液（舌下奇穴，点刺，本库无穴卡）。合谷孕妇禁针。

Food retention饮食停滞

How it presents辨证要点 Vomiting of sour, foul-smelling or undigested food, with temporary relief of epigastric distension after vomiting; belching with a rotten taste, acid regurgitation, halitosis, poor appetite, abdominal distension or pain, and foul stools or constipation. Often follows overeating, contaminated food or excessive intake. Thick greasy tongue coating and slippery or forceful pulse. Excess signs predominate, often worsening after meals.呕吐酸腐未消化食物，吐后胃脘胀满可暂时减轻；嗳腐吞酸、口臭、不思饮食，腹胀腹痛，大便臭秽或便秘；多有暴饮暴食、饮食不洁或进食过量史。舌苔厚腻，脉滑或实。实证表现明显，常在进食后加重。

Principle治法 Reduce food, guide out stagnation and descend qi (drain)消食导滞，和胃降逆（泻法）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴ST21 Liángmén梁门ST25 Tiānshū天枢

💡 ST21 and ST25 are Stomach abdominal points — ST21 for the stomach, ST25 (Large Intestine front-mu) for the bowel — and together clear food and open the bowel. Not deep on a full stomach; ST25 is forbidden in pregnancy. Relief after vomiting is typical of food retention.梁门、天枢是胃经腹部穴，梁门偏胃、天枢（大肠募穴）偏肠，合用消食通腑。饱食后不深刺，天枢孕期禁针。吐后轻松是食积的特点。

Excess实证

Phlegm-fluid obstructing the middle痰饮内阻

How it presents辨证要点 Intermittent vomiting of clear fluid, phlegm or mucus, with chest and epigastric oppression, dizziness, palpitations or a splashing sensation in the stomach. May accompany excessive sputum and nausea, and can be triggered by drinking or eating. White greasy or slippery tongue coating and slippery pulse. Cold-fluid predominance may include chills and absence of thirst; phlegm-damp predominance may include heaviness.呕吐清水、痰涎或黏液，时作时止；胸脘痞闷、头晕目眩、心悸，胃内有振水感，常伴痰多、恶心；遇饮水或进食后容易发作。舌苔白腻或滑，脉滑。寒饮偏重可伴畏寒、口淡不渴；痰湿偏重可伴身体困重。

Principle治法 Transform phlegm, resolve fluid and descend qi (even method; moxa for cold fluid)化痰除饮，和胃降逆（平补平泻；寒饮可灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴ST40 Fēnglóng丰隆SP4 Gōngsūn公孙

💡 ST40 transforms phlegm; SP4, the Spleen luo point opening the Chong, with PC6 governs stomach, heart and chest and descends counterflow — especially with clear fluid and a splashing stomach.丰隆化痰；公孙是脾经络穴、通冲脉，配内关「合于胃、心、胸」降逆。呕吐清水、胃中有振水声时尤其合适。

Excess实证

Liver qi attacking the stomach肝气犯胃

How it presents辨证要点 Nausea and vomiting fluctuate with emotional stress, often triggered or worsened by anger, tension or anxiety; frequent belching, distension of the chest and flanks, epigastric fullness or pain, not necessarily relieved by vomiting; bitter taste and dry throat may occur. Tongue edges may be red or normal, with a thin white or slightly yellow coating; wiry pulse. Often recurrent, with a clear emotional trigger.恶心呕吐随情绪波动，常在生气、紧张、焦虑后发作或加重；嗳气频作、胸胁胀满、胃脘胀痛，吐后不一定缓解；可有口苦、咽干。舌边红或正常，苔薄白或微黄，脉弦。病程可反复，情志因素明显。

Principle治法 Soothe the liver, regulate qi and descend (drain)疏肝理气，和胃降逆（泻法）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴LR3 Tàichōng太冲LR14 Qīmén期门

💡 LR3, the Liver yuan point, soothes and descends; LR14, the Liver front-mu, frees the flanks. LR14 is needled only obliquely or transversely outward along the rib space to avoid pneumothorax.太冲是肝经原穴、疏肝降逆；期门是肝募穴，宽胸胁。期门只沿肋间向外斜刺或平刺，防气胸。

Deficiency虚证

Spleen-stomach deficiency cold脾胃虚寒

How it presents辨证要点 Vomiting clear fluid or undigested food, fluctuating in severity and worsening with fatigue or cold foods; dull epigastric pain relieved by warmth and pressure, aversion to cold, cold limbs, little thirst, poor appetite and loose stools. Usually prolonged, with fatigue. Pale swollen tongue, white slippery coating and deep slow weak pulse. Clear deficiency-cold features.呕吐清水或未消化食物，时轻时重，劳累或进食生冷后加重；胃脘隐痛、喜温喜按，畏寒肢冷，口淡不渴，食欲差，大便稀溏；病程较长，精神疲倦。舌淡胖、苔白滑，脉沉迟无力。虚寒特征明确。

Principle治法 Warm the middle, strengthen the spleen and descend qi (tonify, heavy moxa)温中健脾，和胃降逆（补法，重灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴BL20 Píshū脾俞BL21 Wèishū胃俞CV6 Qìhǎi气海

💡 BL20 and BL21 are back-shu points paired with CV12 front and back to warm and strengthen; CV6 tonifies qi, all with moxa; CV8 may be warmed over ginger or salt as in the textbook. Back-shu points go obliquely toward the spine and CV6 is forbidden in pregnancy.脾俞、胃俞是背俞，与中脘前后配穴，温中健脾；气海补气，都可灸；教材亦可隔姜或隔盐灸神阙。背俞斜刺向脊柱，气海孕妇禁针。

Deficiency虚证

Stomach yin deficiency胃阴不足

How it presents辨证要点 Recurrent dry retching or small-volume vomiting, sometimes with sticky material or only a little gastric fluid; dry mouth and throat, dull burning epigastric discomfort, hunger without desire to eat and constipation. Often worse in the afternoon or at night, sometimes with low-grade fever and hot palms and soles. Usually chronic, following prolonged illness, febrile disease or long-term dietary disturbance. Red tongue with little fluid and scanty or peeled coating; thin rapid pulse.反复干呕或呕吐量少，吐物黏稠或仅有少量胃液；口干咽燥、胃脘隐隐灼痛、饥不欲食、大便干结；午后或夜间加重，可伴低热、手足心热。病程较长，常见久病、热病后或长期饮食失调。舌红少津、少苔或剥苔，脉细数。

Principle治法 Nourish stomach yin and descend (tonify; no moxa)滋养胃阴，降逆止呕（补法；不灸）

Main主穴CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里PC6 Nèiguān内关
Add配穴BL20 Píshū脾俞SP6 Sānyīnjiāo三阴交

💡 The textbook adds BL20 and SP6: BL20 strengthens the spleen to make fluids and SP6 nourishes yin; add KI3 or KI6 for marked dryness. SP6 is forbidden in pregnancy.教材加脾俞、三阴交：脾俞健脾生津，三阴交养阴；口干明显可加太溪或照海。三阴交孕妇禁针。

PC6 Nèiguān内关

Best for最适合 Core point across vomiting patterns, especially prominent nausea, retching, motion-related nausea and rebellious stomach qi各型呕吐的首选基础穴，尤其恶心明显、干呕、晕动相关恶心及胃气上逆

Why依据 Pericardium luo-connecting point and confluent point of the Yin Wei vessel; regulates chest qi and redirects rebellious stomach qi downward手厥阴心包经络穴，八脉交会穴通阴维脉；宽胸理气、和胃降逆，是止呕常用核心穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More directly targets nausea, retching and upward rebellion than ST36; more focused on qi regulation than the local stomach action of CV12. A core point in acute vomiting, but does not replace assessment of the cause.比足三里更直接针对恶心、干呕和上逆感；比中脘更偏调气降逆而非局部调胃；急性呕吐常作为基础穴，但不能替代病因评估

CV12 Zhōngwǎn中脘

Best for最适合 Epigastric fullness, nausea, postprandial vomiting and local stomach dysfunction胃脘胀满、恶心、食后即吐、胃气上逆等胃腑局部症状

Why依据 Stomach front-mu point and influential point of the fu organs; front-mu and influential points regulate the corresponding organ systems胃之募穴、腑会；募穴调理相应脏腑，八会穴中的腑会加强调腑作用

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Local to the stomach where PC6 descends; perpendicular 1–1.5 cun, not deep on a full stomach or with an enlarged liver or spleen, caution in pregnancy.比内关偏胃腑局部，内关偏降逆；直刺 1–1.5 寸，饱餐后、肝脾大者不深刺，孕妇慎用。

ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

Best for最适合 Vomiting with poor appetite, fatigue and impaired digestive function, especially deficiency and chronic presentations呕吐伴食欲差、乏力、脾胃功能失调，尤其虚证和久病

Why依据 Stomach he-sea and lower he-sea point; he-sea points regulate the fu organs and support digestive function胃经合穴、胃下合穴；合治内腑，调理脾胃、扶正和中

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More focused on strengthening the spleen and stomach than PC6; more suitable for chronic deficiency than ST21. Acute food stagnation may require additional food-dispersing points rather than tonification alone.比内关更偏健脾和胃、调理虚弱；比梁门更适合久病虚证；急性实性食积可配消导穴，不宜只用补法

CV13 Shàngwǎn上脘

Best for最适合 Cold vomiting, vomiting right after eating, full epigastrium寒吐、食入即吐、胃脘满

Why依据 Meeting point of the Ren with the Stomach and Small Intestine任脉与足阳明、手太阳交会穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Higher and more descending than CV12; never angled upward (left liver lobe) and not deep with an enlarged liver or full stomach.比中脘位置更高、更偏降逆，中脘偏和胃；不向上斜刺（肝左叶），肝大、饱食后不深刺。

BL21 Wèishū胃俞

Best for最适合 Chronic vomiting from stomach deficiency, postprandial epigastric oppression and stomach dysfunction, especially with BL20胃虚久吐、食后脘闷、胃部功能失调，尤其与脾俞配伍

Why依据 Stomach back-shu point; forms a shu–mu combination with CV12胃之背俞穴，与胃募中脘构成俞募配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More directly regulates the stomach than BL20; provides back-shu rather than the anterior front-mu action of CV12. Deep back needling can cause pneumothorax and requires a practitioner trained in thoracic anatomy.比脾俞更直接调胃腑；比中脘更偏背部俞穴调理；背部深刺可造成气胸，须由掌握胸背解剖的专业人员操作

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

In modern medicine, vomiting is the expulsion of stomach contents through the mouth. It is a symptom rather than a diagnosis. Common causes fall into several groups: (1) gastrointestinal infection and food poisoning, including viral gastroenteritis and bacterial infection; (2) structural or motility disorders, including gastroparesis, bowel obstruction, peptic ulcer disease and biliary disorders; (3) medications and metabolic conditions, including chemotherapy drugs, some antibiotics, opioids, diabetic ketoacidosis, uraemia and electrolyte disturbances; (4) neurological and vestibular conditions, including migraine, vestibular vertigo, meningitis and intracranial disease; (5) pregnancy-related vomiting, including hyperemesis gravidarum; and (6) psychological and functional gastrointestinal disorders. Recurrent or persistent vomiting can cause dehydration, hypokalaemia, hyponatraemia, metabolic alkalosis and kidney injury. Investigations depend on history and examination and may include vital signs, hydration assessment, glucose, electrolytes, renal function, complete blood count, liver and pancreatic markers, pregnancy testing, urinalysis and abdominal imaging. Blood or coffee-ground vomit, green bilious vomit, severe abdominal pain, neurological abnormalities, significant dehydration or inability to retain fluids require medical assessment first. The seven TCM patterns do not correspond one-to-one with modern diagnoses. Acute gastroenteritis may resemble cold attacking the stomach, internal heat or food retention; delayed gastric emptying may present with features resembling phlegm-fluid obstruction or spleen-stomach cold deficiency; emotionally triggered functional nausea may resemble liver qi attacking the stomach; and prolonged inadequate intake or chronic wasting may resemble stomach yin deficiency. These are symptom-level overlaps, not a basis for diagnosing a biomedical condition. Research suggests that PC6 has some evidence for postoperative nausea and vomiting and certain pregnancy-related nausea presentations, but evidence varies by cause, treatment protocol and study quality. This does not establish effectiveness for every cause of vomiting. Acupuncture should be considered only as an adjunct after appropriate assessment and must not delay fluids, antiemetic medication, surgery or other necessary treatment.现代医学中的呕吐是胃内容物经口排出的症状，本身不是独立诊断。常见病因可分为几类：①消化道感染及食物中毒，包括病毒性胃肠炎、细菌感染；②胃肠道结构或运动问题，包括胃轻瘫、肠梗阻、胃溃疡及胆道疾病；③药物和代谢因素，包括化疗药、部分抗生素、阿片类药物、糖尿病酮症酸中毒、尿毒症及电解质紊乱；④神经系统和前庭系统问题，包括偏头痛、前庭性眩晕、脑膜炎及颅内病变；⑤妊娠相关呕吐，包括妊娠剧吐；⑥心理及功能性胃肠病。反复或持续呕吐可能导致脱水、低钾、低钠、代谢性碱中毒及肾功能损害。检查根据病史和体征决定，可能包括生命体征、脱水程度、血糖、电解质、肾功能、血常规、肝胆胰指标、妊娠检测、尿液检查及腹部影像。出现血性或咖啡渣样呕吐、绿色胆汁性呕吐、剧烈腹痛、神经系统异常、明显脱水或无法保留液体时，应优先医学评估。中医七型与现代病因并不一一对应：急性胃肠炎可能表现为寒邪客胃、热邪内蕴或饮食停滞；胃排空障碍可能出现痰饮内阻或脾胃虚寒的证候；情绪诱发的功能性恶心可能出现肝气犯胃；长期摄入不足或慢性消耗状态可能出现胃阴不足。但这些只是症候层面的交集，不能根据中医证型推断具体现代医学诊断。针灸研究中，内关（PC6）在术后恶心呕吐及部分妊娠相关恶心研究中有一定证据，但不同病因、治疗方案和研究质量之间存在差异，不能据此宣称能治疗所有原因的呕吐。针灸应作为经过评估后的辅助措施，不能延误补液、止吐药物、手术或其他必要治疗。
