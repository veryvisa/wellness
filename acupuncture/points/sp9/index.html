🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is SP9 Yīnlíngquán?阴陵泉（SP9）在哪里？

On the medial leg, in the depression between the inferior border of the medial condyle of the tibia and the medial border of the tibia.小腿内侧，胫骨内侧髁下缘与胫骨内侧缘之间的凹陷中。

How is SP9 needled?阴陵泉（SP9）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–2 cun.直刺；1–2 寸。

Special-point roles of SP9?阴陵泉（SP9）是哪类特定穴？

He-sea (water)合穴（水）

Main safety point at SP9?阴陵泉（SP9）最要注意的安全问题？

The great saphenous vein usually passes superficially: identify it first, and do not drive deep posteriorly toward the posterior tibial neurovascular bundle.大隐静脉多在浅层经过：先看清；针不向后方深刺至胫后血管神经束。
