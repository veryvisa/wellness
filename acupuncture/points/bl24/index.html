Posture体位 · Prone position with the lumbar spine relaxed; a thin pillow under the abdomen or ankles can reduce lumbar lordosis and slightly open interspinous spaces.俯卧位，腰部放松，可在腹部或踝下垫薄枕以减轻腰椎前凸并使棘突间隙略开。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    With the arms hanging, medial border of the scapula to the posterior midline = 3 cun (the standard width across the back, loins and sacrum); the point is at the midpoint, 1.5 cun from the midline. Vertically it is set by bony level, not by cun: below the L3 spine.两臂自然下垂时，肩胛骨内侧缘到后正中线折作 3 寸（背腰骶部横寸通用），气海俞在这段的中点，即后正中线旁开 1.5 寸；纵向不量寸，按第 3 腰椎棘突下定。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    First palpate the line across the highest points of the iliac crests (commonly near the L4 spinous process or L4–L5 interspace), move superiorly to the L3 spinous process, mark the posterior midline below it, and measure 1.5 cun laterally for BL24.先摸髂嵴最高点连线（多平第 4 腰椎棘突或 L4–L5 棘突间隙），向上摸到第 3 腰椎棘突，在其下缘定后正中线，再旁开 1.5 寸取气海俞。

  3. ✋
    Finger cun手指同身寸

    Lateral to the lower border of the L3 spinous process, measure roughly 1.5 cun using the patient's combined index and middle finger width (or 1.5 proportional cun), always verifying lateral distance against the spinous process and erector spinae contour.在 L3 棘突下缘旁开处，以患者食、中指并拢横量约 1.5 寸（或按患者自身同身寸 1.5 寸）定位，最后仍需结合棘突与竖脊肌隆起核对横向距离。

  4. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Located 1.5 cun lateral to the inferior border of the L3 spinous process; superficial layers include skin, subcutaneous tissue (with posterior cutaneous branches of lumbar nerves and dorsal lumbar venous branches), and thoracolumbar fascia, over erector spinae and multifidus, with lumbar transverse processes, laminae, and posterior rami of lumbar nerves deeper. Keep clear of deep lumbar posterior ramus trunks and avoid overly deep or medial angulation toward the lamina/interlaminar space, following textbook oblique insertion toward the spine for 0.5–1 cun. Deep and lateral lies the lower pole of the kidney (closer when the right kidney sits low), so never insert deep and outward.位于第 3 腰椎棘突下旁开 1.5 寸处，局部浅层为皮肤、皮下组织（含腰神经后支皮支及腰背静脉分支）与胸腰筋膜，深层为竖脊肌与多裂肌，更深邻近腰椎横突、椎板及腰神经后支。临床需避开深层腰神经后支主干及内侧椎管方向，按教材向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–1 寸，切忌过深或偏向内侧椎板间隙。外侧深部可及肾下极（右肾较低者更近），不可向外深刺。

  5. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently press and slide the thumb pad 1.5 cun lateral to the L3 spinous process to feel the erector spinae belly or fascial tension/tenderness; local soreness or distension often accompanies deqi, whereas radiating pain or paresthesia along a nerve distribution indicates nerve branch irritation and calls for lighter pressure.拇指指腹在 L3 棘突下旁开约 1.5 寸处轻柔按压与横向滑动，常可触及竖脊肌肌腹或筋膜结节、压痛或条索感；触压时若出现向臀部或腰部局部酸胀多属得气反应，若沿神经分布出现放射痛或麻木则提示刺激到神经分支，应减力调整。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Miscounting vertebrae: the iliac-crest line is level with the L4 spine or the L4–L5 space and can vary by one vertebra; taking it as L3, or skipping a level while counting, puts the point at BL23 or BL25 instead.椎序数错：髂嵴最高点连线平 L4 棘突或 L4–L5 间隙，个体差异可达一个椎体；若把它当成 L3，或数时漏一节，气海俞就会落到肾俞或大肠俞的位置。
  • Measuring lateral distance too narrow or too wide from 1.5 cun: too medial hits the spinous process or lamina, while too lateral shifts toward more lateral paraspinal musculature and changes the target tissue layer.横向旁开量取过宽或过窄（偏离 1.5 寸）：偏内侧易抵触棘突或椎板骨面，偏外侧则偏向华佗夹脊外侧或腰方肌/外侧肌群，改变刺激靶区与针感层次。
  • Too steep, too deep, or deep and outward: irritates the dorsal ramus, hits periosteum, and laterally can reach the lower pole of the kidney. Textbooks insert obliquely toward the spine, 0.5–1 cun; lumbosacral points are forbidden after the third month of pregnancy, so do not needle in pregnancy.角度过大、过深或向外深刺：易刺激腰神经后支、抵骨膜，向外深入可及肾下极。教材向脊柱方向斜刺 0.5–1 寸；腰骶部腧穴怀孕三个月以上禁针，孕妇不针。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Suitable when low back pain involves qi deficiency, chronic aching, or local channel stagnation; for acute severe pain, trauma, or radicular signs, do not rely on a single point—assess first and compare with BL23 (more focused on kidney-deficiency lumbar soreness) or BL40 (stronger distal channel-clearing action along the posterior leg).偏于腰部气虚、慢性酸痛或局部经气不利者可选用；若急性剧痛、外伤或伴神经根体征，不以单穴为主，应先评估并结合循经与局部/远端穴位鉴别对比（如与 BL23 肾俞侧重肾虚腰酸、BL40 委中侧重循经通络相比较）。

Sciatica坐骨神经痛 ⚖️

Used only as an adjunctive local point when lower-limb discomfort or gluteal referral accompanies lumbar involvement, not as a primary point for sciatica; if radiating leg pain/paresthesia, weakness, or sensory loss is present, evaluate nerve compression first and compare with lower-limb channel points such as GB30 and BL40.仅可作为下肢循经不适或腰臀牵涉痛的辅助局部选穴之一，不作为坐骨神经痛主治穴；若出现下肢放射性麻痛、肌力下降或感觉减退，需先明确神经受压程度并与 GB30、BL40 等下肢循经穴位鉴别选用。

Urinary retention and enuresis癃闭与遗尿

Suited to enuresis or dysuria from lower-jiao qi deficiency and impaired qi transformation, often paired with CV6 or BL23; for acute urinary retention, hematuria, or urinary tract infection/obstruction, seek medical diagnosis first rather than using acupuncture in place of medical care.偏于下焦气虚、气化不利所致的遗尿或小便不利，常配 CV6、BL23 等穴；若为急性尿潴留、血尿或泌尿系感染/梗阻，须先就医明确病因，不以针灸替代医疗处置。

🏛 In the classics古籍怎么说

「十五椎下兩旁，相去脊各一寸五分，主腰痛痔漏」——杨继洲《针灸大成》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

The Zhenjiu Dacheng places Qihaishu 1.5 cun lateral on each side below the fifteenth vertebra (counted downward from T1, corresponding to L3) and indicates it for low back pain and anal fistula/hemorrhoidal bleeding, reflecting traditional focus on local lumbar qi alongside lower-jiao qi and channel connections.《针灸大成》记载气海俞位于第十五椎下两旁、距脊柱各 1.5 寸（古人自第一胸椎向下数至第十五椎即第三腰椎），主治腰痛与痔漏，体现了其以局部腰部经气为主、兼顾下焦气机与经脉循行关联的传统主治思路。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Chronic lumbar muscle strain and thoracolumbar fasciitis慢性腰肌劳损与胸腰筋膜炎

Common after prolonged sitting or bending/lifting, causing aching over the L3 erector spinae or fascia that may vary with posture; differentiate from lumbar disc herniation, facet joint pain, and spinal infection or malignancy, seeking medical evaluation first if pain is prominent at night/at rest or accompanied by fever or weight loss.常见于久坐、弯腰搬重物后 L3 水平竖脊肌或筋膜酸痛，活动后可略缓解或久坐加重；需与腰椎间盘突出、小关节源性疼痛及脊柱感染/肿瘤鉴别，若夜间痛、静息痛明显或伴发热体重下降应先就医排查。

Third lumbar transverse process syndrome and lumbar posterior-ramus pain第三腰椎横突综合征与腰神经后支痛

Localized tenderness lateral to the L3 spinous process near the transverse process tip, sometimes referring toward the buttock or posterolateral thigh, typically without classic radicular straight-leg-raise findings; distinguish from disc herniation with nerve-root compression and obtain imaging when indicated.L3 棘突旁约 1.5–3 寸处（横突尖端附近）出现局限压痛，可向臀部或大腿后外侧牵涉，直腿抬高试验多无典型根性阳性；须注意与腰椎间盘突出压迫神经根鉴别，必要时行影像学检查。

Lumbar spondylosis or facet joint dysfunction腰椎退行性变或小关节紊乱

Common in middle-aged and older adults with morning stiffness or pain on lumbar extension/rotation; combine with neurological exam and imaging to rule out spinal stenosis or cauda equina compression.中老年人群常见，晨僵或腰部伸展、旋转时局部酸痛，须结合神经查体与影像学排除椎管狭窄或马尾受压。

How is BL24 needled?气海俞（BL24）怎么刺？

Main safety point at BL24?气海俞（BL24）最要注意的安全问题？

Deep lumbar tissues include muscle, bony structures, and neurovascular branches; avoid forceful deep insertion. This is a lumbosacral point: do not needle during pregnancy.腰部深层有肌肉、骨性结构和神经血管分支；避免用力深刺。腰骶部穴，孕期不针。
