Belly circles for babies and children摩腹：给宝宝和孩子揉肚子

A baby whose tummy feels tight at night, grunting and kicking, or a child who has not had a bowel movement for two or three days: many parents rub the belly by instinct. Direction, pressure and when not to rub all matter, and belly pain that keeps getting worse, green vomit or blood in the stool are not for rubbing at all.宝宝晚上肚子胀、哼哼唧唧蹬腿，或者孩子两三天没大便，很多家长会本能地给他揉肚子。揉的方向、力度和什么时候不能揉，都有讲究；肚子痛越来越重、吐绿水、便里有血，就不是揉肚子的事了。

⏱ 1–2 minutes for babies, 3–5 for older children婴儿 1–2 分钟，大孩子 3–5 分钟 · Between feeds or meals, after a bath, at bedtime; once or twice a day两顿奶或两餐之间、洗澡后、睡前；一天 1–2 次

👶 Ages 1 months to 12 years适合 1 个月到 12 岁

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • A baby under 3 months with a fever (rectal 38°C or higher) needs a doctor urgently, with no massage first; any fever under 6 months needs a doctor3 个月以下的宝宝发烧（肛温 38°C 或以上）要立即就医，不做推拿；6 个月以下发烧都要看医生
  • Seizure, struggling to breathe or blue lips, cannot be woken or is floppy and unresponsive: call 911抽搐、呼吸费力或嘴唇发青、叫不醒或软绵绵没反应：打 911
  • Signs of dehydration: fewer than 4 wet diapers in 24 hours (fewer than 3 for older children), no tears when crying, dry mouth and tongue, sunken eyes or soft spot, refusing to drink: see a doctor the same day脱水的样子：24 小时湿尿布少于 4 片（大孩子小便少于 3 次）、哭时没有眼泪、嘴和舌头干、眼窝或囟门凹下去、不肯喝水：当天看医生
  • Vomiting green or yellow-green fluid (bile): go to emergency right away吐绿色或黄绿色的液体（胆汁）：立即去急诊
  • Belly pain that keeps getting worse, a hard swollen belly that makes the child cry when touched, bloody or black stools: see a doctor the same day肚子痛越来越重、肚子胀硬一碰就哭、大便带血或黑色：当天看医生

🛑 When to stop and see a doctor什么时候停手去看医生

  • The child cries or curls up as soon as you touch the belly, or one spot is especially hard or painful: stop and see a doctor一摸肚子孩子就哭、身体缩起来，或某一处特别硬、特别痛：停手看医生
  • The child vomits during or after: stop and watch for the red flags above揉的时候或揉完吐了：停，观察有没有上面的红旗
  • Constipation that has not changed after a week, or keeps coming back with pain or poor weight gain: see the family doctor rather than relying on belly rubbing便秘超过一周仍没改善，或便秘反复、伴肚子痛、体重不长：看家庭医生，不要只靠揉肚子

🤲 Who does it, and how to start谁来做、开始之前

Ages 1 months to 12 years适合 1 个月到 12 岁

Done by a parent or the adult who usually cares for the child. Trim and smooth your nails, warm your hands, keep the room warm; a little baby oil is fine, but no talc (babies breathe it in). Do it when the child is calm. Crying, squirming or pushing your hand away is the signal to stop; never hold a child down to finish.由家长或固定照顾孩子的大人做。先剪短磨平指甲、把手搓热，房间暖和；可以用一点婴儿润肤油，不用爽身粉（宝宝会吸进去）。孩子情绪平稳时做，哭闹、扭动、推开你的手，就是停手的信号——这时候不要按住孩子硬做。

✋ When not to do it这些情况先别做

  • A newborn whose cord stump has not fallen off, or a red, weeping navel: keep away from the navel新生儿脐带残端还没脱落、肚脐发红流水时不碰肚脐一带
  • A bulge at the navel or groin (possible hernia), or after belly surgery: ask the doctor first肚脐或大腿根鼓起一个包（可能是疝）、做过腹部手术的孩子，先问医生
  • Not within 30 minutes after a feed or meal, to avoid spit-up刚吃完奶或饭 30 分钟内不揉，免得吐奶
  • Not during sudden severe belly pain, fever or vomiting肚子突然剧痛、发烧、呕吐时不揉

👐 Step by step一步一步来

Use the finger pads; aim for a mild ache, never pain. Every point name opens its full card with location and anatomy.用指腹，以微酸胀为度，不要按到痛。点穴位名可以打开完整穴位卡：定位与针下解剖。

  1. 1

    Lay the child on the back with knees bent so the belly is relaxed. Warm your hands and rest a palm quietly on the belly for a few seconds.孩子仰卧，膝盖弯起来让肚子放松；你把手搓热，先把手掌静静放在孩子肚子上几秒。

    How much力度与时间 A few seconds几秒钟

  2. 2
    Circular rubbing摩法

    With the whole palm (two or three finger pads for a baby), circle the navel clockwise: up the child's right lower belly, across the top, down the left side, which is the direction the large bowel runs.用整个手掌（宝宝用两三根手指的指腹），绕着肚脐顺时针画圈：从孩子的右下腹往上，横过上腹，再从左边往下——这是大肠走的方向。

    How much力度与时间 About 1 minute for babies, 2–3 for older children婴儿约 1 分钟，大孩子 2–3 分钟

    ⚠️ Only enough pressure for the skin to move with your hand; do not press in. Watch the child's face: carry on if relaxed, go lighter at any frown.力度只是让皮肤跟着手动，不往下压；看孩子的脸色，舒服就继续，皱眉就更轻。

  3. 3

    For babies: hold both feet and gently 'pedal a bicycle', easing the knees toward the belly and back.婴儿：握住两只小脚，轻轻做「蹬自行车」，把膝盖慢慢推向肚子再伸直。

    How much力度与时间 About 10 times10 次左右

    ⚠️ Move slowly and never push against resistance.动作慢，碰到阻力不硬推。

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E2 A 2026 meta-analysis pooled 16 randomised trials adding pediatric tuina to usual care for functional constipation (1,387 children): results were better overall with tuina added, but the trials varied widely, the prediction intervals crossed 'no difference', and most follow-ups were under 2 months; the authors themselves call the findings exploratory.一篇 2026 年的荟萃分析合并了 16 项小儿推拿配合常规治疗功能性便秘的随机试验（1387 名儿童）：配合推拿的组整体结果更好，但各试验差异很大，预测区间跨过「没有差别」，随访大多不到 2 个月，作者自己说只能算探索性结果。 [1]
  • E1 A systematic review of 52 randomised trials of non-drug treatments for childhood constipation listed abdominal massage as 'promising', while noting that most studies were at high risk of bias and better trials are needed before guidelines change.一篇覆盖 52 项随机试验的儿童便秘非药物治疗系统综述把腹部按摩列为「有希望」的方法，同时说多数研究偏倚风险高，在改指南之前还需要更好的试验。 [2]
  • E1 A Cochrane review of massage for healthy babies under 6 months found differences in crying time and some other outcomes when studies were pooled, but 20 of the 34 studies were at high risk of bias, and most differences did not hold once those were removed.Cochrane 对 6 个月以下健康婴儿抚触的系统综述：合并后哭闹时间等有差别，但 34 项研究里 20 项偏倚高，去掉这些研究后大多数差别不再成立。 [3]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Pediatric massage books use belly circling often, with the saying that clockwise 'drains' and counter-clockwise 'tonifies'; research has not tested that. We use clockwise because it follows the large bowel and is the direction used in adult belly-massage studies. This is a traditional view, not a research finding.小儿推拿书里摩腹是常用的一法，有「顺时针为泻、逆时针为补」的说法；这个说法研究没有检验过。我们按大肠走向用顺时针，是因为成人腹部按摩研究里用的也是这个方向。这是传统说法，不是研究结论。
