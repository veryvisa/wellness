  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The point is on the anterior scalp over the frontal bone, with vessels and frontalis/galea beneath. Stay superficial; avoid orbital or intracranial direction and do not work through scalp infection or an open wound.穴在额顶部头皮正中、额骨表面，皮下有血管和额肌/帽状腱膜；保持浅表，勿向眼眶或颅内深部操作，头皮感染或开放伤处避开。
