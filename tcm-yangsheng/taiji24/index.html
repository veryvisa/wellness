Tai chi (simplified 24-form)太极拳（简化二十四式）

If you worry about falling, tai chi is one of the best-studied things you can practise. It is slow, but every step trains standing on one leg and moving your weight under control, which is exactly what walking, stairs and turning ask of you. The simplified 24-form is the most widely taught beginner set; the first eight forms alone will keep you busy for months. One rule matters most: stand high. Bending the knees about 30 degrees is plenty; you need not squat as low as the people in the park, and half-size steps are still tai chi.怕摔跤的人，有一样被研究得很多的运动可以练：太极拳。它慢，可是每一步都在练单腿站稳、慢慢移重心：这正是走路、下台阶、转身时用的本事。简化二十四式是入门最通行的一套，名字听着多，其实先学前八式就够用几个月。最要紧的只有一条：架子站高。膝盖弯到约三十度就好，不必追求公园里蹲得很低的样子；步子小一半，也一样是太极。

🚩 Before you start开始之前

  • If you have fallen in the past year, walk unsteadily or feel dizzy: see your family doctor or a physiotherapist first; practise holding a chair back or a counter, leave the stepping out of form 6, only lift the knee in the heel kicks (forms 13 and 15), and do the one-leg stands of forms 16 and 17 high and holding a chair近一年跌倒过、走路不稳、头晕：先请家庭医生或物理治疗师看过；练时扶着椅背或台面，第六式倒卷肱先不退步，第 13、15 式蹬脚只抬膝不蹬，第 16、17 式下势独立只做高架、扶椅
  • Knee pain, knee osteoarthritis, or a hip or knee replacement: stand high (knees bent about 30 degrees or less), knees in line with the toes and not past them; when you turn, move the toes before the weight; the full low stance in forms 16 and 17 is a deep one-legged squat, so with knee pain do only the high version; see a doctor first if the knee is swollen, hot, locks or gives way膝盖痛、膝骨关节炎、换过髋或膝关节：架子站高（膝弯约 30 度以内），膝盖对着脚尖、不超过脚尖；转身先动脚尖再移重心；第 16、17 式完整的下势是单腿深蹲，膝盖痛的人只做高架；膝盖肿、热、卡住或打软，先看医生
  • With heart disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure, recent surgery or a fracture, or pregnancy, check with your doctor before starting; with high blood pressure or glaucoma, leave out the low stance in forms 16 and 17 (the head drops with it)有心脏病、未控制的高血压、近期手术或骨折、怀孕，开始前先问医生；高血压、青光眼的人，第 16、17 式不做低的下势（头会跟着往下低）
  • Osteoporosis or a past spinal fracture: do not bend to the floor or twist while bent; have a physiotherapist look at your movements first骨质疏松或有过椎体骨折：不弯腰够地，不弯着腰拧转；先请物理治疗师看一遍你的动作
  • Stop and call 911 for chest pain, marked breathlessness, trouble speaking, or numbness or weakness on one side; also call 911 after a fall if you cannot get up, hit your head or take a blood thinner练的时候出现胸痛、明显气短、说不出话、一侧手脚发麻无力，马上停下，拨 911；摔倒后站不起来、撞到头或正在吃抗凝药，也拨 911

📜 Where it comes from从哪里来

The popular story is that a Daoist, Zhang Sanfeng, invented tai chi. The early texts that can actually be checked say something else. In the early Qing, Huang Zongxi wrote an epitaph for the boxer Wang Zhengnan about 'internal' boxing, which 'uses stillness to control movement, so that attackers fall at a touch'; he says it 'seems to begin with Zhang Sanfeng of the Song', who 'dreamed at night that the Dark Emperor taught him boxing'. That is a dream reported in an epitaph, about internal boxing; the words 'taijiquan' do not appear in it. The Draft History of Qing adds a separate sentence: 'In the middle of the Qing there was taijiquan in Hebei; it is said that its methods came from Wang Zongyue of Shanxi... By the end of the Qing a great many people practised it.' Note the 'it is said': even the official historians reported hearsay. When UNESCO put taijiquan on its Representative List in 2020, it gave the origin as the mid-seventeenth century in Wen County, Henan. The simplified form most people learn today was put together in the mid-1950s, when China's sports commission had teachers pick representative movements from Yang-style tai chi (a Chinese university textbook gives 1955, English sources usually 1956). It has 24 movements, hence the name; shorter 8-form and 16-form sets followed around 2000. It was made for the general public, which is why it is learnable.太极拳从哪里来，流传最广的说法是张三丰创拳。能查到的早期文字，说的其实是另一回事：清初黄宗羲给拳师王征南写墓志铭，讲的是「内家」拳，「以静制动，犯者应手即仆」，并说它「盖起于宋之张三峰」，「夜梦玄帝授之拳法」。一篇墓志里的梦，说的是内家拳，全文没有「太极拳」三个字。《清史稿》在内家拳之后另起一句：「清中叶，河北有太极拳，云其法出于山西王宗岳，其法式论解，与百家之言相出入。至清末，传习者颇众云。」注意那个「云」字：连史官也只是说「据说」。联合国教科文组织 2020 年把太极拳列入人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录，写的起源是十七世纪中叶的河南温县。今天最通行的简化太极拳，是 1950 年代中期国家体委组织拳师从杨式太极拳里挑出代表动作编成的（大学体育教材写 1955 年，英文资料多写 1956 年），全套二十四个动作，所以又叫二十四式；2000 年前后又编了更短的八式、十六式。为普及而编，是它好学的原因。

🗓 How to practise怎么练

For the first two weeks practise only the commencing form and the arms of grasping the sparrow's tail, holding a chair if you like; then add one form a week, so eight forms take about two months and all 24 about six months; these are estimates, and slower is fine. Forms 13–17 include heel kicks and low stances: start with the knee-lift-only and high, no-low-stance versions on those pages and move to the full versions only when you are steady. Ten minutes a day is easier to keep up than one long session a week. For stance height, start with the knees bent about 30 degrees, and consider going lower only when your knees are not sore the next day. Trials often used two 60-minute sessions a week for 12 weeks; one used four a week for 12 months. Both classes and video self-study have been tested; if your balance is poor, have someone beside you the first few times.起步：头两周只练起势和揽雀尾的手，扶着椅子也行；之后每周加一式，八式约两个月学完，整套 24 式大约半年；这些是估计，慢一点不要紧。第 13–17 式有蹬脚和下势，先做页面上「抬膝不蹬」「不下势、只做高架」的简化做法，站稳了再换完整版。每天 10 分钟比每周练一次长的好坚持。架子高低按这条来：膝盖弯到约 30 度的高架，练到第二天膝盖不酸痛，再考虑放低。研究里常用的量是每周 2 次、每次 60 分钟，连续 12 周；也有每周 4 次、12 个月的。跟老师学或跟视频自学都有人试过有结果；平衡不好的，头几次最好有人在旁边。

Opening stance预备势

  1. Practise on a level floor in flat, non-slip shoes; keep two steps of clear space around you and three behind在平地上练，穿平底、不打滑的鞋；四周至少两步空地，身后三步
  2. Keep a sturdy chair or a counter within reach旁边放一把稳当的椅子，或靠近台面，随时能扶
  3. Warm up in place: circle the ankles, bend and straighten the knees and turn the waist a few times先原地慢慢转脚踝、屈伸膝、转腰各几次
  4. Stand with feet together, crown of the head lightly lifted, shoulders loose, breathing naturally, and go into form 1两脚并拢站好，头顶像轻轻往上提，肩放松，呼吸自然，再接第一式起势

Closing收势

  1. When you practise the whole set, form 24, the closing form, is the ending: lower the hands slowly, bring the left foot back beside the right and stand still for a few breaths练完整套，第 24 式收势就是收尾：两手慢慢落下，左脚收回并步，静站几次呼吸
  2. When you practise only the first 8 forms (or stop partway), after that form bring the weight back between the feet and set the left foot shoulder-width from the right, toes forward; bring the hands together in front of the chest in a slanting cross, turn the palms down and press slowly to the belly; straighten up slowly, let the hands fall and bring the left foot back beside the right只练前 8 式（或练到一半）的时候，做完那一式，重心收回两脚中间，左脚收到与右脚同宽、脚尖向前；两手在胸前合拢成斜十字，再翻掌向下，慢慢按到腹前；慢慢站直，两手垂到体侧，左脚收回并步
  3. Walk about for half a minute before you sit down原地走动半分钟再坐下，不要马上停

🔬 What the research says研究怎么说

  • E1 Falls: in the 2019 Cochrane review (108 randomised trials, 23,407 older people living in the community), tai chi cut the number of people who fell by about 20% (high certainty) and may cut the rate of falls by about 19% (low certainty). In the same review the evidence for balance and functional training was stronger: a 24% lower rate of falls, high certainty.跌倒：2019 年 Cochrane 综述（108 项随机试验、23,407 名社区老人）里，太极让跌倒过的人数减少约 20%（高确定性），跌倒总次数可能减少约 19%（低确定性）。同一篇综述里，平衡与功能训练的证据更硬：跌倒次数减少 24%，高确定性。 [1]
  • E1 The US Preventive Services Task Force recommended in 2024 that people 65 and older at increased risk of falls do exercise to prevent them (grade B). The recommendation covers exercise as a class, not tai chi alone.美国预防服务工作组 2024 年建议：65 岁以上、跌倒风险较高的人，用运动来预防跌倒（B 级建议）。建议讲的是运动这一大类，不是只指太极。 [2]
  • E2 Which tai chi: in a network meta-analysis of 17 trials with 3,470 people, the 24-form groups fell about 0.59 times as often as controls and ranked ahead of Yang and Sun styles. The comparison is indirect and the trials few, so it does not prove the 24-form is always better.哪一种太极：一篇 17 项试验、3470 人的网状荟萃分析里，二十四式组跌倒的比例约是对照组的六成（RR 0.59），排名在杨式、孙式之前。比较是间接的，试验数不多，不能当成「二十四式一定更好」。 [3]
  • E1 Knee and hip osteoarthritis: the American College of Rheumatology's 2019 guideline strongly recommends tai chi, alongside exercise, weight loss and self-management.膝、髋骨关节炎：美国风湿病学会 2019 年指南对太极作「强烈推荐」，与运动、减重、自我管理并列。 [4]
  • E2 Trials in knee osteoarthritis: with 204 people, tai chi twice a week for 12 weeks matched standard physical therapy on pain and function, and the gains held at 52 weeks. The 2026 RETREAT trial (178 people) had participants learn Yang-style tai chi at home from videos for 12 weeks; walking pain fell 1.4 points more (on a 0–10 scale) than with information alone. Neither reported serious adverse events linked to the practice.膝骨关节炎的试验：204 人的试验里，每周两次、12 周的太极，疼痛与功能评分和标准物理治疗改善得差不多，52 周仍保持。2026 年的 RETREAT 试验（178 人）让人在家跟视频自学杨式太极 12 周，走路时的膝痛比只看资料的人多降 1.4 分（满分 10 分）。两项都没有报告与练习有关的严重不良事件。 [5] [6]
  • E1 For context: the 2024 Cochrane review (139 trials of all kinds of exercise) concluded that exercise probably improves pain and function in knee osteoarthritis in the short term, but how much that matters to patients is uncertain. Tai chi is one kind of exercise; do not expect magic.要放回背景看：2024 年 Cochrane 综述（139 项试验，各种运动）的结论是，运动对膝骨关节炎的疼痛和功能大概有短期好处，但幅度对病人有多大意义并不确定。太极是运动的一种，别指望它比别的运动神奇。 [7]
  • E3 Knee load: in laboratory measurements of 14 professional practitioners, a low stance (knees bent about 60 degrees) loaded the knee clearly more than a high one (about 30 degrees), and Parting the Wild Horse's Mane and Step Back and Whirl the Arms loaded it more than Cloud Hands and Grasping the Sparrow's Tail. The sample is small and expert, but it gives beginners a clear rule: stand high, and do fewer of those two.膝盖的负荷：14 名专业练习者的实验室测量里，低架（膝弯约 60 度）的膝部力矩明显大于高架（约 30 度）；野马分鬃、倒卷肱的膝部负荷又比云手、揽雀尾大。样本小、都是熟手，但给了初学者一个明确的做法：站高，这两式少做。 [8]
  • E2 Insomnia: in a Hong Kong trial of 200 middle-aged and older adults with chronic insomnia, three months of tai chi did worse than cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia; only at month 15 were the two comparable. If you need better sleep soon, start with the therapy.失眠：香港 200 名中老年慢性失眠者的试验里，练三个月太极，睡眠的进步不如失眠认知行为疗法；到第 15 个月，两组才算相当。急着要睡好，先用认知行为疗法。 [9]
  • E2 Blood pressure: in 342 adults with prehypertension, 12 months of tai chi four times a week lowered systolic pressure about 2.4 mmHg more than aerobic exercise. If you take blood pressure medication, keep taking it as prescribed.血压：342 名血压偏高（未到高血压）的成年人练 12 个月、每周四次，太极组收缩压比快走等有氧运动组多降约 2.4 mmHg。已在吃降压药的人，药照常吃，别自己改。 [10]
  • E1 Uncertain: for rheumatoid arthritis, the 2019 Cochrane review rated the evidence very low quality and could not tell whether tai chi affects pain or function.没有把握的：类风湿关节炎，2019 年 Cochrane 综述认为证据质量极低，无法确定太极对疼痛和功能有没有作用。 [11]
  • E2 Safety: a meta-analysis of 24 randomised trials (1,794 people) found no more adverse events with tai chi than control; in trials that actively recorded them, the tai chi groups had slightly more minor complaints than inactive controls.安全：一篇汇总 24 项随机试验、1794 人的荟萃分析没有发现太极组的不良事件比对照多；在有专人记录不良事件的试验里，太极组的轻微不适比不运动的对照略多。 [12]
  • E5 Retraction notice: a trial reporting that the 24-form helped older women with knee osteoarthritis (2010, J Clin Rheumatol) was retracted by the journal the same year; this page does not use its conclusions. A PubMed title search for tai chi in November 2026 found 5 retracted papers; the other four concern the lumbar spine, tai chi softball, mild cognitive impairment and cognition in diabetes.撤稿提醒：一篇讲「练二十四式让膝骨关节炎老年女性功能变好」的试验（2010，J Clin Rheumatol）同一年就被期刊撤稿，本页不引用它的结论。2026 年 11 月在 PubMed 按题名检索太极，共查到 5 篇被撤稿的论文，另外 4 篇分别讲腰椎、柔力球、轻度认知障碍和糖尿病认知。 [13] [14] [15]
  • E5 The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) says tai chi appears to be safe; the reported problems were mostly minor muscle and joint aches.美国国家补充与整合健康中心（NCCIH）的页面认为太极看起来是安全的，报告过的不适多是轻微的肌肉、关节酸痛。 [16]

🏛 The traditional view传统怎么看

🏛 Traditional teaching says 'the crown lightly lifted, the breath sinking to the lower belly; leaning neither way, now hidden, now seen' (the Taijiquan Treatise attributed to Wang Zongyue), and 'step like a cat, move as if drawing silk'. The treatise was about sensing an opponent in contact; people today borrow it to train their own bodies. These are teaching cues, not research findings.传统教拳讲「虚领顶劲，气沉丹田。不偏不倚，忽隐忽现」（题王宗岳《太极拳论》）：头顶轻轻上领，气往小腹沉，身体不歪不斜。还讲「迈步如猫行，运劲如抽丝」。拳论本来是讲两人交手时怎么感觉对方，今天的人借它来练自己的身体。这些是传统的教学语言，不是研究结论。
