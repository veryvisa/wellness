Posture体位 · Supine with knees flexed and the abdomen relaxed; measure between the umbilicus and pubic symphysis using the patient's proportions, not the practitioner's finger width.仰卧屈膝、腹部放松；以脐和耻骨联合为端点折量，不将施术者手指宽当作患者寸。

The bar is the standard span between two landmarks, divided into cun; the dot is where the point falls by definition.横条是两个标志之间的标准寸数，等分后穴位按定义落在圆点处。 How proportional measurement works →骨度分寸怎么用 →

  1. 📏
    Proportional bone measure骨度分寸

    The umbilicus-to-upper-pubic-symphysis span is 5 cun; CV6 is 1.5 cun below the umbilicus, or 3.5 cun above the symphysis.脐中至耻骨联合上缘为 5 寸；气海在脐下 1.5 寸，即从耻骨联合上缘向上 3.5 寸。

  2. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Measure one and a half cun below the umbilicus on the midline, below CV8 and above CV5; cross-check any finger estimate with proportional measurement.沿前正中线从脐向下量一寸半，位于神阙下方、石门上方；短距离手指估量后须用骨度折量复核。

  3. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The linea alba and abdominal-wall tissue are superficial, with bowel and bladder deeper. Confirm the midline superficially and avoid lateral vessels, scars and any visible hernia.浅层为腹白线和腹壁软组织，深部可邻肠管与膀胱；保持正中浅层确认，避开腹壁外侧血管、瘢痕及可见疝包块。

  4. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Gently confirm the midline and proportion below the umbilicus; muscle guarding, distension or a full bladder alters palpation, so relax or empty first.轻柔触诊确认中线及脐下比例；腹肌紧张、腹胀或膀胱充盈会影响触感，先放松或排空后再评估。

Diarrhoea泄泻 ⚖️

An adjunct for chronic loose stools with fatigue and traditional qi-deficiency features; acute bloody diarrhea, fever or dehydration needs medical care.久泻伴乏力、传统气虚表现时可作辅助；急性血便、发热或脱水时不以此代替治疗。

Abdominal pain腹痛 ⚖️

Consider for chronic or deficient infraumbilical midline pain; focal acute pain or peritoneal signs need urgent evaluation, not point treatment.脐下中线、偏虚或慢性表现时考虑；急性定位痛或腹膜刺激征不选穴处理，先排急腹症。

Where is CV6 Qìhǎi?气海（CV6）在哪里？

On the lower abdomen, 1.5 cun below the centre of the umbilicus, on the anterior midline.下腹部，脐中下 1.5 寸，前正中线上。

How is CV6 needled?气海（CV6）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of CV6?气海（CV6）是哪类特定穴？

Source point of the huang (membranes)肓之原

Main safety point at CV6?气海（CV6）最要注意的安全问题？

No needling in pregnancy (textbook rule: no lower-abdominal needling in the first 3 months; after 3 months avoid the abdomen and lumbosacral region as well); empty the bladder first (a full bladder rises above the pubic symphysis against the abdominal wall). Go shallower in a thin abdominal wall.孕妇禁针（教材针刺注意事项：妊娠 3 个月内小腹部禁针，3 个月以上腹部、腰骶部亦不宜针）；针前排空膀胱（充盈膀胱可越过耻骨联合贴近腹壁）。腹壁薄者减浅。
