🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is LI4 Hégǔ?合谷（LI4）在哪里？

On the dorsum of the hand, between the first and second metacarpals, at the radial side of the midpoint of the second metacarpal.手背，第 1、2 掌骨之间，约平第 2 掌骨中点的桡侧。

How is LI4 needled?合谷（LI4）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or oblique toward the second metacarpal; 0.5–1 cun.直刺或向第 2 掌骨方向斜刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of LI4?合谷（LI4）是哪类特定穴？

Yuan-source point; Four Command Points rhyme: face and mouth at LI4原穴；四总穴歌：「面口合谷收」

Main safety point at LI4?合谷（LI4）最要注意的安全问题？

Contraindicated in pregnancy; traditional use is associated with inducing labour. Avoid dorsal hand veins and the superficial radial nerve; withdraw and redirect if shooting tingling occurs.孕期禁针；其传统用法与引产相关。避开手背静脉和桡神经浅支，出现放射样麻感应退针调整。
