Cinnamon Twig Decoction桂枝汤

Guìzhī Tāng · Zhang Zhongjing, Shanghan Lun (Eastern Han), Taiyang disease, part one东汉·张仲景《伤寒论》太阳病上篇

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Cinnamon Twig Decoction桂枝汤

Original text原文

「太陽中風，陽浮而陰弱，陽浮者，熱自發，陰弱者，汗自出。嗇嗇惡寒，淅淅惡風，翕翕發熱，鼻鳴乾嘔者，桂枝湯主之」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Taiyang wind strike: the pulse floats at the surface and is weak beneath; it floats because of fever, it is weak because sweat comes by itself; the person shivers, dislikes wind, has a mild fever, a snuffling nose and dry retching: Gui Zhi Tang governs it.太阳中风：脉象浮在外而按下去弱，浮是因为发热，弱是因为自汗出；人怕冷、怕风，微微发热，鼻塞有声、干呕——用桂枝汤。

「桂枝三兩（去皮）芍藥三兩甘草二兩（炙）生薑三兩（切）大棗十二枚（擘）」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

Ingredients and original doses.组成与原书剂量。

「服已須臾，啜熱稀粥一升餘，以助藥力。溫覆令一時許，遍身漐漐微似有汗者益佳，不可令如水流漓，病必不除」——伤寒论

Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage)

After the dose drink a large bowl of hot thin porridge to help the medicine, stay covered and warm for about two hours, and a light all-over moisture is best; a pouring sweat means the illness will not go. Zhang wrote how to take it as carefully as what to take.服药后喝一大碗热稀粥帮助药力，盖被保暖约两小时，全身微微潮润出汗最好；大汗淋漓反而病不去。仲景把「怎么服」写得和「服什么」一样细。

Actions and indications (traditional)功效与主治（传统）

🏛 Traditional statements, not efficacy claims.传统说法，不是疗效主张。

  • Releases the muscle layer and the exterior解肌发表
  • Harmonises nutritive and defensive qi调和营卫

Wind-cold exterior deficiency: dislike of wind, fever, sweating, headache, snuffling nose and dry retching, white tongue coating without thirst, a floating moderate or floating weak pulse.外感风寒表虚证：恶风发热，汗出，头痛，鼻鸣干呕，苔白不渴，脉浮缓或浮弱。

How the formula is built方解

The key is that the person is already sweating. With the same wind-cold, no sweating calls for Ma Huang Tang to open the surface; sweating means the surface qi is already weak and nutritive and defensive qi are out of step, and Ma Huang would only add more sweat. Gui Zhi scatters the pathogen from the defensive layer while peony holds in the nutritive yin, one dispersing and one astringing; ginger helps the twig, jujube helps the peony, and honey-fried licorice keeps the middle. Hot porridge and a light sweat under covers are part of the prescription. Later writers called it 'the head of all formulas', and dozens of formulas grew from it: Gui Zhi Jia Ge Gen Tang, Gui Zhi Jia Fu Zi Tang, Xiao Jian Zhong Tang, Chai Hu Gui Zhi Tang and more.关键在「有汗」：同是风寒感冒，无汗用麻黄汤开表，有汗说明表气已虚、营卫失和，再用麻黄会汗出更多。桂枝散卫分之邪、芍药敛营分之阴，一散一收；生姜助桂、大枣助芍，炙甘草居中调和。服后喝热粥、盖被取微汗，是方子的一部分。后世称它「群方之魁」，加减出桂枝加葛根汤、桂枝加附子汤、小建中汤、柴胡桂枝汤等数十首方。

Modern research, graded现代研究（分级）

  • E4 Modern work on Gui Zhi Tang is mostly animal studies (two-way effects on body temperature and sweat glands); well-designed human randomised trials are few. It is a common clinical formula for colds and sweating disorders, but the evidence level is experience and small observational series.桂枝汤的现代研究以动物实验为主（体温与汗腺的双向调节等），设计良好的人体随机试验很少；临床上它是感冒、汗证的常用方，但证据级别属于经验与小样本观察。

Cautions使用注意

Not for exterior excess without sweating, wind-heat, early warm disease or internal damp-heat; avoid raw and cold food, sticky and greasy food, meat and noodles, pungent vegetables, alcohol and dairy while taking it (as the original says). See a doctor first for fever beyond three days, high fever, breathing difficulty or confusion.表实无汗、外感风热、温病初起、内有湿热者不宜用；服药期间禁生冷、黏滑、肉面、五辛、酒酪（原书）。发热超过三天、高热、呼吸困难、意识改变，先就医。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Contains Gui Zhi (pharmacopoeia: caution in pregnancy); in pregnancy leave it to a licensed practitioner.含桂枝（药典孕妇慎用），孕期由持照中医师判断。

Exam notes考点

Exam essentials: (1) versus Ma Huang Tang (sweating or not, exterior deficiency or excess); (2) why Gui Zhi and peony are equal; (3) how to take it (hot porridge, warm covers, light sweat, stop when it works); (4) its family of formulas.PRA/HER 必考：①与麻黄汤的鉴别（有汗无汗、表虚表实）；②桂枝芍药等量的配伍意义；③服法（啜热粥、温覆、微汗、中病即止）；④「群方之魁」与类方。
