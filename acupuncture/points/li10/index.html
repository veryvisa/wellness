Where is LI10 Shǒusānlǐ?手三里（LI10）在哪里？

On the radial dorsal forearm, 2 cun below the elbow crease, on the line from LI11 to LI5.前臂背面桡侧，阳溪与曲池连线上，肘横纹下 2 寸。

How is LI10 needled?手三里（LI10）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.8–1.2 cun.直刺；0.8–1.2 寸。

Main safety point at LI10?手三里（LI10）最要注意的安全问题？

Radial nerve branches lie deep; withdraw and redirect if an electric radiating sensation occurs.深部有桡神经分支；若有电击样放射感，退针调整。
