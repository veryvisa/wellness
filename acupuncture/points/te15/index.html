📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit with shoulders relaxed and arms hanging naturally; do not shrug or raise the arms, which moves the scapula.坐位，肩部放松下垂，双臂自然垂放；不可耸肩或抬臂，以免肩胛骨移位。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Palpate the superior angle of the scapula, then locate the adjacent bony hollow near the midpoint between GB21 and SI13. The scapular angle is more reliable than the highest point of the shoulder.先触到肩胛骨上角，在肩井（GB21）与曲垣（SI13）连线中点附近、肩胛上角骨际的凹陷中定位；上角比肩上最高点更可靠。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Gently palpate the superior scapular angle and adjacent upper-medial border; the point is a superficial hollow beside palpable bone. Trapezius and levator scapulae are superficial to dorsal scapular neurovascular structures; the pleural apex and lung apex lie deep inferomedially. Any insertion must remain shallow and oblique along the scapular surface inferolaterally, never deeply inferomedially toward the thorax or lung.轻触肩胛骨上角及其内上方骨缘，穴位落在可触骨缘旁的浅层凹陷。深层有斜方肌、提肩胛肌，邻肩胛背神经血管；内下方深面接近胸膜顶与肺尖。仅允许沿肩胛骨面向外下方浅斜，绝不可向内下、胸腔或肺尖方向深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the shoulder relaxed, trace upward along the medial scapular spine to the superior angle and gently palpate the adjacent hollow. Reposition if the location depends only on tense shoulder muscles or the acromion.肩放松时沿肩胛冈内侧向上找到肩胛上角，指腹轻压骨缘旁凹陷；若只能依赖肩部肌肉紧张或肩峰位置定位，需重新摆位。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Use the midpoint of the GB21–SI13 line only as a rough cross-check, then confirm the superior scapular angle. A measured line cannot replace bony confirmation or assessment of the pleural-risk direction.在GB21与SI13连线上取中点作粗略交叉核对，再以肩胛上角骨缘确认；单独量线不能替代骨性标志和胸膜风险方向判断。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Using the shoulder summit or GB21 as TE15 shifts the point superolaterally; failure to identify the superior scapular angle followed by deep inferomedial insertion may reach the pleural or lung apex and cause pneumothorax. Only shallow inferolateral insertion along the scapula is acceptable; chest pain, breathlessness, or pain on inhalation requires emergency care.把肩上最高点或肩井直接当作天髎，会偏外上；未摸准肩胛上角而向内下方直刺，可能穿向胸膜顶或肺尖导致气胸。仅可沿肩胛骨面向外下浅斜，胸痛、气短或吸气痛须立即急诊。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Shoulder pain肩痛（漏肩风） ⚖️

Consider TE15 for tenderness beside the superior scapular angle between GB21 and SI13; acromial or lateral-deltoid pain is closer to TE14 or LI15, and TE15 carries substantial needling risk.压痛位于肩胛上角骨缘旁、肩井与曲垣之间时可辨证选天髎；肩峰或三角肌外侧痛更近肩髎、肩髃等穴，且天髎刺法风险高。

Stiff and painful neck颈项强痛（落枕、项痹） ⚖️

May be a local adjunct for neck pain with superior-scapular-angle tenderness and levator tension; isolated midline cervical pain or radicular symptoms are not primarily a TE15 indication.颈项痛伴肩胛上角压痛和肩胛提拉肌紧张时可作局部配穴；单纯颈椎中线痛或神经根症状不以天髎为首选。

Medial scapular pain with impaired scapular control needs differentiation among nerve, tendon, and cervical causes; do not self-needle or deeply press the pleural-risk area.肩胛内侧缘痛伴肩胛控制无力需鉴别神经、肌腱和颈源性疼痛；不能在胸膜风险区自行深压或针刺。

Needling-related pneumothorax risk针刺相关气胸风险

The pleural and lung apices lie deep inferomedially to TE15; even accurate localization can result in pneumothorax if direction or depth is wrong.天髎内下方深层邻胸膜顶和肺尖；即使定位准确，错误方向或过深仍可能造成气胸。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • After needling, new chest pain, shortness of breath, worsening pain on inhalation, persistent cough, or blue lips requires calling 911 and emergency assessment for pneumothorax.针刺后出现新发胸痛、呼吸困难、吸气痛加重、持续咳嗽或口唇发绀，立即拨打 911 并急诊评估气胸。
  • Prompt care is needed for progressive arm weakness, sensory loss, or marked scapular winging after shoulder or neck trauma.肩颈外伤后上肢进行性无力、感觉缺失或肩胛骨明显翘起，应尽快就医。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is TE15 Tiānliáo?天髎（TE15）在哪里？

Suprascapular region, in the depression at the superior angle of scapula; with shoulders relaxed, midway between GB21 and SI13.肩胛上区，肩胛骨上角骨际凹陷中；正坐垂肩时，在肩井（GB21）与曲垣（SI13）连线中点。

How is TE15 needled?天髎（TE15）怎么刺？

Oblique inferolaterally along the scapular surface; 0.5–0.8 cun.向外下方斜刺，沿肩胛骨面；0.5–0.8寸。

Special-point roles of TE15?天髎（TE15）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Triple Energizer, Gallbladder, and Yang Wei vessel手少阳、足少阳、阳维脉交会穴

Main safety point at TE15?天髎（TE15）最要注意的安全问题？

The pleural apex and lung can lie inferomedially. Needle only inferolaterally along the scapular surface, never deeply inferomedially. Chest pain, shortness of breath, or pain on breathing after needling is an emergency: get care now (911 in BC).内下方深面可能到达胸膜顶和肺；仅可沿肩胛骨面向外下斜刺，绝不可向内下深刺。针后胸痛、气短或吸气痛为急症，立即就医（BC拨911）。
