📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Sit or lie with the head turned to the opposite side; gently open the mouth to change mandibular position, with neck and shoulders relaxed.坐位或仰卧，头转向对侧，轻轻张口使下颌支位置变化；肩颈放松。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    In the hollow behind the earlobe and anterior to the lower mastoid. With the mouth gently open, confirm the shallow depression bounded by the earlobe, lower mastoid, and posterior mandibular ramus.在耳垂后方、乳突下端前方的凹陷中；轻张口后以耳垂、乳突下缘和下颌支后缘三者围成的浅凹陷复核。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    The hollow behind the earlobe, in front of and below the mastoid, overlies the stylomastoid foramen, where the facial nerve trunk leaves the skull and enters the parotid. The great auricular nerve and posterior auricular vessels are superficial; the styloid process, internal jugular vein and internal carotid artery lie deeper. Limit insertion to 0.5–1 cun, never deep and forward-medially; withdraw at once at facial twitching or sharp radiating pain, and take care in people prone to bleeding.耳垂后、乳突前下方的凹陷，深面是茎乳孔——面神经干就在这里出颅，进入腮腺。浅层有耳大神经、耳后血管；再深是茎突、颈内静脉与颈内动脉。直刺 0.5–1 寸为限，不可向前内深刺，出现面部抽动或剧烈放射痛立即退针；有出血倾向者慎针。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    With the mouth slightly open, gently palpate the natural hollow behind the earlobe and anterior to the mastoid. Stop and assess if there is a clear arterial pulse, parotid mass, or unusual tenderness.让患者轻张口并以指腹轻压乳突前、耳垂后的自然凹陷；若触及明显动脉搏动、腮腺肿块或压痛异常，应停止操作并评估。

  4. 👌
    Simple method简便取穴

    Find the hollow bounded by the lower earlobe and mastoid inferiorly and the posterior mandibular ramus anteriorly; it is behind the ear, not the preauricular condylar region.以耳垂下缘和乳突下端为上下界、下颌支后缘为前界找凹陷；位置在耳后而不是耳前髁突区。

⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Facial palsy面瘫（口眼歪斜） ⚖️

Traditionally used for facial deviation, but new palsy first requires stroke exclusion; postauricular tenderness supports TE17 only after peripheral facial-nerve disease is assessed.传统用于口眼歪斜，但新发面瘫须先排除卒中；若面神经周围性病变已评估，翳风的耳后局部压痛才支持选用。

Tinnitus and hearing loss耳鸣耳聋 ⚖️

Consider TE17 for a tender postauricular hollow with periauricular or cheek symptoms; preauricular condylar pain is closer to TE21 or SI19, and sudden hearing loss is urgent.耳后凹陷压痛、伴耳周或面颊不适时可考虑翳风；耳前髁突痛更近耳门/听宫，突发听力下降先急诊。

Toothache牙痛 ⚖️

May be an adjacent point for jaw symptoms with restricted opening near the posterior ramus; clear dental-source pain needs dental care first, and facial swelling with fever is urgent.耳周或下颌支后缘伴口张不利的牙颌症状可作邻近配穴；明确牙源性肿痛应先牙科处理，面部肿胀发热要急诊。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Parotitis or parotid-duct obstruction腮腺炎或腮腺导管阻塞

Painful swelling in the parotid region below and anterior to the earlobe, worse with meals, may reflect inflammation or obstruction; fever, spreading redness, or trismus needs prompt medical assessment.耳垂前下方腮腺区肿痛、进食时加重可见腺体炎症或阻塞；发热、红肿扩散、张口困难需及时医疗评估。

Peripheral facial nerve palsy面神经周围性麻痹

Incomplete eye closure, loss of forehead movement, or weak mouth corner needs prompt differentiation of central and peripheral causes; new facial palsy warrants same-day assessment.闭眼不全、额纹消失或口角无力需及时判断中枢与周围性原因；面瘫新发须当天评估。

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is TE17 Yìfēng?翳风（TE17）在哪里？

Neck, in the hollow posterior to the earlobe and anterior to the lower end of mastoid process.颈部，耳垂后方、乳突下端前方凹陷中。

How is TE17 needled?翳风（TE17）怎么刺？

Perpendicular or slightly oblique anterosuperiorly; 0.5–1 cun.直刺或微向前上方斜刺；0.5–1寸。

Special-point roles of TE17?翳风（TE17）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Triple Energizer and Gallbladder channels手少阳、足少阳交会穴

Main safety point at TE17?翳风（TE17）最要注意的安全问题？

The parotid, facial nerve, and external carotid-related structures lie deep; do not blindly needle deeply or repeatedly stimulate strongly, and avoid visible vessels.深面是腮腺与面神经、颈外动脉相关结构；不可盲目深刺或反复强刺激，避开可见血管。
