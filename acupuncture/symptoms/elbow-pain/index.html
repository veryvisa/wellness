Elbow and forearm pain肘臂痛（肘劳）

Elbow strain (zhoulao) is elbow pain from repeated forearm effort. The textbook has a single core point: the most tender ashi point. The key differentiation is the side of the tenderness — lateral (lateral epicondyle, tennis elbow) belongs to the Hand Yangming sinew channel, medial (medial epicondyle, golfer's elbow) to the Hand Taiyang sinew channel, and posterior (olecranon) to the Hand Shaoyang sinew channel — and local and distal points are added for that sinew channel. Finger numbness or muscle wasting is not elbow strain but nerve entrapment, and needs separate assessment.肘劳是前臂反复用力劳损引起的肘部疼痛。教材的主穴只有一个：压痛最明显的阿是穴；辨证的核心是压痛在哪一侧——外侧（肱骨外上髁，网球肘）属手阳明经筋，内侧（肱骨内上髁，高尔夫球肘）属手太阳经筋，肘后（尺骨鹰嘴）属手少阳经筋，再按经筋配局部与远端穴。手指麻木、肌肉萎缩不是肘劳，是神经卡压，要另查。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • After a fall, elbow deformity, severe swelling, inability to bend or straighten, or cold, pale, numb fingers (especially dangerous in a child's supracondylar fracture): emergency now跌倒后肘部变形、剧烈肿痛、不能屈伸，或手指冰冷、发白、麻木（儿童肱骨髁上骨折尤其危险）：立刻急诊
  • A red, hot swelling over the point of the elbow, or a red hot swollen joint with fever, a wound, diabetes or a weak immune system: same-day assessment for septic bursitis or arthritis肘后鹰嘴处鼓起红热肿痛，或肘关节红肿发热伴发烧、有伤口、糖尿病或免疫低下：当天就医，排除化脓性滑囊炎或关节炎
  • Numb little and ring fingers, wasting of small hand muscles, weak pinch, or numbness of the thumb to middle finger worse at night: see a doctor soon for cubital tunnel, carpal tunnel or cervical radiculopathy, not just tennis elbow小指和环指麻木、手部小肌肉萎缩、捏东西无力，或拇指到中指麻木并夜间加重：尽快就医，查肘管综合征、腕管综合征或颈神经根病，不要只按网球肘处理
  • Left arm or inner elbow pain with chest tightness, breathlessness or sweating, brought on by exertion: call 911, this may be referred pain from angina or a heart attack左臂或肘内侧痛同时有胸闷、气短、出汗，活动时诱发：拨 911，可能是心绞痛或心肌梗死的牵涉痛
  • Sudden red, very painful, shiny swelling of the elbow, often at night, with a history of gout: same-day assessment to confirm gout or pseudogout before treatment肘关节突然红肿剧痛、夜间发作、皮肤发亮，有痛风史：当天就医，确认痛风或假性痛风后再处理

「肩肘中痛，難屈伸，手不可舉，腕重急，曲池主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi jing: pain in the shoulder and elbow, difficulty bending and straightening, inability to lift the hand, heavy tight wrist — LI11 governs it. LI11 lies at the lateral end of the elbow crease, right over the tennis-elbow area, and is the most used local point for the Hand Yangming sinew type.《针灸甲乙经》：肩、肘中疼痛，屈伸困难，手抬不起来，手腕沉重拘急，以曲池为主治穴。曲池在肘横纹外侧端，正当网球肘的痛区，是手阳明经筋证最常用的局部穴。

「肘痛，尺澤主之」——皇甫谧《针灸甲乙经》卷三（公版）

Jiayi jing: elbow pain — LU5 governs it. LU5 lies on the elbow crease on the radial side of the biceps tendon, at the front of the elbow; it is used for anterior pain and pain on flexion, while lateral tennis elbow still centres on LI11, LI12 and LI10.《针灸甲乙经》：肘痛，以尺泽为主治穴。尺泽在肘横纹上、肱二头肌腱桡侧，偏肘前；肘前痛、屈肘痛时取之，外侧网球肘仍以曲池、肘髎、手三里为主。

Hand Yangming sinew type (lateral elbow, tennis elbow)手阳明经筋证（肘外侧痛，网球肘）

How it presents辨证要点 Tenderness at the lateral epicondyle, worse wringing a towel, lifting a kettle, shaking hands or extending the wrist against resistance, sometimes spreading down the outer forearm; common with repetitive wrist-extension work or sport.肘外侧肱骨外上髁处压痛，拧毛巾、提壶、握手、伸腕用力时疼痛加重，可向前臂外侧放散；多见于反复伸腕用力的工作或运动。

LI11 Qūchí曲池

Best for最适合 Lateral elbow pain and stiffness; the most used local point for tennis elbow肘外侧痛、屈伸不利，网球肘最常用局部穴

Why依据 Hand Yangming he-sea point, midway between the lateral end of the elbow crease and the lateral epicondyle with the elbow flexed手阳明合穴，屈肘时肘横纹外侧端与外上髁连线中点

Versus others与其他穴的区别 At the crease rather than above it like LI12, and at the tender spot rather than below it like LI10; if tenderness is 3–4 cm below the epicondyle and worse with resisted middle-finger extension, think of radial tunnel syndrome比肘髎偏肘横纹处，比手三里偏痛点本身；痛点在外上髁远端 3–4 厘米、伸中指抗阻痛更明显时要想到桡管综合征

LI12 Zhǒuliáo肘髎

Best for最适合 Tenderness just above the lateral epicondyle, when the tennis-elbow spot sits high外上髁上方压痛，网球肘痛点偏上

Why依据 Hand Yangming point 1 cun above and lateral to LI11 at the humeral border; the textbook's added point for the Hand Yangming sinew type手阳明经，曲池外上方 1 寸、肱骨边缘；教材手阳明经筋证配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Higher than LI11 and against the lateral humerus; the radial nerve is nearby deep, so withdraw and redirect at radiating tingling比曲池偏上、贴近肱骨外缘；深部附近有桡神经，出现放射样麻感退针调整

LI10 Shǒusānlǐ手三里

Best for最适合 Lateral elbow pain spreading into the forearm extensors, weak grip肘外侧痛向前臂伸肌放散、握物无力

Why依据 Hand Yangming point 2 cun below LI11; the textbook's added point for the Hand Yangming sinew type手阳明经，曲池下 2 寸；教材手阳明经筋证配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the forearm muscle belly than LI11, suited to pain spreading downward; the deep radial (posterior interosseous) nerve lies beneath, so insert 1–1.5 cun and withdraw at tingling比曲池偏前臂肌腹，适合痛向下放散者；深部有桡神经深支（骨间后神经），直刺 1–1.5 寸遇麻感即退

LI4 Hégǔ合谷

Best for最适合 Distal point for the Yangming and Shaoyang sinew types, also for wrist and hand pain手阳明、手少阳经筋证的远端配穴，兼腕手痛

Why依据 Hand Yangming yuan point; moves qi and stops pain手阳明原穴；行气止痛

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Not on the elbow, it works only with local points; more for the Yangming (lateral) side than TE5, which covers the Shaoyang (posterior) side; contraindicated in pregnancy不在肘部，配局部穴才有针对性；比外关偏阳明（外侧），外关偏少阳（肘后）；孕妇禁针

SI8 Xiǎohǎi小海

Best for最适合 Medial and ulnar-side elbow pain (golfer's elbow, posteromedial pain)肘内侧、尺侧痛（高尔夫球肘、肘内后方痛）

Why依据 Hand Taiyang he-sea point in the ulnar groove between the olecranon and the medial epicondyle手太阳合穴，尺骨鹰嘴与肱骨内上髁之间的尺神经沟中

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Behind HT3 and directly over the ulnar nerve: needle only 0.3–0.5 cun and withdraw at once if an electric sensation shoots to the little finger; if the little finger is already numb, check for cubital tunnel syndrome first比少海偏后，正在尺神经上：只浅刺 0.3–0.5 寸，出现向小指的触电感立即退针；小指麻木已存在时先查肘管综合征

HT3 Shàohǎi少海

Best for最适合 Medial elbow pain with tenderness in front of the medial epicondyle肘内侧痛、内上髁前方压痛

Why依据 Hand Shaoyin he-sea point, midway between the medial end of the elbow crease and the medial epicondyle with the elbow flexed手少阴合穴，屈肘时肘横纹内侧端与内上髁连线中点

Versus others与其他穴的区别 In front of SI8 and a little farther from the ulnar nerve, so better as the local medial point; do not angle behind the medial epicondyle and avoid the basilic vein比小海靠前、离尺神经稍远，更适合做内侧痛的局部穴；针尖不向内上髁后方偏，避开贵要静脉

SI5 Yánggǔ阳谷

Best for最适合 Distal point for the Hand Taiyang sinew type, also for ulnar-side wrist pain手太阳经筋证的远端配穴，兼腕尺侧痛

Why依据 Hand Taiyang jing-river point on the ulnar back of the wrist, in the hollow between the ulnar styloid and triquetrum手太阳经穴，腕背尺侧、尺骨茎突与三角骨之间凹陷

Versus others与其他穴的区别 A distal channel point used with SI8 and HT3; closer to the wrist than SI3, so preferred when the ulnar wrist also hurts远端循经，配小海、少海用；比后溪更靠近腕部，腕尺侧也痛时优先

TE10 Tiānjǐng天井

Best for最适合 Pain above the olecranon, painful pushing and straightening肘后鹰嘴上方痛、伸肘撑物痛

Why依据 Hand Shaoyang he-sea point in the hollow 1 cun above the olecranon tip with the elbow flexed手少阳合穴，屈肘时鹰嘴尖上 1 寸凹陷

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Covers the back of the elbow while LI11 covers the outer side; a red hot lump over the olecranon needs bursitis and infection ruled out first, and is never needled into与曲池分管肘后和肘外侧；鹰嘴处鼓包红热先查滑囊炎和感染，不在肿胀处进针

TE5 Wàiguān外关

Best for最适合 Distal point for the Hand Shaoyang sinew type, also for pain on the back of the forearm手少阳经筋证远端配穴，兼前臂背侧痛

Why依据 Hand Shaoyang luo point, confluent point of the Yang Linking vessel手少阳络穴，八脉交会通阳维脉

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for the Shaoyang back of the elbow and forearm than LI4; as a distal point, the patient can slowly bend and straighten the elbow while it is needled比合谷偏少阳肘后与前臂背侧；作为远端穴，可在针刺时让患者缓慢屈伸肘关节

LU5 Chǐzé尺泽

Best for最适合 Anterior elbow pain, pain on flexion (near the distal biceps tendon)肘前痛、屈肘痛（肱二头肌腱远端附近）

Why依据 Hand Taiyin he-sea point; Jiayi: 'elbow pain, LU5 governs it'手太阴合穴；《甲乙经》「肘痛，尺泽主之」

Versus others与其他穴的区别 For the front rather than the outer elbow, unlike LI11; the radial nerve is deep, and drifting medial to the tendon brings the brachial artery and median nerve close; sudden front-of-elbow pain with a 'pop' needs a distal biceps rupture ruled out比曲池偏肘前而非外侧；深部有桡神经，偏到肌腱内侧会接近肱动脉和正中神经；肘前突发剧痛伴「啪」一声要查肱二头肌腱断裂

GB34 Yánglíngquán阳陵泉

Best for最适合 Distal sinew point for tight, repeatedly strained elbow sinews肘部筋脉拘急、反复劳损的远端舒筋配穴

Why依据 Influential point of sinews, Foot Shaoyang he-sea point筋会，足少阳合穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Not on an arm channel, it relaxes sinews as their influential point and is only supportive; it lacks the channel targeting of LI4 or TE5, so it is added only when several sinews are tight or strained不循上肢经络，靠筋会舒筋，属辅助；比合谷、外关少了循经针对性，只在多处筋伤、筋紧时加

ST36 Zúsānlǐ足三里

Best for最适合 Long-standing recurrent pain with weak grip from qi and blood deficiency久病反复、握力差的气血亏虚

Why依据 Stomach he-sea point; tonifies qi and blood胃经合穴；补益气血

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Treats the root rather than local pain and must be combined with elbow points; not needed in an acute strain治本不治局部痛，须与肘部穴同用；急性劳损期不需要

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

Lateral epicondylitis (tennis elbow) is a degenerative tendinopathy of the common extensor origin, especially extensor carpi radialis brevis, rather than true inflammation; it hurts with resisted wrist extension (Cozen test) and resisted middle-finger extension. Medial epicondylitis (golfer's elbow) is a tendinopathy of the common flexor–pronator origin and hurts with resisted wrist flexion. Most cases settle within 6–12 months with fewer provoking movements, a forearm strap and eccentric strengthening; steroid injections relieve pain faster in the short term but leave worse results at one year than waiting. Differentials include radial tunnel syndrome (posterior interosseous nerve entrapment, tender below the epicondyle), cubital tunnel syndrome (ulnar nerve: numb little and ulnar ring finger, worse with the elbow bent, positive Tinel sign), olecranon bursitis, elbow osteoarthritis or gout, and C6–C7 radiculopathy referring to the elbow. Acupuncture has evidence for short-term pain relief in tennis elbow and fits alongside exercise.肱骨外上髁炎（网球肘）是前臂伸肌总腱、特别是桡侧腕短伸肌起点的退行性腱病，不是真正的炎症，伸腕抗阻（Cozen 试验）和伸中指抗阻时痛；肱骨内上髁炎（高尔夫球肘）是屈肌—旋前圆肌总腱起点的腱病，屈腕抗阻时痛。两者多数在 6–12 个月内自行缓解，主要靠减少诱发动作、前臂护带和离心力量训练；激素注射短期止痛快，但一年后的结果反而比观察更差。要鉴别的有：桡管综合征（骨间后神经卡压，压痛在外上髁远端）、肘管综合征（尺神经，小指和环指尺侧麻、屈肘加重、Tinel 征阳性）、鹰嘴滑囊炎、肘关节骨关节炎或痛风，以及颈椎 C6、C7 神经根病牵涉到肘。针刺对网球肘有短期止痛的证据，适合配合训练使用。
