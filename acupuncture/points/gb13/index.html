⚖️ When to choose this point and not another同症鉴别：什么时候选它、不选别的

Headache头痛 ⚖️

Choose GB13 with GB20 and LR3 for frontal or temporal headache linked to emotional stress or poor sleep; do not rely on GB13 first for sudden severe pain, post-trauma headache, or febrile headache.偏前额、颞侧或伴情志紧张、睡眠不佳的头痛可选本神配风池、太冲；突发剧痛、外伤后或全身发热伴头痛不选本神作首选处理。

Dizziness and vertigo眩晕 ⚖️

Consider GB13 for dizziness accompanied by insomnia, constrained Yangming/Shaoyang qi, or frontal fullness; seek medical evaluation first for severe spinning vertigo with hearing loss, ataxia, or hypertensive crisis.头晕目眩伴失眠多梦、少阳气郁或头侧发胀时可选本神；剧烈旋转性眩晕伴耳聋耳鸣、共济失调或血压危象先就医排查。

Insomnia不寐（失眠） ⚖️

Use as an adjunctive point (such as with HT7, SP6, and GV20) for trouble falling asleep or light sleep with restlessness, lightheadedness, or low mood; severe sleep apnea or major depression requires formal medical care.入睡困难或浅眠伴心烦、头昏、情志不舒时可作辅助配穴（如配神门、三阴交、百会）；严重睡眠呼吸暂停或重度抑郁需规范医疗评估。

Tension-type headache and frontal myofascial tension紧张型头痛与前额肌筋膜紧张

Sleep-disturbance-related lightheadedness and fatigue睡眠障碍相关头昏与疲劳
