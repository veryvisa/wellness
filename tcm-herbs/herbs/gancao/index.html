How to recognise it怎么认

Growing植株 A perennial herb 30–120 cm tall, glandular-hairy; odd-pinnate leaves; pale purple pea-type flowers in summer and curved pods. The root and rhizome are long and thick, reaching metres into the soil.多年生草本，高 30–120 厘米，全株有腺毛；奇数羽状复叶；夏季开淡紫色蝶形花，结弯曲的荚果。地下根和根茎粗长，可深入土中数米。

Decoction pieces饮片 Round or oval thick slices; red-brown or grey-brown wrinkled skin; yellowish-white, starchy cut face with a clear cambium ring, radiating lines and clefts. Faint smell, distinctively sweet. Honey-fried slices are deep yellow and slightly sticky.类圆形或椭圆形厚片；外皮红棕色或灰棕色，有纵皱纹；切面黄白色，粉性，形成层环明显，有放射状纹理和裂隙。气微，味甜而特殊。炙甘草片表面深黄色，略有黏性。

Processing炮制

  • Raw Gan Cao生甘草：: Cooler; clears heat and toxins, dispels phlegm偏凉，清热解毒、祛痰止咳
  • Honey-fried Gan Cao (Zhi Gan Cao)炙甘草（蜜炙）：: Warmer; supplements the spleen and restores the pulse; the 'fried' licorice of the Shanghan Lun formulas偏温，补脾和胃、益气复脉；伤寒方中注「炙」者多为此

Cautions and incompatibilities使用注意与配伍禁忌

Not for fullness from dampness or for oedema; not with Hai Zao, Jing Da Ji, Hong Da Ji, Gan Sui or Yuan Hua (the eighteen incompatibilities); large or long-term doses cause salt and water retention, swelling, raised blood pressure and low potassium. Avoid long-term large doses with high blood pressure, heart failure, kidney disease or low potassium.湿盛胀满、水肿者不宜用；不宜与海藻、京大戟、红大戟、甘遂、芫花同用（十八反）；大剂量或长期服用可致水钠潴留、浮肿、血压升高、低血钾。高血压、心衰、肾病、低钾者避免长期大量。

Interactions with medicines与西药的相互作用

  • E3 Thiazide and loop diuretics (e.g. hydrochlorothiazide, furosemide)噻嗪类与袢利尿剂（如氢氯噻嗪、呋塞米）：: Both waste potassium; together the risk of low potassium adds up.两者都失钾，合用低血钾风险叠加。
  • E3 Digoxin地高辛：: Licorice lowers potassium, and low potassium raises the risk of digoxin toxicity.甘草致低血钾，低血钾增加地高辛中毒风险。
  • E3 Blood-pressure medicines; corticosteroids降压药、皮质类固醇：: Glycyrrhizin inhibits 11β-HSD2, producing pseudo-aldosteronism: raised blood pressure and sodium retention, working against blood-pressure drugs and amplifying the mineralocorticoid effect of steroids.甘草酸抑制 11β-羟类固醇脱氢酶，表现为假性醛固酮增多：升压、留钠，削弱降压药，放大激素的盐皮质作用。

Modern research, graded现代研究（分级）

  • E2 Cases and mechanism of high blood pressure and low potassium from long-term heavy licorice (including licorice candy) are well established; it is licorice's most solid modern evidence, and it is safety evidence.长期大量吃甘草（包括甘草糖）致高血压、低血钾的病例与机制都很明确，是甘草最可靠的现代证据——而且是安全证据。
  • E4 Anti-inflammatory and antiviral findings for glycyrrhizin and glycyrrhetinic acid come mostly from lab and animal work; they echo the traditional 'clears heat and toxins' but do not support efficacy claims.甘草酸、甘草次酸的抗炎、抗病毒等作用多来自实验室与动物研究，与「清热解毒」的传统说法相呼应，但不足以支持疗效主张。

In the classics古籍原文

「甘草味甘平。主治五臟六腑寒熱邪氣，堅筋骨，長肌肉，倍力，金創，尰，解毒」——神农本草经

Shennong Bencao Jing (Divine Farmer's Materia Medica)

The Shennong Bencao Jing ranks Gan Cao in its top grade; 'resolves toxins' is the seed of the later 'harmonises all herbs, resolves the poison of a hundred drugs'. Tao Hongjing called it the 'state elder', the respected minister who keeps the other herbs in order.《神农本草经》列甘草为上品，「解毒」二字是后世「调和诸药」「解百药毒」的源头；陶弘景称它「国老」，说它在方中像德高望重的老臣调停诸药。
