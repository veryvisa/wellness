Restore the Spleen Decoction归脾汤

Guī Pí Tāng · Yan's Jisheng Fang by Yan Yonghe (Song; addition of Dang Gui and Yuan Zhi first recorded in Xue Ji's Jiaozhu Furen Liangfang); anchor text from Wang Ang's Tangtou Gejue (with reference to Yifang Jijie)宋·严用和《严氏济生方》（加当归、远志始见于明·薛己《校注妇人良方》）；原文锚点取清·汪昂《汤头歌诀》（并参《医方集解》）

Herbs & formulas中药与方剂 › Restore the Spleen Decoction归脾汤

Original text原文

「治思虑過度，勞傷心脾，怔忡健忘，惊悸盜汗，发熱体倦，食少不眠；或脾虛不能摄血，致血妄行，及婦人經带」——医方集解

Yifang Jijie (Collected Explanations of Formulas)

Indicated for overthinking damaging the heart and spleen, manifesting as severe palpitations, forgetfulness, fright palpitations, night sweats, feverishness with fatigue, poor appetite, and insomnia; or spleen deficiency failing to contain blood, leading to erratic bleeding, as well as women's menstrual and leukorrheal disorders.治思虑过度导致心脾两伤，出现心跳剧烈、健忘、受惊心悸、盗汗、发热疲倦、食欲减退及失眠；或因脾气虚弱不能统摄血液，引起血不循经而妄行出血，以及妇女月经不调、带下等症。

「人參、白朮(土炒)、茯神、棗仁、龍眼肉各二錢，黃耆(蜜炙)一錢半、當歸(酒洗)、遠志各一錢，木香、甘草(炙)各八分。○血不歸脾則妄行，參、耆、草、朮之甘溫以補脾，志、茯、棗仁、龍眼之甘溫酸苦以補心，當歸養血，木香調氣，氣壯則自能攝血矣。」——汤头歌诀

Tangtou Gejue (Formula Verses)

Composition: Ren Shen, earth-fried Bai Zhu, Fu Shen, Suan Zao Ren, and Long Yan Rou, two qian each; honey-fried Huang Qi, one and a half qian; wine-washed Dang Gui and Yuan Zhi, one qian each; Mu Xiang and honey-fried Gan Cao, eight fen each. Rationale: When blood fails to return to the spleen, it moves erratically; the sweet-warm Ren Shen, Huang Qi, Gan Cao, and Bai Zhu supplement the spleen, while Yuan Zhi, Fu Shen, Suan Zao Ren, and Long Yan Rou nourish the heart; Dang Gui nourishes blood and Mu Xiang regulates qi, so strong qi naturally contains the blood.组成：人参、土炒白术、茯神、酸枣仁、龙眼肉各二钱，蜜炙黄芪一钱半，酒洗当归、远志各一钱，木香、炙甘草各八分。方义：血不归脾就会溢出脉道妄行；用人参、黄芪、甘草、白术甘温补益脾气，用远志、茯神、酸枣仁、龙眼肉甘温酸苦补养心神，当归补血，木香理气醒脾，脾气强壮自然能够统摄血液。

Cautions使用注意

Contraindicated in yin deficiency with effulgent fire, interior excess heat, damp-heat obstruction, or bleeding, insomnia, and palpitations caused by qi stagnation and blood stasis; seek immediate medical attention to rule out organic pathology if melena, hematemesis, unceasing uterine bleeding, severe anemia, chest pain, or syncope occurs.阴虚火旺、实热内盛、湿热内阻及气滞血瘀所致的出血、失眠或心悸禁用；若出现便血黑便、吐血、崩漏不止、严重贫血或胸痛晕厥，必须立即就医查明器质性病变。

Caution in pregnancy孕妇慎用 Huang Qi, Ren Shen, Mu Xiang, Gan Cao, and Dang Gui all carry a pregnancy caution on this site (with Dang Gui following Health Canada's blanket warning and herbs such as Ren Shen and Mu Xiang requiring caution during pregnancy), so the formula takes the strictest level of its herbs and requires professional supervision during pregnancy.黄芪、人参、木香、甘草、当归在本站均按孕期慎用（其中当归按加拿大卫生部统一警示从严，人参、木香等教材或临床提示孕期慎用），方随其最严者，孕妇须在执业中医师及产科医生评估指导下使用。
