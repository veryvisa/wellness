ST25

天枢 Tiānshū

Celestial Pivot · Take care需谨慎

Front-mu point of the Large Intestine大肠募穴

📍 Location定位★

On the abdomen, level with the centre of the umbilicus, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.腹部，横平脐中，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

💡 Lie supine with the knees bent; set the level by proportional measure (xiphisternal junction to umbilicus 8 cun, umbilicus to pubic symphysis 5 cun), then measure 2 cun from the midline (the Kidney channel is at 0.5 cun).仰卧屈膝放松腹壁；按骨度（胸剑联合至脐 8 寸、脐至耻骨联合上缘 5 寸）定纵向位置，再从前正中线横量 2 寸（任脉旁开 2 寸，肾经在 0.5 寸）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–1.5 cun1–1.5 寸

Forbidden in pregnancy.孕期禁针。

🛡 Safety安全

Deep insertion through the peritoneum can injure the small intestine (the abdominal aorta lies deep behind the midline); go shallower with a thin wall, distension or surgical scars. Forbidden in pregnancy. Acute abdominal pain or rebound tenderness needs medical care, not needling.深刺穿过腹膜可伤小肠（深部腹主动脉在中线后方）；腹壁薄、腹胀或有腹部手术瘢痕时减浅。孕期禁针。急性腹痛、反跳痛先就医，不取穴。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治★

Abdominal pain, distension, constipation, diarrhoea, dysentery腹痛、腹胀、便秘、腹泻、痢疾 ⚖️Irregular menstruation and dysmenorrhoea月经不调、痛经

  • With BL25: back-shu–front-mu pairing配大肠俞（BL25）：俞募配穴

🃏 Flip cards翻卡自测 · 4

Where is ST25 Tiānshū?天枢（ST25）在哪里？

On the abdomen, level with the centre of the umbilicus, 2 cun lateral to the anterior midline.腹部，横平脐中，前正中线旁开 2 寸。

How is ST25 needled?天枢（ST25）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 1–1.5 cun.直刺；1–1.5 寸。

Special-point roles of ST25?天枢（ST25）是哪类特定穴？

Front-mu point of the Large Intestine大肠募穴

Main safety point at ST25?天枢（ST25）最要注意的安全问题？

Deep insertion through the peritoneum can injure the small intestine (the abdominal aorta lies deep behind the midline); go shallower with a thin wall, distension or surgical scars. Forbidden in pregnancy. Acute abdominal pain or rebound tenderness needs medical care, not needling.深刺穿过腹膜可伤小肠（深部腹主动脉在中线后方）；腹壁薄、腹胀或有腹部手术瘢痕时减浅。孕期禁针。急性腹痛、反跳痛先就医，不取穴。
