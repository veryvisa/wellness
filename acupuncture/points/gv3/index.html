GV3

腰阳关 Yāoyángguān

Lumbar Yang Gate · Take care需谨慎

📍 Location定位★

In the lumbar region, in the depression below the spinous process of L4, on the posterior midline.脊柱区，第 4 腰椎棘突下凹陷中，后正中线上。

💡 The line between the iliac crest tops is roughly level with the L4 spinous process; confirm by counting.两髂嵴最高点连线约平第 4 腰椎棘突，确认后再逐节核对。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 0.5–1 cun0.5–1 寸

Lumbar spinous processes are nearly horizontal, so perpendicular or slightly upward; moxibustion is often used.腰椎棘突近水平，可直刺或略向上斜刺；多用灸法。

🛡 Safety安全

Through the ligamentum flavum lies the canal and cauda equina; withdraw at once on a sudden give or an electric sensation down the legs. Avoid the lumbosacral region after 3 months of pregnancy.穿过黄韧带即入椎管、触及马尾；有落空感或下肢放射麻电感立即退针。妊娠 3 个月以上腰骶部不宜针。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治★

📍 Finding it: several methods, cross-checked多法定位：几种方法互相校核

Posture体位 · Prone or seated leaning forward, with the back relaxed so the lumbar spinous processes can be identified.俯卧或坐位前屈，使腰背肌放松并便于辨认腰椎棘突。

  1. 🦴
    Surface landmark体表标志

    Locate the midline depression below the L4 spinous process. The iliac-crest line is approximately at L4 but may vary by a vertebral level, so count the spinous processes.定位第 4 腰椎棘突下方的正中凹陷；两侧髂嵴最高点连线约平 L4，但个体差异可达一个椎体，须沿棘突逐节核对。

  2. 🔬
    Anatomy解剖结构

    Palpate the lumbar spinous processes and the interspinous soft tissue; the point is midline between L4 and L5. The spinal canal and neural structures are deep to the interspinous space; avoid deep direction toward the canal.触及腰椎棘突及其间的棘间软组织，穴在 L4 与 L5 棘突间正中；棘突间深部为椎管及神经结构，局部操作不得朝椎管深刺。

  3. 👆
    Palpation check触诊确认

    Flexion makes the interspinous spaces easier to feel; recheck the midline in a relaxed position. Scoliosis, obesity or prior surgery makes iliac-crest estimation alone unreliable.前屈时棘突间隙较易触认，回到放松位复核正中；脊柱侧弯、肥胖或既往手术时不能仅凭髂嵴线推定椎节。

⚠️ Common mistakes常见取穴误差

  • Treating the iliac-crest line as a fixed L4–L5 level can miss by a vertebra; excessive depth or a canal-directed approach risks neural structures.把髂嵴连线机械地当作 L4–L5 间隙会因个体差异错开一节；过深或偏向椎管方向可能危及神经结构。

Low-back pain腰痛 ⚖️

Consider for midline L4–L5 mechanical pain without neurologic red flags; coccygeal pain is lower at GV1/GV2, while leg numbness or weakness requires nerve-root assessment.定位于 L4–L5 正中且无神经红旗的机械性腰痛可考虑；骶尾痛选更低位 GV1/GV2，腿麻无力先查神经根。

🩺 Modern medicine: conditions and pain around this spot现代医学：这个部位常见的病痛

Nonspecific mechanical low-back pain非特异性机械性腰痛

Often load- or posture-related without neurologic deficits; leg radiation, weakness or sensory change warrants assessment for nerve-root involvement.姿势或负荷相关、无神经缺损者常见；腿部放射痛、肌力或感觉改变提示需评估神经根受累。

Disc- or facet-related lumbar pain腰椎间盘或椎间关节相关疼痛

Spinous-region tenderness alone cannot identify the pain generator; use history and neurologic examination, with imaging as clinically indicated.单凭棘突旁压痛不能判断结构来源；需结合病史、神经检查，影像按临床需要决定。

🚩 See a doctor先就医
  • Bowel/bladder changes, saddle numbness, progressive leg weakness or fever with back pain: emergency assessment.大小便异常、会阴麻木、进行性下肢无力，或发热伴腰痛：急诊评估。
  • Major trauma, new persistent pain with cancer history or unexplained weight loss: prompt medical review.重大外伤、癌症史伴新发持续腰痛或不明原因体重下降：尽快就医。
