Where is SI16 Tiānchuāng?天窗（SI16）在哪里？

On the lateral neck, level with the laryngeal prominence, at the posterior border of the sternocleidomastoid.颈部，横平喉结，胸锁乳突肌后缘。

How is SI16 needled?天窗（SI16）怎么刺？

Perpendicular; 0.5–1 cun.直刺；0.5–1 寸。

Special-point roles of SI16?天窗（SI16）是哪类特定穴？

Point of the Small Intestine channel手太阳小肠经穴

Main safety point at SI16?天窗（SI16）最要注意的安全问题？

The carotid sheath lies deep anteromedially: never needle toward it or over a pulse. Avoid the external jugular vein first; the cervical plexus branches surface here, so withdraw for shooting tingling.颈动脉鞘在前内侧深面：不向前内侧或搏动处进针；先避开颈外静脉，颈丛皮支在此浅出，窜麻即退针。
