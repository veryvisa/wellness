Low-back pain腰痛

The textbook prescription for low-back pain is a shared core — BL23, BL25, BL40 and ashi points — with points added for cold-damp, blood stasis or kidney deficiency; midline pain belongs to the Governor vessel and adds SI3, pain beside the spine belongs to the Foot Taiyang and adds BL62. A sudden 'ricked back' is a separate section, treated mainly with distal points while the patient moves the back. The dangers of back pain are rarely in the back itself: changes in bladder, bowel or saddle sensation, fever, a cancer history, or a pulsating abdominal mass in an older person must be asked about before any pattern differentiation.腰痛的教材处方是一组共用主穴——肾俞、大肠俞、委中加阿是穴——再按寒湿、瘀血、肾虚加配穴；痛在脊柱正中属督脉，加后溪，痛在脊柱两旁属足太阳，加申脉。突然扭伤的「闪腰」另成一节，以远端穴配合腰部活动为主。腰痛的危险不在腰本身：大小便和会阴感觉的改变、发热、肿瘤史、老人的腹部搏动包块，都比辨证更先要问。

🚩 See a doctor first if…先就医的情况

  • New difficulty passing urine or urinary incontinence, faecal incontinence, numbness of the groin and inner thighs (saddle area), or worsening weakness or numbness in both legs: emergency now for possible cauda equina syndrome, which may need surgical decompression within hours新出现的排尿困难或尿失禁、大便失禁、会阴和大腿内侧（骑马鞍区）麻木、双腿无力或麻木加重：立刻急诊，可能是马尾综合征，需要在数小时内手术减压
  • Over 50, a smoker or with high blood pressure, sudden severe tearing back or abdominal pain, a pulsating abdominal mass, or dizziness, fainting and pallor: call 911 for a possible ruptured abdominal aortic aneurysm50 岁以上、吸烟或高血压者突发剧烈撕裂样腰背痛或腹痛，摸到腹部搏动性包块，或伴头晕、晕厥、面色苍白：拨 911，警惕腹主动脉瘤破裂
  • Back pain with fever or chills, or after IV drug use, recent surgery or with a weak immune system; a history of cancer, unexplained weight loss, or night pain at rest that does not ease: same-day care to rule out spinal infection or metastasis腰痛伴发热、寒战，或有静脉用药、近期手术、免疫低下；有癌症史、不明原因消瘦、夜间静息痛不缓解：当天就医，排除脊柱感染或肿瘤转移
  • Back pain after major trauma, or severe pain after a minor fall or bend in someone with osteoporosis or on long-term steroids: emergency imaging for vertebral fracture严重外伤后腰痛，或骨质疏松、长期服激素的人轻微跌倒、弯腰后剧痛：急诊拍片，排除椎体骨折
  • Colicky one-sided flank pain spreading to the lower abdomen or groin, restlessness, blood in the urine; or back pain with high fever, urinary burning and frequency, and tenderness at the rib–spine angle: same-day care for kidney stones or pyelonephritis一侧腰部绞痛向小腹或腹股沟放射、坐立不安、血尿；或腰痛伴高热、尿频尿痛、肋脊角叩痛：当天就医，排除肾结石、肾盂肾炎
  • Back pain in pregnancy with vaginal bleeding, regular contractions or leaking fluid: contact obstetrics now; lumbosacral points are not needled in pregnancy孕期腰痛伴阴道出血、规律宫缩或流液：立即联系产科；孕妇腰骶部穴位禁针

「腰背委中求」——高武《针灸聚英》（明，公版，维基文库底本）

Zhenjiu juying, Song of the Four Command Points: for the lower back, seek BL40. BL40 is the Foot Taiyang he-sea point, and the Taiyang channel runs down beside the spine through the lower back into the popliteal fossa, so it is core in every kind of back pain; in blood stasis its superficial veins can be pricked to bleed.《针灸聚英·四总穴歌》：腰背的病找委中。委中是足太阳合穴，太阳经夹脊下行过腰、入腘窝，所以腰痛无论寒湿、瘀血还是扭伤都以委中为主穴；瘀血证可在委中浅表静脉点刺出血。

「腰者腎之府，轉搖不能，腎將憊矣」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.17: the lower back is the residence of the kidney; when it cannot turn or sway, the kidney is becoming exhausted. This is the basis for treating chronic back pain through the kidney with BL23, adding GV4 for kidney-yang or KI3 for kidney-yin deficiency.《素问·脉要精微论》：腰是肾的府舍，腰转动摇摆不灵，说明肾气将要衰惫。这是慢性腰痛从肾论治、取肾俞并按肾阳虚（命门）或肾阴虚（太溪）配穴的理论依据。

「足太陽脈，令人腰痛，引項脊尻背如重狀」——《黄帝内经·素问》（公版，维基文库底本）

Suwen ch.41: disease of the Foot Taiyang channel causes back pain pulling on the neck, spine and buttocks as if carrying a heavy load. Pain beside the spine spreading to the neck, back or buttocks therefore belongs to the Foot Taiyang, treated along the channel at BL40, BL62 and BL60.《素问·刺腰痛》：足太阳经的病会引起腰痛，牵连颈项、脊背和臀部，像背着重物一样。说明痛在脊柱两旁、牵连到项背和臀部的，属足太阳经证，循经取委中、申脉、昆仑。

Low-back pain腰痛

Cold-damp back pain寒湿腰痛

How it presents辨证要点 Cold, heavy back pain with difficulty turning, gradually worsening; brought on or worsened by wet weather, cold or damp living, not eased by lying still but eased by warmth; white greasy coating, deep slow pulse.腰部冷痛沉重，转侧不利，逐渐加重；阴雨天、受寒或久居湿地后发作或加重，静卧痛不减，得温则舒；苔白腻，脉沉而迟缓。

Principle治法 Dispel cold and damp, warm and free the channels (drain or even method; generous moxa, warm needle, cupping)散寒除湿，温经通络（泻法或平补平泻，重灸、温针、拔罐）

Main主穴BL23 Shènshū肾俞BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞BL40 Weizhong委中
Add配穴GV4 Mìngmén命门GV3 Yāoyángguān腰阳关

💡 Core for back pain: BL23 (kidney back-shu), BL25 (local), BL40 ('for the back seek BL40') plus ashi points. For cold-damp add GV4 and GV3, Governor-vessel points moxaed generously to warm yang and disperse cold.腰痛主穴：肾俞（肾的背俞穴）、大肠俞（腰部局部）、委中（「腰背委中求」）加阿是穴。寒湿配命门、腰阳关，督脉穴重灸以温阳散寒。

Low-back pain腰痛

Blood-stasis back pain瘀血腰痛

How it presents辨证要点 Stabbing fixed back pain that resists pressure, lighter by day and worse at night, with difficulty turning or bending; often after injury, strain or long sitting; dark purple tongue or stasis marks, choppy pulse.腰痛如刺，痛处固定拒按，日轻夜重，转侧俯仰困难；多有外伤、劳损或久坐史；舌暗紫或有瘀斑，脉涩。

Principle治法 Move blood, transform stasis, free the collaterals and stop pain (drain; the superficial veins at BL40 and ashi points may be bled and cupped)活血化瘀，通络止痛（泻法；委中浅表静脉、阿是穴可刺络拔罐）

Main主穴BL23 Shènshū肾俞BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞BL40 Weizhong委中
Add配穴BL17 Géshū膈俞BL32 Cìliáo次髎

💡 Same core; BL17, the influential point of blood, moves stasis. BL40 is also called the 'blood cleft', and in blood-stasis back pain the visible superficial veins there are pricked to bleed — superficial veins only, never deep into the popliteal fossa. The textbook also adds BL32 for stasis in the lumbosacral area; contraindicated in pregnancy.主穴同上；膈俞是血会，活血化瘀。委中又称血郄，瘀血腰痛常在委中处显露的浅静脉点刺放血，只刺浅静脉，不深刺腘窝。教材瘀血证另配次髎，通腰骶局部瘀滞；孕妇禁针。

Kidney-deficiency back pain (kidney yang)肾虚腰痛（肾阳虚）

How it presents辨证要点 Sore, weak, dull back pain that likes pressure and rubbing, weak legs and knees, worse with exertion and better lying down, recurring; tight lower abdomen, cool hands and feet, pale face; pale tongue, deep thin weak pulse.腰部酸软隐痛，喜按喜揉，腿膝无力，遇劳更甚、卧则减轻，反复发作；少腹拘急，手足不温，面色㿠白；舌淡，脉沉细无力。

Principle治法 Warm and tonify kidney yang, strengthen the back (tonify; generous moxa)温补肾阳，强腰（补法，重灸）

Main主穴BL23 Shènshū肾俞BL25 Dàchángshū大肠俞BL40 Weizhong委中
Add配穴GV4 Mìngmén命门BL52 Zhìshì志室

💡 Same core; for kidney-yang deficiency add GV4, which lies between the two BL23 points and warms kidney yang, usually with moxa. Chronic back pain rests on exercise, with acupuncture in support. The textbook also adds BL52, level with BL23, to strengthen the kidney.主穴同上；肾阳虚配命门，命门在两肾俞之间，温补肾阳，多用灸。慢性腰痛以运动锻炼为本，针灸配合。教材肾虚另配志室，与肾俞并列，加强补肾。

Principle治法 Nourish kidney yin, strengthen the back (tonify; no heavy moxa)滋补肾阴，强腰（补法，不宜重灸）

Add配穴KI3 Tàixī太溪BL52 Zhìshì志室

💡 Same core; for kidney-yin deficiency add KI3, the Kidney yuan and shu-stream point, to nourish kidney yin. With deficiency heat, heavy moxa on GV4 is avoided. The textbook also adds BL52, level with BL23, to strengthen the kidney.主穴同上；肾阴虚配太溪，太溪是肾经原穴、输穴，滋肾阴。阴虚有热，不用命门重灸。教材肾虚另配志室，与肾俞并列，加强补肾。

Acute back sprain急性腰扭伤

Acute low-back sprain ('ricked back')急性腰扭伤（闪腰）

How it presents辨证要点 Sudden strain while lifting, bending or twisting, with immediate severe pain and restricted movement — unable to bend or turn — and local muscle spasm and tenderness; no leg numbness or weakness and no bladder or bowel change.搬重物、弯腰转身时突然扭伤，当即腰痛剧烈、活动受限，不能俯仰转侧，局部肌肉痉挛压痛；没有下肢麻木无力和大小便改变。

Principle治法 Relax the sinews, move blood, free the collaterals and stop pain (drain; needle distal points first with strong stimulation while the patient slowly moves the back; BL40 may be bled)舒筋活血，通络止痛（泻法；先刺远端穴，强刺激时嘱患者缓慢活动腰部，委中可点刺出血）

Main主穴BL40 Weizhong委中SI3 Hòuxī后溪
Add配穴GV26 Shuǐgōu水沟BL60 Kūnlún昆仑

💡 Textbook core for acute back sprain: ashi points, Yaotongdian (an extra point on the back of the hand, no code here), BL40 and SI3. Turning the distal needles while the patient moves the back often brings relief on the spot; for midline Governor-vessel pain add GV26, which is a very strong stimulus and used cautiously in frail patients or those prone to fainting. For pain beside the spine on the Foot Taiyang add BL60.教材急性腰扭伤主穴：阿是穴、腰痛点（手背经外奇穴，本站无代码）、委中、后溪。远端穴边捻转边活动腰部，常当场缓解；痛在正中督脉加水沟（人中），水沟针感强，体弱、晕针者慎用。痛在脊旁足太阳经的加昆仑。

BL40 Weizhong委中

Best for最适合 Distal core point for all back pain; bled in acute sprain and blood stasis各型腰痛的远端主穴，急性扭伤、瘀血腰痛可点刺出血

BL62 Shēnmài申脉

Best for最适合 Pain beside the spine pulling into the buttock and back of the leg (Foot Taiyang type)脊旁痛牵连臀腿后侧（足太阳经证）

Why依据 Foot Taiyang point in the hollow directly below the lateral malleolus, confluent point of the Yang Heel vessel足太阳经穴，外踝尖直下凹陷，八脉交会通阳跷脉

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Covers pain beside the spine while SI3 covers the midline; both are confluent points and are often paired upper and lower; 0.3–0.5 cun与后溪分管脊旁和正中，二穴同为八脉交会、常上下配用；直刺 0.3–0.5 寸

BL60 Kūnlún昆仑

Best for最适合 Back pain extending to the heel and back of the calf (along the Taiyang)腰痛连足跟、小腿后侧（太阳经循行）

Why依据 Foot Taiyang jing-river point in the hollow between the lateral malleolus tip and the Achilles tendon足太阳经穴，外踝尖与跟腱之间凹陷

Versus others与其他穴的区别 More for pain pulling into the calf and heel than BL62; contraindicated in pregnancy (classically used for difficult labour)比申脉偏小腿足跟的牵痛；孕妇禁针（古籍主难产）

GV26 Shuǐgōu水沟

Best for最适合 Acute back sprain with midline pain and inability to bend急性腰扭伤痛在脊柱正中、不能俯仰

Why依据 Shuigou (Renzhong) on the Governor vessel; added for midline acute back sprain水沟（人中），督脉；急性腰扭伤督脉证配穴

Versus others与其他穴的区别 A much stronger stimulus than SI3 and stopped once it helps; 0.3–0.5 cun obliquely upward, quite painful, used cautiously in frail or faint-prone patients and not for chronic pain比后溪刺激更强，见效后即止；向上斜刺 0.3–0.5 寸，疼痛明显，体弱、晕针者慎用，慢性腰痛不用

BL52 Zhìshì志室

Best for最适合 Kidney-deficiency back pain, aching at the outer edges of the lumbar muscles肾虚腰痛、腰部两侧外缘酸痛

Why依据 Zhishi, 3 cun lateral to the L2 spinous process, level with BL23志室，第 2 腰椎棘突下旁开 3 寸，与肾俞同一水平

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Lateral to BL23 and often paired with it to strengthen the kidney; the kidney lies right beneath, so only shallow oblique toward the spine, 0.5–0.8 cun; not needled in pregnancy比肾俞偏外，常与肾俞同用加强补肾；深部紧邻肾脏，只向脊柱方向浅斜刺 0.5–0.8 寸；孕妇腰部禁针

Best for最适合 Lumbosacral pain, pain over the sacroiliac area腰骶部痛、骶髂关节区痛

Versus others与其他穴的区别 Lower than BL25 and more for the sacrum; the needle path points straight at the sacral foramen and nerves, so direction and depth must follow bony landmarks; contraindicated in pregnancy比大肠俞更低、偏骶部；针路直指骶后孔与骶神经，须按骨性标志控制方向深度；孕妇禁针

🩺 The modern-medicine view现代医学视角

About 90% of back pain is 'non-specific': no single structural cause is found, most cases improve markedly within six weeks, and staying active, heat and graded exercise speed recovery more than bed rest, with no need for early imaging. About 5–10% is radicular pain (a disc herniation or stenosis compressing a nerve root), with pain past the knee in a dermatome and a positive straight-leg raise; under 1% has a specific cause needing urgent care — cauda equina syndrome, spinal infection, metastasis, vertebral fracture or abdominal aortic aneurysm. Referred pain from kidney stones, pyelonephritis, gynaecological or pancreatic disease must also be considered, as must inflammatory back pain such as ankylosing spondylitis (onset before 45, morning stiffness over 30 minutes, better with activity, waking in the second half of the night). Disc bulges and degeneration on imaging are common in adults without symptoms and cannot simply be taken as the cause. Acupuncture has moderate-quality evidence for chronic back pain, North American guidelines list it among non-drug options, and it fits alongside exercise therapy.90% 左右的腰痛属于「非特异性腰痛」：查不出单一结构原因，多在 6 周内明显好转，保持活动、热敷、循序锻炼比卧床恢复快，早期不需要拍片。约 5%–10% 是神经根性疼痛（腰椎间盘突出或椎管狭窄压迫神经根），痛过膝、按皮节分布，直腿抬高试验阳性；不到 1% 是需要紧急处理的特异性病因：马尾综合征、脊柱感染、肿瘤转移、椎体骨折、腹主动脉瘤。此外还要鉴别肾结石、肾盂肾炎、妇科和胰腺疾病的牵涉痛，以及强直性脊柱炎这类炎性腰痛（45 岁前起病、晨僵超过 30 分钟、活动后减轻、半夜痛醒）。腰椎影像上的椎间盘膨出、退变在无症状成年人中也很常见，不能直接当作疼痛原因。针刺对慢性腰痛有中等质量证据，北美的临床指南把它列为非药物治疗选项之一，适合与运动疗法同时使用。
