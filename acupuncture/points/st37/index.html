ST37

上巨虚 Shàngjùxū

Upper Great Void · Lower risk较安全

Lower he-sea point of the Large Intestine大肠下合穴

📍 Location定位★

On the anterolateral leg, 6 cun below ST35, on the line connecting ST35 with ST41.小腿外侧，犊鼻下 6 寸，犊鼻与解溪连线上。

💡 3 cun below ST36, one finger-breadth lateral to the tibial crest. Knee flexed: measure down from ST35 along the ST35–ST41 line using the 16-cun span from the popliteal crease (patellar apex level) to the lateral malleolus, about one middle-finger breadth lateral to the anterior tibial crest.足三里下 3 寸，胫骨前嵴外一横指；屈膝，从犊鼻（外膝眼）沿犊鼻与解溪连线向下，按腘横纹（平髌尖）至外踝尖 16 寸折量；距胫骨前嵴外侧约一横指（中指）。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Perpendicular直刺 · 1–2 cun1–2 寸

🛡 Safety安全

The anterior tibial vessels and deep fibular nerve lie deep: redirect for tingling to the dorsum of the foot, and do not drive along the tibial surface.深部有胫前动静脉与腓深神经，出现向足背放射的麻感退针调整；不要贴胫骨骨面直刺。

🏛 Traditional uses传统主治

Borborygmus, abdominal pain, diarrhoea, constipation, intestinal abscess肠鸣、腹痛、腹泻、便秘、肠痈 ⚖️Leg weakness and numbness下肢痿痹
