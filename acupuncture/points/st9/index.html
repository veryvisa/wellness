📍 Location定位★

On the neck, level with the laryngeal prominence, at the anterior border of sternocleidomastoid, over the common carotid artery pulse.颈部，横平喉结，胸锁乳突肌前缘，颈总动脉搏动处。

💡 Find the laryngeal prominence and move laterally to the front border of sternocleidomastoid; the pulse there is the common carotid — the point is at the pulse, and the artery must be pushed aside for insertion.先摸喉结，再向外到胸锁乳突肌前缘，指下的搏动即颈总动脉；穴在搏动处，进针时必须把动脉推开。

🪡 Needling刺法★

Push the common carotid artery laterally with the guiding hand and insert perpendicularly away from it押手把颈总动脉推向外侧，避开动脉直刺 · 0.3–0.8 cun0.3–0.8 寸

No lifting-thrusting or strong stimulation; no moxibustion (Jiayi Jing); never press both sides firmly at once.不提插、不重刺激；禁灸（《甲乙经》）；禁双侧同时重按。

🛡 Safety安全★

Stimulating the carotid sinus can drop heart rate and blood pressure and cause fainting; puncturing the carotid or jugular can form a neck haematoma that compresses the airway — the Jiayi Jing warns that going too deep can kill.颈动脉窦受刺激可致心率骤降、血压下降和晕厥，刺破颈总动脉或颈内静脉可致颈部血肿压迫气道；《甲乙经》即警告「過深不幸殺人」。

Where is ST9 Rényíng?人迎（ST9）在哪里？

On the neck, level with the laryngeal prominence, at the anterior border of sternocleidomastoid, over the common carotid artery pulse.颈部，横平喉结，胸锁乳突肌前缘，颈总动脉搏动处。

How is ST9 needled?人迎（ST9）怎么刺？

Push the common carotid artery laterally with the guiding hand and insert perpendicularly away from it; 0.3–0.8 cun.押手把颈总动脉推向外侧，避开动脉直刺；0.3–0.8 寸。

Special-point roles of ST9?人迎（ST9）是哪类特定穴？

Crossing point of the Stomach and Gallbladder channels; One of the Window of Heaven points足阳明、足少阳交会穴；天牖五部（天窗穴）之一

Main safety point at ST9?人迎（ST9）最要注意的安全问题？

Stimulating the carotid sinus can drop heart rate and blood pressure and cause fainting; puncturing the carotid or jugular can form a neck haematoma that compresses the airway — the Jiayi Jing warns that going too deep can kill.颈动脉窦受刺激可致心率骤降、血压下降和晕厥，刺破颈总动脉或颈内静脉可致颈部血肿压迫气道；《甲乙经》即警告「過深不幸殺人」。
